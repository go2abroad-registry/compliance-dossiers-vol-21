Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; e-portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (www.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A Panama Sociedad Anónima must first be registered in the Registro Público de Panamá and obtain an incorporation certificate. Thereafter, the S.A. or its resident agent files the Ficha de Registro Único de Contribuyente through the DGI Sede Electrónica, submitting: (i) authenticated/legalized copy of the certificate of incorporation and articles; (ii) corporate resolution appointing legal representatives; (iii) copies of identification/passports of officers; (iv) proof of local fiscal domicile; and (v) appointment of a Panamanian resident agent. The DGI issues a permanent RUC number covering both income tax and ITBMS, and activates the electronic invoicing mandate. If the S.A. has physical commercial operations in Panama, an Aviso de Operación must also be filed with the corresponding municipality and, where employees exist, registration with CSS is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation with Territorial_Scope; a Sociedad Anónima is a separate juridical taxpayer taxable on Panama-source income only and is not a pass-through entity. Tax-exempt zone treatment applies only to entities specifically licensed and operating within Panama free-trade or special economic zones.

Available election forms

Not applicable. Panama does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity election regime. A Panama S.A. is mandatorily classified as a separate taxable juridical person; elections are limited to territorial-source attribution determinations and, where applicable, consolidated tax group treatment under DGI procedures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis turnover threshold applies to an S.A.; ITBMS registration is required before the first taxable supply of goods or services in Panama. A small-taxpayer threshold of approximately B/.18,000 annual gross receipts exists for natural persons, but is unavailable to the PA_CORP S.A.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The exportation of goods from Panama and certain services rendered to non-residents may be zero-rated for ITBMS if the goods physically leave Panamanian territory and are exported to a foreign destination outside Panamanian customs jurisdiction, evidenced by the customs export declaration; exported services qualify when performed from Panama for a non-resident and used wholly outside Panama. The exporter must hold a valid RUC, report zero-rated sales in the periodic ITBMS return, issue invoices at 0%, and retain complete customs and remittance documentation

  • Customs Export Declaration / Declaración Aduanera de Exportación duly accepted by Panama Customs (Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas)
  • Bill of Lading (or Air Waybill) issued to the foreign buyer evidencing physical loading/export from a Panamanian port/airport
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing payment of export proceeds by the foreign buyer

Panama DGI enforces a phased electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) mandate. Taxpayers must issue invoices through a DGI-accredited system or portal, transmit invoice XML in near-real time to the DGI validation/clearance platform, include the authorized Sello Digital and unique verification code, and maintain fiscal documents for the statutory period. Larger exporters must integrate via API and the DGI validates registration status, RUC/NIT consistency, and applicable tax rate at issuance; non-compliant invoices are rejected or treated as unauthorized for the buyer's input credit.

If DGI determines that export zero-rating was claimed without sufficient customs, shipping, or foreign-currency remittance evidence, it will reassess ITBMS at 7% on the invoice value, charge statutory interest from the original filing due date, and apply substantial administrative fines under the Código Fiscal, including up to a multiple of the omitted tax. Fraudulent or fictitious export documentation, forged bills of lading, or false remittance certificates can trigger criminal tax fraud and customs smuggling penalties, including imprisonment and monetary sanctions. Any input ITBMS credits applied against zero-rated exports may be retrospectively denied and clawed back together with late-payment surcharges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility under a fully dollarized monetary system. Current-account and capital-account transactions receive identical treatment without prior licensing, repatriation, or surrender requirements. Profit remittances, shareholder loan repayments, and third-party vendor payments face zero statutory FX restrictions. For the lithium carbonate procurement model, trade settlements may be freely denominated in USD, CNY, CLP, or any other currency. The only practical frictions are SBP-oriented AML/KYC compliance, accurate SWIFT purpose-code mapping, and the bank's independent source-of-funds diligence on high-value battery-metals remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory deadline for bringing export proceeds onshore, no minimum conversion ratio, and no export-receipts surrender obligation. Foreign currency and USD receipts may be held offshore or onshore indefinitely without penalty. Under Panama's territorial income-tax regime, overseas-sourced wholesale arbitrage profits from battery-grade lithium carbonate resale are generally outside Panamanian-source taxation; the taxpayer must preserve purchase agreements, supplier invoices, bills of lading, and title-transfer documents to substantiate the foreign source.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank. The US dollar is full legal tender (Law 84 of 1904; 1 USD = 1 PAB balboa coin). Banking supervision and AML regulation are vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent, clearing agent and operator of the national electronic clearing system (SIPE). There is no dedicated foreign-exchange administration, because Panama has never enacted exchange-control or capital-control statutes.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs and non-licensed e-money platforms are not SBP-supervised and do not participate in SIPE or local SWIFT clearing; therefore they are unacceptable as primary clearing rails for documentary letters of credit and wire settlements typical of battery-metals procurement (ticket sizes from USD 250,000 to over USD 5,000,000). Panamanian universal banks are the only fully compliant vehicles for LC issuance, FX hedging, and correspondent-bank settlement. EMIs may be used only as a supplementary invoicing or reconciliation overlay, never as the authorized treasury execution channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route all inbound funds via SWIFT MT103 to the S.A.'s Panamanian USD account through the bank's correspondent in New York (Fedwire or CHIPS). The beneficiary name must match the registered corporate name exactly, and the remittance reference must contain the purpose code plus the underlying agreement identifier (for example, equity subscription, shareholder loan, or LC collateral). There is no DGI or SBP pre-approval requirement for capital inflows; however, alignment with the corporate-purpose clause is mandatory, and aggregated or split inbound payments from multiple third parties should be avoided because they may be interpreted as structuring. All wires should be documented within 48 hours with the source-of-funds file, the executed board resolution, and the underlying trade contract, if applicable.Use two authorized clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers with cash-against-documents for routine supplier payments, including full MT103 remittance data comprising invoice number, purchase-order reference, Incoterm (CIF or FOB), and certificate-of-analysis reference; (2) irrevocable documentary Commercial Letter of Credit issued via SWIFT MT700/MT710 through the Panamanian bank, advised or confirmed by the seller's bank in China, Chile, Argentina, or the applicable exporting jurisdiction, with 30 to 120 day tenors and drawing documents consisting of the commercial invoice, full-set clean bill of lading, certificate of origin, certificate of analysis for battery-grade lithium carbonate (≥99.5% Li2CO3), and insurance certificate.
