Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No legal minimum paid-up capital. Authorized capital is discretionary (customary US$10,000 divided into 100 shares). At least one share must be subscribed and fully paid before registry filing; no escrow or capital deposit account is required by Panamanian law.

Funding & unlock path: Not mandatory for a remote-first digital enterprise. If a physical office is opened in Panama City, the tenant must obtain a fire-safety inspection certificate (Certificado de Seguridad de la Dirección General de Bomberos de Panamá), comply with the municipal building code's structural load requirements (Código de Construcción de Panamá), and register employees with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS) for workplace accident coverage. Each clearance has a 5-10 business day turnaround; noncompliance with fire-safety rules attracts administrative fines under Decreto 25 de 2014.

Paid-in capital rule: No legal minimum paid-up capital. Authorized capital is discretionary (customary US$10,000 divided into 100 shares). At least one share must be subscribed and fully paid before registry filing; no escrow or capital deposit account is required by Panamanian law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; electronic portal: 'Sistema E-Tax 2.0' (SITT / SIT tributario) at https://etax.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panamanian S.A. must apply for a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) before the DGI. Steps: (1) file the certificado de registro mercantil (certified copy of the public deed with the Registro Público) and appoint a resident fiscal agent; (2) submit RUC application Form (Formulario de Registro Único de Contribuyente) along with proof of registered domicile (Aviso de Situación), copy of identity/passport of the incorporator and directors, and the certification of the resident agent; (3) register for ITBMS (Impuesto de Transferencia de Bienes Muebles y Servicios) simultaneously; (4) obtain a digital signature certificate (Firma Electrónica) for electronic filing; within 10 business days the DGI issues the RUC certificate and assigns the permanent tax ID.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — Panamanian S.A. subject to 25% corporate income tax (ISR) only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income and capital gains from abroad are outside the taxable base; no pass-through election available under general law

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style pass-through election exists; available elections include: (i) optional consolidated taxation for affiliated Panamanian companies (Ley 52 de 2016, art. 709-B); (ii) election for the Régimen de Pequeño Contribuyente / Régimen Opcional Simplificado (ROS) for small taxpayers with annual gross income up to B/. 36,000; (iii) the Sede de Empresa Multinacional (SEM) regime (Ley 41 de 2007) for qualifying multinational headquarters performing offshore services; (iv) Panama has adopted BEPS hybrid mismatch rules (Ley 254 de 2021) disallowing deductible payments to hybrid entities, with no election to treat an S.A. as transparent

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary threshold for registration — any person habitually transferring taxable goods or rendering services in Panama must register for ITBMS and obtain a RUC; a simplified optional regime (Régimen Opcional Simplificado) is available for small taxpayers with annual gross income not exceeding B/. 36,000 (equivalent to USD 36,000 under the fixed parity)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated at 0% under Article 1056-O of the Código Fiscal (ITBMS Law). Goods: actual exit from Panama required, evidenced by customs export declaration. Services (including voice-streaming/digital platforms): the service is zero-rated when rendered to a person not domiciled in Panama and effectively used/consumed entirely outside Panama; for digital voice-streaming services the taxpayer must document the non-resident status of the customer (IP/geolocation data, country code, billing address) and the absence of use in Panama; services physically rendered to local recipients are taxable at 7% irrespective of the customer's nationality

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction: the US dollar is legal tender and the national currency, the Balboa, exists only as a fractional coin at parity with the USD. There are no separate exchange controls on capital or current account transactions. Current account convertibility is unlimited, and capital account movements are free from quantitative restrictions. Profit remittance is unrestricted, subject only to anti-money-laundering due diligence and tax withholding compliance on taxable Panama-source income. Dividends paid by a Panama corporation out of income not sourced in Panama are generally exempt from Panama taxation; remittances of such exempt profits encounter no FX hurdle. The absence of central-bank exchange-rate intervention and of mandatory central bank approval eliminates conversion delay risks; however, banks still impose transaction-level compliance controls under SBP AML rules.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio. Exporters may retain foreign currency in any bank account within or outside Panama. Bank regulations require accurate identification of cross-border wire purposes and filing of reports on transactions above USD 10,000 under the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF). Periodic SBP reporting on aggregate foreign-currency positions may apply to banks, but not to the corporate exporter.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) / Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) — Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system without a traditional central bank; the SBP acts as the banking and FX supervisory authority and MEF administers fiscal and foreign-exchange-related policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating is Medium-Restricted. Panama does not yet have a bespoke non-bank EMI licensing regime equivalent to EU EMI directives; offshore digital EMIs cannot operate as full deposit-taking institutions and only limited payment-processing functions may be performed via authorized fintech companies under the SBP's Innovation Hub and sandbox framework. An offshore EMI may provide settlement wallet infrastructure through a Panama-licensed bank, but the EMI itself will not obtain a direct SBP license. The company should therefore rely primarily on a licensed Panama commercial bank as the clearing anchor; direct offshore EMI onboarding is possible for receivables collection from card networks, but disbursements to trade counterparties must settle through a local correspondent or a licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injection routes via SWIFT MT103 from the foreign parent or shareholders using purpose codes 'G.1.0.0' for capital contributions or 'G.1.1.0' for other inward capital transfers. Panama's AML law requires the receiving bank to verify the source of funds and may require filing of a suspicious transaction report for transactions above USD 10,000. For subscription of shares in a Panama S.A., the company must record the payment in the corporate books and obtain a capital contribution certificate signed by the shareholder. Inward remittances should reference invoice or share subscription numbers, and the receiving bank may require a board resolution approving the capital increase.Outbound trade settlements for content licensing, cloud hosting, marketing, and intercompany services should use SWIFT MT103 wire transfers from a Panama commercial bank, with proper purpose codes such as 'P.1.2.0' for service payments or 'M.3.1.0' for royalties. For large infrastructure or content acquisitions, a commercial letter of credit (LC) or standby LC, confirmed by a Panama bank, is the appropriate trade clearing rail. The company must keep documentary evidence of each contract, invoice, and delivery acceptance. Repatriation of dividends to foreign shareholders follows the same MT103 rails with supporting board minutes and audited financials; no separate central bank approval is required under Panama's dollarized system.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) contracts on Latin American currencies (e.g., BRL, MXN, COP) offered by Panama-based broker-dealers and international banks without forward license limitationsDeliverable currency swaps between USD and any freely convertible currency, executed via a Panama-licensed bank or broker under general banking-law authorityPlain vanilla over-the-counter forward contracts on EUR or GBP, provided the counterparty is a supervised financial institutionCross-currency interest rate swaps for intercompany loans denominated in a foreign currency, with transaction documentation held offshore

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends paid or credited by a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima out of Panama-source profits. Dividends declared from clearly segregated foreign-source retained earnings are outside the Panamanian territorial tax and are not subject to dividend withholding.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File preparation applies when annual gross income exceeds PAB 3,000,000 or when related-party transactions exceed PAB 250,000 in the relevant fiscal year. A Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of at least USD 750,000,000, consistent with the OECD Country-by-Country threshold.

Economic substance scope: Panama adopted Economic Substance Regulations under Law 254 of 2021 and implementing Executive Decree 185 of 2022. These apply to Panamanian tax-resident entities carrying on relevant activities, including headquarters, distribution and service centres, leasing, financing, shipping, insurance, and IP holding. A voice streaming app company must evidence local employees, operational premises, and substantive decision-making in Panama when claiming territorial exclusion of foreign-source revenue. An artificial Panama shell used as nominal owner of IP, digital payment flows, or global user contracts will be recharacterized and taxed in Panama.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Unreported Panama-Source Digital Revenue

Statutory sanction: Reassessment at 25% corporate income tax on omitted Panama-source revenue, plus 10% surcharge, statutory default interest, administrative fines up to PAB 50,000, and criminal imprisonment of 2 to 5 years for wilful false declaration under the Código Fiscal.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or authorise revenue routing outside the Panamanian entity may be jointly liable for tax, penalties, and interest. The corporate veil may be pierced for fraud, and directors face travel bans, asset freezing, civil restitution, and extradition exposure.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: The DGI may make an arm's-length price adjustment, assess additional 25% corporate income tax on the adjusted profit, impose a penalty equal to 20% of the transfer pricing adjustment, add default interest, and fine the taxpayer for failure to produce required documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors approving intercompany invoices without economic justification may be named in assessment notices as jointly liable parties. Persistent non-compliance can result in disqualification as directors, suspension of the registered agent, and personal liability for the group's tax exposure.
Artificial Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The DGI may recharacterize the company as a fully tax-resident Panamanian entity, assess 25% corporate income tax on the entire app profit, apply surcharges and default interest, impose ESR penalties under Law 254 of 2021 of up to PAB 1,000,000, and cancel the license of the registered agent.

Director personal liability: Directors and ultimate beneficial owners cannot hide behind nominee arrangements. They face personal liability for fraudulent misrepresentation, civil restitution of diverted profits, travel bans in tax enforcement proceedings, and criminal liability for money laundering if assets are moved to conceal ownership or income.
AML / Unlawful Use of In-App Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Law 23 of 2015 AML/CFT fines of PAB 250,000 to PAB 10,000,000, suspension or revocation of the commercial license, and criminal money-laundering penalties of up to 12 years imprisonment for responsible persons.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and legal representatives are personally liable if they knowingly permit the platform to be used for unlawful financial transfers. They may be named in criminal prosecutions, subjected to travel restrictions, ordered to pay restitution to defrauded parties, and personally barred from holding directorships in Panama.
