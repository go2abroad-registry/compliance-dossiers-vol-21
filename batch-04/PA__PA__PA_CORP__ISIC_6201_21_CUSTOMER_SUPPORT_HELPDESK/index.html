Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; official taxpayer portal and e-services available through https://www.mef.gob.pa/dgi and the SIT@2 Electronic Tax Services platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity without a Panamanian branch must first appoint a resident agent and, for tax purposes, a Panamanian legal representative/fiscal agent. The representative must submit to the DGI the original notarized and apostilled certificate of incorporation and by-laws (translated into Spanish), a board resolution appointing the fiscal agent, the executed power of attorney, and the completed RUC registration form (Solicitud de Inscripción o Actualización del Registro Único de Contribuyente). The DGI will then issue a single RUC number that serves as both the income tax TIN and the ITBMS/VAT identification number. For a Panama-incorporated Sociedad Anónima, the process is initiated after the public deed is recorded in the Public Registry and the Aviso de Operación is obtained from the corresponding municipality. A foreign entity that merely exports services with no Panamanian-source activity should still register with the DGI through the fiscal representative so that it may issue compliant export zero-rated invoices and sustain any ITBMS input credit claim. The DGI does not conduct a substantive review before issuing the RUC, but the completed file must include full beneficial ownership information under Panama's tax information and AML standards.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama has no check-the-box or entity classification election comparable to the United States. A Sociedad Anónima is automatically a separate taxpaying corporation subject to entity-level taxation on Panamanian-source income only. There are no hybrid mismatch elections available under Panamanian domestic law; characterization follows legal form and source rules. Administrative elections are limited to the optional simplified small-taxpayer regime for low-turnover taxpayers, the selection of a fiscal agent for nonresident taxpayers, and the voluntary general regime for taxpayers below the small-taxpayer threshold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.07%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB 0 / no de minimis exemption under the standard ITBMS regime: registration is mandatory from the first habitual taxable transaction of goods or services in Panamanian territory. A simplified small-taxpayer regime may apply for gross annual revenue below approximately PAB 300,000, but that regime does not exempt a foreign or domestic supplier from obtaining an RUC or from issuing valid electronic invoices when a taxable supply is made.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The ITBMS is zero-rated on exports of goods physically departing Panamanian customs territory and on exportation of services rendered outside Panama or supplied for the exclusive benefit of a non-resident recipient. For the target helpdesk and conversational-bot cloud activity, zero-rating requires (i) a contractual recipient that is not a Panamanian resident or a Panamanian permanent establishment, (ii) the service to be performed principally from servers or personnel located outside Panama or delivered into the recipient's foreign environment, (iii) no actual use or consumption of the service by the Panamanian exporter or any Panamanian establishment, and (iv) commercial and banking evidence that the consideration is collected in foreign currency or through an international payment rail such as Stripe or PayPal. Invoicing a foreign customer alone is not sufficient; the taxpayer must maintain service-level documentation, IP addresses or delivery logs, and an executed export services contract demonstrating that the place of use and economic benefit is outside Panama.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduana - DUA) accepted and sealed by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
  • Bill of Lading for ocean freight or Air Waybill for air freight showing the Panamanian exporter as shipper and a foreign consignee
  • Commercial invoice issued in English or Spanish describing the goods, HS code, Incoterms, and export value
  • Packing list and, where applicable, export permit or sanitary/phytosanitary certificate
  • Proof of payment and Inward Remittance Certificate, SWIFT MT103, or bank statement evidencing collection from the foreign buyer within the repatriation window

Panama has implemented mandatory electronic invoicing through the DGI's Factura Electrónica system. Registered taxpayers must issue invoices in an approved XML/JSON electronic format through the DGI portal, direct API integration, or a DGI-authorized technology service provider. Invoices must contain the DGI-assigned validation code and a QR code or digital thumbprint, evidencing real-time receipt and fiscal clearance by the DGI. Once the electronic authorization is obtained, paper folios issued by the DGI are no longer valid for standard ITBMS taxpayers. Foreign suppliers with a Panamanian RUC must likewise transmit all invoices through the same mechanism. Invoicing without the DGI-validated code may result in the invoice being treated as a null document for the buyer's input credit and may expose the issuer to fines and surcharges.

DGI and ANA electronically cross-match export declarations, e-invoices, and bank inflows. If an exporter claims zero-rating for a supply that is recharacterized as domestic or locally consumed, the DGI will assess ITBMS at 7% on the gross amount plus statutory interest from the invoice date. Penalties can reach up to 100% of the omitted tax, plus fines for false invoicing and failure to maintain supporting documentation, and reckless or fraudulent claims may trigger criminal fiscal-fraud proceedings under the Código Fiscal. Retroactive clawback generally covers open tax periods under Panamanian prescription rules, and the taxpayer must then correct all affected VAT credit balances and customer invoices.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The regime is one of full and unrestricted convertibility across both current and capital accounts. With complete official dollarization, there is no Panamanian exchange-control authority, no surrender or repatriation obligation, no prior import authorization, and no central-bank approval for capital inflows or profit remittances. Dividends, branch profits, and royalties may be remitted freely by wire to any jurisdiction, subject only to standard commercial-bank documentary scrutiny, the bank's internal AML/CFT classification, and compliance with Panama's territorial income-tax rules regarding the source of income. A practical remittance hurdle is the availability of clear underlying invoices or audited-reserve evidence that establishes the non-Panamanian source of the revenue, thereby confirming the absence of 10% dividend withholding (which applies only to Panamanian-source profits). Non-resident shareholders face no capital-gains tax on disposal of shares in a Panama S.A., and no stamp duty applies to remittance documentation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no statutory timeline for the repatriation of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio. A Panamanian exporter of SaaS/helpdesk support services may lawfully retain USD receivables in offshore bank accounts, provided the income is formally reported to the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) under the territorial principle and reflected in the corporate books and annual tax return. Export of services by the PA_CORP helpdesk entity is exempt from Panamanian value-added tax (ITBMS at 7%) when the service is actually used or exploited abroad; there is no foreign-exchange certificate, no surrender requirement, and no requirement to liquidate proceeds into Balboa or any other currency.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), acting together with the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) on monetary-fiscal policy coordination. The Republic of Panama maintains no formal central bank; the United States Dollar is unlimited legal tender, and the Panamanian Balboa exists solely as a divisional coin at a fixed 1:1 peg. The SBP therefore performs banking conduct, solvency, liquidity, and Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) supervision rather than traditional central-bank lender-of-last-resort or currency-issue functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM. Offshore Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs), including Airwallex, Wise Business, Dukascopy-side platforms, and other non-bank payment switching providers, have no legally recognized depository status in Panama; the SBP's exclusive banking-license perimeter prevents an unlicensed EMI from receiving Panamanian deposits or acting as the primary clearing institution. However, such EMIs are practicable as secondary disbursement rails for contractor payouts and foreign-subsidiary expenses, provided the PA_CORP S.A. maintains its principal liquidity in an SBP-regulated bank. For inbound merchant acquiring, Stripe and PayPal are viable but operate through overseas acquiring sponsors, requiring the Panamanian entity to effect settlement through its own SBP-regulated bank account; direct onshore payment-institution licensing by the SBP (Reglamento de Banca Digital / sandbox) is optional, yet operationally unnecessary for a Low_Risk_Standard SaaS helpdesk scope. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing should use a SWIFT MT103 message with UETR (Unique End-to-End Transaction Reference) and DCBR-style ordering-customer detail. The transferer's bank credits the Panamanian beneficiary account through the bank's U.S. correspondent (usually via CHIPS or Fedwire at the Fed funds layer where the Panamanian bank maintains a USD nostro in New York). The beneficiary fields must reflect exactly the name of the Sociedad Anónima registered in the Registro Público, its full address, the beneficiary bank's BIC, and the local account number (Panama has no IBAN system, so the full account number and optional CLABE-style internal code are required). In Field 70-77 'Remittance Information', the investor must insert a precise purpose narrative: 'Aporte de capital social / Capital contribution to paid-in shares', referencing the specific board resolution approving the subscription. The assisting bank may request a certified copy of the shareholder resolution, an updated share register, and, for statistics, an indication that the transaction qualifies as 'Inversión Directa' (direct investment equity) rather than debt, thereby avoiding the 5% withholdings payable on intercompany debt interest remitted abroad under the territorial Income Tax Code. No prior Central-Bank approval or exchange-control declaration is required; however, the receiving Panamanian bank will perform an OFAC/FATF screening of the sending institution. For intra-LatAm parent funding, the transfer should also include the country-level tax ID (RUC or equivalent) of the payer to permit the receiving bank to classify the transaction cleanly within its automated BOP reporting framework.Outbound trade and operational disbursements for the PA_CORP helpdesk entity should be effected primarily via SWIFT MT103 (single customer credit transfer) for supplier payments, contractor remuneration, and overseas software licensing, applying the charge option RIP (shared-cost or beneficiary-charges as negotiated). For larger capital-equipment or infrastructure purchases, a Commercial Letter of Credit (documentary credit governed by UCP600) may be issued by the Panamanian corporate bank through its trade-finance desk, typically confirmed by an international correspondent bank, with the beneficiary's documents including original commercial invoice, bill of lading/AWB, inspection certificates, and insurance vouchers; for SaaS the LC is rarely required. However, such EMIs are practicable as secondary disbursement rails for contractor payouts and foreign-subsidiary expenses, provided the PA_CORP S.A. maintains its principal liquidity in an SBP-regulated bank. For inbound merchant acquiring, Stripe and PayPal are viable but operate through overseas acquiring sponsors, requiring the Panamanian entity to effect settlement through its own SBP-regulated bank account; direct onshore payment-institution licensing by the SBP (Reglamento de Banca Digital / sandbox) is optional, yet operationally unnecessary for a Low_Risk_Standard SaaS helpdesk scope. Legal constraints include FATF Recommendation 16 transparency expectations, the prohibition on bearer-style anonymity, and the need to ensure that any EMI-associated Mastercard/Visa virtual corporate card is backed by a customer-funded account at an SBP-regulated bank rather than an unlicensed PSP.
