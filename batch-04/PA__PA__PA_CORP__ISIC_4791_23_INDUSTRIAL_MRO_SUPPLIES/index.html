Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), consolidated within the Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP); official portal: https://www.anip.gob.pa / https://www.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must first be validly incorporated and registered in the Registro Público de Panamá, acting through a licensed Panamanian resident agent. The resident agent submits the public deed of incorporation, amended corporate articles, proof of registered agent and registered office, and identification of directors/officers to ANIP-DGI to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente). The RUC is the mandatory tax identification for all VAT/ITBMS and income tax filings. Foreign entities not yet incorporated may obtain a provisional RUC only where Panamanian-source revenue is anticipated; physically absent non-resident sellers deriving Panamanian-source e-commerce income must appoint a resident tax agent and register for ITBMS before commencing taxable sales.

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election exists under Panamanian law. Tax classification follows corporate law and the Código Fiscal. The Sociedad Anónima is mandatorily taxed as a corporation on Panama-source income only. Specific special-regime elections available in lieu of general territorial tax treatment include the SEM (Servicios Empresariales Multinacionales) regime for multinational headquarters and the Panama-Pacifico special economic zone regime; these elections impose alternative income tax treatment and must be registered with ANIP and SEM authorities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general minimum turnover threshold; any person habitually engaged in taxable commercial, industrial or service activities in Panama must register for ITBMS before commencing activities. Small taxpayers with annual gross receipts not exceeding USD 36,000 (equivalent in PAB/ballboas) may elect the simplified small-taxpayer quota regime instead of full ITBMS compliance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Panamanian territory are zero-rated for ITBMS provided the sale is made to a foreign purchaser and the goods leave Panama; export status is evidenced by the customs export declaration. Services consumed outside Panama, including e-commerce and MRO catalog support services rendered to non-resident B2B customers with no Panamanian use or enjoyment, qualify as territorial zero-rated (non-taxed) exports to the extent the service is not effectively connected with activities performed in Panama. The exporter must maintain proof that title and risk passed abroad and that no local consumption occurred.

  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Panama mandates electronic invoicing pursuant to ANIP-DGI's Sistemas de Facturación Electrónica. Taxpayers must issue invoices through the ANIP digital platform or certified private software connected by API/web services to the tax authority; each invoice receives a digital fiscal stamp and verification code (Código de Verificación) upon pre-validation. Large taxpayers are on phased mandatory e-invoicing with full national rollout targeted across all business sizes; retail and unintegrated merchants may still print authorized pre-numbered invoices or fiscal-machine receipts, but such documents must be reported to ANIP within stated deadlines. Real-time clearance is required for B2B and B2C e-commerce transactions where the supplier operates through the electronic issuance channel.

ANIP-DGI may audit export transactions and, if zero-rating is assessed to be fraudulent or unsupported, will retroactively assess unpaid ITBMS at the standard rate plus daily surcharges, apply fines ranging from 100% to 200% of the omitted tax, impose business closure for repeated violations, and refer willful falsification of export declarations for criminal prosecution under the Código Fiscal and criminal law (including imprisonment). The tax statute of limitations is generally five years, extended where fraudulent or unfiled returns are found; refund claims for excess input ITBMS are subject to desk review and field audit, with denial of refund and clawback of credits previously granted if documentation is incomplete or inconsistent with customs SISCOM records.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates under a fully convertible, dollarized regime with no foreign-exchange control statute. Current-account and capital-account transactions, including trade settlements, profit remittances, dividend distributions, loan servicing, and capital repatriation, require no prior authorization from any Panamanian authority; there is no BOP reporting system for individual corporate transfers. MRO e-commerce earnings are subject only to Panamanian-source income taxation on a territorial basis, and undistributed foreign or offshore-derived proceeds may be retained freely overseas. Statutory taxes on distributions such as the dividend tax are fiscal obligations, not convertibility impediments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Industrial MRO e-commerce exporters may retain 100% of export proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency in Panama or abroad, without surrender to the Banco Nacional de Panamá. Under Law 23 of 2015, banks must retain supporting trade documentation for transactions above USD 10,000 and report anomalous accumulations of export proceeds, but no central-bank deposit or conversion requirement applies.

Central bank supervision: Panama does not maintain a central bank. The monetary system is fully dollarized: the United States Dollar (USD) is legal tender alongside the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) at a fixed 1:1 parity, with no fiduciary issuance of PAB paper. Banking licensing, conduct, and cross-border intermediation are supervised by the Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, SBP) pursuant to Cabinet Decree No. 328 of 1998 and SBP regulations. The Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and clearing bank for government flows. The Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) executes AML/CFT supervision under Law 23 of 2015.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for disbursement-pass-through infrastructure but LOW for primary treasury settlement. Panama does not issue a standalone e-money institution (EMI) licence; fintechs such as Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, or Payoneer typically hold foreign licences and may enter Panama only as registered payment-service providers under SBP regulations or as foreign agents under Law 23 of 2015. An unlicensed offshore EMI cannot maintain a settlement account with a Panamanian clearing bank and cannot sponsor local USD ACH rails, so it should be used only to receive card-scheme settlement from the e-commerce gateway and to route supplier disbursements via SWIFT into the Panama S.A. primary account. Legal constraints include SBP licensing thresholds for digital-payment merchants, UAF suspicious-transaction reporting for stored-value floats, and contractual restrictions on card scheme fund acceptance through e-commerce stores.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injections are received in USD via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers routed through the bank's New York-based correspondent network (CHIPS or Fedwire), with the transaction narrative clearly labelled as paid-in capital subscription and, where required, using purpose code GTR-7699 for commercial payments. No currency-control or central-bank filing is required. The receiving Panamanian bank will authenticate the remitting bank, verify the source of funds against the shareholder's UBO file, and post the funds to the S.A.'s capital escrow or operational account.
