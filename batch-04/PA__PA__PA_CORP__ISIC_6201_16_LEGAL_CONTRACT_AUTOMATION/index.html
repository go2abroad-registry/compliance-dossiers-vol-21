The Sociedad Anónima must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) from the DGI. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — the Panamanian S.A. is a taxable juridical person taxed on Panama-source income only (territorial principle). Foreign-source income is generally exempt, except where remitted to Panama, in which case the remittance basis of taxation applies. Not eligible for pass-through/check-the-box treatment.

Available election forms

Panama does not implement a US check-the-box regime. Statutory options include: (i) election for the Régimen Opcional Simplificado (simplified presumptive income tax at 2% of gross revenues for taxpayers with annual gross receipts up to USD 200,000); (ii) election to apply the territorial remittance rule to foreign-source service income; (iii) election under Law 52/2023 to treat specified electronic/digital services (SaaS, e-signature, cloud automation) as sourced at the location of service use, with proof obligations on the provider; (iv) election to pay the optional 10% advance dividend tax; and (v) voluntary self-assessment in the DGI's Large Taxpayers (PGA) regime once turnover exceeds statutory thresholds.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de-minimis registration threshold — any person habitually transferring movable goods or providing services in Panama must register for ITBMS in the RUC and file monthly returns (Formulario FS-02) irrespective of turnover. The USD 200,000 threshold applies only to the optional simplified Income Tax regime, not to ITBMS registration. For a PA_CORP SaaS exporter without local clients, ITBMS registration is still mandatory for the zero-rated export declarations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for services delivered to a non-resident and effectively consumed, used or enjoyed abroad, including LegalTech SaaS and e-signature authentication. Conditions: (i) the recipient has no permanent establishment or tax domicile in Panama; (ii) the service is executed/used outside Panama; (iii) the invoice is issued in USD (Panama's legal tender) without local ITBMS; (iv) payment is received from abroad and credited through the local banking system; (v) the reserved '0% Exportación' code is used in the electronic invoice. If services are used in Panama, the 7% (or 10% digital-services rate) applies to the Panama-source portion.

  • Customs/administrative export declaration for services (Declaración de Exportación de Servicios) when filed voluntarily or upon DGI request
  • Executed service contract with the non-resident purchaser clearly describing the offshore delivery model
  • Zero-rated electronic invoice with export digital stamp and QR code
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill or equivalent digital service delivery record (for goods; for SaaS, the server log and usage data evidencing offshore consumption)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (certificado de remesa del exterior) issued by the receiving Panamanian bank evidencing FX inflows
  • Certificate of fiscal residence or foreign VAT/ID registration of the counterparty, where available

Mandatory since September 2022 for all taxpayers: Facturación Electrónica (FE) must be issued through the DGI-authorized platform (Sede Electrónica DGI or certified third-party issuers). Every invoice requires real-time DGI validation, a unique electronic stamp (clave de comprobante digital) with QR code, and transmission to the DGI before fiscal use. Zero-rated exports must use the 'Exportación' invoice class with the digital export code; service-provider export declarations must be transmitted electronically. Automated billing via API-integrated TPAs is required for SaaS platforms; fiscal printing devices are no longer accepted.

The DGI may scrutinize export zero-rating claims for several years (statute of limitations generally 5 years, extended to 6+ years for non-declared refunds). The burden of proof lies on the exporter to demonstrate foreign use and actual repatriation. Upon audit challenge the DGI can: (i) claw back the zero-rating and assess full 7% ITBMS on the invoice amount; (ii) add a surcharge of up to 100% of the omitted tax (Código Fiscal Art. 762); (iii) impose per-invoice fines of USD 500 to USD 50,000 for incorrect electronic invoicing; (iv) initiate criminal proceedings for tax fraud (Art. 799-A) with imprisonment up to 10 years; and (v) suspend the taxpayer's RUC and CEDE (foreign-trade) authorizations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full free-market convertibility exists for both current-account and capital-account transactions in USD. The IMF regards Panama as a jurisdiction free of restrictions on payments and transfers for current international transactions. SaaS subscription revenue, shareholder capital injections, intercompany loans, royalty payments, and profit remittances may all move cross-border without quantitative FX limits or prior central-bank approval. Profit remittance is restricted only by tax withholding rules, not by FX controls: dividends from locally taxable profits generally face a 10% withholding tax, and a 5% rate applies to dividends from profits taxed under a preferential regime such as a Multinational Headquarters license. Dividends from purely foreign-source income can escape Panamanian dividend tax only when the territorial nature of the income is fully documented with contracts, issuer bank statements, and a coherent off-shore profit ledger; otherwise the DGI may impose a complementary levy. Practical hurdles are confined to OFAC sanctions screening, AML source-of-funds verification, and correspondent-bank validation of transaction purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no surrender, mandatory conversion, or statutory repatriation timeline for export proceeds. USD receipts from SaaS services and electronic document-automation contracts may be retained freely in any SBP-licensed bank and redeployed for outbound disbursements without exchange-control approval. The operative compliance requirement is evidential, not monetary: the exporter must maintain the underlying foreign-client agreements, payment-acquirer settlement statements, and a clear reconciliation trail proving that the income is foreign-source under Panama's territorial tax system, thereby supporting the annual corporate income tax return (Form 150) and avoiding potential recharacterization of foreign income as local-source income.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no traditional central bank, no domestic note issuance, and no central exchange-control authority. The United States Dollar is the de facto legal tender and monetary anchor, while the Panamanian Balboa exists solely as 1:1 subsidiary coinage. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panama (SBP) supervises all licensed banks and enforces AML/CFT rules under the General Banking Law (Decree Law 2 of 2008) and Law 23 of 2015. The Banco Nacional de Panama acts as the Republic's fiscal agent, clearing-house operator, and interbank settlement agent for the domestic ACH and the high-value payment system, giving the jurisdiction a fully dollarized, market-driven liquidity environment with no official FX intervention mechanism.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium as an auxiliary rail, Low as a primary treasury layer. Panama does not issue EU-style Electronic Money Institution licenses and does not allow unlicensed foreign EMIs to obtain local IBANs or join the domestic ACH and SPAV clearing systems. Offshore digital banks and EMIs, including UK- or EU-authorized payment firms, may only operate on a sponsor-account basis through an SBP-licensed Panamanian bank or as non-bank money transmitters, which creates a 'conduit entity' profile that correspondents often flag. The recommended architecture is a Tier-1 Panamanian USD bank account as the settlement anchor for Stripe payouts and card-acquirer settlements, with EMIs confined to fringe functions such as vendor payables in non-USD currencies and back-office disbursement sub-ledgers. Over-reliance on EMIs as the exclusive bank relationship is not acceptable for a Panama S.A. seeking clean correspondent banking.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The capital call is executed by the non-resident shareholder wiring USD from its home bank through global correspondent banks, typically JPMorgan Chase or Citibank New York, directly to the Panama S.A.'s USD operating or capital escrow account at the chosen Tier-1 Panamanian bank. The SWIFT MT103 message must carry the exact beneficiary corporate name matching the resolution, a complete originator account and address, and a clear field 70 narrative reading 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - APORTE DE CAPITAL - PA_CORP SOCIEDAD ANONIMA'. The transaction is cleared via Fedwire or CHIPS at the New York covering bank and credited to the Panamanian account as equity, not commercial income. The receiving bank will require the capital subscription sheets, the board approval minutes, and, for larger deposits above USD 100,000, a source-of-funds breakdown from the shareholder. Under Panamanian law, equity injections are not taxable, and the bank will code the credit as an equity inflow in its AML transaction-monitoring system rather than as an operating receipt.Outbound clearing relies on SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers for all foreign vendor payments, SaaS subcontractor fees, and international disbursements; MT202 COV is used as the cover message for high-value same-day obligations. For settlements in USD inside Panama, the rails are the domestic ACH operated under the Banco Nacional de Panama and the high-value SPAV system, both clearing in USD with real-time final settlement. Stripe payouts and credit-card acquirer settlements arrive as daily or weekly USD sweeps into the operational account using the bank's standard settlement instructions, providing a predictable liquidity inflow. For clients or suppliers that require documentary trade instruments, Panamanian banks can issue and advise commercial letters of credit under MT700/MT710 and handle documentary collections under MT400/MT412, although this is atypical for LegalTech SaaS transactions, which normally settle on open account. Every outbound disbursement must be matched with a vendor invoice, attested service delivery, and sanctions-screened beneficiary identity to protect the bank's correspondent relationships.EUR/USD deliverable FX forward contracts with a maturity of up to 12 months, booked with a Tier-1 Panamanian bank or its global parent, used to hedge EUR-denominated vendor invoices that exceed the SaaS company's EUR receivables.USD/EUR FX swaps for short-dated liquidity bridging between the Panamanian USD operating account and a dedicated EUR disbursement float, protecting the treasury from temporary currency mismatches on intercompany recharges.Cross-currency swaps on USD/EUR shareholder loans or acquisition notes, where the legal entity debt is denominated in EUR but the operative cash flows are USD; non-deliverable forwards are permissible only for ancillary exposure to regional Latin American currencies and must always be backed by a documented commercial or balance-sheet exposure, since Panama imposes no FX-control approval for derivatives but SBP and OFAC compliance remains mandatory.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Medium as an auxiliary rail, Low as a primary treasury layer. Panama does not issue EU-style Electronic Money Institution licenses and does not allow unlicensed foreign EMIs to obtain local IBANs or join the domestic ACH and SPAV clearing systems. Offshore digital banks and EMIs, including UK- or EU-authorized payment firms, may only operate on a sponsor-account basis through an SBP-licensed Panamanian bank or as non-bank money transmitters, which creates a 'conduit entity' profile that correspondents often flag. The recommended architecture is a Tier-1 Panamanian USD bank account as the settlement anchor for Stripe payouts and card-acquirer settlements, with EMIs confined to fringe functions such as vendor payables in non-USD currencies and back-office disbursement sub-ledgers. Over-reliance on EMIs as the exclusive bank relationship is not acceptable for a Panama S.A. seeking clean correspondent banking.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No statutory Local/Master File thresholds enacted. The Código Fiscal (Art. 702-A) mandates an arm’s-length standard for related-party transactions; documentation must be prepared and retained for audit, but there is no numeric threshold.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not implement formal ESR for standard Sociedades Anónimas. However, entities must prove administrative and physical substance in Panama to claim tax residency or to avoid classification as a shell under Law 52 of 2016 (Beneficial Ownership Registry). OECD BEPS and FIU supervision impose practical substance requirements.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to 100% of the evaded tax plus interest; criminal penalties up to 5 years imprisonment under Código Fiscal Art. 702-B.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for tax, subject to civil restitution, travel bans, and asset seizure.
Shell Company Misuse

Statutory sanction: Financial penalties up to USD 1,000,000 by the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) under Law 52 of 2016; corporate dissolution and tax disqualification.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for restitution and face imprisonment up to 12 years under Panama’s anti-money-laundering regime.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Daily fine of USD 5,000 per day; criminal penalties of up to 5 years imprisonment for responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors personally responsible for fines and may be barred from holding any Panamanian corporate office; travel bans apply pending enforcement.
