Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Complete convertibility on both current and capital accounts; no licensing, approval, quota, or allocation restrictions apply to any currency. Profit remittance faces zero FX impediment; the sole friction is the 10% Panamanian dividend withholding tax on distributions made by the Sociedad Anónima (reduced statutory rate is available to qualifying small taxpayers), which the paying bank must evidence as settled before disbursement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no mandatory conversion ratio, no statutory repatriation deadline, and no surrender obligation on export receipts. Proceeds from the hotpot condiment line and franchise supply may be retained 100% in USD or any freely chosen foreign currency, in Panama or abroad.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank and no exchange-control authority. Banking supervision lies with the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the State's fiscal agent and clearing-house operator. The country is fully dollarized: the US Dollar is legal tender, and the Balboa circulates only as coinage pegged 1:1 to the USD (Decree-Law No. 84 of 1941, as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium as a supplementary liquidity channel, Low as a substitute for local clearing rails. The SBP confines deposit-taking and merchant-acquiring to licensed banks and to entities admitted into its Innovation Hub sandbox; cross-border EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) without a Panamanian license may not lawfully hold client funds or settle USD/ACH locally. They remain viable for non-core procurement cards and virtual disbursements, but all POS acquiring and payroll accounts for the hotpot chain must sit within an SBP-licensed bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% marginal rate (progressive: 20% on the first PAB/USD 1,500,000 of net taxable income; 25% on the excess)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding on gross dividends paid out of Panama-source profits; 5% reduced rate where the distributing S.A. is fiscally domiciled and has paid the complementary tax on distributed reserves; 0% on dividends paid exclusively from exempt foreign-source income under the territorial principle.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB/USD 6,000,000 of gross annual revenue in the preceding tax year triggers mandatory preparation and disclosure of the Local File, Master File, and transfer pricing annex under Executive Decree No. 233/2016; transactions below that amount still require arm's-length pricing and contemporaneous support.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not apply the Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations; instead, it applies the binary test of fiscal domicile. An S.A. with offshore hotpot operations remains non-domiciled and outside Panamanian ISR if board control, management, and records remain abroad; if board decisions are made in Panama, local employees are hired, or Panama-source revenue is generated through merchant acquiring or POS rails, the S.A. becomes fiscally domiciled and must maintain real local substance, books, and employees to sustain its tax position.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Under-Declaration of Panama-Source Revenue

Statutory sanction: Criminal and tax sanctions under the Código Fiscal and Código Penal: fine of 100% to 300% of evaded tax, monthly 5% surcharge capped at 25%, 1% monthly moratory interest, RUC cancellation, and imprisonment from 2 to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Signing directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for evaded tax and penalties; the DGI may issue travel bans, freeze local assets, and the responsible director faces up to 5 years' imprisonment.
Shell Company / Sham Non-Domiciled S.A.

Statutory sanction: Re-characterization of the S.A. as fiscally domiciled with retroactive ISR at 20%/25% on all Panama-source income; fines up to PAB/USD 5,000; cancellation of RUC and Mercantile Registry registration; loss of limited liability shield.

Director personal liability: Nominee and managing directors face piercing of the corporate veil and personal liability for unpaid corporate taxes, travel bans, and a prohibition on acting as directors for up to 5 years.
AML / Money Laundering Through Cash-Intensive Restaurant

Statutory sanction: Sanctions under Ley 23 de 2015 and the Panamanian AML framework: fines from USD 10,000 to USD 1,000,000 per violation, asset forfeiture, and imprisonment from 5 to 10 years for principals.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives are personally charged with money-laundering offenses, disqualified from financial and corporate management, subjected to travel bans during investigation, and exposed to seizure of personal assets.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Artificial Condiment Supply Pricing
