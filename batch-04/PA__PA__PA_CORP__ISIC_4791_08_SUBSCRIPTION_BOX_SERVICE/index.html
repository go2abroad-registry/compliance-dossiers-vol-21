Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is imposed; the S.A. may be formed with zero issued and paid shares, and share payments may be deferred by subscription installments. No escrow or capital-subscription bank deposit is required by the Public Registry. A customary nomimal authorized capital of B/.10,000 (USD 10,000) is nonetheless recommended for bankability and municipal permitting.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from SINAPROC (exits, extinguishers, electrical inspection) is required for any office or fulfilment warehouse; structural load and occupancy inspection is performed by the Municipal Engineering Directorate; occupational health registration with CSS (Riesgos Profesionales) and MITRADEL workplace safety clearance must be completed before the first employee is onboarded.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital is imposed; the S.A. may be formed with zero issued and paid shares, and share payments may be deferred by subscription installments. No escrow or capital-subscription bank deposit is required by the Public Registry. Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No national industrial manufacturing license applies because subscription-box retailing (ISIC 4791) is a non-manufacturing trading activity; the operational authority is the Municipality of Panama for the Aviso de Operación, together with DGI/ANIP for tax control. The Ministry of Commerce and Industries (MICI) would intervene only for controlled imports, and no manufacturing permit is triggered.

Category: Municipal Aviso de Operación (annual commercial operating notice) — no national industrial or manufacturing permit is required; no sanitary registration is triggered for non-food subscription merchandise unless regulated food products are added to the box assortment.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Impuestos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; online portal: Sede Electrónica (e-Tax) — https://sede.dgi.gob.pa/

E-government filing portal

A Panama Sociedad Anónima or foreign entity engaging in taxable activities in Panama must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC). The process requires: (1) engaging a resident agent/licensed attorney; (2) filing the entity's public deed, bylaws and power of attorney with the Public Registry; (3) obtaining an Aviso de Operación from the local municipality if the activity requires physical premises; (4) submitting the RUC application through the DGI Sede Electrónica with authenticated digital documents; and (5) receiving the RUC certificate and activating the e-Tax account. The RUC is mandatory before the commencement of commercial operations and no foreign entity may access e-invoicing clearance without an RUC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or US-style pass-through classification exists. The Panama S.A. is automatically an entity-level taxpayer for Panamanian-source income. A simplified small-taxpayer regime may be elected if annual gross receipts remain below the statutory threshold. Hybrid mismatch elections are not separately available; compliance with Law 254 of 2021 (BEPS Actions) must be observed for cross-border payments and taxable presence determinations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No absolute monetary exemption; all persons or entities making taxable supplies in Panama must register for ITBMS before commencing activity. The simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross receipts not exceeding B/.300,000 (Panamanian Balboas), but RUC registration and e-invoicing are still obligatory under that regime. Foreign e-commerce suppliers of subscription boxes to Panama-based consumers are subject to ITBMS registration regardless of turnover unless the small-taxpayer threshold is met and applicable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for the export of movable goods, including subscription box merchandise, when legal title passes to a non-resident buyer and the goods physically leave Panama. Services may be zero-rated when rendered exclusively to non-residents for use or benefit entirely outside Panama. The taxpayer must maintain documentary proof that the goods departed customs territory, the sale was to an international customer, and the export consideration is received from abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduana — DUA)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Export Documentation evidencing physical departure of the goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Comprobante de Ingreso de Divisas) issued by a Panama-licensed bank

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) through the DGI Sede Electrónica. All registered taxpayers must issue e-invoices via an approved integrator or the DGI e-Tax module, transmit each invoice to the DGI in real time for fiscal validation, and include a unique authorization code (Código de Autorización), QR code and electronic stamp. The transaction must be accepted by the DGI before the invoice is considered valid; export invoices must be flagged 'Exportación' and linked to the corresponding customs declaration for clearance.

The DGI may retroactively assess ITBMS on fraudulent zero-rated exports, deny input VAT credits, and impose surcharge interest plus fines up to 100% of the tax evaded. Criminal tax fraud liability may extend to directors and officers; false customs declarations further expose the exporter to customs seizure, monetary sanctions and suspension of operating licenses.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains full currency convertibility under both current and capital accounts. There are no exchange controls, foreign investment restrictions, or mandatory investment repatriation timelines. Dividends, royalties, service fees, and capital proceeds may be freely remitted to non-residents in USD or other convertible currencies, subject only to standard AML/KYC verification and corporate tax compliance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no holding period on export proceeds, including recurring subscription revenue. All receipts may remain in USD or be freely converted without central bank notification.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); in the absence of a conventional central bank, the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent. No separate foreign exchange administration exists, and the U.S. dollar is legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Panama does not issue electronic money institution (EMI) licenses equivalent to the EEA model. Offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Wise Business, Revolut Business, Payoneer) can be used to originate or receive cross-border card payments, but they are not regulated by SBP as deposit-taking institutions. Feasibility rating for a Panamanian S.A. subscription business: Medium — acceptable as a payment-in/out origination rail, but not as the primary treasury concentration account. Legal constraints: funds held with unlicensed EMIs may not be recognized as 'bank deposits' for Panamanian corporate law purposes, and local banks may require proof that merchant settlement flows originate from a licensed acquirer.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For shareholder equity or subordinated loan contributions into the Panamanian S.A., the remitting bank must use SWIFT MT103 with a clear payment narrative: 'Capital Contribution under Resolución de Junta Directiva', quoting the company corporate ID (RUC or S.F.), the share certificate reference, and the final beneficiary. For subscription card receivables, routing is through a licensed card acquirer (Stripe, Authorize.Net) settling in USD to a US-based commercial bank account, followed by an Interach/Ach payment or SWIFT to the Panamanian account. If the settlement is wired directly from the acquirer, use OFAC-compliant correspondent banks and note 'subscription revenues from internet transactions' as the purpose of payment.Trade disbursements for product sourcing and logistics are executed via SWIFT MT103 for medium‑value payments (under USD 250,000) with invoicing and packing list references. For high‑value or repetitive supplier contracts, banks in Panama issue and confirm Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) against presentation of transport documents, commercial invoices, and insurance certificates. The standard clearing rail is the SWIFT network through correspondent relationships with US and EU banks; domestic large-value movements within Panama may use the Interbank System of the Panamanian Banking Association (ASBL).Currency forwards executed with an SBP‑and/or CCy-regulated banking counterparty in Panama for EUR/USD, GBP/USD, and other convertible pairs.Non‑deliverable forwards (NDFs) offshore for currencies not liquid in the Panamanian inter‑bank market, provided the counterparty is a licensed international bank.Cross‑currency swaps for net investment hedging on any long‑term foreign currency supplier contracts.OTC foreign exchange swaps with horizons of up to twelve months allowed under Panamanian banking practice, without central bank approval.
