Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official portal: www.dgi.gob.pa; e-filing system: SITIO / e-Tax 2.0 (sitioweb.dgi.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima: (1) incorporate before a Notary and inscribe in the Registro Público de Panamá (mandatory resident agent); (2) file the municipal Aviso de Operación and notify MEF before commencing operations; (3) apply online via SITIO for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) attaching certified bylaws, public registry certificate, Aviso de Operación, and legal representative ID/POA (apostilled if executed abroad); DGI issues the RUC in approximately 5-15 business days; (4) for a purely foreign-owned entity with no local branch, appoint a fiscal representative and opt for a non-resident RUC limited to ITBMS compliance and invoicing; (5) obtain the DGI-issued Firma Electrónica digital certificate to activate mandatory e-invoicing. Annual B/.300 franchise tax is payable each January; the RUC number serves as the unified NIT/TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation | Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is an irrebuttable corporate taxpayer taxed only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income (e.g., API subscription fees paid by non-residents and enjoyed abroad) falls outside the Panamanian tax base, subject to the annual sworn declaration of non-subject income (DISN) where gross foreign income exceeds applicable thresholds.

Available election forms

Panama has no check-the-box or pass-through election; an S.A. cannot be treated as transparent. Available elective regimes in the Código Fiscal: (a) optional 'Método Alterno' presumptive basis for small taxpayers; (b) simplified small-taxpayer regime (pequeño contribuyente) for annual gross income up to B/.36,000; (c) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Law 41 of 2007 with preferential territorial taxation for qualifying regional head-office activities; (d) special economic zones (Panama Pacifico, City of Knowledge) for licensed users. No hybrid mismatch or orphan entity elections exist in Panamanian law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.0 — registration is mandatory from the first taxable transaction (no general de minimis threshold); the simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross income up to B/.36,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are ITBMS zero-rated upon physical departure from Panama, evidenced by customs filings. Services — including data APIs, scraping pipelines, and proxy gateways — qualify for the 0% rate when rendered from Panama but 'used and enjoyed' entirely outside Panama: the recipient is a non-resident with no Panamanian permanent establishment, payment originates from abroad, and the service result is not exploited in Panama. The DGI applies the territorial use-and-enjoyment test; the invoice must expressly state 'exportación de servicios' and identify the foreign recipient. Input VAT credits attributable to zero-rated export supplies are creditable and refundable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera / DUA) evidencing physical exit of goods (goods exports only)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (Conocimiento de Embarque) issued to the Panamanian exporter
  • Inward Remittance Certificate — bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing entry of foreign currency proceeds
  • Export service contract specifying the foreign beneficiary and offshore use-and-enjoyment of the API/data services
  • DGI-authorized 0% export invoice and service acceptance certificate (acta de recepción) signed by the foreign customer

Nationwide mandatory e-invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) administered via SITIO: every invoice must be generated in DGI-approved fiscal software and transmitted in real time (API/web-service integration) to the DGI for validation and assignment of an electronic authorization number; XML data structure, QR code, and Firma Electrónica digital signature are required; credit/debit notes are likewise cleared; physical stamps are abolished; full mandatory phase-in for all taxpayers completed as of 2024.

DGI audits cross-match zero-rated invoices against customs, banking, and SWIFT records; export proceeds must enter Panama within the 180-day documentary benchmark. False or unsupported zero-rating is reclassified as taxable ITBMS at 7% plus 10% annual interest arrears; fines of up to B/.5,000 per infraction and up to 100% of omitted tax may be imposed; criminal tax fraud under Law 8 of 2010 carries 3-5 years imprisonment. Assessment/clawback windows: 3 years for filed returns, 6 years for omitted returns, and extended in cases of demonstrated fraud; refunds obtained on fictitious export documentation trigger immediate recovery and fraud prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains full and unconditional convertibility on both current-account and capital-account transactions. There are no exchange-control permits, no central-bank registration of investments, no repatriation licences, and no restriction on the inflow or outflow of foreign exchange. USD circulates freely alongside the balboa, making the economic currency identical to the settlement currency. Profit remittance is likewise unimpeded by FX regulation; the only fiscal friction is the territorial-tax dividend withholding applied on Panamanian-source income (generally 5% where corporates tax was previously paid, rising to 10% on residual untaxed profit pools; foreign-source profits distributed to non-resident shareholders are entirely outside Panamanian tax). For the target S.A. engaged in cross-border API/data services, all operating revenue attributable outside Panama to non-Panamanian sources is structurally exempt and freely remittable without any central-bank authorization file.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation threshold for export proceeds or service-receipt balances. A Panama-domiciled S.A. may retain USD, EUR, or any freely convertible currency in overseas accounts indefinitely, may credit export/service proceeds directly to offshore fintech vaults, and may net outbound disbursements against inbound invoices. The absence of a central bank necessarily eliminates the classical instruments of an export proceeds surrender regime: there is no FX rationing, no selling requirement to a monetary agency, and no declaration obligation to the SBP for commercial trade flows. Only general AML record-keeping under SBP regulations (e.g., Acuerdo de Banca y AML Rules) applies to the underlying payment trail.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama has no issuing central bank. Monetary supervision and banking regulation are discharged by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the Superintendency of Banks of Panama — while Banco Nacional de Panamá (National Bank of Panama) acts as fiscal agent of the Republic and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) oversees fiscal policy. The system is dollarized: the United States dollar is official legal tender and the Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a coin-only monetary expression pegged by law and practice at the rigid ratio of one-to-one with the USD. No Panamanian monetary authority issues paper currency, and no domestic reserve requirement or exchange-control backstop is enforceable.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-High at the treasury-optimization layer but Low for primary statutory banking. Panama does not issue an 'electronic money institution' (EMI) licence and has no equivalent EU-style EMI regime; therefore Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Mercury are not SBP-licensed in-country. Their onboarding of a Panamanian S.A. is discretionary, and many offshore EMIs decline Panama-flagged legal entities because of FATF/GAFILAT mutual-evaluation follow-up sensitivity and non-resident-ownership complexity. However, the target business can legally use a two-tier treasury architecture: (i) overseas fintech and USDC-denominated collecting agents for merchant/API receipts, followed by (ii) a SBP-licensed Panamanian bank account for payroll, statutory maintenance, and audited shareholder distributions. Direct collection of Stripe payouts into Panama-domiciled structures is constrained by Stripe's own country-support matrix rather than by Panamanian law; a common compliant bridge is a US or Ireland-constituted operating subsidiary contracting with Stripe, remitting to the Panama S.A. by SWIFT MT103.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident capital contributions enter Panama by SWIFT MT103 (customer transfer) or MT202 COV (cover transfer) to the S.A.'s operational or escrow account, using a narrative such as 'Share subscription — paid-in capital — Sociedad Anónima' or 'Capital contribution per directors' resolution'. No SBP prior-approval, no exchange-control filing, and no ministry permit is required; Panama's dollarized regime constitutionally tolerates free capital entry. The documentation packet bank-side consists of: notarized and apostilled board resolution approving the share issue, shareholders' subscription ledger, updated Registro Público corporate certificate, valid UBO attestation, and the remitter's bank reference confirming the licensed origin of funds. For the USDC treasury route, the compliant flow is (a) investor deposits USDC into a regulated offshore VASP/exchange account, (b) the VASP converts to USD fiat under its own KYC, and (c) a fiat SWIFT MT103 is credited to the Panama S.A.; any wire whose purpose line persists with a crypto terminology will be rejected at SBP-regulated bank compliance regardless of dollar amount. Funds above USD 100,000 per single injection trigger enhanced source-of-wealth review but remain fully lawful without quantitative limitation.Recurring supplier payments (cloud hosting, proxy-operator bandwidth, outsourced data-labour, licensing fees) are cleared by SWIFT MT103 direct transfer to the beneficiary's bank, with MT202 COV used for correspondent-bank cover where the local clearing architecture requires it. For high-value IT hardware procurement or software-IP licensing commitments, SWIFT MT700 commercial letters of credit and standby MT760/MUT standbys constitute the permissible documentary-clearing rails. Domestic USD disbursements within Panama clear through the SBP-regulated national clearing infrastructure (ACH and RTGS) without any FX conversion because the settlement currency is already USD. Multi-currency outflows to suppliers in EUR or GBP are settled in the bank's nostro network via MT103 with standard EU/G7 clearing; Panamanian banks, unlike some jurisdictions, do not quarantine outgoing service payments for data merchants, provided beneficiary names and invoices align with the declared commercial activity. Outbound disbursements funded from foreign-source API revenue are not subject to Panamanian exchange controls, though banks will require invoice-level reconciliation conformity with the corporate tax-file for amounts above USD 50,000 per transaction.Deliverable outright USD/EUR and USD/GBP forwards — fully permissible and enforceable when executed with an SBP-licensed Panamanian bank or a corresponding G7-clearable foreign counterpart; no central bank authorisation is required because Panama imposes no OTC derivative restriction.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on unhedgeable Latin American currencies (COP, BRL, ARS, MXN) transacted through local bank treasury desks with offshore booking — lawful for matched supplier payables.Cross-currency swaps (USD/EUR/USD and USD/GBP/USD) for matched multi-currency receivable/payable books, involving plain-vanilla interest differential exchange with no underlying export-finance restriction.Vanilla FX options (EUR/USD, GBP/USD) purchased for budget certainty on large cloud-infrastructure transactions, available at Tier-1 desks such as Banco General and Banistmo.No PAB/USD hedging instrument is necessary or available: statutory dollarization fixes the balboa at an absolute 1:1 parity with USD, so only USD money-market instruments (time deposits, repo-style liquidity) are used for cash optimisation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: Medium-High at the treasury-optimization layer but Low for primary statutory banking. Panama does not issue an 'electronic money institution' (EMI) licence and has no equivalent EU-style EMI regime; therefore Wise Business, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Mercury are not SBP-licensed in-country. Their onboarding of a Panamanian S.A. is discretionary, and many offshore EMIs decline Panama-flagged legal entities because of FATF/GAFILAT mutual-evaluation follow-up sensitivity and non-resident-ownership complexity. However, the target business can legally use a two-tier treasury architecture: (i) overseas fintech and USDC-denominated collecting agents for merchant/API receipts, followed by (ii) a SBP-licensed Panamanian bank account for payroll, statutory maintenance, and audited shareholder distributions. Direct collection of Stripe payouts into Panama-domiciled structures is constrained by Stripe's own country-support matrix rather than by Panamanian law; a common compliant bridge is a US or Ireland-constituted operating subsidiary contracting with Stripe, remitting to the Panama S.A. by SWIFT MT103.
