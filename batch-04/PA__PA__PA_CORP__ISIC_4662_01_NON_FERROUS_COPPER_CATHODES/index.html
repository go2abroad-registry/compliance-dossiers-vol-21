Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital and no escrow requirement. The authorized capital is typically fixed at US$10,000 represented by shares of US$1.00 par value (or no-par shares); shares may be issued fully paid against cash or other lawful consideration, and no deposit or escrow with a Panamanian bank is mandated by law.

Funding & unlock path: Office premises require a fire safety inspection certificate from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá; occupational health clearance is issued by the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL) pursuant to the OHS regulations and the General Labor Law; if storage racking is installed, a structural load certificate from the Municipality must be obtained; for pure office trading, the standard occupancy certificate (Certificado de Uso de Suelo) suffices.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance, Republic of Panama; official online taxpayer portal: E-Tax (Panama's DGI electronic tax administration system).

E-government filing portal

For an active Sociedad Anónima: DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) upon application filed by the company's resident agent or accredited legal representative through the E-Tax portal, accompanied by the public deed of incorporation, last corporate registry certificate, and identification documents of directors and officers. A non-resident shareholder need not appear in person. Where the S.A. is not yet in taxable activity, no RUC is required; the RUC becomes mandatory when the company commences Panama-source or export activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available. Under the Código Fiscal, an S.A. is automatically an entity-level corporate taxpayer for all Panama-source income. No pass-through status is elective. Special tax regimes such as SEM (Sede de Empresa Multinacional), Colon Free Zone, or free-zone licences are separate statutory concessions, not entity classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold in Balboa/USD; any person making taxable transfers or providing taxable services in Panamanian territory must register for RUC/ITBMS before commencing activities. The 'pequeño contribuyente' simplified fixed-tax regime is a limited domestic small-business scheme and does not constitute a general registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to tangible goods, including copper cathodes, physically exported from Panamanian customs territory, to re-exports from Colon Free Zone or other free zones, and to ancillary freight or logistics services directly connected to exports. Pure offshore LME warrant transactions in which the goods never enter Panamanian territory and title passes outside Panama are outside the territorial tax base and require no Panama export documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration lodged in SIGA (Sistema Integrado de Gestión Aduanera)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill (original negotiable or certified copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank certificate of receipt of export proceeds in Panama)

Mandatory DGI-authorised electronic invoicing (facturación electrónica) through E-Tax; taxpayers must transmit canonical XML with digital signature for each invoice. DGI validates the transaction and returns a Código de Autorización (authorization code) functioning as a real-time fiscal clearance stamp. Export invoices must reference the SIGA customs export declaration and must be issued with ITBMS at 0%.

DGI and Panama customs co-ordinate audits of SIGA export declarations, export invoices, and FX remittance records. Missing or inconsistent documentation is treated as evidence of a domestic sale and may trigger retroactive 7% ITBMS assessment plus statutory interest, surcharges, and fines. Fraudulent zero-rating claims may give rise to criminal tax exposure and suspension of the exporter's RUC and E-Tax electronic invoice authorisation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Free and complete convertibility across both current and capital accounts. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) circulates only as coins at a fixed 1:1 parity with the US Dollar, which is legal tender for all transactions. Panama imposes no exchange permits, no prior licensing for inbound or outbound wires, and no restriction on profit remittance, dividend distribution, interest payments, or liquidation proceeds payable to non-resident shareholders. The only operational constraint is bank-level AML/CFT reporting: transactions equaling or exceeding USD 10,000 must be evidenced with supporting documentation, and any flow that triggers the Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) threshold requires a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR). For a copper-cathode arbitrage company, where goods move from a foreign supplier to a foreign buyer without entering Panamanian customs territory, the resulting margin is foreign-source income under Panama's territorial tax model; therefore the repatriation runway for commercial margin and invested capital is entirely unrestricted.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama applies no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory repatriation timeline, and no forced conversion ratio for export proceeds because export receipts are invoiced and settled in US Dollars. A copper-trading S.A. holding an operational account with an SBP-licensed bank may retain 100% of export proceeds onshore or offshore without conversion. The only compliance duty is traceability: each receipt must match a valid commercial transaction under Decree Law 2 of 2008 and Law 23 of 2015, evidenced by the commercial invoice, LME warrant transfer, bill of lading, and the corresponding SWIFT MT103 reference.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the Republic of Panama maintains no central bank and no separate foreign-exchange administration; the SBP acts as the prudential supervisor of banks, trust-service providers, and custody institutions, while the United States Federal Reserve functions as the de facto monetary anchor via full dollarization.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% on dividends distributed out of profits already taxed under Panamanian CIT; 10% on dividends paid from untaxed or exempt earnings, including foreign-source margins; no withholding applies to capital redemptions below paid-in capital

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD/PAB 250,000 aggregate related-party cross-border transactions in a fiscal year; this threshold also triggers the mandatory transfer-pricing annex to Formulario 100 and the preparation of Local File and Master File where either related-party transactions exceed USD/PAB 250,000 or gross income exceeds USD/PAB 3,000,000

Economic substance scope: Panama does not maintain a Gulf-style ESR, but the territorial system itself demands an economic activity analysis: only margins produced by functions, assets and risks located in Panama are taxed. Law 134 of 2023 inserted a general anti-avoidance clause allowing the DGI to recharacterize transactions lacking business purpose and to disregard shell companies; a copper-trading S.A. must evidence real board meetings in Panama, a resident agent, substantive bank accounts, and documented negotiation, invoicing and risk decisions, otherwise the foreign-source exemption is denied and the entire margin is reassessed as Panama-source

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud sanctioned by up to six years imprisonment and fines of multiples of the evaded tax; administrative surcharge of 10% and monthly interest of 1%; DGI asset seizure after final assessment

Director personal liability: Directors signing false declarations or approving fictitious invoicing are jointly liable for the defrauded tax, face civil restitution, prohibition to leave Panama (travel ban), disqualification from board positions, and imprisonment as co-authors
Shell Company / Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: DGI recharacterizes the entity, assesses the full trading margin as Panama-source CIT, adds 1% monthly interest and penalties; the S.A. may be flagged as an 'empresa fantasma' and struck from the Public Registry

Director personal liability: Nominee and front directors are personally assessed for unpaid tax and can be prosecuted under Law 23 of 2015 for facilitating fraud; personal bank accounts and assets are subject to freezing orders and travel bans
