Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); electronic portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa), supplemented by the National Customs Authority (ANA) for export controls and the Superintendencia del Mercado de Valores for listed SPAC vehicles.

E-government filing portal

A Panama-domiciled SPAC (Sociedad Anónima) must first complete incorporation in the Sección Mercantil of the Registro Público de Panamá and obtain its Ficha/Certificado de Registro and aviso operación municipal. The SPAC's resident legal representative must then file Formulario RUC-01 through the DGI Sede Electrónica, attaching the authenticated certificate of incorporation, the registered by-laws, the directors' resolution naming the tax representative, proof of the registered office, a notarized power of attorney, and valid identification of the ultimate beneficial owner for AML purposes. The DGI assigns the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) within approximately 5 to 8 business days and simultaneously enrolls the entity in the ISR, ITBMS, and withhold agent registries through the same single-window record. A foreign-incorporated SPAC registering in Panama as a non-domiciled taxpayer must instead obtain a non-resident RUC, appointing a Panamanian resident fiscal representative and filing apostilled incorporation documents from the home registry.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: Panamanian territorial taxation taxes an S.A. only on income derived from a Panamanian source. For the SPAC, foreign-source trust account interest, gains on disposal of shares of non-Panamanian SPAC target entities, and advisory/management fees rendered wholly offshore are outside the ISR base, provided they are not generated by capital deployed in Panama or exploitation of rights in Panamanian territory. The entity remains obliged to file the annual ISR declaration (Formulario 453) with full source-splitting disclosure and to maintain evidence of offshore fund placement. A SPAC admitted to the SEM (Multinational Corporate Headquarters) regime under Law 41 of 2007 and Law 57 of 2018 enjoys a reduced 5% effective ISR rate on qualifying Panamanian-source headquarters services.

Available election forms

Panama does not maintain a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. Available statutory elections are: (i) opt-in to the SEM (Sede de Empresa Multinacional) tax regime by resolution of the Ministry of Commerce and Industries, obtaining a 5% effective ISR rate, ITBMS exemption on intra-group services, and dividend/distribution exemptions for qualifying structures; (ii) election of the special simplified small-taxpayer ISR regime when gross annual income does not exceed B/.200,000; (iii) election under the Fiscal Code to file consolidated annual ISR balance for fiscally linked corporations; and (iv) for a purely offshore SPAC with no Panamanian-source activity, a formal non-domiciliation position is documented to the DGI through the annual affidavit, reducing direct tax exposure to zero without a specific statutory election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration threshold is zero Spanish-balboa: any person carrying out taxable transfers of goods or taxable service provisions in Panama must obtain RUC and ITBMS registration from the first transaction, with no economic turnover floor. A simplified small-taxpayer compliance regime (reduced periodic filing and simplified invoices) applies where annual gross income does not exceed B/.200,000; the SPAC trusts and IPO issuance proceeds placed in escrow are outside ITBMS because share issuance, interest on foreign deposits, and disposal of foreign shares do not constitute a taxable transfer in Panama.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to: (i) goods physically exported from Panamanian customs territory, supported by an approved DUA export declaration; (ii) services rendered to non-resident customers where the service is used, exploited, or consumed entirely outside Panama, as relevant for SPAC advisory, acquisition-management, or registration services billed to foreign target entities; (iii) supplies to duty-free zones and bunkering/provisioning of ships and aircraft. Dividends, interest from offshore institutions, and capital gains from disposal of non-Panamanian shares are not transfers of assets subject to ITBMS at all. The SPAC must not characterize these as zero-rated; instead it must record them as outside the ITBMS tax base in the nil return, supported by documentation of foreign-source fund custody and foreign counterparties.

  • DUA (Documento Único de Aduanas) approved and stamped 'Exportación' by the National Customs Authority (ANA) evidencing physical exit from Panama
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the goods or, for services, a signed service agreement specifying place of performance outside Panama
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR or other transport document evidencing the physical movement of goods from Panama
  • Signed service contract together with attendance or performance records proving the service is supplied to and enjoyed entirely by a non-resident
  • Inward remittance evidence from the foreign counterparty, such as SWIFT MT103 or Panamanian bank credit advice, demonstrating payment from abroad

Real-time electronic invoicing became phased-mandatory under DGI Resolutions 201-3523 (2020) and 202-6684 (2022): large taxpayers from 2022, medium taxpayers from 2023, and small taxpayers through 2024. Every issued invoice must be transmitted to the DGI Sede Electrónica through an authorized electronic billing provider (Proveedor de Facturación Electrónica) or the DGI free web portal; DGI validates the JSON payload and returns a fiscal authorization number and QR code that must be embedded on the invoice before delivery. API integration is mandatory for continuous issuance, while offline batch issuance is permitted for limited intermittent scenarios and must be transmitted to DGI on the next business day. SPACs issuing no Panamanian taxable sales invoices remain outside the invoice authorization requirement but must preserve source documentation evidencing the non-taxable character of their receipts.

Panama denominates in Balboas at parity with the United States dollar, so no FX conversion risk applies; the 180-day period functions as the documentary benchmark for inward remittance substantiation. DGI and ANA cross-match DUA export records with ITBMS credits and bank inward remittances. If zero-rating is claimed without a valid DUA or offshore-service substantiation, DGI may assess the omitted 7% ITBMS, default interest at 10% per annum, a fine of B/.250 to B/.5,000 for each irregular invoice, and a 50% tax surcharge for concealment or evasion. The use of forged export or remittance documentation triggers criminal tax fraud liability under the Código Fiscal and, for SEM-licensed SPACs, revocation of the SEM tax resolution with retroactive clawback of all granted exemptions for the preceding five tax years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Unrestricted full convertibility. Panama imposes no exchange controls on current or capital account transactions; profit remittances, dividends, capital repatriation, and trust disbursements may be effected freely in USD without Central Bank authorization. The Balboa remains a nominal 1:1 USD coin-fraction unit and has no independent exchange rate. Tax standing charges under the territorial regime and beneficial-ownership filings with the Dirección de Administración de Fondos y Fideicomisos (DAFI) are the only procedural preconditions to outward remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama does not impose statutory timelines or mandatory conversion ratios for the surrender of export proceeds or any other foreign-currency receipts. Entities may retain USD and freely hold multi-currency accounts with SBP-licensed banks; there is no repatriation window, no central-bank clearing obligation, and no export-invoice registration requirement.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no conventional central bank and no monetary authority issuing legal tender. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) supervises and regulates all banking activity; the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the state's fiscal agent and the Comisión de Valores (Superintendencia del Mercado de Valores, SMV) supervises securities markets. Foreign exchange policy is effectively subsumed by the de jure dollarization statute under which the United States dollar is full legal tender, with SBP and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) jointly enforcing sanctions, AML/CFT, and payment-system rules.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for this transaction profile. Panama's Fintech Innovation Regime (Law 26 of February 17, 2022) permits sandbox licensing for electronic money and payment-system innovators, but offshore digital EMIs (EU, UK, or BVI-licensed) cannot open settlement accounts, access the local automated clearing house (Cámara de Compensación), or serve as the depositary for SPAC trust proceeds without a full license from the SBP or an authorized Panama bank as correspondent. Given the SPAC's mandatory trust-escrow rail and SWIFT wire settlement, an onshore SBP-licensed bank with corporate trust powers is legally required; an offshore EMI may at most execute pass-through treasury payments via that bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound sponsor seed capital and IPO trust subscription proceeds must enter via SWIFT wire into a segregated trust escrow account held at an SBP-licensed qualified trustee bank in Panama. Wires must carry the purpose description 'Capital Contribution for SPAC Trust' or the SWIFT transfer-of-funds category corresponding to securities subscription, must identify the ultimate remitter and beneficial owner in the remitter information field, and must be preceded by the signed subscription agreement, the SMV-registered prospectus, and the escrow trust agreement. The trustee verifies each credit against the capitalization schedule and runs OFAC, UN, EU, and SBP-consolidated watchlist screening on both the remitter and the beneficiary before accepting funds; any wire lacking a valid securities-public-offering nexus is rejected back to the remitting institution.Business-combination and acquisition closings settle via SWIFT MT103 single-beneficiary wires, with the SPAC trustee releasing trust funds only against a trust certificate and closing memorandum jointly executed by the SPAC board and the trustee. Where the acquisition includes trade assets, inventory, or commercial consideration, a commercial letter of credit (documentary LC, ICC UCP 600) opened by the Panamanian bank on the SPAC's account is the permitted rail for commodity or goods-linked tranches. For publicly-listed target consideration, settlement is effected through an SMV-registered intermediary using DTC or Euroclear instructions rather than direct bank transfer. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta, ISR) on net taxable income derived from Panamanian-source activities

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends distributed to non-resident shareholders and resident individuals; 5% when the recipient is a Panamanian-resident legal entity. Distributions of paid-in capital (strict SPAC principal redemption) are not dividends if the board expressly resolves a capital reduction and records it with the Mercantile Registry before payment.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after the fiscal year-end; for calendar-year corporations the general statutory deadline is March 15, but the DGI applies a staggered calendar based on the RUC last digit extending the filing window into April/May. 'Gran Contribuyente' (large taxpayer) status mandates exclusive e-filing and a potential acceleration of deadlines.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File / Master File documentation (Informe de Precios de Transferencia) applies where annual gross income exceeds B/.2,500,000 and the taxpayer engaged in cross-border related-party transactions; a related-party annex is required at lower thresholds. SPACs entering sponsor management agreements, merger-consulting fees, or deferred underwriting arrangements with foreign affiliates must document those transactions even when the taxable base is nil.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) derive from Law 70 of 2019 and Executive Decree No. 43 of 2020. A tax-resident S.A. earning foreign-source income from covered activities—of which investment holding is expressly included—must demonstrate adequate substance in Panama: physical office space, locally engaged and competent staff, board meetings and management decisions held in Panama, and complete accounting records kept in-country. A SPAC relying on the territorial exemption for foreign trust interest is squarely within the ESR review scope; the trust's genuine third-party character and the presence of an independent Panamanian director materially strengthen the substance file. The annual ESR informative return must be filed with the DGI regardless of tax position.
