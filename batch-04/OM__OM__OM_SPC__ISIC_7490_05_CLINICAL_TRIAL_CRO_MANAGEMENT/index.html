Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General of Taxation (SGT), Ministry of Finance, Sultanate of Oman; electronic portal: taxes.gov.om. Primary commercial registration: Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) via Invest Easy (investeasy.gov.om); customs controls: Royal Oman Police Customs (ROP Customs).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Foreign shareholder executes the Single Person Company (SPC) constitutive documents; no statutory minimum capital is imposed and 100% foreign ownership is permissible. Step 2: Incorporate with MoCIIP via Invest Easy and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; for clinical trial CRO operations, attach the license issued by the Omani Ministry of Health (Directorate General of Pharmaceutical Affairs). Step 3: Register the SPC with the SGT within the prescribed period after CR issuance; the SGT issues the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and tax card via taxes.gov.om; the TIN is a mandatory precondition for maintaining bank accounts, customs clearance and public procurement. Step 4: If annual taxable supplies meet the mandatory threshold (OMR 38,500), file the separate VAT registration application with SGT; voluntary registration is available at OMR 19,250. Step 5: Register as a withholding agent if the SPC will pay non-resident counterparties in amounts potentially subject to the 10% withholding tax on management and technical fees.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope. The Omani-incorporated SPC is a resident taxable person taxed on Omani-source income at the corporate income tax rates (3% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income and 15% on the excess); no pass-through or tax-transparent treatment is available. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope. The Omani-incorporated SPC is a resident taxable person taxed on Omani-source income at the corporate income tax rates (3% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income and 15% on the excess); no pass-through or tax-transparent treatment is available. A free-zone income tax holiday is not applicable because the CRO will be located in mainland Muscat; the SPC is a separate juridical person for all tax purposes.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid-mismatch or entity classification election exists under Omani law. Available elections and administrative options include: (1) approval by SGT of a fiscal year different from the calendar year; (2) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 19,250 threshold; (3) VAT tax-group registration for related Omani entities subject to SGT approval; (4) automatic application of the small-business 3% income tax slab, which is not elective; and (5) structured contracting to support zero-rating of cross-border CRO services, with local clinical site services remaining standard-rated in the absence of substantive export evidence.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 annual taxable supplies (mandatory); OMR 19,250 annual taxable supplies (voluntary registration). Non-resident suppliers supplying taxable services electronically or in practice to Omani recipients may be required to register regardless of the local threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Oman qualify for 0% VAT if evidenced by a customs export declaration. Services qualify for zero-rating where supplied to a non-resident recipient that has no Oman establishment and the services are consumed wholly outside the Sultanate. For a clinical trial CRO SPC, only the genuinely offshore management-services component (protocol design, data management, overseas regulatory coordination) may qualify if contractual terms, place-of-benefit and substance evidence support exclusive foreign use; clinical site operations, patient-facing services and specimen testing performed in Oman are place-of-supply Oman services and must be standard-rated at 5%. The SPC must maintain sponsor agreements evidencing foreign recipient status, SGT-registered place-of-supply reasoning, Ministry of Health clinical trial authorization, and segregation of locally consumed services.

  • Customs Export Declaration endorsed by Royal Oman Police Customs for any physical goods component (clinical trial kits, biological samples), together with Bill of Lading or Air Waybill; for pure services, a place-of-supply reasoning memorandum and Ministry of Health clinical trial authorization replace the customs declaration
  • Commercial invoice incorporating the 0% zero-rating clause, the relevant VAT Law article, recipient identification, and a statement that the service or goods is exported outside the Sultanate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT payment receipt issued by an Omani bank evidencing remittance of the export consideration from the foreign sponsor within 180 days of the supply

No operational real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping or mandatory API clearance mandate exists in Oman as of 2025. SGT accepts electronic invoices provided they satisfy the VAT invoicing fields and archival requirements; taxpayers using ERP systems must retain machine-readable copies for audit. Oman has announced a phased adoption of a unified GCC-aligned e-invoicing framework; until clearance becomes mandatory, the SPC should design its systems to be e-invoicing-ready and should apply the reverse-charge mechanism to imported services from the foreign sponsor.

SGT may open an audit within the statutory limitation period (generally five years, extended in fraud or concealment cases). Unsupported zero-rating claims are treated as deliberate evasion: the SPC will be assessed retrospectively at 5% VAT on the full invoice value, with monthly late-payment penalties, plus administrative fines under the Omani VAT penalty schedule (ranging from OMR 100 to OMR 10,000 per violation). Aggravated tax evasion may trigger a fine of up to 200% of the evaded tax and criminal prosecution. Under parallel income tax rules, any repatriation shortfall after the 180-day period may be recharacterized as Omani-source income for the SPC, exposing the amount to 15% corporate tax plus related penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the U.S. dollar at approximately OMR 0.3845 per USD. Oman maintains full current-account convertibility and does not impose exchange-control licensing on current payments. Capital-account inflows and outflows, including foreign direct investment and profit repatriation by a 100% foreign-owned SPC, are generally permitted without CBO-specific prior approval; however, the receiving bank must satisfy CBO regulatory reporting, AML/CFT screening, and underlying-document verification. Profit remittance is achievable after the SPC has complied with tax registration, transfer-pricing documentation, and any corporate tax filing requirements in force.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratios on export proceeds for a clinical-trial CRO. Service-export revenue denominated in USD/EUR may be retained in a foreign-currency account at an Omani bank or converted to OMR at the prevailing pegged rate. The CRO should retain executed clinical-trial agreements, invoices, milestone confirmations, and incoming swift message evidence to support future tax and balance-of-payments reporting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - Financial Services Supervision Department; no separate foreign-exchange control agency exists

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-Low. Offshore digital EMIs and payment platforms not licensed by the CBO cannot open an onshore local-currency account or function as the SPC's primary treasury bank. Such platforms may be used by the foreign sponsor for its own disbursements, but any Omani-resident SPC should maintain its principal operating accounts at a CBO-licensed bank. EMIs may act only as an auxiliary collection or payment channel when the funds are clearly attributable to the clinical-trial agreement and every payment is accompanied by an audit trail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The sponsor will issue a SWIFT MT103 from the sponsor's bank to the SPC's capital escrow account at an Omani licensed bank. The remittance should use a clear beneficiary description showing the SPC's legal name and Commercial Registration number. In the remittance information field, the payer should state 'Share Capital Contribution - Foreign Direct Investment - Clinical Trial CRO' and reference the subscribed capital amount shown in the SPC's constitutional documents. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: annual related-party transactions aggregating OMR 100,000 or more. Master File: multinational group with consolidated revenue above OMR 20 million. Country-by-Country reporting applies where the group exceeds the higher consolidated threshold prescribed by the Oman Tax Authority.

Economic substance scope: Oman has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations for CROs; however, Article 55 GAAR, beneficial-ownership rules under the AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016), and treaty 'beneficial ownership' gatekeepers require the SPC to maintain real premises, staff, contracts, and decision-making functions in Oman. Substance documentation must be contemporaneous.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Understatement

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 25% of the under-assessed tax (minimum OMR 500), 1% monthly late-payment surcharge, criminal fine up to OMR 25,000, and imprisonment up to 3 years under the Income Tax Law.

Director personal liability: Civil joint liability with the SPC for the evaded tax; criminal conviction of the SPC manager/directors; enforcement measures include travel bans, asset seizure, and disbarment from holding directorships.
