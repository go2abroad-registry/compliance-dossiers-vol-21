Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI / 'T@x Panamá' (https://www.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident owner forms a Panama Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) through a licensed registered agent, files incorporation articles with the Public Registry, obtains a legal domicile, then applies to the DGI for a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente). The application must include corporate excerpts, bylaws, registered agent certification, appointment of directors/officers, and proof of local address. The RUC is granted electronically or at DGI service offices and the entity is simultaneously registered for ITBMS, withholding tax, and other tax obligations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign non-resident owner forms a Panama Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) through a licensed registered agent, files incorporation articles with the Public Registry, obtains a legal domicile, then applies to the DGI for a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente). The application must include corporate excerpts, bylaws, registered agent certification, appointment of directors/officers, and proof of local address. The RUC is granted electronically or at DGI service offices and the entity is simultaneously registered for ITBMS, withholding tax, and other tax obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. All Panamanian S.A. corporations are automatically taxed as separate entity-level taxpayers. Eligible businesses may elect special incentive regimes: Multinational Headquarters (SEM) under Law 41 of 2007, Panama-Pacífico Special Economic Zone, or City of Knowledge, subject to qualifying activities. Small taxpayers with annual gross turnover not exceeding B/.20,000 may elect the simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general minimum sales threshold for ITBMS registration; habitual commercial sellers must register regardless of volume. A simplified small-taxpayer regime is available for annual gross turnover up to B/.20,000, above which standard 7% ITBMS is charged on taxable transfers and services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% ITBMS where they are physically exported from Panamanian territory to a foreign destination under a valid customs export declaration. Services are zero-rated when rendered from Panama to a non-resident recipient with the benefit and use occurring wholly outside Panama, or when provided as part of an export transaction. The exporter must be an active registered ITBMS taxpayer and maintain contemporaneous proof of export and foreign receipt.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill proving physical departure from Panama
  • Commercial Invoice listing the foreign buyer and shipment terms
  • Inward remittance evidence (bank SWIFT/MT103 or payment gateway settlement report showing foreign funds received in Panama or on behalf of the exporting entity)
  • Packaging list and contract of sale showing title transfer to a foreign customer

Panama mandates electronic invoicing for registered taxpayers through the DGI's 'T@x' system. Invoice data must be transmitted in DGI-validated XML format using a digital signature certificate; a QR-code/electronic stamp and DGI transaction authorization code must be included before the sale is considered fiscally complete. Clearance and authorization occur in near real time via the DGI API gateway.

The DGI may audit export dossiers and disprove zero-rating where export entries, transport documents, or payment repatriation are missing or inconsistent. False claims are treated as ITBMS evasion: the 0% rate is reversed, and the taxpayer must pay the full 7% ITBMS on gross proceeds, plus monthly late-payment interest of 1% per month, a penalty ranging from 100% to 300% of the omitted tax, and potential criminal liability of two to six years' imprisonment under Panama's tax criminal provisions (Law 70 of 2019).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a nominal accounting unit pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar; no Balboa banknotes are issued, and the USD is legal tender for all domestic and cross-border obligations. Panama maintains a fully liberalised capital and current account regime: there are no surrender, conversion, licensing, prior-approval, or repatriation restrictions on any currency. Non-resident shareholders may freely remit dividends, loan repayments, branch profits, and liquidation proceeds. The only relevant frictions are fiscal: profits sourced outside Panama fall outside the territorial tax net, while dividends distributed out of Panamanian-source taxable profits attract a 10% withholding dividend tax (or 5% for registered shares); the municipal, educational, and shareholder-accounting formalities apply solely to Panama-source income. No exchange-control approval is required for an e-commerce operation collecting USD revenue from online buyers and settling to a Panama S.A. bank account.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory deadline for export proceeds repatriation, no mandatory conversion ratio, no requirement to channel export receipts through a designated central-bank account, and no withholding on export proceeds. For AML documentary integrity, banks request the commercial invoice, packing list, Bill of Lading/Air Waybill, and the Panama customs declaration to evidence the underlying cross-border sale. For digital maternity and baby supplies sales, an equivalent evidentiary bundle comprises the merchant agreement, platform settlement statements, and order-level invoices.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama has no central bank with monetary, monetary-exchange, or capital-control functions. The banking supervisory authority is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, 'SBP'), established under Decreto Ley No. 2 of 2008 as amended. The Panamanian fiscal agent is Banco Nacional de Panamá, a state-owned commercial bank, which does not perform central-bank FX regulation. The Unidad de Análisis Financiero ('UAF') and the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas jointly enforce AML/CFT obligations, including the beneficial-ownership reporting regime under Law 70 of 2019.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH as a payment-acquirer collection channel (Stripe, PayPal, Shopify Payments) and LOW as a standalone domestic clearing rail. Panama has no dedicated e-money licence; digital banking and fintech pilot programmes operate through the SBP regulatory sandbox and can enable limited electronic-money issuance under a full banking licence. An offshore EMI or non-bank PSP without a Panama banking licence cannot access the Panamanian ACH or the domestic clearing system, and Panamanian Tier-1 banks generally treat incoming credits from an unlicensed foreign EMI as higher-risk third-party payments. The recommended treasury architecture is a Panama S.A. USD account at a Tier-1 licensed bank receiving daily settlement payout wires from Stripe/PayPal through their US correspondent banks; the offshore EMI should be used only for regional payout collection, with periodic SWIFT sweeps into the Panama account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Step 1 - Adopt a board resolution authorising the issuance of registered shares at an agreed subscription price and update the Panama Share Registry. Step 2 - Execute an international SWIFT MT103 wire from the shareholder's account to the Panama S.A. account, entering in Field 70 the ISO 20022 purpose code 'INVS' or 'EQUI' with the narrative 'Paid-in capital subscription' plus the company name and RUC. Step 3 - Deliver to the receiving bank the apostilled Certificado de Registro (Articles of Incorporation), the certified directors' resolution, the share subscription agreement, the economic-substance statement, and the RUC taxpayer card. Step 4 - No SBP approval, registration, or FX declaration is required; the bank will only screen the transaction under its AML thresholds. For operating capital, receivables from Stripe/PayPal must be presented as trade proceeds supported by merchant agreements and platform settlement reports, otherwise the bank may reclassify them as unexplained third-party payments and freeze the funds pending further KYC.Domestic USD disbursements inside Panama settle through the Panamanian ACH and the Cámara de Compensación operated by Banco Nacional de Panamá, suitable for payroll, rent, and local supplier payments. 10% for dividends paid out of profits that were subject to Panamanian corporate income tax; 15% for dividends paid out of profits not subject to tax (including foreign-source or exempt profits). A special 5% rate applies to Colon Free Zone distributions only.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/.250,000 (USD 250,000) - annual aggregate related-party transactions exceeding this threshold trigger mandatory Local File documentation; Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds B/.13 million (Panama TP regulation).

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 70 of 2019, Executive Decree 31 of 2020) apply to companies performing defined 'relevant activities' such as distribution and service centers, IP holding, financing, leasing, shipping, and head-office services. A purely local online retailer of maternity/infant supplies is not a relevant activity unless it operates as a related-party distribution hub or derives passive income; nevertheless, the company must file the annual declaration of beneficial owners and maintain demonstrated local management and operational capacity if any cross-border related-party arrangement exists.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to twice to five times the evaded tax, plus monthly default interest; criminal sanctions under the Penal Code (money fraud) including imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and the loss of all tax incentives; the S.A.'s commercial license may be revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors, the resident agent, or the financial officer who signs false returns are solidarily liable for the evaded tax and face criminal imprisonment, travel bans, asset embargoes, and seizure of personal assets; courts may pierce the corporate veil upon proof of fraud.
Shell Company / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to USD 10,000 per infraction, cancellation of the legal entity's rights and licenses, and immediate recording of the deficiency in the Public Registry; repeat violations may lead to a dissolution order.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and resident agents face personal fines and may be removed from the registry; beneficial owners found to have concealed control may be subjected to AML investigation, asset attachment, travel restrictions, and, if fraud is proven, criminal prosecution.
AML/CPF Abuse via Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Money laundering offenses under Law 23 of 2015 carry penalties of 5 to 12 years' imprisonment, fines equivalent to twice the value of the laundered property, and confiscation of the financial infrastructure (bank accounts and payment processor contracts).

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who knowingly facilitate the channeling of funds face criminal imprisonment, travel bans, and personal confiscation orders; the corporate veil is lifted automatically for money laundering, exposing directors' personal assets to civil and penal liability.
