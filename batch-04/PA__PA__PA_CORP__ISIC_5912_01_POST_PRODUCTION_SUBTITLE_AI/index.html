Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance (Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas); e-portal: Sede Electrónica — https://dgi.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Panamanian S.A.: (1) Notarize and register the by-laws with the Public Registry; (2) obtain an electronic signature (Firma Electrónica) for the Sede Electrónica from a licensed certification authority; (3) file the DGI Form 'Solicitud de Inscripción del Contribuyente (RUC)' together with the registered by-laws, power of attorney of the resident legal representative, and proof of local address; (4) obtain the provisional RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) and then the definitive RUC; (5) activate all obligations: Income Tax (ISR), ITBMS, and withholding taxes; (6) request activation as an 'Emisor Electrónico' for the mandatory e-invoicing system, which is completed through the DGI Sede Electrónica API platform.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — For a foreign-owned Panamanian S.A.: (1) Notarize and register the by-laws with the Public Registry; (2) obtain an electronic signature (Firma Electrónica) for the Sede Electrónica from a licensed certification authority; (3) file the DGI Form 'Solicitud de Inscripción del Contribuyente (RUC)' together with the registered by-laws, power of attorney of the resident legal representative, and proof of local address; (4) obtain the provisional RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) and then the definitive RUC; (5) activate all obligations: Income Tax (ISR), ITBMS, and withholding taxes; (6) request activation as an 'Emisor Electrónico' for the mandatory e-invoicing system, which is completed through the DGI Sede Electrónica API platform.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box exists; each S.A. treated as autonomous corporate taxpayer under the territorial system. Available elections: (i) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Law 41 of 2007 with special ISR/ITBMS exemptions on offshore service income; (ii) Panama Pacifico Special Economic Zone benefits under Law 41 of 2004; (iii) simplified 'Small Taxpayer' regime for gross income up to USD 36,000; (iv) no hybrid mismatch elections recognized; Panama applies OECD-BEPS minimum substance requirements, including the annual Economic Substance Declaration (Form 3770).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.36,000 (USD 36,000) in annual gross taxable income for services and digital services; sellers of goods must register irrespective of turnover; executive decrees and DGI regulations govern the threshold and digital services nexus

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to (i) physical exports of goods leaving the Panamanian customs territory and (ii) export of services to non-residents effectively used, exploited, or consumed outside Panama (territoriality principle under DGI Resolution No. 201-2522). AI subtitle transcription and voice-cloning services delivered from Panama to foreign production houses qualify as exported services when the foreign recipient has no Panamanian establishment, the service contract identifies foreign benefit and use, and the consideration is paid from abroad; input ITBMS may be recovered via DGI-issued credit certificate or offset against other taxes.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA — Declaración Única Aduanera) filed in the SIUCA/SiCEA customs system
  • Bill of Lading (maritime) or Airway Bill (AWB) evidencing physical export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing foreign-currency payment
  • Commercial Invoice and signed service contract or purchase order containing offshore-use clauses
  • Certificate of Origin (optional, applicable when claiming tariff preferences)

Mandatory Facturación Electrónica en Línea (FEL); phased implementation from 2021 for taxpayers with annual income above B/.300,000, extended to all ITBMS registrants by January 1, 2023; requires a digital certificate, DGI pre-authorization of the XML invoice schema, real-time or near-real-time API transmission to the DGI Sede Electrónica, and mandatory QR code, unique correlative number, and digital stamp (sello electrónico) on issued documents

DGI customs and substantive audits re-verify physical exit of goods and economic benefit outside Panama for services. Adverse findings trigger: (i) retrospective ITBMS clawback with monthly interest of 1.5% under Código Fiscal Articles 685/687; (ii) administrative fines of 100% to 300% of the omitted tax for false zero-rating declarations; (iii) criminal liability for tax evasion under Law 8 of 2010; (iv) RUC suspension and cancellation of exporter/refund program status; (v) mandatory reimbursement of refunded credit certificates with surcharges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital and current account convertibility exists without exchange controls. Foreign-owned entities may freely remit profits, dividends, and capital proceeds, subject only to territorial-source income tax assessment. No prior approval or conversion mandate applies as USD is the domestic currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds. All export receipts may be retained or disposed of freely in USD or any convertible foreign currency; there is no timeline or ratio imposed on exporters.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) – the sole banking and financial regulator; there is no autonomous central bank, and the U.S. dollar is the official legal tender under Panamanian dollarization.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-High from a transactional perspective. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise and Stripe are not licensed as banks in Panama and may not hold client deposits; they act as payment processors and e-money proxies. Viability is confirmed solely as an ancillary collection/disbursement layer, while all treasury functions must be anchored in a licensed Panamanian bank account.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File where controlled transactions exceed USD 1,000,000 (B/. 1,000,000); Master File required when the Panamanian taxpayer forms part of an MNE group with consolidated revenue exceeding USD 50,000,000, or upon specific DGI request.

Economic substance scope: Panama ESR (Law 123 of 2020 and subsequent regulations) is applicable to Panamanian S.A.s performing 'relevant activities', expressly including intellectual property holding, financing, distribution, and service companies. An AI subtitling/voice-cloning studio generating IP royalties must maintain real staff, premises, and core income-generating operations in Panama; failure to do so causes the entity to be treated as a shell, with income reclassified as Panamanian-source.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fiscal Code criminal sanctions: fine of 50% to 10x the evaded tax, plus 1.5% monthly interest; administrative closure of the taxpayer's establishment; loss of fiscal credits and permits.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives who sign false declarations are personally liable for evasion; criminal fiscal penalties include imprisonment of 1 to 5 years, travel bans, and freezing of personal and real estate assets.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Panama ESR, failure to file the annual economic substance report or failure to prove substance attracts fines from B/. 10,000 to B/. 100,000; DGI may disregard the entity and assess tax retroactively on the reclassified Panamanian-source income.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and shadow managers can be jointly and severally liable for fines and assessed taxes; the Superintendencia of Non-Financial Subjects may order cancellation of the corporate registration and prohibit the individual from holding director positions in Panama.
Transfer Pricing Mismatch / Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: DGI primary adjustment plus 10% surcharge on under-declared tax and monthly interest of 1.5%; administrative fine of up to B/. 5,000 for each missing TP document; if the adjustment is fraudulent, criminal tax penalties apply.

Director personal liability: Where the TP transaction is a sham, the directors who signed intercompany agreements and expense vouchers can be held personally liable for the underpaid tax and interest; criminal liability arises upon proof of intentional misrepresentation.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Disclosure Failure

Statutory sanction: Panama AML Law 2020 sanctions for non-financial entities range from B/. 100,000 to B/. 5,000,000; loss of banking relations; administrative dissolution; referral to the Public Ministry for money-laundering cases punishable by up to 10 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly conceal UBOs or process proceeds of criminal conduct face criminal prosecution, personal asset seizure, travel bans, and imprisonment; civil liability for damages incurred by third parties may also attach.
