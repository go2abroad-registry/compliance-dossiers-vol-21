Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Republic of Panama, functionally integrated into the Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP) from 2024; official portals: www.dgi.gob.pa and www.anip.gob.pa. Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction (PAB pegged at par to USD), and all tax registrations, returns and payments are denominated in Balboas/USD.

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — incorporate the Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) at the Registro Público de Panamá and obtain the Certificado de Persona Jurídica; Step 2 — appoint a Panamanian resident agent (agente residente) as the statutory legal representative and tax domicile; Step 3 — file the Aviso de Operación with the municipal treasury of the registered address; Step 4 — apply online with ANIP/DGI for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) using the public-registry certificate, the agent's power of attorney and the corporate questionnaire describing ISIC 5820 software and video-game publishing activity; Step 5 — obtain the RUC certificate and enroll in the mandatory electronic invoicing regime; Step 6 — open the Panamanian corporate bank account, which banks cross-validate against the RUC. A foreign-owned S.A. supplying global MMORPG services through app stores is classified as an exporter of services with ITBMS obligations at the 0% rate.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Panamanian law; the S.A. is per se an entity-level territorial taxpayer. Statutorily available options: (i) election of non-operating status — an S.A. whose business and revenue generation occur entirely outside Panama is treated as not economically operating in Panama and is subject only to the flat annual franchise tax (Tasa Única, currently USD 300) and the annual information declaration; (ii) simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime (annual gross retail turnover below PAB 200,000), which is not applicable to an app-store export model; (iii) no pass-through classification is available for an S.A., and Panama does not implement BEPS Action 2 hybrid mismatch rules for this entity type.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general ITBMS registration threshold applies to corporate exporters; any person making taxable transfers or rendering services with Panamanian nexus must register before the first taxable act. A simplified small-taxpayer regime applies only to traditional retail traders with annual gross income below PAB 200,000, not to the MMORPG publisher's app-store export model. Non-resident digital platforms providing services to Panamanian users are separately required to register and remit 7% ITBMS; the Panamanian S.A. exporter remains a resident supplier qualifying for 0% export treatment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (tarifa 0%) under ITBMS, not exemption, is available for (i) goods physically exported from Panama and (ii) services exported from Panama to non-residents, provided the consideration is paid from abroad and the economic use or exploitation of the service occurs outside Panama. The MMORPG publisher qualifies by proving: (a) the Panamanian S.A. is the principal licensor or distributor of the game through the app stores; (b) all end-user consideration originates from foreign users via Apple and Google settlement entities; (c) the digital service is not consumed in Panama; and (d) the revenue is received in foreign currency into the Panamanian bank account. The 0% rate must appear line-item on ANIP-certified e-invoices and on the monthly ITBMS return; resulting ITBMS credit balances (saldos a favor) are refundable through the formal refund application submitted to ANIP after documentary verification.

  • ANIP/DGI Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) filed through the national single window for any physical export of goods, software media or master recordings
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued by the international carrier evidencing physical departure of the goods from Panamanian customs territory
  • Commercial invoice annotated with the 0% ITBMS export legend and the corresponding zero-rated e-invoice with CAD/QR code
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the receiving Panamanian bank, or SWIFT MT103/MT202 evidence, proving foreign-currency collection from abroad
  • For digital services: Apple App Store and Google Play payout statements, the IP license and distribution agreements with the storefronts, monthly export-service reconciliation, and the ITBMS Form 441 filed with ANIP showing the zero-rated export lines

Mandatory real-time fiscal clearance. The Panamanian electronic invoicing regime (Facturación Electrónica), implemented under DGI Resolution No. 201-1663 of 14 April 2021 and administered by ANIP/DGI, requires every RUC holder to issue e-invoices through ANIP-certified billing software, transmit the electronic XML in real time to ANIP's fiscal clearance platform, and display the valid Código de Autenticación Digital (CAD) and QR code on each Documento Tributario Electrónico. Export invoices must be issued at the 0% rate with the corresponding export annexure code, and the monthly ITBMS return (Form 441) and annual income tax return are filed through the ANIP e-filing system. For distribution via Apple App Store and Google Play, zero-rated e-invoices are issued to non-resident customers and reconciled against platform payout statements.

ANIP audits zero-rating claims by matching e-invoice export lines against customs declarations for goods and against cross-border settlement and bank remittance records for digital services. Where export evidence fails traceability, ANIP reclassifies the transactions to 7% ITBMS, assesses accrued late interest and levies fines from PAB 50 to PAB 5,000 under the Código Fiscal. Intentional falsification of export documents to obtain zero-rating or illicit refunds constitutes tax fraud (defraudación tributaria) under Law No. 116 of 2019, punishable by imprisonment and monetary penalties, with retrospective clawback of credited or refunded ITBMS. The statute of limitations for corrective assessments is five fiscal years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility. The Panamanian banking system operates wholly in U.S. dollars, and there is no capital account or current account convertibility control. Dividends, profit remittances, royalty payments, service fees, and capital repatriation may be freely made without prior central bank authorization. Panama applies a territorial tax system, which further reduces compliance friction for offshore digital gaming revenue. The only documentary requirements arise from bank KYC/AML policies and tax reporting obligations (e.g., Form 960 for territorial income under relevant modalities). There is no exchange-rate spread imposed by the government and no mandatory conversion of inbound or outbound payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender or conversion requirement exists. Panama does not mandate the repatriation, surrender, or forced conversion of export receipts into local currency. For the MMORPG global publishing entity, proceeds from Apple App Store and Google Play payouts may be freely retained in USD or any foreign convertible currency, transferred to offshore treasury accounts, and used without time limit or conversion ratio. No export declaration is required for services or digital goods for tax purposes beyond normal invoicing and local territorial tax compliance.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank. The monetary framework is based on the free circulation of the U.S. dollar as legal tender, with the balboa as a nominal unit at parity. Banking regulation is carried out by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); fiscal and macro-prudential policy is coordinated by the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF). No central FX approval body exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate feasibility with significant legal constraint. Offshore digital EMI institutions are not authorized to take deposits or operate as banks in Panama without an SBP banking or payment-services license. They may function solely as supplementary collection or disbursement rails, particularly for App Store/Google Play settlement conversion and low-value disbursements, but must not be the primary treasury concentration point. Funds held with unlicensed EMIs are not protected by Panamanian banking secrecy or deposit guarantees and may trigger heightened AML scrutiny when wired to tier-1 Panamanian bank accounts. A vetted fintech partner offering segregated client accounts and full API audit trailing can be used but must be underwritten by a top-tier bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File threshold: Transactions with related parties (including foreign parent companies, affiliates, and shareholders) exceeding PAB 250,000 (USD 250,000) annually. Full contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is required. Taxpayers below this threshold must still comply with the arm's length principle and file TP informational return (Form 02-TP) with basic data if controlled transactions exceed PAB 75,000.

Economic substance scope: Panama enacted Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) via Law 254 of 2021 and Executive Decree 255 of 2022, applicable to entities licensed in Panama or benefiting from a Panama legal regime (including Sociedad Anónima with a corporate license and tax ID). Covered activities include distribution and service centers, financing, leasing, and intellectual property holding. An MMORPG publisher holding IP or receiving royalties in Panama must demonstrate: (1) core income-generating activities (development, server operations, content updates) occurring in Panama; (2) local employees with qualified staff; (3) adequate local physical premises and expenditures; (4) compliance with annual ESR notification (Form 01-ESR). Failure results in annual penalties up to USD 50,000 for false declarations and potential deregistration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (DGI assessment and criminal referral)

Statutory sanction: Civil: tax due plus 50% surcharge, monthly interest at 10% annual from due date, and a fine of 100% of evaded tax if intentional (Article 709-b of the Tax Code). Criminal: 1–5 years imprisonment for tax fraud, with increased penalties for amounts exceeding USD 300,000.

Director personal liability: In cases of fraud, directors and officers who signed false returns or authorized evasion can face personal civil liability for the corporation's unpaid taxes, criminal fines, and imprisonment. Travel bans may be issued under DGI's 'preventive embargo' powers. Veil-piercing is available where the SA is used as the 'alter ego' of the director.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to 30% of the understated taxable income resulting from TP adjustments; immediate disallowance of deductions. Escalation includes confiscation of related benefits if abusive.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign TP reports without adequate economic analysis may be deemed jointly liable for penalties under DGI fraud provisions, subject to criminal liability if false documents are submitted.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance (ESR)

Statutory sanction: ESR fines up to USD 1,000,000 for significant violations (Law 254). The tax authority may recharacterize the SA as a tax-sheltered entity, impose 25% tax on foreign-source income deemed effectively managed from Panama, and revoke the corporate license.

Director personal liability: Directors of a shell SA can be named in prosecution under the Criminal Code Article 252 (illicit association) if they knowingly facilitate tax abuse; travel restrictions can be issued during criminal investigation, and civil liability may extend to the turnover of all profits derived from the sham.
Money Laundering / AML Violation (banking and game payment rails)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to PAB 5,000,000 under Law 23 of 2015; license revocation; criminal asset forfeiture; imprisonment of 5–10 years for directors involved in laundering organized crime proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors are criminally liable for failing to establish internal AML controls within the legal entity; personal fines up to USD 1,000,000, travel bans, and extradition risk if foreign enforcement cooperates.
Failure to File Sworn Declarations (Informative Returns)

Statutory sanction: Per-form fine of PAB 500 to PAB 10,000; disbarment from government procurement; non-issuance of the Paz y Salvo (tax clearance certificate), blocking any asset sale or dividend distribution.

Director personal liability: Directors of the SA may be prevented from leaving Panama if the entity has outstanding tax debts; DGI may issue an embargo against personal assets if the director is deemed the de facto taxpayer due to fraudulent use of nominee shareholders.
