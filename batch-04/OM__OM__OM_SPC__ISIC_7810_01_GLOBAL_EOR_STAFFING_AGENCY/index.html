Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a foreign-owned Single Person Company under the Commercial Companies Law. The sole member must subscribe to all issued shares and the capital quantum stated in the Articles of Association must be paid in full in cash or in kind. No mandatory escrow account is required for a standard SPC unless the Ministry of Labour or another licensing authority imposes a working-capital guarantee as a condition of the ISIC 7810 staffing license.

Funding & unlock path: Obtain a Civil Defense Fire and Life Safety Certificate from Royal Oman Police Civil Defense for the registered office; secure the municipal occupancy and structural-load clearance for the leased floor space; comply with Ministry of Labour occupational health and safety requirements for employees, including first aid, fire extinguishers, exit signage, and a documented workplace safety policy.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority (Secretariat General of Taxation), Sultanate of Oman – official portal: https://tax.gov.om

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) must: (1) complete incorporation and obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); (2) create an account on the Tax Authority portal using the CR number and the licensed manager's/owner's identification; (3) submit the CR, Articles of Association, valid owner passport/corporate entity documents, Oman bank account details, and declaration of projected taxable supplies; (4) obtain the Corporate Tax Registration Number (TIN) and, where VAT registration is mandatory or voluntary, the VAT ID/registration certificate from the same portal. Tax registration is mandatory before commencing taxable activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) must: (1) complete incorporation and obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); (2) create an account on the Tax Authority portal using the CR number and the licensed manager's/owner's identification; (3) submit the CR, Articles of Association, valid owner passport/corporate entity documents, Oman bank account details, and declaration of projected taxable supplies; (4) obtain the Corporate Tax Registration Number (TIN) and, where VAT registration is mandatory or voluntary, the VAT ID/registration certificate from the same portal. Tax registration is mandatory before commencing taxable activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box or US-style pass-through election regime. An SPC is automatically a taxable juridical person for corporate income tax and VAT. Available administrative elections include voluntary VAT registration below the mandatory threshold and, with Tax Authority approval, selection of a non-calendar 12-month financial accounting year. No hybrid mismatch entity-classification election is available under domestic Oman law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies. Voluntary registration threshold: OMR 19,250. Non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Oman may be required to register regardless of the threshold under place-of-supply rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For EOR and cross-border contractor payroll services, 0% VAT applies where the services are supplied to a non-resident entrepreneur/customer who is not present in Oman at the time of supply, the services are effectively used and enjoyed wholly outside Oman, the supply is exported as documented by contract and invoices, and the consideration is received in convertible foreign currency. The zero-rating claim fails if the recipient is related to the supplier and the arrangement lacks genuine commercial substance or the benefit is ultimately enjoyed inside Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (for physical goods)
  • Bill of Lading or airway bill evidencing physical export (for physical goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign currency bank transfer receipt evidencing receipt of export consideration

Oman does not currently enforce a national real-time e-invoicing clearance API, mandatory digital stamping, or fiscal device clearance protocol. Taxpayers must issue a compliant tax invoice in Arabic or English, retain full electronic records, and file VAT returns through the Tax Authority portal. Digital invoices may be issued through approved accounting systems; any future B2G e-invoicing mandate will be implemented through tax.gov.om.

The Oman Tax Authority may audit zero-rated export claims using desk reviews, VAT assessments, bank records, commercial documentation, and international information exchange. An unjustified zero-rating is recharacterized as taxable at 5% with late-payment interest and statutory penalties; fraudulent export zero-rating or deliberate document falsification triggers tax evasion penalties under the VAT Law, retrospective clawback of the claimed relief, and possible imprisonment of responsible officers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains Article VIII convertibility under the IMF Articles of Agreement, with the Omani Rial (OMR) openly pegged to the US dollar at approximately OMR 0.3845 per USD. Current account transactions, dividends, and profit remittances are freely convertible, subject to banks' AML/CFT controls and the beneficiary SPC being in good standing. Capital account flows for a foreign-owned SPC are generally unrestricted, but inward equity must be supported by a board resolution, updated commercial registration, and accurate balance-of-payments purpose coding. Profit/distribution remittance may require a final tax clearance from the Oman Tax Authority, audited financial statements, and a shareholder distribution resolution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman has no statutory export proceeds surrender rule, no mandatory conversion or repatriation timeline, and no foreign exchange retention quota. For an EOR/Staffing SPC providing cross-border employment services, foreign-currency service fees may be retained in the SPC's USD or OMR account. The CBO requires licensed banks to classify bright-line export/service receipts using balance-of-payments codes and to apply enhanced due-diligence checks; banks may impose internal 180-day collection terms when export receivables are discounted, but that is a credit policy condition, not a CBO statutory conversion mandate.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Currency and Foreign Exchange Department, supervised by the Directorate General of Banking Operations; linked to the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) for licensed corporate activity and the Oman Tax Authority for remittance compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low as a primary settlement rail. Foreign EMIs and digital banks such as Wise, Payoneer, and Revolut are not CBO-licensed banks and cannot hold Omani Rial current-account deposits or clear payroll on behalf of an Omani SPC. CBO's Fintech Regulatory Sandbox permits limited e-money or payment-instrument propositions but does not license an unregulated EMI to act as a corporate treasury bank. Such platforms may be used only as an auxiliary beneficiary payout rail, with the SPC maintaining its primary and escrow accounts at a full commercial bank in Oman to satisfy corporate tax, labour, and AML/CFT expectations.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections must be executed by bank-to-bank SWIFT MT103 or MT202, never via non-bank prepaid cards or crypto-assets. The remitter must reference the Omani SPC commercial registration number, the 'equity contribution' purpose code, and the corresponding board resolution. CBO-licensed receiving banks will require proof of the overseas originator, a clear counterparty name and address, and supporting documents proving that the funds are genuine FDI share capital or a formal shareholder loan. After account opening, the paid-up capital escrow may be transferred to the operational account once the bank verifies the commercial register and tax registration.Local Omani payroll should be cleared through the Oman National Payment System ACH or Central Bank RTGS, with payroll files encrypted and lodged to the bank on scheduled payment dates. Cross-border employee and contractor payments should be made by SWIFT MT103 through the SPC's commercial bank. Letters of credit are permissible only for documented trade settlements, not for ordinary payroll, but may be used when the SPC purchases services or supplies from foreign suppliers requiring a documentary credit. All outbound disbursements must be backed by an approved contract, invoice, payroll register, and applicable Oman Electronic Funds Transfer compliance codes.Outright OMR/USD deliverable foreign exchange forwards booked with a licensed Omani commercial bank.OMR/USD or USD/EUR currency swaps, limited to liquidity hedging and approved by the CBO-licensed counterparty's market risk desk.Cross-currency swaps for multi-currency EOR payroll liabilities.European-style currency options on OMR/USD and other freely convertible currencies, purchased through a licensed bank.Non-deliverable forwards (NDF) for emerging-market beneficiary currencies outside the OMR/USD peg; OMR/USD NDFs are unnecessary because Omani Rial is fully convertible with no delivery restriction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: required where controlled transactions with related parties exceed OMR 30,000 per transaction or OMR 75,000 in aggregate in a tax year. Master File: required where the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue exceeds OMR 50,000,000 or where the taxpayer is the group's Omani hub.

Economic substance scope: Oman's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to entities carrying on 'relevant activities' and require them to maintain substantial economic presence in Oman. A global EOR/staffing agency generally satisfies the substance test through its own Omani office, employees, payroll system, and management decisions. A pure invoice/mailbox vehicle without these operational elements is deemed to lack substance and faces penalties (up to OMR 20,000) and potential re-characterisation of its 'EOR revenue' as taxable Omani-source income in the hands of the foreign parent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Falsified Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to triple the tax evaded, plus 2% monthly surcharge on unpaid tax (maximum 12 months) and prosecution before the Omani tax court; imprisonment of up to 3 years for responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors and finance officers are personally and severally liable for the unpaid tax, any fines, and criminal penalties; the court may also impose a travel ban and asset freezing order.
Shell Company / Lack of economic substance

Statutory sanction: Tax Authority may disregard the legal form, recast the profit as attributable to the foreign parent, impose a deemed tax of up to 100% of the distributed profit, and levy penalties under ESR (up to OMR 20,000) and cancellation of the commercial licence.

Director personal liability: Directors face disqualification from serving as directors of any Omani company for a term up to 5 years, personal liability for the tax shortfall, and sanctions under the Oman Commercial Companies Law.
Anti-Money Laundering Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to OMR 20,000 per breach under the Oman AML Law, surrender of the funds, and revocation of the EOR/staffing licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer (MLRO) are subject to criminal investigation, travel bans, and imprisonment of up to 5 years; personal assets are reachable for fines and forfeiture.
Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: A transfer pricing adjustment to the full arm's-length profit, additional tax at 15%, and a penalty of 10% of the adjusted amount for failure to maintain a Local File; interest accrues on the underpaid tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approved the incorrect TP policy are jointly liable for the additional tax and penalties; the Tax Authority may seek a civil recovery order and impose a director disqualification order.
