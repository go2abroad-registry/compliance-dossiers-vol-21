Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity classification elections exist under Panamanian law. A small-taxpayer regime (Régimen de Pequeño Contribuyente) is electable for natural persons or entities with gross receipts below the statutory threshold. Multinational groups may apply for advance pricing agreements and tax incentive certificates under specific sectoral laws, but no general pass-through election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No universal small-business threshold for ITBMS; any person or entity habitually selling taxable goods or services in Panamanian territory must register before commencing operations. The simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross receipts not exceeding PAB 300,000 (approximately).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export sales of goods qualify for 0% ITBMS rate if the goods physically depart Panama under a duly validated customs export declaration. Services supplied to non-resident clients qualify for 0% if the service is effectively used or exploited abroad, supported by evidence of the recipient's foreign tax residence and the absence of Panama-source benefit. The seller must retain commercial, customs, and banking documentation proving the export transaction is genuine.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Formulario Único de Aduanas / DUA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice issued through FELe
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (or bank SWIFT confirmation evidencing foreign currency receipt)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica (FELe). All active VAT/ITBMS taxpayers must issue e-invoices using a DGI-authorized technology provider or the DGI's free platform. Invoices must include an authorized digital stamp, QR code, and unique sequential number. Real-time transmission to DGI is required for all B2B and export invoices within the legally prescribed timeline (typically within 24 hours or immediately for online issuance). API integration with authorized service providers is mandatory for high-volume e-commerce sellers.

The DGI may audit export zero-rating claims retrospectively for the full limitation period (normally 5 years; 7 years for unregistered or non-compliant taxpayers). If documentation is insufficient or an export claim is fraudulent, the taxpayer is required to pay the applicable ITBMS retroactively, plus a 1% monthly surcharge, late-payment interest, and fines. Intentional fraud may trigger criminal tax evasion penalties and disqualification from exporter privileges. Exporters must structure contemporaneous proof that the goods left Panama and that payment was received from abroad within a reasonable commercial period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The US Dollar is legal tender and circulates alongside the Panamanian Balboa coin at a fixed 1:1 parity; there is no paper balboa and no central monetary authority to impose exchange controls. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible, with no prior approval requirements, no profit-remittance tax, and no limitation on holding foreign-currency accounts domestically or abroad. The territorial tax system taxes only Panama-source income, so a Panamanian S.A. conducting offshore e-commerce may remit dividends and service payments freely. Practical constraints are limited to bank-level KYC, correspondent-bank de-risking filters, and documentary proof of underlying transactions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender period, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation deadline because the US Dollar is the domestic currency. Export receipts may be retained offshore or in Panamanian USD, EUR, or CNY accounts at the holder's discretion. For fiscal and audit integrity, the entity must nevertheless maintain invoices, Swift MT103 records, and settlement statements for review by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI).

Central bank supervision: Republic of Panama does not operate a conventional central bank with monetary policy functions. Bank supervision is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), while Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as clearing-house operator and fiscal agent for the State. Financial intelligence and AML/CFT supervision of cross-border flows rest with the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Panama does not offer a dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) licensing regime, and offshore EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and Currencycloud are not regulated as deposit-taking institutions in Panama, cannot issue Panamanian IBANs, and should be used only as pass-through payment wallets, never as storage of treasury balances. Stripe is not officially supported for Panama-domiciled merchants, so a US- or EU-incorporated settlement entity would be required, whereas PayPal officially supports Panamanian registered business accounts for inbound payments and bank withdrawals. Local acquiring alternatives include PayRetailers, Kushki, and Payway, which provide regional card-acquiring rails compatible with Panamanian USD settlement accounts.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections from a non-resident shareholder are executed by means of SWIFT MT103 credit transfers in USD to the Panamanian S.A.'s corporate account, with the payment narrative explicitly reading 'Capital Contribution' or 'Aporte de Capital' to distinguish the entry from trade receipts. Panama maintains no purpose-code register, so the remitting bank's beneficiary information, the company's updated shareholder register, a board-approved capital increase resolution, and a source-of-funds statement are the determinative compliance documents. Where the remitter is domiciled in an FX-controlled country, the outbound remittance must first clear that country's direct-investment or capital-remittance protocol (e.g., ODI filing in China), and the Panamanian account should keep a separated ledger line for equity versus operating funds.For recurring small-ticket supplier payments, the preferred clearing rail is a direct SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire in USD, or an SWIFT GPI tracked transfer, from the Panamanian bank to the supplier's bank. For large bulk inventory purchases from Asian or Latin American manufacturers, a confirmed irrevocable documentary Letter of Credit routed through a reputable correspondent bank is recommended, with negotiation against original transport documents, commercial invoices, packing lists, and certificates of origin. PayPal payouts are settled to the Panamanian account through PayPal's partner banking network using local RTGS/ACH rails, while Stripe-generated balances are first settled to a US/EU payee entity and then repatriated to Panama by wire.
