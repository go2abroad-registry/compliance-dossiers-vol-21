Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Partially permitted. Ley 32 de 1927 requires every Sociedad Anonima to maintain its registered office at the physical office of a licensed resident agent domiciled in Panama. A pure virtual office consisting only of a mail-forwarding address is not recognized for statutory purposes and will be treated as a red flag by Panamanian private banks and securities custodians. However, the SFO may legitimately use the licensed resident agent's physical office as the registered office without leasing separate commercial premises in Panama, provided that actual management, board decision-making, and asset-holding substance are documented and can be demonstrated to counterparties under the territorial tax regime.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The corporation must issue at least one share; authorized capital is stated in the Escritura Pública (commonly USD 10,000) and is subject to registry stamp fees only. No escrow or deposit of capital is required at incorporation. A single-family office capital structure of 10,000 common shares with no par value is standard.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety inspection and certification by SINAPROC (Sistema Nacional de Protección Civil) covering fire extinguishers, egress markings, and occupancy load; structural load verification per the Panamanian Building Code and Reglamento de Construcción Sostenible; workplace hygiene and occupational health clearance from MITRADEL (Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral) where staff are engaged; compliance with NFPA 101 occupancy limits and municipal zoning approval for office use.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The corporation must issue at least one share; authorized capital is stated in the Escritura Pública (commonly USD 10,000) and is subject to registry stamp fees only. No escrow or deposit of capital is required at incorporation. A single-family office capital structure of 10,000 common shares with no par value is standard.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); tax registration and return portal: SIPT – Sistema Integrado de Pagos Tributarios (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/SIPT).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) as follows: (i) complete notarial S.A. incorporation and inscription in Panama's Public Registry; (ii) appoint a Panamanian resident registered agent and, if necessary, a fiscal representative; (iii) file the RUC application with DGI on Form 800 or through the SIPT official electronic registry, attaching certified incorporation documents, Public Registry certificate, registered-agent consent letter, passport/ID and beneficial-ownership declarations, and proof of registered office; (iv) receive the RUC and automatic ITBMS/withholding registration; (v) activate SIPT digital credentials and, if applicable, apply for e-certificates to engage in facturación electrónica. Foreign legal persons without Panama-source income or permanent establishment are not required to obtain a Panamanian RUC solely by reason of share ownership in a Panama S.A.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone (only under Panama Pacifico, Colón Free Zone, or qualifying SEM/Multinational Headquarters regime); standard S.A. is not Pass_Through_Eligible. — A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) as follows: (i) complete notarial S.A. incorporation and inscription in Panama's Public Registry; (ii) appoint a Panamanian resident registered agent and, if necessary, a fiscal representative; (iii) file the RUC application with DGI on Form 800 or through the SIPT official electronic registry, attaching certified incorporation documents, Public Registry certificate, registered-agent consent letter, passport/ID and beneficial-ownership declarations, and proof of registered office; (iv) receive the RUC and automatic ITBMS/withholding registration; (v) activate SIPT digital credentials and, if applicable, apply for e-certificates to engage in facturación electrónica. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone (only under Panama Pacifico, Colón Free Zone, or qualifying SEM/Multinational Headquarters regime); standard S.A. is not Pass_Through_Eligible.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a U.S.-style 'check-the-box' pass-through election. Mandatory entity-level taxation applies. Available statutory elections include: i) consolidated Panamanian income tax filing for a group that can satisfy the 100% direct/indirect shareholding and prior DGI authorization test; ii) the SEM (Multinational Headquarters) regime under Law 41 for qualifying SFO service hubs; iii) Panama Pacifico/Colón Free Zone fixed-tax incentive contracts; iv) simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime for turnover at or below B/.36,000 per year. No hybrid mismatch or transparent-entity election is available for a Panamanian S.A.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.36,000 (Balboa/USD) of annual gross turnover; all persons habitually performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama above this threshold must obtain ITBMS registration. Persons with turnover at or below the threshold may elect the simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

ITBMS 0% is available for: i) tangible goods that are definitively exported from Panamanian customs territory; and ii) services rendered by Panamanian taxpayers to non-resident persons for use/exploitation outside Panama. The SFO must title the export invoice to the foreign family entity/bank, retain customs exit proof for goods, document the non-resident use/location of services, and evidence the receipt of payment from abroad. The zero-rated supply also carries a right to refund of accumulated input ITBMS attributable to such export activities.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) validated by Panama's Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas, including customs clearance number and exit confirmation
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical shipment and departure of goods from Panamanian territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT/telex confirmation evidencing receipt of export consideration from the non-resident buyer
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and the underlying sale or services contract describing the destination and offshore use
  • For exported services: DGI-compliant invoice, service agreement, and technical evidence of use/benefit outside Panama

Panama's DGI has mandatorily phased in electronic invoicing ('Facturación Electrónica'). Affected taxpayers must issue invoices through DGI-approved systems connected to SIPT via REST APIs using XML/JSON payloads, authentication digital certificates, QR code, and digital stamp; the DGI performs real-time fiscal clearance/validation before the invoice is legally effective. Paper invoices with fiscal seals remain only during transition/complement. Non-compliant invoices cannot be used to claim input ITBMS or income-tax deductions by the SFO.

DGI audits match export declarations, transport documents, and banking credits; incomplete or abusive zero-rating documentation will be recharacterized as taxable Panama-source revenue, evaluated at 7% ITBMS plus accrued interest and monthly surcharges. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims can trigger fines from 50% to 200% of the omitted tax, nullification of refunds and credits, clawback of previously recovered input tax, disqualification from customs facilitation status, and criminal liability for tax fraud (Law 14 of 2010 and Fiscal Code penalties). The ordinary DGI assessment period is generally five years from the relevant filing date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized with the Balboa at a nominal 1:1 parity to the US dollar; US Federal Reserve notes are legal tender. No exchange controls, no foreign-exchange licensing obligation, and no differentiated current-account versus capital-account regime. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, and outbound transfers execute freely in USD without central-bank approval or convertibility delay. Tax withholding and reporting requirements continue to apply under Panamanian territorial taxation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender period, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no hard-currency repatriation requirement applies to export receipts earned by a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima. Export proceeds may be retained offshore, credited to beneficiary accounts in Panama, or reinvested without exchange-control filings.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Banco Nacional de Panamá (not an issuing central bank); Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (macro-fiscal oversight); Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) for AML/Sanctions enforcement

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for unlicensed offshore digital EMIs. Panama does not recognise the EU EMI passport and SBP licensing is required for deposit-taking, banking, and regulated payment services. An SFO should not hold any material liquidity with a non-bank fintech EMI; only locally licensed fintech institutions under Law No. 1 of 2024 (Innovación Financiera) or a fully licensed SBP bank may serve as a compliant clearing rail.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% over the taxable dividend distribution to individuals or non-resident shareholders; distributions of previously taxed profits bear a reduced 4% dividend withholding tax (dividend tax paid out of the corporate income tax). A 5% rate applies to dividends paid from profits subject to a preferential regime. Internal group dividends to non-resident parent companies may qualify for a 0% withholding rate if paid from profits already subject to Panamanian tax and the distribution is not deductible by the payer.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Three months after the fiscal year-end; for a calendar-year taxpayer, the filing deadline is March 31 of the following tax year. Payments of the final assessed tax are due with the return; estimated payments during the year are governed by rules for corporations with taxable domestic income.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 / Line 1a Identificación del Contribuyente: tax identification number (RUC - Registro Único de Contribuyentes) of the Panamanian corporation. The SFO's Panamanian S.A. must register for a RUC with a resident agent (agente residente) and include the RUC on the return. Late filing fines range from USD 50 to USD 5,000 depending on gross receipts, plus 1% monthly interest on unpaid tax. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB 2,000,000 (USD 2,000,000) of annual related-party transactions or gross income above that threshold triggers mandatory transfer pricing documentation (Local File and Master File under Executive Decree No. 56 of 2014 and DGI Regulation 4-2014).

Economic substance scope: Panama does not currently levy a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime for SFOs. However, the General Directorate of Revenue and the Superintendency of Banks apply substance-over-form, and an SFO must demonstrate actual substance in Panama to preserve territorial tax benefits, avoid recharacterization as a laboratory of concealment, and satisfy its resident agent's 'know your client' duties. Having a local office, local board decisions, accounting records kept in Panama, and a Panamanian bank account are critical. The 2015 Law No. 52 on legal entities requires Panamanian corporations to maintain their accounting records in Panama and to present them to the resident agent upon request; failure to do so results in loss of legal personality or fines.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Non-Filing of Territorial Return

Statutory sanction: Fines under the Tax Code of up to 100% of the omitted tax, adjusted penalties, interest at statutory rates, and criminal tax fraud sanctions in aggravated cases. In suspension or revocation of the corporate status (reforma de la sociedad) for failure to comply with resident agent obligations.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is available under Panamanian law where fraud or abuse is proven. Directors and the resident agent may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate taxes. Criminal tax evasion can lead to imprisonment (up to 5 years), travel bans, and seizure of personal assets.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Fines; involuntary dissolution; prohibition of judicial acts; adverse tax residency determination by foreign tax authorities under CFC or substance rules (e.g., U.S. GILTI, EU ATAD); negative impact under the risk-based market entry of international banking compliance standards.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false declarations or knowingly permit the entity to act as a bare shell may face personal liability for resulting fines, civil restitution to creditors or investors, and criminal exposure for fraud. Directory removal and travel bans can be imposed in the context of money laundering investigations.
AML / Money Laundering Through Private Banking Rails

Statutory sanction: Under Panama's AML Law (Law 23 of 2015, expanded by Law 70 of 2017), fines range from USD 10,000 to USD 5,000,000 per violation. The UAF (Unidad de Análisis Financiero) may suspend accounts, and criminal money laundering carries 10 to 15 years imprisonment. Panama is a CFATF member; serious deficiencies can trigger countermeasures.

Director personal liability: Directors, nominee shareholders, and resident agents who are aware of suspicious transactions without filing Suspicious Transaction Reports (RFS) are personally liable. Criminal liability includes imprisonment, pecuniary fines, travel bans, and confiscation of assets.
Undisclosed Beneficial Ownership / Nominee Abuse

Statutory sanction: Fines and sanctions from the Superintendency of Companies and the DGI; refusal to provide Paz y Salvo; cancellation of registration; consequences under international tax transparency standards for being non-compliant with beneficial ownership data (Executive Decree 207 of 2015).

Director personal liability: The nominee director who exercises formal control without written instructions from the beneficial owner is exposed to civil liability for unauthorized transactions; the true owner may be barred from asserting ownership rights; custodial criminal penalties may be applied in case of fraudulent vehicles.
Treaty/DTA Abuse and Tax Clearance Misrepresentation

Statutory sanction: Reversal of treaty benefits, gross-up of withheld taxes, penalties of 50% to 100% of the under-withheld amount, and criminal fraud charges under the Tax Code.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute false certificates or who direct improper treaty applications may be subject to personal fines, civil indemnification for tax liabilities, and, in aggravated cases, up to 6 years of imprisonment for forgery or fraud, plus travel bans.
