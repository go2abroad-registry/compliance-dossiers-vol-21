Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); online fiscal portal SIFIL (Sistema de Información Fiscal en Línea).

E-government filing portal

Foreign shareholders must incorporate the Sociedad Anónima before a Panamanian notary and register with the Public Registry. After domiciliation and appointment of a resident agent and legal representative, the company applies for its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) and ITBMS registration through the DGI/SIFIL portal or at DGI offices. The DGI issues the RUC and fiscal verification code upon submission of the certificate of incorporation, public-registry registration, and statutes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: the Sociedad Anónima is taxed at entity level only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income remains outside the Panamanian tax base, and dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are generally exempt.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available under Panamanian law. A small taxpayer may elect the Régimen Simplificado de Tributación only if gross annual taxable income does not exceed USD 300,000; a wholesale exporter engaged in international distribution may not qualify. No other material entity-classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD/PAB at par: no de minimis turnover threshold; registration with DGI for RUC and ITBMS is mandatory upon the first taxable transfer of goods or services in Panama.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of ophthalmic eyewear frames are zero-rated if the goods are physically exported out of Panama and are supported by a customs exit declaration. Services rendered in connection with the export supply are zero-rated when the recipient is a non-resident and the benefit is effectively used abroad. The zero-rated exporter may credit ITBMS borne on purchases attributable to the exported goods, and any resulting excess credit may be refunded after DGI verification.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) accepted in the SIGA customs system
  • Transport documents (Bill of Lading or Air Waybill) evidencing physical departure
  • Commercial invoice and sales contract
  • Bank payment evidence and, where applicable, inward remittance certificate or SWIFT MT103

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica (SIFE) administered by the DGI. Each invoice must be transmitted to the DGI for validation and fiscal authorization via API or portal, and each authorized invoice carries a unique QR code and digital stamp. Export invoices issued to foreign customers must remain SIFE-compliant, with the corresponding export code when applicable.

DGI and customs authorities may audit export transactions and ITBMS refund claims within the five-year prescription period. If zero-rating is claimed without real export, the refund is clawed back with accumulated interest and surcharges. Tax fraud penalties apply under Ley 76 de 2019 and Ley 8 de 2021, reaching up to 300% of the evaded tax, plus fines and imprisonment; customs smuggling penalties add confiscation and fines of four to ten times the customs value.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD / B/. 2,000,000 of aggregate annual gross revenue. A taxpayer exceeding this threshold must prepare a Master File and Local File and file transfer-pricing schedules with Form 210 under Cabinet Decree Rule 47 of 2013.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not currently enforce an Economic Substance Regulation comparable to BVI or Cayman for ordinary S.A.s, but DGI applies anti-avoidance and re-characterization doctrines where a Panama vehicle has no local personnel, premises, decision-making, or records. In practice, a wholesale eyeglass-frame distributor claiming foreign-source exclusion must maintain substantive evidence: board minutes approving invoices, local agent or employees, warehouse or e-commerce logistics presence, bank accounts in Panama, and audit-ready revenue records. Free-zone and SEM-licensed entities face statutory economic substance thresholds requiring specific headcount and expenditure in Panama.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion — Consistent Understatement or Artificial Foreign-Source Recharacterization

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax-fraud penalties of 2 to 5 years imprisonment and a fine of 2 to 10 times the evaded tax; reinstatement of the unpaid tax plus interest and a surcharge of up to 50%; confiscation of bank accounts and goods in the S.A.'s name.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed or approved deliberately false returns can be stripped of the limited-liability shield; civil liability for the entire evaded amount, travel bans, freezing of personal assets, and imprisonment are ordered against the responsible officers.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: DGI can disregard the lack of substance and reallocate the entire cross-border margin as Panamanian-source, applying 25% CIT plus surcharges and interest; the Public Registry may suspend the entity for failure to renew the fiscal license; administrative fines for each missing annual filing apply.

Director personal liability: When the corporation is found to be a facade for another operating group, the corporate veil is pierced; the actual foreign controller and the registered resident director are jointly and severally liable for underpaid tax and may face deregistration and travel bans.
AML / Anti-Money-Laundering Breach in Cross-Border Payments

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015 (as amended): non-monetary sanctions, public reprimand, and administrative fines up to USD 1,000,000 per violation; repeated non-compliance can lead to the forced dissolution of the S.A. and prohibition from opening bank accounts in Panama.

Director personal liability: Natural persons managing the AML program face criminal prosecution, asset embargo, and imprisonment of up to 10 years for knowingly facilitating money laundering; the corporate veil is per se unavailable for AML violations.
Transfer Pricing / False or Undocumented Intragroup Charges

Statutory sanction: Compulsory TP adjustment of the wholesale margin to an arm's length gross profit; a 15% penalty on the adjusted tax undercharge; the undereported income becomes taxable at 25% plus 10%-15% interest; invoices without identification of the supplier and contracts may be denied entirely.

Director personal liability: Directors and tax officers who knowingly execute a related-party transaction without an existing transfer-pricing file are personally liable for the resulting penalties and interest in civil proceedings; deliberate non-compliance supports criminal action for fraud and can ultimately trigger resignations compulsory at the Mercantile Registry.
