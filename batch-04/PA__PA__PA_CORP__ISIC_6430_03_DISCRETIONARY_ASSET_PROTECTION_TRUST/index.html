Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance of Panama; tax administration and e-filing portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://www.sedeelectronica.mef.gob.pa) and Panama's electronic invoicing platform.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity or its resident legal representative must file the RUC registration application (Formulario de Inscripción) with the DGI before commencing taxable activities, attaching the authenticated public deed (escritura pública), the registered agent certificate in Panama, proof of registered office address, and identification of directors, officers, and beneficial owners. The application is submitted through the Sede Electrónica or in person at the DGI, and results in issuance of the Registo Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) and ITBMS registration. No minimum capital, local shareholding, or prior tax clearance is required for non-resident owners.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope - A Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) is an entity-level taxpayer subject to income tax only on Panama-source income. Foreign-source income, including trust administration fees and distributions from foreign assets, is excluded from the tax base, and capital gains from foreign assets are not taxable in Panama unless effectively connected with a permanent establishment in Panama.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box election. A Panama S.A. may, however, elect qualified resident status for advantageous tax treatment, and may elect the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime which grants preferential ITBMS and income-tax rules. Private interest foundations (Fundación de Interés Privado) formed under the Foreign Private Foundations Law enjoy territorial treatment. Panama has not enacted OECD-style hybrid mismatch rules, but the tax and AML authorities may apply general anti-abuse principles and economic-substance scrutiny to treaty- or classification-driven structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0 - no general turnover threshold: any person habitually performing taxable transfers of goods or services within Panama must register as an ITBMS taxpayer. Small taxpayers with annual gross income not exceeding USD 36,000 may file a simplified quarterly ITBMS return instead of monthly filing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods and certain services performed for non-residents are zero-rated for ITBMS. To qualify, the seller must be a Panama taxpayer, the title and risk in goods must pass to a non-resident buyer, goods or services must be destined for actual use or consumption outside Panama, and the transaction must be supported by a written contract and documentary evidence of payment. Export services require that the service be wholly or predominantly rendered outside Panama or be directly related to export goods.

  • Declaración Única de Mercaderías (DUM) / Unified Customs Export Declaration filed with the National Customs Authority of Panama
  • Bill of lading or airway bill evidencing physical export and destination outside Panama
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit confirmation showing the foreign currency payment received in a Panamanian bank account
  • Fiduciary trust agreement and service contract specifying the non-resident beneficiary, trustee powers, and exported-service description

Mandatory electronic invoicing applies under Law 24 of 2020 and DGI regulations. Invoices must be issued through the DGI's Sede Electrónica or an authorized digital integrator, transmitted in XML format before or at the time of supply, digitally signed by a certificate issued by the National Authority for Government Innovation (AIG), and validated in near-real-time with a QR code and digital stamp for fiscal clearance.

DGI may audit zero-rated export claims for up to five fiscal years. Fraudulent or artificial export documentation triggers tax evasion penalties under Panama's Tax Procedure Code, including fines from USD 500 to USD 50,000, monthly default interest of 10% of the omitted tax, and surcharges of 100% to 300% on wrongfully refunded ITBMS. Where the false export structure is used to repatriate fiduciary trust assets, the tax penalty is compounded by AML/CFT investigation and criminal prosecution under Law 23 of 2015 (AML law).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full, unrestricted convertibility exists on both current and capital account. Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction with no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements for inbound or outbound transfers, and no profit-remittance hurdles for foreign beneficiaries. The only frictions are AML/CFT documentation checks by SBP-supervised banks and the Unidad de Analisis Financiero (UAF). Because Panama applies territorial taxation, foreign-source income of discretionary asset-protection trusts is not taxable, so trust distributions, dividends, and capital repatriations flow freely in USD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender, repatriation, conversion, or holding-period requirements for export proceeds. There is no central-bank reserve system, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no exchange-surrender certificate regime; exporters and fiduciary entities retain USD proceeds freely in domestic or foreign settlement accounts.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no issuing central bank. The de facto regulatory authority is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panama (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP), which supervises banks and licensed fiduciary entities under Decree Law No. 9 of 1998 (General Banking Law) and Law No. 1 of 1984 (Trust Law). Banco Nacional de Panama (BNP) is the state-run fiscal agent but does not perform central-bank functions. The U.S. dollar is legal tender pursuant to the 1904 Monetary Agreement and the Political Constitution, Article 143; the Balboa (PAB) is solely a nominal unit at fixed 1:1 parity with the USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Panama does not grant electronic-money-institution (EMI) licenses; offshore digital EMIs (e.g., UK/FCA or EU-authorised firms) are not recognised as Panamanian clearing participants and cannot hold domestic settlement accounts. Use of such EMIs is limited to ancillary, low-value expense disbursements and must remain externally reportable to the SBP. The SBP FinTech Regulatory Sandbox (Acuerdo No. 4 de 2019) permits supervised pilots but does not confer a full banking licence; final USD clearing for fiduciary trust accounts must be executed through a Tier-1 Panamanian bank with a U.S. correspondent relationship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant USD inbound flows clear through the U.S. correspondent banking network (Fedwire/CHIPS) because Panama has no central-bank RTGS. The settlor instructs a SWIFT MT103 with the full remitter name and address in Field 50K, the Panamanian bank's U.S. correspondent in Field 57A/C, and the trust's registered legal name and fiduciary operations account number in Field 59. Field 70 must carry an unambiguous narrative (e.g., 'Irrevocable Settlement Contribution - Trust Deed dated [date]') and Field 72 should reference the SBP fiduciary licence number. The receiving bank applies enhanced due diligence, holding funds for up to 48 hours to verify the source-of-wealth documentation filed at onboarding before crediting the trust account.For disbursements below USD 20,000, the bank may settle domestically via Panama's automated clearing house (Camara de Compensacion of the Asociacion Bancaria de Panama). For material trade settlements and asset acquisitions, the fiduciary instructs an authenticated SWIFT MT103 executed through the bank's New York correspondent (CHIPS, T+0). Where the trust purchases commercial assets, the bank may issue a commercial letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) to the vendor's presenting bank.
