Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá — Dirección General de Registro de Personas Jurídicas y Bienes Muebles (Mercantile Section); online portal: www.registropublico.gob.pa.

Governing statute

Ley 32 de 26 de febrero de 1927 (General Law on Corporations), as amended by Ley 12 de 2016 and Ley 60 de 2019; Articles 1-10 (formation), Articles 19-29 (articles of incorporation), Articles 48-50 (board of directors), and Article 58 (registered office and resident agent). Complementary application of the Código de Comercio, Articles 3-40 for commercial activity.

Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The S.A. may incorporate with nominal capital of US$1,000 or higher (commonly US$10,000 divided into 100-500 registered shares); capital must be expressed in USD and may be partially paid in installments per Article 35 of Law 32/1927. No escrow account or capital deposit requirement applies.

Funding & unlock path: Bomberos de Panamá (National Fire Department): mandatory 'Certificado de Requisitos Técnicos de Seguridad' prior to occupancy of any office or warehouse, covering automatic fire extinguishers, emergency exits, electrical load certification, and preventive maintenance logs. MITRADEL (Ministry of Labor and Workforce Development): occupational health and safety clearance under Resolution MITRADEL No. 041-2018, including work accident prevention plans and ergonomic protocols for dispatch workers, enforceable when the entity employs 10 or more workers.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas – official portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Panamanian corporations must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) through DGI's e-Tax system after inscription in the Registro Público. The procedure requires appointing a resident legal representative, submitting the public deed and identity documents, and obtaining a digital signature certificate (Firma Digital) for electronic filings. Non-resident shareholders may act through a resident agent; no minimum paid-in capital is required.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — Foreign-owned Panamanian corporations must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) through DGI's e-Tax system after inscription in the Registro Público. The procedure requires appointing a resident legal representative, submitting the public deed and identity documents, and obtaining a digital signature certificate (Firma Digital) for electronic filings. Non-resident shareholders may act through a resident agent; no minimum paid-in capital is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists. A Sociedad Anónima is automatically taxed as a territorial corporation. Optional elective regimes include the Multinational Companies Headquarters (SEM) tax regime and the Panama International Services City regime, both administered by the Ministry of Commerce and Industries, which grant preferential tax treatment on qualifying foreign-source activities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB (Balboa) 0 – no general turnover threshold; any person or entity engaged in taxable activities in Panama must register for ITBMS. Small taxpayers may use a simplified ITBMS regime on annual turnover not exceeding PAB 36,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% ITBMS applies to (i) physical goods that are duly exported from Panama to a foreign destination and (ii) services rendered to non-resident clients for use entirely outside Panama, provided the service is not economically consumed within Panama. For cash-on-delivery e-commerce, the exporter must demonstrate that the goods were delivered abroad, that title and risk passed to the foreign buyer, and that the consideration was received from foreign sources.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera – DUA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill issued by the international carrier
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a Panamanian bank or equivalent SWIFT/FX confirmation evidencing repatriation of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Last-mile delivery confirmation or customs clearance proof from the foreign destination country

Mandatory electronic invoicing under DGI Resolution No. 201-2712 and No. 202-3374 through the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica de Panamá. Invoices must be transmitted via approved REST API or web services, signed with a valid digital certificate, validated by DGI in near real-time, and printed or issued with a mandatory QR code stamp.

DGI may disallow the zero-rating and assess the unpaid ITBMS at 7% plus monthly surcharges and fines up to 200% of the omitted tax in cases of fraud. Criminal tax evasion penalties include fines from PAB 5,000 to PAB 50,000 and imprisonment of two to five years. Customs penalties for false export declarations may also reach 20% of the FOB value of the goods, and the exporter may be retroactively stripped of all zero-rated credits.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de facto and de jure convertibility for both current and capital transactions. Inbound investment, dividend repatriation, loan repayment, and internal treasury transfers denominated in USD suffer no exchange-control preapproval, no stamp duty, and no mandatory central-bank registration under the post-2016 foreign investment liberalization regime. Capital-account operations require only standard bank-level source-of-funds documentation and, where the operation exceeds the SBP reporting threshold, a periodic 'Reporte de Transacciones en Efectivo y Transferencias' (electronic FIU filing). The only structural constraint is that local legal tender is USD; the PAB is not an independent currency, so treasury accounting must reflect USD as the functional currency.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or repatriation requirement exists. A Panamanian Sociedad Anónima may retain 100% of export and COD receivables in USD in any SBP-licensed bank, and may use such proceeds directly to settle trade payables without any mandatory conversion ratio or customs-linked surrender window. Logistics companies and payment aggregators settling COD remittances into the S.A. must comply with the 'know your counterparty' and beneficial ownership disclosure duties imposed by SBP Acuerdo 7-2022, and with the UAF reporting rule for aggregated transfers exceeding USD 10,000 per calendar day, but no percentage of export proceeds is forced into any specified instrument.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as fiscal agent under Law 102 of 1941; the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the exclusive banking and currency-stability regulator; the Capitulo V of Law 42 of 2001 empowers SBP to administer official reserves and clearing. There is no Latin American-style issuing central bank: Panama is fully dollarized, the Balboa (PAB) exists only as coinage equivalent to the USD, and the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) exercises limited monetary-policy functions. SBP Acuerdo 4-2015, Acuerdo 1-2015 and the Unified Rules on AML/KYC remain the definitive operational mandates.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-Low as primary treasury rail; Medium-High as secondary disbursement rail. Airwallex, Payoneer, Wise, and similar offshore EMI/PSP entities are not SBP-licensed banks and are therefore prohibited from operating a Panamanian deposit-taking or clearing account. They may lawfully hold funds offshore in the company's name, execute settlement to local last-mile couriers, and issue MT103-equivalent transfers, but they cannot receive cash-on-delivery remittances generated inside Panama, nor can they act as the custodian of statutory share capital. Practical blueprint: keep the S.A. local current account at an SBP bank as the collection and reconciliation hub, while using Airwallex's global payout rails to reach logistics partners in emerging markets. A local money-service business license under Decree 12 of 2017 is mandatory if the S.A. itself were to warehouse unrelated-party remittances, which is outside the described model.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

High-frequency cash-on-delivery inflows for electronic retail, causing banks to suspect circular cash generation or layering.

Root cause: High-frequency cash-on-delivery inflows for electronic retail, causing banks to suspect circular cash generation or layering.

Defense evidence: Provide the underwriter with a 'Treasury Evidence Pack' containing: (i) 12-month payment-rail transaction ledger from the e-commerce platform (Shopify/WooCommerce equivalent) showing merchant order IDs; (ii) courier-level delivery confirmation slips or digital proof-of-delivery API export; (iii) monthly logistics settlement statements from the last-mile operator proving collection dates and remittances; (iv) a sworn statement issued by a Panamanian licensed public accountant ('Idóneo') reconciling the COD receivables to accounting revenue; and (v) the S.A.'s documented purchasing-invoice trail from the underlying emerging-market suppliers.
Non-resident ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) with bearer-share heritage or nominee structures common to Panama S.A.

Root cause: Non-resident ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) with bearer-share heritage or nominee structures common to Panama S.A.

Defense evidence: Bank-grade 'Transparency Dossier': current Registro Público 'ficha' showing the exact share ledger, an updated Declaración de Beneficiario Final as filed with the UAF under Law 129 of 2020 and SBP Acuerdo 7-2022, apostilled passport copy of each UBO, sworn source-of-wealth statement, bank reference from the UBO's home banking institution, and a legal opinion from a licensed Panamanian attorney confirming no bearer shares subsist.
Electronic retail with 'Cash0nDelivery' creates velocity-of-funds mismatches with initial registered capital (typically USD 10,000 or less in the foundational deposit).

Root cause: Electronic retail with 'Cash0nDelivery' creates velocity-of-funds mismatches with initial registered capital (typically USD 10,000 or less in the foundational deposit).

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant Protocol: (1) The foreign shareholder or parent performs a direct wire instruction via SWIFT MT103 with the beneficiary bank in Panama, identifying the beneficiary S.A., the exact 12-digit account number, and the RUC. (2) In Field 70 (Remittance Information), use the character-limited text 'APORTE DE CAPITAL – CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – [Entity Legal Name] – RUC [number]'; Panamanian law does not assign an official purpose-code taxonomy, but stating the operation as capital contribution avoids misclassification as financing or royalty. (3) The S.A. must retain the incoming MT103, the bank credit proof, and the board resolution approving the capital increase; all documents must be timestamped and internally archived because SBP supervisors may request them during annual compliance review. (4) No Ministry of Economy prior approval, no foreign-investment registration, and no exchange-control permit; however, the bank may require an updated shareholders' registry, the source-of-wealth letter, and the completion of the 'Declaración de Origen de Fondos' within 30 days. Funds are then available intraday and may be swept into the operational account.Recommended multi-rail structure for the COD e-commerce treasury: (i) Supplier payments in USD up to USD 50,000 per transaction: SWIFT MT103 directly from the SBP bank, with Invoice Number and Purchase Order ID in Field 70; (ii) Larger or trust-critical purchases from emerging-market fabric or equipment suppliers: irrevocable Commercial + Documentary Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the Panamanian bank in favor of the beneficiary via an advising bank, with Incoterms clearly set (usually FOB or DAP), payable at sight; this avoids pre-shipment counterparty risk. (iii) Logistic-payout remittances in small ticket volumes: use the Airwallex or equivalent global account formatted as an on-behalf-of file, sweeping from the domestic operational account into the EMI, which then executes local-currency payouts to couriers in Colombia, Peru, Mexico, or China; the MT103 or SEPA/SEPA-INST equivalent must include the PANAMANIAN entity name consistent with the underlying logistics agreement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% ISR (Impuesto sobre la Renta) on Panama-source net income of Sociedades Anónimas

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% on dividends paid out of Panama-source profits already subject to 25% ISR; 10% on dividends paid out of untaxed, exempt, or foreign-source profits. No additional tax is imposed on the non-resident shareholder receipt if the correct withholding has been made.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File, Master File, and annual transfer-pricing return (Form 205) for taxpayers with gross revenue of PAB/USD 3,000,000 or more in the preceding year, or with controlled transactions exceeding PAB/USD 1,000,000 in the fiscal year.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) apply to entities carrying on relevant activities, including distribution/service centres, logistics management, IP holding, and pure equity participation. A mail-order COD e-commerce S.A. must demonstrate real staff, physical premises, local board decisions, and core income-generating activities in Panama; otherwise the DGI may treat it as a shell, deny a tax-residence certificate, and impose substance-related penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion under Panamanian fiscal law is punishable by imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and a fine equal to 100% of the evaded tax, plus monthly late-payment interest of 1% and cancellation of the RUC/operations license.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives are jointly and severally liable for fraudulent returns and unpaid withholding taxes; courts may pierce the corporate veil, imposing civil restitution, travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment where the S.A. is a mere alter ego.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Panama's ESR and entity-registry rules impose administrative fines ranging from USD 10,000 to USD 100,000 for first and repeat breaches, together with possible registration cancellation, loss of tax-residence status, and denial of treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Officers who certify false substance declarations face personal fines, director disqualification, travel bans, and criminal imprisonment; the corporate veil is pierced for sham incorporation and the shareholders may be held personally liable for unpaid corporate taxes.
AML / Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Panama's AML framework (Law 23 of 2015 and Penal Code provisions) penalises money laundering with fines up to USD 500,000 and imprisonment of 5 to 10 years; the UAF (Unidad de Análisis Financiero) may impose administrative penalties and freeze bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners are subject to criminal indictment, personal asset forfeiture, civil restitution, travel bans, and prison terms; the corporate veil does not shield knowing participants in payment-rail abuse.
Profit Repatriation / Withholding Tax Infraction

Statutory sanction: Withholding agents are liable for the unwithheld amount, a non-compliance penalty of up to 10% of the omitted tax, and monthly default interest; the DGI may recharacterise artificial fees as dividends and deny the related deduction.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly authorise distributions without withholding are personally liable to restore the unlawful distributions, face civil and criminal claims, travel bans, and director disqualification, and can be joined personally in DGI enforcement actions.
