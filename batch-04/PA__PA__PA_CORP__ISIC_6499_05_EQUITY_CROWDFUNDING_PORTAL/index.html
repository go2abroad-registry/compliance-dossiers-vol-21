Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas – Sede Electrónica de la DGI, https://www.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima, the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) is obtained by submitting the notarized Public Registry corporate certificate, identification of the resident legal representative, and an initial application to the DGI. Foreign entities not incorporated in Panama must first register a branch in the Public Registry, appoint a resident agent, and then apply for a RUC. There is no separate VAT ID; the RUC serves as the universal taxpayer identification. An aviso de operación municipal may also be required before commencing business.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Panamanian law. Taxpayers may elect optional special regimes, including the Multinational Corporate Headquarters (SEM) regime or the simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS system, where eligible. No flow-through or disregarded-entity election exists for a Sociedad Anónima.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.36,000 annual gross sales of goods or services subject to ITBMS (Balboas, at parity with USD)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods are zero-rated when physically transported out of Panama. Services are zero-rated when rendered to non-residents, used entirely outside Panama, and paid from abroad with funds entering through the national banking system. An equity crowdfunding portal may zero-rate platform, escrow, or consultancy services if the counterparty is non-resident, the service is performed for a foreign business, and no economic use is deemed to occur within Panama. Documentation must affirm foreign-use and foreign-payment origin.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUCA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank/offshore foreign-payment evidence)
  • Service contract and itemized invoice identifying the non-resident beneficiary
  • Certification of final utilization abroad for services

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica de Panamá, FEP) is phased in for all registered taxpayers. Electronic invoices must be generated in XML, carry a unique cryptographic fiscal code and QR code approved by the DGI, and be transmitted through authorized web services or the DGI portal. Transmission is required within five business days after issuing. There is no real-time clearance in the style of Mexico; however, the DGI validates invoices digitally before fiscal effect.

The DGI will reverse zero-rating if the documentary bundle is incomplete or inconsistent, clumping back all ITBMS credits attributable to the export with annual interest of up to 60%, and administrative fines of up to 50% of the omitted tax. Fraudulent or reckless zero-rating claims may trigger criminal tax-fraud proceedings under Panamanian law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility with no exchange-control regime. Dividend, profit, license fee, and investment remittances are freely transmissible in USD or any convertible currency without central bank authorization. The only frictions are standardized AML/CFT verification and reporting under Law 23 of 2015 (including FATCA/CRS classification), plus a 5% dividend tax on profits derived from Panamanian-source income under the territorial tax system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no statutory repatriation, surrender, mandatory conversion, or earmarking requirements on export proceeds, foreign-currency receivables, or cross-border investment receipts. All USD inflows are treated as domestic funds; banks record transactions solely for prudential and AML/CFT surveillance, not for exchange-control enforcement.

Central bank supervision: Panama maintains no issuing central bank. Under the 1904 Monetary Law, the United States Dollar is unlimited legal tender and the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a notional unit at parity, issued only as commemorative coinage. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) functions as the banking and monetary supervisory authority, Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as the State's fiscal agent and operator of the SIGA high-value payment system, and the United States Federal Reserve effectively anchors monetary conditions for the jurisdiction.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-medium viability, strictly as an operational auxiliary. Panamanian crowdfunding regulation under the SMV 'Financiamiento Participativo' regime requires statutory investor funds to be held in dedicated escrow accounts at SBP-licensed banks; offshore digital EMIs cannot serve as primary custody or escrow rails. Foreign EMIs may be used only for non-investor operating disbursements (vendor payments, marketing, international fees), subject to enhanced due diligence, sanctions screening, and unfreezing-risk analysis under Law 23 of 2015 and the FinTech Sandbox framework of Ley 209 de 2021.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed from Panamanian taxable profits subject to corporate income tax; 20% on distributions from exempt or foreign-source profits. No second-level shareholder income tax is imposed on a non-resident shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after the fiscal year close; for a 31 December fiscal year the return and any tax due are due no later than 31 March. The DGI may grant a one-time extension of up to two months.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: The mandatory preparation and submission threshold is set by Executive Decree 210-2020 and DGI implementing forms; a taxpayer must prepare the Local File and Master File and file the annual transfer-pricing return when related-party transaction value or group consolidated turnover exceeds the published threshold. In the current financial-services practice, an equity crowdfunding portal must have contemporaneous documentation as soon as related-party management fees, licensing payments, escrow commissions or intercompany loans in the fiscal year exceed B/.250,000 (PAB, tied 1:1 to USD); actual triggered deadlines follow DGI notices.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not maintain a separate ESR code for S.A. companies; however, the equity crowdfunding license (SMV) and general tax registration compel the company to keep a physical seat, local management, payroll/bank accounts and records. A company without economic substance is treated as an unlicensed or dormant shell and loses DTA/tax-evidence protection, even though territorial taxation may not require on-shore activity for non-Panama-source business.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Annual Return

Statutory sanction: Interest and surcharges, 5% monthly late penalties, an understatement surcharge of up to 100% of the omitted tax, closure of operations and criminal tax prosecution under the Criminal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives and the resident fiscal agent who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable; the court may order civil restitution, asset seizures, travel bans and imprisonment of 2-5 years.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution by the Registro Público, cancellation of the commercial license and DGI registration, B/.300 annual corporate tax default penalties and disqualification of the resident agent; public listing as an irregular entity.

Director personal liability: The collapse of limited liability: directors and nominee shareholders may be subjected to civil restitution by creditors/investors and, if assets are funnelled abroad, money-laundering prosecution, travel bans and insolvency disqualification.
AML / Unlicensed Crowdfunding Portal

Statutory sanction: Securities-market fines and suspension of operations, administrative revocation of the license, AML/CFT sanctions under Ley 23 de 2015, and forfeiture of the illegally collected platform fees.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are treated as immediate participants in money laundering; they face civil disgorgement, travel bans, pre-trial detention and imprisonment of 5-10 years for predicate securities fraud.
Transfer Pricing and Cross-Border Restructuring

Statutory sanction: DGI primary adjustment of taxable income, 30% penalty on the adjustment (or higher for repeated violations), interest and reclassification of the expense as a constructive dividend subject to 10%/20% dividend tax.

Director personal liability: Officers who signed and justified the intercompany arrangement may be held personally liable for the tax shortfall in disregard-of-substance cases; egregious arrangements also attract criminal tax evasion sanctions, travel bans and imprisonment.
