Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; official portal: 'Portal E-Tax' (https://etax.mef.gob.pa) supported by the DGI public website (www.dgi.gob.pa), with the mandatory 'Sistema de Facturación Electrónica' module for digital fiscal clearance. Panama is a unitary tax jurisdiction with no sub-national income tax; RUC administration is exclusively national.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Notarize the incorporation deed of the Sociedad Anónima and register the deed, by-laws and officer appointments with the Registro Público de Panamá, appointing a resident agent (agente residente) with a Panamanian registered address (no minimum paid-in capital). Step 2: File the Aviso de Operación and obtain the municipal annual operating license (patente) applicable to the tax jurisdiction where the office is physically located. Step 3: Apply to the DGI through the Portal E-Tax for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes), attaching the registered deed, resident-agent appointment, directors' and beneficial-owners' identification, and proof of registered address; a non-resident-owned S.A. may complete and maintain the entire process through a licensed Panamanian lawyer or CPA. Step 4: Simultaneously activate ITBMS registration and obtain the DGI e-invoicing 'habilitación' (fiscal authorization), together with payroll and non-resident withholding registrations. Step 5: Obtain the maritime surveyor professional license required by the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá (AMP) for vessel/cargo inspection activities and, if a preferential regime (SEM under Law 41 of 2007, free-zone license, or Law 76 of 2019 relocation incentive) is intended, file the corresponding application with the competent authority so the operative RUC record reflects the applicable regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone — the S.A. is a per-se taxable corporation (no pass-through election) subject to Panamanian income tax only on Panamanian-source income; marine survey fees earned by a Panama S.A. from foreign underwriters for surveys of non-Panamanian vessels or cargo in international transit are treated as extraterritorial income outside the territorial tax base, and can be further ring-fenced under a SEM or free-zone license.

Available election forms

Panama does not enact US-style check-the-box or general hybrid-mismatch elections; the S.A. is mandatorily taxed at entity level under the territorial system. Available statutory opt-in regimes: (i) SEM (Sede de Empresa Multinacional) under Law 41 of 2007, exempting qualifying offshore services and headquarter activities from income tax and ITBMS; (ii) Law 76 of 2019 relocation incentives, granting temporary income-tax, dividend and ITBMS exemptions for companies moving their statutory domicile to Panama; (iii) free-zone / Área Económica Especial del Pacífico licenses excluding extraterritorial transactions from ITBMS; and (iv) the Law 66 of 2010 simplified fixed-quota regime for micro taxpayers with annual revenue at or below USD 36,000. Elections require prior DGI or MICI/SEM authority approval, and a marine surveyor may not retroactively elect zero-rating on taxable domestic survey work.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold: every person (including the Panamanian S.A. of a marine surveying business) habitually rendering taxable services in Panama must obtain the RUC and register for ITBMS before starting operations; micro taxpayers under Law 66 of 2010 whose annual gross income does not exceed USD 36,000 may opt for the simplified fixed monthly quota instead of the full monthly ITBMS return cycle.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% ITBMS) applies to (i) exports of movable goods physically leaving Panama under a customs export declaration, and (ii) services rendered to non-resident persons where the service is not used, consumed or economically enjoyed in Panama and is not connected with tangible property located in Panama at the time of performance. A marine cargo/hull surveyor can claim 0% only if the foreign underwriter/payer is the sole beneficiary, the survey report and economic benefit are delivered and enjoyed abroad, and the inspected vessel or cargo is in international transit; surveys of Panamanian-flagged domestic traffic, port operations, or Panamanian insurers' risks remain taxable at 7%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Documento Único de Aduana — DUA) or the equivalent DGI service-export registry record identifying the transaction as an export
  • Bill of Lading and/or Air Waybill evidencing the international movement of the inspected vessel/cargo (for cargo survey files)
  • Commercial invoice and the detailed marine survey/inspection report naming the foreign principal and the insured risk/cargo
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit voucher) or equivalent wire-transfer evidence from the non-resident underwriter/payer
  • Signed service contract with the foreign underwriter/broker stipulating place of payment, offshore use, and exclusive economic benefit abroad
  • DGI zero-rated e-invoice (factura electrónica coded as 'exportación') bearing the DGI validation code, plus the corresponding export ledger disclosure

Panama operates a phased mandatory e-invoicing regime: all active taxpayers must issue 'factura electrónica' through the DGI Portal E-Tax or through a DGI-certified software provider (PIS), submitting invoices to DGI in near-real time for fiscal validation, time-stamping and issuance of an electronic authorization code/QR — no paper invoice is valid for input-tax credit or expense deduction. Taxpayers must also maintain digital signature certificates and file the electronic sales and purchases ledgers through the e-Tax portal.

DGI conducts targeted post-clearance audits of zero-rated export claims; non-substantiation, missing documentation, or recharacterization of a domestic survey as an export triggers retroactive assessment at 7% ITBMS plus moratory interest (annual interest indexed to the Panamanian banking rate) and administrative fines of up to 100% of the omitted tax. Intentional false export declarations or fraudulent use of zero-rating may be prosecuted as criminal tax evasion (defraudación tributaria) under the Panamanian Penal Code with imprisonment of 2 to 5 years, and expose the taxpayer to cancellation of its e-invoicing 'habilitación', loss of free-zone/SEM benefits, and inclusion in the DGI's public registry of defaulting or sanctioned taxpayers.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized: the United States Dollar is legal tender and the exclusive clearing currency. Both capital and current account transactions are freely convertible; there are no restrictions on opening foreign-currency accounts, no limitations on profit or dividend repatriation, and no treasury authorisation for external transfers. Withholding tax on Panama-source dividends to non-resident shareholders (5% plus the 2% tax on dividends paid out of taxable income) is a fiscal charge and not an FX convertibility hurdle. Territorial-source rules apply for income tax, and foreign-source maritime survey fees earned abroad may be remitted offshore without any Panamanian exchange-control barrier.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panamanian law imposes no export proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no balance-of-payments reporting obligation for private-sector receipts. Marine survey fees, claim inspection fees, and underwriter remittances received in USD, EUR, or GBP may be retained in any currency, applied to working capital, or transferred offshore without time limit or central bank approval.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the sole prudential supervisor of banks, trust companies, and payment service providers; Banco Nacional de Panamá functions exclusively as fiscal agent of the State. Panama operates without a traditional central bank and without any domestic currency issuance or monetary authority, and there is consequently no FX administration licence, approval window, or exchange-control department.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium as a complementary rail only; Low as a primary settlement infrastructure. Panama does not maintain a dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing law; non-bank payment providers require SBP registration under the 'Empresa de Servicios de Pago' regime, and an offshore EMI (e.g., Wise Business) cannot be licensed as a bank-equivalent credit institution in Panama. Wise is operationally viable for low-value disbursements below USD 50,000 to foreign survey subcontractors and for receiving underwriter fee remittances through its virtual IBAN facilities, but it cannot replace a tier-1 Panamanian bank account for claim escrows, capital preservation, or SBP-supervised AML compliance. The enterprise should maintain a local bank account as the master account and use Wise strictly as an ancillary payout rail.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory transfer-pricing documentation and Form 930 (Declaración Informativa de Precios de Transferencia) are required when gross Panama-source revenue exceeds PAB 3,000,000 AND cross-border controlled transactions exceed PAB 1,000,000 in the fiscal year; Form 930 due by 30 June following the fiscal year-end.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Requirements (Executive Decree 175 of 2021, implementing Law 70 of 2019) bind Panama-tax-resident S.A.s conducting relevant activities, including insurance/reinsurance, shipping, and group service centers. A marine claims-surveying S.A. that is part of a multinational group, earns intercompany commissions from a foreign underwriter, or books predominantly foreign-source fees must demonstrate physical premises, qualified marine surveyor staff, local board meetings, and core income-generating activities in Panama. The company must submit an annual ESR notification; absence of local substance triggers rebuttal of the 'foreign-source income' characterization.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Returns (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of 2 to 5 years; fine of 50%-100% of evaded tax; 20% discrepancy surcharge on under-declared income; monthly 1.5% interest; exclusion from public contracts; seizure of bank accounts and vessels.

Director personal liability: Directors signing false or omission-tainted returns are criminally liable and jointly and severally liable for the unpaid 25% CIT; DGI may embargo personal assets and issue an impedimento de salida (travel ban) with the National Immigration Service.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR administrative fine of PAB 100,000 to PAB 1,000,000; DGI recharacterization of the purported 'foreign-source' income as Panama-source income, imposing 25% CIT plus interest, a 20% discrepancy penalty, and cancellation of the Aviso de Operación (active taxpayer status).

Director personal liability: Directors and the registered resident agent face personal fines for false ESR filings; on continued non-compliance, authorities strike off the entity and hold directors jointly liable for the re-assessed tax; criminal charges apply for using the shell to deceive the tax administration.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: DGI arm's-length adjustment with a 40% concurrency surcharge on the adjusted tax difference plus 1.5% monthly interest; administrative fines of PAB 500 to PAB 50,000 for each missing Form 930 or documentation package; adjustments reclassifying foreign-source fees as Panama-source.

Director personal liability: Directors approving the zero-margin policy or signing a false Form 930 are jointly and severally liable for the adjusted tax; willful manipulation for tax evasion triggers criminal prosecution and travel restrictions.
AML / Unlicensed Insurance Intermediation

Statutory sanction: SSRP administrative fines of PAB 1,000 to PAB 50,000 per unlicensed adjustment and immediate suspension of operations; AML serious-infraction fines of up to PAB 1,000,000 for missing beneficial-ownership records or unauthorized handling of third-party premium funds; criminal money-laundering penalties of 5 to 12 years' imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors and the head of compliance are personally prosecutable for unlicensed insurance intermediation and as 'responsible persons' for AML failures; sanctions include imprisonment without parole for aggravated AML offenses, personal fines, civil restitution of misapplied claimant funds, and disqualification from holding corporate office in Panama.
