Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); e-portal: SITIO (Sistema Integrado de Trámites y Operaciones) via https://dgi.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima must first complete corporate registration with the Registro Público (Public Registry), appointing a local resident agent and filing a corporate designation. Following registration, the entity must obtain a digital identity certificate, appear or transact through the licensed public accountant before the DGI, and complete Form RUC-1 (Registro Único de Contribuyente) to procure a Panama RUC (TIN). The entity must additionally register in SITIO, subscribe to the ITBMS taxpayer registry, and, where applicable, apply for the electronic invoicing authorization and digital fiscal stamp.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime for Sociedades Anónimas. The default and exclusive classification is a taxable corporation subject to entity-level corporate income tax on Panama-source income only; no pass-through or transparent election exists. Certain limited elections are available for segmented income regimes (e.g., multinational headquarters or industrial processing) but do not alter the tax identity of the S.A. for income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 300,000 (Balboas / United States Dollars) in annual taxable gross receipts; businesses with annual turnover below this threshold may opt into the simplified Pequeño Contribuyente regime with a reduced flat rate, but must still obtain an RUC and may register for ITBMS voluntarily.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Panama, including heparin sodium active pharmaceutical ingredient extracted locally, are eligible for the ITBMS zero rate (0%) provided the goods physically depart Panamanian customs territory within the legally permitted term, are sold for consideration to a non-resident consignee, and are supported by the corresponding customs export declaration. Services physically rendered from Panama to non-resident clients for use exclusively abroad may also qualify for zero rating if they meet DGI criteria and are not utilized in Panama. The seller must be a registered ITBMS taxpayer and must issue an electronic export invoice with the export tariff heading.

  • Panama Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación via e-SICE / SICE electronic platform)
  • Commercial Invoice (electronic, DGI-fiscal validated)
  • Bill of Lading (maritime) or Air Waybill (aerial)
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin (when applicable)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT wire confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Panama operates mandatory electronic invoicing through the DGI's Sistema de Facturación Electrónica (SFE), with phased implementation based on taxpayer annual revenue. Affected taxpayers must use DGI-authorized software, transmit invoices in real time to the DGI for fiscal validation, and incorporate a QR code and digital fiscal stamp on each invoice. All additional commercial documents (credit notes, debit notes, vendor's turnover declarations) must be transmitted via the SFE API or through a certified electronic billing operator. Failure to obtain prior DGI clearance renders the invoice non-fiscal and disallows input VAT credits.

Panama's DGI and Customs (ANA) conduct joint audits of zero-rated export transactions. A false or unsupported zero-rating claim triggers the immediate assessment of ITBMS at 7% on the unduly exempted gross value, plus statutory late interest and surcharges, and the denial of input VAT credits. Civil penalties under Articles 712 and 713 of the Código Fiscal range from B/. 500 to B/. 50,000 depending on the gravity and recurrence; deliberate fraud or use of falsified Bills of Lading, false custom declarations, or fictitious export destination instruments may constitute criminal tax fraud and money laundering predicate offenses, carrying imprisonment up to ten years, disqualification from electronic invoicing authorization, and retroactive revocation of prior zero-rating decisions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a de facto fully dollarized economy with unrestricted convertibility on both capital and current account transactions. There is no central-bank approval requirement for dividends, profit remittances, capital injections, loan repayments, or trade payments. Balboa token coins are convertible 1:1 with USD by statute. The only material frictions are prudential KYC/AML documentation, OFAC sanctions screening, corporate record keeping, and potential tax substance review for payments that exceed declared operational capacity. Profit remittance is generally subject to a 10% dividend withholding tax if the profits derived from Panamanian-source income are not reinvested, but no currency convertibility hurdle exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion ratios, and no minimum holding period for export receivables. Exporters may retain USD freely in Panamanian bank accounts. However, for customs and bank compliance, export collections must be supported by a commercial invoice, Bill of Lading / Airway Bill, packing list, and, for heparin sodium products, the certificate of analysis, sanitary registration, and export license issued by the Panama Ministry of Health (Dirección Nacional de Farmacia y Drogas). Panamanian banks may impose internal documentary thresholds for large dollar receipts and will file an Unusual Transaction Report (UTS) or Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) if the underlying trade documentation is incomplete.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama has no true central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP/SUPERBANCOS) acts as the prudential regulator of the banking system, while Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and clearing-house counterparty. The Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a fixed 1:1 token currency backed by the U.S. dollar, which is legal tender in Panama. No separate foreign-exchange authority issues exchange-control permits.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cross-border fintech EMI viability is LOW for a Panamanian operating pharmaceutical-extraction company. Panama does not recognize an offshore EMI as an authorized deposit-taking institution merely by virtue of a foreign license. Any non-bank payment institution or digital bank wishing to accept or disburse USD in Panama must be a locally incorporated entity, obtain a license from the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, and maintain a physical compliance office with designated AML/CFT officers. An S.A. may use a licensed Panamanian fintech for low-value trade-related payments, but for a Stage 4 treasury blueprint involving capital escrow, LC disbursements, and high-value SWIFT settlements, tier-1 commercial banks remain the only fully reliable clearing rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections from a foreign parent or founding shareholder should be routed by SWIFT MT103 in USD to the Panamanian S.A.'s USD settlement account. The payment narrative should state 'AUMENTO DE CAPITAL' or 'EQUITY CONTRIBUTION', accompanied by the corresponding board resolution, updated share registry, and UBO declaration. Because Panama has no exchange-control approval, the inbound credit may be cleared freely, but the bank will verify the source of funds and require an advance or same-day presentation of corporate documents for any credit exceeding USD 25,000 or such lower amount as the bank's internal threshold prescribes. If the injection is structured as a shareholder loan, the bank should receive the loan Agreement, a board resolution authorising external debt, and an interest-rate and repayment term consistent with arm's-length principles to prevent transfer-pricing recharacterization.Outbound trade disbursements for heparin sodium procurement and manufacturing may be executed via SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments or through irrevocable documentary letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) for large repeated orders. Commercial LCs should be issued in USD and governed by the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600), with payment at sight upon presentation of commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, certificate of analysis, sanitary/regulatory licence, Bill of Lading / Airway Bill, and insurance certificate. For domestic USD clearing within Panama, the Banco Nacional de Panamá clearinghouse may be used; for international USD clearing, funds will settle over the Fedwire or CHIPS network via correspondent banks in the United States. All MT103 messages must comply with SBP AML screening, include accurate ordering and beneficiary identifiers, and avoid generic references such as 'trade settlement' that lack invoice numbers.USD/PAB parity instruments: none required because the balboa is fixed 1:1 and USD is legal tender; there is no measurable PAB conversion exchange risk.Cross-currency forwards in EUR/USD, USD/GBP, USD/CNH, and USD/JPY for procurement of non-USD heparin crude supplies, provided the bank has a signed ISDA Master Agreement or G-20 OTC derivatives documentation and board authorization.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on selected Latin American currencies such as COP, MXN, or CLP where settlement of the trade receivable occurs in local Latin American currencies with no corresponding deliverable USD cross-border clearing route.Cross-currency swaps for long-term trade-debt restructuring or supply-chain finance between USD and EUR or USD and CNY, subject to SBP market-risk guidelines, credit-support annex collateral, and internal risk committee approval.

5% withholding on dividends distributed from Panama-source taxable profits (final tax withheld by the S.A.); 20% withholding on distributions, deemed distributions, reserves, or credits deriving from untaxed foreign-source profits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/. 2,500,000 (USD/Balboas 2.5 million) of aggregate related-party transactions in the fiscal year triggers mandatory Local File and Master File under Executive Decree 108 of 2016; entities below the threshold must still keep arm's-length evidence and be prepared for DGI audit requests.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (Law 254 of 2021, Cabinet Decree 8 of 2022) subject entities performing 'Relevant Activities' (distribution, services, financing, leasing, headquarters, IP holding, insurance and shipping) with foreign-source income to a substantive local presence test: physical office, adequate employees, and core income-generating activities performed in Panama. Heparin fractionation/manufacturing (ISIC 3250) is not per se a relevant activity; however, any offshore procurement office, IP royalty collection, treasury coordination or group invoicing hub operating in Panama must satisfy the substance test. Non-compliance may result in fines and potential cancellation of legal personality.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent ISR Filing

Statutory sanction: Surcharge of 15%-25% of the unpaid tax plus 1% monthly interest; administrative fines up to USD 50,000 for serious infractions; criminal tax fraud penalties under the Fiscal Code and Criminal Code, including imprisonment and forfeiture of proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers and controlling shareholders who sign false returns or direct the scheme are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax plus surcharges; a criminal conviction triggers imprisonment, travel bans, and civil restitution of the tax fraud proceeds.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: ESR non-compliance declarations, administrative fines, strike-off or cancellation of the corporate registration at the Public Registry; DGI may reclassify income as Panama-source and assess 25% ISR, plus recargo and interest.

Director personal liability: Where the corporate form is a mere instrumentality (no substance, no segregation of assets, commingled funds), courts may pierce the veil to hold directors personally liable for corporate tax, labor and ELSSA obligations; travel restrictions may be imposed while debts remain outstanding.
AML / Illicit Procurement and Payment Structuring

Statutory sanction: Asset forfeiture (extinción de dominio), fines of up to twice the laundered amount, closure of banking facilities, cancellation of the S.A. before the Public Registry, and criminal prosecution under Law 23 of 2015 and the Criminal Code; notification to the OCDS and Financial Analysis Unit (UAF).

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and beneficial owners who knew or should have known face personal criminal liability: imprisonment (up to 15 years), travel bans, prohibition on practicing corporate office, and civil liability for damages caused by the unlawful acts.
