Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) — official portal https://www.dgi.gob.pa; registration, return filing, and electronic invoicing services are delivered through the DGI online services portal. Customs and export clearance sit with Panama's Dirección Nacional de Aduanas (Customs) through the SICE/VUCE foreign-trade single window.

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima: (1) incorporate by public deed and register in the Registro Público, obtaining the Ficha/Juridical Certificate and appointing a licensed resident agent; (2) submit the DGI 'Solicitud de Inscripción' registration application within the statutory window (generally 30 days from incorporation), attaching the public deed, juridical certificate, resident agent acceptance, local fiscal address, and identification of the legal representative and directors; (3) obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) certificate and, where taxable supplies are made, the ITBMS registration and electronic invoicing authorization; (4) register as an exporter in the SICE single window. A foreign entity not constituted in Panama may file for a non-resident RUC only if it derives Panama-source income or makes taxable supplies in Panama; a pure offshore commodity trader with no Panamanian presence does not require a RUC.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is a separate corporate taxpayer with no look-through or pass-through attributes; Panama levies corporate income tax (headline ISR rate 25%, with 15% applying to taxable income not exceeding USD 1.5 million) only on Panama-source income, while non-Panamanian source income such as offshore trading spreads on goods that never touch Panama falls outside the territorial net. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is a separate corporate taxpayer with no look-through or pass-through attributes; Panama levies corporate income tax (headline ISR rate 25%, with 15% applying to taxable income not exceeding USD 1.5 million) only on Panama-source income, while non-Panamanian source income such as offshore trading spreads on goods that never touch Panama falls outside the territorial net. Income from the export of goods produced in Panama is statutorily exempt from ISR.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election exists in Panama; the S.A. is mandatorily taxed at the entity level. Available statutory elections are limited: (i) small-taxpayer simplified ITBMS regime for annual taxable turnover not exceeding USD 36,000; (ii) simplified income-tax methods for small taxpayers (Régimen Simplificado); (iii) optional RUC/ITBMS registration for non-resident exporters where no permanent establishment is created; (iv) no hybrid-mismatch or GILTI-type elections are recognized, and territorial sourcing applies automatically without election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis turnover threshold: every person engaged in taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS upon obtaining the RUC. Small taxpayers with annual taxable turnover up to USD 36,000 (US dollars are legal tender; the balboa is pegged 1:1 with the USD) may use the simplified regime; exporters of goods must register regardless of volume.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A supply of VHP raw cane centrifugal sugar qualifies for 0% ITBMS when: (i) the sale is made to a non-resident purchaser; (ii) the goods are physically removed from Panama's customs territory by ocean vessel under a definitive export declaration; (iii) the exporter holds a valid RUC and ITBMS registration; and (iv) the goods depart within the period stipulated by the SICE export declaration. The 0% rate preserves input ITBMS credits attributable to the export. For income-tax purposes, territorial sourcing statutorily exempts export proceeds derived from Panamanian-produced goods, and a pure offshore arbitrage margin on sugar that never enters Panama is outside Panama-source taxation.

  • Declaración de Exportación (export declaration) filed through the SICE/DGA single window with the assigned export consignment number
  • Definitive commercial export invoice (factura comercial de exportación) issued as an authorized electronic invoice bearing the DGI Sello Digital
  • Ocean Bill of Lading (conocimiento de embarque) evidencing a Panamanian load port and overseas discharge port
  • Packing list and VHP sugar quality/polarization certificate supporting the consignment description
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice/SWIFT record evidencing collection of export proceeds under the revolving letter of credit or wire payment rail
  • Certificate of Origin (required only where a preferential tariff claim is made at destination)

Panama mandates fiscal electronic invoicing for ITBMS-registered businesses under DGI Resolution No. 201-0080 and subsequent technical regulations. Exporters must issue authorized electronic export invoices through the DGI fiscal billing platform: invoice XML data is transmitted to the DGI through the assigned API/web-service channel, and a unique 'Sello Digital' digital fiscal seal/QR code must be obtained before the invoice is valid for customs purposes. Vouchers are coded as 'Exportación' with a 0% rate. Batch clearance and DGI platform matching are mandatory; unauthorized invoices are invalid for export zero-rating and input-credit purposes.

Because Panama is fully dollarized (USD is legal tender and the balboa is pegged 1:1), there is no statutory foreign-exchange conversion or repatriation deadline; export receipts are deemed received in national legal tender upon credit to the exporter's account. DGI scrutiny cross-matches SICE export declarations, customs departure evidence, vessel manifests, and bank collection records; unjustified non-collection within a 180-day commercial cycle may trigger deemed-price adjustments and transfer-pricing review. Where export zero-rating is misclaimed, the DGI may retroactively assess ITBMS at 7% on the full invoice value plus default surcharges and interest, deny input credits, and impose fines generally ranging from 50% to 100% of the omitted tax; intentional fraud can give rise to criminal tax-fraud liability and director liability under the Fiscal Code.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility under a complete dollarization framework. Panama imposes no exchange control, no capital account licensing, and no current account restrictions. Profit remittances, shareholder loan repayments, dividend distributions, and trade settlements denominated in USD can be executed freely without central bank approval. Panama applies a territorial income tax system: dividends paid out of Panama-source taxable profits attract a 10% dividend withholding tax to the extent applicable, while distributions attributable to foreign-source income retained offshore are not subject to Panamanian withholding; no exit tax, migration approval, or mandatory reinvestment hurdle applies to outward remittances from the S.A.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Panama. Export proceeds in USD from VHP raw cane sugar vessel shipments may be retained indefinitely in Panamanian or foreign accounts, remitted abroad, or pledged as collateral without exchange-control intervention. The only compliance obligations are bank-level AML/CFT monitoring thresholds and the SBP transaction-reporting rules, which require documentation of origin of funds for large trade-related inflows but do not impose repatriation or conversion obligations.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) in coordination with the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); the Republic of Panama has no conventional central bank issuing fiat currency, and the United States Dollar is legal tender under the Panamanian monetary system, with the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) existing solely as coinage at a 1:1 parity. Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent for the State, while the SBP exercises comprehensive banking regulation, AML/CFT supervision, and oversight of all licensed banks operating in Panama.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Panama does not recognize offshore EMI passporting, and a foreign digital bank or electronic-money institution cannot clear USD through Panamanian domestic rails, issue commercial letters of credit, or provide regulated trade-account facilities without a locally licensed banking entity or a physical branch authorized by the SBP. The SBP operates a fintech innovation hub and regulatory sandbox, but no cross-border EMI license exists for full corporate treasury intermediation; therefore the physical-sugar commodity treasury must be executed through a licensed Panamanian bank with US correspondent clearing capacity.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when related-party transactions exceed B/. 3,000,000 (USD 3,000,000 at the fixed 1:1 peg) in the fiscal year. Master File: required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above B/. 50,000,000. Country-by-Country Report: triggered at consolidated group revenue above B/. 750,000,000. Basis: Executive Decree 285 of 2017 and DGI administrative guidance.

Economic substance scope: Panama has not enacted a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but substance requirements operate through: (i) the territorial tax rule, which denies the foreign-source exemption where management and commercial control are exercised from Panama; (ii) DGI practice requiring real presence, employees, premises, and bank accounts for tax-residence certificates; and (iii) EU/FATF-driven transparency measures (Law 254 of 2021 on multinational transparency and Country-by-Country Reporting; Law 23 of 2015 on AML). An S.A. directing sugar trades from Panama while claiming offshore exemption is exposed to full reclassification.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Falsified Returns

Statutory sanction: Surcharge of up to 200% of the evaded tax, 1% monthly default interest, administrative fines up to B/. 10,000, and criminal referral for defraudación tributaria under the reformed Código Penal, carrying imprisonment for aggravated evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns or authorize sham documentation are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and surcharges; judicial piercing of the corporate veil permits seizure of personal assets, and criminal conviction triggers immediate travel bans and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of the territorial exclusion; reclassification of the entire trading margin as Panamanian-source income subject to 25% CIT, surcharges, and interest; cancellation of the tax-residence certificate; and administrative dissolution for non-payment of the annual B/. 300 franchise fee.

Director personal liability: Directors' personal liability for unpaid dividend withholding and franchise obligations; disqualification from serving as directors; banks treat substance-free S.As. as high-risk under AML rules, causing account closures and regulatory filings against the directors.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: DGI adjustment of the taxable margin to the arm's-length standard, penalties of up to B/. 100,000, disallowance of the non-compliant expense, and inclusion in the public DGI debtors registry, blocking the 'Paz y Salvo' clearance.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly execute a non-arm's-length pricing policy face joint liability for the underpaid tax and penalties; repeated non-filing is treated as fraudulent non-compliance and may be prosecuted alongside the tax evasion offense.
AML / Trade Sanctions Exposure

Statutory sanction: Corporate monetary penalties up to B/. 1,000,000 under Ley 23 de 2015, freezing of funds by the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF), forfeiture of the sugar cargo and proceeds, and parallel OFAC enforcement for any U.S. dollar clearing touchpoint.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and compliance personnel face imprisonment of 5 to 12 years for money laundering under Article 254 of the Código Penal, preventive travel bans, provisional detention, and civil confiscation of personal assets held through related structures.
