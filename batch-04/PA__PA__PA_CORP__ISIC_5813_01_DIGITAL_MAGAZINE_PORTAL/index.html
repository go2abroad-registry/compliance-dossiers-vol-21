Governing statute

Ley No. 32 de 1927 (General Corporation Law of the Republic of Panama), as amended by Ley No. 5 de 2010 and Decreto Ley No. 2 de 2010; Articles 1–10 (formation), Article 9 (minimum capital and single-share rule), Articles 17–24 (registered office, resident agent, duties), Articles 30–31 (shares and no-par value), Articles 33–35 (directors and officers).

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; the S.A. may declare any capital sum and must subscribe and issue at least one share with or without par value. No escrow or mandatory bank deposit of capital is required under Ley 32 de 1927; the subscribed share capital must be stated in the deed and is deemed fully paid as declared by the founding shareholder.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas – Panama; online portal: Sede Electrónica at https://sede.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by registering with the DGI through a local appointed legal representative or registered agent before commencing Panama-sourced economic activities. The application includes entity incorporation documents, Panamanian apostilled articles, identification of shareholders/directors, and notarized power of attorney. After approval, the DGI issues the RUC and registers the entity for ITBMS.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation_with_Territorial_Scope — A foreign entity must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by registering with the DGI through a local appointed legal representative or registered agent before commencing Panama-sourced economic activities. The application includes entity incorporation documents, Panamanian apostilled articles, identification of shareholders/directors, and notarized power of attorney. After approval, the DGI issues the RUC and registers the entity for ITBMS.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_with_Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Panamanian law. A taxpayer may elect the small-taxpayer simplified ITBMS regime if annual gross receipts do not exceed the statutory threshold, and may elect between available withholding agent and simplified remittance procedures; branch and subsidiary formations are structural choices, not tax elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory upon commencement of taxable operations; no general economic threshold applies. A simplified small-taxpayer regime is available only for annual gross receipts up to USD 36,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Panama and services rendered to non-residents for use wholly outside Panama are zero-rated for ITBMS. The taxpayer must maintain proof of physical departure of goods or cross-border service provision, commercial invoicing, and evidence of payment received from the non-resident counterparty.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill or alternative international transport document
  • Commercial Invoice
  • RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) certificate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or payment proof from the foreign buyer
  • Contract or service agreement for offshore service exports

Designated large taxpayers must issue electronic invoices using DGI-approved software, digitally sign XML documents, transmit them to the DGI in real time, and display a QR code; fiscal clearance is performed through the DGI's Sede Electrónica authorization process.

The DGI and Customs authorities may retroactively assess unreported ITBMS, claw back zero-rating benefits, apply interest and fines ranging from 100% to 200% of the deficiency, and initiate criminal proceedings where fraudulent export documentation is proven, in accordance with the Código Fiscal and related anti-smuggling and tax evasion statutes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full, free and unrestricted convertibility under Panama's dollarized monetary system; the Balboa is pegged at a hard 1:1 par to the USD. There are no exchange-control laws, no FX permit requirements, no capital-movement restrictions, and no barriers to profit remittance, dividend distribution, loan repayment or liquidation proceeds. Panama's territorial tax regime subjects only Panama-source income to corporate taxation; foreign-source digital publishing income from Stripe, AdSense and sponsored media placements is outside Panamanian income tax. The only practical friction points are the AML/KYC standards imposed under Law 254 of 2021 on economic substance and the SBP's customer due diligence directives, which require full beneficial-ownership transparency for non-resident-owned Sociedades Anónimas.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds, no forced conversion ratio, no repatriation deadline and no custodial restriction on foreign receipts. Stripe and AdSense payouts in USD or any other currency may be retained overseas or swept at will into the Panamanian operational account, subject solely to the receiving bank's ordinary AML verification of the underlying platform settlement records.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as the apex banking and payment-systems regulator; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts solely as a state-owned commercial bank and fiscal agent. Panama maintains no central bank, no monetary-policy authority, and no sovereign issuance of paper currency, with the US Dollar serving as legal tender and the Balboa minted only as a coin unit.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-High as a collections and payout aggregation node, but Low-Medium as a primary treasury spine. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer and Airwallex may lawfully receive Stripe/AdSense settlements as receivable-collection agents, yet they cannot replace a Panamanian SBP-licensed banking relationship for corporate economic-substance evidence, statutory record-keeping and audit-proof cash positioning. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit
