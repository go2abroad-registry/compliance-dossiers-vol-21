Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance of Panama; official portal: https://www.dgi.gob.pa (Sede Electrónica system for electronic filings and e-invoicing)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima must first register with the Public Registry (Registro Público) and appoint a resident agent. It then applies through the DGI Sede Electrónica or its regional offices for an RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes); the application requires notarized articles of incorporation, certificate of deposit of paid-in capital, proof of resident agent appointment, and identification of directors. The DGI assigns the RUC which simultaneously operates as the income tax (ISR) account and the ITBMS registration. For customs brokerage, the company must also obtain a provincial customs broker license and an active customs code linked to the RUC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not administer US-style check-the-box elections. Available elections include: (i) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime election under Law 41/2007 for qualifying service companies; (ii) City of Knowledge and Panama Pacifico special economic zone elections; (iii) simplified fixed-quota small taxpayer ISR method for entities under statutory turnover thresholds; (iv) election to pay dividend tax at the 10% advancing withholding on behalf of shareholders; and (v) elective treatment of transit-related brokerage income as non-Panamanian source where the service is executed abroad or the beneficiary is a non-resident.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold; any person habitually transferring goods or rendering services within Panamanian territory must register for ITBMS. All RUC holders are automatically registered; the monetary threshold for mandatory invoicing compliance is zero. Amounts are denominated in PAB/USD (Balboa at parity with USD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for 0% ITBMS where the DUA (Declaración Única de Aduanas) evidences physical departure of the goods from Panamanian customs territory and the sale is made to a foreign buyer. Supply of customs brokerage services qualifies for non-taxation rather than zero-rating when the service is consumed or economically exploited outside Panama, is rendered to a non-resident recipient, and is supported by a written contract specifying the offshore benefit; services in respect of pure transit or transshipment cargo (régimen de tránsito internacional) are considered outside the Panamanian taxable event and may be invoiced without ITBMS where the service relates to cargo not entering Panama's national commerce.

  • Declaración Única de Aduanas (DUA) / Customs Export Declaration with validated exit stamp
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing shipment to foreign destination (with port/airport exit certificate)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa) from a Panamanian bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Contract or service agreement with the non-resident client (for brokerage service export)
  • Certificate of Origin (only where preferential treaty treatment is claimed)
  • Customs broker license and power of attorney record

Mandatory e-invoicing under DGI Resolutions (including 2011-3102 series and subsequent technical standards). Sales invoices, purchase records, and credit/debit notes must be generated in XML, digitally signed with the taxpayer's electronic signature (e-firma), transmitted in real time to the DGI's factorial clearance platform (Sede Electrónica), and must bear a DGI-issued authorization code and QR code. Enterprise resource planning systems must integrate via DGI-approved Web Services API, SFTP, or the bidirectional 'DRG' data reception channel; a digital fiscal stamp must be printed on the PDF/A representation. Customs brokers additionally transmit declarations through the SENAL/ASYCUDA World customs platform linked to the ITBMS interface at import clearance.

DGI conducts documentary audits of export dossiers, cross-matched with customs exit records, tax repatriation data and bank remittance certificates. Fraudulent zero-rating or simulated export transactions attract: (i) administrative clawback of the full ITBMS or income tax evaded with retrospective effect for up to ten years in fraud cases; (ii) statutory surcharges and moratory interest (up to 3% per month on the unpaid amount); (iii) fines of 100% to 200% of the evaded tax under Articles 761-762 of the Fiscal Code; (iv) criminal prosecution for tax fraud (defraudación fiscal) punishable by imprisonment of 3 to 5 years; and (v) customs penalties under the customs law, including goods seizure and monetary sanctions up to ten times the merchandise value, plus administrative suspension or definitive revocation of the customs broker license by the National Customs Authority.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists on both current and capital accounts: the USD is legal tender at a fixed 1:1 parity with the Balboa (PAB, coin-only), and no prior authorization, quota, exchange rate, or repatriation restriction applies to service fees, customs brokerage commissions, capital injections, or shareholder distributions. The only operational constraints are (i) AML reporting for cash and certain transactions at or above USD 10,000, (ii) the obligation to declare Panama-source income to the DGI before remitting profits, and (iii) a final dividend withholding tax (DID), generally 5% for non-resident shareholders on Panama-source profits, which the S.A. must pay before distribution.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion percentage, and no obligatory repatriation period for export or service proceeds. A customs brokerage house may hold USD and any other convertible foreign currency freely in Panamanian banks or abroad; the only compliance requirement is that each receipt and disbursement be traceable, referenced to the SIGA customs clearing file, and retained for five years for UAF and SBP inspection.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no issuing central bank; monetary policy is effectively set by the US Federal Reserve under a fully dollarized regime. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the banking and financial regulator, Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the State and settlement agent for the SLBTR real-time gross settlement system, and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) is the financial intelligence unit administering the AML/CFT reporting regime under Ley 23 de 2015.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-Low as a primary treasury rail, acceptable only as an auxiliary corridor. Panama's Ley 44 de 2023 introduced a fintech authorization and regulatory sandbox regime supervised by the SBP, but no standalone EU-style EMI license exists; offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud, similar) cannot settle customs duty payments or open separate clearing accounts in Panama without a licensed local partner bank. Such EMIs may be used only for incidental foreign operating expenses, provided all ANA duty payments remain routed through the Panamanian banking system (ACH/SIGA or SLBTR). Local regulated e-wallets (e.g., Nequi Panamá operated with Banistmo) are suitable for small-value client collections but are not treasury-grade for duty reconciliation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders route equity injections via SWIFT MT103 into the S.A.'s USD operating account at a Tier-1 Panamanian bank, using field 70 with the reference 'Aporte de Capital - Sociedad Panameña [RUC] [Registro Público folio]'. The bank requires a notarized board resolution authorizing the capital increase, the registered agent's certificate of good standing, and, for injections above USD 100,000, a source-of-funds letter from the remitting bank. If the shareholder is a PIF, the foundation deed excerpt and the UAF beneficial-owner filing receipt must accompany the wire. No government approval is required for the inbound transfer.Three clearing rails are used: (1) daily customs duty payments to ANA are executed through the SIGA electronic payment module via Panama's national ACH (same-day USD settlement, analogous to the Automated Broker Interface model in the US); (2) international supplier and consignee payments are cleared via SWIFT MT103 with full invoice and BL references; and (3) commercial Letters of Credit for bonded transit and high-value imports are issued by the Panamanian bank via SWIFT MT700/MT707, with negotiation documents presented through the bank's trade desk. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/. 1,000,000 (Panama Balboa, pegged 1:1 to USD 1,000,000) in aggregate related-party transactions during the tax year. Above this threshold, a Form 733 sworn statement and a full Local File (and Master File where group turnover exceeds USD 50,000,000) must be filed.

Economic substance scope: Law 254 of 2021 ESR requires Panamanian entities deriving income from relevant activities abroad to prove economic substance. Custom clearance brokerage is not a named relevant activity, but the DGI and customs authority will nevertheless require physical office, dedicated staff, revenue commensurate with assets and risks, and board control in Panama for treaty and efiling claims; a pure letter-box customs broker is treated as a shell and is subject to ESR penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fiscal Fraud)

Statutory sanction: Penalty of triple the evaded tax (200% surcharge), plus default interest; in-court fiscal crime proceedings can impose imprisonment of 3 to 10 years. The corporate status of the S.A. may be cancelled by the Registro Público, and the preferential tax record revoked.

Director personal liability: Directors and registered legal representatives who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax deficiency, face civil restitution of evasion proceeds, travel bans restricting exit from Panama, and custodial sentences based on the amount defrauded.
Shell Company / Private Interest Foundation (PIF) Misuse

Statutory sanction: Notification to the Unidad de Inteligencia Financiera (UIF) and seizure of bank accounts under Law 23 of 2015; fines up to PAB 1,000,000 for AML shortcomings and cancellation of the S.A.'s license to practice customs brokerage.

Director personal liability: Directors used to conceal beneficial ownership are treated as principals of a criminal violation, subject to administrative dissolution of the entity, personal fines of B/. 2,000 to B/. 50,000, 5-year travel bans, and imprisonment for up to 10 years for money structuring and money laundering.
Customs Fraud and False Brokerage Declarations

Statutory sanction: The Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas imposes administrative fines from 3% to 100% of the evaded duty and may suspend or permanently revoke the customs brokerage license (Registro de Agentes Aduaneros). Criminal smuggling carries imprisonment of 5 to 15 years and confiscation of cargo and warehouse.

Director personal liability: The director who signs or licenses the custom broker registration is personally liable for all compromised duty amounts, is subject to the immediate freezing of personal bank accounts, judicial travel bans, and the revocation of his or her individual customs brokering credentials.
Transfer Pricing and Base Erosion Red Flags

Statutory sanction: DGI adjusts the taxable income upward, disallows the excessive deduction, and applies a deficiency penalty of 40% of the underpaid tax, plus monthly default interest. The taxpayer loses the preference to request advance pricing agreements and is barred from DTA relief until corrected.

Director personal liability: Directors who design artificial TP schemes are held jointly responsible for the tax adjustment and interest; they may be criminally indicted for misleading the DGI, face civil restitution for the hidden profit, and are subject to a travel ban pending the final ruling.
