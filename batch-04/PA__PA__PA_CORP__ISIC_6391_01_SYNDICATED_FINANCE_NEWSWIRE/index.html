Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for Sociedad Anónima. The Articles must state authorized capital, commonly US$10,000, and may provide for shares without nominal value. Shares may be issued for consideration determined by the board; there is no escrow account or deposit requirement at incorporation. Paid-in capital may be zero until shares are subscribed and paid.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); e-portal: SIREC (Sistema Informático de Recaudación), https://sirec.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned S.A. must first be incorporated before a Notary and registered in the Registro Público. The S.A. must file with the DGI through the SIREC portal (or via a licensed Contador Público Autorizado) an application for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente), submitting: certified copy of the Escritura Pública, corporate bylaws, resolution of appointment of directors and officers (Presidente, Secretario, Tesorero), resident agent (agente residente) certificate, notarized fiscal domicile, and the Aviso de Operación issued by the corresponding municipality. The DGI issues a provisional RUC and assigns fiscal attributes, followed by registration for ITBMS, INSS employer registration (if hiring), and municipal permit validation. Non-resident owners are permitted; a resident agent and a fiscal representative are mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - Territorial_Scope (Panama-source income only; foreign-source income is outside ITBMS and corporate income tax)

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists; an S.A. is an opaque entity taxed under the territorial regime. Optional elections include: (i) acceptance into the ITBMS small taxpayer simplified regime (Régimen Especial Simplificado) where gross turnover is at or below B/. 36,000 per year; (ii) election of the 'sociedad extraterritorial' or tax-neutral holding vehicle treatment under Law 41 of 2007 for foreign-owned entities engaged in qualifying international operations; (iii) no hybrid mismatch election is available under Panama law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general exemption threshold for full registration; all persons engaged in commercial, industrial, or service activities must obtain a RUC and register for ITBMS. 'Pequeño contribuyente' simplified regime is available for gross taxable turnover up to B/. 36,000 per annum (B/. = USD at parity).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are taxed at 0% ITBMS. Services, including financial newswire/API subscription feeds, qualify only if: (i) the recipient is a non-resident (no Panamanian tax identification); (ii) the service is effectively used or consumed outside Panama; (iii) the consideration is paid from a foreign bank account; and (iv) the supplier is registered with the DGI and maintains an exporter file. The exporter must claim the zero-rating in the monthly ITBMS return, include the customer's foreign tax ID/address, geolocation evidence of subscription use, and remittance records of foreign currency entering the Panamanian banking system.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación via SICE/Digitrac)
  • Bill of Lading (Conocimiento de Embarque) or Airway Bill / digital freight equivalent
  • Commercial Invoice and executed service contract (newswire/API subscription)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa de Fondos) evidencing foreign-source payment
  • Proof of customer's non-resident status (exporter's retained KYC file)

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) via SIREC, phased per DGI Resolution No. 201-4295 and Law 134 of 2020: large taxpayers from 2022; all other RUC-holders from 2023. Issuers must integrate ERP/API with the DGI's REST/JSON endpoints, have each invoice or batch validated in real-time (transmisión en línea), affix a digital stamp (QR code/folio), and report monthly through the 'Declaración Mensual de ITBMS'. Offline contingency batches and fallback modes are permitted only with prior DGI authorization.

Under the Código Fiscal and Law 134 of 2020, erroneous or fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger: reversal of input VAT credits, a 10% monthly recargo on the unremitted tax (capped definitively), statutory interest, and fines of 50% to 200% of the omitted ITBMS. Misrepresentation of export documentation, false bank remittance certificates, or failure to retain the beneficiary owner file may constitute tax fraud under Law 70 of 2019, punishable with imprisonment (up to 8 years) and disqualification from future export zero-rating. The DGI's post-audit unit cross-verifies customs declarations, SIREC e-invoice data, INSS employee records, and the favorable economics of the foreign payment before approving any export VAT refund (devolución del crédito fiscal).

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with no capital account or current account restrictions. Profit remittance, dividend distribution, and repatriation of invested capital are free from prior authorization or mandatory conversion into local currency. The Balboa exists only as fractional currency units; all material financial flows are denominated in USD, eliminating foreign exchange conversion risk for USD-denominated operations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender rules. Panama imposes zero export proceeds conversion requirements, no currency repatriation timelines, and no exchange control filings. Exporters may hold foreign currency in any account, domestic or offshore, without restriction.

Central bank supervision: None. Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system without a central bank; the US Dollar is legal tender and the Balboa circulates exclusively as paper currency at a 1:1 peg. The banking regulator is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium for major international EMIs (e.g., Payoneer, Wise) only if the account is used for revenue collection and existing Panamanian bank references are available. Legal constraints: Foreign digital EMIs generally require corporate legalization (Apostille) and proof of beneficial ownership; many EMIs restrict high-risk sectors (news aggregators) or require a minimum operating history. Regulatory constraint: Panama's banking law (Decreto Ley 2 de 2008) applies to domestically licensed entities; offshore EMIs without an SBP license cannot offer local payment services, but cross-border electronic money services to Panamanian companies are not prohibited unless they involve deposit-taking.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocol: route the initial equity injection from the ultimate parent owner’s bank account via SWIFT MT103 to the Panama S.A.'s USD-denominated operational account at a local licensed bank. Required purpose code for capital contribution under the IMF Balance of Payments Manual 6: 'Direct Investment – Equity capital' (code 5101) or 'Other capital than reserves for corporations' (code 5102). The supporting verification bundle must include: (i) the Panama S.A.'s registered capital clause (no minimum), (ii) corporate resolution approving the contribution, (iii) the shareholder ledger evidencing issuance of shares, (iv) notarized and apostilled board resolution if a non-resident director is signing, and (v) a banking reference from the remitting bank stating the basis of remittance. The bank will apply a standard compliance review of 48-72 hours before crediting, and the same-day availability is typical after approval.Clearing rails for trade settlements are (a) standard SWIFT MT103 for commercial invoice payments and vendor disbursements, and (b) Commercial Letters of Credit (issued under UCP600) for high-value purchases of dedicated software, servers, and data partnerships. For recurring subscription revenue from global clients, the primary rail is the Stripe payment gateway settlement to the bank account; ancillary rails include local Automated Clearing House (ACH) participation through commercial banks in Panama and direct debit cards (Visa/Mastercard) from US-based processors. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Distributions from profits already subjected to Panamanian ISR: 10% dividend withholding. Distributions from profits that escaped ISR, including territorially exempt foreign-source revenue, attract a 15% dividend withholding rate. The S.A. must withhold upon dividend declaration and remit with the next monthly return.

Due on or before the last business day of the third month following fiscal year-end; 31 March for calendar-year companies. The balance of tax due is paid with the return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB 250,000 in aggregate annual related-party transactions triggers mandatory filing of the sworn Anexo de Operaciones con Partes Relacionadas with the annual return; PAB 2,000,000 in aggregate related-party transactions creates an expectation of a full OECD-consistent Local File, Master File, and benchmarking study, particularly for intra-group data licensing and API procurement.

Economic substance scope: Panama's economic substance framework, in force from fiscal year 2021, requires juridical persons carrying on relevant activities — including financing/leasing, headquarters, and distribution or service-centre functions — to evidence real presence when claiming territorial exemptions. A financial newswire operating an API syndication feed to non-residents is a mobile foreign-source-type activity: the S.A. must file the annual economic substance report and demonstrate (i) physical premises in Panama, (ii) qualified full-time employees, (iii) local management and direction, and (iv) operating expenditure proportionate to revenue. Failure converts the territorial exemption into an assessed commercial-activity tax base and triggers fines.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Defraudación Tributaria

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud: fines of 100%–300% of the evaded tax and imprisonment of up to 5 years in aggravated cases; civil reassessment with a 30% uplift on the omitted base and 1% monthly interest; loss of the statute-of-limitations defense; embargo of bank accounts and denial of Paz y Salvo.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, and nominee directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the assessed tax, surcharges, and fines; courts may issue travel bans, prevent corporate dissolution, and order personal asset seizure to secure restitution.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Defect

Statutory sanction: Economic substance penalties of up to PAB 10,000 per fiscal period; loss of territorial exclusions; retroactive assessment of gross foreign-source revenue at the full 25% corporate rate; suspension of the RUC (tax ID) and judicial dissolution of the entity by the DGI.

Director personal liability: Directors authorizing the interposition of a shell may be civilly liable for clawed-back taxes and barred from incorporating new entities; criminal exposure arises when the shell is used for laundering or fraud.
AML / Cross-Border Payment Abuse

Statutory sanction: Violations of Law 23 of 2015 (AML/CFT): administrative fines of up to USD 1,000,000 per breach imposed by the UAF; asset freezes; provisional closure of the RUC and bank accounts; predicate money-laundering offenses carry imprisonment of 5–10 years under the Criminal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors and designated compliance officers face imprisonment of up to 10 years, confiscation of assets, travel bans, and civil restitution claims; nominee directors cannot invoke ignorance where AML controls were absent.
Withholding Agent Failure

Statutory sanction: Withholding-agent liability for 100% of the unremitted amount plus surcharges of up to 150% and monthly interest; DGI refusal to issue tax clearance for the S.A. and its directors; embargo of deposits and revocation of electronic filing privileges.

Director personal liability: Where the S.A. is insolvent, the DGI pierces the corporate veil and pursues controlling shareholders and signing directors personally for the withheld sums as de facto responsible parties; criminal conviction may add imprisonment terms.
