Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) — Panama's national tax administration. The statutory e-filing and taxpayer portal is the 'e-Tributación (SITIO)' system, accessible via https://sitio.dgi.gob.pa, hosting RUC registration, ISR/ITBMS returns, electronic-invoice validation, CUF code allocation, and exporter dashboards.

E-government filing portal

To procure the statutory tax ID (RUC — Registro Único de Contribuyente) for a foreign-owned Panama S.A.: (1) incorporate the S.A. by public deed before a Panamanian notary and record it in the Registro Público to obtain the legal-existence certificate; (2) appoint the resident agent (agente residente), directors, and registered office, and open the commercial operation notice ('aviso de operaciones') at the local Alcaldía; (3) file the DGI RUC application (Form 1747/1 through e-Tributación) attaching the public deed, bylaws, municipal operation notice, premises proof, and identity documents of officers, signed by the resident agent; (4) upon DGI verification (approximately 5-10 business days), receive the 10-digit RUC together with the ITBMS general-taxpayer registration and the corporate income tax obligation set; (5) obtain an advanced electronic signature certificate through the AIG (Autoridad Nacional para la Innovación Gubernamental) for digital filings; and (6) register as an exporter with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA), obtaining the exporter customs code required for zero-rated shipments of dialysis hemofilters.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — Panama S.A.s are taxable as corporations on net income derived from Panamanian sources only (Código Fiscal Arts. 694 and 699); foreign-source profits are excluded from the territorial tax base, subject to the substance, CFC, and preferential-regime constraints of Law 254 of 2020. No pass-through or transparent status exists for shareholders, and the entity files annual ISR returns at the 25% corporate rate regime. — To procure the statutory tax ID (RUC — Registro Único de Contribuyente) for a foreign-owned Panama S.A.: (1) incorporate the S.A. by public deed before a Panamanian notary and record it in the Registro Público to obtain the legal-existence certificate; (2) appoint the resident agent (agente residente), directors, and registered office, and open the commercial operation notice ('aviso de operaciones') at the local Alcaldía; (3) file the DGI RUC application (Form 1747/1 through e-Tributación) attaching the public deed, bylaws, municipal operation notice, premises proof, and identity documents of officers, signed by the resident agent; (4) upon DGI verification (approximately 5-10 business days), receive the 10-digit RUC together with the ITBMS general-taxpayer registration and the corporate income tax obligation set; (5) obtain an advanced electronic signature certificate through the AIG (Autoridad Nacional para la Innovación Gubernamental) for digital filings; and (6) register as an exporter with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA), obtaining the exporter customs code required for zero-rated shipments of dialysis hemofilters.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — Panama S.A.s are taxable as corporations on net income derived from Panamanian sources only (Código Fiscal Arts. 694 and 699); foreign-source profits are excluded from the territorial tax base, subject to the substance, CFC, and preferential-regime constraints of Law 254 of 2020. No pass-through or transparent status exists for shareholders, and the entity files annual ISR returns at the 25% corporate rate regime.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election is available under Panamanian law. The relevant statutory elections/regimes for a Panamanian manufacturing exporter are: (1) the SEDO multinational-headquarters license election under Law 41 of 2007, which can be elected when licensing a regional headquarters and grants 10-year tax-stability treatment on qualifying services; (2) the election to be treated as a small taxpayer under the simplified ITBMS regime when annual gross receipts do not exceed approximately B/. 250,000 — generally unsuitable for an exporting manufacturer claiming input-VAT refunds; (3) the consolidated/group corporate income tax filing election available to a controlling Panamanian parent for its subsidiaries, subject to DGI prior authorization; and (4) free-zone license elections (Colón Free Zone or Panamá Pacífico) that modify ITBMS and income-tax treatment. Hybrid-entity mismatches are constrained by Law 254 of 2020 (BEPS-aligned), which imposes CFC, royalty, and substance rules; no preferential hybrid-mismatch election may be made domestically.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains a fully dollarized, open-currency system with no exchange-control bureaucracy. Current-account transactions, capital-account movements, profit remittances, dividend distributions, and shareholder capital repatriation are fully convertible and freely transferable in USD. Non-resident owners face no FX approval, round-tripping restriction, or prior-import license for dividend outflows. The only treasury-side constraints are fiscal: corporate income tax is territorial, dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are subject to Panamanian dividend withholding (generally 5% for payers determining taxable income by the revenue-minus-cost method and 10% under the general method), and ongoing final-beneficial-owner reporting is required under the unified Regime of Final Beneficial Owners administered by the DGI (Dirección General de Ingresos). No convertibility hurdle exists for a dialysis-consumables manufacturer collecting export sales in USD.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no minimum forced conversion ratio, and no central-bank repatriation duty on export proceeds. Export invoices for hemodialyzer and hollow-fiber dialyzer manufacturing may remain freely in USD in the exporter's Panamanian operational account, be applied to intercompany repayments, or be re-dispatched overseas without authorization. Banks nevertheless perform standard transaction-level AML screening on export documentation, which typically triggers a request for the commercial invoice, transport document, customs export filing (e-Sigex), and underlying purchase order.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the national banking supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as fiscal agent of the State and settlement bank for public-sector funds; the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) sets fiscal policy. Panama has no issuing central bank and no dedicated foreign-exchange administration. The US dollar is unlimited legal tender under Panamanian monetary law; the Balboa is a nominal unit of account pegged at exactly 1 Balboa = 1 USD and circulates only as fractional coinage.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for unlicensed offshore digital EMIs and MEDIUM only under the SBP innovation-hub and sandbox pilots. Panama does not currently maintain a standalone electronic-money institution license; institutions engaging in electronic-money issuance and payment services require a banking or payment-institution-type authorization reviewed by the SBP. Offshore EMIs such as Wise or Payoneer are not licensed to maintain local Panamanian corporate accounts, cannot access the onshore clearing system, and generally decline Panama S.A. clients due to residual beneficial-ownership disclosure and bearer-share-history concerns. They may be used tactically as supplementary inbound collection rails for low-value intercompany receipts, but all core treasury, LC issuance, and supplier settlement should be executed through at least one fully licensed Tier-1 Panamanian bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder equity injections must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 or MT202 with the beneficiary being the Panamanian S.A.'s USD account; the remittance-information field should state 'CAPITAL INCREASE' or 'CAPITAL APORTE' and reference the authenticated board resolution approving the capital increase. The Panamanian bank's New York correspondent clears USD via Fedwire or CHIPS, followed by same-day or next-day value crediting through the local interbank clearing system. On the compliance side the bank will require the escrow deposit confirmation, the updated share ledger, the notarized board resolution, and the DGI final-beneficial-owner filing before releasing funds for operating use. No Panamanian government authorization is required for the inbound injection beyond standard KYC/AML verification.Two principal clearing rails govern trade settlements: (1) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for direct supplier payments and distributor settlements, with the remittance field identifying the underlying invoice and contract; and (2) commercial letters of credit, irrevocable and UCP600-compliant, issued by Banco General, Banistmo, or BAC International Bank for raw-material imports such as polyethersulfone resin and dialyzer components. Documentary collections (D/P or D/A) instructed by SWIFT MT400/MT412 are permissible for regional distributor sales in Latin America and the Caribbean. High-value outbound USD payments clear through the Panamanian bank's New York correspondent on Fedwire/CHIPS; all outward payments are unrestricted but are subject to SWIFT-originator and beneficiary name screening, sanctions checks, and transaction monitoring.
