Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá — Dirección de Registro de Sociedades Mercantiles (Mercantile Section), Panama City.

Governing statute

Ley 32 de 1927 (General Corporation Law of Panama, 'Ley General de Sociedades Anónimas'), as amended by Ley 5 de 2001; applicable sections: Arts. 1-4 (incorporation), Art. 6 (authorized capital), Art. 23 (registered office), Art. 24 (resident agent), Arts. 28-30 (directors and officers); supplementary provisions of the Código de Comercio.

Formation / constitutional filings

Pacto Social / Escritura Constitutiva de Sociedad Anónima (Articles of Incorporation), executed as a private deed and presented for mercantile inscription.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 26 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Ley 32 de 1927; customary authorized capital is USD 10,000 divided into 10,000 shares of USD 1.00 par value, fully subscribed but not required to be paid in. No escrow account or capital deposit obligation; a subscribed-share declaration in the shareholders ledger suffices.

Funding & unlock path: Not applicable without physical premises. If a Panama office or warehouse is opened, the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá issues the 'Visto Bueno de Bomberos' (fire-safety clearance); the municipal engineering department performs structural load inspection; and the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL) requires employer registration and occupational accident insurance coverage (RIESA) under the applicable occupational safety regulations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Ley 32 de 1927; customary authorized capital is USD 10,000 divided into 10,000 shares of USD 1.00 par value, fully subscribed but not required to be paid in. No escrow account or capital deposit obligation; a subscribed-share declaration in the shareholders ledger suffices.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; e-services portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa). Export and customs evidence is handled by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá (ANA) through the Sistema de Gestión Aduanera (SGA) and the Ventanilla Única de Comercio Exterior (VUCEA).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) before commencing taxable operations. The standard procedure is: (1) appoint a Panamanian registered agent and maintain a licensed law-firm address; (2) obtain a Panamanian digital signature (Firma Digital) issued by the Autoridad Nacional para la Innovación Gubernamental (AIG); (3) file the Aviso de Operación and RUC application with the DGI through Sede Electrónica; (4) obtain the municipal business license in the relevant district if a local establishment is maintained; (5) register with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS) if local employees are hired; and (6) open a Panamanian bank account for tax settlement. For a pure cross-border Amazon FBA exporter with no local retail establishment, the RUC is nevertheless used for territorial income-tax filing, ITBMS compliance, and any export-refund claim.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (entity-level taxation applies only to Panama-source income; the S.A. is not a pass-through entity and is not classified as a Tax_Exempt_Zone entity under the general corporate regime)

Available election forms

None. Panama has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity classification election. The S.A. is automatically a separate taxable legal person. Qualifying small taxpayers may elect the simplified MIPYME tax regime under applicable Panamanian law; special elective regimes exist for SEM/Multinational Headquarters but do not apply to general Amazon FBA operations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover threshold. Registration is mandatory before making taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama or before supplying digital services to Panamanian recipients. A non-resident digital service provider must register and charge 7% ITBMS on applicable digital sales. An exporter whose Amazon FBA sales are exclusively to foreign end customers may have no local ITBMS collection obligation, but RUC registration is still required for income-tax territorial determinations and to substantiate zero-rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods are subject to 0% ITBMS where the goods are physically removed from Panamanian territory, title transfers to a non-resident buyer, and the export is evidenced by customs and transport documents. Offshore services supplied for use/consumption abroad, including e-commerce platform services and fulfillment services performed outside Panama, are also zero-rated if not economically connected to Panama. Amazon FBA cross-border sales will qualify only where the final customer is outside Panama and the goods are not sold or delivered within Panamanian territory. The exporter must maintain a documented file demonstrating the foreign destination and actual receipt of funds from the marketplace.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) accepted by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or courier receipt showing shipment from Panama to a foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of the Amazon FBA settlement from outside Panama
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matching the customs declaration
  • Amazon central seller statement or settlement report identifying the country of the end customer
  • Customs broker invoice and ANA export-register entry

Panama mandates electronic invoicing for taxpayers registered with the DGI, through DGI-authorized electronic invoicing operators or the DGI Factura Electrónica channel. The electronic invoice must comply with the DGI XML format, contain the taxpayer's RUC, and include an approved invoice number/authorization. For exports, the invoice must identify the zero-rated nature of the transaction and be supported by customs export records. Large taxpayers and digital-service providers are subject to periodic electronic filing and DGI data-matching; while not every transaction is cleared in real time, DGI may require transmission and validation of invoices before or shortly after issuance for specified taxpayer segments.

The DGI may deny zero-rating, reclassify the sale as taxable, and assess 7% ITBMS plus surcharges and interest if the export documentation is incomplete or inconsistent. Penalties range up to 100% of the omitted tax, with additional fines and criminal liability for fraudulent export declarations or forged customs documents. The statute of limitations is generally five years, but the period is extended in cases of fraud. If funds are retained abroad beyond 180 days without credible banking evidence and the territorial-source test is not satisfied, the DGI may treat the receipts as Panama-source and assess income tax, corporate taxes, and penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de facto dollarization. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a nominal unit pegged 1:1 to the United States dollar, and U.S. dollar bills and coins circulate as legal tender. There are no exchange-control laws, no conversion permits, and both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible and transferable. Cross-border profit remittance requires no FX authorization; however, dividends paid from Panamanian-source income are subject to a 5% or 10% withholding tax depending on the corporate regime and distributed-reserve status. Foreign-source income of a Panamanian corporation remains outside Panama's territorial income tax base, so Amazon marketplace income sourced abroad is generally not taxed by Panama, but banking source-of-funds evidence is still required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion requirement on export receipts or marketplace sales proceeds. The Amazon FBA seller may freely maintain foreign-currency balances abroad or clear them into a licensed Panamanian USD account without reporting to the SBP; only AML/CFT and tax-information duties apply.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as the apex banking, foreign-exchange compliance, and AML/CFT supervisor; the Republic of Panama does not operate a monetary-issuing central bank. Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) performs only limited fiscal-agency functions for the State.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium. Panama does not recognize a dedicated 'electronic money institution' charter, though Law 1 of 2024 created a fintech and virtual-asset licensing regime under the SBP. Digital-only offshore EMIs such as Airwallex, Payoneer, and Stripe cannot issue local PAN-denominated IBANs; they can, however, settle funds into a Panamanian USD account via U.S. correspondent banks. For treasury purposes, a licensed SBP-supervised bank remains the mandatory primary clearing account, and any prolonged use of an unlicensed EMI as the sole collection account will trigger elevated AML scrutiny from Panamanian banks.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta, ISR) for FY2019 onward, replacing the former 12.5% band on the first USD 1.5 million of taxable income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends paid from profits that were subject to the Panamanian corporate income tax; 20% final withholding tax on dividends distributed from profits that were not subject to ISR (e.g., foreign-source FBA profits or tax-exempt profits) unless the corporation has paid the complementary 10% corporate tax to 'thicken' the profits; both rates apply irrespective of the shareholder's residence. No Panama currency controls restrict outgoing USD dividends.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after the close of the fiscal year; calendar-year S.A. must file and pay on or before March 31 of the following fiscal year. A non-calendar fiscal year-end (approved by the DGI) shifts the deadline to the third month after that fiscal year-end.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filed simultaneously with Formulario 105, i.e., within three months after fiscal year-end (31 March for calendar-year taxpayers). The underlying contemporaneous TP documentation must be prepared no later than the return due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Reporting threshold in Panamanian balboas (B/. pegged 1:1 with USD): a contemporaneous TP study and Form 1221/1222 are required when (i) annual gross income exceeds B/. 15,000,000, or (ii) the aggregate value of transactions with related parties in the fiscal year exceeds B/. 5,000,000; a Master File/CbC report is required for multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue of EUR/USD 750,000,000 or more. Below these thresholds, the taxpayer must still be able to demonstrate arm's length pricing on request.

Economic substance scope: Law 254 of 2021 (Panama Economic Substance Regulations) requires locally registered entities carrying out 'relevant activities' to maintain substantive presence in Panama. An Amazon FBA S.A. managing a distribution/service-centre function, or holding IP and receiving related income, is a relevant entity. There is no specified economic substance requirement for a purely passive non-Panamanian FBA flow, but the S.A. that claims 100% foreign-source income must prove that the trading, procurement, fulfillment, and contracting functions are executed abroad; a shell S.A. with nominee directors, no local employees, no records and no physical office will be treated as conducting its business in Panama, making the profit taxable at 25% and exposing the entity to ESR penalties for failure to file the substance return with the DGI.
