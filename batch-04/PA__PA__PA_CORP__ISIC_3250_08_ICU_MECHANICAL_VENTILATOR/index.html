Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP), created by Law 366 of 2023, incorporating the former Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI); official taxpayer portal: Sede Electrónica de ANIP (https://sede.anip.gob.pa) and corporate website https://www.anip.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

Foreign entity must (1) execute a public deed before a Panamanian Notary appointing a resident legal representative and register the branch or foreign company with the Registro Público de Panamá; (2) hire a licensed and resident agente residente if a Sociedad Anónima; (3) file the Aviso de Operación / Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) application via the ANIP Sede Electrónica using Formulario 708, attaching the certified corporate documents, Public Registry existence certificate, domicile proof, and representative's power of attorney; (4) obtain the RUC with a single taxpayer number used for both income tax and ITBMS, and (5) activate the fiscal address and digital certificate (Firma Electrónica/FIE) for e-invoicing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama applies no U.S.-style check-the-box system; available statutory elections include: (i) election to be treated as an International Company (Empresa Internacional) under Law 41 of 2007 for strictly foreign-source activities; (ii) election under the Panama-Pacifico Special Economic Area or International Financial Center regimes for exempt foreign-source operations; (iii) election for consolidated group tax filing subject to ANIP approval for corporate groups with 100% direct or indirect control; (iv) election under the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime for qualified service exporters; and (v) optional simplified ITBMS small-taxpayer regime for annual gross turnover not exceeding the statutory limit.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No quantitative turnover threshold; any person habitually engaged in the sale of movable goods or provision of services in Panama must obtain a RUC and register for ITBMS before commencing operations. The simplified Pequeño Contribuyente ITBMS regime (optional) is available to taxpayers with annual gross income up to B/. 30,000 (US$ 30,000), while mandatory full ITBMS filing applies to all other registrants.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods are zero-rated for ITBMS purposes when the goods are physically removed from Panamanian territory under a valid export customs declaration, and the exporter may credit or claim refund of prior-stage input ITBMS. Offshore services supplied to non-residents and consumed entirely abroad also qualify as zero-rated service exports if invoiced to a non-resident beneficiary and supported by evidence of effective use outside Panama. Sales in the Colon Free Zone, Panama-Pacifico, and duty-free export processing zones are likewise treated as zero-rated export transactions.

  • Declaración de Exportación (electronic export customs declaration processed through the SISCOA/VUPA customs system and accepted by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill issued to a foreign consignee evidencing physical departure of the ventilators from Panama
  • Factura Comercial de Exportación (the electronic fiscal invoice carrying the ANIP validation code)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign bank credit advice demonstrating the export proceeds were received into a Panamanian or U.S. banking account

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) is phased-in under ANIP Resolution No. 201-001 of 2023 and related technical rules effective from 2022 to 2026, covering large taxpayers first and then all RUC holders. All electronic invoices must be transmitted to ANIP-authorised digital platforms using the Firma Electrónica, must obtain a digital fiscal seal ('Sello de Validación Fiscal') with a unique verification code, QR code and crypto-graphic hash, and must be cleared in real time or near-real time (maximum 24-hour clearance for non-MSME taxpayers); invoices issued outside the approved platform or lacking the ANIP validation are not deductible for purchaser ITBMS credit purposes.

ANIP applies a comprehensive risk-based audit module (Modelo de Gestión de Riesgos) to all zero-rating claims; any discrepancy between the customs export declaration, shipping documents, e-invoice and banking records triggers reassessment at 7% ITBMS plus monthly interest of 1% and a surcharge up to 50% of the omitted tax. Deliberate falsification of export documents or fraudulent collection of refunds constitutes tax fraud under Articles 752 to 757 of the Código Fiscal de Panama, carrying fines of 100% to 300% of the evaded amount, imprisonment of 2 to 10 years, disqualification of the legal representative, cancellation of the RUC and operating licence, and retrospective clawback of all zero-rated benefits and input tax credits claimed for the falsified transactions.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully dollarized economy: both capital-account and current-account convertibility are unrestricted; there are no exchange-control regulations, no licensing of foreign-exchange transactions, no foreign-investment registration, and no prior authorization for profit repatriation. Dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders requires only payment of the 5% dividend withholding tax under Panama's territorial tax regime and routine filing of the income-tax return (Form 202) with the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI). The practical hurdles are documentary, not FX: banks require the company to remain 'al día' with DGI, the Municipality (real estate and aviso taxes) and the Social Security Fund (CSS), plus an audited balance sheet where shareholder loans or retained earnings are distributed. Once cleared, remittance executes in USD within one banking day with no convertibility cap.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None — Panama imposes no statutory surrender deadline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters of medical devices (ISIC 3250) may retain 100% of USD export receipts in local or foreign currency accounts, use them directly for imports or corporate disbursements, and convert freely to any currency. The only conditions are statutory AML record-keeping under Ley 23 de 2015 (transaction files retained five years) and the SBP's reporting thresholds: aggregated cash transactions at or above USD 10,000 require a Currency Transaction Report, and export documentary credits are supported by the DGI fiscal clearance (Paz y Salvo), the corporate R.U.C., and the export DUA lodged through the National Single Window (VUCE).

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the sole sovereign banking regulator — together with the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) and Banco Nacional de Panamá (the State's fiscal agent and controlling operator of local dollar clearing). Panama has no issuing central bank and no domestic currency authority; the U.S. dollar is legal tender and the balboa (PAB) exists strictly as a unit of account at a fixed statutory 1:1 parity.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Legally constrained. Decreto Ley 9 de 1998 reserves deposit-taking and payment-account operation to banks licensed by the SBP; a foreign or offshore electronic-money institution (EMI) cannot solicit deposits, issue payment accounts, or clear USD transactions to Panamanian beneficiaries without either being incorporated and licensed in Panama as a bank or contracting through a locally licensed sponsor bank. The Panama Fintech Hub (Ley 166 de 2020 and SBP Acuerdo 08-2021) permits virtual-asset service providers to obtain a VASP license, but this does not create EMI pass-through powers for treasury settlement of government LCs. Net feasibility: LOW as a primary liquidity rail; MEDIUM only as a supplementary treasury dashboard using a local sponsor bank's third-party custody account so that each MT103 settles onshore.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% Impuesto Sobre la Renta (ISR) for juridical persons under the territorial income tax system (Law 8 of 2010, Cabinet Decree 55 of 2010).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% dividend tax (impuesto a los dividendos) is withheld by the distributing Panamanian corporation when the dividend is paid out of net profits already subject to ISR. A 20% dividend tax applies to distributions made from profits that escaped ISR, e.g., non-Panama-source receipts or exempt-income reserves.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after the fiscal year-end. For a calendar-year corporation, the return is due no later than March 31. Advance ISR installments are filed monthly using Formulario DGI-104 no later than the 10th business day of the following month.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File (Informe de Precios de Transferencia) is mandatory when gross revenue exceeds B/.5,000,000 (USD5,000,000) or when the aggregate related-party transactions exceed B/.1,500,000 (USD1,500,000). Master File is required by a multination group with consolidated revenue exceeding B/.50,000,000 (USD50,000,000). Country-by-Country Report is required when the ultimate parent group revenue exceeds USD750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Panama’s Economic Substance Rules (Law 254 of 2019, Executive Decree 246 of 2020) impose on a resident legal entity performing a “relevant activity” the requirement to demonstrate that (i) the entity is managed and directed in Panama, (ii) it has adequate physical premises, (iii) it has suitable employees qualified to perform the core income-generating activity, and (iv) sufficient operating expenditure is incurred in Panama. For a medical device manufacturer, the activity is usually outside the list of relevant activities unless it also operates a distribution/service center or holds intangible property generating income. If the entity holds ventilator-related US patents and feeds royalties back into Panama, the “intellectual property activity” category applies and full ESR compliance is mandatory; failure to pass the economic substance test invalidates the exemption.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Omission of Panama-Source Income

Statutory sanction: Panama’s Penal Code Article 433 imposes imprisonment of three to five years and a fine up to ten times the evaded tax. The DGIT may also apply a 10% surcharge and interest at 8% per annum, plus administrative penalty of 0.5% of the affected sum per month.

Director personal liability: A director who signs a false return is jointly and severally liable for the ISR, interest, and penalties; courts may issue a travel ban, a precautionary seizure of personal assets, and a prohibition on leaving the national territory during the criminal investigation.
Shell Company Abuse / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Law 254 of 2019, the DGIT and the Superintendency of Non-Financial Entities may impose fines of USD10,000 to USD250,000; the entity may lose its license as a corporate vehicle and its registration can be struck from the Public Registry.

Director personal liability: Directors personally owe the unpaid tax when they authorize payments to a shell with knowledge of its lack of substance; they may be subject to the same criminal prosecution for tax evasion plus a separate civil restitution claim brought by the tax authority.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: DGIT may adjust the taxable income upward, disallow the deduction, and apply a 20% penalty on the underpaid tax, plus daily interest; repeat offenders receive blacklisting from the “tax good standing” registry, effectively blocking public tenders.

Director personal liability: A TP adjustment executor who signs the invoice with a related-party entity in the absence of a formal TP study is liable for the unpaid withholding tax; further, if the adjustment exceeds B/.500,000, criminal prosecution for attempted tax evasion follows, with imprisonment from two to four years.
AML / Financing of Proliferation of Medical Hardware

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015, fines range from USD50,000 to USD5,000,000 per violation, and the Government can suspend the manufacturing license; if the transaction includes false invoices, Penal Code Article 293 on false documentation adds a two-to-six-year prison term.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly structure the transaction to conceal the true end user may be charged as principals in the criminal organization; the court may issue restrictive travel bans, freeze passport renewal, and order personal confiscation of proceeds derived from the transaction, in addition to a term of imprisonment of three to seven years.
