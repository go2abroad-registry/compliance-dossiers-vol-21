Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; taxpayer portal: Sistema Integrado de Tributación (SIT) at https://sit.tributacion.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A Panama Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) must first be incorporated before a licensed Panamanian attorney and obtain the 'Aviso de Operación' (business operation notice) from the Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias (MICI). Thereafter the foreign-owned entity must register with the DGI through the SIT portal to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes), appoint a resident agent and fiscal representative, provide notarized constitutional documents and proof of registered address in Panama, and, if engaged in taxable activities, complete the ITBMS (VAT) registration module. The resident agent must stamp and certify all corporate books; the RUC is the single statutory tax identification number required for invoicing, customs, and banking compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope - Panama applies entity-level taxation only to Panama-source income; foreign-source income of the PE Buyout SPV is outside the Panamanian gross income base, while Panama-source revenue is subject to the 25% corporate income tax. Panama does not treat an S.A. as a pass-through entity for national tax purposes.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists under Panamanian law. The S.A. may elect to channel all operations toward foreign-source activities (thereby remaining outside Panama's territorial corporate tax base), may elect the Régimen Simplificado para Pequeños Contribuyentes for ITBMS purposes when annual gross receipts fall below the statutory threshold, and may be classified as a Gran Contribuyente by the DGI for centralized compliance. Panama has not adopted comprehensive hybrid mismatch anti-abuse elections, but the taxpayer may administratively elect the treatment of permanent establishments and transfer pricing documentation based on the territorial scope.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No turnover-based exemption for commercial S.A. entities; ITBMS registration is mandatory upon commencement of any taxable activity in Panama. A simplified small-taxpayer quota regime applies to qualifying natural persons and low-reach economic operators with annual gross income at or below USD 36,000 (Balboa-equivalent), subject to DGI administrative classification.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A registered ITBMS taxpayer may apply the 0% export rate when: (i) tangible goods physically leave Panamanian territory within ninety (90) days from the date of invoice; (ii) services are wholly consumed abroad by a non-resident recipient; (iii) the transaction involves international freight, fuel, or directly related air/sea transport services; (iv) goods are introduced to or removed from the Colon Free Zone, Panama-Pacifico, or other licensed free-zone regimes. The zero rate applies only where the supplier holds a valid RUC-ITBMS registration, the exporter is named on the customs declaration, and documentary evidence of departure and foreign payment is retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA/SISCOMEX electronic declaration)
  • Commercial Invoice and zero-rated ITBMS invoice
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Panamanian bank or SWIFT MT103 advising receipt of foreign currency proceeds
  • Executed sales contract/purchase order identifying the non-resident buyer
  • Certificate of origin or free-zone entry documentation when applicable

Panamanian law mandates electronic invoicing through the DGI's Sistema de Facturación Electrónica embedded in the SIT portal. Certified billing software must integrate with DGI via REST/SOAP application programming interfaces; invoices and credit notes are transmitted to the tax authority in real-time or within the filing window prescribed by DGI resolution. Every issued document must contain a DGI-assigned electronic authorization code (Código de Autorización) and QR control; preprint authorization (CAI) applies to physical invoice pads. Exports and zero-rated operations require the electronic transmission of both invoice and supporting customs data to obtain fiscal clearance.

The DGI may retroactively review export zero-rating claims for six (6) tax years; absent complete documentation, the ITBMS is clawed back with monthly late-payment surcharges of up to 1.5% (capped at 100% of tax) and fines ranging from 5% to 10% of the omitted amount. Submission of forged customs documents or false payment certificates constitutes aggravated tax fraud under Ley 70 de 2019 and may result in imprisonment of four (4) to six (6) years, monetary fines, debarment from the SIT electronic system, and joint liability of the legal representative and resident agent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account with no foreign-exchange restrictions, multiple-currency practices, or surrender obligations. Capital inflows, outbound acquisitions, and profit repatriation may be freely effected in US dollars. The only practical remittance hurdle is the corporate tax-clearance certificate ('Certificado de Paz y Salvo') issued by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), which Panamanian banks routinely require to process large outbound dividends or distributions to non-resident investors.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no forced local-currency conversion timeline, and no mandated percentage of export receipts to be retained in Panama. Export receipts may be deposited directly into corporate USD-denominated operational accounts without central-bank reporting.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the independent banking regulator and de facto currency-control authority in a dollarized economy; Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as the fiscal agent and clearing bank of the Republic. Panama does not maintain a monetary-issuing central bank, as the United States dollar is statutory legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Panama does not currently recognise a standalone e-money institution (EMI) charter, and offshore digital EMI licences are not passportable into Panama. A non-resident EMI cannot access the Panamanian interbank clearing and settlement infrastructure, and Panamanian banks will generally not accept EMI-obtained funds without significant correspondent de-risking scrutiny. Use of offshore EMIs may be confined to ancillary vendor payments with strict supporting documentation; they cannot serve as a primary treasury clearing or liquidity rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and shareholder-loan injections must be executed via SWIFT MT103 across the US-dollar clearing backbone (Fedwire/CHIPS correspondent banks). The remittance narrative must precisely reflect the STP-sanitised purpose: e.g., 'SPV Capital Contribution — [fund name] under LPA dated [date]' or 'Shareholder Loan Drawdown under Facility Agreement [ref]'. The receiving Panamanian bank will conduct OFAC and internal AML screening before releasing credited funds; no central-bank exchange approval is required.
