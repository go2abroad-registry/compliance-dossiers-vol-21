Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas de Panamá; official portal: SITIO (Sistema Integrado de Información Tributaria) at https://sitio.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must appoint a resident legal representative and obtain an RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) by submitting incorporation documents, notarized appointment of representative, proof of registered address in Panama, and registering for the taxpayer registry at DGI. RUC issuance is prerequisite for ITBMS registration and customs operations. Processing is done online via SITIO or in-person at DGI offices.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Non-Tax-Exempt-Zone (standard S.A.) — Foreign entities must appoint a resident legal representative and obtain an RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) by submitting incorporation documents, notarized appointment of representative, proof of registered address in Panama, and registering for the taxpayer registry at DGI. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Non-Tax-Exempt-Zone (standard S.A.)

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a check-the-box regime. No elective pass-through treatment exists for Sociedad Anónima. Tax consolidation is not available for income tax. Foreign entities may elect to register under the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime only with separate authorization from the SEM Commission.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold for general registration in the ITBMS; any person or entity engaged in taxable economic activities within Panamanian territory must register. A simplified small-taxpayer regime exists for annual gross income of up to PAB/USD 30,000, subject to DGI approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services (including services rendered to non-residents and supplies for the operation of the Panama Canal, free trade zones, and Panama-Pacifico) are subject to 0% ITBMS. Eligibility requires the taxpayer to hold a valid RUC and an exporter certificate issued by the DGI, demonstrate that the goods or title to the goods physically left Panamanian territory (or, for services, that the recipient is a non-resident and the benefit is realized outside Panama), and maintain complete transactional records evidencing a direct causal link between the export sale and input costs. Zero-rating also applies to supplies to vessels in international traffic and commercial aircraft operations.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) duly validated by the National Customs Authority – full exit record
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export and destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank transfer proof from the foreign buyer demonstrating actual payment remittance

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the DIGIFACT system administered by the DGI. Authorized taxpayers must transmit electronic invoices (facturas electrónicas) and credit/debit notes in SPL (Sistema de Facturación Electrónica) format using a digital certificate issued by the General Directorate of Public Revenue. Eligible taxpayers pay a fee for the 'Sello Digital' (digital stamp). Real-time validation with DGI servers is required for high-risk taxpayers; phased roll-out applies by turnover and economic activity sector. Exporters must incorporate customs exit records in the electronic invoice flow.

Panama applies strict audit scrutiny to export zero-ratings. If the DGI determines that zero-rating was improperly claimed, it will claw back the ITBMS not paid, plus interest from the date the tax originally became due, and impose fines under Article 756 of the Tax Code (typically 100% of the omitted tax when fraud is found). Intentional submission of false customs or invoice documentation may trigger criminal tax fraud charges (Ley Penal Tributaria) with penalties of imprisonment up to 5 years and additional monetary sanctions. Exporter blacklisting and suspension of the exporter certificate may also apply.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction: the US dollar is legal tender and circulates freely, while the Balboa (PAB) exists only as sub-unit coinage pegged 1:1. There is total currency convertibility on both current-account and capital-account transactions; no prior exchange-control authorization, no capital-account licensing, and no profit-remittance approval are required. Dividends and branch profits may be remitted freely, subject only to Panama's territorial-source tax and the 5% dividend tax on active income; FATF increased-monitoring (grey-list) status raises EDD intensity but does not create FX conversion hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None exist. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation obligation for export receipts. Defense-tactical export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD or any foreign currency, subject only to AML/CFT books-and-records obligations under Law 23 of 2015 and SBP customer-due-diligence rules.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), established by Decree Law No. 2 of 2008, is the sole prudential banking regulator; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal and clearing agent of the State; no traditional central bank exists under Panama's dollarized monetary framework in force since 1904.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Law 123 of 2020 permits SBP-supervised fintech innovation licenses, but major offshore EMIs (UK/EU e-money institutions) universally exclude military-tactical hardware supply chains under acceptable-use policies. A domestic SBP-licensed digital-payment provider could support local collection only; the Government_Direct_Wire and Diplomatic_LC rails require a full-service SBP-licensed correspondent bank, so EMIs cannot anchor this treasury structure.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final dividend withholding tax on gross distributions to shareholders, regardless of residence. The Panama paying company is the withholding agent; distributions from profits already taxed in Panama may carry an imputation credit, eliminating residual double taxation in most domestic cases. There is no separate branch remittance tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/. 2,000,000 (2,000,000 Panamanian Balboas, equivalent to USD 2,000,000) of aggregate related-party transactions per fiscal year; transactions with affiliates resident in zero/low-tax jurisdictions and all high-risk defence-sector supply chains are given special scrutiny even below threshold.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 254 of 2021, implementing OECD/EU pressures) require a Panama tax-resident company carrying on a 'relevant activity' (including manufacturing, distribution, and financing) to demonstrate in Panama: (i) physical premises, (ii) qualified full-time employees, (iii) real expenditure proportionate to income, and (iv) senior management/board decisions in Panama. A PMC S.A. engaged in ISIC 3040 military-vehicle production must also hold the Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias (MICI) manufacturing licence and any arms/defence-export permit issued by the competent Panamanian security authority; absence of substance converts the S.A. into a reportable shell entity, risks cancellation of the licence, and triggers exchange-of-information sanctions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and False Tax Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under Panamanian Fiscal Code (in force with Law 69 of 2015 aggravation rules), evaded tax is assessed with a 100% penalty plus 1.5% monthly interest; criminal tax evasion carries a fine of 2 to 5 times the evaded amount and imprisonment of 2 to 5 years. The DGI may close the establishment and publish the taxpayer in the delinquent-taxpayer list.

Director personal liability: Directors, administrators, and accountants who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax debt. Judicial proceedings can freeze personal bank accounts, impose travel bans, and order extradition (under bilateral agreements) for directors abroad. Directors' personal visas and Panamanian residency may be revoked.
Shell Company and Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Non-compliance with Law 254 of 2021 substance regulations triggers written warning, a corrective period, and penalties up to 1% of retained economic activity revenues (minimum B/. 10,000). The Economic Substance Compliance Unit may impose fines of up to B/. 100,000 and strike the entity from the tax registry; DGI may treat the entity as 'tax resident of a no-substance jurisdiction' and deny treaty benefits.

Director personal liability: Directors of shell entities face administrative fines of B/. 10,000-100,000, disqualification from holding directorships in Panama, personal civil liability for corporate tax debts under reverse-veil-piercing theories, and criminal exposure for false filings if the DGI proves wilful misconduct.
Defence-Sector AML and Arms Embargo Violations

Statutory sanction: Law 23 of 2015 (Panama AML/CFT) authorizes fines up to B/. 1,000,000 for regulated entities; criminal money-laundering under Panamanian Criminal Code Article 277 carries 5 to 12 years' imprisonment per count plus confiscation of vehicles, bank accounts, and intermediate companies. The Ministry of Commerce can cancel the manufacturing/export licence, and the Office of the Comptroller will flag the company to FinCEN and OFAC networks.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally indictable for money laundering if they knew or wilfully ignored the illicit origin or destination of funds; travel bans and Interpol red notices apply to directors abroad; bank-account freezes and forfeiture orders extend to personal property traced to the offence.
Transfer Pricing and Withholding Non-compliance

Statutory sanction: DGI can make TP adjustments and impose a 40% surcharge on any adjustment; unpaid withholding taxes are collected with a 100%-200% penalty for failure to withhold. The tax lien attaches to all corporate assets and government receivables.

Director personal liability: Non-withholding is a personal statutory liability: directors and the legal representative are jointly liable for the unwithheld amount, plus interest. Serious repeat violations can lead to criminal charges for misappropriation of public tax funds and a personal travel ban until the tax debt is satisfied.
