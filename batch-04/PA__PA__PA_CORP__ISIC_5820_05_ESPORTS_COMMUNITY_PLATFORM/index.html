Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama. Official e-Tax electronic portal: https://etax.dgi.gob.pa (institutional site: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

A Panama S.A. must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC). Procedure: (1) register the public deed (Escritura Pública) with the Registro Público de Panamá; (2) file the Aviso de Operación with the DGI through the municipal/Ventanilla Única integration within 30 days of commencing operations; (3) submit corporate constitutional documents and the appointing resolution of directors and officers to the DGI, including the licensed resident agent's details; (4) the DGI issues the RUC and assigns a fiscal authorization number multiplier (rango de multiplicador) for bill issuance; (5) enrol in the mandatory Electronic Invoice System (Facturación Electrónica) before any taxable transaction; (6) open a Panamanian bank account for inward remittance of export revenues. Non-resident shareholders and directors must appoint a local resident agent, and full beneficial-ownership declarations are filed under DGI transparency requirements.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: The Sociedad Anónima is a separate taxable entity. Only Panama-source income is subject to Panamanian corporate income tax; foreign-source income (including offshore esports platform revenues) falls outside the tax base. Dividends paid from foreign-source profits to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Panamanian withholding tax.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections exist under Panamanian law. Available elections and regimes: (a) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Law 41/2007 for regional headquarters services; (b) optional simplified taxpayer regime for gross receipts ≤ USD 36,000 per annum; (c) registration as Exportador de Servicios to apply the 0% ITBMS export rate; (d) election to use the ITBMS pro-rata credit method for mixed taxable/export operations; (e) investment incentives and accelerated depreciation under Law 76/2019; (f) tax settlement agreements under the Jurisdiction of International Commerce. Panama has not implemented BEPS Action 2, so hybrid outcomes are resolved only through territorial sourcing rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold: any person habitually selling movable goods or rendering services in Panama must register for ITBMS from the first taxable act. The small-taxpayer simplified regime applies only to annual gross income ≤ USD 36,000 (optional). The esports platform, once it monetizes any Panama-domiciled player, sponsor, or tournament organizer, is mandatorily registered for ITBMS; service exports to non-residents remain zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% ITBMS rate applies to exported services when the recipient is domiciled outside Panama and the service benefit is deemed to occur wholly outside the Republic (including SaaS access, esports matchmaking, tournament management, and virtual goods for non-resident players). The S.A. must hold a DGI 'Exportador de Servicios' registration and file the annual export-of-services declaration. Revenues collected abroad through Stripe/PayPal are eligible if consumption tests are met and funds are repatriated to a Panamanian bank account. Panama-domiciled users' revenue is taxable at 7%, with entitlement to the input VAT credit on platform infrastructure costs apportioned via the pro-rata method. Exporters may claim periodic refunds of excess ITBMS credits through the DGI electronic refund module.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) and export permits — not required for pure digital service exports; the equivalent is the DGI Service Export Registration (Registro de Exportador de Servicios) and the annual export declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — not applicable to digital services; substituted by the DGI-validated electronic service agreement and server/transaction logs evidencing offshore matchmaking and tournament delivery
  • Service Agreement with non-resident clients stating place of performance and exclusive offshore use of the esports platform
  • Electronic Export Invoice (Factura Electrónica de Exportación) bearing the DGI online authorization and the 0% tax-rate code
  • Stripe and PayPal merchant settlement statements, charge back logs, and IP/origin data demonstrating non-resident end users
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa) issued by a licensed Panamanian bank evidencing the foreign-currency proceeds entered Panama
  • Certificate of Residence / non-domiciled status of the foreign tournament organizers, where applicable

Mandatory nationwide electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) is fully phased in for all taxpayers since 2023. Each invoice requires real-time fiscal clearance: the billing system transmits the invoice JSON/XML payload to the DGI e-Tax authorization service via certified API (MEF-DGI); the DGI returns a unique fiscal control number (multiplicador de comprobante) and verification digit; the invoice is digitally stamped with a QR code and fiscal signature before delivery to the customer. All e-invoices and export credit notes must be reported to the DGI within 24 hours of issuance. Third-party billing solutions (including those integrated with Stripe/PayPal settlement data) must be certified by the DGI as Authorized Electronic Billing Providers. Physical pre-printed receipts are prohibited for registered VAT taxpayers.

The DGI subjects service-export zero-rating claims to strict audit review. Where export substantiation fails or funds are not repatriated within 180 days, the export sale is reclassified as a domestic taxable service; the omitted ITBMS is clawed back with a 10% monthly surcharge on the unpaid tax plus interest, and fines under Articles 745/745-A of the Código Fiscal ranging from USD 100 to 200% of the omitted tax. Recurrent fraudulent zero-rating or simulated export transactions trigger the Law 76/2019 criminal tax fraud regime (2 to 5 years imprisonment), disgorgement of all claimed input credits, retroactive adjustment of tax returns, and extension of the statute of limitations to 10 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full capital-account and current-account convertibility: no exchange-control approvals, surrender quotas, repatriation timelines, or foreign-investment licensing apply. Profit remittances and shareholder loans may exit freely once Panamanian tax obligations are settled and the bank's source-of-funds and anti-money-laundering due-diligence checks are passed. Balboa/USD parity eliminates internal currency conversion friction, and both residents and non-residents may hold, transfer, and exchange funds without central-bank authorization.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, repatriation, or mandatory conversion of export proceeds exists for a Panama S.A. An esports platform's revenues from sponsorships, tournament tickets, digital goods, and prizing services may be retained in any currency in Panama or abroad. Standard Panama AML reporting thresholds to the Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) apply for cash and suspicious transactions, and banks require underlying commercial invoices or settlement reports for unusual wire volumes.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). The Republic of Panama does not operate a central bank; the U.S. dollar is official legal tender, and the Panamanian Balboa exists solely as a 1:1 coin subdivision. The SBP enforces the Banking Law (Decree-Law 9 of 1998), AML/CFT rules, and prudential supervision; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the state clearing and depository institution without monetary policy authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low as a regulated deposit holder; Medium as a payment collection gateway. Panama has no domestic EMI licensing category, and an offshore digital EMI cannot solicit or hold deposits in Panama without a banking license. Stripe and PayPal are acceptable only as merchant collection and settlement rails, with daily or periodic settlement flows redirected to an SBP-licensed local operational account. Using an unlicensed EMI as a treasury warehouse would likely constitute unlicensed financial intermediation under Decree-Law 9 of 1998 and Law 23 of 2015, exposing the entity to SBP enforcement and correspondent-bank debanking risk.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Non-resident shareholders and a zero-local-substance Panama S.A. receiving high-volume online payments from consumers across Latin America.

Root cause: Non-resident shareholders and a zero-local-substance Panama S.A. receiving high-volume online payments from consumers across Latin America.

Defense evidence: (i) Apostilled Articles of Incorporation and registered amendments from the Registro Público; (ii) current good-standing certificate and certified 'Aviso de Operación'; (iii) Panamanian RUC tax ID and tax registration; (iv) Beneficial Owner Declaration under Law 52 of 2016; (v) board resolution approving the account mandate and signatories; (vi) notarized passport copies and professional references of all signatories; (vii) audited or accountant-reviewed financial statements; (viii) proof of business substance: tournament platform URL, esports community agreements, sponsor contracts, prize-pool schedules, and P&L; (ix) source-of-funds evidence for all injected capital, including the share subscription and investor wiring records.
Misclassification of esports/competitive-gaming operations as regulated online gambling, triggering correspondent-bank scrutiny and denial of merchant payment rails.

Root cause: Misclassification of esports/competitive-gaming operations as regulated online gambling, triggering correspondent-bank scrutiny and denial of merchant payment rails.

Defense evidence: (i) Legal opinion from Panamanian counsel confirming the platform is ISIC 5820 software and game-publishing services, not casino or sports betting under Panama's gaming law; (ii) published game rules and tournament format demonstrating skill-based competition and removal of chance as the primary outcome; (iii) anti-fraud, anti-money-laundering, sanction-screening, and responsible-gaming policies; (iv) OFAC, EU, UN, and PEP screening logs for all counterparties; (v) no-loot-box or no-gambling terms stated in the platform's monetization policy; (vi) if wagering on esports is later offered, a separate Junta de Control de Juegos license application and audited player-protection procedures.
Rapid distribution of inbound settlement funds to overseas prize winners, tournament sponsors, and wallet addresses in multiple jurisdictions.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All capital injections must be routed via SWIFT MT103 to the Panama S.A.'s designated escrow or operational account at an SBP-licensed bank. Beneficiary and remitter names must match the registered entity and shareholder/investor legal name exactly as recorded in the Registro Público and the bank's KYC file. Panama uses no statutory FX purpose-code list; banks require in SWIFT Field 70 or 72 a clear commercial description such as 'capital contribution under share subscription,' 'shareholder loan,' or 'esports platform tournament advance.' Verification must include the board resolution, signed subscription or loan agreement, investor bank-source-of-funds statements, UAF/AML screening results, and confirmation of no sanctions exposure. Inbound wires flagged for unexplained wealth must be re-sent only after the bank has cleared the capital-circuit documentation.Outbound prize, sponsor, and supplier payments should be executed through SWIFT MT103 for final beneficiary transfer, or MT202 for intermediate correspondent-clearing instructions, always with full beneficiary bank identifiers, SWIFT/BIC, and IBAN or account number. Invoices and sponsorship agreements must be attached to the bank's compliance channel for each payout. For larger tournament prize disbursements or equipment purchases, commercial letters of credit under UCP 600 may be drawn through the same Panama clearing network. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat tax on net Panama-source taxable income (territorial regime; no municipal or provincial corporate income taxes)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid out of Panama-source taxable profits (Article 736, Código Fiscal); dividends sourced from non-Panama foreign income are exempt; a 4% advance dividend withholding applies to interim distributions where prior-year taxable reserves are distributed.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Defraudación Fiscal

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of up to 10 times the evaded tax and imprisonment of 2 to 5 years (Article 758-A, Código Fiscal); civil reassessment with a 10% surcharge and monthly 1% interest.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign fraudulent returns are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax, face criminal prosecution, and may be subject to travel bans and personal asset embargoes.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Annual fines up to USD 500,000 for ESR non-compliance and up to USD 100,000 for late or false ESR filings under Law 254 of 2021; deregistration and presumptive DGI assessments may follow.

Director personal liability: Resident directors risk personal fines and disqualification; knowingly false substance declarations expose directors to criminal liability and loss of limited-liability protection.
Withholding Agent Default

Statutory sanction: DGI assesses the unremitted withholding tax against the company plus monthly interest of 1% and a 10% surcharge; enforced collection via embargo of corporate bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Officers who knowingly fail to withhold are personally liable under Article 736 of the Código Fiscal; restitution orders and travel restrictions may be imposed.
AML / Money Laundering (Ley 23 de 2015)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines up to USD 1,000,000 per violation; criminal money-laundering penalties of 5 to 12 years' imprisonment and asset forfeiture under Article 254 of the Código Penal.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face personal criminal prosecution, asset freezes, travel bans, and extradition; the corporate veil is pierced for AML purposes.
