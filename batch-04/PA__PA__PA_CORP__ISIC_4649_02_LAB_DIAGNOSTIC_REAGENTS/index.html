Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); e-tax portal: Sede Electrónica (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

Non-resident foreign entities must appoint a Panamanian resident legal representative or resident agent, provide notarized corporate documents with apostille (or legalization) and certified Spanish translation, obtain a resident agent certificate, and submit the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application through the DGI Sede Electrónica or in person at the DGI branch. The process requires filing Form 021-RUC, presenting entity constitutive documents, and validating the local fiscal domicile. A digital signature token is also issued for e-invoicing and fiscal procedures. Entities without a permanent establishment may obtain an RUC for import/export compliance and invoicing obligations.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation_Territorial_Scope — Non-resident foreign entities must appoint a Panamanian resident legal representative or resident agent, provide notarized corporate documents with apostille (or legalization) and certified Spanish translation, obtain a resident agent certificate, and submit the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application through the DGI Sede Electrónica or in person at the DGI branch. The process requires filing Form 021-RUC, presenting entity constitutive documents, and validating the local fiscal domicile. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not offer a check-the-box entity classification election for private limited companies (Sociedad Anónima); all corporations are taxable entities under national law. There is no elective pass-through regime and no hybrid mismatch election. Foreign taxpayers may elect to be taxed on a remittance basis through withholding at source when no permanent establishment is maintained, while resident corporations are taxed territorially on Panamanian-source income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold exists; any person carrying out taxable supplies of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS. Small taxpayers with annual gross income not exceeding PAB 36,000 may benefit from the simplified monthly regime, but registration remains mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Wholesale exports of IVD diagnostic reagents and assays from Panama to non-resident buyers are eligible for the 0% ITBMS rate, provided the goods physically leave Panamanian territory, an export sale is documented through a purchase order and invoice, and the exporter maintains proof of departure. Services physically performed abroad or directly in connection with international transport of exported goods may also be zero-rated. Domestic sales of the same reagents within Panama are subject to 7% ITBMS.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Formulario de Exportación/DEX) validated by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure
  • Commercial Invoice with Incoterms and IVD device references
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or equivalent bank document confirming receipt of export proceeds

Panama requires phased mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) through the DGI Sede Electrónica. Invoices must be transmitted in real time before issuance and obtain fiscal authorization from the DGI; the system requires compliant XML data structure, digital signature certificate, and a fiscal code or electronic stamp. Large taxpayers are already in scope, with mandatory rollout extended to all registered taxpayers. The DGI performs automated validation and denial of non-compliant invoices. For import/export operations, the Panama Customs Single Window (VUPA) also electronically links goods declarations to the taxpayer RUC.

DGI audits export transactions using Panama Customs export records, bank remittance data, and beneficiary manifests. Where the 0% rate is claimed fraudulently or without actual physical export, the taxpayer is subject to immediate reassessment of the omitted 7% ITBMS, late payment surcharge (recargo), compensatory interest, and fines of up to 100% of the tax due. Willful false export declarations may constitute tax evasion under the Código Fiscal de Panamá and can trigger criminal prosecution, monetary penalties up to PAB 50,000, and the temporary suspension or revocation of the RUC and operating licenses.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains full and unrestricted currency convertibility across both current and capital accounts because the USD is the statutory circulating currency. There are no exchange-control laws, no central-bank authorization for foreign-exchange transactions, and no capital-account restrictions. Profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are unrestricted and Panama's territorial income-tax system imposes no withholding tax on outbound dividends. The only formalities are banking-level anti-money-laundering documentation under Law 23 of 2015, BPM6 balance-of-payments statistical classification of each wire by the SBP-licensed bank, and OECD Common Reporting Standard (CRS) reporting of account-holder tax residence. A non-resident-owned S.A. may hold accounts in USD without valuation, conversion, or repatriation hurdles.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no mandatory export-proceeds repatriation timeline, no percentage-based surrender quotient, and no statutory requirement to convert export receipts into local currency. A Panamanian seller may retain export proceeds in offshore USD accounts or convert them into any foreign currency freely. However, under Law 23/2015 and SBP AML guidelines, the S.A. must preserve underlying commercial documentation for each export transaction so that the account-holding bank or the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) can verify the legitimacy of the funds upon request.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama has no central bank of issue; monetary authority is effectively exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP) for banking supervision, while the U.S. Federal Reserve System supplies the circulating monetary base through full dollarization. The U.S. dollar (USD) is legal tender for all public and private obligations; fractional Balboa (PAB) coinage is minted by the Ministry of Economy and Finance at fixed 1:1 parity with the USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. Panama is not a member of the European economic-area e-money institution (EMI) passporting regime and maintains no equivalent principal EMI licence. Panamanian law recognizes payment-service companies under SBP payment-systems rules, but these entities may not hold unrestricted corporate operational funds as full commercial banks. Offshore digital EMIs licensed in the United Kingdom, Lithuania, or other EU/EEA states can be used only as auxiliary vendor-payment rails and are generally discouraged for a distributor of regulated IVD reagents because of correspondent-bank de-risking, the residual compliance legacy of the 2019-2023 FATF grey-listing, and the absence of a Panama-domiciled settlement account. The recommended treasury architecture is to use SWIFT MT103 clearing through a tier-1 local account and to restrict EMI usage to minor auxiliary expenses with an approved transaction cap.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan injections into the PA_CORP S.A. are executed by ordinary SWIFT MT103 credit transfers in USD from the contributor's account to the designated SBP-licensed bank. No exchange-control approval or central-bank clearance is required in Panama because of full dollarization; the receiving bank, however, requires (i) the statutory Declaración de Origen de Fondos (source-of-funds declaration), (ii) a board resolution or capital-increase minute if subscribed shares are being issued, (iii) the use of a standard BPM6 purpose description such as 'equity injection into non-resident direct-investment enterprise for working capital', and (iv) an active MINSA sanitary licence and municipal Aviso de Operación on the instruction date to avoid compliance seizure. Correspondent banks favour a single treasury bank in Panama with clean UBO records and a 30-day advance notice of the first high-value injection.Outbound settlement for ELISA reagent inventory is executed through (i) direct SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for open-account suppliers with 30-60 day payment terms, (ii) irrevocable commercial letters of credit issued via MT700 and amended via MT707 for large or first-time supplier contracts, preferably confirmed by the supplier's correspondent bank under UCP 600, (iii) documentary collections (DP/DA) for mid-value shipments, and (iv) SWIFT MT798 structured-message trade applications where the SBP-licensed bank operates a specialised trade desk. Payment rails remain strictly limited to Swift_Wire and TT_Payment per the treasury policy; cryptocurrency transfers, cash courier, and third-party payment portals are excluded. Controls require a single authorised signatory for disbursements below USD 100,000 and dual signatures plus compliance pre-screening of the beneficiary bank for any payment above that threshold.Plain-vanilla OTC deliverable forward contracts on USD/EUR and USD/CNY to hedge purchase flows from European and Chinese ELISA reagent manufacturers; no FX-control approval is required under Panama's dollarised regime, although ISDA or equivalent master documentation is mandated by SBP-regulated counterparties.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on regional Latin American currencies such as USD/COP, USD/BRL, and USD/MXN to protect re-export revenue collections from health-sector buyers in Colombia, Brazil, and Mexico.Cross-currency swaps for medium-term intercompany financing or supplier match-funding structures, executed under ISDA documentation with a licensed international bank and reported to the SBP within the bank's derivatives risk-management framework.OTC foreign-exchange option purchases (European-style, bought options only, no naked option sales) on EUR and CNY with tenures of 3 to 12 months, subject to a board-approved derivatives policy and suitability assessment by the account-holding bank.
