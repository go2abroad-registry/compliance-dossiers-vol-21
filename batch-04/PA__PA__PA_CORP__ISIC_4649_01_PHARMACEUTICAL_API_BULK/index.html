Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; taxpayer portal: DGI electronic services portal (e-Tax and electronic invoicing systems at dgi.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

For a Panama Sociedad Anónima: after incorporation at the Public Registry, the company or its resident legal representative must apply to DGI for an RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) using Form RUC-1, accompanied by public registry certificate, articles of incorporation, registered agent confirmation, fiscal address proof, required government fees, and, for non-resident persons acting on behalf of the entity, notarized power of attorney or 2025 electronic credential authorisation. If the company is a non-resident person/entity deriving Panama-source income, it must also obtain a RUC through a licensed abogado or registered agent and may be required to appoint a resident agent. A digital signature certificate must be obtained before activating e-invoicing.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch entity election comparable to US IRC §7701. A Sociedad Anónima is taxable as a juridical separate person at entity level exclusively on Panama-source income. Options available: (1) election to be treated under the simplified small taxpayer (Pequeño Contribuyente) ITBMS regime where annual gross receipts meet the statutory threshold, subject to prior DGI authorization; (2) election by a foreign legal person to operate its Panamanian trade through a regulated branch (sucursal) rather than a local S.A.; (3) election to apply the optional special fiscal incentive regimes applicable to certain export/service activities. No general consolidation, look-through, or classification election is otherwise available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold for ITBMS registration: any person obliged to pay ITBMS in respect of Panamanian taxable services or goods must register with DGI before commencing operations. A reduced-definition Pequeño Contribuyente regime may be elected when annual gross receipts are equal to or below B/. 36,000, subject to DGI approval and restrictions on input tax credits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods, including active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), are subject to 0% ITBMS if the goods physically leave Panamanian customs territory and are sold to a non-resident purchaser for use outside Panama. The exporter must be the registered RUC holder and must maintain proof of ownership, actual shipment, and receipt of payment in foreign currency or US dollars. Services performed in Panama for a non-resident and wholly consumed abroad are also zero-rated to the extent supported by contractual, delivery and payment evidence. Goods introduced into Colon Free Zone or other free trade zones are treated as exempt/zero-rated, but subsequent local sale in Panama enters Panama-sourced territory. DGI requires the exporter to retain all substantiation for the statutory prescription period and may condition refund/credit entitlement on verified clearance and FX repatriation evidence.

  • Customs Exit Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera / SICE export permit)
  • Through Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Panamanian port/airport
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT bank confirmation evidencing receipt of payment from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice with despcription of pharmaceutical APIs and terms of sale (CIF/FOB)
  • Packing list and weight certificate
  • Purchase/procurement records evidencing the exporter's ownership of the goods
  • Regulatory/pharma export clearance evidence if required by Ministerio de Salud or Panama customs
  • Free Trade Zone certificate, if applicable

Panama mandates electronic invoicing for all RUC holders through the DGI electronic invoicing system (Facturación Electrónica) or certified third-party providers. Invoices must be digitally signed with the taxpayer’s digital certificate and transmitted to DGI in real time or through approved batch/API channels for fiscal validation before issuance; no unsystematic manual invoices are acceptable for formal wholesalers. DGI performs automatic validation, assigns invoice acceptance numbers, and rejects non-conforming documents. Customs/export-related electronic documentation must be coordinated with the Panama customs system (SICE) for exports of pharmaceutical APIs.

DGI may audit export zero-rating transactions for up to the full statute of limitations; where a purported export is found not to have been physically exported, to have been delivered in Panama, or to lack genuine payment evidence, the ITBMS becomes payable at 7% on the gross consideration, with default interest, surcharges, and late-payment penalties. Administrative penalties under the Fiscal Code may range up to twice or more of the omitted tax; criminal tax and customs fraud sanctions, including imprisonment and disqualification of directors/legal representatives, may be imposed on responsible persons. Retrospective clawback may also extend to credits or refunds already claimed for the relevant periods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with zero exchange controls. Panama operates a de facto dollarized open capital account: current-account payments (imports, services, royalties, interest) and capital-account flows (equity injections, loan disbursements, dividends, loan repayments) require no prior central-bank authorization, no repatriation licence, and no currency-conversion mandate. The sole profit-remittance hurdle is territorial-source taxation: distributions of Panama-source taxable profits to shareholders attract a 10% dividend withholding tax (5% for small taxpayers qualifying under the simplified regime), whereas distributions out of foreign-source or non-taxable profits are exempt. No impediment, queue, tax-clearance certificate, or government pre-approval attaches to dividend wires by the treasury bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, no minimum conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation for export receipts or pharmaceutical API trade receivables. Export proceeds may remain held offshore in USD or any fully convertible currency indefinitely. The only compliance layer is documentary: the SBP-regulated bank will expect underlying invoice, customs-export declaration, and transportation-document consistency for the treasury account's expected credit pattern, and the UAF reporting threshold applies to suspicious movements rather than to repatriation of proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the sole banking and financial-system supervisor. Panama has no central bank and no monetary authority; the US Dollar (USD) is full legal tender under Decree Law No. 15 of 1904, and the Panamanian Balboa exists only as fractional coinage pegged 1:1 to USD. Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the State, while the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) handles fiscal policy. Foreign-exchange administration is absent by design; the SBP regulates AML/CFT conduct of banks and the Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) supervises suspicious-transaction reporting.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as a standalone liquidity node; MEDIUM only as a supplementary low-value collections/disbursements channel. Under Law 81 of 2021 (Panama Financial Innovation Regime), e-money issuance, PSP licensing, and open-banking sandbox participation require SBP authorization; unlicensed offshore digital EMIs cannot lawfully maintain a local omnibus settlement account or on-board the Panamanian S.A. without triggering the SBP bank's correspondent-account AML risk. The optimal structure is to use a fully licensed SBP-regulated bank as the central treasury concentration point and treasury-signing node, and to interface with a foreign EMI solely for small-ticket transactions (freight prepayments, ancillary logistics, sample purchases) through the bank's approved API payment-initiation rails, never concentrating more than 10% of monthly liquidity outside the SBP banking perimeter, thereby preserving the corporate veil and avoiding FATF-related jurisdictional rating harm to the Sociedad Anónima.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: inbound equity contributions from the shareholder's account are transmitted by SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for same-bank interbank) to the S.A.'s USD treasury account at its SBP-regulated bank, with the reference field clearly stating 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION' / 'APORTE DE CAPITAL'. Panama maintains no statutory purpose-code matrix requiring government approval; however, the bank will internally code the credit under its transaction-classification framework (equity/trust-services category) and will request, for credits equal to or above USD 500,000 or involving a 10%+ UBO, a 'source of wealth' questionnaire, the executed capital-subscription minutes, and the updated share register matching the declared beneficial owners. No exchange-control certificate, no CBP (banking clearance), and no tax clearance is required; only standard OFAC/UN sanctions screening, UAF recording, and bank-internal AML checks apply. For escrow-backed injections, the bank accepts the escrow agreement, subscription undertaking, and funds-origin letter from the escrow agent as sufficient documentary basis.API import purchases from India, China, or EU suppliers are executed via (1) irrevocable confirmed Commercial Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700/MT720) for larger shipments, with presentation of full set of clean-on-board B/L or AWB, commercial invoice, packing list, Certificate of Analysis, WHO-GMP certificate, and any destination-country registration at the LC counters; (2) Documentary Collections (DP/DA) for tested recurring counterparties; (3) direct SWIFT MT103 USD wire transfers cleared through the Panamanian bank's US correspondent (Fedwire/CHIPS) via ABA routing and CHIPS UID, with the invoice number and DNFD import-record references in the remittance information. All trade settlements must be reconciled to the ANA customs declarations and to the MINSA/DNFD import documentation trail in the corporate treasury file. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final dividend withholding on distributions paid from profits already subject to Panamanian CIT; 15% total (10% statutory dividend tax plus 5% complementary levy) when the distributed profits derive from exempt or non-subject income. The payor Panamanian S.A. applies the withholding equally to resident and non-resident shareholders.

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed no later than the third month following fiscal year-end; calendar-year corporations must file by 31 March of the immediately following year. DGI-approved extensions may defer only the filing, not the payment of the tax due.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Panama uses the Balboa (PAB), pegged 1:1 to the USD. The annual Transfer-Pricing Informational Return is mandatory when related-party transactions in the fiscal year exceed B/. 250,000. The Local File (Informe Local) is required when related-party transactions exceed B/. 2,000,000. The Master File (Informe Maestro) is required when the multinational group's worldwide consolidated revenue exceeds B/. 50,000,000 and the taxpayer meets the Local File threshold. Documentation must be contemporaneous, in Spanish, and delivered to the DGI within the filing period or upon tax audit request.
