Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); operational portal: SISTEMA TAX (https://sistematax.dgi.gob.pa). Municipal fiscal permit (Aviso de Operación) is issued by the local Alcaldía.

        
E-government filing portal
Foreign entities must first incorporate or register a Panama entity/branch at the Registro Público through a licensed attorney and appoint a resident agent. Subsequently: (1) obtain the Aviso de Operación from the competent municipality; (2) register with the DGI to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente), submitting corporate charter, bylaws, registered office certificate, resident agent appointment, and notarized power of attorney; (3) enroll in the electronic invoicing system and obtain the ITBMS registration; (4) if exporting, register with the Dirección Nacional de Aduanas as an exporter. Foreign entities with a permanent establishment may obtain a Panamanian RUC directly through their resident agent.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is a separate juridical person subject to corporate income tax only on Panama-source income; capital gains and foreign-source income are generally outside Panamanian tax jurisdiction; distributions to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Panama withholding tax. — Foreign entities must first incorporate or register a Panama entity/branch at the Registro Público through a licensed attorney and appoint a resident agent. Foreign entities with a permanent establishment may obtain a Panamanian RUC directly through their resident agent.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is a separate juridical person subject to corporate income tax only on Panama-source income; capital gains and foreign-source income are generally outside Panamanian tax jurisdiction; distributions to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Panama withholding tax.

        
Available election forms
No U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election is available. Available statutory options include: (i) Simplified Small-Taxpayer Regime (Régimen de Pequeños Contribuyentes) for annual gross turnover not exceeding B/. 36,000 (services) or B/. 60,000 (goods); (ii) Multinational Headquarters Regime (SEM) for qualifying regional headquarters applying a 5% effective rate on specified Panama-source income; (iii) tax-exempt zone regimes (Panamá Pacífico Special Economic Area, Ciudad del Saber, Colón Free Zone); (iv) administrative elections on ITBMS input credits for export-related inventories and fixed assets.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
7%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
No general de minimis threshold — registration is mandatory for any person undertaking taxable transactions in Panama. A reduced-compliance simplified regime applies to small taxpayers with annual gross turnover not exceeding B/. 36,000 (services) or B/. 60,000 (goods), subject to DGI verification.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rating at 0% ITBMS applies to: (i) physical exports of movable goods evidenced by a valid customs export declaration (DUA) confirming departure from Panamanian territory; (ii) services rendered to non-residents for use or consumption entirely abroad, provided the benefit is realized outside Panama and the service is not connected with taxable activities in Panama; (iii) services directly linked to exported goods. For ISIC 5210 cold-storage warehousing, services physically performed in Panama for local principals remain standard-rated at 7%; zero-rating is available only where the foreign principal receives title/risk offshore and documentary proof of offshore use is maintained.

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) through the DGI SISTEMA TAX platform. The online modality requires real-time transmission of each invoice to the DGI and receipt of a digitally stamped QR/authorization code before issuance; an offline (batch/lot) modality is available only in limited DGI-authorized scenarios. High-volume taxpayers must integrate ERP systems through DGI APIs; phased mandatory rollout is based on economic activity and annual revenue, with non-compliance triggering suspension of fiscal authorization codes.

      
The DGI cross-audits customs declarations, e-invoice data, and banking remittance records. Failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days, or submission of false documentation, will trigger retrospective reassessment, reversal of the zero-rating with collection of the full 7% ITBMS, surcharges of up to 100% of the omitted tax, monthly recargo/interest, and fiscal closure of the credit position. Law 70 of 2019 criminalizes aggravated tax fraud, with imprisonment applicable for evaded amounts exceeding B/. 50,000; penalties apply jointly to the entity, its directors, and the responsible resident agent.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Panamanian regime provides full current-account and capital-account convertibility with zero exchange-control approvals, zero prior licensing of foreign-currency transactions, and zero profit-remittance authorization requirements. A Sociedad Anónima engaged in cold-chain logistics may freely receive equity injections, borrow externally, and remit dividends or loan repayments in USD or any convertible currency. The only fiscal frictions are Panamanian territorial taxation (corporate income tax at 25%; dividend tax of 10% on distributions out of taxed reserves, 20% out of non-taxed or foreign-source income) and standard AML/FIU reporting, including currency transaction reports for cash operations above US$10,000. No balance-of-payments registration or IMF-purpose-code declaration is required by the SBP.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
Panama imposes no statutory surrender rules, no mandatory conversion ratios, no repatriation deadlines, and no export-proceeds retention quotas. Refrigerated warehousing and logistics service exports may be invoiced and collected in USD or any freely convertible currency, with the full commercial value retained offshore or onshore at the company's discretion. The only obligations are (i) proper recognition in G/L in balboas at the fixed rate, (ii) invoice substance for territorial-tax purposes, and (iii) standard FIU (Unidad de Análisis Financiero, UAF) transaction reporting by the receiving bank.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Panama operates no conventional central bank. Prudential supervision of all licensed banks is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Decree Law 2 of 2008; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts solely as fiscal agent of the Republic and issues no currency. The U.S. dollar is statutory legal tender alongside the balboa (PAB), which circulates only as fractional coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity. Monetary and exchange-rate policy are therefore effectively the policy of the United States Federal Reserve, and Panamanian authorities impose no independent FX monetary framework.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

      
      

        Newly incorporated S.A. receiving a large lump-sum capital injection (above US$1,000,000) within 90 days of registration
        
Root cause: Newly incorporated S.A. receiving a large lump-sum capital injection (above US$1,000,000) within 90 days of registration

        
Defense evidence: Certified Escritura Pública and Mercantile Registry certificate dated within 30 days; share subscription and payment agreement; board resolution attesting the source of funds; audited parent-company financial statements; bank reference letter from the remitting institution; the underlying share-purchase or facility agreement; and the Panama FIU-pattern declaration of purpose (proposed use of funds for cold-store fit-out and chiller equipment).

      

      
      

        Beneficial-ownership chain routed through BVI, Seychelles, Bahamas, or other offshore vehicles
        
Root cause: Beneficial-ownership chain routed through BVI, Seychelles, Bahamas, or other offshore vehicles

        
Defense evidence: Complete ownership chart through to the final natural persons; notarized and apostilled passports; tax-residence certificates of each UBO; evidence of physical substance (lease or owned facility for the cold-storage operation); legal opinion from a licensed Panamanian counsel confirming the absence of bearer shares and nominee structures under Law 47 of 2013 reform; and a written group commercial rationale.

      

      
      

        PEP, politically exposed, or sanctions-adjacent shareholders or counterparties in Latin American pharma supply chains
        
Root cause: PEP, politically exposed, or sanctions-adjacent shareholders or counterparties in Latin American pharma supply chains

        
Defense evidence: Source-of-wealth report and independent wealth verification; full PEP status disclosure; corporate authorization from the ultimate holding company; transaction-monitoring commitment letter; enhanced due-diligence questionnaire; and evidence of arm's-length rental/concession contracts for the warehousing premises.

      

      
      

        Pharmaceutical cold-chain operator appearing without visible health or sanitary licensing
        
Root cause: Pharmaceutical cold-chain operator appearing without visible health or sanitary licensing

        
Defense evidence: Valid MINSA (Ministerio de Salud) / Dirección Nacional de Farmacia y Drogas (DDRP) operating permit for storage and distribution of perishable and temperature-controlled products; GDP/GMP certification of the facility or contractual joint-venture with a licensed operator; quality agreement with the pharmaceutical client; temperature-monitoring validation report; and the client offtake contract.

      

      
      

        Sanction-list name hits affecting freight forwarders, equipment vendors, or beneficial owners
        
Root cause: Sanction-list name hits affecting freight forwarders, equipment vendors, or beneficial owners

        
Defense evidence: Written UAF/OFAC screening logs proving due diligence; copies of corporate registries demonstrating distinct identity; attestation from the resident agent; request for a refreshed World-Check/Bureau van Dijk report under applicable data-protection consents; and an updated AML compliance manual covering the Panamanian statutory framework (Law 23 of 2015) and post-FATF enhanced-monitoring expectations.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
25%

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
10% statutory withholding on dividends distributed from profits already subject to corporate income tax; 20% on distributions sourced from untaxed profits or sheltered reserves. Panamanian S.A.s are additionally subject to an annual minimum advanced dividend tax equal to 10% of taxable income, creditable against the final dividend WHT.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Panama's transfer-pricing regime (Law 62 of 2020, Fiscal Code Art. 762-A) mandates a Local File and the transfer-pricing annex to Form 125 where the taxpayer's annual gross revenue is ≥ B/. 2.5 million or related-party transactions in the fiscal year are ≥ B/. 500,000. A Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue ≥ USD 750 million. Documentation must be submitted to the DGI within 10 business days of a formal request and is subject to penalty for untimely or incomplete delivery.

      
Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 129 of 2020 and Executive Decree 82 of 2021) apply to S.A.s conducting 'covered activities,' which include distribution and logistics centers. A refrigerated cold-storage operator classified as a Large Taxpayer (annual gross revenue exceeding B/. 30 million or total assets exceeding B/. 40 million) must file an annual economic-substance declaration and demonstrate: physical premises in Panama, adequate qualified local staff, material local expenditure, and core income-generating activities (CIGA) performed in Panama. Non-compliance triggers fines up to B/. 500,000 and revocation of the company's tax-residence certificate.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion (Defraudación Tributaria)
        
Statutory sanction: Civil: deficiency assessment plus 10% first-month surcharge and 1.5% monthly interest, together with a penalty of 1.0 to 1.5 times the evaded tax. Criminal (Law 70 of 2019): imprisonment of 3–5 years where the evaded amount exceeds B/. 300,000; aggravated fraud through false invoices raises the penalty to up to 7 years.

        
Director personal liability: Directors, CFOs, and the resident legal representative are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax, surcharges, and fines. The DGI may impose travel bans, seize personal bank accounts, and refer non-resident directors for criminal indictment, including extradition where applicable.

      

      
      

        Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure
        
Statutory sanction: Economic-substance declaration failure: fines from B/. 1,000 per month up to B/. 500,000 (Law 129/2020); deregistration of the RUC and cancellation of tax-residence certification; Penalties under the Beneficial Ownership Registry (Law 52/2016 and Decree 82/2021) of up to B/. 50,000 for non-registration or false disclosure.

        
Director personal liability: Nominee directors face personal liability for acts executed without corporate substance, piercing of the corporate veil against the beneficial owners who directed the sham, disqualification from holding Panamanian directorships, and criminal exposure under the AML and economic-substance frameworks.

      

      
      

        AML Compliance Failure (Law 23 of 2015)
        
Statutory sanction: UAF fines of USD 10,000–100,000 per violation, elevated to USD 200,000 for serious or repeated breaches; administrative suspension of the operating license for refrigerated logistics; and judicial freezing of the S.A.'s assets pending investigation.

        
Director personal liability: Directors and designated compliance officers are criminally liable for deliberate failure to report suspicious operations (Law 23, Art. 23) with imprisonment of 2–5 years, personal fines, travel bans, and permanent disqualification from regulated logistics activities.

      

      
      

        Repatriation / Withholding Tax Evasion
        
Statutory sanction: Undocumented remittances: penalty of 30% of the gross amount remitted (Fiscal Code Art. 762-A); default assessment of the omitted WHT at 20% of the distribution plus 10% surcharge and 1.5% monthly interest; recharacterization of shareholder loans as deemed dividends with full WHT, interest, and penalties.

        
Director personal liability: Directors who sign or authorize distributions without proper WHT become personally liable to the DGI for the withheld amounts, penalties, and interest. If the concealed amount exceeds B/. 300,000, criminal tax-fraud charges under Law 70/2019 attach, exposing directors to imprisonment, civil restitution, and international asset-tracing measures.
