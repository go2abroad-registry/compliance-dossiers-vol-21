Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is required beyond the rule that the subscribed capital shall be at least US$100 and at least 10% thereof (US$10) be paid in cash or property at subscription; no escrow bank account is mandated, but the paid-in portion must be declared in the deed and may be demonstrated by receipt or property contribution minutes.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official tax portal: E-Tax 2.0 / SITIO (Sistema Integrado de Tributación en Línea) at https://etax.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity incorporating a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) from the DGI. Process: (1) register the public deed of incorporation with the Registro Público de Panamá; (2) appoint a resident legal representative (agente residente), board officers, and finance officer (tesorero); (3) file the RUC registration form with the DGI via E-Tax 2.0 or at the corresponding Regional Administration, attaching the registered deed, corporate identification, proof of the resident agent appointment, and, if the company operates, the Aviso de Operación issued by the Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias (MICI); (4) receive the RUC certificate and digital credentials; and (5) register in the electronic invoicing system. A foreign entity without local incorporation but conducting Panamanian-sourced commercial activity must obtain a non-resident RUC and appoint an agent for service of process and tax purposes; no minimum capital is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not recognize check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections; a Sociedad Anónima is mandatorily taxable at entity level and its worldwide tax liability is limited to Panamanian-source income (territoriality). Available elections include: (i) the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) Regime under Law 41 of 2007, electively exempting eligible international services and related income from income tax and ITBMS; (ii) the Panama Pacífico Special Economic Zone regime under Law 41 of 2004, granting an elective full/reduced tax exemptions package; (iii) the Simplified Small-Taxpayer Regime (Régimen Simplificado del Pequeño Contribuyente) under Article 713-H of the Código Fiscal, election for gross income not exceeding USD 36,000 per year to pay a presumptive fixed monthly quota; and (iv) the allocation election under Law 254 of 2021 permitting taxpayers to apportion shared expenses between territorial and extraterritorial income. No OECD BEPS hybrid-mismatch elections are available in Panamanian domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis turnover exemption: every person or entity in possession of a RUC that habitually transfers taxable goods or renders taxable services in Panama must be registered for ITBMS before commencing activities. Importers, wholesalers, and port-located exporters must register regardless of periodic turnover. Small taxpayers with annual gross income not exceeding USD 36,000 (as periodically adjusted) may elect the Simplified Regime and pay a fixed monthly quota in lieu of full ITBMS compliance. Non-resident suppliers without Panamanian nexus are not registrable, but the import of Portland clinker into Panamanian customs territory is subject to ITBMS at the border via the customs import declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Panama are zero-rated at 0% ITBMS provided the export is evidenced by an authorized customs export declaration. For the target operation (bulk Portland clinker sold on FOB/CIF/CFR terms to non-resident buyers and loaded in Panamanian ports for international carriage), zero-rating applies when the merchandise leaves Panamanian customs territory, title and risk pass to the foreign buyer, and the freight is contracted with a non-resident principal; the sale must not be consummated within Panama's internal market. Services performed in Panama but legally consumed or used abroad (including ocean freight and international logistics services invoiced to non-resident customers) are likewise outside the ITBMS tax base under the territoriality principle. Domestic feeder movements and port services rendered within Panamanian territory remain taxable at 7%, unless separately zero-rated as part of an export service contract.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) validated by Aduanas through SIGA (Sistema Integrado de Gestión Aduanera)
  • Bill of Lading (ocean B/L) or Sea Waybill evidencing physical departure of the bulk cargo from a Panamanian port
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List cross-referenced to the export declaration
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Ingreso de Divisas) from a licensed Panamanian bank, or equivalent SWIFT wire records, evidencing collection of export proceeds
  • ITBMS export ledger and input credit support schedule substantiating the 0% claim and refund computation

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (factura electrónica) for all RUC-holders under the DGI phased mandate (Resolution 201-3594 of 2021 and related implementing resolutions). Structured XML invoices and credit/debit notes must be transmitted to the DGI's E-Tax 2.0 fiscal platform for validation, receive a unique electronic authorization code and digital stamp (QR code), and be delivered to the counterparty in PDF/A or XML. Real-time clearance is required in the current phase for key sectors and large taxpayers; wholesale distribution (ISIC 4663) operates under near-real-time validation (intraday transmission) with mandatory API integration for ERP/SAP systems or certified billing software. Issuance of false or non-stamped invoices invalidates the document and forfeits the corresponding input ITBMS credit.

The DGI and Aduanas jointly cross-verify the export declarations, B/Ls, and banking remittance certificates; a failure to evidence lawful collectible export proceeds within the documentary window triggers retrospective clawback of the 0% benefit, re-assessment of the uncollected 7% ITBMS, disallowance of related input credits, and recapture of refund amounts, plus monthly interest of 1% and late-payment surcharges of 10% of the omitted quota. Where the exporter presents falsified documentation or engages in fictitious exports, the conduct is prosecuted as tax fraud under Articles 755-758 of the Código Fiscal, carrying fines of 50% to 100% of the evaded tax, potential suspension of the RUC and closure of the establishment, export log-book license cancellation, and criminal liability of directors, treasurer, and legal representatives.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system with no exchange controls and no distinction between current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Balboa exists only as 1:1 fractional coins; the U.S. Dollar is the circulating legal tender. There are no restrictions on the conversion, repatriation, or remittance of dividends, branch profits, or contributed capital in USD, and no prior SBP or Ministry of Commerce authorization is required for a standard wholesale corporation. Treasury operations of PA_CORP may freely settle intercompany loans, equity injections, and trade payables in USD without balance-of-payments reporting or conversion taxation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for clinker, hydraulic cement, or vessel-freight receivables. Export earnings in USD may be retained in offshore accounts, applied to charter-party or supplier obligations, or credited to local USD demand deposits without any central-bank conversion duty. The only compliance overlay is the bank's AML/CFT recordkeeping and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) reporting thresholds under Decree Law 2 of 2008, which do not constitute exchange control.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the sole licensing and prudential regulator of banks, trust companies, finance houses and electronic-money issuers. Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the fiscal agent of the Republic and issues only Balboa coins. There is no central bank empowered to set monetary policy or issue paper currency; the U.S. Federal Reserve effectively anchors monetary conditions through full U.S.-dollar legal tender status.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low to Medium. The SBP licensing framework permits electronic-money companies, but no licensed Panamanian EMI can independently provide MT700 documentary credit issuance or full SWIFT nostro clearing for bulk portland-clinker vessel trade. Offshore digital EMIs are generally unsuitable as primary treasury rails due to their lack of Panamanian licensing, uncertain U.S. correspondent relationships, and UAF scrutiny of non-bank money movement. Their acceptable use should be confined to incidental operating expenses below USD 50,000 per month; all material inbound capital and trade settlements must be executed through licensed tier-1 banks.
