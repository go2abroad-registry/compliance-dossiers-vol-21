Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Permissible for a standard Sociedad Anónima used for international bulk export activities, provided that the company maintains a bona fide registered agent (Agente Residente) with a physical office address in Panama City or the Republic of Panama. The registered agent must be a licensed attorney or a licensed corporate service provider. The registered office address will be the same as the resident agent's office address. A virtual office (without physical occupancy in any Panamanian territory) is acceptable at the pre-incorporation and compliance stage, but the Board of Directors must ensure that operational substance (such as banking arrangements and decision-making) does not take place from a blacklisted or high-risk jurisdiction. For the import/export trading of dried goji berries and herbal botanicals, the commercial substance is evidenced through contracts, invoices, insurance, shipping documents, and retention of a Panamanian resident agent together with (recommended) maintenance of a local local bank account and the filing of annual franchise tax returns.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; taxpayer portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (Panama e-Tax system)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must maintain a resident agent in Panama and register with the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) before the DGI. The application is made through the Sede Electrónica portal using a power of attorney, executed articles of incorporation, and a pre-registration form. Upon approval, the DGI issues a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) which serves as the definitive tax identification number for all domestic tax and ITBMS filings.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign entity must maintain a resident agent in Panama and register with the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) before the DGI. The application is made through the Sede Electrónica portal using a power of attorney, executed articles of incorporation, and a pre-registration form. Upon approval, the DGI issues a Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) which serves as the definitive tax identification number for all domestic tax and ITBMS filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama has no check-the-box or federal entity classification election. Under Panamanian law, a Sociedad Anónima is always treated as a separate taxable corporation subject to territorial income tax on Panama-source income. Foreign tax law classifications are ignored by the DGI; a Panama S.A. cannot elect to be transparent for domestic purposes. No hybrid mismatch election is available under Panamanian tax legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis turnover threshold. Registration for ITBMS is mandatory for all persons engaged in taxable activities, including importing goods into Panama, selling goods, or granting services for consideration. A foreign supplier that provides digital or offshore services to Panamanian resident customers is also required to register and pay ITBMS unless the service constitutes an exported service eligible for zero rating.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Panama and international services are eligible for 0% ITBMS when the goods physically depart Panama within the statutory timeframe, title passes to a non-resident purchaser, or services are executed and exploited outside Panama. The exporter must retain a completed export declaration, the corresponding customs exit record, the contract of sale, and proof of payment from the foreign buyer. Failure to substantiate the export within the fiscal audit period causes the sale to be reclassed as taxable at 7%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUI/DEX under SIGA Panamá)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (comprobante de ingreso de divisas)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) through the DGI's Sistema de Facturación Electrónica. Invoices must be digitally transmitted to the DGI in near-real-time, encrypted with a valid 'quiniela fiscal' (fiscal verification code), and cleared through authorized web-service APIs. Large taxpayers are subject to phased mandatory e-invoicing based on annual gross income thresholds; non-compliance results in invoice invalidity and administrative penalties.

The DGI conducts substantive audits of zero-rated ITBMS export claims. If the export documents are absent, false, or incomplete, the 0% rate is annulled retroactively and the exporter must pay the deferred ITBMS together with inflation-adjusted interest. Administrative fines range from 100% to 300% of the evaded tax depending on the degree of fraud. Submission of falsified customs or banking export proof triggers criminal liability under Panama's tax procedure law and anti-money-laundering legislation for documentary fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange controls, no surrender mandates, and no restrictions on inbound or outbound wire transfers. Profit and dividend remittances to non-resident shareholders of a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima incur no withholding tax. The only frictions are documentary: SBP-due-diligence rules (Acuerdo 3/1997 as amended by Law 23/2015) and bank source-of-funds verification for trade-related receipts, which require underlying invoices, customs declarations, and shipping evidence. The financial system is U.S.-dollar native, effectively eliminating FX risk within Panama and enabling direct USD clearing via Federal Reserve correspondent accounts and SWIFT.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign bank accounts at the exporter's discretion. However, all shipments of dried goji berries and herbal botanicals must be declared through the Ventanilla Única de Comercio Exterior (VUCE) of the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA) for customs and trade statistics; the bank will require the ANA export declaration, commercial invoice, packing list, and bill of lading to evidence the commercial nature of received funds and to satisfy SBP anti-money-laundering obligations.

Central bank supervision: Panama operates under a de facto full-dollarization regime with no central monetary authority. Banking, prudential supervision, and FX oversight are vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Decree Law 2 of 2008, as amended; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) oversees monetary policy coordination, and Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the Republic. Legal tender is the U.S. dollar; the balboa (PAB) exists solely as a unit of account and subsidiary coinage.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium for ancillary low-value flows; Low as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI accounts (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Mercury, Airwallex) are not licensed banks under Decree Law 2 of 2008 and cannot issue Panamanian corporate IBANs, hold escrow balances, or originate Commercial Letters of Credit. The SBP's innovation hub regime (Acuerdo 007 of 2022) authorizes sandbox testing of payment and EMI-type services, but full statutory bank-guarantee and clearing privileges are not yet granted. The Panamanian S.A. should therefore designate a fully licensed SBP bank as the principal liquidity and clearing venue and use EMIs solely for collection of small receivables in foreign markets with immediate onward settlement to the SBP-bank account.
