Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; taxpayer portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

The Panamanian S.A. must be duly registered with the Registro Público de Panamá and must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) before commencing taxable business. The foreign-owner procedure is: (1) execute and notarize the corporation's by-laws/powers and register the company and its resident legal representative with the Public Registry; (2) appoint and maintain a resident agent and designate a branch/registered address in Panama; (3) file with DGI, through a licensed Panamanian attorney or CPA, the RUC registration application, the Public Registry certificate, legal representative's identity/passport copy and proof of address; (4) obtain the RUC number, which simultaneously operates as the ITBMS (VAT) registration identifier; (5) if physical or local activity is conducted, obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación. For a purely offshore digital publisher not maintaining a Panama office, the DGI still expects the S.A. to hold RUC for corporate filing purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is an entity-level taxable corporation but is taxed only on Panama-source income; foreign-source revenue, including offshore digital game publishing income received from Steam/Valve and PlayStation Store, is generally excluded from Panamanian corporate income tax. Non-resident ownership is permissible and does not create a permanent-establishment/nexus difference.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide IRS-style 'check-the-box' elections or hybrid mismatch elections for S.A.s. The territorial regime is mandatory. Available elections are limited to tax-accounting matters, e.g., depreciation methods and, where applicable, the small-taxpayer simplified ITBMS regime for low-turnover micro-businesses; a corporate digital publisher will not normally qualify for the simplified regime.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold (B/.0.00) for registration as a corporate taxpayer. A Panamanian S.A. must obtain RUC/ITBMS registration before making taxable transfers or providing services in Panama. Small taxpayers may have a simplified ITBMS-collection regime, but it is not applicable to an ordinary S.A. engaged in Steam/console publishing.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For physical goods, 0% ITBMS applies only when title passes and goods physically leave Panama, evidenced by an accepted export declaration. For digital game publishing through Steam and PlayStation Store, the supply of games/licenses to non-resident end users is treated as an export/offshore service eligible for 0% ITBMS when the customer is outside Panama and the game is received, used or downloaded outside Panama. The publisher must separate Panamanian end-user sales (subject to 7% ITBMS) from non-Panamanian sales (0% or outside scope). Platform settlements received from Valve/Sony are acceptable payment evidence, but DGI scrutiny will focus on the location and identity of end users and the place where the digital service is exploited.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) for physical goods; for digital supplies, the platform settlement/export report from Steam/Valve or PlayStation Store
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical exports; digital distribution statement showing non-resident account locations for digital exports
  • Commercial invoice and packing list for physical shipments; platform purchase/sales reports and payout statements for digital sales
  • License or publishing agreement with Valve/PlayStation evidencing the territories, rights, and non-resident end-user market
  • Bank/inward remittance certificate, SWIFT credit advice, or USD payout statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • DGI ITBMS tax returns and zero-rated export schedule identifying the invoices and documentary evidence

Panama mandates electronic invoicing for DGI-registered taxpayers under the Facturación Electrónica framework. Invoices must be generated through an authorized billing system or the DGI's free electronic invoicing platform, digitally signed by the taxpayer's Panamanian e-signature certificate, and transmitted to DGI in real time through API/web-service integration. The DGI validates the invoice, assigns a unique fiscal authorization/stamp, and returns a validation code and QR code for the printable customer copy. For platform payouts, invoices to the platform or supporting zero-rated export invoices must carry the DGI clearance data; failure to issue compliant e-invoices can create penalties.

The DGI may reclassify claimed zero-rated/offshore supplies as taxable Panama-source services if documentation is missing or end users are actually located in Panama. Reassessment will collect unpaid ITBMS at 7%, add statutory interest and surcharges, and impose fines for the omitted tax, typically up to 100% of the deficiency. False export declarations, fabricated offshore user evidence, or abusive arrangements intended to avoid 7% ITBMS can trigger criminal tax-fraud prosecution, personal liability of directors/legal representatives, and retrospective clawback of any refunds or VAT credits claimed. Panama does not impose foreign-exchange repatriation controls; the 180-day field represents the administrative documentary benchmark used for export-substantiation files.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current and capital account convertibility. The economy is dollarized; capital injections, dividend distributions, branch remittances and liquidation proceeds can be transferred without prior exchange approval. Profit repatriation is subject only to ordinary Panamanian tax rules. For this entity, digital-store publishing revenue collected from non-Panamanian marketplaces qualifies as foreign-source income under Panama Law 8 of 2010 and Executive Decree 476 of 2011, provided that no Panamanian end-user market is served and no Panamanian permanent establishment exists; therefore no taxable remittance or withholding obstacle is expected. The site of digital game purchases must be contractually documented at the Steam and PlayStation marketplace level to preserve the foreign-source characterization.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender requirement applies to export or platform proceeds in Panama. Receipts from Valve (Steam) and Sony (PlayStation Store) may remain in USD indefinitely at a licensed commercial bank. The SBP requires no declaration for a standard software-publishing treasury flow; however, the bank will expect monthly platform payout evidence and a tax-situs memorandum prepared by a licensed Panamanian attorney.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) acting as the banking and foreign exchange regulator under Decreto Ley 9 of 1998; Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as fiscal agent of the State. Panama has no conventional central bank and no monetary authority issuing national paper currency. The Panamanian balboa is a nominal coin-only unit statutorily pegged 1:1 to the United States dollar, and the USD is legal tender and the default settlement currency.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail, acceptable only as a secondary receivable conduit. Panama does not operate an EU-style Electronic Money Institution licensing regime, so a Panama S.A. cannot obtain a locally licensed EMI with passported clearing access. Cross-border platforms (Payoneer, Mercury, Airwallex, Wise Business) may accept the entity on a discretionary basis; Payoneer is the most practical bridge for Valve/Steam settlements, while Sony PlayStation payout contracts generally require classical SWIFT wires to a legal-entity bank account. The recommended structure is a Tier-1 Panama bank USD master account for all liquidity, with a Payoneer sub-account restricted to Steam receivable concentration and swept weekly into the master account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Dispatch all capital contributions via SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's existing bank account using the BPM6 balance-of-payments classification code for equity capital; a suitable wire reference is PA_CORP S.A. - CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - BOARD RESOLUTION OF [DATE] - NON-REIMBURSABLE. The receiving bank must be instructed to book the credit as direct investment equity and not as a loan. Attach the notarized capital increase or shareholder resolution and a funds-viability letter from a licensed Panamanian CPA. Because the USD is legal tender, no conversion is required; no SBP approval is applicable. For shareholder loans, execute a formal promissory note with arm's-length OECD-compliant interest and record the charge where the approved bank facility threshold applies.Primary clearing rail for trade obligations is SWIFT MT103. Valve/Steam settlements typically arrive through Payoneer from a US partner bank; Sony PlayStation settlements arrive by classical MT103 from the PlayStation partner treasury. Payments for engine royalties, middleware licenses, localization, marketing and distribution support should be sent via MT103 using purpose codes for trade-related services and cross-border fees, with the text line reflecting ISIC 5820 PC and console publishing services. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat rate on Panama-source net taxable income

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% withholding tax on dividends paid from Panama-source profits to both resident and non-resident shareholders. If the corporation opts for the 2% complementary tax on distributable income, the applicable rate on actual distributions may be reduced to 3%; if dividends are paid from retained untaxed Panama-source profits, the 5% withholding tax applies in full.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For calendar-year entities: on or before 31 March of the following year. For fiscal-year entities ending other than 31 December: no later than 3 months after the fiscal year-end. A 2-month extension is available on written request with justified cause.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File documentation is triggered when annual consolidated gross revenue exceeds B/. 2,000,000 (USD 2,000,000) or when related-party transactions, including cross-border platform fee payments, royalties, and management charges, individually or collectively exceed B/. 1,000,000 (USD 1,000,000). The Panamanian entity must file the Transfer Pricing Disclosure Form (Formulario Anexo de Precios de Transferencia) together with the annual income tax return; full transfer pricing study must be available within 30 working days of DGI request.

Economic substance scope: Yes. Panama Economic Substance Regulations (Ley 254 de 2020 and Reglamento de la Ley 254) apply to resident legal entities engaged in specified relevant activities. A video game publishing company deriving digital distribution and IP royalty income from Steam and PlayStation is potentially caught as either a 'distribution and service centre' or 'intellectual property business'. The S.A. must demonstrate adequate physical office, board meetings, qualified employees, operating expenditure, and decision-making presence in Panama. Outsourced development to a foreign related party does not satisfy substance. Exemptions exist for non-tax-resident entities whose effective management is not in Panama, but such entities must be separately reportable under the mandatory registration regime.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Penal Code of Panama: imprisonment of 2 to 6 years plus a fine equal to 3 to 9 times the evaded tax. Administrative penalty of 100%-200% of the omitted tax and delinquency surcharge of 1.25% per month; seizure of bank accounts and judicial collection of unpaid taxes are automatic.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent who sign a knowingly false return are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax, penalties, and interest. Courts may impose travel bans, preventive detention, and 2-6 year custodial sentences. Corporate veil piercing is available under Articles 152-161 of the Panamanian Corporation Law where fraud or abuse of the S.A. structure is shown.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulations: annual penalties of USD 10,000 for first offense, USD 20,000 for second offense, and up to USD 100,000 or five (5) times the relevant income for continued noncompliance, whichever is higher. DGI may file for judicial winding-up, cancellation of the RUC, and revocation of the resident agency license.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign a false economic substance declaration are personally liable for the penalty amounts, and resident agents can be deregistered. Repeat offenses justify disqualification of directors from any Panama company and freezing of personal assets where the entity is an alter ego of the ultimate beneficial owner.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: DGI may adjust taxable income upward using the most appropriate arm's length method, applying a 25% tax on the adjustment plus a penalty of 40% to 100% of the additional tax due, plus monthly surcharges. Non-filing of the transfer pricing annex is fined USD 500 to USD 10,000; late filing of the corresponding documentation is fined USD 100 to USD 2,000 per day.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve related-party contracts without an arm's length study can be held civilly liable for the tax adjustment and penalties. In cases of fraudulent documentation, criminal liability extends to imprisonment and travel bans, based on the tax fraud provisions of the Penal Code.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Violations

Statutory sanction: Panama AML Law (Ley 5 de 2021): fines from USD 10,000 to USD 500,000 for entities, and cancellation of corporate registration for persistent failure to disclose ultimate beneficial owners. Criminal money laundering penalties: 5 to 12 years' imprisonment plus fine of 1 to 5 times the laundered amount. The Superintendency of Non-Financial Entities (ARAP) may suspend the resident agent.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the resident agent who failed to identify the natural person behind the company may be sanctioned jointly and severally. Travel bans and freezing of personal funds are standard enforcement tools; criminal proceedings can pierce the corporate veil to seize company assets beneficially owned by the director personally.
