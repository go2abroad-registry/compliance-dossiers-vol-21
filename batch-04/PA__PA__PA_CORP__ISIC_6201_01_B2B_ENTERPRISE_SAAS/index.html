Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) — the General Directorate of Revenue under the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF); electronic portal: SIE (Sistema de Información Económica) accessible via the official DGI website (dgi.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panamanian S.A. must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) from DGI. The procedure requires: (i) filing the RUC application form; (ii) attaching the notarized public deed of incorporation, the certified appointment of a resident legal representative, and apostilled corporate documents; (iii) obtaining the RUC certificate which simultaneously serves as the ITBMS registration; (iv) activating electronic tax services on the SIE portal with a digital signature.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign-owned Panamanian S.A. must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) from DGI. The procedure requires: (i) filing the RUC application form; (ii) attaching the notarized public deed of incorporation, the certified appointment of a resident legal representative, and apostilled corporate documents; (iii) obtaining the RUC certificate which simultaneously serves as the ITBMS registration; (iv) activating electronic tax services on the SIE portal with a digital signature.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

None. Panama does not provide check-the-box elections or hybrid mismatch elections. A Sociedad Anónima is automatically treated as a separate taxable corporation. Income is taxed on a territorial basis – only Panamanian-source income is subject to corporate income tax. Foreign-source income, including SaaS revenue from offshore clients, is excluded from the Panamanian tax base.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0 – No minimum turnover exemption; all commercial taxpayers performing taxable supplies within Panama must register for ITBMS. Entities with annual sales not exceeding USD 36,000 may qualify for the simplified small-taxpayer regime but are still required to be registered.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services to non-residents are zero-rated where the economic use, benefit, or consumption occurs outside Panama. For B2B SaaS, the following conditions must be met: the customer is a non-resident company; the software subscription is performed, maintained, and technically hosted outside Panamanian territory unless solely for internal back-office functions; payment originates from a foreign bank account; and the contracting documents contain a foreign governing law and place of performance clause. The exporter must maintain documentation evidencing the offshore character of the supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) for tangible goods; for SaaS services, a service export invoice containing a zero-rated ITBMS line and a statement of export service
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill for physical goods; for software, the executed SaaS agreement, order form, or subscription contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/MT103 bank transaction statement evidencing receipt of payment from a foreign source

Panama mandates DGI-certified invoicing for VAT-registered taxpayers. The electronic invoicing (factura electrónica) system requires issuance of digital invoices for all B2B supplies. Invoices must be signed with a digitally certified credential and transmitted to DGI's SIE platform within five (5) calendar days after issuance. DGI performs asynchronous validation through web services; there is no mandatory real-time fiscal clearance API or permanent digital stamping requirement for standard B2B SaaS transactions.

DGI may audit zero-rating claims up to five (5) years from the filing date. Unsupported or fraudulent export zero-rating triggers reassessment of ITBMS, a monthly surcharge of 10% on the tax due (capped at 100%), administrative fines ranging from 100% to 200% of the tax evaded, and criminal prosecution for tax fraud with possible imprisonment of five to ten years. Retroactive clawbacks include interest and adjustment of VAT input credits claimed in prior periods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Currency convertibility is unrestricted. The U.S. dollar is legal tender and circulates freely, with Panama issuing fractional Balboa coins pegged 1:1 to the USD. Both current and capital account transactions are fully liberalized. There are no exchange control restrictions on the conversion or repatriation of capital, dividends, interest, royalties, or service fees. Profits generated by a Panama Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) may be remitted abroad freely, subject solely to transactional banking due diligence. No prior central bank approval or licenses are required for ordinary business payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no export proceeds surrender rules. Exports of goods and services, including enterprise SaaS software subscriptions, are not subject to mandatory conversion, forced sale, or mandated repatriation timelines. Exporters may retain, freely use, or pay out foreign currency proceeds without any statutory holding period, and may invoice in USD or any other currency at commercial discretion.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank; monetary sovereignty is effectively delegated to the U.S. Federal Reserve through the full dollarization regime. Banking supervision and regulatory oversight of financial institutions are conducted by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), acting as the de facto financial regulator, while the Ministry of Economy and Finance manages public fiscal policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-High with specific structural limitations. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, or Mercury) are operationally viable for USD-based cross-border collections and treasury settlement only if they maintain a Panamanian money-service license or partner with a fully licensed Panamanian bank. Pure offshore EMI accounts, when opened without a physical Panamanian licence, face severe compliance friction because Panamanian banking law (Carta Orgánica de la Superintendencia de Bancos) prohibits unlicensed persons from conducting money transmission to or from Panama. For a B2B enterprise SaaS company, EMI rails are best used as a supplementary collection channel, not as the primary bank account, in order to avoid statutory licensing exposure under Legislative Decree No. 2 of 2008 (as amended) and to preserve a clean audit trail with domestic banking counterparties.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital contributions to a Panamanian S.A. are executed through standard SWIFT messaging using wire transfer purpose codes that align with Panama's balance-of-payments classification and SBP non-financial transaction reporting. For software companies, the recommended routing is as follows: shareholder equity injections must be marked as 'Contribution for equity participation' or 'Quota subscription to increase paid-in capital' and referenced in the beneficiary descriptor with the S.A.'s full corporate name and RUC. Intercompany loans must be documented with a formal loan agreement and entered as 'Loan disbursement – intercompany financing' to ensure proper tax registration and deductible interest treatment. Third-party customer receipts for SaaS subscriptions are classified as 'computer software fees' or 'cloud software subscription charges' under the IMF BPM6 service account, and may be received via Stripe Billing (via integrated banking partners) or Wise B2B into the operational account. For initial capital funding, the bank will request a sworn declaration of source of funds and a board resolution approving the contribution; the absence of a central bank reduces the official approval burden, but the SBP's AML rules require the presenting bank to verify the origin of funds for any wire above US$10,000.Outbound trade and operating disbursements are cleared using the following rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct interbank transfers to corporate suppliers, subcontractors, and licensing partners; (ii) Commercial letters of credit (UC, UCP 600) for high-value equipment or intellectual property acquisitions that require documentary protection, though SaaS services generally use standard MT103 remittances; (iii) local ACH clearing (ACH Panama / Sistema de Pagos Electrónicos Nacionales, SPEN) for domestic fund transfers in Balboa/USD; and (iv) global FinTech rails (Wise B2B, Stripe Treasury) for low-cost cross-border settlement to overseas accounts, provided the counterparty bank is not in a prohibited jurisdiction. MT103 payment messages must contain a clear invoice reference and the relevant ISIC 6201 service description to reduce compliance friction. Outbound dividends and profit distributions are executed as 'Dividend payment to shareholders' MT103 transfers, subject to the withholding tax regime (5% on dividends from Panamanian-source income, if any, but exempt for profits taxed under the territorial system in many cases) and accompanied by a stamped corporate resolution and audited financial statements.Panama's full-dollar legal system eliminates the need for USD/PAB hedging; however, for a B2B SaaS exporter invoicing in EUR, GBP, or LATAM currencies, Panamanian corporates may access cross-currency forwards (FX Forwards) offered by international licensed banks (e.g., Bac Panama, Citibank, or Global Bank) subject to bilateral ISDA/CSA master agreements.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are permissible for settlement of Latin American ALM-related residual risk, provided the NDF is booked and cleared with an external OTC counterparty authorized by the SBP and not for speculative purpose, but they are rarely used due to USD-centric operating model.Cross-currency swaps (USD/EUR, USD/GBP) are available for longer-duration balance-sheet hedging of licensed software royalty streams, documented under ISDA and CSD rules; require clear economic justification and compliance with SBP anti-speculation interpretations.Interest rate swaps and plain-vanilla currency swaps may be executed with any SBP-licensed financial entity domiciled in Panama or a qualifying international bank, provided that the end-user documentation includes a resolution from the Board of Directors authorizing the derivative transaction.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 250,000 aggregated annual intercompany transactions with non-resident related parties trigger mandatory Local File, Master File, and annual TP disclosure in the Form 109 TP annex; lower procedures apply below threshold but arm's-length evidence must still be available

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Rules (EU-aligned, effective 2020) apply to entities conducting 'relevant activities' (banking, insurance, logistics, IP holding, headquarters, retail/wholesale); a B2B enterprise SaaS S.A. engaging in genuine software development and delivery is outside the core scope, but any intercompany IP ownership or turbine-license arrangement requires demonstrable physical presence, employees, and management in Panama, otherwise deductions are challenged and the S.A. is treated as a shell

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fraude Fiscal)

Statutory sanction: Fines of 2 to 10 times the evaded tax plus monthly default interest; criminal conviction carries imprisonment of 2 to 5 years under Panama Código Penal tax fraud provisions

Director personal liability: Directors jointly civilly liable for the unpaid tax; personal asset freezing, exit bans, and confiscation of proceeds of the evasion
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of all related-party deductions; recharacterization of transactions with 12.5%-20% gross withholding re-assessment; administrative penalty of 30%-100% of adjusted tax; registry suspension and potential cancellation of the corporate personería

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil: directors and resident agent personally liable for tax restitution, fines, and civil damages; travel bans may be imposed during prosecution
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to USD 200,000 per violation under Law 23 of 2015; enhanced AML scrutiny and possible FINCEN/FATF-list consequences; account suspension and compulsory liquidation of the S.A.

Director personal liability: Criminal liability for money laundering predicates (up to 5 years' imprisonment); directors subject to financial restitution to defrauded creditors and restricted from leaving Panama
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DGI adjustment of taxable income; 10% penalty on the corrected tax plus 30%-80% additional administrative sanction; interest compounding until full restitution

Director personal liability: Directors signing tax returns remain personally liable for the additional tax where the adjustment results from their negligence or willful misstatement; designation as co-debtor in collection proceedings
