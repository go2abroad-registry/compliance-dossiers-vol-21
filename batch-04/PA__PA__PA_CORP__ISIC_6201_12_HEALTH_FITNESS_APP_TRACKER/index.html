Tax ID Application Infrastructure

E-government filing portal

For a Panama Sociedad Anónima (S.A.), the statutory RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) is obtained by filing the grantor's public incorporation deed (Escritura Pública) and the DGI registration application with the corporate name certification, bylaws, appointment of directors, and resident agent details. Foreign shareholders are permitted; the application is signed by the authorized legal representative and submitted through the DGI state electronic platform or physically at DGI service centers. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity classification election. All Sociedades Anónimas are automatically treated as juridical persons subject to entity-level taxation on Panama-source income only. The optional small-taxpayer simplified regime (annual gross income ≤ USD 36,000) may be elected for income tax purposes, but does not alter entity classification; special territorial economic zone elections (e.g., SEM, City of Knowledge) may apply separately.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold for general ITBMS registration; every person undertaking taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama must register with the DGI. The small-taxpayer simplification threshold of USD 36,000 applies to administrative burden reduction, not to registration exemption. Non-resident digital service suppliers must register upon first taxable supply to a Panama-resident consumer, subject to de minimis administrative rules.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported from Panama qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate provided the transfer is supported by a valid customs export declaration and the goods physically leave Panamanian territory. Services, including digital health and fitness application services, qualify for zero-rating if the service is provided to a non-resident recipient, the recipient's utilization of the service occurs entirely outside Panama, and the service is not partially performed within Panama. The supplier must maintain contemporaneous documentation showing the non-resident status of the customer, the offshore place of use or enjoyment, and the foreign currency consideration received.

  • Declaración Única Aduanera (Customs Export Declaration)
  • Bill of Lading (BL) or Airway Bill (AWB)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Respaldo de Divisas)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) for all taxpayers through the DGI-authorized clearance system or accredited private electronic invoicing service providers (PSFEs). Invoices must be transmitted to the DGI in real time using the official XML schema, carry a digitally generated verification hash and qualified electronic stamp, and include the ITBMS breakdown and RIS (Registro de Información del Sujeto) codes. The DGI validates the invoice, assigns a unique control number, and performs digital fiscal clearance prior to taxpayer issuance.

The DGI may require proof of export zero-rating during standard or targeted audits. Failure to produce the customs declaration, transport document, and FX inward remittance certificate within the statutory deadline (generally 180 days from shipment or service invoicing) triggers reversal of the zero rate, immediate assessment of unreported ITBMS at 7%, late interest, and surcharges. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims (including falsified bills of lading, over-invoicing, or fake non-resident certifications) are treated as tax evasion subject to administrative fines equal to 100% of the omitted tax, criminal penalties under the Panama Criminal Tax Offenses Act (Ley de Evasión Fiscal), including imprisonment up to 5 years, and a statutory clawback limitation period of 11 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a nominal accounting unit pegged at exactly one balboa per U.S. dollar and exists only as coinage. There are no restrictions on the purchase, sale, transfer, or remittance of foreign exchange. Non-resident shareholders may freely inject capital and remit profits, dividends, and proceeds without central bank approval. No prior licensing or exchange-control permit is required for foreign direct investment in a Panamanian S.A. engaged in software publishing and digital health application services. The only relevant hurdle is compliance with commercial bank AML/KYC documentation requests and, where applicable, tax payment or withholding obligations under the territorial tax system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama does not maintain an exchange-control regime, and there are no statutory surrender or conversion requirements for export receipts. Exporters, including providers of digital services, may retain foreign-currency earnings in Panamanian bank accounts, move them offshore, and use them for payments without mandatory conversion ratios or return timelines. This applies to USD and all other freely convertible currencies.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — Panama has no central bank; the SBP is the banking and monetary-system regulator under Panamanian banking law, while the United States dollar serves as the legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low direct viability. Panama has no separately regulated electronic money institution (EMI) licence comparable to the EU. Under the Panamanian Banking Law, only licensed banks are authorised to take deposits or intermediate public funds. Offshore digital EMIs may not service Panamanian clients as deposit-taking institutions or operate local settlement accounts without a banking licence. They may, however, act as payment facilitation or white-label software providers to a licensed local bank under a written outsourcing or business-correspondent arrangement, subject to SBP AML/CFT approval and the local bank retaining full responsibility for the customer relationship. For the specific Health Fitness App Tracker, the practical onboarding route remains a tier-one Panamanian commercial bank with robust USD clearing, supplemented by Apple IAP and Google Play Billing settlement via their global acquiring networks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections for a Panamanian S.A. are executed through the international banking network in USD. Recommended routing: remitting bank → U.S. correspondent bank (e.g., JP Morgan Chase, Bank of America, or Citibank) → Panamanian beneficiary bank's SWIFT/BIC → the company's operational account. The wire must include the beneficiary's account name and number, the company's SAP/Inscripción, and a narrative in the SWIFT MT103 field 70 stating 'Equity Contribution to PA_CORP S.A. under corporate resolution; no prior FX authorization required.' For amounts above USD 25,000, the receiving Panamanian bank will request the corporate resolution authorising the capital increase, a share-subscription register or stock ledger, the notarised certificate of the registered capital, and identification and ultimate beneficial ownership documentation. No filing or registration with any exchange-control authority is required; the funds are freely available for operational use and settlement of trade obligations.Outbound trade disbursements are cleared through Panamerican and international USD rails. Domestic USD transfers use cheque clearing and the local automated clearing house managed by the Panamanian Bank Association and Banco Nacional de Panamá. Cross-border trade settlements use SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments, documentary letters of credit issued by the purchaser's bank through SWIFT MT700 and governed by UCP600, and SWIFT MT202 for bank-to-bank reimbursement.
