Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) — Commercial Registration Department, through the Invest Easy portal (business.gov.om).

Governing statute

Commercial Companies Law of the Sultanate of Oman (Royal Decree No. 18/2019, as amended by Royal Decree No. 56/2021), Part on Single Person Companies; Foreign Investment Law (Royal Decree No. 50/2019); Commercial Registration Law (Royal Decree No. 3/1974, as amended) and the Executive Regulations (Ministerial Decision No. 99/2020).

Formation / constitutional filings

Arabic-language Articles of Association of the Single Person Company, notarized before an Omani Notary Public and attested by MoCIIP, together with the Commercial Registration Application, Capital Deposit Certificate, and supporting corporate governance signatures.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 40 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum share capital for an SPC after Royal Decree No. 56/2021; the sole foreign shareholder must declare a capital amount adequate for the digital publishing activity and prove that the full declared amount is paid up and held in a blocked pre-incorporation escrow bank account at a licensed Omani bank, with the Capital Deposit Certificate filed at incorporation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Royal Oman Police — Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA); structural load and occupancy inspection certificate from a licensed building engineer; occupational safety and health clearance under the Ministry of Labour, covering emergency evacuation plans, smoke detectors, tested fire extinguishers, electrical load safety, and employee occupational health cards for the Oman-based workforce.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum share capital for an SPC after Royal Decree No. 56/2021; the sole foreign shareholder must declare a capital amount adequate for the digital publishing activity and prove that the full declared amount is paid up and held in a blocked pre-incorporation escrow bank account at a licensed Omani bank, with the Capital Deposit Certificate filed at incorporation.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OmanTax), established by Royal Decree No. 33/2023 as successor to the Secretariat General of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance; official e-services portal: https://taxoman.gov.om. Customs matters are administered by Royal Oman Police Customs through the Bayan single-window system.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC): (1) incorporate with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR); (2) open a user account on the Oman Tax Authority portal (taxoman.gov.om); (3) submit the unified Tax Registration application attaching the CR, constitutive documents, power of attorney, legalized shareholder identification, and Omani bank account confirmation; (4) undergo OTA verification and receive the single Tax Identification Number (TIN) that serves for both Income Tax and VAT. Non-resident platforms supplying electronic services to Omani consumers must register or appoint a tax representative where the applicable threshold is exceeded.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. The foreign-owned Omani SPC is a restricted-liability juridical person under the Omani Companies Law (Royal Decree No. 18/2019); it is a resident taxpayer subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide taxable income under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (as amended), with no pass-through or tax-transparent status. It is a mainland SPC and does not enjoy free-zone income tax holiday benefits unless licensed within SEZAD Duqm or another designated tax-exempt zone. — For a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC): (1) incorporate with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR); (2) open a user account on the Oman Tax Authority portal (taxoman.gov.om); (3) submit the unified Tax Registration application attaching the CR, constitutive documents, power of attorney, legalized shareholder identification, and Omani bank account confirmation; (4) undergo OTA verification and receive the single Tax Identification Number (TIN) that serves for both Income Tax and VAT. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The foreign-owned Omani SPC is a restricted-liability juridical person under the Omani Companies Law (Royal Decree No. 18/2019); it is a resident taxpayer subject to entity-level corporate income tax on worldwide taxable income under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (as amended), with no pass-through or tax-transparent status. It is a mainland SPC and does not enjoy free-zone income tax holiday benefits unless licensed within SEZAD Duqm or another designated tax-exempt zone.

Available election forms

None applicable. Oman law contains no check-the-box or partnership-transparency election; the SPC cannot elect to be disregarded, fiscally transparent, or treated as a partnership for Omani tax purposes. No domestic hybrid mismatch elections are available, and Oman has not yet enacted OECD Pillar Two/GloBE implementing legislation for in-scope multinational groups.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration is triggered when annual taxable supplies reach or exceed OMR 500,000; voluntary registration is permitted from OMR 250,000. Non-resident suppliers of digital services to Omani consumers are registrable without threshold where the place of supply is Oman and no local establishment exists.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is claimable for: (a) supplies of goods physically exported from Oman, provided the supplier holds conclusive evidence of removal from Oman within the statutory period; and (b) supplies of services, including serialized digital webnovel subscriptions and interactive reading app content, to a non-resident recipient where the Omani place-of-supply rules attribute the supply outside Oman or the recipient is not entitled to input VAT recovery in Oman. App-store sales settled through Apple IAP or Google Play Billing are generally treated as supplies to the app platform operators; direct international B2B licensing requires executed contracts and IP-ownership evidence. Supplies made to Omani end-consumers remain subject to 5% VAT without any offshore escape.

  • Validated Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) bearing the customs registration number and exporter SPC legal name
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Waybill naming the SPC as shipper and evidencing physical removal from Oman
  • Commercial invoice and packing list matched to the export declaration and underlying subscription or licence contracts
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a licensed Omani bank proving receipt of foreign-currency export proceeds
  • WebNovel platform transaction logs, app-store settlement statements, and IP licensing ledgers substantiating zero-rated digital-service export turnover
  • VAT return export-attachment schedule reconciling zero-rated supplies to customs, banking, and platform settlement evidence

No mandatory real-time e-invoicing or fiscal-data clearance API is currently in force in Oman. VAT-registered suppliers must nevertheless issue progressive numbered tax invoices in Arabic/English carrying all prescribed VAT fields, retain full auditable transaction records, and file VAT returns electronically through the Oman Tax Authority portal with customs and bank attachments as required. The OTA is progressively evaluating a phased e-invoicing framework; interim compliance requires structured XML/PDF archival and no digital stamping is yet mandated.

The Oman Tax Authority conducts risk-based post-clearance and refund audits of zero-rated export claims. Failure to produce the mandatory substantiation bundle within the record-retention window triggers retrospective VAT clawback at 5%, accompanied by statutory late-payment penalties and administrative fines of up to OMR 10,000 per document, declaration, or invoice violation. Where deliberate evasion or fraudulent zero-rating is established, the SPC faces fines of up to three times the tax evaded, criminal prosecution under Royal Decree No. 121/2020, suspension of the tax clearance certificate, and loss of future export-refund eligibility.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains a de facto open current account and largely liberalised capital account. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the US Dollar (USD) at a fixed rate of OMR 0.3845 per USD 1.00, eliminating bilateral currency conversion risk for USD-denominated flows. Capital account convertibility is generally unrestricted for foreign direct investment, but all inward and outward transfers are routed through CBO-licensed banks and are subject to anti-money-laundering (AML), sanctions screening, and customer due diligence controls. Profit remittances by a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) are permitted without exchange control approval, provided the SPC has valid Commercial Registration (CR), filed audited financial statements, settled any corporate income tax liabilities under the Oman Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009 as amended), and maintains a tax clearance certificate (TCC) where required. Repatriation of liquidation proceeds is likewise permitted after deregistration and clearance of all dues.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory obligation under Omani exchange control regulations requiring mandatory surrender or conversion of export proceeds into OMR. Exporters may retain foreign currency receipts in their operational accounts, but banks may require supporting documentation such as invoices, airway bills, bills of lading, and proof of service delivery for file-based audit readiness. For digital services and subscription receipts, the CBO does not prescribe a specific conversion timeline or ratio; however, all foreign currency receipts must be credited through a licensed Omani bank and reported in the bank's periodic regulatory returns. Practical policy is to convert or hold foreign currency as dictated by treasury strategy, subject to the bank's in-house policy requiring a trade or service transaction trail for remittances.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) and virtual account providers are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman to hold OMR or to act as a payment settlement institution in Oman. CBO's regulatory perimeter requires any entity engaged in payment services, remittances, or e-money issuance in Oman to obtain a CBO Payment Service Provider (PSP) licence under the National Payment Systems Act. Offshore EMIs can only be used as a non-settlement aggregation layer for inbound foreign-currency microtransactions (e.g., App Store or Google Play proceeds) and must eventually route cleared funds through a licensed Omani bank account for corporate operational purposes. Reliance on an offshore EMI for principal treasury concentration is not legally viable; it creates unlicensed money service business exposure under Omani law and triggers adverse KYC findings.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Offshore digital revenue platform (Apple/Google) with cross-border micro-transactions and non-face-to-face driver.

Root cause: Offshore digital revenue platform (Apple/Google) with cross-border micro-transactions and non-face-to-face driver.

Defense evidence: Provide (i) CBO-licensed bank account statements showing the lawful inflow trail; (ii) executed platform agreements from Apple Distribution International and Google Ireland Ltd, including payment entity identity; (iii) monthly settlement reports itemising user subscription transactions by jurisdiction; (iv) a documented flow-of-funds matrix from App Store/Google Play to the Oman SPC; and (v) an external audit opinion confirming provenance of digital content revenue and absence of prohibited activities.
Foreign natural-person UBO with multiple residencies or tax statuses triggering enhanced due diligence.

Root cause: Foreign natural-person UBO with multiple residencies or tax statuses triggering enhanced due diligence.

Defense evidence: Submit (i) notarised and apostilled passport copy; (ii) proof of residential address in the country of tax residence; (iii) an up-to-date tax residence certificate (TRC) from the relevant tax authority; (iv) a clean criminal record certificate from the country of residence issued within the last three months; and (v) a personal source-of-wealth statement supported by bank account statements or employment/divestment documentation.
High-risk jurisdiction exposure in App Store / Google Play user payment flows.

Root cause: High-risk jurisdiction exposure in App Store / Google Play user payment flows.

Defense evidence: Provide (i) an AML risk assessment of the webnovel platform's user-base countries; (ii) proof of platform-level user identity verification procedures and device fingerprinting; (iii) rejection logs for sanitised jurisdictions; (iv) sanctions screening matrices demonstrating no CBO/UN/OFAC/UK sanctions overlap; and (v) a contractual commitment to freeze and report any suspicious transaction to the Omani Financial Intelligence Unit (OFIU) and the designated Compliance Officer.
Capital injection for SPC minimum capital or subsequent shareholder loan without clear economic purpose.

Root cause: Capital injection for SPC minimum capital or subsequent shareholder loan without clear economic purpose.

Defense evidence: Prepare (i) a board resolution covering the paid-in capital amount and loan terms; (ii) notarised shareholders' agreement and amended Memorandum of Association if capital is increased; (iii) investor funds source documentation including bank credit path and swift MT103 references; (iv) audited financial projections demonstrating the SPC's funding need; and (v) a statement from the Oman tax advisor confirming compliance with thin capitalisation provisions, transfer-pricing requirements, and withholding tax rules under the Oman Tax Law.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital and shareholder loans into an Omani SPC are routed through the SWIFT network via a licensed Omani bank and the Central Bank of Oman clearing system. Recommended protocol: (i) send from the investor's bank using purpose code 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' or 'Capital Contribution' with payment reference matching the SPC's Commercial Registration (CR) number; (ii) comply with remitter KYC by ensuring the sending bank includes originator legal name, full address, account number, and the investor's tax identification number; (iii) prior to the initial transfer, the SPC should execute a formal 'Advance Intimation Letter' to the receiving Omani bank identifying the expected amount, date, currency, and economic purpose; (iv) the bank will verify the SPC's CR, articles of association, central bank registration, and ownership structure; (v) funds above OMR 10,000 or equivalent are reported by the Omani bank through the CBO regulatory reporting gateway; and (vi) incoming funds must not be structured to avoid reporting thresholds. Shares in a foreign-owned Omani SPC do not require prior capital control approval, but the SPC must file the actual share capital inflow with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) and update the CR if any capital alteration is required. For shareholder loans, execute a written loan agreement, maintain arm's-length interest, and comply with transfer-pricing documentation thresholds to avoid interest disallowance and 10% withholding tax on interest, royalties, or services paid offshore under the prevailing Oman Tax Law provisions.Outbound treasury and trade settlements from an Omani SPC use the established clearing rails of the Central Bank of Oman's Real-Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) system for OMR obligations and the SWIFT network for cross-border USD and major currency payments. For operational, royalty, licensing, content acquisition, and subcontracting payments, banks execute SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers with full KYC and sanctions screening. Documentary trade settlement is conducted via irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) issued by Omani banks, with MT707 amendment, MT710/MT720 negotiation/transfer, and MT734 refusal notices where applicable. For digital service providers and content licensing counterparties, the Omani bank will require an invoice, signed contract, withholding tax clearance or certificate of residency for treaty relief, and valuation support. Profit distribution to a foreign shareholder can be paid as dividend only after audited financial statements are approved by the shareholder and after the SPC obtains a tax clearance certificate; the remitting bank will require the corporate resolution, audited accounts, and confirmation that the tax position is fully reconciled. There are no exchange control surrender rules for outbound settlement, but cross-border payments above OMR 50,000 require the customer to complete a foreign exchange transaction form and the bank to retain the underlying trade contract in its compliance file.FX Forwards: Delivered forwards between OMR and USD are permitted when an underlying genuine commercial, investment, or trade exposure exists, provided the tenor does not exceed 12 months and the counterparty is a CBO-licensed bank.FX Swaps: Cross-currency OMR/USD spot-offset swaps and short-term rollovers are permissible for managing liquidity mismatches between subscription receipts and disbursement cycles, subject to the bank's internal limit framework and CBO derivatives governance requirements.Currency Swaps: Long-dated OMR/USD currency swaps are permissible on a documented basis where the SPC has a demonstrable hedge for a capital project, asset purchase, or multi-year distribution obligation, with banks adhering to CBO's risk-management directives.Interest Rate Swaps (IRS): Permissible where the SPC has taken a floating-rate credit facility and requires fixed-rate certainty in its P&L, though such swaps are generally bundled with an Islamic finance or conventional treasury product by the Omani bank.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs): NDFs referencing OMR are not practically available or CBO-sanctioned as a standard onshore instrument due to the fixed peg and deep USD/OMR liquidity; any offshore NDF would be executed outside the CBO-licensed banking framework and is not recommended for treasury compliance.Plain-Vanilla FX Spot: Spot trades up to USD two million equivalent may be executed with same-day value without documentation; larger or frequent trades require an underlying trade/service or capital-flow dossier.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% standard corporate income tax (CIT) rate for commercial and industrial activities under Royal Decree No. 9/2023, effective for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2024; a marginal 17% rate applies to taxable income exceeding OMR 1,000,000. The 55% hydrocarbon/mining rate is not applicable to a foreign-owned webnovel subscription platform.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%. Oman imposes no dividend withholding tax on profit distributions by an Omani SPC to its sole foreign corporate or individual shareholder. After-tax profits may be remitted freely to offshore bank accounts without additional Omani WHT, tax clearance certificate, or exchange-control approval, provided the SPC has discharged its CIT obligations.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four (4) months after the close of the tax year; for a 31 December year-end, the return and full payment are due on or before 30 April. A discretionary extension of up to two (2) months may be granted only upon a reasoned written application submitted before the original deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when the aggregate value of related-party transactions in a tax year exceeds OMR 500,000. Master File: mandatory when the worldwide group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 50,000,000 and the local transaction threshold is triggered; documentation must be produced within 30 days of a Tax Authority request. The Executive Regulations of RD 9/2023 adopt the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines (2022).

Economic substance scope: Oman applies an economic substance doctrine through the General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) in RD 9/2023, the Commercial Companies Law beneficial-ownership filing obligation, and anti-shell jurisprudence in the Unified Tax Agreement context. The foreign-owned SPC must evidence a physical registered office in Oman, an Omani bank account, a locally appointed manager, board decisions taken in Oman, and tangible payroll or operational presence where subscription income is booked locally. Artificial structures lacking commercial purpose may be recharacterized and the income attributed to a deemed permanent establishment, subjecting the foreign shareholder to 15%-17% Omani CIT on the full revenue and exposing the SPC to strike-off from the Commercial Register.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Underreporting App-Store Revenue

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalty of 40% of the evaded CIT plus administrative fines up to OMR 5,000 per violation, monthly late-payment surcharge of 1%, criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment of up to 5 years and fines up to OMR 100,000 upon conviction, and reassessment using the Tax Authority's indirect audit methods.

Director personal liability: Directors and the Omani resident manager are jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall if they signed or approved false returns; the Tax Authority may obtain court-ordered freezing of personal bank accounts, seize assets, and impose travel bans pending investigation; conviction leads to personal restitution and imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment recharacterizing the intercompany transaction and computing deemed Omani profit at 15%-17%; penalty of 20% of the additional tax assessed, increased to 40% for deliberate manipulation; fixed fines up to OMR 5,000 for each unfiled document; disallowance of the underlying payment and gross-up of WHT.

Director personal liability: The Omani-registered manager is statutorily answerable for incorrect TP filings; the foreign parent's officers may be treated as de facto directors if they control transfer pricing policy, enabling veil-piercing and joint personal liability; criminal liability for fraudulent bookkeeping applies where profit-stripping is proved, with risk of imprisonment up to 3 years.
Shell Company and Artificial Substance / GAAR Avoidance

Statutory sanction: GAAR recharacterization disregarding the SPC's separate existence; the foreign shareholder is deemed to have a permanent establishment in Oman and is assessed at 15%-17% on total Omani-source revenue; 25%-40% civil penalty, 1% monthly late-payment surcharge, strike-off from the Commercial Register, and loss of separate legal personality limiting liability.

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the SPC is undercapitalized, funds are commingled, and formalities are ignored; the sole shareholder and nominal directors become jointly and severally liable for the full corporate tax debt and third-party claims, with civil asset seizure, personal bankruptcy exposure, and prohibition on re-entering the Omani market.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Reporting Default

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 10,000 per violation under the Commercial Companies Law and AML Law, suspension of the commercial registration, classification of the SPC as high-risk by Omani financial institutions, freezing of bank accounts, and blocking of dividend remittances until remediation.

Director personal liability: The registered manager and any nominee director face personal criminal liability under Omani AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016), including imprisonment up to 7 years and fines up to OMR 25,000 each for knowingly facilitating beneficial-ownership concealment; disqualification from serving as a director or manager in any GCC jurisdiction follows conviction.
Withholding Tax Default on Cross-Border Payments

Statutory sanction: The uncollected WHT is grossed-up and collected from the SPC as statutory agent; penalty equal to 100% of the unrecovered WHT for non-compliant payments; interest at 1% per month from the due withholding date; disallowance of 50%-100% of the related deduction upon repeated violation.

Director personal liability: A director or manager who conspires with the non-resident payee to circumvent WHT becomes personally liable for the grossed-up amount plus the 40% fraud penalty and may be criminally prosecuted for aiding tax evasion; the Tax Authority may issue a travel ban against the responsible officer and execute against personal assets where corporate assets are insufficient.
