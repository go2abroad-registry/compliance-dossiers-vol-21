Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá (Public Registry of Panama) - Sección Mercantil (Mercantile Section), General Directorate of Registries, Panama City

Governing statute

Ley No. 32 de 26 de febrero de 1927 (General Law of Sociedades Anónimas of the Republic of Panama), as amended; together with the supplementary corporate provisions of the Commercial Registry framework. Governing sections cover the execution of the notarized deed of incorporation, the issuance and subscription of shares without a statutory minimum capital, the designation of directors and officers, and the mandatory appointment of a resident agent.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública No. ___ (Notarized Public Deed of Incorporation / Articles of Incorporation) executed before a Panamanian Notary Public and inscribed in the Mercantile Folio (Ficha) of the Registro Público de Panamá

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up or authorized capital is required. A single no-par-value share subscribed by each incorporator is sufficient to constitute the company. There is no escrow, deposit, or capital payment account requirement at formation. A variable capital clause (capital variable) may be adopted in the deed.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety inspection and Certificate of Occupancy by the Benemérito Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá for terminal and workshop premises; structural and seismic load clearance by the Ministerio de Obras Públicas (MOP) for warehouses or parking structures exceeding permitted loads; occupational health and workplace clearance from the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL) with worker registration at the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS); vehicle safety and load-dimension compliance certified by ATTT.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up or authorized capital is required. A single no-par-value share subscribed by each incorporator is sufficient to constitute the company. There is no escrow, deposit, or capital payment account requirement at formation. A variable capital clause (capital variable) may be adopted in the deed.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; official e-portal 'Sede Electrónica' at https://sede.dgi.gob.pa (RUC registration, tax filings, ITBMS returns, and electronic invoicing 'Facturación Electrónica' services).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Panama S.A. (Sociedad Anónima): (1) notarize the incorporation deed and complete registration at the Registro Público de Panamá (obtain the Ficha and certificate of registration); (2) record the resident legal representative, directors/secretary, and officers; (3) obtain the municipal 'Aviso de Operación' from the competent municipality (e.g., Municipio de Panamá) via PanamaTramita; (4) file DGI Form D-01 'Registro Único de Contribuyente' in the Sede Electrónica attaching the public deed, Registro Público certification, identity and power of attorney of the legal representative, municipal Aviso, and AML/beneficial-ownership declaration; (5) DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) as a single statutory TIN covering income tax (ISR), ITBMS (VAT-equivalent), and withholding obligations; no separate VAT certificate or ID exists; (6) register fiscal domicile and e-invoicing credential in the Facturación Electrónica system. Timeline: 3-7 business days after the municipal Aviso; foreign documents require notarization and apostille under the Hague Convention.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid passthrough election exists; a Panama S.A. is a mandatory entity-level taxpayer under the territorial income tax regime. Available statutory elections: (a) SEM (Sede de Empresas Multinacionales) regime under Law 41 of 2007 and Executive Decree 140 of 2017 — an elective 5% ISR rate and ITBMS exemptions for qualifying multinational headquarters executing cross-border trucking contracts, invoicing, and fleet management for foreign affiliates; (b) 'Régimen Simplificado' (Law 66 of 2017) for taxpayers with gross annual income up to USD 200,000, replacing standard ISR/ITBMS compliance with a consolidated presumptive tax based on gross receipts for small trucking operators; (c) advance pricing agreements for related-party cross-border freight charges; (d) express annual election to segregate foreign-source active business income as non-taxable territorial income (no CFC rules apply; DGI anti-avoidance powers derived from Decree-Law 14 of 2010 restrict artificial profit diversion).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold: every person or entity performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panamanian territory must obtain an ITBMS RUC before commencing activities. Taxpayers with gross annual income equal to or below USD 200,000 may elect the simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' regime with quarterly consolidated presumptive taxation. Special ITBMS exemptions apply to Zona Libre de Colón operators and SEM headquarters.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

ITBMS zero-rating (0%) applies to: (a) exports of tangible goods evidenced by a customs export declaration (DUA) in the SIECAE platform; and (b) export of services, i.e., services rendered from Panama to a non-resident client provided the economic benefit or use occurs abroad. For cross-border TIR trucking: the international freight segment (Panama border exit to foreign destination) qualifies as zero-rated export of services when the invoice is issued to a foreign principal/consignee, the TIR Carnet and CMR consignment note prove the frontier exit, and freight payment is received in USD from abroad. The Panama domestic pick-up/delivery leg constitutes a domestic 7% ITBMS service and must be separately invoiced. Zero-rating is claimable only after the export declaration reaches effective-exit status in Panama customs records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) with effective-exit status verified in Panama's SIECAE/IMA customs platform
  • TIR Carnet and CMR consignment note (road freight title equivalent to a bill of lading) evidencing the border crossing and foreign delivery
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 credit advice demonstrating USD payment received from the foreign principal or consignee

Mandatory electronic invoicing applies under DGI's 'Facturación Electrónica de Panamá' program (phased from 2019 through successive DGI resolutions). Large taxpayers have electronically issued all invoices since January 2022; medium and small taxpayers were progressively phased in through 2024, with nationwide mandatory use extended through 2025. Requirements: pre-registration of taxpayer data and invoice series in the Sede Electrónica; issuance through DGI-approved software, commercial ERP APIs, or the free DGI-hosted 'Solución Fácil' application; real-time or session-based batch transmission to the DGI for fiscal validation; each invoice receives a unique digital authorization code (string-based stamping) and a validation QR. Invoices lacking DGI validation are non-deductible and non-creditable and expose the issuer to fines under the Código Fiscal.

DGI audit statute of limitations extends 5 years (up to 7 years for non-filing or fraud, with no limitation for criminal prosecution). The taxpayer bears the burden of proving the foreign-source/export character. Where zero-rating is rejected, DGI recharacterizes the revenue as domestic: 7% ITBMS is assessed retroactively plus a statutory late-payment surcharge (10% if settled within two months of the due date and increasing thereafter) and monthly default interest fixed by the MEF. Intentional fraudulent export claims attract a fine of 100% of the evaded tax (Código Fiscal Article 785) and criminal tax fraud exposure under Decree-Law 14 of 2010, potentially with imprisonment up to 5 years. Ancillary consequences: revocation of 'contribuyente excelente' status, exclusion from SEM regime benefits and public procurement for up to 3 years, and suspension of ITBMS export-refund/credit claims.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized, open-economy monetary regime with unrestricted convertibility across both current and capital account items. Profit remittances, dividend distributions, interest payments, and repatriation of invested capital are not subject to exchange licensing, repatriation quotas, convertibility taxes, or prior central bank approval. The practical bottleneck for foreign shareholders is purely bank-level compliance: source-of-funds verification, updated beneficial-ownership attestations under Ley 254 de 2021, and complete documentary support required by SBP's enhanced due-diligence framework. No statutory hurdle to convert USD into any offshore currency exists for treasury purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Because Panama has no central bank exchange-control apparatus, export proceeds from cross-border TIR trucking operations are not subject to mandatory surrender, forced conversion, minimum retention ratios, or any prescribed repatriation timeline. Freight receivables in USD and other currencies may be held abroad or in Panamanian banks without restriction. Nevertheless, prudent AML practice requires that export and transport invoices, CMR/TIR manifests, customs or CBP clearance records, and corresponding SWIFT MT103 credits be retained to substantiate the lawful origin of all operational inflows during bank surveillance reviews.

Central bank supervision: Panama maintains no central bank and no autonomous monetary authority; legal tender is the U.S. Dollar, with the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) circulating only as fractional coinage at a locked 1:1 parity. The banking and financial system is supervised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP / Superintendency of Banks), while the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) functions as the national financial intelligence unit. The Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent of the State and offers public banking services, but it performs no central-bank monetary or exchange-control functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-Low for fully remote foreign digital EMI-only arrangements; medium under a supervised partnership model. Panama has no dedicated EMI licensing passport for offshore fintech providers, and Decree-Law No. 9 of 1998 (banking licensing law) requires that any entity taking deposits or executing payment accounts on behalf of the public obtain SBP authorization. A foreign fintech EMI cannot independently open local settlement accounts for a Panamanian regulated entity without a bank-agent or outsourcing arrangement with an SBP-licensed bank. The practical viable structure is a Tier-1 Panamanian bank as the statutory clearing bank, with a foreign fintech EMI used peripherally for fleet fuel-card program administration and driver prepaid cards; in that case, the EMI transaction flow must be contractually subordinate to the Panamanian bank for treasury and regulatory-reporting purposes.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (top CIT bracket for taxable net income exceeding B/. 1,500,000; first B/. 1,500,000 of taxable income taxed at 15%; Balboa at 1:1 parity with USD)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends distributed by the Panamanian S.A. out of Panamanian-source taxed profits, payable before distribution; reduced to 5% for qualified Multinational Headquarters (SEM) licensees; distributions sourced from foreign-source profits that were never within the Panamanian tax base are outside the scope of Panamanian dividend tax, subject to DGI anti-abuse review.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after the fiscal year-end. For a calendar-year S.A. the hard deadline is 31 March of the following year; no automatic extension exists, and late filing immediately triggers statutory surcharges.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filed by 30 June of the calendar year following the closed fiscal year for a 31 December year-end; short-term extensions may be granted only upon justified, documented cause.

Part I - Identificación RUC of the Panamanian S.A., fiscal year, global transfer pricing policy statement, ultimate parent jurisdiction and identification of the TIR operator's related enterprises. The non-resident controlling shareholder, whether the foreign trucking parent or private foundation, must be identified by jurisdiction of incorporation, tax residence, and beneficial ownership. Non-filing or incomplete filing of Form 400: fines from B/. 5,000 to B/. 25,000 per fiscal year plus the 10% monthly recargo; failure to produce contemporaneous documentation permits the DGI to apply presumption-based adjustments, deny intercompany expense deductions and recharacterize uncovered payments as constructive dividends.
Part II - Operaciones con Partes Relacionadas Aggregate value of related-party transactions: intercompany diesel and spare-part purchases, vehicle lease or rental payments, subcontracted transport, management fees, shareholder loans and fuel-card clearing settlements. Disclose each related party, jurisdiction, transaction type and exact B/. amount; attach Swift wire confirmations and fuel-card clearing statements as supporting evidence. For a Panamanian trucking operator acting as the tested party, TNMM using operating margin on total freight revenue is the recommended method; a CUP based on third-party TIR freight per kilometer may be used for fuel pass-through transactions. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Form 400 and contemporaneous Local File documentation are required when the taxpayer's aggregate gross receipts exceed B/. 3,000,000 in the preceding fiscal year; an additional documentation obligation arises when intercompany transactions exceed B/. 250,000 in a single year; a Master File is expected where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds B/. 13,000,000 or the Panamanian entity operates under the SEM regime. B/. denotes the Panamanian Balboa at 1:1 parity with USD.

Economic substance scope: Panama applies a management-and-control and territorial-source test rather than a Gulf-style codified ESR. Nevertheless, to sustain treaty access, territorial exemption and the S.A.'s integrity, the cross-border TIR operator must demonstrate real Panamanian substance: resident agent, local board resolutions, a Panamanian dispatch and accounting office, local bank accounts with Swift/fuel-card clearing, and the operational exercise of the TIR authorization. A letterbox trucking conduit whose board sits abroad, whose treasury is directed offshore and whose fleet is controlled by a foreign parent will be recharacterized under substance-over-form, denied treaty relief, and have its freight income reattributed to Panama at the 25% rate, with consequent beneficial-owner registry disclosure.
