Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) – Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas de Panamá; electronic portal: Sede Electrónica (sede-electronica.dgi.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must: (i) engage a resident attorney and file organizational documents with the Registro Público de Panamá; (ii) obtain Aviso de Operación if conducting activities requiring a municipal business license; (iii) apply for RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) through DGI Sede Electrónica by submitting corporate identification, resident-agent proof, registered office credentials, and authenticated powers of attorney; (iv) complete the DGI registration forms to activate ITBMS and income-tax obligations. A non-resident company without a permanent establishment may obtain a non-resident RUC only if it derives Panama-source income; failure to register before commencement of taxable activity attracts fines under the Código Fiscal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists under Panamanian corporate law. Foreign entities may elect to operate as a registered branch ('sucursal') or form a local Sociedad Anónima (S.A.). Under Law 41 of 2007, a qualifying multinational headquarters (SEM) may elect a reduced 10% income tax rate and special ITBMS treatment for covered headquarters services. Domestic taxpayer classification remains territorial: Panama-source income is taxed at 25%, foreign-source income is excluded. No statutory anti-hybrid election regime applies, though transfer-pricing documentation and substance requirements must be respected.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary turnover threshold – ITBMS registration (via RUC) is mandatory for every person or entity performing taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama, irrespective of volume. A simplified 'Pequeño Contribuyente' fixed-payment regime may apply to small taxpayers with annual gross receipts up to B/. 200,000 (Balboas / USD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is available for tangible goods physically exported from Panama when the exporter maintains proof of departure from the national customs territory. Panama-source services are zero-rated when the recipient is outside Panama and the service is economically used or consumed exclusively outside Panamanian territory; typical examples include offshore software/services, cargo transport ancillary services, and repair services on exported goods. Sales to Zona Libre de Colón are exempt from ITBMS if re-export is documented and completed within the free-trade zone regime.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUCA / DUS – submitted through ANA's SICA system)
  • Bill of Lading (ocean, air, or land transport evidence)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa / SWIFT credit advice showing FX repatriation)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Sales Contract or Purchase Order demonstrating economic substance and end-recipient identity

Electronic invoicing is mandatory. DGI Sede Electrónica operates the 'Facturación Electrónica en Línea' platform. Taxpayers must use DGI-authorized invoicing software that transmits documents to the DGI in real time, or use DGI's virtual invoicing web service. Invoices must include a unique validation code and QR fiscal stamp; suppliers must implement the DGI-approved API / web-service integration. Non-conforming invoices are not valid for ITBMS credit or expense deduction.

DGI and the National Customs Authority (ANA) jointly audit export transactions. The DGI has access to customs declarations, banking records, and electronic invoicing data. Unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating triggers immediate clawback of input VAT credits previously claimed, plus a surcharge of 10% per annum on the omitted tax and fines ranging from 10% to 200% of the tax due under Código Fiscal. Criminal liability may include disqualification of the legal representative, business closure, and imprisonment for up to six years for tax fraud under Law 38 of 2003.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama applies a full dollarization system, and both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully convertible with no exchange controls or central-bank approval requirements. There is no mandatory conversion into a local paper currency. Profit remittance abroad is legally unrestricted; the only treasury-level hurdle is the 10% dividend tax levied on distributions of Panamanian-source profits, which is withheld and settled by the paying entity. No additional FX convertibility barrier or repatriation permit applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender rules exist. Panamanian exporters are permitted to retain 100% of their USD proceeds in local bank accounts without any mandatory conversion, holding timeline, or repatriation requirement. The only compliance duty is the general AML framework, under which covered customers and transactions must be reported to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) when applicable thresholds are breached.

Central bank supervision: No dedicated central bank; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent for the Republic of Panama; regulation of licensed banks is undertaken by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). The United States dollar circulates as legal tender under a full dollarization regime, with the Panamanian balboa issued only as fractional coins.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderate — offshore digital EMIs and payment platforms may serve as supplementary collection/disbursement rails, but they cannot replace a licensed Panamanian banking relationship. Panama has no standalone EMI licensing law; virtual IBANs and payment accounts are only offered through partnership with licensed banks or under the International Banking Center umbrella. Legal constraints include compliance with Law 23 of 2015, the AML/CFT antinominal regime administered by the UAF, and the practical requirement for local correspondent bank settlement. For this low-risk manufacturing entity, an EMI may be viable for supplier payments and receivable collection, but its use should be restricted to secondary rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder-loan capital should be routed by SWIFT MT103 wire from the investor's bank to the Panama S.A. USD operational or capital escrow account at a licensed Panamanian bank. The wire must include the exact beneficiary name and account number, a purpose string such as 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION', 'EQUITY INJECTION', or 'SHAREHOLDER SUBORDINATED LOAN', and be preceded by a board/shareholder resolution authorizing the contribution and identifying the contributing party. For wires above USD 10,000, the receiving bank may request the injector's KYC file, source-of-funds evidence, and an updated corporate extract. No central-bank approval or exchange permit is required because the US dollar is legal tender and Panama imposes no capital controls.Outbound trade settlements for imported components and production machinery can be executed through SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for advance payments, open-account settlements, or under an irrevocable documentary commercial letter of credit (L/C) issued by a Panamanian commercial bank and confirmed through a US or Asian correspondent bank.
