Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama City; online portal: SITe (Sistema Integrado Tributario en Línea) at https://etsitio.mef.gob.pa; corporate/registry one-stop window: Paenlinea (https://www.paenlinea.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities must: (1) appoint a licensed resident agent (abogado idóneo) and maintain a fiscal address in Panama; (2) register the Sociedad Anónima (or file an apostilled certificate of incorporation and power of attorney) with the Registro Público de Panamá; (3) apply to DGI through SITe for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes), submitting the Escritura/estatutos, directors' identification, resident agent acceptance, object clause (ISIC 4661 – physical wholesale brokerage of mineral fuels), and estimated turnover; the RUC is the single TIN for both ISR and ITBMS; (4) obtain municipal Aviso de Operación; (5) register as an exporter with ANA (Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas) and in the DGI exporter registry to claim 0% export treatment and ITBMS refunds; (6) where annual turnover exceeds approximately B/.5 million, the account is migrated to DGI's Núcleo de Grandes Contribuyentes (Large Taxpayers Unit) with intensified e-filing and e-invoicing duties.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope — a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is a separate taxable corporation under the Código Fiscal; tax is imposed at 25% on net income from Panamanian-source activities only; foreign-source income is excluded; pass-through treatment is not available to S.A. corporations; optional preferential regimes and zone exemptions apply only upon election/agreement.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists. Available elections: (a) Régimen Simplificado de Tributación (Law 66/2016, Decree 84/2018) for small taxpayers with annual gross income ≤ B/.36,000 (services) or ≤ B/.200,000 (goods) — presumptive quarterly payments replacing ISR+ITBMS; (b) SEM (Multinational Headquarters) regime (Law 41/2007) — 5% ISR and 0% ITBMS on qualifying regional services, subject to MEF agreement; (c) Panama-Pacifico Special Economic Zone, Colon Free Zone, and City of Knowledge exemption packages; (d) tonnage tax election for international shipping under Law 8/2011, relevant only to affiliated vessel-owning SPVs rather than the broker entity. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available; Panama applies BEPS-oriented CFC rules (Law 29/2010) for outbound passive income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold in the General Regime: RUC registration, which includes ITBMS, is mandatory before any taxable supply. An alternative Simplified Regime (Law 66/2016) applies to small taxpayers with annual gross income ≤ B/.36,000 (services) or ≤ B/.200,000 (goods), with quarterly presumptive payments (3.5% services / 2% goods) in lieu of ISR+ITBMS; a wholesale marine-fuel broker will normally exceed the ceiling and be subject to the General Regime (7%).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to: (a) physical exports of goods leaving Panamanian customs territory, including marine fuel and refined distillates delivered as bunkers/provisions to vessels engaged in international traffic (Panama Canal transit or foreign-bound), evidenced by a Bunker Delivery Receipt (BDR) signed aboard; and (b) export of services rendered to non-residents where the benefit is realized wholly outside Panama, including brokerage/fee income from international oil trading arranged for non-resident principals. The exporter must hold RUC and exporter registration, issue 0% e-invoices, and maintain the documentation bundle. Cabotage deliveries within Panama, sales to domestic end-users, or local commission income do not qualify for the 0% rate (ITBMS 7% applies) and remain Panamanian-source income for ISR purposes.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA/Transmisión) filed through ANA's SICE platform evidencing departure from customs territory
  • Commercial invoice with 0% ITBMS rate indication and legal reference to the export exemption
  • Bill of Lading (cargo) or Bunker Delivery Receipt (BDR) with Port Log signature and tank survey certificate for ship stores
  • Bank Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificación Bancaria de Ingreso de Divisas) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • DGI ITBMS export register (Libro de Ventas de Exportación) extract and, if claiming a refund, the 'Solicitud de Devolución' form with reconciliation of input credits

Mandatory Electronic Invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) under Law 23/2016 and DGI Resolutions, phased by taxpayer groups; Large Taxpayers (Núcleo de Grandes Contribuyentes) and subsequently all General-Regime taxpayers must issue e-invoices. The taxpayer's ERP/billing software must connect to the DGI SITe fiscal platform via registered APIs/web services using XML schemas and a digital certificate; each invoice is transmitted in real time to DGI for clearance and, once validated, receives a unique authorization code and mandatory QR fiscal stamp that must appear on the PDF/printed invoice. Only DGI-validated e-invoices support ITBMS input-credit claims by B2B buyers; non-compliant issuers face rejection of invoices for credit, fines, and suspension.

DGI's assessment window is generally 5 years, extended where returns were not filed. If export proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days of shipment, or the customs/bank documentation is incomplete, the DGI recharacterizes the supply as taxable at 7%, assessing the tax with moratory interest, late-payment recargos (up to 50%), and fines. Fraudulent export zero-rating or refund claims attract fines of 100%-200% of the evaded or erroneously credited amounts, closure or suspension of operations, cancellation of exporter registration, joint liability of the resident agent and directors, and criminal tax-fraud prosecution under Decree-Law 76/2019 (custodial penalties for fraud exceeding B/.100,000 per fiscal year).

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unconditional convertibility; the U.S. dollar is legal tender, so no exchange-rate regime, no capital account quotas, no balance-of-payments registration, and no currency convertibility limitation apply to either current or capital transactions. Profit remittance is commercially unrestricted; the only fiscal frictions are a 10% withholding tax on dividend distributions and 12.5% withholding on certain outbound interest and royalties, which are tax matters, not FX controls. Practical hurdles are limited to SBP AML/CFT source-of-funds verification and OFAC/EU sanctions screening by U.S. correspondents in the clearing chain.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory deposit, surrender, or mandatory conversion requirement on export proceeds. Wholesale brokerage of marine fuel oil and refined products requires a hydrocarbon commercialization license from the Ministry of Energy, Dirección Nacional de Hidrocarburos; once licensed, the exporter may retain USD proceeds freely in demand deposit or treasury accounts, book income on territorial principles, and make offshore allocations without prior central bank approval. Conversion to Balboa is neither required nor needed because bank balances are ledger credits denominated in U.S. dollars at par.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Decreto Ley 2 de 2008; Panama has no monetary central bank because the economy is fully dollarized. Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and depositary of the State; the effective anchor institution is the U.S. Federal Reserve. The Balboa (PAB) exists only as a unit of account at 1:1 parity and circulates solely as coinage pursuant to Article 159 of the Political Constitution of Panama.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder funds flow via SWIFT MT103 from the remitting bank to the beneficiary's Capital Escrow Account at an SBP general-license bank, settled in USD through Fedwire/CHIPS via New York correspondents. The payment purpose field should use ISO 20022 purpose code 'CFDI' or 'SWIV' with memo 'CAPITAL INJECTION - PA_CORP'; the beneficiary bank will require a board resolution authorizing the capital increase, the share-emission ledger, and a Declaración de Origen de Fondos. No central bank approval, exchange declaration, or clearing permit is required for any amount. For wires above USD 1 million, pre-notification of two to three business days with the shareholder's enhanced due-diligence pack is recommended to avoid provisional holds. Funds are booked as paid-in capital; subsequent dividends on Panamanian-source profit incur 10% withholding.Settlements are executed in USD using SWIFT MT103/MT202 for open-account bunker and cargo payments; MT700/MT707 for documentary letters of credit issued by the buyer's bank; and MT760 for standby letters of credit issued in favor of refiners, suppliers, or storage operators (Pemex, Ecopetrol, Trafigura, Vitol, Gunvor, Glencore). Clearing occurs via CHIPS/Fedwire to the beneficiary's account at major correspondents in New York, Miami, or Houston; each message is list-screened in real time. The treasury control framework requires two authorized signatories or approved TMS release, callback verification for wires above USD 5 million, and archiving of the Bunker Delivery Note and invoice pack for post-settlement audit.Vanilla USD/PAB forward (synthetic only; rate is constitutionally fixed at 1:1, so the Balboa leg is purely a ledger convention).Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on USD/COP, USD/MXN, USD/BRL, or USD/CLP via international desks of SBP-licensed tier-1 banks, cash-settled in USD.Cross-currency swaps (USD vs EUR, USD vs GBP) for Rotterdam or Gibraltar cargo invoicing exposures.Vanilla FX options and zero-cost collars for regional freight and currency mismatches, tenors up to 12 months.Exchange-traded commodity futures and cleared swaps on ICE or CME through licensed broker-dealers; collateral posted in USD.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility for ISIC 4661 wholesale petroleum brokerage. Panama law creates no standalone EMI charter; fintech entrants need a full banking or specialized license under SBP supervision and the same AML/CFT duties as banks. Foreign digital EMIs (Wise, Mercury, Airwallex, Payoneer) generally refuse high-risk physical commodity traders and cannot issue LC, SBLC, or trade wires of bunker-scale ticket size. Such rails are acceptable only for incidental micro-opex below USD 10,000 per month and must never become the treasury backbone.
