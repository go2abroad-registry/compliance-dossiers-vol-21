Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance; taxpayer portal: Sistema de Facturación Electrónica and Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must appoint a resident legal agent, deliver apostilled constitutional documents and proof of a local registered address, and apply to the DGI for a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC). The RUC serves as the combined income tax and ITBMS registration identifier and is issued via DGI online offices or the TPV (Terminal de Punto de Venta) system.

Tax ID type & application path: — A foreign entity must appoint a resident legal agent, deliver apostilled constitutional documents and proof of a local registered address, and apply to the DGI for a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC). The RUC serves as the combined income tax and ITBMS registration identifier and is issued via DGI online offices or the TPV (Terminal de Punto de Venta) system.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election is available; a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is taxed as a juridical person. Panama applies statutory territorial sourcing rules, so foreign-source income is generally excluded from tax. Hybrid mismatch elections are not applicable in the domestic ITBMS or income tax framework. A simplified small taxpayer regime is available for entities with low annual gross receipts.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general registration threshold; all businesses carrying on taxable activities in Panama must register for ITBMS. A simplified small taxpayer regime applies to persons with annual gross receipts not exceeding B/. 300,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Panama to a foreign destination qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate. Offshore services rendered to non-residents and not used or consumed in Panamanian territory may also be zero-rated. The exporter must demonstrate the actual departure of goods from Panamanian customs territory and receipt of payment from abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Mercancías de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading (Conocimiento de Embarque)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Depósito o Transferencia Bancaria del Exterior)

Electronic invoicing is mandatory for all registered taxpayers. Invoices must be issued through DGI-authorized emission systems, transmitted in XML format to the DGI's Factura Electrónica platform in real time, carry a digital signature, and include a QR quick-response code for validation and fiscal clearance.

Misuse of export zero-rating constitutes tax evasion. Penalties include interest on underpaid tax, administrative fines of up to B/. 50,000, and surcharges of up to 200% of the omitted tax amount. Criminal prosecution under the Panamanian Tax and Criminal Codes is possible for fraudulent documentation. The DGI may retroactively claw back refunds for a statute-of-limitations period of at least five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains full convertibility of the Balboa (PAB) at parity with the U.S. dollar, and the U.S. dollar itself is legal tender. There are no current-account or capital-account exchange controls, no prior approval for foreign direct investment, no restrictions on profit remittance, and no requirement to convert earnings into local currency. The only practical hurdles in remitting dividends or repatriating capital are standard corporate formalities, documentary banking checks, and applicable Panamanian tax rules, specifically the 10% dividend tax on distributions derived from Panama-source taxable profit and the absence of tax on foreign-source income. Banks will still require full source-of-funds documentation and updated beneficial ownership declarations before releasing funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement in Panama, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no time limit for repatriation of export receipts. Exporters may retain 100% of proceeds in U.S. dollars in any Panamanian or foreign banking account and may use them freely for operations, imports, dividends, or reinvestment. The only compliance obligation is to maintain clear commercial and customs records (invoice, bill of lading, packing list, customs declaration) and to ensure that proceeds flow into the same legal entity's bank account so that banking AML reviews and DGI territorial tax filings can be reconciled.

Central bank supervision: No Panamanian central bank exists; Panama is fully dollarized under the Monetary Agreement with the United States. The de facto monetary policy is determined by the U.S. Federal Reserve. The domestic regulator with oversight of banking, foreign-exchange-related banking operations, and payment clearing is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). The Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas administers tax and legal-entity compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium with legal limitations. Panama has no standalone 'electronic money institution' (EMI) license; overseas digital EMIs cannot take deposits or settle against the local clearing system without a licensed Panamanian bank or registered payment processor. A foreign EMI may be used as a virtual account and spend-management layer, but it is not deposit-insured in Panama and cannot issue Panamanian IBAN-equivalent rails. For this frozen dim sum factory, the treasury backbone should be licensed Panamanian bank accounts, with any EMI used only for non-core supplier disbursements or petty operational flows, never as the principal capital or sales-receipts repository.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound U.S.-dollar capital injections should be routed by SWIFT MT103 to the Panama S.A.'s licensed operating or escrow account in Panama City. Although Panama imposes no exchange-control pre-approval or mandatory destination purpose code, the wire narrative must identify the economic substance, e.g., 'Capital contribution to Panama S.A. for frozen food factory project', and reference the notarized capital increase or share subscription resolution. The bank will verify the corporate name, Registered Public Registry filing, and source of funds. To ensure clean clearing, use a direct correspondent chain, avoid third-party consolidation accounts where possible, and ensure the ordering customer name matches the funding entity's KYB records. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding tax on gross dividends paid by a Panamanian S.A.; the rate is reduced to 5% when the profits distributed have been subject to, and effectively paid, the full 25% Panamanian corporate income tax at the company level. Dividends paid from profits that enjoyed an exemption or were taxable at a lower effective rate generally attract the 10% rate.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The corporate income tax return must be filed within three months after the close of the fiscal year. For a calendar-year taxpayer, the deadline is 31 March of the following fiscal year. Fiscal-year taxpayers may adopt a different annual period only with DGI prior authorization.

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 – RUC / Identificación del Contribuyente Enter the taxpayer’s 10-digit Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) number, legal name of the Sociedad Anónima, and fiscal year-end date. For a Panamanian S.A. owned by non-residents, the RUC is obtained through a resident fiscal representative or legal agent. The return must be signed by the legal representative with a valid Panama address. Intentional non-filing or fraudulent filing is treated as criminal tax evasion and can attract imprisonment from 2 to 5 years and fines up to PAB 25,000, in addition to reinstatement fees, inventory seizure, and cancellation of the factory’s operating licence.

Expenses must be supported by invoices legally issued in Panama or, for foreign suppliers, by commercial invoices with proof of payment through the banking system. Unsupported or non-documented related-party expenses are disallowed. Non-resident shareholders should ensure the factory does not shift deductible profits abroad through excessive management fees. Line 4 – ISR Causado al 25% Calculate the corporate income tax due at the 25% statutory rate on the net taxable income. Apply the territorial adjustment: any foreign-source income not related to Panama activities may be excluded before application of the 25% rate, but only if it is properly documented and recorded separately in the accounting books. Late filing penalties include a 10% surcharge on the unpaid tax, monthly interest at 1% or the applicable legal rate, and administrative fines ranging from PAB 50 to PAB 500 for minor filing defects. Intentional non-filing or fraudulent filing is treated as criminal tax evasion and can attract imprisonment from 2 to 5 years and fines up to PAB 25,000, in addition to reinstatement fees, inventory seizure, and cancellation of the factory’s operating licence.

Non-resident dividend or interest recipients may have had Panama withholding taxes withheld at source; such credits may be claimed only in the name of the Panama taxpayer, not the foreign beneficial owner. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Taxpayers with related-party transactions exceeding PAB 2,500,000 (Panamanian balboas, equivalent to USD) or with annual gross revenue or assets above PAB 2,500,000 are generally required to maintain and submit Annual Transfer Pricing Documentation (Local File). Master File and Country-by-Country Reporting obligations apply where the multinational group has consolidated revenue equal to or above USD 850,000,000. The documentation threshold must be evaluated annually, and the DGI may issue a formal request for the Local File even below the quantitative thresholds.

Economic substance scope: Panama’s Economic Substance Regulations (Ley 254 de 2021) apply to mobile-income relevant activities, such as headquarters, distribution and service centres, financing and leasing, insurance, shipping, and holding of intangible assets. A quick-freezing dim sum and dumpling factory is an industrial manufacturer and therefore does not automatically fall within the ESR relevant-activity list. However, the Panamanian territorial tax regime requires real economic nexus for recognition of tax residence and source rules: the factory must maintain premises, refrigerated production equipment, employees, board minutes, bank accounts, and inventory records in Panama. A shell arrangement in which manufacturing is performed abroad while the S.A. merely issues invoices or holds funds will be re-characterised as Panama-source income and will violate DGI anti-avoidance doctrine and ESR if it conducts any relevant activity on the side.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Corporate Income Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion is punishable under Panamanian Penal Code Article 253/254 with imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and fines from PAB 10,000 to PAB 25,000. The DGI will also assess the omitted tax plus a 10% surcharge, monthly interest at 1%, and updated taxes. Goods, raw materials, factory equipment, and bank accounts may be seized as enforcement measures.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives, and accountants who sign or cause the filing of a false corporate return are personally jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and penalties. The corporate veil is pierced where the S.A. is used as an alter ego; directors face travel bans, asset freezes, and preventive detention during criminal proceedings, and may be personally ordered to make full civil restitution.
Shell Company / Sham Substance and Misclassification of Territorial Income

Statutory sanction: Under the Panamanian Tax Code and Economic Substance Legislation, the DGI may reclassify the income as Panama-source, assess the 25% ISR plus a 20% penalty, and apply monthly interest. Administrative fines under the Economic Substance compliance regime can reach PAB 1,000,000 in serious cases; the Commercial Registry may strike off the entity, cancel licences, and impose a prohibition on holding corporate roles for up to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute sham transactions and sign declarations of foreign-source income despite no foreign presence become personally liable for the resultant tax deficiency. They are subject to civil restitution, disqualification from serving as directors in Panama, travel restrictions, and criminal prosecution for fraud where the sham is part of a larger asset-protection or money-laundering scheme.
Transfer Pricing Violations / Disguised Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: The DGI will make a transfer-pricing adjustment using an approved arm’s-length method, assess 25% ISR on the adjusted Panama-source profit, add a 20% penalty on the omitted tax and 1% monthly interest, and may impose secondary adjustments to reflect the recharacterised distribution. Interest deductions on excessive shareholder debt are disallowed to the extent the loan is not at arm’s length.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives who sign TP documentation, approve loans, or issue related-party invoices knowing them to be non-arm’s-length are jointly liable for the adjusted assessment. The court may pierce the corporate veil and order directors to repay the shortfall, impose travel bans pending collection, and refer the case to criminal prosecution for fraudulent misrepresentation.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering Through Export Payments

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015 and the Panamanian Penal Code, trade-based money laundering is punishable with imprisonment of 5 to 10 years and fines of 5 to 10 times the value of the illicit transaction. The Superintendencia de Bancos and the Financial Intelligence Unit may impose administrative fines up to PAB 1,000,000 on the corporate entity, freeze bank accounts, seize export shipments, and initiate in rem forfeiture of the production plant.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners who approve suspicious wire transfers, sign false commercial invoices, or fail to maintain an AML compliance programme for export payments are individually criminally liable. They face personal arrest and travel bans during investigation, asset freezes on personal and corporate accounts, disqualification from banking roles, and personal civil restitution for funds laundered through the corporate entity.
