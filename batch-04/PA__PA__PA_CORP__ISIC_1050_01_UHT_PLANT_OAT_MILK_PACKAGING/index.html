Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas de Panamá; official online portal: SIT (Sistema Integrado de Tributación) at https://sit.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Panamanian S.A., the entity must first be incorporated in the Registro Público de Panamá and obtain a resident agent and statutory address. The company must then file an Aviso de Operación with the Municipality and register in the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) through DGI's SIT online system, submitting incorporation documents, the resident agent appointment, and identification of directors and officers. The DGI assigns the RUC number, which also serves as the ITBMS registration code, before any taxable activity commences. For exporters, a simplified RUC procedure applies, but ITBMS registration remains mandatory to support zero-rating claims.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election is available under Panamanian law; a domestic S.A. is automatically taxed as a corporate entity on territorial-source income. Optional regimes for qualifying taxpayers include the Multinational Enterprise Headquarters (SEM) and Panama Pacifico special zone regimes, but no hybrid mismatch elections are recognized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 0.00 (zero) – no de minimis turnover threshold; all persons engaged in taxable transactions must register for RUC and ITBMS before commencing activities. Reduced quarterly filing applies to taxpayers with annual turnover up to B/.300,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of manufactured oat milk products and directly related export services qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate (zero-rating) under the Código Tributario. To be eligible, the exporter must be an ITBMS registrant, goods must physically leave Panama within the statutory export period, the sale must transfer title to a non-resident purchaser, and payment must be received from a non-resident through an authorized Panamanian banking channel.

  • Certified customs export declaration (Declaración Única de Exportación/DUE) authenticated by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá
  • Bill of Lading or original multimodal transport document (B/L) naming the Panamanian exporter as consignor
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency from the non-resident buyer

Panama's DGI mandates electronic invoicing through the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica (SFE). Taxpayers must issue invoices in DGI-authorized software and transmit them in real time for validation, receiving a unique digital stamp/authorization code from the SFE platform. Large taxpayers are on mandatory monthly electronic filing; API integration is required for high-volume issuers, with offline contingency limited to 48 hours. Export sales require an electronic export invoice linked to the customs declaration.

Under the Código Tributario, fraudulent zero-rating or export credit claims trigger a penalty of 100% of the omitted ITBMS, plus default interest at the monthly statutory rate (approximately 1% per month), and a retrospective clawback of all input VAT credits claimed for the open prescriptive period of five years. Criminal tax fraud is punishable with imprisonment of 2 to 5 years, disqualification from export operations, and publication in the DGI default list; the exporter's RUC may be suspended pending final resolution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility. The Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a purely nominal unit existing only as coinage at a fixed 1:1 ratio to the US dollar. No exchange-control approvals, currency licensing, quantitative capital-flow limits, or prior central-bank authorizations apply to foreign investment, profit repatriation, or dividend remittances. The only practical hurdles for the PA_CORP Sociedad Anónima are fiscal in nature (Código Fiscal dividend withholding of 5% on distributions from taxed profits, or 10% flat-rate when profits were untaxed) and documentary (bank AML/KYC proof of lawful source of funds).

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender ratio, repatriation timeline, or mandatory conversion requirement on export receipts. One hundred percent of UHT oat-milk export proceeds may be retained in USD or any convertible currency, either in Panama or abroad, with no SBP notification or approval. Only ordinary customs export declarations and DGI statistical and tax reporting obligations apply.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as prudential banking and AML supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) as state fiscal agent. Panama has no traditional central bank and no foreign-exchange administration; the US dollar is unlimited legal tender, rendering the economy fully dollarized.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for auxiliary treasury functions; LOW as primary deposit or liquidity hub. Foreign digital EMIs are not licensed deposit-taking institutions under Decreto Ley 9 de 1998 (Panama Banking Law) and cannot access Panama's local clearing, issue commercial letters of credit, or produce acceptable bank references for trade finance. An offshore EMI may function only as a sponsored payment gateway in partnership with a licensed Panamanian bank; the SBP Fintech Regulatory Sandbox (Acuerdo 4-2020, as amended) governs such supervised pilots. The PA_CORP entity should maintain its operational and CapEx accounts at a tier-1 Panamanian bank, restricting the EMI to e-commerce settlement rails.

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% on distributions made out of profits already subjected to the 25% ISR; 10% withheld and paid to the DGI on distributions of retained earnings not previously taxed (e.g., foreign-source or free-zone exempt profits) whenever the dividend is declared or paid.

Late filing is subject to fines of USD 10-500 plus, for unpaid tax, a 10% surcharge and monthly interest of 1% on arrears (Fiscal Code Articles 709, 725, 726). Line 15 - Renta neta gravable (Taxable net income) Gross taxable revenue minus deductible costs and expenses. Only Panama-source income falls within the territorial tax base; foreign-source income is excluded solely when genuinely generated outside Panama. The oat milk plant is physically in Panama; product sale or export from Panama is Panama-source. Attempts to structure the sale as a foreign-source transaction by Bing paper-shifting will be recharacterized. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 250,000 aggregate annual related-party transactions triggers the Local File (Informe Local) and Form 853; Master File required where the multinational group's consolidated revenue equals or exceeds USD 50,000,000; Country-by-Country Report required for groups with consolidated revenue of at least USD 750,000,000. The UHT plant's raw-material purchases from foreign sister entities or centralized aseptic packaging procurement will readily exceed the USD 250,000 threshold.

Economic substance scope: Panama follows the OECD BEPS alignment introduced by Law 188 of 2020 and Executive Decree 75 of 2021: taxpayers claiming benefits under preferential regimes or performing relevant activities (manufacturing, distribution, financing, IP holding) must evidence adequate substance in Panama - physical manufacturing premises, local payroll, local management, and operational expenditure proportionate to the income generated. The physical UHT plant naturally satisfies these tests for ordinary manufacturing, but the group must avoid letterbox structures where trademark royalties, purchase functions, or management decisions are artificially booked outside Panama while the operational risk sits in the S.A.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion under Fiscal Code Articles 752-A to 752-C: imprisonment of 2 to 5 years, criminal fines, full civil restitution of evaded tax, a 10% late-payment surcharge and 1% monthly interest; DGI may embargo bank accounts and leviable assets ex parte.

Director personal liability: In fraud or mock-transaction cases, the civil court pierces the corporate veil under Panamanian corporate and civil doctrine, making directors personally liable for unpaid tax, penalties and interest; criminal conviction carries automatic bar on holding office and preventive travel bans (arraigo).
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Application of Economic Substance and effective-taxation rules under Law 254 of 2021: denial of deductions, recharacterization of revenue as taxable Panama-source income, fines established by the DGI, and loss of any preferential regime benefits. Repeated violations may trigger forced dissolution by the Registry.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent are jointly liable for resulting tax deficiencies; a judicial finding of sham corporation extends liability to the de facto foreign management, who become subject to extradition risks and asset freezes if flagged by the Financial Analysis Unit.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Registry

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to USD 50,000 per violation under Law 70 of 2019, material monetary penalties under Law 23 of 2015 AML provisions (up to USD 1,000,000 for institutional failures), suspension of the entity's corporate rights, and DGI restrictions on tax clearance certificates.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners face criminal AML prosecution with custodial sentences for money laundering in a commercial context; travel bans and freezing orders are routinely issued pending investigation.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DGI adjustment of taxable income to the arm's-length amount, disallowance of the excess deduction, application of the 10% surcharge and 1% monthly interest; fines for non-filing or incomplete documentation under the Law 188 of 2020 TP regulations.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the tax return remain liable for the additional assessments; willful TP falsification is treated as tax evasion, carrying imprisonment and personal civil liability for the deficiency.
Improper Dividend Repatriation / Passive WHT Leakage

Statutory sanction: The 10% dividend tax is immediately due and collectible from the corporation with a 10% surcharge and monthly interest; mischaracterized payments are reclassified as hidden dividends, and an additional 25% ISR may be assessed on the disallowed fee deduction.

Director personal liability: Where the board knowingly signs false declarations, each director becomes jointly and severally liable; criminal tax evasion charges may be pursued, with resulting prison time and judicial prevention of departure from Panama.
