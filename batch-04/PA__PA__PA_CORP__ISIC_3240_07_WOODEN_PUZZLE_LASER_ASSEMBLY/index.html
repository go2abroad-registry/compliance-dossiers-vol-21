E-government filing portal

No check-the-box or partnership pass-through election exists for a Sociedad Anónima. Panama taxes the S.A. as a separate entity-level taxpayer on Panamanian-source income only. Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.0.00 — no turnover threshold. Any person carrying out a taxable activity in Panama must obtain RUC and register for ITBMS before starting operations; small taxpayers may be placed in the pequeño contribuyente simplified bracket but still require registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Sales of tangible goods physically exported from Panama qualify for 0% ITBMS, including precision CO2-laser-cut 3D wooden puzzles assembled in Panama and sold through Amazon or Stripe to non-resident consumers. The goods must physically leave Panamanian territory within the applicable customs term and be delivered to the foreign buyer. Services are zero-rated only when rendered to non-resident clients and economically used abroad, without attachment to Panamanian real estate or tangible assets. ITBMS paid on taxable raw materials, packaging, laser consumables, and logistics inputs incurred to produce export goods is creditable or refundable against the zero-rated export transaction upon submission of the export evidence bundle.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera - DUA) accepted by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill naming the Panamanian exporter as shipper/consignor
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list
  • Inward remittance / SWIFT credit advice or payment provider settlement report evidencing receipt of foreign-currency payment from the non-resident buyer
  • E-commerce fulfillment documentation showing the final delivery destination outside Panama, such as Amazon logistics records or Stripe order summary

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) is implemented by DGI through the Sistema de Facturación Electrónica de Panamá. Designated taxpayers, including mid-size and large exporters, must issue XML e-invoices using official schemas, sign each invoice digitally, transmit invoices to DGI in near-real time through the REST/API endpoints of TIC@ or the web portal, and obtain an official clearance status before delivery. Each accepted e-invoice must carry the DGI authorization code and a QR code. Invoices not transmitted or rejected are non-deductible and subject to fines.

DGI may cross-match export claims against ANA customs declarations, carrier manifests, courier delivery data, and banking or payment-rail records. False zero-rating, overstated export values, or undocumented exports are recharacterized as domestic taxable sales, resulting in ITBMS assessment, statutory late-payment interest, surcharges of up to 50% for intentional omission, monetary fines, and criminal liability for tax fraud. Retrospective clawbacks generally cover a five-year prescription period, and the exporter bears the full burden of proving physical export and receipt of foreign consideration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system with no exchange controls. The Panamanian Balboa is a nominal monetary unit pegged 1:1 to the USD, circulating only as coins. Both capital account and current account transactions are freely convertible and remittable. There is no requirement for prior authorization on dividend distributions, capital repatriation, repayment of intercompany loans, royalties, or imported goods invoices. Profit remittance is unrestricted, but distributions must be supported by audited or reviewed financial statements and must respect Panama's territorial-source income tax rules for Panama-source earnings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for repatriation of export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in USD or any freely convertible currency, deposited in local or foreign bank accounts, and used for any lawful working capital or trade settlement purpose.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank issuing domestic currency; the banking regulator is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), while the Ministry of Economy and Finance and Banco Nacional de Panamá (state-owned, performing fiscal agency and clearing functions) together provide monetary-liaison and treasury operations. The US dollar is legal tender under Panama's Currency Law.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Panama does not provide an 'electronic money institution' (EMI) license category; non-bank payment service providers must obtain a Payment Services Provider authorization from the SBP under the reformed banking framework. Offshore digital EMIs offering virtual IBANs cannot lawfully hold Panama corporate deposits or provide local clearing services without a licensed local presence or formal banking partnership. Offshore EMIs remain commercially useful only for aggregating Amazon Pay and Stripe settlement flows outside Panama, but all funds destined for the Panama entity must ultimately enter through a regulated Panama bank or a SBP-authorized payment provider to avoid AML/CFT compliance gaps.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat statutory corporate rate; from fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025, Law 365 of 2024 introduces a progressive scale of 19% on the first B/.50,000 of taxable income, 25% on the next B/.50,000, and a 27% marginal rate on taxable income exceeding B/.100,000.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends distributed from earnings that already bore Panamanian ISR (Código Fiscal Article 735); 20% on dividend distributions paid from earnings not previously subjected to Panamanian corporate income tax.
