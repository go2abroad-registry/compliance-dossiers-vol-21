Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, effective through the online portal 'Tributos Digitales' / 'Sede Electrónica' at dgi.gob.pa.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima must first be created by public deed and registered in the Registro Público (Public Registry), with a resident agent and local legal representation. To obtain the statutory RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) for income and ITBMS purposes, the corporation or its duly notarised representative must file the DGI registration application through the 'Tributos Digitales' portal or in person at DGI offices, submitting the corporate certification, registered address, director/officer details, and a Panama bank reference. In parallel, the company must obtain the municipal 'Aviso de Operación' (business operation notice) from the local municipality and, if it employs staff in Panama, register with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box system. A Panamanian S.A. is automatically subject to corporate income tax on Panama-source income only. Optional regimes include the SEM (Multinational Company Headquarters) regime under Law 41 of 2007, which requires qualifying regional-headquarters activity, minimum local employment, and investment thresholds. Taxpayers also retain the standard election to attribute direct and indirect costs to Panama-source versus foreign-source income for territorial allocation, and may apply DGI-approved transfer-pricing and treaty elections where relevant. No pass-through election is available to a Sociedad Anónima.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold. Registration for ITBMS is mandatory for any person, whether Panamanian resident or non-resident, that makes taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama, including e-commerce sales delivered to Panamanian buyers. Foreign suppliers of digital services and remote products are required to register under the simplified non-resident electronic registration procedure with no minimum threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to actual export of movable goods from Panamanian customs territory, including cosplay apparel, prop costumes, synthetic wigs, and licensed theatrical costume merchandise, provided the seller is registered with DGI and the documentary evidence establishes that ownership and risk of loss pass to a foreign buyer outside Panama. Services physically rendered in Panama but linked to exported goods may also be zero-rated. Deliveries to Panamanian domestic customers remain subject to ITBMS at 7%. The export zero-rating claim is allowed to be recognised on the ITBMS return only when the goods have physically left Panama and all customs and payment evidence are retained.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera de Exportación – DUA Exportación) filed with Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from Panama to the foreign consignee
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank SWIFT payment confirmation evidencing collection and/or receipt of export proceeds from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, purchase order, and certificate of origin (where applicable)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing for all VAT/ITBMS-registered taxpayers under the DGI's 'Facturación Electrónica' system. Billing software must be homologated with DGI and integrate through DGI web services/API for real-time validation and transmission of invoices. Invoicing data is exchanged digitally and each final invoice bears a unique serial code and QR code; there is no physical fiscal stamping. This applies to both local sales and export evidence packages.

DGI and ANA cross-match DUA export declarations against ITBMS returns and shipping manifests. Any false documentation, misdeclared destination, circular shipment, or absence of actual foreign payment will result in immediate reversal of the zero-rating, subjecting the exporter to the 7% VAT, late-payment surcharge, interest, and fines under the Panamanian Fiscal Code (including penalties of up to 200% of the omitted tax). Fraudulent export zero-rating schemes are treated as tax fraud and may trigger criminal prosecution, imprisonment, debarment from exporting privileges, and joint liability of responsible directors and legal representatives.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates full de jure dollarization with no exchange-control regime, no central-bank conversion licensing, and no current- or capital-account restrictions. Capital flows, foreign direct investment, profit repatriation, and dividend remittances are fully convertible and unrestricted. Under the territorial tax system, income arising outside Panama is not Panama-source; dividends paid from foreign-source profits are not subject to Panamanian dividend withholding, whereas distributions from Panama-source taxable profits attract a flat 10% dividend withholding tax (Law 8 of 2010, as amended). Because this S.A. will sell cosplay and theatrical apparel cross-border and settle in USD, no currency conversion is required at any stage of the inbound or outbound flow.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panamanian law imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no balance-of-payments filing for export receipts. The S.A. may retain export proceeds indefinitely in USD or any freely convertible currency, subject only to ordinary bank-level source-of-funds verification and record-keeping under SBP Acuerdo No. 1 de 2014 (AML/CFT) and the supervision of the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF).

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the sole integrated banking regulator under Decree-Law No. 2 of 2008. Panama has no note-issuing central bank; Banco Nacional de Panamá is the state-owned commercial bank and fiscal agent. The U.S. dollar is full legal tender pursuant to Executive Decree No. 90 of 1904, and the Balboa (PAB) exists only as a nominal unit of account at a fixed 1:1 parity with USD.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections settle in USD via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 messages through the bank's U.S. correspondent chain (typically Fedwire/CHIPS via JPMorgan, Citibank, or Bank of America) into the S.A.'s Tier-1 Panamanian account. Panama imposes no capital-account registration or foreign-exchange approval; the bank internally records the credit as 'aportación de capital' against a notarized board resolution and updated share register. Recommended protocol: instruct the remitting bank to include the free-format narrative 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / APORTE DE CAPITAL — S.A. [legal name]', obtain an MT199 covering message where requested, and pre-deliver a signed subscription agreement for any related-party transfer exceeding USD 50,000. Settlement completes in one to two business days with full UETR tracking.Outbound trade payments to cosplay apparel suppliers (mainly China-based fabric, wig, and trims vendors) use two primary rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 open-account remittances with UETR for advance T/T (30-50 percent prepayment, balance on presentation of bill of lading) and (ii) commercial documentary letters of credit issued by the Panamanian bank under UCP 600, payable at sight or by acceptance, with SWIFT MT700/MT710/MT754 message flows. For domestic payables and payroll, the local ACH Panama (S.A.C.H.) rail is used in USD. Cross-border disbursements should always designate correspondent charges for the account of the beneficiary ('OUR') to preserve the full gross amount for the seller. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Annual return due within three months after the fiscal year-end; for calendar-year taxpayers, no later than 31 March. Quarterly advance declarations due on the 15th day following each calendar quarter end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory when aggregate related-party transactions exceed USD 2,000,000, which equals USD-denominated Panamanian balboas; documentation must be available within six months after the fiscal year-end and submitted to DGI on request.

Economic substance scope: Panama applies economic substance expectations through Law 254 of 2021 and FATF/beneficial ownership standards. A cosplay apparel S.A. must maintain a registered office, a physically present administration, staff or qualified agents, accounting records, and commercial reason for the transaction. Lack of substance will cause foreign-source revenue to be re-characterized as Panama-source and trigger transfer pricing and dividend withholding audits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Fine of 25% of the omitted tax plus monthly default interest; criminal tax evasion penalty of up to five years imprisonment and monetary penalty of USD 50,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved or signed the false declaration are subject to civil restitution, seizure of personal assets, travel bans for tax debts above USD 50,000, and temporary disqualification from serving on boards of Panamanian companies.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Administrative dissolution of the S.A., fines up to USD 10,000, loss of the territorial tax exclusion, and inclusion in the public beneficial ownership registry enforcement list.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are jointly liable for unpaid ISR and penalties and may be banned from holding corporate office for two years; foreign beneficial owners face exclusion from banking channels and criminal liability for misuse of the corporate form.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Infraction

Statutory sanction: Fines imposed by the Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) up to USD 1,000,000; bank account freezing; revocation of company registration if the beneficial owner is non-compliant.

Director personal liability: Directors face civil liability for disguising the actual shareholder’s identity, plus imprisonment of up to three years under Law 23 of 2015 where the concealment facilitates money laundering.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalty equal to 1% of the aggregate intercompany transaction amount, capped per Panama TP rules, together with an upward adjustment of taxable income; interest applies to the adjusted tax debt.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false transfer pricing affidavits or materially misleading comparability analyses are personally liable for the resulting tax deficiency and civil restitution; criminal proceedings arise if the document is used to evade tax.
Improper Dividend Withholding

Statutory sanction: Collection of the unpaid dividend withholding tax plus a 10% surcharge, monthly default interest, and disqualification of the distribution for tax purposes.

Director personal liability: Corporate directors act as statutory withholding agents; failure renders them personally liable for the withheld amount and penalties, with the S.A. denied future dividend payment registry until the debt is settled.
