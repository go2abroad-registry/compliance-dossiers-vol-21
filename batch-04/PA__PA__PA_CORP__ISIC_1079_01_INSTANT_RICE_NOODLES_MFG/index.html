E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) established to manufacture ISIC 1079 rice-noodle products, the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) is procured as follows: (1) incorporation in the Registro Público de Panamá through a licensed Panamanian attorney and a resident agent (agente residente), with no minimum paid-in capital; (2) issuance of the municipal Aviso de Operación (business license) by the competent municipal district for the production facility; (3) online RUC application at DGI's SIST portal attaching the certificate and articles of incorporation, resident agent acceptance, power of attorney, municipal license, identification of foreign shareholders/directors, and declared fiscal domicile; (4) simultaneous activation of ITBMS registration and electronic invoicing credentials, and the CSS (Caja de Seguro Social) employer account when employees are hired; (5) issuance of the permanent legal-entity RUC in the statutory 8–12 digit format, generally within 3–5 business days after complete filing. Foreign corporations that do not incorporate locally may obtain a DGI RUC for a Panama permanent establishment, but the standard inbound vehicle is the S.A. itself.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility under a complete dollarization model. Panama maintains no distinction between capital-account and current-account convertibility, imposes no exchange permits, no repatriation restrictions, and no registration thresholds for profit remittances. Dividends and capital distributions sourced from Panama are unrestrained in FX terms and are subject only to ordinary corporate formalities and the standard 10% Panamanian source-income dividend withholding tax. The absence of a central bank issuing domestic money eliminates any convertibility ceiling.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory requirement exists in Panama for export proceeds surrender. Exporters are free to retain U.S. dollars, Renminbi, or any other contracted currency in domestic or foreign accounts without mandatory conversion ratios or repatriation timelines. The only applicable constraints are the SBP-driven reporting of large cash operations and compliance with the entity's own banking agreement regarding foreign-currency deposit limits.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in Panama. De facto governing authorities: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as the prudential banking and financial-compliance regulator; Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) as the fiscal policy body; and Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) as the State's fiscal agent and local clearing-account operator. The U.S. dollar (USD) is legal tender and circulates alongside the Balboa (PAB) at a fixed 1:1 peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a primary treasury rail, acceptable only as an auxiliary collections/settlement channel. Panama pioneered the 'Solventa' regulatory innovation hub (Law 96 of 2022, Ley Fintech) and enacted Law 697 of 2023 on digital assets, yet SBP-authorized electronic-money-institution (EMI) licenses remain narrow in issuance, and most licensed 'fintech' banks are digital-banking arms of traditional institutions. Offshore EMIs chartered in the United Kingdom or the EEA may legally open USD settlement accounts in Panama under foreign-client rules, but they are not SBP-supervised, cannot issue or negotiate documentary letters of credit, and will raise elevated correspondent-banking due-diligence concerns given Panama's FATF/GAFILAT follow-up jurisdiction status. The primary corporate account for statutory-licensed food manufacturing must therefore be held with an SBP-licensed commercial bank; the EMI is viable only as a segregated sub-collection account for small-value e-commerce receipts.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File apply to taxpayers whose annual gross revenue exceeds B/. 3,000,000 (USD 3,000,000) and who carry out transactions with related parties. Country-by-Country reporting applies to multinational groups with consolidated annual revenue exceeding B/. 750,000,000 (USD 750,000,000).

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 254 of 2021 and subordinate DGI rules) require tax-resident entities performing relevant activities to demonstrate physical premises, qualified employees, and core income-generating activities within Panama. A manufacturing company operating an active noodle plant automatically meets substance requirements. Shell S.A.s deriving passive income without local management risk recharacterization, loss of residency benefits, and penalties under both tax and AML laws.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Underreported Sales

Statutory sanction: Evaded tax assessment, surcharge of up to 100% of the omitted tax, monthly interest of 1%, administrative fines, and criminal prosecution under Panama Fiscal Code and Criminal Code: imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and fines equal to double the evaded amount.

Director personal liability: Directors and the legal representative are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debt where they signed false returns or ordered the concealment; civil restitution, precautionary travel bans (impedimento de salida), and disqualification from serving as directors may apply.
Transfer Pricing and Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: DGI recharacterization and adjustment to arm's length, disallowance of the excessive deduction, additional 25% tax on the reconstituted profit, plus penalties of up to 50%–100% of the adjustment and a fine for failure to submit TP documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve non-arm's-length intercompany agreements may be personally assessed for the unpaid portion when the company cannot satisfy the liability; criminal charges for fraudulent insolvency if assets are stripped abroad.
Dividend Withholding Evasion

Statutory sanction: Withholding tax assessed on the gross distribution, surcharge of 10% plus 1% monthly interest; the company remains liable as withholding agent; criminal penalties for fraud if the scheme is proven.

Director personal liability: The corporate officer responsible for authorizing the distribution is personally liable for the unwithheld tax; Panamanian courts may issue travel bans and seize personal assets to ensure collection.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of taxable presence, inclusion of income sourced in Panama at 25%, invalidation of tax residency certificates, fines under Law 254 of 2021 for ESR non-compliance up to USD 1 million, and cancellation of the company's license.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who certify false substance declarations face personal criminal liability, civil exposure to creditors, and imprisonment of up to 5 years for false documentation.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines per infraction ranging from USD 10,000 to USD 250,000, suspension of the operating license, and criminal money-laundering exposure where proceeds are integrated into the banking system; forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the legal representative may be fined personally, prohibited from leaving Panama, and sentenced to 5 to 12 years' imprisonment for aggravated money-laundering offenses involving corporate accounts.
