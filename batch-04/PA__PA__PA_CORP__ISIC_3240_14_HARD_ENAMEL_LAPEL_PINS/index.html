Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; official electronic portal 'T@P - Trámite en Panamá' (https://www.tap.gob.pa/).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity first appoints a resident Panamanian agent and executes a public deed before a notary, which is filed with the Mercantile Registry of Panama. Upon registration, the entity must enroll in the Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) through the DGI T@P portal; the RUC is the statutory tax identification number used for all ITBMS and income tax filings.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign entity first appoints a resident Panamanian agent and executes a public deed before a notary, which is filed with the Mercantile Registry of Panama. Upon registration, the entity must enroll in the Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) through the DGI T@P portal; the RUC is the statutory tax identification number used for all ITBMS and income tax filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; all Sociedades Anónimas are automatically taxed at entity level on Panama-source income only. Statutory elections are limited to specially applied regimes: Multinational Corporate Headquarters (SEM) under Law 41 of 2007, Panama Pacifico operations, free-zone operators, and the optional small taxpayer simplified regime for businesses with qualifying annual turnover thresholds established by the DGI.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory exemption threshold; registration is compulsory from the first taxable transfer, service, or importation in Panama. The DGI's small taxpayer simplified regime may apply to qualifying taxpayers below the administrative turnover threshold set by the DGI.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods and offshore services qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate when the goods leave Panamanian customs territory permanently or the services are supplied to non-residents for consumption entirely abroad and are not related to Panamanian real property. The exporter must hold a valid RUC, be registered as an exporter with the DGI, and maintain documentary proof of the shipment and receipt of foreign proceeds.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduanas, DUA) validated by the National Customs Authority of Panama
  • Bill of Lading or equivalent air/sea waybill demonstrating physical exit from Panamanian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank payment evidence showing receipt of export proceeds from non-resident debtors

Panama's mandatory electronic invoicing regime requires authorized taxpayers to transmit invoices in real time to the DGI through the T@P platform or an accredited technological provider (PROV) before issuance; each validated invoice must include a QR code, Coded Unique Electronic Invoice Identifier (CUFIN), and the DGI clearance stamp. Non-compliant exporters are suspended from electronic issuance and may be excluded from zero-rating privileges.

The DGI and the national customs authority cross-match export declarations, shipping records, and bank remittance evidence within the 180-day repatriation deadline. Deficient substantiation or untimely repatriation causes the zero rate to be clawed back and reclassified as taxable ITBMS at 7%, accruing late-payment interest, a statutory surcharge, and fines of up to twice the evaded tax. Repeated or fraudulent export zero-rating claims may additionally result in loss of the exporter registration and criminal tax-fraud prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system: the US dollar is legal tender and the balboa (PAB) is a 1:1 nominal unit of account with zero independent central-bank issuance. Current-account and capital-account convertibility is unrestricted; there are no exchange controls, no capital-movement authorizations, no repatriation restrictions for dividends, royalties or loan repayments, and no requirement to channel transactions through any central clearing system. The only statutory frictions are the AML reporting grid: threshold-based transaction and cash reports to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF), and the 5% dividend withholding tax applicable to distributions funded by taxed Panamanian-source profits under the territorial income tax regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panamanian law contains no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, no retention timeline, and no duty to repatriate foreign sales proceeds by any fixed date; export receivables may be retained indefinitely in USD or any foreign currency, either in Panama or abroad. The exporter is only required to maintain standard customs and tax traceability (commercial invoices, póliza de exportación, beneficiary declarations) and to satisfy routine UAF reporting thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), established under Decree-Law 9 of 1998, is the sovereign banking supervisor; Panama has no central bank and Banco Nacional de Panamá functions solely as fiscal agent and governmental treasury counterpart of the State. The Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) administers fiscal policy but exercises no foreign-exchange control authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM as a supplementary collection rail, LOW as the primary treasury bank. Foreign digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Mercury) and payment facilitators (Stripe, Etsy Direct Pay) can lawfully onboard a Panama-domiciled S.A. to aggregate USD/EUR settlements, but they are not licensed by the SBP, do not provide Panamanian banking secrecy protections, and cannot operate local clearing rails. The compliant treasury structure is therefore a two-tier rail: the foreign EMI/PSP functions solely as a pass-through collection account with automated nightly sweeps into the Tier 1 Panamanian bank, which remains the sole SBP-supervised repository of liquidity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections from non-resident shareholders should be executed by SWIFT MT103 or Fedwire credit to the S.A.'s USD account at the Panamanian Tier 1 bank, with the payment reference 'Aporte de capital accionistas / Capital Contribution' so the bank classifies the credit as paid-in equity rather than shareholder debt; this avoids any interest-withholding and thin-capitalization documentation burden under the territorial income tax regime. No prior SBP or MEF authorization is required; the receiving bank will record the board resolution, stock-registry entry and updated RUC in the client file, and will file applicable UAF threshold reports. Marketplace proceeds from Etsy Direct Pay and Stripe must never be labelled as capital: those credits arrive as trade receivables through ACH/SEPA/USD clearing and are reconciled against the platform payout reports.Outbound supplier settlements use the USD SWIFT rail (MT103) via each Panamanian bank's US correspondent network (CHIPS/Fedwire) for Chinese raw-material vendors; where suppliers cannot accept USD, the bank's treasury desk executes a USD/CNH spot conversion or the S.A. opens a Hong Kong CNY-denominated account. For high-value or repeat trade orders, the S.A. should employ SWIFT MT700 commercial letters of credit issued by the Panamanian bank and confirmed by the supplier's bank, supported by the documentary collection package (shipping documents, inspection certificates, bills of lading). Domestic USD disbursements (warehousing, local subcontractors, freight forwarders) clear same-day through the local ACH Panama interbank system, and Etsy/Stripe payouts in the reverse direction enter through the same USD clearing grid.Plain vanilla OTC EUR/USD forwards and swaps (1–12 month tenors) documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with local Tier 1 bank treasury desks, hedging EUR-denominated Etsy/Stripe settlement flows into USD operating costs.USD/CNH forwards and CNH nondeliverable forwards (NDFs) executed through the international sales desks of Banistmo, BAC International Bank, or Banco General to lock the USD cost of Chinese enamel-pin raw materials (zinc alloy blanks, enamel paints, plating, packaging).Zero-cost collars and bought EUR/USD options to protect the gross margin of the retail marketplace channel without upfront premium leakage.Cross-currency basis swaps (EUR/USD and USD/CNH) for long-dated committed sales or procurement contracts, collateralized under a negotiated Credit Support Annex to the ISDA Master Agreement.No PAB/USD hedging instrument is required or available: the balboa is a 1:1 legal-tender shadow of the USD, so treasury risk is confined to third-currency cross pairs arising from platform settlement geography and suppliers' CNY invoicing.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/.2,000,000 (United States dollar equivalent) in aggregate related-party transactions during the prior fiscal year; above this threshold, the Panama S.A. must prepare and maintain transfer pricing documentation, including a Local File and, where group requirements apply, a Master File.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 70 of 2019 and implementing decrees) apply to Panama legal entities carrying out relevant activities such as distribution, logistics, agency, headquarters services, financing, leasing, and intellectual property holding. A Sociedad Anónima that derives foreign Etsy revenue but maintains no physical office, no employees, no independent corporate records, and no genuine Panamanian decision-making will be treated as a shell arrangement and may be subject to DGI reconstruction of Panama-source income, forfeiture of treaty benefits, and administrative sanctioning. Even where core manufacturing of hard enamel lapel pins occurs abroad, the Panama entity must demonstrate economic substance commensurate with its contractual function, assets, and risks if it books revenue or holds the trademark, tooling, and Etsy seller account.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion Through False Territorial Allocation

Statutory sanction: The DGI may re-determine taxable income under normal 15%/25% rates, apply the full unpaid tax plus monthly default interest, impose a late-payment surcharge up to 100% of the tax, and refer the taxpayer to criminal tax evasion proceedings. Administrative fines may reach tens of thousands of balboas, and repeated false returns can lead to cancellation of the RUC number.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false territorial-source returns, maintain hidden payment rails, or cause the corporation to underreport Panama-source revenue lose the protection of the corporate entity. The DGI and courts may pierce the corporate veil, order civil restitution of omitted taxes, freeze personal bank accounts, impose a travel ban (impedimento de salida), and convict responsible directors or beneficial owners of tax fraud with imprisonment.
Shell Company and Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Registro Público may suspend corporate rights, the financial system may close its bank accounts, and the DGI may assess all proceeds as Panama-source through anti-abuse rules. Fines for failure to file beneficial ownership and economic substance declarations can reach B/.50,000 or more, and chronic inactivity can lead to cancellation of the corporate registrations.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors, resident agents, and beneficial owners acting as the straw controllers can be held jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax and AML fines. Courts in Panama have imposed civil restitution, asset embargos, travel restrictions, and disqualification from acting as directors of any Panama corporation.
Money Laundering and AML Reporting Violations

Statutory sanction: Panama's AML Law 23 of 2015 and regulations of the Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) provide substantial monetary penalties, seizure of funds, and criminal liability. Penalties can range from B/.100,000 to B/.1,000,000 depending on gravity; convicted persons may face imprisonment and permanent disqualification from regulated financial activities.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is set aside for persons who knowingly use the Panama S.A. to receive dirty funds. Directors, shareholders, payment-rail administrators, and resident agents can be named as investigation subjects and face forfeiture of personal assets, travel bans, civil suits for restitution, and prison sentences.
Failure to File Corporate Income Tax Return and Dividend Withholding

Statutory sanction: DGI will issue an automatic assessment based on available information, add a monthly surcharge of 10% and default interest, and may embargo bank accounts in custody of the Etsy/Stripe settlement accounts. Penalties for failure to withhold dividend tax are assessed on the payer, and dividends are treated as effectively distributed if the corporation holds undistributed profits without credible business justification.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers responsible for deciding not to file, not to withhold, or not to disburse quarterly prepayments become personally liable for the withheld amounts, penalties, and defrauded tax. The DGI may pursue the individuals through the fiscal courts, request preventive embargo orders, and obtain travel bans restricting departure from Panama.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: The DGI may adjust the taxable base to the arm's-length price, apply valuation and documentation penalties, and deny deductions for undocumented intercompany invoices. Penalties are calculated on the adjusted tax difference and can in egregious cases reach 20% to 40% of the tax adjustment.

Director personal liability: Directors signing the intercompany contracts and certifying transfer pricing returns are personally exposed to penalty assessment when they have knowingly selected an unrealistic methodology or suppressed required documentation. The relevant officers may also be subjected to criminal referral for fiscal fraud and curtailment of their right to leave Panama pending resolution.
