Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility on both current account and capital account. Because the economy is fully dollarized, there are no exchange controls, no transactions licensed by a central monetary authority, no import/export currency authorization, and no profit-remittance restrictions. Dividends, intercompany loans, royalty payments, capital repatriation, and liquidation proceeds may be transferred abroad without SBP or MEF approval. The sole practical frictions are AML/CFT reporting thresholds (cash transactions at or above USD 10,000 are reportable to the UAF; international fund transfers of USD 50,000 or more are included in UAF surveillance) and correspondent-bank due diligence imposed by US clearing banks under OFAC sanctions screening. A foreign shareholder's dividend remittance from a Panamanian S.A. is subject to Panamanian dividend withholding tax: 5% on distributions of already-taxed Panamanian-source profits and 10% on distributions of untaxed foreign-source profits, under the territorial income tax regime reformed by Law 57 of 2023.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. Income generated by the export of digital micro-drama content (app-store sales, licensing, subscription streaming, and ad-supported revenue) may be collected in USD, retained offshore or onshore indefinitely, and redeployed without surrender, conversion, or central bank approval. The applicable obligation is purely documentary: the exporter must correctly identify the foreign-source or Panamanian-source character of each revenue stream for the 5%/10% dividend distinction and for the Law 57 of 2023 economic-nexus (nexo económico) sourcing rules, and must retain underlying contracts and settlement records for banking and tax file reviews.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists in the Republic of Panama. The US dollar (USD) circulates as full legal tender pursuant to Law 84 of 1904, and the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) exists solely as an accounting unit at a 1:1 parity without circulating banknotes. Monetary supervision is performed indirectly: prudential regulation of banks and financial institutions is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent of the State; the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) manages public finances; and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) executes AML/CFT financial intelligence.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-High as a supplementary settlement rail; Low as the primary treasury host. Selected global fintech EMIs (for example Payoneer, Dukascopy, or Multitude/LeaBank) can supply multi-currency wallet accounts and low-cost MT103/SEPA payment execution; however, none holds a Panamanian banking license and none may lawfully take deposits from the Panamanian public under Ley 23 de 2015 and the SBP money-remittance licensing regime. An unlicensed EMI cannot offer Panamanian regulatory protection, deposit insurance, or access to the SBP complaint and freeze-remedy framework. Recommended structure for the micro-drama group: the Panama S.A. owns a US Delaware or Hong Kong merchant-of-record subsidiary that contracts Apple App Store Connect, Google Play Console, and any Stripe-based flows; the subsidiary collects app-store receivables and then repatriates funds to Panama by intercompany dividend or trademark/license royalty via SWIFT MT103. Stripe has no Panama-domiciled merchant onboarding, so the foreign merchant-of-record layer is mandatory for Stripe rail coverage.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Wire protocol: SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire for the US domestic leg) from the shareholder's bank to the S.A.'s Panama USD account through New York clearing correspondents (typically Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, or Bank of America). The remittance information field should state CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION or SHARE SUBSCRIPTION, and the ordering customer field must identify the exact UBO remitter. Because Panama is dollarized, no currency conversion and no central bank authorization are required; the SBP-licensed receiving bank applies standard KYC, UAF monitoring for transfers at or above USD 50,000, and cash reporting at or above USD 10,000. Supporting documents: shareholder resolution authorizing the capital increase, notarized amendment of registered capital, sworn attribution of the contribution, and the bank's corporate KYC pack. The dossier must be retained for at least five years.Clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for production licensing fees, cross-border crew payroll, supplier invoices, and FX-converted payments to vendors in EUR, GBP, INR, BRL, or MXN; (2) commercial documentary credits under UCP 600 and standby letters of credit where a vendor demands bank risk intermediation for larger physical purchases such as studio equipment or co-production logistics; (3) domestic Panama ACH clearing (ACH Panama through the national clearing house) and high-value USD wire via Fedwire or CHIPS for local and US-domiciled post-production, legal, and ad-tech vendors; (4) Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console proceeds first credit the foreign merchant-of-record subsidiary, then enter Panama by MT103 as intercompany dividend or royalty. No SBP exchange authorization applies to any outbound transfer; the bank applies OFAC/UN sanctions screening and may request underlying commercial invoices for transfers exceeding USD 100,000.
