Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Directorate of Revenue (Dirección General de Ingresos, DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas), Panama; electronic portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity conducting economic activities in Panama must first appoint a resident agent and a legal representative in Panama and inscribe the entity, or form a Panama Sociedad Anónima, in the Public Registry (Registro Público) to obtain the corporate juridical person identification number. The entity then files an 'Aviso de Operación' through the DGI Sede Electrónica platform, submitting the public registration certificate, identification of directors and the resident agent, registered office address, and a description of planned activities. Upon approval, the DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes), which serves as the single tax identification number for income tax and ITBMS/VAT purposes. For a pure aircraft-engine dry-leasing structure with no Panamanian payroll, no local branch premises, and no Panamanian-source operations, registration is typically limited to the RUC and the corporate registry; municipal operation licenses and social security registration are required only if local employees or physical activities are present.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a US-style check-the-box entity election or an OECD hybrid mismatch election. A Panama Sociedad Anónima is mandatorily treated as a taxable corporation subject to entity-level taxation under the territorial-source principle. The principal statutory election available to a qualifying small taxpayer is the simplified Pequeño Contribuyente regime for annual gross income not exceeding B/. 36,000. In addition, a taxpayer may elect to apply the alternative minimum tax (IMAS) calculation basis instead of the normal income tax basis where permitted by the Código Fiscal. No passthrough treatment, tax consolidation, or hybrid entity classification is available for a Panama S.A.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover registration threshold; every person performing taxable supplies in Panama must obtain an RUC and register for ITBMS before commencing activities. Small taxpayers with annual gross income up to B/. 36,000 may elect the simplified regime, but this does not exempt them from registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The zero rate applies to (i) physical exports of goods from Panamanian territory under a valid customs export declaration, and (ii) services, including the dry leasing of aircraft engines, supplied to a non-resident customer where the asset is used, operated, and remains outside Panama and the benefit of the lease is deemed to accrue outside Panama. The lessor must maintain documentary evidence of the lessee's non-resident status, the foreign place of use, and the non-Panamanian registration or permanent location of the aircraft engine. If the engine were installed on an aircraft operating within Panama or used to produce Panamanian-source revenue, standard ITBMS at 7% would apply. Free-zone or special-zone regimes may provide separate preferential treatment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) accepted by the Panama Customs Authority (ANA)
  • Air Waybill (AWB) or Bill of Lading evidencing physical export of the aircraft engine from Panama
  • Commercial Invoice and dry-lease agreement identifying the non-resident lessee and the contractual place of use
  • Packing List (where goods are physically exported)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or banking evidence of receipt of hard currency from the non-resident lessee, demonstrating cross-border payment

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) under DGI Resolution No. 093-2020 and complementary regulations. Taxpayers must transmit invoice data in real time through the DGI Sede Electrónica web-service API (REST/JSON) or through an authorized third-party facturador platform before the invoice acquires fiscal effect. The DGI validates each invoice and issues a unique authorization code and digital stamp, which is displayed on the invoice as a QR code and associated code. The electronic invoice must be issued for every taxable supply regardless of the customer's registration status. Monthly digital transmission of all e-invoice records is mandatory. An invoice without DGI acceptance is fiscally void and the corresponding input ITBMS credit is disallowed.

The DGI may audit export zero-rating claims retrospectively, generally within a four-year statute of limitations, extended where returns were not filed. If a zero-rated supply is recharacterized as taxable, the DGI claws back ITBMS input credits previously claimed, assesses the 7% tax on the invoiced amount, and charges interest, late-payment surcharges, and administrative fines for formal infractions. Fraudulent misuse of export zero-rating, including false export declarations, fictitious non-resident lease documentation, or failure to repatriate foreign currency within the required period, constitutes tax fraud (defraudación tributaria), punishable by fines of one to three times the evaded tax and imprisonment for approximately five to eight years under the Código Fiscal and Panama's criminal tax legislation. Directors, resident agents, and legal representatives of the lessor may be held jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar is legal tender in Panama, and both current and capital accounts are fully convertible. There are no exchange controls, no surrender obligations, and no restrictions on dividend distributions, profit repatriation, intercompany loans, or lease rental remittances. Panama applies a territorial tax system: Panamanian-source income is taxed, while foreign-source income derived by a Panama S.A. is generally outside the tax net. The only practical remittance hurdle is compliance with bank AML/KYC documentary requirements and, where dividends are paid from Panamanian-source profits, the applicable dividend tax enforced by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI).

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion rules apply in Panama. Exporters, including providers of cross-border dry aircraft engine leasing, may freely retain U.S. dollars or other currencies in local or foreign bank accounts. A Panama S.A. is not required to repatriate export proceeds within any statutory timeline; however, the entity must maintain commercial documentation and bank transaction records sufficient to evidence the underlying trade purpose under SBP AML requirements.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (state bank and fiscal agent; not a central bank). Monetary, banking, and FX-sector supervision is exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Ley 2 de 2008. The Panamanian territory is fully dollarized; no Central Bank approval or foreign exchange administration permit exists for cross-border capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low. Panama currently does not maintain a bespoke offshore EMI license that grants direct access to the national payment or clearing infrastructure. An offshore digital EMI may be used only as a peripheral collection or expense tool provided that the underlying settlement funds are ultimately held in an SBP-regulated bank account. For the aircraft engine dry leasing treasury, core liquidity, escrow, foreign exchange, and SWIFT payment operations must be conducted through a locally licensed commercial bank.
