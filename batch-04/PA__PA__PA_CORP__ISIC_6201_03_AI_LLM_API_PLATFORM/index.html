Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Directorate of Revenue (Dirección General de Ingresos, DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance of Panama; official web portal: https://www.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) must obtain its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) before engaging in taxable operations. The registration procedure includes: (1) filing the RUC application with DGI through the web portal or authorized service window; (2) presenting certified articles of incorporation, the notarized public deed, registered agent (agente residente) confirmation, municipal business license or aviso de operación, and identification documents for the officers; (3) receiving the RUC certificate, which also registers the company for the transfer tax on goods and services (ITBMS) where applicable.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) must obtain its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) before engaging in taxable operations. The registration procedure includes: (1) filing the RUC application with DGI through the web portal or authorized service window; (2) presenting certified articles of incorporation, the notarized public deed, registered agent (agente residente) confirmation, municipal business license or aviso de operación, and identification documents for the officers; (3) receiving the RUC certificate, which also registers the company for the transfer tax on goods and services (ITBMS) where applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box or comparable pass-through election. A Panamanian S.A. is an entity-level taxpayer taxed only on Panama-source income. No domestic hybrid mismatch election is available. A qualifying small taxpayer may elect the simplified small-taxpayer regime for income tax purposes within statutory turnover limits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover-based ITBMS registration threshold; registration is compulsory for any person habitually selling taxable goods or services in Panama. An optional simplified small-taxpayer regime is available only for taxpayers with annual gross turnover below B/.36,000 (PAB 36,000, equal to USD 36,000).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods from Panama qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate when the goods physically leave customs territory under a customs export declaration. Services supplied to non-residents and effectively consumed or exploited abroad qualify for 0%, as do services directly related to exported goods. The taxpayer must be ITBMS-registered, issue an export invoice at 0%, and retain documentary evidence of the export transaction.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) with customs exit validation
  • Bill of Lading or air waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a licensed Panama banking institution
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Electronic export invoice with DGI fiscal authorization code/QR

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the DGI Facturación Electrónica system. E-invoices must be transmitted to DGI or through an authorized service provider for validation, must include a DGI-authorized fiscal authorization code and QR stamp, and must be digitally signed with the taxpayer's electronic trust certificate. Certified billing software must integrate with DGI web services/APIs for issuance, cancellation, authorization, and real-time clearance.

DGI audits export zero-rating by cross-matching electronic invoices, customs DUA records, transport documents, and inward remittance certificates. Failure to substantiate the export or repatriation of payment within the required deadline results in reversal of the 0% rate, assessment of 7% ITBMS plus monthly interest, penalties generally from 50% to 100% of the omitted tax, and criminal liability where fraudulent zero-rating is established. Limitation periods are extended in cases of fraud, and retrospective clawbacks can cover at least six fiscal years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized economy with absolute currency convertibility and no exchange-control restrictions. Current-account and capital-account transactions are freely permitted, including profit repatriation, dividend distributions, loan servicing, and capital redemption. There is no prior central-bank authorization for transfers of any amount, and no withholding or transfer tax on outward remittances is imposed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no mandatory surrender, no minimum conversion ratio, and no statutory timeline for export receipts. Exporters may retain proceeds indefinitely in U.S. Dollar accounts or in foreign currency accounts, and may freely use such funds for domestic or cross-border obligations.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá — National Banking Superintendency; Banco Nacional de Panamá operates as fiscal agent of the Republic of Panama. No central bank exists in Panama and no national currency is issued; the U.S. Dollar is legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium for unlicensed offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs). Panama's Banking Law (Cabinet Decree 23 of 1970) prohibits deposit-taking without an SBP banking license, and there is no distinct EMI license. Offshore EMIs may operate only through a carefully structured correspondent or sponsorship arrangement with an SBP-licensed bank. The Innovation Hub/Sandbox supervised by SBP provides a legal path for fintech payment services, but high-risk crypto-linked EMIs face additional AML/CFT scrutiny. A Panama S.A. should prefer a local banking relationship rather than an unlicensed offshore EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections are executed via SWIFT MT103 transfer to the Panama S.A.'s U.S. Dollar operating account at the SBP-licensed bank. Payment must identify the beneficial shareholder and the purpose code as 'Foreign Direct Investment' or 'Capital Contribution' — Panama does not require central-bank approval for such inflows. The sending bank should reference the entity's corporate registration number and the receiving bank's correspondent instructions (typically routed through a U.S. Fedwire/CHIPS participant due to Panama's dollarized system). For USDC-based capital injection, conversion should occur at the regulated digital-asset service provider and only the resulting fiat wire should be presented to the bank, accompanied by the source-of-funds and conversion report, to avoid triggering blanket crypto-to-bank blocked transfers.For trade and operational settlements, Panama S.A. disburses primarily through SWIFT MT103 for invoice payments, license fees, and intercompany costs, with MT202/COV used for bank-to-bank obligations. Commercial documentary Letters of Credit (SWIFT MT700 series) are available through Tier-1 Panamanian banks for goods-backed transactions. Domestic clearing is handled by the Cámara de Compensación and dollar transfers between local banks execute same-day; international settlement routes via CHIPS/Fedwire through each bank's U.S. correspondent network. 25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on the gross distribution, withheld by the S.A. at the time of dividend payment. This 10% applies equally to distributions out of Panama-source and foreign-source profits unless a lower tax treaty rate is successfully invoked.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File must be prepared and filed when annual related-party transactions (including intercompany hosting, token fees, or royalties) exceed B/. 1,000,000, or when any related-party transaction involves a low-tax jurisdiction and exceeds B/. 250,000. Master File compliance is mandatory where the MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds B/. 30,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not maintain the full Cayman-style ESR, but the DGI applies substance-over-form and business-purpose anti-avoidance rules. An S.A. claiming Panama tax residency or treaty benefits must demonstrate genuine local operations: physical office or server infrastructure, local decision-making, daily records, and board minutes maintained in Panama. A pure nominee shell that enjoys foreign cloud AI revenues but has no Panamanian substance will risk recharacterization, recapture of the foreign income, and cancellation of tax residency certificates.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Omission of Income

Statutory sanction: Fiscal Code Articles 785-786: reassessment plus a surcharge up to 50% of the omitted tax; criminal fraud proceedings may impose 2 to 5 years imprisonment and a monetary fine of 2 to 10 times the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign false returns become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax and interest; courts may issue travel bans, attach personal assets, disqualify the individual from board service, and order the judicial dissolution of the S.A.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The DGI may disregard the entity for tax purposes, reattribute the foreign API income to the ultimate beneficial owner, revoke the tax residence certificate, and impose fines between B/. 5,000 and B/. 50,000.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face administrative sanctions and criminal liability under the beneficial ownership law, with imprisonment from 6 months to 3 years for falsely certifying economic substance; civil restitution can be ordered against individual directors for the avoided tax.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The DGI may adjust the royalty or service fee by the full difference from arm's length, impose a 15% accuracy-related penalty, and disallow the deducted intercompany expense.

Director personal liability: CFOs and senior officers who knowingly sign deficient documentation can be held civilly liable for the adjustment shortfall, suspended from appearing before the DGI, and fined personally when gross negligence or intentional distortion is proven.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Reporting

Statutory sanction: Under Panama Law 23/2015 and Executive Decree 163/2021, fines range from B/. 100,000 to B/. 1,000,000; the company's commercial registration and residency certificate may be suspended or cancelled.

Director personal liability: Directors, resident agents, and compliance officers incur criminal liability under anti-money laundering rules, with imprisonment up to 12 years, travel bans, and prohibition from managing any Panamanian corporation if the AI/LLM platform is used for illicit funds flow.
