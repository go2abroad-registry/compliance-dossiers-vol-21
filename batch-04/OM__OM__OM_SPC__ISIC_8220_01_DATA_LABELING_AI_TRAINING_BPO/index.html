Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (Wizarat al-Dhara'ib / Omani Tax Authority), Ministry of Finance – electronic portal: EmTax (https://emtax.om; also accessible via https://taxoman.gov.om). The Tax Authority administers corporate income tax (CIT), VAT, and withholding taxes under Royal Decree 9/2017 (Income Tax Law) and Royal Decree 121/2020 (VAT Law).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) and MOCIIP establishment card. The CR is the master record for tax registration: (1) CIT registration – apply via EmTax within 3 months of licensing/commencement of business; Tax Authority issues a Tax Card bearing the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and taxpayer classification (10-digit VAT + 15-digit CIT number); (2) VAT registration – file through EmTax within 30 days after the mandatory threshold is met; (3) non-resident shareholders must designate a resident tax/fiscal agent or authorized representative for service of process and filing compliance; (4) each Omani legal entity and each branch requires a separate TIN; there is no umbrella registration for a foreign group head.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (SPC is a deemed resident Omani company under RD 9/2017 when incorporated in Oman and managed/controlled from Oman; profits are taxed at the entity level with no transparent/pass-through treatment. 100% foreign ownership is permissible and does not alter the classification; no limited-partnership or dual-status election arises. Territorial sourcing rules apply to foreign-source income of residents, and a credit is granted for foreign taxes on Oman-source income repatriated to a resident). — A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) and MOCIIP establishment card. The CR is the master record for tax registration: (1) CIT registration – apply via EmTax within 3 months of licensing/commencement of business; Tax Authority issues a Tax Card bearing the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and taxpayer classification (10-digit VAT + 15-digit CIT number); (2) VAT registration – file through EmTax within 30 days after the mandatory threshold is met; (3) non-resident shareholders must designate a resident tax/fiscal agent or authorized representative for service of process and filing compliance; (4) each Omani legal entity and each branch requires a separate TIN; there is no umbrella registration for a foreign group head.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (SPC is a deemed resident Omani company under RD 9/2017 when incorporated in Oman and managed/controlled from Oman; profits are taxed at the entity level with no transparent/pass-through treatment. 100% foreign ownership is permissible and does not alter the classification; no limited-partnership or dual-status election arises. Territorial sourcing rules apply to foreign-source income of residents, and a credit is granted for foreign taxes on Oman-source income repatriated to a resident).

Available election forms

Oman does not apply the US check-the-box or hybrid-entity elections; classification follows the Commercial Companies Law. Available statutory elections: (i) CIT – selection of a 12-month financial accounting period (instead of calendar year), and the small-business zero-rate band on taxable profits up to OMR 100,000 (subject to aggregation and conditionality); no group/consolidated CIT filing election; (ii) VAT – VAT group election available under Article 85 of the VAT Law (joint liability, single registration, one return) and the election for quarterly (rather than monthly) VAT return filing for smaller registrants; (iii) exemption/relief elections under the Investment Law for qualifying sectors; (iv) no hybrid mismatch or OECD BEPS-driven elective rule is currently enacted – OECD/G20 hybrid mismatch recommendations are not yet domesticated in Omani law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

VAT mandatory registration threshold: OMR 500,000 of taxable supplies (zero-rated + standard-rated) in the preceding 12 months or expected in the next 12 months; voluntary registration threshold: OMR 100,000. CIT has no turnover threshold – any taxable income from an Omani activity or Oman-source income triggers registration. Social Protection Fund employer registration applies upon first Omani employee.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The AI data-labeling, bounding-box annotation, and RLHF curation services qualify as zero-rated export of services where: (1) the recipient is a non-resident person with no establishment in Oman and is outside Oman at the time of supply; (2) the B2B place-of-supply rule (recipient-location rule) places the supply outside Oman; (3) the services do not directly benefit goods or real property located inside Oman; and (4) the economic and effective use/benefit of the service occurs outside Oman. Performance in Oman does not itself defeat zero-rating for B2B recipients, but the supplier must retain a copy of the client's foreign TIN, the executed service agreement, annotation/delivery records, and the AI data-quality acceptance report evidencing offshore consumption. Pure B2C digital supplies to non-residents may fall outside the scope of VAT; inbound B2B services are outside the scope with reverse charge on the Omani recipient.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayaan) – required only where physical data storage media or goods accompany the zero-rated service
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / international courier receipt (for physical media or equipment export)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank SWIFT MT103 evidence of hard-currency settlement from the foreign client
  • Executed zero-rated export service contract with AI data-delivery and acceptance provisions
  • VAT tax invoice bearing the zero-rating reason code and statutory invoice particulars
  • Non-resident status evidence of the client (foreign VAT/GST registration certificate, TIN, or entity registry proof)

Oman currently has no statutory real-time e-invoicing clearance, no fiscal device seal, and no mandatory third-party continuous transaction control. Tax invoices must contain mandatory fields (serial number, TINs, invoice date, supply description, VAT treatment indicator); the VAT return and payment must be filed electronically through EmTax within 28 days after the end of the tax period (monthly for large registrants, quarterly for others). The Tax Authority is actively piloting a phased national e-invoicing and data-exchange program expected to become mandatory in 2025–2026; meanwhile, registrants must maintain full digital records in Arabic/English, retain data for 10 years, and provide machine-readable extraction upon audit demand.

Oman does not impose exchange controls, but export proceeds are expected to be repatriated in hard currency within 180 days of invoice date as bank and Tax Authority practice; in a VAT audit, unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating is invalidated, the supply is re-characterized at 5% VAT, late-payment interest is applied at 1% per month, and penalty exposure reaches up to OMR 10,000 per false invoice/record violation plus a tax-evasion fine of up to 100% of the tax due (with criminal referral and license implications). Separately, CIT audit may deem unrepatriated proceeds as constructive distributions, disallow related deductions, apply a 15% assessment plus late-payment penalties, and invoke the GAAR provisions under RD 9/2017 for circular financing.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the US Dollar at a fixed rate of 1 OMR = USD 2.6008. The current account is fully convertible without prior CBO licensing, allowing unrestricted settlement of cross-border trade and service transactions. The capital account permits non-resident 100% ownership of Omani SPCs (Single Person Companies) under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree No. 50/2019), but inward direct investment and profit repatriation must be routed through licensed Omani commercial banks and may be subject to bank-level compatibility checks against anti-money laundering (AML), sanction, and source-of-funds obligations. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to corporate tax clearance certificates (from the Oman Tax Authority) and the absence of open enforcement actions or COMARA (Companies Registry) cancellation proceedings; there is no general capital gains withholding tax outside of a taxable branch scenario.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory mandatory surrender ratio applies in Oman; however, export receipts from AI data-labeling services (ISIC 8220) must be repatriated through a CBO-licensed bank within 180 days of invoice date per standard commercial banking practice. CBO requires commercial banks to obtain underlying contract and invoice documentation for large IT services exports. Proceeds may be retained in foreign currency accounts at licensed banks, but the operating account in OMR is recommended for operational liquidity, clearing, and payroll execution.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) acting concurrently as the Foreign Exchange Administration and monetary authority under the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 114/2000) and the Foreign Exchange Regulations issued by CBO.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW. Oman has no framework for foreign digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) licenses. CBO only recognizes licensed banks and authorized payment service providers under the National Payment Systems Law; foreign EMIs such as Wise, Airwallex, or PayPal are permissible only as ancillary collection / payout rails for the underlying SPC’s trade payables or staff incentives, not as the primary bank account. Direct use of Wise as an onboarding bank will expose the SPC to unlicensed money service business risk and potential violation of the Commercial Companies Law. Use Wise only as an execution layer after the primary corporate bank has cleared the cross-border transfer.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (flat corporate income tax under Sultani Decree No. 8/2023 applicable to Omani-resident companies)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose statutory withholding tax on dividend distributions made by a resident SPC to non-resident shareholders, nor on dividends distributed to resident shareholders

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the fiscal year-end, unless a tax filing extension is granted by the Oman Tax Authority (new Law permits extension up to 2 months)

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 500,000 aggregate related-party transaction threshold for mandatory Local File; Master File required for Group turnover above OMR 30 million (CbC threshold equivalent per Oman TP rules)

Economic substance scope: Oman's Income Tax Law introduces comprehensive transfer pricing rules mirroring OECD BEPS Action 8-10; although Oman has no separate Economy Substance Regulations for every SPC, foreign-owned SPCs performing AI data labeling must prove actual management, local offices, and substantive payroll in Oman to avoid BEPS-style mismatch challenges and to access DTA benefits; the tax authority may treat artificial intercompany structures as economic-substance failures and impose penalties

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of up to 3 years; fine of up to 200% of the evaded tax; additional 25% late-payment surcharge; revocation of tax settlement certificate and CR cancellation

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners may be jointly liable for the full evaded tax and penalties if corporate veil is pierced; criminal travel ban and confiscation of personal assets in cases of wilful fraud; potential disqualification from serving as directors in Oman
Shell Company / Artificial Arrangement

Statutory sanction: TP recharacterization, disallowance of intercompany charges, reclassification of an Omani SPC as a shell/non-substantive entity, reassessment at 15% on deemed profits; registration may be suspended or cancelled under commercial law; fines up to OMR 10,000

Director personal liability: Civil misfeasance proceedings; directors may be required to return distributed dividends to satisfy corporate tax debt; if transactions intentionally defeat tax, personal liability for unpaid tax plus interest; travel restrictions imposed until full settlement
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income by shifting profit back to Oman; penalty of 25% of additional tax assessed; mandatory late filing penalty of OMR 1,000; possibly double taxation loss in source jurisdiction if no MAP is initiated

Director personal liability: Directors and tax agent may be jointly assessed where documentation failure is attributable to intentional negligence or false certification; potential administrative fine imposed on each responsible director up to OMR 5,000; aggravated sanction in case of fraud and concealment
