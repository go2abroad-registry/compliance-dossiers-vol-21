Governing statute

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

None. Law 32 of 1927 as amended by Law 62 of 2021 abolishes any mandatory minimum subscribed or paid-up capital and no escrow deposit is required. The articles may declare an authorized capital of US$10,000 in nominal shares (one share minimum), fully subscribed by the incorporator, but payment may be deferred indefinitely at the directors' discretion.

Funding & unlock path: Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá - Certificado de Seguridad de Higiene (fire safety inspection) for the registered office; Dirección General de Salud Ambiental (MINSA) structural and sanitary clearance for the physical business premises; and MITRADEL (Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral) workplace safety registration for any local employees, including compliance with the occupational hazard insurance policy required under Ley No. 23 de 2017.

Paid-in capital rule: None. Law 32 of 1927 as amended by Law 62 of 2021 abolishes any mandatory minimum subscribed or paid-up capital and no escrow deposit is required. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) before a Panamanian notary and file the By-Laws with the Registro Público de Panamá; appoint a resident agent (agente residente) for foreign-owned corporations. 2) Complete the Aviso de Operación filing through the DGI single-window web channel, supplying corporate data, registered office, and statutory representatives. 3) Upon acceptance of the Aviso de Operación, the DGI automatically issues the Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) and an associated VAT/ITBMS taxpayer registration; a separate Certificado de Registro is downloadable from the DGI portal. 4) For a non-resident digital-supply vehicle without a Panamanian branch, the DGI permits remote registration through the special digital-services module, requiring an appointed in-country fiscal representative and an e-Tax digital signature credential. 5) No municipal business licence is required for a pure offshore royalty-holding and cross-border licensing S.A. that conducts no physical activities in Panama; if physical premises are later opened, municipal and social-security registrations become mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation;Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a check-the-box entity-classification election. The S.A. is by default an entity-level taxpayer subject to entity-level income tax exclusively on Panama-source income (Código Fiscal territorial-source rule). Available voluntary elections include: (i) the Sede de Empresas Multinacionales (SEM) regime under Ley 41 de 2007 for qualifying regional service headquarters; (ii) the Régimen Especial de Pequeño Contribuyente for annual turnover up to B/.36,000; (iii) election of monthly or quarterly ITBMS filing frequencies under the general regime; (iv) election of the corporate fiscal-year close date; and (v) investment-incentive elections such as accelerated depreciation under the reformed Código Fiscal. Hybrid-mismatch and transparent-entity elections are not available under Panamanian law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full de facto and de jure dollarization: the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a non-issued unit coinage at 1:1 with USD. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully liberalized; no FX authorization, registration, or prior deposit requirements apply to royalty income, license fees, or capital injections. Profit and dividend remittance is unrestricted under territorial taxation, subject only to bank-level identification of the economic justification for large transfers and settlement of any outstanding DGI (Dirección General de Ingresos) obligations evidenced by Paz y Salvo fiscal clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender regime in force: export and licensing receipts (including digital-scope royalties from stock-music platform sales) are freely retainable and payable in USD without statutory conversion. Statutory timeline: none; mandatory conversion ratio: 0%. The legal framework (liberalized foreign-exchange position under Decree Law 318 of 1971 and subsequent SBP regulations) imposes no repatriation deadline or bank liquidity reserve on trade proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — banking and trust regulator; Panama has no central bank. The US Dollar is legal tender under Decree Law 318 of 1971; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) administers fiscal and monetary policy coordination, while Banco Nacional de Panamá operates solely as a state-owned commercial bank. No currency issuance or exchange-rate management function exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium — offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) are usable as payout-collection convenience rails but cannot serve as the sole treasury nexus. SBP does not license third-country EMI deposit-taking within Panama; the Fintech Law (Ley 208 de 2021) provides a sandbox (Innovation Hub) but no full EMI license category. To ensure regulatory defensibility, platform payout accounts (Stripe, PayPal, EMI wallets) must settle into a USD account at an SBP-regulated bank within 60 days.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing: shareholder wire via SWIFT MT103 to the capital escrow account (in USD) with purpose code 'CAPT — Capital Contribution' and beneficiary narration referencing the capitalization minute (e.g., "Acuerdo de Junta Directiva #___ de [date] — Aporte de Capital Social"). No SBP prior approval, exchange-rate participation, or registration is required; the bank will request a notarized capital-contribution resolution and, for sums above USD 25,000, source-of-funds evidence. All injections must be booked as Paid-In Capital/Additional Paid-In Capital in the statutory books and remain distinct from license revenue for territorial-tax tracing.Clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for cross-border royalty payouts, licensor participation fees, and licensing marketplace withdrawals above USD 10,000 — narration should quote the license reference (Licence ID / Sales Order) to evidence the underlying commercial transaction; (2) domestic USD ACH (ACH Panamá) for local disbursements to Panamanian licensors and suppliers; (3) Commercial LC (UCP 600) is available and technically permissible but economically redundant for the pure-digital audio-licensing model — used only if physical audio-equipment procurement occurs; (4) card-scheme settlement rails (Visa/Mastercard networks) via Stripe/PayPal for outbound refunds and merchant-payout obligations.
