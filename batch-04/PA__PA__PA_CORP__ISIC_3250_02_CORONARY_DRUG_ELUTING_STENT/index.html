Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP), the unified revenue authority under Law 279 of 2023 integrating the former Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) and the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (Customs). Taxpayer e-services portal: SISTAP (https://sistap.anip.gob.pa), used for RUC registration, electronic filing (SIAF), ITBMS returns, and e-invoice validation.

E-government filing portal

Step 2: Obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) from ANIP and complete the municipal Aviso de Operación (business operation notice). Step 3: Secure the MINSA sanitary registration and sanitary operating permit for Class III medical device manufacturing (mandatory prerequisite for ISIC 3250/NAICS 339112 before production starts). Step 4: Register as an ITBMS taxpayer, obtain the fiscal digital certificate (firma electrónica), and activate the Facturación Electrónica (electronic invoicing) authorization. Step 5: If exporting, register as an exporter (Registro de Exportador) with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas and, if hiring employees, open the employer file with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation_with_Territorial_Scope; Tax_Exempt_Zone available under special regimes. A Panamanian S.A. is a juridical person taxed only on Panamanian-source income; foreign-source income is exempt by territoriality. No pass-through applies to the S.A.; shareholders are taxed on dividends paid from Panamanian-source profits (5% for registered shares, 10% for bearer shares, as applicable). Qualified operations may elect Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment under Panama Pacifico, Colon Free Zone, or Ciudad del Saber regimes.

Available election forms

Panama has no US-style check-the-box and no statutory hybrid-mismatch entity elections; an S.A. is always treated as a taxable corporation. Available elections and special regimes: (i) Panama Pacifico Special Economic Area (Law 41 of 2004) and Colon Free Zone (Law 18 of 1948, as amended), granting ITBMS exemptions and substituting ISR with a fixed annual zone fee; (ii) Multinational Company Headquarters (SEM) regime (Law 41 of 2007) - services only, not applicable to manufacturing; (iii) Simplified pequeno contribuyente regime (Law 42 of 2017) for annual gross income up to USD 150,000; (iv) export promotion and drawback regimes under Panamanian export laws. Panama is an MLI signatory and applies territorial sourcing; no hybrid entity classifications are recognized absent specific anti-abuse findings.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold in PAB/USD: any person habitually performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS (Código Fiscal arts. 1058 et seq.). The optional simplified regime applies to 'pequeno contribuyente' taxpayers with annual gross income up to USD 150,000. Exporting manufacturers must also hold the customs Registro de Exportador linked to the same RUC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Panama are zero-rated (0% ITBMS) provided the seller is an ITBMS registrant and the export is evidenced by a definitive export customs declaration (DUA) validated by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas. Services are zero-rated when rendered to a non-resident and economically consumed abroad (Código Fiscal art. 1059-G and Decreto 84 de 2005). For coronary drug-eluting stents, the manufacturer must demonstrate physical dispatch, MINSA-sanitary conformity, and absence of local consumption, and must operate in the general tax regime (free-zone exemption would disqualify ITBMS input credit recovery).

  • Declaración Única Aduanera (DUA) de exportación definitiva validated by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas
  • Electronic commercial invoice (Factura Electrónica) issued under the approved ANIP framework, expressly coded as zero-rated ITBMS 0%
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (conocimiento de embarque) issued to the foreign buyer naming the Panamanian S.A. as shipper
  • Banking inward remittance certificate (comprobante bancario de ingreso de divisas) evidencing collection from the foreign buyer
  • Packing list, certificate of origin, and MINSA Class III medical device sanitary certificate
  • Customs seal or export inspection report evidencing the physical exit of the goods from Panamanian territory

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) administered by ANIP/DGI. All ITBMS-registered taxpayers must issue validated e-invoices through ANIP-certified issuers or direct API integration, transmit each document in real time to the ANIP validation platform, obtain a digital authorization number, and display a QR code. Export invoices must reference the export DUA and the zero-rate code. Non-validated invoices are inadmissible as documentary support for ITBMS input credits, refund claims, or customs clearance.

ANIP audits cross-match DUA data, e-invoice metadata, shipping manifests, and banking inflows in USD to verify genuine export transactions. Discrepancies trigger retrospective assessment at 7% ITBMS with statutory delinquency interest, fines of up to 200% of the evaded tax under Código Fiscal arts. 1132-1134, clawback of any refund previously paid, cancellation of the customs exporter registration and the MINSA license, and criminal prosecution for defraudación tributaria (Código Fiscal art. 1134) carrying imprisonment in cases of intentional documentation fraud.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panamá is a fully dollarized economy with no exchange-control law. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible into US dollars, and profit remittances, dividends, and intra-group payments may be transferred offshore without exchange-control approval. The relevant limitations are tax-related, principally the Panamanian territorial income tax and withholding rules on dividends paid from Panama-source earnings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts. Exporters may retain 100% of USD proceeds in Panamanian banks, use them freely for payments, or remit them abroad without central-bank authorization, time limit, or conversion requirement.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (fiscal agent) and Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (banking supervisor); Panamá has no central bank, and the US dollar is legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low for offshore digital EMIs. An unlicensed offshore EMI cannot maintain a payment account or clear funds for a Panamanian corporate; holding client money or performing clearing without a banking or finance license violates Decreto Ley 2 de 2008. A foreign EMI may serve only as a limited remittance channel through a licensed Panamanian bank, and a Class III medical device manufacturer will still require a full local banking relationship for capital and trade settlement.
