Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá - Dirección de Personas Jurídicas / Sección Mercantil, via the Sede Electrónica (Electronic Registry Portal).

Governing statute

Law 32 of 1927 (General Corporation Law of Panama), as amended, particularly Articles 1-51 covering incorporation, name, registered office, shares, and officers; supplementary provisions of the Código de Comercio apply to commercial activities.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública (public deed of incorporation) executed before a Panamanian Notary Public and filed electronically or physically with the Registro Público de Panamá; alternatively the Public Registry's electronic incorporation form for a Sociedad Anónima.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 112 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The articles of incorporation must state authorized capital, and at least one share must be subscribed and paid. No escrow account is required under Panamanian law; however, for port terminal licensing, the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá may require proof of financial solvency or a bond.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá, including emergency exits, extinguishing systems, and hazardous material storage; structural load and crane inspections by licensed engineers and approved by the competent AMP or municipal building authority; occupational health and workplace safety clearance from MITRADEL covering stevedoring operations, personal protective equipment, and operating procedures in accordance with Executive Decree No. 226 of 2011 on occupational safety in port work.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The articles of incorporation must state authorized capital, and at least one share must be subscribed and paid. No escrow account is required under Panamanian law; however, for port terminal licensing, the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá may require proof of financial solvency or a bond.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); principal portal: SITIO - Sistema Integrado Tributario de Ingresos Online at sitio.dgi.gob.pa; the RUC issuer is DGI while the corporate identity record is held by the Registro Público de Panamá.

E-government filing portal

The foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima must: (1) be incorporated and recorded in the Registro Público with an appointed resident agent (Agente Residente) and Panamanian fiscal domicile; (2) file the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application and Aviso de Operación with the DGI through SITIO, attaching the public deed, resident agent acceptance, beneficial owner declaration (positive data) and, where the sole office is the registered agent, a designation of fiscal address; (3) obtain the DGI resolution assigning the RUC and registration in the ITBMS and withholding taxpayer registers; (4) activate the Firma Electrónica (digital signature) credential and request authorization of invoice numbering (Serie Fiscal), including e-invoicing configuration; (5) for the port concession exercise, complement the process by registering with the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá and the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas as a customs and port operator.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is subject to entity-level corporate income tax at 25% on Panama-source net income only; foreign-source income and intercompany dividends are outside the taxable base; an ordinary S.A. has no pass-through or check-the-box classification; port concessionaires may elect the special port incentive regime under Ley 7 de 1988, in which case qualified new terminal investment can enjoy income tax and ITBMS exemptions (Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment within the concession perimeter). — The foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima must: (1) be incorporated and recorded in the Registro Público with an appointed resident agent (Agente Residente) and Panamanian fiscal domicile; (2) file the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application and Aviso de Operación with the DGI through SITIO, attaching the public deed, resident agent acceptance, beneficial owner declaration (positive data) and, where the sole office is the registered agent, a designation of fiscal address; (3) obtain the DGI resolution assigning the RUC and registration in the ITBMS and withholding taxpayer registers; (4) activate the Firma Electrónica (digital signature) credential and request authorization of invoice numbering (Serie Fiscal), including e-invoicing configuration; (5) for the port concession exercise, complement the process by registering with the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá and the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas as a customs and port operator.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is subject to entity-level corporate income tax at 25% on Panama-source net income only; foreign-source income and intercompany dividends are outside the taxable base; an ordinary S.A. has no pass-through or check-the-box classification; port concessionaires may elect the special port incentive regime under Ley 7 de 1988, in which case qualified new terminal investment can enjoy income tax and ITBMS exemptions (Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment within the concession perimeter).

Available election forms

Panama does not adopt a US-style check-the-box election. Available elections and mechanisms: (a) Multinational Company (SEM) regime under Ley 41 de 2007, with election to apply reduced 50% progressive rates on the first US$200,000 of qualified income; (b) election for the simplified IDRB (Impuesto Decimal sobre los Ingresos Brutos) when annual gross income does not exceed PAB 150,000, in lieu of regular ITBMS; (c) election of the port incentive regime (Ley 7 de 1988) for capital-intensive terminal expansion; (d) hybrid-mismatch and general anti-avoidance clarifications filed with the DGI under consultations because no formal multi-jurisdictional hybrid election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB 150,000 (Panama Balboa at parity with USD) of estimated annual gross taxable turnover; above the threshold regular ITBMS registration is mandatory, while below it the S.A. generally files the quarterly IDRB; a bulk-port stevedoring terminal holding a state concession must register for ITBMS on a de jure basis to zero-rate its export services and recover input credits.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating (0% ITBMS) is available if the supply constitutes a statutory export: (a) tangible goods physically removed from Panama through a customs export declaration processed by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas; or (b) services performed for non-residents and effectively used or exploited abroad, which for a bulk-port stevedoring terminal includes longshore and cargo-handling services on goods in transit, transshipment or destined for export, services to vessels engaged in international trade, and dock access charges billed to foreign shipowners or their local agents. The claimant must be an ITBMS registrant, charge the zero rate on the e-invoice with the export code, and retain the export support file connecting the service order to the customs records.

  • Customs export declaration (DUS/SIGA manifest) electing the definitive export regime or transshipment status for the goods handled at the terminal
  • Bill of Lading / sea waybill and the port terminal stevedoring report showing outbound motor-vessel movement from the Panamanian port
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/credit advice evidencing collection of the foreign proceeds into a Panamanian bank account within the repatriation window
  • Zero-rated e-invoice (with the export code) and the signed stevedoring tariff or service contract with the non-resident shipowner, charterer or multi-modal carrier
  • Vessel fixture and arrival/departure declarations filed with the Autoridad Marítima de Panamá, evidencing the vessel call for each service performance

The DGI requires every ITBMS registrant to issue invoices within a pre-authorised fiscal series (elementos de facturación) obtained through SITIO. Panama has phased in the mandatory electronic invoice (Facturación Electrónica) with QR code, digital signature and XML generation; large taxpayers (annual sales exceeding PAB 1,000,000) and port operators must transmit e-invoice registers to the DGI within the first ten business days of the following month. The model is certification-based digital stamping rather than full real-time fiscal clearance for every document; the DGI maintains real-time audit access and electronic ledger obligations (transmission of purchase and export books).

The DGI audits the export file together with the customs declaration, bill of lading and collection evidence. If the foreign exchange is not credited to a Panamanian account within 180 days after year-end, the related revenue is automatically recharacterized as Panama-source taxable income; the ITBMS 7% becomes payable with recargo surcharge up to 50%, quarterly compound interest (currently 10% annual), and principal clawback of the input credits previously recovered. A deficient or fraudulent export claim constitutes defraudación tributaria under Código Fiscal Art. 1042-B and Ley 8 de 2010, producing fines of two to three times the evaded tax and imprisonment from 2 to 5 years for willful falsification; the AMP may administratively terminate the concession and the DGI may suspend the taxpayer from the exporter registry.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. The U.S. dollar is unrestricted legal tender (Decree-Law 9 of 1998); the Panamanian balboa (PAB) exists only as fractional coinage at a fixed 1:1 USD parity. There are no exchange controls, no restrictions on foreign ownership of domestic accounts, and no prior authorization for inward or outward capital movements. Profit remittance is equally unrestricted, subject to a definitive 10% dividend withholding tax on distributions of Panama-source net income (Fiscal Code Art. 733) and to the bank's request for a DGI paz y salvo on large disbursements. The territorial tax model ensures that non-Panama-source earnings are not taxed, and no IFA/CFC repatriation surcharge applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation window for export proceeds. Stevedoring and port terminal receipts may be settled directly in USD offshore or swept into local operational accounts at the treasury's discretion. The only binding duties are documentary: DGI-compliant invoicing (Facturación Electrónica where applicable), UAF reporting of cash transactions above USD 10,000 per Law 23/70, and full traceability of vessel tally and cargo documentation to support the economic substance of each trade settlement.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the consolidated prudential supervisor and, under Law 15 of 2023, the licensing authority for payment service providers. Panama has no traditional central bank; full dollarization delegates monetary policy de facto to the U.S. Federal Reserve. Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the State's fiscal agent and treasury clearing intermediary. The Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) administers AML/CFT reporting under Law 23 of 2015 as amended by Law 70 of 2019, while the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) enforces fiscal compliance on fund remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium-Low. Offshore electronic money institutions from the EU, UK, or BVI cannot lawfully provide primary in-country clearing in Panama without an SBP-authorized local license. Since Law 15 of 2023, SBP Rule 001/2023 requires payment-service providers serving Panamanian residents to obtain authorization under the new Empresa de Pagos regime. Practical viability is limited to card-acquiring and payroll aggregation through SBP-licensed fintech processors; the master liquidity account must remain at a Tier-1 commercial bank. Recommendation: use a licensed local PSP solely as a payment aggregator and never as the primary settlement rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound equity injections must route as USD-denominated SWIFT MT103 wires from the investor's home account to the S.A.'s operational or escrow account at the designated Tier-1 bank. The wire beneficiary name must exactly match the registered name in the Registro Público; the remittance narrative field must state 'Capital contribution to PA_CORP (S.A.) – bulk port stevedoring terminal' and reference the shareholder resolution number. For injections above USD 250,000, submit a source-of-funds packet, a DGI tax-clearance certificate (paz y salvo) where requested, and the signed beneficial-owner declaration. Optional FDI registration with the Ministry of Commerce secures a formal foreign-investment certificate useful for future free-repatriation evidence. No exchange-control approval is required for entry of USD capital.Operational outflows (bunkers, stevedoring labor, port dues, insurance, equipment maintenance) clear via SWIFT MT103 for medium-value payments and via the local ACH Panamá scheme for payroll and domestic supplier settlements. Port-authority clearing, including berthage, wharfage, and navigation services, is executed through the terminal concessionaire's clearing account structure at the port operator (e.g., Panama Ports Company settlement windows) or directly with AMP. Heavy capital expenditures (ship-to-shore cranes, conveyor systems, dredging contracts) require documentary import Letters of Credit issued under UCP600 by Banco General or Banistmo, with seller drafts, inspection certificates, and ocean bills of lading as the document set. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Form 185 where annual gross income exceeds B/.3,000,000 (equivalent to USD 3,000,000 at parity) or related-party transactions exceed B/.750,000; Master File required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above B/.50,000,000; Country-by-Country reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue of USD 85,000,000 or more

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Executive Decree 592 of 2020, effective 1 January 2021) apply to relevant activities, including logistics and maritime services conducted with related parties; the bulk port terminal has natural substance through physical assets, licensed marine equipment, and local longshore staff, but intercompany stevedore-charterer arrangements with offshore affiliates must be supported by annual substance statements, local decision-making records, and contemporaneous operational documentation

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Fraudulent Form 101)

Statutory sanction: Fiscal penalty of 100% to 200% of the evaded tax, monthly 5% surcharge (capped at 50%), statutory interest, criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment of up to five years for serious evasion, and DGI re-determination based on deemed gross income

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent who sign fraudulent returns are joint and severally liable for the evaded tax and fines; criminal convictions expose directors to preventive detention, asset embargo, and an impedimento de salida (travel ban) during proceedings
Shell Company / Artificial Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: DGI reattribution of the full Panama-source profit to the S.A., denial of deductions, fines of B/.1,000 to B/.50,000, and ESR registry strike for failure to file the annual substance statement

Director personal liability: Courts pierce the corporate veil where the S.A. is a mere instrumentality; directors are personally liable for diverted profits, face precautionary attachment of personal assets, and may lose their commercial licenses where shell structures link to regulated activities
Transfer Pricing Documentary Failure

Statutory sanction: Fine of 1% of intercompany transaction value (up to B/.50,000), separate fine up to B/.15,000 for non-filing of Form 185, and a transfer pricing adjustment that increases Panama-source income and triggers the 10% dividend tax on deemed distributions

Director personal liability: Directors who approve intercompany transactions without substantive evidence are personally jointly liable for the additional assessment; the DGI may impose a travel ban pending satisfaction of the revised tax debt
Money Laundering (AML - Law 23 of 2015, as amended by Law 70 of 2019)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of 5 to 12 years for money laundering, confiscation of terminal assets, fines of up to double the value of the laundered property, suspension of banking and port licenses, and blacklisting by the UAF (Financial Analysis Unit)

Director personal liability: Natural-person principals face pre-trial detention, INTERPOL red notices, travel bans, and forfeiture of personal and corporate property for willful blindness to suspicious cargo-related transactions
Undeclared Cross-Border Dividend Remittance

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the 10% dividend tax on the gross remittance, increased by a 50% non-declaration penalty, monthly 5% surcharges capped at 50%, and DGI enforcement by bank levy and restriction of vessel clearance documents until the debt is secured

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize or effect the distribution without retaining the tax are jointly and severally liable; the dividend tax constitutes a liquidation obligation attaching to directors' personal assets and supports an impedimento de salida
