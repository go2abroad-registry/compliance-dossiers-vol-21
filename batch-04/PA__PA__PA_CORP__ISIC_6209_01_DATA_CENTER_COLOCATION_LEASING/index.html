Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; taxpayer portal: DGI e-Tax / T@ Pagas (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must appoint a Panamanian resident agent or legal representative, execute a public deed or private document recognized by Panamanian counsel, and file registration with the DGI to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) and ITBMS VAT registration before commencing commercial activity. Registration is performed through the T@ Pagas online portal using a digital signature.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. Panama's Tax Code does not provide a domestic hybrid mismatch election. Affiliated Panamanian corporations may elect consolidated income tax filing under Law 52 of 2013, subject to DGI authorization and fulfillment of ownership and common-control requirements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold; any person or entity making taxable transfers of goods or providing taxable services in Panama, including non-residents with Panamanian-source nexus, must register for RUC and ITBMS before the first taxable operation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% ITBMS) is available for exports of movable goods physically shipped outside Panama and for services rendered and effectively used entirely outside Panama. To qualify, the taxpayer must substantiate that the benefit of the service is not derived from Panamanian territory. Data center colocation services performed at a Panamanian facility do not automatically qualify; the DGI may allow zero-rating only where the service is structured as a genuine offshore service with foreign consumption, supported by executed contracts and certificates of service delivery abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación de Mercancías)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa de Divisas) or bank transfer evidence
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foreign service contract and/or Certificate of Services Rendered Abroad (for offshore services)

Mandatory electronic invoicing under the DGI's 'Facturación Electrónica' regime. Taxpayers must use DGI-approved electronic invoicing software or connect via the DGI validation platform's API web services to transmit invoices in near real time for pre-issuance authorization. Each authorized invoice must display a unique authorization number or CUFE plus a QR code/digital stamp; non-compliant invoices are not valid for tax credit or deduction purposes.

DGI may audit zero-rated transactions for up to five years; failure to substantiate export or offshore service status results in full VAT clawback at 7%, plus monthly late-payment interest (approximately 3% per month) and penalties ranging from 25% to 200% of the omitted tax. Willful fraudulent claims constitute tax evasion under the Panamanian Penal Code and may lead to monetary fines and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility by statute and by design. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a currency unit fixed 1:1 to the US Dollar, and USD notes and coins are legal tender throughout the Republic. There is no legal distinction between capital-account and current-account transactions, no exchange licence regime, and no prior authorisation for dividend distributions, loan repayments, or capital disinvestment. The only remittance impediment is fiscal, not monetary: dividends distributed from Panama-source income of a territorial-tax Sociedad Anónima are subject to 10% withholding under Law 52 of 2012, whereas distributions funded from foreign-source income are not subject to remittance-linked taxation. A colocation entity whose servers and rack-leasing activity are physically situated in Panama will be treated as generating Panama-source income; profit repatriation to a foreign parent is freely executable via SWIFT within 3-5 business days once the applicable 10% dividend tax is declared and settled under the company's RUC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation deadline on export receipts or service income. Monthly colocation and bare-metal hosting revenues received from offshore anchor tenants in USD remain immediately available for operating expenditures, debt service, capex, or shareholder distributions without reporting to the SBP, the UAF, or the MEF. For corporate tax purposes the revenue is taxable in Panama because the servers, network equipment, and colocation halls are physically located within Panamanian territory; however, no FX conversion or delivery obligation attaches to those proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), created under Decree Law No. 2 of 2008, is the banking and FX market regulator and supervises authorisation, capital, and AML/CFT compliance of all licensed banks. Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and public-sector clearing agent but holds no monetary policy or exchange control authority. The Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF), established under Law 23 of 2015, is the national financial intelligence unit for cross-border funds surveillance. The Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) administers taxation but exercises no FX control function.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium-Low as a primary treasury rail; acceptable only as a secondary collection layer for e-commerce ingress. Legal constraints: foreign electronic-money institutions and digital neobanks hold no Panamanian banking licence, are not supervised by the SBP, and possess no passporting rights into Panama; a Panama S.A. may lawfully open such accounts as ordinary offshore payment accounts, but Tier-1 Panamanian banks apply a reputational-risk overlay and may treat heavy treasury dependence on unlicensed EMIs as an adverse AML-CFT signal during the first 18 months after account opening. Recommended structure: connect a single EMI (Wise or Mercury-type) to card-acquiring rails for small colocation clients, execute daily sweeps of balances into the primary Panamanian operating account, and never retain more than three months of operating expenses on the EMI platform.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Protocol: the shareholder executes a capital-contribution board resolution; funds are remitted via SWIFT MT103 from the parent's regulated bank account to the Panamanian operating account. Wire template: Field 52A (ordering institution); Field 56A (intermediary, e.g., Citibank N.A. New York or JPMorgan Chase New York for the receiving Panamanian bank); Field 59 (beneficiary: exact S.A. legal name and the bank account number — Panama does not use IBAN); Field 70 (reference: 'BOARD RESOLUTION 2025-XXXX / CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION'). Panama imposes no statutory purpose codes, no central-bank notification, and no prior approval; compliance verification is bank-driven and comprises the shareholder register update, UBO declaration, and source-of-funds evidence. For injections above USD 250,000 the bank may temporarily impose a 3-to-6-month compliance escrow overlay before free availability; treasury must pre-clear the transfer with the onboarding officer at least three business days before value date.Clearing rails are USD-dominant. (1) Domestic disbursements — data-centre facility rent, ENSA/Edemet utilities, payroll and CSS contributions, and local maintenance vendors — settle via the private ACH operated by the Panamanian Banking Association Clearing House and the Banco Nacional de Panamá government-clearing channels; execution is T+1 and fee-based for cross-bank transfers. (2) International trade payments — bandwidth backbone contracts, subsea cable carrier fees, and server imports from Dell and HPE — execute via SWIFT MT103 over Fedwire/CHIPS correspondent networks with T+0 to T+1 value; EUR-denominated European carrier invoices are paid through a multicurrency USD/EUR account at Citibank Panama. (3) Commercial letters of credit under UCP 600 are permitted and recommended only for equipment imports exceeding USD 100,000 when the supplier requires a sight LC confirmed by a foreign bank; the colocation services contract itself should never be structured as a documentary credit. Monthly invoicing uses ACH for domestic tenants and SWIFT MT103 for offshore tenants; inbound client USD is credited same-day, and remittances originating from Latin American banks routed through New York correspondents may incur one additional value date.FX spot (USD/EUR, USD/GBP, USD/CHF) executed OTC with any SBP-licensed bank; no prior Central Bank approval is required because Panama has no exchange controls; settlement is T+2 via U.S. correspondent networks (Fedwire/CHIPS) and euro-area correspondent accounts.Deliverable FX forwards (DF) of 1-to-12-month tenor for EUR, GBP, and CHF against USD under ISDA or a local master agreement; banks require documented underlying exposure such as EUR-nominated equipment leases or European bandwidth invoices.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing Latin American currencies (COP, MXN, CLP, ARS) cash-settled in USD, offered by the international desks of Citibank Panama, BAC, and Banistmo for tenants paying in local currencies; NDFs avoid any onshore exchange-control filing by the Panamanian entity.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) USD/EUR for synthetic conversion of a USD-denominated fit-out or infrastructure debt facility into EUR liabilities, available from Citibank Panama subject to ISDA plus credit-support annex and an approved board treasury policy.Multicurrency deposit accounts and cash-pooling (USD/EUR/GBP/CHF) at Banco General or Citibank Panama enabling natural netting of receivable and payable currencies without derivative exposure; this passive hedge is the recommended first-line treasury strategy for a colocation business.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% withholding at the shareholder level on dividend distributions to residents and non-residents. Instead, the distributing Panamanian S.A. bears a 5% 'Impuesto sobre Dividendos' on dividends paid out of profits derived from Panamanian-source income. If the S.A. has fully paid the 25% corporate income tax on the distributed profits, the dividend tax is effectively creditable/eliminated. Dividends paid from foreign-source (territorial exempt) profits are entirely excluded from the dividend tax. Thus, a non-resident data-center investor can repatriate net profits at a maximum effective burden of 25% CIT plus, where relevant, a minor dividend tax on the residual; no additional dividend WHT is levied.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed within 3 months following the fiscal year-end. A calendar-year taxpayer must file by March 31 of the following year. Advance payments ('pagos a cuenta') computed at 2% of gross revenue are payable in two installments due in June and September of the fiscal year.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Same statutory deadline as Formulario 20: within 3 months after fiscal year-end (e.g., March 31 for calendar-year taxpayers). Extension of the CIT return does not extend the Form 4126 deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD (Balboa) 250,000: Formulario 4126 and a formal Local File study are mandatory when the aggregate annual value of transactions with related parties equals or exceeds USD 250,000. A Master File is required for multinational groups with consolidated group revenue equal to or above USD 5,000,000, and the Panamanian entity must also file Form 4126 if it engages in related-party transactions above the USD 250,000 threshold. A contemporaneous transfer-pricing study must be prepared by the filing date; penalties apply for absence or late preparation.

Economic substance scope: Panama has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute equivalent to the BVI/Cayman model. However, Law 70 of 2019 and DGI administrative criteria require an S.A. invoking the territorial 'export of services' exemption or an offshore income exclusion to evidence genuine economic activity: a physical data-center facility in Panama, local contracted employees or responsible officers, operational decision-making in Panama, and the actual generation of revenue through servers physically located in Panamanian territory. Non-resident-owned S.A.s that hold colocation contracts and bank accounts but lack any local operational presence, employees, or power/network utilities are treated as shell entities; the DGI may disregard the corporate form, reclassify all global income as Panamanian-source, assess 25% plus penalties, and impose interest. Since 2015, Law 23 of 2015 also requires all legal entities to maintain minimal accounting records; failure to present these records to the DGI triggers administrative fines and supports the presumption that the entity is a facade.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Territorial Claim

Statutory sanction: Surcharge of 10% of the tax due plus monthly interest of 1%; under Law 23 of 2015 and Article 279 of the Código Penal, tax evasion is a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and a fine of 2 to 5 times the evaded tax; the DGI may redetermine taxable income by factoring external indicators and apply the 2% gross-income minimum.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and the resident agent who knowingly sign false returns are criminally liable; civil restitution of the evaded tax plus interest is imposed personally, and the DGI can obtain an 'Aviso de Salida' (departure prohibition) against them to prevent flight, alongside asset seizures.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The DGI will apply the 'levantamiento del velo corporativo' (piercing the corporate veil), reclassify all income as Panamanian-source, assess 25% tax, add the 10% surcharge and monthly interest, and impose fines under Law 70/2019; the S.A. may be administratively dissolved and struck from the Public Registry.

Director personal liability: Directors and shareholders who use the shell to evade tax are personally liable for the full assessed tax; courts may order travel bans, freeze Panamanian bank accounts, and impose sentences of 2 to 5 years under the tax-evasion statute, with a further penalty for violation of the beneficial-ownership registry rules.
AML / Failure to Maintain Minimal Accounting Records

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of up to USD 1,000,000 per violation under Law 23/2015 for legal entities; the S.A. can be barred from banking activity; money laundering charges under Article 254 of the Código Penal carry imprisonment of 5 to 10 years, extended to 12 years for aggravated amounts or criminal-organization involvement.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers, directors, and the beneficial owner are subject to personal criminal prosecution; the court can issue travel bans, freezing orders, and forfeiture of corporate assets; civil liability extends to restitution of laundered funds, and directors found to have knowingly ignored AML controls can be incarcerated for up to 10 years.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The DGI may reprice each transaction using the most appropriate OECD method, disallow deductions for the excessive portion, assess 25% tax on the upward adjustment, apply the 10% surcharge and monthly interest, and levy a fine of up to 1% of the transaction value (minimum USD 5,000) for the missing Form 4126.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve the non-arm's-length intercompany contracts are jointly and severally liable for the additional tax, interest, and penalties; in cases of documentation fraud or false certification, directors face criminal tax-evasion charges with imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and a departure ban pending resolution.
