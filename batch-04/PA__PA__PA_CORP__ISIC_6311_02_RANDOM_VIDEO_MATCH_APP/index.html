Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; online services portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

For a Panama S.A. (PA_CORP), the statutory TIN is the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes), issued by the DGI. A company must first obtain registered articles / aviso de operación from the Public Registry of Panama. The legal representative or resident fiscal agent must then submit to DGI: (i) notarized RUC registration form; (ii) certified copy of the public deed and articles of incorporation; (iii) copy of the mercantile registry inscription (Ficha/Inscripción); (iv) identity documents (CED/PASSPORT) of directors, officers and resident agent; and (v) evidence of the Panamanian registered office address. DGI assigns the permanent RUC and, where required, activates the ITBMS/VAT ID. A foreign entity without a Panama branch that undertakes taxable activities in Panama registers through an appointed fiscal representative under the same procedure. PA_CORP must keep the RUC active and file zero returns where no Panama-source taxable activity is generated, although foreign-source income remains outside Panama's territorial tax charge.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope (Entity_Level_Taxation): a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is a separate juridical person automatically taxed at entity level; only Panama-source income is assessable, foreign-source income is exempt, and there is no pass-through/transparent status. — For a Panama S.A. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (Entity_Level_Taxation): a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is a separate juridical person automatically taxed at entity level; only Panama-source income is assessable, foreign-source income is exempt, and there is no pass-through/transparent status.

Available election forms

None equivalent to a U.S. check-the-box or OECD hybrid-mismatch election. Panamanian law mandates entity-level taxation of a S.A.; no classification election is available between corporate and pass-through status. Administrative elections limited to accounting basis (accrual/cash where permitted), voluntary ITBMS registration, small-taxpayer simplified regime, and territorial-source apportionment methods for mixed activities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 0 - no general economic threshold; every person habitually engaged in taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama must register with DGI before operating. Occasional non-business transfers are outside the regime. A DGI-designated small-taxpayer simplified regime may reduce compliance obligations for low-turnover traders, but does not eliminate the RUC/ITBMS registration duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

For goods: zero-rated ITBMS only when the goods are physically exported from Panama under a valid customs export declaration and the sale is to a non-resident customer. For services (including real-time video matching and cross-border social telephony): zero-rating applies where the recipient is not resident in Panama and the service is economically consumed/used outside Panama. PA_CORP must maintain per-transaction evidence that the user/subscriber is non-resident and that the digital service is not enjoyed in Panama; Apple_IAP and Paymentwall settlement reports and IP/geolocation logs are acceptable corroboration. If a Panamanian consumer obtains the service or payment is received with a Panama card/IP, the 7% local ITBMS must be collected, even if the platform is foreign-incorporated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) - applicable to physical goods only; not applicable to pure digital video-match services
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill - applicable to physical goods export only; not used for offshore services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or bank settlement statement evidencing the amount paid by the non-resident customer and credited to a Panamanian account
  • Apple IAP and Paymentwall payout statements, service contracts, user terms, and IP-geolocation evidence demonstrating non-Panamanian counterparty and offshore place of use

All DGI-registered taxpayers designated for online invoicing must issue digital invoices through approved or DGI-hosted software in the SIFE (Sistema de Facturación Electrónica) environment. Invoices must be submitted to DGI through its web-services/API in real time or near-real time and incorporate a DGI-assigned acceptance code, electronic control stamp and QR/fiscal hash. The DGI may validate/censure invoice series before and after issuance; failure to transmit transactional data through the API is treated as non-issued tax documentation.

DGI may reclassify a claimed zero-rated transaction as taxable at 7% if the export/foreign-use evidence is incomplete or contradictory; assess omitted tax, monthly statutory surcharges and interest; apply fine scales of 50% to 300% of evaded tax; deny or claw back VAT credits and refunds with retrospective effect (generally 5 years, or longer where fraud); and file criminal tax-fraud charges. Directors, the resident agent and signatories are jointly and severally liable for overstated export exemptions or fabricated export documentation, and criminal conviction carries imprisonment of up to 6 years and disqualification from DGI e-invoicing and export facilitation programs.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system in which the United States dollar is the sole unrestricted legal tender for all obligations; the balboa is merely a coin-denominated unit equal to 1:1 with the USD and is not independently issue-prone. There is no exchange control law, no central bank discount window, and no statutory restriction on current account or capital account convertibility. Repatriation of branch profits or dividends to foreign shareholders is freely permitted via wire transfer in USD, although Panamanian territorial tax rules impose a 5% corporate dividend tax on distributions sourced from Panamanian business profits and a nominal annual registration fee on licensed corporations. The absence of a formal central bank means that foreign exchange rate formation is purely market-driven, and balance-of-payments settlement occurs through the private banking correspondent network in the United States.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement under Panama law to surrender export receipts to any official authority, nor is there a mandatory conversion ratio or time-bound repatriation deadline. Export proceeds may be legitimately retained in USD or foreign currency accounts at authorized Panamanian banks, and the free disposition of export earnings is expressly permitted because the USD is the national legal tender. The only relevant obligations relate to documentary transparency (e.g., customs declarations, invoices, and bill-of-lading records) and to bank record-keeping under Panamanian Law 41 of 2000, as amended, where receipts exceeding US$10,000 trigger customer due diligence and suspicious transaction reporting obligations to the UAF.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) in coordination with the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) act as ancillary supervisory bodies

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Cross-border digital EMI banking, including foreign payment e-money institutions such as Airwallex, Meridian, or US-based chartered bank-partner platforms, is viable only as an ancillary treasury rail, rated 'Medium' overall. Panamanian banking law does not formally prohibit the initiation of accounts with non-resident EMIs, and the USD-denominated ecosystem permits direct sweep structures; however, SBP circulars and UAF guidelines impose an 'enhanced due diligence register' for any corporate client using an underlying foreign EMI, requiring proof of EMI licensing in its home jurisdiction, audited financial statements of the EMI, and a written rationale for why a domestic Panamanian bank account is insufficient. Foreign EMI onboarding for the PA_CORP video-match application is further constrained by the enhanced AML risk tier assigned to digital dating and social telephony platforms, making domestic bank sponsorship or a co-account at a Tier-1 Panamanian bank materially more efficient.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be injected through the regular SWIFT channel using MT103 from the shareholder's account at a foreign bank to the Panamanian operational or escrow account at a Tier-1 licensed bank. The beneficiary SWIFT/BIC, ABA routing, and account statement should explicitly reference the purpose of the disbursement as 'CAPITAL_CONTRIBUTION' or 'INVESTMENT' using standard banking comment fields, accompanied by a board resolution file in the bank's internal KYC system. While Panama does not assign official customs codes or central-bank purpose codes, UAF-prescribed due diligence requires the bank to write 'REPRESENTATIVE OFFICE FUNDING – VIDEO MATCH PLATFORM' in the instruction; electronic verification can be expedited by issuing a SWIFT MT199 pre-advice from the investor's bank 24 hours before the principal transfer. The full routing path should be optimized through US correspondent banks (e.g., JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, Bank of America) as Panama has no central bank settlement infrastructure, and the transaction should never be segmented to avoid the US$10,000 due-diligence reporting threshold.Clearing rails for trade settlements are domestic USD clearings (ACH Panama, operated through the Panamá Clearing House) for local disbursements and SWIFT-based MT103 wire transfers for all cross-border commercial payments. For merchant payouts to licensors, content providers, or technology vendors, the recommended rail is MT103 'for the account' via correspondent U.S. banks with standard ISO 20022 remittance data (payment reference: INVOICE NUMBER; platform transaction ID; ISIC_6311 service descriptor). For larger settlement flows (e.g., ≥US$500,000 or monthly recurring infrastructure licensing), the entity should utilise Commercial Letters of Credit (L/C) with confirmed irrevocable undertakings from a Panamanian issuing bank, with documents to include commercial invoice, provider acceptance certificate, and UAF-compliant transaction reports. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory when annual related-party transaction volume exceeds B/.5 million (Panamanian Balboas, 1:1 USD), or when the taxpayer's total gross revenue exceeds B/.5 million and it engages in controlled transactions. A simplified Transfer Pricing Declaration (Form 200 annex) is required above B/.5 million; full contemporaneous documentation is required for intra-group licensing, app-store commissions, and service fees.

Economic substance scope: While Panama has not adopted EU/Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), the DGI and the SEM law require economic substance for tax residence and treaty benefits. A Sociedad Anónima selling a link-only video match app must maintain: a physical office or registered agent with factual activity, local board minutes, a Panamanian bank account, and records showing effective management and control. Failure to demonstrate substance will lead to reclassification as a tax dominus of the shareholders' foreign income and can void transactionality.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Surcharge of 100% on the evaded tax, interest at the Banco Nacional rate, seizure of bank accounts, and criminal fine equivalent to 2 to 5 times the tax evaded. Criminal imprisonment of 5 to 10 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and legal representatives are personally liable for the full tax liability if they signed a knowingly false return; civil restitution, travel bans (impedimento de salida), and up to 10 years' imprisonment.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The DGI may nullify the transfer of tax residence and treat all worldwide revenue as Panamanian-source, imposing 25% tax on gross receipts without deductions; administrative fine up to B/.100,000; cancellation of the corporate registration (matrícula) by the Registro Público.

Director personal liability: The registered agent may be struck off; directors face personal joinder for the corporate tax debt, disqualification from holding a position in Panama-based companies for 5 years, and potential criminal sanctions for operating an undeclared exercise of commerce.
AML / Anti-Money Laundering Failure

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015, fines from 100,000 to 5,000,000 Balboas per violation; dismantling of the legal entity if used for money laundering; up to 12 years' imprisonment for directors who knowingly fail to report.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable for AML fines; freezing of personal assets; travel bans; extradition may be sought by foreign jurisdictions for connected fraud.
Withholding Tax & Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Withholding failure: 10% surcharge on the unpaid WHT plus interest back to the payment date; no offset credit to the shareholder. Transfer pricing: DGI adjusts the taxable base and imposes a 25% tax on the undeclared margin, plus a penalty of 50% of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: The directors who caused the corporation to declare dividends without WHT are jointly liable for the unpaid WHT; audit findings can be converted into criminal tax evasion proceedings; personal bank guarantees and judicial mortgages may be imposed.
