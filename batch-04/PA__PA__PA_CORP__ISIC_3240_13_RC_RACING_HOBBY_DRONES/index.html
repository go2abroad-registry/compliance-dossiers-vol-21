Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; electronic taxpayer portal: SITIO (Sistema Informático Tributario de Ingresos Online) — https://sitio.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by: (i) filing the RUC registration application with the DGI, either directly or through the mandatory Panamanian resident agent, attaching the public deed of incorporation, the certificate of appointment of directors, and the municipal Aviso de Operación; (ii) completing the DGI RUC registration and ITBMS taxpayer enrollment; and (iii) obtaining the DGI electronic credentials (digital signature or E-Token and SITIO user profile) for online filing. Non-resident shareholders are permitted and no minimum paid-in capital is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is subject to the 25% standard corporate income tax only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income, including profits derived from exports sold to non-resident customers and manufacturing income effectively earned abroad, is outside the Panamanian territorial tax base.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a US-style check-the-box election. Statutory elective mechanisms include: (i) registration as a Multinational Headquarters (SEM under Law 41 of 2007) entitling qualifying income to a 5% effective corporate income tax rate; (ii) incorporation within the Colon Free Zone or other free-zone regimes that exempt or reduce ITBMS and income tax on international operations; and (iii) execution of legal stability agreements (contratos de estabilidad jurídica) fixing the applicable tax regime for the agreed term. Hybrid-mismatch outcomes are structurally limited by the territorial source doctrine rather than by express elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary de-minimis threshold for ordinary registration: any person habitually effecting taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS from the first taxable operation. A simplified fixed-quota regime for 'Pequeños Contribuyentes' is available to taxpayers with annual gross income not exceeding B/. 30,000 (USD 30,000), subject to DGI regulations; exports are zero-rated.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% ITBMS rate applies to exports of tangible goods that physically leave Panamanian customs territory under a definitive export declaration and to services rendered to non-resident clients that are effectively used outside Panama. The exporter must substantiate the actual cross-border supply, the customs exit of the goods, and the receipt of payment from abroad through the Panamanian banking system. Input ITBMS attributable to zero-rated export supplies remains creditable; where creditable input tax exceeds output tax, the exporter may apply for a refund via the SITIO electronic procedure.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación Aduanera validated by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing the physical export and destination of the goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice issued by a Panamanian licensed bank evidencing receipt of the foreign buyer's payment)

Mandatory electronic invoicing was introduced by DGI Resolution No. 201-4528 of 31 December 2019 and subsequent implementing resolutions. Enrolled taxpayers must transmit invoice data through DGI-certified billing software or direct API integration with the DGI SITIO module for near-real-time validation and must display the DGI fiscal authorization seal, which includes a unique invoice series number and a QR verification code. The mandate is phased by annual income bracket, beginning with large taxpayers whose annual income exceeds B/. 300,000, and covers both B2B and B2C transactions, with monthly electronic reporting of issued and received invoices.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to ex-post audit by the DGI and the customs authority. Unsupported or fraudulent claims trigger retrospective clawback of the 7% ITBMS plus statutory monthly late-payment surcharges and default interest, administrative fines up to 100%–300% of the omitted tax, and criminal liability for tax fraud (defraudación tributaria) under the Código Fiscal, carrying imprisonment of up to five years and suspension from simplified, free-zone, and other preferential regimes. The State's assessment period is generally seven years from the relevant filing date.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists for both current-account and capital-account transactions. The dollarized regime eliminates convertibility risk and foreign-currency rationing; there are no capital-flow restrictions, no pre-approval requirements for cross-border transfers, and no central-bank registration for foreign investment. Dividend and profit remittances to non-resident shareholders are freely remittable subject only to the standard Panamanian final dividend withholding tax (statutory rate generally 10% of distributed dividends). The operative friction points are bank-level KYC/AML due diligence under Law 23 of 2015, beneficial-ownership disclosure, and secondary OFAC compliance risk embedded in the US correspondent clearing chain.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no mandatory surrender, no conversion quota, and no statutory repatriation timeline for export proceeds. Export receivables from FPV drone sales may be retained indefinitely in USD in Panamanian or foreign bank accounts. The only reporting obligations arise from SBP transaction-monitoring thresholds and suspicious-transaction report rules, not from any exchange-control statute.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the sole prudential banking and AML/CTF supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as a commercial bank and fiscal agent of the State. The US Dollar is full legal tender and circulates at par with the Balboa (PAB), which exists only as coinage. There is no monetary authority issuing currency, setting reserve policy, or administering exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Medium-Low for offshore EMIs and Medium-High for SBP-licensed domestic fintechs. Law 133 of 2020 (as amended by Law 141 of 2021) creates a digital-hub licensing regime enabling SBP-licensed fintechs to perform fully digital onboarding, e-wallet issuance, and payment aggregation in USD, which is operationally attractive for a low-risk e-commerce FPV drone seller. However, Panamanian fintechs lack commercial LC issuance, trade-loan facilities, and treasury-grade FX execution. Offshore EMIs (Wise Business, Payoneer, Revolut Business) are not regulated in Panama and cannot serve as the primary concentration account because US correspondent banks treat Panama-domiciled flows with heightened de-risking scrutiny; they are acceptable only as auxiliary settlement conduits for PayPal/Stripe payouts, with the SBP-licensed local bank retaining all core liquidity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Panama's dollarized regime allows a straightforward SWIFT MT103 USD wire from the shareholder's bank to the S.A.'s Panamanian account. No central-bank filing, no purpose-code registry, and no FX approval exist; the remittance field should state 'Capital Injection – PA_CORP S.A.' or 'Aporte de Capital'. To prevent bank compliance holds, execute a notarized shareholder loan or equity contribution agreement, update the share register in the corporate book, pass a board resolution recording receipt and allocation, and retain the SWIFT confirmation plus bank statements. Verification by the receiving bank follows standard SBP protocol: identity confirmation, source of funds, and matching against the corporate ownership file. No minimum paid-in capital is required for a Panamanian S.A., so a B/. 10,000 (USD 10,000) contribution is bank-acceptable.(1) Ordinary supplier payments via SWIFT MT103 wires denominated in USD; (2) commercial Letters of Credit opened by the local bank via SWIFT MT700/MT707 where suppliers require documentary security, especially for China-sourced drone components; (3) interbank cover settlements for beneficiary banks without direct accounts using SWIFT MT202 COV; and (4) PSP aggregations, where PayPal and Stripe settle in USD by ACH or card-rail credit into the Panamanian account without FX conversion. For the China supply chain, a 30–60 day LC with inspection-based documents against payment is the recommended clearing structure to avoid prepayment risk.USD/CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed offshore via the bank's correspondent network, used to hedge CNY-denominated supplier exposure where deliverable CNY is otherwise unavailable;USD/EUR currency forwards and FX swaps for European component and material purchases;USD/GBP outright forwards for UK e-commerce disbursements and logistics costs;Cross-currency basis swaps on financed trade payables to align USD funding costs with CNY/EUR procurement exposures;Plain-vanilla USD call/put currency options covering contingent FX risk on supplier contracts above USD 500,000.
