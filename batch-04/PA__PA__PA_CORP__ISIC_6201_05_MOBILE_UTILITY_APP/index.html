Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá (Panama Public Registry), Sección de Personas Jurídicas, Panama City.

Governing statute

Ley No. 32 de 1927 (General Corporation Law for Sociedades Anónimas), as amended — Articles 1 to 14 (incorporation and capital), Article 5 (minimum authorized share capital), Article 22 (resident agent and registered office), Article 27 (directors and officers); complemented by the Regulations of the Registro Público de Panamá (Acuerdo No. 62 de 2009) and the general commercial registry provisions of the Código Judicial.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública de Constitución de Sociedad Anónima (notarial public deed of incorporation) inscribed in the Registro Público de Panamá.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum authorized capital of US$10,000 per Article 5 of Ley No. 32 de 1927. No escrow account or paid-in capital deposit is required by statute; capital is subscribed and may be paid in installments as determined by the board before inscription. The authorized capital amount determines the Registry inscription tariff and the annual corporate franchise tax (currently US$300 under the effective tax regime).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, Republic of Panama; online portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (Panama e-Tax), https://sede.dgi.gob.pa.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima, the statutory procedure is as follows: (1) incorporate at the Registro Público de Panamá and maintain a resident agent; (2) file the Aviso de Operación with DGI before commencing taxable activities; (3) submit the public deed, corporate charter, by-laws, identity documents and proof of local address through the Sede Electrónica; (4) obtain the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC), which serves as the TIN and ITBMS registration number; (5) activate the DGI electronic invoicing account; and (6) file monthly ITBMS returns and annual income tax returns using the assigned RUC.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — For a foreign-owned Panamanian Sociedad Anónima, the statutory procedure is as follows: (1) incorporate at the Registro Público de Panamá and maintain a resident agent; (2) file the Aviso de Operación with DGI before commencing taxable activities; (3) submit the public deed, corporate charter, by-laws, identity documents and proof of local address through the Sede Electrónica; (4) obtain the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC), which serves as the TIN and ITBMS registration number; (5) activate the DGI electronic invoicing account; and (6) file monthly ITBMS returns and annual income tax returns using the assigned RUC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch election exists in Panamanian law. A Panamanian S.A. is a separate juridical person automatically subject to entity-level taxation on Panama-source income. Optional regimes may be elected if qualifying: the small taxpayer simplified regime for limited-turnover businesses, the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Law 41/2007, and special territorial regimes available at Panama Pacífico and Ciudad del Saber; none of these convert the S.A. into a pass-through vehicle for Panamanian tax purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None (0 USD/Balboa); ITBMS registration is mandatory from the first taxable supply. Any person habitually carrying out taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama, including digital app sales, must obtain the RUC and register for ITBMS before operating; no general economic turnover threshold applies under the ITBMS law.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods qualify for 0% ITBMS when physically removed from Panama under a customs export declaration and consumed abroad. Services, including mobile utility app downloads and digital functionality sold through app stores, qualify for the 0% export rate where the recipient is a non-resident of Panama, the economic use of the digital service occurs outside Panama, and the service is not benefited or consumed in Panamanian territory. The exporter must maintain documentary evidence of the non-resident customer, the export contract, the offshore payment channel, and the inward remittance of the proceeds to a Panamanian bank account within the statutory period. Export services are also foreign-source income under Panama's territorial tax model, subject to the same evidentiary requirements.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera / DUA) with customs validation and security token
  • Commercial invoice issued by the Panamanian S.A.
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical shipment from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit voucher evidencing FX entering Panama within 180 days
  • Export contract or purchase order identifying the non-resident customer
  • For digital app exports: Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console settlement reports showing end-user country and payment remittance
  • Geo-location/IP-log evidence demonstrating economic use of the app outside Panama

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the DGI's Sistema de Facturación Electrónica (SFE). Registered taxpayers must issue invoices in the DGI-approved XML/CAdES format, transmit them online through the SFE web portal or machine-to-machine API, obtain DGI acceptance and clearance, and print or attach a security QR code and unique digital stamping code. Fiscal clearance is near real-time and phased by taxpayer segment; mobile-utility-app businesses must reflect Apple IAP and Google Play Billing transactions accurately in SFE records and ITBMS monthly returns when the supplies are subject to Panama ITBMS.

DGI may retrospectively disallow the 0% export rate where the export documentation is incomplete, the export proceeds are not repatriated to Panama within 180 days, the digital service is shown to be used or consumed in Panama, or the transaction is recharacterized as domestic-source revenue. The clawback includes the full 7% ITBMS due, plus interest and late-payment surcharges, and can be treated as fraudulent evasion subject to fines under the Código Fiscal, suspension of the RUC, and criminal liability for tax fraud. Additional administrative penalties apply for non-compliant electronic invoicing, failure to issue SFE invoices, or submission of false export certificates.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Panamanian economy is fully dollarized, with the U.S. Dollar constituting legal tender. There are no exchange controls or restrictions on current account or capital account convertibility. Profit remittance and dividend distributions are freely permissible, subject only to the territorial corporate income tax regime and the 10% dividend withholding, which may be reduced by tax credits. No separate foreign exchange approval is required for profit repatriation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds because Panama does not maintain a domestic foreign exchange market or a central bank. Export receipts, including revenues from digital exports and mobile utility app sales, may be retained in U.S. Dollars or converted into any other freely traded currency without a surrender obligation.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (fiscal agent of the State) acting alongside the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, which supervises licensed banks; Panama does not maintain an independent central bank and operates on full dollarization.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility is moderate and legally constrained. Offshore digital EMI platforms (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Mercury) may be used as treasury concentration points for non-banking payment intermediation, but they are not Panamanian licensed banks and cannot issue local account structures equivalent to a Banco General or Banistmo account. They are not subject to the Superintendencia de Bancos license, and their entry into Panama requires careful AML/KYC alignment with FATF standards. For a mobile utility app generating Apple IAP and Google Play Billing proceeds, such EMIs are viable only as a supplementary clearing rail, not as the primary corporate banking backbone.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital for the Panamanian S.A. is executed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers. The remittance detail (field 70) must cite 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – APORTE DE CAPITAL' and reference the corporate shareholder resolution authorizing the capital increase. The beneficiary account should be titled in the exact legal name of the S.A., and the operating account must be pre-cleared with the bank to accept capital injections.
