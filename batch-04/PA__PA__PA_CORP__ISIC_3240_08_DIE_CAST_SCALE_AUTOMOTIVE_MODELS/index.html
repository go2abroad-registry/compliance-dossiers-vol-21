Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP), successor to the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) under Law 365 of 2023; operational portal: Sede Electrónica ANIP (https://sede.anip.gob.pa). Export customs processing is done through the SICE portal of the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA).

E-government filing portal

The foreign shareholder incorporates the Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) through a Panamanian resident agent; no minimum paid-in capital is required. After registration in the Registro Público and publication of the Aviso de Operación, the company must apply online to ANIP for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente), register for ITBMS and the mandatory e-invoicing system, and obtain the municipal patent. The RUC application requires a notarized power of attorney appointing a local attorney or CPA, certified bylaws, officer identity documents, and proof of registered address; ANIP issues the RUC with verification digit before commercial operations commence.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is an entity-level taxpayer subject to 25% corporate income tax (ISR) solely on Panama-source income; foreign-source income and gains from foreign-situs assets are excluded from the tax base. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — the S.A. is an entity-level taxpayer subject to 25% corporate income tax (ISR) solely on Panama-source income; foreign-source income and gains from foreign-situs assets are excluded from the tax base. No pass-through or entity-classification election exists; all S.A.s are automatically taxable corporations. Dividends declared out of already-taxed profits are subject to the separate 10% dividend withholding tax framework.

Available election forms

Panama has no U.S.-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election, and no branch-equivalent classification. Available statutory elections are: (i) the Simplified Taxation Regime (Régimen Simplificado) for small taxpayers with annual gross revenue up to USD/B/. 300,000; (ii) the Multinational Enterprises Headquarters (SEM/EMMA) regime under Law 41 of 2007 for qualifying regional service centers; (iii) the choice of monthly versus quarterly ITBMS filing; and (iv) the election to transmit electronic invoices through the ANIP web portal or via system-to-system API integration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold — every person carrying on an economic activity in Panama must obtain the RUC and register as an ITBMS collector before issuing its first invoice. Small taxpayers with annual gross revenue not exceeding USD/B/. 300,000 may instead prepay ITBMS under the simplified quarterly regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) tangible goods physically exported from Panama to a foreign destination and (ii) services rendered entirely abroad or supplied to foreign beneficiaries for use outside Panama. The die-cast model exporter must be registered in SICE as an exporter, file the corresponding export declaration, and issue the electronic invoice at 0% ITBMS. Input ITBMS borne on domestic purchases of zinc alloy, paints, tampo inks, tooling, packaging, and logistics attributable to export sales is creditable and recoverable as a refundable 'saldo a favor' through a written ITBMS refund application to ANIP.

  • SICE-validated customs export declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera) with exit stamp
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing shipment from Panama to the foreign customer
  • Purchase order or sales contract with Incoterms
  • Inward remittance evidence (SWIFT MT103 or bank credit advice) matching the invoice value
  • 0% ITBMS electronic invoices and the corresponding input-VAT purchase invoices

Electronic invoicing (facturación electrónica) is mandatory for all taxpayers through ANIP's Sede Electrónica system (Resolution 201-4669 of 2018 and implementing rules; fully phased in by 2023). Invoices are issued on the web portal or via ANIP API, bear a unique ANIP-assigned sequential number and QR digital seal, and must be transmitted in real time (API) or daily (portal) so ANIP validates each invoice at or immediately after issuance.

ANIP audits export zero-rating claims within the five-year verification window; refunds are reversible with default surcharges of up to 300% of the evaded tax plus monthly interest where export documentation is missing, invoice values are misstated, or proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days. Knowingly fraudulent zero-rating claims trigger criminal tax fraud (defraudación tributaria) under Law 70 of 2019, exclusion from the SICE exporter registry, cancellation of the RUC, and imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with no exchange controls applies to both current-account and capital-account transactions. Trade invoices, freight, reinsurance, royalties, dividends, equity injections, shareholder loans, and principal repatriation move freely in and out of Panama in US dollars without prior authorization, quota, declaration, or conversion requirement. The only practical hurdles are bank-level AML/CFT due diligence under Ley 23 de 2015 (as amended by Ley 70 de 2019), the mandatory beneficial-owner declaration with the Registro Único de Beneficiario Final (RUBF) maintained by the DGI, sanctions screening, and, for distributions to shareholders, the paying S.A.'s obligation to withhold Panamanian dividend tax under the Código Fiscal. No FX conversion is ever required because the functional currency, the invoicing currency, and the settlement currency are all US dollars; profit remittance is therefore structurally free of currency red-tape and the sole cost of repatriation is the applicable dividend withholding tax administered separately by the DGI.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory timeline for repatriation of export proceeds, no mandatory conversion ratio, no export-payment collection window, and no surrender requirement, because export receipts are earned and retained directly in US dollars and the Balboa has no paper currency. For the die-cast scale-model exporter, the mandatory external reporting obligations are limited to: (i) filing each export shipment through the customs single-window platform of the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA); (ii) issuing commercial invoices and bills of lading consistent with the declared 1:18 scale-model merchandise (HS code 9503.00); and (iii) recording export revenue in the territorial income-tax return filed with the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI). The SBP may request documentary rebuttal evidence where deposit patterns are irregular, but no statutory conversion timeline or ratio applies to the PA_CORP entity.

Central bank supervision: Panama operates under full US-dollarization and has no central bank of issue. Banking supervision is vested exclusively in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), which licenses, inspects, and sanctions all banks under Decreto Ley No. 2 de 2008 (the Banking Act). The Banco Nacional de Panamá functions as fiscal agent for the State, operates the government clearing accounts, and performs quasi-central-bank administrative duties without any note-issuing power. The US dollar is unlimited legal tender under the 1904 Monetary Agreement between the Republic of Panama and the United States and under Ley 84 de 1941; the Balboa is a nominal monetary unit minted only as coins at a strict 1:1 peg to the US dollar. There is therefore no monetary authority that sets base rates, conducts open-market operations, or licenses money exchangers, and no foreign-exchange statute administered by a central bank exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for collection-only use; LOW as a primary corporate treasury rail. Panama does not issue European-style electronic-money-institution (EMI) licences. Foreign EMIs such as PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, and Airwallex operate only on a reverse-solicitation basis and cannot offer a settlement-grade corporate treasury account inside Panama without an SBP fintech licence under Acuerdo SBP-RG-004-2022 and related resolutions. PayPal is expressly viable as a merchant collection rail for direct-to-consumer die-cast model sales and constitutes an authorized target payment rail for this entity; collected funds must then be swept via SWIFT MT103 or batch transfer into the SBP-licensed USD operating account, with order IDs, shipping proofs, and PayPal settlement reports retained for AML traceability. Legal constraints: (i) PayPal may restrict or require export documentation for cross-border volume exceeding approximately USD 10,000 per month; (ii) an SBP-regulated bank will treat EMI pass-through movements as customer payment flows, not as treasury concentration, so only invoice-backed and sanctions-cleared flows are acceptable; (iii) any advance balance parked inside an EMI must be documented as a customer prepayment or short-term loan to preserve the S.A.'s beneficial-ownership and income characterization.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound funds route via SWIFT MT103 (or Fedwire/CHIPS for US-sourced transfers) to the S.A.'s USD operating account at an SBP-licensed bank, with the beneficiary field exactly matching the registered name of the Panamanian Sociedad Anónima and the correct account number and SWIFT/BIC of the receiving branch. Panama maintains no statutory purpose-code taxonomy, no IBAN, and no FX quota; the remitting bank's form should nevertheless state 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - SHARE ISSUANCE' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN' to align with the S.A.'s corporate records. For a capital injection above USD 500,000, the bank requires the board resolution, the updated share register, and a source-of-funds letter; for a shareholder loan, an executed loan agreement with interest and maturity terms is required. Verification is completed within one to two business days, and the receiving bank will credit USD without conversion; no approval from a central bank or foreign-exchange commission is needed at any stage.Clearing rails for the die-cast production cycle: (i) supplier tooling deposits of approximately 30% of mould cost disbursed via SWIFT MT103 with 'Charges OUR' to the Chinese or Hong Kong foundry, executed through the bank's trade desk; (ii) balance settlement of approximately 70% against scanned bill of lading via MT103 with matching invoice and packing list; (iii) commercial irrevocable letters of credit, under UCP 600, transmitted via SWIFT MT700/MT798 and confirmed by Banco Nacional de Panamá or Citibank, for individual production runs exceeding USD 500,000; (iv) PayPal as a de minimis refund-only corridor for retail customers; and (v) general disbursements for freight forwarders, packaging suppliers, customs brokers, trademark attorneys, and marketing agencies via MT103 with purpose narratives 'Trade Settlement', 'Freight and Logistics', or 'Professional Services'. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for collection-only use; LOW as a primary corporate treasury rail. Panama does not issue European-style electronic-money-institution (EMI) licences. Foreign EMIs such as PayPal, Payoneer, Wise, and Airwallex operate only on a reverse-solicitation basis and cannot offer a settlement-grade corporate treasury account inside Panama without an SBP fintech licence under Acuerdo SBP-RG-004-2022 and related resolutions. PayPal is expressly viable as a merchant collection rail for direct-to-consumer die-cast model sales and constitutes an authorized target payment rail for this entity; collected funds must then be swept via SWIFT MT103 or batch transfer into the SBP-licensed USD operating account, with order IDs, shipping proofs, and PayPal settlement reports retained for AML traceability. Legal constraints: (i) PayPal may restrict or require export documentation for cross-border volume exceeding approximately USD 10,000 per month; (ii) an SBP-regulated bank will treat EMI pass-through movements as customer payment flows, not as treasury concentration, so only invoice-backed and sanctions-cleared flows are acceptable; (iii) any advance balance parked inside an EMI must be documented as a customer prepayment or short-term loan to preserve the S.A.'s beneficial-ownership and income characterization.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Annual TP return (Form 191) and Local File are mandatory where related-party transactions exceed B/. 2,000,000 (USD 2,000,000) per fiscal year; Master File required for multinational groups with consolidated revenue above USD 40,000,000; Country-by-Country Report follows OECD threshold of EUR 750,000,000 group revenue.

Economic substance scope: Panama has no standalone ESR statute comparable to the UAE, but the Fiscal Code (Art. 14) ties tax residence to effective management. A zinc die-casting factory operating in Panama with a physical plant, local payroll, mold-injection machinery, utility contracts, and locally approved board minutes fully meets economic substance. A letterbox S.A. controlled from abroad, without local activity, risks reclassification as foreign-managed, loss of territorial benefits, CRS information exchange, and penalty exposure under Law 254 of 2021 (UBO registry, fines up to USD 10,000).

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – False Invoicing and Revenue Omission

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine up to 5 times the evaded tax and imprisonment of 2–4 years; administrative 50% surcharge on the omitted tax plus 1% monthly interest; the DGI publicly blacklists fraudulent invoice issuers and rescinds their invoicing license.

Director personal liability: The resident director who signs the false return is jointly and severally liable with the company; penal prosecution attaches to the director personally, triggering travel bans (salida del país), seizure of personal assets, and disqualification from Panamanian directorships.
Shell Company – Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: UBO non-filing fines up to USD 10,000; registrable agent license suspension; the entity may be struck off the Public Registry; foreign tax authorities may reclassify income under CFC or managed-entity rules.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face criminal liability for false declarations before the Public Registry; courts may pierce the corporate veil and order restitution of diverted profits from the directors' personal estates, including freezing of personal accounts and withdrawal of passports.
AML – Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Fines up to USD 1,000,000 per violation under Law 23 of 2015; closure of the commercial establishment; money laundering under Penal Code Arts. 254–256 carries imprisonment of 5–12 years.

Director personal liability: Directors, the compliance officer, and any manager who knowingly processes inflated trade invoices are criminally prosecutable; the UAF may freeze personal and corporate accounts, and a conviction imposes lengthy banking and travel restrictions across Latin America.
Chronic Non-Filing – Statutory Limitation and Forced Assessment

Statutory sanction: RUC suspension, prohibition on issuing invoices, DGI forced assessment using net-worth and bank-deposit indicators, and embargo of receivables; the general 5-year limitation period for tax debts doubles to 10 years in non-filing cases.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly liable for unwithheld dividend tax (ISD) and unpaid employer obligations; courts may compel payment from personal bank accounts and impose exit bans preventing shareholders from leaving Panama until the tax debt is settled.
