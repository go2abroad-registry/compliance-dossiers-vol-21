Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official digital portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI; auxiliary one-stop corporate/RUC registration window: Panamá Emprende.

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima, the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) is obtained at incorporation through the Panamá Emprende single window. A foreign entity must appoint a resident agent; present apostilled/legalized constitutive documents, a notarized power of attorney, and a local fiscal address; and file the DGI registration forms via Sede Electrónica. Following DGI validation, the RUC is issued and the taxpayer is simultaneously enrolled for income tax (ISR) and ITBMS obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or passthrough election exists under Panamanian law; the S.A. is always an entity-level territorial taxpayer. Available elections include the annual choice between the general ISR method and the elective Alternative Minimum Tax (ATM at 1% of average net assets), the simplified ITBMS bracket regime for small taxpayers, and, for MNE groups within scope, the OECD Pillar Two global minimum top-up tax treatment effective from 1 January 2025.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.36,000 (Panamanian Balboa at 1:1 parity with USD) of gross taxable turnover in the preceding calendar year; registration is voluntary below the threshold but mandatory once exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Containerized drum and bag exports of penetration-grade bitumen qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate when: (i) goods are physically shipped from Panamanian customs territory to a foreign destination under a valid Customs Export Declaration; (ii) the seller holds an active ITBMS registration and transfers title to a non-resident buyer; (iii) payment is received in convertible currency evidenced by banking records; and (iv) the exporter files the corresponding export settlement with DGI. Panama applies no statutory exchange-control repatriation period, but the 180-day evidentiary benchmark applies for banking and DGI review.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación – ANA)
  • Bill of Lading (clean on-board ocean bill for containerized cargo)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation of foreign payment

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica): every ITBMS-registered taxpayer must issue invoices through DGI-authorized software or the DGI portal, transmitting each invoice in real time to the DGI fiscal clearance server. Invoices must incorporate a QR code, the DGI digital stamp, and an electronic signature; cleared/valid status is required for export documentation and customs purposes.

DGI may retroactively reassess improperly zero-rated exports within the three-year statutory limitation period (extendable to six years in fraud cases). False zero-rating triggers ITBMS clawback with a 10% late-payment surcharge plus 1% monthly interest, customs fines and forfeiture under Ley 16 de 2016 (Código Aduanero), and criminal tax fraud liability under Ley 8 de 2010 with imprisonment of three to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary regime: the US dollar is legal tender and the Panamanian balboa exists only as a 1:1 coin sub-unit. There are no exchange controls, no FX licensing, no funds import/export restrictions, and no prior authorization for profit remittance. Current-account and capital-account flows are freely convertible in and out. Non-resident shareholders may receive dividends in USD without repatriation hurdles, subject only to the standard Panamanian dividend withholding (5% on profits already taxed at the 25% corporate level; 10% on untaxed and foreign-source profits under the territorial system) and the remitting bank's KYC/OFAC documentary review.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory repatriation timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation on export receipts. An ISIC 4661 containerized bitumen exporter may retain USD or other convertible currencies indefinitely in any SBP-licensed bank or abroad. The only binding constraints are commercial (proper invoicing of the containerized sale), tax (RUC registration, territorial income declaration, and transfer-pricing documentation where applicable), and AML/CFT (retaining underlying trade documents and correspondent-routing records for UAF inspection).

Central bank supervision: Panama operates no traditional central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the banking and financial supervisor under the Decreto de Gabinete No. 9 de 1998 Banking Law (Ley Bancaria, as amended). Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the Republic's fiscal agent, clearing agent, and a state-owned commercial bank. The Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) administers the RUC, the aviso de operación, and the beneficial-owner registry; the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) is the AML/CFT financial intelligence unit.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Pure offshore digital EMIs without an SBP general banking license cannot lawfully intermediate deposits or issue UCP 600 letters of credit for a Panamanian S.A.; the Ley Bancaria requires SBP authorization for financial intermediation, so most UK/EU-style EMI challengers reject Panama-domiciled corporate accounts. SBP-licensed local neobank platforms can be used as a secondary collection rail for customer receivables but are not suited as the primary treasury vehicle for capital escrow, commodity trade settlements, or LC issuance. The recommended structure is a Tier-1 licensed bank as the sole primary account, with fintech rails reserved for small-value working-capital flows.
