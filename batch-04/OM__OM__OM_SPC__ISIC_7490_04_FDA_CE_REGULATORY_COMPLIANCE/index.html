Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up share capital for SPCs under Omani law; capital may be set at any positive amount and contributed in cash or in kind. No escrow account is mandated, but a local licensed bank may ask for notarised capital contribution evidence when opening a corporate account.

Funding & unlock path: The registered office must obtain a Fire Safety Clearance from the Royal Oman Police Civil Defence Department, verifying portable extinguishers, means of egress, and emergency lighting. Occupational workplace clearance is required from the Public Authority for Social Insurance and the Ministry of Labour confirming that the premises meet structural load, lighting, ventilation, and sanitation standards; structural load inspection is necessary only if the office floor is used for storage above the intended capacity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; the foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) incorporated in Oman is a tax-resident juridical person taxed on worldwide income (with a foreign tax credit for foreign-source profits); non-residents are taxable only on Oman-source income. No pass-through treatment is available.

Available election forms

No check-the-box régime exists. Available statutory elections: (i) financial year-end selection subject to SGT approval; (ii) fixed-asset depreciation method election (straight-line or declining-balance); (iii) foreign tax credit election under the overall limitation; (iv) voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250 annual supplies; (v) irrevocable VAT tax-group election for related Omani entities; (vi) managing-partner filing election for civil joint ventures; (vii) automatic small-business CIT concession (3% rate on qualifying first bracket).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration: OMR 19,250; zero-rated cross-border services are excluded from the mandatory threshold computation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

FDA 510(k) and CE Mark consulting qualifies as an exported service eligible for 0% VAT where the recipient is resident/established outside Oman, the benefit of the service is enjoyed outside Oman, and the service has no nexus to Omani goods or real estate; B2B clients in GCC states apply reverse charge. Physical export of goods requires documented movement outside Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) with customs stamp
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing FX receipt from abroad
  • Executed service contract / engagement letter and service delivery report
  • Certificate of tax residence of the overseas recipient

FATOORA national e-invoicing under the TMS: phased mandatory rollout beginning with large taxpayers; certified fiscal systems must integrate via API for real-time fiscal clearance; each invoice receives a validated tax control number and QR-code digital stamp; all invoice types, including zero-rated exports, enter the same clearance pipeline, with 5-year retention.

SGT audits verify economic substance and documentation sufficiency. Deficient support triggers reversal of zero-rating to 5% VAT, monthly late-payment accrual, administrative fines of OMR 100–OMR 25,000 per violation, denial of refunds and input VAT credit, and retrospective clawback of previously claimed refunds. Fraudulent claims attract tax-evasion penalties of up to three times the tax evaded plus imprisonment; assessments remain collectible for 5 years, extended for fraud or misrepresentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD since January 1986. Current account transactions are fully convertible and freely transferable through licensed banks without CBO pre-approval. Capital account transactions are substantially liberal: a foreign-owned SPC under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019) may hold 100% of the share capital and open OMR, USD and EUR accounts with the prior authorization of the bank only. Profit and dividend remittance is permitted provided (i) audited statutory financial statements are filed, (ii) the Oman Tax Authority issues a Tax Clearance Certificate confirming no overdue corporate income tax or penalties, and (iii) the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019 as amended by RD 88/2021) solvency test is evidenced. Corporate income tax applies at 15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 100,000 per the 2023 amendments, with small-business reduced rates for qualifying lower-income filers. No CBO licensing or conversion quota applies to consulting fee receipts; the cross-border convertibility risk is negligible given the USD peg.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio and no prescriptive repatriation deadline for export or service proceeds. Practical CBO and banking prudential requirements mandate that the SPC's fee income is received into a licensed Omani bank account via the formal clearing circuit (SWIFT MT103), is fully reconciled to signed commercial contracts, and is not warehoused offshore in a manner that conceals the economic substance of the Oman entity. Export proceeds accompanying declarations under the CBO's External Trade statistics programme apply to goods only; for ISIC 7490 regulatory consulting services, the invoiced amount must match the credited amount and be coded with the appropriate service purpose code (regularly Code 2 for business services or the bank-specific revenue classification). Banks will scrutinize pass-through transactions for FDA pay.gov fee payments and EU notified body settlement; treasury policy should maintain an invoice-to-cash reconciliation ledger for every US/EU client engagement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Foreign Exchange Regulation and CBO Circulars governing payments, foreign currency accounts and AML/CFT (Royal Decree 30/2016 on Combating Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism). The Oman Financial Services Authority (established under Royal Decree 26/2023, operational from January 2025) supervises non-bank financial services but does not regulate banking or FX; all liquidity, FX and treasury compliance for the Oman SPC remains with CBO.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Oman does not operate a statutory electronic money institution (EMI) licensing framework; non-bank fintechs such as Wise, Payoneer or Airwallex cannot hold OMR balances, issue an Omani IBAN or onboard customers in Oman without a CBO licence under the Payment and Settlement Systems Law (Royal Decree 18/2020). Such EMIs may be used merely as an offshore USD/EUR collection concentration vehicle ancillary to the Omani banking account, but CBO and correspondent bank diligence treats this as a nested-account risk, and the SPC must disclose the EMI relationship to its Omani onboarding bank. Full operating liquidity must remain in the licensed Omani bank; card acquiring for FDA and CE fee payments should be arranged through the bank's corporate card programme rather than an EMI wallet.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the SPC, the Commercial Companies Law (RD 18/2019 as amended) sets no minimum issued capital; the articles of association define the capital figure, yet banks commonly require a paid-up amount of at least OMR 5,000 to OMR 50,000 to evidence viability. Inbound share capital is routed via SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder's bank to the SPC's capital account at the Omani bank with purpose code 501 (equy equity participation / capital contribution). The corporate banking onboarding team must receive the CR, constitutional documents, foreign investment certificate under RD 50/2019, and the UBO declaration before the funds are released for operational use. The bank records the credit in its CBO-mandated reporting systems with the investor statement, and subsequent repatriation of contributed capital is permitted only upon audited net-asset verification and a Tax Clearance Certificate from the Oman Tax Authority.Since ISIC 7490 is a consultation-and-compliance services activity, the dominant rails are (a) outbound SWIFT MT103 wire transfers for larger payments to EU notified bodies and US service providers, including EUR via Target2/SEPA relay and USD via CHIPS/Fedwire correspondent chains; (b) corporate Visa/Mastercard credit cards for smaller-value FDA 510(k) application fees through the Pay.gov channel, because the FDA accepts Visa, Mastercard and GSA SmartPay; (c) Commercial Letter of Credit is not applicable to a pure services company and should be explicitly excluded from the treasury policy as it introduces documentary and financing cost without any security benefit; the SPC should confirm the merchant category code and recurring-no-gateway configuration with the issuing bank to prevent card blocks on government-payer transactions. The SPC must maintain full reconciliation to the regulatory filing event and must run sanctions screening against the US BIS, OFAC and EU consolidated list prior to disbursement, particularly when the notified body or its parent group has an international footprint.Onshore deliverable forwards in USD/OMR and EUR/OMR executed with the licensed Omani bank; USD/OMR forwards are permitted but deliver minimal economic benefit due to the USD peg.Offshore and onshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on EUR/USD for hedging Euro-denominated notified body fee liabilities where the Omani bank relays to an international counterparty; speculative OMR NDF booking is not supported by CBO-regulated banks.Cross-currency swaps between EUR, USD and OMR for defined multi-year EU client contracts, provided the notional is evidenced by an underlying commercial agreement.Plain vanilla FX spot transactions on the bank's electronic dealing platform without CBO pre-approval, used for interim conversion between invoice currencies and OMR for payroll and corporate expenses.Natural hedging via dual-currency (USD/EUR) concentration accounts with daily netting to minimize outright exposure rows to the payment date.
