Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; official electronic portal: DGI Sede Electrónica / SISTEMA at https://www.dgi.gob.pa (RUC registration, statutory filings, e-invoice validation). Panama's legal tender is the Panamanian Balboa (PAB), at parity with the United States dollar.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity forms a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) before the Registro Público and appoints a resident legal agent (Agente Residente). The company then authenticates in the DGI Sede Electrónica (SISTEMA) and files the Formulario de Inscripción/Actualización del Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC), attaching the certified articles of incorporation (Ficha de la Sociedad), a notarized power of attorney, and the municipal Aviso de Operación. The DGI issues the single statutory RUC, which serves simultaneously as the income tax (ISR), ITBMS (VAT) and employer contribution taxpayer identifier. A pure foreign seller may alternatively register as a non-resident for customs/ITBMS incidents through a fiscal agent and customs broker, but that limited registration does not confer territorial-source income tax neutrality on Panamanian-source operations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Panamanian law; a Sociedad Anónima is a juridical person taxed at entity level exclusively on Panama-source income (territorial principle). Available administrative elections and regimes include: (i) the DGI simplified presumptive small-taxpayer regime for micro businesses, where applicable on turnover; (ii) registration as an exporter and enrolment in the statutory automatic ITBMS credit/refund mechanism for exporters; (iii) the alternative presumptive minimum tax (Cuota Mínima) treatment applicable in certain maritime and free-zone sectors; and (iv) the irrevocable source-of-income election for specific international financial, reinsurance, logistics and transportation services. Elections must be filed on DGI-prescribed forms and bind the taxpayer for the respective fiscal period.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold in PAB/USD: any person habitually transferring movable goods or rendering taxable services in Panamanian territory must register for ITBMS from the first taxable act. Micro businesses eligible for the DGI simplified presumptive small-taxpayer regime remain subject to ITBMS registration and simplified compliance; imports of goods (including ingredients and packaging for hotpot soup base) are taxable at importation irrespective of the importer's declared turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Pre-packaged spicy tallow hotpot soup base bricks qualify for ITBMS zero-rating (0%) where: the buyer is a non-resident; legal title transfers and physical custody moves outside Panamanian territory; the goods leave through an authorized customs office under an approved DUA (Declaración Única de Aduanas) in the VUPA (Ventanilla Única) electronic platform; and the exporter holds an active RUC with exporter status, enrols in the statutory automatic ITBMS refund mechanism, and files the zero-rated invoices in the periodic ITBMS returns. Services performed and consumed wholly abroad, and deliveries to the Zona Libre de Colón, receive export-equivalent treatment; documented ITBMS borne on inputs and attributable to exported units may be credited or refunded monthly under the exporters' regime.

  • Export Customs Declaration - Declaración Única de Aduanas (DUA) accepted in VUPA/ANA (Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas), with exit confirmation, cargo manifest and transit closure records
  • Transport document - negotiable sea Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or Carta de Porte evidencing physical departure from Panama and the foreign consignee destination
  • Financial document - Inward Remittance Certificate and SWIFT MT103/MT700 credit advice, or a bank certificate of Letter of Credit negotiation, evidencing receipt of proceeds from the foreign buyer

Panama mandates phased electronic invoicing ('Facturación Electrónica de Panamá') under DGI technical regulations and Resoluciones. Every sales invoice, credit/debit note and export invoice must be generated in DGI-endorsed XML format, transmitted in near real time to the SISTEMA fiscal validation service via web service/API using the taxpayer's electronic signature certificate, and carry a DGI-approved QR code / fiscal stamp before delivery to the buyer. Large taxpayers have been fully bound since the fiscal year 2022; successive DGI Resolutions have extended the mandate to the entire taxpayer population. Unvalidated documents do not support ITBMS input credits and expose the issuer to administrative fines and cancellation of the fiscal authorization.

DGI and ANA audit export zero-rating at monthly settlement and within the statutory assessment window (five years, extended where fraud appears). Missing, inconsistent or falsified export dossiers cause invoices to be rescheduled as domestic taxable supplies; the resulting ITBMS is clawed back with monthly default interest, together with graduated fines (commonly 50%-200% of the tax evaded) and suspension or cancellation of the exporter's automatic refund enrolment. Knowingly false declarations additionally trigger tax-fraud penalties and customs offences against the company and its responsible directors, and may disqualify the taxpayer from future zero-rating and refund privileges. In current audit practice, the banking trail must reconcile with the DUA value and consignee identity within the 180-day repatriation window; non-reconciliation defeats the zero-rate claim and disgorges input ITBMS credited on the shipment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility on both current and capital account. Because the U.S. dollar is legal tender and the Balboa exists only as a nominal accounting unit issued in coins at a fixed 1:1 parity, Panama maintains no foreign exchange control law. Import payments for tallow, seasonings and packaging, freight and agent commissions, shareholder capital injections, intercompany loans, dividend distributions and liquidation or sale proceeds may all be freely converted, transferred and remitted without prior approval, exchange licenses, currency budgets, or economic justification filings. No SWIFT-related FX declaration forms are attached to inbound or outbound transactions. The only outflow friction is fiscal: profit distributions are subject to Panamanian dividend withholding, generally a 5% advance dividend tax on distributed profits, or 10% for certain small-taxpayer bites, and must be planned under the Territorial income tax regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender, no mandatory conversion ratio, no 180-day repatriation window, and no obligation to convert export receipts into Balboa or U.S. dollars. An exporter of pre-packaged hotpot soup base may lawfully retain USD or third-currency receivables in Panama or abroad indefinitely. The only binding obligations are AML/CFT record-keeping and transaction reports under Law No. 23 of 27 April 2015, including reporting of cash-equivalent transactions at or above USD 10,000, retention of the commercial file, and filing of foodstuff export declarations through the VUCE single window supported by the sanitary export authorization of the Ministry of Health (MINSA).

Central bank supervision: Panama has no issuing central bank. The monetary and financial regulatory functions are discharged by: (i) the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP), the prudential regulator and AML/CFT supervisor of banks, trusts and finance entities; (ii) Banco Nacional de Panamá, the Republic's fiscal agent and clearing agent; and (iii) the U.S. dollar as statutory legal tender, which anchors monetary policy through full dollarization without a local lender of last resort.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. After Panama's removal from the FATF grey list in October 2023, international EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex and Currencycloud may onboard a Panamanian S.A. using apostilled corporate documents, beneficial owner declarations and trade-flow evidence. However, an EMI is not a bank under Decreto Ley No. 9 de 26 February 1998 and is not SBP-regulated as a deposit-taking institution; balances are outside Panama's deposit guarantee regime. The S.A. should therefore use EMIs only as auxiliary rails for small buyer collections or CNY settlement, never as the primary treasury account, capital escrow vehicle, or LC clearing platform.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital is injected by SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's home-country bank into the S.A.'s Tier-1 USD account in Panama. The remittance information field should state 'Aporte de Capital / Suscripcion de Acciones' and cite the corporate resolution approving the contribution. Panama applies no exchange-control registration, purpose-code, or prior-authorization requirement; the receiving bank verifies source of funds and screens the wire against OFAC and consolidated lists. The inflow must be booked as paid-in capital in the shareholder register, and if the aggregate paid-in amount exceeds the authorized capital stated in the public deed, a capital-increase inscription in the Registro Público de Panamá should be completed before disbursement to preserve future dividend and audit integrity.Because Panama is dollarized, outbound trade payments bypass any local FX conversion. The operative clearing rails are: (i) SWIFT MT103 and MT202 payment instructions settled through U.S. correspondent accounts over Fedwire and CHIPS, which are the true finality rails for USD; and (ii) irrevocable UCP600 commercial letters of credit, possibly confirmed and discounted by the Panamanian bank for containerized deliveries of hotpot soup base to Asia, Europe and Latin America. Each payment package must contain the beneficiary's tested KYC record, invoice and bill of lading references, the export customs declaration, and the MINSA sanitary dossier; repeated payments to freight-forwarder consolidated accounts without a goods-flow file will be deferred by the SBP-regulated bank or by the U.S. intermediary. The local ACH Panama mechanism is reserved for domestic USD clearing and is not a cross-border rail.Plain-vanilla deliverable forwards on USD/EUR and USD/GBP written bilaterally by Tier-1 Panamanian banks under ISDA terms; no SBP license or exchange-control authorization is required for the corporate end-user.USD/CNH non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) executed through New York or Miami dealer desks, because Panamanian banks do not maintain CNY balance sheets; NDF is the standard permissible instrument to hedge CNY-denominated hotpot-base buyer receivables.USD/EUR and USD/CNH cross-currency swaps booked offshore with SBP-licensed international banks under ISDA/CSA, admissible when supported by genuine commercial exposure and an approved bank credit line.Vanilla FX options on EUR/USD and CNH/USD acquired on a covered or collateralized basis from an offshore dealer; Panamanian law does not impose a derivatives license on corporate end-users, subject to the bank's suitability and concentration review.
