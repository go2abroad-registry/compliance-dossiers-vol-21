Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on an Omani SPC; the sole shareholder fixes the capital in the MOA and must fully subscribe it (payment may be spread over not more than 10 years from incorporation). No escrow account is mandated by company law; however, client funds received for study-abroad and residence-visa facilitation must be segregated in a dedicated escrow/retainer account under ROP DGPR licensing conditions and CBO AML/CFT expectations.

Funding & unlock path: Fire-safety and structural clearance from the Civil Defense and Ambulance Department (Royal Oman Police) covering extinguishers, emergency exits, alarms, signage, and fire-load inspection of the leased office; an occupancy/structural load certificate for the office unit issued by the Muscat Municipality Building Regulation Directorate; and an occupational health clearance from the Ministry of Labour confirming adequate floor area, ventilation, sanitary facilities, and first-aid provision for staff engaged in visa-processing and student-advisory work.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman, Ministry of Finance; official e-tax portal: taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

The foreign shareholder must first incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) in the Commercial Register at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) number. Using the CR certificate, constitutional documents, authorized signatory details, and a certified power of attorney, an online application is filed with the Oman Tax Authority e-tax portal to obtain the Tax Card / Tax Identification Number (TIN). The VAT registration is a separate online application to the OTA VAT system and is required only when taxable supplies equal or exceed the statutory threshold, or may be filed voluntarily when the voluntary threshold is met. Completion of procurement is conditional on valid commercial licensing, economic substance documentation, and local municipal registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — The foreign shareholder must first incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) in the Commercial Register at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) number. Using the CR certificate, constitutional documents, authorized signatory details, and a certified power of attorney, an online application is filed with the Oman Tax Authority e-tax portal to obtain the Tax Card / Tax Identification Number (TIN). The VAT registration is a separate online application to the OTA VAT system and is required only when taxable supplies equal or exceed the statutory threshold, or may be filed voluntarily when the voluntary threshold is met. Completion of procurement is conditional on valid commercial licensing, economic substance documentation, and local municipal registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a U.S.-style 'check-the-box' election or a domestic hybrid-mismatch election. The SPC is taxed as a standalone resident juridical person under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (Income Tax Law) on its worldwide taxable income, with foreign tax credit relief available under applicable tax treaties. Tax grouping may be elected by forming a resident corporate group under OTA conditions; an Advance Pricing Arrangement may be applied for cross-border transactions; treaty-reduced withholding rates may be accessed through a valid Oman tax residence certificate. No pass-through or LLC-style classification election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 in annual taxable supplies for mandatory registration; OMR 250,000 for voluntary registration

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under Oman VAT is available for goods exported outside the Sultanate where the supplier or authorized exporter proves that the goods physically left Oman within the prescribed period and that the supply is made to a person outside Oman. For cross-border services supplied by the SPC (e.g., study-abroad placement and golden visa consultancy), zero-rating is claimable if the recipient is a non-resident, the recipient belongs outside Oman, the services are not performed in whole or in part for exploitation in Oman, and the underlying documentation establishes the foreign destination and the economic benefit outside Oman. Ancillary services directly related to exported goods may also be zero-rated if supported by the export evidence. Documentation must be contemporaneous and kept for audit.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Bayan export declaration accepted by Oman Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency
  • Commercial Invoice and Tax Invoice
  • Service Agreement and engagement letter for cross-border study-abroad or immigration consultancy
  • Evidence of non-resident status of the foreign recipient (e.g., overseas passport, foreign tax identification number, residence address)

Oman currently does not mandate a real-time fiscal clearance, continuous transaction control digital stamping, or authority-validated e-invoicing platform for VAT. Taxpayers are obliged to file VAT returns electronically via the taxoman.gov.om portal and to maintain digital and machine-readable tax invoice records in Arabic and English. Commercial invoices and tax invoices must be retained for at least five years. The OTA may conduct automated risk-based audits and require API-based bulk data submission in the course of an audit.

The Oman Tax Authority may reopen VAT returns within the statutory period and deny export zero-rating where the mandatory export evidence or foreign currency repatriation proof is incomplete, non-contemporaneous, or unreliable. Reassessment will claw back all input VAT credits previously claimed, require payment of the unrecovered 5% VAT, and impose late-payment charges calculated on each unpaid month, in addition to administrative fines per invoice. Fraudulent or repeated misuse of export zero-rating is subject to enhanced financial penalties, interest, criminal prosecution and possible imprisonment for responsible directors or managers. The OTA may also apply retrospective clawback extending across multiple tax periods where a scheme or arrangement demonstrates intent to defraud the VAT system.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible on both the current and capital accounts; a no-exchange-control policy is applied and non-resident 100% ownership is permitted under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019, amended by Royal Decree 74/2022). The Rial operates a fixed peg to the USD (USD 1 = OMR 0.3845; OMR 1 = USD 2.6008) maintained by the CBO, so repatriation of dividends, profit remittances, repayment of shareholder loans, and capital repatriation are cleared freely through authorized banks subject only to AML/CFT documentary due diligence and CRS/FATCA reporting. For the ISIC 7810 study-abroad and immigration-advisory SPC, education-consultancy and visa-service fees may be received in OMR, USD, EUR, or GBP and converted without prior central-bank approval, provided the underlying invoices and client mandates are retained.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable: Oman imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no deferred-receipt timeline for service or consulting receipts. The SPC may retain its cross-border education-placement and immigration-consultancy earnings in the original currency or convert them freely on the CBO pegged parity; the only statutory duties are the general obligation to maintain auditable source-of-funds evidence, to report suspicious transactions to the CBO FIU under Royal Decree 30/2016, and to submit balance-of-payments statistical declarations where requested by the CBO.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO; البنك المركزي العماني) acting under the Law of Banking Business (Royal Decree 114/2000, as amended), the Central Bank of Oman Law, and the Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism Law (Royal Decree 30/2016, as amended); AML/CFT supervision is coordinated with the National Committee for Combating Money Laundering and Financing of Terrorism (NCFC) and the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) domiciled at the CBO.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Rating: LOW. Offshore digital EMIs (UK/EEA/MENA e-money institutions) are not regulated by the CBO and cannot act as the primary treasury or escrow clearing rail for the Omani SPC; holding client deposits outside the CBO-licensed banking system breaches the Banking Law payment-settlement framework and creates material CRS/FATCA misclassification risk. An EEA- or FCA-licensed EMI may be used only as an ancillary online student-deposit collection node if it sweeps funds automatically on a daily basis into the CBO-licensed country account and the SPC never maintains overnight substitutive balances outside Oman.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing for equity injection: UBO or foreign parent initiates SWIFT MT103 from its own bank account to the SPC's CBO-licensed beneficiary account, using IMF BPM6 category 521 (Direct Investment Equity Capital) in the purpose/beneficiary information field together with the Omani Commercial Registration number and CIF number in field 72. There is no minimum paid-in capital, but the credit must be supported on arrival by a board resolution recording the subscription and a letter from the applicant's home bank confirming the remitter's identity; the USD-to-OMR conversion, when executed at the CBO peg, must be evidenced by the FX confirmation ticket. If the injection is structured as a shareholder loan instead of equity, the SPC must file the executed loan agreement with the corporate signatory to prevent the bank from reclassifying the receipt as taxable revenue; statistical reporting of the inward FDI receipt to the CBO is completed by the receiving bank within 30 days.Primary rail is SWIFT MT103 for service-based outflows: tuition deposits and admission fees to overseas universities, visa-package remittances to embassies or immigration counsel, and agent-commission payments to foreign education partners, each carrying a BPM6 service purpose code (Education Services, Business Consultancy, or Legal Services) and a narrative citing the underlying contract number and student file or visa reference. The SPC must maintain a segregated client-money escrow ledger so collected student deposits are never co-mingled with operational fee income, and all outbound wires to higher-risk recipient corridors (Pakistan, Egypt, Iraq, Yemen and Iran-adjacent banks) must include sanctions-screening certificates and, where routed via Islamic windows, a Shari'ah compliance declaration. Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends paid by the Omani SPC to a non-resident shareholder; no Omani WHT applies to dividends distributed to Oman-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four months after the close of the fiscal year; for a calendar-year SPC, on or before 30 April of the following year. A branch or permanent establishment (PE) of a non-resident company files on the same schedule.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Simultaneously with the corporate income tax return, i.e., no later than four months after fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required when annual Oman controlled transactions exceed OMR 2,000,000; Master File required when consolidated group revenue exceeds OMR 30,000,000; Country-by-Country Report required when group revenue exceeds OMR 375,000,000; all taxpayers with any related-party transactions must file TP-1 with the annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted standalone UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations; however, the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 55/2023) codifies substance-over-form GAAR, controlled foreign company rules, and OECD Inclusive Framework implementation (Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax, Income Inclusion Rule and Undertaxed Profits Rule at 15% for MNE groups above the EUR 750 million threshold). The SPC must show real management in Oman, an operating office, resident placement and visa advisory staff, independent decision-making, and full income-recording substance; a passive letterbox vehicle will be disregarded, profits reattributed to Oman, and treaty or small-taxpayer benefits revoked.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of up to 100% of the under-assessed tax, compulsory recovery with monthly interest (2%), cancellation of loss-carryforward benefits, suspension of the SPC's tax clearance certificate, and criminal prosecution for tax fraud with fines up to OMR 20,000 and imprisonment of up to three years.

Director personal liability: Statutory veil-piercing: the sole shareholder, manager and any signatory director are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax, penalties and interest; travel bans, asset freezes, disqualification from managing Omani companies and personal bankruptcy proceedings are triggered.
Shell Company Disguise and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: GAAR disallowance of intercompany payments, income recharacterisation, full 15% attribution of profit to the Omani PE or deemed resident entity, denial of treaty benefits, application of the 15% Qualified Domestic Minimum Top-Up Tax for MNE-parented groups, and strike-off or licence revocation under the Commercial Companies Law and the sectoral licensing regime.

Director personal liability: Directors and the sole shareholder may be assessed personally where transferred assets or shell distributions render the SPC unable to pay tax; penalties of 50%–100% of recharacterised profit are imposed; immigration enforcement includes travel bans and debarment of the director's Oman residence card.
AML/CFT Violations in Escrow and Wire Payment Handling

Statutory sanction: Under Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law (RD 30/2016 and 2020 amendments): administrative fines of OMR 10,000–50,000 per breach, revocation of the SPC's commercial registration and immigration-services licence, and criminal money-laundering prosecution with imprisonment of up to 15 years and fines up to OMR 250,000; confiscation of the escrow balance.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and the ultimate beneficial owner face personal criminal liability where tainted funds were known or should have been known; personal bank accounts are attached, international alerts trigger travel bans and asset freezes, and extradition becomes possible under bilateral mutual legal assistance instruments.
Unlicensed Immigration and Visa Placement Practice

Statutory sanction: Cease-and-desist order from the competent licensing authority; administrative fine up to OMR 10,000 for unlicensed practice; voidance of client service contracts; restitution of all escrow-held fees; criminal sanctions under the Commercial Fraud and Consumer Protection Law with imprisonment of up to two years in serious cases.

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder-director is personally liable to refund misappropriated escrow funds where the escrow account was used as a proprietary account, lifting the corporate veil; the director's Oman residence status is revoked, and the person is placed on the Royal Oman Police travel-lock list during investigation and prosecution.
