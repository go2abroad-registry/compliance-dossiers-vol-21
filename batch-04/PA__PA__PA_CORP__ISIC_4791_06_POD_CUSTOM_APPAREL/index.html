Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá (Public Registry of Panama), Dirección General, Sección de Personas Jurídicas (Mercantil)

Governing statute

Ley No. 32 de 26 de febrero de 1927 (Ley General de Sociedades Anónimas de la República de Panamá), as amended; Articles 3, 4, 5, 21, 25, 27, 38 and 57; Acuerdo No. 16 de 2015 of the Registro Público; Código de Comercio de la República de Panamá, Book II.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública de Constitución de Sociedad Anónima (Public Deed of Incorporation), executed before a Notary Public of the Republic of Panama and compulsorily inscribed in the Public Registry; legal existence commences upon inscription and is evidenced by the Ficha de la Sociedad (Certificado de Inscripción).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The authorized capital of a Sociedad Anónima must be at least USD 10,000 (B/.10,000) under Article 3 of Law 32 of 1927, but no paid-up capital is required at formation and no escrow account deposit is mandated by Panamanian law. Shares may be subscribed and issued without initial payment; fully non-resident ownership is permitted without exchange-control restriction.

Funding & unlock path: Home-office / outsourced-fulfillment POD model: no fire-safety civil-defense certificate is required absent physical premises. If a local warehouse or printing workshop is established, the following clearances become mandatory: (i) fire-safety and evacuation certificate from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá (inspection per NFPA 101); (ii) structural-load and occupancy inspection by the municipal engineering department; and (iii) workplace occupational-safety clearance by the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL) under the applicable COPANIT standards.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), Panama; official portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa, with electronic services via Sede Electrónica.

E-government filing portal

To register the Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.): (1) complete incorporation in the Public Registry of Panama and obtain certified certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and registered Resident Agent; (2) file the Aviso de Operación / municipal operating notice where the company has a physical or registered business presence; (3) submit the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application to DGI, either online or in-person, accompanied by the public registry certification, constituent documents, power of attorney and identification of the legal representative/resident agent, and proof of the municipal notice; (4) DGI issues the RUC and, where applicable, activates the ITBMS seller registration. A foreign entity without Panamanian operations is not generally required to obtain a RUC, but any cross-border sale with Panamanian consumption nexus may trigger a separate simplified digital-services registration obligation.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — To register the Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.): (1) complete incorporation in the Public Registry of Panama and obtain certified certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and registered Resident Agent; (2) file the Aviso de Operación / municipal operating notice where the company has a physical or registered business presence; (3) submit the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application to DGI, either online or in-person, accompanied by the public registry certification, constituent documents, power of attorney and identification of the legal representative/resident agent, and proof of the municipal notice; (4) DGI issues the RUC and, where applicable, activates the ITBMS seller registration. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or disregarded-entity election exists under Panamanian law. A Panama S.A. is an entity-level taxpayer on Panama-source income only. Available elections include the simplified small-taxpayer regime for ITBMS within turnover limits and the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Law 41 of 2007 for qualifying re-export/service platforms. No OECD-style hybrid mismatch election is recognized in domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general monetary threshold; ITBMS registration is mandatory before a business habitually transfers taxable goods or services in Panama. A simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' regime is available for low-turnover micro businesses below approximately B/.300,000 annual gross receipts (confirm current DGI threshold), but it is an administration/filing simplification, not an exemption from registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Tangible goods exported from Panama (including POD custom apparel) qualify for the 0% ITBMS if title and physical possession pass to a nonresident buyer and the goods leave Panamanian customs territory. The exporter must be ITBMS-registered, issue an e-invoice flagged as export/zero-rated, file a valid customs export declaration, and provide the carrier/warehouse records showing physical departure and delivery outside Panama. Services are zero-rated/excluded when the benefit is deemed consumed outside Panama, but the support must show a nonresident beneficiary and offshore use. Related ITBMS input credits are creditable and, if in excess in the period, may be applied to other liabilities or claimed as an export refund after DGI audit.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduana / DUA or export declaration generated in the Single Window system, as required by Panamanian Customs)
  • Bill of Lading (maritime) or Air Waybill (air) evidencing shipment out of Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/Payment-Service-Provider transfer confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • DGI electronic invoice with export/zero-rated indicator and control number
  • Exporter's RUC certificate and, if applicable, customs exporter registration

Panama has a mandatory phased e-invoicing mandate administered by DGI. VAT/ITBMS-registered taxpayers must issue electronic invoices through DGI-approved software or the DGI's online issuance platform, transmit invoice data electronically to DGI in near real-time, obtain a DGI-assigned control/authorization number, and render the invoice to the buyer with a QR code or digital stamp. The DGI generates acceptance/rejection responses and the electronic documents must be retained for the statutory period. For POD export retail, zero-rated export e-invoices must be identified separately and linked to customs export records.

DGI, jointly with the National Customs Authority, cross-checks export declarations, e-invoices, shipping records, and banking/proceeds records. If zero-rating is claimed without timely physical export, complete documentation, or exports proceeds not received within the expected 180-day commercial/repatriation cycle, DGI may disallow the 0% rate and assess 7% ITBMS retroactively on the invoice from its issuance date, plus late-payment interest and fines. Penalties under the Código Fiscal can range from 10% to 100% of the omitted tax, with higher amounts and criminal prosecution for fraud, including imprisonment for tax and customs evasion. The fiscal clawback limitation period generally reaches five to seven years, and the full documentation bundle must be preserved for that period.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility with no foreign-exchange controls. Panama maintains an entirely open capital account and an open current account: capital injections, dividend repatriations, royalty payments, debt-service transfers, intercompany settlement, and liquidation proceeds may move freely in and out of Panama in USD without prior SBP authorisation or any currency-licence requirement. Profit-remittance friction is limited to statutory withholding taxes (5 percent on dividends distributed out of Panamanian taxable income; 10 percent on distributions made out of non-taxable foreign-source earnings) and to standard source-of-funds documentation; there is no exchange-control approval gate. Because the Balboa is pegged 1:1 to the USD and the US dollar is the de facto functional currency of the economy, no residual conversion, devaluation, or blocked-account risk exists for the POD e-commerce treasury.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, repatriation-timeline, or conversion-ratio rules apply. Panama imposes no obligation on exporters, including operators of cross-border e-commerce and print-on-demand (POD) apparel retailing, to repatriate export receipts, to convert foreign currency into Balboas, or to hold proceeds in designated accounts within a statutory period. A Panamanian Sociedad Anónima may retain USD earnings in local or foreign bank accounts, deploy profits to inventory purchase, marketing, or supplier settlement, and distribute residual cash at its discretion; the only associated obligations are AML record-keeping under Law 23 of 2015, full invoicing traceability for territorial tax purposes, and accurate beneficial-ownership declarations with the DGI registration unit.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no independent central bank. The monetary and financial architecture is centered on the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP - Superintendency of Banks of Panama), which is the prudential regulator and AML/CFT supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP), which acts as fiscal agent of the Republic and channels public-sector payments; and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF), the designated financial-intelligence unit. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is the statutory monetary unit, pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar, and US banknotes and coins circulate as legal tender under Article 152 of the Political Constitution and the 1904 Currency Law. Exchange-rate policy is effectively replaced by the fixed statutory dollar peg, the absence of a discresionary monetary authority, and full, unrestricted circulation of the USD as the banking settlement currency.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore electronic-money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Airwallex, and Payoneer do not operate under a dedicated Panamanian EMI licence, because Panama does not issue a standalone 'electronic money institution' authorisation; digital-payment providers must instead be authorised through the SBP fintech sandbox framework introduced by Law 231 of 2022 or be treated as unregulated payment agents. Their practical viability for a POD S.A. is Medium for multi-currency receivable collection and low-value supplier payouts, but Low as a substitute for a Tier 1 Panamanian bank account, because unlicensed EMIs cannot obtain direct access to ACH Panama, the SPAV high-value clearing system, or the local cheque-clearing network without a licensed Panamanian correspondent bank. The controlling legal constraints are that EEA or UK EMI passports do not extend to Panama; funds held with unlicensed EMIs are outside SBP supervision and are not covered by Panamanian depositor-protection rules; and Panamanian banks frequently reject or hold inbound credits originating from unknown or low-transparency EMI wallets until the source and economic purpose have been substantiated.
