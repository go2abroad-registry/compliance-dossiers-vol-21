Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) – Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); electronic portal: Sistema T@X (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa/) and the Panamanian e-invoicing platform SISTEMA e-FACTURA

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity seeking to establish a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) for dental consumables manufacturing must: (1) execute and notarize the corporation's articles of incorporation and, where applicable, apostilled corporate resolutions of the foreign shareholder; (2) file the incorporation documents with the Registro Público de Panamá and pay the registry fees; (3) obtain the 'Aviso de Operación' / municipal operating license; (4) appoint a resident legal representative or fiscal agent; and (5) apply to the DGI for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) using the T@X platform or in-person submission, providing: proof of incorporation, corporate documentation, documentation of the legal agent, registered address in Panama, and identification of shareholders/directors. The RUC integrates both income tax and ITBMS obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide US-style check-the-box elections; every Sociedad Anónima is mandatorily treated as a separate taxable corporation. No hybrid mismatch elections are available. The only relevant elective regime is the 'Régimen de Pequeños Contribuyentes' for companies with annual gross receipts at or below B/. 36,000, and optional investment/export incentive regimes such as SEM (Sociedad de Emprendimiento Marítimo), EASE, or free-zone licences for specific qualifying manufacturing activities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

There is no general monetary threshold for ITBMS registration; any entity performing taxable activities in Panama must register and obtain an RUC. However, the simplified small-taxpayer regime ('Pequeños Contribuyentes') applies to annual gross receipts ≤ B/. 36,000 (Balboas), under which the business is exempt from full ITBMS invoicing and may pay a reduced simplified tax.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under Panama's ITBMS applies to: (a) tangible goods physically exported from Panama, evidenced by a valid customs export declaration; and (b) services rendered to non-resident clients where the benefit and use of the service occur entirely outside Panama. In the case of dental zirconia CAD/CAM block manufacture, export sales of the finished biocompatible ceramic blocks qualify for 0% ITBMS when the goods leave the national territory under a formal customs export procedure and the manufacturer retains proof of physical shipment and foreign payment. Supplies to Panama's Colón Free Zone, Panama Pacifico, or other bonded custom regimes may be exempt rather than zero-rated and are subject to special rules.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduana Electrónica)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign client payment proof (bank SWIFT/telex confirmation)
  • Commercial invoice connected to the taxpayer's RUC and ITBMS registration
  • Packing list, certificate of origin, and, where requested by customs, proof of purchase of inputs used in production

Panama mandates e-invoicing for large taxpayers (Grandes Contribuyentes) and phasing in for all businesses. The DGI's electronic invoicing system requires the issuance of Comprobantes Fiscales Electrónicos (CFE) with a unique digital stamp and cryptographic validation, submitted through the T@X portal or certified software. There is no real-time per-transaction fiscal clearance; taxpayers must transmit invoices and credit notes to the DGI periodically and provide immediate digital access to a transaction ledger for DGI inspections. Authorized fiscal printers are required for paper-regime fallback.

The DGI can audit export zero-rating claims for up to five fiscal years (extended to eight years in cases of omission or fraud) and may retroactively claw back any ITBMS credits or refunds where documentation fails to establish actual export. Fraudulent zero-rating constitutes tax evasion under Law 42 of 2003 and Panama's Tax Code, subject to administrative fines of up to 100% of the evaded tax, monthly surcharges from 5% to a cumulative cap, repatriation of principal excluding foreign-source income if unpaid, and criminal penalties of imprisonment between one and five years for senior management.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) exists solely as coinage; the US Dollar (USD) is legal tender and the operational currency of the financial system. There is complete convertibility between the Balboa and the US Dollar at the fixed 1:1 ratio, and there are no capital account controls, no mandatory conversion requirements, and no restrictions on repatriation of profits, dividends, or invested capital. Profit remittance from a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima is free of exchange control but is subject to a 10% dividend withholding tax on distributions from Panama-source profits (with a 20% withholding for non-declared distributions), while foreign-source income under Panama's territorial tax system is generally outside the scope of domestic taxation unless remitted through the Panamanian banking system in a manner that triggers source characterisation. CRS and FATCA reporting applies to non-resident beneficial owners, but does not impede convertibility or remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is zero statutory surrender or repatriation obligation on export proceeds in Panama. Dental zirconia block exporters may freely retain USD receivables in any SBP-licensed bank account, offshore account, or foreign correspondent account without conversion or mandated timelines. The only compliance obligations are SBP anti-money-laundering transaction reporting thresholds, standard commercial invoice/transport documentation for banking record-keeping, and the obligation of the local receiving bank to report cash transactions exceeding USD 10,000 or unusual wire patterns to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) of the FIU-Panama.

Central bank supervision: Panama does not operate a conventional central bank. Monetary stability is achieved through full dollarization, with the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) exercising prudential supervision over all banking and fintech entities, and Banco Nacional de Panamá acting as fiscal agent and clearing operator for the national ACH system. The Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) co-regulates foreign exchange statistics, but no central monetary authority controls currency issuance or capital movements.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium. Panama recognizes licensed 'Digital Banks' and 'Fintech Innovation Hubs' under Law 697 of 2021 and SBP regulations, but offshore or foreign-domiciled Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) are not automatically authorized to accept deposits or conduct payment services in Panama without an SBP license. An offshore EMI may be used only as an ancillary collection channel for foreign customers, not as a primary treasury rail, due to correspondent banking de-risking, absence of deposit insurance, and the risk that the SBP will treat unlicensed digital ledger payment activity as illegal taking of deposits. Recommended structure: SBP-licensed local digital bank or a supervised wallet provider for e-commerce payments, with a Tier-1 Panamanian bank as the settlement ledger of record.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity or shareholder loan capital must be remitted as a USD SWIFT MT103 from the investor's account to the Panamanian operating account, using a transaction narrative on the SWIFT field 70 stating 'Capital Contribution – Panama Sociedad Anónima – ISIC 3250 Dental Zirconia Block Manufacturing'. The source bank should use SWIFT purpose code 'GIFT' or 'CAPT' equivalents; Panama's receiving bank will apply SBP's established client provenance rules, requiring the corporate resolution approving the capital increase (or shareholder loan agreement with repayment schedule), the updated shareholder registry, and proof of the remitter's bank account ownership. Any single wire above USD 50,000 will be internally verified; the transaction must be recorded in the corporate capital ledger and noted on annual tax filings to remain consistent with Panama's territorial tax regime. If the injection originates from the United States, the corresponding MT103 should reference the Exporters' Declared Export Type Form A if equipment is imported; otherwise no exchange control authorization is necessary in Panama.Supplier settlements for zirconia powder, Y-TZP stabilizers, milling tools, and packaging are to be executed through SWIFT MT103 with SWIFT purpose code 'PRD' (payment for goods) or 'TRP' (travel? correction: use 'GDS' for goods trade), supported by commercial invoice, purchase order, bill of lading, and a certificate of origin where applicable. For high-value strategic equipment procurement, an irrevocable documentary letter of credit (L/C) at sight is recommended through Banco General, Banistmo, or Multibank, with the L/C issuance protocol in compliance with UCP 600; the L/C may be issued without needing SBP prior approval because there are no exchange controls. Domestic supplier payments may be routed through Panama's local ACH (Cámara de Compensación of Banco Nacional) only for Balboa- or USD-demominated transactions within Panama; in practice all payments are USD-denominated and the ACH is leveraged for utility, payroll, and minor intra-country obligations, while all cross-border trade disbursements are cleared via the correspondent banking network of the Tier-1 institution.Natural hedge in USD-denominated trade (preferred: all operating cash flows, capital injections, and trade payables are in USD, eliminating primary currency risk in the dental zirconia block sales channel).Currency forward contracts (USD/EUR, USD/JPY) executed with Banco General, Banistmo, or Towerbank treasury desks for exposure arising from purchase of Japanese-sourced zirconia powders or European-milled block finishing tools, with tenors of up to 365 days and no regulatory margin restriction beyond the bank's internal credit policy.FX swaps (USD/CNY, USD/EUR) available through authorized SBP banks for conversion of multi-currency inbound collections into USD at settlement, without a futures exchange authorization.Non-Deliverable Forward (NDF) contracts for USD/PAB are not applicable and are essentially nonexistent because PAB exists only as coinage and carries a statutory hard peg to USD; Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB 5,000,000 in aggregate related-party transactions (local legal tender is USD at par with PAB)

Economic substance scope: Under Law 82 of 2019, a company carrying on 'relevant activities' must demonstrate economic substance. Manufacturing of physical dental blocks is not generally a relevant activity, but the S.A. must still maintain a real production facility, employees, and active direction in Panama. A nominally registered S.A. without local physical operations would be re-characterized as a shell, triggering B/.5,000 ESR filing penalty and B/.10,000 per month until substance is corrected.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanctions under Panama Criminal Code Art. 298-A: imprisonment 2 to 5 years and fine equal to evaded tax; civil assessment of omitted tax plus 25% surcharge and 10% interest; registration closure and disqualification from obtaining export permits.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the false Form 101 are jointly and severally liable; the court may impose travel bans to prevent asset flight; director assets may be seized for full restitution when the company is insolvent.
Shell Company & False Substance

Statutory sanction: Law 82/2019 ESR non-compliance: B/.5,000 per relevant activity for failure to file; B/.10,000 per month of ongoing failure; DGI can disregard corporate form and tax the economic owner at Panama-source rates; possible judicial dissolution.

Director personal liability: Directors can be ordered to settle corporate tax arrears out of pocket; ten-year ban on holding directorships; international police (INTERPOL) notice possible when fraud is alleged.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: DGI adjustment to arm's length price; 30% penalty on the adjusted tax plus annual interest; no offset by DTAs; customs liabilities if reporting mismatch in export bulletins.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed intercompany agreements may be held personally for the tax adjustment if they knowingly approved non-arm's-length terms and distributed the cash as dividend.
AML & Trade-Cycle Dirty Money

Statutory sanction: Law 23 of 2015 anti-money laundering: fines up to B/.1 million plus criminal imprisonment 5-12 years; seizure of bank accounts; prohibition on holding licenses; Panama International Financial Center sanctions such as SIB suspension.

Director personal liability: Directors personally exposed to criminal money-laundering charges; travel bans, house arrest, extradition from allied states, and civil forfeiture of personal assets may apply.
