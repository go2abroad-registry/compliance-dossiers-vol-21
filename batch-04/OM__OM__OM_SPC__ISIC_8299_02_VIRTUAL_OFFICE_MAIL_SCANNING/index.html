Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed under RD 18/2019 for a Single Person Company, but for foreign-owned SPCs, the declared capital of OMR 1,000 (or higher as prudentially required) must be deposited in a Central Bank of Oman (CBO) licensed commercial bank in an escrow/blocked corporate capital account prior to the issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate. The deposit must remain blocked until the e-CR is issued and the entity is formally incorporated, after which the funds are released to the SPC's active corporate account.

Funding & unlock path: The applicant must obtain a Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (Royal Oman Police) covering the virtual office / mail-scanning premises, together with a municipal structural-occupancy inspection confirming safe electrical wiring, adequate emergency exits, suitable lighting, and general workplace occupational-health clearance for personnel engaged in digital mail scanning and handling.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed under RD 18/2019 for a Single Person Company, but for foreign-owned SPCs, the declared capital of OMR 1,000 (or higher as prudentially required) must be deposited in a Central Bank of Oman (CBO) licensed commercial bank in an escrow/blocked corporate capital account prior to the issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate. The deposit must remain blocked until the e-CR is issued and the entity is formally incorporated, after which the funds are released to the SPC's active corporate account.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - the successive tax administration to the former Secretariat General of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance - with the national integrated e-services platform 'Oman Tax Portal' at taxoman.gov.om.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; 100% foreign ownership is permitted and no statutory minimum capital is required. Step 2: On the Oman Tax Portal (taxoman.gov.om), register the SPC for corporate income tax under Royal Decree 9/2017 within the statutory deadline (generally within three months of obtaining the Commercial Registration) to receive the Income Tax Number (TIN). Step 3: Assess the 12-month rolling taxable supply position under Royal Decree 121/2020; if taxable supplies are expected to exceed OMR 500,000 (mandatory) or OMR 250,000 (voluntary), file the VAT registration application within 30 days of the obligation arising; the OTA will issue a separate VAT certificate and registration number. Step 4: Where the SPC is managed as a non-resident supplier, appoint an Omani tax representative and formalize the tax representative designation on the Oman Tax Portal before chargeable supplies begin or the threshold is tested, ensuring that CIT and VAT obligations are linked to the same taxpayer master record.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to USD at 2.6008 OMR/USD (0.3845 USD/OMR). Oman is an IMF Article VIII jurisdiction with full current-account and broadly liberal capital-account convertibility. Foreign shareholders and SPCs may freely inject equity, repatriate dividends, and settle international trade without CBO prior approval for standard transactions. No binding capital controls or approval quotas apply to outward direct investment by resident SPCs. Residual restrictions: bank discretion on large cross-border remittances, CBO screening for sanction-tainted counterparties, and the withholding tax net on outbound passive payments (10% WHT on dividends, interest, royalties, and management fees paid to non-residents under SD 28/2009 as amended).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory surrender, conversion, or holding-period requirements on export or trade-service proceeds. The SPC may retain USD/EUR collections in foreign-currency accounts at licensed Omani banks and convert to OMR at the peg rate without CBO approval. Banks apply standard trade documentation checks (invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, contract, Commercial Registration) for outbound settlement; no percentage-based conversion quota applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – البنك المركزي العماني

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore digital EMIs (Wise-type) are not viable as the primary treasury rail in Oman. Such EMIs generally lack CBO payment-institution licences, cannot hold OMR settlement accounts, and cannot obtain a local IBAN for OMR-clearing. Practical feasibility is Low for core payroll, tax, and LC operations; usable only as a secondary low-latency corridor for collecting foreign card-recurring (Stripe) revenues pre-onshore sweep.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Standard routing is SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's home bank to the SPC's OMR current/escrow account at a tier-1 Omani bank. Remittance field 70 (Remittance Information) must state 'Equity capital contribution – SPC CR No. [X]' and field 50K must mirror the UBO's name disclosed during KYC. No CBO approval is required for a clean foreign-investment injection under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (SD 50/2019).
