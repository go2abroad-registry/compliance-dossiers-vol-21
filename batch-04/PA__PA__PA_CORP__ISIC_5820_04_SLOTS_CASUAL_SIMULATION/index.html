Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); electronic portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI at https://www.dgi.gob.pa, which is the mandatory channel for RUC registration, ITBMS and ISR filings, e-invoicing validation, and tax certificates. Ancillary registries: Registro Público de Panamá (corporate incorporation) and the Municipality of the registered office (Aviso de Operación/business license).

E-government filing portal

Foreign shareholders operate through a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) incorporated by a licensed resident agent. SOP: (1) execute and register the incorporation deed at the Public Registry; (2) obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación for the registered office location; (3) file the RUC/NIT registration application with the DGI, attaching the normalized corporate documents, the Aviso de Operación, the resident agent's power of attorney, identification documents of directors and the beneficial owner, and the declared economic activity code (ISIC 5820); (4) activate the Sede Electrónica e-Tax profile using the issued RUC and obtain or register the taxpayer's digital signature; (5) maintain the RUC file updated (address, directors, shareholders, and any secondary tax roles such as withholding agent or digital-services intermediary). No minimum capital and no local ownership requirement; foreign-owned S.A. registration to operational status typically completes within 7-15 business days after incorporation.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: the S.A. is a separate taxable corporation, not eligible for pass-through treatment; it is taxed only on Panama-source income at the standard 25% corporate income tax rate (Código Fiscal Art. 699), while foreign-source income is outside Panamanian income tax unless effectively connected with a Panama permanent establishment or local economic presence. The entity is not automatically a Tax_Exempt_Zone vehicle; preferential income tax treatment applies only if physically established in Panamá Pacífico, a Zona Libre, or under the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime. — Foreign shareholders operate through a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) incorporated by a licensed resident agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: the S.A. is a separate taxable corporation, not eligible for pass-through treatment; it is taxed only on Panama-source income at the standard 25% corporate income tax rate (Código Fiscal Art. 699), while foreign-source income is outside Panamanian income tax unless effectively connected with a Panama permanent establishment or local economic presence. The entity is not automatically a Tax_Exempt_Zone vehicle; preferential income tax treatment applies only if physically established in Panamá Pacífico, a Zona Libre, or under the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime.

Available election forms

Panama has no U.S.-style check-the-box election and no OECD hybrid-mismatch election rules; the available elections are tax-regime options: (i) the optional ITBMS input-credit method (Crédito Fiscal Optativo, Art. 177-A of the Código Fiscal) in lieu of the statutory restricted-credit pro-ration; (ii) election of the annual ISR return with quarterly advance payments versus the optional simplified payment methodology under recent fiscal reforms; (iii) the Pequeño Contribuyente flat-quota regime for gross annual income up to PAB 400,000, which substitutes monthly ITBMS filings; and (iv) a one-time election on the taxable treatment of foreign branches or permanent establishments under the territorial-source rules. Multinationals commonly structure offshore service flows contractually rather than through entity-classification elections.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Zero monetary threshold: any natural or juridical person that transfers goods, imports goods, or renders services in Panama must obtain a RUC before carrying out the first taxable act. The only de minimis simplification is the Pequeño Contribuyente regime for taxpayers with gross annual income not exceeding PAB 400,000 (balboa at par with the US dollar), under which full ITBMS monthly returns are replaced by a fixed monthly quota; this significantly constrains input-credit recovery and is unsuitable for a digital export operation.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% ITBMS rate applies to (i) physical goods exported out of Panama evidenced by a customs export declaration (Código Fiscal Art. 1058), and (ii) services rendered by a Panamanian taxpayer to a non-resident beneficiary when the use, enjoyment, or exploitation of the service occurs entirely outside Panama (Código Fiscal Art. 1059, numeral 4, as developed by DGI regulations on service exports and by the digital-economy provisions of Law 151 of 2021). For the simulated social-casino application monetized through Apple IAP and Google Play Billing, zero-rating eligibility requires transactional evidence that each in-app purchase is attributable to a foreign end-user whose residence and consumption point are offshore (storefront country, payment billing address, and geo-IP); sales to users located in Panama are subject to the 7% ITBMS, or to the Law 151 withholding regime where invoicing is done offshore. Panama imposes no exchange-control repatriation deadline because the US dollar is legal tender; however, the 180-day documentary window for inward remittance is applied by DGI audit practice and bank compliance as the benchmark for recognizing exported-service proceeds, and input VAT attributable to zero-rated sales remains creditable under the optional credit regime.

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) applies in phased waves since 2021 under Law 91 of 2019 and DGI administrative resolutions. B2B and B2C invoices must be transmitted to the DGI Sede Electrónica via API/REST-FACTFEL EDI; the DGI validates each invoice in near-real time and returns an authorization number and hash, which replaces physical fiscal stamping as the condition of legal validity. Medium and large taxpayers transmit continuously; small taxpayers may batch at least daily. Revenue from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing must be reconciled with the authorized e-invoice records and reported on the monthly ITBMS return, including any reverse-charge or intermediary documentation for foreign end-users.

DGI fiscalización procedures will verify the offshore use-and-enjoyment substance of the digital service export, reconcile Apple/Google settlement reports with declared gross revenue, and test the arm's-length character of any affiliated platform arrangement. On disallowance, the 0% rate is reversed, ITBMS is assessed at 7% on the gross consideration, and the taxpayer faces: (i) retrospective assessment within the general 5-year limitation period, extended to 10 years where no return was filed or fraud is found; (ii) a monthly late-payment surcharge of 10% up to a 100% cap plus moratory interest at 10% per annum; (iii) fines of up to 100% of the omitted tax for fraudulent omission; and (iv) criminal liability under the Código Fiscal for wilful evasion, exposing the S.A., its directors, and the resident agent to custodial sanctions, RUC suspension, and loss of the resident agent's license. Refunds of zero-rating excess credits are payable only after prior DGI desk or field verification and are not automatic in the presence of discrepancies with banking or platform data.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility in both current and capital account. The United States dollar is legal tender and the Panamanian balboa exists only as a subsidiary coin at parity. There are no exchange controls, no prior approval requirements for profit repatriation, and no currency conversion restrictions for non-resident shareholders. Dividends, capital returns, and cross-border payments settle freely in USD. Only standard bank-level source-of-funds and AML due diligence documents are required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender timeline or mandatory conversion ratio exists in Panama. Full dollarization means export receipts, including proceeds from digital publishing and app-store revenues, may be retained, disbursed, or repatriated without mandatory conversion to any local currency.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá (fiscal agent) and Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as banking supervisor; Panama operates without an issuing central bank.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium as a primary treasury conduit. Offshore digital EMIs are not licensed banks in Panama and cannot provide Panamanian local settlement accounts. They may be used only as ancillary collection or disbursement gateways if licensed in their home jurisdictions, but all final liquidity must be routed into a SBP-licensed Panamanian bank to maintain statutory tax records, corporate substance, and beneficiary-owner transparency. A pure EMI structure without a local licensed bank relationship is not viable for the PA_CORP treasury blueprint.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat corporate income tax on net Panama-source taxable income; a graduated statutory schedule applies to net taxable income not exceeding B/. 1,500,000.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% final withholding on gross dividends declared or paid from Panama-source profits; a complementary additional withholding may arise when the distributed profits have been taxed at an effective rate below the 25% headline corporate rate.
