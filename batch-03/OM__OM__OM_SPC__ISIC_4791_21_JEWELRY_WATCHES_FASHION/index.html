Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital is imposed on an Omani SPC under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the sole shareholder fixes the subscribed and paid-up capital in the Articles of Association. 100% foreign ownership is permitted. The Commercial Registry does not mandate an escrow bank deposit at registration; however, if a cash capital model is adopted, the capital amount should be funded and parked in the company's corporate bank account immediately after incorporation to support AML/KYC and bank reference requirements.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), the unified national revenue and customs authority created from the merger of the former Secretariat General for Taxation and Oman Customs; principal portal is the OTA e-services platform at taxoman.gov.om, with customs declaration processing through the integrated customs system commonly referred to as Bayane. The OTA administers corporate income tax, VAT, excise tax under GCC framework laws, and customs duty, and issues all taxpayer-facing registrations, certificates, rulings, and assessments.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) through the Invest Easy single-window platform and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate, which is the primary corporate identifier. Step 2: The CR is automatically referenced in the OTA master data system; the SPC must complete its online taxpayer file on taxoman.gov.om, including corporate details, beneficial ownership information, and banking data, in order to obtain the OTA Tax Card and statutory Taxpayer Reference Number/TIN. Step 3: For VAT, apply separately in the OTA VAT module within 30 days from the end of the month in which taxable supplies or imports exceed the mandatory registration threshold of OMR 38,500, or immediately for voluntary registration once the OMR 19,250 threshold is crossed; the OTA issues a VAT registration certificate with a VAT ID. Step 4: Obtain and activate the digital certificate credentials used for OTA e-invoicing once the applicable phase-in cohort starts. Step 5: Where a non-resident owner or foreign principal acts without a physical Oman establishment, all filings and communications with OTA must be effected through a licensed tax agent with a valid power of attorney filed in the OTA system.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (not pass-through eligible). An Omani-incorporated SPC is a resident juridical person for Omani income tax and is taxable on its worldwide income; the SPC has no check-the-box or fiscal transparency option, and profits are taxed at the SPC level before any distribution to the foreign shareholder. Territorial scope: resident companies are taxed on global income with relief for foreign-source profit only where a double tax treaty substantially applies or OTA practice conditions are met. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (not pass-through eligible). An Omani-incorporated SPC is a resident juridical person for Omani income tax and is taxable on its worldwide income; the SPC has no check-the-box or fiscal transparency option, and profits are taxed at the SPC level before any distribution to the foreign shareholder. Territorial scope: resident companies are taxed on global income with relief for foreign-source profit only where a double tax treaty substantially applies or OTA practice conditions are met. Tax exempt zone: a mainland SPC does not automatically benefit from free-zone tax holidays; exemption requires a separate license in a designated economic zone such as Duqm SEZ or Sohar Freezone and satisfaction of economic substance conditions, which is not assumed in this scope.

Available election forms

Omani law does not provide a U.S. style check-the-box election or a domestic hybrid mismatch election. Available statutory elections: (i) application to adopt a non-calendar fiscal year-end subject to prior OTA approval; (ii) VAT group registration election for two or more related resident persons that jointly meet the VAT registration conditions, allowing a single VAT return; (iii) voluntary VAT registration when annual taxable supplies and imports are at least OMR 19,250 but below the mandatory threshold; (iv) for transfer pricing documentation, the taxpayer may select the most appropriate method and, in approved cases, execute an advance pricing arrangement; and (v) treaty relief claims, including foreign tax credit or exemption, may be elected via the applicable double taxation agreement and must be substantiated before OTA.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration threshold: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies and imports over the preceding 12 months, or where the person has reasonable grounds to expect such value in the following 12 months. Voluntary registration threshold: OMR 19,250. Special nexus rules apply to e-commerce and distance sellers: foreign suppliers without an Oman establishment that sell goods or digital services to non-registered consumers in Oman may be required by OTA to register from a lower or nil threshold; imported low-value e-commerce consignments not exceeding OMR 100 in value are collected by importers, postal operators, and courier companies under a simplified OTA regime. Omani registered business customers acquiring imported services or foreign digital supplies remain liable to reverse-charge VAT at 5%.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: VAT zero-rating applies to a supply of goods by a registered person where the goods are exported from Oman to a destination outside Oman within 90 days of the date of supply and the supplier retains proof of physical departure and direct export title (either by itself or by a customer outside Oman). Services: zero-rating applies to services directly connected with exported goods and supplied to a recipient outside Oman, services supplied to a non-resident customer that are not performed in Oman and are consumed or economically benefited outside Oman, and international transport services. The SPC must maintain contemporaneous commercial evidence linking the export order, the OTA e-invoice zero-rated line, the customs export declaration, the transport contract, and the foreign settlement. Input VAT on goods or services used in making zero-rated supplies is creditable; if output VAT is less than input VAT, the excess credit is refunded from the OTA subject to a refund application review, normally completed within approximately 60 days of a complete electronic submission.

  • Oman Customs export declaration generated in the Bayane system, evidencing physical exit of the goods from Oman within 90 days
  • Original commercial invoice and OTA-compliant zero-rated e-invoice bearing the electronic invoice reference
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill covering the entire transit to the consignee outside Oman
  • Packing list and detailed description of goods with HS codes, weight, and declared export value
  • Jewelry/timepiece-specific documentation: assay or hallmark certificate, authentication or warranty card for fine watches, and Kimberley Process Certificate where the shipment contains rough or polished diamonds
  • Bank inward remittance certificate, SWIFT MT103, or exporter export-proceeds credit advice evidencing receipt of funds from the foreign buyer

The OTA has implemented a phased, mandatory e-invoicing program for B2B and B2C supplies. The system operates on a clearance (real-time validation) model: taxpayers must transmit structured invoices in the OTA-prescribed XML/UBL schema to the OTA platform through an approved API, certified e-invoicing service provider, or OTA official portal before the invoice is issued to the buyer; the OTA validates the invoice encryption, digital signature, and business rules and returns a unique electronic invoice identifier and a stored digital stamp. B2C transactions require a QR code on the printed or displayed e-invoice. Phase-in dates are cohort-based, beginning with the largest VAT registrants in 2025 and rolling to remaining taxpayers in subsequent tranches according to OTA circulars; non-compliance with clearance or stamping rules may lead to rejection of input VAT recovery and penalties.

OTA audit practice and Omani banking documentation norms treat 180 days as the standard benchmark for the inward repatriation of export proceeds; where payment is not received within this period, the taxpayer must demonstrate bona fide foreign receivables terms and ongoing enforceability. In a VAT audit, the OTA cross-matches e-invoices, customs export declarations, transport documents, and banking inflow records; if the zero-rating evidence is incomplete, false, or the physical export is not proven, the OTA retrospectively recharacterizes the supply as taxable at 5%, issues a reassessment for the unpaid VAT plus late payment charges, and claw back the related input VAT. Fraudulent export zero-rating, including fabricated customs declarations, backdated shipping documents, or artificial payment circulations, constitutes VAT evasion under the Omani VAT Law and may attract substantial administrative penalties, interest, suspension of the exporter's VAT registration and e-invoicing access, joint liability for responsible officers, and imprisonment for culpable individuals.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani rial is legally pegged to the US dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD and is fully convertible on current and capital accounts. Oman maintains IMF Article VIII status and does not impose a general foreign-exchange control law or a CBO permit system for ordinary trade payments, profit remittances, or capital repatriation by a 100% foreign-owned SPC. Practical hurdles are documentary and AML-driven: the bank must verify the underlying economic purpose, the shareholder must evidence legitimate source of funds, and the SPC must obtain an OTA tax-clearance certificate before making an outward dividend or capital distribution. No sector-specific conversion quota applies to e-commerce export receipts from jewelry, watches, or fashion accessories.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds. Exporters may retain foreign currency, including USD, in onshore foreign-currency accounts with CBO-licensed banks without a CBO allocation approval. Commercial banks require the underlying trade file for crediting proceeds: signed commercial invoice, customs export declaration from the Bayan system, bill of lading or air waybill, shipping certificate, and, where payee is not the seller, an assignment or collection instruction. Conversion from USD to OMR is at the bank's prevailing market rate, and foreign-currency balances may be reinvested or hedged freely as long as the transaction has a genuine trade or working-capital basis.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), the monetary authority of the Sultanate of Oman, acting concurrently with the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) on profit-repatriation clearances and with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) on foreign-investment registration. CBO licenses all commercial banks, exchange houses, and payment-service providers and enforces the Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism Law (Royal Decree 30/2016) and CBO electronic-banking and cross-border payment circulars.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary collection on-ramp, but Low as a substitute for a CBO-licensed bank account. Offshore electronic-money institutions and cross-border treasury platforms (e.g. Stripe Treasury, Payoneer, Airwallex, or UK/EU EMIs) are not licensed by CBO, cannot participate in Oman's local clearing and RTGS-equivalent settlement infrastructure, and cannot hold an Omani IBAN or provide OMR domestic payment initiation. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder's equity contribution must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from a bank account in the shareholder's legal name directly to the SPC's USD or OMR account at the CBO-licensed Omani bank. The remittance field must clearly state 'FDI equity injection - Omani SPC - Commercial Registration Number [CR No.] - Share Subscription Agreement dated [date]'. The receiving bank will require a board resolution, a share-register extract, a notarized copy of the SPC's constitutive documents, and source-of-funds evidence such as six months of shareholder bank statements. Where the capital injection exceeds the equivalent of OMR 20,000, the bank will additionally require a written investment declaration for the beneficial-ownership register and will classify the credit as financial-account-underwriting exposure under CBO AML reporting protocols. No separate exchange-control authorisation is required, but funds must remain booked to the SPC's capital account and cannot be co-mingled with trading receivables before the commercial registration extract reflects the paid-up share capital.For ordinary trade supplier payments in raw materials and finished inventory, use direct SWIFT MT103 corporate credits through the Omani bank unless the bank's risk policy imposes a threshold for documentary intervention. For high-value timepiece components, precious metals, and gemstone procurement, the preferred rail is a commercial letter of credit issued by the Omani bank and transmitted by SWIFT MT700/MT707, with at-sight payment or usance linked to clean transport documents, inspection certificates, and insurance certificates. Domestic OMR vendor disbursements should be settled using the local interbank RTGS-equivalent and ACH-equivalent rails accessible through the licensed bank's e-banking service. E-commerce refunds to end customers are discharged through Stripe or PayPal's treasury in the card-scheme rail, then reflected as a payout credit into the Omani bank account. Profit repatriation to the foreign shareholder is executed by a separate SWIFT MT103 after OTA clearance, with the bank retaining the distribution resolution, tax clearance, and audited financial statements in the treasury file.Outright OMR/USD forward contracts up to 12 months, permitted only with a CBO-licensed onshore bank and only when supported by an underlying export receivable, import invoice, or committed payroll/capex cash-flow forecast.Deliverable foreign-exchange swap between OMR and USD for rolling operational liquidity, each leg mapped to settled trade flows or working-capital facilities and subject to CBO anti-speculative market-conduct standards.Non-deliverable forward (NDF) on the Omani rial permitted through a CBO-licensed counterparty or a recognized non-resident bank solely to hedge USD-denominated balance-sheet exposure arising from cross-border e-commerce settlements; speculative NDF positions are not permissible and do not satisfy CBO substance requirements.Cross-currency interest-rate swap (CCIRS) limited to hedge accounting on a financed working-capital facility for raw-material imports, with full IFRS 9 hedge-designation documentation and mark-to-market reporting to the Omani bank's treasury and risk committee.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as an ancillary collection on-ramp, but Low as a substitute for a CBO-licensed bank account. Offshore electronic-money institutions and cross-border treasury platforms (e.g. Stripe Treasury, Payoneer, Airwallex, or UK/EU EMIs) are not licensed by CBO, cannot participate in Oman's local clearing and RTGS-equivalent settlement infrastructure, and cannot hold an Omani IBAN or provide OMR domestic payment initiation. For a jewelry-and-watches e-commerce SPC, such EMIs may be used only as merchant-of-record or card-acceptance processors whose funds are subsequently swept into the Omani licensed bank account. CBO AML standards require the SPC's complete and traceable treasury trail through a licensed institution before any tax or supplier payment is executed.
