Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under the Oman Companies Law, RD 18/2019 as amended; the sole shareholder must irrevocably subscribe and deposit the declared share capital in a domestic Omani bank account before incorporation. No mandatory escrow account is required for the courier activity itself, but the Communications and Information Technology Regulatory Authority may impose a bank guarantee (typically OMR 5,000–50,000) as a financial-fitness condition of the express courier license.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA): mandatory fire-safety certificate covering smoke and gas detection in sorting halls, emergency exit plans, and annual renewal audits. Ministry of Labour — Occupational Safety and Health Centre: workplace health clearance, forklift-operator certification, first-aid kit compliance, and personal protective equipment program. Muscat Municipality: structural stability certificate and occupation permit for the sorting facility and administrative offices.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under the Oman Companies Law, RD 18/2019 as amended; the sole shareholder must irrevocably subscribe and deposit the declared share capital in a domestic Omani bank account before incorporation. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA); e-services portal: https://eservices.taxoman.gov.om / taxoman.om; customs clearance portal: Royal Oman Police Directorate General of Customs Bayan Single Window.

E-government filing portal

For the Omani SPC, incorporate with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration certificate, then complete CIT/VAT registration through the OTA e-services portal by filing the taxpayer application and uploading the CR certificate, constitutional documents, authorized signatory identification, registered office proof, and notarized power of attorney. OTA issues the TIN and VAT registration certificate. A foreign entity with no Oman commercial presence but which makes taxable supplies connected with Oman (including international courier services) must apply for VAT registration in Oman before the first relevant supply using the same portal with a resident tax representative where applicable; foreign persons are not entitled to defer registration using the domestic threshold when the place of supply is Oman and statutory registration requirements apply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman has no check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. The SPC is compulsorily taxed as a separate juridical person under the Income Tax Law; there is no pass-through election. For VAT, a taxable person may elect voluntary registration when taxable supplies/sum of taxable imports exceed OMR 19,250 but do not exceed the mandatory threshold; qualifying related persons may elect to form a VAT group with a representative member. No other entity-level hybrid classification election is available in Oman.

5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies when goods are exported from Oman within the statutory time limit (generally 90 days of the time of supply) to a destination outside Oman, for directly connected international transport and associated incidental services, or for services provided to a non-resident recipient who receives the benefit wholly outside Oman. For express courier operations, the international carriage and related handling/transport services may be zero-rated where the waybill, customs export declaration, and delivery evidence demonstrate physical export and final foreign destination; a separately invoiced domestic collection/delivery leg in Oman is standard-rated unless it is performed as part of the single international transport supply and covered by the export evidence.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Bayan Single Window export declaration or electronic export manifest
  • Bill of Lading or Master/House Air Waybill or courier waybill evidencing dispatch from Oman
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated VAT invoice issued in the OTA-prescribed format
  • Proof of export with date stamp showing departure from Oman within the statutory 90-day period
  • Delivery confirmation / POD showing arrival at the foreign destination
  • Freight and insurance documentation or logistics records evidencing the international route
  • Inward remittance certificate / bank credit advice or equivalent export proceeds proof

Oman is transitioning to a phased national e-invoicing framework. For taxpayers in scope of the OTA e-invoicing mandate, invoices must be generated through a certified billing/ERP/API integration connected to the OTA e-invoicing platform, submitted for near-real-time fiscal clearance before being issued, and contain a unique fiscal identifier/digital QR stamp for authentication and validation by customs and tax auditors. The exact phase obligation is determined by turnover and OTA decision; even outside the mandate, VAT invoices must include the standard OTA invoice data fields, issue dates, VAT number, and be retained in tamper-evident format for the statutory retention period.

The OTA will scrutinize zero-rating claims against customs manifests, courier waybills, payment flows, and export proceeds repatriation. An unsupported claim is reversed and the VAT is assessed at the 5% standard rate with late-payment penalties and administrative violation fines under the VAT Law. Fraudulently claimed zero-rating or deliberately false export documentation constitutes VAT evasion and may attract criminal penalties (imprisonment and/or fines), disqualification from future zero-rating, and clawback of incidental input VAT recovery, together with CIT/additions on unrepatriated or artificially diverted export revenues.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current account transactions and is contractually pegged to the USD at a fixed mid-rate of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (USD 1 = 0.3845 OMR). Capital account convertibility is de facto unrestricted for foreign direct investment in an SPC (100% foreign ownership permitted), with no prior CBO exchange control approval for equity injection or profit repatriation. Practical hurdles: (i) all cross-border payments are bank-dependent and subject to AML/KYC verification; (ii) dividend and profit remittances may require an audited balance sheet, proof of tax compliance (Tax Card and final tax settlement), and a bank-beneficiary compliance review; (iii) no withholding or remittance tax applies to dividends per Oman Law (Royal Decree 28/2009), but the distributing SPC must settle its corporate income tax and prove beneficial owner status. The peg removes market convertibility risk but not bank deleveraging risk on USD clearing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender of export proceeds and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. All export proceeds must be received through an Omani licensed bank or an approved correspondent account to enable CBO balance-of-payments statistical reporting, and must be repatriated or utilized in accordance with the SPC's commercial documents; practically, banks enforce As soon as available (ASAP) policy and may apply a 365-day repatriation review for courier/trade C&F settlements; no CBO requirement exists to convert foreign currency receipts, so USD/GBP/EUR can be maintained directly.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), operating under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000, as amended) and the CBO Foreign Exchange and AML/CFT Circular framework; the Oman Tax Authority handles cross-border remittance tax clearance where required.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, Currencycloud) are not licensed by the CBO to hold OMR or open domestic accounts for an Omani SPC. They may serve solely as outbound non-resident settlement nodes for USD/EUR/AED flows, but they introduce sanctions and AML concentration risk and are not accepted under the Oman minimum AML/CFT standards (Royal Decree 30/2016 and CBO AMF Circular 85/2019). The SPC must maintain at least one primary account at a tier-1 licensed commercial bank; use of an EMI is limited to ancillary downstream payouts outside Oman.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer: the parent shareholder transfers equity or working capital via SWIFT MT103 from its home bank to the SPC's account at a tier-1 Omani bank. Required underlying documents: CR; investment approval or FDI notification to the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; Corporate Board Resolution confirming share capital contribution and no debt encumbrance; source of wealth for high-risk clients (e.g., audit-confirmed EBITDA or exchange listing). Routing must utilise a non-sanctioned correspondent (typically J.P. Morgan, Citi, or Bank of New York Mellon for USD legs via Fedwire/CHIPS; Standard Chartered, HSBC or J.P. Morgan for EUR target2). Payment reference field 70 must include 'EQUITY INJECTION' plus CR number and the SPC legal registration trademark; the receiving bank will code the transaction under CBO BoP category 'FDI - Equity Capital.' No prior CBO approval is required; however, delays are possible if the inward transfer exceeds 100,000 OMR equivalent, requiring enhanced due diligence review (3-5 days).Clearing rails for trade settlements: (1) SWIFT MT103/MT202 for intercompany settlement of express courier operational costs (air network fees, ground handling, airline freight invoices) - best executed OMR/USD; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700) and deferred payment LCs (MT798) for procurement of aircraft/sorting machines or spare parts, and SWIFT MT760 for stand-by LC issuance to guarantee network partner obligations; (3) Domestic settlement of fuel and payroll via Oman Automated Clearing House (OACH) / electronic payment order; (4) International parcels use correspondent-operated USD clearing through the SWIFT network, with a low-latency pre-settlement reconciliation to avoid FedWire cut-off. Because of the hard peg, the SPC is exposed only to USD/OMR zero-coupon conversion drift; for EUR/GBP/AED disbursements, bank auto-booking is applied at the bank's spot rate, with caution against bank spread stack in cross-rail transfers.OMR/USD Spot (prescribed spread, current benchmark 2.6008-2.6015 T.T. selling/buying)OMR/USD Outright Forward (up to 12 months) - allowed for courier-import payment coverOMR/USD FX Swap (short-dated rollover to manage liquidity mismatches between receipt and payment cycles)Cross-currency Swap (USD/OMR, up to 3 years, on a non-deliverable benchmark linked to CBO peg)FX Options (plain vanilla, OMR/USD, banks risk-manage internally; exotic options not typically permitted)Non-deliverable Forwards (NDFs) on OMR are irrelevant and not offered because of the hard peg to USD; for EUR/USD and GBP/USD exposure on European and UK lanes, the SPC may use deliverable FX forwards and options with Bank Muscat/Sohar International Treasury, or a hedging line approved by the CBO-regulated bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

1. Under Oman domestic law, dividends distributed to non-resident shareholders are subject to withholding tax at 10%. (The 0% rate applies only to dividend distributions made to Omani resident individuals or wholly-Omani-owned entities.) For a foreign-owned SPC, outbound profit distributions therefore attract 10% withholding unless reduced under an applicable Double Tax Agreement.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the end of the fiscal year; the Tax Authority may grant an extension of up to 4 additional months. For a calendar-year fiscal year, the regular deadline is 30 April of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 in aggregate related-party transactions during a fiscal year. If the SPC's cross-border related-party revenue or expenses (including courier network fees, hub charges, IT service fees, management fees, loans, and goods purchases) exceed OMR 1,000,000, the SPC must prepare contemporaneous Local File and Master File documentation and file the transfer pricing disclosure form with the annual corporate income tax return. Even below the threshold, the Tax Authority may request documentation on a risk-assessed basis. An SPC with a single intercompany arrangement exceeding OMR 1,000,000 for any type of transaction triggers the documentation requirement.

Economic substance scope: Oman applies Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) to legal entities undertaking relevant activities, including logistics and transportation services. The SPC must demonstrate that its core income-generating activities (strategy, management, courier operations, invoicing, and risk-taking) are performed in Oman with adequate premises, qualified staff, and operational expenditure. Cross-border express courier businesses must ensure intercompany allocation of revenue and headcount reflects actual functions in Oman. Failure to meet ESR: first breach incurs penalties up to OMR 5,000; repeated breaches up to OMR 10,000 plus potential license suspension, strike-off, or forced local substance directions. The Tax Authority may also recharacterize transactions and disallow deductions if economic substance is absent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Penalty of 100% of the undercharged tax plus daily late payment surcharges; additional fines of OMR 2,000 for failure to file; criminal prosecution; imprisonment up to 3 years; publication of the offender's name in the Official Gazette; disqualification from government courier contracts.

Director personal liability: Directors and the foreign sole shareholder are jointly and severally liable for the full tax underpayment, penalties, and surcharges if the veil of the SPC is pierced for fraud. Courts may impose asset freezes, travel bans, and up to 3 years' imprisonment on the directing mind.
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Tax Authority may disregard the SPC's legal personality, recharacterize all outbound fees as dividends, disallow all intercompany deductions, impose 100% penalties, and refer to Public Prosecution. ESR penalties of OMR 5,000 to OMR 10,000 per fiscal year apply. Commercial Registrar may strike off the company and blacklist the manager for 5 years.

Director personal liability: Corporate veil piercing exposes shareholders and directors to unlimited liability for corporate tax and trade debts. Foreign directors may be subject to travel bans and entry restrictions until tax settlement. Criminal imprisonment up to 3 years may apply for deliberate shell operation to defeat Omani tax.
AML / Sanctions Violation / Terrorist Financing

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016 as amended) and implementing regulations: fines up to OMR 100,000 per violation; revocation of the courier license; confiscation of assets linked to illicit funds; criminal prosecution for money laundering; individual liability for compliance failures. The Financial Intelligence Unit may impose freezing orders; failure to report suspicious transactions attracts penalties up to OMR 50,000.

Director personal liability: Directors, the designated AML compliance officer, and beneficial owners face personal criminal liability for gross negligence in AML monitoring, including imprisonment up to 7 years where funds are laundered through courier operations. Travel bans and asset confiscation may be issued.
Withholding Tax Failure / Unreported Outbound Remittances

Statutory sanction: Penalty equal to 50% of the under-withheld amount plus monthly 1% late payment surcharge (capped at 25%). The Tax Authority may deny the deduction of the related expense entirely. Repeated infractions lead to blocking certificate preventing any foreign remittance through Omani banks until taxes are settled.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize or sign outbound wire transfers without withholding tax certificates become personally liable for the withheld amounts and penalties. A travel ban may be issued against directors until settlement.
Commercial Licensing and CR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 3,000; immediate closure of business premises; suspension of customs broker authority; rejection or nullification of tax filings; personal criminal liability for carrying on unlicensed business. A suspended CR may also invalidate the SPC's tax registration, leaving the foreign shareholder exposed to tax at higher rates.

Director personal liability: The foreign sole shareholder of a non-compliant SPC can be personally liable for all contracts, debts, and taxes incurred during the period of unlicensed activity. Directors may be criminally fined or imprisoned for conducting an unlicensed courier network.
