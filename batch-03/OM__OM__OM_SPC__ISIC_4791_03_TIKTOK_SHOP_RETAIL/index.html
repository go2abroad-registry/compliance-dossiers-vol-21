Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Sultanate of Oman Tax Authority (OTA); e-services via TANATHOR portal at https://tax.gov.om

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) and a Unified National Number from the Oman Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP). Within 30 days of CR issuance, the SPC must apply online through the Tax Authority's TANATHOR e-portal for corporate income tax registration, submitting the CR, constitutional documents, board/shareholder resolution, passport copies and powers of attorney for directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and the registered office/resident agent address in Oman. The OTA issues a separate TIN for income tax. If taxable supplies exceed OMR 386,000 per annum, the SPC must separately register for VAT and obtain a VAT Tax Registration Number (TRN); voluntary VAT registration is available on reaching OMR 38,600 taxable turnover, and VAT registration is also required for taxable import activity.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) and a Unified National Number from the Oman Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP). Within 30 days of CR issuance, the SPC must apply online through the Tax Authority's TANATHOR e-portal for corporate income tax registration, submitting the CR, constitutional documents, board/shareholder resolution, passport copies and powers of attorney for directors and ultimate beneficial owners, and the registered office/resident agent address in Oman. The OTA issues a separate TIN for income tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election system. Available statutory elections are limited to: (1) voluntary VAT registration at the lower threshold and VAT de-registration where turnover falls below the mandatory threshold; (2) election for small business income tax relief where profits are within statutory bands; (3) election of a non-calendar accounting/fiscal year period with Tax Authority approval; and (4) application of double taxation treaty relief where a foreign parent confirms residency and provides a beneficial ownership declaration.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 386,000 of annual taxable supplies/imports (mandatory); OMR 38,600 (voluntary registration threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Oman VAT Law to goods exported outside Oman (and, per GCC rules, outside the GCC member states) when title passes to a non-resident customer and the goods are physically removed from Oman within the prescribed time period. Zero-rating also applies to certain services connected to exported goods, international transport services, services directly related to overseas goods, and supplies to export-processing/exempt-zone businesses under the relevant legal conditions. The SPC must prove that the supply was made to an exporter/non-resident and that the goods or services are outside the Omani VAT scope.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

As of the current effective framework, Oman has no mandatory real-time national e-invoicing clearance or digital stamping regime. VAT taxpayers must issue electronic VAT tax invoices in Arabic or English through their own ERP/billing systems, retain VAT ledgers, and support all invoices with valid VAT TRN. Monthly/quarterly VAT returns are filed electronically via the TANATHOR portal and settled through e-payment channels. The Tax Authority is developing a centralized e-invoicing/clearance platform but no API clearance mandate is yet in force.

The Tax Authority aggressively audits zero-rated export claims by matching customs export declarations, transport documents, invoices, and proof of receipt of the full export consideration from the non-resident buyer. Where zero-rating is claimed fraudulently or cannot be substantiated, the VAT is retrospectively clawed back at 5% from the date of supply, late-payment penalties and administrative fines are imposed under the VAT Law, and intentional tax evasion exposes officers/shareholders to criminal penalties including imprisonment and fines of up to double or treble the evaded tax in addition to blacklisting and business licence suspension.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, and the Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the US Dollar at approximately 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Current account transactions are fully convertible, with no restrictions on payments or transfers for trade, services, or profit and dividend repatriation. Capital account transactions are not subject to general exchange control legislation, but are effected through licensed commercial banks under CBO reporting and AML/CTF scrutiny. Foreign-owned single-person companies are permitted full repatriation of paid-up capital and accumulated profits, provided that audited financial statements, tax filing evidence, and distribution resolutions are presented; applicable withholding taxes, if any, must be settled first. No approvals from the CBO or the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion are required for routine profit remittances, although banks may require source-of-funds documentation above certain thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio in Oman. Exporters may retain foreign currency receipts in licensed foreign currency accounts at Omani banks without any central bank requirement to convert into OMR. The only obligations are documentary: the export must result from a genuine commercial transaction, trade documents must support the inflow, and the proceeds must be credited to the recipient Omani entity within the normal trade-settlement period agreed with the bank. For social commerce export flows, TikTok Shop platform payout reports and freight/shipping documents are sufficient to demonstrate the underlying receivable.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Moderate. Oman does not automatically recognize foreign-registered electronic money institutions (EMIs) as banks or as authorized payment service providers within the Omani jurisdiction. Wise B2B and similar EMIs may be used by the Omani SPC as an ancillary collection/payout channel for TikTok Shop proceeds settled in USD, GBP, or EUR outside Oman, but they cannot serve as the primary treasury account for OMR-denominated tax, VAT, payroll, or supplier settlements. Any entity providing payment services to that SPC inside Oman must be licensed by CBO under the Banking Law and CBO Digital Payment Services rules. The SPC should therefore maintain one domestic OMR current account with a tier-1 Omani commercial bank and use the foreign EMI only for cross-border receivables and platform payouts.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injection must be routed as a standard international wire (SWIFT MT103) from the foreign shareholder's account to the SPC's capital contribution account at a licensed Omani commercial bank. The purpose code / remittance information field must clearly describe the transfer as 'Share Capital Contribution to Omani Single Person Company' and include the SPC commercial registration number. The receiving bank will verify the underlying corporate documents and, for the initial contribution, may require the audited resolution and source-of-funds report. OMR clearing of the incoming transfer is processed through the CBO-operated RTGS system. For inbound amounts below OMR 500,000, no separate CBO approval is normally required; larger amounts may prompt enhanced due diligence but remain permissible if the corporate documentation is clean. The funds can be maintained as OMR or placed in a foreign-currency current account under the SPC's bank treasury arrangement.Outbound trade payments from the Omani SPC to overseas TikTok Shop supply-chain partners and suppliers shall be executed either via SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for low-value recurring settlements or via commercial documentary letters of credit (governed by UCP600) for larger bulk import orders. The Omani issuing bank will require the underlying commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading/waybill, and a sale-purchase agreement. For disbursements to mainland China, funds may be routed in RMB via correspondent banking or in USD via the SWIFT system, depending on the suppliers banks and the clearance channel. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No explicit statutory threshold in Oman; transfer pricing documentation (Local File and Master File) becomes mandatory under Cabinet Decision No. 55/2022 (effective for tax years starting after 1 Jan 2023) for taxpayers with related-party transactions exceeding OMR 500,000 (local file) and OMR 1,000,000 (master file) per tax year, or at OTA request on risk assessment. De minimis threshold: related-party transactions under OMR 50,000 are exempt from contemporaneous documentation but still must comply with arm's length principle.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not issued standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) like UAE; however, general anti-avoidance rules under Royal Decree 28/2009 (Art. 24-25) and the tenet of 'real economic activity' apply. A foreign-owned SPC conducting TikTok Shop retail must demonstrate actual management, physical presence (office/warehouse), employees, and decision-making in Oman to (a) qualify for tax residence and (b) avoid deemed-shifting of profits to non-resident principal. Oman OTA practice applies substance-over-form: shell companies with no genuine activities may be disregarded for tax purposes. Additionally, pursuant to OECD Global Base Erosion rules, Oman has no mandatory CbC reporting for MNE groups below EUR 750M, but if the parent operates via Oman SPC with limited functions, TP documentation must reflect that the SPC bears only routine risks (distribution entity) and earns arm's length remuneration.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Income Tax Law RD 28/2009 as amended)

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 1 year and/or fine equal to 100% of evaded tax (per Article 25). Tax amount assessed plus 2% per month (max 200%) and possible asset freezing. OTA can impose additional penalty up to OMR 100,000 for serious fraud.

Director personal liability: Directors and the sole shareholder (manager) of the SPC are personally liable to remit unpaid corporate tax if there is fraudulent concealment or misuse of corporate form. Travel bans can be issued by OTA for unpaid assessments. Imprisonment directly exposes director to criminal record and personal asset seizure.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance & Thin Capitalization

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment increasing taxable income by disallowed expenses; 10% additional penalty on TP adjustment; 2% monthly late payment interest (max 200%). Failure to file contemporaneous TP documentation on OTA request triggers OMR 5,000 to OMR 50,000 penalty.

Director personal liability: Where TP adjustment arises from a gliding path of corporate transactions to strip Oman profits, OTA may apply GAAR, void artificial structures, and seek restitution from directors if the entity is insolvent. Travel bans are possible until adjustment paid.
Shell Company, Beneficial Ownership and AML (AML Law RD 30/2016)

Statutory sanction: AML fines: OMR 5,000 to OMR 100,000 per violation; OTA/Regulator may dissolve the SPC and revoke registration. Beneficial ownership infringement carries imprisonment up to 3 months and OMR 1,000-50,000 fine. Shell companies face immediate deregistration and deemination.

Director personal liability: Director and beneficial owner can be held jointly liable for AML and tax debts. A criminal conviction can lead to disbarment from any company management in Oman. Travel ban is automatically triggered for tax/AML summons non-compliance.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance on Cross-Border Payments

Statutory sanction: WHT under-withheld amount plus a fine of 50% of the tax if not withheld; 100% if misleading records; interest at 2% per month on the late remittance; OTA can third-party direct recovery from bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Law expressly makes the taxpayer (SPC) liable; however, director who knowingly does not remit WHT becomes personally jointly liable because the debt is statutory debt. Persistent evasion leads to criminal court action against director.
Failure to File / Digital Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Automatic OMR 1,000 fine; up to OMR 2,000 plus a 20% evaded tax enhancement penalty. Late payment interest remains accruing until closure. OTA can enforce via public prosecution for tax debt and notify immigration to block departure.

Director personal liability: Directors are statutorily obliged to ensure proper tax filing. Civil restitution to the company for any penalties caused by gross negligence; potential personal bankruptcy if tax liability exceeds assets; travel ban subject to OTA administrative order.
