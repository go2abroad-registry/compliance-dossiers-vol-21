Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Omani law. The Sole Shareholder must fully subscribe and fund the share capital stated in the Memorandum of Association into a corporate bank account with a licensed Omani bank. No escrow account is mandated; for foreign-owned SPCs, the declared capital amount must be evidenced as available at the time of bank account opening.

Funding & unlock path: Fire and life safety clearance from the Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) is required for the commercial premises or any warehouse space; structural load and building safety certification is verified as part of the municipal license inspection. If employees are engaged, the Ministry of Labour occupational safety clearance and Social Protection Fund registration are mandatory before staff commence work.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Omani law. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — national revenue authority established under Royal Decree 75/2021, absorbing the former Secretariat General of Taxation. Official portal: https://taxoman.gov.om (Taxpayer e-Services for CIT/VAT registration, returns, and electronic payments). Customs export clearance is operated separately by the Royal Oman Police, Directorate General of Customs, via the Bayan single-window system.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) via the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) Invest Easy portal (investeasy.gov.om) to obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. Step 2: Within 3 months of commencement, apply online to the OTA for income tax registration and obtain the statutory Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN); the CR number is the primary identifier and the TIN is issued against it. Step 3: Register for VAT via OTA e-Services within 30 days after taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000 in any rolling 12-month period (or apply voluntarily once supplies or deductible expenses exceed OMR 125,000). Step 4: Register as an exporter in the Bayan customs platform to obtain a customs client code for export declarations. A foreign entity with no Omani place of business that supplies taxable goods/services in Oman must appoint a resident tax agent to register for VAT on the OTA portal; such a foreign entity will not be treated as having a permanent establishment for CIT solely by making VAT-registered distance sales unless further economic activity occurs in Oman.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Omani Single Person Company is a resident juridical person, fully opaque and taxable at entity level on worldwide income at the standard 15% corporate income tax rate, with a 3% preferential rate applicable to the first OMR 30,000 of taxable income only for qualifying small businesses. Non-resident branches and non-resident service suppliers without a permanent establishment are taxed on Oman-source income only on a territorial basis. This standard retail SPC enjoys no automatic free-zone exemption.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, pass-through, fiscal unity, or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Omani tax law; the SPC is per se an entity-level taxpayer. Available statutory elections and concessions: (i) application to the OTA for approval of a non-calendar financial year; (ii) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 125,000 threshold, useful for exporters seeking input VAT refunds; (iii) VAT group election whereby two or more related taxable persons may be treated as one taxable person, subject to OTA approval; (iv) free zone / special economic zone licensing election (e.g., Duqm, Sohar, Salalah) which may confer a statutory CIT exemption/holiday period, but only if the SPC is actually licensed and economically established inside the zone.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman operates a conventional (non-Islamic, dollar-pegged) currency regime: OMR pegged to USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Current account convertibility is unrestricted; no prior approval is required for trade and service payments. Capital account openness is broad for 100% foreign-owned SPCs; profit repatriation is permitted after corporate tax clearance and submission of audited financial statements. Practical hurdles: bank-level end-beneficiary KYC, compliance with CBO anti-money-laundering and sanctions reporting, and the requirement to route all transfers through licensed Omani banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBO Circular (as amended by the Law on the Regulation of Foreign Exchange, Royal Decree 35/2017, art. 8), export proceeds must be repatriated to Oman within 180 days of the shipment date unless CBO grants a written extension. There is no mandatory conversion ratio (0% surrender obligation); exporters may retain foreign currency in Omani bank accounts or convert voluntarily to OMR. Non-repatriation without approval is a compliance breach subject to CBO penalties.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – Directorate of Foreign Exchange Operations

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW-MEDIUM. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business) are not licensed by CBO to open local OMR-currency settlement accounts or to act as full banking intermediaries. They can be used as auxiliary payout/payment channels only when connected through a licensed Omani payment aggregator (e.g., OmanNet, Bank Muscat's PayTabs, or NBO's shopping gateway). Stripe/PayPal currently operate as regulated payment facilitation services via Malaysian/Luxembourg/Singapore entities; they are acceptable for receiving card payments but are not substitutes for a licensed Omani bank account for FX clearing, payroll, or tax settlement. Legal constraint: CBO 'New Bank Activity' circular prohibits any non-licensed institution from conducting cross-border treasury and settlement activities in OMR.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For investor funds arriving from abroad: (1) Instruct foreign bank to issue SWIFT MT103 with field 50 (ordering customer) as the exact SPC shareholder name, (2) field 72 remittance information must include 'INVESTMENT CAPITAL / FDI EQ' and the SPC Commercial Registration (CR) number, (3) the destination bank must classify under CBO Balance of Payments 'FDI – Equity' (code X100), and (4) require the beneficiary bank to issue an e-advice with a UTR. Verification at the SPC side must include a board resolution authorising the inward remittance, the share allocation ledger, and the Oman Chamber of Commerce (OCCI) foreign-investor certificate. Accumulated capital injections must be declared in the annual audited statutory filing with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry, and Investment Promotion.For overseas supplier payments and operational imports of hydroponic equipment: preferred clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 for less than USD 100,000, Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) governed by UCP 600 (URGV 600 for standby LC) for larger purchases, and direct cross-border bank transfer via CBO's RAWAN electronic clearing for local OMR counterparties. The bank will verify documentary basis (Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading / Air Waybill) and CBO reporting code for imports (code 121 / 122). Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation is expected when aggregate same-side related-party transactions exceed OMR 3 million in a fiscal year; Master File alignment is generally expected where the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 30 million. Country-by-Country reporting applies for groups with consolidated revenue above OECD-aligned thresholds. These thresholds trigger full disclosure and substantiation of intercompany pricing for the online retail business.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations. However, the Oman Income Tax Law and Executive Regulations require the SPC to show real business substance in Oman. A foreign-owned SPC without local staff, premises, inventory, or decision-making may be disregarded, treated as a dependent agent permanent establishment of the foreign owner, or recharacterized as an artificial arrangement under anti-abuse principles.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Filing Fraud

Statutory sanction: The Oman Tax Authority may assess the full tax due, add penalties of up to 100% of the evaded tax, and impose administrative fines for incorrect or incomplete returns. Egregious conduct may be prosecuted criminally with fines up to OMR 10,000 and imprisonment up to 5 years.

Director personal liability: The SPC manager who signs a false return or knowingly participates in concealment can be held personally liable for restitution, fines, and civil damages. Omani courts may impose travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment on responsible directors and beneficial owners.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may adjust the SPC's taxable income, disallow excess deductions, assert 10% withholding tax on cross-border royalties, interest, and services, and impose late payment surcharges and documentation penalties for failure to substantiate arm's length pricing.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or implement non-arm's length arrangements may be jointly liable for the adjusted tax, penalties, and interest; travel bans and personal financial liability can follow enforcement proceedings if the company fails to pay.
Shell Company / Artificial Routing

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's anti-abuse rules, the Tax Authority may disregard the SPC, deny treaty benefits, reattribute income to the foreign beneficial owner, and apply underpayment penalties of up to 50% of the recharacterized income. Administrative penalties for failing to meet bookkeeping and tax registration obligations may also apply.

Director personal liability: A nominee or foreign manager who allows the SPC to operate as a shell may be disqualified, held civilly liable for unpaid corporate tax, barred from leaving Oman, and prosecuted for fraud or abuse of legal entity forms.
AML / Payment-Rail Misuse

Statutory sanction: Violation of Oman's Anti-Money Laundering Law may result in criminal fines up to OMR 20,000, imprisonment up to 10 years, confiscation of proceeds, and disallowance of tax deductions for unreported or unsubstantiated receipts.

Director personal liability: Company directors, beneficial owners, and the SPC manager are exposed to criminal AML liability, personal civil restitution, travel bans, freeze orders, and imprisonment where the company structure is used to disguise the origin of funds.
