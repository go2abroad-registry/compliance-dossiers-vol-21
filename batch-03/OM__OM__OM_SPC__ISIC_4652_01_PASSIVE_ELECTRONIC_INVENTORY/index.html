Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), established under Royal Decree 121/2020 and operating under the Ministry of Finance; principal e-services portal and digital filing interface: taxes.gov.om (unified portal for Corporate Income Tax, VAT registration, VAT returns, and VAT refunds); customs export declarations are processed through the Royal Oman Police Customs single-window 'Bayan' system.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) via the Invest Easy portal to obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate and CR number. Step 2: Register the SPC with the OTA for Income Tax purposes within three months from issuance of the CR certificate, obtaining a Tax Identification Number (TIN) through the OTA e-portal using the CR certificate, constitutional documents (MOA/SOC), audited financial statements or opening balance sheet, lease agreement for local premises, and shareholder/UBO passport copies. Step 3: Apply for VAT registration separately on the same OTA portal (mandatory where annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000; voluntary where taxable supplies or taxable expenses exceed OMR 250,000), obtaining a VAT Registration Number (VRIN). Step 4: Where the non-resident shareholder is in a jurisdiction without mutual administrative assistance with Oman, or for practical compliance, appoint a local Omani tax representative or liaison mandated by power of attorney to handle VAT and tax filings; all subsequent registrations, zero-rated export declarations, and refund applications are executed electronically under the OTA e-services framework.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The Omani SPC (single-member LLC wholly owned by a foreign corporate or natural person) is a separate juridical taxable person for both direct and indirect tax purposes; it is subject to Corporate Income Tax under Royal Decree 28/2009 (Income Tax Law) on its worldwide income and is an independent legal person for VAT under Royal Decree 121/2020; no pass-through, partnership, or branch-pooling treatment is available to an SPC, and no territorial exemption applies to foreign-source income unless specifically provided under a double tax treaty.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Omani law. Available statutory elections: (1) VAT grouping election under the VAT Law for two or more related persons (50% or greater common ownership, financial/economic interdependency) to be treated as a single taxable person, subject to prior OTA approval; (2) related-company single-taxpayer election under Article 59 of the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009, as amended) allowing associated companies to apply to be assessed as one taxpayer, subject to OTA consent; and (3) voluntary VAT registration election for persons above the optional OMR 250,000 threshold. No election permits a foreign parent to file on a tax-transparent or consolidated composite basis outside these statutory regimes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies (excluding exempt and capital-asset supplies). Voluntary registration: OMR 250,000 of annual taxable supplies, or OMR 250,000 of annual taxable expenses for a person seeking input VAT recovery. Non-resident persons making taxable supplies in Oman are required to register with the OTA regardless of threshold, without the right to the voluntary-threshold relief.

Oman has not yet enacted a mandatory real-time e-invoicing clearance regime with government API clearance, digital stamping, or continuous fiscal control of B2B/B2C tax invoices. However, all VAT invoices must satisfy the substantive and formal requirements of Articles 47–50 of the VAT Law and the Executive Regulations, including sequential numbering, supplier and customer VAT registration numbers, date of supply, description and quantity of goods, consideration, VAT rate and tax amount; invoices and VAT-accounting records are filed and retained digitally and VAT returns are submitted exclusively through the OTA e-portal. The OTA has publicly announced a phased roadmap toward mandatory structured electronic invoicing (e-invoicing) with API-integrated reporting; taxpayers in the wholesale electronic-components distribution sector are advised to adopt VAT-compliant ERP/EDI e-invoice exchange as a future-proofing measure.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is de jure and de facto fully convertible on the current account, with no quantitative restrictions on trade-related payments, service transactions, dividend remittances, or profit repatriation by foreign-owned entities. The OMR is pegged to the US Dollar (USD) at a central parity of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD, with a symmetric intervention band of ±0.175%, effectively eliminating currency convertibility risk for USD-denominated treasury operations. The capital account is broadly liberalised for inbound foreign direct investment (FDI), but all cross-border financial flows must be executed through CBO-licensed authorized dealers (Omani commercial banks). Prior CBO approval is required only for resident borrowing abroad and for non-resident issuance of Omani financial instruments, not for routine FDI capital injections or trade settlement. Profit remittance hurdles are minimal: the company must possess valid commercial registration, tax clearance from the Oman Tax Authority (where applicable), and a completed e-statistical declaration form submitted to the National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI) for each outward remittance exceeding OMR 10,000.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds to the Central Bank and requires no specified statutory conversion ratio. Exporters are permitted to retain export earnings in foreign currency accounts maintained with Omani banks, provided the transactions are routed through CBO-authorized dealers. However, all export receipts from electronic inventory distribution must be recorded in the E-Commerce and Trade Valued Added Tax system (TAS) and must be fully traceable through the commercial banking channel to satisfy CBO, AML/CFT, and tax audit requirements. There is no prescribed time limit for repatriation, yet for transfer pricing and corporate tax reporting, export proceeds should be recorded no later than the month-end after shipment is evidenced by an Airway Bill or Bill of Lading.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), the sole apex monetary and foreign exchange authority exercising regulatory jurisdiction over all banking, clearing, and external payment operations within the Sultanate of Oman.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Regulatory Prohibitive. Offshore Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Airwallex are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman and cannot open or hold resident corporate bank accounts or issue Omani IBANs. They may be used purely as a pre-funded payment card provider for low-volume administrative spend, but they cannot serve as a primary clearing rail for trade settlement, import payments, or FX conversion for an Omani SPC. Any physical or virtual account operated by an unlicensed EMI for Oman-incorporated entities would constitute unlicensed banking, a criminal offense under the Omani Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000). Thus, the SPC must maintain all cross-border liquidity and trade settlement accounts with licensed Omani banks or their licensed overseas branches.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the foreign shareholder to inject share capital into the Oman SPC, the remittance must originate from the beneficial owner's international bank account and arrive via SWIFT MT103 to the SPC's capital escrow account at an Omani authorized dealer. The payment must be denominated in USD, EUR, or GBP, and the instructing bank must append an unambiguous purpose code corresponding to 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' (BCP6X) under the CBO Balance of Payments manual. The Oman receiving bank will book the credit as a capital inflow and will issue a Foreign Investment Registration Certificate (FIRC) on request, which is required for subsequent transfer pricing documentation and for the issuance of shares to the foreign investor. The SPC must within 14 days file a Foreign Direct Investment Survey with the National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI) and, if the capital injection exceeds OMR 1,000,000, notify the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for statistic and economic impact review. Verification is completed by matching the SHWIFI reference (SWIFT Sender Reference) with the bank's SWIFT FIN GFP1 message; the source of funds evidence is retained under CBO circular BM/1120/07/2015 for a minimum of 5 years.All supplier payments for the procurement of capacitors, resistors, and other electronic components must be executed via one of the following CBO-compliant clearing rails. For payments below USD 200,000 and standard goods, banks require SWIFT MT103 (Customer Credit Transfer) with a valid purpose code (BCP7Y 'Trade Credit – Payment for Goods') and a copy of the endorsed commercial invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, and packing list. For settlements exceeding USD 200,000, the preferred rail is a Commercial Letter of Credit (Irrevocable, Confirmed, or Unconfirmed) opened at the request of the Omani SPC in favor of the foreign supplier, typically full-at-sight (L/C at Sight) to maximize supplier confidence; the expiring period must not exceed 6 months to comply with CBO's clean payment guidelines. The LC application must attach the underlying purchase order and proforma invoice; the issuing bank will debit the SPC's operational account for margin funds, or, if the SPC holds an undrawn credit line, the bank will allocate a dedicated line of credit for the trade bill. For urgent high-frequency consignments, banks may accept a Documentary Collection (DP/DA) but this is not recommended due to the high counterparty risk of passive electronic inventory. All outbound trade payments are scrutinized against the Omani Custom's Single Administrative Document (SAD) and the Ministry of Commerce's TAS system; failure to match shipment value within 20% error triggers automatic filing of a Suspicious Transaction Report (STR).Outright FX Forward (USD/OMR) with licensed Omani banks: a mandatory tool for fixing the conversion rate on future USD-denominated payments or receipts, given the OMR/USD peg, with maturities up to 12 months; no CBO approval required for forwards covering trade flows.FX Swap (USD/OMR) – a simultaneous spot and forward transaction allowing treasury to manage overnight liquidity mismatches and ensure the SPC does not carry an unhedged OMR exposure beyond regulatory limits.Cross-Currency Swap (EUR/USD or GBP/USD plus OMR/USD leg) – permitted for hedging foreign-currency-denominated supplier payables when economically justified; must be documented under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement with a CBO-licensed dealer.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) for OMR are not permissible in practice; the official exchange rate is set by CBO and there is no offshore OMR deliverable market. Therefore all OMR hedging must be executed through deliverable forwards onshore.Commodity Murabaha (Tawaruq Islamic Hedging) – permissible Shari'a-compliant alternative for Omani Islamic banks (Bank Sohar, Nizwa Bank) to hedge currency exposures without conventional derivatives, based on the OIC Fiqh Academy standards.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid by an Omani company to non-resident shareholders, unless reduced under an applicable double taxation agreement (DTA). Dividends paid to resident shareholders are not subject to withholding tax.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 (approx. USD 2,600,000) in aggregate related-party transactions during a tax year; if exceeded, the taxpayer must prepare a Local File and a Master File in accordance with the Oman Transfer Pricing Guidelines (based on the OECD TP Guidelines). The threshold applies separately to controlled transactions in goods, services, interest, and intangibles.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not levy a separate Economic Substance Regulation filing akin to the UAE; however, the Tax Procedure Law, anti-avoidance rules, and the substance-over-form doctrine require all taxpayers to demonstrate real economic activities in Oman. A passive distribution SPC with no local staff, no premises, and no actual risk-bearing is exposed to tax recharacterisation, denial of treaty benefits, and assessments under the general anti-abuse provisions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years and/or a fine between OMR 5,000 and OMR 50,000; additional penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax; publication of the judgment in a local daily newspaper.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are jointly and severally liable to pay the evaded tax, penalties, and interest; travel bans, asset freezes, and criminal imprisonment may be imposed on officers who ratified the evasive conduct.
Shell Company / Sham Structure

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, recharacterisation of the entire structure, disallowance of loss carryforwards, full assessment of tax at 15% with back interest and fines up to OMR 10,000, and potential criminal referral for fraud.

Director personal liability: Civil restitution of all avoided taxes; directors may be disqualified from serving as directors in Oman for up to 5 years, subject to criminal prosecution if the sham was intentional.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment upward to the arm's length result; additional tax plus late payment interest; penalty of 10% of the adjusted amount, or OMR 500 per month for non-submission of documentation up to OMR 5,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign incorrect TP documentation may be personally liable for the additional tax and penalties assessed against the SPC, and can be barred from holding board positions in Oman.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 50,000 per violation, seizure of the goods or equivalent value, revocation of the commercial licence, and criminal referral under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who facilitated trade-based money laundering are subject to imprisonment up to 7 years and fines up to OMR 100,000; personal assets are subject to confiscation.
