Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies Registration Department, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP), Sultanate of Oman, acting through the Invest Easy One-Stop-Shop at Muscat.

Governing statute

Royal Decree 18/2019 Promulgating the Companies Law, Articles 208-217 (Single Person Company SPC); Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019); Commercial Register Law (Royal Decree 3/1974 as amended); Executive Regulations of the Companies Law (MCIIP Ministerial Decision 108/2020).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Incorporation of a Single Person Company with authenticated bilingual (Arabic/English) Articles of Association and Statement of Capital, filed on the Invest Easy e-Portal (Form SPC-INC-02).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 28 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; the sole founder must determine, fully subscribe, and deposit the stated capital into the company's corporate bank account, furnishing either a banker's capital confirmation or a sworn capital declaration at incorporation; capital adequacy must be demonstrated for the ongoing data center lease and operational commitments of the colocation facility.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance from CDAA, compliant with NFPA 75 for computer rooms and NFPA 2001 for clean-agent suppression in server halls, including emergency lighting, exits, and combustible load control; structural load certification for raised access floors issued by a licensed structural engineer; occupational workplace inspection covering server hall ventilation, humidity control, fire extinguisher placement, first-aid provisions, and employee safety signage prior to staff occupation and commercial operation.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; the sole founder must determine, fully subscribe, and deposit the stated capital into the company's corporate bank account, furnishing either a banker's capital confirmation or a sworn capital declaration at incorporation; capital adequacy must be demonstrated for the ongoing data center lease and operational commitments of the colocation facility.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Head Office Muscat — national tax authority administering Corporate Income Tax (CIT), VAT, Excise Tax, and Municipal Fees. All statutory filings and registrations are made through the OTA e-Services Tax Portal (taxportal.gov.om).

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the SPC with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); obtain Commercial Registration (CR), Articles of Association, and Chamber of Commerce certificate. (2) File the OTA 'Tax Payer Registration' application on the Tax Portal, attaching the CR, constitutional documents, power of attorney, foreign shareholder passport/certificate of incorporation, and Omani fiscal residency/address evidence; the OTA issues a unique Tax File Number (TFN). (3) Apply separately for VAT registration on the Tax Portal after assessing 12-month prospective taxable supplies; the OTA issues a 15-digit VAT Registration Number (TRN) and the taxpayer becomes a regular VAT registrant. (4) If exporting goods, obtain an Oman Customs registration and interact with the Bayan single-window for export declarations; appoint own or brokerage customs declarant. (5) For data-center colocation, complete any sectoral requirements (e.g., TRA/telecom license, municipal permits) before or concurrent with tax registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Omani SPC is a separate juridical person; taxed at national CIT at 15% on adjusted taxable income; foreign-source income is subject to CIT only when remitted to Oman, giving a territorial/remittance hybrid; no pass-through or transparency election).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or fiscal-transparency election exists for SPCs; an SPC is always taxed at entity level. Available elections: (a) CIT tax grouping/consolidation between a resident parent and a sufficiently controlled (commonly 100%) resident subsidiary; (b) VAT Tax Group registration allowing related persons to file a single VAT return; (c) VAT partial-exemption/special apportionment methods for input VAT recovery; (d) annual accounting and cash-accounting options where prescribed by the VAT Executive Regulations; and (e) Pillar Two qualified domestic minimum top-up tax (DMTT) rules for in-scope MNE groups from 2025.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: taxable supplies exceeding OMR 500,000 in any 12-month period (or expected to exceed OMR 500,000 in the next 12 months). Voluntary registration: taxable supplies or qualifying expenses exceeding OMR 50,000 in 12 months. Non-resident suppliers of digital/B2B services have no threshold; the Omani recipient bears reverse-charge VAT, and a non-resident may register directly for a TRN.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: physical export from Oman to outside the GCC within the statutory period (OTA guidance generally requires removal within 90 days of supply), supported by a customs export declaration, transport documents, and receipt of proceeds. Services: zero-rating is limited to international/related transport and auxiliary services; general export of services is zero-rated only where the recipient is a non-resident not registered in Oman and the benefit is not enjoyed in Oman. Data-center colocation/rack-space and bare-metal hosting are place-of-supply-connected services (servers located in Oman), so the Omani SPC must charge 5% VAT on colocation fees unless a specific treaty/GCC exception is satisfied; zero-rating is therefore not ordinarily available for colocation revenue. Export-credit evidence (customs proof, B/L, IRC) must be retained for every zero-rated supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration validated by Oman Customs (Bayan single-window reference)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / international consignment note evidencing export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing FX receipt within 180 days
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List matching the zero-rated supply
  • Customs Export Proof / gate-out certificate from the port or bonded facility
  • Certificate of Origin or export license/approval for controlled goods, where applicable
  • Insurance certificate or exporter's declaration confirming delivery outside Oman

No mandatory real-time fiscal electronic-invoicing regime is operational in Oman as of 2025. Tax-regulated taxpayers must issue standard VAT tax invoices/credit notes carrying mandatory fields; filing and VAT clearance are processed through the OTA Tax Portal, not via API-based government stamping. Oman Customs operates the 'Bayan' single-window EDI platform for export/import declarations; digital customs declarations carry a customs reference number and are mandatory for zero-rating.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions. OMR is pegged to the US Dollar within a narrow band of 0.3845 to 0.3855 OMR per USD. Foreign investors may hold 100% equity in an Oman SPC under the Foreign Capital Investment Law. Dividend remittances, loan repayments, service fees, and liquidation proceeds are freely transferable; practical hurdles are limited to corporate compliance such as audited financial statements, tax clearance from the Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority, and valid Commercial Register certificates.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists under Omani law. Exporters and service providers may retain foreign currency cash balances in offshore and onshore foreign currency accounts opened with CBO-licensed banks. CBO regulations impose routine reporting obligations for large transactions, but no mandatory conversion ratio or repatriation deadline. For data-center colocation services, invoicing is typically in USD with settlement through correspondent banking and no conversion obligation.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – Banking Supervision and Foreign Exchange Control. CBO is the apex monetary authority, responsible for issuing the Omani Rial (OMR), managing the USD peg band, licensing banks, and supervising all inward/outward remittance and foreign exchange operations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low – Offshore digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) may not operate directly in Oman without a CBO license and physical registered branch. They cannot act as the primary treasury settlement rail; at best they may provide contractor invoicing interfaces through a locally licensed payment service provider or under a CBO regulatory sandbox. For a colocation business requiring stable USD clearing, a CBO-licensed bank is mandatory.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injection must be routed as a SWIFT MT103 wire from the foreign shareholder's bank to the SPC's CBO-licensed account using transaction narrative 'Capital Subscription / Direct Equity Injection under Oman Commercial Companies Law'. Prior documentation includes updated Commercial Register extract, certified shareholder resolution, no-objection from the registry (if required), and the SPC's bank account details with SWIFT BIC. The remitting bank must state the purpose as 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital'. If the injection is in USD to a foreign-currency account, no FX conversion is triggered; if OMR is required, the receiving bank will execute conversion at the CBO interbank rate within the USD/UOM peg band.Cross-border operational payments are cleared through SWIFT MT103 for supplier rent, bandwidth, hardware, and service contracts. For physical equipment imports involving a beneficiary-issued documentary collection, a SWIFT MT700/MT707 letter of credit (sight or usance) is used against shipping documents. Local utility and domestic settlements are cleared via Oman's real-time gross settlement and automated clearing house using the Operational Account. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose any dividend withholding tax on distributions made by an Omani company to its shareholders, whether resident or non-resident. Dividend remittances to the foreign sole shareholder are therefore made free of Oman WHT, though the dividend must be sourced from after-tax profits.

Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.

Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.

Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.

Allocations from the foreign group must be supported by cost-sharing agreements; otherwise may be disallowed as non-deductible. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 6 - Gross Profit Gross revenue less cost of sales, reconciled to audited financial statements. The non-resident owner must ensure the audited statements are Oman-specific and not merely a consolidation stub. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 7 - Other Income Interest on deposits, exchange gains, insurance recoveries, and incidental support-service fees. Foreign-source interest/gains are taxable if effectively connected to the Oman PE or remitted to the SPC's Oman account. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 8 - Deductible Operating Expenses Facility rent, depreciation on servers and cooling equipment, utilities, insurance, professional fees, and employee costs. Head-office management fees and IT charges from the foreign parent are deductible only at arm's length and with valid documentation; withholding tax may apply on services rendered in Oman. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 9 - Taxable Income Before Loss Relief Gross profit plus other income less deductible operating expenses, after statutory add-backs. Non-commercial payments to related parties must be added back if not substantiated by TP documentation and actual service delivery. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 10 - Loss Carryforward Utilized Unabsorbed tax losses from prior years, limited to 5 years from the year of loss. Losses are available only if the SPC filed timely returns for the loss years; change of ownership by the non-resident shareholder may restrict utilization under anti-avoidance rules. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 11 - Net Taxable Income Taxable income after loss relief; nil if below the OMR 30,000 exemption threshold. The exemption threshold applies at the entity level; it cannot be split or allocated to the foreign shareholder. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 12 - Tax Computation 15% of net taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000; rounded to the nearest OMR. No special rate applies to a foreign-owned SPC; the 15% rate is uniform for resident companies. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 13 - Credits and Prepayments Any advance CIT payments, final withholding tax credits, or foreign tax credits claimed. Foreign tax credits are available only under an applicable tax treaty with the shareholder's residence jurisdiction and require a valid TRC. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.
Box 14 - Net Tax Payable or Refund Balance of tax due after credits, payable through the e-Services portal by the filing deadline. The foreign shareholder must ensure settlement via Oman-approved payment channels; unpaid balances accrue a 1% monthly delay surcharge. Late filing attracts a fixed discretionary penalty of OMR 100 to OMR 1,000. Late payment of assessed tax accrues a delay surcharge of 1% per month (prorated daily). Failure to file after formal notice may trigger an additional assessment penalty of 25% of the tax due and criminal referral for repeated delinquency.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File documentation applies to taxpayers with annual turnover exceeding OMR 50 million, or where related-party transactions exceed the thresholds set out in the Executive Regulations of the new Corporate Income Tax Law. A Country-by-Country Report (CbCR) is required for MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least OMR 375 million in the preceding fiscal year. Even below these thresholds, the SPC must maintain contemporaneous arm's length records for intercompany colocation pricing, management fees, and cost-sharing arrangements to defend the return.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not currently maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute of the type used in the UAE. However, the general anti-abuse rule (GAAR) and substance-over-form provisions in Royal Decree 50/2022 require the SPC to demonstrate genuine commercial substance in Oman. For a data center colocation operator, this means local decision-making, employees on Oman payroll, physical infrastructure (servers, cooling, power), operating expenditure in Oman, and intra-group contracts executed with real local management. Failure to demonstrate substance triggers recharacterization, denial of treaty benefits, and penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Returns

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the evaded tax plus a 1% monthly delay surcharge; a fine of up to OMR 50,000; imprisonment of up to 5 years for responsible officers under the criminal provisions of Royal Decree 50/2022 (under Royal Decree 28/2009 the fine ranged from OMR 500 to OMR 5,000 with imprisonment up to 3 years).

Director personal liability: Directors and the foreign sole shareholder who knowingly participate in the evasion face personal criminal liability, civil restitution for the unpaid tax, asset freezes, travel bans, and imprisonment. The corporate veil may be pierced where the SPC is stripped of assets to render enforcement futile.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Application of the GAAR to recharacterize or disregard the structure; disallowance of intercompany deductions; re-attribution of profits to Oman and taxation at 15%; denial of treaty benefits and exemption thresholds; late-payment surcharges on the reassessed tax.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial owner may be jointly and severally assessed for the recharacterized tax; the tax authority may issue travel bans against directors for unpaid assessments and pursue personal assets where the company has no executable assets in Oman.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Base Erosion

Statutory sanction: Arm's length price adjustment with additional tax at 15%; an accuracy-related penalty for understated tax (up to 25% of the understated tax, or higher for deliberate distortions); monthly delay surcharges; non-filing of CbCR triggers separate fixed penalties under the Executive Regulations.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or sign distorted intercompany agreements face joint personal liability for the adjusted tax and penalties; repeated abuse may result in criminal prosecution for tax evasion and disqualification from serving as directors in Oman.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Fines of OMR 1,000 to OMR 20,000 per violation under the Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016, as amended), escalating to OMR 50,000 for repeat or aggravated breaches; freezing of bank accounts; suspension or cancellation of the CR; possible criminal prosecution for money laundering facilitation.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally subject to fines, disqualification, and imprisonment where laundering is knowingly facilitated through the data center infrastructure; the Omani courts may order confiscation of the servers and equipment and impose travel bans pending resolution of the proceedings.
