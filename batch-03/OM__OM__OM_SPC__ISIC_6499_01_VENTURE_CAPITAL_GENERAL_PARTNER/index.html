Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Registry Department, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), Sultanate of Oman - single-window 'Invest Easy' e-portal (investeasy.gov.om).

Governing statute

Oman Companies Law, Royal Decree No. 18/2019 (effective 1 January 2020), Chapter on the Single Person Company (SPC; one shareholder permitted, may be a foreign legal entity) read with the LLC governance, registered office, and manager liability articles mutatis mutandis; Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2019 (100% foreign ownership); Commercial Register Law, Royal Decree No. 3/1975, as amended; Securities Law, Royal Decree No. 80/2003, and CMA Investment Funds Rules for the regulated venture capital GP fund-management scope.

Formation / constitutional filings

Electronic Commercial Registration Application (Form CR-01) with notarized Arabic Memorandum and Articles of Association (MoA/AoA), culminating in the issuance of the Commercial Register Extract (As-Sijil At-Tijari) and unified municipal license rider.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 20 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; declared share capital is fixed in the MoA (commonly OMR 1,000-10,000 for a GP management vehicle). No escrow account regime applies; however, the sole shareholder must evidence subscription through a capital confirmation letter or bank certificate from a licensed Omani bank (temporary 'company under incorporation' account) annexed to the notarized MoA. Upon application for the CMA fund-management license, the CMA may impose an own-funds/regulatory capital floor (typically circa OMR 100,000) per CMA capital-adequacy guidance, which must be remitted to the company's operating account before fund solicitation commences.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety and Civil Defense Clearance certificate from the Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) following inspection of fire detection, alarm, and extinguishing systems; structural load certificate for the leased office premises issued by a licensed structural engineer applying the Omani Building Code (SBC 201); and Occupational Safety and Health clearance under the Labor Law (RD 35/2003), covering emergency signage, first-aid provisioning, workspace ergonomics, and submission of the OSH declaration to the Ministry of Labor.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; declared share capital is fixed in the MoA (commonly OMR 1,000-10,000 for a GP management vehicle). No escrow account regime applies; however, the sole shareholder must evidence subscription through a capital confirmation letter or bank certificate from a licensed Omani bank (temporary 'company under incorporation' account) annexed to the notarized MoA. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Ministry of Finance; e-services portal: Taxomania (https://taxomania.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entity must first obtain a Commercial Register (CR) license from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; thereafter register with the OTA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting certified constitutional documents, passport copies of shareholders, articles of association, and proof of Omani registered address. VAT registration is a separate process on the Taxomania portal and is mandatory if annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000; voluntary registration is available from OMR 100,000.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign entity must first obtain a Commercial Register (CR) license from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; thereafter register with the OTA for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting certified constitutional documents, passport copies of shareholders, articles of association, and proof of Omani registered address. VAT registration is a separate process on the Taxomania portal and is mandatory if annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000; voluntary registration is available from OMR 100,000.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or flow-through election exists under Oman tax law; an SPC is taxed as a distinct legal entity. Permitted elections are limited to VAT administrative choices: monthly vs quarterly filing periods, annual accounting scheme, and margin scheme for certain supply categories. Das hybrid mismatch elections are not part of Oman's domestic tax framework.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration if taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000 in any 12-month consecutive period; voluntary registration threshold OMR 100,000 (VAT Executive Regulations Art. 22)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Oman VAT law zero-rates: (i) goods physically exported out of Oman to a non-GCC destination, provided the export occurs within 90 days of supply and is supported by a customs export declaration; and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident where the direct benefit is enjoyed outside Oman and the service falls within the zero-rated categories of VAT Act Art. 30 (e.g., transportation, insurance, logistics support to exports). An SPC must hold a valid TIN and maintain contemporaneous evidence before claiming zero rating on its VAT return.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Export Contract / Service Agreement

As of 2025, Oman has not introduced a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance or API stamping mandate. Tax invoices must be issued in Arabic and English, contain all mandatory VAT fields, and be retained electronically for five years. Digital XML/PDF issuance is accepted; a phased future e-invoicing mandate is under exploratory consultation, but no technical clearance gateway is operative for taxpayers today.

Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating triggers an immediate output tax assessment at 5%, a late-payment penalty of 1% per month, an additional tax penalty up to 25% of the tax difference under OTA audit procedures, and criminal liability for tax evasion carrying imprisonment up to three years and fines up to OMR 100,000. Retrospective clawback is permitted without limitation periods where misrepresentation is proven, and future VAT refunds may be suspended pending repatriation verification.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current-account transactions, consistent with Oman's acceptance of IMF Article VIII obligations in 1974; the official peg is fixed at 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR. Capital-account transactions are substantially liberalized, and the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019) permits 100% foreign ownership of the Single Person Company (SPC) together with unrestricted repatriation of invested capital, dividends, and profits, subject to production of a valid OTA tax-clearance certificate where required, the SPC's Commercial Registration (CR), and compliance with CMA licensing conditions. No prior CBO approval or currency rationing applies to bona fide equity injections, capital calls, or profit remittances executed through a CBO-licensed bank with the correct purpose code.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender ratio and no fixed conversion deadline; exporters may retain 100% of export receipts in foreign-currency accounts with licensed banks. The binding obligations are limited to (i) repatriating proceeds through the domestic banking system within a commercially reasonable period consistent with underlying trade terms, (ii) enabling CBO regulatory reporting of the receivable, and (iii) utilizing a licensed bank for conversion when OMR settlement is required. The mandatory conversion ratio is effectively 0% (full foreign-currency retention is permitted).

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) in coordination with the Capital Market Authority (CMA) for fund-management licensing and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) for tax-clearance requirements affecting capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital EMI and neobank providers that do not hold a CBO payment-service-provider license cannot lawfully maintain OMR-denominated settlement accounts or deliver treasury services to an Omani SPC. Foreign EMIs may only serve as supplementary disbursement channels where underlying funds ultimately clear through a CBO-licensed bank; the SPC must maintain its primary capital-call, escrow, and operational accounts with a licensed Omani bank. The controlling legal constraints are the CBO payment-services regulatory framework, which mandates a locally incorporated licensed entity, and Oman's Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism Law (Royal Decree 30/2016), which prohibits unlicensed money transmission.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and capital-call wires must be routed as SWIFT MT103 messages from each LP's bank to the SPC's CBO-licensed account, quoting the prescribed inbound purpose code for direct investment or equity participation in a locally incorporated licensed entity under the CBO balance-of-payments classification for nonresident direct investment. The receiving bank will require a purpose-of-transfer declaration, the SPC's Commercial Registration and CMA license references, and a board resolution authenticating the capital call. Funds must be channeled exclusively through correspondent banks with USD or OMR settlement capability; the escrow account must be pre-notified, and each payment instruction must identify the investing LP, the Fund tranche, and the wording 'Capital Contribution' to prevent misclassification as a loan or unlicensed money-service activity.Operational disbursements, management fees, and carried-interest payments clear via SWIFT MT103 customer transfers, with MT202 bank-to-bank cover where interbank settlement is required; underlying mandates must state a documented reason for remittance. For trade-related settlements, commercial letters of credit governed by UCP 600 and documentary collections governed by URC 522 are the standard clearing rails, issued or confirmed through Bank Muscat, HSBC Bank Oman, or other top-tier banks with active LC confirmation lines. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% statutory standard rate on taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000; 3% reduced rate on taxable income up to OMR 30,000

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on dividends distributed by an Omani-resident SPC to non-resident shareholders. No withholding applies to dividends paid to Omani resident corporate shareholders or on distributions regarded as capital returns for accounting purposes.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed within four months after the end of the taxable financial year. For a standard 31 December year-end SPC, the statutory deadline is 30 April of the following calendar year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 100,000 aggregate annual cross-border related-party transactions triggers an obligation to prepare a contemporaneous Local File. A Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 25 million and cross-border related-party transaction value exceeds OMR 1 million. Country-by-Country reporting applies at the OMR 100 million consolidated group revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to UAE ESR. However, the Oman Tax Authority applies general anti-avoidance rules, substance-over-form, and the OECD BEPS Principal Purpose Test. A venture capital GP SPC must maintain real physical presence in Oman, employ qualified investment professionals, hold substantive board meetings and make key management decisions in Oman. Failure to demonstrate this may result in denial of treaty benefits, re-characterisation of carried interest as service income, or treatment as a shell entity for tax purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Corporate Income Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: The Oman Tax Authority will reassess the taxpayer with full tax arrears, a penalty of up to 100% of the tax under-assessed, a late payment surcharge, interest at standard government rate, and potential criminal prosecution for tax fraud with fines up to OMR 100,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and mandated managers who approve false returns are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax; civil restitution, asset seizure, travel bans and imprisonment of up to 5 years may be imposed under Omani tax law and the Penal Code.
Shell Company and Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty withholding benefits, re-characterisation of dividends or carried interest as taxable Omani-source income, 10% final withholding tax, and an administrative penalty of 20% for misrepresentation. Repeated violations can lead to commercial registration cancellation and strike-off.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who merely rubber-stamp fund documentation face civil liability for the full tax undercharge; courts may issue travel bans during tax investigations, personal fines, and, in egregious cases, orders disqualifying directors from serving as managers of any Omani company.
Transfer Pricing and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may adjust taxable income by applying the arm's length principle, disallow related-party deductions, impose a documentation penalty up to OMR 10,000, and add a secondary penalty of 10%-20% of the additional tax resulting from the TP adjustment.

Director personal liability: Finance directors and CFOs who certify inaccurate transfer pricing documentation are personally liable for penalties and additional tax if their declarations caused the underassessment; disqualification from directorship and personal fines may be imposed.
AML, KYC and Sanctions Non-Compliance in Capital Calls

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's AML/CFT Law and Central Bank of Oman regulations, the SPC may be fined up to OMR 100,000, subjected to freezing orders, suspension of the venture capital fund licence, compulsory restructuring, and winding-up.

Director personal liability: The Money Laundering Reporting Officer (MLRO) and each director who knowingly facilitates a breach are personally criminally liable; sanctions include imprisonment up to 7 years, personal fines equal to the funds laundered, travel bans and disqualification from managing financial vehicles.
