Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) – Commercial Registry; Invest in Oman One-Stop Shop.

Governing statute

Royal Decree 18/2019 (New Companies Law), as amended; Articles 57–70 (Single Person Company), Article 5 (foreign participation), Article 13 (registered office), Article 27 (manager appointment) and Article 44 (commercial registration).

Formation / constitutional filings

Single Person Company Contract (Model Articles of Association) executed by the sole shareholder, notarized in Arabic, and accompanied by the Commercial Registration Application (Form CR-01).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 23 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a foreign-owned SPC; authorized capital is fixed in the Articles, must be fully subscribed, and paid-up capital must be deposited in a temporary bank account in Oman before the incorporation application is submitted.

Funding & unlock path: The registered office must obtain a civil defense fire safety certificate from the Royal Oman Police Civil Defense for the leased premises. Structural load inspections are not applicable for office use; if racks or mezzanine for freight documentation are installed, a structural verification certificate must be appended to the municipal license application. Workplace clearance from the Ministry of Labor is required for any employees physically present.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a foreign-owned SPC; authorized capital is fixed in the Articles, must be fully subscribed, and paid-up capital must be deposited in a temporary bank account in Oman before the incorporation application is submitted.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA; Arabic: الهيئة الضريبية العمانية), an independent legal personality established by Royal Decree No. 37/2020 and operating under the Omani Ministry of Finance; e-services portal 'Mwasal' (https://mwasal.tax.gov.om) with public information at https://taxoman.om.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) via the 'Invest Easy' platform and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. Step 2: Open a resident Omani corporate bank account in the SPC's name. Step 3: Within 30 days of CR issuance or commencement of business, file the initial corporate tax registration application through OTA's Mwasal portal attaching the CR, the SPC's Articles of Association/Memorandum and SPC formation deed, passport/ID copies of the sole shareholder and the SPC manager, the registered-office lease, and a bank account confirmation; OTA then issues the permanent Income Tax Registration Number (TIN). Step 4: Separately apply for VAT registration in the same Mwasal account when taxable supplies meet the OMR 38,500 mandatory threshold (or opt for voluntary registration from OMR 19,250); the VAT certificate and VAT Registration Number (VRN) are issued electronically. Step 5: Register with Oman Customs (via the integrated customs/tax clearance platform) as an exporter and obtain a customs identifier linked to the CR for freight-forwarding export declarations.

Step 5: Register with Oman Customs (via the integrated customs/tax clearance platform) as an exporter and obtain a customs identifier linked to the CR for freight-forwarding export declarations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

None material: Oman has no US-style 'check-the-box' classification and no hybrid-mismatch/set-off election; the SPC is mandatorily classified as a corporate taxable person for income tax. The available elections are VAT-level only: (a) voluntary VAT registration once annual taxable supplies reach OMR 19,250; (b) VAT tax-group election whereby 100%-commonly-owned related parties may be treated as a single taxable person subject to OTA approval; and (c) cash-basis (payments) accounting for output VAT in limited business cases. No consolidated or group-loss relief election exists under the Income Tax Law for SPCs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (approx. USD 100,000) of cumulative annual taxable supplies for mandatory registration; voluntary registration permitted at OMR 19,250 (approx. USD 50,000); intermodal freight-forwarding services with export reliance are within the standard regime without sector-specific exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% under Articles 45–46 of the VAT Law applies to: (i) export of goods outside the GCC provided the goods physically leave Oman within 90 days from the date of supply (or within the extended period approved by OTA) and are covered by an electronic customs export declaration; (ii) international transportation of goods by sea, air or land, including intermodal Eurasian-rail freight forwarding, and ancillary services (handling, warehousing, trans-shipment) supplied in connection with such international carriage; and (iii) supplies of transit goods and services where the direct foreign-currency benefit is enjoyed outside Oman. The SPC must hold a valid VRN, issue a zero-rated tax invoice, and secure documented evidence of physical export (departure from Omani customs territory) before the refund can be recognised.

  • Electronic Oman Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) showing export clearance, HS codes and net mass, matched to the invoice line items
  • Multimodal Through Bill of Lading or CIM/SMGS railway consignment note evidencing dispatch from the Omani port/intermodal terminal to the foreign destination
  • Commercial invoice, packing list and the underlying freight-forwarding contract (ISIC 5229 scope) issued in the SPC's name
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 evidencing the foreign buyer's payment in convertible currency for the expedited rail freight services
  • Letter of Credit (for LC payment flows) with complying presentation records, and confirmation that title/insurable risk passed outside Oman
  • VAT zero-rated tax invoice (XML e-invoice) and any export certificate generated in the Mwasal customs/tax integration report

Oman is executing a phased e-invoicing clearance mandate under the VAT Executive Regulations: registered persons must issue structured e-tax invoices (XML/JSON) containing the OTA-mandated invoice data fields and a machine-readable digital (QR/PDF-417) code. The Mwasal platform requires API- or portal-based transmission of invoice data; B2G suppliers, high-revenue taxpayers and VAT groups are already subject to mandatory system-to-system clearance, while the remaining registered population is being onboarded in revenue tranches through 2024–2025. Non-integrated taxpayers risk input-VAT deduction denial, turnover-based penalties and delayed export refunds.

Export zero-rating claims are high-priority audit targets. The OTA will claw back the 0% treatment and assess VAT at 5% on the invoice value where the goods fail to leave Oman within the 90-day export period, the customs export declaration does not match the invoice, or the foreign-currency proceeds are not repatriated through Oman's banking system within 180 days. Evasion with intent (fake declarations, fabricated LCs, circular re-imports) triggers Articles 51–52 of the VAT Law: a penalty of up to OMR 15,000 per violation and/or imprisonment of up to 5 years, plus a fixed penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax and a monthly late-payment charge on unpaid tax. Separate tax liabilities arise under Article 36 of the Income Tax Law (25% additional tax for understatement increasing to 100% for fraud), and CR suspension of the SPC may be imposed. Refund entitlement is suspended and the statutory refund limitation period may be extended throughout the fraud investigation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible on the current account and generally unencumbered on the capital account; no exchange-control approval is required for ordinary trade or investment flows. The Rial is pegged to the USD at 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR official mid-rate, with a trading band of ±0.15%. Profit remittance hurdles are administrative only: audited financial statements, up-to-date tax filing and a tax clearance certificate must be presented to the remitting bank. Withholding tax at 10% applies to interest, royalties and technical/service fees paid to non-residents; dividends distributed by an Omani SPC are exempt from withholding tax. Capital repatriation is permitted upon liquidation or share transfer, subject to settlement of all tax liabilities and CBO balance-of-payments reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement, conversion quota, or mandatory repatriation timeline applies in Oman. Freight-forwarding and logistics service revenue denominated in foreign currency may be retained in CBO-licensed bank accounts or converted freely at the prevailing interbank/PEP rate. All receipts, however, remain subject to CBO sanctions-screening and trade-based money laundering monitoring, and receipts originating from sanctioned adjacent jurisdictions are withheld pending compliance review.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), with coordinated clearance from the Oman Tax Authority for profit remittance and the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) for FDI registration and capital account reporting.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary clearing venue. Offshore digital EMIs and payments fintechs are not CBO-licensed and cannot hold OMR settlement accounts or execute local clearing. Their viable role is supplementary: as foreign-currency collection/payout adjuncts for receivables from non-Omani shippers, with the CBO-licensed bank remaining the sole repository for capital, FX conversion, and trade settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing via SWIFT MT103 (shareholder-to-company) into the SPC's Capital Escrow Account at a CBO-licensed bank, with purpose code 'Capit' (FDI equity contribution) and the CBO-issued FDI reporting number placed in the customer reference field. Supporting documents required: CR, MOCIIP investment license, owners' capital-increase resolution, and proof of source of funds. The receiving bank will run sanctions screening and tag the credit as an inward direct investment for CBO balance-of-payments statistics. Capital may be held in OMR or foreign currency at the company's election.Primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer for rail haulage, traction charges, leasing of wagons, container deposits, and intermediate logistics fees to operators across Eurasia. For large-ticket procurements (rolling stock, container fleets, equipment), commercial letters of credit via SWIFT MT700 (sight or usance) are used. OMR-denominated domestic payments for port handling, road feeder services, and local subcontractors settle over the CBO-RAIS RTGS system. Every cross-border payment must include an ISIC 5229 business-purpose narrative, the underlying contract number, and freight documentation references to satisfy AML/TBBT review.Outright FX forward contracts (generally up to 12 months) for OMR/EUR, OMR/CNY, and OMR/INR exposures, deliverable through a CBO-licensed authorized dealer; USD/OMR forwards are unnecessary due to the fixed peg.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on restricted-convertibility regional currencies, permitted only where the SPC can evidence a real and underlying cross-border trade exposure.Cross-currency swaps (including FX swap straight-leg structures) between the USD/OMR cashflow base and EUR or CNY payment obligations, executed with a CBO-licensed bank under ISDA/CSA documentation.Plain-vanilla FX options on EUR/CNH/INR against USD/OMR, purchased for hedging confirmed freight receivables and payables, subject to bank credit-limit approval and underlying trade documentation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory when aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 1,000,000 in a tax year; Master File is mandatory when group consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 10,000,000; CbC reporting applies when group consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 100,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted a separate Economic Substance Regulations law for all domestic entities, but the Commercial Companies Law and Tax Law require every SPC to maintain a registered office, actual staff, and real management in Oman; the Oman Tax Authority may disregard artificial arrangements under anti-avoidance principles and recharacterize related-party cross-border rail transactions lacking commercial substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine up to OMR 100,000, additional 25% to 100% of the underpaid tax, seizure of assets, and imprisonment for up to 5 years under Oman Income Tax Law criminal provisions.

Director personal liability: Directors and the SPC manager may be jointly assessed for unpaid corporate tax, subjected to travel bans and asset freezes, and personally prosecuted in Oman criminal courts.
Shell Company / Sham SPC

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization by the Oman Tax Authority as a sham, denial of deductions, taxation of all worldwide or Oman-source income at the 15% rate, cancellation of tax registration, and penalties under the Commercial Companies Law.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced; directors may face unlimited civil liability for all unpaid taxes, a five-year travel ban from Oman, and director disqualification orders.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Primary TP adjustment plus late payment interest at 1.5% per month; additional penalties up to 10%-15% of the adjustment amount; failure to submit TP documentation subjects the entity to separate fines and evidentiary disadvantage.

Director personal liability: Directors and tax signatories may be held personally liable for the unpaid adjusted tax and monetary penalties, and may face criminal imprisonment for deliberate concealment of intercompany arrangements.
Money Laundering and AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Oman AML Law include fines up to OMR 100,000, freezing of accounts, confiscation of cargo proceeds, and imprisonment up to 10 years for aggravated money laundering.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners may be prosecuted individually, suffer personal seizure of personal and corporate assets, be subjected to travel bans, and receive extended custodial sentences.
