Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (formerly Secretariat General for Taxation, Ministry of Finance); primary compliance portal: taxoman.om. Corporate income tax and VAT are administered by the Tax Authority as the single national revenue body for all direct and indirect taxes.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Upon CR issuance, the SPC must file the prescribed income tax registration application (Form T01) and VAT registration application through the Tax Authority's portal. The Authority issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a VAT registration certificate. Registration is required prior to commencing commercial activity or within 60 days of CR issuance. For SPCs established in free zones, the same OTA portal is used, with additional zone-specific approvals. Failure to register is subject to administrative penalties.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation – the Omani SPC (single-member limited liability company) is fully separate juridical personality. It is taxed at corporate level on taxable income under the Income Tax Law and is the taxable person for VAT. No pass-through taxation, partnership transparency, or consolidated fiscal filing is permitted for a standard foreign-owned SPC. — A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Upon CR issuance, the SPC must file the prescribed income tax registration application (Form T01) and VAT registration application through the Tax Authority's portal. The Authority issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) and a VAT registration certificate. Registration is required prior to commencing commercial activity or within 60 days of CR issuance. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation – the Omani SPC (single-member limited liability company) is fully separate juridical personality. It is taxed at corporate level on taxable income under the Income Tax Law and is the taxable person for VAT. No pass-through taxation, partnership transparency, or consolidated fiscal filing is permitted for a standard foreign-owned SPC.

Available election forms

No US-style 'check-the-box' or hybrid mismatch election exists in Oman. Statutory entity classification is fixed at incorporation. The only election of note is the VAT Tax Group election under Article 8 of the Executive Regulations of the VAT Law, enabling two or more related legal persons to form a single taxable person for VAT purposes where each is resident and wholly owned by the same owner. Corporate income tax grouping for 100% wholly owned sub-groups is also permissible under limited conditions, but is not a discretionary flow-through election. Hybrid mismatch elections are unavailable under domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration threshold is OMR 40,000 in annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration threshold is OMR 20,000. Non-resident suppliers without a fixed place of business in Oman are required to register for VAT irrespective of the threshold when making taxable supplies to persons in Oman, unless reverse-charge applies. The threshold is calculated on projected or actual taxable supplies, exclusive of exempt supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of whole and skimmed milk powder will qualify for zero-rating (0% VAT) where the SPC, as exporter, supplies goods that are transported to a place outside the Sultanate of Oman and outside the GCC VAT-implementing territories within 90 days of the supply, or such extended period as expressly approved by the Tax Authority. In addition, the SPC must hold documentary evidence of actual physical removal from Oman, a commercial contract with a non-resident customer, and consideration received from a non-resident account. Goods supplied to VAT-registered customers in other GCC states are generally subject to reverse-charge on a 0% basis only if the conditions in the VAT Executive Regulations are satisfied. Services directly related to exporting the goods, including international transport and handling, may be zero-rated under the specified conditions.

Oman currently does not mandate real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or API-based e-invoice clearance. Taxpayers are required to issue VAT invoices (paper or electronic) containing the statutory particulars, and to submit periodic VAT returns via the taxoman.om portal. The Tax Authority has signaled a phased introduction of an e-invoicing system; however, as of the present regulatory stage, no mandatory API-filing or clearance platform is in force. Taxpayers are nevertheless required to retain machine-readable electronic records of all invoices for audit purposes.

Any false, fraudulent, or unsupported zero-rating claim exposes the SPC to: (i) immediate VAT assessment at 5% plus a penalty of 100% of the tax evaded; (ii) administrative fines up to OMR 50,000 per violation; (iii) in serious cases, a penalty equal to double the undercharged tax; (iv) clawback of the zero-rating benefit if the goods do not leave Oman within the statutory 90-day/approved period, if documentation is incomplete, or if the goods are subsequently redirected into Oman or return to Oman without customs approval; (v) suspension or cancellation of the commercial registration; and (vi) imprisonment of up to five years for willful tax evasion. The Tax Authority may issue assessments within five years from the date the return was due, and the burden of substantiating each zero-rating condition lies squarely on the exporting SPC.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible for current-account transactions and is pegged to the US Dollar at a fixed rate of OMR 0.3845 = USD 1.00, with a narrow official intervention band maintained by the CBO since 1986. There are no exchange-control authorizations required for trade-related import/export payments, service fees, interest, or profit remittance through a licensed Omani bank. The capital account is equally liberalized: a foreign-owned Omani SPC may receive inbound equity injections and shareholder loans without prior CBO approval, provided the bank records beneficiary ownership and files the transaction under the CBO's balance-of-payments reporting obligations. Dividend remittance by an SPC to a non-resident shareholder is operationally unrestricted, subject to compliance with the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009 as amended): the statutory 10% withholding tax on dividends has been suspended by successive ministerial decisions and is not currently collected, but a final tax position must be confirmed at the date of distribution with the Oman Tax Authority. Remittance banks routinely require an audited financial statement, annual tax return filing receipt, and ongoing tax-clearance status before releasing dividend/gain repatriation payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose any statutory surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline on non-hydrocarbon export proceeds. A private SPC engaged in wholesale milk-powder trading may retain export earnings in USD, EUR, or other convertible currencies in a multi-currency corporate account, and may either convert to OMR when required or redeploy the foreign currency directly for future import settlements (back-to-back trade operations). Banks must record each export credit with supporting documents (export contract, commercial invoice, full set of bills of lading, certificate of origin, and any required health/phytosanitary inspection certificates) and must file the transaction with the CBO's electronic balance-of-payments reporting gate. The Government's oil-and-gas revenue surrender rules (all hydrocarbon proceeds entering the State's designated revenue accounts) are not applicable to dairy commodity trade. Cash and cross-border declarations under the Oman AML Law apply only at statutory thresholds; the account-based export proceeds are not subject to conversion ratios.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established by Royal Decree No. 31/74 as amended, and supplemented by the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 42/2020). The CBO is the sole monetary and banking regulator, supervisor of licensed commercial banks, operator of the national RTGS clearing system, and custodian of the Omani Rial (OMR) exchange-rate peg.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-moderate direct viability. The CBO has not yet enacted a comprehensive non-bank EMI or digital-payment institution licensing regime; only banks licensed under the Banking Law may hold CBO-regulated settlement accounts, provide OMR IBANs, or access the local RTGS/ACH rails. Foreign fintech EMIs and offshore digital payment institutions (e.g., UK/EU-authorised EMIs or global multi-currency wallet platforms) cannot act as the primary corporate bank for the Omani SPC, cannot issue fully valid OMR account structures, and carry enhanced counterparty risk for AML review. A legally safe deployment is complementary: the overseas EMI may be used strictly as a disbursement-only channel for small-value supplier payments or as a pre-funded payout wallet, with all treasury funding originating from and returning to the CBO-regulated Omani bank. The SPC must maintain its authorized share capital, trade inflows, and statutory record trail within the licensed Omani banking system to satisfy substance and audit requirements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing is a SWIFT MT103 (or CHAPS/Fedwire-equivalent) from the foreign shareholder's bank to the SPC's OMR or USD corporate account maintained at the licensed Omani bank. The payment narrative must state 'equity contribution' or 'shareholder loan disbursement' and must reference the SPC Commercial Registration number. The receiving Omani bank classifies the credit under the IMF Balance of Payments Financial Account (Direct Investment) and applies standard verification: copy of the shareholder resolution, updated Company Register extract showing the sole foreign shareholder's holding, certification of tax registration, and a source-of-funds declaration. Shareholder loans additionally require a signed facility letter with clear interest rate, maturity, and subordination language. There is no CBO pre-approval, no stamp duty, and no minimum floor for the injection; funds clear in one banking day through the CBO RTGS if in OMR or through the bank's USD correspondent if in US Dollars. Amounts above OMR 25,000 are captured by the bank's CBO AML/financial-intelligence threshold reporting but do not trigger additional exchange-control review.Trade settlements execute through four legally recognized rails: (1) SWIFT MT103/M210 customer wire transfers for open-account prepayments, port charges, freight, and post-shipment balances; (2) commercial letters of credit issued by the Omani bank in favor of the milk-powder supplier, commonly regular or transferable, at sight or up to 60-90 days usance, with a document package of full set of clean on-board bills of lading, signed commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, health/phytosanitary certificate, and pre-shipment inspection certificate; (3) documentary collections under URC 522 (D/P or D/A) for repeat low-risk counterparties; and (4) open-account receivables financing (forfaiting or invoice discounting without recourse) against export contracts, bills of lading, and assignment notices where the buyer is a government-linked dairy procurement agency or global food conglomerate. OMR legs clear via the CBO RTGS; USD legs clear through correspondent arrangements (typically JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, or Bank of New York Mellon) held by the Omani bank. Mandatory Audit Threshold Mandatory Audit for All Entities

Turnover: Compulsory for every corporate taxpayer: the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009) requires all companies, including foreign-owned SPCs, to obtain audited financial statements signed by a registered auditor licensed with the Oman Association of Accountants and Auditors. There is no corporate turnover exemption; the SPC carrying on cross-border milk-powder trading is mandatorily VAT-registered above the OMR 38,500 threshold, and the audited accounts must be delivered with the annual CIT return. Small-company relief is not functionally available to a trading SPC exceeding OMR 100,000 (approximately USD 260,000) turnover or employing more than 5 persons.
