Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) – Commercial Registration and Investment Services Directorate, via the Invest Easy e-portal (www.investeasy.om).

Governing statute

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 promulgating the Commercial Companies Law of Oman, especially Articles 1, 5, 8, 106, 109 and Title Ten on Single Person Companies (SPC); together with Executive Regulations issued under Ministerial Decision No. 52/2020 (Directory of Investment and Commercial Licenses).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Registration of a Commercial Company (استمارة تسجيل شركة تجارية) filed with the attached notarized Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association (English/Arabic) on Invest Easy; issuance of the Commercial Register Certificate (Certificat d'Inscription).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

A Single Person Company must have a minimum paid-up capital of OMR 5,000 (approximately USD 12,900). The capital shall be evidenced by a certificate of deposit issued by a licensed Omani bank establishing a temporary capital account in the proposed company name; the deposit must be maintained until the Commercial Register is issued and the account is converted into the operational company account.

Funding & unlock path: A civil defense clearance (fire safety certificate) must be obtained for any warehouse or storage facility from the Public Authority for Civil Defense and Ambulance, confirming that fire extinguishing equipment, emergency exits, and smoke alarms are installed and that the building load is certified to support storage of goods. In addition, the Ministry of Labour workplace clearance (establishment card) must be obtained if the business will employ staff, and any office occupancy must have a certificate of no structural danger issued by the relevant municipality.

Paid-in capital rule: A Single Person Company must have a minimum paid-up capital of OMR 5,000 (approximately USD 12,900). The capital shall be evidenced by a certificate of deposit issued by a licensed Omani bank establishing a temporary capital account in the proposed company name; the deposit must be maintained until the Commercial Register is issued and the account is converted into the operational company account.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED that the founder and sole shareholder of the Single Person Company do hereby: (i) approve the subscription and immediate payment of the total registered capital of OMR 5,000, evidenced by the bank deposit certificate; (ii) adopt and authorize the registered office situated at [Muscat address], Sultanate of Oman; (iii) appoint [given name, family name] as Chief Executive Officer and [given name, family name] as Company Secretary, with authority to execute statutory filings and represent the company before MoCIIP, the Oman Tax Authority, and commercial banks; (iv) authorize the opening of operational bank accounts and the completion of KYC/AML procedures.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

No specific industrial licensing board applies to ISIC 4791 online luxury resale; the MoCIIP Commercial Licenses and Investment Services Directorate acts as the sole authorization authority. If a physical storage warehouse is used, the municipality of the Governorate where the warehouse is located issues a warehouse operating endorsement.

Category: Online Retail and Second-hand Goods Trading License (issued as part of the Commercial Registration certificate and the Municipal Trading License; no separate sectoral license is statutorily required for mail-order/internet retail).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) under the Ministry of Finance; national e-services platform Emtaaz (https://emtaaz.taxoman.gov.om); general OTA portal (https://www.taxoman.gov.om); customs export declarations are processed through the Bayan Customs single-window system operated in coordination with the Royal Oman Police / Oman Customs.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. Step 2: Register with the Oman Chamber of Commerce and Industry (OCCI); obtain municipal and land-use approvals if the SPC operates a physical warehouse or office in Oman. Step 3: Apply through Emtaaz for the corporate Tax Card / Tax Registration Number (TRN) issued for income tax purposes; the TRN is the master identifier for later customs, bank, and VAT processes. Step 4: Submit a VAT registration application via Emtaaz within 30 days of the date the value of taxable supplies exceeds the OMR 500,000 mandatory threshold (or the OMR 250,000 voluntary threshold); a non-resident supplier without an Omani establishment is registrable at the OTA's discretion and must appoint an Omani tax representative where required. Step 5: Maintain registration data, file quarterly VAT returns electronically, and link the VAT account to the Bayan customs code for export zero-rating substantiation. Required registration documents: CR, constitutional documents (Articles of Association), Tax Card/TRN, proof of physical presence (lease or title deed), audited or projected financial statements covering 12 months, board resolution appointing authorized signatories, passport/ID copies of the sole shareholder and manager, and a bank letter confirming the Omani account.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - An Omani SPC is a resident juridical person chargeable to Corporate Income Tax in its own name; no transparency, pass-through, or imputation of income and credits to the sole shareholder is permitted. Under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019), 100% foreign ownership is allowed, and the SPC is taxed as a separate taxable entity regardless of shareholder nationality or residence; Omani-source income of the foreign-owned SPC is chargeable, and the entity cannot elect partnership or disregarded-entity treatment.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, hybrid-mismatch, or flow-through classification election exists under Omani law; tax classification follows the Commercial Companies Law legal form. Available statutory elections: (1) VAT group registration under Article 6 of the VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020) allowing related Omani persons to be treated as a single taxable person; (2) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 250,000 turnover threshold, including for an entity whose deductible input expenses are expected to exceed that threshold; (3) election of a non-calendar fiscal year for income tax purposes subject to OTA approval; and (4) for non-resident suppliers, registration through an appointed tax representative as an alternative to reverse-charge self-assessment by the Omani customer.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration threshold: OMR 500,000 (approximately USD 1.30 million) of annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration threshold: OMR 250,000 (approximately USD 650,000) of annual taxable supplies; a person making only reverse-charge-accounted supplies may apply for registration below the threshold; non-resident suppliers have no fixed threshold and are registrable at the OTA's discretion where they make taxable supplies in Oman and no reverse-charge mechanism applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) applies where the statutory export conditions of the Oman VAT Law and Executive Regulations are satisfied: (1) goods export - the SPC sells goods to a non-resident buyer, dispatches them from Oman, and the goods physically leave the Sultanate within 90 days of the date of supply, with export evidence retained; (2) services export - services are supplied to a non-resident person located outside the GCC Implementing States and the services directly relate to goods to be exported or to movable property located outside Oman; (3) international transport of goods and passengers and related ancillary services. For the online vintage-luxury resale model: cross-border sales to overseas end-customers via the SPC's online channel qualify for zero-rating only where Bayan customs export declarations and transport/remittance evidence confirm physical exit; sales or consignment deliveries to buyers inside Oman are standard-rated at 5%; commission and consignment fees charged to Omani consignors are taxable at 5%, while fees charged to foreign consignors for goods exported from Oman may qualify for zero-rating under the exported-services conditions. Input VAT on procurement (inbound logistics, authentication, warehousing, and customs costs) is creditable through the quarterly VAT return, and excess input VAT is refundable or carried forward per the OTA refund protocol; the zero-rated export position must be reconciled line-by-line with customs and payment records.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) evidencing physical exit of the goods from Omani territory, matched to the applicable VAT zero-rating line items
  • Ocean Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or multimodal transport consignment note showing Oman as the port/place of departure and the non-Omani destination
  • Commercial invoice (and consignment contract where applicable) bearing the SPC's VAT registration number, sale price, and an explicit zero-rated export justification
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit confirmation evidencing receipt of foreign-currency consideration into the SPC's Omani bank account
  • Packing list and insurance certificate or freight-forwarder dispatch certification supporting the declared consignment movement
  • Oman Customs exit gate pass/tracking history from the courier or logistics provider for the same consignment period

As of the current reporting period, Oman has not implemented a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, government-to-business API clearance, or digital cryptographic stamping regime. Electronic tax invoices are legally acceptable, but paper or ERP-generated PDF invoices satisfying the VAT Law invoice particulars are also accepted. VAT returns (quarterly standard filing) must be submitted electronically through Emtaaz; during audit, the OTA may require invoice-line data in machine-readable format (CSV, XML, or accounting-system extraction) but no prior fiscal authorization of each invoice is currently mandated.

The OTA will disallow a zero-rating claim where the export evidence bundle is incomplete, falsified, or fails the 90-day physical-exit or 180-day foreign-exchange repatriation timelines; the tax is clawed back retroactively at 5% from the date of supply, with late-payment charges and administrative fines up to OMR 10,000 per violation under the VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020). Deliberate fraud in export declarations, including false customs statements or artificial circular transactions, attracts criminal penalties of up to three years' imprisonment, monetary sanctions, exclusion from the VAT refund list, and suspension of the Commercial Registration; exporters must retain all zero-rating substantiation records for the statutory 10-year retention period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman is an IMF Article VIII member and maintains full current-account convertibility for bona fide trade and service transactions. The OMR is pegged at 1 USD = OMR 0.3845, and both current and capital account transactions may be routed freely through licensed banks subject to banking KYC/AML documentation. Profit and dividend remittances are permitted without exchange-control hurdle once statutory accounts are prepared, tax obligations are settled or secured, and the bank receives the board resolution, audited financial statements and source-of-funds evidence. There is no restriction on holding foreign currency accounts, but OMR cash withdrawals and opening of bank accounts require valid Commercial Registration and beneficial-owner identification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters may retain receipts in foreign currency in onshore foreign-currency accounts or convert to OMR at the prevailing market rate. For vintage luxury resale exports, the supporting export file (commercial invoice, customs declaration from Oman Customs, certificate of authentication for high-value goods and delivery proof) must be retained because CBO-regulated banks require it for wire release and for classification of the remittance as trade proceeds rather than portfolio capital movement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO); currency and banking supervision under Royal Decree 114/2000 (Banking Law). CBO manages the Omani Rial (OMR) exchange-rate peg to the US Dollar, licenses commercial banks and payment service providers, and acts as the foreign-exchange and balance-of-payments statistical authority. Foreign investment approval for foreign-owned SPCs is administered by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), but no CBO exchange-control license is required for ordinary corporate remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Offshore fintech/EMI service providers have Low direct viability in Oman because CBO does not grant banking charters to unlicensed foreign EMIs. An offshore EMI may only act as a payment facilitator for non-OMR flows and must route client funds through a licensed Omani bank for local settlement. The preferred legal structure is a Banking-as-a-Service integration with a CBO-licensed bank or acquisition of a CBO payment-service-provider license for escrow/payment services. Payment rails such as Stripe and Escrow Pay can be integrated as merchant-of-record or escrow-agency layers, but the underlying settlement account for the Omani SPC must remain with a licensed domestic bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholders may inject equity capital by SWIFT MT103 / MT202 to the SPC's OMR or USD account at a CBO-licensed bank. The bank must receive: (i) a board/resolution of the foreign shareholder approving equity contribution; (ii) MoCIIP Foreign Investment Registration showing 100% foreign ownership and no minimum capital threshold; (iii) Commercial Registration of the SPC; (iv) passport and source-of-wealth details of the shareholder; (v) the loan/equity purpose code stated as 'Foreign Direct Investment – Equity Capital Contribution' or 'Increase of Capital' in the wire instruction; and (vi) Oman Corporate Tax registration number. Funds are not blocked and there is no inheritance tax/import surcharge on equity. If the SPC uses Escrow Pay for consignment flows, the capital injection is kept in the corporate operating account, not the buyer escrow pool, to avoid commingling of client funds.For seller/supplier settlements, the primary clearing rails are: SWIFT MT103 for direct interbank credit to beneficiary accounts, SWIFT MT202 for correspondent bank cover payments, and MT700/MT710/MT707 for documentary letters of credit issued under UCP600 when purchasing high-end inventory from overseas suppliers. Domestic OMR liquidity transfers settle through CBO's National Payment System (the electronic clearing and RTGS infrastructure) if both banks are in Oman. For Escrow Pay rails, each outbound disbursement is executed only on the platform's dual-release instruction and must be accompanied by: commercial invoice, consignment dispatch/settlement note, proof of authentication, seller's bank details, and customer refund/chargeback status. The SPC should maintain segregated disbursement approvals for buyer refunds, consignor settlement, and commission income to avoid disgorgement risk.Spot OMR/USD transactions (T+2 settlement at bank market rate or CBO reference rate) for immediate trade settlements.Outright deliverable FX forwards between OMR and USD with licensed Omani banks, tenor matched to the underlying consignment purchase/sale or escrow release cycle, never exceeding 12 months.FX swaps combining a spot OMR/USD deal with a reverse forward to manage temporary trade-related cash mismatches without overnight position speculation.Cross-currency swaps (USD/OMR) for medium-term liability hedging, e.g., matching foreign-currency loan repayments or long-term stock purchase obligations, on a non-speculative and documented underlying-exposure basis.Vanilla FX options purchased from a licensed Omani bank to cap the USD cost of future inventory acquisition or to hedge contingent cross-border purchase risk; option premium must be paid from operating cash flows.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not a standard permissible instrument in the Omani onshore market because OMR is USD-pegged and a deliverable OMR/USD forward market exists; any offshore NDF proposal should be rejected unless a licensed Oman bank confirms enforceability and real economic hedging.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Offshore fintech/EMI service providers have Low direct viability in Oman because CBO does not grant banking charters to unlicensed foreign EMIs. An offshore EMI may only act as a payment facilitator for non-OMR flows and must route client funds through a licensed Omani bank for local settlement. The preferred legal structure is a Banking-as-a-Service integration with a CBO-licensed bank or acquisition of a CBO payment-service-provider license for escrow/payment services. Payment rails such as Stripe and Escrow Pay can be integrated as merchant-of-record or escrow-agency layers, but the underlying settlement account for the Omani SPC must remain with a licensed domestic bank.
