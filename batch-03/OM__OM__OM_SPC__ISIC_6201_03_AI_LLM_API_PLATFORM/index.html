Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is imposed on a Single Person Company under Royal Decree 18/2019; the share capital is fixed in the Articles of Association and must be fully subscribed and fully paid at the time of incorporation, with a bank certificate confirming the deposit of the capital in the company's bank account to be submitted before issuance of the Commercial Registration certificate.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) after inspection of the registered office, covering fire extinguishers, smoke detection, emergency egress, and electrical load safety for IT equipment; structural load inspection and building safety clearance by Muscat Municipality confirming the premises are fit for office use; occupational safety clearance under the Labor Law (Royal Decree 35/2003) confirming a non-hazardous workplace with first-aid provisions and sufficient ventilation for IT server rooms; no industrial zoning clearance is required given the office-based ISIC 6201 classification.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman — online taxpayer portal: https://taxoman.om. Legal basis: Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009, as amended by Royal Decree No. 24/2022), VAT Law (Sultani Decree No. 121/2020) and VAT Executive Regulations (Royal Decree No. 53/2021). Customs is administered by Oman Customs (Directorate General of Customs) through the 'Bayan' national single window.

E-government filing portal

Foreign-founded SPC procedure: (1) register the Single Person Company with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; (2) notarise the SPC Articles of Association and register the resident manager; (3) apply online at taxoman.om for the unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) by uploading the CR, certified memorandum, manager/shareholder passports or company CR, registered-office lease, local bank account confirmation and, where relevant, the shareholder's foreign tax-residency certificate; (4) open the VAT account through the same portal once the registration threshold applies (non-resident persons supplying digital services are registrable regardless of threshold); (5) receive the OTA tax registration certificate and unique VAT/TIN number. OTA processing typically takes 5–15 business days from complete submission.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the OM_SPC is a distinct juridical and taxable person resident in Oman; profits are taxed at the corporate level and are not passed through to the foreign shareholder. The company is subject to income tax on worldwide income arising to it; the standard CIT rate is 15% for tax years beginning from 2023, with the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income of a qualifying Riyada-registered SME effectively taxed at 0%. Distributions of profits to the shareholder are not re-taxed at shareholder level; capital gains form part of ordinary taxable income; no payroll, net-worth or municipal taxes apply.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a US-style check-the-box election, and no hybrid-mismatch election exists under domestic law. Available statutory options: (i) voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250; (ii) VAT group election for related persons; (iii) input-VAT allocation method for mixed-use supplies; (iv) application to the OTA for advance rulings or advance pricing agreements on cross-border digital/API service pricing; (v) small-enterprise CIT treatment (0% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income) upon certification under Riyada SME criteria; (vi) income-tax loss carry-forward of five years. None of these mechanisms converts the SPC into a pass-through vehicle.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 of taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration: OMR 19,250. A non-resident making taxable digital AI/API supplies to Omani customers has no threshold suppression and must register from the commencement of the supply activity; a resident SPC must apply within 30 days after the end of the month in which the threshold is exceeded.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0%) applies to: (i) exported goods consigned outside the GCC where export is proven by customs documentation and occurs within the statutory period; (ii) exported services supplied to a customer established outside the GCC (or to a GCC-resident non-taxable person where the service is effectively used and enjoyed outside the GCC), including AI/LLM API inference, hosted model access, software-as-a-service and licensing, provided the customer-location place-of-supply rule is met; and (iii) international transport and related services. For the AI API platform, zero-rating requires non-resident customers, foreign payment flows, and no effective use or enjoyment of the service inside Oman.

  • Export commercial invoice (serialised; stating supplier TIN, zero-rated treatment and export reference)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, CMR or equivalent transport document evidencing physical departure from Oman
  • Customs port or airport certificate evidencing exit of the goods from Omani customs territory
  • Service contract, SOW or API subscriber agreement identifying the non-resident customer and territorial scope
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC), SWIFT receipts or accepted crypto-to-fiat conversion records evidencing receipt of export proceeds

Oman does not yet mandate real-time fiscal clearance through API stamping or digital stamping for all taxpayers. Currently in force: issuance of sequential statutory Tax Invoices and Simplified Tax Invoices with all prescribed content; electronic filing of periodic VAT returns and payments via the OTA portal; retention of invoice and transaction records in Arabic or English for at least five years; and reverse-charge self-accounting on imported B2B services such as overseas cloud, model-hosting and data-centre services. The OTA has announced a phased national e-invoicing and real-time clearance roadmap; the SPC should maintain machine-readable invoice files and a full audit trail now to ease future migration.

OTA audit processes match zero-rated export claims against customs declarations, port movement logs and domestic bank receipts; supporting documentation must be retained for the statutory periods (five years under VAT rules, and the longer books-and-records period under the Income Tax Law). Penalties: administrative fines generally in the range of OMR 250–10,000 per violation under the VAT Law and Executive Regulations; late payment charge of 5% per month on unpaid tax (capped); clawback of export refunds obtained without entitlement, with reassessment and interest; deregistration and publication as a defaulting taxpayer; and criminal fines with imprisonment of up to three years for fraudulent export and refund schemes. The Income Tax Law separately penalises mischaracterised or non-arm's-length export revenue through CIT reassessment and related penalties.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fixed at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD and is fully convertible for current-account transactions, consistent with Oman's IMF Article VIII obligations. Capital-account flows are liberal: a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) may receive equity injections, draw intercompany loans, and remit dividends overseas without CBO prior approval, provided the paying bank obtains the underlying corporate documentation. Profit-repatriation hurdles are limited to documentary form (valid commercial registration, UBO declaration, banking mandate, articles of association, and proof of tax filing status where the SPC exceeds the Oman Tax Authority filing threshold). There is no repatriation quota, no exchange stabilization levy, and no differential conversion rate for the AI/LLM API services export stream.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no statutory minimum conversion ratio. The Omani SPC may retain its Stripe/USDC-origin export earnings in USD or any convertible foreign currency in its own account at a CBO-licensed bank; conversion to OMR is optional and commercial. Binding conditions are: (i) contemporaneous documentation of the underlying AI/LLM API export invoices; (ii) credit of proceeds to an account in the SPC's name at a licensed Omani bank; and (iii) inclusion of large-value cross-border receipts in the CBO balance-of-payments statistical return. No surrender timeline applies to ISIC 6201 service export receipts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Muscat — the sole monetary authority, foreign-exchange supervisor, and payments-system overseer in the Sultanate of Oman, acting under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000 as amended), the Foreign Investment Capital Law (Royal Decree 50/2019), and the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019 as amended). The CBO operates the national real-time gross settlement system (MIR) and prescribes the balance-of-payments statistical reporting framework that all licensed banks apply to cross-border remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low feasibility as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, Dukascopy) are not CBO-licensed deposit-taking institutions and do not settle OMR through CBO's MIR RTGS. Under the Omani Banking Law no unlicensed entity may carry on banking or payment-service business in Oman, so an onshore licensed bank must remain the domicile of the SPC's liquidity. An offshore EMI may be used only as a supplementary collection conduit for a Stripe merchant account held in a supported jurisdiction, provided the EMI sweeps net proceeds via SWIFT/SEPA into the Tier-1 Omani bank's USD concentration account. The SPC must never maintain its statutory share capital or tax settlement balances at an EMI.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injection: the foreign parent sends a SWIFT MT103 credit to the SPC's USD operational account at the nominated Omani bank with the purpose narrative 'Share Capital Subscription — [CR No.]', which the bank maps to CBO's balance-of-payments statistical classification of direct-investment equity. Intercompany loan drawdown: SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for nostro-level credits) with the narrative 'Intercompany Loan — [Loan Agreement Date]', accompanied by the executed facility agreement and the SPC board resolution. The collecting bank verifies the CR, the account mandate, UBO disclosure, and an expected-value notification for amounts exceeding the CBO statistical reporting threshold. Operating-liquidity receipts use the same inbound rail: Stripe payouts are swept by SWIFT/SEPA from Stripe's banking partners or an approved Merchant-of-Record (Paddle or FastSpring) directly into the USD concentration account; USDC proceeds are converted through a CMA/CBO-licensed VASP or licensed Omani bank OTC desk and swept within 48 hours to the same USD account.Primary clearing rail for trade and operating disbursements: SWIFT MT103 customer credits executed under the bank's online approval matrix for cloud compute, GPU hosting, LLM API vendor fees, licensing payments, and intercompany settlements; each debit is evidenced by a vendor invoice and the SPC board's payment instruction. Documentary collection and irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP600) are available and recommended for hardware procurement (GPU servers, storage arrays) requiring shipping and customs documents. Domestic OMR disbursements — payroll, rent, statutory social-protection contributions, VAT, and corporate tax installments — clear through CBO's MIR RTGS and the national salary-file channel. Treasury control measures: cumulative monthly payment caps per vendor, dual approval by the director/manager, and a standing prohibition on outbound wires referencing crypto-wallet addresses.Deliverable OMR/USD outright forwards and spot-deferred forwards with a licensed Omani bank, tenor up to 12 months, supported by documented trade or balance-sheet exposure and no speculative positioning.FX swaps (spot/forward rollovers and tom-next) that fund OMR working-capital requirements while preserving USD liability alignment.Cross-currency spot conversions (USD, EUR, GBP, OMR) executed at the bank's dealing desk for invoice settlement, intercompany netting, and vendor payments.Plain-vanilla FX options on OMR/USD permitted on a documented exposure basis; not recommended for the SPC given the fixed 2.6008 USD peg.The OMR has no active non-deliverable forward (NDF) market; eligible hedging must be executed onshore as deliverable contracts.Interest-rate or cross-currency swaps on OMR/USD are permissible for term-debt risk management but require execution solely through the onshore bank's treasury division.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low feasibility as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud, Dukascopy) are not CBO-licensed deposit-taking institutions and do not settle OMR through CBO's MIR RTGS. Under the Omani Banking Law no unlicensed entity may carry on banking or payment-service business in Oman, so an onshore licensed bank must remain the domicile of the SPC's liquidity. An offshore EMI may be used only as a supplementary collection conduit for a Stripe merchant account held in a supported jurisdiction, provided the EMI sweeps net proceeds via SWIFT/SEPA into the Tier-1 Omani bank's USD concentration account. The SPC must never maintain its statutory share capital or tax settlement balances at an EMI.
