Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Directorate General of Commercial Registration, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), Sultanate of Oman, acting through the Invest Easy e-registry portal (investeasy.gov.om).

Governing statute

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 (Omani Companies Law), Chapter Four – Single Person Company, Articles 48–58, implemented by Ministerial Decision No. 126/2020 (Executive Regulations); Royal Decree No. 50/2019 (Foreign Capital Investment Law) permits 100% foreign ownership for unrestricted export-oriented medical device manufacturing (ISIC 3250).

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum of Association of the Single Person Company, filed as the incorporation instrument with the Commercial Registration Application; registered in the Commercial Register and evidenced by the Commercial Registration Certificate (CR) and the SPC Formation Certificate.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; the Sole Owner fixes the capital in the Memorandum of Association and must fully subscribe it before registration. The subscribed capital must be deposited into a temporary escrow/integrated account in the SPC's name at a licensed Omani bank, evidenced by a bank capital certificate submitted to the Commercial Registrar; cash and in-kind contributions appraised under the Executive Regulations are permitted.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Directorate General of Civil Defense and Ambulance (Royal Oman Police) covering solvent and accelerator storage limits, hot-work permits, automatic sprinkler and gaseous suppression systems, and evacuation planning; structural load inspection and industrial occupancy certificate from MADAIN/Muscat Municipality verifying floor load capacity of the dipping lines and curing ovens; and Occupational Health and Safety clearance from the Ministry of Labour confirming workplace exposure controls for solvents and nitrosamines, mandatory annual air monitoring, medical surveillance, and the cleanroom hygiene protocol for sterile glove processing.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; the Sole Owner fixes the capital in the Memorandum of Association and must fully subscribe it before registration. The subscribed capital must be deposited into a temporary escrow/integrated account in the SPC's name at a licensed Omani bank, evidenced by a bank capital certificate submitted to the Commercial Registrar; cash and in-kind contributions appraised under the Executive Regulations are permitted.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) operating through the Taxomania electronic portal (https://taxomania.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP). Thereafter, the legal representative must: (1) log into Taxomania with the CR details; (2) submit the standard company incorporation documents (CR, Memorandum/Articles of Association, board/manager resolution, passport copies of shareholders and manager, lease/address proof); (3) receive the statutory Tax Identification Number (TIN) and tax card; (4) separately apply for VAT registration on Taxomania if annual taxable turnover exceeds OMR 500,000 (mandatory) or OMR 250,000 (voluntary), or if the entity is a non-resident supplier engaged in taxable activitication in Oman. A non-resident VAT registration may require appointment of an Omani tax representative. The TIN is generally issued within one business week, and VAT registration can take up to twenty working days where additional documentation is requested.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election is available under Omani income tax law; a foreign-owned SPC is mandatorily taxed as a juridical Omani company on its worldwide income (with certain Omani-source rules for foreign shareholders). No hybrid mismatch use of arrangement election exists in Oman. Under VAT, an entity may elect voluntary registration when turnover is between OMR 250,000 and OMR 500,000, and may elect for quarterly VAT return filing options if eligible; no other material fiscal elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 annual taxable supplies/sum of reverse-charge purchases; voluntary registration: OMR 250,000; non-resident persons making taxable supplies in Oman are registrable without threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of VAT applies to the export of goods (including sterile examination nitrile gloves) where the supplier is the owner/exporter, the goods are physically moved out of Oman to a non-GCC destination (or exported to a designated customs zone), the export is completed within 90 days from the date of supply, and the supplier retains official documentary evidence of export. Export-related international transport services and ancillary freight services are also zero-rated. The zero-rating is denied if the export evidence is incomplete, untraceable, or the goods are not actually removed from Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayaniya) with customs seal
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill evidencing departure from Omani port or airport
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Proof of VAT status of customer (foreign registration certificate or passport/CR of non-resident buyer)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank statement or foreign inward remittance document showing payment for the exported goods from outside Oman)

Oman has not yet implemented a mandatory real-time e-invoicing clearance or fiscal stamping system. VAT invoices must meet prescribed content in Arabic or English and be issued within prescribed timelines. VAT returns are filed electronically on Taxomania on a quarterly basis for most registrants; an API-based clearance regime is planned but not currently statutory. Taxpayers must retain full transactional records, including electronic archives, for at least ten years.

The OTA routinely audits export zero-rating claims and requires reconciliation of export entries, shipping documents, and bank remittances. If any document is missing, the zero-rated output VAT is reversed and the corresponding input VAT previously claimed on procurement of the gloves is clawed back with interest. Penalties under Omani VAT law include a 25% of unpaid tax penalty for aggregate adjustment and a monthly late-payment charge of 1% (capped at 100% of the tax) for failure to repay the corrected VAT. Knowingly furnishing false export documents amounts to tax evasion and may subject the SPC to a fine up to OMR 100,000 plus criminal prosecution under the Tax Procedures Law, in addition to disqualification of the foreign shareholder for clean tax compliance under the OTA's penal registry.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully convertible regime with no general exchange control. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD with a narrow official band of approximately +/-20 basis points. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are broadly liberalised; trade payments, services, loan repayments and direct-investment inflows require no pre-approval. Profits, dividends and liquidation proceeds are freely remittable to non-residents once the Oman Tax Authority issues a tax clearance certificate; there is no withholding tax on dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders. Banks must nonetheless record all transfers through the CBO event-reporting (ER) framework for statistical and FIU purposes.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender rule exists in Oman. Exporters may retain export proceeds in any permitted foreign-currency account (USD, EUR, GBP, etc.) with a licensed bank, without statutory conversion ratios or fixed repatriation deadlines. However, the receiving bank must report the inflow to the CBO in the quarterly balance-of-payments survey, and the SPC must retain the underlying customs declaration, commercial invoice, packing list and transport documents for at least five years for reconciliation and audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Muscat; statutory regulator of banking, currency and foreign exchange under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Foreign Exchange Circulars issued by CBO, and the AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 30/2016). The CBO licenses and supervises all commercial banks regarding currency convertibility, foreign-currency accounts, trade payment validation and cross-border balance-of-payments reporting.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for primary corporate banking. The CBO Payment Service Provider Regulation mandates a local licence for any Electronic Money Institution (EMI) servicing Oman-resident entities. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) cannot lawfully provide a primary settlement account to an Omani SPC without a CBO licence. They may be used only as a supplementary low-value collection corridor if the receiving Omani bank permits such inbound credits, and only under prevailing FIU/travel-rule scrutiny. Core treasury operations must therefore run through CBO-licensed commercial banks with SWIFT and LC capability.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Route the parent's equity injection as a SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder's bank directly to the Omani SPC's designated account, quoting the SPC's Omani Commercial Registration (CR) number and the transfer's unique end-to-end transaction reference (UETR). The receiving bank will record the CBO purpose of transfer as 'FDI – equity injection / initial paid-in capital for OM_SPC' (ISO 20022 purpose code 150/51 where required). The primary remittance may be denominated in USD, EUR or GBP; conversion to OMR occurs at the onshore spot rate (T+1) if operating funds are needed in OMR. The bank will verify the source of funds and will not accept routing through intermediary shell accounts. The first transfer must not exceed the authorised share capital stated in the articles of association unless a capital-increase resolution is filed with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP). Intra-group loans, if any, should be documented as a formal facility agreement and reported by the bank under the CBO direct-investment statistics with purpose code 'IFL' (intercompany financing).For import of machinery, spare parts and raw materials (nitrile latex, coagulants, packaging, tertiary cartons), the SPC will operate three clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 (TT wire) for disbursements typically below USD 50,000 (freight, insurance, bank fees and small suppliers), with purpose code 'IMPPG' and supporting pro-forma invoice; (2) documentary collection (D/P and D/A) under URC 522 for mid-value purchases; and (3) commercial letters of credit (LC) under UCP 600 (MT700/MT707) for major capex and latex supply contracts, with a typical tenor of 90 to 180 days after sight, irrevocable, and confirmed by a top-tier corresponding bank where needed. The Omani issuing bank will require a completed LC application, sanctioned-facility agreement and margin deposit; USD-denominated import settlements will be debited against a USD or OMR account of the SPC. The SPC must ensure all imports are cleared through the Omani Ministry of Commerce trade-registration process and the Royal Oman Police (COPFS) where applicable, retaining bills of lading, certificates of origin, packing lists, health certificates, customs declarations and commercial invoices for five years to satisfy CBO trade reconciliation.Outright FX forwards between US Dollar and Omani Rial, and cross forwards (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, JPY/USD) with tenors up to 12 months, executed as documented hedges against firm commercial commitments (signed invoice, purchase order or LC).FX swaps (spot against forward) for working-capital timing and asset-liability management.Cross-currency swaps (ISDA/CSA documentation) for minor-currency funding lines, provided the Omani bank holds a CBO derivatives licence and the purpose is genuine hedging.Plain-vanilla currency options (calls and puts) on EUR/USD and USD/OMR to protect committed glove-export receivables against adverse spot movement.Natural hedging through multi-currency settlement accounts, permitting the SPC to retain USD export receipts without forced conversion to OMR until trade payables fall due.Onshore NDFs are NOT permissible: the CBO does not authorise non-deliverable forward trading for the OMR; settlement-based forwards and swaps are the only admissible instruments.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low for primary corporate banking. The CBO Payment Service Provider Regulation mandates a local licence for any Electronic Money Institution (EMI) servicing Oman-resident entities. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut, Airwallex) cannot lawfully provide a primary settlement account to an Omani SPC without a CBO licence. They may be used only as a supplementary low-value collection corridor if the receiving Omani bank permits such inbound credits, and only under prevailing FIU/travel-rule scrutiny. Core treasury operations must therefore run through CBO-licensed commercial banks with SWIFT and LC capability.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Royal Decree 54/2023, amending Royal Decree 28/2009). The 15% rate applies to taxable income above OMR 100,000; the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income is taxed at the tier rate of 3%. The Oman-based SPC is taxed at entity level on income arising in Oman and foreign commercial profits, with no further tax on distributions.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividend distributions paid by an Omani SPC to its non-resident shareholder(s). Profit repatriation occurs through the already-taxed net profit of the entity, with no second-level Omani taxation or dividend declaration tax.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four months from the end of the tax year (Article 18, Royal Decree 28/2009). For a December 31 fiscal year-end, the statutory due date is April 30; a maximum one-month extension may be applied for through the OTA portal before the original due date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Advance installments are due on the last day of each quarter of the tax year (Q1: March 31, Q2: June 30, Q3: September 30, Q4: December 31) based on the prior year's audited tax liability. Withholding tax must be remitted by the end of the month following the calendar month in which the gross payment to the non-resident was made.
