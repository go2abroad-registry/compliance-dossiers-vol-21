Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A virtual office is not fully permissible for this specific business scope. A subscription box retail operation (ISIC 4791) requires a licensed physical commercial address for receiving correspondence, maintaining statutory records, and fulfilling consumer protection obligations. A virtual administrative office may serve as the registered address only if a separate operational warehouse/logistics address is lawfully licensed within the same commercial license or as an auxiliary establishment. Pure digital-service virtual offices without physical operational capacity are generally rejected for subscription-box merchandise due to delivery and return obligations.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Royal Decree 18/2019 there is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Single Person Company; however, the sole shareholder must state the declared capital in the Articles of Association, deposit it in a temporary bank account opened in the company's name, and obtain a capital deposit certificate before final CR issuance.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the Civil Defense and Ambulance Department after inspection of sprinkler systems, fire extinguishers, emergency exits, and smoke detectors; structural load inspection certificate from the competent Municipality Building Department; and workplace clearance for occupational safety including electrical safety, ventilation, floor loading capacity, and general employee safety compliance under the Occupational Safety and Health code.

Paid-in capital rule: Under Royal Decree 18/2019 there is no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Single Person Company; however, the sole shareholder must state the declared capital in the Articles of Association, deposit it in a temporary bank account opened in the company's name, and obtain a capital deposit certificate before final CR issuance.

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – official e-portal: taxes.gov.om or otax.gov.om (Taxpayer Services Portal)

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Single Person Company registers with the OTA via the online Taxpayer Portal. Procedure: (1) obtain commercial registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry, and Investment Promotion; (2) submit CR, constitutional documents, memorandum of association, and power-of-attorney of the sole shareholder; (3) register for Income Tax, Value Added Tax (VAT), and employer/CIT registration in one consolidated OTA account; (4) receive a unique Tax Identification Number (TIN) and separate VAT registration certificate; (5) register for Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) VAT exchange if making intra-GCC trade; no in-person inspection is required unless triggered by risk assessment.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign-owned Single Person Company registers with the OTA via the online Taxpayer Portal. Procedure: (1) obtain commercial registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry, and Investment Promotion; (2) submit CR, constitutional documents, memorandum of association, and power-of-attorney of the sole shareholder; (3) register for Income Tax, Value Added Tax (VAT), and employer/CIT registration in one consolidated OTA account; (4) receive a unique Tax Identification Number (TIN) and separate VAT registration certificate; (5) register for Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) VAT exchange if making intra-GCC trade; no in-person inspection is required unless triggered by risk assessment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or partnership-style fiscal transparency election exists in Oman for an SPC. The entity is mandatorily taxed at the entity level under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended) and VAT under the Oman VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2016). LLC/SPC treatment is non-fiscal; no hybrid entity election is permitted except for limited branch-branch allocation regimes; Oman applies the OECD BEPS minimum standard, including country-by-country reporting and anti-hybrid rules consistent with its mutual assistance commitments.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (annual taxable supplies and imports); voluntary registration available at OMR 19,250 in Omani Rial; non-established foreign suppliers are subject to VAT registration when making taxable supplies in Oman regardless of threshold, subject to reverse-charge and financial services nuances.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Supplies of subscription-box goods physically exported from Oman to a recipient outside Oman qualify for zero-rating under Article 59 of the VAT Executive Regulations if: (i) the supplier retains valid proof of export (customs declaration and transport documents); (ii) the goods leave Oman within the required time period; (iii) the sale is made to a person not resident in Oman and the goods are not re-supplied partially or wholly within Oman; (iv) the supplier receives payment in convertible foreign currency or OMR from external sources; (v) for online subscription services the place-of-supply rule is applied correctly—physical goods to a GCC consumer are zero-rated only if the export is documented; international digital services to non-resident consumers may be zero-rated where place of supply is treated outside Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (form OMAN-CED) as authenticated by the Royal Oman Police Customs
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing dispatch from a seaport/airport in Oman
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit statement/MT103 SWIFT showing foreign currency settlement by the overseas buyer
  • Insurance certificate when goods are shipped under CIF terms
  • Subscription contract and order records demonstrating recurring commercial sales to non-residents

Oman implemented an electronic invoicing mandate through the OTA platform. Taxable persons must exchange structured electronic invoices (XML/JSON) using certified e-invoicing providers for both B2B and B2C transactions. A phased roll-out applies: medium and high-risk taxpayers first, small-business phase announced annually. Real-time fiscal clearance is not yet fully operational for all sectors; however, the OTA, via the 'e-invoicing' system, requires invoice transmission to the national electronic clearance portal within agreed timelines, with digital stamping/reporting for tax invoices above OMR 100,000 and for export-related fiduciary invoices. Credit-card payment rails (Stripe, Authorize.Net) must ensure each transaction generates a compliant e-invoice bearing the QR code and VAT number.

Under the Oman VAT Law and Executive Regulations, the OTA may impose a penalty equal to no less than 25% and up to 200% of the tax-relief amount where export zero-rating or refund is claimed fraudulently, plus a late-payment interest at the statutory monthly rate on the unpaid VAT. Claimants are also exposed to retrospective clawback of input VAT recovered on zero-rated supplies, cancellation/exclusion from the zero-rating or refund register, and criminal penalties (in severe cases fines up to OMR 25,000 and imprisonment) for materially false customs declarations or forged export evidence. Where intra-GCC export documentation is incomplete, the OTA will additionally issue an adjusted VAT assessment and may apply a waiting-period verification hold of up to 120 days before refund release.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains full current-account convertibility under IMF Article VIII; no prior CBO approval is required for current-account transactions such as trade settlements, service fees, and profit remittances. Capital-account transactions are similarly liberalised for non-resident foreign investors under the Foreign Investment Law, subject only to MoCIIP investment registration, tax clearance, and documentation of the underlying equity or loan contribution. Profit remittance for a Single Person Company (SPC) is permitted after audited financial statements demonstrate distributable reserves and after filing the annual corporate tax return; no additional exchange-control approval or availability-of-foreign-exchange test applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No mandatory surrender or statutory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds in Oman; exporters may retain foreign currency earnings in licensed bank accounts. Under CBO banking instructions, export proceeds must be repatriated and credited to an Omani licensed bank within 180 days from the date of shipment unless a specific extension or offshore collection arrangement is approved. Failure to repatriate within the deadline requires written justification to the CBO and may be treated as an exchange-control irregularity.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), operating under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) and the Foreign Exchange Regulations, in coordination with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) for investment capital registration

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital EMI providers (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) acting as the primary corporate settlement rail. Such entities are not licensed by the CBO as payment service providers or exchange houses; they cannot hold OMR-denominated corporate accounts, cannot open local branches, and cannot act as acquirers or settlement agents for Omani-licensed merchants. Foreign EMIs may be used as a secondary collection or disbursement channel only for USD/EUR payouts into a CBO-licensed local bank account, but all OMR settlement, export receipt repatriation, and trade payments must flow through a licensed Omani bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholder capital injection must be routed via SWIFT MT103 (or MT202 for interbank transfers) to the SPC's OMR capital account or USD operational account at a CBO-licensed Omani bank. The remittance message must include the MoCIIP commercial registration number, the shareholder's legal name, and a clear purpose code corresponding to 'foreign direct investment – share capital subscription.' The receiving Omani bank will verify the FDI licence and issue a foreign-investment capital confirmation certificate required by MoCIIP. Inward remittances must not be structured as trade payments; separate purpose codes are required for supplier advances, intercompany loans, and equity; all inward foreign direct investment must be reported to the CBO via the bank's periodic report.Outbound trade payments for merchandise suppliers and logistics providers must clear through CBO-licensed channels. Recurring, low-value supplier payments are executed by SWIFT MT103 with associated MT199 or COVER instructions, or via the bank's internet-banking wire module; high-value, documentary bulk purchases are settled through irrevocable commercial letters of credit (LC) at sight or usance, advised through a correspondent bank. For subscription-box card receipts collected by Stripe Billing or Authorize.Net, settlement flows from the international acquirer into the SPC's USD settlement account at the Omani bank, after which OMR conversion at the CBO reference rate can be executed if local operational funding is needed. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is required when aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 500,000; a Master File is required when the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 5,000,000 or when the OTA issues a TP documentation notice.

Economic substance scope: Oman's ESR framework applies to licensed entities conducting specified relevant activities; intra-group distribution and service centres are within scope, while final consumer subscription-box trading generally is not. The SPC must nevertheless demonstrate local offices, staff, inventory, contracts and decision-making so that income is not recharacterized under Oman's general anti-abuse doctrine.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Statements

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the full underpaid tax, a 100% evasion penalty, monthly late-payment interest, administrative fines, and criminal prosecution with imprisonment and/or fine; egregious cases may lead to licence revocation.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorize false filings are jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax under the Commercial Companies Law; the OTA may obtain civil restitution judgments, asset freezes and travel bans pending settlement.
Shell Company / GAAR / Sham Transactions

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization and reassessment under the Oman Income Tax Law, disallowance of artificial deductions, a penalty of up to 100% of underpaid tax, and possible administrative dissolution for lack of commercial substance.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners and directors who misuse limited liability risk piercing of the corporate veil; courts may order restitution against shareholders and issue travel bans while tax liabilities are outstanding.
AML / CFT / Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law: substantial fines, asset confiscation, revocation of commercial registration, freezing of bank and payment processor accounts, and criminal imprisonment for responsible individuals.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and beneficial owners face personal criminal prosecution, travel bans, civil compensation to defrauded parties, and regulatory debarment from managing commercial companies.
