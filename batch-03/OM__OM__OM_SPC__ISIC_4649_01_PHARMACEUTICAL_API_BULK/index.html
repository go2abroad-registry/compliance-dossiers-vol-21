Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital is prescribed for an SPC under Royal Decree 18/2019; the MoA must fix a capital sufficient for the intended activity and declare it fully subscribed and paid by the sole shareholder. The paid-up amount is deposited into the company's own Omani bank account after CR issuance; no escrow account or audited capital certificate is statutorily mandated for non-strategic wholesale activities.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate issued by the Directorate General of Civil Defense and Ambulance (Royal Oman Police) following inspection of fire extinguishers, alarms, emergency egress, and hazardous material segregation; structural load inspection certificate for warehouse racking prepared by a licensed engineering office and filed with Muscat Municipality; Occupational Safety and Health workplace clearance and employee health surveillance under Royal Decree 63/2016 on OSH and Ministerial Decision 286/2008, together with the mandatory workplace safety policy and first-aid provision.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – electronic services portal 'Taxoman' (https://taxoman.om)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned SPC: (1) incorporate and obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); (2) obtain the Oman Gateway 'e-user' account and sign a government digital certificate; (3) submit the OTA Tax Registration application via Taxoman using the CR certificate, articles of association (MOA), board resolution appointing a tax representative, valid POA, TRC of the foreign parent, and financial projections; (4) the OTA assigns a 10-digit Tax Registration Number (TRN) linked to both Corporate Income Tax and VAT, typically within 5–10 business days; (5) separately file a VAT registration application when mandatory thresholds are met; (6) appoint a licensed tax agent for ongoing compliance if the entity has no permanent tax presence in Muscat.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a foreign-owned SPC: (1) incorporate and obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); (2) obtain the Oman Gateway 'e-user' account and sign a government digital certificate; (3) submit the OTA Tax Registration application via Taxoman using the CR certificate, articles of association (MOA), board resolution appointing a tax representative, valid POA, TRC of the foreign parent, and financial projections; (4) the OTA assigns a 10-digit Tax Registration Number (TRN) linked to both Corporate Income Tax and VAT, typically within 5–10 business days; (5) separately file a VAT registration application when mandatory thresholds are met; (6) appoint a licensed tax agent for ongoing compliance if the entity has no permanent tax presence in Muscat.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style 'check-the-box' election and no hybrid mismatch election exists under Omani tax law. The Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009) permits a wholly-owned or substantially-affiliated Omani corporate group to elect consolidated tax filing under conditions set by the OTA, but the SPC itself has no pass-through election and is treated as a standalone taxable legal person.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,000 of cumulative taxable supplies and imports in a 12-month period (mandatory); voluntary registration threshold is OMR 19,000 of taxable supplies and imports

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating (0%) applies to supplies where the place of supply is Oman and the goods are physically exported outside the GCC customs territory, or where services are supplied to non-resident customers, or where a supply directly relates to exports, international transport, ships or aircraft. The exporter must hold documentary proof that: (i) the goods left Oman within the statutory period (generally not later than 90 days from the tax invoice date); (ii) legal full consideration was received from a purchaser outside Oman; and (iii) the supply was made pursuant to genuine commercial export transaction. Refund of input VAT associated with zero-rated export supplies can be claimed in the VAT return or by a separate refund application to the OTA where excess credits result.

  • Customs export declaration (ACSW / ASYCUDA-generated declaration with stamped export certification)
  • Bill of lading, sea waybill, air waybill, or road manifest showing departure of goods from Oman
  • Commercial invoice and signed purchase order/contract matching the exported API batch
  • Inward remittance certificate / bank credit advice evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds into an Omani bank account
  • For API shipments: certificate of analysis, batch lot number, and regulatory export permit from the Ministry of Health or Manufacturing Licensing Authority, where applicable

Oman is implementing mandatory electronic invoicing via the Taxoman platform with phased roll-out under OTA decisions. Registered taxpersons must issue structured tax invoices/e-invoices retaining a machine-readable QR code for validated VAT transactions. Hard-coded real-time continuous controls ('fiscal box') are not yet mandatory in Oman; however, the OTA requires periodic digital declarations through Taxoman APIs for regulated businesses, and any automated clearance requirement applies selectively to large businesses as directed by future OTA circulars. Digital stamping of invoices is not currently required.

The OTA audits zero-rated export claims selectively; where the required export evidence is missing, incomplete, forged, or does not match customs records, the OTA will claw back the entire zero-rated VAT benefit plus charging VAT at the standard rate on the supply, together with statutory delay penalties (typically 10%–25% of the unpaid VAT) and a possible additional evasion penalty of up to double the tax evaded. Criminal liability may arise for fraudulent export zero-rating, with fines of up to OMR 25,000, imprisonment for up to three years, and publication of the offender's consolidated tax declaration breaches. Repeat or pervasive misuse of the export zero-rating regime may result in TRN suspension, mandatory periodic tax audits, and revocation of the SPC's commercial registration by MOCIIP.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for all current account transactions under Article 12 of the CBO's Investment Rules; there are no explicit capital controls on direct foreign investment in non-strategic sectors, and 100% foreign ownership of an SPC is permitted under the Foreign Capital Investment Law. Operational hurdles are documentary rather than regulatory: all inward and outward transactions must be routed through licensed commercial banks, and profit remittances require evidence of paid corporate income tax, audited financial statements, and a certified board resolution. Dividends and capital repatriation require an SPC liquidation balance sheet or a professional indemnity no-objection for retained profits. For the Pharmaceutical API wholesale segment, the Ministry of Health's Directorate General of Pharmaceutical Affairs and Drug Control must also approve the import/re-export model before the banks will treat the entity as a credible trade remitter.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not maintain a statutory export-proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio. The CBO's balance-of-payments framework requires that export receipts be repatriated to, and credited into, an Omani commercial bank account within a reasonable documentary cycle, routinely 90 to 180 days from the bill of lading date, under the corresponding letter of credit or open-account terms. Commercial banks enforce this through the presentation of export documents and the matching of inbound SWIFT MT103 credits to the original trade invoice; no conversion into OMR is forced, and exporters may retain proceeds in a USD-denominated operational account. In practice, for API wholesale, the exporter must file a CBO foreign-exchange receipt form (BOP/FX-01) for any credit exceeding OMR 25,000.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – Foreign Exchange Supervision Department, together with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) for investment-registration matters

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMIs and challenger banks are not licensed by the CBO and cannot hold OMR settlement accounts or access the Omani Automated Clearing House (OACH) or the CBO RTGS system. An offshore EMI may be used only as a payment-information sub-ledger; all actual cross-border fund movements must be cleared through a licensed Omani commercial bank using SWIFT MT103/202. Any attempt to route working-capital or trade disbursements directly through an unlicensed EMI triggers a CBO AML licence violation and will be rejected by correspondent banks. The viable hybrid structure is to maintain the OMR/USD operational account at a Tier-1 Omani bank and use the EMI solely for internal FX-rate visibility and invoice reconciliation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing is a SWIFT MT202 or MT103 transfer from the foreign shareholder's bank to the SPC's Capital Escrow Account at Bank Muscat, SAOG (or at National Bank of Oman SAOG). The purpose code must be assigned under the CBO Balance of Payments guidelines as Direct Investment in Oman – Equity Capital, commonly entered as BOP code 701, and must be supported on the cover message by the SPC's Commercial Registration Number, the MCIIP Foreign Investment Certificate, and the Omani bank's inward remittance reference. For amounts above OMR 50,000, the bank requires the shareholder to complete a Capital Introduction Form with an audited source-of-funds statement. Once the funds are credited, the bank issues a Capital Inward Remittance Certificate (CIRC), which the SPC files with the tax administration for equity-allocation monitoring. Physical cash injection is prohibited for pharmaceutical wholesale; there is no permissible alternative to a bank-wire received into the Omani banking system.For API bulk purchases, the preferred rail is a Commercial Letter of Credit governed by UCP 600: the Omani issuing bank dispatches an MT700 to the seller's bank, an MT707 amendment is used for any changes in shipment/price, and payment is released by MT202/103 after the presenting bank conforms to the LC documents. For open-account restocking with existing vendors, the SPC executes a direct MT103 outward remittance referencing the proforma invoice and the commercial contract, subject to a commodities import license from MOH and a customs commodity code (HS 2934/3001/3006 as appropriate). The clearing route runs through the Omani bank's USD correspondent network (e.g., JP Morgan or HSBC New York) for USD payments, or via the CBO RTGS for local OMR settlement of imports. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and controlled-transaction disclosure are mandatory when annual gross revenue exceeds OMR 10 million or related-party transactions exceed OMR 1 million; Master File is required when consolidated group turnover exceeds OMR 200 million; CbCR applies at the EUR 750 million group-revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted a separate Economic Substance Regulations statute; however, the income tax law, GAAR and transfer pricing rules require real management activity, staff, premises, warehouse capability and operational decision-making in Oman. A letterbox SPC or a purely conduit entity will be denied treaty access, incentives and tax residency benefits.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Tax Filing

Statutory sanction: Assessment of omitted tax plus a deferment penalty of 1% per month up to 25%, an administrative fine up to OMR 50,000, and criminal fines or imprisonment as determined by Omani courts.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign fraudulent returns are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and may face travel bans, asset seizure, disqualification and imprisonment up to five years.
Transfer Pricing Violation / Non-Arm's-Length API Sourcing

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length reallocation of profit, recomputed tax at 15%, a 1% monthly late-payment surcharge up to 25%, and a documentation penalty of OMR 10,000 for failure to provide Local File or Master File upon request.

Director personal liability: Directors knowingly approving mispriced related-party transactions may be jointly assessed for the resulting tax deficiency; criminal liability may arise where mispricing is effected through forgery or tax evasion.
Shell Company / Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterisation and disregard of the SPC for tax purposes, denial of treaty benefits, deemed taxable profit in Oman at 15%, revocation of commercial registration and the pharmaceutical wholesale licence, and monetary penalties for non-filing.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced; the beneficial owner and directors become personally liable for tax, civil restitution, travel bans and wrongful-trading compensation.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's Anti-Money Laundering Law, fines up to five times the value of the laundered property or OMR 500,000, suspension or revocation of the special licence, and mandatory reporting to Oman's Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face criminal prosecution, personal fines, asset freezes, travel bans, disqualification and imprisonment up to seven years where money-laundering knowledge is proved.
