Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — e-filing and registration portal: Taxoman (https://taxoman.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain an Oman Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), secure the trade license, and then complete electronic tax registration through the OTA Taxoman portal. The OTA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) used for corporate income tax and VAT. VAT registration is mandatory when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500, and voluntary registration is available when taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,250. For hazardous goods freight forwarding, the entity must also hold the port/security licenses and the competent ministry approval before the TIN activation is finalised.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists for Omani SPCs; an SPC is mandatorily classified as a corporation and generally taxed at entity level. Tax grouping and reorganisation relief are available only on express OTA approval under Royal Decree 28/2009 (as amended). Oman implements anti-hybrid rules through its OECD BEPS alignment, and no separate domestic hybrid mismatch election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 in annual taxable supplies; Voluntary registration: OMR 19,250 in annual taxable supplies (or anticipated supplies) subject to OTA approval for non-resident/received supplies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (a) tangible goods physically exported outside Oman by or on behalf of the supplier within the period prescribed in the VAT Executive Regulations, normally within 90 days from the time of supply; (b) international transport of goods and passengers where the destination point is outside Oman or includes a transit outside Oman; (c) services directly related to the transportation of exported goods, including handling, loading, storage, customs clearance and other ancillary logistics services performed in connection with the export of the dangerous goods; and (d) services supplied by a taxable person to a non-resident recipient receiving the services outside Oman and not related to land/property in Oman. The forwarding entity must maintain contemporaneous evidence of the export journey and the contract with the overseas principal.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin

Oman does not yet enforce a nationwide real-time fiscal clearance or mandatory API-based invoice stamping regime. VAT-registered persons must issue e-invoices in a structured digital format, retain SAF-T-compliant records, and file quarterly VAT returns through Taxoman. The OTA has announced a phased transition to mandatory electronic invoicing, so the DG freight forwarding entity must build its ERP/invoicing environment for future real-time clearance and API connectivity.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to strict OTA scrutiny using customs data, shipping manifests, port scan records and bank-specific FX inflow matching tests. Any unsupported or fraudulent zero-rating claim results in a retroactive VAT assessment at 5%, late payment penalties at 25% of the assessed VAT, additional administrative fines up to OMR 50,000 per VAT Law infringement, and criminal sanctions (including imprisonment) for intentional evasion. The clawback window for fraudulent claims is 10 years, and 5 years for error-based claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the US Dollar. Oman maintains broadly liberal current and capital account convertibility; no prior central bank exchange control approval is required for foreign direct investment injections, trade settlements, dividends, or profit remittances. In practice, authorized banks apply enhanced due diligence and compulsory balance-of-payments notification for transactions linked to High-Risk Special License entities. Profit remittance requires audited financial statements, a tax clearance certificate from the Oman Tax Authority where applicable, and confirmation that the company has discharged regulatory fees and AML/CFT reporting obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory surrender or compulsory conversion of export proceeds into Omani Rial. Export receipts from DG freight forwarding services must be repatriated to an authorized bank in Oman and reported in the CBO balance-of-payments framework, ordinarily within six months of the invoice due date. The proceeds may be retained in foreign currency, but the bank must record the purpose code and retain supporting bills of lading, export invoices, license proof, and DG declarations to demonstrate lawful provenance.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Sultane Decree No. 38/2000 Banking Law, with operational administration through the Foreign Exchange Operations and Financial Stability Directorate

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as an independent settlement tier. Offshore digital EMI platforms are not recognized as authorized clearing institutions under CBO licensing law and cannot operate direct nostro/OMR facilities, issue confirmed Letters of Credit, or provide regulated FX settlement in Oman. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections shall be routed through SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder to the designated Capital Escrow Account at a licensed Omani bank using the purpose code for foreign direct investment/equity participation. The payment instruction must identify the beneficiary Omani SPC commercial registration number, the DG forwarding license reference, and the tax registration number. Prior to execution, the bank requires a notarized shareholders resolution, updated company incorporation certificate, certified UBO register, and a funds-source statement. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% headline corporate income tax rate under Royal Decree 53/2023 (new Income Tax Law, effective for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2024); small business preferential rate of 9% applies to taxable income up to OMR 100,000 per tax year.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory dividend withholding tax under Oman law; Oman does not impose a withholding tax on dividend distributions by an Omani company. A foreign sole shareholder of the OM_SPC receiving dividends is not subject to Oman WHT. However, excessive distributions or arrangements lacking commercial substance may be recharacterised under the general anti-abuse rule (GAAR) of RD 53/2023 and treated as taxable constructive distributions or hidden profit distributions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months after the end of the tax year; for a 31 December year-end the filing deadline is 30 April of the following calendar year. OTA may grant an extension only in exceptional circumstances via a formal e-Portal application.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Advance instalments fall due on the 15th day of the month following the end of each calendar quarter (or as specified in the OTA-issued provisional assessment), with final reconciliation due at the annual return deadline; OTA may waive quarterly filing for taxpayers with de minimis expected liability.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File where the aggregate annual value of controlled transactions exceeds OMR 1,000,000; mandatory Master File where the worldwide MNE group's consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 50,000,000. A shortened related-party transaction disclosure schedule is required on the annual CIT return whenever any controlled transaction exists, without de minimis exemption. The Oman Tax Authority Transfer Pricing Guidance (issued under RD 53/2023) adopts the OECD Transfer Pricing Guidelines and permits unilateral, bilateral and multilateral Advance Pricing Agreements (APAs) for a maximum prospective term of four years.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code comparable to the UAE. Instead, substantive-activity conditions are embedded in: (1) the GAAR and 'substance over form' provisions of RD 53/2023, which deny tax advantages to artificial structures and sham transactions; (2) the Free-Zone 0% CIT exemption, which requires an OPAZ/Free-Zone 'qualifying activity' licence, actual registered premises, licensed local personnel, and locally exercised management of the DG operations, including vessel scheduling, stowage validation, Material Safety Data Sheet verification, and emergency-response oversight; (3) the place-of-effective-management (POEM) and managed-and-controlled tests, which can treat a foreign parent's de facto control as creating an Omani PE or Omani tax residence for the SPC; and (4) treaty anti-abuse clauses (LOB/PPT) requiring genuine business purpose and real business activity for any reduced withholding rate, PE exemption or DTA relief.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Wilful Misdeclaration of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to 100%-150% of the evaded corporate income tax, additional monthly surcharge of 1% on the unpaid tax up to a maximum of 100%, revocation of the tax clearance certificate, disqualification from government and oil-and-gas logistics contracts, licence revocation by MTCIT/OPAZ, and criminal prosecution with imprisonment of up to 5 years under the Oman Income Tax Law and Penal Code.

Director personal liability: Omani courts and OTA may pierce the corporate veil of a Single Person Company where the sole shareholder directs or personally benefits from evasion. The shareholder and the registered manager become jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, fines, and civil restitution; travel bans, asset-freeze orders, and arrest warrants may be issued; imprisonment is available for wilful tax evasion.
Shell Company / Sham Free-Zone Substance

Statutory sanction: Re-assessment of the SPC at the 15% statutory rate (or 9% small-business rate where applicable) on the full income for all open years; imposition of the 1% monthly late-payment surcharge; administrative penalty of 25%-50% of the adjusted tax; denial of DTA benefits under the principal-purpose test; forfeiture of the OPAZ qualifying-activity licence.

Director personal liability: Directors and the sole foreign shareholder who knowingly orchestrate the sham arrangement face joint and unlimited liability for the additional tax and penalties. Criminal referral may result in a travel ban, seizure of operating vessels and vehicles, and imprisonment where fraud is established, overriding the SPC's limited-liability shield.
AML / Sanctions / Terrorist Financing in Hazardous Cargo

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines of OMR 25,000 to OMR 100,000 per violation under the Oman Anti-Money Laundering Law; cargo seizure and impoundment; suspension or permanent revocation of the DG freight licence by MTCIT and OPAZ; inclusion in the Central Bank of Oman sanctions list; prohibition from operating via Omani seaports (Sohar, Salalah, Duqm, Muscat).

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder, resident manager, and any compliance officer with actual knowledge are personally criminally liable, notwithstanding the SPC's limited status. Personal travel bans are routinely imposed under Omani AML procedures; personal assets may be frozen and confiscated where they represent proceeds of sanctioned cargo transactions; foreign extradition cooperation is available under the GCC and bilateral instruments.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and Sham Related-Party Margins

Statutory sanction: Oman Tax Authority transfer-pricing adjustment to the arm's-length level, resulting in additional taxable income at 15% (or 9%); monthly late-payment surcharge of 1% on the resulting underpayment; separate administrative penalty for documentation defects of OMR 20,000-OMR 50,000; disallowance of cross-border payments where documentation is absent; criminal penalty in cases of grossly false disclosure.

Director personal liability: The legal representative who signs the false TP Disclosure Schedule is personally liable for the penalty portion under the tax law's responsible-person provisions. Where the sole shareholder is the counterparty to the artificial transaction, courts may order equitable restitution, a travel ban, and set-off against the shareholder's personal assets for the extra tax and surcharges.
Withholding Tax Evasion on Outbound Royalties and Service Fees

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the unwithheld 10% WHT from the SPC; disallowance of the underlying expense in full; monetary penalty equal to the unwithheld amount (often 100% of the WHT due); daily/monthly delinquency surcharge and loss of tax clearance status, which freezes renewal of the dangerous-goods forwarding licence and forces closure of the Omani port operator accounts.

Director personal liability: The SPC's registered manager and sole shareholder are jointly and severally liable for the collection and remittance of the withheld amounts under Omani responsible-person rules. Non-remittance constitutes criminal tax evasion, leading to travel bans, a personal fine, and imprisonment of up to 5 years, with the corporate veil pierced to reach personal bank accounts and shares in the SPC.
