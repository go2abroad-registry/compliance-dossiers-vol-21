Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital threshold under RD 18/2019. The sole shareholder declares a nominal capital (commonly OMR 1,000) in the constitutive documents. At least 30% of the cash issued capital must be deposited into a temporary escrow account held in the name of the company-under-formation with a bank licensed in Oman; the bank issues a capital deposit certificate to be attached to the registration filing.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) covering use-of-building approval, fire exits, extinguishers, smoke/dust detection, and annual renewal; structural load inspection by the Muscat Municipality or relevant provincial municipality attesting the building can carry the milling equipment and material storage; and an occupational health and workplace clearance from the Ministry of Labour verifying machine guarding, noise exposure control, respirator zone designation, and first-aid provision.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

The Tax Authority / الهيئة الضريبية, Sultanate of Oman (unified national tax agency for Income Tax and VAT); official electronic portal: https://taxes.gov.om

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) in the Omani Commercial Register (Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion) to obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) number. Step 2: Create a user profile on the Tax Authority's unified portal (taxes.gov.om) and file for Income Tax Registration to obtain the Tax Registration Number (TRN) and Tax Card; registration is due within 3 months of commencing activity. Step 3: If annual taxable supplies/imports exceed OMR 38,500 (mandatory VAT threshold), file VAT registration application within 30 days of the end of the month in which the threshold was exceeded (voluntary registration permitted above OMR 19,250). Step 4: Upload supporting documents through the portal: CR certificate, constitutional documents (Memorandum/Articles of Association), audited financial statements or opening balance sheet, shareholder and manager passport copies, power of attorney, and proof of registered address. Step 5: Receive the integrated tax registration credentials covering both Income Tax and VAT obligations.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No federal check-the-box or pass-through election exists under Omani law; the SPC is mandatorily taxable as a separate juridical entity at the flat CIT rate of 15% (Royal Decree 88/2023, effective for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2024, replacing the former 3%/15% bracket regime). Smaller businesses with taxable income below the statutory de minimis band enjoy no preferential rate except for small business relief provisions where applicable. No VAT tax grouping election is available; the SPC must be treated as an independent taxable person. Transfer pricing disclosure and related-party documentation elections apply through the annual CIT return; dividend and capital gains from qualifying shareholdings may be exempt under the holding company provisions of RD 88/2023.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 (equivalent to USD 100,000) of taxable supplies or imports over the preceding 12 months; voluntary registration: OMR 19,250 (USD 50,000). Threshold applies to the SPC aggregate taxable turnover including zero-rated exports.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the physical export of carbon-fiber FPV racing drones and components to destinations outside Oman and outside the GCC when the exporter is VAT-registered, retains evidence that the goods left the country within 90 days of supply (extension obtainable for good cause), and holds the complete customs and transport documentation. Related services (freight, logistics, and assembly services physically rendered in Oman in connection with exported goods) are zero-rated only where provided to a non-resident recipient and directly linked to the exported goods. International transport services and related ancillary services are also zero-rated. The supply must be recorded on a tax invoice stating 0% VAT with the statutory zero-rating reference.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document / Bayan clearance record issued through Oman Customs ASYCUDA system from the egress port or airport)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (negotiable transport document issued by a licensed carrier evidencing physical movement outside Oman and the GCC, showing exporter as shipper and overseas consignee)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (foreign currency credit advice from an Omani licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds from the overseas customer)
  • Commercial Invoice, Packing List, and Certificate of Origin (supporting declaration of goods description, HS code 9503.00 / ISIC 3240 compatibility, and export destination)

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, digital stamping, or continuous transaction control (CTC) e-invoicing regime is currently in force in Oman. VAT-registered businesses must issue compliant tax invoices (full or abridged) under the GCC VAT Law and retain electronic/paper records for 10 years. Electronic filing of VAT returns and JSON/XML invoice exchange through the Tax Authority portal is accepted voluntarily; the authority is progressively digitizing compliance but has not mandated API-based e-invoicing clearance as of the current tax cycle.

The Tax Authority may deny zero-rating and assess VAT at 5% (plus daily late-payment interest of up to 1% for the first month and 2% per month subsequently, subject to statutory caps) if the export evidence bundle is incomplete, the 90-day shipment deadline is not respected without extension, or the SPC fails to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days without a Central Bank of Oman consent. Intentional fraudulent zero-rating claims attract a penalty equal to three times the evaded VAT plus imprisonment of up to 5 years; negligent errors incur an administrative penalty up to OMR 20,000 per incorrect return and possible suspension of the VAT registration. Under the Income Tax Law, false or forged export documentation triggers penalties up to OMR 10,000 per occurrence and criminal referral, and general anti-avoidance rules permit retrospective recharacterization of supplies with interest and penalties for a look-back period of up to 4 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains a fully convertible Omani Rial pegged to the USD at the official rate of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Current account transactions are unrestricted and free from exchange control approval; capital account transactions, including 100% foreign-owned SPC equity injection, are also liberal. Profit and dividend repatriation is permitted without prior CBO consent, subject only to commercial documentation and a valid audited financial statement evidencing distributable profits. There are no restrictions on maintaining foreign-currency accounts, and the foreign investor may remit proceeds of liquidation abroad after discharging liabilities and settling any tax dues.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender requirement applies to export receipts. There is no mandatory conversion timeline and no required conversion ratio; exporters may retain proceeds indefinitely in approved foreign-currency accounts with licensed Omani banks. Receipts that are converted into OMR are processed at prevailing market rates without CBO intervention.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. Offshore fintech EMIs and payment facilitators (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman and cannot provide direct OMR domestic settlement or local clearing capabilities. They may serve solely as ancillary FX and payout channels for PayPal/Stripe receivables, but all regulated treasury activities must be anchored through a licensed Omani bank account. Legal constraints derive from the CBO Payment System Oversight framework and the AML/CFL Law, which require payment-service providers to be authorised in-country; routing core corporate liquidity through an unlicensed EMI creates regulatory and sanctions risk.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Shareholder capital injection must originate from the home-country corporate or personal bank account. The remitter instructs their bank to send SWIFT MT103 with a purpose message identifying the transfer as equity capital for OM_SPC. The receiving Omani bank applies purpose code compatible with direct-investment and equity participation under the CBO balance-of-payments reporting framework, typically recorded as inward direct investment capital. The SPC must present its EID commercial-registration extract, notarized share-certificate statement, and a notarized shareholder resolution confirming the injection. The funds are credited to the designated Capital Escrow Account and released to the Operational Account only after the bank completes reconciliation and the CBO quarterly survey reporting.Supplier payments and trade settlements clear predominantly through SWIFT MT103 messages for raw-material purchases, freight costs, and marketing services. For higher-value or documentary-risk purchases, a Commercial Letter of Credit (irrevocable, confirmed where available) issued against the SPC's operational account is the standard rail. PayPal and Stripe gross earnings are settled in USD or converted and credited to the Omani operational account via licensed payment aggregators and acquiring banks. Payouts to overseas freight-forwarders and component suppliers may also be executed through straight transfer on the CHIPS/Fedwire secondary rails when banks correspond in USD, while domestic OMR disbursements clear through Oman's electronic clearing system (OCPPS).FX Forwards (USD/OMR, EUR/OMR, GBP/OMR) with tenors of 1-12 months available from licensed Omani commercial banks for receivables and payables exposureFX Swaps between USD and OMR for short-term liquidity management, quoted against the pegged rateCross-currency swaps between EUR/GBP and USD for firms with meaningful multi-currency e-commerce receiptsSynthetic USD hedging through natural balance-sheet offset of USD-denominated receivables and payables given OMR is pegged to USDNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are generally unavailable for OMR due to the hard USD peg and full convertibility; dealers prefer deliverable forwards

Corporate Income Tax Return Due within three months after the end of the financial year; for a 31 December year-end, filing and payment must be completed no later than 30 April of the following year. $0 USD Shortfall in tax is subject to 1% monthly interest; late filing triggers an OMR 100 (approximately USD 260) minimum penalty; repeated failure to file can result in tax estimates, attachment of bank accounts, and registration suspension.
Oman Chamber of Commerce and Industry (OCCI) Annual Membership Renewal Due with the commercial register renewal before the registration anniversary; valid OCCI membership is a precondition to obtaining the MOCIIP renewal stamp. $150 USD Expired membership blocks the MOCIIP annual renewal process and, after ten days, subjects the company to late-fee surcharges and suspension of the commercial licence.
