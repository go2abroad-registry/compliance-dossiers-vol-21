Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SPC under Royal Decree 18/2019; capital is freely determined by the sole shareholder and must be fully paid within 10 years. No escrow/bank capital deposit is mandatory for the ISIC 3240 casting activity, but a bank capital confirmation letter is recommended to evidence substance. If the enterprise applies for foreign investment incentives under Royal Decree 50/2019, the capital amount must be stated in the MOA and satisfied before operational licensing.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OmanTax), Sultanate of Oman; e-services and electronic filing portal: OmanTax website / taxoman.om. The Oman Tax Authority is the successor to the former Secretariat-General of Taxation and operates under the Ministry of Finance.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first complete Commercial Registration with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Upon issuance of the Commercial Registration certificate and constitutional documents, the SPC must apply to the Oman Tax Authority for a Tax Registration Number (TRN) using the OmanTax e-services portal. The application must submit the commercial registration certificate, memorandum of association, sole shareholder identification, manager identification, registered office address evidence, and appointment documents. The Oman Tax Authority issues a unified TRN for income-tax and VAT purposes; VAT registration is separately triggered when the statutory VAT threshold is met, and the VAT identification number is reflected in the taxpayer's OmanTax account. Registration is generally completed within 30 business days after submission of a complete application and issuance of the Commercial Registration. Foreign entities may also be required to appoint a tax representative for VAT compliance if not resident in Oman, although an incorporated Omani SPC is itself an Omani resident taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. An SPC organized under Omani law is a separate legal and taxable person resident in Oman. It is subject to Omani corporate income tax at the entity level and is not eligible for pass-through or transparent treatment. Non-resident shareholders holding the SPC are generally not subject to Omani tax on the SPC's operating income except on distributed dividends or capital gains arising in Oman, subject to applicable treaties.

Available election forms

Omani law does not provide a US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election; a foreign-owned SPC is per se an entity-level taxpayer for income-tax purposes. No pass-through election is available. For VAT purposes, the SPC may elect voluntary VAT registration where taxable supplies or imports exceed the voluntary registration threshold but do not yet reach the mandatory threshold, and may apply to form a VAT group with closely related entities with Oman Tax Authority approval. No statutory hybrid-mismatch tax election exists under Omani domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 of taxable supplies or imports within the preceding 12-month period (mandatory threshold). Voluntary VAT registration is permitted where annual taxable supplies or imports exceed OMR 100,000 but do not exceed OMR 500,000, subject to Oman Tax Authority approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods outside Oman and outside the GCC implementing states are zero-rated where the goods are physically exported and the export occurs within 90 days of the time of supply. The exporter must hold documentary evidence sufficient to prove the goods left Oman, including customs records and shipping documentation. Services are zero-rated where the recipient is a non-resident outside Oman, the services are supplied to a person carrying on business outside Oman, and the services are not effectively consumed or exploited in Oman. The SPC must be the supplier of record and must demonstrate that the economic benefit is transferred overseas.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayaan/e-customs export declaration) confirming the goods departed Oman
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill identifying the SPC as shipper/exporter and the foreign consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank credit confirmation evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds from the overseas buyer
  • Valid VAT tax invoice and original sale contract or purchase order for the export transaction
  • Packing list, transport insurance certificate, and any other customs/competent authority evidence of physical exit

As of the issuance of this dossier, Oman does not operate a real-time electronic invoice clearance or digital stamping regime comparable to Saudi Arabia's FATURA or certain Latin American models. VAT invoices must comply with the Oman VAT Executive Regulations and include the supplier's TRN, invoice number and date, recipient details, description and quantity of goods/services, net amount, VAT rate, and VAT amount. Invoices must be issued electronically or on paper, retained in original form, and submitted with VAT returns through the OmanTax portal. The Oman Tax Authority is progressively expanding digital e-invoicing capabilities and API-based return filing, but individual invoice pre-approval or real-time fiscal clearance is not yet mandatory.

Oman Tax Authority conducts post-clearance audits of export zero-rating claims. If the mandatory export documentation is missing, incomplete, or inconsistent, the zero-rating claim is rejected, the supply is recharacterised as taxable at the standard 5% VAT rate, and VAT together with administrative delay fines becomes payable. A refund already credited may be clawed back with statutory recovery interest. Deliberately fraudulent export zero-rating claims, false invoicing, or aiding in unlawful VAT refunds may attract criminal penalties, including substantial fines and imprisonment under Omani tax law, in addition to full recapture of the VAT underdeclared and exclusion from future export refund schemes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current-account transactions (IMF Article VIII), with the CBO pegging the currency at USD 2.6008/OMR (buy/sell spread of approximately USD 0.3845/OMR). Capital-account flows for 100% foreign-owned SPCs are liberalized: share-capital injections, capital-account loans, and dividend or liquidation remittances are permitted without CBO pre-approval, but each transfer must be supported at the collecting bank by (i) audited financial statements demonstrating solvency and distributable reserves, (ii) an OTA tax-clearance certificate showing settlement or waiver of Oman corporate income tax (statutory band 3%-15% by profit segment) and the 10% dividend withholding tax applicable to the non-resident shareholder where no double-tax-treaty relief has been filed, and (iii) the signed board/shareholder resolution authorizing the wire. Payment institutions and Omani banks will block the outward leg if VAT returns are unfiled, the UBO declaration is stale, or the payment-rail settlement data (Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal) does not reconcile with customs-export documents.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory conversion ratio on export receipts; an export-proceeds surrender rule nevertheless obliges the SPC to repatriate all proceeds from the sale of goods manufactured in Oman into a CBO-licensed bank account within 180 days from the shipment date (Air Waybill / Bill of Lading date). The 180-day ceiling is applied by the trade-finance bank under the CBO Balance-of-Payments framework, and open-account terms exceeding 180 days require pre-approval from the collecting bank. Receipts may be retained in designated USD/EUR foreign-currency accounts, and the SPC must keep a platform-level reconciliation ledger matching each clearing settlement (Kickstarter, Stripe, PayPal) to the 10-digit HS code 9503-category export invoice and courier tracking number. Banks are required to file a suspicious-transaction report if proceeds are held offshore beyond 180 days or if realized amounts diverge from the declared customs value without a documented allowance or refund adjustment.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - Exchange Control and Foreign Exchange Directorate, acting together with the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) on inward-FDI registration, outward-remittance compliance, and profit-repatriation clearance under Royal Decree 50/2019 (Foreign Capital Investment Law).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI providers are not licensed by the CBO and are legally barred under the Omani Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) from taking deposits or providing correspondent settlement to an Omani resident SPC; relying on an unlicensed EMI as the main bank account would constitute prohibited deposit-taking activity and would itself become an AML red flag for any Omani bank subsequently onboarded. Acceptable supplementary role: the EMI or payment processor may serve exclusively as the collection sub-processor for Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal flows, with an automated cascade of net proceeds into the local Omani bank account within T+3 of settlement; the SPC must disclose this cascade structure in its CDD interview and must not allow the EMI balance to exceed USD 25,000 at any month-end.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The 100% shareholder must inject equity via a SWIFT MT103 with field 70 (Remittance Information) precisely tagged: 'FDI SHARE CAPITAL - [CR number] - [Company legal name] - RD 50/2019 EQUITY INJECTION - UBO [full name].' The beneficiary should be the SPC's dedicated OMR or USD account at a licensed Omani bank. No CBO prior approval is required for inward FDI in the toys-and-games manufacturing segment (ISIC 3240); the receiving bank will classify the flow under BOP code 1111 (Direct Investment Inflow), file the FDI registration with OTA, and request: the notarized shareholder resolution / capital statement, six months of source-of-funds statements, and, if cumulative injections exceed OMR 5 million, the OTA foreign-investment certificate. Wires originating from any account other than the registered shareholder's own account are default-blocked, and split transfers below OMR 3,000 are discouraged to avoid structuring typologies.Supplier payments for resin, silicone molds, pigments, and packaging are executed via SWIFT MT103, with field 70 referencing the SAB customs declaration number and the commercial invoice. Every outbound wire is screened against UN/EU/GCC sanctions lists and the CBO external-transfer validation grid; the SPC must upload the underlying invoice, packing list, AWB/BL, and certificate of origin to the bank's trade portal within five business days of the payment. Where the amount claimed by the supplier exceeds the Customs-cleared import value by more than 10% without a justified cost adjustment, the bank places the account under enhanced monitoring and may request the original brokerage statement.OMR/USD outright deliverable forwards: permitted due to the CBO-managed USD peg; Omani banks offer tenors up to 12 months against documented cash-flow exposure, with zero premium in the pegged rate band.EUR/OMR and GBP/OMR deliverable forwards: available from CBO-licensed banks for one to twelve months, requiring underlying payment-rail settlement evidence from Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal; these hedge the SPC's euro and sterling pricing risk before conversion into OMR.FX swaps and rolling spot transactions through the bank's dealing desk: permitted for working-capital management with BOP reporting form filed for each leg.Cross-currency swaps: admissible only for approved project or investment flows above OMR 5 million and subject to explicit CBO pre-authorization; not applicable at this SPC's retail scale.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs): expressly NOT permissible as an onshore instrument; the CBO only recognizes deliverable forwards. Any offshore NDF hedging an Omani corporate exposure is treated as an unauthorized capital-account operation and the resulting gain or loss is excluded from the OTA hedging exemption, triggering a tax and BOP reporting violation.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: LOW as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI providers are not licensed by the CBO and are legally barred under the Omani Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) from taking deposits or providing correspondent settlement to an Omani resident SPC; relying on an unlicensed EMI as the main bank account would constitute prohibited deposit-taking activity and would itself become an AML red flag for any Omani bank subsequently onboarded. Acceptable supplementary role: the EMI or payment processor may serve exclusively as the collection sub-processor for Kickstarter/Stripe/PayPal flows, with an automated cascade of net proceeds into the local Omani bank account within T+3 of settlement; the SPC must disclose this cascade structure in its CDD interview and must not allow the EMI balance to exceed USD 25,000 at any month-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Oman's Income Tax Law does not prescribe an OMR threshold for mandatory Local File or Master File. However, all related-party cross-border transactions must satisfy the arm's-length standard, and the Tax Authority may demand contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation during an audit. Practical threshold: maintain a transfer pricing study whenever annual related-party procurement, royalties, management fees, or intercompany financing exceeds OMR 100,000 or would reduce Oman taxable income materially.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply a standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute comparable to other Gulf jurisdictions. Nevertheless, the Tax Authority will test real commercial substance in anti-avoidance determinations: board meetings, local decision-making, warehouse, employees, contracts, and bank flows. A foreign-owned SPC must not operate as a mailbox entity; otherwise it risks deemed residence or PE recharacterization and loss of treaty benefits.
