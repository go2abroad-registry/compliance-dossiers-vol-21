Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General for Taxation (SGT) of the Sultanate of Oman, operating under the Ministry of Finance; official electronic portal: Oman Tax Portal (https://taxoman.om).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) via the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) electronic registry; 100% foreign ownership is permitted and no minimum paid-up capital is statutorily required. Step 2: Apply online at the Oman Tax Portal for the electronic Tax Identification Number (e-TIN) after commercial registration, submitting the tax-registration form (Arabic/English) within the statutory period, generally within 3 months from commencing business activity. Step 3: Apply separately for VAT registration through the SGT VAT module only where annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500; voluntary VAT registration is available at the OMR 19,250 threshold. Step 4: The SGT issues a single integrated TIN for income tax and VAT purposes; subsequent amendments, deregistration, and annual filings are performed through the same authenticated portal account.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the foreign-owned SPC is an Omani-resident juridical person taxed as a standalone corporate entity under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 25/2024). It is subject to the standard corporate income tax rate of 15% on Omani-source taxable income; passive dividend inflows from qualifying subsidiaries are capable of benefiting from participation-exemption or treaty-based relief in a pure equity-holding structure. The SPC is not fiscally transparent and no pass-through eligibility exists in Oman for a limited-liability company.

Available election forms

Oman applies mandatory classification by legal form; no US-style check-the-box election and no domestic hybrid-mismatch election are available. The principal elective mechanisms are: (i) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 19,250 annual-turnover threshold; (ii) application to the SGT for interpretive rulings or advance pricing arrangements regarding dividend participation exemption, source character, and permanent-establishment attribution; and (iii) election of the simplified VAT invoice in permitted circumstances. While Oman is an OECD/G20 BEPS Inclusive Framework member, Action 2 hybrid-mismatch rules have not yet been codified into Omani domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (mandatory registration where annual taxable supplies, deemed supplies, or imports for business use exceed OMR 38,500); OMR 19,250 (voluntary registration threshold). A pure equity-holding SPC whose activity is limited to receiving dividends is generally outside the scope of VAT (no taxable supply), and therefore is not obliged to register for VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported outside the Sultanate in accordance with the Customs Law and evidenced within the statutory export-completion window; services directly connected to exported goods (transport, handling, loading); international sea and air transport of goods or passengers; and certain offshore/digital services supplied to a customer resident outside Oman. For a pure equity-holding SPC, dividend receipts are supplies outside the scope of VAT and cannot be zero-rated; the SPV would only engage zero-rating if it undertakes qualifying export transactions, which is not inherent to passive shareholding.

  • Customs Export Declaration and corresponding electronic Customs clearance record
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical export movement out of Oman
  • Commercial invoice issued in the name of the exporting SPC
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank statement evidencing receipt of foreign-currency consideration from the overseas buyer

The SGT has issued a phased e-invoicing framework requiring B2B and B2G transactions to be issued as structured electronic invoices in XML/JSON formats, transmitted through SGT-certified solution providers or the national clearance portal, and validated against Oman Tax Portal systems including digital stamping/QR code generation with a unique fiscal code before each invoice becomes legally effective. Implementation proceeds in compliance waves based on taxable turnover; transactions outside the scope of VAT, such as pure dividend distributions of a passive holding SPC, are excluded from the e-invoicing clearance mandate.

The SGT may re-audit zero-rated export claims retrospectively for up to 5 fiscal years. Where the mandatory documentation bundle is incomplete, false, or foreign-currency consideration is not repatriated within the 180-day deadline, the zero-rating is retroactively reversed, VAT is assessed at the standard 5% rate, refunded amounts are clawed back, and the taxpayer becomes liable to late-payment penalties (statutorily graduated and generally capped at 100% of assessed tax). Fraudulent or negligent fabrication of export documents triggers the VAT Law penalty schedule, including administrative fines per violation, aggravated penalties of up to double the tax evaded in serious cases, potential suspension or forced cancellation of VAT registration, and referral of the responsible individuals to the Public Prosecution for criminal tax-evasion proceedings, carrying fines up to OMR 50,000 and/or imprisonment of up to 5 years upon conviction.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman applies a liberal, non-exchange-control regime for both current and capital account transactions. The Omani rial (OMR) is fully convertible and is pegged to the United States dollar at USD 1 = OMR 0.3845. Dividends, interest, loan repayments, capital contributions, and disposal proceeds can be repatriated abroad without CBO prior approval or sweeping foreign-investment lock-in. The only practical remittance hurdles are documentary: the application of Oman Tax Authority withholding tax (10% on applicable dividends/interest/royalties unless reduced by a double-tax treaty) and the KYC/source-of-funds evidence required by the paying bank. No CBO convertibility cap applies to an Omani-resident SPC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under Omani law and CBO practice there are no mandatory surrender timelines, no minimum conversion ratios, and no requirement to sell export proceeds to the banking system. Exporters and SPVs may hold foreign-currency revenue, lend it, invest it abroad, or convert to OMR at market rates. For a pure-equity holding SPV this is irrelevant to dividend and capital flows, but if intercompany service or export income ever arises, the same permissive retention treatment applies. The SPV should preserve SWIFT MT103 records and commercial documents to evidence source of funds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — Sultanate of Oman

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low / legally constrained. Cross-border digital EMI providers (Wise, Revolut Business, Payoneer, Currenxie, etc.) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman as banks or payment institutions, do not have Omani IBAN access to OEPSS/local clearing, and cannot act as the primary treasury counterparty of the SPV. Use of an offshore EMI increases the AML risk classification, creates correspondent-bank frictions, and is not recognized as a substitute for a licensed Omani corporate bank account. Such platforms may be used only for ancillary low-value payments with complete audit trails and after the licensed bank relationship has been established.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections should be remitted from the foreign shareholder's bank by SWIFT wire (MT103 for standard amounts; MT202/202 COV for large interbank movements) to the SPC's operational account at Bank Muscat, NBO, or HSBC Oman. The beneficiary field must show the SPC's registered legal name and Commercial Registration number; the payment narrative must state 'Share capital contribution / equity injection' and use the bank's foreign-investment reason code (ISO purpose 'EQUI' or equivalent). No CBO prior approval is required, but the bank will demand board resolution, subscription instrument, UBO declaration, source-of-funds evidence, and proof of MCIIP/e-investment registration. The amount should match the registered capital in the constitutional documents; any excess contribution should be documented as additional paid-in capital or shareholder loan to avoid reclassification as undeclared deposit and to ensure accurate Oman Tax Authority recording.For a pure-equity SPV, all material payments must use traditional interbank rails: SWIFT MT103 for standard third-party and shareholder payments, MT202/202 COV for interbank transfers, and Omani local ACH/RTGS for OMR-denominated domestic payments. Commercial letters of credit (sight or usance under UCP600) can be established through Bank Muscat, NBO, or HSBC for any underlying trade settlement by operating subsidiaries. Dividend/interest remittances to non-resident shareholders must be supported by board dividend resolutions, audited financial statements, and Oman Tax Authority withholding-tax receipts or applicable treaty-exemption certificates.
