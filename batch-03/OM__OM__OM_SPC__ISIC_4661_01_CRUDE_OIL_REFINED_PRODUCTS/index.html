Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (هيئة الضرائب العمانية); statutory successor to the Secretariat General of Taxation; e-services and registration portal: https://taxes.gov.om; Tax Management System (TMS) for registrations, returns and payments; customs clearance window: Bayan (Oman Customs).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 – Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate plus notarized constitutional documents (MOA/AOA). Step 2 – Apply to the Tax Authority for mandatory tax registration within three months of commencing business (Income Tax Law, Royal Decree 28/2009); submit CR, constitutional documents, Oman-resident leasing/licence evidence, EOI form, and the ultimate beneficial owner (UBO) declaration; receive the unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) used for income tax, VAT and customs. Step 3 – File VAT registration within 30 days after the month in which the OMR 500,000 mandatory threshold is exceeded; voluntary registration is available from OMR 100,000. Step 4 – Activate the TMS taxpayer file, appoint a tax agent (wajib? no, 'licensed tax consultant/lawyer') where relevant, and enrol for phased e-invoicing and Bayan export declaration permissions.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box or pass-through election for commercial SPCs; the SPC is per se a taxable juridical person on entity-level profits. Available statutory elections: (i) choice of financial year-end other than the calendar year, subject to Tax Authority approval; (ii) cash-basis determination for small businesses under conditions in the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009, as amended); (iii) voluntary VAT registration within the OMR 100,000–500,000 band; (iv) monthly versus quarterly VAT return cycles where the regulations permit; (v) zero-rated export determination elections (goods vs. services) documented in the tax file. No hybrid mismatch or entity-classification election is recognized in Omani domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 in annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration: OMR 100,000 to <OMR 500,000; non-resident suppliers of digital/electronic services to Omani consumers are registrable without threshold (reverse-charge safeguard applies where the supplier is unregistered).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export claims under RD 121/2020 and the Executive Regulations require: (a) the SPC holds valid VAT registration; (b) title/ownership passes to a non-resident buyer; (c) goods depart the Sultanate's customs territory within the statutory window (90 days from the date of supply) under an Omani customs export declaration lodged in Bayan; (d) for marine fuel/bunker supplies, the vessel is a foreign-going ship engaged in international trade and the supply is placed on board as ship's stores for voyage beyond Omani/GCC territorial waters; (e) services qualify only where the recipient is outside Oman and the service is not connected with Omani land/movables. All supporting documents must be in hand before the VAT return is filed and preserved for five years.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan electronic single-window clearance record)
  • Original on-board Bill of Lading (or Sea Waybill / mate's receipt for bunker deliveries)
  • Commercial invoice / VAT zero-rated tax invoice referencing the export or ship's stores nature of supply
  • Packing list, cargo manifest and port/terminal delivery receipts
  • Certificate of Origin (GCC or issuing Chamber of Commerce) where contractually required
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Swift MT103 bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export consideration in Oman
  • Delivery and arrival confirmation at destination (port discharge certificate) or foreign-going vessel call and departure records
  • Charter party or international transport/supply contract for the export movement

Mandatory transitional e-invoicing program administered by the Tax Authority: VAT-registered taxpayers must generate tax invoices electronically, adhere to the National Electronic Invoicing framework (structured machine-readable e-invoice, digital signature, QR/security stamp) and exchange invoices through cleared channels on the taxes.gov.om/TMS API. Implementation is phased by taxpayer category, starting with government transactions and large taxpayers (continuous transaction control and pre-clearance in rollout); all taxpayers must retain digital fiscal archives for the statutory 5-year period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible on both current and capital account transactions. The currency is pegged to the United States dollar at 0.3845 OMR per USD, which removes bilateral convertibility risk for all petroleum-product trading, invoicing, and settlement flows. Oman maintains no general exchange-control restriction on import payments, profit remittance, or foreign capital liquidation; the sole constraints are documentary: CBO-licensed banks require a tax clearance certificate from the Oman Tax Authority before dividend or profit repatriation, and withholding tax at 10% applies to certain non-resident remittances. Capital injections into the SPC are registered under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019), which permits 100% foreign ownership without prior capital-authorization approval for the wholesale petroleum brokerage license class.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a statutory mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts, nor any percentage-based surrender requirement; the OMR/USD peg makes any conversion ratio economically neutral. The operative rule, imposed through CBO trade surveillance circulars and standard facility agreements, is full repatriation of 100% of the export invoice value into an Omani authorized dealer account within 180 days from the bill-of-lading date for crude-oil and refined-petroleum-product exports. Receipts may be retained in USD foreign-currency accounts without conversion, provided the USD exposure is reconciled monthly against the SPC trade ledger and the bank's periodic CBO reporting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Muscat, Sultanate of Oman; exercising regulatory oversight under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Foreign Exchange and Capital Account Framework, and the AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 79/2016); coordinated operationally with the Capital Market Authority and the National Anti-Money Laundering Committee.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. The CBO does not license offshore digital EMIs as first-tier settlement banks; therefore a physically present SPC engaged in ISIC 4661 crude-oil and refined-products wholesale brokerage must hold its principal liquidity and clearing accounts with a CBO-licensed commercial bank. Licensed fintech payment service providers may support collections and reconciliation dashboards but cannot hold high-value cargo settlement escrow in USD without a corresponding CBO-licensed custodian. Engagement with unlicensed cross-border EMIs as primary clearing rails is non-compliant and would be categorized as a reporting trigger under the AML/CFT Law R.D. 79/2016.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for inbound equity: the parent bank originates a SWIFT MT103 wire designated as Additional Paid-in Capital for Foreign Direct Investment in Oman, routed via a single clearing corridor such as JP Morgan, HSBC, or Citi to the SPC's USD or OMR account at Bank Muscat S.A.O.G.; the MT103 narrative must cite the CRO number, the Omani CBO Corporate Identification Code, and the Foreign Capital Investment Law R.D. 50/2019 license reference. The receiving bank issues an inward foreign-investment certificate essential for future capital repatriation and dividend remittance. Intercompany loan funding must use MT103 with the purpose code designating intercompany trade-finance subordination, attaching the signed subordination agreement and the latest group audited financial statements. Fragmentation across multiple intermediary banks must be avoided to preserve CBO traceability and the SWIFT UETR chain integrity for the full G20 LSI compliance.Primary clearing rails for outbound flows: SWIFT MT103 for fixed-price invoice settlements and marine-fuel procurement; SWIFT MT202 COVER for treasury liquidity transfers between the SPC's Omani tank and its trade-finance counterpart in the Dubai International Financial Centre; SWIFT MT700 and MT710 for cargo-backed purchasing via commercial letters of credit issued in USD on a sight tenor under UCP600; and SWIFT MT760 for standby letters of credit issued to suppliers as performance security under ISDA and industry fuel-supply terms. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 aggregate annual related-party transaction value triggers mandatory Local File documentation; where consolidated group turnover exceeds OMR 5,000,000, a Master File and country-by-country reporting (CbCR) threshold are activated. All controlled transactions above the threshold require a contemporaneous TP study (OECD TPG-compliant).

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code comparable to the UAE; however, the Oman Tax Authority applies substance-based audit tests under the Permanent Establishment doctrine and Anti-Avoidance Rules in Royal Decree 28/2009. A pure letterbox entity without physical office, local staff, bank account, or actual fuel-brokerage signature authority in Oman is not recognised as a valid tax-resident SPC. The tax authority may deem the Oman entity a sham and reallocate profits to the actual place of management (offshore). For wholesale petroleum product trading, the business must demonstrate real, locally staffed back-office operations, credit valuation, freight negotiation, and counterparty acceptance, with board minutes, employment contracts, and physical presence.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Understating Taxable Profits

Statutory sanction: Additional tax under assessment equal to 15% interest per annum on underpayment; a fine of 100% of the evaded tax or OMR 20,000, whichever is greater; criminal prosecution with imprisonment up to 5 years; revocation of petroleum trading licence.

Director personal liability: Directors are jointly and severally liable for evaded tax if they signed, approved, or directing accounting records; court may issue travel ban, asset freezing order, and corporate veil piercing to recover unpaid taxes from personal assets; custodial sentence is available for directors knowingly involved in the fraud.
Shell Company / Letterbox Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Application of General Anti-Avoidance Rule (GAAR) and denied treaty benefits; recharacterisation of commercial transactions and allocation of profit to actual place of management; 15% tax on deemed Oman-source income; additional penalty up to 25% of adjusted tax due.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face direct liability for corporate failure; courts pierce the veil and order restitution of the full tax shortfall plus penalties; travel bans are commonly issued; nominee director may be personally guilty of 'false declaration' under the Omani Penal Code, punishable by imprisonment up to 3 years.
AML and Sanctions Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Oman AML Law (RD 30/2016) imposes fines from OMR 5,000 to OMR 20,000 per violation; cumulative penalties can reach OMR 50,000 for multiple prohibited transactions; licence suspension or revocation; confiscation of proceeds and instrumentalities used in the payment rails.

Director personal liability: Compliance Officer, MLRO, and each director are personally liable for failures in AML governance; asset freeze and travel bans immediately; criminal imprisonment up to 5 years and personal fines up to OMR 20,000; shareholder liability may extend to compensating victims of the suspicious transaction flow.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Documentary Fraud

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustment to bring taxable income to the 15% rate; penalty equal to 15% to 25% of the adjustment amount; public disclosure of tax debt; criminal indictment for submitting fabricated transfer pricing documentation (imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to OMR 10,000).

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the tax return with knowledge of TP mispricing become personally liable for penalties and interest; travel ban and property freezing are imposed during criminal investigation; if conviction occurs, disqualification from directorship, personal bankruptcy declaration, and restitution equal to the evaded tax.
