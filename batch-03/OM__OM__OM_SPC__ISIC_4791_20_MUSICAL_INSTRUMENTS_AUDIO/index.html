Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A pure virtual/mail-forwarding address with no physical situs is not acceptable as the SPC registered office under the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree No. 18/2019) and Commercial Register Law. For an e-commerce audio-instruments SPC (ISIC 4791), MoCIIP will accept a serviced office, flexi-desk, or business-center address within Oman provided: (i) the address is a licensed physical commercial premises capable of receiving official mail and service of process; (ii) the name plate and basic physical records (shareholders register, contracts, statutory books) are available on site; and (iii) the service-provider lease agreement is notarized when required. Home-office or digital-only structures are generally not permitted for an SPC; however, a licensed co-working operator can meet the commercial substance threshold. The risk of statutory challenge is material if the registered address is later discovered to be a mail-forwarding box only.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (الهيئة الضريبية; 'Tax Authority'), established under Royal Decree No. 39/2018; electronic filing via the unified Emtaaz digital tax platform at https://taxy.gov.om. Commercial registration is administered separately by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 — Incorporate the Foreign-Owned Single Person Company (SPC) at MOCIIP (100% foreign ownership permitted under Royal Decree No. 50/2019 Foreign Capital Investment Law; no statutory minimum paid-up capital) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. Step 2 — File Corporate Income Tax registration with the Tax Authority through Emtaaz to obtain the permanent Tax Card / Tax Identification Number (TIN); this master record covers all taxes administered by the Authority. Step 3 — Once 12-month taxable supplies (or anticipated supplies) exceed the mandatory VAT threshold of OMR 38,500, file online VAT registration and obtain a VAT Registration Number (VRN); a voluntary election is available from OMR 19,250. Step 4 — Register on the Mersad customs electronic single-window platform for physical import/export clearance and obtain the customs numeric code linked to the CR/TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The SPC is a separate juridical person with limited liability, distinct from its sole shareholder; not eligible for pass-through or transparent treatment for Omani tax purposes. It is treated as a tax-resident company by place of incorporation and is liable to Corporate Income Tax (Income Tax Law, Royal Decree No. 28/2009 as replaced by Royal Decree No. 19/2023 for tax periods beginning on or after 1 January 2025) at the 15% standard rate; no shareholder-level taxation is imposed on distributions made to residents, and no tax is levied on the SPC's export turnover beyond VAT compliance obligations.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available in Omani law; entity classification is statutory. Available elections: (1) VAT Tax Group registration election for related persons/subsidiaries with joint and several liability; (2) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 19,250 turnover threshold; (3) small-business simplified CIT treatment for eligible enterprises below the statutory revenue ceiling; and (4) joint-venture economic entity election for jointly performed contracts. Foreign-owned SPCs must document any election in writing via the Emtaaz portal and maintain contemporaneous supporting records.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 of taxable supplies or imports in the preceding 12 months, or expected in the following 12 months. Voluntary registration: OMR 19,250. Non-resident suppliers of digital/remote services may be required to register in Oman when the reverse-charge mechanism does not apply to the non-registered local customer.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (1) goods physically exported by or on behalf of the taxable supplier from Oman to a destination outside Oman and outside the GCC, provided the goods leave Oman within 90 days of the date of supply; (2) supplies of cross-border services to a non-resident recipient not present in Oman and where the benefit is not consumed in Oman, including offshore warranty and technical-support services ancillary to audio gear sales; and (3) supplies transferred to approved free zones/special economic zones (e.g., Duqm SEZ, Salalah Free Zone) with customs-controlled movement. For the online retail of musical instruments and studio audio equipment, zero-rating is recognized where the supplier is the exporter of record, the buyer's delivery address is outside Oman, and the delivery is effected via international courier or freight forwarder under a transport document naming the SPC as consignor.

  • Mersad customs export declaration (outbound customs single declaration) with zero-rated VAT code
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill / international courier waybill evidencing physical departure from Oman within 90 days of supply
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export sales proceeds into the SPC's Oman bank account
  • Commercial invoice stating HS 9207.00 (musical instruments) / 8518.xx (audio gear) codes, Incoterms, and the zero-rating statutory basis
  • Packing list and freight booking manifest
  • Buyer's identity record and overseas delivery address proof retained under customer-due-diligence norms

No mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, e-invoice clearance API, or digital tax stamping is currently in force. The Tax Authority has announced a phased e-invoicing roadmap for roll-out from 2025; until activation, taxpayers must issue conventional e-invoices (XML/PDF) and submit periodic VAT returns electronically through Emtaaz within 28 days after the end of each tax period.

The Tax Authority conducts document-matching audits against shipping manifests, customs exit records, courier tracking, and bank remittance data. Failure to evidence export within the 90-day window, or submission of forged transport or remittance documentation, results in retrospective clawback of the 0% rate with VAT assessed at 5% plus late-payment penalties. Fraudulent export claims constitute tax evasion under the Oman VAT Law, attracting a penalty of up to 100% of the tax evaded, additional administrative fines of up to OMR 50,000, suspension/revocation of the commercial registration, imprisonment of responsible directors up to 5 years, and personal liability of the SPC's sole shareholder and manager. Tax records, customs documents, and FX inward remittance certificates must be retained for the statutory minimum five-year period, extended to ten years where required by the Tax Authority.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is a fully convertible IMF Article VIII currency pegged to the US Dollar at 1 OMR = USD 2.60076 (USD 1 = OMR 0.3845). Current-account convertibility is unrestricted; capital inflows into a 100% foreign-owned Oman SPC are freely permitted but must be registered with the CBO through a licensed bank to secure a Certificate of Capital Registration for future repatriation. Profit and dividend remittance requires: (i) compliance with annual corporate income tax (15% CIT), (ii) settlement of the 10% withholding tax on outbound dividends, and (iii) presentation of the company's valid tax card and Commercial Registration (CR) certificate to the Omani bank at each remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBO exchange-control requirements derived from the Banking Law, proceeds from online retail exports of musical instruments and audio gear must be repatriated and credited to an account maintained with a licensed bank in Oman within six months from the date of shipment/invoice. No mandatory percentage of proceeds must be converted to OMR because the USD peg makes foreign-currency retention permissible; exporters may keep USD in a foreign-currency current account or request conversion at the CBO-published OMR/USD band, subject to supporting trade documentation (order-level statements, invoices, courier waybills and Stripe/PayPal payout records).

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), the Sultanate's central bank, foreign-exchange control authority and prudential banking supervisor. The CBO's Banking Supervision and Foreign Exchange Operations departments administer the Banking Law of the Sultanate (Royal Decree 114/2000), all licensed-bank foreign-currency positions, export-proceeds repatriation, and CBO capital-import registration for direct foreign investment.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat corporate income tax rate on taxable income of commercial and industrial entities under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended by Royal Decree 9/2017); the former 12% band on the first OMR 30,000 of taxable income was eliminated for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2017.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0%; Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividend distributions made by an Omani-resident company, including an SPC, to its non-resident sole shareholder. The distribution is made free of Omani withholding tax at source.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Oman Transfer Pricing Regulations 2023 (effective for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2023): a Local File is mandatory when aggregate annual related-party transactions reach or exceed OMR 5,000,000; a Master File is required when the ultimate parent's consolidated annual group revenue reaches or exceeds OMR 100,000,000. Documentation must be contemporaneous, available in Arabic or English, and produced within 30 days of an OTA notice; a TP disclosure schedule is attached to the annual corporate income tax return.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) framework equivalent to the UAE model. Nonetheless, the OTA applies an economic substance doctrine through the general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) in the Income Tax Law, the beneficial-ownership register under the Commercial Companies Law, and the requirement that a Single Person Company carry on genuine business from a fixed place in Oman. An SPC used purely as an invoicing conduit for online sales of musical instruments and audio gear, with no local staff, no local management and no substantive inventory or logistics function, risks recharacterisation as a sham entity or as the PE of a foreign principal, with profits reallocated and prior exemptions denied.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the evaded tax plus the 10% late-payment penalty and 1% monthly surcharge; a criminal fine of OMR 1,000 to OMR 10,000 and imprisonment of up to 3 years under the Income Tax Law and Omani penal provisions; loss of the SPC's tax clearance certificate, which blocks commercial licences renewal and customs transactions.

Director personal liability: The sole director/shareholder who signs a false return is personally liable for the evaded tax and penalties; OTA can impose travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and the criminal court may order confiscation of assets.
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement / GAAR

Statutory sanction: OTA may disregard the SPC's separate legal personality, reattribute the whole e-commerce gross profit to the SPC or to the non-resident principal, deny treaty benefits and the 0% dividend withholding position, and impose tax on the true economic beneficiary; under GAAR the tax difference is collected with a penalty of up to 100% of the underpaid tax plus 1% monthly surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly participate in a sham structure face civil restitution claims and joint assessment; travel bans and asset freezes are routinely issued pending the tax audit; a criminal conviction for fraudulent shell activity can carry imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Compulsory arm's-length adjustment of taxable income, reassessment of corporate income tax at 15%, a late payment surcharge of 10% plus 1% per month, and an administrative penalty for TP documentation failure of OMR 1,000 to OMR 10,000; in aggravated cases OTA may double the adjusted tax.

Director personal liability: Directors approving non-arm's-length intercompany prices can be held jointly liable for the adjusted tax and penalties; OTA may pursue director personal assets where the TP records are found to be fraudulent or fabricated.
Anti-Money Laundering and Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016, as amended), fines of up to OMR 100,000 per violation and imprisonment of up to 5 years for laundering of criminal proceeds; suspension of the SPC's Commercial Registration and revocation of payment processor agreements by Stripe and PayPal for compliance breaches.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer are personally accountable for inadequate AML controls; travel bans, prohibition from leaving Oman, freezing of personal assets and criminal prosecution of the natural person behind the SPC are available sanctions.
