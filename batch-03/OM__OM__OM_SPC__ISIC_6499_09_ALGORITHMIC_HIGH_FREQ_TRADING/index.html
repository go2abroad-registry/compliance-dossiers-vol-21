Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Single Person Company under Omani law; the sole shareholder declares the capital in the Articles of Association, and the amount must be paid into an Omani bank account before Commercial Registration is issued. Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority (ZTCA) of the Sultanate of Oman, successor to the Oman Tax Authority; direct tax, VAT and customs administration is conducted through the Taxomania e-Portal for tax returns, registrations and clearances, while customs declarations are processed through the Oman Customs single-window system.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) is incorporated under the Commercial Companies Law (Sultani Decree 18/2019) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. The SPC then creates an authenticated Taxomania user profile and submits the Commercial Registration certificate, constitutional documents, registered office evidence, shareholder and director identification, and board resolutions. The ZTCA activates a single Tax Identification Number (TIN) for income-tax purposes and, where mandatory or voluntary VAT registration is due, issues a separate VAT-registration number under the same TIN master record. The SPC is registered as a resident legal entity from the date of commercial registration or commencement of business in Oman, whichever is earlier; registration is completed online before the first tax filing period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — an Omani SPC is a juridical person separate from its sole foreign shareholder, is centrally managed and controlled in Oman, and is taxable on its worldwide income as an Omani resident company. The shareholder is not taxable or liable in Oman solely by reason of equity ownership, and the SPC has no statutory pass-through or transparency classification.

Available election forms

Oman has no check-the-box regime, no pass-through election and no hybrid-mismatch elective rules. The SPC is per se a taxable corporation under Omani law. Available administrative elections include transfer-pricing advance pricing agreements, VAT-group registration once the statutory eligibility criteria are met, and the election to claim a foreign-tax-credit relief for foreign tax paid on assessable income. An election made in the shareholder’s home jurisdiction to recharacterize the Omani SPC as transparent is ignored for Omani income-tax purposes, thereby avoiding hybrid mismatch deductions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 in annual taxable supplies, excluding exempt financial supplies, for mandatory VAT registration; OMR 19,250 for voluntary registration over a rolling twelve-month period. For an algorithmic market-making SPC, exempt financial services are not counted toward this threshold, but zero-rated services and separately chargeable data, connectivity or licensing supplies must be counted.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Oman zero-rates qualifying exports of goods and certain exported services. For goods, 0% VAT applies only where the goods physically depart Oman to a destination outside the GCC and the departure is evidenced by an accepted Customs Export Declaration. For services, 0% VAT applies to a direct supply to a non-resident who is outside Oman and does not use or benefit from the service in Oman. Proprietary algorithmic market-making and investment spreads are inherently exempt financial services and are not eligible for zero-rating unless a separate fee-bearing service is explicitly contracted to a non-Omani exchange, liquidity platform or offshore fund and the foreign-use test is proven. The SPC must substantiate any zero-rated service with a written contract, a certificate of non-residence or foreign VAT status, place-of-use evidence and proof of receipt of foreign consideration. Where input VAT is incurred on HFT infrastructure, telecommunications and data services, the recoverability of that input tax depends on the direct and exclusive attribution to a qualifying zero-rated supply; to the extent it is attributable to exempt financial services, input VAT is irrecoverable and must be apportioned or clawed back.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document) generated through the Oman Customs single window for any physical export of HFT hardware, servers, cryptographic modules or data-bearing media; not applicable to a pure offshore financial service.
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Oman together with the commercial invoice and packing list for any goods exported.
  • Electronic service contract, non-resident customer status file and place-of-use evidence for any zero-rated exported service.
  • Certificate of Inward Remittance or SWIFT MT103 credit confirmation from a licensed Omani bank proving that foreign consideration was paid into the SPC's Oman bank account within 180 days of the deemed supply date.

As of the effective stage of this dossier, Oman does not impose a fully deployed continuous transaction-control (CTC) QR-code or real-time fiscal-clearance mandate on all taxpayers. The ZTCA has, however, launched the national Mawatid e-invoicing framework; once an operative enforcement phase applies to the SPC by published resolution, the SPC must issue structured electronic tax invoices in machine-readable XML/UBL format, transmit them through the ZTCA API channel, and incorporate the tax-authority digital stamp. Until the applicable phase activates, the SPC must issue valid VAT invoices with sequential numbering, supplier and recipient tax identification numbers, supply description, tax-exclusive amount and the 5% VAT amount, and must retain all invoice and trade logs for not less than ten years.

The ZTCA reviews export files, freight movements, contracts, trade logs and bank confirmations before accepting a zero-rating claim. If the documentary chain does not prove the actual export or the foreign use of the service, the 0% rate is denied retroactively, the VAT is assessed from the original supply period, and late-payment interest and penalties are applied. Knowingly using forged invoices, false export declarations, or artificial offshore routing triggers tax-evasion penalties of up to 300% of the under-assessed tax, criminal fines for the responsible directors and possible imprisonment for up to five years. For an SPC that incorrectly zero-rates exempt financial services, the output tax is clawed back at 5% on the full gross fee, all attributable input VAT is disallowed, and an additional penalty of up to 100% of the avoided tax may be imposed; the assessment period is extended for fraud, and the sole shareholder remains exposed to liability to the extent of any distributed benefit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current account transactions including trade, service, and income remittance flows, subject only to standard AML/CFT documentary requirements. Capital account transactions are also substantially convertible: foreign shareholders may inject equity and repatriate capital and profits provided the entity has valid Commercial Registration, a tax clearance certificate from the Tax Authority of Oman, and no outstanding regulatory enforcement. The OMR is pegged to the USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD, which eliminates currency convertible risk but requires all banking conversions to be executed through CBO-licensed banks. Profit remittance hurdles are limited to final audited accounts being filed, a zero/nil tax liability certificate from the Tax Authority, and bank-level authentication of the ultimate beneficial ownership (UBO).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not apply mandatory surrender of export proceeds. An Omani exporter may retain 100% of foreign currency export receipts in a foreign currency account with a licensed domestic bank and convert at commercial rates without prior CBO approval. There is no statutory conversion timeline; however, exporters must ensure that each export receipt is substantiated by a customs export declaration, invoice, bill of lading, and the bank's standard inward remittance disclosure. Any force-conversion threshold is not established under Omani law, though CBO statistical self-declaration forms may be required for daily conversion equivalents above OMR 20,000.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), operating as the exclusive foreign exchange authority and prudential regulator for all licensed banks and financial institutions in the Sultanate of Oman.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs). The CBO does not recognise offshore EMI licences for the purpose of corporate settlement account access; customer money acceptance and payment execution are reserved to CBO-licensed commercial banks, exchange houses, and payment service providers authorised under the CBO's Payment Systems and Settlement regulation. An offshore EMI cannot independently provide bank-grade clearing rails in Oman. The only viable integration path is through a local licensed commercial bank acting as the settlement sponsor and sub-account operator, or by incorporating a CBO-authorised payment service provider in-country, which removes the cost advantage of the offshore EMI structure.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injections from the foreign parent must be transferred by SWIFT MT103 using the onward payment purpose 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital Injection'. The Omani beneficiary bank will apply the CBO's practice of issuing an Inward Foreign Remittance certificate only after verifying the Commercial Registration, a passed KYC interview, and a board resolution authorizing the account opening and capital increase. A first-stage capital escrow account may be required at the chosen Tier-1 bank to evidence the capital used for license-base regulations, after which funds are released to the operating account only when the CBO/CMA approval, if applicable, is granted. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% for taxable income exceeding OMR 100,000; 3% for taxable income up to OMR 100,000 (Income Tax Law, Royal Decree 38/2022, effective for tax periods beginning on or after 1 January 2023).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid by an Omani resident SPC to a non-resident shareholder (Royal Decree 38/2022); no WHT applies to distributions made to Omani tax-resident companies in respect of taxed profits; distributions sourced from exempt free-zone profits may be exempt.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four (4) months of the fiscal year-end (30 April for a 31 December year-end). An extension of up to six (6) months may be granted by OTA upon written application before the original deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000: contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation (Local File) is expected when aggregate controlled transactions exceed OMR 1,000,000 per tax year; a Master File and group disclosure schedule is expected where intra-group service, financing, or IP flows exceed OMR 5,000,000. An HFT SPC must maintain a full TP policy even below the threshold because OTA may invoke substance and GAAR rules.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not maintain a formal Economic Substance Regulations regime analogous to the UAE; however, Royal Decree 38/2022 includes a General Anti-Avoidance Rule and beneficial-ownership doctrine permitting OTA to disregard artificial, substance-less arrangements. The SPC is expected to demonstrate real local decision-making through licensed trading desks, co-location contracts, local risk and compliance officers, and profit allocation consistent with functions, assets, and risks.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – False or Suppressed Trading Returns

Statutory sanction: Restitution of the full tax evaded plus a 100% penalty and a monthly late-payment surcharge of 1% per month; criminal fine of up to OMR 25,000; referral to the Public Prosecutor; publication of the taxpayer's name in serious cases.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial sole shareholder who signed a knowingly false declaration are jointly and severally liable; travel bans, asset freeze orders, and imprisonment of up to three years may be imposed by Omani courts.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing – Offshore Recharacterisation

Statutory sanction: Primary adjustment under the arm's-length standard; secondary adjustment recharacterised as a constructive dividend subject to 10% WHT; administrative penalty of 10% of the under-assessed CIT plus applicable late-payment surcharges.

Director personal liability: Directors and tax officers who approved the transfer pricing policy may be personally assessed for the uncovered tax where the SPC is insolvent, with concurrent travel-ban execution.
Shell Company – Artificial Substance Avoidance

Statutory sanction: GAAR disregards the Omani entity or reattributes income to the foreign shareholder; CIT at 15% and dividend WHT at 10% are imposed on recharacterised distributions; the registry may strike off the SPC and cancel its tax card.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil where capital was clearly inadequate for the ultra-low-latency trading infrastructure; directors face civil restitution, travel bans under the civil enforcement regime, and criminal liability for tax evasion.
AML / Sanctions – Prohibited Clearing Flows

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 500,000 under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering and Combating Financing of Terrorism Law; cancellation of the CMA market licence; freezing of settlement accounts; confiscation of trading proceeds.

Director personal liability: Senior managers and compliance officers are personally liable for AML failures; travel bans, disqualification from directorships, and imprisonment of up to seven years may apply under money-laundering offences.
