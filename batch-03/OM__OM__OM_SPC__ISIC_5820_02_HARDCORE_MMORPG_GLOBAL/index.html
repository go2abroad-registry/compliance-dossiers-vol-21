Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The Sole Shareholder determines the capital in the Memorandum of Association, which must be fully subscribed, denominated in Omani Rials, deposited into a licensed Omani bank account, and evidenced by a bank capital-certificate before registration. No escrow account or phased payment is mandated.

Occupancy and workplace health clearances must be issued by the Ministry of Labour and the licensed civil engineer, covering ergonomic workstation arrangements, emergency exits, and fire extinguishers.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — electronic portal: Tax Portal at https://taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities forming a Single Person Company (SPC) in Oman must register for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) through the Oman Tax Authority portal. The SPC must submit its articles of association, Commercial Registration (CR) certificate issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, and proof of registered address. The application is filed online through the Tax Portal; upon approval, the OTA issues a TIN and VAT registration certificate where applicable. For VAT, the SPC must register if taxable supplies exceed the mandatory threshold; voluntary registration is available if the threshold is met. Foreign-owned SPCs with no local establishment but making taxable supplies may require registration through a tax representative.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the SPC is a separate legal person subject to corporate income tax at rates depending on income bracket; no general pass-through regime. Territorial scope: Oman taxes resident companies on Oman-sourced income and foreign income attributable to a permanent establishment in Oman. Non-resident shareholders distributing dividends from an SPC may be subject to withholding tax. Tax-Exempt Zone status may apply under the Special Economic Zone at Duqm (SEZAD) or Free Zones subject to separate tax incentives but not default for an SPC registered in the mainland.

Available election forms

Oman does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election for SPCs. An SPC may elect to be taxed as a partnership? No, SPC is a corporate entity. No hybrid mismatch elections are available under Omani tax law. The SPC may, however, elect to adopt IFRS for tax accounting, and may apply for advance pricing agreements or tax treaty relief on double taxation where relevant. No statutory entity classification election exists; the SPC is mandatorily taxed as a corporation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to countries outside the GCC are zero-rated for VAT upon satisfying customs export requirements. Exports of services related to real property or goods outside Oman may be zero-rated if supplied to a non-resident who is outside Oman at the time of supply, and the recipient does not receive the service in Oman. For digital gaming services, offshore services provided to non-resident customers (overseas app store users) can be zero-rated if the place of supply is outside Oman and evidence of export exists. Intra-GCC B2B supplies may be zero-rated if the recipient has a VAT number and goods are transported to another GCC state.

  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank statement showing receipt of foreign currency from abroad
  • Copy of commercial invoice
  • Proof of payment of consideration and foreign currency settlement
  • Copy of customer's non-resident proof and VAT registration certificate, if applicable

Oman does not yet mandate real-time e-invoicing clearance or digital fiscal stamping for B2B transactions as of the knowledge cutoff, but the Oman Tax Authority has announced phased implementation of electronic invoicing. Tax invoices must be issued for taxable supplies, and VAT returns are filed electronically via the OTA Tax Portal. For digital services and imported telecom/electronic services, the supplier may need to register under the simplified non-resident VAT regime; no real-time API clearance is currently operative.

The Oman Tax Authority may conduct audit and demand VAT refunds claimed in respect of zero-rated supplies. Penalties for false or fraudulent zero-rating claims include fines, imprisonment for tax fraud, retrospective cancellation of zero-rating, and recovery of VAT plus late payment penalties up to 25% of the unpaid tax. The Taxpayer Name and Shame procedure may apply for serious non-compliance. Customs penalties for improper export documentation include monetary fines and potential confiscation of goods.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman operates a broadly liberal current-account and capital-account convertibility regime. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Profit remittance and foreign-currency transfers for permitted current business purposes, including digital game publishing revenues, are freely allowed once corporate tax filings and economic substance obligations are current. Capital inflows by way of foreign equity contribution are permissible under the SPC ownership framework, although shareholder loans and direct foreign borrowings may require CBO notification or prior authorization depending on tenor and external indebtedness. No material dividend repatriation restriction exists for a compliant SPC.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a statutory export-proceeds surrender rule or mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts in foreign currency, including proceeds derived from Apple App Store and Google Play payouts for MMORPG subscriptions and in-app purchases, may be retained in foreign-denominated settlement accounts held at licensed Omani banks or remitted to the CBO-licensed authorised dealer. For corporate tax and financial reporting purposes, receipts must be converted into OMR or recorded using transparent exchange rates consistent with CBO-disclosed rates; however, no fixed regulatory timeline compels physical conversion.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for foreign investment authorization

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI providers and non-resident fintech wallet issuers cannot lawfully maintain primary OMR transaction accounts or serve as the core treasury rail for an Omani SPC. Only banks and financial institutions licensed by the CBO may operate payment accounts in Oman. Overseas EMI platforms such as Wise, Revolut Business, or Payoneer may support supplementary receipt of foreign platform revenues, but the corporate treasury backbone must remain a CBO-regulated commercial bank account to satisfy tax, audit, and regulatory traceability requirements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial equity or shareholder contribution must be routed by BIC-coded SWIFT MT103 transfer from the parent's bank to the Omani SPC's bank account. The remittance shall be clearly labelled in Field 70 with 'SHARE CAPITAL SUBSCRIPTION / OMAN SPC / CR NUMBER [CR-XXXX] / FOREIGN INVESTMENT FILE NO.' The receiving Omani authorised dealer will categorise the funds as a direct foreign equity inflow supported by the foreign investment approval, incorporation certificate, and SPC constitutional documents. If the inbound remittance is structured as a shareholder loan, a signed facility agreement and repayment schedule are required, and CBO prior approval thresholds may apply. The sender bank must perform source-of-funds compliance; the receiving bank will verify purpose, account name, and UBO classification before crediting.Operating expenditures, platform commissions, intellectual property royalties, software infrastructure fees, and international vendor payments should be settled through SWIFT MT103 outbound transfers from the Omani treasury account. For higher-value third-party licensing or physical equipment procurement, documentary commercial letters of credit opened through a CBO-licensed authorised dealer provide the preferred clearing rail because they preserve title documents, insurance, and settlement integrity. In-country OMR-denominated payments, including payroll, tax duties, social insurance premiums, and administrative costs, should be cleared through the CBO-operated electronic clearing system and Oman Real Time Gross Settlement System (ORTGS). Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% of taxable income under Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 9/2017), applicable to all non-state commercial entities, including a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% – Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividends distributed by an Omani SPC to its shareholder, whether resident or non-resident, provided the distribution is not a disguised return of capital and the company has met all CIT filing and payment obligations.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing is mandatory within 6 months after the end of the fiscal year; for a 31 December year-end, the statutory deadline is 30 June. The Oman Tax Authority may grant a one-time extension of up to 3 months upon a request filed before the original due date.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall

Enter the Omani SPC's TIN and Commercial Registration number. The foreign parent has no Omani TIN unless it has a permanent establishment in Oman; if the parent is a party to related-party transactions, its home jurisdiction TIN must be listed in the transfer pricing disclosure field. Late filing exposes the SPC to a fixed administrative fine range of OMR 100–OMR 1,000, a 1% per-month (or part thereof) late-payment surcharge up to 50% of the unpaid tax, potential automatic assessment from available taxpayer data, suspension of trade license document application, and enforced collection including attachment of bank accounts. Willful non-filing is referred for criminal prosecution.

Willful non-filing is referred for criminal prosecution.
Line 2 Deductible expenses: directly attributable operating, development, marketing, hosting, tax, legal, licensing and distribution costs; separately identify related-party payments to foreign affiliates. Support every deduction with a valid commercial invoice and bank transfer record. Transactions with the foreign parent or its affiliates must be priced at arm's length and be capable of supporting the arm's length position; otherwise the Tax Authority will disallow the deduction. Late filing exposes the SPC to a fixed administrative fine range of OMR 100–OMR 1,000, a 1% per-month (or part thereof) late-payment surcharge up to 50% of the unpaid tax, potential automatic assessment from available taxpayer data, suspension of trade license document application, and enforced collection including attachment of bank accounts. Willful non-filing is referred for criminal prosecution.
Line 4 Taxable income / adjusted accounting profit: after formal profit and loss account, Omani tax accounting adjustments, transfer pricing adjustments, prior-year loss set-off, and depreciation/amortization rules. Compute the Omani taxable profit without assuming foreign parent losses are available. Loss carryovers from the SPC may be applied for maximum 5 years from the year incurred, subject to continuity of ownership and business activity. Late filing exposes the SPC to a fixed administrative fine range of OMR 100–OMR 1,000, a 1% per-month (or part thereof) late-payment surcharge up to 50% of the unpaid tax, potential automatic assessment from available taxpayer data, suspension of trade license document application, and enforced collection including attachment of bank accounts. Willful non-filing is referred for criminal prosecution.
Box 6 Tax payable computation: taxable income multiplied by 15%, minus available foreign tax credits, offsetting any advance payments, and resulting final tax payable or refund claimed. Non-resident shareholder credits do not reduce the SPC-level Omani tax. Foreign tax credits are claimable only if the income is also subjected to foreign tax and a valid tax residency certificate provides the double-tax treaty basis. Late filing exposes the SPC to a fixed administrative fine range of OMR 100–OMR 1,000, a 1% per-month (or part thereof) late-payment surcharge up to 50% of the unpaid tax, potential automatic assessment from available taxpayer data, suspension of trade license document application, and enforced collection including attachment of bank accounts. Willful non-filing is referred for criminal prosecution.
Box 8 Transfer pricing disclosure and related-party transaction schedule: aggregate values of sales, purchases, royalties, interest, cost-sharing and loans with related parties. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 10,000,000 (Omani Rials) aggregate related-party transactions; when exceeded, the taxpayer must prepare a Local File and, for multinational groups, a Master File, and the TP disclosure schedule must be filed with the annual CIT return. Transactions below this threshold must still be arm's length and evidence must be contemporaneously retained.

Economic substance scope: Oman applies a substance-over-form doctrine in income tax administration. The Oman Tax Authority may disregard an SPC or treat a foreign parent as having an Omani taxable permanent establishment where core strategic decisions, employees, servers, or digital operations are in Oman while the taxpayer purports to have no local substance. No standalone Economic Substance Regulation filing is currently mandated for a pure MMORPG publisher, but the SPC must demonstrate real operational authority in Oman: board meetings in Oman, Omani bank account control, local order-to-cash management, and local bookkeeping. Digital platform activities that are managed and controlled from Oman will be assessed as taxable in Oman regardless of billing routing through Apple or Google.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Willful Understatement

Statutory sanction: Payment of the full evaded tax plus up to 50% penalty on the evaded amount, increased to 100% in fraud cases; criminal fine of up to OMR 2,000 and imprisonment for up to 5 years; publication of the conviction and debarment from future Oman Investment Authority approvals.

Director personal liability: Directors and tax representatives who signed the false return are personally jointly liable for the tax penalty and restitution if the SPC is insolvent; criminal conviction may carry a travel ban, asset freeze, and imprisonment of the responsible director.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance / Abusive Treaty Claim

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the substance abroad as a foreign permanent establishment in Oman or disallowance of the claimed treaty benefit; assessment of full 15% tax on revenue attributable to Oman plus late-payment surcharges; denial of dividend WHT relief; fines for failure to exhibit substance or maintain accounting records.

Director personal liability: Directors of the Omani SPC are exposed to personal responsibility for tax debts if the entity is found to be a sham; a foreign parent is also open to Omani court enforcement, which can include prohibitions against purchasing new Omani assets and travel restrictions during investigation.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of profits on an arm's length basis and additional tax at 15%; penalty of 10%–20% of the additional tax assessed; late-payment surcharge at 1% per month; in gross misstatement, referral for criminal fraud proceedings and a fine of up to OMR 1,000.

Director personal liability: A director who knowingly executed the non-arm's length arrangement is jointly liable with the company for the ultimate tax shortfall; the Tax Authority may pursue personal assets and impose a travel ban pending settlement.
AML and Sanctions Compliance with Global App Store Payouts

Statutory sanction: Under Oman AML law, fines from OMR 1,000 to OMR 200,000 per occurrence or up to twice the value of the illicit transaction; asset confiscation and cancellation of commercial registration; referral to the Public Prosecution for imprisonment; freezing of the Omani SPC accounts by the Central Bank of Oman.

Director personal liability: Directors and beneficial owners are personally liable for AML penalties where they knowingly or negligently permitted the SPC to accept proceeds in violation of the AML Law; personal travel bans, five-year disqualification from opening new corporate bank accounts, and criminal imprisonment of up to 7 years apply in aggravated cases.
