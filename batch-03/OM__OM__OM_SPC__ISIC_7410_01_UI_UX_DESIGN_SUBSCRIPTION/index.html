Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Ministry of Finance; e-Services portal: taxoman.gov.om (Muheet digital tax administration platform)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must obtain a Tax Registration Number (TRN) from the Oman Tax Authority before commencing taxable activity. Step 1: Incorporate the SPC under the Omani Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) and obtain the Commercial Register (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Step 2: Register on the OTA e-Services portal and file the income tax registration application, attaching the CR certificate, constitutional documents, authenticated passport copies of the foreign owner and manager, registered office lease, and anticipated turnover declaration. Step 3: Receive the income tax TRN certificate. Step 4: Where taxable supplies exceed or are expected to exceed OMR 500,000 in any rolling 12-month period, file a mandatory VAT registration application on the same portal; voluntary VAT registration is available from OMR 200,000. The TRN and VAT registration number may be consolidated in one taxpayer master record under the Muheet platform.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. Available statutory elections and options include: (i) election of a non-calendar financial year for income tax purposes subject to OTA approval; (ii) voluntary VAT registration upon exceeding OMR 200,000 of taxable supplies; (iii) small and medium enterprise relief under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended) applied by formal declaration for qualifying SMEs; (iv) advance pricing arrangement applications for cross-border design-subscription revenue; and (v) application for group relief or loss-carry-forward substantiation within the five-year statutory carry-forward window. Tax classification is non-transparent: the SPC is taxed as a separate juridical person at the standard corporate rate of 15% on worldwide income, with no pass-through attribute to the foreign shareholder.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of exported goods and offshore services is available under the Oman VAT Law where: (i) goods are physically exported and leave Oman/GCC customs territory within 90 days of supply with full customs evidence; (ii) services are supplied to a non-resident recipient who is neither registered nor obliged to be registered for VAT in Oman and who receives the benefit wholly outside Oman, applicable to UI/UX and digital design subscription services delivered electronically to overseas clients under an enforceable offshore contract; and (iii) supplies are directly related to international transport or exported goods. The taxpayer must issue a tax invoice annotated with the zero-rated code and retain the complete documentary chain proving exportation, offshore use of the service, and settlement from abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (where tangible goods or promotional kits form part of the supply)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / OTA-recognised customs release evidence
  • Signed UI/UX design subscription agreement with the foreign recipient
  • Commercial invoice issued under the zero-rated VAT code
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT/Wise credit evidence showing foreign-currency settlement
  • Offshore end-use statement confirming the recipient's foreign establishment and absence of Oman consumption nexus

Mandatory e-invoicing applies through the OTA 'Fatora' invoice clearance and digital stamping system. VAT-registered taxpayers must issue structured e-invoices, transmit invoice data to the OTA in real time or near-real time via approved API integration or the Fatora portal, and include an OTA-issued QR code/digital stamp on every tax invoice. Implementation is phased from 2023, commencing with the largest taxpayers (annual turnover exceeding OMR 100 million), then cascading to medium and smaller registrants; UI/UX design subscription businesses must maintain Fatora-compatible billing systems, including automated invoice clearance for cross-border zero-rated supplies.

The OTA conducts risk-based audits of zero-rated export claims using Fatora data, customs manifests, and bank settlement records. Failure to substantiate or fraudulent zero-rating triggers: (i) retrospective reversal of all input VAT recovered on the related procurement with statutory late-payment interest; (ii) fixed penalties up to OMR 5,000 per violation and OMR 500 per incorrect VAT return; (iii) for deliberate evasion, imprisonment of 1 to 5 years and/or fines up to OMR 100,000; (iv) customs-law sanctions and asset seizure for falsely declared exports; and (v) a look-back clawback period of up to five years where export documentation or FX repatriation evidence within the circa 180-day settlement window is missing, including deemed taxable treatment where consideration is not repatriated.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations since 1974; the Omani Rial (OMR) is de facto hard-pegged to the USD at 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR (1 OMR = 2.6008 USD). Both current-account and capital-account flows for a 100% foreign-owned SPC are freely convertible without prior CBO approval, subject only to bank due diligence, transaction purpose documentation, and statistical BOP reporting. Profit and dividend remittances are unrestricted once annual accounts are audited and Oman Tax Authority obligations (including 15% CIT and any 10% WHT) are settled or secured; no exchange-control consent decree is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory surrender ratio, retention timeline, or conversion requirement on export proceeds, including cross-border design-service export receipts. The SPC may hold receipts in OMR or any convertible currency, and banks must simply record each inflow as a BOP service transaction under the EBOPS business-services classification. The practical control is documentary: for receipts at or above the equivalent of OMR 10,000, the bank requires the underlying export invoice, subscription contract, and, where requested, a brief service-performance statement.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), monetary and banking supervisor established under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), with exchange-control administration assigned under the Foreign Exchange Law (Royal Decree 38/2000). The National Committee for Combating Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing (NCMLTFC) coordinates AML/CFT policy, while CBO's Banking Supervision Department enforces BOP and prudential reporting obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for standalone use. Stripe does not support Omani-incorporated merchants and Wise Business is not licensed by CBO as a deposit or payment institution; the CBO Payment Services Regulations (Circular BM 1295/2021) require on-shore licensing for payment services. Viable hybrid: collect subscription revenue through a CBO-licensed acquiring partner (e.g., PayTabs Oman or an approved gateway) or through the existing international gateway contract, then settle via SWIFT MT103 into the Omani operating account; all disbursements to unlicensed EMIs such as Wise must be individually disclosed to the relationship manager, supported by the underlying service agreement, and may be blocked absent such proof.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000; taxable income up to OMR 30,000 is subject to a reduced 3% bracket under RD 9/2017 as amended.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividend distributions or other profit remittances by an Omani resident SPC to its non-resident shareholder. The distribution is made free from Omani WHT; the shareholder may be taxable in its own jurisdiction, and the SPC's payment instruction does not require an OTA clearance.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 50,000 annual aggregate value of cross-border related-party transactions (invoices, subscriptions, royalty charges, service recharges, cost sharing). When this threshold is exceeded, the SPC must prepare and maintain in Arabic/English a contemporaneous transfer pricing file (Local File) and, if the ultimate parent group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 10 million, a Master File. A simplified arm's-length benchmark is required for each controlled transaction above OMR 10,000.

Economic substance scope: Although Oman has not promulgated a separate Economic Substance Regulation like the UAE, the Oman Commercial Companies Law, the tax GAAR (RD 9/2017), and the OECD-based PE tests require the SPC to have real substance in Oman: physical office, qualified UI/UX employees, management meetings and decision-making localised in Oman, and actual execution of the subscription-delivery workflow. A foreign-owned digital agency relying solely on a mailbox address and remote foreign contractors will likely be characterised as a sham/captive provider; OTA may disregard the SPC's corporate personality, assess tax in the hands of the foreign principal, and apply treaty limitation-of-benefits. The application of transfer pricing methods requires the SPC to prove its profit reflects a profit split or TNMM, not a residual royalty to the foreign shareholder.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion / concealment of subscription income

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the full evaded tax plus a penalty of three times the evaded amount; administrative fine up to OMR 10,000; publication of taxpayer name; criminal case referral to the Public Prosecutor.

Director personal liability: The designated manager and the beneficial non-resident shareholder who signed false declarations may face joint, several civil restitution of the evaded tax and personal travel bans; Oman courts have imposed imprisonment of up to five years under RD 9/2017 and the Omani Penal Code for fraudulent tax filings.
Sham or shell 'design-as-a-service' SPC

Statutory sanction: OTA strike-off from the tax register, nullification of the SPC, confiscation of the indirectly held contractual assets, and recharacterisation of the revenue as taxable profits of the non-resident owner; ancillary fines under the Commercial Companies Law can reach OMR 1,000.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil can be pierced: the shareholder is treated as the real taxable enterprise; personal assets may be seized; the non-resident director/UBO may be banned from future Omani business licences and subject to an Interpol travel ban on OTA request.
Transfer pricing adjustment failure

Statutory sanction: OTA may adjust taxable profit to an arm's-length amount, assess the resulting 15% tax, add a 1% monthly late-payment fine, and impose a TP penalty equal to 15% of the adjusted amount (or OMR 1,000 minimum). Under Audit, a false TP certificate may be treated as tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors who executed the below-market invoice are jointly and severally liable for the additional tax plus penalties if they profited from the scheme; the managing director's criminal liability arises if the pricing documentation is falsified.
