Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) — Commercial Registration Directorate, operating through the 'Invest Easy' electronic portal (www.investeasy.gov.om).

Governing statute

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 promulgating the Commercial Companies Law (Sultanate of Oman), especially Articles 264–276 (Single Person Company), read with ordinary LLC rules under Articles 229–263; Royal Decree No. 3/1975 as amended by Royal Decree No. 99/2016 (Commercial Register Law); and Executive Regulations issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Commercial Registration of a Single Person Company (SPC), accompanied by the notarized Memorandum of Association and Articles of Association in Arabic.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under RD 18/2019 unless mandated by sector-specific law; the subscribed capital is determined solely by the Memorandum of Association and must be fully subscribed by the sole foreign member. Capital is not required to be deposited in an escrow account for non-banking administrative services (ISIC 6430); proof of capital allocation is filed with the MoA.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense & Ambulance Authority (CDAA) fire safety certificate for the commercial office covering fire alarm and extinguisher systems, clear egress signage, and annual electrical inspection; structural load/occupancy certification from the municipality confirming safe floor loading for personnel and file storage; and Ministry of Labor workplace occupational safety clearance, including adequate ventilation, lighting, first-aid kit, and employee insurance policy.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under RD 18/2019 unless mandated by sector-specific law; the subscribed capital is determined solely by the Memorandum of Association and must be fully subscribed by the sole foreign member. Capital is not required to be deposited in an escrow account for non-banking administrative services (ISIC 6430); proof of capital allocation is filed with the MoA.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (Oman Tax Authority); official portals: https://taxoman.gov.om and https://tms.taxoman.gov.om. Customs functions relevant to export substantiation are administered by Oman Customs through the Bayan national single-window platform.

E-government filing portal

Foreign investors establishing an Oman Single Person Company (SPC) first complete incorporation through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP), obtaining the Commercial Registration (CR). After the CR and SPC constitutional documents are issued, the SPC must file the Tax Registration Application electronically via the Oman Tax Portal. Supporting documentation includes the attested CR, notarized and legalized constitutive documents, passport copies of the foreign shareholder, and, where applicable, an executed power of attorney appointing an Omani tax representative or tax agent. The Tax Authority issues a corporate tax registration number, and where actual or projected taxable supplies exceed the Omani VAT registration threshold, a separate 15-digit VAT registration number is issued through the same application. A non-resident person not yet physically present in Oman but required to charge or reclaim Omani VAT must register using the simplified non-resident registration route, normally through an Omani fiscal representative.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign investors establishing an Oman Single Person Company (SPC) first complete incorporation through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP), obtaining the Commercial Registration (CR). After the CR and SPC constitutional documents are issued, the SPC must file the Tax Registration Application electronically via the Oman Tax Portal. The Tax Authority issues a corporate tax registration number, and where actual or projected taxable supplies exceed the Omani VAT registration threshold, a separate 15-digit VAT registration number is issued through the same application. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No Check-the-Box, civil-law look-through, or pass-through election is available for a foreign-owned Oman SPC; the SPC is mandatorily taxed as an Omani juridical person on an entity-level basis. Available elections and administrative mechanisms include: (i) consolidated income tax filing for closely related group companies after obtaining the Oman Tax Authority's prior approval; (ii) VAT group/single taxable person election under the Omani VAT Executive Regulations for persons with close economic, organizational, and capital ties; (iii) small business or simplified filing relief elections where the applicable statutory turnover and ownership tests are satisfied; (iv) election to claim foreign tax credit instead of deduction for foreign taxes subject to authority confirmation; and (v) filing for an advance tax ruling to confirm the characterization of hybrid instruments, trust distributions, and ESOP-related cross-border payments. Oman does not adopt a domestic foreign check-the-box regime, and the general anti-avoidance rules preserve Oman's right to tax hybrid mismatch benefits sourced to the Omani entity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 mandatory annual taxable-supplies threshold computed over the preceding or expected next 12-month period; OMR 100,000 voluntary registration/refund threshold. Non-resident suppliers of certain digital and remote-supply services may be required to register from the first Omani taxable supply under the non-resident registration specifics of the VAT Law and Executive Regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for zero-rated VAT when they are physically exported from Oman to a destination outside Oman or outside the non-original GCC boundary and are supported by customs exit records. Services qualify for zero-rating if exported to a person resident outside Oman and either the direct and immediate benefit of the service is enjoyed outside Oman or the service is directly connected with exported goods and transportation. For the ESOP administration vehicle, corporate, trustee, payroll administration, and equity-transfer services will be subject to zero-rating only to the extent rendered for non-resident beneficiaries or a non-resident principal and demonstrably consumed outside Oman; services supplied for Omani beneficiaries, Omani employees, or Omani grantor companies remain taxable at the 5% standard rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration, Bill of Lading, Inward Remittance Certificate

Oman mandates electronic issuance and retention of sequentially numbered tax invoices for B2B and, where applicable, B2C supplies, and requires export invoices to be reconciled with Omani customs declarations. There is, however, no operational mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, API-based continuous clearance, or digital tax-stamping requirement for Omani VAT as of the current compliance window. Export filters operate through Oman Customs 'Bayan' electronic declaration, which in practice acts as the real-time clearance event for export zero-rating. All invoice and customs data must remain in the taxable person's records and be available digitally to the Oman Tax Authority upon request in the prescribed XML/portal-specific file structure.

Facilitating false export zero-rating claims triggers retrospective reassessment of the 5% VAT on the full consideration, statutory late-payment penalties and daily delay fines, and, in tax-evasion cases, penalties of up to 100% of the evaded VAT amount subject to the Tax Authority's discretion. The five-year assessment limitation period is extended for fraud or wilful denial, and the Oman Tax Authority may issue customs-veto/watch notices. Non-repatriation of export consideration within the prescribed period or without documentary proof of inward remittance exposes the claim to clawback and may be treated as an indicator of fictitious export. Forged customs declarations, falsified bills of lading, or unreported re-importation into Oman can additionally lead to criminal proceedings, fines, disqualification of responsible officers, and imprisonment in serious cases, along with annulment of the SPC's VAT registration.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman is an IMF Article VIII country; current-account transactions are fully convertible and no central bank approval is required for profit remittance or trade-related payments. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the U.S. dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Capital-account movements are broadly liberalized for foreign direct investment and foreign-owned SPCs, but banks apply prudential due diligence and CBO reporting thresholds. Profit and dividend remittance to non-resident shareholders requires audited financial statements, proof that tax and Commercial Registration obligations are settled, and a bank-level profit transfer declaration. For an ESOP trust, the CBO will not treat the trust as a regulated financial institution if, and only if, the SPC does not hold third-party funds other than plan assets and does not engage in financial intermediation or trust company services beyond the employee incentive plan scope.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman maintains no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency accounts with CBO-licensed banks, used directly for overseas trade disbursements, or converted into OMR at the prevailing bank exchange rate. Where conversion is elected, the transaction is executed at the bank's benchmark spot rate, which is anchored to the USD/OMR peg; no surrender certificate or CBO authorization is required. All export and inward-settlement transactions remain subject to standard AML/KYC and suspicious transaction reporting obligations under the CBO AML/CFT regulations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Banking Supervision Department, in coordination with the Oman Tax Authority for external account reporting and tax compliance verification.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Oman does not operate a passporting regime for foreign electronic money institutions or offshore digital EMIs. A non-resident EMI cannot obtain a CBO settlement account, cannot issue OMR IBANs, and cannot participate in the national payment switching/clearing infrastructure. Offshore digital EMIs may be used only as an auxiliary FX execution or payment initiation channel with the express approval of the Omani relationship bank, but the primary statutory clearing route must remain a CBO-licensed bank account in the name of the Omani SPC. Any EMI that appears to hold trust funds without a CBO payment service license will be treated as an unlicensed payment institution and will be rejected in onboarding.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity contributions must be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the overseas sponsor or shareholder into the SPC's designated Capital Escrow Account at a CBO-licensed bank. The payment instruction should use Field 70 Remittance Information to state 'Equity investment in Omani SPC, Commercial Register No. [CR number], ESOP plan funding'. No prior CBO exchange control approval is required, but the bank will require a corporate resolution, the updated Commercial Registration extract, the trust deed, and a source-of-funds declaration before accepting the credit. Foreign currency may be retained in the capital account or converted to OMR at the official peg; transfers must not be labeled merely as 'services' unless a fully documented service agreement exists, because misclassification will trigger AML investigation and possible account suspension.Direct participant payroll and benefit distributions are cleared via SWIFT MT103 through the local bank's correspondent network, with the beneficiary name exactly matching the participant's ID or passport and a payment narrative referencing the plan name, vesting date, and distribution type. OMR-denominated local payments may be routed through the national electronic clearing system to local bank accounts. Commercial trade settlements, equity purchases, and broker settlements should be executed via documentary credit (MT700/MT710) or covered wire transfer where the underlying trade invoice or broker contract permits; large equity transfers involving delivery-versus-payment risk should be settled through a licensed Omani bank's escrow arrangement. All outbound transfers are subject to the CBO travel-rule and AML/CFT requirements and must be consistent with the SPC's registered business scope; periodic transfers to participants who are not on the approved trust schedule will be rejected.Deliverable FX forward contract with a CBO-licensed bank under an ISDA or master FX agreement, limited to the OMR/USD peg and major non-usd currencies, with a tenor approved by the bank's credit risk limit.European-style OTC FX option on OMR/USD or major currencies, permitted only with a licensed bank and subject to board approval and CBO conduct-of-business rules.Cross-currency swap hedging foreign-currency-denominated ESOP funding or external debt, provided the underlying exposure is documented and the swap is executed with a licensed bank under an ISDA schedule.FX swap for temporary liquidity conversion between OMR and USD, executed at the pegged central rate plus bank margin, without the creation of a separate onshore NDF market.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 100,000; 12% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends and profit distributions paid to non-resident shareholders. No Oman withholding tax applies to distributions to resident corporate or individual shareholders.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 500,000 annually of aggregate cross-border related-party transactions triggers a mandatory local file; a master file is required at the ultimate parent level where group consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 10 million, and a country-by-country reporting threshold applies for multinational groups with revenue exceeding OMR 100 million.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not codified a separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime, but substance principles are embedded in the Income Tax Law and tax treaty provisions. An SPC administering an ESOP trust must show real management and control in Oman, maintain local board minutes, employ qualified trustees, and hold supporting records on-shore. A shell or brass-plate SPC without economic activity cannot claim treaty benefits and risks being treated as a taxable permanent establishment of the foreign beneficiary.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion & Fraudulent Filing

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment for up to 3 years and a fine up to OMR 5,000; in serious cases a penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax plus the evaded tax itself, and the Tax Authority may apply criminal judgment before the Economic Court. The tax liability is also increased by the 2%-per-month late payment surcharge.

Director personal liability: A director or general manager who knowingly signs incorrect tax returns is personally liable for the unpaid tax, risk a travel ban, disqualification from board seats, and criminal imprisonment; any dissenting director who did not act in good faith can be sued by the company for civil restitution.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Reclassification as a non-resident company with Omani-source income, full reassessment at 15%, denial of treaty relief, retroactive withholding taxes on deemed profits, and penalty equal to the tax undercharged plus additional fines up to OMR 10,000.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who allow the SPC to act as a sham vehicle are jointly liable for corporate tax debts; a court may pierce the corporate veil and order civil restitution of diverted trust assets, impose travel restrictions, and refer for prosecution for fraud.
AML / Trust Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Violation of the Oman AML Law (RD 30/2016 as amended): fines from OMR 10,000 to OMR 500,000, forfeiture of underlying assets, cancellation of the SPC registration, and criminal prosecution for money laundering (imprisonment up to 10 years), plus the freezing or confiscation of the trust's bank accounts.

Director personal liability: Trustees and directors face personal criminal liability for knowingly facilitating AML violations; courts may impose travel bans, freezing orders, and confiscation orders against personal bank accounts, with civil liability to all defrauded beneficiaries.
Transfer Pricing & Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income to the arm's-length profit, with a liability on the increased tax plus 2% monthly late payment surcharge and a possible accuracy-related penalty up to 25% of the additional tax; in flagrant schemes, the amount is treated as a dividend subject to 10% withholding tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve non-commercial intra-group pricing without adequate documentation are jointly and severally liable for any tax adjustment and penalty; a court may issue a personal restitution order equal to the diverted profits and disallow foreign travel until the liability is settled.
Withholding Tax Defaults

Statutory sanction: The defaulting SPC is liable for the entire unpaid withholding tax as a separate penalty, cannot deduct the amount for corporate tax purposes, and is exposed to fines of up to OMR 10,000 and interest at 5% per month of late remittance; continued defaults trigger forced collection by attachment.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the unremitted withholding tax and any fines if the company fails to pay within 60 days of the tax authority's demand; criminal prosecution can result in imprisonment and a travel ban for the duration of the debt.
