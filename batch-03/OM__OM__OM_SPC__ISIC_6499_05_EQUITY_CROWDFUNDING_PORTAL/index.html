Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the issued share capital must be fully subscribed and declared in the Memorandum of Association. For regulated equity-crowdfunding activities, the Capital Market Authority's Crowdfunding Platform Rules require a licensed corporate vehicle to maintain paid-up capital of not less than OMR 150,000 (approx. USD 390,000), which must be deposited into an escrow bank account and retained as net equity.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – official electronic portal: taxoman.gov.om; customs matters are administered separately via the Royal Oman Police / Oman Customs Single Window.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must file an online application with the OTA through the Tax Administration System portal. Required steps: (1) obtain the commercial registration (CR) and investment license from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; (2) submit the CR, constitutional documents, audited financials or opening balance sheet, sector-specific license (including the financial services/crowdfunding portal license), and proof of ownership; (3) provide director and ultimate beneficial owner passport copies and a valid power of attorney if locally registered; (4) apply for both Direct Tax (TIN) and VAT registration as applicable; (5) receive a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) for income tax and indirect tax filings. The OTA will issue the TIN after verification, normally within 5-15 business days, after which the entity must apply for VAT registration within the statutory threshold timeframe.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box pass-through election in Oman. The SPC is mandatorily treated as a separate legal person for corporate income tax (under Income Tax Law) and VAT. There are no hybrid mismatch or partnership classification elections available; however, an SPC may choose voluntary VAT registration below the mandatory threshold if it meets the OMR 187,500 voluntary registration criteria. No other statutory tax regime elections apply.

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies for mandatory registration; voluntary registration available for taxable persons with annual supplies exceeding OMR 187,500 or with appropriate business rationale for recovery.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods may be zero-rated if they are physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the GCC within the statutory time limit, with a valid customs export declaration and proof that ownership and control transferred to a non-resident buyer. Services, including equity crowdfunding portal services, may be zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident beneficiary who is outside Oman, when the direct benefit is enjoyed outside Oman, or when the services are directly attributable to exported goods. A digital intermediary must maintain evidence of the non-resident status of investors, the offshore benefit, and the receipt of consideration in foreign currency from outside Oman to claim zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Oman Customs Single Window / SCE Bayan)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement outside Oman
  • Commercial Invoice and packing list
  • Certificate of Origin (for export goods)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or foreign currency credit advice demonstrating payment transfer from the non-resident exporter
  • Service-specific contract or engagement letter evidencing offshore benefit for zero-rated crowdfunding services

Oman is implementing a phased mandatory e-invoicing regime administered by the OTA through the 'Mawgib' platform. Once phase-in applies to the SPC, all VAT invoices must be issued electronically through approved tax invoicing software, transmitted in real time to OTA via certified APIs, assigned a digital fiscal identifier, and carry a QR code generated by OTA-compliant middleware. The OTA's clearance engine validates the format, VAT calculation, and buyer/seller tax registration data before acceptance. Non-resident-exempt and pure export sectors may remain subject to simplified invoicing until the full mandatory digital stamping schedule applies.

The OTA may conduct post-export audits with retrospective review of zero-rating claims. Fraudulent or unsupported zero-rating results in full clawback of VAT refunded, imposition of a penalty up to OMR 25,000, and criminal prosecution under VAT Law, potentially leading to imprisonment terms for responsible officers. Customs Law penalties may also apply for false export declarations. Delayed recovery of VAT erroneously credited is subject to an additional 2% monthly late payment amount. The statute of limitation is generally 10 years where fraud or deliberate misstatement is proven; the tax authority may use bank records, customs data, and digital platform evidence to trace consumption, management, and control of exported property or services.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the USD at 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD. Current-account transactions are freely convertible and remittances of lawfully earned profits by foreign-owned SPCs are generally permitted, subject to documentary proof of source of funds, settled tax obligations, and compliance with the Commercial Companies Law and CBO Anti-Money Laundering (AML) rules. Capital-account flows, including foreign direct investment capital injections and outward investment, are subject to standard reporting to CBO and the National Centre for Financial Information (NCFI) / Oman Credit and Financial Information Centre; no routine exchange-control licensing is required, but banks apply the CBO Customer Due Diligence (CDD) framework for every cross-border flow. Profit-remittance friction is low for fully compliant SPCs; the main hurdles are prolonged KYC evidence collection, are balanced by the zero-income-tax threshold for small businesses, and must be accompanied by audited financial statements if the transfer exceeds bank risk thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a statutory mandatory surrender of export proceeds to the CBO; exporters may retain foreign currency in domestic or offshore accounts. However, for AML and BOP compilation purposes, all export receipts above the CBO reporting threshold must be repatriated to Oman within the timeframe specified in the underlying trade documents or within the bank's internal compliance limit (commonly 90–180 days), and must be reported under the CBO Balance of Payments return with the relevant ADV / undisputed export invoice number. Since the company is an equity crowdfunding portal rather than a physical exporter, residual export-proceeds treatment applies only to the sale of equity instruments and platform fee remittances originating from foreign investors, which are classified as portfolio-investment or digital-services receipts and must be routed through a licensed Omani bank with purpose codes consistent with CBO taxonomy.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting as the monetary authority and regulator of banking, FX, and payment systems; the Capital Market Authority (CMA) separately licenses and supervises the equity crowdfunding portal.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cross-border electronic-money institutions (EMIs) domiciled outside Oman have low viability as primary settlement or escrow rails. The CBO does not recognise an offshore EMI licence as sufficient to conduct e-money, payment-facilitation, or client-money custody services in Oman; any provider must either hold a CBO-issued Payment Service Provider (PSP) licence or operate through a sanctioned CBO Regulatory Sandbox arrangement with a licensed Omani bank as settlement agent. Foreign EMIs may be used only as a supplementary user-invite front end, provided that all OMR-denominated investor funds settle exclusively into an escrow account at a CBO-licensed bank. A locally licensed equity crowdfunding portal must also comply with CMA Rules on Crowdfunding and a mandatory escrow arrangement with a licensed bank; therefore, offshore EMI-only rails are legally inadequate.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign direct capital contributions from the non-resident shareholder must be received via SWIFT MT103 / international wire transfer in USD, EUR, or OMR into the SPC’s capital escrow account at a licensed Omani bank. The remitter must quote the true economic purpose using CBO-friendly payment narratives such as 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION CAPITAL – SPC OM', 'SHARE CAPITAL INCREASE', or 'CONTRIBUTION TO RESERVES', and must include the corresponding inward remittance reference number. For equity crowdfunding investments from foreign residents into portfolio and startup issuers, each investor subscription must be tagged with a unique escrow sub-reference and, where applicable, the purpose code for direct or portfolio investment under the CBO Balance of Payments classification. Banks will require: (a) an inward remittance form; (b) a CMA-approved crowdfunding document; (c) a certificate of incorporation; (d) board resolution confirming the capital injection; (e) passport / UBO KYC; and (f) source-of-funds evidence. Funds are released from escrow to the respective issuer only upon completion of the offering threshold and written instruction from the platform, subject to CMA confirmation where applicable; all transfers are monitored for round-tripping and layering patterns.Platform payouts to issuers, investor refunds, and operational disbursements must be executed over the domestic Real-Time Gross Settlement system through a licensed Omani bank, and cross-border settlements must be cleared by SWIFT MT103 (Cover or Serial) for standard transfers and by Commercial Letters of Credit (LC) for trade-backed distributions where the underlying crowdfunded enterprise requires import/export settlement security. For intra-Oman liquidity movements, the portal must use the Oman Electronic Clearing System and IBAN-validated ACH transfers; for international investor refunds, banks will enforce a dual-authorisation matrix, FX conversion at the prevailing CBO-issued indicative rate, and strict compliance with the Beneficial Owner and Correspondent Banking Due Diligence questionnaire. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat corporate income tax for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2023 under the new Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 36/2023). The previous graduated band of 3% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income is no longer available for the SPC. The 15% statutory rate applies to taxable profits derived from the equity crowdfunding portal's commission, advisory, facilitation, technology, and escrow interest income attributable to Omani sources.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

De facto 0% dividend withholding tax in current Omani practice. The Income Tax Law retains a statutory headroom of 10% as a withholding source for certain distributions and payments to foreign companies, but its operational application remains suspended. A distribution of the SPC's retained post-CIT profits to the foreign sole shareholder is therefore settled at the 15% corporate level with no additional Omani withholding in the ordinary course. The shareholder remains exposed to any future reinstatement of the 10% statutory headroom and must re-verify implementation circulars before each distribution.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled transactions between the SPC and any related party, including the foreign sole shareholder, platform associates, escrow providers, and technology affiliates. Above this threshold, the SPC must prepare and retain OECD-aligned transfer pricing documentation (Local File) and complete the SGT related-party disclosure schedule; entities within a multinational group are also expected to maintain a Master File where group turnover exceeds the threshold adopted under the Executive Regulations. Below the threshold, arm's-length pricing must still be documented contemporaneously for audit defense.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but the substance obligation arises from the complementary operation of the significant-economic-presence test and the General Anti-Avoidance Rule introduced by Income Tax Law RD 36/2023, together with the Commercial Companies Law single-member governance requirements and the CMA crowdfunding licensing conditions. The Omani SPC must maintain board meetings, key management, contract conclusion, accounting, escrow oversight, and risk management in Oman; a pure nominee or mail-box entity holding a crowdfunding license is exposed to tax recharacterization, treaty denial, license revocation, and personal liability of its managers.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Wilful False Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Income Tax Law (RD 36/2023): imprisonment and/or a fine of not less than OMR 500 and not exceeding OMR 10,000, plus civil liability for the evaded tax, an additional penalty of up to 100% of the evaded tax, SGT best-judgment reassessment, mandatory tax clearance revocation, and CMA license revocation.

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder, director, and compliance officers may be jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and for restitution of investor funds held in escrow; a travel ban and asset freeze may be imposed during criminal investigation; disqualification from company directorship follows conviction; imprisonment is available, and the corporate veil is pierced where the SPC operates as a fraudulent instrument.
Shell Company / Deficient Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization under the significant-economic-presence rule; denial of DTA benefits; disallowance of head-office or offshore cost recharges; SGT penalties for incorrect returns; CMA suspension and revocation of the crowdfunding license; mandatory deregistration and strike-off of the SPC from the Commercial Register.

Director personal liability: Shareholders and directors risk personal liability for investor misrepresentation and negligence claims; regulatory fines are not indemnified by the SPC; travel bans and disqualification orders may issue; the single-person shareholder cannot invoke the corporate veil where the SPC is a mere agent, sham, or instrumentality of the foreign owner.
Unlicensed Capital-Market Activity / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: Sanctions under the Omani Capital Market Law and the AML Law (RD 30/2016): unlimited administrative fines, confiscation of criminal proceeds, blacklisting of the SPC, suspension or revocation of the platform license, referral to the Public Prosecution for money-laundering offenses, and cumulative SGT penalties for tax filings based on unreported unlawful proceeds.

Director personal liability: Directors are civilly liable for misappropriated escrow balances and investor losses; senior management faces imprisonment of up to several years and personal fines under the AML regime; travel bans and asset freezes are routinely imposed; the corporate shield is pierced for fraudulent commingling of client money and for willful AML non-compliance.
Transfer Pricing Non-Arm's-Length / Profit Stripping

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment under RD 36/2023: upward adjustment of taxable income, denial of the deduction, recalculation of the arm's-length consideration, application of the 1% monthly late-payment surcharge, a GAAR-based penalty for the avoidance scheme, and possible secondary adjustment treating the excessive payment as a dividend or distribution subject to applicable Omani source taxation and DTA limitations.

Director personal liability: The sole director who signs or sanctions the related-party declaration may be personally assessed for the adjusted tax and surcharges where the declaration is knowingly false; continuing non-compliance blocks profit distribution and leads to director disqualification; the foreign shareholder may be deemed to have an Omani permanent establishment through the SPC's activities, exposing the foreign parent to Omani registration, filing, and economic-substance examination, with consequential personal exposure for the directors of both entities.
