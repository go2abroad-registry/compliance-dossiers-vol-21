Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), e-Services portal taxoman.om; company incorporation and pre-tax registration through Invest Easy (Oman Business Platform, investeasy.om) under the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP).

E-government filing portal

(1) Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) under Oman Commercial Companies Law RD 18/2019 via Invest Easy - 100% foreign ownership is permitted for the ISIC 6201 software/analytics activity, no minimum share capital, registered office required; (2) after issuance of the Commercial Registration (CR), file income tax registration and tax card application with the OTA through taxoman.om using the CR deed, passport/director IDs, lease contract, and SPC constitutional documents; (3) obtain the OTA Tax Card / Tax Identification Number (TIN); (4) complete a separate OTA VAT registration whenever taxable supplies/imports exceed OMR 38,000 in a 12-month period (voluntary registration from OMR 19,000); non-resident shareholders do not require a local sponsor, but a local tax payable bank account and OTA e-filing credentials are mandatory.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman has no check-the-box or pass-through classification election; SPC is automatically a resident juridical person taxed at entity level on Oman-source income (15% headline CIT, reduced 3% regime for qualifying SMEs; export-oriented software/SaaS income may benefit from OTA administrative zero-rating reliefs). Statutory elections: alternative financial year (OTA approval), depreciation/inventory methods, loss carry-forward utilization, voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,000, quarterly VAT filing elections, and OECD Pillar Two jurisdictional safe-harbour elections for multinational groups. Hybrid mismatch elections: not separately codified; OTA GAAR (RD 9/2023) and CFC rules apply to cross-border mismatch arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,000 taxable supplies plus taxable imports in any consecutive 12 months for mandatory registration; OMR 19,000 for voluntary registration; non-resident digital service suppliers to consumers in Oman become registrable at the same threshold (or reverse-charge applies where the Omani importer is registered; SaaS/SEO analytics supply to Omani B2B customers is within the importer's reverse-charge unless the Omani SPC is registered).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: zero-rated when actual export is completed to a destination outside Oman/GCC within the statutory 90-day window evidenced by OTA/customs export records. Services (including SEO/data-analytics SaaS): zero-rated where (a) the recipient is a non-resident/non-Omani person present outside Oman, (b) the service is not directly connected to Oman real estate and is not consumed in Oman, and (c) the exporter holds contemporaneous contracts, reports of overseas user benefit, and foreign-currency payment evidence; services ancillary to exported goods, and intellectual-property/software rights supplied to non-residents, qualify. Refunds of input VAT are claimable via the VAT return where output tax is nil; net refunds are processed after OTA verification.

OTA national e-invoicing programme (Nuqood platform) imposes phased, API-based real-time fiscal clearance, digital validation and QR-code stamping of tax invoices for notified taxable persons; large/medium taxpayers have been rolled out first, with SMEs and sub-threshold SPCs exempt until later phases. In the interim, every tax invoice must still be filed in the OTA e-services portal and contain the required validation fields (CRN/TIN, VAT rate, total, hash/QR).

OTA audits may retrospectively claw back all 0% export claims where the required exports/offshore-benefit evidence is absent or the export fails within the statutory period; 5% VAT is reassessed from the original supply date, plus a 14% per annum late-payment addition (Tax Procedures Law RD 28/2017), and administrative fines escalate 25-50% for repeat violations. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract criminal fines of approximately OMR 5,000-20,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years, with 5-to-10-year retroactive reassessment windows, blacklisting, attachment of assets, and personal liability of directors/controlling shareholders.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains a fully convertible open-market foreign exchange regime for both current account and capital account transactions. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the US Dollar at a central parity of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (1 USD = 0.3845 OMR), and no Central Bank prior approval is required for genuine inward or outward remittances. Profit distribution, dividend remittance, capital redemption, intercompany loan repayment, royalty payments, and liquidation proceeds may be transferred abroad freely once the underlying corporate tax and withholding tax obligations under the Oman Tax Law are settled or certified as exempt. Banks execute such transfers on the basis of standard supporting documentation and anti-money laundering verification; there is no cumulative profit remittance cap and no exchange approval questionnaire. Inward FDI equity contributions for an SPC may be channeled directly to an Omani commercial bank account without exchange control approval, provided the bank obtains the correct balance-of-payments purpose classification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The Central Bank of Oman imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no statutory conversion ratio for export receipts. An Omani SPC may retain foreign currency export income in a multi-currency account or convert it at the commercial exchange rate offered by licensed banks. In the country-of-origin context of the relevant transaction, export proceeds should be repatriated to the Omani corporate bank account within a reasonable commercial period, typically not exceeding six months, in alignment with standard bank due diligence and documentary trade practice. Banks will register export receipts for central bank balance-of-payments statistics, but neither the CBO nor the Oman Tax Authority prescribes a fixed repatriation timeline nor a compulsory conversion percentage. Supporting export invoices, customs declarations, bills of lading, and counterparty payment records should be retained for at least five years for audit and compliance defense.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established under the Banking Law of the Sultanate of Oman (Royal Decree 114/2000), acting in coordination with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for foreign direct investment registration, and the Oman Tax Authority for tax clearance and withholding tax compliance obligations.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for primary treasury banking and MEDIUM-LOW for ancillary receivables collection. Under the Central Bank of Oman’s regulatory perimeter, only licensed banks and CBO-authorized payment service providers may legally hold corporate balances, open OMR-denominated settlement accounts, or perform deposit-taking functions for domestic treasury purposes. Offshore fintech EMIs and foreign digital banks generally do not hold an Oman banking license and cannot be used as the statutory corporate account for capital contribution, commercial registration capital deposit certification, tax settlement, or audited financial reporting. Their acceptable use is limited to merchant payment collection, such as Stripe and Paddle for SaaS subscriptions, from which net proceeds are automatically or periodically settled into the Oman-licensed bank account. Even for that use, the fintech gateway must verify merchant KYC/AML status and comply with CBO cross-border payment system rules, standard card-network operating regulations, and Oman’s data protection obligations under the Personal Data Protection Law. The legal treasury record for the SPC shall remain the domestic Omani banking account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound share capital must be injected through a regulated banking channel, normally SWIFT MT103 from the shareholder’s external bank to the SPC’s operational account in Oman, or by a contribution deposited temporarily in a Capital Escrow Account at the same Omani bank and later converted to paid-up capital. The transfer should be denominated either in OMR or a freely convertible foreign currency, using the purpose classification of foreign direct investment equity participation under the bank’s balance-of-payments reporting framework, and instruct suitability of the beneficiary reference matching Commercial Registration No. and Company ID. The remitter must cite the underlying share subscription agreement or board resolution; the receiving bank will issue a Capital Deposit Certificate where required by the Commercial Registry. The SPC must not conceal the equity nature of the remittance as ordinary service revenue. If the shareholder advances working capital as a shareholder loan, the remitter must identify the transfer as an intercompany shareholder loan rather than equity, and the SPC must execute a separate loan agreement to avoid tax recharacterization. All inbound funds must pass through CBO-compliant banks and be recorded in full in the company’s books; the SPC shall promptly submit the receiving bank receipt, purpose code, and underlying agreement to the external auditor for capital verification.Outbound trade and operational disbursements from the Omani SPC will use the SWIFT MT103 message standard for direct wire transfers to foreign beneficiaries for software development fees, cloud infrastructure costs, contractual services, data center licences, marketing outsourcing, and permitted shareholder distributions. For higher-value goods-related procurement, the SPC may employ documentary commercial letters of credit under UCP600 through its Oman bank, payable via integrated bank-to-bank clearing systems and correspondent banking. For recurring SaaS disbursements to fintech gateways, including Stripe and Paddle, the highest quality clearing path is commercial card settlement and automated clearing house payout rails into the Oman corporate account, followed by bank-initiated SWIFT transfers to the fintech intermediary as a merchant payout. Disbursements relating to royalties and intra-group license fees must include a tax-relevant purpose text, and the Oman Tax Authority’s 10% withholding tax must be deposited at the Oman Tax Authority before remittance unless reduced by an applicable tax treaty. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (flat statutory rate on taxable income of a foreign-owned Single Person Company under the Omani Income Tax Law, Royal Decree 28/2009 as amended by Royal Decree 9/2017; the 3% preferential SME band on the first OMR 30,000 is available only to wholly Omani-owned SMEs and is therefore unavailable to a 100% non-resident SPC).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividend distributions by an Omani company to its non-resident shareholder. Dividends distributed by a resident SPC are tax-exempt in the hands of the foreign beneficial owner under domestic law, with no further Omani tax at distribution.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed within 4 months following the fiscal year-end (i.e., 30 April for a 31 December tax year). The OTA may grant an extension of up to 2 months upon written application submitted before the deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File maintenance is triggered where the SPC's gross revenue exceeds OMR 1,000,000 or where related-party controlled transactions in the tax year exceed materiality benchmarks prescribed in the OTA Transfer Pricing Guidelines. A Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated global revenue exceeds OMR 30,000,000. An annual transfer-pricing disclosure statement must accompany the Omani tax return even where the documentation thresholds are not met.

Economic substance scope: Oman's Economic Substance Regulations (Economic Substance Law, Royal Decree 89/2020, implemented by Ministerial Decision 90/2020) apply to all resident entities including foreign-owned SPCs carrying on a 'relevant activity'. For an ISIC 6201 SEO content analytics SaaS company, the relevant activities include IP-rich digital services, distribution and service centre activity, and holding-company activity. The SPC must demonstrate in Oman: (a) direction and management of core business activities; (b) adequate physical premises and equipment; (c) qualified full-time employees; (d) proportionate operating expenditure; and (e) performance of core income-generating activities within Oman. Annual ESR notification, information filing, and record retention are mandatory; failure attracts administrative fines up to OMR 50,000 and may result in denial of treaty benefits and profit recharacterisation.
