Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for an Omani Single Person Company; the sole shareholder must subscribe to at least one issued share and pay it in full. For a foreign-owned REIT vehicle, the Capital Market Authority requires that the declared capital be committed through an Omani licensed bank, with a bank certificate confirming deposit or availability, and any real estate acquisition capital must be held in a segregated escrow/trust account pending CMA disposition instructions.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification is required from the Directorate General of Civil Defence and Ambulance of the Royal Oman Police for each occupied or income-generating building; completion of structural load inspection and occupancy clearance by Muscat Municipality; and, if employees are located in Oman, occupational safety clearance and workplace risk assessments under the Ministry of Labour and the Oman Code of Occupational Safety and Health.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for an Omani Single Person Company; the sole shareholder must subscribe to at least one issued share and pay it in full. For a foreign-owned REIT vehicle, the Capital Market Authority requires that the declared capital be committed through an Omani licensed bank, with a bank certificate confirming deposit or availability, and any real estate acquisition capital must be held in a segregated escrow/trust account pending CMA disposition instructions.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; official portal www.taxoman.gov.om; electronic filing and VAT return portal eservices.taxoman.gov.om. Income tax is governed by the Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020, effective 1 January 2021); VAT is governed by the Oman VAT Law (Sultani Decree No. 121/2016), in force from 16 April 2019, and the OTA VAT Executive Regulations.

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor incorporates the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) through the Invest Easy e-portal, obtains the Commercial Registration (CR), Chamber of Commerce certificate, and relevant municipal or sectoral licences. The SPC is then registered with the OTA for income tax, which allocates the Taxpayer Registration Number / tax file number. A separate 15-digit VAT ID is obtained through the OTA e-Services VAT registration module; VAT registration is mandatory when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500 and is available voluntarily from OMR 19,250. A non-resident-controlled SPC with no Omani establishment must appoint an Omani tax representative for VAT registration purposes. The bank account opening requires the CR, founding documents, licences, and OTA tax ID evidence.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The SPC is a resident juridical person liable to Oman income tax as a separate entity on worldwide adjusted accounting profits prepared under IFRS as accepted by the OTA; it is not fiscally transparent. A CMA-licensed Real Estate Investment Trust or fund may qualify for a statutory fund-level exemption as a collective investment scheme, but the SPC's default classification remains full entity-level taxation unless and until a fund restructuring or CMA licensing election is made.

Available election forms

Oman does not provide US-style check-the-box elections or hybrid-mismatch elections. Available statutory elections and mechanisms include: (a) applying for Capital Market Authority (CMA) licensing as a real estate investment fund or trust to access the investment-fund income exemption; (b) voluntary VAT registration from OMR 19,250 and the resulting election to charge VAT on commercial rents in order to recover input VAT; and (c) submitting an application to the OTA for an advance ruling or advance pricing agreement confirming the treatment of distributions, financing, land supplies, and cross-border services. Small-enterprise income tax relief is available where statutory turnover and market-share conditions are met.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies. Voluntary registration: OMR 19,250. Non-resident suppliers without an Omani establishment making taxable supplies in Oman must register without regard to the threshold, generally through an Omani tax representative, except where the reverse-charge mechanism applies. The SPC must aggregate all taxable commercial rental supplies when applying the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are zero-rated only when physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the GCC, or when moved to another GCC member state under the approved transfer and evidence rules, with export completed within the period fixed by the VAT Executive Regulations. Services are zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Oman and when the service is not directly connected to land or buildings situated in Oman. Inbound investment real estate income is inherently ineligible for zero-rating: commercial rents are subject to 5% VAT, residential rents are exempt, and the first supply of a completed building may be exempt or taxable according to the statutory real-estate rules. Only genuine cross-border services such as offshore distribution services or advisory services to foreign unitholders, with clear enjoyment outside Oman, may qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration with Omani customs exit certification
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill / through cargo manifest evidencing physical export from Oman
  • Commercial invoice and signed sale or service contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT MT103 credit advice showing receipt of foreign currency in Oman
  • Packing list, certificate of origin, and transport or insurance documentation
  • Destination-country customs release or equivalent third-party proof of arrival (when available)

Oman has not yet enacted a mandatory real-time fiscal invoice clearance or digital stamping regime analogous to ZATCA. VAT-registered persons must file periodic returns electronically through the OTA e-Services portal and issue sequential tax invoices in Arabic or English containing the supplier and customer VAT IDs, invoice date, description, quantity, consideration exclusive of VAT, VAT rate, and VAT amount. Simplified invoicing is permitted for low-value retail supplies. The OTA has announced a phased structured e-invoicing roadmap, but no operative clearance API or government-side digital stamping requirement is currently in force.

The OTA may reassess any zero-rated supply within the statutory assessment period of five years from the relevant return, extended to ten years in cases of fraud or evasion. Failure to produce the customs exit evidence, the Inward Remittance Certificate, or proof that export proceeds were received in Oman within the required period converts the supply into a standard-rated supply at 5% VAT, giving rise to retrospective assessment, mandatory clawback of previously recovered or refunded input VAT, and late-payment penalties or interest. Contemporaneous documentary integrity is essential, and files lacking coherent transport, customs, and bank evidence are routinely selected for desk audit. Fraudulent export zero-rating claims expose the SPC and its officers to administrative fines, criminal prosecution, imprisonment, publication as a non-compliant taxpayer, and suspension or revocation of the VAT certificate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the US Dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD under a conventional peg with a narrow CBO intervention band. Current-account items (trade, services, leases, investment income) are fully convertible and require no CBO prior approval. Capital-account transactions are substantially liberalised: a 100% foreign-owned SPC may receive equity capital, shareholder loans, property-sale proceeds, operating income, and REIT distributions without exchange-control licensing; funds may be held, reinvested, or remitted offshore in OMR, USD, or approved foreign currencies through CBO-licensed banks. The only practical remittance hurdles are standard bank-level documentary verification (source-of-funds and underlying contracts) and compliance with any applicable Omani corporate income tax and the 10% withholding tax on distributions to non-residents under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 9/2023), subject to double-tax-treaty relief. Convertibility risk is therefore minimal; residual FX risk is confined to non-USD revenues or liabilities, which can be hedged onshore.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation: there is no mandatory conversion ratio (0% compulsory conversion), no CBO-imposed repatriation timeline, and REIT operators may retain 100% of export and foreign-source receipts in local or foreign-currency accounts at licensed banks. Nevertheless, CBO AML/CFT reporting requirements oblige the receiving bank to evidence each cross-border receipt with authenticated underlying documentation (sales or lease contracts, commercial invoices, customs Bayan declarations where applicable, valuation reports) and to encode the transaction with the appropriate CBO Balance-of-Payments / transaction code for electronic central-bank reporting. All such documentation must be archived by the bank and the SPC for a minimum of five years.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - the Sultanate's central bank, monetary authority, and de facto foreign-exchange administrator under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Foreign Exchange Business Regulation Law (Royal Decree 36/1995), and the AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 30/2016). The Capital Market Authority (CMA) concurrently licenses and supervises the REIT under the CMA Real Estate Investment Funds / Investment Funds Regulations, the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) registers the SPC, and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) administers withholding and income tax compliance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW as the principal treasury or escrow account; MEDIUM only as an ancillary cross-border disbursement rail. Legal constraints: (i) any entity holding customer funds, issuing e-money, or performing payment services in or from Oman must be authorised by the CBO under the electronic payment services rules and the Foreign Exchange Business Regulation Law; (ii) an offshore digital EMI (e.g., a UK FCA-authorised or EU-licensed e-money institution) cannot serve as the SPC's operating bank or escrow account without a CBO branch licence or an approved arrangement with a licensed Omani bank; (iii) offshore EMIs may legally receive outbound SWIFT MT103 transfers from the local bank and onward-settle foreign vendor payments (e.g., international property consultants or construction contractors), but this must be disclosed as a payment-service arrangement and must never be used to circumvent Omani reporting thresholds; (iv) domestic instant clearing (AFAQ) and OmanNet ACH are available only to CBO-licensed local participants. Recommended posture: maintain all inbound capital, OMR liquidity, escrow, and dividend wires at a licensed Omani bank; use a global EMI or FX platform only for niche non-USD vendor payments outside Oman.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 250,000 - aggregate controlled/related-party transactions in a tax year trigger mandatory contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation and Local File requirements; Master File and Country-by-Country reporting thresholds apply for larger multinational groups.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations regime. Substance is tested through tax residence, central management and control, permanent establishment attribution and treaty beneficial-ownership rules. The Omani SPC must maintain a registered office, local directors, local lease management decisions, a local bank account, audited accounts, CMA REIT licence and minutes of board meetings. A foreign-run shell holding Omani real estate will be treated as an Omani taxable PE; all Omani-source rental profit will be taxed at 15% and distributions may be recharacterised as Oman-source dividends or fees subject to 10% WHT.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the full evaded tax, monthly late-payment surcharge, statutory penalties up to 100% of the tax evaded, criminal fines and imprisonment under Oman Income Tax Law for responsible persons.

Director personal liability: Directors, the general manager and any signatory who knowingly files false returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax debt. OTA may issue travel bans, seize personal bank accounts, attach property and pursue criminal imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Abuse / Thin Capitalisation

Statutory sanction: Mandatory OTA adjustment to arm's length profits, disallowance of excess deductions, additional CIT at 15%, transfer pricing penalties and interest; failure to file TP schedules triggers separate fixed penalties.

Director personal liability: Directors who direct or approve the offshore stripping arrangement are jointly liable for the adjusted tax and penalties. Travel restrictions and civil seizure of personal assets may be ordered.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: OTA may recharacterise the SPC as a taxable Omani establishment, assess 15% CIT on all Omani-source revenue, deny treaty benefits, impose late filing and understatement penalties, and recommend de-registration under the Commercial Companies Law.

Director personal liability: Nominee and foreign directors participating in the artificial structure are personally liable under Omani company law; civil penalties, travel bans, disqualification from directorship and criminal money-laundering charges may follow.
Profit Repatriation / Dividend Distribution WHT Evasion

Statutory sanction: Additional 10% withholding tax, disallowance of the underlying artificial expense, 100% understatement penalty, monthly late-payment surcharge, enforced collection and interest.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who authorise disguised distributions are personally liable for the unpaid WHT and penalties, and may face travel bans, attachment of personal assets and criminal prosecution.
Money Laundering / AML Breach in REIT Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Criminal forfeiture of property, fines and imprisonment under Royal Decree No. 30/2016 (Omani AML Law), CMA licence suspension or revocation, bank account freezing and mandatory reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers involved or grossly negligent in the AML breach are personally exposed to imprisonment, personal fines, confiscation of profits and prohibition from managing financial or investment companies in Oman.
