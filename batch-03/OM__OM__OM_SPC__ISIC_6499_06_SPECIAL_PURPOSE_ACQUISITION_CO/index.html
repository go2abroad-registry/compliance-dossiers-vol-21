Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SPC under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the sole shareholder must state a subscribed capital in the Memorandum and Articles of Association. As a condition of SPAC authorization, the Oman Financial Services Authority (OFSA) requires that at least 90% of gross IPO proceeds be deposited into a restricted trust escrow account held with an OFSA-licensed custodian bank, and that the sponsor demonstrate minimum initial capital adequacy of USD 250,000 (or equivalent) pending prospectus approval.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defence and Ambulance Directorate of the Royal Oman Police fire-safety certification for the registered office; Muscat Municipality building structural load inspection and change-of-use clearance; occupational health workplace clearance confirming safe premises, emergency egress, and adequate segregation of trust escrow records from general corporate books.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — the sole national revenue administration under the Oman Ministry of Finance; centralised e-registration and e-filing portal is 'Taxomania' at https://taxomania.gov.om. Export validation data is shared with the Oman Customs Directorate General (Royal Oman Police) via the 'Bayan' single-window customs system. No sub-national tax authority applies to an Oman-registered Single Person Company (SPC).

E-government filing portal

Procedure for a foreign-owned Single Person Company (OM-SPC): (1) Obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP), together with the Foreign Investment Licence where the sole shareholder is non-GCC and the activity is reserved; the CR number is the master business identifier. (2) Within 30 days of CR issuance, apply to the OTA via Taxomania for the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) by submitting the CR, notarised constitutional documents, shareholder identity documents (passport/Civil ID), the appointed manager/board resolution, registered office proof and audited opening statement if required; the OTA issues a Tax Card with the TIN. Non-resident shareholders must appoint a local tax agent/auditor for service of process and filing if no resident manager is designated. (3) Separately apply for the VAT Identification Number (VATIN) through Taxomania once aggregate taxable supplies in Oman exceed OMR 38,500 over any rolling 12-month period (voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250); the VATIN is issued after the OTA verifies the TIN, CR and bank account. (4) For future export activity, register as an exporter in the Bayan customs system. All OTA registration steps are electronic and cross-linked with the CR data.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman offers no check-the-box, no Section 8832-style pass-through election, and no partnership transparency election for an Omani Single Person Company. The SPC is mandatorily taxed as a separate corporate person under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009), regardless of the foreign shareholder's tax attributes. No group fiscal unity or consolidated filing election is available. The foreign shareholder cannot elect flow-through treatment for Oman tax purposes. Tax treaty benefits, where applicable, are claimed through the OTA competent-authority process rather than by domestic entity election; hybrid mismatch elections are not recognised under Oman's entity classification rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (approx. USD 100,000) of cumulative taxable supplies in any consecutive 12-month period for mandatory VAT registration; voluntary registration threshold is OMR 19,250 (approx. USD 50,000). For non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Oman without an Omani establishment, registration is mandatory at the same threshold, or where required under specific OTA instructions, without a threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The SPC may zero-rate (a) goods sold for export where the goods physically leave Oman and the GCC member-state territory within the statutory export period, (b) international transport of goods/passengers, (c) services directly connected to goods being exported or imported and (d) services supplied to a non-resident outside the GCC where the recipient is outside the GCC, the legal effect and actual use of the services occur outside Oman, and the services are not services related to Omani immovable property or performed while the recipient is physically present in Oman. For an OM-SPC acting as a SPAC, zero-rating is available for cross-border merger-acquisition facilitation, due diligence, escrow administration and advisory services supplied to a foreign target/foreign sponsor, provided the contract designates a foreign recipient, the service is used abroad, and contemporaneous evidence of cross-border payment and professional delivery is retained. Goods exported through a customs warehouse with a valid Bayan declaration also qualify. The SPC must document the export within the exporter's records; no minimum value threshold applies for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic Bayan declaration with registered exporter code and customs receipt number)
  • Bill of Lading (ocean/airway bill; for land or GCC-transit exports, a CMR/Carnet TIR with the exit-border seal from Oman Customs)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank-issued Swift MT103/MT950, or OTA-approved foreign exchange realisation record for export proceeds)

Oman does not yet operate a mandatory real-time fiscal clearance model or a continuous-transaction-control API for every VAT invoice. The OTA is progressively rolling out a mandatory e-invoicing framework through Taxomania; when fully effective, registered persons must issue invoices using OTA-certified systems and transmit invoice data electronically to the OTA tax data lake. Currently, VAT invoices may be issued in paper or electronic format (PDF/XML), must contain the full VATIN, supply description, date, tax rate and VAT payable, and must be retained for 5 years. There is no requirement for an ad valorem fiscal stamp or a government clearance stamp on export invoices; export evidence is validated through the Bayan customs declaration and matching of payment remittance data.

The OTA and Oman Customs operate automated risk-based matching between export declarations, shipping data, VAT refund claims and FX remittance records. If the export evidence bundle is incomplete, rejected as forged, or the goods are not shown to have left the GCC customs territory, the SPC is subject to retrospective clawback of the zero-rating benefit: the 5% VAT is reassessed on the export supply, input VAT previously recovered in connection with the export is disallowed, and statutory late-payment penalties apply from the original due date. Deliberate fraudulent zero-rating, including fictitious bills of lading, circular FX remittances, or phantom cargo, triggers administrative fines up to OMR 100,000, criminal prosecution with imprisonment of up to 3 years, and a criminal fine of up to double the tax evaded, under the Oman VAT Law and OTA enforcement rules. In addition, the OTA may revoke the VAT registration, blacklist the SPC from simplified exporting procedures, and impose joint liability on the sole shareholder and the liquidation sponsor where assets have been stripped.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains broad current-account convertibility under IMF Article VIII and applies an open capital-account framework for foreign investment. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the United States Dollar (USD) at a fixed official rate of 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD. There are no exchange-control quotas or prior CBO approvals for ordinary profit remittances by a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC). However, banks require documentary proof of the underlying transaction, source-of-funds verification, and compliance with anti-money laundering and counter-financing-of-terrorism (AML/CFT) reporting requirements. Dividend, interest, royalty, and other remittances to non-residents may be subject to Oman withholding tax; the paying bank will require a tax clearance certificate or withholding tax settlement acknowledgement from Oman Tax Authority before releasing large outward remittances. Capital account convertibility is also broadly available for FDI-backed SPCs, provided the entity is properly licensed and registered with MoCIIP and the bank's KYC file contains a board-signed resolution authorizing the capital movement.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory export-proceeds surrender rules or minimum conversion ratios for Omani exporters. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency accounts at CBO-licensed banks. There is no statutory surrender timeline for export proceeds and the mandatory conversion ratio is 0%. For the SPAC vehicle at hand, this is not directly applicable because the entity is not a goods exporter; nevertheless, any incidental fee or development income received in foreign currency may be kept in USD or converted to OMR at the prevailing interbank rate without a CBO conversion obligation. Banks may require underlying export invoices, customs declarations, or service agreements only for AML transaction-verification purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting as the foreign exchange administration authority; Oman Tax Authority for remittance tax clearances; Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) for investment licensing and corporate registration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium for the SPAC’s core treasury and liquidation operations. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions and non-bank payment service providers (for example Wise, Payoneer, or MultiPass-type platforms) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman to conduct payment services in or into Oman. They cannot access Omani RTGS infrastructure, provide OMR-denominated local clearing, or legally hold funds in a manner acceptable to the CBO for escrow and M&A settlement functions. Foreign EMIs may be used only as ancillary rails for collecting or disbursing funds in foreign currencies outside Oman where no OMR settlement or Omani resident payment initiation occurs. Legal constraints derive from the CBO’s Retail Payment Services Regulatory Framework, Oman’s Banking Law, and the Law on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing; operating an unlicensed payment service from or into Oman may constitute a regulatory violation. Therefore, primary escrow, capital-injection, and acquisition-disbursement accounts must be held with CBO-licensed banks.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound sponsor capital should be routed via SWIFT MT103 from the investor’s licensed overseas bank directly to the SPC’s USD-denominated or OMR-denominated operational or capital escrow account at a CBO-licensed Omani bank. The remittance instruction must clearly state the purpose of payment in SWIFT field 70 as 'CAPITAL INJECTION – OMAN SPC SHARE CAPITAL' or 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – ESCROW FOR SPAC ACQUISITE PROGRAM' to ensure correct booking. For SPAC commitment funding under a subscription agreement, banks require the corresponding signed deed of adherence, escrow agreement, and board resolution. Oman does not operate a mandatory purpose-code regime, but the receiving bank will apply its internal transaction code and may require supporting documents for transfers above approximately OMR 30,000 equivalent. Funds can be credited in USD without mandatory conversion to OMR. If funds are provided as debt rather than equity, a registered loan agreement is required and the interest portion will be subject to Oman withholding tax rules; for a SPAC, capital injections should remain in core equity or committed sponsor tier-equity form to avoid recharacterization by CBO and Oman Tax Authority.Outbound trade and acquisition disbursements should clear through the licensed Omani banking network. Operational expenses and professional fees are routed by SWIFT MT103 on a single-customer-credit-transfer basis. Interbank settlement to correspondent banks for large M&A consideration transfers is handled by SWIFT MT202 on a bank-to-bank basis, with the underlying MT103 or escrow payout instruction retained to evidence the party and purpose. A commercial import letter of credit (SWIFT MT700) and its subsequent MT754/MT799 amendments may be used only where the acquisition program includes physical goods, commodities, or equipment; for a pure SPAC equity purchase, the acquired shares are settled via MT103/MT202 escrow repayment instructions rather than an LC, because an LC is a documentary trade instrument and not an appropriate clearing mechanism for share transfer payments. All outbound disbursements above the bank’s threshold must be supported by: a board-approved disbursement resolution; the executed transaction agreement or escrow release condition certificate; tax withholding clearance from Oman Tax Authority where payments constitute dividends, interest, professional fees, or royalties; and complete KYC/AML verification of the beneficiary bank and ultimate recipient.Deliverable FX forwards between USD and OMR executed with a CBO-licensed Omani bank for genuine outstanding commercial or investment exposure; typically permitted for up to 12 months and require evidence of the underlying SPAC commitment or acquisition obligation.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing USD/OMR for hedging exposure to the pegged exchange rate, acceptable when booked with a reputable non-Omani bank outside the CBO’s direct regulatory perimeter, provided the instrument is clearly documentary backed by a hedging policy and cannot create a speculative position.Cross-currency interest rate swaps and basis swaps with licensed banks for matching sponsor capital and acquisition liabilities in USD, OMR, or other acquisition-denominated currencies, subject to Omani bank internal credit approval and a board-approved treasury and risk management policy.Plain-vanilla FX options or call-spread structures are not explicitly prohibited but are subject to strict CBO guidance regarding genuine underlying exposure; they are permissible only when embedded within a board-approved hedging framework and not offered as leveraged speculative products to the SPAC.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Low-Medium for the SPAC’s core treasury and liquidation operations. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions and non-bank payment service providers (for example Wise, Payoneer, or MultiPass-type platforms) are not licensed by the Central Bank of Oman to conduct payment services in or into Oman. They cannot access Omani RTGS infrastructure, provide OMR-denominated local clearing, or legally hold funds in a manner acceptable to the CBO for escrow and M&A settlement functions. Foreign EMIs may be used only as ancillary rails for collecting or disbursing funds in foreign currencies outside Oman where no OMR settlement or Omani resident payment initiation occurs. Legal constraints derive from the CBO’s Retail Payment Services Regulatory Framework, Oman’s Banking Law, and the Law on Combating Money Laundering and Terrorism Financing; operating an unlicensed payment service from or into Oman may constitute a regulatory violation. Therefore, primary escrow, capital-injection, and acquisition-disbursement accounts must be held with CBO-licensed banks.
