Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Oman; the foreign-owned SPC is automatically a taxable juridical person subject to entity-level territorial CIT on Omani-source income (15% headline; small-business relief at 0% for eligible businesses with annual revenue up to OMR 100,000 subject to conditions). Elective mechanisms available: (i) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 19,250 threshold; (ii) VAT grouping election for related persons; (iii) tax-exempt zone / Special Economic Zone (e.g., Duqm SEZ and designated free zones) licensing elections and CIT incentive regimes as provided by law; (iv) CIT accounting-period alignment election, subject to OTA approval. Overseas branch and foreign-source profits of the Omani SPC are generally outside the Omani territorial tax net unless derived through an Omani commercial activity.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (mandatory) of taxable supplies and/or taxable expenditure over any 12 consecutive months, approximately USD 100,000 equivalent; OMR 19,250 (voluntary registration threshold).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Under the Omani VAT Law and the GCC Unified Agreement, a 0% (zero) rate applies to: (1) goods physically exported out of Oman to a destination outside the GCC implementing states, including exported hotpot condiments, packaged sauces, and brand goods, provided the supplier dispatches the goods within the statutory timeframe and obtains the customs export declaration; (2) supplies of services to a non-resident / non-GCC recipient where the benefit of the service is enjoyed outside Oman (B2B reverse-charge treatment); and (3) international transport and ancillary freight services. Domestic dine-in service and local sale of condiments / packaged goods within Oman remain standard-rated at 5%. To claim 0% output VAT and the corresponding input VAT refund, the export must be supported by the OTA / customs export evidence and the consideration must be received from a foreign buyer.

  • Commercial invoice (original, with volume and value details)
  • Oman customs export declaration (Bayan / Certificate of Export)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (negotiable transport document evidencing dispatch)
  • Terminal / port receipts and container tracking evidence proving physical exit from Omani / GCC customs territory
  • Signed purchase orders or offtake contracts for the exported goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and bank statements proving receipt of foreign currency consideration
  • Packing lists, quality certificates, and insurance documents for the shipment
  • Copy of the VAT refund / zero-rating submission in Taxoman

The Oman Tax Authority has mandated a phased e-invoicing regime under the Musharaka platform (Phase 1 commenced 2 December 2024, with further phases through December 2026). Designated taxpayers must issue structured e-invoices in XML (Oman e-invoicing schema), clear each invoice in real time via application-to-application API transmission to the OTA fiscal clearance system, and print a QR-coded summary on B2C / POS receipts. Full-service restaurant chains with POS terminals are captured in the rollout by turnover tier; after activation, POS-integrated e-invoicing with digital fiscal stamping becomes a legal condition for input VAT deduction. The hotpot / condiment supply chain must integrate OTA-certified software or ERP add-ons via API; failure to clear invoices in real time causes rejection of the invoice for tax purposes and attracts administrative fines.

OTA performs systematic data-matching between Musharaka e-invoice clearances, customs Bayan export declarations, and bank transaction reporting. If a zero-rated export claim fails the documentation or repatriation test, the supply is recharacterized as a standard-rated Omani domestic sale: OTA assesses 5% VAT on the full export value, disallows the related input VAT, and imposes statutory late-interest and daily administrative fines for inaccurate declarations. Wrongfully claimed refunds are clawed back with additional taxes; for deliberate VAT evasion, the VAT Law provides fines up to OMR 100,000 plus imprisonment, and the Tax Procedures Law authorizes criminal referral. Export proceeds not credited / repatriated within 180 days from the date of export also create Central Bank of Oman exchange-control violations, heightened AML/CTF scrutiny, and may independently invalidate the zero-rating benefit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Rial Omani (OMR) is fully convertible on both current and capital account under Omani law; OMR is pegged to the USD at approximately 1 USD = OMR 0.3845 (band ±0.03%). There is no general exchange control regime, prior CBO approval is not required for repatriation of profits, dividends, or foreign shareholder loans, and inbound equity and outbound trade payments flow freely. Residual hurdles are exclusively documentary: a valid tax clearance certificate, proof of commercial registration (CR) in force, compliance with CBO AML/KYC rules, and completion of corporate bookkeeping up to date. No restriction exists on foreign ownership of a 100% foreign-owned SPC (LLC), consistent with Oman's WTO and GCC trade liberalization commitments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory export proceeds surrender or conversion requirements on private-sector exporters; exporters are free to retain or dispose of foreign currency receipts through licensed banks. For the hotpot restaurant and condiment supply model, any cross-border sale through the Omani SPC (e.g., shelf-stable condiment export), the statutory practice requires only that export documentation (commercial invoice, customs declaration from Royal Oman Police Customs, bill of lading/airway bill) be lodged with the receiving Omani bank for statistical and AML tracing; the bank will automatically credit the OMR or foreign-currency account with no prescribed conversion ratio or surrender deadline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established under Royal Decree 114/2000 (Banking Law), vested with monetary policy, bank licensing, and foreign exchange reserve management; Foreign Exchange Administration functions are executed under CBO Circulars, including the Oman Clearing and Settlement System (OCHATS) rulebook for OMR payment rails and the AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 57/2014) for bank documentation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW for offshore digital EMI providers as primary treasury rail; MEDIUM only as an auxiliary inbound collection channel in USD/foreign currency outside the OMR system. CBO does not license foreign electronic money institutions (EMIs) to hold OMR settlement accounts, does not grant them access to OCHATS (the domestic OMR real-time gross settlement and clearing rail), and treats unlicensed foreign EMI activity in OMR as an unauthorized banking service. An offshore EMIs such as Wise or Revolut business accounts may serve only for inbound monies from the overseas shareholder or for FX conversion before local wires; the Omani SPC must maintain its primary operating and capital accounts at a licensed Omani commercial bank to clear POS and merchant-acquiring proceeds and to comply with Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) expectations on capital ingress via a licensed bank.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 50,000; the first OMR 50,000 of annual taxable income is taxed at 0%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose withholding tax on dividends paid by an Omani company to a non-resident shareholder

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filing and final payment due within four (4) months after the end of the tax year; for a 31 December year-end the deadline is 30 April of the following year

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File required where aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 500,000 per tax year; Master File required where the consolidated group annual revenue exceeds OMR 10,000,000; Country-by-Country Report required for groups with consolidated revenue of EUR 750 million or more

Economic substance scope: Oman has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations; substance is enforced through the GAAR, DTA beneficial-ownership requirements, and the Commercial Companies Law obligation that the SPC maintain a licensed Oman address, real premises, employees, bank accounts, and actual management in-country; a passive or letterbox SPC faces DTA denial and deemed-income assessment

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Additional tax at 15% plus a late-payment surcharge of 10% of the unpaid tax and 1% per month of delay; criminal fine of up to OMR 50,000

Director personal liability: Director, manager, or authorized signatory may be sentenced to imprisonment of up to 5 years; personal fine up to OMR 50,000; travel ban and asset freeze order by the OTA
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits; re-characterization of the SPC as a sham leading to tax at 15% on deemed profits; penalty up to OMR 5,000 plus cancellation of the Commercial Registration

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for the SPC's unpaid taxes via veil-piercing; travel ban imposed; imprisonment up to 3 years under the Commercial Companies Law for false declarations
AML / CFT Violation

Statutory sanction: Fines of up to OMR 50,000 under Royal Decree 30/2016; forfeiture of proceeds; suspension or revocation of the merchant acquiring license and the food business license

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer face personal criminal prosecution, imprisonment of up to 7 years for money laundering offenses, and disqualification from managing licensed entities
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length profit; additional tax at 15%; monthly 1% surcharge on the adjusted difference; administrative fine up to OMR 5,000

Director personal liability: Directors signing false TP declarations incur personal criminal fines, director disqualification, and joint liability for the adjusted tax if the SPC is insolvent
