Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), official portal: https://www.ota.gov.om; taxpayer e-services portal: https://tax.ota.gov.om; customs export clearance is integrated via the Oman Customs Al-Bayan/ASYCUDA single window, which shares the same OTA taxpayer account.

E-government filing portal

SPC registration flow: (1) incorporate the foreign-owned Single Person Company through the MoCIIP Invest Easy portal and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR); (2) file Form TAX-REG-01 with OTA within the statutory post-commencement deadline, attaching the CR, notarized Memorandum/Articles of the SPC, sole-shareholder identity documents and authorized signatory forms; (3) receive the unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering both CIT and VAT; (4) submit a separate VAT registration application once annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500 (trailing or forward-looking 12 months), providing turnover projections and the CR; (5) where the foreign shareholder has no Omani civil ID, complete OTA e-KYC remotely or through a licensed tax agent; (6) link the TIN to the customs e-Bayan profile and the LC settlement bank account for export zero-rating and FX remittance matching.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the SPC possesses separate legal personality under Omani Commercial Companies Law (RD 18/2019) and is a juridical taxable person; it is taxed on worldwide income as an independent CIT taxpayer and is separately accountable for VAT; no pass-through or fiscal transparency election exists for an SPC, and a 100% foreign-owned SPC remains fully within the Omani corporate tax net, with distributions to the sole shareholder subject to dividend withholding tax at the time of distribution.

Available election forms

Oman does not have a US-style check-the-box election. Available statutory mechanisms include: (a) small-business CIT concession for qualifying wholly Omani-owned small companies; (b) VAT grouping election under VAT Law Article 35, with OTA approval, allowing the SPC to file a single VAT return with affiliated entities; (c) reverse-charge election for imported services from non-residents; (d) treaty permanent-establishment threshold elections under Oman's DTA network, subject to MLI limitation-of-benefit provisions; and (e) group loss-transfer applications where a 75% group ownership connection is substantiated.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible under a long-standing fixed peg of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD, defended by the CBO through managed intervention, which removes material convertibility risk for USD-denominated grain brokerage flows. Current-account transactions, including cross-border brokerage commissions, shipping disbursements, and trade-related service payments, are freely convertible with no prior exchange-control authorization. Capital-account operations for a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company, comprising equity injection, profit distribution, dividend remittance, and liquidation surplus repatriation, are likewise permissible without quantitative ceilings, provided the SPC holds a valid Commercial Registration from MoCIIP and maintains tax compliance status with the Oman Tax Authority. The only practical hurdles are documentary: Omani banks require a board/shareholder resolution, underlying trade or service contracts, and a tax clearance certificate where outward remittances exceed normal operating thresholds. No exchange-control licensing regime analogous to a restrictive central-planning system applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no legally fixed meaningful repatriation timeline on export or trade-service proceeds. A bulk-grain brokerage SPC may lawfully retain 100% of USD, EUR, CNY, or other convertible currency receipts indefinitely in a current account or foreign-currency account at any CBO-licensed bank. The sole regulatory requirements are statistical and suspicious-transaction reporting: banks log balance-of-payments purpose codes for wire movements and file Suspicious Operations Reports for cash transactions at or above OMR 10,000 or unusual non-cash patterns. Brokerage commissions settled in USD may remain in the USD account without parity conversion into OMR unless the SPC elects to convert for local operating expenses.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - Banking Supervision Department; with full statutory mandate over commercial bank licensing, foreign exchange policy, cross-border payment and clearing infrastructure, trade finance governance, and AML/CFT enforcement under Royal Decree 30/2016 as amended; corporate investment registration oversight is shared with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Rating: LOW / LEGALLY RESTRICTED. The CBO does not license offshore digital EMI-only institutions (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Revolut Business, Currencycloud) to hold OMR, open virtual corporate accounts for Omani legal entities, or perform OMR/foreign-currency acquisition and payout on behalf of an Oman-domiciled issuer or beneficiary. Use of an unlicensed offshore EMI as a primary settlement rail creates an unlawful collection-channel exposure and will be treated as a compliance red flag by CBO-licensed banks when reconciling trade documents. The viable architecture is to use the Omani bank account as the definitive treasury spine and, at most, employ an EMI solely for small ancillary subsistence payments to overseas contract service providers, never for grain purchase settlements, brokerage commission receipts, or capital movements.

Mandatory Audit Threshold Small Company Audit Exemption Regime

Turnover: An SPC engaged in bulk grain brokerage is mandatorily required to appoint a licensed auditor if any of the following thresholds is exceeded: (i) annual gross turnover above OMR 500,000 (approximately USD 1.3 million); (ii) paid-up capital above OMR 1,000,000 (approximately USD 2.6 million); (iii) year-end total assets above OMR 1,000,000; or (iv) more than 50% of turnover derived from transactions with related parties. An SPC below all thresholds may waive the statutory audit by written shareholders' resolution, but the Oman Tax Authority may still require audited accounts in an income tax assessment.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat corporate income tax for all juridical persons under Royal Decree 21/2023 (new Income Tax Law), applicable to tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2024. Prior law (RD 28/2009) applied 3% on the first OMR 30,000 and 15% above; that bracket is abolished for new tax years.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose any withholding tax on dividends distributed by an Omani Single Person Company to its non-resident shareholder. Repatriation of after-tax profits via dividends is therefore free of any additional Omani withholding burden.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four months following the end of the fiscal/tax year; for a 31 December year-end the return and full payment are due by 30 April. One extension of up to two months may be applied for through the OTax portal before the original due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 75,000: aggregate annual related-party transactions triggering the mandatory related-party disclosure annexure in the CIT return. OMR 500,000: aggregate related-party transactions exceeding this threshold require a full Local File (functional analysis, benchmarking study, and arm's length pricing documentation). OMR 25,000,000: consolidated group revenue exceeding this threshold for the ultimate parent requires a Master File. All documentation must be submitted in Arabic or English within 30 days of a formal request by the Secretariat General of Taxation.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute analogous to the UAE or Bahrain. However, economic substance is enforced indirectly through the Place of Effective Management (POEM) doctrine, the General Anti-Abuse Rules (GAAR), and the new TP documentation rules. A foreign-owned SPC acting as a bulk grain brokerage must demonstrate in Oman: a leased office or warehouse, a resident executive manager with authority to conclude contracts, local board minutes, an active OMR bank account, and substantive negotiation and logistics management activity conducted in Oman. Failure to meet these tests risks the entity being treated as a tax resident elsewhere, denial of DTA benefits, and re-allocation of 100% of its profit to the foreign shareholder's jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Penalty equal to 100% to 200% of the evaded tax under RD 21/2023, plus criminal fine up to OMR 20,000 and imprisonment of responsible individuals for up to five years; the SPC's commercial registration may be suspended.

Director personal liability: Directors, managers, and the legal representative of the SPC who sign or cause false returns to be filed are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and fines. Courts may issue travel bans, freeze personal assets, and impose imprisonment without the option of a fine.
Shell Company / Sham Substance Arrangement

Statutory sanction: Re-characterization of the entity as non-resident under POEM rules; assessment of tax on deemed Omani-source profit of up to 100% of the brokerage margin; denial of the benefit of the Oman tax treaty network; administrative penalties of up to OMR 10,000.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced under the Omani Commercial Companies Law; the foreign shareholder may be personally assessed on SPC income if the entity is a façade. Directors face disqualification from serving on any Omani company board and potential asset seizure.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length profit level, additional CIT at 15% on the upward adjustment, a documentation-related fine of up to OMR 10,000, and default interest at 1% per month of the additional tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the TP annexure or approved the intercompany pricing are jointly liable for the additional tax and penalties; uncontested TP adjustments may be converted into a criminal evasion case if concealment is established.
AML / Trade-Based Sanctions Violations

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's AML Law (RD 30/2016): imprisonment of responsible individuals for up to seven years and fines up to OMR 10,000 per violation; fines for the SPC itself of up to OMR 100,000; revocation of the OTax registration card and commercial license.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners are criminally liable for money laundering offenses committed with their knowledge; authorities may impose travel bans, freeze all bank accounts of the SPC and its directors, and confiscate vessels and cargo connected to the illicit transaction.
Profit Stripping in Lieu of Dividends

Statutory sanction: Re-characterization of the payments as dividends coupled with denial of the deduction, additional 15% CIT on the disallowed expenses, a 10% re-characterization WHT review, and a penalty up to double the evaded tax if falsified documentation is found.

Director personal liability: Directors who execute false service agreements are personally subject to fines, civil restitution equal to the disgorged amounts, and criminal prosecution for commercial fraud; the Commercial Registration may be struck off and the directors banned from the Sultanate.
