Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Directorate General of Tax, Muscat; e-service portal at https://www.taxoman.gov.om (OTA Digital Tax Services platform)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) must register for a Tax Identification Number (TIN) with the Oman Tax Authority before commencing activity. The foreign shareholder files the OTA registration form via the Oman Tax Authority e-portal, submitting (i) the certified constitutional deed and articles of the SPC, (ii) certificate of commercial registration from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, (iii) the prior special / high-risk license approval issued by the Central Bank of Oman for virtual card issuance and payment services, (iv) proof of local registered office, and (v) passport/CRP of the shareholder and manager. OTA issues a unique TIN and issues a VAT registration number simultaneously if the SPC's taxable supplies exceed the statutory thresholds. For non-resident entities without a permanent establishment, OTA requires a tax representative appointment before issuing the TIN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Omani SPC is taxed as a separate juridical person under the Omani Income Tax Law, with no pass-through of losses or income to the foreign shareholder; the SPC is also a distinct taxable person under the Omani VAT Law.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election exists under Omani tax law. An SPC may elect voluntary VAT registration under Article 44 of the VAT Executive Regulations where annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 250,000 but remain below the mandatory OMR 500,000 threshold. The SPC may also elect the Small or Medium Enterprise income tax regime if it meets the Omani SME turnover and ownership criteria, subject to prior approval by OTA. Hybrid mismatch election mechanisms are limited to alignment rules under the OECD BEPS Action 2 framework as adopted through Oman's CbCR / international tax compliance; no separate domestic hybrid election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies / reverse-charge / import value in the preceding 12 months. Voluntary registration: OMR 250,000 of annual taxable supplies. A non-resident SPC is subject to mandatory registration where it becomes liable to pay VAT in Oman through a permanent establishment or through specified reverse-charge transactions.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating is available for (i) the physical export of goods outside Oman and outside the GCC customs union, provided export occurs within 90 days of the supply and the supplier retains mandated export evidence; (ii) international transport of goods or passengers where the destination is outside Oman and the transport is provided to a non-resident; (iii) supplies of services to a non-resident customer outside Oman, including overseas card-management and B2B fintech service contracts, subject to the place-of-supply rules and proof that such services are not supplied from a permanent establishment in Oman; and (iv) certain diplomatic/relief supplies. For the virtual-card issuing business, clearing, interchange and B2B spend-platform fees charged to a non-resident principal are zero-rated if the recipient is outside Oman and direct use or benefit of the service occurs outside Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Oman Customs draft declaration / discharge proof)
  • Bill of Lading (or equivalent airway bill, SRO, sea waybill / export cargo manifest)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (FX inward receipt / bank credit confirmation evidencing payment from the foreign customer)

Oman does not currently operate a real-time fiscal clearance or digital stamping mandate. Taxpayers are required to issue tax invoices under VAT Law Article 49; approved e-invoicing software is acceptable, and OTA has published guidelines for archiving XML/PDF invoices. A phased mandatory e-invoicing regime with integrated digital reporting is under development but, as of the current regime, there is no API-based clearance of every invoice with OTA in real time.

OTA may disregard an export zero-rating claim if the required documentation is missing, incomplete or inconsistent, and will reverse the zero-rating with VAT assessed at 5% plus delayed payment interest at 1.5% per month or part month. Fraudulent zero-rating claims or repeated documentation failures are subject to penalties up to 100% of the tax due, administrative fines, imprisonment for tax evasion under the Omani Income Tax and VAT Law enforcement provisions, and indefinite suspension of the SPC's electronic invoicing / refund entitlements; serious cases trigger criminal investigation and the denial of future zero-rating claims.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Royal Decree 9/2023, effective 1 January 2023; replaces the prior 12%/15% tiered structure)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic rate: dividends distributed by an Omani SPC to a non-resident shareholder are not subject to Omani withholding tax under domestic law. The SPC is therefore not legally required to withhold on dividends, but must report distributions if requested.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months from the end of the tax year. For a calendar-year SPC, the return and full tax payment are due by 30 April of the following year. OTA may grant a discretionary extension of up to 2 additional months upon written request and advance payment of estimated tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: No published Oman-specific Local File/Master File monetary threshold for an SPC; however, all related-party transactions must be disclosed annually and substantiated contemporaneously. OTA may request a full TP documentation package where aggregate related-party revenue or expenditure exceeds OMR 500,000 in a tax year. Country-by-Country Reporting applies to Omani-parented MNE groups with consolidated group revenue of at least OMR 375,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Oman has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations statute similar to the UAE, but the Income Tax Law (RD 9/2023) contains substance-over-form, GAAR, and beneficial-ownership rules. For a licensed B2B virtual-card issuer, OTA, the Central Bank of Oman, and CMA require: a physical registered office in Oman, local directors and compliance officers, actual management of card programs, local AML/KYC decisions, and substantive employment. A shell entity or a mere booking vehicle used to divert profits offshore will be disregarded, with profits re-attributed to the controlling foreign entity.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fines equal to 100%-200% of the under-assessed tax (up to 3x under prior law), additional fixed penalties up to OMR 50,000, deemed-profit assessment, seizure of records, and revocation of tax clearance; criminal referral to the Public Prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors and the sole shareholder who sign false returns become jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; the corporate veil is pierced for fraud. Penalties include civil restitution, travel ban, asset freeze, and imprisonment for up to 3 years; the Commercial Registration may be struck off.
Shell Company / Sham Substance

Statutory sanction: OTA disregards the SPC under GAAR/substance-over-form; recharacterizes the structure; denies treaty benefits; imposes a deemed-profit tax assessment of up to 18% of gross revenue; additional fines up to OMR 10,000 and denial of loss carryforward.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owner loses limited liability protection; OTA may issue a travel ban and worldwide asset freeze against the controlling shareholder; director personally liable for restitution where SPC funds were diverted to private use.
Unlicensed Payment Services / AML Breach

Statutory sanction: CBO/CMA enforcement: cease-and-desist order, fines of OMR 10,000-500,000, daily penalty for continuing breach, and forced winding-up; AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016): fines up to OMR 100,000, confiscation of proceeds, and disqualification from the financial sector.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the SPC's sole shareholder face personal criminal liability for money laundering if they knew or should have known of the scheme; imprisonment up to 7 years, travel bans, extradition exposure, and permanent prohibition from serving as director of any licensed entity in Oman.
Withholding Tax and Cross-Border Payment Failure

Statutory sanction: OTA assesses the unpaid 10% WHT as a primary liability plus a penalty up to OMR 10,000; deductibility of the underlying payment is denied in the SPC's CIT return; late remittance surcharges accrue; non-filed WHT has no limitation period.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve or execute un-withheld payments are jointly liable for the 10% WHT, penalties, and interest; enforcement includes bank garnishment and travel ban; criminal referral for deliberate evasion can result in imprisonment up to 3 years.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing / Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to arm's-length results with additional tax at 15%; penalties from 10% to 50% of the adjusted tax; fixed fines of OMR 1,000-5,000 for non-disclosure; interest on underpayment; potential de facto denial of the entire payment if substance is absent.

Director personal liability: If a TP adjustment arises from deliberate misrepresentation or manipulated financial statements, the filing director and beneficial owner are personally liable for the additional tax; OTA may impose a travel ban and refer for fraud prosecution; the SPC's limited liability may be pierced for misconduct.
