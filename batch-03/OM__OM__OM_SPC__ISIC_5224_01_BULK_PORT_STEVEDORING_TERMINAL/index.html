Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) - Commercial Registry and Business Licensing Department (Invest Easy e-Portal).

Governing statute

Oman Commercial Companies Law, Royal Decree No. 18/2019, Articles 255-262 (Single Person Company); Commercial Register Law, Royal Decree No. 3/1974 as amended; Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2019 (100% foreign ownership permitted for non-restricted activities).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Single Person Company Memorandum of Association (MOA), Commercial Registration (CR) application, and immediate Commercial License issued under the MoCIIP licensing system.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 43 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree 18/2019; capital is freely specified in the MOA, fully subscribed and issued to the sole shareholder, and may be contributed in cash or in kind. No escrow account is statutorily mandated for non-state-license port operations, but the competent Port Authority may require a performance deposit under berth lease terms.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) fire safety clearance and annual inspection certificate for quayside warehouses and workshops; structural load and port-crane certification by the competent engineering authority; Port Health Office clearance for grain and agro-bulk cargo handling; Ministry of Labour occupational safety and workplace registration for all stevedoring gangs, including wharf-side working-at-height, confined-space, and hot-work permits; OHSAS 45001-based occupational health and safety audit is required prior to issuance of the final operating license.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree 18/2019; capital is freely specified in the MOA, fully subscribed and issued to the sole shareholder, and may be contributed in cash or in kind. No escrow account is statutorily mandated for non-state-license port operations, but the competent Port Authority may require a performance deposit under berth lease terms.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), the statutory successor to the Secretariat General of Taxation; designated e-portal: https://taxoman.gov.om, supplemented by the MOCIIP commercial registration portal and the Bayan/ASYCUDA customs single window.

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Omani SPC: (1) incorporate the SPC and obtain a Commercial Registration certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) plus Oman Chamber of Commerce (OCEC) membership; (2) register with OTA for income tax on the Taxoman portal within 30 days of CR issuance and obtain the Tax Identification Number (TIN)/tax card; (3) file for VAT registration on the Taxoman portal within 30 days after annual taxable supplies exceed the OMR 38,500 mandatory threshold (voluntary registration at OMR 19,250; non-resident suppliers supplying taxable goods/services in Oman must register regardless of threshold); (4) submit the OTA-prescribed support bundle: authenticated power of attorney, shareholder/director passports, shareholders' register, lease or port terminal service agreement, chamber registration, bank account confirmation, and OTA e-signature credentials; (5) complete customs/export registration in the Bayan single window to validate future export declarations; (6) notify OTA of any change in registered particulars within 30 days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the SPC is a ring-fenced juridical person taxed independently under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 57/2023) and is not eligible for pass-through treatment; residency attaches to Omani-source and Oman-connected business income; no sub-national or municipal tax distinction exists in the unitary Omani fiscal system. — Foreign-owned Omani SPC: (1) incorporate the SPC and obtain a Commercial Registration certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) plus Oman Chamber of Commerce (OCEC) membership; (2) register with OTA for income tax on the Taxoman portal within 30 days of CR issuance and obtain the Tax Identification Number (TIN)/tax card; (3) file for VAT registration on the Taxoman portal within 30 days after annual taxable supplies exceed the OMR 38,500 mandatory threshold (voluntary registration at OMR 19,250; non-resident suppliers supplying taxable goods/services in Oman must register regardless of threshold); (4) submit the OTA-prescribed support bundle: authenticated power of attorney, shareholder/director passports, shareholders' register, lease or port terminal service agreement, chamber registration, bank account confirmation, and OTA e-signature credentials; (5) complete customs/export registration in the Bayan single window to validate future export declarations; (6) notify OTA of any change in registered particulars within 30 days.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the SPC is a ring-fenced juridical person taxed independently under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 57/2023) and is not eligible for pass-through treatment; residency attaches to Omani-source and Oman-connected business income; no sub-national or municipal tax distinction exists in the unitary Omani fiscal system.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box entity classification or hybrid-mismatch deduction election. Available statutory elections are limited to: (a) selection of the accounting period (calendar or fiscal year) on first registration; (b) adoption of capital-allowance pooling/depreciation methods under the Income Tax Law; (c) ordering and waiver of the 5-year operating-loss carry-forward; (d) invocation of tax-treaty reduced rates, PE thresholds and beneficial-ownership documentation (electronic tax residence certificate); and (e) a single structural election at the pre-licensing stage to operate as a branch of a foreign head office instead of an SPC, which is permanently binding absent liquidation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration if the 12-month value of taxable supplies exceeds OMR 38,500 (approx. USD 100,000); voluntary registration where supplies exceed OMR 19,250; no threshold relief applies to non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies in Oman, who are required to register or self-assess.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% rate is available where: (i) the stevedoring, loading/unloading, lashing, tallying or terminal handling service is performed on bulk cargo that is physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the GCC implementing territory; (ii) the supply is directly related to the export of goods or constitutes ancillary support to international sea transport; (iii) the SPC holds the mandatory documentary export and transport evidence before the VAT return period for the supply expires; and (iv) the goods are shown to have left Omani customs territory. Export-port services rendered to a non-resident principal may likewise be zero-rated where the service is not consumed in Oman. Internal terminal cargo handling not linked to a valid export declaration remains taxable at 5%.

  • Validated Customs Export Declaration (Bayan/ASYCUDA electronic declaration with HS codes, weights and marks)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading or through bill of lading consigned to a port outside the GCC territory
  • Port/terminal out-turn tally sheets and stevedoring job dockets signed by the port authority
  • Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing bank transfer/FX receipt from the non-resident principal for the terminal/freight services
  • Commercial invoice, export packing list and cargo manifest showing the Omani port as the first port of exit
  • Copy of the SPC's VAT registration certificate and terminal-gate movement records

OTA e-invoicing is being rolled out in phases: Phase 1 (2025) covers large and selected high-risk VAT registrants, and Phase 2 (2026) will extend to all VAT registrants. In-scope taxpayers must issue machine-readable e-invoices (signed XML with QR code) using OTA-approved software/APIs, transmit invoice data near-real-time to the Taxoman central exchange, retain an electronic audit file, and settle VAT through monthly e-filed returns. No physical tax stamps or fiscal cash-register seals apply to port/terminal B2B stevedoring invoices.

OTA will deny zero-rating and retrospectively claw back VAT previously refunded, adding an administrative penalty of 100% of the tax under the VAT Law where export documentation is deficient or fictitious. Willful evasion exposes responsible persons to imprisonment (three months to five years) and fines of OMR 1,000–25,000, doubled on repeat offenses. The five-year statutory assessment period is reinforced by post-clearance audits of Bayan declarations, port logs, bank remittance trails and bills of lading, with additional sanctions for customs false declarations and potential debarment from the port franchise.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; the current account is fully convertible without foreign exchange control approval. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the USD at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Capital and current account transactions, including profit repatriation by a foreign-owned SPC, require a valid underlying commercial/tax document, a CBO-licensed commercial bank as intermediary, and, for dividends, an audited financial statement and a tax clearance certificate. No multiple currency practices or exchange surrender requirements apply. Foreign equity capital and shareholder loans may be freely injected and repatriated subject to the CBO's Balance of Payments reporting thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory requirement in Oman to surrender or mandatorily convert export or service proceeds into OMR. Stevedoring and terminal service receivables in foreign currency may be retained, transferred, or converted at a licensed bank. For bulk cargo services, the bank will require an underlying invoice/contract, a port authority confirmation, and the filing of the CBO Foreign Exchange Transaction Form (for amounts above OMR 10,000). No specific repatriation timeline is imposed on services; a 90-day repatriation convention applies only to physical goods exporters and is not a statutory CBO rule. All SWIFT credits are subject to standard AML/KYC screening.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – Al Rumaila Complex, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman; the CBO exercises licensing, supervisory, and foreign exchange administration authority under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) and the Foreign Exchange Rating Rules.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-Medium. The CBO Regulatory Framework for Payment Service Providers (PSPs) permits licensed fintech EMIs to offer e-money and cross-border payment services, and a foreign SPC may use a CBO-licensed PSP for small ticket operational payments. However, offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Revolut, Wise) are not authorised to maintain OMR settlement accounts or provide direct SWIFT routing to the SPC's port authority clearing account; the port terminal operator will require tier-1 bank MT103/MT202 transfers. Consequently, a domestic commercial bank account is mandatory for the project, and EMI usage should be limited to employee or minor expense disbursements.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder injects equity capital via a SWIFT MT103 or MT202 from the home bank to the SPC's Capital Escrow Account at the tier-1 Omani bank. Bank instructions must declare the CBO Balance of Payments purpose code 6010 (Direct Investment – Equity Capital) or 6020 (Direct Investment – Shareholder Loan) in the payment field. The SPC must provide: the bank's foreign investment registration letter, the SPC's Commercial Register (CR) certificate, the CR's 'capital' clause, and a board or shareholders' resolution. For shareholder loans, a formal loan agreement and evidence of arm's length interest must be on file. The funds may be blocked for up to 5 business days until the bank verifies the source of wealth and the capital escrow account is debited to the operational account only after the SPC's capital increase or injection is recorded by the Oman Commercial and Industrial Registration Directorate. Non-resident shareholders can also use an inward direct investment declaration filed through the CBO portal.Outbound disbursements for bulk port terminal operations use three primary clearing rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for all international service payments, freight charges, equipment leases, and settlement with overseas vendors; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) issued by the Omani tier-1 bank in favour of the manufacturer of port/trade equipment, with all-incoterms documentary compliance; (3) local OMR payments through the CBO RTGS/ACH system (RAYMENT) for port authority tariffs, customs, salaries, and domestic suppliers. All outbound wires above OMR 10,000 require a completed Foreign Exchange Transaction Form, the commercial invoice or contract, and reporting of the CBO balance-of-payments code (e.g., 7010 – Transportation of goods’ services, 7800 – Merchant services). The stevedoring tariff payment to the Port Authority (Oman Ports Authority or Port of Salalah/Duqm operators) is handled via a local direct debit with the operational account.Direct deliverable OMR forward contracts: bank confirmed; tenors up to 12 months; allowed for monetising USD, EUR, GBP receivables from stevedoring clients without requiring CBO approval, provided the underlying commercial contract exists.FX swaps (USD/OMR, EUR/OMR) for temporary working capital conversion; required for managing intercurrency timing mismatches; subject to standard internal credit cheques and CBO reporting.Options contracts on non-convertible currency pairs (e.g., EUR/USD cross) used only for hedging the Omani rial peg against USD; direct OMR options are permissible with CBO licensed banks, though the market is shallow.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) are not permissible for OMR because the rial is a fully deliverable currency pegged to USD; instead, conventional deliverable forwards are used. NDFs on other regional currencies may be executed only with CBO licensed banks and from otc limits.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat rate for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2024 under Royal Decree 50/2023 amending the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 9/2017); prior structure was 3% on taxable income up to OMR 30,000 and 12% above OMR 30,000.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

15% statutory dividend withholding tax introduced by Royal Decree 50/2023, effective for distributions made on or after 1 January 2024; the rate before that date was 0%. Dividend distributions to resident Omani shareholders and to Omani resident corporate shareholders are outside the scope of the WHT.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Cross-border related-party transactions exceeding OMR 30,000 per tax year require a transfer pricing disclosure in the annual CIT return; controlled transactions above OMR 500,000 require a contemporaneous Local File; an Oman Master File is required where the SPC belongs to an MNE group with consolidated annual revenue of EUR 750 million or more under the OECD GloBE-aligned rules.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted separate Economic Substance Regulations akin to the UAE; however, the General Anti-Avoidance Rule and place-of-effective-management principles oblige the SPC to demonstrate real commercial substance. A bulk port stevedoring terminal holding a terminal concession, physical cranes and berth assets, operational marine employees and local invoicing inherently satisfies the substance test. MNE groups within the Pillar 2 scope must apply the OECD GloBE rules for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Deliberate Under-Declaration of Oman-Source Income

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to 100% of the evaded tax plus a monthly 1% late-payment surcharge; criminal conviction carries imprisonment up to three years and a fine up to OMR 5,000; the Tax Authority may reopen the last five tax years and issue a best-of-judgment assessment; the Port Authority may suspend the terminal operating licence.

Director personal liability: A director, general manager or authorised signatory who presents a false return is jointly and severally liable for the underpaid tax and penalties; a court may impose a travel ban, freeze personal assets and order civil restitution of diverted terminal revenues.
Shell Company - Artificial Offshore Structuring

Statutory sanction: GAAR recharacterisation: deductible expenses are disallowed, the 10% WHT is assessed on gross outbound payments and a penalty of up to 100% of the adjusted tax is imposed; the commercial registry may cancel the registration of the SPC.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where the SPC is a mere facade; directors face personal assessment for corporate CIT, civil restitution of diverted profits, asset freezes and court-ordered travel bans.
