Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Companies Registration Department, Directorate General of Commercial Registration and Licensing, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), Sultanate of Oman; filings are accepted through the InvestEasy One-Stop Portal.

Governing statute

Commercial Companies Law of the Sultanate of Oman, Royal Decree No. 18/2019, as amended by Royal Decree No. 83/2022, including the Single Person Company and Limited Liability Company formation chapters, the general commercial register and publication provisions, and the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree No. 50/2019) national-treatment provisions; applicable MoCIIP fee and e-registration ministerial decisions.

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Single Person Company Constitution Deed (Memorandum and Articles of Association / النظام الأساسي لشركة الشخص الواحد), registered in the Commercial Register and evidenced by the Commercial Registration Certificate (Sijil).

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is fixed by Royal Decree No. 18/2019 for Single Person Companies; the subscribed capital is determined in the constitution deed. The full declared capital must be deposited in an Oman-licensed bank account before registration and evidenced by a Capital Deposit Certificate; the funds become unrestricted once the Commercial Registration Certificate is issued. For this services scope, standard practice recommends a declared paid-up capital of OMR 10,000 (approx. USD 26,000) to support expatriate visa quotas.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – الهيئة العامة للضرائب; central digital platform: Mutanef (منتفع) / Taxa e-services portal (taxa.om), integrated with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) Invest Easy single-window for commercial registration

E-government filing portal

A 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first secure the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate via the MoCIIP Invest Easy platform; following incorporation, the SPC must apply on the Tax Authority Mutanef portal for the unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering both Corporate Income Tax (CIT) and VAT. Mandatory registration is triggered when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500; voluntary registration is permitted from OMR 19,250. Supporting documents include: notarized and attested Articles of Incorporation/SPC constitution, Commercial Registration certificate, business license for ISIC 6920 / NAICS 541219, passport copy and Emirates ID/Omani residency documents of the foreign member and CFO-director nominee, board resolution delegating a registered tax agent, audited financial statements (where requested), and a certified power of attorney for the appointed agent.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation – the Omani SPC is a distinct juridical person subject to the Omani Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended by Royal Decree 9/2023) and the new Corporate Income Tax Law; taxable at a 15% flat corporate income tax rate on profits determined under arm's length accounting with territorial scope over Oman-source income; no pass-through or transparent classification is available

Available election forms

Oman applies a rigid entity-level regime without US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections. Statutory elections are limited to: (i) VAT Tax Group election under the Oman VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020) for 100%-related resident entities; (ii) voluntary VAT registration election above OMR 19,250; (iii) small-business CIT relief/exemption application where the business falls within OMR-prescribed SME thresholds; (iv) election of the standard quarterly VAT filing cycle or annual simplified cycle; (v) irrevocable election to deduct input VAT under the general meaning of business for holding/management activities; and (vi) appointment of a tax agent through the Mutanef portal.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 mandatory registration threshold on annual taxable supplies and imports (approx. USD 100,000); OMR 19,250 voluntary threshold; non-resident suppliers must register regardless of turnover where the state of supply is Oman and the non-resident is the deemed supplier; a separate OMR 1,000 import-based registration trigger applies for persons only carrying imports into Oman

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% VAT) is available where: (i) goods are physically exported outside the GCC by the SPC or on its behalf, delivery occurs within the contract and the goods leave Oman within 90 days of the time of supply, with customs export confirmation; (ii) services are supplied for the direct benefit of a non-resident person who is outside Oman and for whom the benefit is entirely enjoyed outside the GCC (the virtual CFO advisory and multicurrency bookkeeping services must not be consumed in Oman and must not relate to moveable/immovable Omani property); (iii) transport and ancillary transport services relating to the export of goods from Oman to a place outside the GCC; (iv) supplies directly connected to a qualifying GCC customs suspension arrangement. Input VAT attributable exclusively to zero-rated supplies is creditable in full; mixed-use expenditure must be apportioned on an acceptable pro-rata method agreed with the OTA.

  • Customs Export Declaration / SAD issued via the Omani Customs Bayan/e-SABI electronic system, sealed and date-stamped
  • Commercial invoice for the export sale (showing OMR 0% VAT line and export zero-rating note)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical dispatch from Oman to a non-GCC destination
  • CBO bank statement or Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing receipt of foreign currency proceeds into the SPC's Omani bank account
  • Original purchase/contract documentation for the CFO and bookkeeping services demonstrating the non-resident benefit and place of supply
  • Delivery/export movement record (container, seal, port-to-port tracking) and any certificate of origin where required by the destination customs authority

Oman is in the transition phase toward a national mandatory e-invoicing regime. The OTA has announced a phased continuous transaction control (CTC) clearance mandate for B2B and B2G invoices, with sequenced go-live for large filers from 2026 (taxpayer registration, certified provider onboarding, and real-time clearance in the Mutanef platform). Current obligations require all VAT-registered businesses to issue machine-readable e-invoices with QR-compliant digital stamping, to retain structured audit files (SAF) for tax audits, and to report invoice-level data in the annual/provisional VAT returns; paper originals and manual invoice registers are no longer accepted for input VAT recovery.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to documentary and bank-reconciliation review by the OTA during post-clearance audits (five-year statutory time bar). Where the export is challenged, the SPC must substantiate the 0% line within 30 days of inquiry; failure results in reversal of the zero-rating, assessment at 5% VAT with late payment penalties of 13% annualized interest (per the VAT Executive Regulations), and cash fines of OMR 500–10,000 per infraction. Fraudulent or fictitious exports, forged customs declarations, or non-repatriation of proceeds within the 180-day tolerance trigger anti-evasion sanctions: restitution of the input VAT re-credited, aggravated fines of not less than 100% of tax evaded (up to double the evaded amount), disqualification of the foreign member/director, suspension of the CR license, and referral to the Public Prosecution for tax evasion imprisonment penalties. The SPC is also exposed to clawback of any export refund already paid, plus compulsory audit of the prior three tax years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the U.S. Dollar at 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD. Current account transactions, including service fees, royalties, dividends, and profit remittances, are effectively convertible through licensed banks subject to AML/KYC compliance and documentary verification. Capital account transactions, including the injection and repatriation of foreign direct investment capital for a 100% foreign-owned SPC, are permitted under Oman's Foreign Investment Law and require filing of the MOCIIP foreign investment return, audited financial statements, and tax clearance from the Oman Tax Authority before a remitting bank will process the outflow. No generalized surrender or lock-in requirements apply to a Low-Risk advisory SPC, but banks will require proper purpose codes, underlying contracts, and CBO reporting forms for any transactions exceeding regulatory thresholds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a mandatory OMR conversion ratio or an automatic surrender requirement for export receipts. For goods exports, proceeds must be repatriated through a licensed authorized bank within six months from the date of the bill of lading or equivalent customs document, unless an extension has been obtained from the CBO. For cross-border service exports, including outsourced advisory and virtual-CFO services, inward receipts must be settled through the SPC's bank account within a commercially reasonable period, generally not exceeding 180 days from invoice date, and must be reported in the bank's CBO foreign-currency statement. Foreign-currency receipts may be held in transferable USD accounts at Omani banks, but any OMR clearing or government-related payment will require conversion through a CBO-licensed dealer.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — Foreign Exchange Administration under Royal Decree 87/88, Banking Law, and AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 30/2016 as amended).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low. The Central Bank of Oman does not currently license a separate Omani electronic-money-institution license equivalent to the EU EMI framework. A foreign/offshore digital EMI such as Wise or Stripe cannot hold a domestic OMR settlement account or clear through the Oman Automated Clearing House without a licensed Omani sponsor bank or agency arrangement. Such EMIs may be used only as peripheral invoicing/collection rails in USD or EUR for non-Omani clients, provided the EMI is authorised in its home jurisdiction and all final OMR liquidity is routed through a licensed Omani bank. The SPC should treat offshore EMIs as auxiliary collection channels, not as the primary treasury or FX clearing counterparty.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Initial paid-up capital from the non-resident parent or investor must be injected via SWIFT MT103 from the corporate bank account in the home jurisdiction to the SPC's licensed bank account in Oman. The remittance instruction must include the Omani corporate name, the purpose code 'Direct Investment Capital Injection' (BOP equivalent code 101), the SPC's Commercial Registration number, and reference to the MOCIIP foreign investment registration. The receiving Omani bank will validate the capital flow against the investment license, credit the capital escrow or operational account, and file the required CBO inward remittance report. Informal value-transfer methods, cash deposits, or crypto routing are not acceptable and will trigger account freezing and a Suspicious Transaction Report.For outbound settlement of trade or service obligations, the SPC must use licensed clearing rails: SWIFT MT103 for service fees and subcontractor disbursements; SWIFT MT700/MT710 for documentary commercial letters of credit; and MT400/MT202 for bank-to-bank reimbursements or internal cash concentration. The disbursing Omani bank requires an underlying invoice, proof of service receipt, tax-registration certificate, and, where the threshold exceeds the bank's internal reporting trigger, a CBO foreign-remittance form. Cross-border disbursements in OMR are generally restricted; payments must be settled in USD or other freely convertible currencies using the CBO-licensed dealing room rate.Deliverable FX spot transactions for OMR/USD and USD-cross pairs through licensed Omani banks at the official pegged exchange rate.Deliverable FX forwards up to 365 days with a licensed Omani bank, subject to evidence of underlying foreign-currency exposure and ISDA/CSA documentation.Cross-currency swaps between OMR and USD for asset-liability matching or currency-mismatch hedging, executed through a CBO-licensed bank.Simple European-style foreign-currency options with a CBO-licensed bank, permissible only for hedging underlying commercial exposure, not for speculative trading.Overseas offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing OMR/USD are not permissible as the primary hedge; Oman's pegged exchange rate and the absence of an independent offshore onshore OMR deliverability pool make such contracts legally unenforceable for local regulatory capital relief.
