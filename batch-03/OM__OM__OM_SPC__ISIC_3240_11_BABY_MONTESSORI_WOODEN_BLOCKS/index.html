Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No fixed statutory minimum capital applies to an Omani SPC under RD 18/2019; the subscribed capital must be sufficient to achieve the corporate industrial purpose. Cash capital must be fully deposited in a blocked capital/escalation account at an Oman-licensed bank before registration, evidenced by a bank certificate uploaded to Sijilat; in-kind capital contributions require an MCIIP-approved valuer report and full transfer of title to the company. The capital account remains restricted until the Commercial Registration Certificate is issued.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman, operating under the Ministry of Finance. Official unified tax e-services and registration portal: https://tax.om (Taxoman).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned SPC must first be incorporated and obtain a Commercial Register (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP). The foreign sole shareholder then appoints a resident manager or legal representative, who creates a taxpayer account on the OTA e-portal. The application must include: certified CR certificate, memorandum and articles of association, power of attorney for the representative, passport copies and photos of the shareholder and resident manager, registered-office lease or address evidence, and a bank reference. The OTA issues a Corporate Tax Registration Number (TIN). Where the SPC's annual taxable supplies equal or exceed the mandatory VAT threshold, or if the entity elects voluntary VAT registration, the same portal is used to file the electronic VAT registration application; the OTA then issues a separate VAT registration number. Certificates must be kept current, and zero-rated exporters should apply for VAT registration promptly to secure refund rights.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not provide a check-the-box or partnership pass-through election; an SPC is mandatorily taxed at entity level under the Corporate Income Tax provisions of Royal Decree No. 7/2023. Available statutory elections and mechanisms include: (i) tax grouping or consolidated filing for resident taxpayers that directly or indirectly own 100% of qualifying Omani subsidiaries, subject to prior OTA approval; (ii) small- and medium-enterprise relief for qualifying Omani SMEs whose annual taxable profit does not exceed OMR 100,000; (iii) election of accounting period aligned with the Commercial Register financial year; (iv) depreciation-method and capital-expenditure elections; (v) advance pricing agreements and mutual agreement procedures for transfer pricing; and (vi) foreign-tax-credit and relief mechanisms on qualifying overseas income. No hybrid-mismatch or look-through election exists; Oman applies a general anti-avoidance rule and transfer-pricing rules to re-characterize hybrid arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: annual taxable supplies exceeding OMR 500,000. Voluntary registration: annual taxable supplies exceeding OMR 200,000. Import VAT is payable on all commercial imports of goods regardless of registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under the Oman VAT Law (RD 121/2021 and Executive Regulations) applies to: (a) goods physically exported outside Oman/GCC implementing territory by the VAT registrant; (b) services directly connected to, and necessary for, exported goods (transport, freight, loading, packing, insurance) supplied to non-resident recipients; (c) international transport of goods and passengers and ancillary services; (d) supplies used in the operation of international flights and ocean-going ships; and (e) certain other supplies to non-residents. To sustain the 0% rate the exporter must evidence: a genuine commercial contract with a non-resident buyer; transfer of title and risk to the buyer; physical dispatch from Oman within the standard export-evidence window of 90 days from supply; customs exit approval; and settlement of consideration through licensed banks in OMR or foreign currency. Where input VAT exceeds output VAT, the net refund is claimed on the VAT return; the OTA may conduct documentary verification before releasing refunds. Although Omani law does not prescribe a fixed statutory export-proceeds repatriation deadline, the 180-day settlement baseline in this schema reflects the OTA/Central Bank of Oman documentary expectation that export consideration be received within six months.

  • Oman Customs Bayan single-window export declaration (Single Administrative Document) endorsed with exit approval and unique movement reference
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill / Air Waybill / CMR road-freight note evidencing dispatch to the named non-resident consignee
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank credit/telex SWIFT advice evidencing receipt of export consideration through a licensed Omani bank
  • Zero-rated VAT tax invoice, commercial invoice and packing list cross-referenced to the export declaration
  • Sale contract or purchase order, certificate of transfer of ownership/title, and insurance documents showing transfer of risk to the non-resident buyer

Oman does not currently operate a mandatory real-time e-invoicing clearance, fiscal-stamping, or API-based tax-invoice clearance system. However, VAT-registered suppliers must issue tax invoices and credit notes containing all statutory Arabic/English elements in paper or electronic form and retain them in a retrievable format for the five-year VAT record-keeping period. Export declarations are cleared in real time through the Oman Customs single window (Bayan). VAT returns, refund applications and correspondence are filed electronically through the OTA e-Services portal. The OTA may require machine-readable invoice files during audits and is progressively assessing phased e-invoicing data-exchange mandates.

Zero-rating claims are a priority audit area for the OTA Compliance Directorate. Where export documents are incomplete, untimely, inconsistent, forged, or where goods are diverted internally or re-enter Oman/GCC territory (including free-zone transfers), the OTA will: (i) reverse the 0% rate and assess 5% VAT on the supply value from the date of supply, together with statutory late-payment interest and monthly delay penalties; (ii) impose administrative fines, which may escalate to OMR 25,000 or more for false documentation or record-keeping failures, and customs penalties under the GCC Unified Customs Law for misdeclaration; and (iii) refer deliberate evasion or forged-document cases for criminal prosecution with imprisonment and additional fines. The OTA retains a five-year audit and record-retention look-back period, allowing retrospective clawback and penalty assessment on all open tax periods.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully and freely convertible for current international transactions under the Articles of Agreement of the International Monetary Fund (IMF Article VIII). Oman maintains a liberalised exchange control regime wherein both current account and most inward/outward capital account transactions are effected without routine central banking approval. The OMR is pegged to the US dollar at a mid-rate of 0.3845 OMR = 1.00 USD. Legal and business restrictions do not materially impede profit remittances or dividend distributions for a foreign-owned SPC; however, such remittances are subject to corporate tax clearance in Oman, compliance with the US-PEER? Anti-money laundering/counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT) standards, and standard bank documentation verifying the genuineness of the underlying trade or investment. Non-resident shareholders of an Omani SPC may legally repatriate after-tax profits, dividends, and capital upon liquidation, provided the relevant corporate records are current, tax returns have been filed, and the bank's compliance department has approved the transfer under the customer due diligence file. Capital account transactions involving directly regulated sectors (e.g., banks, insurance, upstream hydrocarbons) may require sectoral approvals, but these are not relevant to a manufacturing or trading SPC in ISIC 3240 (toy manufacturing).

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose any statutory obligation requiring exporters to surrender export proceeds to the Central Bank of Oman or to any licensed entity at specified percentages, and there is no statutory timeline within which export receipts must be converted into OMR. Exporters may retain foreign currency receipts in onshore foreign currency accounts (subject to bank account opening documentation) or sell the currency for OMR through licensed banks or exchange houses. In practice, for export proceeds related to a Montessori wooden toy manufacturer, the bank will require supporting export documentation (bill of lading, commercial invoice, sales contract, airway bill, and customs export declaration) to satisfy economic substance, transfer pricing, and AML staging requirements. Repatriation of export earnings is not mandated by exchange control legislation, but commercial debt settlement and Omani tax accounting rules generally require that offshore receivables are eventually collected through the company's local banking channel so that revenue is properly recorded in audited financial statements.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Muscat, Sultanate of Oman, with supervisory jurisdiction over all banking, foreign exchange, money exchange, and payment system activities; allied regulatory authorities include the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (for corporate and foreign investment matters), the Capital Market Authority (for financial market conduct), and the Oman Tax Authority (for transfer and remittance tax clearance matters).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low. The Sultanate of Oman does not provide a statutory or regulatory regime for passporting of foreign Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) into Oman. Offshore digital banks and EMIs (including UK-FCA, EU EBA-authorised or Singapore MAS-authorised entities) may not lawfully solicit deposits, take Omani customer deposits, or provide payment transfer services in Oman unless they obtain a licence from the Central Bank of Oman under the Law on Payment Services (Royal Decree No. 68/2021) and the Banking Law. A foreign SPC's treasury operations may only use such offshore EMI platforms, from an Oman regulatory perspective, for non-Omani inbound collections in foreign currencies where no nexus exists to Omani residents, but any local OMR-denominated operational account must be maintained with a CBO-licensed bank. For the target commercial model (Amazon Marketplace and Stripe payments), the practical and compliant route is to maintain a CBO-licensed beneficiary account for settlements routed via the licensed Omani banking system and onshore corresponding relationships.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The bank will require the following be attached: certified copy of the Commercial Registration (CR) Certificate of the SPC, the Memorandum and Articles of Association, a board resolution or member resolution approving the capital contribution, an overview of the ultimate shareholders, and a declaration confirming the funds are lawful. If the capital increase is under the Oman Law of Commercial Companies in the form of an SPC, the share capital is entered in the Ministry's records; there is no statutory minimum capital requirement for an SPC under Oman law, but the initial deposit must be booked in the accounting records and reflected in a bank certificate. For amounts exceeding the bank's internal threshold (usually > OMR 5,000 / USD 13,000), the bank will mirror the transfer in the compliance report and may request a share premium or capital injection form. No CBO approval is required, but a Tax Authority Form (e.g., Company Transaction Disclosure) may have to be preserved for the annual corporate tax file. For the target commercial model (Amazon Marketplace and Stripe payments), the practical and compliant route is to maintain a CBO-licensed beneficiary account for settlements routed via the licensed Omani banking system and onshore corresponding relationships.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 aggregate annual controlled transactions with non-resident related parties (approximately USD 2.6 million) is the recommended contemporaneous documentation benchmark; a Local File and Master File are strongly advised at or above this threshold in line with OECD BEPS Actions 8-10.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted a dedicated Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code, but the OTA applies an effective management and control doctrine under Article 2 of the Income Tax Law. The Omani SPC must maintain a registered office, hold board meetings in Oman, employ local production and management staff, bear operating expenditure in Oman, and conduct genuine manufacturing from an identified factory location. Any entity claiming a free-zone tax holiday or treaty benefit without real economy presence will be recharacterised as a shell; the tax holiday will be revoked and benefits denied with audit-settlement liabilities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Understated Digital Sales

Statutory sanction: Fixed administrative penalty of OMR 500 plus 25% of the underpaid tax, and increased to 100% of the omitted tax for fraud; criminal sanction of imprisonment for up to five years and fine up to OMR 50,000; plus OTA debt-collection seizure of bank balances and movable assets.

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder and responsible manager are jointly and severally liable for the concealed tax, with the corporate veil pierced; the director may be subject to a travel ban, passport withdrawal, and asset freezing such that limited liability is extinguished.
