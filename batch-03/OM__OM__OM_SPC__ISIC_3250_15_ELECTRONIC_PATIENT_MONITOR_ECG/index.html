Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the capital is fixed in the Articles of Association, must be fully subscribed by the sole shareholder, and must be commensurate with the business purpose. The subscribed capital must be deposited in a licensed Omani bank account in the company's name; a certificate of deposit is required for industrial licensing and Chamber registration. No escrow authority is mandated.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), operating under the Ministry of Finance; electronic portal: Oman Tax Portal (taxes.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

Foreign investor incorporates the Single Person Company (SPC) through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) via the Invest Easy portal. Upon issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate, the SPC must be registered with the Oman Tax Authority for corporate income tax within the prescribed statutory period. For VAT purposes, a separate VAT Tax Identification Number (TIN) must be obtained through the Oman Tax Portal when the entity's annual taxable supplies reach the mandatory registration threshold.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign investor incorporates the Single Person Company (SPC) through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) via the Invest Easy portal. Upon issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate, the SPC must be registered with the Oman Tax Authority for corporate income tax within the prescribed statutory period. For VAT purposes, a separate VAT Tax Identification Number (TIN) must be obtained through the Oman Tax Portal when the entity's annual taxable supplies reach the mandatory registration threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available under Omani tax law. A Singapore-style Single Person Company is mandatorily taxable as a corporate entity. The only statutory elections are limited to voluntary VAT registration below the mandatory threshold and accounting-year selections subject to OTA approval.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to physical export of goods delivered outside the Sultanate and to qualifying offshore/international services supplied to non-resident customers. The SPC claims the 0% rate only if it can demonstrate through customs declarations, transport documents and banking records that the goods left Oman and were delivered to a non-GCC destination.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Oman mandates VAT-compliant sequentially numbered tax invoices for all taxable supplies. The Tax Authority is implementing a national e-invoicing framework with API-based digital clearance for B2B and export invoices; taxpayer ERP systems will require integration with the OTA platform to obtain transaction IDs for real-time validation and fiscal stamping.

The Oman Tax Authority may reopen VAT returns within the standard audit limitation period and use customs data matching to verify physical exports. False zero-rating claims are retrospectively assessed at the 5% VAT rate, together with late-payment penalties, administrative fines, summary seizure of bank accounts, and referral to the Economic Prosecution for criminal sanctions in cases of fraudulent documentation.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the U.S. Dollar at 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR. Oman maintains a liberal exchange control regime: foreign exchange transactions are permitted through CBO-licensed authorised banks, and current account payments for trade and services are freely transferable. Foreign direct investment and capital account transactions are fully permitted for foreign-owned Single Person Companies, subject to Commercial Companies Law registration and CBO balance-of-payments reporting. Profit remittance is permitted after the company is in good standing with the Oman Tax Authority, has submitted audited financial statements where required, and has no outstanding tax liabilities; the remitting bank will require a board resolution, tax clearance evidence, and correct balance-of-payments purpose coding.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender regime exists in Oman. Exporters of electronic patient monitors may receive foreign currency proceeds directly into an OMR or foreign currency account with an authorised Oman bank. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to OMR and no fixed statutory repatriation window. Export proceeds must nevertheless be reported to the CBO through the bank’s international transactions reporting system, and the exporter must retain underlying commercial documents for audit and AML verification purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Ruwi, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary clearing rail. Offshore electronic money institutions hold no CBO licence and cannot open OMR settlement accounts, access Oman’s local clearing house, or provide account-to-account OMR transfers. Such EMIs may be used only as a supplementary channel for foreign-currency receivables from overseas distributors, provided the final treasury consolidation occurs through a CBO-licensed bank. Legal constraints arise under the Banking Law and National Payment Systems Regulations, which require payment service providers to be licensed or passported through CBO sponsorship.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be remitted by SWIFT MT103 using the foreign shareholder’s bank in the home jurisdiction. The remitting bank must apply the CBO balance-of-payments purpose code for direct investment equity capital, typically classified as 'Direct Investment – Equity / Capital Injection'. The receiving Oman bank will credit the SPC’s OMR operational account at the pegged rate of 1 USD = 0.3845 OMR. The sending instruction must show the SPC Commercial Registration number, the beneficiary account IBAN, the UBO name, and a clear narrative 'Capital Injection – SPC Equity Contribution'. The receiving bank is required to complete CBO cross-border transaction reporting and may request the executed board resolution, capital contribution minutes, and source of funds evidence before accepting the credit.Trade settlements for imported components and raw materials will be executed via SWIFT MT103 direct telegraphic transfer or through commercial letters of credit using MT700/MT710 issuance. For confirmed suppliers in Asia or Europe, an irrevocable LC at sight is preferred; for smaller pre-shipment payments, an MT103 with invoice correlation and customs clearance evidence is acceptable. All outbound disbursements must be supported by a pro-forma invoice, purchase order, bill of lading, inspection certificate, and CBO purpose code for trade payments. Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% - Oman does not impose a statutory withholding tax on dividend distributions made to non-resident shareholders of an Omani SPC.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 - aggregate controlled transactions in the tax year trigger the mandatory Local File; a Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 25,000,000; Country-by-Country Reporting applies to groups with consolidated revenue of at least OMR 400,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not maintain a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime analogous to the UAE; substance is instead enforced through the place-of-effective-management (POEM) residency test, the general anti-avoidance rule that disregards arrangements whose main purpose is tax avoidance, branch and permanent establishment attribution, and the requirement for a genuine Omani establishment to access tax treaty benefits. A foreign-owned SPC with no local employees, no physical calibration premises and foreign-controlled boards is exposed to being re-characterised as a tax resident of the parent jurisdiction with profits taxed abroad, or as a non-resident carrying on business in Oman without proper attribution.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Suppression of Revenue

Statutory sanction: Fraud penalty of an additional 50% of the evaded tax (25% for negligent understatement); a 2% monthly late-payment surcharge; administrative fines of up to OMR 1,000 plus OMR 100 per month; criminal referral with imprisonment of up to 5 years and a fine of up to OMR 5,000 under Royal Decree 9/2017; asset seizure and blacklisting from Omani trade and procurement.

Director personal liability: The foreign owner and the registered manager are jointly and severally liable civilly for the unpaid tax, surcharges and penalty; the SGT may obtain a court order restricting departure from Oman (travel ban), impose a personal execution against the director's bank accounts, and a criminal conviction disqualifies the director from managing an Omani SPC.
Shell Company / Sham Commercial Substance

Statutory sanction: Re-characterisation of the SPC as tax-resident in the parent jurisdiction under POEM rules; retroactive reassessment of undeclared non-resident trading profits, disallowance of treaty benefits, penalty of 25-50% of additional tax, cancellation of the tax card and commercial registration for carrying on a fictitious activity.

Director personal liability: Piercing of the corporate veil: the sole shareholder is civilly liable for the SPC's tax debts and trade liabilities; the director may face a travel ban pending the tax liability's settlement, asset tracing into foreign accounts, and criminal exposure for false declarations in the entity residence statement.
Transfer Pricing Evasion and TP-Documentation Default

Statutory sanction: Arm's-length re-adjustment with penal uplift of 25% (negligence) or 50% (intentional) of the additional tax; mandatory late-payment interest; criminal penalties for intentionally false TP declarations; the TP documentation deficiency is separately fined and bars issuance of a tax-clearance certificate for dividend repatriation.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign the CIT Return and TP schedule are personally liable for the penalty portions in cases of deliberate falsification; personal fines, travel bans, and disqualification from holding office in Oman; the foreign parent can be named in a joint SGT assessment where the controlled transaction lacks commercial rationality.
AML and Repatriation-Rails Violation

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Oman's AML/CFT law: fines of up to OMR 50,000-100,000 per violation for legal persons, confiscation of the underlying funds and assets, criminal imprisonment of up to 10 years for wilful circumvention of AML obligations, and suspension or revocation of the SPC's trade licence and tax card.

Director personal liability: The compliance officer, resident manager and beneficial owner face individual fines and imprisonment; departure bans are imposed during investigation; any conviction triggers automatic removal from existing Omani entity directorships, blocking of bank facilities, and inability to obtain future commercial registrations in the Sultanate.
