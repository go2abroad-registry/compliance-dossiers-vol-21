Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), an independent body annexed to the Ministry of Finance; official e-services portal: tax.gov.om.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC): (1) incorporate under the Commercial Companies Law through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain Commercial Registration; (2) register with the Oman Tax Authority through the tax.gov.om portal to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a VAT Registration Number; (3) mandatory VAT registration is required when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500, with voluntary registration available at OMR 19,500 or OMR 19,250 depending on the applicable threshold; (4) submit the Commercial Registration certificate, lease or ownership documents, shareholder identification, constitutional documents, and bank account details via the OTA e-services account; (5) upon approval, the OTA issues a Tax Registration Certificate electronically. A foreign enterprise without a local branch or permanent establishment must register only for Oman-source income and withholding tax compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — For a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC): (1) incorporate under the Commercial Companies Law through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain Commercial Registration; (2) register with the Oman Tax Authority through the tax.gov.om portal to obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and, where applicable, a VAT Registration Number; (3) mandatory VAT registration is required when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500, with voluntary registration available at OMR 19,500 or OMR 19,250 depending on the applicable threshold; (4) submit the Commercial Registration certificate, lease or ownership documents, shareholder identification, constitutional documents, and bank account details via the OTA e-services account; (5) upon approval, the OTA issues a Tax Registration Certificate electronically. A foreign enterprise without a local branch or permanent establishment must register only for Oman-source income and withholding tax compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election is available; an Oman SPC is mandatorily a taxable corporate entity for both income tax and VAT. Available statutory elections include: voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 19,250 threshold; VAT group registration for financially associated resident entities; election to carry forward tax losses for up to five years; and free-zone tax incentive elections for licensed free-zone entities, including tax holidays under Sohar, Salalah, Duqm, and other designated economic zones.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (mandatory registration threshold on annual taxable supplies); OMR 19,250 (voluntary registration threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods are eligible for 0% VAT when physically exported from Oman in accordance with the Customs Law, evidenced by a customs export declaration and removal from the territory of the GCC implementation states. Services may be zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident recipient and effectively used or consumed outside Oman, including cross-border commercial litigation, arbitration, and legal advisory services rendered to foreign clients, provided the supplier retains documentary evidence of the recipient's non-resident status, the offshore nature of the service, and actual foreign economic use or benefit. Transport of goods or passengers to a destination outside Oman and related ancillary services are also zero-rated under the VAT Law.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) / Single Administrative Document evidencing physical departure of goods from Oman
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (or CMR international consignment note for land transport) as proof of export movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Credit Advice from a licensed Omani or GCC bank evidencing receipt of the export sale proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Port loading certificate, warehouse gate pass, or certificate of shipment issued by the carrier or port authority
  • Contract or engagement letter identifying the non-resident recipient and confirming the offshore supply of services

As of the current Omani framework, there is no operational real-time fiscal clearance mandate or mandatory API-based digital stamping requirement. VAT-registered persons must issue compliant tax invoices in electronic or paper form, retain electronic records for ten years, and file VAT returns through tax.gov.om. The OTA has announced a phased e-invoicing strategy that contemplates the future introduction of structured electronic invoices with portal/API integration and potential fiscal clearance obligations for designated taxpayer clusters, subject to OTA decisions and implementation regulations.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to post-clearance audit by the OTA, the Customs Authority, and the Central Bank of Oman. Failure to substantiate the physical export or the non-resident service supply will result in retrospective reassessment of output VAT at 5%, addition of late-payment penalties at the rate of 1% per month or part thereof on the unpaid tax, and administrative fines of up to OMR 25,000 per contravention. Fraudulent or deliberate misuse of export zero-rating to obtain refunds constitutes tax evasion and may attract criminal prosecution, imprisonment of up to five years, fines of up to OMR 100,000, recovery of improperly refunded amounts, and disqualification from future zero-rating treatment or VAT registration reinstatement.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations and maintains full convertibility on current-account payments and transfers; no CBO approval or exchange license is required for legal-fee payments, arbitral institution deposits, expert disbursements or settlement remittances. The Omani Rial is pegged to the US dollar at approximately 2.6008 USD per OMR within a narrow CBO-managed intervention band, which materially eliminates currency conversion risk for USD-denominated litigation and asset-recovery portfolios. Capital-account inflows, including foreign equity injections into an SPC, are freely permitted; outbound profit and capital repatriation are unrestricted once the 10% withholding tax on non-resident dividends, interest and certain service fees under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended) is discharged, with double-tax-treaty relief available for jurisdictions having a tax agreement with Oman. Liquidation proceeds are released after the OTA issues a tax-clearance certificate and the bank validates the external auditor's final report.

Export proceeds surrender rules

For export of services, the material category for an ISIC 6910 litigation chamber, the CBO expects all service-export proceeds (legal fees, arbitration awards and settlement recoveries) to be repatriated through a licensed Omani bank within 180 days of the invoice date. There is no mandatory conversion ratio to OMR under the current liberalized regime: the SPC may retain USD or other foreign-currency receipts in a designated foreign-currency account or convert at the prevailing market rate, provided that the supporting file contains the engagement letter, the fee invoice, the award/settlement agreement and the SWIFT MT103 inward credit advice. Historical CBO directives requiring partial surrender of merchandise export receipts in OMR have been effectively superseded for most goods; the contemporaneous requirement is full repatriation and presentation of the customs export declaration for the CBO balance-of-payments and the bank's statutory reporting. Non-repatriation within the statutory timeline is classified as an exchange-control violation and will trigger a CBO inquiry and bank-account compliance review.

Central bank supervision: The Central Bank of Oman (CBO), constituted under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000, as amended), is the sole Omani monetary authority, prudential bank supervisor and foreign-exchange control regulator. It licenses commercial banks, administers the OMR/USD exchange-rate peg and intervention band, issues AML/CFT directives aligned to FATF standards, and collects balance-of-payments statistics on all cross-border transactions routed through licensed banks. Ancillary authorities are the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) for corporate licensing and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) for withholding and remittance taxation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low for core treasury. The CBO does not authorize passporting of foreign digital EMI/e-money institutions into Oman, and an OM-SPC cannot lawfully hold client settlement funds or IOLTA-class trust balances in offshore EMI accounts, as this breaches the licensed-bank intermediation regime and professional-conduct rules on safeguarding client money. Foreign EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer or Airwallex may be used only for ancillary, low-materiality disbursements (e.g., travel and small witness expenses), with balances periodically swept into the primary Omani bank account. Oman-licensed payment-service operators may support payroll and utility payments but must never serve as the client-trust or settlement rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity injections are transmitted by the overseas shareholder via SWIFT MT103 in USD (or local-currency clearing) to the SPC's OMR/USD account at the licensed Omani bank, using the balance-of-payments purpose code for equity capital / foreign direct investment and citing the MCIIP file number and SPC commercial-registration number in the reference field. The bank's compliance team retains the MCIIP-approved SPC constitution, the capital-deposit certificate and the updated Commercial Registration before crediting; inward remittances exceeding OMR 10,000 are reported by the bank to the CBO for balance-of-payments statistics. Where the SPC already operates, capital increases require the notarized SPC resolution and an updated Commercial Registration filing before the wire is accepted. No exchange-control license is required for the injection given Oman's full capital-account convertibility, but the amount must be recorded in the shares ledger and in the OTA filing for the company's register of shareholders.The SPC's settlement engine is the licensed Omani bank's treasury and correspondent network. Local payments to Omani counsel, court/tribunal fees and domestic disbursements settle through the CBO's real-time gross settlement system (Oman Payment System) or corporate internet banking credit transfers. Cross-border payments to foreign counsel, international arbitral institutions (ICC, LCIA, DIAC, SIAC), expert witnesses and asset-tracing agents are executed by SWIFT MT103 with charge code 'OUR' and an MT202 COVER instruction where bank-to-bank reimbursement is required; each instruction must contain the case-docket reference to satisfy CBO record-retention and the bank's transaction-monitoring obligations. Commercial letters of credit are inapplicable to an intangible legal-services model and must not be used as a distribution rail for client settlement funds because the IOLTA-class trust rail is wire-only. Large settlement awards payable to non-resident clients require a written client distribution instruction carrying the required FATF wire-transmission information, a trust-ledger debit entry and a supporting award conformity certificate for audit trail completeness.Deliverable FX Forwards (OMR/USD, OMR/EUR) with tenors up to 12 months, permitted solely on a non-speculative basis against documented fee invoices, awards or expected settlement inflows.Cross-Currency Swaps (OMR/USD, OMR/EUR) for tenors exceeding 12 months where the SPC holds a long-term, contractually committed settlement stream from commercial litigation or arbitration retainer agreements.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDF) in restricted-convertibility currencies (e.g., INR, PKR, EGP) available through licensed Omani bank treasury desks for 1–6 month tenors, subject to production of the underlying receivable documentation.Spot FX executed via the bank's electronic dealing platform for day-to-day operating conversions; speculative options, leveraged derivatives and crypto-denominated hedging products are prohibited under the CBO's non-speculative hedging policy.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat rate on taxable income under Royal Decree No. 9/2023 (Income Tax Law), effective for tax periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends and other profit distributions paid or credited by an Omani resident company to a non-resident shareholder (introduced by the Income Tax Law RD 9/2023; nil for distributions to Omani resident individuals and exempt only where the recipient is an Omani joint-stock company under participation rules).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Not later than 4 months after the end of the SPC's fiscal year (accounting period). A written extension of up to 2 additional months is possible if applied for before the statutory deadline; the tax payment (if any) is due on the basis of the assessed amount and late-payment surcharges accrue from the day following the due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Disclosure obligation: any single controlled transaction exceeding OMR 30,000 or annual aggregate controlled transactions exceeding OMR 150,000. Mandatory Local File and contemporaneous TP documentation: aggregate controlled transactions above OMR 150,000 per tax year. Master File and Country-by-Country Report: applicable only to MNE groups with consolidated revenue above OMR 300 million, effectively inapplicable to a standalone SPC litigation chamber unless part of a large international group.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not enact the UAE-style 'Economic Substance Regulations' label; however, the Income Tax Law 2023 imposes a de facto substance doctrine: companies must maintain real premises, qualified staff, bank accounts and decision-making presence in Oman. The Tax Authority can disregard sham arrangements, disallow deductions of shell conduits, deny treaty and participation-exemption benefits, recharacterize intercompany contracts, and impose up to a 100% fraud surcharge for artificial structures in the legal-services sector.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Ex officio assessment of the evaded tax plus a surcharge of 100% of the evaded amount and a criminal fine of up to OMR 100,000; imprisonment up to 3 years upon conviction; publication of the conviction; cancellation of the tax clearance certificate and the commercial license.

Director personal liability: The general manager and sole shareholder are jointly and severally liable for evaded tax where the SPC is treated as an alter ego; the Tax Authority stands as a preferred creditor over personal assets, may impose travel bans and asset freezes, and may obtain criminal custodial sentences against the directing mind.
Shell Company / Sham Structure and GAAR

Statutory sanction: GAAR recharacterization of the whole transaction, denial of treaty relief and intercompany deductions, taxation of deemed income at 15%, plus a 50% discretionary penalty for falsification and, where intentional, criminal referral.

Director personal liability: Directors who orchestrated the artificial arrangement are personally assessed to the resulting tax arrears, disqualified from board positions for up to 5 years, banned from holding Omani commercial licenses, and subject to travel restrictions; criminal fraud charges follow where documents were forged.
AML / Client Trust (IOLTA) Misconduct

Statutory sanction: Under the AML Law (RD 30/2016, as amended): fines up to OMR 100,000 against the SPC, confiscation of funds, de-registration of the ISIC 6910 license, and prohibition from managing client accounts; the regulatory authority may additionally order the winding-up of the SPC.

Director personal liability: AML compliance officer, managing director and the sole shareholder face personal criminal prosecution: imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines from OMR 5,000 to OMR 50,000; personal travel bans, asset seizures and disqualification from the legal profession are automatic upon indictment in money-laundering cases.
