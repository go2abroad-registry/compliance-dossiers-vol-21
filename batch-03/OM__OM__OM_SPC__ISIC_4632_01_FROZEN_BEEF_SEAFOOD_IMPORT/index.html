Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (Tax Authority, Sultanate of Oman) via the electronic tax portal https://taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Using that registration, the SPC's legal representative completes the online TIN/VAT registration through the Oman Tax Authority portal by submitting the Commercial Registration certificate, constitutive documents, authorised signatory identification, and bank account details. VAT registration is mandatory once annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500; voluntary registration is available when taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,250. VAT ID and TIN are issued electronically after validation of the supporting documents.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Oman applies entity-level income taxation at 15% for SPCs; VAT also operates on an entity-level registration and filing basis) — A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Using that registration, the SPC's legal representative completes the online TIN/VAT registration through the Oman Tax Authority portal by submitting the Commercial Registration certificate, constitutive documents, authorised signatory identification, and bank account details. VAT registration is mandatory once annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500; voluntary registration is available when taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,250. VAT ID and TIN are issued electronically after validation of the supporting documents.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Oman applies entity-level income taxation at 15% for SPCs; VAT also operates on an entity-level registration and filing basis)

Available election forms

Oman does not have check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch entity election mechanics. Under VAT law, related businesses may apply for permission to be treated as a single taxable person (VAT group); a taxable person may elect monthly filing instead of quarterly filing; and a person whose taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,250 but not OMR 38,500 may elect voluntary registration. No otherwise-taxable entity may elect to be disregarded for tax purposes under Omani law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration at OMR 38,500 (annual taxable supplies including taxable imports); voluntary registration at OMR 19,250

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies when goods are physically exported from Oman to a non-implementing GCC state or to a non-resident recipient located outside Oman, provided the goods leave Oman within 90 days of the date of supply. Offshore services qualify for zero rating when rendered to a non-resident, performed outside Oman, and not effectively used or exploited in Oman. Auxiliary evidence includes transport contracts, cargo movement records, payment receipts, and confirmation of overseas physical receipt.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Bill of Entry
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / CMR
  • Supplier and transport invoices evidencing physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT TT advising export proceeds
  • Contract of sale with the non-resident buyer
  • Letter of Credit or SWIFT TT payment documents
  • Certificate of Origin (where applicable)
  • Oman Tax Authority export schedules in the format required by the VAT return

Oman does not currently mandate real-time fiscal clearance via custom API integration or digital stamping in the standard wholesale cold-chain import trade. VAT-compliant tax invoices and simplified tax invoices must be issued as per the VAT Executive Regulations, submitted through the tax authority's electronic portal, and retained for audit. The Tax Authority may require invoice-level data, export documentation, and customs references during audit; a phased e-invoicing mandate is being monitored but is not yet a live clearance obligation.

Export zero-rating is scrutinised by comparing physical export documentation with VAT filings and FX repatriation records. If the exporter fails to substantiate timely physical export or proceeds repatriation within the permitted 180-day window, the Tax Authority may claw back the zero-rated treatment, assess 5% VAT on the full supply amount, charge statutory late-payment interest at the relevant Omani rate, and impose administrative fines under the Omani VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020). Evasion findings may also trigger customs fraud penalties and referral to public prosecution.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions and is de facto pegged to the US Dollar at a CBO base rate of USD 1 = OMR 0.3845. A foreign-owned SPC may freely remit dividends or repatriate capital after discharging all Omani Corporate Income Tax obligations and any applicable withholding duties, subject to submission to the remitting bank of audited financial statements, tax clearance, Commercial Registration certificate, and a board-approved distribution resolution. No prior CBO exchange licence is required, but the transaction is automatically recorded in CBO's electronic payment monitoring system.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds. Accordingly, for the frozen beef and seafood import scope, the SPC is not required to convert received export-related USD or other foreign currency balances into OMR. However, the bank will require fully substantiated trade documentation and appropriate Harmonized System/purpose-coded declarations for all cross-border amounts, typically for wires above OMR 10,000, in order to meet CBO AML/CFT and Anti-Tax-Evasion circular obligations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) acting together with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) under the Oman Forex/AML framework (Royal Decree 63/2010), the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), and CBO circulars on electronic transfer reporting.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. The Omani legal system does not recognise offshore digital e-money institutions (EMIs) as licensed entities for receiving client funds or executing cross-border trade settlements. CBO-regulated commercial banks and CBO-approved payment service providers are the only valid channels for trade-related clearing. Offshore EMIs may operate only as auxiliary referral intermediaries, never as primary treasury rails, because any systematic routing of payment through an unlicensed digital EMI would violate the Omani Banking Law and AML/CFT framework. A carefully structured legal opinion may allow limited use of an EMI as a disbursement instruction intermediary, but settlement must remain inside a CBO-licensed bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign equity injection for the Oman SPC is transmitted through the international banking system as a SWIFT MT103 credit directly to the SPC's named OMR/USD account at a licensed Omani bank. No prior approval of the Central Bank of Oman is necessary, but the remitter's message must contain the reference 'OM_SPC_Frozen_Seafood_Import – FDI Equity Injection' and the bank will open a temporary capital-blocked account or credit hold to allow verification. The bank must receive the MoCIIP foreign investment notification, the SPC's Articles of Association, commercial registration, capital subscription agreement, and the investor board resolution as the underlying legal basis before releasing the funds to operations. If the transfer exceeds OMR 50,000, the bank must report the credit to CBO's Electronic Financial Reporting System on an automatic line basis; a clear foreign-currency conversion instruction should accompany the wire.Outbound import settlements use two primary clearing rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for advance payments, open-account trade and small-amount freight/insurance top-ups; and (ii) irrevocable, confirmed commercial Letters of Credit issued under UCP600 through Bank Muscat, HSBC Oman, or National Bank of Oman, with SWIFT MT700 issuance and MT707 amendments for large frozen beef/seafood lunar parcels. Documentary collections (D/P and D/A) are acceptable for standard trading documents, provided the SPC holds the import permit, the underlying sales contract, the supplier's commercial invoice, and an Chad- or Oman-approved marine insurance certificate. All outward payments must include the Oman Customs import-licence number and reference the relevant Harmonized System code in the SWIFT field 70/purpose code.
