Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an Oman Single Person Company under RD 18/2019; the capital must be fully subscribed by the sole shareholder, supported by a capital deposit certificate from a licensed commercial bank in Oman, and must be adequate for the approved ISIC 7110 virtual-performance activity. No escrow account is mandated; capital may fund pre-operating expenses after Commercial Register issuance.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) fire-safety clearance is required for the registered office and any occupied premises; a simplified inspection applies (fire extinguishers, illuminated exit signage, emergency lighting, and alarm). The Ministry of Labour General Directorate of Occupational Health applies OSH Regulations (Ministerial Decision 286/2020), requiring a risk assessment and first-aid provisions when more than 25 employees are engaged. The Baladiyah municipal license will not be issued without the CDAA clearance letter.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an Oman Single Person Company under RD 18/2019; the capital must be fully subscribed by the sole shareholder, supported by a capital deposit certificate from a licensed commercial bank in Oman, and must be adequate for the approved ISIC 7110 virtual-performance activity. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (الهيئة العامة للضرائب) — official e-tax portal: taxomania.gov.om

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) first obtains a Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP). Within 30 days of commencing commercial activity, the SPC must apply online via the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) portal for a Tax Identification Number (TIN). VAT registration is processed in the same OTA e-services platform; it is mandatory when annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000, and voluntary when annual taxable supplies or deductible expenses exceed OMR 250,000. The application requires the CR certificate, articles of association, board/shareholder resolution, registered address documentation, and (where applicable) audited financial statements. Non-resident SPCs without permanent establishment in Oman appoint a tax representative for VAT and income tax compliance if required.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box or fiduciary classification election. An SPC is mandatorily classified as a separate corporate tax person under the Income Tax Law. No hybrid mismatch elections are statutorily available; Oman applies domestic anti-avoidance rules aligned with BEPS where transactions would otherwise exploit differentials.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 (mandatory annual taxable supply threshold); OMR 250,000 (voluntary registration threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to: (1) goods physically exported outside Oman within 90 days of supply; (2) services directly and exclusively related to the export of goods, including handling, transport, insurance, and customs brokerage; (3) international transport of passengers or goods; (4) services supplied to a non-resident where the benefit is effectively consumed outside Oman and the place of supply is treated as outside the GCC valuation territory. For the virtual concert metaverse scope, digital ticket sales to non-Omani recipients in a metaverse platform may qualify for zero-rating where the virtual event is exploited outside Oman, the recipient is a non-resident, and the SPC retains conclusive proof of offshore supply and payment.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Al-Bayane single-window filing)
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Waybill (carrier-certified)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Central Bank-approved Omani bank
  • Signed underlying supply contract and invoice specifying the offshore recipient, place of supply, and payment in convertible currency

No national real-time fiscal clearance or compulsory digital stamping is currently in force. The OTA accepts electronically issued valid VAT invoices preserving sole chain of custody and authenticity; a phased mandatory e-invoicing roadmap has been announced, to become obligatory progressively from 2025, with API clearance for large taxpayers. Until then, ordinary XML/PIN-signed invoicing satisfies VAT compliance.

The OTA rigorously tests zero-rating claims through document-to-declaration reconciliations. If the mandatory export bundle is missing, incomplete, or materially false, the VAT treated as zero-rated is clawed back and assessed at 5% from the invoice date, together with late-payment penalties up to 12% per annum, administrative fines of up to OMR 50,000, and, for deliberate fraud with treaty or cross-border evidence, criminal prosecution (potential imprisonment). The statutory audit look-back is five years from the VAT return date, extended to ten years in cases of confirmed evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the US dollar at 0.3845 OMR/USD. Oman accepts IMF Article VIII obligations, giving full current-account convertibility for payments of goods, services, and ordinary profit remittances. The capital account is broadly liberalised for foreign-owned LLCs/SPCs, including direct equity injection, and does not require prior CBO exchange-control approval. Profit distribution to a non-resident shareholder is permitted after completion of statutory corporate tax filing and banking due diligence; practical barriers are entirely KYC/AML and source-of-funds based, not exchange-control based.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio for export or virtual-service receipts. Export and ticket-sale proceeds may be retained in a foreign-currency account or converted into Omani Rial at commercial-bank rates. CBO requires banks to record such proceeds, conduct trade-based money-laundering monitoring, and report suspicious or anomalous flows; no foreign-currency earnings are compulsorily remitted to the CBO.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting under the Banking Law and CBO Foreign Exchange and AML/CFT Circulars; the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) registers the SPC and handles the national foreign-investment record.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Ramp Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for direct onshore EMI licensing and Medium for an offshore EMI used strictly as a payment-routing layer through a licensed Omani bank. Oman has no statutory electronic-money-institution (EMI) licensing category. Non-bank payment services require a central-bank-licensed PSP or a commercial-bank sponsorship; an offshore digital EMI cannot obtain a CBO licence or direct access to Oman Net. It may provide foreign-currency collection, virtual-account sub-ledgers, and USDC off-ramp services outside Oman, but must settle into the SPC's Omani bank account via SWIFT MT103 fiat transfer rather than as a direct clearing participant.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder sends an inbound MT103 SWIFT transfer in USD or EUR to the SPC's designated Omani bank account. The bank message must show the legal remitter and beneficiary; the remitter must be the investor, unless the agreement permits third-party payment. The instruction narrative and beneficiary reference should state 'share capital injection into Oman SPC' or 'inward direct investment – equity capital.' The receiving bank will require the SPC's MOCIIP registration, CBO KYC pack, share-subscription resolution, source-of-funds evidence, and confirmation that the capitalization does not breach Omani law. No CBO foreign-investment licence is needed for this service-scope equity contribution, but the SPC must comply with the foreign-investor registration process. For monitoring, the bank may require future injections to be tied to audited capital accounts.Outbound ticket-settlement and production-cost payments are cleared primarily through SWIFT MT103 for non-Oman beneficiaries, using the SPC's correspondent-banking network. Domestic expenses in Omani Rial are cleared through Oman Net, CBO's RTGS and electronic-clearing platform, with same-day AEDS/ACH settlement. For physical merchandise or event equipment, commercial documentary credits under SWIFT MT700 and ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP600) are usable; virtual-event service fees should use a simple SWIFT transfer with BOP service-code narrative. If the SPC collects through Stripe, funds are settled from Stripe's offshore banking entity to the SPC's Omani account as a fiat remittance, not as cryptocurrency. Any outbound USDC payment must be executed by an offshore licensed VASP after receiving Omani fiat funds; the Omani bank account itself will not transact in virtual assets.Plain-vanilla OMR/USD FX forwards with genuine commercial or financial-underlying exposureFX swaps (exchange of principal and interest or single-currency swap bookings) under a CBO-licensed bank's derivative powerCross-currency interest-rate swaps with OMR/USD legs for matched balance-sheet hedgingEuropean-style FX call/put options used solely for cash-flow and ticket-revenue hedgingNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) only where the bank or group treasury can evidence a genuine Omani Rial or offshore OMR exposure and the counterparty holds a permitted derivatives licence; NDFs are not market-standard in Oman because the OMR/USD parity is tightly pegged and spot liquidity is available from licensed banks
