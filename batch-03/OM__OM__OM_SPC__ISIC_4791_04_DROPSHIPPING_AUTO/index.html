Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) — Commercial Registry Department, operating through the Invest Easy one-stop-shop electronic portal (investeasy.moci.gov.om).

Governing statute

Companies Law of the Sultanate of Oman, promulgated by Royal Decree No. 18/2019, as amended (Single Person Company provisions, Arts. 229-240); Commercial Register Law, Royal Decree No. 3/1974, as amended; Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2019; Implementing Regulations issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

Formation / constitutional filings

Notarized Memorandum and Articles of Association (M&A) executed by the sole foreign shareholder in the Arabic statutory form, filed electronically together with the Commercial Registration Application Form (CR Form) on the Invest Easy portal.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 22 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the sole shareholder declares the issued share capital freely in the M&A. The subscribed capital must be deposited into the company's Oman-licensed bank account before Commercial Registration and evidenced by a bank certificate; no escrow release condition is imposed by the Registry.

Funding & unlock path: Royal Oman Police — Directorate General of Civil Defence and Ambulance clearance is not required for a pure online retailer with no public premises. If an administrative office or fulfilment warehouse is occupied, the company must obtain: (a) fire-safety system certification per Oman Fire Code OS 227:2018 (approved detection, alarm, and extinguishing equipment); (b) annual Civil Defence clearance certificate for the occupied premises; (c) municipal structural-load inspection certificate for any racked storage area (per the applicable structural safety code); and (d) Ministry of Labour workplace clearance under the Occupational Safety and Health Law (Royal Decree No. 46/2020) before engaging employees.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019; the sole shareholder declares the issued share capital freely in the M&A. The subscribed capital must be deposited into the company's Oman-licensed bank account before Commercial Registration and evidenced by a bank certificate; no escrow release condition is imposed by the Registry.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (Oman Tax Authority, OTA) — e-portal: taxoman.gov.om.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must obtain a VAT registration number through the OTA e-Services portal. The standard procedure is: (1) assess whether taxable supplies or reverse-charge supplies in Oman exceed OMR 500,000 in any consecutive 12-month period, making registration mandatory; (2) if the SPC has no Oman establishment, appoint a tax representative resident in Oman as required by OTA practice; (3) submit the non-resident VAT registration application electronically, attaching the constitutional documents of the foreign SPC, a copy of the foreign tax identification number, audited or projected financials, proof of commercial activity in Oman, and an executed power of attorney for the tax representative; (4) receive the OMR VAT identification number and electronic electronic registration certificate; and (5) comply with monthly/quarterly VAT filing obligations from the effective date of registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — an Omani SPC is a separate juridical person subject to entity-level taxation under Oman's Income Tax Law; no pass-through check-the-box election is available and profits are not treated as transparent to the sole foreign shareholder for Oman tax purposes.

Available election forms

Oman does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box election or hybrid-mismatch entity election. Available statutory elections are limited to VAT-side choices: voluntary VAT registration when turnover exceeds OMR 250,000, zero-rating substantiation for exports, appointment of a tax representative for non-residents, and elections regarding taxable-status grouping or business-restructuring relief where OTA approves the treatment in writing. There is no election to treat an SPC as a pass-through entity for Oman income tax.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration applies to taxable supplies and imports/ancillary supplies exceeding OMR 500,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration is available for businesses with annual taxable supplies exceeding OMR 250,000. Non-resident sellers using digital or automated dropshipping channels may be required to register from the first OMR of local taxable supply if the OTA treats an Omani marketplace or logistics hub as their taxable establishment.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Oman zero-rates the supply of goods physically exported by the SPC outside the GCC, provided the goods leave Oman within the statutory movement window, title passes while at export, and the SPC holds contemporaneous evidence of dispatch and destination. Dropshipping arrangements qualify where the goods are purchased by the foreign customer, shipped directly by the supplier or a fulfillment agent, and never enter an Omani taxable onward supply chain. International transport, transport-related services, supplies to approved free zones and other qualifying services may also be zero-rated. Zero-rating requires that the exporter obtain and retain the customs export declaration, carrier transport documents, final delivery proof, tax invoice, and payment evidence showing receipt from the foreign principal, generally within 90 days from the supply date.

  • Customs export declaration accepted by Oman Customs (Bayaan/Nafeza foreign export entry)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export outside the GCC
  • Final destination customs entry or courier proof of delivery to the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice and VAT tax invoice issued by the SPC
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of the export sales amount in foreign currency

Oman does not currently impose a broad real-time continuous transaction control clearance system with mandatory digital stamping for all B2B/B2C invoices. However, all VAT-registered persons must issue compliant VAT invoices and file VAT returns electronically through the OTA e-Services portal. The OTA has been developing an e-invoicing framework and may require API-based JSON/XML invoice clearance or fiscal registration for automated dropshipping platforms in future phases. Taxpayers are advised to retain structured electronic invoice mantra/data readable by OTA and to enable API readiness for the Sultanate's forthcoming e-invoicing and invoice validation requirements.

The OTA examines export zero-rating claims by cross-checking customs records, bank inflows, and courier manifests. If the required export documentation bundle cannot be substantiated, or the FX consideration is not received within 180 days, the OTA may reassess the supply at the standard 5% VAT rate, deny refunds, issue retrospective clawback assessments, and apply administrative fines. Fraudulent export zero-rating may attract penalties of up to double the evaded tax and imprisonment of responsible officers under Oman's anti-evasion provisions. The tax assessment limitation period is generally five years but is extended to fifteen years in cases involving fraud or deliberate misstatement, and a formal objection or appeal must be filed within OTA-prescribed deadlines before recovery proceedings.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible for both current-account and eligible capital-account transactions under the CBO's exchange-control framework, with OMR pegged to the USD at approximately 0.3845 OMR/USD. No general limitations exist on repatriation of profits, dividends, service fees, or liquidation proceeds for foreign-owned SPCs, provided the underlying transactions are documented and routed through a licensed bank in Oman. The SPC's capital account may receive foreign direct investment and shareholder loans without prior CBO approval, except for specific cross-border borrowing thresholds or transactions that may trigger notification requirements. Profit remittance requires audited or internally certified financial statements, a board/shareholders' resolution, and tax clearance or withholding compliance where applicable.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Export proceeds and receivables arising from the SPC's cross-border dropshipping sales must be repatriated to a licensed bank account in Oman within 180 days from the date of shipment or the due date of the underlying trade receivable, unless the CBO approves an extended tenor. There is no mandatory percentage-based surrender requirement on export receipts; proceeds may be credited in OMR or a permitted foreign currency and retained freely without a forced conversion to OMR. However, all export settlement must pass through an authorized CBO dealer bank, with supporting commercial documents (commercial invoices, customs/shipping records, and payment confirmations) retained for audit by the bank and the CBO. The exporter must complete the CBO's periodic foreign-exchange and trade data returns if the aggregate export turnover exceeds reporting thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) acting as the sole monetary, banking, and exchange-control supervisory authority for the Sultanate of Oman; foreign-exchange administration powers are exercised through CBO-licensed authorized dealers and the Banking Supervision Department.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility on a standalone basis is Low because the Central Bank of Oman does not issue independent Electronic Money Institution licenses along the EU-style EMI model. Foreign fintech entities and digital EMIs are permitted only as payment facilitators or technical service providers under an approved partnership with a fully licensed Omani commercial or Islamic bank. The dropshipping SPC may use international payment gateways such as Stripe and PayPal for inbound card-based customer collections, but such platforms must route funds to the SPC's Omani bank account through a CBO-authorized arrangement, and no originating liability may be held locally without a banking license.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign share-capital and shareholder-loan injections are to be routed by SWIFT MT103 from the beneficial-owner's account to the SPC's authorized Omani bank account. The remittance instructions must include the SPC legal name, the full-purpose narrative 'Share Capital Contribution' or 'Shareholder Subordinated Loan', and the unique invoice or agreement reference. The Bank's foreign-exchange and trade desk will apply the appropriate CBO classification, and the originating bank should include the creditor's international bank identifier and the SPC's registered address. Beneficiaries must present the shareholder resolution and the civil/company registration certificate during onboarding so the incoming credit can be booked without delay. For amounts exceeding OMR 10,000, a financial-justification or source-of-funds disclosure is standard; the Omani bank will seek CBO notification for larger capital inflows where applicable.Dropshipping supply-chain payments to third-party suppliers and logistics providers should be cleared via SWIFT MT103 for working-capital-sized disbursements. For high-value or repeat bulk merchandise shipments, a commercial irrevocable documentary Letter of Credit (LC) issued by the SPC's Omani bank and advised through the supplier's correspondent bank is the preferred rail, with required Shipping or air-way bills, commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin, and inspection certificates as the standard document set. Same-day or local OMR payments to domestic logistics and technology providers settle through the Omani Automated Clearing House (OACH); regional GCC transfers may use the Gulf one-stop payment interoperability. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% under the new Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 30/2023) for dividend distributions to a non-resident shareholder; the former regime (RD 28/2009) imposed no dividend WHT, and Omani residents and listed Omani companies are exempt.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return and self-assessment tax payment are due within four calendar months after the fiscal year-end; a 31 December year-end taxpayer must file and pay on or before 30 April. An extension may be requested but does not suspend late-payment interest (1% per month).

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Contemporaneous Local File becomes mandatory when the SPC's annual cross-border related-party transactions exceed OMR 500,000 (approx. USD 1.3 million); a Master File is required where the ultimate parent group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 50 million. Even below the threshold, related-party dropshipping supply or commission arrangements must be arm's-length and evidenced by intercompany agreements and pricing studies.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply the UAE-style Cabinet ESR framework, but the Oman Tax Authority applies a substance-over-form doctrine through tax-residency criteria (incorporation or effective management in Oman) and a General Anti-Avoidance Rule. A foreign-owned SPC deriving automated dropshipping income must maintain real presence — registered office, local bank account (with proceeds routed to Oman), independent management decisions, staff or infrastructure, and Omani-kept records — otherwise the OTA may disregard the entity or recharacterize the income as taxable in the beneficial owner's hands, with penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Statements

Statutory sanction: Under RD 30/2023 and RD 28/2009: surcharge of 100% of evaded tax and a monthly 1% late interest; criminal fine of OMR 1,000–10,000 (approx. USD 2,600–26,000) and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; the OTA may also issue an estimated revised assessment with a 25% negligence uplift.

Director personal liability: Courts may pierce the veil where the SPC is a façade; the manager or shareholder becomes jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax, plus restitution orders, asset freezing, a judicial travel ban, and disqualification from managing any Omani company.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The OTA may disregard the SPC under GAAR, tax the foreign principal as carrying on a permanent establishment in Oman, impose tax at 15% on the full revenue, add evasion-penalty doubling, and strike the SPC off the Commercial Registry under RD 18/2019.

Director personal liability: The shareholder who directs the sham channeling of profits may be personally assessed on the recharacterized income and ordered to make restitution; courts can impose a ban on engaging in trade in Oman and a 5-year travel ban.
Money Laundering (AML/CFT)

Statutory sanction: Under the Omani AML Law (RD 30/2016 as amended by RD 11/2023): imprisonment of 1–10 years and a fine up to OMR 100,000 (approx. USD 260,000); freezing and confiscation of the proceeds and the payment instruments; administrative revocation of the SPC's license and de-risking by PSP partners.

Director personal liability: Beneficial owners and officers who knew or should have known face personal criminal conviction, joint civil restitution to defrauded customers, director bans, and travel bans; extradition may follow under GCC and UN conventions.
Dividend / Cross-Border WHT Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: The withheld tax becomes immediately collectible from the SPC as a debt; a penalty of 100% of the unwithheld amount plus monthly 1% late payment interest applies; criminal fines may be imposed on directors.

Director personal liability: The manager who authorized the distribution in gross breach is personally liable for the unpaid WHT; the Tax Authority may register a lien on personal assets and request a court-ordered travel ban until the debt is settled.
Transfer Pricing and Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The OTA may reprice transactions under the arm's-length rule, levy tax on the adjusted profit at 15%, add a 1% monthly late interest, and impose a fine up to OMR 10,000 for non-documentation; repeated offenses may escalate to evasion proceedings.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify a false pricing analysis are personally liable for the additional assessed tax; professional disciplinary referral applies to any OM-licensed tax agent involved.
