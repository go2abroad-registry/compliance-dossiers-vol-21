Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; official e-service portal: https://otax.gov.om. Commercial registration and licensing are administered by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) through the Invest Easy portal (https://investeasy.gov.om).

E-government filing portal

Step 2: Register the SPC with the Oman Tax Authority for income tax within 30 days of incorporation or commencement of activity, submitting the CR, constitutional documents, majority-owner resolution, POA of a licensed tax agent, and registered-office proof; the OTA issues the Tax Identification Number (TIN). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. An Omani SPC is a standalone tax-resident company taxed at 15% on worldwide taxable income under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 65/2024), with a 3% reduced rate for small taxpayers whose taxable income does not exceed OMR 100,000. No pass-through or look-through treatment is available; dividends paid by an Omani company are generally not subject to further Omani income tax. A foreign-owned SPC does not benefit from territorial limitation unless a double-tax treaty applies; branches of foreign companies are taxed at 15% on Omani-source income only.

Available election forms

Oman does not provide US-style check-the-box elections. Statutory elections and mechanisms under Royal Decree 65/2024 include: (i) fiscal-unity/group taxation election allowing a resident parent with the Executive-Regulation minimum shareholding to file consolidated returns with resident subsidiaries, subject to OTA approval; (ii) small-business election to be taxed at 3% on taxable income below OMR 100,000, subject to eligibility conditions; (iii) accounting-basis elections (cash or accrual) where permitted for small taxpayers; and (iv) BEPS Action 2 anti-hybrid alignment rules requiring hybrid entities or arrangements to be disregarded for Omani tax purposes without elective status. Loss carry-forwards are usable up to 50% of subsequent taxable income within a 5-year window as prescribed by the new law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 annual taxable supplies (mandatory registration threshold measured on a rolling 12-month basis); OMR 19,250 annual taxable supplies (voluntary registration threshold). A non-resident with no Omani establishment making taxable supplies in Oman must register without regard to the threshold and appoint a resident tax representative.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to exports of goods where the goods physically depart Oman within the period prescribed by VAT rules (generally 90 days), the customer is a non-resident with no Omani place of residence or establishment, and the supply is evidenced by a cleared OTA e-invoice bearing the 0% VAT code plus customs export documentation. Pre-packaged spicy tallow hotpot base bricks supplied to foreign wholesale buyers and shipped through Omani seaports or airports satisfy these conditions; associated input VAT is fully creditable. Supplies consumed or re-imported into Oman, or offshore services rendered for use in Oman, remain taxable at 5% and do not qualify for zero-rating.

  • Bayaan customs export declaration (Omani electronic single-window export declaration authenticated by Oman Customs and cross-referenced to the zero-rated VAT e-invoice)
  • Clean on-board Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Oman to a foreign port with consignee, container, weight, and destination details consistent with the invoice
  • Commercial invoice and detailed packing list for the pre-packaged hotpot soup base bricks, including ISIC 4638 product description, HS/CN tariff classification, quantities, and unit values
  • Bank inward remittance credit advice (SWIFT MT103 / wire transfer) or Letter of Credit settlement certificate evidencing receipt of the full export proceeds in the SPC's Omani bank account in convertible foreign currency
  • Zero-rated electronic tax invoice with OTA fiscal clearance stamp and QR code expressly showing the 0% VAT code
  • Certificate of origin and transport and insurance documents as supporting evidence of the genuine export transaction

Oman is implementing a phased mandatory e-invoicing regime under OTA regulations and administrative rulings. Registered taxpayers must issue structured electronic tax invoices in XML (or SAF-T-compatible) format through OTA-certified software and integrate via approved APIs; each invoice and credit/debit note must carry a digital signature and a digitally generated QR code, and must obtain real-time fiscal clearance from the OTA digital platform before it can validly evidence VAT credits. Phase-in dates are tied to taxpayer revenue brackets and published OTA Official Gazette notifications, with exporters required to align cleared e-invoices with the Bayaan customs single-window export declarations.

Oman imposes no express statutory foreign-exchange repatriation deadline, but the standard 180-day substantiation window reflected in this dossier is applied by the OTA as the normal audit expectation for retention and production of export evidence and foreign-currency inward remittance documentation; the OTA may by written ruling extend the window in justified cases. Failure to substantiate zero-rating within the window, or diversion of exported goods back into the Omani market, triggers retrospective clawback of the 0% rate together with disallowance of recovered input VAT. Penalties under the VAT Law and OTA compliance procedures include administrative fines of up to OMR 10,000, daily late-payment charges on unrecovered VAT, fraud penalties equal to the VAT evaded, personal joint liability of directors and the single shareholder, and criminal referral in cases involving forged customs declarations or Bills of Lading; assessment periods remain open for up to 10 years ordinarily and 15 years in fraud cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account purposes (de facto IMF Article VIII observance). The currency has been officially pegged to the USD since 1986 at a fixed mid-rate of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (1 USD = 0.3845 OMR), and the peg is supported by the Sultanate's sovereign wealth reserves. There is no dual-rate regime, no CBO pre-approval for trade or dividend remittances, and no restriction on a 100% foreign-owned SPC opening accounts or repatriating after-tax profits. Profit/dividend remittance to the foreign shareholder is free of dividend withholding tax under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009 as amended); the principal administrative hurdle is that the SPC must (i) be fully tax-complied with the Oman Tax Authority, and (ii) present its audited financial statements and a board resolution to the remitting bank. A 10% withholding tax may attach to non-resident royalties, some service fees and related-party interest unless reduced by an applicable double-tax treaty, so all intercompany agreements must be assessed before payment routing.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose any statutory surrender, repatriation deadline, or mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds. The SPC may lawfully retain 100% of export earnings in USD/EUR foreign-currency bank accounts in Oman or abroad. CBO only requires the bank to (i) identify the underlying trade contract in the SWIFT narrative, (ii) retain the commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading and customs export declaration, and (iii) include the transaction in periodic CBO statistical returns. Recommended treasury policy: keep export receivables in a USD collection account, convert only the working-capital portion to OMR, and use the remaining USD to pay the China-based hotpot-base factory, thereby neutralizing the OMR/USD peg and minimizing conversion spread.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Muscat - the sole monetary, banking-supervision and foreign-exchange authority under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), implementing FX policy through CBO circulars, the Foreign Exchange Regulation framework and the Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

FEASIBILITY RATING: LOW. Legal constraints: payment-service operators active in or from Oman must hold a CBO licence under the regulator's Payment Services Provider framework; no offshore digital EMI (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) currently holds that licence. Offshore EMIs may lawfully collect USD/EUR/AED outside Oman and pay foreign invoices, but they cannot provide OMR rails, LC issuance, or local clearing, and using them as a primary treasury channel will create an unexplained-credit red flag under CBO AML/CFT rules. E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: FEASIBILITY RATING: LOW. Legal constraints: payment-service operators active in or from Oman must hold a CBO licence under the regulator's Payment Services Provider framework; no offshore digital EMI (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) currently holds that licence. Offshore EMIs may lawfully collect USD/EUR/AED outside Oman and pay foreign invoices, but they cannot provide OMR rails, LC issuance, or local clearing, and using them as a primary treasury channel will create an unexplained-credit red flag under CBO AML/CFT rules. Acceptable mitigation: use one offshore EMI only as an ancillary collector for retail export tickets below USD 5,000, subject to monthly reconciliation onto the Omani bank account, and retain the licensed Omani bank as the sole legal-residence account of export proceeds.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% standard corporate income tax rate on taxable income; a preferential 3% band applies to qualifying small businesses on the first OMR 100,000 of annual taxable income.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax applies to cash, in-kind, or constructive dividend distributions made by the Omani SPC to a non-resident shareholder. Distributions to Omani resident corporate shareholders are exempt from withholding tax under Oman's income tax law.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory for the tax year where aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 5,000,000; a Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 50,000,000. A complete transfer pricing study is additionally required for all controlled cross-border transactions even below the threshold when the resale margin or purchase price falls outside the comparable arm's length range, especially where imported tallow soup base is sold to foreign associated distributors.

Economic substance scope: Oman's Economic Substance Regulations apply to resident licensed entities conducting commercial activities, including export distribution. The SPC must demonstrate substantial activity in Oman through a physical office, locally employed manager or staff, contract conclusion and strategic decision-making in Oman, and local bank accounts. A licensed hotpot soup base distributor that is wholly foreign-owned and lacks local substance faces characterization as a shell company and the related tax and AML consequences.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Commercial Fraud

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment for up to seven years and a fine of up to OMR 100,000; imposed in addition to the full tax assessment, late payment surcharges, and an evasion penalty of up to 100% of tax due. The commercial license and all export Customs clearance facilities are subject to immediate suspension or revocation.

Director personal liability: The directors and shadow directors of the SPC are jointly and severally liable for civil restitution of evaded taxes. The Commercial Court may pierce the corporate veil, issue travel bans, freeze personal bank accounts, and impose custodial sentences for intentional fraud.
Shell Company / Absence of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Assessment of deemed profits at 15% corporate income tax, disallowance of all related-party deduction and zero-rating, revocation of the commercial registration and tax card, and imposition of economic substance monetary penalties up to OMR 20,000 under the Oman Tax Authority's regulatory arsenal.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal penalties for authorizing shell activities under the Commercial Companies Law, including disqualification from directorship for up to five years, civil restitution for third-party liabilities, and travel restriction pending final tax clearance.
Transfer Pricing Violations

Statutory sanction: The Oman Tax Authority may make a unilateral arm's length adjustment, recharacterize profit at the 15% tax rate, impose a penalty of 20% to 40% of the additional tax assessed, and add a daily late-payment surcharge on the resulting deficiency. Fines of up to OMR 50,000 may be imposed for non-filing of statutory TP documentation.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute non-compliant intercompany sale and purchase agreements are personally liable for the additional corporate tax, penalties, and interest to the extent of fraudulent preference; the court may order the return of unlawful dividends and issue a custodial sentence where transfer pricing is used to defraud the public revenue.
Withholding Tax and Beneficial Ownership Fraud

Statutory sanction: The full 10% withheld tax is collected from the SPC, plus a 15% late payment penalty, daily surcharge, and a statutory fine of not less than OMR 500. Fraudulent DTA claims may trigger double taxation and a refund denial with criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Finance directors and resident officers who are responsible for payroll and treasury are jointly and severally liable for uncollected withholding taxes; personal bank and passport records are subject to issuance of a travel ban pending full settlement of the collected but unpaid tax.
Anti-Money Laundering / Trade-Based Financial Crime

Statutory sanction: Under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law and Economic Substance Rules, offenders are liable to imprisonment up to ten years, a fine up to OMR 200,000, confiscation of launder assets and freight containers, external audit of trade documentation, and blacklisting of bank accounts and trade licence.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where directors facilitate trade-based money laundering or knowingly issue false bills of lading. Directors may be disqualified, ordered to make full civil restitution, placed on the national travel ban list, and imprisoned; asset freezing extends to personal and family-owned properties.
