Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - official e-services portal available at https://taxoman.om

E-government filing portal

Foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) registers with the Oman Tax Authority by: (1) first obtaining Commercial Registration (Sijil) from the Ministry of Commerce, Investment and Communications (MOCICID); (2) applying for a VAT Tax Registration Number (TRN) via the OTA e-services platform using the CR, Omani address proof, shareholder/officer identification and authorized signatory details; (3) separately applying for income tax registration and obtaining a Tax Card / Income Tax Identification Number within the statutory period after CR issuance or commencement of business; (4) if engaged in imports/exports, completing Royal Oman Police Customs registration and obtaining a Customs Registration Number / Economic Operator Code. All registration filings are made in Arabic/English through the OTA portal, and approved taxpayers receive an electronic VAT certificate containing the TRN.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) registers with the Oman Tax Authority by: (1) first obtaining Commercial Registration (Sijil) from the Ministry of Commerce, Investment and Communications (MOCICID); (2) applying for a VAT Tax Registration Number (TRN) via the OTA e-services platform using the CR, Omani address proof, shareholder/officer identification and authorized signatory details; (3) separately applying for income tax registration and obtaining a Tax Card / Income Tax Identification Number within the statutory period after CR issuance or commencement of business; (4) if engaged in imports/exports, completing Royal Oman Police Customs registration and obtaining a Customs Registration Number / Economic Operator Code. All registration filings are made in Arabic/English through the OTA portal, and approved taxpayers receive an electronic VAT certificate containing the TRN.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not provide US-style 'check-the-box' or hybrid mismatch elections; a Single Person Company is per se an entity-level taxpayer with no pass-through eligibility. Available statutory elections under Omani tax law include: (a) voluntary VAT registration where taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,000 but remain below the mandatory threshold; (b) VAT group registration under the Oman VAT Law for related resident persons with close financial, economic and organizational links, permitting consolidated VAT filing; (c) income tax accounting period alignment with the CR expiry or fiscal year subject to OTA approval; and (d) no domestic hybrid mismatch election - OECD BEPS-aligned substance requirements govern treaty and source characterization.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration threshold: OMR 38,500 for taxable supplies (excluding VAT) over any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration threshold: OMR 19,000. A non-resident supplier without an Oman establishment making taxable supplies in Oman is required to register for VAT irrespective of the OMR 38,500 threshold where Omani VAT is levied or recoverable by reverse charge.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for goods physically exported from Oman to a destination outside Oman, or to a place outside the jurisdiction where the recipient is outside Oman, provided the exporter holds sufficient evidence that the goods left Oman within the applicable time limit (generally 90 days from the date of supply/invoice). Dehydrated garlic flakes and crushed chili pepper sold as bulk export commodities qualify for 0% VAT if the sale is a genuine export, the goods are shipped under a contract of sale, the exporter is the supplier, and the complete customs and payment evidence is retained. Directly related export freight, port and international logistics services may also be zero-rated where the recipient is outside Oman and the services are physically performed overseas or directly connected to the export.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan / ACS) validated by the Royal Oman Police Customs showing the Omani port of exit
  • Bill of Lading, airway bill or equivalent transport document naming the Omani exporter and the foreign destination port/airport
  • Commercial invoice referencing the zero-rated export and containing product, quantity, unit price, total value and HS codes (e.g. 0712.90 / 0904.20)
  • Packing list covering the shipment contents and weight details
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit statement evidencing receipt of export proceeds into the Omani exporter's account
  • Certificate of Origin (where requested by the importing country or letter of credit conditions)

Oman has not yet implemented a mandatory real-time electronic invoice clearance API, fiscal stamping or continuous transaction control regime for VAT. Taxpayers must issue VAT-compliant invoices using OTA-accepted electronic or paper formats, retain XML/PDF or paper records, and submit periodic VAT returns through the OTA e-services portal. Digital stamping is limited to excise tobacco products and does not apply to dehydrated vegetables/spices exports. No OTA clearance gate exists at the invoice issuance stage for the bulk spice export sector; only the customs export declaration and VAT return filing are the mandatory clearance points.

The Oman Tax Authority conducts retrospective audit checks on export zero-rating, requiring a full evidence chain of the customs export declaration, transport documents and the inward remittance certificate within 180 days of shipment. Failure to substantiate physical export leads to VAT reassessment at 5% on the gross supply, clawback of any input VAT previously recovered, late-payment penalties (assessed on a per-day basis as provided under the VAT Law), fixed administrative fines up to OMR 5,000 for record-keeping/invoice failures, and more severe criminal liability, including imprisonment and higher fines, for fraudulent export schemes. The limitation period for OTA assessment and clawback is generally five years from the end of the relevant tax period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current-account transactions including trade settlements, service fees, and profit repatriation, subject to standard bank documentation and anti-money laundering verification. Capital-account flows, including inbound equity injections and shareholder loans, are freely permitted for registered foreign-owned entities but must be routed through licensed authorized dealers and supported by underlying corporate resolutions and proof of beneficial ownership. Dividend and profit remittances require audited financials, tax clearance from the Oman Tax Authority, and a board resolution; no quantitative limits or import-behind-export compensation rules apply.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Under CBO regulations, there is no mandatory currency surrender ratio or forced conversion percentage for export receipts; however, all export proceeds must be repatriated to Oman and credited to a CBO-licensed commercial bank account owned by the exporter within 90 days of the bill of lading date. The exporter may retain foreign currency balances in an approved foreign-currency account or convert to OMR as commercially required. Non-repatriation without prior CBO approval constitutes a exchange control violation subject to fines and bank restrictions.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI (Electronic Money Institution) accounts are not permissible as primary clearing rails for this export entity. CBO Circular BM/1130 and the Payment Systems Act require any payment service provider serving Oman-resident corporate exporters to hold an active payment service provider license issued by CBO and to maintain a physical or licensed agency presence in Oman. Offshore EMIs without CBO license or correspondent banking sponsorship from a licensed Omani bank receive a Low feasibility rating; they may be used only as supplemental collection agents, and full treasury settlement must flow through a licensed Omani bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity and capital injections from the foreign shareholder to the Oman SPC must be wired using SWIFT MT103 or MT202/202COV with the CBO balance-of-payments purpose code for Direct Investment in Oman — Equity Capital. The beneficiary account must be the SPC's capital escrow account at a CBO-licensed commercial bank. The bank will request a board resolution approving the capital increase, a share allotment list, a KYC/EDD questionnaire, and a signed capital contribution agreement. The funds are credited as soon as the reference is cleared; no prior currency conversion is mandated and the bank will open a temporary inward remittance suspense account if documents are incomplete.For supplier payments and service fees, the documented clearing rails are SWIFT MT103 (customer-initiated transfers) for open-account disbursements and Commercial Letters of Credit (MT700/MT710) for documentary trade settlements. Export payments from foreign buyers are accepted via MT103 or documentary collections. All outbound payments must be matched against corresponding trade invoices, bills of lading, and sales contracts, and are processed through the bank's trade finance module.
