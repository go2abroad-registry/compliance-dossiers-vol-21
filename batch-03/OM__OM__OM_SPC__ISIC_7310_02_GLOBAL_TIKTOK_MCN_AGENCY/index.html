Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (الهيئة العامة للضرائب); official national e-portal: Emtax (taxoman.gov.om). The Authority administers corporate income tax, VAT, and excise tax in the Sultanate of Oman.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Obtain Commercial Registration (CR) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for the Single Person Company (SPC). Step 2: File the registration application with the Oman Tax Authority, using the Certified Public Accountant or authorized tax agent, to receive the Tax Identification Number (TIN). Step 3: If annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000 (or voluntary registration is chosen above OMR 100,000), submit a separate VAT registration application via the Emtax portal. Non-resident owners may hold 100% of the SPC; no minimum capital requirement is imposed. The Tax Authority issues one TIN for income tax and one VAT registration number where applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election exists under Omani tax law. A resident SPC is automatically taxed as a separate taxable person on worldwide income and profits. The sole permissible elections relevant to this entity are VAT elections: voluntary VAT registration where taxable turnover exceeds OMR 100,000 but remains below the OMR 500,000 mandatory threshold, and the election to zero-rate qualifying exported services and goods. No partnership pass-through election is available for SPCs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies (excluding zero-rated exports and capital assets sold); Voluntary registration: OMR 100,000 of annual taxable supplies or expenses.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods qualify for 0% VAT if the goods are transported out of Oman and the exporter retains bill of lading, purchase order, and customs export declaration. Cross-border services supplied to a non-resident, including creator management, media agency, and TikTok MCN services provided to a non-resident principal outside Oman, may be zero-rated when the recipient is outside a GCC implementing state, the service is not effectively received for use within Oman, and the supplier holds documentary evidence of the non-resident status of the recipient. Intermediary and commission services to non-resident online platforms are generally treated as exported services under the place-of-supply rules. The taxpayer bears the burden of substantiating that the benefit of the service is enjoyed outside Oman and that the service is neither connected with Oman-situs immovable property nor performed directly on goods in Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (electronic SAD document issued by Royal Oman Police Customs)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill or equivalent transport document evidencing physical departure from Oman
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by an authorised bank in Oman evidencing receipt of the export consideration in foreign exchange

As of the current compliance cycle, Oman has not deployed a mandatory real-time electronic invoicing clearance system, digital stamping, or universal API-based fiscalization. Tax invoices, whether paper or electronic, must meet the content requirements of the VAT Executive Regulations (Article 54), including supplier name, TIN and VAT ID, invoice date, description, consideration, and separate VAT amount. VAT returns are filed electronically through the Emtax portal, and exporters must retain a full evidence trail to substantiate zero-rated export treatment. A phased mandatory e-invoicing regime is expected but not currently operative.

Oman Tax Authority conducts stringent substance-based audits of export zero-rating claims. In the absence of a complete export file, the Authority may claw back the VAT with interest, apply delayed-payment penalties, and impose administrative fines up to three times the tax evaded or exacted by criminal sanctions including imprisonment under the VAT Law. Additionally, the Central Bank of Oman may impose exchange-control penalties for failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days. False export documentation or forged remittance certificates trigger referral to the public prosecutor and cancellation of the exporter's VAT registration.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Oman does not publish a separately codified monetary de minimis threshold in the Income Tax Law; the Tax Authority applies OECD-arm's-length principles and administrative transfer pricing guidelines. In practice, mandatory Local File preparation and contemporaneous documentation are expected when aggregate annual controlled transactions exceed OMR 500,000, and a consolidated Master File is expected when the multinational group's annual turnover exceeds OMR 30,000,000. The Tax Authority may in any event demand transfer pricing documentation for transactions of any size during an audit, and a red flag is immediately raised where commission payments to TikTok, ByteDance, Wise, or offshore creator treasury entities are not benchmarked.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not currently operate a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) code, but the general anti-avoidance rules, the Income Tax Law's tax resident definition, and the Commercial Companies Law require genuine management and control, physical office premises, full-time employees carrying on creator management activities, board powers exercised inside Oman, and actual decision-making functions. A single-person company whose director and general manager live outside Oman, which has no employment contracts with creators, and whose business is wholly directed by TikTok/ByteDance treasury systems will fail economic substance and become susceptible to residence reclassification, treaty denial, shell-company penalties, and the treatment of its accumulated profits as constructive distributions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Recovery of all underpaid tax; civil fines of up to 50% or 100% of the evaded tax under the Tax Authority's administrative penalty matrix; a separate criminal fine of OMR 5,000 to OMR 50,000 and imprisonment for a term of up to five years under Royal Decree 28/2009 as amended, plus daily late-payment surcharge until full settlement.

Director personal liability: Directors, the sole shareholder, and signing managers who approve or sign false returns are personally liable for the restitution of evaded tax. The corporate veil is pierced where fraud, forgery, or sham transactions are proven; the civil court and Tax Authority may impose travel bans, asset freezes, seizure of foreign passports, and disqualification from acting as a director in Oman.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation and Round-Tripping

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may adjust the arm's-length price, disallow the payment, impose a 15% tax on the adjusted profit, add late-payment penalties and monthly surcharges, and impose administrative fines of OMR 1,000 to OMR 20,000. Repeated noncompliance triggers criminal referral for tax evasion and false certification of tax returns.

Director personal liability: The general manager and tax agent who signed incorrect transfer pricing computations become personally liable for penalties attributable to wilful misconduct; the Tax Authority may enforce against their personal assets, restrict their ability to manage any Omani company, and coordinate with foreign tax authorities through exchange of information.
Artificial Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the SPC as tax non-resident; assessment on income sourced in Oman at the 15% rate with withholding obligations; denial of treaty benefits; cancellation of the Commercial Registration and Tax Card; imposition of fines under the Commercial Companies Law and an additional administrative penalty for operating without real management; potential confiscation of funds under anti-shell-company provisions.

Director personal liability: The lawful owner and nominee director cannot claim limited liability when the arrangement is used to defeat tax. Courts may pierce the SPC veil, make the foreign parent and beneficial owner jointly liable for taxes, and issue a travel ban against the directors until all assessed tax and penalties are paid. The Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion may also revoke the directors' fitness status.
AML Violation Through Creator Payouts and Unlicensed Money Transmission

Statutory sanction: Criminal sanctions under the Omani Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016, as amended) impose substantial fines, seizure of the entire value of the laundered property, and imprisonment of up to seven years for natural persons; corporate entities face deregistration, revocation of Commercial Registration, and fines of up to OMR 100,000 or more depending on the gravity of the offence. The Central Bank of Oman may freeze all accounts linked to the MCN agency.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally liable for failing to file suspicious-transaction reports, failing to verify creator identities, and allowing the company's payment infrastructure to be used by an unlicensed money-service business. Personal consequences include criminal conviction, civil restitution of the laundered flows, passport surrender, travel bans, and disqualification from managing financial or non-financial designated businesses in Oman.
