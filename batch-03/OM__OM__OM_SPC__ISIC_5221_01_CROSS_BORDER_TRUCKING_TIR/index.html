Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - Sultanate of Oman. Primary e-services and taxpayer portal: https://taxes.gov.om; OTA corporate domain: https://www.ota.gov.om; customs counterpart for TIR exit control: Royal Oman Police Directorate General of Customs (Al Bayan electronic single-window system).

E-government filing portal

The foreign investor incorporates the SPC under the Oman Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) via the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion portal, obtaining the Commercial Registration and the statutory licence for ISIC 5221 cross-border road freight from the competent transport regulator. The Commercial Registration is pushed electronically to the OTA, which issues the corporate Tax Identification Number (e-Tax Card) automatically within 30 days of incorporation; if not auto-issued, the SPC must register on taxes.gov.om using the CR certificate, notarized articles of association of the Single Person Company, the sole shareholder's passport or ID, power of attorney, registered-office proof and a banking letter. A separate VAT registration application is mandatory within 30 days of crossing OMR 500,000 of taxable supplies (voluntary from OMR 187,500); the approved VAT certificate contains the VAT ID and customs code, which must be activated in Al Bayan and linked to the TIR guarantee chain before zero-rated export declarations can be transmitted.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. The Omani SPC is an opaque resident juridical person taxed on its own income under the Oman Income Tax Law. Headline CIT rate is 15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000; no CIT is levied on the first OMR 30,000 of taxable income. Resident SPCs are taxed on global income, with relief under double-taxation agreements for foreign permanent establishment income. No US-style pass-through or check-the-box election is available; dividends distributed to the sole foreign shareholder may be subject to Omani withholding (generally 10%) unless reduced by treaty.

Available election forms

No entity-classification or check-the-box election exists under Oman law. Available statutory elections for the SPC and its TIR fleet include: (i) tax-group consolidation election for up to five 100%-owned Omani resident companies with elimination of unrealized intra-group profits; (ii) financial year-end election subject to OTA approval; (iii) depreciation elections for rolling assets (straight-line vs declining-balance) with annual capital allowances on trucks, trailers and TIR equipment; (iv) foreign tax credit election in lieu of a deduction for foreign branch income; (v) VAT input-tax recovery election for fuel, tolls and maintenance, requiring the 100% business-use test evidenced by fuel-card telematics; (vi) voluntary VAT registration election once turnover reaches OMR 187,500; (vii) DTA relief election, including treaty-residence certificate and reduced-withholding filings for cross-border freight income.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies measured by a 12-month look-back or 30-day look-forward test, including taxable imports; voluntary registration from OMR 187,500. The resident SPC is within the threshold based on its standard-rated and zero-rated export supplies. Importation of goods is taxable at the 5% rate at the moment of import unless suspended under the TIR transit procedure, regardless of the registration threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) goods exported outside the Sultanate, including goods carried under a valid TIR Carnet with an Al Bayan export declaration and a physical Omani customs exit stamp; (ii) international transport of goods by road outbound or inbound to Oman supplied by the SPC as carrier to a non-Omani customer; (iii) transport of goods in transit across Oman to a third country, plus freight-related services physically performed outside Oman. The 0% rate is available only to VAT-registered persons who issue a compliant e-invoice with the statutory zero-rate reason code and who maintain an unbroken documentary chain from Omani point of departure to the foreign destination. Domestic-leg-only trucking, services consumed in Oman, or supplies where the evidence is incomplete are standard-rated at 5%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD/BC-EX) with the Omani customs exit stamp or electronic acceptance record from the Al Bayan single-window system
  • TIR Carnet (green booklet) issued by the Omani TIR guaranteeing association accredited by the IRU, with entry and exit customs endorsements
  • Signed CMR International Consignment Note (green 9-copy CMR) evidencing the cross-border road journey under ISIC 5221
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill or multimodal cargo manifest where the TIR itinerary includes sea or air segments
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by a licensed Omani bank evidencing foreign-currency collection and repatriation of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice of the Omani SPC showing the VAT registration number and the zero-rated supply code
  • VAT e-invoice with Authority Invoice Number, QR code, OTA digital stamp, and the matching Al Bayan export declaration number
  • B-12 customs registration or the permanent customs authorization card for the truck and driver on the TIR corridor

Oman is implementing a phased mandatory e-invoicing and fiscal-clearance mandate administered by the OTA. Registered taxpayers must issue tax invoices through the OTA e-invoicing portal or an approved service-provider API using an OTA-accepted XML/JSON schema with mutual-TLS authentication. Each cleared invoice receives a unique Authority Invoice Number, a QR code and an OTA digital stamp; the buyer may claim input VAT only on stamped e-invoices. Real-time clearance with automatic rejection of unstamped or incorrectly coded invoices is rolled out progressively by taxpayer segment and turnover. The OTA cross-references e-invoice data with the Al Bayan customs exit declarations, requiring zero-rated export invoices to display the matching export declaration number at the time of stamping.

The OTA audits zero-rated export claims by cross-matching e-invoice data, Al Bayan exit records, TIR Carnet discharge stamps, CMR consignment notes and the inward remittance database of licensed Omani banks. Where the required documentation is missing, forged or inconsistent, the zero rating is denied and the OTA claws back the 5% VAT for the entire open reassessment period (up to five prior tax years), together with statutory late-payment charges accruing from the original filing date and administrative fines of up to OMR 25,000 per violation under the VAT Law. Deliberate fraudulent refund claims or false TIR/CMR/IRC documents attract criminal sanctions of imprisonment of up to five years and fines of up to OMR 100,000, retrospective cancellation of refunds, suspension or termination of the VAT registration, and debarment from the Omani TIR guarantee chain with notification to the IRU.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current account and capital account transactions. The OMR is pegged to the USD at 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD. Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, so no prior CBO approval is required for current international payments, including transport service fees, tolls, insurance, commissions, and profit remittances. Dividends and branch/SPhC profits may be freely transferred abroad after the OTA confirms that corporate tax, withholding tax, and any other liabilities are settled. Capital account movements, including shareholder equity injections, shareholder loans, liquidation proceeds, and sale proceeds, are liberalized but are subject to MCIIP registration and documentary verification of the underlying transaction. CBO does not impose exchange surrender requirements or conversion ratios.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman has no statutory timeline for surrendering export proceeds or any mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. A resident Single Person Company (SPC) is not required to sell foreign currency to the CBO or to convert export proceeds into OMR. Export receipts, including cross-border trucking revenue under the TIR Carnet system, must be routed through a licensed Omani commercial bank and credited to the SPC's local foreign-currency account. Maintaining a foreign bank account outside Oman without prior CBO approval is not permitted. Banks must report all relevant transactions to the CBO for balance-of-payments statistics and apply normal AML/KYC controls if proceeds remain uncollected for a commercially unreasonable period.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting through its Banking Supervision Department and Financial Stability Department, is the foreign exchange control authority for Oman. Supporting authorities are the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) for direct investment registration and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) for tax clearance obligations on fund repatriation.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium viability as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMIs and virtual-account providers are not licensed by the CBO to hold OMR or provide settlement services in Oman. They cannot serve as the SPC's main operating bank or as a local clearing member. A foreign EMI may be used only for narrow outbound payments in foreign currency to contractors or suppliers outside the GCC, provided the SPC's local bank relationship remains the dominant cash pool. Legal constraints include the CBO's payment services licensing framework, the prohibition on unlicensed remittance hehavior, lack of Omani deposit insurance, and the risk that revenue received indirectly through a foreign EMI is characterized as an undisclosed offshore banking relationship. Viability as a supplemental treasury tool is medium; as a replacement for an Omani bank account it is low.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital injection: wire the funds by SWIFT MT103 to the SPC's capital escrow/operations account at a CBO-licensed Omani bank. The payment instructions must state the purpose code for foreign direct investment, which under CBO reporting is the Balance of Payments code for direct investment equity capital. The transferor bank must include a full reference to the SPC's name, Commercial Register number, MCIIP investment registration, and a clear remittance narrative such as 'EQUITY CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION - SPC - OMAN'. In support of the credit, the receiving bank requires the investor's audited or bank statements, the notarized SPC incorporation deed, shareholder resolution, proof of identity and address of the UBO, and a copy of the CBO/monetary licensing of the sending bank. If the injection is a shareholder loan, the inbound wire must be supported by a notarized loan agreement, an intercompany balance-sheet position, transfer-pricing documentation, and the SPC's board resolution. The bank verifies sanctions screening, source-of-funds evidence, and the transaction's consistency with the company's trade cycle. The entire transaction is reported by the bank to the CBO through the International Transactions Reporting System.Operating disbursements and trade settlements should be executed through the SPC's Omani clearing account using SWIFT MT103 for foreign-currency payments to drivers, subcontractors, spare-part suppliers, insurance companies, toll operators, and customs agencies. Domestic OMR payments for fuel, local maintenance, and salaries may be made through the bank's ACH or direct-debit rails. For large capital purchases such as trucks, trailers, and bonded-warehouse equipment, the SPC may use a commercial sight letter of credit issued by its Omani bank under UCP600, with MT799 messages for amendments and MT103 for reimbursement after presentation. All outbound payment instructions must reference the underlying commercial invoice, transport contract, or LC number, and CBO reporting is mandatory.
