Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No escrow account or blocked capital deposit certificate is required; a bank statement evidencing capital remittance suffices for MoCIIP.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) for the registered office, confirming compliance with the Oman Fire Code (NFPA-based), including fire extinguishers, emergency exits, and alarm systems. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (established under Royal Decree No. 39/2020, successor to the Secretariat General of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance); principal e-filing and registration portal: https://taxoman.gov.om (integrated OTA e-services portal).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. Step 2: File the Certificate of Tax Registration application on the OTA e-services portal attaching the CR, the SPC constitutional deed, the foreign shareholder identification, the registered local agent mandate, and opening financial statements where applicable; receive the Tax Identification Number (TIN) and Tax Card. Step 3: Register under the VAT Law (Royal Decree No. 121/2020) if taxable supplies or expenses exceed the OMR 38,500 mandatory threshold (voluntary threshold OMR 19,250). Step 4: Register with the Public Authority for Social Insurance (PASI) for any Omani personnel. The SPC must also maintain a GCC-valid tax residence certificate and notify the OTA of the permanent establishment risk of the offshore lessee.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation: the foreign-owned Omani SPC is an Omani-resident juridical person subject to corporate income tax under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (Income Tax Law, as amended). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the foreign-owned Omani SPC is an Omani-resident juridical person subject to corporate income tax under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (Income Tax Law, as amended). Resident SPCs are taxable on worldwide income at the standard 15% CIT rate; no pass-through treatment to the foreign shareholder is available. Territorial limitation applies only to non-resident service providers (Oman-source income); the aircraft-engine dry-lease rental is sourced to Oman where the lessor is an Omani resident unless the export carve-out applies. An SPC located in the Special Economic Zone at Duqm or a recognized free zone may instead benefit from the statutory income-tax holiday under RD 19/2016 if the tax-exempt zone status is formally elected.

Available election forms

Oman has no US-style check-the-box election or group loss-relief consolidation. Available elections: (i) a reduced 3% CIT basis for qualifying small/medium enterprises with taxable income up to OMR 30,000 where the legal structure satisfies the FDI/Omani-ownership conditions set by the OTA; (ii) the statutory straight-line depreciation election under Article 18 of the Income Tax Law, including accelerated rates supported by professional evaluation; (iii) loss carryforward election up to five years (no carryback); (iv) the Foreign Tax Credit election for tax paid to a treaty counterparty on the cross-border lease; and (v) in free-zone/Duqm cases, the election to be governed by the tax-exemption regime in lieu of the standard code. Hybrid mismatch elections are not presently recognized under Omani domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 (mandatory registration where the value of taxable supplies or taxable expenses exceeds this amount in any consecutive 12-month period); OMR 19,250 (voluntary registration threshold)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies under Articles 29-32 of the Oman VAT Law for (i) goods physically exported from Oman and (ii) services supplied to a non-resident customer who is outside Oman and who benefits from the service wholly outside the Sultanate. For the dry lease of a turbofan engine, the monthly lease payments qualify at 0% where the engine leaves Oman within 90 days of the date of supply/invoice, or is exported via an international air waybill, and where the lessee is a foreign airline/operator and the engine is used outside Oman during the lease term. Input VAT incurred on engine acquisition, maintenance, and finance costs is recoverable to the extent the engine is used exclusively for zero-rated leases.

  • Customs Export Declaration (ECC/Bayan electronic clearance from Royal Oman Police Customs)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical movement of the engine out of Oman
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or bank foreign-currency receipt evidence for the lease rentals
  • Signed aircraft-engine dry lease agreement with the non-resident lessee
  • Commercially reasonable flight/usage log proving the engine location during the lease term
  • VAT invoice issued at 0% with the zero-rated supply reason code
  • Customs re-export or outward-processing permit where the engine was previously imported

Oman is transitioning to mandatory B2B electronic invoicing under the OTA e-invoicing programme ('Sanad'). Once the applicable phase covering aviation/asset lessors is gazetted, the SPC must issue fiscal invoices through OTA-approved APIs, obtain real-time fiscal clearance, apply digital stamping/QR codes, and retain an immutable audit trail. Until activation, the SPC must issue VAT invoices in the OTA-prescribed format with sequential numbering and submit periodic VAT returns through taxoman.gov.om; the OTA may require a prior authorization for bulk credit-note issuance.

The Oman Tax Authority may disallow zero-rating where export evidence or the lease contract is absent, reassessing VAT at 5% with statutory late-payment penalties (generally 5% per 30 days of delay, capped at 25% of the assessed VAT), and imposing administrative penalties for incorrect refund claims. Wilful overstatement to obtain a refund constitutes tax evasion under the OTA enforcement framework: the VAT refund is subject to retrospective clawback, assessment periods may be extended beyond the ordinary five-year limitation in cases of fraud, and criminal sanctions including imprisonment and fines may be imposed on the SPC and its responsible officers; voluntary disclosure programmes may reduce monetary fines but do not eliminate liability.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible under a conventional peg to the U.S. dollar (1 OMR = 2.6008 USD; OMR/USD 0.3845). Oman maintains no general foreign-exchange controls on current or capital account transactions for bona fide commercial purposes, including profit remittance by a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company. Dividend and capital remittances are permitted once the SPC has fulfilled corporate registry, tax clearance, AML/KYC verification, and the underlying profit is identified by audited financial statements. Banks have internal compliance gateways on large remittances and round-tripping, but there is no statutory convertibility restriction. Practical hurdles are documentary, not regulatory: present approved board resolution, audited financials or capital account schedules, and valid tax clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory export-proceeds surrender, a fixed statutory timeline, or a compulsory conversion ratio for export receipts in a freely convertible currency. The CBO requires bank reporting of cross-border export proceeds for balance-of-payments statistical purposes, and the Omani Rial peg ensures conversion may be executed at the official USD/OMR rate through any licensed bank. Proceeds from aircraft-engine dry-lease rentals should remain identifiable in the SPC's foreign-currency export/escrow account; non-Omani currency balances may be retained or converted without a CBO surrender order.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — the registered central monetary authority and foreign-exchange regulator; banking supervision is governed by the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 114/2000), with money-exchange and payment services licensed under CBO circulars.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium feasibility. Oman does not recognize an offshore fintech electronic-money institution (EMI) as a licensed bank or as a clearing/settlement participant; a foreign EMI cannot independently hold OMR, access the CBO clearing rails, or provide a capital escrow account. The SPC may use a licensed-EMI card facility for ancillary expense flows only if it maintains a primary relationship with a CBO-licensed commercial bank. Legal constraints: EMIs must be licensed under CBO fintech/electronic-payment regulations and are subject to strict safeguarding and no-lending rules; offshore EMIs are not permitted to act as the treasury core.

Use a CBO balance-of-payments code matching direct investment/capital contribution into a resident SPC and name the beneficiary as 'Oman SPC - Aircraft Engine Dry Leasing Account'. Prior to credit, the receiving bank verifies the beneficiary IBAN, screens OFAC/EU/UN/Omani sanctions, matches the purpose code to the executed equity or loan agreement, and requests a board resolution plus source-of-funds statements. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and TP schedule are mandatory when related-party transactions in a tax year exceed OMR 1,000,000; Master File is required when the worldwide group's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 100,000,000 and the Oman SPC's related-party transactions exceed OMR 1,000,000. Thresholds are assessed separately for goods, intangibles, services and financing.

Economic substance scope: As an Omani licensed company engaged in financial leasing, the SPC must satisfy Oman's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR): board meetings and aircraft-engine lease-management decisions must take place in Oman, the SPC must employ technically qualified local staff, maintain physical office premises, exercise strategic command over the engine-leasing activity, and file an annual ESR report. A conduit arrangement with no core income-generating activity (CIGA) in Oman is treated as abusive and automatically triggers tax evasion, GPI/AML and strike-off proceedings.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Deliberate Misreporting

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of 50% of the evaded tax (non-negotiable); additional late-payment charge of 2% per month capped at 50%; criminal fine reaching OMR 50,000 and imprisonment for up to five years.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced for deliberately false filings; directors and officers are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and face asset freezes, travel bans, confiscation orders and disqualification from Omani boards.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disallowance and recharacterisation of the underlying transactions, denial of treaty benefits, cancellation of the commercial registration, and an Economic Substance penalty of up to OMR 25,000 per accounting period.

Director personal liability: Directors signing ESR declarations knowing the SPC has no real substance are personally liable for the penalty and are subject to immediate travel bans and director-disqualification orders.
AMLA / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's AML Law, fines up to OMR 500,000, forfeiture of the leased engine and lease proceeds, suspension of the SPC's commercial licence, and a prison term of up to seven years for convicted persons.

Director personal liability: Knowingly involved directors and beneficial owners face criminal prosecution, joint civil restitution, travel bans, asset-freeze orders and imprisonment; confiscation extends to equivalent-value assets held abroad.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation / Incomplete TP Documentation

Statutory sanction: Primary TP adjustment plus an additional penalty of 25% of the additional tax assessed; late-payment penalties accrue from the original return due date and may raise the total liability beyond 100% of the adjustment.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for the TP adjustment if they certified the return knowing that tenant/owner relationships were mispriced; repeated non-compliance triggers travel bans and joint liability for the entire additional tax.
