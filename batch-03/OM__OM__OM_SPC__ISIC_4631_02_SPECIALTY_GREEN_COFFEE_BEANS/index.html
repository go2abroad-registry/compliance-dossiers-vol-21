Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority clearance for fire safety systems and means of escape at the registered warehouse or storage facility; structural load inspection certification by a licensed engineer for racking systems; and occupational safety clearance from the Ministry of Labour covering emergency exits, extinguishers, and safe material handling equipment.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – Tax Oman e-Services Portal: https://taxoman.gov.om (operated under the Ministry of Finance; also accessible via the unified government services portal)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration Certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, then register through the Tax Oman e-Services Portal by submitting certified constitutional documents, the commercial registration certificate, registered office address proof, and a designated tax agent appointment if required. The OTA issues a Tax Card (TIN) which also functions as the VAT identification number (VAT ID) once VAT registration is completed. VAT registration is integrated into the same portal and is mandatory once the statutory turnover threshold is crossed.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company must first obtain a Commercial Registration Certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, then register through the Tax Oman e-Services Portal by submitting certified constitutional documents, the commercial registration certificate, registered office address proof, and a designated tax agent appointment if required. The OTA issues a Tax Card (TIN) which also functions as the VAT identification number (VAT ID) once VAT registration is completed. VAT registration is integrated into the same portal and is mandatory once the statutory turnover threshold is crossed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elective regime; the Single Person Company is mandatorily taxed as a separate juridical person under the Oman Income Tax Law. Optional VAT registration is available to taxable persons whose annual taxable supplies and expenses exceed the voluntary threshold, and no other classification elections are available to change the entity-level taxation character.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 per annum of aggregate taxable supplies and taxable expenses; Voluntary registration: OMR 100,000 per annum of taxable supplies or taxable expenses

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The export of specialty green coffee beans qualifies for the 0% VAT rate where the goods are physically exported from Oman within the prescribed time limits, title and risk pass to the non-resident buyer, and the supplier retains compelling export documentation. Supporting services that are directly connected to the export transport or performed for a non-resident recipient outside Oman may also be zero-rated. The zero-rating claim must be substantiated by official customs evidence and usual international trade documents proving that the goods left Oman and the sale is an export rather than an internal supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Phytosanitary Certificate (for green coffee beans, where required)
  • Proof of payment / bank transfer record from the overseas buyer

No real-time fiscal clearance, API-based clearance, or digital stamping mandate is currently operational in Oman. VAT-compliant invoices may be issued electronically or on paper, provided they include all statutory invoice data fields. The Oman Tax Authority has publicly announced phased introduction of mandatory e-invoicing; until the scheme becomes operative, there is no requirement for API integration or clearance of invoices through a central fiscal platform.

Zero-rated export transactions are subject to substantive review by the Oman Tax Authority, which may request all underlying export and payment documents. Failure to satisfy the documentation and substance requirements within the statutory period exposes the exporter to clawback of the 5% VAT, late penalty interest of up to 7% per annum, administrative fines of up to OMR 5,000, and, for fraudulent or deliberate misdeclaration, recovery of tax together with criminal penalties under the VAT Law and Omani Penal Code, including imprisonment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible on both current and capital account; Oman is an IMF Article VIII jurisdiction. No exchange-control permissions are required for equity injections, import settlements, or dividend repatriation to non-resident shareholders. Remittance hurdles are limited to bank-level corporate governance documents: a board resolution, audited financial statements, and a BOP reporting form signed by the authorised signatory. CBO retains statistical and suspicious-transaction reporting rights but does not restrict profit outflows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline applies to export earnings under Omani law. The SPC may retain green coffee export proceeds in OMR or any freely convertible currency; the commercial bank captures export receipts in CBO balance-of-payments statistics only. There are no mandatory conversion ratios for export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) and applicable Foreign Exchange Regulations, in coordination with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low. Oman has no licensing framework for foreign electronic-money institutions (EMIs); CBO restricts deposit-taking and payment-account operation to licensed commercial and Islamic banks. Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Revolut Business) cannot hold OMR or in-country accounts, cannot connect to the CBO RTGS system, and are not accepted as primary banking counterparties. They may operate only as an unregulated supplementary collection rail for overseas receivables, which must be disclosed to the principal Omani bank for AML/BOP transparency.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The sole shareholder executes an SWIFT MT103 remittance from the shareholder's own bank account into the SPC's OMR/USD operational account. The bank mandates the remitter name to match the shareholder name in the Memorandum of Association and the CR file; the payment narrative must cite the CR number and the purpose description 'Share Capital Injection' as defined in the bank's systems. The Omani bank verifies the receipt against the SPC's stated paid-up capital, obtains a Capital Verification Certificate from an accredited auditor, and reports the inflow to CBO under balance-of-payments statistics; no central-bank approval license is required.Two permissible clearing rails cover containerised specialty green coffee settlement: (i) direct SWIFT MT103 for cash-against-documents and cash-in-advance payments where the named beneficiary matches the trade contract and shipping documents; and (ii) an irrevocable Commercial Letter of Credit issued under UCP600 by a tier-1 Omani bank, requiring a compliant drawdown package including commercial invoice, full set of clean Bills of Lading, phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, insurance policy, and weight/quality certificate. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% under Oman domestic law. Oman does not impose a withholding tax on dividends or profit distributions made by an Omani resident company to its non-resident corporate shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

No later than 4 months after the end of the fiscal or tax year; a legal extension of no more than 2 months may be requested with justified cause before the initial deadline.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 Tax Identification Number (TIN) and Commercial Registration Number of the Omani Single Person Company. The foreign-owned SPC must register with the Oman Tax Authority before filing and enter the issued 12-digit Oman TIN; the foreign shareholder's home-country tax identification may be entered in Appendix A. Late filing triggers a fixed OMR 500 penalty. Late payment accrues a monthly surcharge of 3% of the outstanding tax per month or part-month, with a maximum aggregate surcharge of 100% of the tax due. Failing to attach audited financial statements or TP documentation adds a further OMR 1,000 penalty plus possible loss of loss carryforward. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000

Economic substance scope: Oman applies OECD-profiled substance requirements through its corporate tax law, the Common Reporting Standard, and the Multilateral Convention for tax treaty enforcement. There is no separate Economic Substance Regulations code as in the UAE, but a foreign-owned SPC trading specialty green coffee must show active management in Oman, a permanent office, physical inventory control, and genuine commercial contracts for import and distribution. A shell entity with no substance is not entitled to tax treaty benefits and may be re-attributed to the foreign shareholder, triggering Oman tax on the entire income and denial of loss carryforwards.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Tax Return

Statutory sanction: Under Sultanate of Oman tax legislation, tax evasion is punishable by imprisonment of up to 5 years and a fine of up to OMR 100,000. In addition, the Tax Authority will assess the omitted tax, impose the 3% monthly late-payment surcharge up to 100%, and may impose an additional penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax. Criminal referral to the Public Prosecution is automatic for evasion exceeding OMR 20,000.

Director personal liability: Directors and the sole shareholder of the SPC who authorise false filings are subjected to piercing of the corporate veil. They become personally jointly and severally liable for the full tax, surcharges, and penalties. The Tax Authority may obtain asset freezes, travel bans, and seizure orders against the managers and beneficial owner. Conviction may lead to imprisonment from 1 to 5 years and mandatory restitution of the tax loss.
Shell Company / Artificial Structure

Statutory sanction: The Oman Tax Authority exercises general anti-abuse and substance-over-form powers. It may disregard the SPC, tax income in the hands of the foreign beneficial owner at the full 15% rate, reject loss carryforwards, deny treaty benefits under the Principal Purpose Test, and impose the 3% monthly late-payment surcharge. The Commercial Registrar may nullify the CR if no genuine business is proven, and the entity may be struck off without a dissolution process, leaving unpaid tax liabilities attached to the transferred assets.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors, the foreign shareholder, and any shadow director may be treated as de facto partners. Civil restitution is imposed for the tax avoided, plus damages. Travel bans are issued under the enforcement law. If the scheme is used to launder funds or to evade customs duties, imprisonment of up to 7 years is possible.
Anti-Money Laundering / Trade-Based Money Laundering

Statutory sanction: Violation of Oman's Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016) carries imprisonment of up to 7 years and fines of up to OMR 50,000. The Financial Intelligence Department may order freezing of bank accounts, confiscation of goods, and forfeiture of the value of the laundered property. The competent court may order cancellation of the commercial registration and permanent exclusion from licensed import activity.

Director personal liability: Officers who approved or facilitated a money laundering transaction incur direct personal criminal liability if they failed to file the required suspicious transaction report. Directors may face disqualification, personal asset confiscation, and travel bans. If the company lacks sufficient assets, the directors and the beneficial owner are personally liable to the state for the confiscated amounts.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement

Statutory sanction: The Oman Tax Authority makes a transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length profit. The resulting additional tax is due with 3% monthly late-payment surcharge, and a penalty of up to 10% of the adjustment is imposed if the taxpayer failed to file TP documentation. Consistent failure can lead to higher criminal fines and disclosure to treaty partners under information exchange.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve the intercompany pricing, sign the TP declaration, or conceal the related-party relationship can be personally assessed when the company is insolvent. They may be prohibited from leaving Oman until the tax debt is settled, and continued non-payment is converted to a criminal fraud charge.
