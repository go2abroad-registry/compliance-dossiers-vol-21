Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) - Directorate General of Commerce, Commercial Registration Department

Governing statute

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 (Oman Companies Law), Part II, Articles 7-13 (Single Person Company); Royal Decree No. 50/2019 (Foreign Capital Investment Law), Article 3 (100% foreign ownership); Commercial Registration Law, Royal Decree No. 3/1974, as amended

Formation / constitutional filings

Application for Commercial Registration submitted through the Invest Easy e-Portal (investeasy.gov.om), attaching the authenticated Arabic Memorandum and Articles of Association

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 34 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; however, the subscribed capital must be fully committed by the sole shareholder, deposited into an Oman-licensed bank account, and evidenced by a bank capital certificate prior to the issuance of the Commercial Registration certificate.

Funding & unlock path: The building owner must hold a valid civil defense safety certificate (fire alarms, extinguishers, and evacuation systems) issued by the Royal Oman Police - General Directorate of Civil Defense before the municipal license is approved. As tenant, the SPC must complete the workplace safety checklist and occupational health registration under the Labour Law (Royal Decree No. 35/2003) and the occupational safety and health rules of the Ministry of Labour. If the SPC employs ten or more staff, it must appoint a dedicated health and safety officer, and the premises become subject to annual Civil Defense inspection.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; however, the subscribed capital must be fully committed by the sole shareholder, deposited into an Oman-licensed bank account, and evidenced by a bank capital certificate prior to the issuance of the Commercial Registration certificate.

Pro-Forma First Board Resolution Draft
RESOLVED, that the Sole Shareholder subscribes to and takes up the entire issued share capital and commits it to be deposited in the SPC's bank account with a Central Bank of Oman-licensed bank before or upon Commercial Registration issuance. FURTHER RESOLVED, that the registered office be fixed at the premises described in the Memorandum and Articles of Association, Muscat Governorate; such office may be relocated within Oman by later resolution. FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Founder be appointed Chief Executive Officer and General Manager with authority to sign bank mandates, appoint licensed auditors, and complete municipal, chamber, tax, and civil defense registrations on the SPC's behalf.
Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Exempted / Not Required

Not applicable - the MCIIP Directorate General of Industry issues industrial licenses exclusively for manufacturing and processing ISIC sectors; the customer helpdesk and conversational bot cloud activity is classified as a pure service and falls entirely outside the mandatory industrial licensing regime.

Category: None required - the SPC operates under the Commercial Registration certificate combined with the municipal business premises license (Baladiya license); no manufacturing, industrial operating, or sectoral permit is mandated for this helpdesk SaaS activity.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), also referenced as the Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman, operating under the Ministry of Finance; primary portal: O-Tax eServices (https://otax.tax.gov.om) and the OTA corporate website (https://tax.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, then submit an online tax registration application through the OTA portal using a certified tax agent or accountant; the OTA issues a single Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering corporate income tax and VAT, while non-resident suppliers of electronic services must complete simplified VAT registration before making taxable supplies in Oman and every commercial invoice must display the VAT registration number

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (Omani resident juridical persons are taxed as separate taxable persons; a Single Person Company is not a pass-through entity, and its profits are subject to Omani corporate income tax before distributions; Oman applies territorial-source rules to non-resident investors but a resident SPC is assessed on Omani-source income and on foreign-source income only when it is attributable to a permanent establishment in Oman or remitted to the SPC's Omani bank account) — A foreign-owned Single Person Company must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, then submit an online tax registration application through the OTA portal using a certified tax agent or accountant; the OTA issues a single Tax Identification Number (TIN) covering corporate income tax and VAT, while non-resident suppliers of electronic services must complete simplified VAT registration before making taxable supplies in Oman and every commercial invoice must display the VAT registration number

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (Omani resident juridical persons are taxed as separate taxable persons; a Single Person Company is not a pass-through entity, and its profits are subject to Omani corporate income tax before distributions; Oman applies territorial-source rules to non-resident investors but a resident SPC is assessed on Omani-source income and on foreign-source income only when it is attributable to a permanent establishment in Oman or remitted to the SPC's Omani bank account)

Available election forms

Oman does not allow a US-style check-the-box election or disregarded-entity classification for a Single Person Company; taxation at entity level is mandatory. VAT grouping election is available under the VAT Law to qualifying resident entities with close financial, economic and organizational links, subject to OTA approval; hybrid mismatch accounting treatment is constrained by Oman's domestic legislation and any applicable tax treaty, and no separate elective regime is available for classification of an SPC as a transparent vehicle.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,000 annual taxable supplies for mandatory VAT registration of resident businesses and non-resident electronic-service suppliers; a voluntary registration threshold of OMR 19,000 applies to resident suppliers; mandatory registration is triggered on the date the value of taxable supplies, imports and cross-border services supplied in the Sultanate exceeds the OMR 38,000 threshold over the preceding 12 months or is expected to be exceeded in the following 12 months

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export zero-rating is available to a registered Omani supplier that proves the physical export of goods outside Oman and the GCC Common Market within the period set by VAT law, and for intra-GCC transfers to a final destination outside the GCC where title passes to a non-resident; services supplied to a non-resident recipient qualify for zero-rating only where the recipient is outside Oman, the services are not effectively used in Oman, and the supplier retains standard evidence of the recipient's non-resident status; exported goods must be removed from Oman within 90 days from the date of supply unless an extension is obtained; goods or services must be invoiced by the registered supplier and export proceeds must be received through a licensed Omani bank or authorized payment channel

  • Original customs export declaration (SAB) signed and stamped by Royal Oman Police Customs
  • Customs release notification or electronic customs acknowledgement reflecting the Omani export entry
  • Full commercial invoice issued by the Omani SPC referencing its VAT registration number and the zero-rating basis
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or CMR road consignment note evidencing departure from Oman and destination outside the GCC
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice showing receipt of foreign currency export proceeds from the non-resident purchaser
  • Sea or air cargo manifest copy where applicable, together with any certificate of origin or end-use declaration required by OTA.

Oman mandates structured electronic VAT invoices for all VAT-registered suppliers, including XML or PDF with embedded XML, machine-readable QR codes containing the TIN, invoice amount and VAT amount, and integration with the OTA's electronic fiscal systems; OTA is implementing a phased continuous transaction control (CTC) model under which approved billing software or an API connector clears invoices in real time for designated taxpayer segments; digital stamping of invoices is not currently a general requirement, but OTA may require transmission of invoice metadata for risk-assessment and refund-validation purposes.

The OTA may retrospectively claw back zero-rating where the export evidence is incomplete, goods are diverted to the Omani domestic market, or proceeds are not repatriated within the applicable deadlines; penalties apply under the Tax Procedures Law and VAT Law, generally including a fine for failure to provide records, interest on unpaid tax, and an additional penalty of up to 100% of the tax shortfall where the wrongful zero-rating is attributable to fraud or gross negligence; knowingly submitting false export documents may constitute VAT evasion, rendering the responsible person liable to criminal prosecution, imprisonment and fines in addition to payment of all undercharged VAT plus late payment penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman operates a liberalized and fully convertible exchange control regime with no requirement for CBO approval on current or capital account transactions. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the United States Dollar (USD) at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (0.3845 OMR per USD) under the fixed-exchange-rate architecture in force since 1986; there is no dual-tier market, no surrender requirement, and no differential taxation between residents and non-residents on repatriation flows. For a 100-percent foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC), profit and dividend remittance is unrestricted once the Oman Tax Authority corporate tax (15% standard, with applicable special economic zone incentives at 0%-3% in Sohar/Salalah/FREE zones) and Zakat/end-of-service levy obligations are settled, and provided the SPC maintains audited financial statements evidencing distributable reserves. Capital repatriation on liquidation or share disposal is equally unconstrained subject to settlement of all statutory liabilities and CBO-mandated debanking reporting via the licensed settlement bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

The CBO imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation freeze on export or service receipts for a USD-pegged Rial economy. Under CBO Circular BM 1043 and the unified GCC banking guidelines, a service exporter, including a helpdesk/SaaS operator, must credit all export and cross-border service proceeds to a designated account opened with a CBO-licensed commercial bank within six (6) months from the earlier of the invoice date or the completion-of-service milestone. Settlement proceeds may be retained in USD, EUR, or any freely tradable currency at the exporter's discretion without a mandatory conversion to OMR. The SPC remains obliged to maintain a documentary trail (commercial invoices, SWIFT payment references, signed SaaS subscription agreements, and support-ticket delivery evidence) for the annual KYC update and the bank's CBO statistical reporting regime.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), Foreign Exchange and Monetary Operations Department, in coordination with the National Centre for Financial Information (Omani Financial Intelligence Unit) and the Capital Market Authority for securities-related treasury instruments; the CBO acts as the sole licensing authority for commercial banking, money exchange, and payment service provider activities in the Sultanate.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000; first OMR 30,000 of taxable income is tax-exempt (small-business band). Domestic Minimum Top-up Tax of 15% applies from 1 January 2025 only to in-scope MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ USD 750 million.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman does not levy withholding tax on dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders (dividends are excluded from the taxable income of the recipient), and consequently no DTA dividend rate is applicable; the Omani payer's obligation is limited to filing the annual dividend distribution register.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months of the fiscal year-end (for a 31 December year-end: by 30 April of the following year); the OTA may grant an extension upon application; tax payment is due on the same date as the filing deadline.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 50,000 (aggregate annual related-party transaction value): lease a TP disclosure schedule in the annual CIT return; Local File is required when such transactions exceed OMR 500,000; Master File is required for MNE groups with consolidated global revenue ≥ OMR 30 million (approximately USD 78 million); CbCR applies to groups with consolidated revenue ≥ OMR 330 million (approximately USD 857 million). All related-party transactions — irrespective of amount — must comply with the arm's-length principle.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime; however, tax residency requires either incorporation in Oman or central management and control in Oman, and the OTA may disregard any arrangement or entity lacking commercial substance under the general anti-avoidance rule (Article 91, Royal Decree 9/2017). A helpdesk SPC must maintain a genuine local office, employee payroll, bank accounts, board minutes and supporting contracts in Oman; the beneficial-ownership register (Commercial Companies Law, RD 18/2019, as amended) is enforced by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Wilful Understatement

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years and/or a fine up to OMR 25,000; civil assessment of the evaded tax plus a 1% monthly late-payment surcharge (capped at 25%) and interest accrual.

Director personal liability: Where board members/SPC owner direct or participate in the fraud, the Oman courts may pierce the corporate veil: directors are personally and jointly liable for the company's tax debts, restitution to the OTA, and confiscation of assets; travel bans and freezing orders may be issued by the enforcement court.
Shell Company / Sham Arrangement

Statutory sanction: GAAR (Article 91, RD 9/2017) allows the OTA to recharacterize or ignore the entity/transactions and to impose cumulative penalties up to 100% of the underpaid tax (plus the 1%/month surcharge capped at 25%); the commercial registration may be suspended or canceled.

Director personal liability: Under RD 18/2019 and the 2022 Beneficial Ownership Regulations, the resident director/beneficial owner may be fined, and directors who willfully present false substance filings or cause the company to trade without real Omani management are personally liable for resulting tax and company debts; serious cases trigger criminal prosecution.
AML / Sanctions / KYC Failure

Statutory sanction: Penalties under the AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016) and the Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) regulations: fines up to OMR 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 10 years; ancillary orders include license revocation and asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers face separate fines of up to OMR 10,000, disqualification from management, and travel bans; the Central Bank of Oman may also blacklist the pay-through rails and cancel the entity's banking facilities.
Withholding Tax Default

Statutory sanction: The unpaid WHT is immediately recoverable from the SPC together with a monthly 1% penalty (cap 25%); prosecution of the tax-deducting officer is possible under Articles 129–130 of RD 9/2017.

Director personal liability: The resident manager/shareholder of the SPC is jointly and severally liable for the unrecovered WHT where he or she knew or should have known of the remittance failure; persistent default leads to a personal attachment, travel ban, and criminal fines up to OMR 25,000.
