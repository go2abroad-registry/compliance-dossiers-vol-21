Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; official electronic services portal: taxoman.gov.om. Customs-related excise and import/export procedures are integrated with Oman Customs through the national Bayan electronic clearance system.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion and obtain the Commercial Registration Certificate. Step 2: File the tax registration application through the OTA Tax E-Services portal using the Commercial Registration number, constitutional documents, shareholder and beneficial owner identification, board resolutions, Oman bank account details, and the bonded-transport license issued by the competent transport and customs authorities. Step 3: Receive the OTA Tax Registration Number and Tax Card. Step 4: If taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold, submit a separate VAT registration application to obtain the VAT Registration Number. Non-resident shareholders may own the SPC 100% and are not required to be Oman tax residents; a non-resident person deriving Omani-source income through a permanent establishment in Oman must separately register for Oman income tax in respect of that permanent establishment.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election and no hybrid mismatch or transparent-entity election is available. An Omani Single Person Company is a resident juridical person taxable at entity level on its worldwide income, with an available foreign tax credit for foreign-source income. Statutory elections are limited to administrative claims such as the method of tax depreciation with OTA approval, loss utilization within statutory carryforward rules, and treaty/general anti-avoidance positions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 500,000 in annual taxable supplies; voluntary VAT registration: OMR 150,000 in annual taxable supplies or as otherwise prescribed in the VAT Law and Executive Regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to goods physically exported outside Oman under a valid customs export declaration; to international transport of goods that begins in Oman and ends outside Oman, including the bonded road feeder service performed as part of a through international movement; to services supplied directly in connection with exported goods where the services are ancillary to the international carriage; and to supplies made to or from approved free zones/customs bonded zones where title, risk, and transport evidence demonstrate the goods have left Oman. The bonded truck fleet must document the foreign consignee, foreign destination, and actual border exit of the goods.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Oman Bayan customs export declaration and approved exit manifest)
  • CMR consignment note under the Convention on International Transport of Goods and/or Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for the underlying international movement
  • Inward Remittance Certificate and foreign-currency bank wire evidence from the non-resident counterparty
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and transport operator invoice
  • Bonded movement authorization and customs transit guarantee records covering the trucked land leg to the exit seaport, airport, or border crossing
  • Electronic border exit confirmation and evidence of arrival at the foreign destination from Oman Customs and the destination customs records

Oman does not currently impose a comprehensive real-time fiscal clearance and digital stamping mandate for all B2B tax invoices; however, the OTA requires VAT e-filing through the Tax E-Services portal and accepts electronic tax invoices meeting the VAT Law substantive requirements. Bonded truck and airport-feeder operations must be electronically supported by Oman Customs Bayan transit/bond records, electronic CMR or equivalent consignment documentation, and departure/exit confirmations before export zero-rating may be substantiated.

Oman Tax Authority and Oman Customs may cross-match export declarations, CMR/transport logs, bonded transit records, and inbound FX remittance evidence during audit. Defective substantiation results in retrospective disallowance of the zero-rating, assessment of VAT at 5% on the full supply value, statutory late-payment/additional tax, and administrative fines per violation under the VAT Law. Intentional or fraudulent zero-rating claims may trigger criminal prosecution for tax evasion, enhanced monetary penalties, and imprisonment; the OTA may raise retrospective assessments and claw back improperly zero-rated outputs within the statutory limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible and pegged to the US Dollar at a fixed rate of OMR 1 = USD 2.6008. Oman has no general foreign exchange control regime; both current account and capital account transactions are treated as free from licensing or prior CBO approval, subject to commercial banks' anti-money laundering and know-your-customer verification requirements. Foreign profits, dividends, capital proceeds, and liquidation balances are freely remittable upon submission of audited financial statements and tax clearance certificates where required. No foreign investment profits retention obligation applies to wholly foreign-owned SPCs operating bonded logistics services.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender timelines or mandatory conversion ratios in Oman. Exporters, including customs-bonded trucking operators providing international road feeder services, are permitted to retain foreign currency receipts in their domestic bank accounts or offshore accounts, and remittance to creditors abroad does not require CBO approval. Banks only require underlying supporting documentation such as customs declarations, airway bills, transport contracts, and commercial invoices for routing verification and statistical reporting.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting as the sole monetary authority and foreign exchange administration under the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 114/2000, as amended)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Low. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) are not licensed as deposit-taking institutions by the Central Bank of Oman, and their ability to maintain settlement accounts, clear currency, and execute corporate disbursements for an Omani customs-bonded trucking entity is materially restricted. CBO-licensed payment service providers operate only within the local financial market and typically fall short of meeting the documentary and correspondent banking requirements of the airport logistics corridor. For international wire clearing, a tier-1 Omani commercial bank with SWIFT capability is the only fully compliant and operationally viable channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital for the customs-bonded trucking SPC is routed via international SWIFT credit to the account named in the shareholding agreement, using purpose code 'G/GS' (goods) or 'P/I' (direct investment) as instructed by the receiving local bank. The remitter must identify the SPC's IBAN and include a clearing reference linking to the Commercial Registry number and share capital minute. The receiving bank verifies the remitter's identity, abstracts the source of funds, and files the transaction in its CBO electronic reporting system. In the absence of Omani exchange controls, no prior CBO permit is required, but the bank will require the share subscription form, bank memorandum, and a signed declaration that the funds represent a contribution of share capital or shareholder loan, free of money-laundering concern.Trade disbursements from the SPC's operational account are executed primarily through SWIFT MT103 for direct wire payments to foreign suppliers, fuel vendors, and insurance providers, with proper purpose codes and underlying transport documentation archived for bank audit. For high-value truck procurement and maintenance spare-part imports, commercial letters of credit opened by the Omani issuing bank through its correspondent network provide the most reliable and compliant clearing rail. Domestic payments for local wages, tolls, and airport handling charges use the local Real Time Gross Settlement (RTGS) mechanism and ACH direct debits, while USD-denominated airline settlement flights are cleared via correspondent banking chains in the United States only when the counterparty mandates it, with the Omani bank performing sanctions screening and OFAC compliance.USD/OMR outright forwards between the SPC and an Omani licensed bankUSD/OMR foreign exchange swapsCross-currency swap contracts for non-USD sources, subject to CBO acceptable derivative practicePlain-vanilla currency options with a licensed Omani bank when expressly permitted under the bank's treasury product eligibility

Box 5 Taxable income reconciliation and loss utilisation: add non-deductible provisions, fines and entertainment; subtract tax-exempt income (free-zone profit) and qualifying capital allowances. Late filing: OMR 5 per day for each day of delay, subject to a maximum of OMR 500; late payment: an additional 1% per month on the outstanding tax, capped at 100% of the tax; failure to submit audited financials: OMR 5,000; fraudulent understatement: additional 25% of tax and criminal liability.
Box 6 Computation of tax due: apply 15% statutory rate; deduct instalment payments already remitted under previous installments; state balance due or refund. Non-resident shareholders cannot offset personal losses against corporate tax; if parent operates branch, ensure P.E. threshold. Late filing: OMR 5 per day for each day of delay, subject to a maximum of OMR 500; late payment: an additional 1% per month on the outstanding tax, capped at 100% of the tax; failure to submit audited financials: OMR 5,000; fraudulent understatement: additional 25% of tax and criminal liability.
Box 7 Auditor certification and certification of the preparer (with CPA licence). Mandatory for entities whose paid-up capital or annual turnover exceeds OMR 150,000. Audited financial statements must be consolidated into parent's report if applicable; submit with return where threshold met. Late filing: OMR 5 per day for each day of delay, subject to a maximum of OMR 500; late payment: an additional 1% per month on the outstanding tax, capped at 100% of the tax; failure to submit audited financials: OMR 5,000; fraudulent understatement: additional 25% of tax and criminal liability.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when total related-party transactions in a tax year exceed OMR 10 million; Master File: when the Omani entity belongs to an MNE group with consolidated group revenue above OMR 100 million; CbCR: group revenue over OMR 100 million. Transactions below these thresholds still require a contemporaneous gross-margin analysis to substantiate arm's length pricing.

Economic substance scope: Oman applies a substance-over-form doctrine and a GAAR in the Income Tax Law. Although Oman has not enacted UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, an SPC must have real operational substance in Oman (physical fleet, drivers, maintenance facility, contracts and management control) to access treaty benefits and avoid recharacterization as a conduit. For customs bonded trucking, holding the Oman Customs license and operating the bonded fleet in Oman constitute relevant economic substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Additional tax of up to 100% of evaded tax; fine of OMR 5,000 to OMR 25,000; court-ordered closure of business for up to 1 year.

Director personal liability: Director and manager jointly and severally liable to restore shortfall; civil asset freeze; personal travel ban; criminal imprisonment up to 10 years under Oman Penal Law.
Shell Company / Abuse of Treaty

Statutory sanction: GAAR uplift; denial of treaty relief; recharacterization of payments as dividends; fine up to 50% of tax understated; mandatory rectification.

Director personal liability: Directors may be named in tax assessment and held personally liable for 25% of unpaid tax if the structure is found to be created for tax avoidance; revocation of commercial license.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Primary adjustment plus secondary adjustment (deemed dividend); penalty of 5% of adjusted income or 10% of additional tax; interest at 1% per month from due date.

Director personal liability: Registered director must sign contemporaneous TP file; failure to maintain documentation exposes the director to personal fine of OMR 10,000 and possible suspension.
Anti-Money Laundering / Escalation to Sanctions

Statutory sanction: Fine OMR 50,000 up to OMR 500,000; seizure of trucks; withdrawal of customs bonded warehouse license; criminal confiscation of profit by court order.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal term of imprisonment up to 7 years; personal asset confiscation; ban from managing any company in Oman; Omani border travel ban.
