Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under the 2019 SPC regime; however, the declared capital must be adequate for the licensed business activity, and for a Class III medical-device manufacturing undertaking a fully paid declared capital of OMR 150,000 is recommended and must be evidenced by an escrowed bank certificate or audited deposit confirmation in an Omani bank account.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate issued by the General Directorate of Civil Defense (Royal Oman Police) following inspection of fire-suppression systems, emergency exits, and hazardous-material storage; structural-load inspection certificate from an MOCIIP-recognized engineering office confirming the floor capacity for laser equipment; occupational clearance certified by the Ministry of Labour and a licensed occupational-health physician, incorporating laser-safety eyewear regimens, controlled-access signage, and compliance with Oman's occupational exposure limits for airborne particulates and biohazard wastes.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital under the 2019 SPC regime; however, the declared capital must be adequate for the licensed business activity, and for a Class III medical-device manufacturing undertaking a fully paid declared capital of OMR 150,000 is recommended and must be evidenced by an escrowed bank certificate or audited deposit confirmation in an Omani bank account.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (formerly the Secretariat General for Taxation), with the Taxoman e-services portal at taxoman.om and official information published at tax.gov.om.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (OM_SPC) must first complete commercial registration with the Oman Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, obtain the Certificate of Incorporation and the Commercial Register (CR) record, then register with the Tax Authority to receive the unified Tax Registration Number (TRN). The same TRN serves for income tax and VAT purposes. Required documentation includes the attested constitutional documents, CR certificate, registered office lease or title deed, board/shareholder resolution appointing the tax representative, power of attorney granted to a licensed tax agent where relevant, and evidence of existing or intended Omani bank account for the SPC. Non-resident signatories must provide passport copies and proof of registered address; registration must be completed before commencing taxable operations or incurring the first taxable supply.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman's legal framework does not provide a check-the-box pass-through election; all foreign-owned resident SPCs are automatically taxable persons under the Income Tax Law and are treated as separate entities. The SPC may elect its annual accounting reference date with prior notification to the Tax Authority, may apply for VAT group registration with related resident taxable persons if control and 95% ownership thresholds are satisfied, and may select voluntary VAT registration when turnover exceeds the OMR 250,000 voluntary threshold. Hybrid mismatch elections are unavailable under Omani tax law, and no entity classification election is offered to resident SPCs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration is required where annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000 (five hundred thousand Omani Riyals); voluntary registration is available where annual taxable supplies exceed OMR 250,000 (two hundred fifty thousand Omani Riyals). Non-resident persons making taxable supplies in Oman are subject to mandatory registration without regard to the threshold when a reverse charge applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under the Omani VAT Executive Regulations applies to tangible goods physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the Sultanate, including goods removed to other GCC states for use or consumption outside Oman, where the goods are dispatched within the statutory period to a customer located outside Oman, the SPC holds title and risk during export, the transportation documents are consistent with the SPC's shipment records, and the export is evidenced by the customs export declaration in the SPC's name. Export related to the coronary drug-eluting stent business must satisfy medical device export licensing requirements and the relevant harmonized System classification (HS 9021 for coronary stents or HS 3006 for sterile medical products, as applicable). Offshore medical consulting, research quality data services, and prototype validation services are zero-rated only when rendered directly to a non-resident recipient that is not present in Oman at the time of supply, the benefit is enjoyed entirely outside Oman, and the supply is not connected with Omani real property or goods situated in Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill endorsed to the Omani SPC
  • Commercial invoice expressly annotated as zero-rated VAT export and reflecting correct HS codes
  • Packing list, weight/freight certifications, and shipping certificate
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank foreign exchange settlement proof issued by an Omani bank
  • Certificate of origin and valid Oman medical device export permit for Class III stents
  • Destination customs entry or port delivery receipt proving arrival in the overseas market
  • Customs seal logs, container tracking records, and pre-shipment inspection report

Oman has a national e-invoicing and fiscal clearance mandate requiring registered taxpayers to issue machine-readable XML or JSON invoices, signed with a digital certificate, containing a unique invoice UUID and a cryptographic QR code generated from the Tax Authority's defined fiscal fields. Invoices must pass through real-time validation and clearance via the Tax Authority's national e-invoicing hub or an approved service provider before the legally valid version is issued to the buyer. The regime applies to B2B and B2C invoices, credit and debit notes, and requires simultaneous electronic archiving for the full VAT record retention period. The SPC must also integrate its accounting, ERP, and customs documentation channels with the e-invoicing API, and must ensure that zero-rated export invoices are cleared under the same mandatory digital stamping process.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to a demanding documentary continuity audit; if the SPC fails to produce a complete bundle at audit time, the Tax Authority may treat the supply as taxable at 5%, assess the unpaid VAT, and impose administrative penalties equal to the tax under-declared or failed to declare, with additional late payment charges. Materially false declarations, forged Bayan or shipping documents, fictitious recipient arrangements, or collusive diversion of goods to the Omani market constitute tax evasion and attract lawful penalties of up to OMR 10,000, imprisonment for responsible officers, suspension of the tax agent, seizure of goods, and mandatory clawback of any VAT refund received with statutory interest. Foreign exchange proceeds that remain unrepatriated beyond the 180-day deadline without a documented commercial or currency-control justification will result in reversal of the zero-rating benefit, disallowance of the export receivable, and may trigger exchange control penalties under CBO requirements.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains full current account convertibility and operational capital account convertibility. The Omani Rial is freely usable for trade and remittance purposes; there are no exchange controls on profit repatriation for a foreign-owned SPC. Profit remittance requires audited financial statements, board approval, tax clearance, and standard corporate documentation. Capital account flows such as foreign equity injection are permitted without prior CBO licensing as long as the funds enter through the formal banking system and comply with CBO reporting. For a Class III medical device manufacturer, no sector-specific currency restriction applies. Practical liquidity concerns relate to correspondent bank due diligence rather than convertibility.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory export proceeds surrender or conversion ratios. Export receipts may be retained in foreign currency indefinitely. However, the SPC must maintain full outward remittance reporting and inward remittance documentation for CBO balance-of-payments and AML recordkeeping. All statutory financial statements must be in OMR, but bank accounts may hold USD/EUR. Export proceeds must arrive via formal banking channels and be supported by customs declarations and sales contracts.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) exercising oversight under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Foreign Exchange Business Law (RD 38/2000), and the CBO Payment Systems and AML/CFT regulatory framework.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility as primary treasury rail. Oman does not issue standalone cross-border EMI licences to non-bank fintechs; electronic money and payment services are confined to licensed banks and CBO-supervised payment service providers. Offshore digital EMIs cannot open onshore OMR settlement accounts. They may act only as auxiliary collection channels through Omani licensed banks, but this adds compliance complexity and is not recommended as the SPC's principal clearing route.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (fixed rate for juristic persons under the Corporate Income Tax Law, Royal Decree 59/2023, applicable to tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2024; transitional rules applied 3% on taxable income up to OMR 30,000 and 15% above that under prior law RD 28/2009).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% gross, imposed on dividends distributed by the Omani SPC to non-resident shareholders; dividends to Oman-resident corporate shareholders are excluded from withholding.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

CIT return must be filed and full tax paid within 4 months after the end of the fiscal year; the SPC's fiscal year normally coincides with the calendar year, and a different financial year requires prior OTA approval.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory where aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 300,000 in a tax year; Master File: required for members of an MNE group exceeding the executive-regulation turnover threshold (commonly OMR 300 million); Country-by-Country Report: required for MNE groups with consolidated revenue ≥ OMR 300 million; documentation must be contemporaneous and submitted within 30 days of an OTA request.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but RD 59/2023, the general anti-abuse rule, and Omani treaty jurisprudence require genuine economic substance: local strategic and operational decision-making, full-time employees, physical manufacturing/QC facilities, board meetings held in Oman, and local bank accounts. The SPC must also satisfy operational substance for Class III medical devices through a valid MOH establishment license, a functioning quality management system, and complete batch records. A shell SPC used as a treaty-shopping conduit is exposed to GAAR recharacterization, denial of treaty benefits under the Principal Purpose Test, and reclassification of assets and profit as belonging to the foreign parent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Reporting

Statutory sanction: Best-judgment revised assessment; financial penalty of 100% of evaded tax plus 1% monthly late-payment surcharge; criminal fine up to OMR 50,000; revocation of the medical device manufacturing establishment license.

Director personal liability: The SPC manager and de facto directors face joint and several liability for restored tax and penalties where company funds were misappropriated or books were falsified; conviction carries imprisonment up to 5 years, travel bans, and confiscation of personal assets in Oman.
Transfer Pricing Mispricing on Related-Party Flows

Statutory sanction: Adjustment of taxable income to arm's length price; recomputation of 10% WHT on excess royalty payments; penalty up to 50% of the additional tax; separate documentation penalties of OMR 3,000–10,000 for missing or defective Local/Master Files.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly executed non-arm's length agreements may be joined in recovery proceedings for the TP adjustment; false supporting documentation triggers criminal fraud referral and personal fines up to OMR 20,000.
Shell Company / Substance Deficiency (Treaty-Shopping Conduit)

Statutory sanction: Disallowance of treaty benefits under the Principal Purpose Test; reinstatement of full 10% WHT and 15% CIT on restructured income; GAAR adjustment with a 100% penalty on the additional assessment and forfeiture of loss carryforwards.

Director personal liability: The legal representative who signs CIT declarations with knowledge of the shell arrangement is exposed to prosecution for false statements; courts may freeze director accounts, order restitution to the Treasury, and impose professional disqualification from Omani board positions.
Withholding Tax Default on Dividends / Royalties / Interest

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the deferred WHT from the SPC as a statutory charge; 1% monthly late-payment surcharge; administrative fine of OMR 1,000 plus OMR 500 per month until compliance; suspension of OTax filing and Commercial Registration renewal.

Director personal liability: The SPC manager who ordered the payment without WHT is jointly and severally liable for the uncollected tax, with a personal fine up to OMR 5,000 and imprisonment up to 3 years for a knowing and continued default.
AML / High-Risk MedTech Payment-Rail Abuse

Statutory sanction: Freezing of SPC bank accounts; civil forfeiture of suspect funds; fines up to OMR 100,000 under the Omani AML Law and Customer Due Diligence rules; revocation of Commercial Registration; enhanced intrusive CIT audit of unexplained credits.

Director personal liability: Money laundering convictions impose imprisonment of 3 to 10 years on the manager and compliance officer; directors face lifetime bans from managing licensed entities in Oman, personal travel bans, and confiscation of assets acquired with suspect funds.
