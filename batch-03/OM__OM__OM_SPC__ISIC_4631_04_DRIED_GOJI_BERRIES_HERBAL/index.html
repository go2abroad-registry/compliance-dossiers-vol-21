Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General of Taxation (SGT), Ministry of Finance, Sultanate of Oman; official national portal: Oman Tax Portal (https://taxes.gov.om)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Omani SPC the following sequence applies: (1) Obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) after notarizing the Notarial Deed and Articles of Association; (2) Register on the Oman Tax Portal and validate the CR; (3) File the electronic tax registration application, selecting 'juridical person (Single Person Company)' as the entity class and 'wholesale trade of herbal botanicals / ISIC 4631' as the activity; (4) Upload supporting documents: CR certificate, constitutional documents, registered-office address proof, passport and residency documentation of the sole foreign shareholder, appointment documents of the manager and auditor, and a power of attorney to a licensed tax representative, if any; (5) The SGT issues the official Tax Identification Number (TIN) for income tax and, where the OMR 38,500 VAT registration threshold is met or expected to be met, a separate VAT Registration Number (VRN); (6) Activate e-filing credentials and the e-invoicing connection. Typical processing time is 5-15 business days from submission of a complete file; late registration subjects the SPC to fixed administrative fines under the VAT and income tax laws.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - The Omani SPC is a resident juridical person and a separate taxable entity under Royal Decree No. 56/2023 (Oman Income Tax Law, effective for tax periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024). It is taxed at entity level on worldwide taxable profits; the sole foreign shareholder is not assessed on the SPC's profits at the shareholder level in Oman. Oman does not provide partnership pass-through or subchapter-S style classification; there is no US-style check-the-box election. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The Omani SPC is a resident juridical person and a separate taxable entity under Royal Decree No. 56/2023 (Oman Income Tax Law, effective for tax periods beginning on or after 1 January 2024). It is taxed at entity level on worldwide taxable profits; the sole foreign shareholder is not assessed on the SPC's profits at the shareholder level in Oman. Oman does not provide partnership pass-through or subchapter-S style classification; there is no US-style check-the-box election. Small-enterprise tax relief and the standard Omani corporate tax rate apply based on taxable income, and dividends distributed to a non-resident shareholder are generally free of Omani withholding tax.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election is recognized by Omani law; tax classification follows the Commercial Companies Law. Available statutory elections include: (i) adoption of a fiscal year different from the calendar year with SGT approval; (ii) utilization of carried-forward tax losses within the statutory five-year window; (iii) VAT group registration election for related Omani entities satisfying the 100% ownership and control conditions; (iv) voluntary VAT registration election at the OMR 19,250 threshold; (v) advance pricing arrangement and mutual agreement procedure applications; and (vi) depreciation and amortization policy elections in the CIT return. No hybrid-mismatch entity election is currently available in Oman.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies and imports measured over the preceding 12 months or expected over the next 12 months; Voluntary VAT registration: OMR 19,250

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to supplies of goods, including dried goji berries and herbal botanicals, that are physically exported outside the GCC territory. Conditions: (i) the goods are sold to a customer outside the GCC; (ii) the goods are dispatched and leave Oman within 90 days from the date of supply/invoice; (iii) the export movement is evidenced by an electronic customs export declaration; (iv) the exporter retains transport and delivery documentation such as a Bill of Lading showing an Omani load port and a non-GCC destination; and (v) the export proceeds are received through an Omani licensed bank and repatriated pursuant to Central Bank of Oman exchange-control deadlines. Input VAT incurred on purchases attributable to zero-rated exports is creditable and recoverable through the VAT return and refund process.

  • Oman electronic customs export declaration (Arabian Gulf Customs System / AFAL clearance record)
  • Commercial invoice and zero-rated VAT tax invoice issued in accordance with the Oman VAT Law
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Oman to a non-GCC destination, with container and seal numbers
  • Packing list and certificate of origin for the dried goji berry / herbal botanical consignment
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 credit advice from an Omani licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency

Oman is implementing a nationally mandated e-invoicing regime through the SGT digital infrastructure. Taxable persons must issue structured electronic VAT invoices (JSON/XML) through SGT-approved, API-connected invoicing systems; real-time fiscal pre-clearance and digital stamping are being phased in, with initial mandatory phases applying to large taxpayers. Every tax invoice must contain the mandated line-item data fields, the seller's and buyer's VAT registration numbers, the applicable tax rates, and a QR code. Invoices not compliant with the e-invoicing format may be denied as evidence for input VAT deduction and expose the issuer to administrative penalties.

The SGT and the Royal Oman Police Customs Directorate jointly audit export supply chains through AFAL trade-data matching, banking records, VAT returns, and corporate filings. If goods do not physically leave Oman within 90 days, or if required documentation is missing or false, the zero-rating is reversed and VAT is assessed at 5% on the supply together with statutory late-payment penalties and administrative fines. Deliberate fraudulent zero-rating, including fictitious export documents, circular shipments, or false declarations of destination, constitutes tax evasion and may lead to clawback of any VAT refunds paid, criminal fines, and imprisonment in serious cases. Exporters should also expect periodic SGT audits requiring submission of the customs bundle, transport contracts, insurance certificates, and bank credit advices to substantiate each zero-rated export consignment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible on both current account and capital account. The OMR is pegged at a fixed central rate of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (1 USD = 0.3845 OMR) with only a narrow dealing spread, so no material FX conversion risk attaches to USD-priced goji berry export receivables. CBO imposes no exchange-control licence, prior approval, or repatriation permit for equity injections, shareholder loans, dividend distributions, profit remittances, or supplier payments. Profit-remittance hurdles are purely documentary: the remitting bank verifies the SPC's valid Commercial Registration, Tax Card and tax good-standing, audited or management-account evidence of distributable profit, and a clear purpose narrative in the SWIFT instruction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender, conversion timeline, or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export receipts under Royal Decree 38/1996. The SPC may retain 100% of USD, EUR, GBP, or CNY export proceeds indefinitely in CBO-licensed foreign-currency accounts; conversion to OMR, when required, is executed at the commercial bank's published spot rate. All export proceeds must enter through the licensed banking system, be classified on the bank's balance-of-payments reporting as 'export proceeds of goods', and be reconcilable to Oman Customs single-window declarations and shipping documents.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO; البنك المركزي العماني), Muscat. Established by Royal Decree No. 4/1974; sole licensing, supervision and resolution authority for banks, exchange houses and payment-service providers under the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 114/2000) and the Foreign Exchange Law (Royal Decree No. 38/1996). The CBO administers the OMR/USD peg, operates the national RTGS and OMR clearing infrastructure, and collects cross-border transaction classification data from licensed banks for balance-of-payments statistics.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The return must be filed within 6 months after the end of the tax year (the tax year is the financial year, normally 1 January to 31 December unless a different accounting period is pre-approved). Final tax is payable on the filing date; the OTA may grant up to a 3-month extension if applied for before the due date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Submitted together with the corporate income tax return within 6 months of the tax year-end; contemporaneous Local File and Master File must be finalised by the filing deadline even though they are produced to the OTA only on request.

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
