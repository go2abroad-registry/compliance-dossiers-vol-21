Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) - Commercial Registration Directorate and Investor One-Stop Shop 'Invest Easy' (investeasy.om), in coordination with the Oman Chamber of Commerce and Industry (OCCI).

Governing statute

Commercial Companies Law, Royal Decree No. 18/2019, as amended by Royal Decree No. 53/2020 (Arts. 1-4 on company forms, legal personality and share capital; Single Person Company chapter); Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2022 (100% foreign ownership of an Omani Single Person Company/SPC); implementing circulars and fee schedules issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

Formation / constitutional filings

Single Person Company Incorporation Application together with notarized Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA) filed electronically via the Invest Easy portal and authenticated by the MoCIIP Notary Public Office.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 37 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (RD 50/2022) there is no mandatory minimum paid-up capital for the seafarer staffing activity; however, the SPC chapter of the Commercial Companies Law requires an issued capital of not less than OMR 500 (approx. USD 1,300) to be subscribed and fully paid in cash before commercial registration. The amount must be deposited into a commercial bank account opened in the SPC's name and evidenced by a paid-up capital certificate; no separate government escrow account is required.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Clearance Certificate from the Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) covering office premises (fire detection, extinguishers, emergency exit and evacuation plan); municipal structural/occupancy inspection and commercial occupancy permit from the respective Governorate Municipality; Ministry of Labour workplace clearance validating passage widths, ergonomic workstation layout and seafarer interview/medical assessment room requirements. No nuclear, radiation, or high-risk industrial clearances are applicable to this activity.

Paid-in capital rule: Under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (RD 50/2022) there is no mandatory minimum paid-up capital for the seafarer staffing activity; however, the SPC chapter of the Commercial Companies Law requires an issued capital of not less than OMR 500 (approx. USD 1,300) to be subscribed and fully paid in cash before commercial registration. The amount must be deposited into a commercial bank account opened in the SPC's name and evidenced by a paid-up capital certificate; no separate government escrow account is required.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) through the unified national e-Tax portal (https://eservices.taxoman.gov.om), integrated with the Invest Easy single-window of the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and the Royal Oman Police – Customs Authority (Bayane single-window) for border/customs procedures.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: The foreign shareholder incorporates the Single Person Company via MOCIIP Invest Easy and obtains the Commercial Registration Certificate together with the sectoral licence for maritime crew recruitment and vessel crew management (ISIC 5222) issued by the competent transport/maritime licensing authority. Step 2: The commercial registration is digitally linked to the OTA, which automatically generates the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) for the SPC. Step 3: Where the SPC's annual taxable supplies or chargeable turnover equals or exceeds OMR 38,500, the SPC must file a VAT registration application with the OTA through the e-Tax portal within the statutory deadline, submitting the Commercial Registration Certificate, the activity licence, the constitutional documents (singular shareholder resolution), proof of registered office, bank confirmation, and a completed VAT registration questionnaire; a non-resident taxable person is obliged to appoint an Omani tax representative. Step 4: The SPC completes employer registration with the Public Authority for Social Insurance for any Omani/GCC national administrative staff and obtains the final VAT registration certificate. Step 5: The SPC is then activated for corporate income tax filings, withholding tax submissions, VAT return filing, and electronic invoice issuance in the OTA portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the Omani SPC is a juridical person resident in Oman for tax purposes, subject to corporate income tax on its worldwide income at the statutory rate of 15%, with a reduced small-business rate of 3% applicable to taxable income not exceeding OMR 100,000; the SPC is separately liable for VAT and no pass-through or disregarded-entity treatment is available to the foreign shareholder.

Available election forms

Omani law does not provide a US-style check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT group registration for related persons satisfying common ownership and financial control conditions; (ii) voluntary VAT registration where taxable supplies exceed OMR 19,250 but remain below the OMR 38,500 mandatory threshold; (iii) election of the small-business corporate income tax rate for taxpayers with taxable income not exceeding OMR 100,000; (iv) application for advance clearance or tax rulings regarding characterization, withholding tax, and transfer pricing treatment; and (v) election of payment schedule alternatives under the tax procedure rules where available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 on annual taxable supplies for mandatory registration; voluntary registration is available from OMR 19,500 in annual taxable supplies; registration obligation is assessed on a rolling 12-month basis.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for (a) export of goods physically removed from Oman to a destination outside the GCC; and (b) supplies of services that are directly connected with international transportation of passengers and goods, or services supplied to a non-resident recipient where the benefit of the service is enjoyed wholly outside Oman. For a ship crew recruitment and vessel crew management agency, the 0% rate may be claimed where the SPC supplies seafarer recruitment, manning, payroll administration, or crew management services to a non-resident shipowner, fleet operator, or overseas manning office for vessels engaged in international voyages, provided the recipient's non-resident status is documented and the service is not consumed for cabotage included in domestic Omani trade. The zero-rated invoice must satisfy the OTA invoicing form and content requirements and the claim must be supported by the documentary bundle retained for audit.

  • Zero-rated VAT invoice issued in the OTA-prescribed electronic format
  • Signed crew manning/recruitment service agreement and the non-resident shipowner's or fleet manager's foreign tax status evidence (overseas VAT registration certificate, foreign TIN, or certificate of residence)
  • Bayane Customs Export Declaration and Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (applicable when the engagement involves physical movement of goods, crew articles, or ancillary export consignments)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank foreign-currency settlement record evidencing the repatriation of export consideration into the Omani banking system
  • Vessel and seafarer deployment records, including crew manifests, seafarer employment contracts, discharge books, international port-call itinerary, and evidence that the vessel is engaged in international rather than domestic Omani voyages
  • Copy of the OTA export/vessel services filing record and any supporting certificate requested by the OTA during post-audit verification

The OTA has published the national e-invoicing framework with a phased implementation roadmap; as of the current compliance stage, real-time B2B API fiscal clearance, digital stamping, and e-fiscal clearance of every invoice is not yet fully enforced. However, all VAT invoices and credit notes must be issued electronically in the OTA-prescribed digital format, made available in machine-readable form (XML/QR-code integrated) during audits, and the SPC must maintain API-ready invoicing infrastructure in anticipation of the mandatory live-reporting and clearance obligation once the OTA fully activates the national e-invoicing platform.

The OTA may reverse zero-rated export treatment and reclaim the VAT benefit where documentation is incomplete, the vessel or service is found to have been used for domestic cabotage, the recipient is determined to be a resident of Oman, or the service benefit is enjoyed within Oman. The remedial consequence is a retrospective assessment of output VAT together with input VAT credit clawback, monthly late-payment delinquency penalties applied to unpaid tax, fixed administrative fines per the OTA penalty grid for documentary and invoicing violations, and, in cases of deliberate misstatement or fraudulent zero-rating claims, criminal prosecution carrying imprisonment and significant financial penalties; the SPC is required to retain all export and zero-rating evidence for at least five years, or a longer period where litigation or audit is pending.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for all current account transactions, including trade, payroll, and service payments, subject to standard anti-money-laundering checks. Capital account flows, including inbound foreign direct investment and shareholder loans, require only bank intermediation and statistical reporting to the CBO; there is no prior exchange control approval for profit remittance. Profit remittance from a foreign-owned Single Person Company is unrestricted after corporate tax clearance (Oman corporate income tax at 15%) and submission of audited financial statements; no withholding tax is imposed on dividends or branch profit remittances to foreign shareholders. Practical hurdles include strict KYC documentation, source-of-funds substantiation, and CBO transaction reporting thresholds for outward remittances above OMR 10,000 equivalent.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory mandatory surrender or conversion ratio on export proceeds. Marine crew recruitment and vessel management service receivables may be retained in OMR or any approved foreign currency. There is no prescribed timeline for conversion or repatriation, but supporting export/remittance documentation, including invoices and crew contracts, must be retained for at least five years for CBO and tax audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — Sultanate of Oman; the CBO is the sole foreign exchange and banking supervisory authority, with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion regulating the commercial register and foreign investment license.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital EMIs. Under CBO regulations applicable to electronic banking and payment services, only CBO-licensed financial institutions may hold client operational balances or process local-currency payroll. Offshore EMIs such as Wise, Revolut, or Payoneer are not permitted as primary treasury or payroll clearing accounts for a licensed Oman SPC; they may be used only as ancillary disbursement rails where funds are first cleared through a CBO-licensed Omani bank, and even then legal exposure remains due to unlicensed foreign capture. Clients with strong compliance posture may engage a CBO-licensed payment service provider for cross-border payroll aggregation before migration to a full-tier bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital injections for the Omani SPC are routed through the CBO-licensed banking system exclusively via SWIFT MT103 or MT202 for high-value transfers. The remitter must instruct the foreign bank to use the standard FDI / capital contribution purpose code and cite the Oman commercial registration number (CR) in the remittance narrative. The receiving Omani bank will require the notarized share subscription agreement, CR certificate, SPC incorporation documents, and a board resolution confirming the shareholder's capital commitment. The CBO imposes no prior approval threshold for equity injections below OMR 100,000, but banks must file a balance-of-payments/statistical return for all inbound investment; funds must be held in the SPC's name before conversion to operating assets. For working capital loans, a documented shareholder loan agreement and a CBO notification form are mandatory to avoid future characterization as deemed equity.Outbound trade and payroll disbursements are cleared through tier-1 Omani banks using SWIFT MT103 payments for all crew wages and ordinary supplier disbursements. For vessel charter, major fuel, or repair contracts exceeding an equivalent of OMR 500,000, an irrevocable commercial letter of credit (LC) confirmed by a reputable international bank is the standard clearing rail; the LC must reference the underlying crewing or maritime service contract and be issued in compliance with the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600). Smaller operational payments are executed via corporate internet banking after FX confirmation and AML sanctions screening. Every outward payment is subject to CBO reporting if above OMR 10,000 equivalent, and the bank must retain a copy of the crew payroll or commercial invoice in its AML/CFT file. Cross-border payroll to seafarers is best executed through a dedicated multi-currency account with local correspondent banks in the seafarers' home countries to minimize fees and counterparty risk.CBO-approved OMR/USD spot and forward transactions; the forward is primarily used to lock in USD liquidity rates for vessel-owner settlements due to the OMR's pegged exchange rate, eliminating USD basis risk.Cross-currency outright forward contracts between OMR and seafarer home currencies (notably PHP, INR, BDT, IDR, EUR, and GBP) to hedge payroll liabilities and operating expenses.Offshore non-deliverable forward (NDF) on OMR where onshore hedging limits do not cover the full notional; permitted only with CBO-licensed counterparties or international banks authorized under CBO market practice, and subject to mandatory underlying exposure verification.Cross-currency interest rate swap (CIRS) or principal-only currency swap with a CBO-licensed bank for long-term multi-currency crew financing or vessel acquisition debt.Natural hedging through dual-currency invoicing and multi-currency payroll netting, combined with a rolling 30-day forward hedging ladder to reduce FX conversion costs.
