Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Commercial Registry Division, Directorate General of Commerce, Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP), Sultanate of Oman; online via the Invest Easy one-stop shop at investeasy.gov.om.

Governing statute

Commercial Companies Law, Royal Decree No. 18/2019, as amended by Royal Decree No. 3/2023, and the Executive Regulations issued under Ministerial Decision No. 304/2020: formation and registration provisions, the Limited Liability Company chapter, and the Single Person Company (One Person Company) rules permitting one foreign natural person to hold the entire capital with limited liability.

Formation / constitutional filings

Executed Articles of Association (Memorandum and Articles of Association) in Arabic, notarized before an Omani Notary Public, together with the Commercial Registration Application (Talebl al-Qayd al-Tijari) submitted through Invest Easy.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 23 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Royal Decree No. 18/2019 no statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an Single Person Company; the stated capital must be fully subscribed by the sole shareholder and the paid-up portion must be deposited into an Oman-licensed bank account in escrow and evidenced by a signed bank attestation letter before notarization of the Articles of Association. Foreign 100% ownership is permitted without a local partner; a customary bank minimum deposit is approximately OMR 1,000-5,000 (USD 2,600-13,000); the escrow balance is released only after issuance of the Commercial Registration.

Funding & unlock path: CDAA Fire Safety Clearance Certificate for the registered office, comprising verified evacuation plans, portable fire extinguishers, and compliant emergency exits; landlord-supplied Building Structural Completion Certification or structural load inspection for the occupied floor; and occupational health clearance including basic first-aid provisions under the Occupational Safety and Health Law (Royal Decree No. 25/2021).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Ministry of Finance, Sultanate of Oman; official e-services and self-assessment portal: https://www.taxoman.gov.om (STAR e-filing system). Trade registration is separately administered by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion through the Invest Easy portal.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate via Invest Easy; a foreign individual or corporate entity may hold 100% of the SPC with no statutory minimum capital. Step 2: Register the SPC for corporate income tax with the OTA within three months of the CR date through the OTA's electronic portal; the OTA issues a unified Tax Identification Number (TIN) and activates the taxpayer record for annual income tax returns. Step 3: Register for VAT with the OTA when actual or anticipated taxable supplies in Oman exceed the statutory threshold (OMR 38,500 per rolling 12 months), or elect voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250; the VAT registration certificate states the VAT registration number and reporting periods. Step 4: Register as an employer with the Public Authority for Social Insurance (PASI) if Omani staff are engaged. A non-resident SaaS supplier without an Omani establishment is exempt from income tax registration but must complete foreign VAT registration through the authorized agent procedure once Oman-sourced digital-service turnover crosses the VAT threshold.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist. The SPC is mandatorily taxed at the entity level under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended); profits are computed separately from the sole shareholder and no pass-through or consolidation election is available. Available statutory elections are limited to: (a) voluntary VAT registration below the mandatory threshold; (b) selection of the financial/accounting year; (c) approved accounting method election for qualifying micro taxpayers; (d) VAT basis-of-supply election (invoice/cash basis where permitted); and (e) designation of mixed-use assets for VAT recovery purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 of taxable supplies in any rolling 12-month period (mandatory registration threshold); OMR 19,250 (voluntary registration threshold). The same mandatory threshold applies to non-resident suppliers of electronic, telecommunication, and digital services where place-of-supply rules treat the consumer as located in Oman.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for exports of goods where the supplier transfers ownership to a non-resident customer and the goods leave Oman (or another GCC member state) within the period required by the Omani Customs Law and VAT Regulations. For the OM_SPC's SaaS business, zero-rating additionally applies to supplies of cloud software services to non-resident customers where the place of supply is outside Oman, evidenced by the customer's residence, use, and enjoyment outside Omani territory. Services physically performed and fully consumed outside Oman, ancillary to exported goods, and internationally transported goods/services also qualify. Supplies of digital services that are actually used or enjoyed inside Oman remain taxable at 5%, regardless of the customer's contractual location.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Outward 'Bayan' e-declaration filed via the Oman Customs electronic portal) evidencing shipment and the exporter of record
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical transportation of goods out of Omani customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT/telex credit confirmation proving collection of export proceeds into a bank account in Oman within the prescribed repatriation period
  • For SaaS/services exports: zero-rated VAT invoice issued in e-invoice/DRS format, the underlying customer contract, and objective customer-location evidence (IP geolocation logs, billing address, payment gateway records, product usage logs) where no customs movement exists

Oman is implementing a phased national e-invoicing framework under OTA supervision; the full mandate will require issuance of structured digital tax invoices (XML/UBL 2.1), cryptographic digital signatures, QR-code stamps, and real-time or near-real-time API-based clearance and reporting through the OTA's integration platform. In the interim, every VAT-registered person must operate a compliant Data Reporting System (DRS), issue VAT invoices per the VAT Executive Regulations, and retain invoice records in Arabic or Arabic-labelled dual-language format. Taxpayers are segmented by annual turnover, and the micro-SaaS SPC (low turnover) is expected to be in a later implementation wave; until the applicable wave, conventional electronic tax invoices and DRS logs satisfy compliance.

The OTA conducts document, field, and desk audits cross-comparing customs Bayan export declarations, VAT return zero-rating claims, bank credit records, and Merchant of Record settlement data. An unjustified zero-rating claim is corrected by tax assessment, and the denied credit is clawed back together with the VAT underpaid. Deliberate falsification of export or offshore-consumption evidence constitutes tax evasion, attracting a fine of not less than 100% and up to 300% of the evaded tax, imprisonment of not less than six months, and issuance of an audit-imposed bank guarantee requirement before future refunds are released. Late or inaccurate documentation also triggers administrative fines under the VAT Executive Regulations and negative compliance scoring that elevates the taxpayer to a high-risk audit cohort.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible for current account transactions, including subscription revenues, royalties, and profit remittances, subject to standard documentary compliance. Capital account transactions, including foreign direct investment equity injections, are freely permitted under Oman's 100% foreign ownership framework, but require supporting bank documentation such as the Commercial Register extract, shareholders' resolution, and source-of-funds evidence. There are no legal restrictions on profit repatriation for a licensed Single Person Company, provided the SPC has filed its tax returns, obtained a tax clearance certificate where required, and settled all outstanding corporate tax and social insurance liabilities. Banks apply routine anti-money laundering and sanctions screening to all outward remittances, but no CBO pre-approval is required for documented trade or service payments.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory surrender or conversion ratio for export proceeds. The SPC is required to repatriate all export and service proceeds into Oman through a licensed bank, normally within 12 months from the date of invoice or receipt, and must retain supporting contracts, payment gateway settlement reports, and bank credit advices. For USD-denominated subscription receipts routed through Paddle or Lemon Squeezy, the SPC must ensure that the payment aggregator sweeps net proceeds to the SPC's Omani bank account on a regular settlement cycle; no mandatory conversion into OMR is required, and the SPC may retain USD in a foreign-currency account.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low-to-medium feasibility as a principal treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI platforms such as Mercury, Airwallex, or Currenxie are not licensed by the CBO and cannot provide OMR settlement, local direct debits, or a recognized Omani IBAN for tax and VAT registration. They may be used only as auxiliary non-resident USD collection accounts for Paddle or Lemon Squeezy payouts, subject to strict evidence of ultimate settlement back to a licensed Omani bank account. Reliance on an offshore EMI as the primary treasury account would raise substance and control concerns for Omani corporate tax residence and transfer-pricing compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity capital must be wired from the foreign shareholder's personal or corporate bank account to the SPC's capital account at the Omani bank via SWIFT MT103. The remittance must include the SPC's Commercial Register number, the purpose code 'Foreign Direct Investment in Share Capital' (Oman classification code under CBO reporting), and the shareholder's identity. The bank requires a notarized subscription agreement, the post-incorporation shareholders' resolution, and the Commercial Register extract showing the foreign shareholder as the sole owner. No CBO prior approval is needed for the equity injection, but the bank will file the inflow with the CBO's Balance of Payments statistics as a foreign direct investment liability. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (top marginal Omani corporate income tax rate; lower progressive SME brackets apply to the first OMR 350,000 of taxable income, and a zero-bracket applies to very small profits).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman does not impose a domestic dividend withholding tax on distributions made by an Omani SPC to its foreign shareholder.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within four months after the end of the SPC's fiscal year. For a calendar-year SPC, the return must be filed no later than 30 April of the following year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 aggregate annual related-party transactions is the operative administrative threshold above which an Omani taxpayer should hold contemporaneous transfer pricing documentation. Oman Tax Authority may also require Local File or Master File documentation for multinational groups with consolidated revenues exceeding OMR 20 million, or whenever an audit file is opened for related-party pricing.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not maintain a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations code. However, economic substance is effectively enforced through Article 38 anti-avoidance powers, tax-residence and beneficial-ownership tests, commercial companies law, and OECD-aligned treaty abuse rules. The micro-SaaS SPC must therefore maintain substantive functions, assets, management decisions, staff, and records in Oman; a mailbox entity without operational reality may be disregarded or treated as a foreign-controlled branch.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Return

Statutory sanction: Criminal prosecution under the Omani Income Tax Law; fines in the range of OMR 1,000 to OMR 50,000 and/or imprisonment for up to 5 years. In addition, unpaid tax, late-payment surcharges, and penalty tax of up to 100% of the tax evaded may be imposed.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil may be pierced where directors or shareholders knowingly prepare false returns, divert revenue, or cause fraudulent insolvency. Directors can be held jointly and severally liable for the SPC's tax liabilities and may face travel bans, asset freezes, and imprisonment.
Transfer Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Oman Tax Authority may adjust the consideration to an arm's-length price, disallow artificial deductions, raise additional CIT at 15%, apply monthly late-payment surcharges, and impose transfer-pricing documentation penalties of up to OMR 10,000. Persistent deliberate manipulation escalates to criminal tax evasion.

Director personal liability: Directors and transfer-pricing compliance officers are personally exposed to administrative penalties for failing to supply documentation; where the manipulation is intentional, they may face criminal liability, personal fines, and travel restrictions.
Shell Company / Substance Abuse / DTA Abuse

Statutory sanction: Re-characterisation of the entity as a non-resident or as a branch of the foreign principal; adjustment of all Oman-source revenue at the full 15% CIT rate; denial of treaty relief; dissolution of the commercial registration; and administrative fines up to OMR 50,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who allow a sham entity to operate can be disqualified, banned from leaving Oman, and held personally liable for unpaid tax and fines if the entity is liquidated or stripped of assets before tax clearance.
AML / CFT / Payment-Rail Misuse

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 30/2016, as amended), fines can reach OMR 500,000, with confiscation of connected funds, suspension or cancellation of financial licences, and imprisonment for up to 7 years. The 'low-risk standard' tier attached to consumer SaaS does not waive these obligations.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and beneficial owners may face personal criminal fines and imprisonment, travel bans, disqualification from future directorships, and joint civil liability where the AML breach is linked to concealment or dissipation of assets.
