Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (Oman Tax Authority) - Tax Management System (TMS) e-portal: https://tms.taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain a Commercial Registration certificate from the Oman Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP). The SPC must then apply to the Oman Tax Authority for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN/VAT ID) via the TMS online portal. The application requires the Commercial Registration, executed Memorandum/Articles of Association, a board-or-shareholder resolution confirming officers, copies of passports, registered office evidence, and opening financials; the TIN is issued within 5-15 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain a Commercial Registration certificate from the Oman Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP). The SPC must then apply to the Oman Tax Authority for a Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN/VAT ID) via the TMS online portal. The application requires the Commercial Registration, executed Memorandum/Articles of Association, a board-or-shareholder resolution confirming officers, copies of passports, registered office evidence, and opening financials; the TIN is issued within 5-15 business days.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 annual taxable supplies for mandatory registration; OMR 19,250 for voluntary registration

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods to destinations outside the GCC qualify for 0% VAT if evidenced by a customs export declaration. Supplies of services to non-resident recipients located outside Oman may be zero-rated where the benefit is enjoyed outside Oman and the supply meets the requirements of the Oman VAT Law's place-of-supply rules. For digital social-casino and virtual slots content distributed through Apple App Store and Google Play Billing, the operator must demonstrate that the consumer's location is outside Oman and that the digital supply is performed and enjoyed offshore.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Purchase Order
  • Digital Service Delivery Log (for online digital supplies)

No real-time electronic invoicing clearance or digital stamping regime is currently implemented in Oman. Taxpayers may issue invoices via ERP systems compliant with VAT Law format and must file periodic VAT returns electronically through the TMS. The Tax Authority may request digital sales records and transactional data during audits.

The Oman Tax Authority may scrutinize export zero-rating claims, including the actual receipt of foreign currency and the completeness of export documentation. For fraudulent zero-rating or inadequate documentation, the taxpayer may be required to reverse the zero-rated treatment, resulting in an assessment of the undercharged VAT (at the standard 5% rate), plus administrative penalties of up to 3 times the unpaid tax, and criminal sanctions including fines and imprisonment in cases of deliberate evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible and pegged to the US Dollar at OM 1 = USD 2.60076 (official mid-rate, maintained within narrow margins). Current-account transactions (digital-content export revenue, platform commissions, royalties, operating expenses) are fully convertible without prior CBO approval for a licensed commercial-bank client. Capital-account flows (share capital injection, shareholder loans, dividend distribution) are also liberal subject to bank-level BOP reporting, AML/CFT screening, supporting corporate documentation, and OTA clearance where applicable. Oman permits 100% foreign ownership of a Single Person Company (SPC) in ISIC 5820 software publishing, imposes no dividend withholding tax, and allows profit remittance once audited financial statements, a valid CR, and OTA/Zakat clearance evidence are presented to the paying bank.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. The SPC may retain all digital-export receipts in USD/EUR in foreign-currency accounts at licensed banks and may convert at the pegged CBO rate at prevailing commercial bank rates. The receiving bank must tag every inward remittance with the correct BOP purpose code under the IMF BPM6 statistical framework and retain underlying settlement evidence (Apple/Google payout statements, aggregator settlement reports) for CBO reporting; failure to evidence the underlying export service can trigger a BOP-misclassification inquiry and enhanced compliance review.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), operating under the Omani Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), the Anti-Money Laundering and Combating Terrorism Financing Law (Royal Decree 30/2016), and CBO Circulars on foreign exchange, balance-of-payments (BOP) reporting and bank customer due diligence (CDD). The Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) administers foreign-ownership registration and Commercial Registration (CR); the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) governs tax registration, CIT (15%) clearance, and the related-party documentation required for capital and profit remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Constrained / Low. Oman does not operate a full non-bank Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime. Offshore or SEPA-licensed EMIs and virtual banks cannot lawfully hold OMR balances, open Omani bank references, or act as the primary treasury settlement rail for the SPC. The CBO Payment Service Provider framework recognises only locally licensed PSPs/aggregators, so all consumer-platform settlement (Apple IAP, Google Play Billing) must ultimately clear into a CBO-regulated commercial bank account in the SPC's name. Foreign EMIs may be used only as ancillary disbursement or FX-orchestration rails for non-Omani counterparties, subject to full pass-through reconciliation and BOP reporting.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: 12-month Apple App Store Connect and Google Play Console payout statements; payment-aggregator settlement reports (Stripe/Adyen/Braintree schema); a legal opinion from Omani counsel classifying the product as simulated social casino (play-money, no real-money gambling, no redemption to cash); consumer Terms of Service and privacy policy; and a platform revenue-flow diagram (consumer IAP to Apple/Google to aggregator to SPC bank account).

Defense evidence: Apostilled passports and proof of address; source-of-wealth statement with corroborating income and asset evidence; 12-month bank reference letters from a qualifying home-country bank; group organisational chart; audited parent-entity financial statements; and a written beneficial-ownership declaration mapped to the SPC register.

Defense evidence: Notarised and apostilled board resolution appointing signatories; specimen signature card attested by a correspondent bank; a CBO-compliant signatory declaration; and a limited power of attorney to a Muscat-licensed corporate-services agent for liaison with bank compliance.

Defense evidence: Executed intercompany and licence agreements; written transfer-pricing documentation aligned with Oman transfer-pricing expectations; invoice-to-ledger trace; debt-matching vendor schedule; and a related-party due-diligence pack containing beneficial-ownership KYC for each payee.
Expected high-velocity multi-currency outbound flows disproportionate to stated revenue.

Root cause: Expected high-velocity multi-currency outbound flows disproportionate to stated revenue.

Defense evidence: Board-approved annual treasury plan and cash-flow model; Apple/Google payout reconciliation; audited annual financial statements; and a BOP reporting history demonstrating that every inbound and outbound flow carries a coherent business purpose and matching contractual documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The foreign shareholder wires equity or a shareholder loan via SWIFT MT103 from a qualifying source bank into the SPC's capital escrow account at Bank Muscat or National Bank of Oman. Remittance instructions must state 'Share Capital Contribution' or 'Shareholder Loan' as the purpose and reference the CR number. The receiving bank applies a BPM6 direct-investment code (equity other than investment-fund shares for capital injections; other direct-investment capital for shareholder loans) and requires the capital deposit certificate, a board/shareholder resolution approving the injection, and, for a loan, an executed intercompany loan agreement with arm's-length interest. After the capital certificate is issued, funds are swept to the operational account; no CBO prior approval is required, but the bank files the transaction in the BOP report and may request an amended CR if paid-in capital exceeds the registered amount.Because ISIC 5820 digital-content publishing is a service business, outbound clearing uses SWIFT MT103 credit transfers (telegraphic transfer) rather than documentary credits; commercial letters of credit are inapplicable. Key streams: (i) platform commissions owed to Apple and Google are netted from payout statements and therefore do not leave the Omani bank account; (ii) royalty and licence payables to overseas licensors are paid by MT103 with invoice, licence contract, and transfer-pricing documentation attached for AML review; (iii) dividend repatriation to the foreign shareholder is executed by MT103 with audited financial statements, OTA tax-clearance certificate, Zakat clearance where triggered, and the BPM6 dividend purpose code; and (iv) cloud, SaaS, hosting, and marketing vendor payments are executed by MT103 or, for recurrent low-value flows, through the bank's MT101 electronic instruction and SWIFT gpi payment services. The bank will enforce dual-authorisation controls and reject payments to sanctioned entities.Deliverable vanilla FX forwards (OMR/USD at pegged parity; OMR/EUR; OMR/GBP) for bona-fide commercial exposure under an ISDA or IFEMA master agreement with a CBO-licensed bank; speculative hedging is prohibitedFX swaps (OMR against foreign currency, and USD/EUR/GBP cross-currency swaps) to rebalance treasury liquidity without creating unhedged positionsOTC vanilla FX options (call and put structures) for covered hedging of projected Apple/Google receivables, subject to bank product approval and CBO anti-speculation policyCross-currency interest-rate swaps on shareholder-loan liabilities (USD/EUR floating-to-fixed) where the underlying exposure is documented and non-speculativeOMR NDFs: not available and not permissible because the OMR/USD parity is a fixed CBO-administered peg with a fully deliverable market; hedging must be executed through deliverable forwards or by naturalising treasury balances in USD
