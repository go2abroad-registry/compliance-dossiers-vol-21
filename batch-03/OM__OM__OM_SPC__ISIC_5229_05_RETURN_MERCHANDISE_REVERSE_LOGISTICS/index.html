Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General of Taxation (SGT), Ministry of Finance, Sultanate of Oman; official digital portal: https://tax.oman.om.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must procure a single unified Tax Registration Number (TRN) for both Corporate Income Tax (Royal Decree No. 25/2020, as amended) and Value Added Tax (Royal Decree No. 121/2022). Prior to tax registration, the foreign shareholder must obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP). Upon CR issuance, the SPC must file an online application through the SGT tax portal, submitting the (i) CR certificate, (ii) certified constitutional documents (Articles of Association/Memorandum), (iii) audited opening financial statement where required, (iv) passport copies and proof of address of the single foreign shareholder and resident manager, and (v) local registered-office evidence. The SGT issues the TRN for CIT and, in parallel, a VAT registration certificate where annual taxable supplies equal or exceed the mandatory registration threshold or where voluntary registration is elected.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: The Omani SPC is a resident juridical person subject to entity-level Corporate Income Tax at graduated rates of 3% on taxable income up to OMR 30,000 and 15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 30,000. No pass-through or tax-transparent classification is available for this legal form.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box election. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT group registration for related Omani entities under the VAT Executive Regulations; (ii) voluntary VAT registration upon annual turnover reaching OMR 19,250; (iii) election to apply zero-rating to exported goods and services with documented export evidence; (iv) advance pricing arrangement applications for cross-border reverse-logistics service fees; and (v) claims for treaty relief under Oman's double tax treaties (including the GCC Unified Agreement and OECD-aligned bilateral treaties).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies (goods and services); voluntary VAT registration: OMR 19,250. Non-resident suppliers supplying taxable goods or services through a taxable facility or making distance sales are registrable from the first Rial of taxable turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available where the SPC is the principal supplier of the exported goods; the goods physically depart Oman or another GCC member state within the statutory window (90 days from invoicing or 90 days from import into Oman for re-export); goods are delivered to a non-GCC destination under a direct transport or a qualifying series of transport contracts; global reverse-logistics services relating to export freight, international handling, quality grading performed outside Oman, and cooling-period cargo care supplied to a non-resident customer for exclusive offshore use qualify as cross-border services subject to 0%. The exporter must hold the commercial invoice, proof of payment in freely convertible currency, and conclusive transport evidence before applying the zero rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (SAD) processed by Royal Oman Police Customs
  • Original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing departure from Oman to a non-GCC destination
  • Commercial Invoice and detailed packing list
  • Electronic tax invoice in e-invoicing XML/PDF-A3 format with QR code and customs reference
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 confirmation issued by a licensed Omani bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds in foreign currency

Oman mandates electronic tax invoicing in a prescribed Arabic/English XML or PDF/A-3 format containing QR codes and VAT identification data; returns and recapitulative statements must be filed exclusively via the SGT tax portal API prior to the 28th day following the end of each tax period. Continuous fiscal clearance, digital stamping, and real-time pre-clearance authentication of tax invoices are staged for phased implementation consistent with GCC e-invoicing standards. Export invoices for reverse-logistics flows must reference the customs declaration number and be transmitted via the national e-invoicing clearing solution once fully operational.

Export zero-rating claims are subject to rigorous SGT audit review on a five-year look-back basis. Filing a false zero-rated export claim or failing to evidence physical departure and foreign-currency repatriation renders the VAT immediately due and payable with retrospective clawback, and triggers administrative penalties of up to 100% of the tax evaded under Royal Decree No. 121/2022, additional discretionary penalties of OMR 500 to OMR 3,000 per violation, and criminal liability including imprisonment up to five years for wilful tax fraud. The SPC may additionally lose its compliant-taxpayer status, be excluded from fast-track VAT refund processing, and have its TRN suspended until full discharge of assessed VAT, interest at the SGT determination rate, and penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current-account and capital-account transactions applicable to a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company operating under Oman's Foreign Capital Investment Law. Profit repatriation, dividend transfers, service-fee remittances, and liquidation proceeds may leave Oman without a separate foreign-exchange license, subject to tax clearance, audited financial statements, and standard bank documentary verification. The OMR is pegged to the US Dollar at a fixed rate of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD, equivalent to approximately 0.3845 OMR per USD, which eliminates USD/OMR convertibility risk. Current-account flows incidental to cross-border reverse logistics, including customer refunds, quality-grading fees, inward freight reimbursements, and returned-goods depreciation credits, are freely transferable. Capital-account injections into a licensed SPC require no prior CBO approval but must be correctly coded under the balance-of-payments framework for monetary reporting.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory conversion percentage, and no domiciliation timeline for export proceeds. The reverse-logistics SPC may lawfully retain 100% of USD, OMR, EUR, GBP, or other convertible currency receipts in onshore or offshore accounts without a CBO conversion mandate. All inbound proceeds must nevertheless be identified through a balance-of-payments purpose code attached to the incoming SWIFT MT103 so that the receiving Omani bank can complete monetary statistics reporting and AML screening; there is no deadline for repatriation or mandatory exchange into OMR.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), with the Capital Market Authority (CMA) for securities-related foreign investment flows and the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) for SPC licensing, commercial registration, and inward investment approvals.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium. Airwallex and other non-resident e-money institutions are not licensed by the CBO to hold deposits, issue local OMR IBANs, or execute domestic OMR direct debits inside Oman. Their legally viable function is to serve as an offshore treasury aggregator for multi-currency collections and payouts in USD, EUR, GBP, and AED, with all CBO-reportable OMR transactions routed through a licensed Omani commercial bank. The SPC should therefore maintain a licensed local operational account as the primary treasury node and use Airwallex as a supplementary cross-border payout and collection rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound shareholder capital is transferred via SWIFT MT103 from the overseas shareholder's bank to the SPC's OMR or USD account at the chosen Omani commercial bank. The wire must carry the CBO balance-of-payments purpose code for direct-investment equity capital, the SPC's Commercial Registration number, and the share-subscription reference. USD funds are automatically converted to OMR at the fixed peg of approximately 0.3845 OMR per USD upon credit. The receiving bank applies enhanced source-of-funds verification; therefore the SPC should pre-authorize the injection through board minutes and a board resolution specifying the exact funding amount and source account before the wire is sent.Outbound disbursements are executed through three clearing mechanisms: SWIFT MT103 for international refunds and supplier payments; commercial letters of credit for first-time principal or OEM purchase commitments; and Central Bank of Oman domestic clearing rails, comprising RTGS for large-value OMR wires and OmanNet retail clearing for local ACH refunds, warehouse fees, and customs-duty payments. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% standard corporate income tax rate under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree No. 28/2009 as amended). A reduced rate of 3% applies to qualifying small and medium enterprises on taxable income not exceeding OMR 100,000, with 15% applying to the excess.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman does not impose dividend withholding tax on profit distributions to non-resident shareholders.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The tax return must be filed within six months after the end of the fiscal year. For a 31 December 2025 year-end, the deadline is 30 June 2026, unless an extension is granted by the Tax Authority under formal application.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when the aggregate value of connected-party transactions booked by the Oman SPC equals or exceeds OMR 5,000,000 per tax year. Master File: mandatory where the worldwide group consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 75,000,000 and the Oman entity transacts with connected parties above the Local File threshold. The Tax Authority may raise an audit-specific TP documentation request for any lower threshold where facts show aggressive pricing.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations statute, but economic substance is embedded in Oman tax residency, permanent establishment, and beneficial ownership tests. A reverse logistics SPC claiming Oman treaty benefits or local deductions must demonstrate actual personnel, independent decision-making, storage/return-processing infrastructure, and commercial continuity in Oman. A pure booking/financing vehicle without physical warehouse or operating staff can be re-domiciled by the Tax Authority as conduit and face denial of treaty benefits and reclassification of profits to the foreign principal.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of up to OMR 10,000 plus restitution of the underpaid tax; the Tax Authority may impose a 100% penalty of the tax evaded and freeze relevant bank accounts. False accounting evidence may trigger parallel money-laundering charges.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable for taxes withheld but not remitted and may be summoned for civil restitution. Courts may impose travel bans on directors and orders denying exit from Oman. Imprisonment for up to three years is possible for fraudulent filings.
Withholding Tax Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the unwithheld amount personally on the payer, plus monthly late-payment penalties of 0.5% and administrative fines of up to OMR 3,000. Non-payment can be converted into an enforced tax lien against the SPC's warehouse assets.

Director personal liability: The Tax Authority can pierce the corporate veil for unremitted withholding obligations; directors are jointly and severally liable. The Enforcement Court can issue travel bans and asset freezing orders against the board of the Omani SPC.
Transfer Pricing Documentation and Pricing Manipulation

Statutory sanction: Tax adjustments to restore arm's length profit, additional tax at 15% plus 1% monthly default charge, administrative penalties of up to OMR 5,000 for missing documentation, and possible public exposure as a non-compliant taxpayer.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve false intercompany invoices become liable for tax adjustments and fines. They can be barred from serving as directors of any Oman tax-registered company in cases of fraudulent conduct.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may strike the entity as a sham, deny treaty benefits, assess back taxes with penalties, and report the entity under Oman AML regulations for suspicious processing of payments. The Commercial Register may cancel the SPC's business license.

Director personal liability: Directors face separate personal liability for unpaid corporate tax where the corporate veil is pierced. Travel bans, passport holds, and up to seven years of imprisonment are possible for laundering funds through a sham logistics company.
AML / Financial Crime Trigger on Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 100,000 under Oman AML law, freezing of the SPC's accounts, seizure of returned-goods assets, and referral to the Public Prosecution for serious criminal filing. Tax evasion enhancement can increase penalties by 50%.

Director personal liability: Personal exposure includes criminal imprisonment, confiscation of proceeds, travel restrictions during investigation, and permanent debarment from operating in the GCC logistics sector. Directors who knowingly sign or authorize suspicious transfers are treated as principal offenders.
