Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - the Sultanate's unified national tax administration and successor to the Secretariat General of Taxation of the Ministry of Finance. Official e-services and TIN/VAT portals: https://taxoman.gov.om (returns, zero-rating schedules, refunds); corporate registration through the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) 'Invest Easy' portal at https://investeasy.gov.om; customs export clearance through the Royal Oman Police Customs 'Bayan' platform.

E-government filing portal

A 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) is incorporated under the Oman Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) through MoCIIP's Invest Easy portal at zero statutory minimum capital. The resulting Commercial Registration (CR) number is the master national identifier. The SPC must then: (1) obtain the municipal business license; (2) register for Corporate Income Tax with the OTA and obtain the unified TIN and corporate Tax Card; (3) register for VAT on taxoman.gov.om within 30 days after taxable supplies cross OMR 38,500, or elect voluntary registration once taxable supplies or expenses exceed OMR 19,250, with TIN issuance normally completed within 5 working days; (4) open and maintain an Omani settlement bank account in the SPC's own CR/TIN name, which is required for the Inward Remittance Certificate on export proceeds; (5) enrol on the Bayan customs platform as an exporter where any goods-based API/hardware flow exists; (6) update the TIN for any qualifying free-zone establishment. No Omani partner, minimum capital, or prior tax clearance is required for foreign shareholders.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation (the SPC is a separate juridical person taxed at 15% corporate income tax under Royal Decree 9/2017 on worldwide taxable income; no pass-through status is available and no territorial exclusion applies to Omani-source income) — A 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) is incorporated under the Oman Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) through MoCIIP's Invest Easy portal at zero statutory minimum capital. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation (the SPC is a separate juridical person taxed at 15% corporate income tax under Royal Decree 9/2017 on worldwide taxable income; no pass-through status is available and no territorial exclusion applies to Omani-source income)

Available election forms

Omani law contains no check-the-box election; the foreign-owned SPC is irrebuttably taxed at entity level. Available elections are limited to: (i) VAT Tax Group treatment under the VAT Executive Regulations for related Omani resident entities; (ii) voluntary VAT registration once taxable supplies or relevant expenses exceed OMR 19,250; (iii) margin-scheme election for eligible resale of second-hand goods or profit-yielding API data packages; (iv) OTA-approved input-tax allocation (pro-rata) methodology election where mixed exempt and taxable supplies arise in the same tax period. No hybrid-mismatch, transparent-entity, or OECD GloBE/Pillar Two elections are presently domesticated in Omani law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration at OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration from OMR 19,250 of annual taxable supplies or expenses (the latter to open input-VAT refund claims); non-resident digital/API sellers into Oman must register without threshold where they make B2C supplies, while B2B supplies by non-residents are reverse-charged to the Omani customer

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exports: 0% VAT is available where goods are physically exported from Oman within 90 days of the supply date, evidenced by customs declarations and transport documents. Services (the principal route for ISIC 6201 web-scraping and proxy-API gateways): 0% applies where the API/scraping services are supplied to a recipient established outside Oman who is not VAT-registered in Oman, the place-of-supply rules fix the supply outside Oman, and the service is not effectively used or enjoyed in Oman. Offshore data-streaming, scraping-as-a-service, and proxy-egress capacity supplied to non-resident enterprises therefore qualify for zero-rating, entitling the SPC to credit input VAT on Omani data-centre, bandwidth, cloud-hosting, and compliance expenses and to claim any resulting excess credit as a cash refund through the OTA e-portal.

  • Bayan customs export declaration / single-administrative-document record (goods) plus the OTA export-of-services schedule endorsed in the VAT return (digital services)
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or equivalent multimodal transport document evidencing physical dispatch out of Oman (goods); for digital services, a cryptographically time-stamped API delivery log and server egress report evidencing offshore consumption
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) issued by the Omani settlement bank, or SWIFT / USDC on-chain credit confirmation, evidencing receipt of foreign-currency export proceeds in the SPC's own bank account
  • Signed export contract or purchase order identifying the non-resident recipient, its jurisdiction of establishment, and the exact API subscription or scraping deliverable scope
  • Overseas customer VAT/TIN registration certificate or a notarised non-resident and non-Omani-establishment declaration where the customer is not VAT-registered abroad
  • Payment gateway settlement report (Stripe) or verified stablecoin ledger report (USDC) reconciled to the invoiced export consideration
  • Certificate of Origin and free-zone movement records where re-invoicing through Omani free zones is used

OTA operates an e-filing-first regime on taxoman.gov.om. Full e-invoicing is being introduced in administratively disclosed phases from the 2024-2025 compliance cycle: designated taxpayers must generate structured XML/UBL invoices via certified platforms, synchronise VAT invoice data through API to the OTA, embed the official QR/stamp code in PDF/hybrid invoices, and route zero-rated export declarations through the Bayan customs system. Fiscal-cash-register clearance is not yet enacted for data/API services; until the real-time clearance phase is mandatory, certified private invoicing platforms or the OTA free invoicing utility may be used, with an 8-year record-retention obligation running from the close of each tax period.

Zero-rating is a claim, not a presumption. OTA auditors test each zero-rated supply against the full 8-year documentation pack, and any missing IRC, Bayan record, or delivery log automatically reverses the claim. Reckless or fraudulent export claims trigger reassessment of VAT at 5% on the full value, late-payment interest from the original due date, escalating per-violation administrative fines, and on proven evasion, criminal imprisonment and fines up to the statutory maximum for the SPC's manager as well as loss of good standing with the OTA. The SPC's entire input-VAT credit becomes immediately payable, refunds and set-offs are suspended, and OTA may retrospectively deregister the SPC for the affected supplies, disqualifying all further export zero-rating. Crypto-rail proceeds (USDC) require enhanced CBO anti-money-laundering traceability and matched wallet evidence to count as export remittance documentation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 (related party transactions threshold and OMR 50,000,000 aggregate group turnover threshold for mandatory transfer pricing documentation; local file required above OMR 1,000,000; master file required above the group turnover threshold).

Economic substance scope: Oman's Economic Substance Regulations (per Royal Decree 37/2022 and related implementing decisions) apply to designated activities including banking, insurance, fund management, headquarters operations, and holding companies. A web scraping and API gateway SPC is not automatically within those designated activities; however, the SPC must demonstrate genuine operational substance (physical/market presence, decision-making in Oman, active employment, service infrastructure) to support residency, treaty eligibility, and to defeat anti-avoidance reclassification as a shell company or a permanent establishment of foreign principal providers.
