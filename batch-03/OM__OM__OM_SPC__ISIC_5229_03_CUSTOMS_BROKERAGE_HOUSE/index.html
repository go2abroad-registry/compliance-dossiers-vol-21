Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SPC under the Commercial Companies Law. The share capital stated in the M&A must nonetheless be fully subscribed by the sole shareholder. Cash capital must be deposited into a dedicated capital contribution (escrow/by-product) bank account in the company's name with a licensed Omani bank; in-kind contributions must be independently valued by a licensed assessor and fully particularized in the M&A prior to issuance of the Commercial Registration (CR).

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Tax Authority of the Sultanate of Oman (Al-Hai'a Al-Dharibiya), established under Royal Decree No. 57/2021; e-services and Tax Management System (TMS) portal at https://tax.gov.om. Customs declarations are processed through the Royal Oman Police – Directorate General of Customs 'Bayan' (ASYCUDA World) single-window, which feeds customs export proofs to the Tax Authority.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned SPC must (1) incorporate at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; (2) open the Tax Authority e-file by submitting the CR, constitutional documents, POA, and leased-premises evidence within the CR validity period; (3) obtain the CIT Tax Card (TIN) covering income tax registration — no threshold applies to CIT entity registration; (4) separately apply for VAT registration on the TMS portal once annual taxable supplies/imports reach OMR 500,000 (mandatory, application within 30 days of the month in which the threshold is met) or voluntarily from OMR 200,000; the VAT certificate carries the 15-character GCC-harmonised Omani VAT registration number (OM prefix). Registration must be evidenced before the Customs Brokers License can be renewed.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — A foreign-owned SPC must (1) incorporate at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; (2) open the Tax Authority e-file by submitting the CR, constitutional documents, POA, and leased-premises evidence within the CR validity period; (3) obtain the CIT Tax Card (TIN) covering income tax registration — no threshold applies to CIT entity registration; (4) separately apply for VAT registration on the TMS portal once annual taxable supplies/imports reach OMR 500,000 (mandatory, application within 30 days of the month in which the threshold is met) or voluntarily from OMR 200,000; the VAT certificate carries the 15-character GCC-harmonised Omani VAT registration number (OM prefix). Registration must be evidenced before the Customs Brokers License can be renewed.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman has no check-the-box or look-through election: the SPC is a tax-resident juridical person taxed at entity level under Royal Decree No. 28/2009 (Income Tax Law) on taxable income from Omani sources. Relevant statutory elections are limited to (i) voluntary VAT registration from OMR 200,000 turnover and (ii) standard VAT accounting (accruals) with no cash-accounting scheme and no VAT grouping. There is no hybrid mismatch election mechanism in Omani domestic law; tax treaties apply following domestic source rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 500,000 of cumulative taxable supplies and imports in the previous 12 months or reasonably expected in the next 12 months; voluntary registration: OMR 200,000. Customs brokerage commission and bond-management fees are taxable consideration and count towards the threshold; a non-resident principal without an Omani establishment triggers reverse-charge mechanisms rather than standard registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% applies when the supplier exports goods outside the GCC implementing states within 90 days of the supply, title has passed, and the goods are evidenced by a customs export declaration. Services: 0% applies to services directly connected to exported goods, services of international transport, and services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Oman and does not benefit (directly or indirectly) a person present in Oman; a customs brokerage SPC must therefore map each engagement — clearance services for local importers/exporters are standard-rated at 5%, while services supplied to and consumed by non-resident principals (with clean documentation and no Oman nexus of the recipient) may qualify for 0%.

  • Validated customs export declaration and customs abstract printed from 'Bayan' (ASYCUDA World) showing exit through an Omani or GCC-approved exit point
  • Bill of Lading, air waybill, or road/sea transit manifest evidencing physical removal of goods from Oman/GCC within the statutory 90-day window
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, and sales contract/export order
  • Bank credit advice or inward remittance certificate evidencing receipt of export consideration in an Omani bank account
  • Certificate of dispatch/export endorsed by the licensed customs broker and the exporter's VAT export register (minimum 10-year retention)

Oman is phasing in mandatory e-invoicing through Tax Authority-approved electronic invoicing solutions integrated by API to the Tax Management System (TMS); VAT-registered taxpayers must generate tax invoices containing all prescribed VAT Executive Regulation fields, transmit digital records, retain Arabic/English originals for 10 years, and may be selected for real-time or post-clearance validation. Customs-side clearance is real-time through 'Bayan' (ASYCUDA World) with mandatory electronic customs declarations, risk-scoring, and digital stamping of export declarations; no physical stamp requirement applies to the export evidence retained for VAT zero-rating.

The Tax Authority crosses-matches zero-rated exports against Bayan exit data, bank FX inflow records and the exporter's VAT returns; unsupported zero-rating is recaptured at 5% with default interest of 1%-per-month late payment penalties, administrative fines per violation (up to OMR 50,000 for serious violations), and criminal exposure of up to 5 years' imprisonment and/or fines if fraudulent evasion is established; deliberately false export documentation also triggers Customs, AML and directors' personal liability under the Omani Penal Law.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible and Oman maintains IMF Article VIII status, meaning no exchange-control restrictions apply to current international transactions. The OMR is pegged to the US Dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are liberalized: foreign-owned SPCs may receive equity injections, remit dividends, repatriate capital and transfer liquidation proceeds without obtaining prior CBO exchange-control approval. The practical hurdles are documentary and AML-driven rather than FX-control driven: banks require the SPC's Commercial Register certificate, valid Oman Tax Registration Number, audited financial statements for profit distributions, notarized board or shareholder resolutions, and a defensible source-of-funds trail. A 10% Omani income-tax withholding may apply to certain outbound service fees, royalties and interest paid to non-residents, but standard dividend distributions by an SPC to its foreign shareholder are not blocked by the CBO.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio into OMR, and no fixed liquidation timeline for export proceeds. Export receipts derived from customs-brokerage-facilitated shipments may be retained in foreign currency in OMR/USD/EUR accounts at CBO-licensed banks, freely converted at the prevailing market rate (pegged at OMR 2.6008 per USD), or transferred abroad without prior CBO approval. The receiving bank's only obligation is to report the transaction to the CBO for balance-of-payments statistical compilation and to perform AML/CFT and sanctions screening in line with the Oman Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016) and FATF recommendations.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), the sole monetary, exchange-control and bank-licensing authority of the Sultanate of Oman, headquartered in Muscat.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW for offshore or unlicensed digital EMI/e-money institutions. The CBO's National Payment System regulatory framework and its 'Regulatory Framework for Payment Service Providers' require any payment service provider touching Omani customers or OMR settlement to hold a CBO licence. A UK/EU digital EMI without a CBO branch licence cannot act as the SPC's primary transaction bank, cannot clear OMR customs-duty payments, and cannot itself open a settlement rail into the Oman domestic ACH/RTGS. Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for globally licensed PSPs only when they operate through a CBO-licensed bank as agent and never hold client funds directly in Oman; the SPC must keep its legal-entity bank accounts at a licensed Omani bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

100% foreign single-shareholder ownership combined with a trade-services activity class (customs brokerage) can trigger enhanced due diligence for Trade-Based Money Laundering risk.

Root cause: 100% foreign single-shareholder ownership combined with a trade-services activity class (customs brokerage) can trigger enhanced due diligence for Trade-Based Money Laundering risk.

Defense evidence: Commercial Register certificate; valid customs brokerage licence issued by Oman Customs (Royal Oman Police Customs); notarized constitutional documents (Company Agreement / Memorandum and Articles); fully disclosed Beneficial Ownership Register declaration; signed AML/CFT policy; customer risk-rating self-assessment; audited opening balance sheet and capital evidence; business plan with forecast transaction volumes and counterparty jurisdictions.

Client funds held in advance of customs duty settlement create a custodial/escrow profile that banks may classify as higher risk for rapid onward movement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity contributions from the non-resident shareholder must be routed by SWIFT MT103 (or equivalent correspondent-wire) from the shareholder's own bank account to the SPC's operational account at a CBO-licensed Omani bank. The remittance must carry a correct balance-of-payments purpose code in the message field, using an accurate narrative such as 'Share capital contribution / equity injection under Commercial Register No. [CRN]' and referencing the SPC's company name. The bank will file the receipt against the CBO's external-transactions statistical code for direct investment equity. Supporting verification documents to be lodged at the beneficiary bank: executed share-subscription resolution, CR certificate, SPC incorporation/Company Agreement, capital-allocation schedule and a source-of-funds declaration. For any large transfer, the bank will run enhanced sanctions and AML screening and may seek a capital-injection certificate from the SPC's auditor.The customs brokerage SPC will disburse outbound payments through three complementary rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for freight forwarding fees, ocean/air carrier charges, overseas agent commissions and customs-brokerage sub-agent payables, in OMR, USD, EUR or other trade currencies; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit (Sight and Usance LC) issued or received via the bank's trade finance desk for high-value consignment duties, bonded-warehouse collateral and import-advance settlements where a secure documentary layer is required; and (3) domestic OMR-denominated clearing for payment of customs duties, excise and VAT to Oman Customs exclusively through the 'Bayan' electronic customs clearance portal, settled over the CBO-supervised domestic ACH and RTGS rails from the SPC's duty-settlement sub-account. All outbound instructions must be accompanied by the underlying invoice, contract or customs declaration to satisfy CBO balance-of-payments and AML reporting.Deliverable outright FX forward contracts (OMR/USD, OMR/EUR, OMR/GBP, OMR/AED, OMR/INR) for committed freight, logistics and broker-fee exposures up to 12 months tenor, supported by underlying trade invoices.FX swaps (simultaneous spot/forward or two-leg swap) to bridge temporary OMR liquidity deficits before large customs-duty settlement dates.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) for long-term foreign-currency liabilities, including equipment, IT-system leases or USD-denominated working-capital facilities.Non-deliverable forwards (NDF) permitted only for exotic or illiquid non-GCC currencies where a physical OMR leg cannot be paired; OMR itself is fully deliverable under the fixed USD peg, so OMR-based NDFs are redundant and generally not offered by CBO-licensed banks.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% statutory rate on dividends paid by the Oman SPC to a non-resident shareholder, because distributions are made from after-tax profits and are not deemed taxable income in Oman. Any formal dividend paid out of untaxed accounting reserves is adjusting and taxed at 15%.
