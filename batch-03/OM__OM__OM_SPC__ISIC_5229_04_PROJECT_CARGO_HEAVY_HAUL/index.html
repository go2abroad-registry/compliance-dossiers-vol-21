Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman - unitary national revenue and customs authority; official portal: https://taxoman.gov.om (OTA e-services); customs filings are administered through the OTA Customs Directorate on the 'Bayan' single-window system.

E-government filing portal

The foreign-owned SPC must (1) incorporate via the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) 'Invest Easy' portal under the Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR); 100% non-resident ownership is permitted and no minimum paid-up capital is statutorily required for an SPC; (2) complete OTA taxpayer registration (income-tax 'tax card' / TIN) within the statutory window following commencement of activity; (3) file VAT registration through the OTA e-portal once the mandatory threshold (OMR 38,500 in any 12-month period) is reached, or voluntarily at OMR 19,250; (4) enrol with OTA-Customs on Bayan for export declarations and customs procedure codes, including Temporary Admission or Inward Processing relief for OOG mobile equipment; (5) link the CR, TIN, VAT ID and local bank account records supporting the milestone-wire and letter-of-credit payment rails.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope - the SPC (Single Person Company) is a separate juridical taxable person under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009) and is not pass-through eligible; there is no 'check-the-box' or LLC-transparency election. A resident Omani SPC is subject to corporate income tax on Oman-source and, where applicable, foreign-source income, generally at 3% on the first OMR 30,000 of taxable income and 15% above that band; dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are generally exempt from Omani withholding tax. — The foreign-owned SPC must (1) incorporate via the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) 'Invest Easy' portal under the Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR); 100% non-resident ownership is permitted and no minimum paid-up capital is statutorily required for an SPC; (2) complete OTA taxpayer registration (income-tax 'tax card' / TIN) within the statutory window following commencement of activity; (3) file VAT registration through the OTA e-portal once the mandatory threshold (OMR 38,500 in any 12-month period) is reached, or voluntarily at OMR 19,250; (4) enrol with OTA-Customs on Bayan for export declarations and customs procedure codes, including Temporary Admission or Inward Processing relief for OOG mobile equipment; (5) link the CR, TIN, VAT ID and local bank account records supporting the milestone-wire and letter-of-credit payment rails.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope - the SPC (Single Person Company) is a separate juridical taxable person under the Oman Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009) and is not pass-through eligible; there is no 'check-the-box' or LLC-transparency election. A resident Omani SPC is subject to corporate income tax on Oman-source and, where applicable, foreign-source income, generally at 3% on the first OMR 30,000 of taxable income and 15% above that band; dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are generally exempt from Omani withholding tax.

Available election forms

No hybrid-mismatch or pass-through election is available in Oman. Available statutory elections and options: (i) VAT tax-group election (related taxable persons in Oman may elect to be treated as one taxable person); (ii) voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250; (iii) non-calendar financial-year election for income tax with OTA approval; (iv) designated-zone / free-zone customs-suspension and zero-rating treatment for qualifying movements to or from Oman free zones (e.g., Sohar, Salalah, Duqm); (v) OTA-approved VAT return amendment or instalment arrangements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration: OMR 38,500 of taxable supplies or reverse-charge imports in any 12-month period (non-residents supplying taxable goods or services in Oman are also registrable); voluntary VAT registration: OMR 19,250.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% VAT applies to (i) exported goods that leave Oman for destinations outside Oman, or to GCC states or qualifying designated zones, within the statutory export window (generally 90 days from the date of supply unless the OTA approves an extension), evidenced by customs export declarations; and (ii) international transport of goods and directly related services, including international ocean freight and OOG project-cargo logistics services performed for the export movement. Inland OOG heavy-haul legs that commence and terminate inside Oman, or deliver to a domestic EPC site without physical export, remain standard-rated at 5%; the export evidence chain must show that the OOG units physically crossed the Omani customs frontier on the declared ocean Bill of Lading.

  • Customs Export Declaration / Single Administrative Document (SAD) lodged on the Bayan single window, bearing the OTA-customs clearance number
  • VAT-compliant zero-rated commercial tax invoice (sequential fiscal number and QR stamp)
  • Ocean Bill of Lading / multimodal OOG Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing port of loading in Oman and foreign port of discharge
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) / SWIFT MT103 or MT202 evidencing receipt of export proceeds through milestone-wire or letter-of-credit rails
  • OOG unit packing list, dimensional and weight (DIM+W) certificate, and lashing calculation sheet
  • Charter party / freight contract and haulage work orders (project-cargo logistics scope, ISIC 5229)
  • Certificate of Origin or GCC/EUR certificate where required by destination customs
  • VAT return zero-rated schedule and any free-zone / designated-zone movement records

Mandatory phased e-invoicing and invoice-clearance regime: VAT-registered suppliers must issue structured electronic tax invoices (XML/JSON) through the OTA national e-invoicing solution; invoices are fiscally cleared and reconciled, carry OTA-issued invoice identifiers and QR validation (digital stamp), and require API or certified-solution integration for B2B transactions; e-archive obligations apply. Export invoices must be cross-referenced to the corresponding Bayan customs export declaration.

OTA may issue a retrospective assessment restoring the 5% VAT plus late-payment penalties where the export evidence bundle is incomplete, the statutory export period or administrative 180-day repatriation record is breached, or the cargo is diverted to domestic use. Deliberate or reckless zero-rating abuse is treated as tax evasion and attracts additional tax of up to 100% of the tax avoided, administrative fines of up to OMR 100,000 for serious breaches, CR suspension or compulsory strike-off, and criminal liability for directors and signing officers. OTA cross-matches Bayan export declarations with VAT returns, banking remittance records and Bill-of-Lading registries to detect false or inflated export zero-rating claims.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman is an IMF Article VIII member; current-account payments and transfers are fully convertible and unrestricted, with no quantitative limits on trade-related settlements or service payments. The capital account is largely liberalized for inbound direct investment under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019), which permits 100% foreign ownership of an Omani Single Person Company (SPC). Profit and dividend repatriation are unrestricted and are not subject to Omani withholding tax; the only practical hurdles are administrative — the SPC must maintain a valid Commercial Registration, an active Oman Tax Authority (OTA) tax file, a complete beneficial-owner record, and must pass the bank's AML/KYC and sanctions screening before each outward remittance. Residual friction arises where the funds originate from high-risk jurisdictions, the beneficial-ownership chain is opaque, or the bank cannot verify the underlying project-cargo contract; these are evidentiary hurdles, not statutory convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender obligation, no mandatory OMR conversion ratio, and no fixed repatriation deadline for export receipts. Exporters may retain export proceeds in foreign-currency current accounts at licensed Omani banks. For balance-of-payments statistics, the receiving bank records each credit under the applicable CBO classification and requires underlying export documents, including the commercial invoice, the customs (Bayan) declaration, and the bill of lading or sea waybill. Enhanced due diligence is triggered where export receipts are materially delayed, fragmented without commercial rationale, or inconsistent with the OOG heavy-haul cargo profile. Non-repatriation is not an offence per se, but unexplained delay or diversion of proceeds above the statutory occasional-transaction threshold will raise AML red flags and may result in account restrictions.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000), exercising exclusive regulatory authority over banking licensing, foreign-exchange operations, payment and settlement systems, and all licensed financial intermediaries in the Sultanate. The foreign-exchange legal base comprises the Foreign Exchange Regulations implemented by CBO circulars, while the Anti-Money Laundering and Combating the Financing of Terrorism Law (Royal Decree 20/2019) and its Executive Regulations govern the AML/KYC perimeter for all cross-border treasury activity. The Oman Tax Authority separately administers the corporate tax file and TIN requirement that banks verify for onward remittances.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

LOW for offshore digital EMI/e-money institutions. The CBO Regulatory Framework for Payment Service Providers (Circular BM 1136 of 2020, as amended) requires any entity issuing e-money, operating payment accounts, or providing domestic payment clearing in Oman to hold a CBO-issued PSP licence, be incorporated in Oman, maintain a local compliance office with senior management physically present, and settle through CBO-regulated rails. An offshore EMI cannot lawfully provide OMR settlement accounts, Omani IBANs, RTGS clearing, or letter-of-credit services to the SPC. Domestic licensed PSPs can serve as supplementary collection front-ends for invoicing, but all statutory treasury operations — capital injection, trade milestone wires, LC issuance — must route through a tier-1 licensed bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity is routed by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign parent's bank to the SPC account at a tier-1 Omani bank, with field 70 carrying a concise purpose narrative, e.g., 'FDI EQUITY CONTRIBUTION / SHARE CAPITAL INJECTION under Commercial Companies Law RD 18/2019'. Oman does not operate a formal numeric purpose-code regime; instead the receiving bank records the balance-of-payments classification (direct investment equity) on the CBO transaction-declaration form. The pre-credit verification pack includes the SPC Commercial Registration, MOA/AoA, OTA tax card, beneficial-owner register, bank account mandate, and a pro-forma capital schedule. Enhanced due diligence is triggered where a single transfer equals or exceeds the statutory occasional-transaction threshold under the AML/CFT Executive Regulations or where the remitting jurisdiction is higher-risk. The bank issues an inward-remittance credit voucher, which the SPC retains as capital-placement evidence; the equity is then recorded in the share-capital register and board minutes. No prior CBO approval is required for FDI equity injection.Primary settlement rails: (a) SWIFT MT103 USD/EUR/OMR payment orders for milestone invoices to overseas shipowners, heavy-haul subcontractors, and surveyors, each backed by the approved project certificate, commercial invoice, transport documents, and a reference to the master LC or contract, with sanctions and anti-money-laundering screening applied before release; (b) irrevocable documentary letters of credit issued by the Omani bank under UCP600 for equipment and third-party logistics procurements, with a confirming bank in USD or EUR and typical tenors from sight to 120 days; the required document set includes commercial invoice, full set of bill of lading or sea waybill, packing list, independent surveyor certificate, insurance certificate, and shipper's beneficiary declaration; (c) domestic OMR legs (port charges, customs deposits, land-haulage payments) settle through the CBO-operated RTGS and national clearing rails, including the local instant-payment channel (OmPay) where the beneficiary is a local merchant; (d) GCC cross-border payments in AED or SAR settle through the Omani bank's nostro arrangements via SWIFT at standard FX spreads. Credit facilities, LC sub-limits, and FX enablement must be obtained at the time of onboarding to support the planned project-cargo volumes.Outright deliverable FX forwards (EUR/OMR, GBP/OMR, and major GCC crosses) with tenors up to 12 months, extendable to 24 months against credit-line collateral; USD/OMR forwards are functionally limited by the OMR/USD peg (1 OMR = 2.60076 USD), so spot conversion is standard for USD flowsDeliverable cross-currency swaps and FX swaps for project-capex and freight cash-flow protection, executed bilaterally with a licensed Omani bank under ISDA or equivalent master documentation and subject to CBO statistical reportingNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for currencies with no liquid OMR deliverable market (e.g., INR, PKR, BDT, and CNH), permitted where the SPC evidences a genuine underlying trade payable or receivable and the bank grants an NDF credit facilityPurchased OTC currency options on a selective basis for large public-contract tenders, typically conditional upon an underlying project-export award and never writing naked optionsIslamic hedging structures (Wa'd-based bilateral undertakings and ta'widh compensation arrangements) available through Islamic banking windows, subject to Shari'a board approval, for OMR and foreign-currency exposures

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: LOW for offshore digital EMI/e-money institutions. The CBO Regulatory Framework for Payment Service Providers (Circular BM 1136 of 2020, as amended) requires any entity issuing e-money, operating payment accounts, or providing domestic payment clearing in Oman to hold a CBO-issued PSP licence, be incorporated in Oman, maintain a local compliance office with senior management physically present, and settle through CBO-regulated rails. An offshore EMI cannot lawfully provide OMR settlement accounts, Omani IBANs, RTGS clearing, or letter-of-credit services to the SPC. Domestic licensed PSPs can serve as supplementary collection front-ends for invoicing, but all statutory treasury operations — capital injection, trade milestone wires, LC issuance — must route through a tier-1 licensed bank.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 annual aggregate related-party transactions (local currency threshold mirrored in Omani TP rules; Master File applies to groups with consolidated turnover above OMR 30 million)

Economic substance scope: Oman has no general Economic Substance Regulations (ESR); however, tax residence and beneficial ownership tests, anti-abuse rules, and the substance-over-form doctrine apply; comprehensive commercial substance documentation (e.g., actual project logistics operations, staff, and decision-making in Oman) is critical for treaty relief, WHT reduction, and audit defense

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Penalty of up to 100% of evaded tax; criminal fines; mandatory back-assessment of unreported tax with interest; referral to the Public Prosecutor for criminal prosecution

Director personal liability: Directors may be held personally liable for tax debts where corporate formalities ignored; travel bans, asset freeze orders, and imprisonment of managing director and officers for willful evasion
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty relief; imposition of 10% withholding on deemed repatriated dividends; application of general anti-abuse rules; re-characterization of arrangements; substantial back-tax assessments and administrative penalties

Director personal liability: Directors responsible for misleading tax filings may be liable for restitution, banned from board service in Oman, and subject to deportation in severe cases
AML / Transfer Mispricing

Statutory sanction: TP adjustment to arm’s length plus 20% late payment surcharge on additional tax; penalties up to OMR 50,000 for incomplete documentation; AML financial penalties and revocation of commercial registration

Director personal liability: Directors subject to civil restitution for wrongfully distributed profits; travel restrictions pending tax investigation; criminal sanctions for money laundering predicate offenses
