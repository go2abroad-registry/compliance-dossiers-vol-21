Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Omani law. The sole shareholder determines the capital amount in the Memorandum of Association, and that capital must be deposited into a corporate bank account in Oman; a bank certificate confirming receipt of the capital is required for the registration filing.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority, Royal Oman Police, issues the Fire Safety and Fire Fighting Equipment Certificate after inspection of the premises and verification of automatic detection, extinguishing systems, and emergency evacuation routes. The Ministry of Labour (Public Authority for Social Insurance applies separately) grants occupational health clearance confirming adequate lighting, ventilation, safe machinery guarding, personal protective equipment, and compliance with labour safety codes.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Omani law. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; electronic filing and registration through the official e-Services Tax Portal (https://taxoman.gov.om), complemented by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for Commercial Registration and the Royal Oman Police Customs Directorate for export documentation.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first complete Commercial Registration at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; thereafter the authorized signatory registers online on the OTA Tax Portal using the official electronic authentication (e-ID or civil ID), completes the combined business and VAT registration forms, and obtains a single Tax Identification Number (TIN) plus a dedicated VAT registration number where the registration threshold is met or exceeded. No minimum capital deposit is required for SPC registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — the resident Omani SPC is treated as a separate taxable juridical person under the Oman Income Tax Law; there is no pass-through or look-through status for foreign-owned limited companies, and taxable income is computed and taxed at the entity level at the standard corporate rate. — A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) must first complete Commercial Registration at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion; thereafter the authorized signatory registers online on the OTA Tax Portal using the official electronic authentication (e-ID or civil ID), completes the combined business and VAT registration forms, and obtains a single Tax Identification Number (TIN) plus a dedicated VAT registration number where the registration threshold is met or exceeded. No minimum capital deposit is required for SPC registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — the resident Omani SPC is treated as a separate taxable juridical person under the Oman Income Tax Law; there is no pass-through or look-through status for foreign-owned limited companies, and taxable income is computed and taxed at the entity level at the standard corporate rate.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is available under Omani tax law. Limited statutory election rights exist only for VAT purposes: related Omani businesses may apply to form a VAT group, and Omani non-resident suppliers may choose to nominate a tax representative for VAT registration and compliance.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman maintains open currency convertibility on both current and capital accounts. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the US Dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (official rate) with a narrow band of ±0.5%. Foreign-owned SPCs may freely credit/debit foreign-currency accounts. Profit and dividend remittance requires audited financials, a certified tax clearance certificate from the Oman Tax Authority, and corporate formation documents; no separate exchange-control approval is required. Countertrade or restrictive practices are statutorily prohibited for licensed trading companies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory foreign-exchange surrender. Export receipts from scrap copper and other goods must be credited to the SPC's licensed bank account within 180 days from the date of the Customs Single Window export declaration. The exporter must match the SWIFT MT103 credit note to the bill of lading and commercial invoice. No conversion ratio applies; the holder may retain USD/EUR balances or convert to OMR at the official pegged rate. Failure to repatriate within the statutory window constitutes a violation under CBO's Foreign Exchange Circular (38/77) and can result in restricted access to trade facilities.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Low feasibility. Offshore digital EMI (electronic money institution) service providers are not permitted to operate in Oman without a CBO license. Foreign EMIs such as Airwallex, Wise, or iBan First may only act as unregulated payment facilitators and cannot execute OMR-denominated settlements or maintain capital escrow accounts. All cross-border trade funds must transact through a CBO-licensed commercial bank or registered exchange house.

Defense evidence: Batch-level supply chain pack: commercial contracts with registered suppliers, operator weighbridge tickets, lot-level copper content assay from a NABL/ISO-17025 laboratory, Customs export declaration, and a shipping line non-negotiable copy of bill of lading.
Trade-based money-laundering red flags (consistent under/over-invoicing, mismatched HS codes, or recycling sector activities).

Defense evidence: LME Grade A copper benchmark price history, inspection certificate issued by SGS/Intertek/Cotecna, independent pre-shipment survey, and bank-compliant transfer pricing analysis with margin benchmarking.

Defense evidence: Swift MT103 matching the invoice reference, BOI (beneficiary ownership) confirmations, an indemnity/compelling declaration showing the commercial reason for tripartite payments, and irrevocable LC from the buyer's issuing bank.

Defense evidence: Archived negative media report with a qualified legal opinion, OFAC/EU/CBO sanctions screening certificate, declaration of no-due-diligence gaps from a licensed corporate agent, and audited financial statements for two prior fiscal years.
Failure to obtain the mandatory MoCIIP scrap-trading license or omission of environmental/export permits.

Root cause: Failure to obtain the mandatory MoCIIP scrap-trading license or omission of environmental/export permits.

Defense evidence: Certified license from Oman's Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), the Trade Establishment Code certificate under the Business Registration System, and an Environment Permit for non-ferrous recycling operations issued by the Environment Authority.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholder capital injection must be routed through a CBO-licensed bank using SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'EQUI' (Direct Investment) or 'GTR' (General Trade). The funds should be credited to the SPC's Capital Escrow Account (non-operational) and matched against the registered FDI declaration in the Oman Business Portal. The Oman Tax Authority and MoCIIP must receive the inward remittance notification within 30 days; the SPC may subsequently reallocate funds to its operational account for working capital after conversion to OMR at the official 0.3846 pegged rate (buy side). The capital injection cannot be pledged or withdrawn for the duration of the 'Locked-in Period' applicable to foreign-owned SPCs (normally two years).Outbound trade disbursements are cleared primarily through (i) SWIFT MT103 (wire transfers) for spot supplier payments, (ii) irrevocable documentary letters of credit (SWIFT MT700) for scrap copper import settlements under UCP600, and (iii) MT202 and MT199 for correspondent bank funding and treasury adjustments. For local OMR settlement, Oman's RTGS (OMR-GROSS) clears high-value transactions; retail payments use the OmanNET clearing switch.
