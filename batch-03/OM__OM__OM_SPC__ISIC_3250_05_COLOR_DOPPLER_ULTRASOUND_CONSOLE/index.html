Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 does not prescribe a blanket minimum capital for an SPC; the capital is fixed in the Memorandum of Association but must be fully paid-up by the date of incorporation. For a wholly foreign-owned industrial SPC, MoCIIP industrial licensing practice expects a minimum paid-up capital of OMR 100,000; the cash portion must be deposited in the SPC's Omani bank account before issuance of the Commercial Register Certificate, evidenced by a bank capital deposit certificate. No statutory escrow account or sequestration requirement is imposed under Omani law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

      
Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

    

    

      
Tax ID Application Infrastructure

      

        
Competent tax authority
Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - National e-Services portal: taxes.gov.om (Tax Administration System 'TAS'). Customs and export clearance: Oman Customs (Royal Oman Police) via the Bayan single-window (bayan.om). Commercial registration: Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

        
E-government filing portal
The foreign-owned SPC (100% foreign ownership permitted, no minimum paid-up capital) must: (1) incorporate with MoCIIP and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) and Oman Chamber of Commerce membership; (2) within 3 months of commencing operations, register for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) with OTA through the TAS portal by uploading the CR, constitutional documents (notarized SPC deed/articles), board resolution, Omani bank account confirmation, and provisional financial statements, thereby obtaining a Tax Registration Number (TRN); (3) register separately for VAT in TAS once annual taxable supplies reach OMR 38,500 (mandatory) or OMR 19,250 (voluntary), receiving a distinct VAT identification number; (4) register as an exporter with Oman Customs/Bayan to obtain an exporter code enabling valid export declarations; (5) appoint a certified tax accountant or in-country tax agent for filings, as the SPC must maintain permanent books and records in Oman.

      

      
Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - the SPC is a resident Omani juridical person taxed at entity level on taxable income (standard CIT 15% under Income Tax Law RD 28/2009 as amended; no pass-through election). Taxation is source-based/territorial: Omani-source profits are taxable; foreign-source income of the SPC is taxable only when attributable to activity in Oman or as specified by law. Licensed free-zone/SEZAD operations may apply for statutory tax holidays; exports of medical diagnostic devices are not subject to export duties. — The foreign-owned SPC (100% foreign ownership permitted, no minimum paid-up capital) must: (1) incorporate with MoCIIP and obtain a Commercial Registration (CR) and Oman Chamber of Commerce membership; (2) within 3 months of commencing operations, register for Corporate Income Tax (CIT) with OTA through the TAS portal by uploading the CR, constitutional documents (notarized SPC deed/articles), board resolution, Omani bank account confirmation, and provisional financial statements, thereby obtaining a Tax Registration Number (TRN); (3) register separately for VAT in TAS once annual taxable supplies reach OMR 38,500 (mandatory) or OMR 19,250 (voluntary), receiving a distinct VAT identification number; (4) register as an exporter with Oman Customs/Bayan to obtain an exporter code enabling valid export declarations; (5) appoint a certified tax accountant or in-country tax agent for filings, as the SPC must maintain permanent books and records in Oman.

    

    

      
Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Entity_Level_Taxation - the SPC is a resident Omani juridical person taxed at entity level on taxable income (standard CIT 15% under Income Tax Law RD 28/2009 as amended; no pass-through election). Taxation is source-based/territorial: Omani-source profits are taxable; foreign-source income of the SPC is taxable only when attributable to activity in Oman or as specified by law. Licensed free-zone/SEZAD operations may apply for statutory tax holidays; exports of medical diagnostic devices are not subject to export duties.

        
Available election forms
No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Omani law. Available statutory elections and reliefs: (1) CIT accounting basis (cash or accrual, subject to OTA approval) and depreciation method (straight-line default; accelerated write-offs for eligible medical-imaging production assets); (2) VAT return frequency (monthly or quarterly) and voluntary VAT registration once the lower threshold is exceeded; (3) VAT input-tax recovery treatment for mixed standard-rated and zero-rated output (no proportional de minimis election under current Omani VAT rules); (4) customs duty suspension and inward-processing regimes with OTA/Customs approval; (5) no VAT grouping - each juridical person must register and file separately.

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
The export of goods to a destination outside the Sultanate is zero-rated under Art. 47 of the Omani VAT Law when the goods are physically dispatched from Oman within 90 days of the date of supply and the exporter holds documentary proof of export. Zero-rating also applies to: (i) services performed directly on exported goods; (ii) international transport and ancillary services; (iii) supplies to prescribed free-zone/SEZAD operators and qualifying international organizations; and (iv) services to non-resident recipients where the services are not consumed in Oman, subject to anti-avoidance. For color Doppler ultrasound consoles (ISIC 3250 / HS 9018.12), the exporter must trace recoverable input VAT to the zero-rated export, with input tax attributable to exempt or non-business use being non-recoverable. Export proceeds must be received via banking channels from the foreign buyer.

As of the stage date, Oman has not implemented mandatory real-time fiscal e-invoicing or digital tax-clearance stamping for VAT. VAT taxpayers must issue compliant tax invoices/credit notes (Arabic/English, sequential numbering, VAT registration references) and retain them digitally. VAT returns and payments are cleared electronically through the TAS portal using e-payment channels. All export goods must pass the Bayan single-window electronic customs clearance (declaration pre-processing, tariff/quota validation, digital release), including HS 9018.12 ultrasound/scanner line items, with freight movements subject to GCC-wide automated transit controls.

      
OTA may issue a VAT or CIT assessment within 5 years of the end of the relevant tax period (extended to 10 years where evasion or fraud exists) and may retroactively claw back input VAT recovered on a failed zero-rated export, charging late-payment interest at 1% per month on the unpaid tax (approx. 12% p.a.). Administrative fines for misreporting may reach up to OMR 25,000 per offence; tax evasion penalties under the VAT Law RD 121/2020 can reach OMR 100,000 plus imprisonment of up to 3 years. Customs false-declaration penalties include fines and confiscation of goods under the GCC Customs Law. The OTA and Oman Customs may jointly inspect stock records, transport contracts, ocean freight bills, and banking evidence; failure to demonstrate physical export or FX repatriation within the prescribed period results in reversal of zero-rating, re-issuance of the tax invoice at 5%, and loss of input-credit on the exported goods.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible under Oman's IMF Article VIII obligations; no restrictions apply to current international transactions and the capital account is effectively open for 100% foreign-owned Single Person Companies. The currency is officially pegged to the US dollar at a fixed mid-rate of approximately 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (0.3845 OMR per 1 USD), maintained by the CBO within narrow dealing margins. Profit remittance and capital repatriation are lawful under the Foreign Investment Law, but banks will block an outward dividend or liquidation payment unless the entity is fully tax-compliant and the outward remittance can be matched to audited, after-tax retained earnings. Practical hurdles include CBO balance-of-payments reporting for every conversion above the bank's documentation threshold, strict ultimate beneficial ownership identification, and a comparatively thin tax treaty network for certain supplier jurisdictions.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
There is no statutory mandatory surrender obligation and no fixed conversion ratio for export proceeds under the Omani exchange-control regime. Exporters may retain 100% of collections in a permitted foreign-currency account at a licensed Omani bank. In banking practice, the CBO requires each export collection to be matched with the underlying customs export declaration and the bill of lading within 180 days from shipment for balance-of-payments statistics; failure to repatriate on time will trigger a trade-finance review and may reduce the company's LC facility headroom. For this SPC exporting color Doppler ultrasound consoles, the bank will open a USD settlement sub-account and expect full documentation flow (commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and Ministry of Health-compatible export permit) on every export receipt.

      

      
Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails
            Fit: High
          

          
Low-Medium feasibility for purely offshore digital EMI providers. The Central Bank of Oman supervises payment services under its Payment Service Provider licensing framework; foreign EMIs cannot lawfully open a settlement account or provide inward/outbound collection services to Omani residents without a CBO license or a formal sponsorship arrangement with a licensed Omani bank. A credible path exists for an overseas EMI to act as a technical payment aggregator for the SPC's foreign distributors, but all treasury liquidity must be swept at least weekly to the Omani beneficiary account via SWIFT MT103. Legal constraint: invoicing, customs documentation, and CBO balance-of-payments reporting must remain in the name of the SPC and the local bank; the EMI may not be interposed as the counterparty of record.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

      

        The foreign shareholder effects the capital contribution by a single SWIFT MT103 (or, for very large contributions, MT103 with MT202 cover transfer) from its home bank to the Omani beneficiary bank, using the beneficiary name 'OM_SPC' with its IBAN and a clear Remittance Information (Field 70) reference: 'EQUITY SUBSCRIPTION / COMMERCIAL REGISTRATION No. OMAN-SPC / ISIC 3250 / SHARE CAPITAL'. The bank credits the funds to a temporary Capital Suspense Account until the SPC presents the Commercial Registration certificate, notarized Articles of Association, the CBO Foreign Investment data form, and license confirmation from the Ministry of Health or the regulatory medical-device authority. Purpose code on any digital banking declaration must be 'Direct Investment - Equity' (or CBO-equivalent); a licensed Omani auditor issues a Capital Verification Certificate within 30 days. To avoid structuring detection, the entire planned share capital should arrive in one or two tranches, not fragmented sums below the reporting threshold; the CBO peg ensures no significant FX conversion loss on USD inflows.Outbound procurement and settlement flows run over dual rails: (1) For imported phased-array probes, application-specific integrated circuits, and display modules, use irrevocable Commercial Letters of Credit issued under SWIFT MT700/MT707 with 90-180 day tenor to preserve working capital, negotiated with documents through the same Omani bank; (2) For urgent components, freight, and confirmed distributor commissions, use SWIFT MT103 Telegraphic Transfers with exact beneficiary identifiers and a stated underlying contract reference. Royalty and software-licence payments to non-resident algorithm vendors must be remitted net of the 10% Omani withholding tax under the Income Tax Law, with the withholding voucher submitted to the bank. Local OMR-denominated liquidity clears through the Oman Real-Time Gross Settlement (OmRTGS) system, providing same-day finality for payments to domestic suppliers; USD-denominated export receipts are cleared via correspondent banking or the US dollar clearing network, while all export letters of credit are advised via the SWIFT MT700/MT710 series with confirmation by a top-tier correspondent bank.Deliverable outright and swap FX forwards on USD/OMR, EUR/OMR, and GBP/OMR with tenors up to 12 months (extendable to 24 months by bilateral agreement), executed under ISDA 2002 with a CBO-licensed bank dealerNon-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing OMR at offshore desks for price discovery where deliverable interbank limits are constrained, used strictly to hedge receivables and payables in convertible currenciesCross-currency interest rate swaps between USD and OMR for medium-term asset-liability matching of LC facilities and capex loansFX swaps for temporary OMR/USD liquidity bridging to fund margin deposits on import LCs and pre-shipment financeEuropean-style currency options on USD/OMR, EUR/OMR, and JPY/OMR purchased for risk-capping of large supply contracts, subject to the bank's derivative dealing mandate and a board-approved non-speculative hedge policy
      

      
E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Low-Medium feasibility for purely offshore digital EMI providers. The Central Bank of Oman supervises payment services under its Payment Service Provider licensing framework; foreign EMIs cannot lawfully open a settlement account or provide inward/outbound collection services to Omani residents without a CBO license or a formal sponsorship arrangement with a licensed Omani bank. A credible path exists for an overseas EMI to act as a technical payment aggregator for the SPC's foreign distributors, but all treasury liquidity must be swept at least weekly to the Omani beneficiary account via SWIFT MT103. Legal constraint: invoicing, customs documentation, and CBO balance-of-payments reporting must remain in the name of the SPC and the local bank; the EMI may not be interposed as the counterparty of record.

Stage 06
Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

      
Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

    

    

      
Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)
15% (standard rate on taxable income exceeding OMR 100,000; a reduced rate of 3% applies to the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income under the new Income Tax Law, Royal Decree 9/2024)

      
Dividend withholding tax (WHT)
0%. Oman does not impose a domestic withholding tax on dividends or profit distributions made by an Oman SPC to its non-resident shareholder; the complete distribution may be repatriated free of Omani source withholding, leaving only home-jurisdiction taxation of the foreign shareholder.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File with transactional benchmarking analysis applies where aggregate annual controlled transactions with related parties exceed OMR 500,000 per the OTA Transfer Pricing Guidelines; a Master File and Country-by-Country Report are expected where the multinational group reports consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million; controlled-transaction disclosure on the corporate return is mandatory irrespective of the threshold.

      
Economic substance scope: Oman does not administer a standalone Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) statute as in the UAE, but the new Income Tax Law enforces economic substance through the place of effective management corporate-residency test, a general anti-avoidance rule allowing the OTA to disregard transactions lacking commercial substance, and the conditionality of the 30-year free-zone tax holiday. A foreign-owned SPC manufacturing medical imaging consoles must maintain in Oman a registered physical premises, active board and management functions, an Omani payroll, core asset ownership, and a governing corporate paper trail; otherwise OTA will re-characterize the entity, recapture tax benefits, and deny treaty access.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Filing
        
Statutory sanction: Criminal fine of OMR 5,000 to OMR 20,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years under Royal Decree 9/2024; civil recovery of the evaded tax at up to triple the shortfall plus 1% monthly surcharges; a second offence within 5 years raises the imprisonment term to up to 7 years.

        
Director personal liability: Where the SPC manager or foreign shareholder directed or approved the fraudulent filing, the corporate veil is pierced; the individual becomes jointly and severally liable for all taxes, penalties and civil restitution, and the OTA may impose travel bans, asset freezes and attachment of personal assets.

      

      
      

        Shell Company and Sham Substance
        
Statutory sanction: Denial of the tax-exempt status, re-characterization of the arrangement as non-resident or transparent, recapture of the tax holiday benefit for the entire indulgence period, administrative fines up to OMR 5,000, suspension or cancellation of the Commercial Registration, and denial of DTA benefits.

        
Director personal liability: The shareholder-manager and any de facto director may be jointly assessed for the recaptured tax and interest; in fraud scenarios the same travel-ban and personal restitution regime applies as for tax evasion.

      

      
      

        Transfer Pricing Misdeclaration
        
Statutory sanction: Primary transfer pricing adjustment by the OTA using TNMM or CUP with re-computation of taxable income; a secondary penalty of 10% of the net upward adjustment; separate documentation fines of OMR 100 to OMR 5,000; late-payment surcharge applies to the incremental tax.

        
Director personal liability: Directors and the SPC manager who signed false declarations on arm's-length compliance incur personal liability for the additional tax assessment and civil restitution to the OTA and the foreign parent.

      

      
      

        AML and Sanctions-Evasion Receivable Routing
        
Statutory sanction: Under Royal Decree 30/2016 (Anti-Money Laundering Law): imprisonment up to 10 years and fines up to OMR 50,000, in addition to confiscation of the proceeds and revocation of the commercial registration and export license; the Low_Risk_Standard banking classification does not waive customer due diligence, beneficial-ownership registration and suspicious-transaction reporting obligations.

        
Director personal liability: Directors and the SPC manager face criminal prosecution, personal disqualification from board positions, travel bans during investigation, and loss of limited liability because intentional AML violations are treated as personal acts of the natural persons behind the company.

      

      
      

        Willful Non-Filing and Persistent Late Payment
        
Statutory sanction: Estimated assessment by the OTA at the 15% head-line rate, monthly surcharge of 1% on unpaid tax capped at 50% of the liability, late-filing fines up to OMR 5,000, forfeiture of loss carryforward rights, and refusal of tax clearance leading to striking-off from the Commercial Register.

        
Director personal liability: OTA denial of tax clearance triggers personal guarantee enforcement and joint liability of the manager; directors may be subjected to travel restrictions and prohibition on leaving Oman until the corporate tax arrears are fully settled.
