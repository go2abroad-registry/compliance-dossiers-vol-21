Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — official e-service portal at https://otax.gov.om/ (Arabic/English)

E-government filing portal

The foreign-owned Omani Single Person Company (SPC) must first obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. Thereafter, the SPC must register for corporate income tax through the OTA online portal by submitting the CR, the authenticated Memorandum of Association, the sole-shareholder's passport copy, and proof of the registered office address in Oman. The OTA issues a Tax Identification Number (TIN). If the SPC makes taxable supplies in Oman exceeding the mandatory VAT threshold, it must also apply for a separate VAT registration number through the same OTA portal, submitting the TIN and the CR-compatible financial data. No notarization or local agent is required solely for tax registration, although the CR process may require a licensed commercial agent only in specific regulated sectors.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Omani tax law does not provide a US-style check-the-box election or any partnership pass-through election for an SPC; the SPC is mandatorily taxed as a separate legal entity. The only available statutory election of material relevance is the tax-group consolidation election under the Omani Executive Regulations, which allows a resident group with direct or indirect ownership of at least 75% to file a single consolidated corporate tax return. There are no hybrid mismatch elections available at the domestic level.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration is triggered by taxable supplies exceeding OMR 500,000 in the preceding 12 months; voluntary registration is available from OMR 100,000 of taxable supplies. Special mandatory registration applies to non-resident suppliers making taxable supplies that are subject to reverse charge in Oman.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating of goods is available when the goods are physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the GCC within a period not exceeding 90 days from the date of supply, provided that the exporter holds the export declaration, shipping contract and transport documents evidencing departure. Zero-rating of services is available for services supplied to a non-resident person who is outside Oman at the time of supply and who has no direct link with goods located in Oman, or for services directly ancillary to export goods. The SPC providing cybersecurity SaaS must document the contract, the non-resident status of the customer, the actual place of performance (overseas servers), and the absence of domestic exploitation to qualify for zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Single Administrative Document or electronic equivalent)
  • Bill of Lading (or airway bill / consignment note)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / Foreign Exchange Credit Statement from an Omani licensed bank

Oman is at an advanced stage of introducing mandatory e-invoicing. At present, VAT-registered persons must issue authentic invoices in a digital format that ensures gallant integrity, and should preserve the original tax invoice data files for audit. A QR-code-based invoice verification mechanism is in place for business-to-consumer invoices. However, the OTA does not yet operate a live real-time pre-clearance/fiscal-stamping API for every invoice; phased mandatory clearance through an authorized tax technology provider (TTP) network is expected in the next statutory window. The SPC should nonetheless architect billing systems to support future B2B e-invoice exchange and real-time fiscal reporting.

The OTA may retrospectively scrutinize export zero-rated claims within 5 years from the end of the relevant tax period. Where the export documentation is incomplete or the FX proceeds are not repatriated within 180 days without an approved extension, the OTA will reverse the zero-rating, assess the 5% VAT as unpaid, and impose late-payment penalties at 1% per month. Fraudulent zero-rating or refund claims can attract an additional administrative penalty of up to 100% of the tax understated, forfeiture of refund entitlements, and criminal prosecution pursuant to the Oman VAT Law and the Royal Decree on tax procedures. Voluntary correction before an audit may reduce penalties by up to 50%.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is freely convertible and maintains a hard peg to the US Dollar at 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD (CBO fixed band of approximately ±0.15%). Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; therefore current-account payments and transfers are unrestricted and do not require exchange-control approval from the CBO. Capital-account operations for a foreign-owned Oman Single Person Company are de facto liberalized: 100% foreign ownership is permitted under the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019), foreign shareholders may inject capital by SWIFT wire without a CBO permit, and profit remittances/dividends may be distributed to a non-resident shareholder once audited financial statements, board resolution, and any bank-requested tax clearance are presented. The only practical constraints are bank-level source-of-funds verification, sanctions screening, and customer due diligence; Oman does not impose a statutory convertibility restriction, multiple-currency-practice regime, or approval bottleneck for SaaS-export revenue.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman maintains no export-proceeds surrender regime and no statutory mandatory repatriation timeline. An exporting Omani SPC may retain 100% of export-related receipts in foreign-currency accounts with licensed Omani banks; there is no mandated conversion ratio into OMR, no surrender deadline, and no obligation to convert export earnings. For CBO balance-of-payments statistics and bank record-keeping, the exporter must retain underlying documentation (customs export declaration, commercial invoice, air waybill / bill of lading, and bank payment evidence with the applicable purpose code). Failure to produce underlying documents for an export credit into the SPC's account may trigger enhanced due diligence and narrative reporting to the CBO's AML/CFT unit.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — البنك المركزي العماني, established under the Central Bank of Oman Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) and functioning as the sole monetary authority and regulator of commercial banking, foreign exchange bureaux, and payment service providers in the Sultanate of Oman.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Direct onboarding of offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Stripe Treasury) as a primary treasury rail for an Omani SPC is Low viability: such entities are not licensed as banks or payment service providers by the CBO, cannot hold OMR balances, and cannot effect settlement into the Omani banking system without a locally licensed bank partner or branch. As a supplementary rail for collecting USD card revenue from offshore SaaS marketplaces, they are Medium viable provided the EMI accepts Omani CR documentation and onward settlement to the SPC's Omani bank account occurs via SWIFT MT103. For merchant card acquiring within Oman, the SPC must use a CBO-licensed payment gateway or an international acquirer with local licensing (e.g., PayTabs Oman or OmanNet-linked acquiring banks); direct Stripe acquiring is not available to an Omani-registered SPC without a foreign legal entity and offshore payment-facilitator arrangement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Foreign shareholder injection is executed via SWIFT MT103 to the SPC's account at an Omani licensed bank. Required wire particulars: Omani IBAN (23 characters beginning 'OM', comprising country code, check digits, CBO bank code, branch, and account number); beneficiary legal name matching exactly the CR extract; receiving bank's SWIFT BIC; and purpose code in field 70 as 'EQUITY INJECTION / CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / SPC SHARE CAPITAL'. The remitting sender must match the registered shareholder named in the Commercial Register and Articles of Association. On receipt, the bank may place the funds under pending-AML verification where the aggregate exceeds the SPC's declared subscribed capital or where the sender appears on sanction screening; no CBO prior approval is required, although wires above the statutory reporting threshold (approximately OMR 9,000) are captured in CBO transaction reporting and unusual same-day aggregation may trigger a Suspicious Transaction Report. After credit, the SPC should update the paid-up capital record on the Oman Business Platform using the banker's capital certificate.For a cybersecurity SaaS SPC, outbound flows comprise (i) cloud-infrastructure invoices (AWS/Azure/GCP), (ii) SaaS platform and IT tooling subscriptions, (iii) contractor consultancy fees, (iv) local payroll settled through the CBO-regulated Wages Protection System, (v) tax and banking fees, and (vi) dividends to the foreign shareholder. Morgan, Citibank, or BNY), EUR via TARGET2 correspondents — with the underlying contract, invoice, and any required tax-clearance evidence attached. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is mandatory where related-party revenue or costs exceed OMR 7,500,000 per annum; Master File applies to groups with worldwide consolidated turnover exceeding OMR 37,500,000 (Oman Tax Authority administrative thresholds) – the cybersecurity SaaS SPC must also prepare a simplified TP documentation file when related-party service/royalty flows exceed OMR 2,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations, but under Income Tax Law and OECD BEPS principles, the Tax Authority applies a substantive economic-presence test: the SPC must have a real office, operational management, board decisions in Oman, Omani bank accounts, and qualified employees performing ISIC 6201 services. A non-resident shareholder cannot claim treaty benefits or tax residency if the SPC is a shell with no local substance; the place of effective management test may reallocate profits to the foreign parent's jurisdiction.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion – Underreporting SaaS Revenue & Wire/Stripe Ommission

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment up to 5 years and/or fine of OMR 5,000; administrative penalty of 50% of the evaded tax plus 2% monthly interest; automatic rejection of the audit and reopening of all prior assessments (10-year statute of limitations).

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder and resident manager are jointly and severally liable for evaded tax, penalties, and interest; a court may pierce the SPC's limited liability, impose a travel ban, freeze personal bank accounts, and in aggravated cases, order confiscation of personal assets and actual imprisonment of directors.
Shell Company / Sham Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: Denial of tax-resident status, voidance of treaty benefits, reinstatement of 15% CIT plus 10% dividend WHT on deemed distributions; application of 5% penalty on all undeclared foreign-source income; possible 10-year tax audit window.

Director personal liability: Directors can be personally liable for the company's tax liability if the corporate form is used as a sham; the Oman Central Bank and Commerce Ministry may disqualify the director from board service, issue a travel ban, and the shareholder may be criminally prosecuted for fraudulent misrepresentation.
AML & Cross-Border Payment Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 1,000,000 under the AML Law; freezing of the SPC's bank accounts and Stripe merchant account; criminal referral to the Public Prosecutor for money laundering; revocation of the commercial license.

Director personal liability: Directors and the beneficial owner may face imprisonment of up to 10 years, personal fines up to OMR 500,000, asset forfeiture, and travel bans; the limited-liability shield is lost where AML failures are intentional or reckless.
