Governing statute

Companies Law of the Sultanate of Oman, Royal Decree No. 18/2019 (as amended), Chapter Eight on the Single Person Company (Articles 226 to 246), read together with the Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2019; supplemented by the Implementing Regulation for the Companies Law issued by MoCIIP Ministerial Decision No. 343/2019.

Formation / constitutional filings

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Under Royal Decree No. 18/2019 and Royal Decree No. 50/2019 no statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company; the sole shareholder fixes the nominal capital in the SPC Contract. The full cash capital must nevertheless be deposited in a temporary under-incorporation escrow-capital account maintained with a licensed Omani bank, and the bank's capital-deposit certificate (Shahadat Al-Iid'a) must be filed with MoCIIP before the Commercial Registration Certificate is issued. In-kind contributions are admitted only after independent valuation by an accredited valuator. All capital introductions must be traceable and documented under the AML/CFT source-of-funds rules of Royal Decree No. 30/2016.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (Royal Oman Police) fire-safety certification of the leased office premises, including inspection of fire extinguishers, emergency exits, and low-hazard occupancy compliance; structural-load and safe-occupancy verification for equipment rooms and storage areas; workplace health-and-safety clearance through the Directorate General of Labour Welfare, Ministry of Labour, confirming ergonomic and electrical safety for the digital-services workforce before staff occupancy and activation of the municipal license.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) — e-Services portal: https://otax.taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

Following company incorporation with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, the foreign-owned SPC must register with the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) within 30 days to obtain the Tax Identification Number (TIN). The TIN is issued electronically via the OTA e-Services portal after submitting the certified Articles of Association, Commercial Registration Certificate, registered office address evidence, legalized power of attorney, and identity documents of the beneficial owner and authorized signatory. For VAT purposes, Tax Registration Number (TRN) is obtained separately under Oman VAT law, required once taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold. Application processing generally completes within 5–10 business days.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — Following company incorporation with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion, the foreign-owned SPC must register with the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) within 30 days to obtain the Tax Identification Number (TIN). The TIN is issued electronically via the OTA e-Services portal after submitting the certified Articles of Association, Commercial Registration Certificate, registered office address evidence, legalized power of attorney, and identity documents of the beneficial owner and authorized signatory. For VAT purposes, Tax Registration Number (TRN) is obtained separately under Oman VAT law, required once taxable supplies exceed the statutory threshold. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election is available in Oman for a Single Person Company. The SPC is necessarily taxed at the entity level as a distinct juridical enterprise under the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009, as amended). No fiscal unity, group tax consolidation, or flow-through election is permitted. Tax treaty election mechanisms are only available with respect to treaty benefits for remittance of foreign royalties, which fall outside the scope of the SPC's internal classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,000 annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration: OMR 19,000; non-established foreign suppliers are required to register from first supply.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under Oman VAT (RD 121/2020, implementing GCC VAT Agreement) applies to the supply of goods exported to any destination outside Oman, including Gulf Cooperation Council states. For services, zero-rating applies to services supplied to a non-resident recipient if those services are not directly linked to tangible property located in Oman and are not enjoyed in Oman. This includes the supply of royalty-free stock music and commercial soundscape licenses through digital platforms to foreign end-customers, where the place-of-supply rules treat the service as external. Eligibility requires actual legal export from Oman, commercial evidence of the customer's jurisdiction, and full compliance with OTA transactional documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Royal Oman Police Customs — Bayan)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (from a licensed Omani bank evidencing receipt of foreign currency consideration)

Oman has introduced mandatory e-invoicing and e-reporting in phases from 2023 under the OTA digital invoicing framework. The OTA operates a continuous transaction control (CTC) initiative requiring VAT-compliant XML e-invoices for B2B supplies, digital signatures, and near real-time reporting for large taxpayers. For digital and remote services, registration on the OTA portal and issuance of e-invoices with secure QR codes is required. No separate physical stamping is required; fiscal clearance is mainly electronic for in-scope taxpayers.

Fraudulent or erroneous zero-rating claims attract retrospective clawback of all zero-rated VAT benefits, resulting interest at rates prescribed by the OTA, administrative fines of up to 200% of the tax evaded, and criminal penalties for intentional violations, including imprisonment up to 5 years and fines between OMR 10,000 and OMR 250,000. The Oman Tax Authority actively scrutinizes digital service exporters through cross-border banking data (FATCA/CRS) and requires full retention of the export evidence bundle for at least 10 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations, ensuring unrestricted current-account convertibility. The Omani Rial (OMR) is pegged to the U.S. dollar at USD 1 = OMR 0.3845. For a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company, capital-account transactions are administratively free of exchange control but documentary hurdles apply to profit and capital repatriation: MCIIP registration, CBO notification through a licensed bank, audited financial statements, and an Omani Tax Authority (OTA) tax clearance certificate are required before remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio. The SPC may retain 100% of export receipts (including Stripe and PayPal settlements) in foreign-currency accounts at licensed banks. There is no statutory conversion timeline; however, proceeds must ultimately be traceable into an onshore licensed-bank account, recorded in the annual commercial register filing, supported by customer invoices and platform settlement statements, and remain available for CBO BoP reporting and OTA inspections.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – Foreign Exchange Inspectorate / Banking Supervision Department

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Feasibility rating: Low as a primary local banking rail; Medium only as a supplementary foreign-currency collection processor. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The sole shareholder injects equity via SWIFT MT103 directly to the SPC's licensed commercial-bank account, using the CBO balance-of-payments classification group for direct-investment equity capital (1121–1200) and an unambiguous narrative: 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / OM_SPC / CR-XXXX / MCIIP APPROVAL REF'. The receiving bank authenticates the credit against the current CR extract and shareholders' capital-increase minutes, secures an MCIIP/KYC source-of-funds statement, files the CBO inward foreign transfer report, and issues an inward remittance certificate for the audit file. No OMR surrender or conversion is required; the balance may remain in OMR or USD as chosen at account opening. Correspondent-bank straight-through credits and foreign-currency check assimilation are permissible alternative rails provided the beneficiary name exactly matches the SPC and the investment-purpose test is satisfied.Outbound disbursements are dominated by intangible-license operational payments rather than physical trade. The primary clearing rail is SWIFT MT103/MT202 for (i) contractual royalty sharing with music producers, (ii) Stripe/PayPal platform fees and SaaS subscriptions, and (iii) profit repatriation to the foreign shareholder, each supported by contracts, invoices, and tax invoices as applicable. Retail refunds are cleared through Stripe/PayPal card-network treasury and periodically swept into the bank account with reconciliation memos. Commercial documentary letters of credit (MT700) are unsuitable for digital licensing services and are not recommended. Domestic OMR statutory fees, lease payments, and salaries are settled via the CBO's domestic ACH/eFT clearing rails. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat corporate income tax on all taxable income of a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC); the 0% up to OMR 10,000 and 3% up to OMR 100,000 preferential bands apply exclusively to wholly Omani/GCC-owned small enterprises and do not extend to foreign-owned SPCs.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 4 months of the tax year-end; for a 31 December fiscal year-end, the return is due by 30 April of the following year. The OTA may not grant blanket extension, and payment of the final tax liability falls due on the same date.
