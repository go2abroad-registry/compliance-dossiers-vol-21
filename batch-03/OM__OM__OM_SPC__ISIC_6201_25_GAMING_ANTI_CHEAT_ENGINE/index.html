Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), e-Services Portal at www.taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

Foreign entity must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); after CR issuance, entity files tax registration with Oman Tax Authority via the e-Services portal and is issued a Tax Identification Number (TIN) for CIT and a separate VAT registration number where the VAT mandatory threshold is met. Non-resident entities with no Oman establishment must appoint a tax representative and register for withholding tax obligations through the same portal.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope — Foreign entity must first obtain Commercial Registration (CR) from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP); after CR issuance, entity files tax registration with Oman Tax Authority via the e-Services portal and is issued a Tax Identification Number (TIN) for CIT and a separate VAT registration number where the VAT mandatory threshold is met. Non-resident entities with no Oman establishment must appoint a tax representative and register for withholding tax obligations through the same portal.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election regime in Oman. An SPC is mandatorily taxed at entity level on Oman-source income; shareholders are not separately taxed on entity profits, and distributions are neither deductible nor taxable. Sector-specific elections (e.g., real estate investment schemes, hydrocarbon production sharing contracts) and the standard deemed-deduction rules apply; no elective pass-through classification is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 of annual taxable supplies; voluntary registration: OMR 19,250; non-resident suppliers of taxable goods/services to non-taxable customers in Oman must register irrespective of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies where goods are physically exported from Oman to a non-GCC destination and evidence of customs export and physical movement exists; services supplied to a non-resident recipient who is outside Oman at the time of supply, where the benefit of the service is consumed outside Oman and the service is not directly connected to immovable property in Oman; and supplies of internationally transported goods and related services. For gaming anti-cheat software licensing as an offshore service, zero rating requires the recipient to be a non-resident taxable or non-taxable person outside Oman, payment in foreign currency, and no effective use or enjoyment in Oman.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill
  • Commercial Invoice evidencing sale
  • VAT export invoice and proof of VAT status of recipient
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT evidence of foreign currency receipt
  • Certificate of Origin (where required by export rules)

Oman's e-invoicing mandate is being phased in by OTA and the Oman Electronic Transaction Authority through approved e-invoicing providers. Large taxpayers must issue machine-readable XML invoices and transmit them via API to the OTA clearance gateway within prescribed timelines; no physical fiscal stamp or real-time inline clearance exists for all taxpayers yet, but digital signature, QR-code encoding, and structured e-invoice exchange are mandatory once phased in for the entity's turnover bracket.

Oman Tax Authority may re-audit zero-rated export claims within the statutory limitation period; upon failure to substantiate export movement, offshore receipt, or timely FX repatriation, the zero rating is clawed back as standard-rated output VAT, assessable with late payment penalties of 1% per month and a maximum fixed penalty; deliberate fraudulent claims attract an administrative penalty of up to 200% of the tax evaded and/or criminal liability including imprisonment under the Oman VAT Law, and responsible officers/members of the SPC may be jointly and severally liable.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the US Dollar (USD) at 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD and is fully convertible for current account transactions, including trade in services, royalties, and operating expenses. Capital account convertibility is open for registered foreign investment under the Foreign Capital Investment Law, with direct share capital contributions and profit repatriation permitted after tax and other statutory clearances. Practical hurdles are limited to compliance with CBO's monitoring thresholds, anti-money laundering documentation, and the absence of any general exchange allocation system for OMR.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory surrender or mandatory percentage conversion of export proceeds into OMR. Exporters may retain foreign exchange receipts in external or domestic foreign-currency accounts. CBO guidelines require repatriation of trade proceeds through licensed banks within a period consistent with underlying trade terms (typically 90–180 days) and submission of entry/(shipment) documents for the purposes of balance-of-payments reporting; no conversion requirement otherwise applies.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) – the exclusive monetary, banking, and foreign exchange regulatory authority under the Omani Banking Law and the CBO Foreign Exchange Regulations; subsidiary exchange control administration is exercised through licensed domestic commercial banks acting as reporting and settlement agents.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: LOW. The Central Bank of Oman does not permit foreign e-money institutions (EMIs), non-bank virtual account providers, or offshore fintech 'neo-banks' to establish operating accounts for Omani corporate entities without obtaining a full CBO licence and, in practice, a physical representative office or branch. International payment platforms such as Stripe are not licensed or locally settled in Oman; a Stripe merchant account for the OM_SPC would require a separate legal entity in a supported jurisdiction and does not replace the mandatory CBO-licensed banking relationship and SWIFT-based clearing rails.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident shareholder's bank account to the SPC's CBO-licensed bank account (OMR or USD). The remittance information field (Field 70) must expressly state: 'Equity capital contribution to OM_SPC' and include the CR number. For CBO balance-of-payments classification, instruct the remitting bank to quote the transaction as 'Direct Investment – Equity Capital' (typically purpose code 'C1' or the corresponding ITRS/BOP code used by the receiving bank). The Oman receiving bank will require a board/shareholder resolution, CR registration, and, when the amount exceeds automated reporting thresholds, a formal source-of-funds declaration. No capital deposit to a separate escrow is legally required, but the bank may open a capital escrow sub-account solely to evidence the receipt and conversion of equity funding.The OM_SPC will use SWIFT MT103 single-shot payments and MT202 for interbank liquidity concentration for all SaaS-royalty, licencing, and contractor disbursements. For high-value or recurring contractual settlements, a periodically netted multi-currency cash pool may be established at the local bank using the same correspondent network. Commercial letters of credit (MT700) and standby LCs are not typical for software middleware; where a software contract requires a documentary instrument, Omani banks may issue a standby LC only after the company provides an audited financial position and sufficient collateral. Stripe is not a viable clearing rail inside Oman; the company may integrate Stripe only through a foreign subsidiary or marketplace processor, meaning that the legally compliant and priority clearing route is SWIFT global payment innovation (gpi) participants and CBO-regulated correspondent banks.OMR/USD forward contracts – permitted with any CBO-licensed commercial bank; margin-based and collateralised, though the fixed peg minimises hedging need.Cross-currency swaps for EUR/OMR, GBP/OMR, and INR/OMR exposures – available from licensed Omani banks and international branches for trade and capital-flow matching.Currency options (put/call, OTC customised) for non-USD revenue in EMEA markets – authorisable on a deal-by-deal basis under CBO dealer guidance.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) – legally permissible in Oman for exotic or inconvertible trading currencies; practically unavailable against OMR because of the USD peg, but usable for third-currency receivables where local withdrawability is uncertain.
