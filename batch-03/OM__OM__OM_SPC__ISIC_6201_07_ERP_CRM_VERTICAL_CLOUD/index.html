Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), Commercial Registration Department, Invest Easy One-Stop Shop.

Governing statute

Royal Decree No. 18/2019 (Oman Companies Law), as amended, including provisions on Single Person Companies, share capital, registered office, and corporate governance; Royal Decree No. 50/2019 (Foreign Capital Investment Law) and Ministerial Decision No. 133/2020 (Executive Regulation of the Foreign Capital Investment Law).

Formation / constitutional filings

Authenticated Memorandum and Articles of Association (Memorandum of Incorporation), accompanied by Form M-01 Application for Commercial Registration and Invest Easy electronic filing.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 27 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for an SPC under RD 18/2019; subscribed capital is fixed in the Memorandum. For a wholly foreign-owned SPC, the stated capital must be deposited into a temporary Oman-based bank account and the Capital Deposit Certificate must be delivered before issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate. Capital can be expressed in OMR or USD.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) for the registered office; structural load and occupancy compliance approval from Muscat Municipality; and workplace health-and-safety clearance under the Oman Occupational Safety and Health Ministerial Decision No. 286/2008 must be obtained before physical occupation of the office.

For a wholly foreign-owned SPC, the stated capital must be deposited into a temporary Oman-based bank account and the Capital Deposit Certificate must be delivered before issuance of the Commercial Registration Certificate. Capital can be expressed in OMR or USD.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General of Taxation (SGT) of the Sultanate of Oman, attached to the Ministry of Finance; unified tax administration for corporate income tax and VAT. Official e-portal: taxes.gov.om (SGT e-Tax System), which hosts TIN registration, VAT registration, VAT return filing and taxpayer e-services.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) through the Commercial Registry / Invest Oman e-portal to obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; the SPC may be 100% foreign-owned without a statutory minimum capital. Step 2: Register for corporate income tax with the SGT on taxes.gov.om, filing the CR certificate, constitutional documents (AOA/MOA), Power of Attorney, shareholder and manager identification, and the Omani registered address, to obtain the Tax Identification Number (TIN). Step 3: Register for VAT on the same portal (VAT ID) once taxable/zero-rated supplies exceed OMR 500,000 in any 12-month period, or voluntarily at OMR 250,000; a zero-rated SaaS/ERP exporter should voluntarily register to recover input VAT.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation - The Omani SPC is a distinct juridical person and is not fiscally transparent; profits are taxed at the entity level and there is no pass-through to the foreign shareholder. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as replaced by the new Income Tax Law issued under Royal Decree 78/2024 for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) at a graduated rate of 3% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income and 15% on the excess. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation - The Omani SPC is a distinct juridical person and is not fiscally transparent; profits are taxed at the entity level and there is no pass-through to the foreign shareholder. Resident companies are taxed on worldwide income under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as replaced by the new Income Tax Law issued under Royal Decree 78/2024 for tax years beginning on or after 1 January 2025) at a graduated rate of 3% on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income and 15% on the excess. The cloud software/ERP revenue is Oman-source to the extent the SPC is managed from Oman; qualifying foreign-branch profits are exempted upon satisfaction of foreign-tax-paid documentation.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or pass-through election exists; an SPC cannot elect to be tax-transparent. Available statutory elections: (a) voluntary VAT registration at the OMR 250,000 threshold; (b) VAT group registration allowing qualifying related Omani entities to file as a single taxable person; (c) election of the financial year-end for CIT and VAT filing periods; and (d) application of double tax treaty relief (including PE and foreign-tax-credit claims) via the SGT advance ruling/clearance process. Hybrid-mismatch rules are not enacted in Oman, so no elective treatment is available for reverse-hybrid or disregarded-entity structures.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 of taxable and zero-rated supplies over any trailing 12-month period; voluntary registration: OMR 250,000. A foreign-owned SPC whose principal output is zero-rated exported SaaS/ERP cloud services is eligible and generally recommended to register voluntarily to recover input VAT.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rated when physically exported to a destination outside the GCC, supported by a customs export declaration proving removal from Oman within the prescribed time window. Services: zero-rated when supplied to a non-resident recipient located and established outside the GCC, provided the services (including hosted ERP/CRM cloud subscriptions and software licenses) are not used or enjoyed in Oman and are not directly connected with immovable property or goods situated in Oman. The supplier must evidence the non-resident status and location of the customer, the offshore place of supply, the service delivery and usage records, and, in related-party SaaS arrangements, compliant transfer pricing documentation filed with the SGT.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) duly manifested by Oman Customs (Directorate General of Customs) evidencing physical export of goods outside the GCC member states.
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) showing the Omani port/airport of loading, the non-GCC destination, and the consignee located outside the GCC.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT MT103 bank credit confirmation showing receipt of the invoiced foreign-exchange export proceeds in the SPC's Omani bank account within the statutory FX repatriation period.
  • For digital cloud/SaaS exports: signed electronic service-delivery certificate, IP/geolocation usage logs of the non-resident customer, and a customer VAT-status certificate evidencing that the recipient is established outside the GCC.

Oman does not currently enforce a real-time fiscal-clearance e-invoicing regime, and there is no mandatory API clearance or digital stamping requirement. The SGT e-tax portal requires electronic filing of VAT returns and the issuance of tax invoices containing the statutory particulars (seller and buyer VAT IDs, line-level rates, and the applicable zero-rating basis). Taxpayers must retain electronic tax invoice registers in the prescribed SGT format for the statutory record-keeping period; the SGT is progressively expanding digital tax administration, so cross-border SaaS providers should maintain machine-readable transaction logs and invoice-exchange protocols to be audit-ready.

Export zero-rating is a priority SGT audit area. Where a zero-rating claim is unsupported or the export proceeds are not repatriated within the 180-day statutory window, the SGT recharacterizes the output as taxable at 5%, issues an assessment recovering output VAT, and clawbacks the input VAT credits previously refunded. Monetary penalties escalate with gravity: administrative infraction fines, a penalty equal to or exceeding the tax understated for fraudulent claims, and, in criminal cases of forged export documentation, fines up to OMR 25,000 and imprisonment. The taxpayer bears strict liability to produce the complete documentary bundle (customs declaration, B/L/AWB, IRC and usage logs) during audit or refund review.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible and officially pegged to the United States dollar at approximately 1 USD = OMR 0.3845. Oman accepts IMF Article VIII obligations; current-account payments, capital transfers, equity injections, and profit/dividend remittances are generally free from exchange-control approval. There is no mandatory conversion of export receipts and no rationing of foreign currency. Practical profit-remittance hurdles are documentary rather than regulatory: the Oman-domiciled SPC must maintain accurate statutory share capital records, board/shareholder resolutions for any distribution, audited or management financial statements, and evidence of settlement of Oman Tax Authority obligations. Banks apply standard KYC/AML verification of the underlying source of funds and of the beneficial owners before executing large outward remittances.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio applies to export proceeds in Oman outside specific hydrocarbon/mining concession arrangements. Exporters and cross-border service providers may retain receipts in foreign-currency accounts with CBO-licensed banks and may convert voluntarily. For physical merchandise trade, banks customarily monitor that export proceeds are collected within a reasonable commercial period, generally aligned with the underlying contract terms and shipping documentation; overdue export receivables may trigger bank-level follow-up for provisioning under CBO prudential norms. For software and vertical ERP/CRM cloud services, there is no customs-controlled export surrender; receipts follow the contract and invoicing terms and are reported to the bank as current-account service exports.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), the monetary authority and banking supervisor of the Sultanate of Oman, currently governed by the CBO Banking and Financial Institutions Law (Royal Decree 114/2000 as amended). Oman does not maintain a separate foreign-exchange administration; exchange-control and AML/CFT supervision are conducted through CBO-licensed commercial banks and authorized money exchange companies.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-to-Medium without CBO licensing or a local sponsorship structure. Oman has no passporting regime for offshore electronic-money institutions. A non-resident EMI cannot independently open an OMR-denominated corporate account or act as a regulated payment service provider to the Omani public unless it holds an authorized licence from the Central Bank of Oman or operates through a licensed local bank/payment-service provider. For the SPC's own treasury, an overseas EMI may be used only as an ancillary collection-side node for Stripe/card settlement if the SPC maintains its principal operating account and capital accounts at an Omani bank. The legally safer design is to use a CBO-licensed Omani bank as the treasury anchor and to receive cross-border card settlement directly into that bank account.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Group organizational chart; certificates of incorporation and good standing for every intermediate entity; completed UBO register declaration; passport and residential address proof for each natural person, including percentage of shareholding; bank references; source-of-wealth statement; independent AML/sanctions/PEP screening report; legal opinion confirming the chain of control and absence of politically exposed persons.
Newly registered SPC with minimal local substance and economic activities performed mainly outside Oman

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The preferred routing is a SWIFT MT103 from the non-resident shareholder's bank to the SPC's OMR/USD operational account at a CBO-licensed Omani bank. Field 70 should state the clear purpose: 'Equity capital injection under Omani Commercial Companies Law' and reference the SPC's commercial registration number. The receiving bank will classify the transaction for balance-of-payments purposes as direct investment/equity capital. No prior CBO licence is required for a 100% foreign-owned SPC in permitted non-strategic sectors; the bank requires the shareholder/sponsor board resolution, share subscription journal, capital allocation letter, source-of-wealth dossier, and updated company extract showing the paid-up capital. Funds arriving in USD should be converted to OMR only when needed to minimize foreign-exchange friction. The SPC must post the capital receipt in its statutory share register and file any required Oman Tax Authority or commercial registry modifications.For cross-border supplier and trade payments, use SWIFT MT103 for third-party commercial payments and MT202 for interbank/cover transfers when using a correspondent bank. Large hardware or platform procurement may be supported by a commercial documentary letter of credit using MT700/MT707. For the SPC's software and SaaS revenue collections, inbound settlement arrives through card networks and Stripe Billing in USD/EUR and is credited to the Omani bank account by SWIFT. For domestic recurring collections, the Omani bank should establish a bank-sponsored direct-debit mandate under the local automated clearing-house rail, with the SPC as creditor and its clients as paying parties.
