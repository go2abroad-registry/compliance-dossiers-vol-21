Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019 as amended; the Articles of Association must state the subscribed capital and the sole shareholder must fully pay the subscribed capital within the period fixed therein. The free zone authority may require a bank certificate evidencing adequate working capital or a bond threshold (commonly OMR 3,000–10,000) proportionate to the bond operations, and paid-up capital may be held in an ordinary corporate bank account in the Company's name rather than a statutory escrow account unless the zone authority so directs.

Funding & unlock path: CDAA (Royal Oman Police) Fire and Life Safety Certificate: suppression system design reviewed against NFPA and GCC Fire Code requirements; fire hydrant, extinguisher and alarm coverage verified for the warehouse envelope; structural load certificate from a licensed civil engineer confirming racking and mezzanine load capacities; occupational safety clearance covering emergency exits, evacuation signage, ventilation, lighting, and hazardous-substance segregation; annual recertification is mandatory, and a pre-opening on-site inspection must be completed before the bonded depot commences operations.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019 as amended; the Articles of Association must state the subscribed capital and the sole shareholder must fully pay the subscribed capital within the period fixed therein. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) - General Authority for Taxation - official e-services portal: https://tax.gov.om (OTA Tax Portal). Customs administration is exercised by the Royal Oman Police - Customs Directorate through the single-window Bayan system; business incorporation is handled by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) via the Invest Easy portal; free-zone licensing is issued by the respective free zone authorities (Salalah Free Zone, Sohar Free Zone, Al-Mazunah Free Zone, Khazaen Economic City, and Duqm Special Economic Zone).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) through the Invest Easy portal (MCIIP) to obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate with the precise bonded-warehouse activity classification under ISIC 5210; Step 2 - Apply to the Oman Tax Authority for the Corporate Tax Number (Tax Card/TIN) within 3 months of commencing business, submitting the CR, free-zone operating licence, audited opening balance sheet, lease or usufruct agreement, and passport/POA copies of the sole shareholder and directors; Step 3 - For bonded depots located inside a Free Trade Zone, first obtain the Free Zone Operating Licence (which incorporates Customs Bonded Warehouse registration) and then file the CIT tax registration with the OTA citing the free-zone licence number; Step 4 - Submit the VAT registration application (Form VAT-001) through the OTA e-Services Portal within 30 days after the mandatory registration threshold is met or is reasonably expected to be met, and receive the VAT Registration Number (VRN); foreign entities with no physical Omani establishment may be required to appoint a resident tax representative for VAT filing and service of process.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the SPC is a resident juridical person and is mandatorily taxed at the entity level on worldwide income. Oman CIT headline rates: 3% on taxable income up to OMR 100,000 (only for Omani-majority SMEs) and 15% on income above OMR 100,000; a 100% foreign-owned SPC normally falls outside the SME reduced rate and faces the flat 15% CIT. Free Trade Zone entities receive a statutory CIT exemption of 10-30 years (renewable for 15 additional years in Duqm SEZ) on qualifying zone-source income, conditional on maintaining audited free-zone books, satisfying the substance requirements, and complying with the free-zone licensing conditions; however, the exemption is territorial and does not extend to sales made into the Omani customs territory or to services consumed in Oman. For VAT purposes, the free-zone bonded depot operates under the outside-the-scope-of-VAT carve-out for goods entering and leaving the zone, although taxable supplies made from the zone into Oman attract VAT at the statutory rate.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box pass-through election; the SPC is unambiguously an entity-level taxpayer under the Tax Procedure Law (RD 62/2023) and the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009, as amended). Available statutory elections: (a) VAT Grouping election for related parties under Article 41 of the VAT Law (RD 121/2020), permitting the bonded-depot SPC to aggregate VAT liabilities with its parent or sister entities; (b) HIVA election for VAT return frequency - monthly filing may be elected by exporters and zero-rated suppliers to obtain speedier refunds, otherwise quarterly filing is the default; (c) irrevocable election to apply the free-zone outside-the-scope VAT treatment on goods warehoused in the bonded depot pending export; (d) election to zero-rate (rather than exempt or outside-scope) qualifying supplies of storage, handling, and inspection services used in connection with export; (e) CIT tax-loss carry-forward election allowing losses to be carried forward for 5 years, subject to formal continuity-of-business documentation; (f) no hybrid-mismatch or reverse-hybrid election is available under Omani domestic law, and the OECD BEPS Action 2 hybrid rules apply as a default mandatory rule.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Rial Omani (OMR) is fully convertible for current account transactions, including trade settlement, service fees, and profit remittances, subject to documentary verification by licensed banks. Capital account flows, including inbound direct investment into a 100% foreign-owned SPC, are permissible with an approved purpose code and require maintaining supporting documentation. There is no foreign exchange law blocking profit repatriation; however, banks apply standard know-your-client and anti-money-laundering checks, and the Oman Tax Authority must issue a clearance certificate before material dividend remittances. No beyond-resident statutory conversion of FX is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory surrender or forced conversion of export proceeds. A Free Zone bonded depot operator may retain foreign currency receipts in a CBO-licensed foreign currency account or convert as desired. The licensed bank is obligated to report export flow data to the CBO's statistics and balance-of-payments system, and exporters must retain customs warehouse evidence and underlying sales contracts to ensure banks' AML/CFT and sanctions compliance.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Offshore fintech EMIs are a Low primary viability for this official bonded depot because the SPC needs a CBO-regulated relationship, documentary credit capacity, and direct access to Omani local clearing. An offshore EMI may be used only as an ancillary collection or disbursement tool where it holds a FATF-rated license, provides transparent audit trails, and the SPC still maintains its main liquidity ors and trade-finance rail at an Omani commercial bank. Banks additionally impose ultimate beneficial owner transparency and may reject flows from EMIs that are not licensed as credit institutions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where the annual aggregate value of controlled transactions (purchases, storage fees, warehousing charges, interest, management costs) between the Omani SPC and related parties exceeds OMR 500,000. Master File required where the group-wide Oman-related connected-party transactions exceed OMR 3,000,000 and the group's consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 37,500,000. Country-by-Country (CbC) Report required for groups with consolidated revenue of OMR 375,000,000 or above, under OTA Ministerial Decision No. 44/2021 incorporating the OECD three-tier documentation principle.

Economic substance scope: Oman has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations code comparable to the UAE. However, economic substance is enforced through four channels: (i) free-zone licensing and customs-bonded warehouse statutes require an actual physical depot, resident warehouse manager, licensed customs bond, and regular inventory turnover within the zone; (ii) the Oman Tax Authority applies general anti-avoidance rules and place-of-effective-management concepts under Sultan Decree 7/2023 to disregard shell arrangements without people, premises and operations; (iii) controlled foreign company rules attribute undistributed low-taxed offshore profits to an Omani controlling shareholder where no genuine economic activities support the foreign structure; and (iv) treaty entitlement is denied where the recipient lacks beneficial ownership or a substantive business reason for holding the Omani SPC.
