Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Omani law does not prescribe a statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Foreign-Owned Single Person Company. The issued capital must be fully subscribed and paid in at incorporation as stated in the MAA, and deposited in a bank account opened for the company; no separate escrow account is required unless the activity-specific regulator imposes such a condition.

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Secretariat General of Taxation (SGT), Ministry of Finance, Sultanate of Oman; mandatory e-services, VAT returns, and invoicing clearance are operated through the Oman Tax Portal 'Taxoman' (apps.taxoman.gov.om / TMS e-Services).

E-government filing portal

1) Establish the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) through the integrated investor platform and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) and municipal trade license; the CR embodies the foreign-investor registration approval. 2) File the statutory Tax Registration application on the Oman Tax Portal within 60 days of CR issuance, attaching the CR, SPC articles/constituent deed, foreign shareholder identity documents, power of attorney, local bank account certificate, and fiscal-domicile statement; the Secretariat General of Taxation issues the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN). 3) Register for VAT through the same portal when actual or expected 12-month taxable supplies exceed OMR 500,000, or voluntarily once turnover exceeds OMR 38,500, and obtain the 15-digit VAT registration number and e-invoice account activation. 4) Onboard to the e-invoicing environment by certifying the XML invoice issuer profile and API/token credentials of the SPC's billing or ERP system. 5) Keep the SGT profile updated on shareholding, legal-form, fiscal-year, and invoice-issuing changes and archive all registration certificates for the statutory retention period.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation: the SPC is a resident juridical person taxed in its own name under the Omani corporate income tax regime, with chargeable profits assessed at the current Omani head rates (3% and 15% bands depending on taxable income). No pass-through election is available to the sole foreign shareholder. Oman applies a worldwide-profits scope to resident companies with relief for tax-paid foreign branch profits and participation-exempt foreign dividends; Omani-source patent-agency and trademark-prosecution fees remain within the domestic tax net.

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a US-style check-the-box classification or OECD hybrid-mismatch election for single-member companies. Election rights available in the Oman tax framework for the SPC include: (i) choice of a non-calendar fiscal year with SGT approval; (ii) eligible election of depreciation/amortization methods for fixed and IP assets; (iii) participation-exemption election for qualifying foreign shareholdings subject to foreign tax; (iv) grouping election for VAT registration of related Omani legal persons under a single VAT account; (v) voluntary VAT registration election once taxable turnover exceeds OMR 38,500 and prior to reaching the mandatory OMR 500,000 threshold; and (vi) application for an Advance Pricing Arrangement or mutual agreement procedure to resolve transfer-pricing and double-tax exposure.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration when taxable supplies in the preceding 12 months or expected next 12 months reach OMR 500,000; voluntary VAT registration when taxable supplies exceed OMR 38,500; for income tax, every incorporated SPC must obtain a TIN regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically removed from Oman to a destination outside the GCC are zero-rated; services qualify for zero-rating where the purchaser is a non-resident outside the GCC, the benefit is wholly received and enjoyed outside the Sultanate, and the services do not relate to land, goods located in Oman, or an Omani recipient. For the SPC's core business, international PCT patent prosecution, trademark registration, and foreign IP renewal services satisfy the zero-rating test where the client is a foreign non-GCC principal, the filings are made at overseas intellectual-property registries, and the rights are not exploited in Oman. Intra-GCC supplies to clients resident in another GCC state remain subject to the normal place-of-supply VAT rules and cannot be zero-rated as exports.

  • Customs export declaration (Bayan) lodged through the Oman Customs National Single Window/Bayangate showing the export procedure code, HS classification, quantity, and non-GCC destination
  • Transport document evidencing physical exit from Oman: ocean Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or international CMR road waybill
  • Zero-rated VAT tax invoice, in Arabic/English, carrying the SPC's 15-digit VAT number and the statement 'zero-rated export supply under the Oman VAT Law'
  • Inward remittance evidence: SWIFT MT103/MT202 credit advice, bank statement entry, or Inward Remittance Certificate from the Omani receiving bank matching the commercial invoice and foreign principal's identity
  • Service completion file replacing the customs transcript for pure IP services: signed engagement contract, PCT/foreign trademark filing receipts, power-of-attorney docket, and foreign registry correspondence proving delivery and beneficial enjoyment of the supply outside Oman

The SGT's national e-invoicing program is operational via the Taxoman platform. VAT-registered taxpayers must issue Arabic/English tax invoices in structured XML format carrying the 15-digit VAT number, unique sequential invoice identifier, date of supply, customer details, and the statutory zero-rating certificate wording where applicable. Taxpayers onboarded in SGT clearance waves transmit invoice datasets to the Taxoman portal in near real time through API/ERP integration and receive a digital clearance token or QR mark before issuance; taxable persons not yet onboarded remain subject to the standing invoice-data retention, monthly listing, and clearance-readiness duties. Non-compliant invoice architecture may void the documentary defense of a zero-rated export in audit.

SGT audit performs bill-level reconciliation of zero-rated claims against Bayangate manifests, shipping track-and-trace records, freight forwarder certifications, foreign-registry filing confirmations, bank payment flows, and FX repatriation within the 180-day administrative benchmark. Incomplete or contradictory export files cause reversal of the 0% rate, re-assessment at 5% VAT, monthly statutory late-payment surcharge from the original return due date, and imposed administrative fines per defective invoice (commonly OMR 1,000–10,000). Deliberate fabrication of export or foreign-receipt evidence is treated as VAT evasion, triggering retrospective clawbacks across open assessment periods, fines up to OMR 25,000 in aggravated cases, imprisonment, public listing of the taxpayer, and disqualification of the foreign client's input-credit position in Oman.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible on both current and capital accounts under Oman's IMF Article VIII obligations and the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019). The CBO maintains a de jure peg of 1 OMR = 2.6008 USD, with commercial banks quoting narrow dealing spreads around parity and the CBO defending the rate in the interbank market. For this PCT prosecution agency: (a) current-account payments - PCT international fees, trademark office fees, WIPO annuities, and disbursements to foreign associates - require no prior CBO approval and clear freely through licensed banks; (b) capital-account flows - equity injection into the SPC, shareholder loans, dividend repatriation, and liquidation proceeds - are lawful but must be classified under BPM6 direct-investment codes and evidenced with documentation retained for ten years under the AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree 30/2016). Profit-remittance hurdles are documentary only: the SPC must produce audited financial statements and a tax-clearance certificate from the Oman Tax Authority, and must settle or treaty-relieve the 10% dividend withholding tax imposed by the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009) before the outward dividend wire is released. No other convertibility restriction, currency rationing, or approval gate exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Oman imposes no export-proceeds surrender deadline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation timeline for export receipts or cross-border service revenues. The SPC may retain 100% of foreign-currency income in a CBO-licensed USD/EUR/GBP account, convert only the OMR tranche required for local operating expenditure, and apply the remaining balance directly to USD-denominated patent-office payments. The receiving bank is required only to record each inflow under the applicable BPM6 trade-in-services classification for legal and IP advisory services and, where the remittance reaches the SPC through a fintech intermediary, to preserve the signed engagement contract, payer identification data, and payment-method trail supporting the underlying transaction.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - established under Royal Decree 7/1974 and governed by the Banking Law promulgated by Royal Decree 114/2000 - is the licensing, supervisory, and monetary authority for all banks and payment institutions in the Sultanate of Oman. The CBO administers the Omani Rial (OMR) peg to the US Dollar, supervises the Muscat Clearing House and national payment rails, and receives BPM6 balance-of-payments and foreign-exchange transaction reports from all licensed commercial banks.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium - legally viable only as a supplementary disbursement rail, never as the primary treasury or settlement account. Wise Business (UK FCA-authorised EMI) and similar offshore digital EMIs cannot hold OMR, deploy OMR clearing, or issue Omani IBANs without a CBO payment-institution licence under the National Payment Systems Law; hence all client receipts must ultimately settle into a licensed Omani bank account to satisfy the SPC's commercial registration, VAT, and statutory-audit obligations. Wise is operationally attractive for low-value (under USD 10,000) official-fee payments to WIPO, USPTO, EPO, and CNIPA owing to embedded FX conversion and reachability to SEPA, Fedwire, CHATS, and CIPS local rails, but the SPC must maintain a dual-control approval matrix, a pre-approved beneficiary registry, monthly reconciliation sub-ledgers, and must route the capital injection, dividend payments, and Oman Tax Authority payments exclusively through the Tier-1 bank.
