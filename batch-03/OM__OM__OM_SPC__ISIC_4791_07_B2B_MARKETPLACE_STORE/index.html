Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman - unified national tax portal: https://pta.om

E-government filing portal

The foreign founder incorporates a Single Person Company (SPC) under the Oman Companies Law (RD 18/2019) with 100% non-resident ownership and no statutory minimum capital. Following issuance of the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate and municipal license, the SPC must register with the Oman Tax Authority for income tax and obtain a Tax Identification Number (TIN) and annual Tax Card within 30 days of incorporation or commencement of taxable activity. Registration is effected through the OTA electronic portal (pta.om) by submitting the CR, notarised Memorandum/Articles of Association, registered office lease, proof of shareholders and beneficial owners, and a locally executed power of attorney. The SPC must separately enroll for VAT (including, where applicable, the non-resident VAT registration gateway) and register with the Social Protection Fund. The Tax Card is renewed annually and is a precondition for customs clearance, bank account operation and government contracting.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: The SPC is a resident juridical entity taxed separately from its shareholder at 15% on Oman-source income under the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009 as amended by RD 9/2017). No partnership pass-through is available. Dividends distributed out of Omani-sourced profits already taxed in Oman are exempt from further income tax. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope: The SPC is a resident juridical entity taxed separately from its shareholder at 15% on Oman-source income under the Income Tax Law (RD 28/2009 as amended by RD 9/2017). No partnership pass-through is available. Dividends distributed out of Omani-sourced profits already taxed in Oman are exempt from further income tax. Non-resident shareholders are generally not subject to withholding tax on dividend distributions from an Oman-incorporated SPC, but interest, royalties and management fees paid to non-residents are subject to 10% withholding tax unless reduced by a Double Tax Treaty.

Available election forms

Oman does not employ a check-the-box classification system. Available statutory elections and administrative mechanisms include: (i) election of fiscal year and accounting method, subject to OTA approval; (ii) consolidated tax filing for a wholly-owned group of Omani resident companies; (iii) tax holiday and exemption elections for licensed entities in SEZAD and free zones (e.g., Duqm, Sohar, Salalah) offering up to 30-year income tax exemptions; (iv) simplified tax prepayment regimes for qualifying SMEs; and (v) transfer-pricing contemporaneous documentation waivers within OECD-aligned safe harbours. Hybrid mismatch and CFC rules constrain cross-border classification arbitrage; no elective local entity recharacterisation is permitted.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration for taxable supplies or imports exceeding OMR 500,000 in any consecutive 12-month period; voluntary registration for taxable turnover between OMR 100,000 and OMR 500,000. Non-resident suppliers of digital or electronically supplied services to Omani non-taxable persons are required to register without a turnover threshold once Omani-supplied digital services exceed OMR 100,000 in 12 months; B2B imports of services by Omani customers are generally taxed under the reverse-charge mechanism, removing an Omani registration obligation for the non-resident supplier.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% VAT rate applies to: (i) exports of goods physically dispatched from Oman to a destination outside the GCC, provided the exporter retains valid customs export evidence; (ii) supplies of services exported to a non-resident recipient when the benefit of performance is enjoyed wholly outside Oman, including digital B2B marketplace facilitation and platform services; and (iii) supplies of goods and services to a free-zone registered or licensed person where the goods are subsequently exported or the services are used in a qualifying free-zone activity. Pure domestic B2B supplies remain taxable at 5%; the zero-rating claim fails if the supplier does not maintain contemporaneous export contracts, transport documents, customs declarations, and settlement evidence.

  • Customs single-window export declaration (Bayan/ASYCUDA) showing the SPC as exporter, HS codes, and exit confirmation
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / multimodal transport document evidencing physical departure from Oman
  • Commercial sales contract, purchase order and zero-rated VAT invoice issued by the SPC
  • Inward remittance certificate or SWIFT/MT103 evidence of settlement received from the non-resident counterparty
  • B2B platform transaction log and counterparty due-diligence records evidencing the non-resident taxable status of the buyer or recipient

Oman is implementing a phased national e-invoicing mandate through the OTA digital transformation programme. Taxpayers in the initial cohorts must use OTA-certified electronic billing and e-invoicing systems integrated via documented APIs, submitting structured invoice data (XML-based interoperable format) to the OTA fiscal data exchange and obtaining a digital tax clearance marker for each VAT invoice. Real-time transaction clearance is being progressively enabled for large taxpayers, with smaller taxable persons required to produce QR-coded e-invoices compliant with OTA technical specifications. Full real-time digital stamping for all taxpayers is expected once phased cohorts are completed; taxpayers must retain structured e-invoice records for 10 years.

The OTA may conduct post-export audit reviews, requiring reconstruction of the full documentary chain before approving refunds or sustaining the zero-rating. Failure to produce statutory evidence within the prescribed period triggers an assessment reverting the supply to 5% VAT, with late-payment penalties of 1% per month on the unpaid tax and interest. Fraudulent or repeated zero-rating claims constitute tax evasion under RD 121/2020, punishable by fines of not less than the tax evaded and up to three times the tax amount, plus imprisonment of up to five years, suspension or cancellation of the tax registration and CR, clawback of all refunded amounts, and publication of the penalty decision. Records substantiating export zero-rating must be retained for a minimum of five years for direct tax purposes and ten years for VAT purposes.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is a fully convertible currency pegged to the United States Dollar at approximately 1 USD = OMR 0.3845. The Sultanate of Oman does not impose general exchange-control restrictions on current account transactions, and capital account transactions may also be freely executed for foreign investors in a lawfully registered Single Person Company (SPC). Profit repatriation, dividend distribution, and liquidation proceeds transfer are permitted once the obligations imposed by the Oman Tax Authority (including tax clearance certificates and final settlement of all outstanding taxes) are satisfied. Payments between Omani resident entities must be settled in OMR; foreign currency settlement is permitted for cross-border exports, imports, and services. There is no centralised convertibility license requirement, but non-resident shareholders should maintain a clear documentary trail of the source of inward remittance to satisfy the banking AML/KYC framework.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion requirement. A B2B marketplace operator may retain 100% of its export sales proceeds in foreign currency or convert them to OMR at market rates through a CBO-licensed commercial bank. Export receipts must be repatriated through the local banking system; local operating expenditure in the Sultanate must be settled in OMR. For regulatory reporting, the receiving bank will request a declaration containing the exporter's Commercial Registration (CR) number, underlying commercial documents, invoice description, and expected settlement terms. There is no prescribed percentage for conversion, and the SPC retains full treasury flexibility over the currency composition of bank deposits.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating is MEDIUM-LOW for offshore digital EMI / payment processors. A fully offshore digital EMI, such as Wise Business, Payoneer, or Airwallex, is not licensed by the CBO and may not hold OMR deposits or make local payments on behalf of Omani residents. It is acceptable as a purely offshore collection and disbursement auxiliary for non-OMR cross-border B2B sales, provided that all OMR-local operating payments flow through a CBO-licensed banking institution. The SPC's statutory capital verification, if required by counterparties, must be evidenced by SWIFT-related bank statements issued by a licensed Omani bank rather than an offshore EMI statement. Relying solely on an EMI without a domestic banking relationship will trigger regulatory scrutiny under the CBO's AML/CFT supervision and Omani Commercial Companies Law.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Non-resident shareholders will route overseas equity injections through the international banking system using SWIFT MT103 corporate wire transfers to the Oman SPC's capital escrow account held at a CBO-licensed commercial bank. The remitted amount must be matched to the share subscription resolution and the SPC's constitutional document. The transfer must carry a standard foreign direct investment purpose code consistent with CBO balance-of-payments reporting, and the receiving bank will file the statutory inward remittance report. Documentation required by the third-party bank and the CBO-reporting bank includes: the bank's internal remittance instruction form; a notarised board resolution approving capital call; share subscription letter; basis of the foreign exchange rate applied; breakdown by currency; and a source-of-funds disclosure. The bank may request the underlying commercial licence (ISIC 4791 electronic retail/wholesale platform) to build the account-risk profile. To frustrate laundering concerns, the shareholder shall not use mixed third-party wallets; the remitting bank, the beneficial owner, and the invoiced shareholder must be the same legal person.Outbound trade settlements will be executed using a dual-rail architecture: (i) low-value and recurring supplier payments through SWIFT MT103 single credits or MT104 batch transfers; and (ii) high-value and capital goods purchases through commercial documentary letters of credit with MT700/MT707 issuance and MT742 reimbursement. The SPC shall not net receipts and disbursements on a single account without proper treasury segregation; a distinct operational account will be maintained for outbound disbursements. For local vendor payments, the operator will utilise the CBO-operated domestic OMR clearing and settlement infrastructure, including real-time gross settlement for large value payments and the standard automated clearing house for recurring retail B2B trade payments. Inbound Alibaba Pay batches will be swept at agreed intervals into the Omani current account and converted immediately to OMR where the operating cost base is local. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 7,500,000 gross annual turnover in Oman (and related-party transactions above OMR 500,000 for the year) mandates contemporaneous Master File and Local File.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not currently operate a separate Economic Substance Regulations regime comparable to the UAE, but the Income Tax Law contains an anti-abuse rule: transactions with no real commercial purpose or with no Omani decision-making substance will be re-characterized or disregarded. The Oman SPC must maintain staff, board meetings, IT servers or contractual commissioning, bank accounts and accounting records in Oman; otherwise, the platform profits are assessable in the hands of the foreign parent and the SPC may be treated as a shell.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion by False Accounting

Statutory sanction: Assessments may be raised on the best judgment basis with a 100% evaded-tax uplift and a fixed fine from OMR 5,000 to OMR 15,000 per violation; the Oman Tax Authority may freeze bank accounts and revoke the tax clearance certificate.

Director personal liability: Directors and the SPC's owner-manager are personally and severally liable for unpaid tax if they signed or caused false returns; the authority imposes travel bans, asset seizure, and in aggravated cases, imprisonment from 3 to 5 years under the Omani Anti-Fraud and Financial Crime Law.
Shell Enterprise / Sham B2B Platform

Statutory sanction: Application of General Anti-Abuse Rules to disregard the Oman SPC for tax purposes; all platform income is reallocated to the controlling non-resident company; fines for deliberate structural absence up to OMR 25,000 and forced deregistration under the Commercial Companies Law.

Director personal liability: The protective corporate veil is pierced; shareholders and directors are liable for all corporate taxes, penalties, and third-party claims; the director's passport may be flagged by the Royal Oman Police and travel prohibited during the audit.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The tax authority may make an upward profit adjustment, impose additional tax at 15%, levy a TP penalty of 30% of the under-reported adjustment, and attach the Oman SPC's bank balances.

Director personal liability: Directors approving non-arm's-length contracts without establishing business justification are personally liable for the deficiency to the extent of the adjustment; tribunal-awarded fines and a two-year disqualification from board positions apply.
Money Laundering & Payment-Rail Misuse

Statutory sanction: Oman AML Law imposes a fine of OMR 50,000 to OMR 100,000 per violation for institutions, immediate suspension of the SPC's trade license, freezing of merchant settlement accounts, and confiscation of undeclared revenue.

Director personal liability: Directors and the compliance officer face criminal liability including imprisonment up to 7 years, personal fines, travel bans and a lifetime prohibition on acting as company directors if AML failures are found to be deliberate.
