Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for a Single Person Company under RD 18/2019; share capital is fixed in the AoA. However, the Central Bank of Oman (CBO) special lending license requires designated paid-up capital to be deposited and held in escrow with a licensed Omani bank, with an escrow certificate issued to the CBO prior to license grant.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), established under Royal Decree 38/2023 in succession to the Ministry of Finance's Secretariat General of Taxation; national e-services and filing portal: 'Taxoman'.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the SPC and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. 2) Register online on Taxoman using the CR data and obtain the unified Tax Registration Number (TRN) covering both Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended) and VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020). 3) Foreign non-resident owners must file the corporate CR/TIN application through a licensed national tax representative in Oman before making taxable supplies. 4) Submit MOA, board resolutions, lease agreement, proof of registered office and FATCA/CRS self-certification; the OTA issues an electronic tax-registration certificate. Registration must precede invoice issuance.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — 1) Incorporate the SPC and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate from the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion. 2) Register online on Taxoman using the CR data and obtain the unified Tax Registration Number (TRN) covering both Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 28/2009, as amended) and VAT Law (Royal Decree 121/2020). 3) Foreign non-resident owners must file the corporate CR/TIN application through a licensed national tax representative in Oman before making taxable supplies. 4) Submit MOA, board resolutions, lease agreement, proof of registered office and FATCA/CRS self-certification; the OTA issues an electronic tax-registration certificate. Registration must precede invoice issuance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman has no check-the-box, pass-through or explicit hybrid-mismatch election for SPCs. The SPC is mandatorily an entity-level taxpayer. Statutory elections are limited to: (i) tax-group consolidation for 100% owned Omani subsidiaries; (ii) carry-forward of tax losses to offset future taxable profits (subject to continuity and disclosure); (iii) small-business/SME incentive exemption claims; and (iv) choice of accelerated depreciation or investment incentive methods where regulation allows. No transparent classification is available for foreign-owned Omani SPCs.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory VAT registration when the value of taxable supplies exceeds OMR 38,500 (approx. USD 100,000) in any 12-month period; voluntary registration from OMR 19,250 (approx. USD 50,000). Non-resident suppliers of electronically supplied services to Oman have a separate physical-presence exception and may be captured by reverse charge even below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: zero-rated when physically exported from Oman within 90 days of the tax invoice, evidenced by customs exit declarations and transport documents. Services: zero-rated when supplied to a recipient located outside Oman and the benefit is received outside Oman, including fintech underwriting and software-as-a-service supplied to non-resident platform clients. Interest margins and credit-granting services to Omani borrowers are exempt financial supplies, not zero-rated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (single administrative document or e-export manifest)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill proving physical export
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT payment evidence to the Omani SPC
  • Zero-rated tax invoice annotated with export and TRN references
  • Exported-service contract with non-resident recipient and place-of-supply analysis
  • Algorithm-consumption log/loan ledger substantiating offshore performance of the micro-lending platform

VAT Law requires a tax invoice (16 mandatory fields, TRN of supplier/receiver) or simplified invoice, issued within one month of the supply, with reverse-charge annotations for imported services. Oman does not currently operate mandatory real-time fiscal clearance, API e-invoice stamping or digital stamping; returns are submitted through Taxoman, and the OTA may require pre-clearance of high-risk export invoices and structured e-ledgers.

The OTA may audit up to five prior tax years. For fraudulent zero-rating (false export declarations or sham offshore recipients), the OTA will claw back the 5% VAT previously zero-rated, impose statutory late-payment fines capped at 100% of the unpaid tax, levy a tax-evasion penalty of up to 100% of evaded tax plus administrative fines, and may refer the operator to the National Centre for Financial Information for anti-money-laundering investigation, with potential suspension or revocation of the TRN and e-services credentials.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is pegged to the US dollar and is fully convertible for current account transactions. Capital account inflows are liberalized for registered foreign investments, including 100% foreign-owned SPCs, subject to bank-level compliance and the underlying investment license. Dividend and profit remittance to non-resident shareholders is permitted without exchange control license, but banks require audited financial statements, proof of tax compliance (tax clearance certificate where applicable), and full traceability of the source funds. The material practical hurdles are not convertibility but AML/KYC documentation and the classification of the payment purpose as investment capital or operating remittance. The Central Bank of Oman does not maintain discretionary approval for ordinary profit repatriation, provided the platform holds the required statutory license and has no CBO restriction on its banking relationships.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose mandatory export proceeds surrender or statutory mandatory conversion ratios. Export receipts must be repatriated through a CBO-licensed bank, credited to a resident Omani account in a freely convertible foreign currency or converted to OMR at the prevailing market rate. For a P2P micro-lending platform, export proceeds are not a primary source of liquidity; no surrender timeline therefore applies. Where lending funds originate from cross-border capital contributions, the only applicable rule is the general CBO/AML requirement to identify and record the inward SWIFT payment and retain supporting board resolutions and investment license records.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting under the Banking Law and implementing the Sultanate's foreign exchange administration; investment licensing is coordinated with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low to conditional medium. A cross-border or offshore electronic money institution (EMI) is not authorized to open depository accounts or hold client funds in Oman. It may supply payment initiation, card acquiring, or wallet infrastructure only through a CBO-licensed payment service provider or a local commercial bank acting as the settlement bank. The EMI would be classified as a foreign-designated non-financial business and subjected to enhanced due diligence. Legal viability is therefore dependent on a documented agent/custodian arrangement with an Omani licensed institution and approval of the CBO fintech or payment services framework; a fully independent direct clearing presence is not permissible.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound investment equity must be remitted through SWIFT MT103 from the investor's regulated bank to the SPC's CBO-licensed settlement bank. The wire reference field must identify the legal basis and purpose, for example 'SHARE CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — OMAN SPC — FOREIGN CAPITAL INVESTMENT LAW / COMMERCIAL COMPANIES LAW', together with the SPC's investment license number and the bank's internal reference. Oman does not require a prior CBO exchange control approval, but the receiving bank must archive the underlying investor identification, source of funds, board resolution, and final share register entry. The funds are then credited to the capital escrow account and released to the operating account only after the compliance officer confirms that the stated purpose matches the licensed financial activity.For operational disbursements to Omani borrowers, use the national electronic clearing system, ACH direct-debit schemes, and locally licensed wallet rails, with funds remaining in OMR. Cross-border trade disbursements for platform technology, software, or third-party services must be settled through SWIFT MT103 or, where correspondent banks require cover, MT202 COV, with the underlying agreement and Omani tax documentation attached. Commercial letters of credit are permitted under UCP600 for procurement of physical assets and banking infrastructure equipment. Any cross-border remittance made for service fees must be classified with the proper purpose code and supported by a service agreement and invoice so that withholding tax obligations are evidenced at the Omani Tax Authority level.
