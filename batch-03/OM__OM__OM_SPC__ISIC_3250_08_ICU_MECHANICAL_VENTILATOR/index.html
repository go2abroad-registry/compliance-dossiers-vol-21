Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA, هيئة الضرائب العُمانية), the unified national revenue authority reporting to the Ministry of Finance. Official e-services portal: https://taxoman.om (TAXoman e-services platform) covering corporate income tax (CIT), VAT registration, filing, payment and refunds. Customs export clearance is administered by the Royal Oman Police Directorate General of Customs through the Bayane single-window system.

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned Omani SPC: (1) incorporate under the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019) at the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate; (2) within 90 days of commencing activity, file CIT registration with OTA via taxoman.om and obtain the unified 15-digit Tax Identification Number (TIN), which also serves as the VAT registration number; (3) download the electronic Tax Card as proof of registration; (4) register for VAT under the same TIN once annual taxable supplies equal or exceed OMR 500,000 (mandatory) or OMR 200,000 (voluntary); (5) upload the CR, audited financial statements, manager/shareholder resolutions, Oman address proof and bank account details; (6) complete separate employer registration with the Public Authority for Social Insurance (PASI). Non-resident suppliers with no fixed establishment in Oman must VAT-register before their first taxable supply in Oman.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation. An Omani SPC is a resident juridical person taxed as a distinct entity at 15% on worldwide taxable income under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 9/2017, as amended by Royal Decree 36/2023), with a preferential 3% rate on the first OMR 100,000 for qualifying small businesses. No pass-through treatment applies to a Single Person Company; the sole shareholder is disregarded only for liability, not for tax. Territorial source rules apply only to non-residents. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation. An Omani SPC is a resident juridical person taxed as a distinct entity at 15% on worldwide taxable income under the Income Tax Law (Royal Decree 9/2017, as amended by Royal Decree 36/2023), with a preferential 3% rate on the first OMR 100,000 for qualifying small businesses. No pass-through treatment applies to a Single Person Company; the sole shareholder is disregarded only for liability, not for tax. Territorial source rules apply only to non-residents. A standard SPC outside an approved Free Zone or SEZ has no automatic Tax_Exempt_Zone status.

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election and no hybrid-mismatch election exist in Omani law; entity classification follows the statutory CR form. Available statutory elections/reliefs: (i) voluntary VAT registration at OMR 200,000 annual turnover before the mandatory OMR 500,000 threshold; (ii) the small-business income tax band where taxable income does not exceed OMR 100,000; (iii) election for a Free Zone / Special Economic Zone (e.g., Duqm SEZ, Sohar Free Zone) tax-holiday regime by obtaining a qualifying zone licence and OTA approval; (iv) limited group/consolidated filing for affiliated Oman-resident companies with OTA approval; (v) appointment of a tax representative for non-resident VAT registrants.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 (approx. USD 1,300,000) of taxable supplies (goods and services) in Oman or the wider GCC over the preceding 12 months or, by a written business plan, the next 30 days. Voluntary registration: OMR 200,000 (approx. USD 520,000). Non-resident suppliers without any place of establishment or fixed establishment in Oman must register from the first taxable supply, subject to reverse-charge rules for imported services. Public bodies and government entities are separately mandatorily registrable.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: a zero rate (0%) applies where the supplier physically exports goods outside Oman and the GCC within 90 days of the time of supply, either directly or through a third party on behalf of the non-resident buyer, and the export is evidenced by the Omani customs export declaration (Bayane) and transport documents. Services: zero-rating applies to services supplied to a non-resident person who is outside the GCC during the performance, to services directly connected with goods exported outside the GCC (e.g., international freight and logistics for ICU ventilator shipments), and to intangible rights used wholly outside the GCC, provided the recipient is not a GCC VAT-registered person entitled to input recovery. Residual input VAT attributable to zero-rated export sales is refundable through the OTA portal on a periodic refund application; the exporter must retain full due-diligence files for the statutory retention period.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayane) validated by Omani customs, evidencing the goods left Oman/GCC within 90 days
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill showing Oman as port of origin and the overseas destination port
  • Commercial invoice and packing list describing the exported ICU ventilators and confirming zero-rated export nature
  • Certificate of Origin and any required export licence for medical devices
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (IRC) or SWIFT MT103 evidencing receipt of export proceeds in the Omani bank account
  • Proof of delivery and customs clearance at the destination country (delivery notes, destination customs entry)

No mandatory state-operated real-time fiscal clearance, API clearance or digital stamping platform is yet operational in Oman; OTA has announced a phased national e-invoicing mandate with architectural target dates around 2026-2027. Current obligations: issue a sequential electronic tax invoice within 14 days of supply, showing the 15-digit TIN of both parties, description, quantity, consideration, VAT rate and VAT amount; exports must bear a clear zero-rated/export statement and be supported by the customs export declaration. Returns and export schedules are filed electronically at taxoman.om; high-volume exporters may use OTA's web-service API on a voluntary basis; customs data is exchanged via the Bayane platform in XML/EDIFACT.

OTA conducts post-clearance audits cross-matching customs Bayane records, carrier manifests, VAT returns and bank receipts; export refunds may be suspended pending document verification. Where zero-rating evidence fails, OTA reverses the 0% claim, assesses 5% output VAT, claws back input VAT previously credited, and imposes statutory penalties: administrative contravention fines in the range of OMR 100-5,000 with doubling for repeat offences within two years; wilful evasion, including forged export documents, is criminalized with imprisonment of up to five years, fines of up to OMR 1,000,000, and recovery of the full tax evaded. Retroactive assessments may be reopened for five years from the filing date (extended in fraud cases) and refund claims are time-barred after the statutory limitation period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is formally pegged to the United States Dollar at a maintained mid-rate of OMR 1 = USD 2.6008, with authorized banks quoting within a small dealing band. Oman has accepted IMF Article VIII obligations; consequently, current-account payments and transfers for trade, services, and profits are fully convertible and freely transferable without prior CBO approval. Capital-account inflows, including foreign direct investment equity injections, are liberalized for licensed entities under the Foreign Capital Investment Law and the Ministerial decision on foreign ownership, but banks are required to collect and report full balance-of-payments data. Profit and dividend remittances by a foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) are permitted without withholding tax, subject to audited financial statements, renewal of the Commercial Registration, and the satisfaction of all OTA filing and payment obligations; a tax clearance certificate is normally required by the remitting bank to evidence that tax liabilities have been discharged.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no forced conversion percentage into OMR. Pursuant to CBO Foreign Exchange Operation Regulations, all proceeds from exports of goods and services must be repatriated to the exporter's licensed bank account in Oman through an authorized bank within a commercially reasonable period, and banks customarily apply a 180-day repatriation benchmark for documentary export transactions. Exporters may lawfully retain receipts in a USD or other foreign-currency account and convert at their discretion, provided each receipt is substantiated by the customs export declaration, bill of lading or airway bill, and the commercial invoice for CBO balance-of-payments verification. There is no CBO prescription of a minimum summary conversion ratio, which materially reduces forced FX conversion exposure for the ventilator manufacturing SPC.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), established under the Banking Law promulgated by Royal Decree No. 114/2000, which regulates banking business, foreign exchange operations, and the Payment and Settlement Systems Law; the CBO Foreign Exchange Operation Regulations, issued under Circular No. 92/2017, govern all cross-border transfers, while the Capital Market Authority (CMA) and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) exercise ancillary oversight of securities- and tax-related cross-border transactions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-CRITICAL. Oman does not passport any offshore regulatory license from a foreign Electronic Money Institution (EMI) or digital cross-border payment operator. Under the CBO Payment and Settlement Systems Law and its implementing payment-service provider licensing rules, any entity carrying out payment services, digital wallets, cross-border collection, or settlement for an Omani tax-resident company must be licensed by the CBO. No UK FCA, Singapore MAS, or European authorizations are recognized as substitutes; therefore, offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) can serve only at most as a supplementary low-value collection conduit behind a licensed Omani bank omnibus arrangement, and they may not constitute the primary treasury clearing rail for capital injections, export proceeds, or Government of Oman LC disbursements. Legal exposure to a non-licensed operator may trigger CBO sanctions against the SPC's own banking facilities.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opaque Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) of the foreign parent undertaking and absence of a clear Omani Commercial Registration extract for the SPC's medical-device manufacturing scope.

Root cause: Opaque Ultimate Beneficial Ownership (UBO) of the foreign parent undertaking and absence of a clear Omani Commercial Registration extract for the SPC's medical-device manufacturing scope.

Defense evidence: Deliver a fully notarized and apostilled Certificate of Incumbency, a Registry of Directors and UBO register showing every natural person with directly or indirectly more than 10% ownership or effective control, certified passport copies of all such persons, a signed statement of source of wealth, and the Omani Commercial Registration (SR/CR) extract issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) describing the licensed trade activity in terms aligned to ICU mechanical ventilator manufacturing under ISIC 3250; the CR must match the company object exactly and be renewed for the vigorous financial year.
Enhanced due diligence (EDD) triggered by trade flows linked to Ministry of Health public-procurement advance payments and cross-border freight of a life-support medical device, posing trade-based money-laundering and misinvoicing suspicion.

Root cause: Enhanced due diligence (EDD) triggered by trade flows linked to Ministry of Health public-procurement advance payments and cross-border freight of a life-support medical device, posing trade-based money-laundering and misinvoicing suspicion.

Defense evidence: Provide the executed public-procurement or supply contract with the Ministry of Health, the full commercial-invoice series for ventilator exports, the Omani customs export declarations (Bayan statement), bill-of-lading and packing lists, and certificates of origin; add the GCC/UAE transit customs documentation when the goods clear through GCC hub ports; submit the same bundle for the bank's internal EDD committee and hold a corroborative statement from the independent auditor.
Sanctions, PEP, or adverse-media screening flags attached to the non-resident shareholder's jurisdiction, to the freight/courier route, or to the ventilator model's potential dual-use export classification.

Root cause: Sanctions, PEP, or adverse-media screening flags attached to the non-resident shareholder's jurisdiction, to the freight/courier route, or to the ventilator model's potential dual-use export classification.

Defense evidence: Commission a fresh conflicts-and-sanctions screening report from an independent international compliance vendor (e.g., Dow Jones or Refinitiv integrated output), legally confirm that the invasive ICU ventilator is not a controlled item under Oman's strategic-goods export-control law implementing UN Security Council Resolutions and the Wassenaar/COCOM dual-use lists, attach a product technical datasheet and clinical-use certificate from the manufacturer, and append an Omani external-counsel opinion confirming the lawful end-use profile; thereafter complete the bank's EDD questionnaire under CBO Circular BM 1116.
Refusal by the bank to open an account solely under non-resident foreign director signature authority without any locally resident person assuming operational responsibility.

Root cause: Refusal by the bank to open an account solely under non-resident foreign director signature authority without any locally resident person assuming operational responsibility.

Defense evidence: Appoint a resident Omani or GCC-national manager with an executed employment contract and commercial-registration amendment, furnish the manager's original civil ID and address proof, execute a notarized Power of Attorney vesting treasury and signatory authority in the resident manager for daily operations, and present both the non-resident UBO and the resident manager at the same branch interview; a stable physical office lease in Oman plus the SPC's Warehouse/Trade License for the ventilator assembly line should also be produced.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity injection from the foreign parent to the Omani SPC shall be executed exclusively by SWIFT MT103 Single Customer Credit Transfer to the SPC's designated capital account at a CBO-authorized commercial bank (preferentially Bank Muscat or HSBC Oman). The funds should be structured as an FDI equity contribution under the IMF BPM6 balance-of-payments classification, with the SWIFT narrative containing the ISO purpose descriptor 'FDI / Equity Capital Injection' and the reference to the Omani Commercial Registration number plus the MOCIIP Foreign Capital Investment approval reference. Before remittance, the bank may require the SPC's audited opening balance sheet, the share-certificate ledger for the SPC, and the MOCIIP investment license, which together evidence lawful capitalization. This assessment confirms Oman imposes no mandatory CBO approval for FDI equity injections, but the receiving bank must complete inward remittance reporting within the statutory time frame; same-day value dating can be achieved when the remittance is cleared via the USD New York correspondent banking channel (CHIPS/Fedwire), given the OMR-USD peg.Outbound trade and operating disbursements for the ventilator-manufacturing SPC shall rely on: (a) SWIFT MT103 for open-account settleable payments to overseas component suppliers, with the SWIFT purpose code reflecting 'General trade in goods and services' and full 50K/57A/70 field transparency; (b) Commercial Letters of Credit (sight or usance) issued by the Omani issuing bank in favor of foreign suppliers and governed by UCP 600, with the MT700/tancular advices routed through the issuing bank's US or UAE correspondents; (c) documentary collections (MT750/MT752) for smaller supply relationships; and (d) direct settlement capacity against the Government of Oman central treasury account for Ministry of Health LC receivables — Omani government payments are credited by the CBO-operated account via a clean MT103, providing high finality. All outbound T/Ts must be matched against underlying invoices, transport documents, and the SPC's approved trade-compliance matrix to prevent trade-based laundering findings; USD payments are netted through the OMR-USD peg with minimal conversion spread.Deliverable OMR/USD foreign exchange forwards (FX Forwards) transacted under an ISDA 2002 Master Agreement or the Omani Banks Association FX terms, with licensed CBO banks, given the OMR-USD peg requiring effective hedge tenors matching the SPC's trade receivable cycles.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) between USD fixed-rate liability legs and OMR floating-rate operational surplus legs, authorized to match long-term government LC procurement cash flows without speculative position taking.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for supply-chain counterparty currencies that are not fully convertible in the region, primarily INR, PKR, and certain East Asian currencies, net-settled in USD at maturity under licensed bank approval, but strictly not used for OMR/USD as both are settled onshore.Permitted currency options and FX swaps (rollover/swaps) for treasury windows covering quarterly tax payments and intercompany settlements to the foreign parent, with counterparty limits set under the SPC's board-approved treasury policy.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-CRITICAL. Oman does not passport any offshore regulatory license from a foreign Electronic Money Institution (EMI) or digital cross-border payment operator. Under the CBO Payment and Settlement Systems Law and its implementing payment-service provider licensing rules, any entity carrying out payment services, digital wallets, cross-border collection, or settlement for an Omani tax-resident company must be licensed by the CBO. No UK FCA, Singapore MAS, or European authorizations are recognized as substitutes; therefore, offshore EMI platforms (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, Currencycloud) can serve only at most as a supplementary low-value collection conduit behind a licensed Omani bank omnibus arrangement, and they may not constitute the primary treasury clearing rail for capital injections, export proceeds, or Government of Oman LC disbursements. Legal exposure to a non-licensed operator may trigger CBO sanctions against the SPC's own banking facilities.
