Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; electronic portal: SICE (Sistema de Información de Control de la Facturación Electrónica)

E-government filing portal

A foreign non-resident entity acting as a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must appoint a resident agent and obtain an Número de Identificación Tributaria (NIT) by filing the corresponding registration form (Formulario de Registro Único de Contribuyente) with the DGI. The resident agent submits the foundation charter, appointment documents, and proof of the registered office. A separate municipal license (Aviso de Operación) is required for local business operations, although a PIF with no Panamanian-source income remains registrable for ITBMS compliance when importing taxable services.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a check-the-box entity classification system comparable to the U.S. or a full hybrid mismatch election regime. A PIF is a legal person sui generis under the Private Interest Foundations Law (Law 25 of 1995); it is not classified as a corporation or trust for Panamanian tax purposes. No elective pass-through status exists. For cross-border purposes, the PIF may be characterized per the applicable foreign tax law; Panama law does not provide an entity-level election that would alter its territorial-source taxation status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

All persons performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register with the DGI. A special small-taxpayer regime applies to individuals and entities with annual gross receipts not exceeding B/.36,000 (equivalent to US$36,000), allowing an optional simplified flat-tax scheme instead of standard ITBMS registration. Non-resident digital and cross-border service suppliers without a local permanent establishment are required to register and charge ITBMS upon the first taxable supply to a Panamanian recipient.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under ITBMS applies to services rendered to non-resident recipients when the benefit of the service is effectively used or consumed outside Panama. Marine insurance surveying services (hull and cargo damage inspection) qualify for the 0% rate when performed on vessels or cargo located outside Panamanian territory, or when the service is supplied to a foreign underwriter with no Panamanian establishment and the survey report is delivered abroad. Supporting documentation must demonstrate that the recipient is a non-resident and that the service is not connected with any Panama-situs asset or local consumption.

  • Customs Export Declaration (if any physical cargo samples or adjustable equipment are exported from Panama)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (for the movement of any physical items supporting the survey)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Comprobante de Ingreso de Divisas) evidencing receipt of foreign currency within the applicable repatriation period
  • Duly executed marine survey service contract with the foreign underwriter or charterer
  • Final survey report and delivery acknowledgment issued to the non-resident client
  • Commercial invoice bearing the zero-rating legend referencing the ITBMS export exemption

Electronic invoicing through the SICE platform is mandatory for all registered taxpayers, including marine insurance surveyors. Invoices must be digitally signed and transmitted using DGI-authorized billing software or the SICE web portal. The DGI validates the invoice series and provides a unique authorization code before issuance; continuous real-time fiscal clearance is not yet implemented, but each invoice requires immediate validation and digital stamping. Failure to comply with invoicing rules may trigger fines and the disallowance of input ITBMS credits.

The DGI audits export zero-rating claims thoroughly. If the documentation requirement is not met or funds are not repatriated within 180 days, the zero-rated supply is retroactively re-characterized as a taxable Panamanian supply. The taxpayer is then liable for the full 7% ITBMS, interest, and surcharges. Fraudulent claims carry administrative fines of up to 100% of the omitted tax, monetary penalties per infraction, and criminal prosecution under Articles 708-B and 793-A of the Fiscal Code for tax fraud, including imprisonment for serious violations. Additionally, the exporter may lose the right to input tax credits and face temporary suspension of invoicing privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Unrestricted full convertibility on both current and capital accounts. Panama has no exchange-control statute, no conversion licensing, no repatriation quotas, and no remittance impediment for profits, dividends, or foundation distributions to non-resident beneficiaries. Commercial banks record accounts directly in USD, eliminating dual-currency settlement risk for the PIF. Fiscal friction arises only from the territorial tax model: a Private Interest Foundation engaged entirely in offshore marine surveying is exempt from Panamanian corporate income tax on non-source income; Panamanian-source activity would attract the 25% corporate income tax plus a 10% dividend withholding on distributions sourced locally. Survey fees invoiced from shipowners, P&I clubs, and underwriters located outside Panama remain territorial exclusions with zero effective tax, subject to the annual USD 350 operating-license franchise tax, the PIF registration maintenance fees, and accounting-record retention under Law 254 of 2018.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender obligation on export or cross-border service proceeds. A marine insurance surveyor may retain full USD, GBP, or EUR balances in domestic accounts or sweep them abroad without regulatory approval. Banking counterparties nonetheless apply Law 23 of 2015 AML controls: structured cash transactions exceeding USD 10,000 trigger currency-transaction reports, and all trade invoices must be retained alongside the foundation's registered accounting books to satisfy economic-substance requirements. No SBP or UAF filing is required for the mere crediting of export service fees.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the Republic of Panama maintains no currency-issuing central bank; the US Dollar is legal tender and the Balboa exists solely as a coin-denomination pegged at 1:1 par. The SBP functions as the consolidated banking, foreign-exchange and payments-systems supervisor, while Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the Government's fiscal agent and the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) performs AML/CFT financial-intelligence functions. There is no central-bank exchange-control department, no prior-authorization mechanism for capital movements, and no reserve-requirement restriction on foreign-currency deposits.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a secondary outbound settlement rail (Wise Business, Mercury, or Currencycloud) for invoices at or below USD 15,000, and Low for primary treasury custody. Legal constraints: offshore digital EMIs hold no Panamanian banking license, no EU EMI passport extends into Panama, and any EMI receiving funds on behalf of the PIF acts only through its US or Belgian licensed banking partner executing MT103-equivalent transfers. Panamanian tier-1 banks will require the foundation's council resolution expressly designating the EMI as an approved disbursement channel; any circulation pattern between the tier-1 account and the EMI triggers enhanced due diligence against shell-sweep typologies. Safest architecture: use Wise solely to collect Lloyd's-market fee remittances, convert GBP/EUR proceeds to USD within Wise, then sweep to Banco General within five banking days.
