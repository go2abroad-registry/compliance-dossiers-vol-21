Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama generally applies territorial taxation; a PIF may elect to be treated as a separate taxable person for tax purposes, but the standard default is pass-through or transparent treatment under Special Law 25 of 1995; no formal check-the-box election exists; taxpayers may file an annual sworn statement (Declaración Jurada) electing to report only Panamanian-source income and may opt into the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime or special regimes where available; hybrid mismatch elections are limited and subject to General Directorate of Revenues criteria.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Annual taxable gross turnover exceeding USD 300,000 (or as established by DGI regulations; registration is required for persons engaging in taxable activities, with a simplified registration for occasional imports or services).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and certain offshore services are zero-rated for ITBMS purposes; eligibility requires proving that the goods physically leave Panama or that the service is provided wholly for use outside Panama; the seller must issue an export invoice indicating zero-rate, maintain transport evidence, and obtain the DGI export certificate; copper cathodes exported via port or bonded warehouse may qualify if shipped under customs export procedures.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación Electrónica, comprising forma DUA / manifiesto)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/LC transfer records proving payment received from abroad
  • Purchase invoice or LME warrant documentation supporting acquisition of the copper cathodes
  • DGI export certification and, if applicable, Free Trade Zone or warehouse movement documents

Panama mandates electronic invoicing and fiscal clearance through the DGI's 'Facturación Electrónica' system; authorized e-invoice issuers must use DGI-certified software, transmit invoices in the specified XML/JSON format through the Sede Electrónica API, and incorporate the digital stamp (código de seguridad) and validation QR element before issuance to buyers; real-time validation applies for large taxpayers, with deferred validation for smaller or newly registered taxpayers.

The DGI may audit export documentation including customs records, shipping manifests, and FX reconciliation; false zero-rating declarations or failure to prove physical export can result in retroactive assessment of ITBMS at 7%, late interest, fines up to 100% of the omitted tax, administrative penalties under the Tax Code and Código Fiscal, and criminal prosecution for tax fraud; transfer-pricing and economic-substance documentation may also be required for SEM or free zone entities.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions, as Panama operates a dollarized monetary system without exchange control legislation. Profit remittances and capital repatriation are unrestricted; however, all transfers are subject to Panama’s anti-money laundering (AML), counter-financing of terrorism (CFT) and know-your-client (KYC) rules, including mandatory reporting of suspicious transactions to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF). No prior central bank approval is required for trade-related cross-border payments or dividend distributions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratios exist under Panamanian law. Exporters of physical commodities such as copper cathodes may freely hold, disburse, or reinvest USD proceeds. Compliance obligations are limited to accounting transparency, tax reporting under the territorial tax system, and AML/CFT record-keeping for international transfers exceeding USD 10,000 or structuring thresholds.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no autonomous central bank. Monetary authority is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) for banking supervision; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) and the Superintendencia del Mercado de Valores (SMV) oversee financial policy and securities matters. The U.S. dollar is sole legal tender; the balboa (PAB) is a nominal monetary unit circulating only as divisional coins pegged 1:1 to USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low feasibility for offshore digital EMIs as primary banking solution. Panama does not issue electronic money institution (EMI) licenses under a standalone framework; firms offering e-money or mass payment services must be licensed by the SBP under the Panamanian Banking Law (Legislative Decree 2 of 2008) or operate under the Fintech Innovation Hub regime. A PIF held by a non-resident would face heightened due diligence when attempting to transact through an unlicensed foreign fintech, and the SBP will treat unregistered EMIs as shadow financial service providers. Use of such a fintech is viable only as a secondary payment corridor with strict AML-name screening and after due diligence on the EMI’s underlying banking partnerships.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Usable rails are USD-denominated wire transfers through the Clearing House Interbank Payments System (CHIPS) or Fedwire for US-based correspondent banks, and SWIFT MT103/202 for Europe and Asia. Recommended routing: parent/shareholder bank → correspondent in Panama or the PIF’s bank in Panama with purpose code TRADE/FINANCING (e.g., 'Grants or capital contribution to a foundation', 'Trade receivable', or 'Payment for goods and services' under the IMF/OECD BPM6 classification). The remitter must include the PIF’s full legal name, account number, and a reference to the original copper purchase contract. For capital injections, a board resolution of the PIF must be provided to the bank, together with the notarized source-of-funds statement, to avoid AML flagging. All inbound capital should be booked as 'capital contribution' or 'trust beneficiary advancement' in local accounting ledgers.Primary clearing rails will be (a) SWIFT MT103 for payment of copper suppliers and logistics providers using standard purpose codes (e.g., '1101 - Trade in goods', '1111 - Trade in services'), (b) Commercial Documentary Letters of Credit (LC) issued via MT700, MT707 amendments, and advised through the exporter’s bank for purchases of copper cathodes from producers or large traders, and (c) LME Warrant transfer through registered warehouse receipts, with settlement via online payment at the LME clearing house (LMEclear) for brokers. Domestic Panamanian disbursements are processed through Central de Riesgo (SCR) for luca pepper? Actually through local ACH (ACH Panama – Sistema de Pagos de Alto Valor, SPAV) for USD transfers or direct SWIFT for amounts over USD 10,000, but the preferred commercial rail remains the USD wire/Fedwire/CHIPS rail through correspondent banks. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% on net Panama-source income; territorial scope excludes foreign-source income unless remitted to a Panama permanent establishment.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% withholding on dividends or foundation distributions paid from profits that have borne the 25% corporate tax; 10% on distributions from profits exempted or benefited at the corporate level; 0% on distributions of profits derived from foreign-source income under Panama's territorial model where no Panama-source earnings are distributed.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after fiscal year-end; for a calendar-year entity, on or before 31 March of the following year. Taxpayers eligible for digital payment extension may obtain an additional two months by request.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File are required where annual related-party transactions exceed USD 2 million, or where consolidated group revenues of the ultimate parent exceed USD 100 million and Panama operations conduct cross-border controlled transactions. Overview/two-line TP disclosure is required for all controlled transactions above USD 250,000.

Economic substance scope: Panama Law 254 of 2020 applies Economic Substance Regulations to entities carrying on 'relevant activities' from within Panama. Wholesale commodity trading is a relevant activity when the PIF is managed, directed, or staffed in Panama. Such an entity must evidence a local office, local board meetings, qualified employees, and strategic risk management in Panama. A PIF relying solely on offshore status while exercising management from Panama is exposed to full re-characterization and the associated tax.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Deliberate Territorial Source Misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 40% of the omitted tax plus 1% monthly interest; administrative fine up to USD 200,000; criminal tax fraud constitutes imprisonment from one to five years under the Criminal Tax Procedure Code.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false returns or directed the misdeclaration are jointly and severally liable for the corporation's unreported tax. Courts may issue a travel ban preventing exit from Panama pending restitution, order civil attachment of personal assets, and impose disqualification from serving on any Panamanian company for up to five years; imprisonment applies to the criminal conviction.
Violation of Economic Substance Requirements (Shell Company Masking)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine from USD 2,500 to USD 75,000 per annum; immediate re-assessment at the 25% statutory rate on previously excluded foreign-source revenue; 50% surcharge for false declaration and possible dissolution of the foundation by the Registro Público.

Director personal liability: Foundation council members are civilly liable for the amount of tax found deficient to the extent they approved unlawful distributions. Directors may be subject to asset freezing orders, 90-day temporary travel suspension, and criminal indictment for criminal conspiracy where the foundation was deliberately designed to obstruct tax collection.
Transfer Pricing Misstatement and Base Erosion Through Related-Party Sourcing

Statutory sanction: Portfolio transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's-length price, collection of the underpaid tax plus interest at 4.5% semi-annual accumulative, and a misstatement penalty of 30% of the corrected deficiency for a 'serious' infraction.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed the transfer pricing declaration without verifying economic substance hold personal civil liability for 50% of the assessed deficiency. The Panama Tax Court may order a director's professional license suspension for two years, and a criminal penalty applies if false documentation is supplied to the DGI.
AML-Compliance Failure in Commodity Arbitrage Payment Rails

Statutory sanction: Panama's AML Law 23 of 2015 authorizes fines of up to USD 1 million or 10% of the gross value of the suspicious transactions, whichever is greater; a licensed PIF agent may have that authorization revoked with immediate asset freezes in connection with the transaction.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers who knowingly process fraudulent commodity trades are personally indictable for money laundering predicated on tax evasion; penalty in Panama is five to ten years of imprisonment and permanent removal from the natural person registry of regulated commercial agents.
