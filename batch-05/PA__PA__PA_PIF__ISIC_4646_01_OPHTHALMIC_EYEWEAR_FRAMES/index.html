Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama City; official portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa (RUC registration, electronic filings, Facturación Electrónica module). Export customs authority: Administración Nacional de Aduanas (ANA), operating the SIGA/VUCE single-window clearance platform.

E-government filing portal

A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) obtains its tax identity exclusively through its licensed Panamanian resident agent: (1) inscribe the foundation charter with the Registro Público de Panamá under Law 25 of 1995; (2) file the DGI RUC application (Formulario RUC) with certified charter, resident agent acceptance, legal domicile, and director/protector details; the DGI issues the 11-digit RUC which simultaneously serves as the income tax and ITBMS identifier; (3) if the foundation carries on local commercial activity, also obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación and patente comercial; (4) the resident agent files the annual B/.300 foundation tax (Ley 25, Art. 19). Foreign founders and beneficiaries never appear before the DGI; Panama has no public registry of foundation beneficiaries.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No domestic check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists in Panamanian law; the civil-law entity is fixed by Ley 25 de 1995. Panama applies a pure territorial system: Panamanian-source income is taxable at entity level at 25% corporate rate, while foreign-source income and export revenues fall outside the tax base. The PIF may elect the simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime where statutory thresholds are met, and may elect between refund and tax-credit treatment of excess export ITBMS. Foreign classifications (e.g., US Form 8832) are independent of Panamanian treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.0.00 (no economic turnover threshold): any person habitually or occasionally transferring taxable goods or rendering taxable services in Panama must obtain an RUC and register for ITBMS; the simplified small-taxpayer regime is an administrative collection simplification, not an exemption from registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods, including ophthalmic eyeglass frames, are zero-rated (0% ITBMS) when the goods physically depart Panama and are declared through the ANA customs export system (DUI export mode). Services supplied to non-residents and consumed abroad are also zero-rated when the recipient is foreign and the benefit is realized outside Panama. Outright drop-shipment from third countries directly to foreign buyers, without Panama entry, falls outside Panamanian ITBMS nexus altogether and requires no Panamanian zero-rating claim. Goods delivered into the Zona Libre de Colón are excluded from ITBMS under the free-zone regime but require separate free-zone operator authorization.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Mercancías – export DUI filed via ANA SIGA/VUCE)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exit from Panama
  • Commercial invoice and packing list (HS 9003.11 / 9003.19 frame classification)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (SWIFT MT103 or foreign bank statement evidencing settlement in US dollars)
  • Destination-market documentation (certificate of origin, purchase order, or distribution contract)

Mandatory electronic invoicing for substantially all registered taxpayers (phased roll-out completed January 2023). Each invoice must be transmitted through a DGI-certified software provider or the DGI portal (API/web-service integration) and receive real-time DGI online validation before delivery; the DGI affixes a fiscal stamp with digital signature and QR code. B2B invoices must be issued in standard digital format (XML/PDF-A) and retained for the full five-year prescription period; transitional paper printing is limited to marginal micro-taxpayers.

DGI may audit export zero-rating claims within the five-year statute of limitations. Unsupported or simulated exports trigger full ITBMS reversal as a domestic taxable sale, plus 1.5% monthly surcharge interest, fines up to B/.10,000 under the Código Fiscal, and ANA penalties for false customs declarations. Fraudulent refund claims are clawed back with up to 100% surcharge and referral to criminal tax fraud prosecution (2–5 years imprisonment in Panama). Missing B/L or SWIFT settlement evidence alone is sufficient basis for rejecting the zero rate.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system; the Panamanian Balboa circulates solely as a 1:1 divisional currency of the United States dollar. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are freely convertible, with no central bank approval, no surrender obligation, and no foreign-exchange licensing for trade payments or profit remittances. Panama applies a territorial tax system: a Private Interest Foundation engaged in wholesale distribution of acetate eyeglass frames is subject to Panamanian income tax only on Panamanian-source commercial income. Outbound profit remittances are not exchange-control-blocked; however, if commercial income is distributed through a taxable corporate vehicle, the statutory 10% dividend withholding tax may apply. A PIF structured as a non-commercial vehicle avoids entity-level tax on foreign-source proceeds, and no remittance quarantine or prior administrative authorization exists.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts under Panamanian law. Export proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency may be retained in Panamanian bank accounts, re-invoiced, or redeployed abroad without surrender to the State. For audit and tax evidence, the exporter should retain the commercial invoice, customs exit declarations (ECP/DTA), the SWIFT credit confirmation, and the underlying purchase contract to demonstrate that the receipt corresponds to a genuine wholesale optical-frames transaction.

Central bank supervision: Republic of Panama has no formal central bank. Banking conduct, AML/CFT supervision, and financial licensing are administered by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and settlement bank for the public sector, but it does not exercise exchange-control or reserve-management authority over private cross-border flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium, with significant structural constraints. Panama does not issue European-style e-money institution (EMI) licences; only SBP-licensed commercial banks are permitted to take deposits from the public. An offshore digital EMI cannot operate independently in Panama and cannot replace a local bank account. It may only serve as an alternative disbursement rail where the EMI is regulated in its home jurisdiction, holds a correspondent account with an SBP-licensed or internationally recognized bank, and applies equivalent KYC/AML standards. In practice, SBP-regulated banks may reject wire transfers originating from opaque offshore EMIs unless the underlying commercial purpose and beneficial ownership of the PIF are fully documented.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The preferred compliant routing is a direct SWIFT MT103 from the contributing shareholder's or founder's regulated bank account to the PIF's operating account at an SBP-licensed Panamanian bank. The wire's field 70 (Payment Information) should identify the transaction as 'Capital Contribution to Private Interest Foundation PA_PIF' and reference the Foundation's RUC or bank account number. Panama has no detailed national purpose-code table, but banks may require a sworn source-of-funds declaration and a council resolution. The onboarding verification file must include the Board/Foundation Council minutes, the contributor's certified identity documents, the source account statement, and an no-third-party funding undertaking. For larger injections, the bank may require a written capital contribution agreement and confirmation that the funds were cleared through an OFAC-compliant correspondent before final credit is given.For supplier payments in optical-frames wholesale, the standard clearing rails are (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for open-account trade, and (ii) irrevocable documentary letters of credit issued via SWIFT MT700/MT710 for first-time or high-value supplier purchases. A commercial LC is preferred where the supplier requires title and inspection documents and where the PIF's payment history has not yet been established. USD clearing should be executed through a US correspondent bank selected by the Panamanian bank to preserve finality. For recurring platform payouts, the PIF may use a licensed bank's API-based payment gateway or an authorized processor, provided that every transaction is screened against OFAC and SBP local sanctions lists.
