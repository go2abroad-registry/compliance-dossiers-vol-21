Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital under Law 25 of 1995. The founder must allocate a reasonable initial patrimony consisting of movable or immovable assets, rights, or a cash endowment. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF); online portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

Foreign entities obtain a Panamanian RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by filing the entity registration application with the DGI via the Sede Electrónica portal or through a licensed public accountant/attorney. The foreign entity must submit the authenticated foundation deed, the currently valid registered agent resignation/acceptance letter, proof of appointment of a local fiscal resident representative, and the completed taxpayer application form (Aviso de Inscripción). Upon approval, the DGI issues the RUC and activates the e-Tax 2.0 profile for filing and compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope (Panamanian Private Interest Foundations are separate taxable persons; only Panama-source income is subject to income tax. Foreign-source income, capital gains, and dividends are generally outside the Panamanian territorial tax net unless repatriated through Panamanian banking channels or connected to a permanent establishment in Panama). — Foreign entities obtain a Panamanian RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by filing the entity registration application with the DGI via the Sede Electrónica portal or through a licensed public accountant/attorney. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (Panamanian Private Interest Foundations are separate taxable persons; only Panama-source income is subject to income tax. Foreign-source income, capital gains, and dividends are generally outside the Panamanian territorial tax net unless repatriated through Panamanian banking channels or connected to a permanent establishment in Panama).

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Panamanian law. A foundation voluntarily carrying on taxable business in Panama may elect between the ordinary ITBMS scheme or the simplified small-taxpayer regime (for operators under the B/.200,000 annual turnover threshold), and such election is irrevocable for a minimum period under DGI regulations.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.0 – registration for ITBMS is mandatory for any person or entity performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama or in the other taxable Panamanian territory. Small taxpayers with annual gross receipts up to B/.200,000 may register under the simplified ITBMS regime, which substitutes a reduced composite rate for full credit/refund mechanics.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under ITBMS applies to (i) goods physically exported from Panama to a foreign destination, and (ii) services rendered to non-residents, consumed entirely abroad, or directly performed on goods destined for export. The bonded truck fleet feeder service for international air cargo qualifies when the services are covered by a customs transit regime, the transport begins in Panama and ends abroad, and title and risk in the goods transfer to a foreign consignee. The exporter must be an ordinary ITBMS taxpayer and must issue an e-invoice with the zero-rated indicator, retaining all required transport and customs documents.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Exportación/DUI)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / Master Road Manifest (CMR for bonded truck transit)
  • Commercial invoice and underlying purchase/sales contract
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank SWIFT MT103 or letter of credit) evidencing receipt of payment from the foreign buyer
  • Customs custody/bonded transit documentation (T-document or similar transit regime) proving movement from entry point to exit point
  • GPS manifest log and sealed trip report for the bonded truck fleet, if required by customs

Panama mandates phased electronic invoicing for all ordinary ITBMS taxpayers. Invoices must be generated through DGI-approved software or the DGI web portal, carry a digital signature and a QR code, and be transmitted in real time (API/XML exchange) to the DGI for fiscal validation and stamping. Final acceptance is recorded electronically before the invoice is issued to the client; invoices not cleared through the DGI fiscal repository are null for tax deduction and credit purposes.

The DGI may audit zero-rating claims retrospectively for up to five years. If the documentary bundle is incomplete, the evidence of foreign payment or physical export is missing, or the exporter does not receive and repatriate the proceeds within the governing commercial term (benchmark 180 days), the DGI will recharacterize the transaction as a taxable domestic supply. The full 7% ITBMS becomes due, plus late surcharges and default interest from the original invoice date. Fines range from 50% to 100% of the omitted tax, and fraudulent documentation or false customs declarations constitute tax evasion, carrying criminal penalties of up to five years’ imprisonment, disqualification of the taxpayer, and loss of eligibility for future export zero-rating and bonded-cargo licenses.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized, exchange-control-free regime. Both current and capital account transactions are freely convertible; no central bank approval is required for profit remittances, dividends, loan repayments, or inbound capital. The Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a subdivision coinage unit pegged 1:1 to the USD. Practical compliance friction is limited to SBP AML reporting of cash transactions above USD 10,000 and DGI tax-filing obligations, not to convertibility restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Statutory retention is 100% of export and eligible service receipts; no mandatory surrender timeline, no conversion ratio, and no repatriation window applies to bonded trucking revenues. Exporters and service providers must, however, preserve settlement evidence, bills of lading, and airway bills for at least five years for SBP and DGI audit purposes.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Banco Nacional de Panamá (state-owned fiscal agent and clearing hub); monetary and exchange policy administered by the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF). There is no issuing central bank; the U.S. Dollar is full legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium-Low. Panama does not maintain a dedicated EMI license class. Offshore digital banks and EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Mercury, Payoneer, Currencycloud) generally decline Panamanian Private Interest Foundations due to enhanced due diligence exposure, or require an SBP-licensed local branch or sponsor bank. Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound founding capital is wired from abroad via Fedwire or CHIPS to a U.S. correspondent bank of the Panamanian institution, then credited domestically through RTGS/ACH to the PIF's Banistmo or Banco National operating account. The SWIFT MT103 free-text and purpose code must state: 'Capital contribution to a Private Interest Foundation constituted under Law 25 of 1995; funding for GPS-sealed bonded truck fleet engaged in airport feeder service (ISIC 5222)'. Verification protocol requires notarized council minutes, a capital allocation schedule by individual truck unit or equipment line, and a Beneficial Owner declaration.
