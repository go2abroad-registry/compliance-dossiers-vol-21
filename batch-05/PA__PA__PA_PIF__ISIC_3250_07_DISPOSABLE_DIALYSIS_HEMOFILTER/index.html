Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), attached to the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) of Panama; electronic taxpayer portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa (Sede Electrónica / SITA system).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned PIF obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by: (1) filing the foundation’s charter and registered agent certificate with the Registro Público de Panamá; (2) legalizing/apostilling and providing official Spanish translation when foreign incorporation is involved; (3) presenting the RUC application to the DGI via the taxpayer portal or agency window together with the foundation’s bylaws, certificate of existence and a designation of local fiscal representative; (4) paying the applicable Registry and DGI fees; and (5) receiving the RUC certificate and, if taxable supplies will be made, the ITBMS (VAT) account. Non-resident entities without a PE register expediently for ITBMS purposes only, using a temporary non-resident RUC.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Panamanian law; classification follows statutory legal form and activity. A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) may, however, elect its operating posture: (a) remain a passive asset-holding foundation with no Panamanian-source commercial activity, which keeps it outside entity-level income tax and ITBMS; or (b) register its Panamanian-source commercial activity (manufacturing, invoicing, shipments) and thereby become an entity-level territorial taxpayer subject to ISR and ITBMS. The Foundation may also designate in its bylaws that its assets are used only for the purposes described in its deed, which affects DGI characterization and the availability of the annual flat foundation fee.

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 0 (no threshold once any taxable Panamanian-source supply is made); small-taxpayer simplified regime threshold: B/. 36,000 annual gross turnover. Non-residents supplying into Panama must register for ITBMS in the same manner as residents when their actions are performed in Panama or the taxable act is to be used in Panama.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported from Panama to a foreign destination qualify for the 0% ITBMS rate, provided the exporter holds a valid RUC/ITBMS registration, has obtained the definitive export authorization from the Panamanian customs authority (ANA) and retains evidence of territorial exit. Offshore services, and services rendered outside Panama to non-residents, are also treated as non-Panamanian-source and are excluded from ITBMS (zero-rated) when the beneficiary and place of consumption are abroad. The rate also extends to construction and repair services on vessels/aircraft used in international traffic and to goods entered into Colon Free Zone operatives when the physical removal to foreign territory is demonstrated.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera, DUA) approved by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA), showing the exported product classification and destination.
  • Bill of Lading (ocean/air/international modal transport document) evidencing actual physical departure from Panamanian territory to the overseas consignee.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank certificate evidencing deposit/credit of the export sales proceeds to a Panamanian bank account, where proceeds repatriation is contractually required).

Panama mandates electronic fiscal invoicing ('Facturación Electrónica DGI') for all registered ITBMS taxpayers. Invoices must be generated through the DGI-approved fiscal channel (web portal or certified ERP API) and obtain real-time DGI authorization/clearing before delivery to the buyer. Each invoice must contain a DGI digital stamp, GUID/folio, QR code and the taxpayer’s RUC. Export invoices must identify the customs export declaration and are cancellable only with customs exit evidence. Failure to clear electronically renders the invoice invalid, disallows the buyer’s input-credit and exposes the seller to fiscal-infraction fines.

Zero-rating is scrutinized against DUA, transport, bank, and insurance records. If the DGI or ANA determines that an export was fictitious, misdescribed, or that the goods did not leave Panama, the exporter is retroactively assessed the unpaid 7% ITBMS, with late interest, and subject to a monetary surcharge of 25%–75% of the omitted tax (higher when fraud is proven). Fraudulent zero-rating may also trigger criminal tax-fraud liability, customs seizure, suspension of the issuer’s electronic-invoice authorization, cancellation of the RUC, and an extended audit limitation period of up to seven years in cases of scheme/fraud. Refunds already paid can be clawed back, with statutory interest running from the date of the false refund.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full and unrestricted convertibility under a completely dollarized economy. The Panamanian balboa (PAB) exists solely in metallic coinage at a fixed 1:1 legal parity with the U.S. dollar (USD), which constitutes the entire paper-money base and legal tender for all transactions. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are fully open, with no restrictions on inflow, outflow, lending, repatriation, or pricing of foreign exchange. For the PIF's dialysis-hemofilter export business, profit remittance is entirely free in USD. Panama applies a territorial tax system: the 10% dividend/distribution tax applies only to profits derived from Panama-source income; export-derived proceeds are exempt Panama-source income and may be remitted to non-resident beneficiaries without additional tax withholding, provided the entity maintains clean transfer-pricing and substance documentation under Law 254 of 2021.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No compulsory surrender, conversion, or repatriation timeline exists for export proceeds. Exporters may retain 100% of export receipts in USD or any other convertible currency held in Panamanian or offshore bank accounts. Regulatory obligations are limited to (i) MINSA/DIGESA sanitary registration of the medical device, (ii) filing of the export customs declaration through the SIMDA single-window system, and (iii) retention of the export invoice, bill of lading, and purchase order in the trade dossier for bank and tax audit purposes. None of these documents triggers a foreign-exchange conversion or timeline-based surrender event.

Central bank supervision: Panama operates without an independent central bank or monetary authority. The Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá - SBP) is the sole banking, AML/CFT, and financial-system supervisor under Decree Law 9 of 1998, as amended by Law 79 of 2020. Banco Nacional de Panamá, the state-owned commercial bank, acts as fiscal agent and clearing agent for the central government but possesses no exchange-control or currency-issuance powers. There is therefore no Central Bank FX window, no prior-approval mechanism for foreign investment, and no exchange-rate policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low-to-Medium. Panama's Law 112 of 2023 establishes an SBP-regulated Fintech Sandbox but does not create an EU-style electronic-money institution (EMI) license; locally licensed sandbox firms may perform pilot payment activities with reduced capital during a 12–36-month window. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Mercury, Payoneer) frequently decline Panamanian Private Interest Foundations due to the foundation's private bylaws and the absence of a conventional shareholding register for automated beneficial-ownership extraction. Legal constraint: if an offshore EMI lands in Panama, executes payments on behalf of the entity to Panamanian counterparties, or holds funds in custody, it must obtain SBP authorization; failure to do so shifts tax and regulatory risk to the PIF. Recommended architecture: use the tier-1 Panamanian bank as the principal treasury account and employ the EMI strictly as a pass-through collection rail for export invoices, with the EMI contract explicitly stating it is not a Panamanian-constituted deposit-taking intermediary.

Stage 06

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat corporate income tax on net taxable income derived from Panamanian sources under the territorial income tax system (Código Fiscal, Ley 77 de 1956 as amended). Income sourced outside Panama, including export manufacturing sales, is outside thePanamanian tax net.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding on dividends distributed from net income subject to Panamanian-source taxation (Código Fiscal on dividends). Dividends and PIF distributions paid out of non-Panamanian, foreign-source patrimony are subject to 0% Panamanian withholding. Treaty-resident corporate shareholders holding at least 10% may benefit from a reduced withholding rate of 5% where a Double Tax Agreement applies.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Form 102 must be filed no later than 31 March following the fiscal year end (three months after close of the calendar year). A discretionary extension of up to two months may be requested from the DGI before the original due date; the extension does not postpone payment of assessed tax.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 1,500,000 (Balboa parity) in annual gross revenue is the general threshold triggering the mandatory transfer pricing sworn declaration and contemporaneous Local File. Separate mandatory documentation is required where annual related-party transactions exceed USD 250,000, even if total revenue is below USD 1,500,000. Multi-national groups with consolidated revenue above EUR 750,000,000 must file Country-by-Country Reports through the DGI under Panama's OECD BEPS minimum standards (Law 23 of 2015 provisions).

Economic substance scope: Panama has no standalone Economic Substance Regulations of the UAE/BVI type for ordinary PIFs, but the territorial tax regime applies a de facto economic-situs test: profits attributed to foreign-source activity are exempt only if substantive activities (manufacturing, purchasing, invoicing, and decision-making) occur outside Panama. Panama-Pacifico and SEM preferential regimes are conditioned on headcount, local premises, and core-income-generating activities per OECD substantial-activity factor. A PIF with personnel, contracts, and bank accounts all in Panama and merely labeling income as 'foreign-source' risks recharacterization. Law 254 of 2021 strengthened transparency and beneficial-ownership reporting for foundations; entities with gross revenue above USD 3,000,000 must file DGI-audited financial statements, and any artificial structuring to avoid the USD 250,000 related-party threshold is an aggravating circumstance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Panama Código Fiscal criminal tax fraud provisions (introduced by Law 38 of 2010): imprisonment of 2–5 years and a fine of up to 200% of the evaded tax; civil re-assessment of the full tax, monthly 12% annual interest, and late-filing surcharges of 5% per month capped at 50%.

Director personal liability: Directors, financial officers, and the PIF council members who sign fraudulent returns are personally liable for civil restitution; the Public Ministry may issue travel bans (pedimento) and preventive detention; conviction triggers disqualification from holding office in Panamanian companies and full corporate-veil piercing against personal assets.
Shell Company / PIF Economic-Substance Misuse

Statutory sanction: DGI recharacterizes the transaction under the real-economic-situs principle, taxes the hidden Panamanian beneficiary, and imposes the 50% penalty on the undeclared tax; failure to maintain accounting records at the registered office triggers administrative fines; repeated misuse may lead to court-ordered foundation dissolution and cancellation of the resident agent's license.

Director personal liability: The Consejo de Fundación and protector owe fiduciary duties; fraudulent asset transfers make them personally liable to creditors and the DGI to the extent of assets transferred; criminal prosecution can follow under Law 70 of 2019 where the shell was used for laundering trade proceeds.
AML and Sanctions Violations in Trade Finance

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015 (amended by Law 70 of 2019), administrative fines up to USD 1,000,000 per violation for legal persons; money-laundering penalties under the Código Penal of 5–10 years' imprisonment for the underlying predicate offence; DGI and UAF joint freezing of bank accounts and revocation of the RUC.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly execute or authorize transfers to proscribed persons are subject to criminal liability; the UAF may place them on the politically exposed persons watchlist, impose travel bans, and recommend extradition under Panama's mutual legal assistance treaty with the United States; personal assets are reachable via criminal forfeiture.
Withholding, Social Security, and Advance Dividend Tax Abuses

Statutory sanction: Non-remitted withholdings attract the 5%-per-month surcharge (capped at 50%) plus 12% annual interest; CSS crimes for conversion of employee payroll deductions expose the entity to fines up to USD 100,000 and imprisonment up to 10 years for repeat violations; the ISD delinquency blocks the issuance of solvency certificates and the ability to transfer company equity.

Director personal liability: Directors and financial officers who knowingly divert withheld staff contributions are jointly and severally liable for the full shortfall; criminal prosecution by the Ministerio Público on complaint of CSS can result in preventive detention, professional disbarment, and personal bankruptcy orders for restitution.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: The DGI may make a presumptive re-assessment using the highest-arm's-length comparable; the adjustment is subject to 12% annual interest and a 50% penalty on the recomputed tax; the taxpayer loses the loss-carryforward entitlements for the audited year; formal fines of USD 10,000–50,000 apply for the missing annex.

Director personal liability: Signing officers are jointly liable for the 50% surcharge if the company is insolvent; the DGI sends the file to the Public Prosecutor for criminal tax fraud; a negative solvency certificate blocks the PIF from paying dividends, liquidating assets, or financing operations, and the resident agent may resign and trigger compulsory winding-up.
