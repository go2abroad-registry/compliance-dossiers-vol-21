Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance – taxpayer portal: Sede Electrónica (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must appoint a local registered agent in Panama, present notarized constitutional documents (certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and good-standing certificate from the home jurisdiction), obtain a local address mandate, and file the corresponding DGI Form with supporting documents. The DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) as the combined TIN/VAT ID. For ITBMS registration, the taxpayer completes the RUC registration and selects applicable economic activities.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign entity must appoint a local registered agent in Panama, present notarized constitutional documents (certificate of incorporation, bylaws, and good-standing certificate from the home jurisdiction), obtain a local address mandate, and file the corresponding DGI Form with supporting documents. The DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) as the combined TIN/VAT ID. For ITBMS registration, the taxpayer completes the RUC registration and selects applicable economic activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama follows a territorial tax system; a Sociedad Anónima is automatically taxed only on Panama-source income. No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are applicable. Certain entities may elect simplified small-taxpayer regimes under thresholds, but no general entity classification election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No universal monetary threshold; any person performing taxable acts of circulation, delivery, transfer, or import of movable goods or supplying services in Panama must register for ITBMS. Reduced obligations apply to small taxpayers below B/.36,000 annual gross receipts (approximately).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible personal property and offshore services are eligible for the ITBMS zero rate provided the goods physically leave Panama or the services are rendered for use outside Panama. The zero rate applies where the exporter holds documentary evidence of the export transaction and meets DGI registration and reporting requirements. Services supplied to non-resident clients and used entirely abroad also qualify.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing foreign currency repatriation
  • Commercial Invoice
  • Service Agreement or Contract
  • DGI Zero-Rating Support Form (if applicable)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) through the DGI's Sede Electrónica. Taxpayers must transmit invoice data using DGI-approved XML schemas and receive a digitally authorized stamp. Certain large and medium taxpayers are required to use real-time electronic transmission; all taxpayers must maintain digital records and submit periodic ITBMS returns electronically.

The DGI may audit zero-rated claims and require proof of export and foreign exchange repatriation. Fraudulent or substantively deficient claims trigger clawback of input ITBMS credits, fines up to 200% of the omitted tax, surcharges, interest, and criminal liability under the Panama Fiscal Code for tax evasion.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Complete convertibility, both de jure and de facto. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a non-issued subsidiary currency pegged irrevocably at 1:1 to the U.S. dollar, which circulates as legal tender; no prior approval, licensing, or surrender regime applies to current-account flows, such as creator-net-revenue remittances or advertising rebates, nor to capital-account flows, such as shareholder capital injections or repatriation of equity. Profit remittance is unrestricted: foreign-source income earned by the Panama S.A. from TikTok creator management is outside the territorial income-tax base, and dividend distributions sourced from such income carry no Panama withholding, provided the income is properly traced and documented; domestic-source distributions remain subject to the applicable statutory dividend withholding but are freely payable in USD without exchange-control clearance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no percentage of export or digital-service proceeds that must be repatriated or sold into local currency. The MCN's export receivables, comprising global TikTok creator-net-revenue shares and advertising commissions, may be retained offshore in intercompany treasury accounts or remitted directly to the Panamanian account in USD without coerced conversion. The sole ancillary obligation is the annual reporting of balances and related-party flows to the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) under the fiscal transparency and transfer-pricing framework.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — the sole licensing, supervisory, and prudential authority for all banking and fintech operators in the Republic of Panama. Panama maintains no issuing central bank and no foreign-exchange control commission; monetary policy is effectively delegated to the U.S. Federal Reserve through full dollarization, while the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) exercises residual fiscal authority over capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary payment aggregator, but never as a primary treasury account. Panama's SBP licensing regime restricts deposit-taking, USD settlement, and the operation of clearing accounts to licensed banks; offshore digital EMIs, such as Wise B2B and Payoneer, may lawfully open multi-currency collection accounts for a Panamanian S.A. to receive creator payouts or prefund advertising wallets under commercial service agreements. However, such EMIs are not supervised banks in Panama, offer no access to the local automated clearing system, create unilateral freeze and unlicensed-deposit risk, and cannot support capital-escrow attestation, correspondent USD clearing, or documentary instruments. The compliant treasury blueprint therefore uses the EMI only as a receivable gateway, with the principal treasury ledger, liquidity, and clearing functions residing in the SBP-licensed tier-1 institution.

Master / Local File threshold: USD 250,000 aggregated related-party transactions annually: mandatory Local File (Información de Sustentación), full transfer-pricing study and Form 290 annexation; the Master File/CbC obligation attaches to multinational groups whose consolidated turnover exceeded USD 750,000,000 in the preceding period.

Economic substance scope: Applicable: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations apply to all legal entities (including an S.A. MCN agency) carrying on relevant activities — provision of management, distribution, and intellectual-property services. Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent filing

Director personal liability: Signing directors and the resident agent are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax and penalties; courts may order travel bans, precautionary seizure of personal assets, domiciliary arrest, and disqualification from holding corporate offices in Panama.
