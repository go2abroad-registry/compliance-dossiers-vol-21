Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No legal minimum paid-up or subscribed capital for an S.A. under Ley 32 de 1927; a customary authorized capital of USD 10,000 is adopted, with at least one share issued and subscribed, which may remain unpaid. Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; operational portal: 'Sede Electrónica de la DGI' and the SIFE e-invoicing platform (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa; https://sife.mef.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

After incorporation of the Sociedad Anónima (S.A.) in the Registro Público, the appointed resident agent/legal representative obtains the Ficha de Registro and the certificate of incorporation. Through the DGI Sede Electrónica, the representative authenticates with the Digital Signature issued by the Registro Público, files the Inspección/Inscripción de Contribuyente application and obtains the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente). The applicant must simultaneously activate the ITBMS taxpayer status under the Régimen General and declare the principal business activity under ISIC 7830/NAICS 561312. A municipal Aviso de Operación is a separate local business registration. No minimum paid-in capital is required; foreign ownership is fully permitted and all filings may be executed through an appointed resident legal representative with notarized powers of attorney.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not maintain a US-style check-the-box classification; an S.A. is always a juridical person subject to entity-level income tax only on Panama-source net income. Within this territorial framework, the entity may elect: (i) the Régimen Especial de Pequeños Contribuyentes where gross annual operations fall below the legal ceiling; (ii) qualification of cross-border executive-search services as export services under Article 1057-K of the Código Fiscal, enabling the 0% ITBMS treatment; (iii) either annual or quarterly ISR estimation under the saldos trimestrales system; and (iv) the Régimen de Transparencia Fiscal Internacional to attribute passive foreign-source income for Panamanian residents where the statutory threshold applies.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.0 (no statutory de minimis threshold). Any S.A. habitually supplying taxable services in Panama must register for ITBMS before the first billing. Micro-operators with annual taxable receipts below B/.36,000 may qualify for the simplified cuota regime, subject to current DGI regulations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Executive-search and C-suite headhunting fees qualify for the 0% ITBMS export rate when: (1) the contracting client is a non-resident corporation or a foreign individual not domiciled in Panama; (2) the services (candidate research, selection, assessment, and recruitment management) are rendered for use, exercise, and exploitation exclusively outside Panama; (3) the consideration is received from abroad in USD through SWIFT or Wise B2B rails into a Panamanian bank account; and (4) the S.A. maintains a complete contemporaneous export file. DGI scrutiny focuses on the economic substance and the real destination of the service; back-to-back arrangements with related foreign parties are disregarded.

  • DGI-authorized SIFE export invoice endorsed at 0% (with fiscal QR/authorization code)
  • Executed executive-search retainer agreement specifying the Panama-to-foreign client supply and foreign-use jurisdiction
  • Foreign client's certificate of tax residence or sworn non-resident declaration and foreign corporate registration excerpt
  • Bank SWIFT MT103 or Inward Remittance Certificate evidencing payment from abroad in USD
  • Sworn declaration by the S.A. confirming exclusive offshore exploitation of the recruitment results
  • Delivery package: candidate shortlists, search reports, and engagement letters evidencing that placement decisions took effect abroad

Mandatory electronic invoicing through the DGI's SIFE (Sistema de Facturación Electrónica) platform. Invoices are issued via DGI-approved API software or the DGI's free web portal. Each valid invoice receives a unique fiscal authorization code and QR stamp; the SIFE validates the invoice against the taxpayer's RUC and returns the authorization in near-real time, with limited batch mode permitted during DGI system outages. The executive-search S.A. must integrate SIFE in order to issue both 7% invoices for Panamanian clients and 0% invoices for export services.

Export zero-rating remains subject to DGI audit for the 5-year fiscal prescription period. If the export classification is disallowed, the full 7% ITBMS is assessed as unpaid and the S.A. faces: (i) the statutory recargo (10% for the first two months of delinquency plus 5% for each additional month, capped at 50%); (ii) default interest; (iii) administrative fines of up to 100% of the omitted tax for incorrect or false documentation; (iv) revocation of the SIFE export-invoice authorization; and (v) for wilful misrepresentation of foreign use, criminal defraudación tributaria liability under Law 70 of 2019 with imprisonment of one to five years, plus disqualification of the resident legal representative.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains a fully dollarized monetary system with no foreign-exchange controls on either current or capital account transactions. There are no legal restrictions on the conversion of USD into any other currency, no surrender obligations, and no licensing requirements for foreign-currency remittances. Profit and dividend repatriation is freely permitted; however, dividends paid from Panama-source taxable income are subject to a 5% dividend tax under Cabinet Decree No. 152 of 2010, while distributions sourced from non-Panama foreign-source income fall outside the territorial tax base.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory timeline for export-proceeds surrender and no mandatory conversion ratio to local currency because the U.S. dollar is itself the domestic currency. Exporters may freely retain, deposit, or transfer export receipts in any offshore or onshore USD account.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank of issue; the U.S. dollar is fully legal tender under Decree Law 1 of 1904 and Legislative Decree 3 of 1988. Banking supervision is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). The Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent of the Government, but neither it nor the SBP exercises conventional central-bank monetary policy or lender-of-last-resort functions.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium for regulated EU/UK EMIs (e.g., Wise Business) when used solely as non-resident non-Panamanian payment corridors. Legal constraints: such EMIs are not licensed by the SBP and cannot maintain Panamanian local accounts or provide Panamanian IBANs; they may operate only as unregulated third-party payment providers for inbound/outbound USD remittances. Any account denominated in USD held with an EMI is not a Panama banking deposit and therefore enjoys no SBP depositor guarantee. For a Panama-domiciled S.A., local treasury must be conducted through an SBP-regulated bank, while EMIs are acceptable only for disbursements to beneficiaries outside Panama and must be blocked if active inbound collections within Panama are detected.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound capital injections to a Panama S.A. are routed via SWIFT MT103 using the message field 70 (Remittance Information) explicitly stating 'CONTRIBUTION TO SHARE CAPITAL' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN'. A capital escrow account at an SBP-regulated bank receives the funds; the bank will require the corporate resolution, UBO declaration, and source-of-funds evidence before crediting. For purpose codes, Panama's banking system does not impose central-bank FX codes, but banks commonly require a standardized Economic Activity Code (e.g., ISIC 7830) and may request the remitter's bank to include 'LY1 – Personal Income' for an equity contribution or 'LX1 – Trade in Services' for a retainer. Verification includes concordance between the corporate name on the wire and the SBP-registered licence, and an active Compliance Officer review at the receiving bank.For treasury disbursements, the primary clearing rail is the Onshore US Dollar Clearing System operated by the SBP in conjunction with U.S. Federal Reserve correspondent banks. Operational payments (vendor invoices, candidate disbursements, payroll) are executed via SWIFT MT103 with beneficiary account details and rationale in Field 70. For high-value executive-retainer arrangements where a commercial letter of credit is required, Panama banks issue SBLC or stand-by letters of credit (ISBP 745 / UCP 600) against the client's collateral. Given the low risk rating of ISIC 7830, an LC is rarely necessary, but when a client insists, a standard irrevocable documentary LC payable at sight via SWIFT MT700 may be used. The treasury desk is expected to reconcile each outbound wire to an underlying signed placement agreement, and to use only the corporate bank's sanctioned-beneficiary screening channel.USD/EUR Forward Contracts: OTC outright forwards with 1-week to 12-month tenors provided by SBP-licensed banks, used to hedge retainer fees denominated in EUR if the corporate invoicing currency under contract is EUR.USD/GBP Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs): Available at large Panamanian banks for speculative or hedge positions against severely restricted currencies; permissible in Panama because there is no FX surrender regime, but must be reported under the bank's internal derivative risk policy.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after the fiscal year-end; calendar-year Sociedades Anónimas file no later than 31 March of the following year. Taxpayers subject to the advance-payment regime remit Form 105 anticipos monthly and credit those amounts against the DD-101 liability.

Box 1a Gross Panama-source revenue from executive-search retainers, success fees, and C-suite technology headhunting commissions. Report only fee income whose contract negotiation, candidate delivery, and invoice performance occurred in Panama; foreign-source fees are separately disclosed at Box 2 and excluded from the taxable base.
