Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); principal online portal: Sistema de Gestión de Ingresos (SIGIR) / e-Tax at https://etax.mef.gob.pa and https://www.dgi.mef.gob.pa.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) is registered in the Registro Público de Panamá; for tax purposes it must request a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) through the DGI portal or at the DGI offices only if the entity is engaged in activities generating Panamanian-source income or is required by a Panamanian counterparty or bank to present a tax ID. The PIF's resident agent (Apoderado) completes the contributor registration form, presents the foundation certificate and the generic status from the Public Registry, appoints a fiscal representative if applicable, and obtains the RUC and ITBMS registration in the same procedure. For a pure offshore PIF with no Panama-source commercial activity and no digital services sales into Panama, no RUC or ITBMS registration is statutorily required, but financial institutions may request a RUC or an affidavit of non-tax liability.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama applies a territorial source-of-income system without US-style check-the-box elections. A PIF is not classified for tax as a pass-through trust; it is a separate legal person taxed at entity level only on Panama-source income. The foundation may elect to carry on no taxable activity as a pure asset-holding vehicle or may elect to submit an annual tax return when it receives Panama-source income. Special regime elections include Multinational Headquarters (SEM) under Law 41/2007 and Free Zone/City of Knowledge incentives; no hybrid mismatch or transparent entity elections exist.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold for ITBMS registration; any person habitually transferring taxable goods or rendering taxable services in Panama must register. However, small taxpayers with annual gross income up to B/.250,000 may use the simplified small-taxpayer regime (régimen de pequeños contribuyentes), which replaces the ordinary ITBMS return with a simplified monthly quota.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods physically exported out of Panamanian customs territory are zero-rated under ITBMS if the exporter is registered and the sale is evidenced by a valid export customs declaration and transport document. Services are treated as exported and zero-rated when the recipient is located abroad, the service is not physically or legally connected to Panama, and the benefit is received and used outside Panama; supporting contracts and payment evidence must identify the non-resident beneficiary. Merely invoicing a foreign customer from Panama is insufficient; the exporter must demonstrate that the economic effect of the operation occurs abroad. Input ITBMS related to zero-rated exports may be refunded through the saldo a favor mechanism after DGI verification.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduanas de Exportación / DEX) endorsed by Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas
  • Transport document — original Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Panama
  • Final Commercial Invoice and Packing List identifying foreign buyer and export terms
  • Inward remittance certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice for payment of exported goods/services
  • Copy of export contract or service agreement and non-resident beneficiary's tax identity evidence

Panama has phased mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) via the DGI Facturación en Línea/SFE platform. Invoices must be issued in DGI-validated XML/JSON formats with a unique digital stamp (clave de aceptación), a QR code, and sequential fiscal authorization. Medium and large taxpayers must transmit invoices in near real-time using DGI-approved API/REST endpoints or the certified web portal; the DGI performs before-issuance authentication and returns acceptance or rejection codes. Credit notes, debit notes, and payment receipts follow the same electronic flow; exports must be marked with the zero-rated indicator.

The DGI may audit export zero-rating claims up to six years from the filing deadline, and this period becomes unlimited in cases of non-filing or fraudulent intent. If documents are false, missing, or the goods or services are not in fact exported, the DGI claws back all zero-rated output VAT, disallows associated input VAT credits, and imposes a surcharge of 1.5% per month plus statutory interest. Tax evasion penalties range from 100% to 200% of the tax omitted; use of falsified export declarations can trigger criminal prosecution under Ley 38/2004 and customs smuggling sanctions. The absence of a Panamanian exchange-control regime means the 180-day remittance term is a documentary benchmark rather than a strict currency requirement, but failure to show collection can still defeat the zero-rating claim.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized economy with no central exchange control regime. Capital and current account transactions are generally free from convertibility restrictions. Profit remittances, dividends, and capital repatriations by Private Interest Foundations (PIFs) are permissible without prior central bank approval, provided the foundation is in good standing, economic substance obligations (where applicable) are satisfied, and the remittance is effected through a licensed Panama banking entity or recognized international clearing channel. No surrender requirements or FX licensing hurdles exist for inbound or outbound flows.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama does not impose statutory export proceeds surrender requirements or mandatory conversion ratios. Export receipts may be held freely in U.S. dollars or any other convertible currency, and may be disbursed offshore without repatriation obligation.

Central bank supervision: Banco Nacional de Panamá; Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) regulates licensed banks; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) oversees fiscal matters. Panama does not operate a traditional central bank issuing national fiat currency; the U.S. dollar is the legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Moderately viable with legal constraints. Panama recognizes digital wallet operators and payment service providers under SBP regulation, but non-licensed offshore Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) cannot directly open settlement accounts in Panama regulated banks without a local license or a formal correspondent banking arrangement. An offshore EMI may be used only as a payment collection conduit, not as the primary treasury clearing rail, unless it is licensed by the SBP or operates under a recognized fintech passport arrangement. Adverse regulatory perception and de-risking policies of correspondent banks increase the compliance burden; therefore, the PIF should maintain a primary bank relationship with a Tier 1 Panama licensed bank and use EMIs strictly for merchant acquiring and low-value SaaS subscription collections.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections into the PIF's Panama bank account must comply with general KYC and source-of-funds verification protocols. Recommended routing: founder's originating bank sends a SWIFT MT103 wire to the PIF's account at a licensed Panama bank using appropriate purpose codes such as 'Capital contribution to private interest foundation' or 'Initial foundation endowment'. The Panama bank will require the remitter to complete a funds-origin questionnaire, and the PIF must provide the Foundation Charter and council resolution authorizing the capital contribution. For amounts above approximately USD 10,000, enhanced due diligence reports are triggered; amounts above USD 100,000 may require additional written explanation of the wealth accumulation path. No exchange-control approval is required because Panama is fully dollarized, but the receiving bank may request clarification for unusually large or rapid injections.Outbound trade and service disbursements from the PIF are executed via SWIFT MT103 wire transfers, commercial letters of credit (documentary credits), and, where appropriate, SWIFT MT202/MT205 for bank-to-bank transfers between the PIF's treasury accounts. The PIF must maintain a clear invoice ledger, signed disbursement instructions, and beneficial purchase records for each outgoing payment. For SaaS operational disbursements to vendors and contractors, ACH rails within Panama and SWIFT for international counterparties are acceptable.
