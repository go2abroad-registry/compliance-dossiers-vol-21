Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

Ley 32 de 26 de febrero de 1927 (General Corporations Law on Sociedades Anónimas), as amended by Law 5 of 2001 and Law 49 of 2002; Public Registry Regulations (Acuerdo No. 4 of 2003); Decree Law 2 of 2011 for AML obligations.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública de Constitución / Pacto Social (Articles of Incorporation executed as a public deed before a licensed Panamanian Notary Public and subsequently inscribed in the Public Registry).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 30 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital required under Law 32 of 1927; the authorized and subscribed capital is freely determined in the Escritura Pública, with at least one share issued. No statutory escrow, bank deposit, or capital verification certificate is required.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate (Certificado de Seguridad, Prevención y Protección contra Incendios) issued by the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá after inspection of the office premises; structural and occupancy clearance from the Municipality of Panama (Dirección de Obras y Construcciones) validating the building load and occupational capacity; and workplace health & safety registration with MITRADEL, including first-aid, emergency-exit, and ergonomic compliance for the professional advisory staff.

Paid-in capital rule: No minimum paid-up capital required under Law 32 of 1927; the authorized and subscribed capital is freely determined in the Escritura Pública, with at least one share issued. No statutory escrow, bank deposit, or capital verification certificate is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — the Panamanian S.A. is a separate legal entity taxed at the corporate level under the Impuesto sobre la Renta (ISR) only on Panamanian-source income; foreign-source M&A advisory income falls outside the Panamanian tax net, and dividends paid to non-resident shareholders are exempt from Panamanian withholding tax. No pass-through status is recognized for domestic tax purposes.

Available election forms

Panama does not implement U.S.-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. Available statutory elections: (i) Sociedad de Inversión (Investment Holding Company) election under Law 32 of 2011, covering foreign-source dividends, interest and capital gains; (ii) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) licence under Law 41 of 2007, granting reduced income and indirect tax benefits for regional service hubs (subject to MICI criteria); (iii) small taxpayer simplified regime (Régimen de Pequeño Contribuyente) under Law 318 of 2022 where annual gross income does not exceed PAB/USD 20,000.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

0.07%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold: every taxable person supplying professional services in Panama must register for ITBMS from the first taxable transaction. A small taxpayer alternative applies only where annual gross turnover is PAB/USD 20,000 or less, in which case the DGI permits a fixed simplified payment instead of full monthly declarations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% ITBMS rate applies to (i) goods physically exported under Panama Customs procedures and (ii) services rendered to non-resident clients provided the benefit/economic use occurs outside Panama — which includes cross-border M&A advisory, merger structuring, due diligence and success-fee brokerage services performed for foreign acquirers or targets. Zero-rating is denied where the services are effectively exploited, or the direct beneficiary is resident, in Panama.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera — DUA, processed through SIGA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (Conocimiento de Embarque / Guía Aérea) for goods-based exports
  • Commercial invoice and signed M&A advisory agreement identifying the non-resident counterparty and the offshore use of the service
  • Certificado de exportador de servicios / service-export certificate issued by DGI/MICI for cross-border advisory revenue
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from the receiving Panamanian bank evidencing timely collection of the foreign-currency fee

Mandatory electronic invoicing for all active taxpayers: invoices must be generated through DGI-certified billing software connected to the Sede Electrónica e-invoicing module (Sistema de Facturación Electrónica), transmitted electronically (API/web-services) with a fiscal security code (Código de Seguridad Fiscal) and QR; the DGI validates and returns an acceptance token in near-real-time. Export invoices (facturas de exportación) are separate e-document types referencing the customs or service-export registration. Paper-only invoicing is invalid except under transitional DGI regulations.

The DGI may audit zero-rating files within the statutory prescription window (typically 3–5 years). Missing or inconsistent substantiation triggers retroactive assessment of the 7% ITBMS, monthly moratory interest, and fines: 10%–50% for documentary infractions and up to 100%–200% of the omitted tax for fraudulent invoices or simulated exports, plus criminal liability (fines and imprisonment) for falsified customs/export certificates. The taxpayer may also be removed from the e-invoicing and exporter registers, suspending the right to zero-rate future invoices.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Panamanian balboa is a nominal unit of account with no physical currency; only U.S. dollar banknotes circulate. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible without exchange control restrictions. No central bank approval is required for repatriation of capital, dividends, or profits. Banks may impose their own internal documentation requirements for large remittances under AML/CFT obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no surrender requirements, no mandatory conversion timelines, and no legal quota for foreign currency earnings generated by Panamanian entities. Export proceeds may be retained in any currency, freely transferred, or offset against imports and trade settlements. Panama's territorial tax system taxes only income derived from Panama-sourced operations, and the tax authorities do not impose foreign exchange conversion or repatriation conditions.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank; monetary supervision is exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), which supervises all banking activities. The U.S. dollar serves as the sole legal tender and fiscal currency under the Monetary Agreement with the United States (1904), so there is no domestic monetary issuance authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium–Restricted. Panama does not recognise a standalone 'Electronic Money Institution' (EMI) licence in the EU/UK sense. Law 23 of 2021 introduced a fintech sandbox and permits digital banking and 'technological financial innovation' activities, but a full banking or specialised licence is needed to hold client funds. Offshore EMIs without a local banking licence cannot maintain accounts directly, but can operate as white-label payment facilitators through local banks or through authorised 'Interbank National Payment Services' under SBP regulations. The SBP requires local presence and a full compliance infrastructure for any entity receiving deposits or executing payments. Thus, cross-border EMIs should partner with a tier-1 Panamanian bank rather than attempt direct account access.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital for the Panamanian advisory boutique is typically routed via U.S. dollar wire transfers (SWIFT MT103) from the parent entity or non-resident shareholder. No exchange-control approval is needed. Banks require the remitter to include a clear payment reference such as 'Capital Contribution', 'Additional Paid-in Capital', or 'Retained Earnings Distribution', and require the local entity to submit a Certificate of Due Incorporation, corporate resolutions, and a sworn statement of source of funds. Larger injections may require the bank's AML committee to review the economic substance and the underlying acquisition mandate. If the capital is injected as a loan, a loan agreement with commercial terms and signature legalisation is mandatory.Trade settlement and success-fee disbursements are executed primarily through SWIFT MT103 (serial/cover payments) for wire transfers and commercial letters of credit for documentary transactions. For M&A advisory fees, SWIFT MT103 with appropriate purpose codes (e.g., service fee, IP royalty, professional fees) and supporting invoice/engagement letter are required. There are no restrictions on outbound transfers, but the bank must complete a transaction form covering the beneficiary, purpose, and source of funds. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% under the general ISR (Impuesto sobre la Renta) regime on net taxable income; territorial sourcing applies, so only Panamanian-source M&A advisory success fees and retainers are within the headline rate.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before March 15 of the calendar year following the close of the fiscal year; for a non-calendar fiscal year, within 90 days of fiscal-year close.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Same statutory deadline as Form 200: March 15 for calendar-year taxpayers; within 90 days of fiscal-year close for off-calendar years.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box 1 - RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) The S.A.'s 12-digit tax identification number; must be obtained before any filing and kept active with the DGI. A non-resident-owned S.A. must maintain a Panamanian legal representative holding a current taxpayer PIN; the representative must authenticate every e-filing. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 2 - Periodo Fiscal Beginning and ending dates of the fiscal year (standard: January 1 to December 31). Changes to the fiscal year require prior DGI notification and must be reflected in the company minutes for non-resident shareholders. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 3 - Ingresos Gravables de Fuente Panameña All gross revenue from M&A advisory, diligence, retainer and success-fee engagements where the service is economically performed in Panama or used in Panama. Document the Panamanian sourcing engagement-by-engagement; do not route Panamanian-sourced fees directly to an offshore holding company. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 4 - Ingresos Exentos y de Fuente Extranjera Foreign-source success fees (services wholly rendered abroad) and exempt Panamanian-source income; declared for information only under territoriality. Maintain time sheets, client location evidence and execution files showing the engagement was performed outside Panama to support the exclusion. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 5 - Costos y Gastos Deducibles Salaries, office rent, data and market-research costs, professional fees and other expenses supported by valid invoices and bank transfers; deductions require that corresponding WHT was paid on local invoices. Non-resident suppliers' invoices for royalties or technical assistance are deductible only if the 12.5% WHT advance was actually withheld and remitted. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 6 - Pérdidas por Compensar Tax losses from the preceding five fiscal years applied against current net income. Loss carry-forward schedules must reconcile with prior-year accepted returns; the DGI may reject carry-forwards if the underlying returns were filed late. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 7 - Impuesto a Pagar antes de Créditos ISR computed at 25% on net taxable income; CAIR-adjusted large taxpayers reflect the required supplementary calculation. Verify that the advance payments system does not convert a withholding obligation into an apparent additional corporate tax burden. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Box 8 - Créditos y Saldo a Pagar o a Favor Credits for monthly Pago Directo advances and WHT suffered on income; result is the final balance payable with the return or the refund claim. Non-resident shareholders may not claim corporate tax credits personally; overpaid credits remain at the S.A. level. Late filing or late payment generates a 1% per-month surcharge (maximum 50% of the unpaid tax) plus default interest; a minimum administrative fine of USD 500 applies to a missing or materially defective return; intentional under-declaration is criminal tax evasion with 2 to 5 years' imprisonment and fines of USD 20,000 to USD 200,000.
Section A - Identificación de la Parte Relacionada Name, jurisdiction, tax identification and relationship of each related foreign counterparty (parent, subsidiaries, shareholder holding companies). Non-resident parent groups must annually complete a related-party questionnaire to permit accurate disclosure of intra-group fee sharing. Failure to file Form 850 triggers an administrative penalty of USD 5,000 to USD 50,000 plus re-assessment of cross-border transactions at arm's length with a 25% surcharge on the under-declared tax and 1% per-month default interest.
Section B - Tipos y Montos de Transacciones Cross-border transactions exceeding USD 1,000,000: management fees, success-fee splits, loans, interest, royalties and IP transfers. M&A boutiques commonly enter international fee-split and referral arrangements; all must be disclosed even if economically immaterial to the group. Failure to file Form 850 triggers an administrative penalty of USD 5,000 to USD 50,000 plus re-assessment of cross-border transactions at arm's length with a 25% surcharge on the under-declared tax and 1% per-month default interest.
Section C - Método de Precios de Transferencia Aplicado Identification of the arm's length method applied (CUP, Resale, Cost Plus, TNMM or Profit Split) and the selected tested party. Method selection requires contemporaneous benchmark comparables; do not adopt a method solely because the non-resident parent uses it in its own filing. Failure to file Form 850 triggers an administrative penalty of USD 5,000 to USD 50,000 plus re-assessment of cross-border transactions at arm's length with a 25% surcharge on the under-declared tax and 1% per-month default interest.
Section D - Ajustes Voluntarios Voluntary transfer pricing adjustments made by the taxpayer to bring the tax base to arm's length before the DGI review. A voluntary adjustment reduces the 50% fraud surcharge to the standard rate and demonstrates cooperative compliance; however it also reveals the original under-declaration. Failure to file Form 850 triggers an administrative penalty of USD 5,000 to USD 50,000 plus re-assessment of cross-border transactions at arm's length with a 25% surcharge on the under-declared tax and 1% per-month default interest.
