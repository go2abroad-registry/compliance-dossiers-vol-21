Competent corporate registry

Governing statute

Ley General de Sociedades (General Corporation Law), Ley No. 32 of 26 February 1927, as amended by Ley No. 47 of 2020 and related provisions; formation, share capital, and administration governed principally by Articles 1 to 7 and 30 to 40; specimens of perpetual duration suffice for a standard S.A.

Formation / constitutional filings

Escritura Pública de Constitución (Public Deed of Incorporation / Articles of Incorporation), executed before a Panamanian Notary and subsequently inscribed in the Mercantile Register.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No minimum paid-up capital is required by Panamanian law. The Articles may declare a fully subscribed but unpaid nominal authorised capital, customarily USD 10,000 divided into ordinary shares. No escrow account or deposit with a bank is required at incorporation; the subscribers may validly declare the shares as paid-up by contribution of services or without a capital deposit.

Funding & unlock path: Before commencing operations, the leased office must obtain the annual Fire Safety Certificate ('Certificado de Prevención de Incendios') issued by the Benemérito Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá, including inspection of extinguishers, emergency exits and electrical panels. The Municipality's Engineering/Architecture Directorate must validate structural load and electrical adequacy of the premises for the Aviso de Operación. Workplace clearance: compliance with MITRADEL's occupational-hygiene and safety rules and the applicable professional-risk legislation, including ergonomic workstations, adequate ventilation, and the employer's obligation to enrol staff in CSS occupational-risk coverage.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); online portals: DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa) and SIME (https://sime.mef.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity must act through a Panamanian licensed attorney and registered agent: (1) incorporate the Sociedad Anónima and record it in the Registro Público; (2) execute corporate resolutions identifying officers and local domicile; (3) submit the RUC application to DGI with the certified articles of incorporation, public registry certificate, officer passports, domicile evidence, and sworn statement of intended activities; (4) DGI issues the RUC and ITBMS registration, generally within five business days. No minimum capital requirement applies.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Panamanian law; a Sociedad Anónima is subject to entity-level tax only on Panamanian-source income. Available elections include voluntary ITBMS registration to claim zero-rating and input VAT credits on export activities, and election of the simplified small-taxpayer regime for annual gross receipts up to USD 120,000.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

None; registration is mandatory from the first taxable transfer of goods or services occurring in Panama. A simplified ITBMS regime is available for small taxpayers with annual gross receipts up to USD 120,000.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (1) registered ITBMS taxpayers exporting tangible goods from Panamanian customs territory and (2) services rendered to non-residents for use or consumption outside Panama, including multilingual translation, subtitling, dubbing, and localization project management. The zero rate is allowed only when the export documents link the invoice to the physical exit or irrevocable offshore delivery; services effectively consumed in Panama remain taxable at 7%.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Formulario Único de Aduana / DUA) endorsed by Panama Customs Authority
  • Commercial invoice issued to the non-resident buyer under the zero rate
  • Bill of Lading (sea freight) or Air Waybill (air freight) evidencing exit from Panamanian territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or banking voucher (e.g., SWIFT MT103, Wise B2B, Payoneer statement) evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Service agreement, work order, and beneficiary certificate confirming the localization services were used outside Panama

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the DGI's SIFE ecosystem. Each invoice must be generated by DGI-certified fiscal software or the web-based DGI module, transmitted to DGI in real time, and validated with a unique authorization code and QR stamp. Invoices not cleared by DGI are treated as undocumented for ITBMS credit purposes and subject the issuer to fines and suspension.

DGI audits zero-rated exporters by reconciling customs export data, transport documents, electronic invoices, and banking inflows. Deficient or false export evidence triggers reversal of the zero rate, assessment of 7% ITBMS on gross proceeds, interest, a surcharge of up to 100% of the omitted tax, and RUC suspension. Criminal fiscal-fraud prosecution under Articles 746 to 753 of the Código Fiscal may be brought, with penalties including imprisonment of two to five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. The Panamanian Balboa is a nominal unit of account pegged at exactly 1:1 with the USD. There are no exchange controls, no restrictions on profit remittances, dividend distributions, or repatriation of foreign capital. Panama's territorial tax system taxes only Panama-source income, and foreign-source income is generally exempt.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Exporters have no legal obligation to surrender, repatriate, or convert export proceeds. All export receipts may be retained in USD in local or offshore accounts, and no central bank approval or conversion requirement applies.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) – No Central Bank exists; Panama operates under a dollarized monetary system with the US Dollar as legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Medium-High. Wise B2B and Payoneer can provide USD-denominated settlement accounts through partner banks in Panama, but they are not licensed as banks or deposit-taking institutions under Panamanian law. They are feasible for collecting international receivables and making cross-border vendor disbursements, but formal treasury operations, payroll, and high-value settlements should be anchored in a licensed Panamanian bank account to maintain clean legal titling, segregation of client funds, and audit integrity.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing uses SWIFT MT103 from the foreign parent or shareholder account in USD to the Panamanian operational account. There is no electronic purpose-code requirement under Panama law, but the bank's internal file should include: a board resolution authorizing the capital contribution or shareholder loan, a signed loan agreement (if applicable), a copy of the corporation's ficha and corporate registration, and foreign UBO identification. No Central Bank approval is required; only usual AML/CFT enhanced due diligence applies to large or complex transfers.Outbound trade settlements are executed through SWIFT MT103 via global clearing rails (with GPI tracking) for USD payments to international vendors. For local Panamanian settlements, the domestic ACH or same-day electronic transfer may be used, but cross-border vendor payments in USD should be cleared correspondent-banking through banks such as JP Morgan, Citibank, or Bank of America. High-value purchase orders for physical media delivery can use a commercial letter of credit under UCP600 issued by a Panamanian bank; for services, a PO-backed wire transfer is standard. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat corporate income tax rate (Impuesto Sobre la Renta, ISR) applied to taxable net income from Panamanian-source activities; small taxpayer bracket of 15% applies to the first USD 11,000 of taxable income for qualifying micro enterprises.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% dividend withholding tax on distributions made by a Panamanian S.A. to resident or non-resident shareholders (Código Fiscal Art. 733); 5% applies to distributions by issuers listed on the Panama Stock Exchange. Dividends paid from already-taxed net income remain subject to the 10% rate unless treaty relief applies.

Late filing or late payment triggers a monthly surcharge of 5% of the unpaid tax (maximum 50%), plus statutory moratorium interest; failure to file after DGI notice carries fines from USD 50 to USD 5,000. Fraudulent non-filing is prosecuted under the tax crimes statute with imprisonment.
Box 11 - Renta Neta Gravable Taxable net income equal to Box 5 minus deductible expenses and NOL offsets. If negative, record the loss for 5-year carryforward tracking; no apportionment of foreign-source costs is permitted against Panamanian taxable income. Late filing or late payment triggers a monthly surcharge of 5% of the unpaid tax (maximum 50%), plus statutory moratorium interest; failure to file after DGI notice carries fines from USD 50 to USD 5,000. Fraudulent non-filing is prosecuted under the tax crimes statute with imprisonment.
Box 13 - ISR Causado Income tax computed at 25% on the taxable net income. Apply the general 25% rate; no special surtax applies to localization services. Late filing or late payment triggers a monthly surcharge of 5% of the unpaid tax (maximum 50%), plus statutory moratorium interest; failure to file after DGI notice carries fines from USD 50 to USD 5,000. Fraudulent non-filing is prosecuted under the tax crimes statute with imprisonment.
Box 18 - Saldo a Pagar o Saldo a Favor Net tax due after credits for monthly prepayments (Form 1056) and withholdings. Pay through DGI E-Tax; refund requests require the A-4 procedure and full bank verification. Late filing or late payment triggers a monthly surcharge of 5% of the unpaid tax (maximum 50%), plus statutory moratorium interest; failure to file after DGI notice carries fines from USD 50 to USD 5,000. Fraudulent non-filing is prosecuted under the tax crimes statute with imprisonment.
Box 1 - RUC Taxpayer identification number. Same RUC as the annual return; this is a corporate-level payment, not a shareholder-level obligation. Late filing of Form 1056: monthly surcharge of 5% of the unpaid advance (maximum 50%) plus moratorium interest; DGI may impose automatic fines per infraction and deny credit in the annual return.
Box 3 - Mes Fiscal Month for which the advance is computed. Enter every calendar month; nil declarations are required for months with no Panamanian-source revenue. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 250,000 (Balboas) in aggregate annual related-party transactions triggers the mandatory Local File and Form 1200 disclosure; a Master File is required for groups with worldwide consolidated revenue above USD 250 million. Below the threshold, the S.A. must still keep contemporaneous evidence of arm's-length pricing.

Economic substance scope: Panama applies Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) under Law 70 of 2019 and Executive Decree 195 of 2020. A translation, localization, and dubbing S.A. operating with real staff, physical premises, and genuine client contracts falls outside the high-risk ESR categories unless it is licensed under the Multinational Enterprise Headquarters (SEM) regime or acts as an intra-group service center for a foreign group. A passive or nominee-managed entity with no demonstrated ability to render the localization services is exposed to ESR review, exchange-of-information inquiries (CRS/FATCA), and DGI recharacterization, which invalidates the territorial exclusion of foreign-source income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Evasión Fiscal)

Statutory sanction: Civil penalty of 50% to 100% of the tax deficiency plus moratorium interest under Código Fiscal Arts. 759-761; criminal conviction under Panama's tax crimes statute carries imprisonment of 3 to 10 years and restitution of the evaded tax.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the evaded tax; the Fiscal Tribunal may order preventive embargoes, travel bans, and immigration alerts; convicted directors face imprisonment and disqualification from managing commercial entities.
Shell Company with Nominee Management

Statutory sanction: DGI applies substance-over-form recharacterization (Código Fiscal Art. 7), reclassifies the foreign-source exclusion as Panamanian-source income, and assesses 25% tax plus 100% evasion penalty; the Registry may cancel the corporate license (Aviso de Operación) and dissolve the entity for inactivity.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors face criminal charges for false corporate declarations; directors may be barred from leaving Panama, have their own assets frozen, and incur civil restitution when corporate funds were dissipated as undisclosed distributions.
AML/CFT Violations (Law 23 of 2015)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of USD 10,000 to USD 1,000,000 under Law 23 of 2015 and the Superintendency of Banks conduct rules; cancellation of the business license; criminal money-laundering charges where predicate tax evasion exists, carrying 5 to 10 years of imprisonment plus asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and compliance officers are personally indictable for money laundering; personal bank accounts, real property, and travel documents may be seized; travel bans remain in force until the criminal case is resolved.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DGI reassesses taxable income using the most appropriate arm's-length method, adds a 50% penalty on the resulting tax deficiency, and imposes fines of up to USD 50,000 for non-filing of the TP report; systematic manipulation is treated as tax fraud with imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors who approved the pricing policy are jointly liable for the adjusted tax and penalties; where the pricing scheme was designed solely to strip Panamanian profits, directors face criminal tax evasion charges and debarment from serving as directors of Panamanian entities.
