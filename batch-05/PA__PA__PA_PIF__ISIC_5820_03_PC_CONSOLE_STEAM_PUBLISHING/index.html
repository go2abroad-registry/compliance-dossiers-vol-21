E-government filing portal

Default tax classification

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a US-style check-the-box entity-classification election. Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full dollarization ensures absolute currency convertibility for both capital and current account transactions. Panama maintains no exchange control restrictions on the transfer of dividends, royalties, licensing fees, or profit remittances originating from the commercial activities of a Private Interest Foundation (PIF). Cross-border repatriation is unrestricted and requires no prior authorization from any monetary authority; the only operative gate is the anti-money-laundering and counter-terrorist-financing (AML/CFT) verification regime under Law 23 of 2015, including the filing of mandatory currency and suspicious transaction reports where thresholds apply. Profit remittances from a PIF engaged in digital publishing are treated as ordinary commercial transfers and are free from foreign exchange approval, conversion, or surrender obligations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panamanian law imposes no statutory timeline, percentage, or escrow requirement for the surrender of export proceeds. All proceeds from digital publishing sales, Steam/Valve payouts, PlayStation Store settlements, and other software licensing revenue may be retained in U.S. Dollars within domestic or offshore accounts without exchange notification or mandatory conversion. The PIF must nonetheless maintain documentary evidence of each commercial receipt, including platform-generated settlement statements and the underlying Steamworks / PlayStation Partner agreements, to demonstrate the lawful origin of funds for banking due diligence and tax transparency purposes. No surrender rule exists because the functional and reporting currency of Panama is the U.S. Dollar.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — Full banking and foreign exchange administration authority; the Republic of Panama has no issuing central bank under the 1904 Monetary Agreement with the United States; the U.S. Dollar is the official legal tender pursuant to the National Constitution and Decree Law No. 9 of 1970, and Banco Nacional de Panamá acts solely as the fiscal agent of the State.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Low-Medium as the primary treasury rail; foreign digital Electronic Money Institutions (EMIs) and non-resident fintech licensees have no passporting rights into Panama and cannot open domestic settlement accounts in the name of a Panamanian PIF. Digital payment services operating in Panama must be authorized by the SBP under the applicable payment-services regulatory framework, which generally requires a local presence and a licensed bank or trust partner. Global EMIs such as Wise, Nium, or Stripe can be used only as alternative collection or disbursement interfaces where payouts are ultimately cleared through an SBP-licensed correspondent institution; they are not substitutes for a regulated Panamanian bank account. The PIF should maintain a Tier-1 domestic bank account as the single point of truth for liquidity, foreign exchange, and compliance reporting, with fintech rails limited to non-settlement data exchange and reconciliation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections and platform remittances to the PIF's Panamanian account must be routed via the SWIFT network (MT103) or through the U.S. Fedwire/CHIPS clearing system for same-day USD settlement, as Panama's banking system clears USD by correspondent relationship with U.S. banks. The wire narrative must precisely state the economic purpose, such as 'Steamworks Royalties under Agreement No. XXX' or 'PlayStation Store Publishing Revenue' accompanied by the relevant platform settlement reference number. Since Panama is fully dollarized, no foreign-exchange authorization or purpose-code registration is required by the central bank; however, the receiving bank's AML operation will classify the inbound transfer under the customer's declared commercial profile. Verification documentation must be retained for each inbound credit: platform payout receipts, invoice copies, and digital-store sales dashboards to ensure audit- and tax-ready traceability of every capital entry.Outbound trade disbursements for a digital publishing PIF are executed primarily as corporate wire transfers (SWIFT MT103) via the PIF's SBP-licensed bank to licensors, co-publishers, contractors, and tax authorities. For physical console merchandise or co-production obligations, a Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) may be opened by the Panamanian bank, with documents comprising bill of lading, commercial invoice, certificate of origin, and conformity certificate. Recurring portal payments, such as monthly developer royalties or store-level contributions from the Steam and PlayStation ecosystems, are settled on open-account terms through SWIFT or ACH batches supported by platform-generated clearing statements.
