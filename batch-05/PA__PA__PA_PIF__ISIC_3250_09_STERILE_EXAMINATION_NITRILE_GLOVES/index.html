Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permitted as a statutory registered office for a Private Interest Foundation. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not have a check-the-box election. The PIF is a separate juridical person and is taxed at entity level only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income is outside the scope of the Income Tax Code. For ITBMS, a taxpayer with annual gross receipts not exceeding PAB 36,000 may elect the simplified small-taxpayer regime, but a manufacturer-exporter of sterile gloves should elect the general regime in order to claim input VAT credits and zero-rate exports. No hybrid mismatch elections are available as Panama has not enacted domestic OECD BEPS Action 2 rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB 0 - no general registration threshold; simplified small-taxpayer regime only for annual gross receipts not exceeding PAB 36,000

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to export sales of goods by the PIF-manufacturer when the goods physically leave Panamanian customs territory under an approved export declaration and title passes to a foreign buyer. The exporter must be the legal seller and consignor on the shipping documents. Input ITBMS incurred on the production of zero-rated exports may be credited and refunded. Services used abroad are zero-rated only when the recipient is foreign and the benefit is realized abroad.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill (B/L or HAWB)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 payment confirmation

Mandatory DGI-authorized electronic invoicing for all registered taxpayers. Invoices must be transmitted in real time through DGI's Facturación Electrónica system using a digital signature, QR code, and DGI fiscal authorization code. Export invoices must clearly indicate the zero-rated cause and reference the customs export declaration. No paper invoices are permitted unless DGI has granted a specific temporary authorization.

Panama imposes no exchange-control repatriation deadline. DGI may audit zero-rated exports within a five-year statute of limitations, extended to ten years in cases of fraud. If the mandatory export documentation bundle is incomplete or false, DGI will claw back the uncollected ITBMS plus monthly interest, impose fines up to 100% of the omitted tax, and refer criminal tax fraud cases which may result in imprisonment of four to six years. Exporters must retain the full documentation bundle for at least five years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system with no exchange controls. Current and capital account transactions, including profit remittances, dividends, foundation distributions, and capital repatriation, are freely convertible and transferable in U.S. dollars. The absence of a domestic central monetary authority removes the contractual convertibility risk present in managed-float regimes, though AML/CFT compliance remains the primary operational gate.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender requirements in Panama. Exporters, manufacturers, and trading entities may retain, allocate, and disburse export receipts in U.S. dollars without mandatory conversion timelines or forced sale ratios. The only applicable obligations are those imposed by banks for transaction records, economic substance documentation, and tax disclosure under Panama's territorial taxation regime.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank; the banking regulator is the Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de la República de Panamá), and the U.S. dollar is legal tender, with the Panamanian balboa serving only as a nominal fractional unit pegged 1:1.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Cross-border digital EMI/fintech providers without a Panamanian banking license have Low-to-Medium viability as primary treasury rails. The Superintendency of Banks does not recognize offshore electronic money institutions as licensed banks for deposits, and such institutions normally cannot open clearing accounts in Panama or participate in local ACH. The PIF should rely on Panama-licensed banks for all settlement; offshore EMIs may be used only for contract tracking and secondary payment orchestration with strict client money segregation and sanctions compliance.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound wire transfers should be sent in U.S. dollars via SWIFT MT103 from the original contributor's regulated bank account to the PIF's Panama operational or escrow account. The SWIFT field 70 remittance information must clearly state the capital contribution, e.g., /INVP/FOUNDATION CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION, and reference the foundation name. The originating bank should apply Customer Due Diligence before sending; the receiving Panamanian bank will rely on its own AML file. For shareholder loans, a formal loan agreement and interest schedule should be retained. For high-value export transactions, the buyer's bank can open an LC with the Panamanian bank presented as the confirming or advising bank. Inbound export proceeds are collected through the Panamanian bank's correspondents on Fedwire or CHIPS for U.S.-dollar checks and remittances.
