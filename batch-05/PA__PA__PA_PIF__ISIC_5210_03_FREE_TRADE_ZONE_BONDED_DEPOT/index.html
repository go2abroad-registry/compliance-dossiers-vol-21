Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual office is not permitted for the registered office of a Private Interest Foundation. The foundation must maintain a registered agent (abogado inscrito) with a physical office within the Republic of Panama, and the registered office address must correspond to the actual physical office of the registered agent. However, the foundation's underlying business operations, including bonded warehouse activities, may be carried out in separate premises in a Free Trade Zone with the required operational license. Virtual or shared directors' meeting spaces are permissible for non-resident directors, but the statutory registered office cannot be virtual.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, Panama; principal digital portal is the DGI e-Tax platform at https://dgi.mef.gob.pa.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity establishing a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) for Panama-source taxable activities. The procedure requires: (1) notarized and apostilled foundation charter (Pacto Fundacional) and registration in the Registry of Private Interest Foundations; (2) appointment of a resident agent in Panama; (3) filing the application via the DGI with the foundation's corporate documents, directors' identification, and proof of fiscal address; (4) obtaining a provisional RUC and registering as an exporter if export zero-rating will be claimed; and (5) activating the electronic invoicing system with DGI-authorized invoice numbering.

The procedure requires: (1) notarized and apostilled foundation charter (Pacto Fundacional) and registration in the Registry of Private Interest Foundations; (2) appointment of a resident agent in Panama; (3) filing the application via the DGI with the foundation's corporate documents, directors' identification, and proof of fiscal address; (4) obtaining a provisional RUC and registering as an exporter if export zero-rating will be claimed; and (5) activating the electronic invoicing system with DGI-authorized invoice numbering.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not maintain a general check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election. A PIF engaged in bonded warehouse operations within a free trade zone may elect to be taxed as a free trade zone operator under Law 32 of 2011 and related FTZ regulations, which achieves territorial treatment and ITBMS exemption for export-related supplies. PIFs may also elect the small taxpayer simplified regime if annual gross revenue is below the threshold. No consolidated return election is available; hybrid mismatch rules under Panama's BEPS-influenced framework have limited application because foreign-source income is outside the territorial tax net.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 36,000 annual gross taxable turnover for simplified taxpayer status; mandatory full RUC registration applies regardless of threshold when the entity carries on taxable business activities or exports goods or services.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating is available for ITBMS purposes on the export of tangible goods from Panamanian customs territory and for services physically or effectively rendered abroad to non-resident customers. A free trade zone bonded depot qualifies as an exporter for this purpose, provided the goods are introduced into an FTZ, stored in a bonded warehouse, and subsequently transferred to a foreign buyer or shipped to an export destination. Supporting evidence must demonstrate that title, possession, and risk of loss passed outside Panama.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA or electronic customs export form)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing exit from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire transfer statement evidencing payment from the foreign buyer
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bonded warehouse entry and exit inventory records
  • Free trade zone operator certificate and movement permit

Panama mandates DGI-authorized electronic invoicing for all registered taxpayers. The DGI operates a real-time fiscal control system whereby invoices must be legally authorized, sequentially numbered, and transmitted through the e-Tax/facturación electrónica platform. Free trade zone bond warehouses must use the DGI's electronic clearance APIs for dispatch notes, customs-linked exit declarations, and zero-rated export invoices. Digital stamping of receipts and fiscal codes is required before invoices become valid for audit purposes.

DGI audit review will scrutinize the link between goods evidenced in the customs declaration and the bond warehouse inventory records, the existence of genuine foreign counterparties, and the actual repatriation of sale proceeds. Failure to repatriate export collections within 180 days, fabrication of export documentation, or unjustified zero-rating triggers retrospective clawback of ITBMS at 7%, late payment surcharge, inflation-adjusted interest, and fines of up to 200 percent of the tax evaded. Criminal tax evasion and customs fraud penalties can include imprisonment and suspension of the bonded warehouse license, and the PIF loses the free trade zone operating permit.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains a fully dollarized monetary regime with unrestricted currency convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. There are no exchange controls, no central bank authorization for FX operations, and no restrictions on profit remittances or capital repatriation by a Private Interest Foundation (PIF). Under the territorial tax system of Panama (Ley 254 de 2021 and prior Fiscal Code rules), income derived from bonded warehouse operations serving third-country goods is not Panamanian source income; distributions to foreign founders and beneficiaries are free of Panamanian withholding tax. Practical hurdles are limited to AML/financial-intelligence gates imposed by SBP-regulated banks: wire transfers above USD 10,000 are subject to automated monitoring and the FATF Travel Rule; remittances of large PIF capital injections (≥ USD 500,000) typically require a source-of-funds affidavit and, for certain jurisdictions, a foreign tax-residency certificate. Despite these documentation gates, the jurisdiction is rated A1 (full convertibility) with no forced conversion, no surrender requirements, and no multiple-currency-practice restrictions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export proceeds in Panama. Bonded depot operators within Free Trade Zones (Law 41 of 2007; Executive Decree No. 6 of 2015; ANA resolutions) may invoice in USD or any freely convertible currency, retain export earnings in domestic or foreign bank accounts, and disburse them without time limitation or conversion ratio. The sole compliance obligations are: (i) reporting of cash transactions exceeding USD 10,000 through the SBP's financial intelligence system (Law 23 of 2015; Decreto 131 de 2020); (ii) including complete originator/beneficiary information in every international wire (FATF Recommendation 16 / Travel Rule); and (iii) filing customs and trade statistics declarations through the Aduana Nacional (SIGA/Impune) for each shipment. Export proceeds may be held indefinitely offshore, provided that the PIF maintains an Economic Substance Return (Ley 254 de 2021) evidencing real management and commercial activity in Panama.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP), established by Decreto Ley No. 2 of 22 February 2008, as the sole prudential regulator and foreign-exchange data supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the fiscal agent and settlement bank for the national clearing system. Panama has no central bank with monetary issuance power: the US Dollar circulates as unlimited legal tender under Cabinet Decree No. 238 of 1904 and Article 206 of the Panamanian Constitution (full dollarization). The Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) coordinates external financing and sovereign treasury policy, while the SBP compiles balance-of-payments statistics in accordance with IMF BPM6.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH as a supplementary liquidity accelerator; LOW-MEDIUM as a primary treasury bank. Panama's fintech regulatory sandbox ('Centro de Innovación Fintech', SBP Agreement No. 004-2020) permits licensed processors to test digital payment products, but non-bank electronic money institutions domiciled abroad (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) hold no Panamanian banking license and cannot operate nostro accounts in Panama. The Banking Law (Decreto Ley No. 2 of 2008, Articles 2 and 83) requires that deposit-taking and payment intermediation for a Panamanian FTZ operator be performed through an SBP-supervised bank. Consequently, the optimal treasury architecture is an SBP-licensed Tier-1 bank as the principal USD clearing and custody rail, with a regulated fintech EMI (FCA/ASIC/EMI-licensed) used only for incidental non-USD vendor payouts, customer collections in EUR/GBP/CNY, and sub-threshold trade tickets. All fintech-originated balances must be swept daily into the Panamanian master account to comply with the 'banking exclusivity' principle and to avoid an unlicensed deposit-taking characterization.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol: (1) Pre-move notification by the PIF Council to the designated account officer detailing the injection amount, the underlying FTZ lease and warehouse contracts, and the UBO. (2) Remittance via SWIFT MT103 from the founding entity, with the ordering customer field matching the documented founder name and the beneficiary account field (party 57) reflecting the PIF's registered exact name and account number at the Panamanian bank; use a prime correspondent such as JPMorgan Chase New York, Bank of New York Mellon, or HSBC US. (3) Purpose/remittance codes under Panama's SBP BPM6 statistical classification: for equity injection use 'Direct investment — equity capital' (code 1110); for shareholder loans use 'Direct investment — intercompany lending' (code 1120); for ordinary trade-related funding use 'Trade credit — non-financial enterprise' (code 2A2B). (4) Verification bundle for the AML desk: apostilled Foundation Deed, registered agent undertaking, PIF tax ID (RUC), active Free Trade Zone operator licence issued by the Administración General de Aduanas (ANA), and the sworn Economic Substance Declaration evidencing real activity. There is no Panamanian domestic RTGS for USD beyond BNP's clearing window; same-day settlement is achieved only through SENI business-hours processing.
