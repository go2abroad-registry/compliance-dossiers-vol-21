Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), República de Panamá; tax administration online portal: SITIO (Sistema Integrado Tributario de Ingresos en Línea) via https://sitio.dgi.gob.pa. Customs counterpart: Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas de Panamá (ANA) via Sistema Integrado de Aduanas (SIA). Foundation registry counterpart: Registro Público de Panamá (RPP).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Draft and register the Private Interest Foundation (PIF) with the Registro Público de Panamá under the Private Interest Foundations Law (Ley 25 de 1995), filing the Foundation Charter and Foundation Regulations, appointing a resident registered agent, and satisfying the minimum capital requirement (typically USD 10,000 or such amount specified in the charter). Step 2: Determine Panamanian tax nexus. A PIF conducting Amazon FBA cross-border e-commerce where sales, customers, and fulfillment centers are located outside Panamá derives only foreign-source income, which is outside Panamá's territorial tax base; no RUC is statutorily required in that posture. Step 3: If any Panamanian-source activity arises (Panamanian payroll, local warehouse, domestic marketplace sales, import/export from Panamá), apply for RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) through SITIO by submitting the RUC application, certified copy of the Foundation Charter, registered agent certificate, evidence of fiscal address in Panamá, and a notarized power of attorney appointing a resident legal representative. Step 4: For non-resident foreign digital platforms and suppliers, obtain a non-resident RUC through SITIO and appoint a local fiscal agent for ITBMS compliance on supplies to Panamanian consumers. Step 5: If a physical Panamanian establishment is maintained, obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación (business license) and any applicable health/fire approvals. Step 6: Register with ANA for a customs exporter code if goods will be physically exported from Panamá. Step 7: Request from DGI a taxpayer-registration certificate, tax-residence certificate, or non-residence certificate, as required for foreign reporting and banking compliance.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (Panama-source income only). The PIF is a separate juridical person subject to entity-level Panamanian income tax solely on income derived from Panamanian sources; foreign-source online retail, Amazon FBA order fulfillment, marketplace intermediary fees, and offshore service profits are excluded from Panamanian income tax and ITBMS. The PIF is not a pass-through entity; distributions to beneficiaries are non-deductible and, when originating from foreign-source foundation income, are not taxable in Panamá.

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a check-the-box election comparable to U.S. Treasury Regulation § 301.7701-3. Entity tax classification follows foundation and corporate statute. Available elections and mechanisms: (i) voluntary registration as an ITBMS and income-tax taxpayer to claim input tax credits and file export refunds; (ii) election to use the simplified small-taxpayer indirect-tax regime for annual gross turnover up to USD 36,000; (iii) election to classify qualifying services supplied to non-residents as exported services under DGI approval (Declaración de Servicio Exportado), applying 0% ITBMS; (iv) election to domicile the PIF or the commercial activity in a special economic zone or SEM regime (e.g., Panamá Pacífico) where statutory; and (v) adoption of foundation distribution and investment policies to maintain territorial-source treatment and mitigate hybrid-mismatch exposure abroad. Panama has no domestic CFC or hybrid-mismatch rules but participates in OECD/G20 Inclusive Framework information exchange and substance-review practices.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold: any person habitually transferring taxable goods or services in Panamá must register for ITBMS. A simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross income not exceeding USD 36,000 (B/.36,000, Panamanian Balboa pegged 1:1 to USD). Non-resident digital service suppliers and marketplace platforms are required to register as non-resident ITBMS taxpayers for supplies made to Panamanian consumers under Executive Decree 223 of 2020, with no published numeric threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods: 0% ITBMS applies where tangible goods are permanently exported from Panamanian customs territory, evidenced by an ANA/SIA customs export declaration and physical departure of the goods. Services: 0% applies to exported services rendered to a non-resident recipient, used or exploited wholly outside Panamá, and paid with funds originating from abroad, as certified by DGI. In an Amazon FBA model where orders are fulfilled from overseas Amazon warehouses, the goods never enter Panamanian customs territory; the corresponding FBA seller profits are foreign-source and remain entirely outside Panamá's territorial tax base and ITBMS, while any Panamanian input ITBMS (e.g., local warehouse or logistics services) is recoverable only where the complete export documentation chain is maintained and the ITBMS return (Form 204) is filed.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduanas / DUA authorized through ANA-SIA)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure from Panamanian customs territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103 / bank credit evidence demonstrating receipt of export proceeds from abroad
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • DGI exported-services declaration (Declaración de Servicio Exportado) for service zero-rating
  • Amazon marketplace settlement report and FBA shipment evidence substantiating foreign fulfillment and foreign customer destination

Mandatory fiscal electronic invoicing applies to registered taxpayers under Law 25 of 2017 and DGI regulations, administered through SITIO. Taxpayers must issue electronic invoices and electronic tax documents in XML with a DGI authorization code and QR code, transmitted to DGI before or at the moment of issuance (near-real-time fiscal clearance). Large taxpayers transmit documents via DGI-authorized web services/API (JSON/XML/SOAP); smaller taxpayers use SITIO's online invoice tool or certified invoicing software. Non-resident ITBMS taxpayers must issue DGI-compliant electronic invoices bearing the non-resident RUC. All amounts must be expressed in USD/Balboa.

DGI may audit export zero-rating claims within the standard five-year prescription period, extended to seven years where returns are omitted or supported by false documents; fraud is not subject to prescription under Panama's fiscal enforcement framework. If the export or foreign-source nature is not substantiated, DGI reclassifies the transaction as a domestic taxable transfer, retroactively assessing ITBMS at 7%, statutory default interest, a negligence surcharge of up to 50% of the omitted tax, or a fraud surcharge of up to 100% (with higher sanctions for repeat violations), and disallowing any refunded input credits. Possession or use of false customs declarations, false bills of lading, or false foreign-remittance certificates exposes the PIF and its officers to Tax Code fines, administrative suspension from ANA authorized-exporter programs, and criminal liability under Ley 70 de 2019 (fiscal criminal procedure), including imprisonment for aggravated tax evasion.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full current-account and capital-account convertibility exists. Panama imposes no exchange controls, no prior approval for cross-border payments, no restrictions on profit remittance, dividends, interest, capital repatriation, or distributions by a Private Interest Foundation (PIF). Because the base currency is the U.S. dollar, Amazon FBA revenues, which are predominantly USD-denominated marketplace settlements, require no jurisdiction-level conversion. The practical profit-remittance hurdles are not legal but operational: banks will require proof of lawful source of funds, updated PIF council resolutions, disclosure of beneficiaries, CRS/FATCA tax self-certification, and evidence that the PIF's foreign-source e-commerce income is outside Panama's territorial income tax scope under the Código Fiscal. A PIF with opaque reserved-beneficiary provisions will face enhanced due diligence and lower onboarding viability despite the absence of exchange controls.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama applies no surrender requirement, no mandatory export-proceeds conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline. The required conversion percentage is 0%. Export proceeds, including Amazon FBA marketplace settlements, may be retained abroad in USD, converted freely, or remitted to Panama without authorization. The only compliance overlay is ordinary AML/KYC and source-of-funds documentation, together with documentary consistency between Amazon settlement reports and the bank transaction narrative.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no central bank and no foreign exchange administration. The monetary system is fully dollarized: the U.S. dollar is legal tender and the Panamanian balboa exists only as a nominal unit for coinage. The Banco Nacional de Panamá (National Bank of Panama, established 1904) acts as the State's fiscal agent and clearing agent and operates the domestic clearinghouse, but it is not a monetary authority and issues no currency. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP) supervises banking activity under Decreto Ley 2 de 2008 and administers AML/CFT obligations. The Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) administers the beneficial ownership registry under Ley 21 de 2017. Monetary and exchange conditions are effectively determined by the U.S. Federal Reserve under Panama's complete dollarization.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

RAITING: MEDIUM-HIGH as a settlement and collection layer, but MEDIUM as a regulatory substitute for a Panamanian licensed banking relationship. Panama does not maintain a standalone electronic money institution (EMI) license. Offshore fintech/EMI platforms such as Airwallex, Stripe, Payoneer, and Amazon Pay can lawfully serve the PIF as payment processors, disbursement agents, and USD collection-account providers in their own jurisdictions, and can sweep funds into Panama by SWIFT MT103 or ACH-to-SWIFT bridges. However, any entity performing deposit-taking or financial intermediation in Panama requires authorization under the banking regime of Decreto Ley 2 de 2008, so a foreign EMI cannot hold a Panamanian client balance or issue a Panamanian IBAN. The SBP KYC rules require the Panamanian bank to perform equivalent customer due diligence or rely on the fintech only with adequate risk controls. For Amazon FBA, the practical flow is Amazon Seller Central settlement to a USD account or PSP account in the United States, followed by a treasury sweep into the PIF's Panama operational account; Airwallex and Stripe are viable for revenue collection, currency conversion at market rates, and supplier disbursement, provided the fund trail is transparent and the PIF remains the principal seller of record. Legal blockers include unlicensed pass-through of third-party funds, Amazon settlement reports inconsistent with declared revenues, and failure to identify reserved beneficiaries in the fintech's KYC file.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Opaque PIF structure with reserved beneficiaries, protector powers, and undeclared ultimate beneficial owners

Root cause: Opaque PIF structure with reserved beneficiaries, protector powers, and undeclared ultimate beneficial owners

Defense evidence: Apostilled Foundation Charter and registered statutes (Registro Público), full UBO schedule filed with the Registro de Beneficiarios Finales under Ley 21 de 2017, notarized Protector consent, Council resolution appointing signatories and approving account opening, Panamanian legal opinion confirming the PIF's existence, non-distribution constraints, and beneficial ownership under Ley 25 de 1995.
Amazon FBA revenues without matching commercial documentation, invoices, or inventory trail

Root cause: Amazon FBA revenues without matching commercial documentation, invoices, or inventory trail

Defense evidence: Amazon Seller Central settlement reports covering all payout cycles, Payout reconciliation statements from Stripe, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Amazon Pay, order-level commercial invoices or tax invoices issued to the marketplace, FBA inbound and warehouse inventory receipts, supplier purchase orders and supplier invoices, customs entry summaries for goods imported into fulfillment countries, and a signed directors' resolution identifying Amazon and PSPs as settlement agents only.
High-velocity pass-through pattern indicating third-party collection or undisclosed factoring of funds

Root cause: High-velocity pass-through pattern indicating third-party collection or undisclosed factoring of funds

Defense evidence: Written treasury and fund-flow policy, flow charts showing buyer to Amazon to PSP to Panama PIF and then to suppliers, marketplace seller agreement establishing the PIF as principal and seller of record, transaction monitoring records, absence of unrelated third-party payments, and an AML compliance program proportionate to the Low_Risk_Standard classification.
Sanctions, PEP, or adverse media hits on founder, council members, or beneficiaries

Root cause: Sanctions, PEP, or adverse media hits on founder, council members, or beneficiaries

Defense evidence: Time-stamped OFAC, UN, EU, and UK sanctions screening results, PEP self-certification forms, source-of-wealth and source-of-funds declarations, passport and proof of residence, negative-media search report, and an enhanced due diligence memorandum prepared by a compliance officer or external counsel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The initial PIF endowment under Ley 25 de 1995 must be fully constituted, commonly with a minimum of USD 10,000, and should be wired into a dedicated Capital Escrow Account or the operational account using SWIFT MT103. For a USD transfer to Panama, the remitting bank sends the payment through a U.S. correspondent or directly by SWIFT MT103 to the receiving Panamanian bank's BIC, with the recipient's name and account number exactly matching the PIF's bank record. Field 70 should state: PIF CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION / FOUNDATION CHARTER NO. [registration number]. Panama does not maintain an official FX purpose-code table; the remitter may map the transaction to the relevant SWIFT ISO purpose code, commonly GIFT for a funded endowment or INVE for a capital investment, while avoiding LOAN if the transaction is equity-like. Verification documents required by the receiving bank include the apostilled Foundation Charter, Registro Público good-standing certificate, Foundation Council resolution accepting the endowment and naming signatories, founder's passport, source-of-wealth documentation, and Travel Rule originator information showing the founder's name, address, and account number. Funds from a U.S. collection account can alternatively be sent by ACH to the SWIFT bridge and then as an MT103 cover payment to Panama.For Amazon FBA outbound flows, the primary clearing rail is marketplace settlement: Amazon Seller Central pays net revenue via USD ACH or through a PSP to the PIF's U.S. collection account with Stripe, Airwallex, Payoneer, or Amazon Pay; the PIF then executes a treasury sweep by SWIFT MT103 into the Panamanian operational account. Direct Amazon payouts to a Panamanian local bank account are generally not available, so the U.S. or PSP collection account is the practical settlement hub. For supplier and inventory payments, open-account trade is cleared through SWIFT MT103 in USD, with CNY payments routed through Bank of China (Panama), S.A. via SWIFT or CIPS where required and fully documented with purchase orders and invoices. For higher-value or first-time suppliers, a commercial documentary letter of credit (UCP600) issued by a Panamanian bank and transmitted by SWIFT MT700 is permissible; the PIF typically collateralizes the L/C with USD term deposits at the issuing bank. Within Panama, low-value domestic transactions clear through the local ACH/clearinghouse operated under Banco Nacional de Panamá, while high-value USD positions settle through U.S. correspondent banks because Panama has no central-bank RTGS system; all outbound payments remain free of exchange control authorization.USD/CNH and CNY non-deliverable forwards (NDF) executed with a licensed Panamanian bank or an offshore regulated prime broker, backed by documented Amazon import/supplier exposure in China.Deliverable USD/CNY or USD/HKD forwards arranged through Bank of China (Panama), S.A. or another SWIFT/CIPS-connected bank for physical CNY trade settlements.USD/EUR and USD/GBP foreign-exchange forwards and FX swaps under ISDA 2002 or bilateral master FX agreements with SBP-licensed banks.Cross-currency interest-rate swaps between USD and operating foreign currencies for supplier credit, inventory financing, or long-term Amazon working-capital hedges.European-style FX options and zero-cost collars purchased on a non-speculative cash-flow hedging basis for Amazon settlement proceeds and supplier disbursements.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: RAITING: MEDIUM-HIGH as a settlement and collection layer, but MEDIUM as a regulatory substitute for a Panamanian licensed banking relationship. Panama does not maintain a standalone electronic money institution (EMI) license. Offshore fintech/EMI platforms such as Airwallex, Stripe, Payoneer, and Amazon Pay can lawfully serve the PIF as payment processors, disbursement agents, and USD collection-account providers in their own jurisdictions, and can sweep funds into Panama by SWIFT MT103 or ACH-to-SWIFT bridges. However, any entity performing deposit-taking or financial intermediation in Panama requires authorization under the banking regime of Decreto Ley 2 de 2008, so a foreign EMI cannot hold a Panamanian client balance or issue a Panamanian IBAN. The SBP KYC rules require the Panamanian bank to perform equivalent customer due diligence or rely on the fintech only with adequate risk controls. For Amazon FBA, the practical flow is Amazon Seller Central settlement to a USD account or PSP account in the United States, followed by a treasury sweep into the PIF's Panama operational account; Airwallex and Stripe are viable for revenue collection, currency conversion at market rates, and supplier disbursement, provided the fund trail is transparent and the PIF remains the principal seller of record. Legal blockers include unlicensed pass-through of third-party funds, Amazon settlement reports inconsistent with declared revenues, and failure to identify reserved beneficiaries in the fintech's KYC file.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File and Master File are mandatory when related-party transactions exceed B/. 1,000,000 (Panama Balboa, parity with USD) in a fiscal year; also required when gross income exceeds B/. 2,000,000. Transactions below these thresholds must nevertheless maintain contemporaneous evidence of arm's length pricing.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regime (Ley 254 de 2018) applies to entities conducting 'relevant activities' such as distribution and service centers, headquarter operations, or IP holding. Cross-border e-commerce via Amazon FBA generally does not constitute a relevant activity unless the PIF engages in physical distribution or procurement functions within Panama. Taxpayers claiming treaty benefits must demonstrate economic substance (mind and management, employees, premises) to avoid being treated as a shell. The DGI may disregard the entity and reattribute income to the beneficial owner if substance is lacking.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Criminal fine equivalent to twice the evaded tax plus imprisonment from 2 to 6 years (Código Tributario). Civil penalty of 50% of the omitted tax and interest at 1.5% monthly.

Director personal liability: Directors or foundation council members who knowingly sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes, civil penalties, and potential criminal prosecution (imprisonment up to 6 years); travel bans may be imposed during investigation.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under Ley 254, the DGI may levy a fine of up to US$1,000,000 for failure to provide substance evidence; the entity may be disqualified as a 'resident' for tax purposes, causing full Panamanian-source and worldwide-source income to become taxable with retroactive interest.

Director personal liability: Directors or council members who knowingly assent to a sham foundation may be held personally accountable for the entity's tax liabilities and subjected to civil restitution orders, asset freezes, and criminal prosecution for aiding tax evasion.
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Fines under the AML Law (Ley 23 de 2015) from US$10,000 to US$500,000 per violation; the PIF may be dissolved and its assets confiscated if used for money laundering; criminal penalties include imprisonment from 5 to 12 years.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, or foundation council members face criminal liability including up to 12 years' imprisonment, personal fines, asset forfeiture, and mandatory travel bans; professional licenses (legal, accounting) may be revoked.
