Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); tax administration portal: DGI Sede Electrónica (https://www.dgi.gob.pa/sede-electronica.html).

E-government filing portal

A non-resident-owned Sociedad Anónima obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) by: (1) appointing a resident agent and a legal representative acting in Panama; (2) submitting the sworn 'Aviso de Operación' together with corporate taxpayer pre-registration documentation to the DGI; (3) enrolling the company in the DGI Sede Electrónica portal using an authorized digital signature; (4) receiving the RUC certificate and the ITBMS registration confirmation from the DGI. A local fiscal representative with notarized power of attorney is mandatory for foreign shareholders, and no share capital deposit is required.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation with Territorial_Scope legacy regime; Law 254 of 2024 progressively transitions Panamanian S.A. entities to worldwide income taxation from fiscal year 2025.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a U.S.-style check-the-box election; an S.A. is per se a taxable legal entity. Available statutory elections include: (i) transitional elections under Law 254/2024 governing the shift to worldwide taxation, including deferral of certain realization gains and foreign tax credit election against distributions; (ii) election between the general ITBMS system and the small-taxpayer simplified regime (Régimen Simplificado) where annual gross income does not exceed B/.36,000; (iii) optional advance election to register for ITBMS before commencing operations in order to recover input VAT on pre-operating expenses.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold: every business engaged in taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS before commencing activity. A simplified small-taxpayer withholding regime is available only to businesses with annual gross income of B/.36,000 or less.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% ITBMS applies to exports of goods leaving Panama customs territory and to 'servicios internacionales' supplied to non-residents, including multilingual remote helpdesk and outbound tele-sales BPO services, provided the services are effectively consumed or exploited outside Panama, the client is a non-resident taxpayer, payment originates abroad, and the Panamanian supplier maintains a complete export support file with its ITBMS registration.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera - DUA) for goods, or a signed Panamanian Export Service Declaration for BPO services
  • Signed client contract or binding purchase order evidencing the offshore service
  • Commercial invoice issued through the DGI electronic invoicing system
  • Bill of lading or airway bill evidencing departure from Panama for goods, or a dated service-delivery / completion record for remote services
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a licensed Panamanian bank proving that the foreign currency proceeds entered Panama from the overseas client
  • Certificate of the foreign client's non-resident status and foreign tax identification number

DGI mandates electronic invoicing ('Facturación Electrónica') through the SFE system, requiring XML/API web-service transmission by authorized emitters (emisores autorizados). Mandatory for all State suppliers since January 2024; large taxpayers with gross income of at least B/.3,000,000 are being phased in between 2025 and 2026. Every invoice must carry a DGI-assigned authorization code and a QR code; issuance of invoices through fiscal printers or digital stamping requires prior DGI authorization.

DGI cross-checks export zero-rating claims through the SFE, customs data and bank remittance records. Missing or fraudulent documentation triggers retrospective ITBMS clawback at 7%, plus monthly surcharge interest of 1.5% and fines of 100% to 300% of the evaded tax under the Panamanian Código Tributario. Falsified export documentation may additionally constitute criminal tax fraud under Decree Law 392/2021, with the DGI fraud assessment window extended to 7 years versus the general 5-year statute of limitations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system with no exchange controls. The Panamanian balboa exists only as divisional coins pegged at 1:1 to the US dollar. Convertibility is unrestricted for both current account and capital account transactions; profit remittances, dividend distributions, and capital repatriations do not require central bank approval or foreign exchange permit. Panama's territorial tax system does not impose any FX-conditioned barrier to remittance, although a 10% dividend withholding tax may apply under Panamanian tax law. The absence of capital controls makes the jurisdiction de facto fully convertible for USD and freely convertible major foreign currencies through commercial banks.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export proceeds surrender requirement, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no timeline for repatriation of export or services receipts. Panamanian law does not require BPO call center export earnings to be brought into Panama, converted into balboas, or settled through any official exchange window. Exporters and remote service providers may maintain 100% of proceeds in USD or any freely convertible currency in Panama or abroad, subject only to general corporate and banking recordkeeping rules.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Panama has no traditional central bank or dedicated Foreign Exchange Administration. Banco Nacional de Panamá is a state-owned commercial bank, not a monetary authority. The effective monetary anchor is the United States Federal Reserve through full dollarization, with PAB legal tender at a fixed 1:1 rate to USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Wise B2B and similar foreign electronic money institutions are operationally viable for cross-border contractor and vendor disbursements, but they are not licensed banks in Panama and cannot legally substitute for a Panamanian corporate bank account. Panama's financial regulatory framework under the SBP does not allow unlicensed foreign EMIs to take deposits or offer payment accounts to Panamanian-resident entities without SBP authorization. Their use is therefore limited to outbound payment execution and collections from foreign counterparties, with the Panamanian entity retaining an SBP-regulated bank account as the treasury anchor.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The remittance should identify the originator as the registered shareholder and include the payment reference 'Capital Contribution' or 'Shareholder Funding' to satisfy the bank's source-of-funds verification. The Panamanian entity must present a notarized capital increase resolution and updated share registry where the injection involves issuance of new shares. Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25.00%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10.00% on dividends and distributions paid by a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima out of Panama-source profits already subject to corporate income tax. Distributions sourced from foreign-source or otherwise exempt profits are generally subject to a 5% dividend tax under Panama's dividend-reconciliation rules; if the reconciliation mechanism is not properly maintained, the 10% rate may apply to the entire distribution.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For a calendar-year Sociedad Anónima, the annual ISR return and any balance of tax must be filed and paid on or before 31 March of the following tax year. If the DGI approves a different fiscal year, the filing deadline is three months after the approved fiscal year-end. Extension requests are exceptional and must be expressly authorized by the DGI.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB 2,000,000 (equivalent to USD 2,000,000 because the Panamanian Balboa circulates at par with the U.S. dollar) in aggregate annual cross-border related-party transactions. Mandatory Local File / Master File documentation is triggered when the taxpayer's controlled transactions exceed this threshold. Lower thresholds and additional obligations may apply for transactions with entities in jurisdictions identified by Panama as low-tax or non-cooperative.

Economic substance scope: Panama applies economic-substance principles through its international tax-transparency commitments, SEM licensing requirements, and general anti-abuse doctrine. A Panamanian Sociedad Anónima used as a remote BPO call center must maintain real operational substance: full-time employees, physical or measurable local infrastructure, invoices, IT systems, insurance, and active management in Panama. If the entity operates as a mere invoice company or strips value from a foreign operating affiliate without adequate functional and risk profile, the DGI or an overseas tax authority may recharacterize the arrangement, allocate the profit to the actual place of management, or treat the S.A. as a transparent shell. Licensed SEM activities are subject to stricter economic-substance verification and annual reporting; failure to meet those tests can revoke the license and subject the company to full 25% taxation plus penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Restitution of the omitted tax, monthly default interest, an administrative surcharge of up to 100% of the understated tax, embargo of bank accounts and personal assets, suspension of the RUC, and criminal tax-fraud prosecution carrying imprisonment and additional fines.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and resident agents who sign false declarations, direct the booking scheme, or knowingly conceal taxable income may be held jointly and severally liable for the corporate tax, penalties, and interest. The corporate veil can be pierced where the S.A. is used as a sham; personal consequences include civil restitution, travel bans, disqualification from directorships, and imprisonment.
Transfer-Pricing Documentation Failure / Intercompany Mispricing

Statutory sanction: The DGI may disregard the controlled price, apply the most appropriate arm's-length method, recharacterize the transaction, deny deductions for mispriced payments, add 1% monthly default interest, and impose penalties ranging from 1% of the undocumented transaction value up to 100% of the resulting tax adjustment.

Director personal liability: Directors and chief financial officers who knowingly approve false intercompany contracts, maintain circular payment flows, or use fabricated comparables can be personally assessed for the adjustment where fraud or gross negligence is established. Travel bans and asset attachment are available where tax collection risk is severe.
