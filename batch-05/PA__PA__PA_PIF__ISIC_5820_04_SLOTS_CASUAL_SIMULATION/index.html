Competent tax authority

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope — The PIF is a separate legal person without shareholders and without beneficiaries at the entity level. Panama-source income (for example, in-app social-casino sales to Panamanian users) is subject to corporate income tax at 25% and ITBMS. Foreign-source income (for example, Apple IAP or Google Play Billing receipts derived from non-Panama end-users) is exempt under Panama's territorial tax system, provided the profit is realized and substantiated abroad. The PIF is not a pass-through entity; beneficiary distributions are not deductible by the foundation and are generally not taxable to non-resident beneficiaries.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box regime and no hybrid-mismatch election exist in Panama; entity tax classification is fixed by statute. Practical elections available: (i) optional Multinational Headquarters (SEM) incentives under Law 41 of 2007 for qualifying regional headquarters or shared-services operations; (ii) the simplified fixed-payment regime (Régimen Simplificado de Pago) for micro and small taxpayers meeting statutory turnover caps; (iii) group consolidation of ITBMS filings for affiliated registered taxpayers; and (iv) filing of the territorial-source allocation notice that formally rings-fences excluded foreign-source gaming revenue. Treaty-based relief may apply only under Panama's limited DTT network and does not override territorial sourcing.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory turnover threshold — registration is triggered by the first taxable supply made in Panama, including digital services sold to Panamanian consumers. Micro and small taxpayers with annual gross receipts not exceeding USD 200,000 may elect the simplified fixed-payment regime (Régimen Simplificado de Pago) in lieu of full ITBMS compliance.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are zero-rated (0%) under ITBMS. For digital social-casino publishing (ISIC 5820), the criteria are: (i) the downloaded virtual-gaming service must be economically exploited outside Panama, evidenced by the end-user's foreign billing address, IP/geolocation outside Panama, and non-Panama residence of the purchaser; (ii) the app-store operator (Apple/Google) must be a non-resident and the settlement funds must originate abroad; (iii) the PIF must issue e-invoices classified as 'Exportación de Servicios' and retain contemporaneous territorial-revenue allocation workpapers. The 0% rate supports refund or crediting of related input ITBMS incurred on Panama purchases.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) — required only where physical media or hardware is shipped; for pure digital downloads, the DGI-accepted substitute is the e-invoice marked 'Exportación de Servicios' together with the app-store settlement report
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill — or, for intangible supplies, the digital delivery/download log generated by the Apple IAP or Google Play Billing platform
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Panamanian bank or SWIFT/MT103 statements evidencing receipt of export proceeds from abroad
  • App-store disbursement and royalty statements segregating Panama versus non-Panama end-user sales, plus the foundation's territorial-source revenue allocation workpaper

Mandatory nationwide electronic invoicing (facturación electrónica) through the DGI Sede Electrónica and the DGI Sistema de Facturación Electrónica. All registered taxpayers, including foreign digital suppliers, must issue e-invoices validated in near-real time via DGI web service APIs; each invoice must carry a QR code and a digital stamp. The mandate covers B2B and B2C transactions, and app-store settlement vouchers from Apple IAP and Google Play Billing must be reconcilable to DGI-validated e-invoices.

The DGI may audit zero-rating claims retroactively for the statutory prescription period, generally five years and extended where fraud is alleged. If documentation fails or the 180-day repatriation requirement is unmet, the 0% benefit is clawed back: ITBMS is assessed at 9% on gross export receipts, plus monthly compensatory interest and penalties ranging from 50% to 200% of the omitted tax for false or incomplete documentation, together with removal from the exporters' registration. Fraudulent use of forged customs or remittance certificates exposes the foundation, its resident agent, and signatories to criminal tax-evasion prosecution with imprisonment up to five years and disqualification from RUC and e-invoicing privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates a unilingual currency board regime by adoption: the Balboa exists only as a nominal unit of account and circulates exclusively as coinage at parity with the US Dollar. There is no separate monetary policy, no capital account convertibility restriction, and no exchange rationing. The current account and capital account are both fully convertible; foreign investors may repatriate dividends, interest, and liquidation proceeds without prior central bank approval or statutory conversion mandates. Profit remittance is limited solely by corporate law solvency tests and any contractual lock-up in the Foundation Charter. For the Social Casino PIF, the FX conversion hurdle is immaterial because both the revenue inflow (Apple IAP and Google Play Billing) and the treasury operations are denominated in USD, with no legal restriction on holding offshore USD earnings.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no statutory surrender or repatriation timeline on export proceeds or service revenue. Under Law 23 of 2015 and its implementing regulations, tax-registered and banking-sector clients must maintain documentary trail of the underlying transaction, but there is no mandatory conversion ratio into local currency because the export receipts are already received in the legal-tender USD. Automatic 100% retention of export receipts is the default rule. Any PIF receiving app-store revenue must preserve the store-level settlement statements to match SBP documentary examination thresholds, particularly because the banking AML risk tier is Enhanced Medium-Risk.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no traditional central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) serves as the primary banking regulator and licensing authority, while Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the State's fiscal agent. Exchange control functions are effectively vestigial because the US Dollar is legal tender under Panama's fully dollarized monetary regime (Decree Law No. 183 of 1941, amended). The Ministry of Commerce and Industries maintains trade statistics but does not administer FX controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Medium-High for virtual account architecture, but legally constrained. Panama does not have a statutory category of 'Electronic Money Institution'; SBP only recognizes banks, trust companies, and finance companies under the Banking Law (Legislative Decree 2 of 2008). A foreign offshore EMI such as an EU-authorised (e.g., Lithuania or UK FCA-licensed) EMI may legally maintain a client-identity relationship with a Panamanian PIF, but the EMI's settlement account must remain domiciled in a correspondent USD bank that is itself SBP-regulated or a reputable New York/London clearing bank (e.g., BNY Mellon, Citi or J.P. Morgan). Direct EMI use as the primary treasury arrowhead is not recommended for the PIF because of correspondent-bank de-risking of social-casino-adjoining merchants; rather, the EMI should serve as a secondary reconciliation tool for up to 10% of traffic originating in the Middle East and EU where Apple/Google settle through regional aggregators. Legal constraint: the EMI must comply with Panama's FATF-aligned Law 23 of 2015 and cannot hold funds without a written custody agreement naming the PIF as beneficial owner.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility Rating: Medium-High for virtual account architecture, but legally constrained.
