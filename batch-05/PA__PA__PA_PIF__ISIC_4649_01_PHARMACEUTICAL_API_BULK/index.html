Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), Panama; electronic portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa) and Panama Emprende / E-Tax integrated platforms.

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity or Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must first have its PIF charter duly recorded with the Public Registry of Panama, which confirms its juristic personality. The entity then registers with the DGI to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente). Procedural steps: (i) appoint a resident legal representative or fiscal agent, (ii) submit certified constitutive documents (foundation charter and reglamento) notarized in Panama, (iii) file Form 02 (Aviso de Operación / Registro de Contribuyente) with proof of registered local address and apostilled foreign due-diligence documentation (passport, director/shareholder identification), and (iv) obtain a business license (Aviso de Operación) from the municipal authority for local operations. The DGI assigns a permanent RUC which is the statutory TIN used for all ITBMS, income tax, and customs filings.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or entity classification election exists under Panamanian law. A PIF is treated as a hybrid investment vehicle under Law 25 of 1995: Panamanian-source income is taxed at the entity level, while foreign-source income is outside Panamanian territorial jurisdiction (Renta Mundial not applicable; territorial taxation applies). Elections available include: (i) election for the Simplified Taxpayer Regime (Régimen Simplificado de Pequeño Contribuyente) if qualified, (ii) registration as a Regular VAT (ITBMS) taxpayer, and (iii) annual audit appointment under the non-tax General Accounting Rules. Hybrid mismatch elections per OECD Base Erosion rules are not domestically codified but are domestically neutral given the territorial system.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 36,000 (Panamanian Balboa, pegged 1:1 to USD) of annual gross taxable income from domestic supplies; entities with higher turnover are mandatory ITBMS registrants, while smaller operators may use the simplified regime.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Export supplies, including the sale and offshore delivery of Active Pharmaceutical Ingredients (APIs) (HS Chapters 29/30) to customers outside Panama, qualify for a 0% ITBMS rate provided the goods physically leave the Panamanian customs territory and the exporter holds a valid RUC. The zero-rating also applies to services rendered outside Panama, international transport, and re-export operations. The exporter must register the corresponding export operation through the ANA customs export process; sale to recognized Free Trade Zone or Colon Free Zone businesses may alternatively qualify as an exempt/re-export transaction under special regimes.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) filed through the SICE/Aduanas system with assigned export registration number
  • Bill of Lading (or Airway Bill) evidencing departure from Panama
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List referencing the exact API commodity code and exported quantity
  • Purchase/Sales Contract or pro-forma invoice supporting the export price
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank wire confirmation evidencing receipt of export proceeds in a Panamanian banking entity

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) pursuant to DGI Resolution No. 201-0734 of 2019 and subsequent amendments, with phased roll-out by taxpayer size starting 2022. Authorized taxpayers must issue structured facturas (XML/PDF) through DGI-approved software or the free Sede Electrónica issuer portal, with near-real-time transmission of the electronic document to the DGI for fiscal validation (receipt of acceptance/rejection codes). For businesses not yet in the mandate, approved fiscal printers (impresoras fiscales) remain necessary. VAT clearance on imports is integrated with the ANA (Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas) customs single window (SICE), where the ITBMS is settled before final customs release.

Zero-rating claims are subject to enhanced DGI/ANA real-time system cross-checks (customs departure records vs. invoicing and banking receipts). Misdeclaration, under-invoicing, or fraudulent zero-rating to domestic consumption triggers retrospective VAT extraction at 7% plus monthly compounding interest (currently approximately 0.833% per month) and fines from 20% to 200% of the tax involved (Código Fiscal, Articles 700 and 712). Criminal liability arises for third-party misdeclarations designed to distort revenue, with the tax authorities retaining a prescription period of 6 years, which is extended to 10 years in cases of non-filed returns or fraudulent conduct. Note: Panama maintains no separate FX-repatriation statute because the Balboa/USD are equivalent and no surrender obligations exist; a 180-day commercial benchmark is nonetheless applied by DGI audit practice to corroborate receipt of export proceeds through local banking channels.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama implements a fully dollarized monetary system; the PAB is redeemable at exactly 1:1 against the USD and is not a floating or controlled currency. Current-account convertibility (trade settlements, service fees, dividends, royalties) and capital-account convertibility (inbound equity contributions, shareholder loans, profit repatriation, and portfolio flows) are unrestricted for both residents and non-residents. Profit-remittance hurdles: Panama applies a territorial income-tax system; a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) that derives only foreign-source income and does not engage in Panamanian-source trade incurs no Panama income tax, and distributions or supplementary benefits to beneficiaries are exempt from Panamanian taxation. Dividends paid from Panamanian-source profits are subject to a single 4.5% withholding dividend tax on the excess over the corporate rate (typically effectively 10% for distributed PAN-source earnings), but no additional exchange-control permit, Central Bank authorisation, or prior clearance is required. There are no restrictions on maintaining USD, EUR, or other multicurrency accounts in Panama, and no limits on transferring funds into or out of the Republic for trade or treasury purposes. Note: banks apply liquidity-risk KYC to validate the underlying trade for all high-risk pharma API transactions, but this is a compliance gate, not a convertibility restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no mandatory repatriation timeline, and no forced conversion ratio on receivables. Pharmaceutical API wholesale exporters may retain all proceeds indefinitely in USD (or any permitted currency) in Panama or abroad. There is no statutory 15/30/60/90-day surrender clock and no obligation to convert export receipts into Balboas. Under the territorial system, foreign-derived gross receipts from API export sales are outside Panama's tax net provided the sale activity (negotiation, execution, and risk-taking) occurs offshore or benefits from a qualifying industrial-export regime. However, banks will expect transaction-level documentary proof of each export receivable in accordance with SBP Acuerdo No. 7-2013 and Law 23 of 2015 AML rules; the practical compliance requirement is to maintain commercial invoices, bills of lading, certificates of analysis, and SWIFT credit notices in the account file.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama maintains no traditional central bank with monetary-issuance or exchange-control authority. The official currency is the Balboa (PAB), now a pure unit of account pegged 1:1 to the U.S. Dollar, with the U.S. Dollar having full legal tender status since 1904. The primary financial regulatory institution is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panama (Superintendency of Banks of Panama - SBP), an autonomous state body established under Decreto de Gabinete No. 238 de 1970 and reformed by Decreto Ley No. 2 de 2008. The Banco Nacional de Panama (National Bank of Panama) acts as fiscal agent of the Republic and performs select central-bank administrative roles but does not issue currency or operate an FX reserve desk. The Ministerio de Economia y Finanzas (Ministry of Economy and Finance) coordinates macroeconomic policy, and the Unidad de Analisis Financiero (UAF) supervises AML/CFT compliance. No foreign-exchange administration, exchange-control board, or capital-flow authorization authority exists in Panama.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM for the specified high-risk pharmaceutical API wholesale model. Panama has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime equivalent to the EU PSD2 framework; payment-service providers operate under SBP-sanctioned 'Empresa de Pagos' authorisations pursuant to Decreto Ley 2 de 2008 and Acuerdo 5-2018, which require a substantive local licensed presence. Offshore digital EMIs (UK FCA-authorised EMI, US MSB, or Singapore MPI) legally may serve a Panama-domiciled PIF only for low-value clearing and expense floats, and only when their activities do not constitute banking intermediation inside Panama; unlicensed foreign EMIs soliciting Panamanian residents risk classification as unauthorised banking activity under Article 4 of Decree-Law 2 of 2008. In practice, were a PIF to use an offshore EMI for high-volume API settlements, the EMI's correspondent bank would likely reject UBO-transparent foundation structures and the rail would fail for documentary letter-of-credit settlement. Recommended architecture: anchor all LC and SWIFT MT103 trade-clearing flows in an SBP-licensed Tier-1 bank, and use an offshore EMI only for incidental non-trade operational expenses (travel, courier, registration fees) capped below USD 5,000 per transaction.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The compliant routing for inbound capital is a SWIFT MT103 from the founder's or investor's foreign account to a debited USD settlement account at a Tier-1 Panamanian bank. Panama applies no mandatory purpose-code classification (unlike BPM codes in strict FX-control jurisdictions); banks instead rely on the SWIFT field 70 (Remittance Information) plus the underlying KYC file. For equity-type foundation capital the recommended field-70 text is: CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION TO PRIVATE INTEREST FOUNDATION / FOUNDATION REGISTRY NO. [XXXXX] / NON-COMMERCIAL / BENEFICIARY UBO [NAME]. For inbound commercial receipts arising from API trade, the narrative must read REIMBURSEMENT FOR COMMERCIAL INVOICE NO. [XXXX] / PHARMACEUTICAL API SUPPLY / INCOTERMS [CPT/CIP] with a matching Proforma Invoice, Commercial Invoice, and Bill of Lading retained by both the remitting and receiving bank. On receipt, the Panamanian bank screens the PIF, UBO, and remitter against UN/OFSI/EU/UK sanctions lists, validates the source-of-funds statement, and credits value the same business day; funds above USD 250,000 typically trigger an enhanced due-diligence hold of 24-72 hours pending documentary confirmation.Three statutory/permissible clearing rails are available for API wholesale settlement. (i) Documentary Letters of Credit: SWIFT MT700 issuance under ICC UCP 600, with MT710/MT720 for transferable or back-to-back credits; LC proceeds are cleared to the confirming bank via SWIFT MT754/MT202 on maturity, with USD settlement through the New York CHIPS/Fedwire corridor; EUR-denominated LCs settle via TARGET2 through Frankfurt, and CNY LCs through CIPS. (ii) Outward SWIFT MT103 for open-account disbursements to pre-approved vendors: Panama's local ACH (Camara de Compensacion) handles only PAB/USD domestic items and is not used for cross-border trade; all external USD transfers must route through a US correspondent. (iii) Documentary Collections (CAD - Cash Against Documents) under ICC URDS 522, preferred when counterparty credit is untested. High-risk pharma disbursements require pre-validation of the vendor's MINSA licence and product-specific sanitary registration prior to execution. Daily disbursement limits and a second-person authorisation protocol are standard for the HIGH_RISK_SPECIAL_LICENSE segment; the bank's UAF interface monitors each MT103 for structuring patterns and files STR/SAR where documentary mismatch is found.Plain vanilla FX forwards (USD/EUR, USD/CNY, USD/GBP) with tenors of 1-12 months; no Central Bank approval is required because the PAB/USD parity is legally fixed and the Balboa is a non-deliverable nominal unit.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) for exotic supply-chain currencies (INR, IDR, VND, COP) executed through the Panamanian bank's offshore treasury desk in New York or London; settlement is made in USD against reference fixings since the Balboa is not an offshore deliverable currency.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) between USD and EUR or USD and CNY for term-matched trade-finance liabilities of the API wholesale operation.Currency options (call and put on EUR/USD and USD/CNY) to hedge future tender proceeds and supply-contract receivables without premium leakage in the fixed PAB/USD environment.Synthetic USD interest-rate hedges are unnecessary under dollarisation; forward rate agreements on USD Libor/SOFR may nonetheless be used to stabilise financing costs on the LC discounting lines.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM for the specified high-risk pharmaceutical API wholesale model. Panama has no dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime equivalent to the EU PSD2 framework; payment-service providers operate under SBP-sanctioned 'Empresa de Pagos' authorisations pursuant to Decreto Ley 2 de 2008 and Acuerdo 5-2018, which require a substantive local licensed presence. Offshore digital EMIs (UK FCA-authorised EMI, US MSB, or Singapore MPI) legally may serve a Panama-domiciled PIF only for low-value clearing and expense floats, and only when their activities do not constitute banking intermediation inside Panama; unlicensed foreign EMIs soliciting Panamanian residents risk classification as unauthorised banking activity under Article 4 of Decree-Law 2 of 2008. In practice, were a PIF to use an offshore EMI for high-volume API settlements, the EMI's correspondent bank would likely reject UBO-transparent foundation structures and the rail would fail for documentary letter-of-credit settlement. Recommended architecture: anchor all LC and SWIFT MT103 trade-clearing flows in an SBP-licensed Tier-1 bank, and use an offshore EMI only for incidental non-trade operational expenses (travel, courier, registration fees) capped below USD 5,000 per transaction.
