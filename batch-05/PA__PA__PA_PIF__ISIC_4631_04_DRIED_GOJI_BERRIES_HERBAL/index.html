Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) – Panama; official portal: dgi.gob.pa and e-Service platform 'SIE' (Sistema de Información Económica) at sie.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) from the DGI for any business activity. The foreign owner appoints a resident Foundation Agent who completes Form RUC-1, submits the foundation charter and agent’s ID, and registers with the DGI (and MCI's Aviso de Operación for commercial operation). Non-resident owners do not require a local presence; the foundation may also register as an exporter via the SEM (Sistema Electrónico de Comercio) of the Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias.

The foreign owner appoints a resident Foundation Agent who completes Form RUC-1, submits the foundation charter and agent’s ID, and registers with the DGI (and MCI's Aviso de Operación for commercial operation). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Panamanian law. An eligible small taxpayer may opt into the Régimen Simplificado de Tributación (simplified tax regime) for ITBMS; exporters may elect to register under the Export Regime and apply zero-rating. No other statutory entity classification elections are available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

USD 0 (mandatory RUC and ITBMS registration for all persons carrying out taxable economic activity in Panama; any threshold is limited to the small-taxpayer simplified regime at annual gross sales ≤ USD 36,000)

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to the effective sale and export of physical goods (e.g., dried goji berries) that leave Panamanian territory to a foreign buyer. The seller must maintain proof that title and risk passed outside Panama. Services are zero-rated only when performed and used entirely offshore. The exporter must hold a valid RUC and be enrolled in the DGI export registry and the Ministerio de Comercio e Industrias' export registry.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Exportación — DUA)
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Panama mandates phased electronic invoicing ('factura electrónica') through the DGI’s SIE platform. Registered taxpayers must issue invoices in a digital format containing a unique authentication hash code and QR, and transmit them electronically to the DGI for validation at issuance. Authorized software must integrate via DGI web services/API. Export operations must also be reported through the customs electronic single-window (VUPA).

Export zero-rating claims are subject to documentary audit by the DGI and customs authorities. Any fraudulent claim to zero-rate an internal sale or misrepresent export documents triggers the application of ITBMS at 7% on the transaction plus interest (1% per month) and fines under Arts. 780–817 of the Código Fiscal; documentary forgery is a criminal offense punishable by imprisonment. The DGI may claw back refunds for up to 3 years after the tax period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible with no exchange controls, no surrender obligations, and no panamanian-source remittance withholding on PIF distributions. Profit remittance by a Private Interest Foundation (Ley 25 de 1995) to foreign beneficiaries is unrestricted, subject only to documentary proof that income is non-Panamanian-source (territorial tax model). USD settlement of dried goji berry trade, EUR or CNY invoices, and overseas supplier payments is lawful without pre-approval; only reported via FECI/Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) when individual wire movements exceed USD 300,000 equivalent or trigger suspicious-transaction filing under Ley 23 de 2015.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no mandatory surrender, conversion ratio, or prescribed repatriation window for export proceeds. The PA_PIF exporter must, however, channel receipts through SBP-regulated banks or licensed fintech institutions, denominate the Foundation books in USD, retain SBP-compliant MT103 records, and preserve the electronic export declaration plus customs clearance issued by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas for at least five years (Código de Comercio Art. 64).

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama has no Central Bank and issues no paper currency. Banking and foreign-exchange administration is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under the General Banking Law (Decreto Ley No. 9 de 26 de febrero de 1998). The U.S. Dollar (USD) is unlimited legal tender under the Monetary Regime Law (Ley 84 de 1941); the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) circulates solely as fractional coins issued through Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) at a fixed 1:1 ratio to USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH for licensed domestic entities, LOW for unlicensed offshore EMIs. Under the SBP Fintech framework (Ley 21 de 2017 and SBP e-money regulations), a locally licensed EMI or digital wallet provider (e.g., Wallter, Nexsys, Criptofacil) may open B2B treasury accounts with remote KYC for vendor disbursements under USD 50,000 per wire; however, correspondent USD rails for suppliers are indirect and LC/SWIFT MT103 settlement remains limited to commercial banks. Offshore EMIs without an SBP license may not legally hold Panamanian foundation deposits unless they partner with a Panamanian licensed bank or act as mere payment facilitators.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% on distributions made out of profits already subject to Panamanian corporate income tax; 10% on distributions made out of Panamanian-source profits that have not borne the general corporate tax (provided they are not properly documented foreign-source income, which remains outside the tax net under the territorial system).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three months after the close of the PIF's fiscal year. For a calendar-year PIF, the return is due on or before 31 March of the following year; for an authorized special fiscal year, the due date is the last day of the third month following the fiscal year-end.
