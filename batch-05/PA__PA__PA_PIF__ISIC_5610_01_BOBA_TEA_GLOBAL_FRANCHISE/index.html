Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is fixed by Law 25 of 1995; the Foundation must be endowed with an initial patrimony (patrimonio inicial) sufficient to fulfil its purpose and proportionate to the value of the licensed franchise operations. No escrow account is mandatory, but cash endowments should be held in a Panama bank account opened in the Foundation's name, and in-kind endowments must be supported by a sworn valuation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety inspection certificate from the Panama Fire Department (Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá) covering extinguishers, exits, and alarm systems; municipal certificate of structural/electrical load and land-use occupancy issued by the Municipality of Panama; and occupational health and safety workplace clearance, including registration of the Joint Health and Safety Committee and accident-risk prevention program with the Ministry of Labor and Workforce Development (MITRADEL).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance, Republic of Panama; statutory electronic portal: e-Tax (https://etax.mef.gob.pa) and institutional DGI portal (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must appoint a local licensed attorney or registered agent; protocolize the foundation deed in the Registro Público; submit the sworn registration application, constitutional documents, and agent authorization through the DGI e-Tax portal; obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente); if the PIF supplies taxable goods/services in Panama, activate the ITBMS ledger, register for Facturación Electrónica, and obtain the digital invoicing certificate. Annual filing of the PIF fixed tax (currently B/.350.00) and payment via e-Tax are mandatory regardless of foreign-source-only activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panamanian law provides no check-the-box, CFC, or hybrid mismatch elections. The PIF is mandatorily territorial: only Panama-source income is taxed at entity level; foreign-source income is outside DGI reach. The PIF may voluntarily register as an ITBMS withholding/collection agent to preserve input VAT credits and may elect in the foundation charter whether distributions to beneficiaries are accumulated or distributed; such election affects beneficiary-level Panama-source taxation but not the territorial default.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold: any person habitually or periodically transferring goods or providing services in Panamanian territory must register and charge ITBMS. A simplified cash-based small-taxpayer regime is available for annual gross income not exceeding B/.36,000.00; Panama-source supply nexus is determined by place of delivery, use, or exploitation of the service.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating under ITBMS applies to (i) exports of goods physically leaving Panamanian customs territory and (ii) services or intangible licensing rendered for use, consumption, or exploitation entirely outside Panama. Franchise royalties, brand licensing fees, and syrup/supply-chain FOB sales by the PA_PIF to foreign franchisees qualify as extraterritorial export service or goods supply when supported by written agreements indicating foreign licensee nexus, off-shore use, and payment received from abroad. Exported tangible goods require physical customs exit; pure offshore services require proof of foreign place-of-use to avoid recharacterization as taxable domestic supply.

  • Panama Customs Export Declaration (electronic filing via SIGA/WISE or DEX) evidencing actual exit of goods
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill, or equivalent title of transport showing exporting shipper and foreign consignee
  • Inward Remittance or Foreign-Wire Settlement Certificate evidencing receipt of export proceeds in a Panamanian bank account
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, underlying international franchise/royalty agreement, and certificates of foreign registration or tax domicile of the licensee

Mandatory electronic invoicing on the DGI e-Tax platform, phased by taxpayer class starting with Gran Contribuyente and extending to all new RUC holders; invoices and equivalent documents must be issued in XML format with a qualified digital signature, transmitted in real time to DGI for fiscal validation, and returned with an acceptance/stamping code before delivery to the counterparty. API/web-service connectivity, digital certificate lifecycle management, and offline contingency procedures follow the DGI 'Facturación Electrónica' technical standard; issuance outside the mandated electronic channel is unlawful except during approved contingency windows.

DGI applies literal- and documentary-evidence audits to zero-rating claims. Missing export declarations, transport documents, or bank remittance evidence causes the supply to be recharacterized as domestic taxable at 7%, with assessment of omitted ITBMS plus monthly late-payment charges, statutory fines up to 200% of the evaded tax, cancellation of export tax credit certificates and refunds, prohibition against new invoicing, and criminal tax-fraud liability of the founder, director, or authorized agent under Law 8 of 2010.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility. There are no exchange controls, no requirement for central bank authorization for current or capital account transactions, and no restrictions on profit remittance. Banks apply only documentary checks such as tax clearance certificates and AML/KYC due diligence. Dollarization eliminates FX conversion risk for USD, but transactions in non-USD currencies are freely negotiated at market rates.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Because the U.S. dollar is the domestic legal tender, export receipts may be retained or converted without mandatory surrender or conversion ratio requirements. No statutory timeline applies to the holding or repatriation of export proceeds.

Central bank supervision: Panama does not operate an independent central bank. The Superintendency of Banks of Panama (SBP) is the primary banking regulator; the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the State under a full dollarization regime using the U.S. dollar as legal tender.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Medium feasibility. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) without a Panamanian banking license cannot legally take deposits or operate as standalone banks. They may enter through sponsor arrangements with a licensed local bank or by obtaining a funds-transfer service provider license from the SBP. A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) may use a foreign EMI for auxiliary payment processing, but tier-1 banks will impose enhanced due diligence and may restrict inter-account clearing flows to licensed onshore banks.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% flat rate applied to the totality of annual taxable income once taxable income exceeds PAB 1,500,000; below that threshold, progressive brackets apply: 10% on the first PAB 30,000, 15% on PAB 30,001-50,000, 20% on PAB 50,001-75,000, and 25% on PAB 75,001-1,500,000. Panama's currency is the Balboa at parity with the US dollar, and all filings are denominated in PAB/USD.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final dividend withholding on distributions paid out of Panamanian-source income already subject to corporate income tax; 5% final withholding on distributions from untaxed or foreign-source income. Distributions by a Private Interest Foundation are not legally 'dividends' under the Código Fiscal because the PIF is a patrimonial vehicle under Law 25 de 1995, not a corporation; repatriation of foreign-source foundation assets to beneficiaries is thus outside the dividend withholding regime unless the foundation operates a commercial business in Panama.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Form 120 must be filed within 90 days after the close of the fiscal year; for a calendar-year taxpayer the hard deadline is 31 March of the year immediately following the tax period. No automatic extension exists: a late payment accrues interest from the day following the statutory deadline, and the annual annexes (Form 124/125) fall due on the same date as the principal return.

Box-by-Box Return Breakdown (non-resident focus)

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall
Box: Periodo (Año Fiscal) Fiscal year covered, e.g. 2025-01-01 to 2025-12-31, identified by the taxpayer in the DGI system. A non-resident PIF without Panamanian-source income must still open a DGI file and may record 'Sin Operaciones' for the period, but only after the RUC and resident agent are in order. Late filing is sanctioned with fines from PAB 100 to PAB 5,000 and a surcharge of 10% on the tax due, plus compensatory interest at 1% per month (0.033% daily) on the unpaid amount; failure to file after DGI notice leads to a presumptive assessment, automatic loss of the five-year loss carryforward, RUC suspension, and for repeated omissions the digital closure of the establishment (clausura) and registry cancellation of the foundation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File documentation is triggered when the aggregate annual value of related-party transactions exceeds PAB 250,000 (USD 250,000), and the Formulario 124 declaration must be filed even at nil amounts by any taxpayer with controlled transactions; a Master File is expected for multinational groups consolidated at or above the OECD Action 13 threshold of EUR 750,000,000 in annual group revenue, and the DGI may additionally require a TP study on request for large taxpayers.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not maintain a formal annual ESR filing regime in the style of the Gulf States, but the substance-over-form doctrine is embedded in tax law: Law 25 de 1995 requires every PIF to have a registered agent, a resident foundation council, a Panama registered office, and books that allow verification of the foundation's purpose; DGI audits under Law 76 de 2019 disregard shell foundations that fail these indicators and re-source the international royalty and supply profits as Panamanian income. A PIF that merely holds the global boba tea franchise IP and receives royalties without local administration, local physical activity, or local decision-making will be treated by foreign tax authorities as tax resident elsewhere and by Panama as an artificial arrangement ineligible for territoriality protection.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Administrative surcharge of 50% to 100% of the under-declared ISR, interest at 1% monthly, and a fine of one to five times the evaded tax under Law 61 de 2012; DGI may also order suspension of the RUC, closure of the commercial establishment for up to 10 days (60 days on repetition), and asset forfeiture in aggravated cases.

Director personal liability: Foundation council members and the senior director who signed the false return face imprisonment of 2 to 5 years (3 to 8 years when committed through a PIF), joint and several civil liability for the entire tax debt under Article 764-A of the Código Fiscal, preventive travel bans (impedimento de salida), freezing of personal and corporate accounts, and extradition exposure where the evasion is connected to a foreign jurisdiction.
Shell Company / Sham Foundation (Falta de Sustancia Económica)

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the structure by DGI under the substance-over-form doctrine with retroactive ISR assessment at the 25% headline rate on the accumulated royalty and supply margin, application of the 10% or 5% dividend withholding on deemed distributions, plus the 10% surcharge and 1% monthly interest; the Registro Público can cancel the foundation for breach of Law 25 de 1995, and the foundation agent is exposed to fines of PAB 500 to PAB 10,000.

Director personal liability: Panamanian courts permit piercing the foundation veil to execute judgments against the founder's assets; directors who certified false 'management in Panama' minutes are civilly liable to the foundation's creditors and beneficiaries; the resident agent may be disbarred, and backdated minutes constitute criminal forgery carrying 3 to 6 years of imprisonment.
