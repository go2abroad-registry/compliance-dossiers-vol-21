Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) - General Directorate of Revenue, Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF); official e-filing portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

1) File the Private Interest Foundation formation deed with the Public Registry of Panama and maintain a resident agent; 2) Inscribe the foundation in the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) via DGI Sede Electrónica, attaching the foundation deed, resident-agent certificate, and by-laws; 3) Activate the RUC and register for ITBMS, irrespective of active trading activity; 4) Obtain DGI pre-authorization for the electronic invoicing (SFEP) scheme at the Panamanian fiscal address. A PIF with exclusively foreign-source income receives a territorial-classification endorsement but remains obligated to file periodic nil or informative returns.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — 1) File the Private Interest Foundation formation deed with the Public Registry of Panama and maintain a resident agent; 2) Inscribe the foundation in the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) via DGI Sede Electrónica, attaching the foundation deed, resident-agent certificate, and by-laws; 3) Activate the RUC and register for ITBMS, irrespective of active trading activity; 4) Obtain DGI pre-authorization for the electronic invoicing (SFEP) scheme at the Panamanian fiscal address. A PIF with exclusively foreign-source income receives a territorial-classification endorsement but remains obligated to file periodic nil or informative returns.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama applies a territorial-source income tax regime; no US-style check-the-box election exists, but the following statutory options are available: (a) election to be treated as a taxed entity on Panama-source income versus a passive foundation with nil liability; (b) election of the Pequeño Contribuyente small-taxpayer presumptive ITBMS regime where annual gross revenue does not exceed PAB 36,000; (c) voluntary ITBMS registration election to recover input credits during the export pre-shipment cycle; (d) written election to the DGI to zero-rate cross-border service export invoices under the Comprobante Fiscal Digital mechanism.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general turnover threshold: any person performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS before commencing activities. Simplified small-taxpayer threshold: PAB 36,000 annual gross taxable income.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to: (a) export of goods physically shipped from Panama under a maritime export customs entry; (b) services rendered from Panama to non-residents 'effectively used or consumed abroad', including international brokerage of bulk agricultural commodities, whenever the beneficiary is abroad, payment originates from foreign funds, and no economic benefit arises within Panama. Supporting evidence must demonstrate receipt of foreign payment and foreign-based usage of the service.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Aduanas / DUI) with port exit confirmation
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) for ocean transport of bulk grain parcels
  • Commercial Invoice annotated 'ITBMS 0% - Exportación'
  • Packing list and warehouse/silo delivery receipts
  • Inward remittance certificate: SWIFT MT103 or bank credit advice evidencing foreign payment
  • Charter-party or voyage contract identifying foreign principals / non-resident buyers

Mandatory electronic invoicing through the DGI Sistema de Facturación Electrónica de Panamá (SFEP). Invoices must be issued in XML/JSON and transmitted in real time to the DGI fiscal clearance platform (Sede Electrónica/API) to obtain a digital stamping authorization code; taxpayers must integrate ERP/billing systems via SFEP API web services; QR-code validation on the Comprobante Fiscal Digital is obligatory.

The DGI audits zero-rated export claims with high scrutiny on the transfer-pricing and fiscal nature of intra-group agri-brokerage commissions. Unsupported zero-rating gives rise to clawback of the 7% ITBMS plus a 10% surcharge, 1% monthly default interest, and fines up to PAB 10,000 under the Panama Fiscal Code; deliberate falsification of export documentation constitutes tax fraud punishable with imprisonment and prospective disqualification of the foundation's resident agent and legal counsel.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full, unrestricted convertibility for both current and capital account transactions. The Panamanian balboa is pegged at par with the USD, and the USD itself is legal tender. There are no exchange-control authorisations, no licensing gates on profit remittance, and no repatriation tax or capital flight restrictions. Distributions, dividends, and beneficiary payments from a Private Interest Foundation are freely remittable after meeting routine AML/CFT record-keeping and banking verification requests.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation exists under Panamanian law. Exporters may retain all USD and other foreign currency receipts indefinitely and are not required to convert any percentage or observe a prescribed timeline for repatriation or monetisation.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — Superintendency of Banks of Panama; Panama does not maintain a central bank, with the United States dollar serving as the circulating legal tender and monetary anchor.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium viability. Panama has no dedicated non-bank electronic-money institution (EMI) licensing regime; offshore EMIs cannot open a regulated settlement account or access the local clearing system without obtaining a banking licence from the SBP or operating through a licensed Panamanian bank as intermediary. Legal constraints include FATF-related enhanced due diligence expectations, a conservative custody over correspondent banking, and the requirement that any PAN PIF trade settlement flow pass through an SBP-regulated or equivalent high-tier foreign bank.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

PIF established with bearer participation certificates or undisclosed foreign beneficiaries.

Root cause: PIF established with bearer participation certificates or undisclosed foreign beneficiaries.

Defense evidence: Public Registry Certificate of the Foundation showing the Foundation Council, a sworn declaration of beneficiary designation, original deed with registered participation interests, certified passport copies, independent legal opinion from a Panamanian counsel, and notarised source-of-funds statement covering the initial endowment.
Cross-border commodity brokerage payments involving counterparties from high-risk jurisdictions or unregulated exchanges.

Root cause: Cross-border commodity brokerage payments involving counterparties from high-risk jurisdictions or unregulated exchanges.

Defense evidence: Fully signed purchase and sale contracts, commercial invoices, bills of lading with vessel names, independent SGS/Bureau Veritas weight and quality certificates, payment-to-trade matching schedules, and proof that all counterparties maintain accounts at SBP-licensed or top-tier correspondent banks.
Rapid fund velocity, thin initial capital, or interposed third-party funding inconsistent with the declared brokerage business.

Root cause: Rapid fund velocity, thin initial capital, or interposed third-party funding inconsistent with the declared brokerage business.

Defense evidence: Capital escrow account statement evidencing the minimum endowment, bank-source questionnaire, six to twelve months of prior bank statements (if applicable), projected soybean and corn brokerage cash-flow model, and an executed AML/KYC policy showing adherence to Panama's Law 23 of 2015 and SBP Agreement 004-2023 KYC rules.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound USD injection is routed by SWIFT MT103 via a New York correspondent bank to the PIF's commercial bank trust account in Panama. The wire instruction must include the underlying purpose reference (e.g., 'PIF initial capital endowment' or 'ISIC 4631 bulk grain brokerage settlement pre-funding'); the remitter's bank must provide corresponding beneficial-owner and source-of-wealth evidence. No Panamanian exchange-control approval is required, but the receiving Panamanian bank will require a copy of the PIF deed, the commercial contract, and AML clearance before crediting the operational account.Outbound grain-trade payments are cleared through SWIFT MT103/MT202 for direct disbursements and via irrevocable commercial letters of credit (MT700/MT707) when the counterparty is a Brazilian, Canadian, U.S., or European grain originator. All USD flows use the U.S. correspondent banking system (Fedwire/CHIPS) and are executed by the PIF's Panamanian bank under the reference 'ISIC 4631 bulk grain - soybean/corn purchase' together with the invoice and shipping summary.
