Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official portals: Sede Electrónica (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa) and institutional website (https://www.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Private Interest Foundation: (1) a resident registered agent (abogado/notario) executes the Foundation Charter (Acta de Fundación) before a Notary Public; (2) the foundation deed is filed and registered in the Registro Público de Panamá (Public Registry, Private Interest Foundations Section); (3) the foundation council and resident agent are appointed; (4) obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) via the DGI Sede Electrónica by submitting the authenticated foundation deed, registered-agent certificate, fiscal-address proof, and identity documents of the founder, council members, and beneficiaries; (5) complete the municipal Aviso de Operación or consolidated DGI registration; (6) apply for ITBMS taxpayer status and electronic invoicing authorization. The DGI typically issues the Certificado de Registro del RUC within 10-15 business days; a PIF with no Panamanian-source activity remains registered but files nil returns as applicable.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No 'check-the-box' pass-through election exists under Panamanian law; a PIF is an opaque juridical person subject to tax only on Panama-source income. Available elections: (a) election to register and collect ITBMS as a taxable person for Panamanian-resident sales; (b) election to enter the simplified quota-based tax regime (Régimen Simplificado) for small taxpayers meeting DGI eligibility criteria; (c) election under Law 25 of 1995 to apply the foundation's statutory confidentiality protections and annual flat tax; (d) no domestic hybrid-mismatch election, though foreign jurisdictions may independently characterize the PIF as transparent or opaque under their own check-the-box or CFC rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration is mandatory upon commencement of any taxable activity in Panamanian territory; there is no numeric turnover exemption threshold in the Código Fiscal. The local currency is the Balboa at parity with the US Dollar, and accounts are kept in USD. A small enterprise may apply to the DGI for Pequeño Contribuyente (simplified quota) status subject to the DGI's activity-volume limits, which substitutes full ITBMS returns with fixed periodic quotas.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

A 0% ITBMS rate applies to exports: (i) tangible goods leaving Panamanian customs territory evidenced by a customs export declaration; and (ii) services, including digital VPN/network-security SaaS, where the recipient is domiciled outside Panama, the use or benefit of the service occurs entirely abroad, and the consideration is effectively remitted from abroad. The VPN privacy application qualifies for export zero-rating if invoiced to non-resident end users, delivered through international channels, and settled via foreign payment rails such as Stripe or Paymentwall; zero-rating is denied where services are supplied to persons located in Panama.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única de Mercancías - DUA) for goods, or a formal service-export invoice with contractual description of offshore use for services
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill for physical goods, or an electronic delivery/usage record for SaaS licensing
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Ingreso de Divisas) issued by the collecting Panamanian bank, or PSP settlement statements proving remittance from abroad
  • Foreign-client affidavit or certificate confirming non-resident status and absence of Panamanian-source exploitation

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing for all RUC holders: invoices must be issued through the DGI Sede Electrónica SV-T system under the Facturación Electrónica regime. The DGI must grant prior authorization for the invoice series/código de operación; invoices are transmitted in real time in DGI-specified XML format via web services/API; each fiscal document must display a QR code and digital stamp and receive DGI validation before being deemed authentic. The phased mandate is set out in DGI Resolution DGI-2019-0712 and subsequent implementation calendars.

DGI audits zero-rated export documentation retrospectively for up to six years; where substantiation is deficient, the supply is recharacterized as taxable locally: ITBMS is assessed with monthly interest, a surcharge of 100%-300% of the omitted tax, and administrative fines under the Código Fiscal, and wilful evasion may trigger criminal tax-fraud prosecution with imprisonment. Additionally, a PIF that misstates foreign-source service income risks clawback of the Law 25 of 1995 annual flat-tax benefits, loss of territorial exemption, and potential cancellation of foundation registration by the Public Registry for lack of legitimate economic purpose.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account transactions. Panama imposes no exchange controls, no restrictions on the repatriation of equity, dividends, profits, or service fees, and no prior regulatory approval for cross-border capital flows. In practice, outbound remittances are subject to bank-level compliance verification (source of funds, sanctions screening, beneficial-ownership transparency) rather than government exchange control. The territorial taxation regime (taxed within Panama only) further facilitates clean profit remittance; a 5% dividend tax is withheld on distributions of Panamanian-source income, but VPN SaaS revenue earned globally outside Panama is generally outside scope.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama law does not mandate repatriation, surrender, or conversion of export or service receipts. VPN subscription and licensing proceeds receivable in USD, EUR, or other convertible currencies may be retained offshore or settled into Panamanian bank accounts without a statutory timeline or conversion minimum. The SBP, however, expects inflows to be reconciled against commercial invoices under the 2015 AML Due Diligence rules and the 2021 Beneficial Ownership regulations.

Central bank supervision: Republic of Panama operates no traditional central bank. Monetary stability derives from full dollarization, with the U.S. dollar as legal tender and the Panamanian balboa (USD 1:1) as nominal unit. Prudential banking supervision is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); foreign-exchange policy and financial-stability coordination rest with the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF). The SBP implements AML/CFT Law 23 of 2015 and regulates all licensed banking activities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium - Feasible subject to regulation. Panama's Financial Technology Law 169 of 2020 permits authorized fintech/electronic-money institutions to operate under SBP/SMV oversight, but offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex) are not exempt from licensing and cannot autonomously acquire local account or clearing access. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard corporate income tax rate under Article 256 of the Panamanian Tax Code, applied on a strictly territorial basis to Panama-source income only; a reduced progressive rate of 15% may apply to taxpayers with taxable income not exceeding the small-taxpayer threshold established by the DGI).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding tax on dividends distributed out of Panama-source profits, leviable on distributions to both resident and non-resident shareholders; dividends paid by a PIF exclusively from territorial-exempt foreign-source VPN revenues are not Panama-source income and are therefore not subject to the 10% withholding. In-treaty cases, the DTA may reduce the rate to 5% or exempt the distribution entirely where the beneficial owner is a tax resident of the treaty counterparty and meets limitation-of-benefits conditions.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For calendar-year taxpayers, the return and full payment are due no later than 31 March of the year immediately following the close of the fiscal year; for taxpayers authorized to adopt a different fiscal year-end, the return is due within three months after the close of that fiscal year. A one-month extension may be requested from the DGI with documented justification, but payment of the estimated tax liability is still required by the original deadline.

Advance payments are due on the last business day of the month immediately following each fiscal quarter according to the DGI calendar (typically the 15th day of April, July, October, and January for calendar-year taxpayers), with the fourth quarter installment reconciled against the annual return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory preparation and filing of the Local File and Master File under Panama's transfer-pricing regime (Decreto Ejecutivo 125 de 2019 and the DGI TP annexes to the ISR return) where annual gross revenue exceeds USD 3,000,000 or where cross-border related-party transactions exceed USD 1,000,000 in the fiscal year; a TP disclosure annex is required for any related-party transaction above USD 200,000, and the PIF must sustain arm's-length pricing for intercompany SaaS licensing, VPN infrastructure leases, and management service fees. The threshold is expressed in US dollars/balboas, reflecting the dollarized Panamanian currency.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not maintain a UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime applicable to PIFs, and a PIF holding only foreign-source VPN subscription income is not required to demonstrate substantial local activity. However, the territorial exemption is conditional: the DGI may recharacterize the PIF as a Panama-resident commercial operator if VPN network management, developer personnel, decision-making, or servers are located in Panama. To protect the exemption, the PIF should maintain a Panama registered office, a resident registered agent, a foundation council (or protector) with formal minutes, complete accounting books, a beneficial-ownership file filed under Law 52 of 2016, and documentary proof that customers, servers, payment flows, and operational management are located outside Panama. The OECD Harmful Tax Practices framework and EU listings also pressure Panama to require adequate substance for entities claiming tax benefits; a no-substance PIF routed through Panama banks faces account termination and enhanced AML scrutiny.
