Governing statute

Formation / constitutional filings

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital; authorized capital is fixed at a nominal amount, commonly US$10,000, and capital may be subscribed without escrow or deposit requirements. Subscribed shares must be recorded in the share register and may be paid in cash, property, or services; issuance of shares without full payment is permitted unless the articles state otherwise, but distributions must follow due solvency rules.

Funding & unlock path: SINAPROC fire-safety inspection and certificate for the registered office; structural load and occupancy safety check by the building owner (if applicable); workplace clearance under MITRADEL occupational health and safety rules, including registration with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS) employer registry.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministry of Economy and Finance (Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas); transactional portal: Sede Electrónica at https://sede.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

1) Appoint a licensed resident agent; register the Sociedad Anónima's public deed in the Registro Público and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; 2) File the Aviso de Operación (Municipal Business Notice) with the competent municipality; 3) Apply online through the DGI Sede Electrónica for the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC), submitting the Registro Público certificate, the resident agent's certification, the registered by-laws and the municipal notice; 4) Receive the RUC certificate, which operates simultaneously as the income tax and ITBMS identification number; 5) Obtain from the Autoridad Nacional de Gobierno Digital (AIG) the PKI digital-signature certificate and activate the electronic invoicing taxpoint in the DGI Facturación Electrónica system; 6) Register the bank account and legal representative with DGI to complete the registration profile.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (Panamanian-source income taxable at the 25% corporate rate; foreign-source market-entry consultancy fees are excluded from Panamanian income tax; no pass-through treatment) — 1) Appoint a licensed resident agent; register the Sociedad Anónima's public deed in the Registro Público and obtain the Certificate of Incorporation; 2) File the Aviso de Operación (Municipal Business Notice) with the competent municipality; 3) Apply online through the DGI Sede Electrónica for the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC), submitting the Registro Público certificate, the resident agent's certification, the registered by-laws and the municipal notice; 4) Receive the RUC certificate, which operates simultaneously as the income tax and ITBMS identification number; 5) Obtain from the Autoridad Nacional de Gobierno Digital (AIG) the PKI digital-signature certificate and activate the electronic invoicing taxpoint in the DGI Facturación Electrónica system; 6) Register the bank account and legal representative with DGI to complete the registration profile.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (Panamanian-source income taxable at the 25% corporate rate; foreign-source market-entry consultancy fees are excluded from Panamanian income tax; no pass-through treatment)

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid entity-reclassification election exists in Panamanian law. Available statutory elections: (i) simplified small-taxpayer regime (Ley 66 de 2019) where annual gross turnover does not exceed B/. 36,000; (ii) advance pricing agreement (APA) election with DGI for cross-border intra-group services; (iii) election to apply exclusive territorial-source treatment, documenting the foreign-source character of consulting income; (iv) election to pay the 10% dividend tax on Panamanian-source profits only (5% for registered or publicly traded shares); (v) election under Ley 41 de 2007 for multinational headquarters (SEM) treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis threshold: all persons habitually selling movable goods or rendering services in Panama must register for ITBMS from the first taxable supply. Small taxpayers with annual gross turnover not exceeding B/. 36,000 may elect the simplified regime, but RUC/ITBMS registration remains mandatory.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (1) physical exports of goods evidenced by customs declarations, and (2) services performed for non-domiciled recipients where the services are used or economically exploited entirely outside Panama, including professional market-entry consultancy (Articles 66-68, Ley 14 de 2013; Código Fiscal). The consulting S.A. may invoice at 0% ITBMS when it retains: the foreign client's proof of non-domiciliation, the executed cross-border consulting agreement, delivery and performance records evidencing overseas utilization, and bank documents tracing the consideration from abroad. No prior refund is required for zero-rated services; input ITBM credits remain claimable through DGI Sede Electrónica refund procedures.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera / Formulario de Exportación)
  • Commercial invoice duly endorsed at 0% ITBMS
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill for physical goods; for consultancy services, the signed cross-border service agreement with deliverables and performance logs
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Ingreso de Divisas) issued by the receiving Panamanian bank
  • Foreign client tax identification or certificate of non-domiciliation (e.g., foreign VAT register extract or incorporation certificate)

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing through the DGI Facturación Electrónica platform. Every invoice must be transmitted in real time to the DGI authorization web service via a certified API (SOAP/REST) before delivery; DGI responds with a unique Número de Autorización (digital stamp) and a QR security code to be embedded in the electronic XML and printed purchase forms. Non-integrated invoicing invalidates the buyer's ITBMS input credit, triggers a per-invoice fine, and may suspend the issuer's electronic fiscal stamp.

If DGI recharacterizes a zero-rated export, it claws back the uncollected 7% ITBMS plus monthly default interest at the Banco Nacional lending-rate corridor, and applies late-payment surcharges. Fraudulent zero-rating claims are prosecuted as tax evasion under Articles 779-A et seq. of the Código Fiscal, attracting fines between 50% and 200% of the evaded tax, automatic denial of input credits, annulment of fiscal invoices, loss of the electronic stamp, and criminal imprisonment of up to five years for the legal representative or resident agent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized, with the US dollar as legal tender and complete freedom of payment on both current and capital account. There are no exchange-control approvals for profit remittance, capital repatriation, or dividend distribution. The only potential hurdles are documentary (corporate board resolutions, beneficial ownership declaration under Law 23 of 2015) and standard bank KYC validation of source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama does not impose any statutory surrender requirement, retention obligation, or mandatory conversion ratio on export proceeds; receipts may be held and used freely in USD.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama) acting as the principal banking and AML/CFT regulator; the Banco Nacional de Panamá functions as the government's fiscal agent and settlement bank. Panama has no formal central bank and no dedicated foreign exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for cross-border payment execution via Wise B2B and comparable EC/EMI channels, provided such entities hold a valid money-transmitter or e-money license in a reputable jurisdiction. Legal constraints: an offshore EMI must not conduct deposit-taking or lending in Panama without a banking license; it may only transmit funds for a Panama-domiciled user if it registers as a money remittance service provider under Law 23 of 2015 and complies with Panama's AML/CFT regulatory framework. Therefore, Wise B2B is viable for making outward trade disbursements and receiving inward consultant fees, but it cannot serve as a primary bank account for holding capital.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Panamanian shell company with no visible economic substance or physical footprint in Panama

Root cause: Panamanian shell company with no visible economic substance or physical footprint in Panama

Defense evidence: An authenticated corporate registry certificate (Ficha) evidencing incorporation; sworn affidavit of good standing; lease agreement for registered office or shared services arrangement with the registered agent; detailed commercial plan for overseas market-entry consultancy; brief history of the clients’ consulting mandates; and a certified copy of a valid passaport plus proof of address for each UBO.
Non-resident beneficial owners speaking and directing from a high-risk jurisdiction or a jurisdiction appearing on FATF or EU blacklists

Root cause: Non-resident beneficial owners speaking and directing from a high-risk jurisdiction or a jurisdiction appearing on FATF or EU blacklists

Defense evidence: Source-of-wealth narrative supported by audited financial statements or bank reference; certified tax-residence certificates from the home country; an external legal opinion confirming that the home-country audit trail proves the origin of funds; transactional heat-map of the consultancy's expected inbound and outbound flows; and a heightened monitoring agreement with the bank.
Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or negative adverse-media hit on a director or beneficial owner

Root cause: Politically Exposed Person (PEP) or negative adverse-media hit on a director or beneficial owner

Defense evidence: A professional due-diligence report from a licensed screening provider; a notarized declaration stating the exact nature and extent of the PEP role; a board resolution restricting the PEP person from bank-signing authority if not logically required; and a demonstration that the stated consultancy fee profile is consistent with ordinary overseas expansion projects.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections must be routed as SWIFT MT103 wire transfers to the Panama-domiciled account of the Sociedad Anónima, with the reference line containing the legal entity name, the Panama tax ID (RUC), and the corporate registry 'Ficha' number. Banks will typically accept only transfers with a purpose code consistent with a capital contribution (e.g., 'CAPT' or 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION') and may require a covering board resolution and source-of-funds declaration for inflows above USD 25,000. For non-resident investors, the initial wire should be accompanied by a notarized certificate of the ultimate beneficial owner and a bank reference from the sending institution.Clearing rails include SWIFT MT103 for operator payments and supplier disbursements; documentary letters of credit under UCP 600 issued via a major Panamanian bank's correspondent banking network in New York or Miami; and local payments through Panama's Public Payment System (Sistema de Pago del Gobierno) or same-bank transfers for domestic disbursements.
