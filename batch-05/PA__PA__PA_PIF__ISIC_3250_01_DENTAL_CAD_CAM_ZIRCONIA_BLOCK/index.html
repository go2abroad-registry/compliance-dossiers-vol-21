Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital for Private Interest Foundations under Panama law; however, the Foundation Charter must declare an initial endowment (patrimonio inicial) of at least USD 10,000. This amount does not require placement in a public escrow; it is evidenced by a written subscription certificate and remains under the administration of the Foundation Council.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification (Certificado de Seguridad del Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá) before operations, including structural load inspection for kilns and milling equipment, electrical capacity review, connection to public fire suppression systems; occupational hygiene clearance from MITRADEL (Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral) and CSS medical-risk evaluation for workers exposed to ceramic dust; industrial noise control to below 85 dB during an 8-hour shift.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; operational portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://sede.dgi.gob.pa) with the official DGI domain https://www.dgi.gob.pa.

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Private Interest Foundation (PIF): (1) The resident foundation agent (licensed Panamanian attorney) files the foundation charter in the Registro Público de Panamá under Law 25 of 1995 and obtains the public-inscription certificate evidencing legal personality; (2) the same agent files the Aviso de Operación (Business Operation Notice) with the DGI; (3) the agent submits the inscription certificate, foundation by-laws (estatutos), Panamanian registered address, and identification of officers/directors via the Sede Electrónica portal, authenticated with the agent's advanced digital signature (Firma Electrónica Avanzada), to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente — for legal entities the RUC is prefixed with 'N' and carries a numeric body and DGI check digit); (4) the RUC application is made in parallel with the ITBMS registration and the application for DGI electronic-invoicing accreditation. Foreign owners and beneficiaries are permitted without local residency or shareholding; the only hard localization requirements are the registered Panamanian agent and a registered address in Panama.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election and no OECD hybrid-mismatch election mechanism are recognized under Panamanian tax law. Statutory elections available to the PIF include: (a) irrevocable election for the Simplified Small Taxpayer Regime under Law 43 of 2016 where annual gross turnover does not exceed B/.36,000; (b) election by the foundation council to activate the Law 25 of 1995 framework so that only Panama-source income falls within the taxable base (territorial scope); (c) filing of a Consulta Previa (advance tax ruling) with the DGI, which creates a binding administrative determination as to the Panama-source or foreign-source characterization of dental zirconia block sales and related CAD/CAM support services.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Registration is mandatory when the entity commences commercial operations; there is no general de minimis exemption above that trigger. However, the Simplified Small Taxpayer Regime (Régimen Simplificado de Pequeños Contribuyentes) is available for taxable turnover not exceeding B/.36,000 per annum. The Balboa is pegged 1:1 to the US dollar; Panama issues no physical currency and uses USD for all tax settlements.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and qualifying offshore services are zero-rated ITBMS under Article 1058 of the Panamanian Tax Code. For dental CAD/CAM zirconia blocks: (1) the goods must physically depart Panama under an authorized customs export process through SUCA/VUCEA (the national single-window foreign trade platform); (2) legal title must transfer to a non-resident buyer outside Panama; (3) the sale consideration must flow through banking channels and ultimately be credited to a Panamanian account in compliance with FX repatriation rules; and (4) supporting purchase orders, Incoterms, and payment wiring instructions must be retained. Offshore services (e.g., CAD/CAM design files, digital milling support) also qualify for zero-rating when rendered to non-resident customers and economically enjoyed/utilized outside Panama.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación presentada ante SUCA/VUCEA), Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Ingreso de Divisas or equivalent bank-issued credit confirmation evidencing repatriation of export proceeds into Panama)

Mandatory near-real-time electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) applies to general-regime taxpayers. Invoices are issued via DGI-certified software or the DGI web portal and transmitted through web services/API to the DGI's Sede Electrónica, signed with the taxpayer's Firma Electrónica Avanzada. Each invoice is centrally validated and receives a unique fiscal authorization code and correlative number; the DGI issues an automatic acceptance/rejection response (fiscal clearance). Legacy pre-printed CAF paper invoice series are progressively prohibited and are not permitted for mandatory e-invoicing taxpayers. Automotive digital stamping of each invoice is performed by the DGI in this clearance flow.

DGI Fiscalización conducts retrospective export substantiation audits. Failure to substantiate a zero-rated export results in retroactive reassessment at the standard 7% ITBMS, plus monthly statutory interest and recargo (surcharge), and clawback of any ITBMS input credits or refunds generated by that transaction. Under the Tax Procedure Code (Law 76 of 2019), fraud or deliberate falsification of export documentation carries monetary penalties ranging from 50% to 200% of the tax evaded, in addition to interest and surcharges; criminal tax-fraud liability, including imprisonment, may be pursued under Panama's Criminal Code. The DGI may also administratively suspend the taxpayer's RUC, revoke e-invoicing authorizations, and block future export clearance certificates until amounts are settled.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Unrestricted full convertibility under a United States Dollar legal-tender regime; the national Balboa is coin-only and pegged 1:1 at par. Current-account flows (export settlements, import payments, service fees) and capital-account flows (foundation capital injections, distributions, loan repayments) are freely convertible with no exchange license, no prior-authority approval, and no surrender requirement. Cross-border profit or asset distributions are freely remitted, but a taxable dividend/asset distribution withholding of 10% is triggered under Section 733 of the Panama Fiscal Code unless the underlying income had already been subject to the 10% non-distributed profits tax; the dental block manufacturer should therefore either retain earnings, obtain a certificado de reinversión, or settle the distribution tax before wiring abroad.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. No statutory timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio exist for export receipts; all export proceeds may be retained in USD. The exporter must register the export through the Panama Customs Authority single window using the Declaración Única de Ingreso, evidence the receipts through bank SWIFT credits, and report the taxable export margin under Formulario 709, being mindful that exports are deemed Panamanian-source if title and risk of loss pass within Panamanian territory.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system without a conventional central bank; the SBP, created by Legislative Decree No. 9 of 1998, is the supreme banking and foreign-exchange compliance authority, while Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the fiscal agent of the Republic.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM-LOW. Panama's deposit-taking monopoly rests with financial institutions licensed by the SBP; non-bank foreign electronic-money institutions such as Wise, Airwallex, and Mberry are not authorized to hold segregated business deposits or to open on-behalf-of accounts for a Panama Private Interest Foundation. The SBP Innovative Activity Hub, established under Resolution SBP 026-2021, permits sandboxed payment-service providers only when sponsored by a licensed Panamanian bank. Consequently, direct EMI boarding of the PIF is LOW viability, while MEDIUM viability exists for card-acquiring settlements and online collections from dental laboratories through a licensed sponsor bank e-money arrangement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant inbound routing uses SWIFT MT103 or MT202 wires from the settlor home bank to the PIF USD account at a Panamanian Tier-1 bank, cleared through New York CHIPS or Fedwire correspondents such as Citibank N.A. Panama or the Banco General New York agency. The purpose code and field 70 remittance information must read FOUNDATION CAPITAL ALLOTMENT — ISIC 3250 DENTAL CERAMIC OPERATIONS, and the beneficiary name must exactly match the PIF account title vetted at onboarding. Documentary verification comprises the notarized and apostilled Foundation Charter, the Foundation Council resolution authorizing the capital subscription, source-of-funds bank statements, settlor financial statements if the injection exceeds USD 250,000, a sworn statement of no third-party fungibility, and the registered agent beneficial-ownership attestation. Panama territorial taxation makes such capital injections tax-neutral; however, any injection structured as a shareholder loan above USD 100,000 bearing interest requires loan documentation and triggers 10% withholding on gross interest paid to the non-resident lender under the Fiscal Code.Two dominant clearing rails apply. First, high-value recurring trade settlements are executed by SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers from the Panamanian operating account to supplier beneficiary banks, cleared directly through New York in USD or via Hong Kong CNH clearing for Mainland Chinese powder suppliers. Second, documentary commercial letters of credit compliant with UCP600 are issued by the Panamanian issuing bank in favor of Chinese or European machinery suppliers for large CAD/CAM milling-center acquisitions, with drafts and full shipping documents. For European export sales, direct SWIFT MT103 transfers are used to Scandinavian clinical-group bank accounts; for Latin American distributors, USD telegraphic transfers or local-currency permitted proxies are used, always accompanied by commercial invoices, packing lists, and the Panama export declaration.
