Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

A Private Interest Foundation may maintain its registered office at the address of the Panama-licensed resident agent. However, the express courier trade license under ISIC 5320 requires a local commercial establishment in Panama, a physical mail-and-parcel sortation facility or bonded warehouse, and operational customs clearance capacity. Virtual office-only arrangements are not permissible for courier operations, although the foundation's own registered office may remain at the agent's address if no operational activities are conducted there.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is fixed by Ley 25 of 1995; the foundation deed must declare an initial endowment of at least USD 100 or equivalent property, to be recorded in the foundation books. No escrow account is required by law; the endowment may be subscribed in cash or assets and transferred upon formation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá covering extinguishers, emergency exits, and storage-area fire separation; municipal structural and occupancy inspection for load-bearing capacity of warehouse shelving and office areas; occupational health and workplace safety clearance from the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL), including first-aid provisions, ergonomic standards for parcel handling, and electrical installation certification for the operating premises.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; operational portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://www.dgi.gob.pa), which includes RUC management, the Facturación Electrónica system, ITBMS and income-tax return filing, and taxpayer certificate issuance.

E-government filing portal

For a non-resident-owned Private Interest Foundation (PIF): (1) Execute the PIF charter (Acta Constitutiva) before a Panamanian notary and record it in the Registro Público de Panamá; appoint the mandatory resident agent (Secretario) pursuant to Law 25 of 1995. (2) Obtain the certified Registry Certificate (Certificado de Registro). (3) File the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) online through the DGI Sede Electrónica, attaching the registered charter, Registry Certificate, resident agent Cedula, and registered-office proof; the minimum foundation capital is fixed by the charter and no physical deposit is required. (4) Upon approval, DGI issues the RUC (with verification digit) and, where the foundation makes taxable supplies in Panama, opens the taxpayer account under ISIC 5320 (courier activities) with ITBMS and advance income-tax quotas. (5) Obtain a Panamanian PKI digital certificate from an authorized certification authority. (6) Enroll in the DGI Facturación Electrónica portal (free web system) or activate DGI-certified billing software. (7) Register separately with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA) as an express-delivery/courier operator to use simplified export declarations and substantiate the 0% export VAT treatment.

Default tax classification

Available election forms

No check-the-box or pass-through election exists under the Código Fiscal; the PIF is mandatorily a separate legal person taxed at entity level on Panama-source income only (foreign-source income is exempt). Available elections: (a) optional Simplified Tax Regime (Régimen Simplificado) for taxpayers with annual gross turnover up to US$200,000, using presumptive quotas; (b) election of direct attribution versus the prorrata method for ITBMS input credits between 7% domestic supplies and zero-rated export supplies; (c) voluntary early adoption of Facturación Electrónica ahead of the mandatory DGI calendar; (d) DGI advance ruling/certification on the territorial characterization of a specific cross-border courier revenue stream, which mitigates hybrid mismatch characterization with the foreign principal.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama operates an open, dollarized economy with full current-account and capital-account convertibility; there is no local-currency foreign-exchange bottleneck because the United States dollar is legal tender and the balboa is a fixed coinage counterpart. Profit remittance is unblocked, but practical hurdles include AML-channeling duties under Law 21 of 2013, SBP transaction-reporting thresholds (reportable cash and wired transactions above USD 10,000), and the need to evidence tax good standing for distribution instructions to be processed by custodian banks. Capital injection, dividend payments, intercompany loans, and liquidation proceeds can be remitted without Central Bank authorization, but every wire must carry a valid economic-purpose narrative and supporting foundation governance record.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable: Panama imposes no statutory surrender, conversion ratio, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. Couriers and commercial exporters may retain 100% of USD export receipts freely. Mandatory conversion ratio: 0%; statutory remittance timeline: none. Nevertheless, express-courier export receipts flowing through Panama-licensed banks must be reconciled with airway bills, customs declarations, and invoices to prevent trade-based money-laundering classification; accumulated credits requiring onward wire transfer remain subject to the SBP's client-due-diligence and UAF cash-reporting rules (Law 21 of 2013 as amended).

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) exercises consolidated banking and financial-system supervision; Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) serves as the AML/CFT Financial Intelligence Unit; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the State. No conventional central bank with banknote-issuance powers exists because the Republic of Panama is de jure dollarized under Law 84 of 1 July 1904; the balboa exists only as subsidiary coinage pegged strictly 1:1 to the US dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Panama has no electronic-money institution (EMI) charter class comparable to EU/UK regulation; non-bank payment services are not passportable into Panama. A foreign EMI can clear USD only through its own US correspondent and cannot join the local clearing, acquire card rails, or hold client funds locally without acting through an SBP-licensed bank. The practical compliant architecture is to have the fintech stakes in the PIF's Switzerland/UK EMI serve only as a non-resident collection front, with daily settlement wires into the Panamanian operating account; this is permitted but imposes strict caused-of-payment documentation and may trigger enhanced UAF monitoring if monthly throughput exceeds USD 10,000.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing uses USD-denominated SWIFT MT103 (and MT202 COVER for interbank funding) directly to the PIF's operational or capital-escrow account at a Panama-licensed bank; the payment narrative must state 'Capital subscription — Private Interest Foundation [name]' with a corresponding foundation charter reference. The initial statutory foundation capital (minimum USD 10,000; 50% payable at creation) must be evidenced by bank receipt stamped at the Registro Público. Additional injections require council authorization, a notarized corporate resolution, UBO source-of-wealth statement, and source-of-funds verification for amounts above USD 50,000.
