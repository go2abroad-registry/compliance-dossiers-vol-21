Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); self-service portal: Sede Electrónica 'E-Tax 2.0' at https://www.dgi.gob.pa (RUC activation, filing, and fiscal e-invoicing dashboard).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Sociedad Anónima must (1) incorporate via the Registro Público de Panamá and file certified articles of incorporation, resolution appointing directors/officers, and the resident agent's acceptance; (2) file the Aviso de Operación (business operation notice) with the DGI; (3) complete the RUC application in the Sede Electrónica, attaching passport copies, share-registry excerpt, registered-office utility bill, and proof of the resident agent designation; (4) obtain a Firma Digital (certified electronic signature) from the Registro Público/AIG to transact digitally; the DGI then issues the provisional RUC with assignment of the applicable tax obligations (ISR, ITBMS, social contributions where payroll exists). Non-resident shareholders do not need a local tax ID for merely holding shares; the S.A. itself is the registrant.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a US-style check-the-box election; a Panama S.A. is, by default, an entity-level taxpayer on Panama-source income only. Available elections include: (a) simplified small-taxpayer regime (Régimen Simplificado) with 2% presumptive ISR where annual gross revenue does not exceed B/. 36,000; (b) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime under Ley 41 de 2007 applying an elective 5% ISR on qualifying Panamanian-source services; (c) Panama Pacifico Special Economic Zone incentives under Ley 41 de 2004; (d) elective accelerated depreciation and job-creation investment incentives under Ley 76 de 2019 (Fiscal Transparency Law); and (e) optional ITBMS credit or refund treatment for exporters under the Código Fiscal.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 36,000 (Balboa, at parity with USD) of annual gross revenue from taxable activities; registration is mandatory for all entities holding an Aviso de Operación, with voluntary registration permitted below the threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services are subject to the ITBMS rate of 0% where (i) goods are physically exported from the national customs territory, or (ii) services are rendered to non-residents and qualify as foreign-source income under territorial rules — for AI data-labeling/BPO services this requires the client to be domiciled abroad, the service contract to be governed by and performed for foreign beneficiaries, and remuneration to be credited to Panama; exporters may claim refunds or credits of accumulated input ITBMS, subject to DGI verification of the underlying export transaction.

  • DUA — Documento Único de Aduanas (customs export declaration filed via SICA customs system)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical exportation (goods)
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Export service agreement, service certificate, and invoice (for offshore BPO/AI data services)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit confirmation showing settlement of export proceeds
  • ITBMS export declaration and tax-credit refund application forms (Formulario DGI)

Mandatory fiscal e-invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) under DGI rules: invoices must be issued through certified billing software or the DGI web portal, transmitted in near-real time to the DGI Sede Electrónica for validation and digital stamping, carry a QR code and sequential authorized fiscal series, and be integrated via Web Services/API for certified ERP vendors; phased enforcement is complete for all active RUC taxpayers, and credit for input ITBMS is denied on non-compliant documents.

DGI audit review may extend to 5 years from the filing date (longer where returns are fraudulent or not filed). Unsubstantiated or artificially constructed export zero-ratings are reclassified as taxable domestic supplies, triggering the unpaid ITBMS plus monthly interest, and fines up to 100% of the omitted tax under Código Fiscal Articles 759-760; deliberate falsification of export documentation constitutes tax fraud with potential criminal penalties, loss of export registration status, and joint liability of directors and the resident agent.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama presents full current-account and capital-account convertibility: no exchange controls, no conversion licensing, and no restriction on profit or liquidation remittances to non-resident shareholders. A Panamanian Sociedad Anónima may freely hold, borrow, wire, and convert USD, EUR, GBP, or other currencies through SBP-licensed institutions. Resident banks report FX positions solely for prudential statistics, not for conversion approval. The only fiscal drain on repatriation is Panama's territorial tax: dividends paid out of Panama-source corporate profits attract a 10% withholding, whereas dividends funded from foreign-source business income are exempt from Panamanian tax if separately documented in the corporate treasury records.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no export-proceeds surrender regime in Panama. No statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export earnings within any period. BPO and AI data-labeling exporters may retain 100% of US Dollar receipts onshore or offshore, subject only to normal DGI e-invoicing, revenue-recognition, and SBP record-keeping obligations under Law 23 of 2015; all proceeds are fully and freely usable for payroll, capital expenditure, and shareholder distributions.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama operates without a central bank issuing domestic fiat. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the sovereign banking and foreign-exchange prudential regulator, licensing banks under General, International, and Representation categories. Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as fiscal agent of the State, Caja de Ahorros administers mandatory savings and pension deposits, and the Ministry of Economy and Finance manages residual official reserves. The US Dollar is de facto and de jure legal tender under the 1904 monetary regime, with Balboa coinage circulating only as a 1:1 representation of the Dollar.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: HIGH for Wise Business and PayPal Mass Payout as auxiliary treasury rails; MEDIUM for unlicensed offshore EMI neo-banks (Airwallex, Payoneer) due to the absence of a Panamanian e-money license category. Panama does not charter standalone EMIs; the SBP regulates payment systems and the national clearing house but does not authorize offshore EMI deposit-taking in Panama. A Panamanian S.A. may lawfully onboard with Wise and receive PayPal Mass Payout settlement into a verified local bank account, provided the entity maintains client-facing Master Service Agreements and invoice reconciliations; Tier-1 local banks will escalate review if more than 30% of monthly inflows arrive via digital EMI rails, so treasury must preserve documentary provenance of every AI data-labeling client payment.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 5,000,000

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Panama has adopted economic substance regulations that apply to any S.A. carrying on relevant activities, which include service activities and IT/BPO services. The data-labeling entity must maintain in Panama a real office, employees with actual work autonomy, board meetings and management decisions, and a cost profile consistent with its revenue. A standard S.A. cannot rely on nominee directors or interposed invoicing structures to report BPO income; the DGI and the Superintendencia may disregard the entity, reallocate income, impose penalties, and revoke the corporate license for lack of substance in relevant activities.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Administrative surcharge of 50% to 200% of the tax evaded, interest, financial sanctions up to USD 50,000, administrative embargo, RUC suspension, and criminal conviction carrying imprisonment of 2 to 6 years under Panamanian fiscal criminal law.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign or approve the fraudulent return are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, penalties, and interest; if the Public Prosecutor proves participation in fraud, they face civil restitution, travel bans, asset freezing, and imprisonment.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Abuse

Statutory sanction: The DGI may recharacterize the S.A. as a transparent conduit, allocate the full BPO income to the foreign parent or beneficial owner, deny deductions and loss carryforwards, apply a 2%-10% turnover penalty, and cancel the company’s operating license. Economic Substance Regulations separately impose penalties from USD 8,000 to USD 200,000 for non-compliant entities engaged in relevant activities.

Director personal liability: Nominee and statutory directors may be held personally accountable for restitution of avoided tax and penalties, disqualified from serving as directors, prohibited from leaving Panama under judicial travel restrictions, and charged with illicit enrichment if they facilitated the sham structure.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of up to 10% of the value of the related-party transactions, plus a primary adjustment of taxable income with corresponding interest and a 33%-50% undervaluation surcharge; repeated violations result in RUC suspension and referral to criminal tax authorities.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign transfer-pricing documentation knowing it is fictitious are jointly and severally liable for the tax adjustment, and courts may order restitution, impose a travel ban, and, where fraud is established, impose imprisonment under fiscal fraud provisions.
AML & Beneficial Ownership Reporting Failure

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines of USD 1,000 per day after the first 30-day breach, additional penalties up to USD 500,000 under Panama’s AML and beneficial-ownership laws, and denial of the application for the annual operating license; laundering of proceeds derived from a predicate offense is prosecuted separately.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines, disqualification from holding office, asset confiscation, restriction on leaving Panamanian territory, and imprisonment of up to 12 years where the AML violation exposes the BPO to money-laundering liability for tainted client funds.
