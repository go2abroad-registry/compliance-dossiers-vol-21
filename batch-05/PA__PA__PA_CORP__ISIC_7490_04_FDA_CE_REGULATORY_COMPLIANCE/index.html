Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Sociedad Anónima at the Registro Público de Panamá through a licensed resident agent and obtain the certified incorporation records. Step 2: File the municipal Aviso de Operación with the Circuito Municipal of the registered office. Step 3: Register online via the DGI Sede Electrónica to obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente), submitting the certificate of incorporation, resident agent acceptance, director/shareholder identification (passport copies), and proof of registered address. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope (No_Pass_Through). Panama S.A. is taxed at entity level on Panama-source income only; foreign-source income is exempt; the entity is treated as a per-se corporation for U.S. tax purposes and cannot elect partnership pass-through treatment; no Tax_Exempt_Zone treatment arises automatically, except where the S.A. opts into special regimes such as SEM headquarters, Free Zone, or Panama-Pacifico licensing.

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists on the Panamanian side. Available statutory elections include: (a) consolidated group filing election by affiliated companies (Art. 699-A Código Fiscal); (b) election to apply the Simplified Regime (Régimen Simplificado) at 2% of gross receipts for small-scale operations; (c) election between the standard 25% ISR bracket schedule and presumptive taxation for taxpayers failing adequate accounting records; (d) optional ITBMS registration for service exporters whose annual transfers fall below the statutory threshold so they may lawfully issue 0% export invoices; and (e) election to apply the Multinational Company Headquarters (SEM) regime for regional MedTech consulting services, which grants special tax incentives and enhanced territorial benefits.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/.36,000 (Balboas 36,000; equivalent to USD 36,000) of annual gross taxable transfers gives rise to mandatory ITBMS registration; export-only service providers may register voluntarily to access the export zero-rating mechanism.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

0% ITBMS applies to export services where the recipient is a non-resident, the service is performed from Panama but is wholly consumed or economically exploited outside Panamanian territory, and payment originates from abroad. FDA 510(k) filing and European CE Mark regulatory consulting sold to foreign medical device sponsors qualify if the deliverables (regulatory submissions, technical files, dossiers) are made available abroad and the client has no Panamanian permanent establishment or local taxable engagement. Eligibility is conditioned on prior filing of the export service registration/aviso with the DGI and ability to evidence foreign utilization; the same regime preserves the territorial ISR exemption for foreign-source consulting income. Panama has no foreign-exchange control, hence no statutory FX repatriation deadline; the 180-day figure stated under this schema is used only as a documentary discipline target for inward remittance evidence.

  • DGI-validated export service registration (aviso/declaración de exportación de servicios) filed via Sede Electrónica
  • 0%-rated electronic invoice bearing the DGI fiscal control code and QR with ITBMS zero-rate legend
  • Signed consulting agreement / statement of work with the foreign sponsor evidencing foreign utilization
  • Proof of foreign execution: submitted FDA 510(k) eCopy transmission or EU CE Mark dossier lodged with a Notified Body/competent authority
  • Inward remittance certificate and SWIFT payment evidence from the overseas client
  • Annual export-services reconciliation forming part of the income tax return (Formulario 330) and ITBMS monthly return
  • Corporate records supporting territorial sourcing if the DGI requests an economic substance demonstration

Mandatory electronic invoicing (factura electrónica) implemented by DGI Resolution 201-0446/2019 and successive amendments; all invoices must be issued through DGI-validated billing software (Proveedor Tecnológico de Facturación, PTF) with real-time online transmission and assignment of a unique ministerial fiscal validation number; invoices carry a QR code and DGI control code; monthly electronic annexure and filing of the ITBMS return (Formulario mensual) through Sede Electrónica are mandatory. No traditional physical fiscal stamps remain operative for standard commercial invoices.

DGI can retroactively claw back zero-rating within the general 4-year audit window where the exporter fails to substantiate foreign use, foreign client residence, or foreign-source payment; re-characterized invoices are taxed at 7% plus monthly interest of 1% (Art. 1118 Código Fiscal) and late-payment surcharges; false export documentation or artificially routed service-export schemes attract fines up to 200% of the underpaid tax and administrative sanctions; deliberate fiscal fraud on zero-rated exports is criminally actionable under Panamanian law with imprisonment of up to 10 years. Export invoices must be matched to bank wire receipts and retain the full documentation bundle for the statutory retention period to avoid penalty and interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama imposes no exchange-control legislation: both the current account and the capital account are fully convertible for residents and non-residents alike. Inbound foreign direct investment, shareholder loans, service fees, dividends, and intra-group payments can be freely credited, debited, and remitted in USD or any convertible currency without prior central bank approval, repatriation license, or destination-specific clearance. Profit remittance by a Sociedad Anónima is unrestricted from a treasury standpoint; the only practical gatekeeper is the commercial bank's own risk-based KYC/CDD framework, which requires transparent economic purpose and clear SWIFT payment references. Because the entity is territorial for income tax purposes, no FX control does or can impede the movement of consulting fees from FDA/CE clients in the United States or the European Union.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama maintains no export proceeds surrender regime, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no statutory repatriation timeline because the domestic economy is denominated in USD. The MedTech regulatory consulting company (ISIC 7490) may invoice its advisory services in USD, EUR, or any conventional currency and retain 100% of proceeds in its Panamanian operational account; no conversion into balboas is required or even commercially functional. If EUR-denominated CE-mark filing fees are collected, the company may either hold EUR in a multicurrency account or execute an immediate spot conversion into USD at the bank's daily telex rate.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system and maintains no traditional central bank. Banking and FX surveillance is exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); AML/CFT supervision is conducted by the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF); Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the fiscal and financial agent of the State. The US dollar is legal tender, and the Panamanian balboa (PAB) circulates only as coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity with the USD, thereby eliminating monetary policy, reserve requirements, and exchange-rate intervention by national authorities.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM for transaction collection only; LOW for deposit concentration or treasury function. Under SBP General Rule 004-2010 and the SBP Fintech Innovation Hub framework, a foreign electronic money institution (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, Merco) may not hold Panamanian deposits, maintain a local settlement account, or act as the primary bank of a Panamanian S.A. without a full banking or fintech license issued by the SBP. A practical derived-rail architecture is permissible: the consulting company may use an offshore EMI exclusively to collect EUR/USD card and wire fees from FDA/CE clients, provided funds are auto-swept on a strict daily basis into the Panamanian operational account via MT103; the EMI will not appear as the corporate bank of record for statutory or tax registry purposes. Panamanian payroll, vendor payments, and tax obligations must execute through a tier-1 local bank account.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

For the Treasury Blueprint, initial shareholder funding flows through SWIFT MT103 from the UBO's home account to the Panamanian operating account. Panama imposes no exchange-control filings, so no official purpose-code certification is required by the State; however, the receiving bank's compliance system demands a precise economic reference in Field 70, in particular 'Capital Contribution – Share Subscription' or 'Shareholder Loan Disbursement' if the injection is a bilateral loan. Large subscriptions above USD 250,000 may trigger a request for the shareholders register, a board resolution, the UAF beneficial-ownership certificate, and a 12-month source-of-funds statement. When the injection originates from a New York correspondent bank (e.g., Citi, JPMorgan, Bank of America), the USD credit posts on the same day; EUR capital injections are converted under a one-leg spot at the receiving bank's rate and booked in the US-dollar statement. For small working-capital top-ups, the entity may also receive funds via card acquire rails (Stripe or PayPal) provided those receipts are supported by genuine client invoices for regulatory advisory services.Because the company is a professional consulting firm (ISIC 7490, NAICS 541690) with no physical goods movement, the primary outbound rail is the standard corporate SWIFT MT103 for vendor payments (subcontracted testing laboratories, translation/localization vendors, EU regulatory consultants, software subscriptions, payroll, tax, and retention of legal counsel). Commercial Letters of Credit are structurally unnecessary when no title documents or transit goods exist; a standby Letter of Credit or bank guarantee may be issued by Banco General or Banistmo only where a European client's procurement protocol requires a repayment guarantee, though this remains an exceptional instrument rather than a standing rail. Panamanian payroll, vendor payments, and tax obligations must execute through a tier-1 local bank account.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25.00% on taxable net income derived from Panama-source activities (territorial tax system).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final withholding on dividends distributed from both taxed profits and non-taxed profits (exempt or foreign-source). The paying corporation may elect to absorb the 10% tax, in which case the dividend is treated as franked and no further shareholder withholding applies.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within the first three calendar months following the close of the fiscal year-end, i.e., by 31 March for calendar-year taxpayers; no automatic extension is available beyond the statutory period.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/.4,000,000 (four million Panamanian Balboas, at par with the US dollar) of annual gross revenue, or aggregate related-party transactions exceeding B/.4,000,000 in a fiscal year, triggers the mandatory preparation of Master File and Local File together with the annual informative related-party return under Law 50 of 2011 and Executive Decree No. 310 of 2012.

Economic substance scope: Panama applies substance-over-form and general anti-avoidance rules (GAAR). A corporation is treated as a Panama tax resident when management and control are exercised from Panama under Article 860 of the Fiscal Code, as reinforced by Law 66 of 2014. For SEM and free-zone beneficiaries, Economic Substance Regulations require physical premises, local staff, and substantive revenue-generating activity in Panama. Treaty benefits, dividend relief, and territorial exclusions are denied to shell entities lacking economic substance.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud is punishable by imprisonment of 2 to 6 years, aggravated to 5 to 10 years when committed through fictitious companies or forged documents; civil penalty of 100% to 300% of the evaded tax; default interest; surcharges; and forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Legal representatives and directors who sign the sworn return expose personal criminal liability; courts authorize piercing the corporate veil, freezing personal bank accounts, imposing travel bans, and extradition; unpaid taxes attach personally where the corporation is liquidated without settling DGI claims.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization as a Panama tax resident under the control-and-management test of Article 860 of the Fiscal Code; retroactive assessments with penalties of 100% to 300%; denial of treaty and territorial benefits; fines for failure to maintain books; and cancellation of the corporate registration at the Registro Publico de Panama.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors are jointly and severally liable for unpaid income tax and penalties; criminal responsibility arises when signing declarations of non-activity in bad faith; individuals may be disqualified from serving as directors of Panamanian entities.
Money Laundering / AML Violation

Statutory sanction: Money laundering is punishable by imprisonment of 5 to 12 years and fines equal to twice the value of the property involved; administrative penalties include suspension of the commercial license and forced dissolution of the entity.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and resident agents involved face personal criminal prosecution, asset freezes, passport seizure, travel bans, and extradition; the corporate veil is disregarded in predicate-offense proceedings.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and False Documentation

Statutory sanction: The DGI may re-determine the arm's-length result with a secondary adjustment and impose penalties of 30% to 50% of the recomputed tax difference; additional fines apply for non-filing of the transfer pricing annex; the taxpayer is published in the official registry of tax delinquents.

Director personal liability: Directors attesting false transfer pricing documentation are personally liable for fraudulent misstatement; the foreign parent may face deemed dividend treatment; travel bans and asset embargos are imposed pending clearance of substantial assessments.
