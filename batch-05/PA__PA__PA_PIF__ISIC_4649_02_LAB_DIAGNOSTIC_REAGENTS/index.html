Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Virtual or administrative-only addresses are not acceptable as the sole business premises for this industry. Every Private Interest Foundation must maintain a registered agent (agente residente) with a physical office in Panama, and the registered office is the agent's physical address for statutory correspondence. For the wholesale distribution of clinical diagnostic ELISA assays and reagents (ISIC 4649), MINSA (Dirección General de Salud) and the National Customs Authority (ANA) require a physical, customs-domiciled warehouse and cold-chain storage evidence; a virtual office cannot satisfy sanitary registration, import permits or substance review.

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá — Special Registry of Private Interest Foundations (Sección/Archivo Especial de Fundaciones de Interés Privado), Dirección General del Registro Público.

Governing statute

Law No. 25 of 12 June 1995 on Private Interest Foundations (Ley No. 25 de 12 de junio de 1995), Articles 1 through 24, as amended; supplemented by the Registry Regulations of the Registro Público de Panamá and, for the commercial object, the sanitary authorization regime administered by the Ministerio de Salud (MINSA) applicable to wholesale distribution of medical devices and in vitro diagnostic reagents.

Formation / constitutional filings

Foundation Charter (Carta Fundacional / Acta de Fundación) executed before a Notary Public of the Republic of Panama and inscribed in the Special Registry of Private Interest Foundations.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 15 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

The initial endowment (patrimonio fundacional) must be at least US$10,000.00 (B/.10,000.00) per Article 4 of Law No. 25 of 1995, declared in the Foundation Charter and contributed in cash, securities, or other lawful assets. No mandatory escrow account is required by statute; however, the Resident Agent must verify the effective contribution, record the disposition of the endowment, and maintain the foundation's internal asset registry in order to evidence compliance in any bank or regulatory procedure.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate (Certificado de Prevención de Incendios) issued by the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá, renewable annually, covering type ABC dry chemical extinguishers, hydrant inspection, emergency exits, and evacuation signage; structural load inspection certificate from the Municipalidad de Panamá confirming floor-loading capacity of warehouse shelving and cold-storage units; occupational health and safety workplace clearance administered by the Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral (MITRADEL), including the approved 'Programa de Higiene y Seguridad Industrial,' GHS chemical hazard labeling, material safety data sheets, spill-response kits and training, and temperature-controlled storage certification; notification to SINAPROC is required only if aggregate reagent quantities exceed the minor threshold for dangerous goods.

Paid-in capital rule: The initial endowment (patrimonio fundacional) must be at least US$10,000.00 (B/.10,000.00) per Article 4 of Law No. 25 of 1995, declared in the Foundation Charter and contributed in cash, securities, or other lawful assets. No mandatory escrow account is required by statute; however, the Resident Agent must verify the effective contribution, record the disposition of the endowment, and maintain the foundation's internal asset registry in order to evidence compliance in any bank or regulatory procedure.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); tax administration portal: Sede Electrónica DGI/MEF (https://sede.mef.gob.pa) for registrations, returns, and e-certificates.

E-government filing portal

1) Execute the foundation deed and register the Private Interest Foundation at the Registro Público de Panamá; 2) Maintain a Panamanian resident agent and registered address; 3) Submit the RUC application at DGI (Form 987 or online through Sede Electrónica) with the foundation deed, public registration certificate, identity documents of the foundation council/resident agent, and appointment of the local certified public accountant; 4) Obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación from the relevant municipal treasury for any business premises; 5) DGI issues the RUC certificate. If the PIF performs no taxable Panamanian supply, it is not required to register for ITBMS.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope; Entity_Level_Taxation on Panamanian-source income; Private Interest Foundations are not pass-through for Panamanian tax purposes and are not automatically tax-exempt-zone entities. — 1) Execute the foundation deed and register the Private Interest Foundation at the Registro Público de Panamá; 2) Maintain a Panamanian resident agent and registered address; 3) Submit the RUC application at DGI (Form 987 or online through Sede Electrónica) with the foundation deed, public registration certificate, identity documents of the foundation council/resident agent, and appointment of the local certified public accountant; 4) Obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación from the relevant municipal treasury for any business premises; 5) DGI issues the RUC certificate. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope; Entity_Level_Taxation on Panamanian-source income; Private Interest Foundations are not pass-through for Panamanian tax purposes and are not automatically tax-exempt-zone entities.

Available election forms

Under Law 25 of 1995, the foundation deed may configure objects and beneficiary rights; Panama does not operate a US-style check-the-box election. A PIF confined to foreign-source assets is taxed on territorial income only. If the PIF elects to carry on local commercial activities permitted by the deed, it becomes an entity-level taxpayer for Panamanian-source income and ITBMS. No hybrid mismatch or entity classification election is available under domestic Panamanian law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Balboa / US Dollar (B/.): no general turnover threshold for corporations or legal persons. Any person making habitual taxable transfers of movable goods or rendering services in Panama is subject to ITBMS registration. Importers are always taxable persons. Occasional natural-person suppliers may be treated as not required if below DGI-listed low-turnover parameters and if not engaged in commerce, subject to DGI facts-and-circumstances application. The PIF in this dossier has no Panamanian taxable supplies, so ITBMS registration is not mandatory; registration becomes required when it sells reagent inventory in Panama or exports from Panama.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods sold and physically exported from Panama to a foreign destination are zero-rated under ITBMS. Offshore services performed wholly outside Panama and supplied for the direct use of non-resident clients are also zero-rated. For diagnostic reagents, the shipment must be internationally transported, and the exporter must retain the documentary trail evidencing physical departure from Panamanian customs territory. Exports are excluded from the 7% ITBMS; input VAT on purchases related to exports is creditable/refundable to the exporter.

  • Aduanas Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) with customs clearance stamp / SICE number
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill / multimodal transport document
  • Commercial invoice and packing list expressly identifying the IVD reagents, quantity, and foreign consignee
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank SWIFT credit advice evidencing collection of export proceeds within the 180-day substantiation window

Panama operates DGI-managed electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica). Mandatory segments are phased by taxpayer category; once obliged, invoices must be transmitted to the DGI Sede Electrónica via the approved XML web-service/API flow, with an X.509 digital signature and QR code or DGI validation stamp before issuance to the buyer. Non-cleared invoices are not tax-effective for the buyer. Export invoices must also be filed in the electronic system where the exporter is in the mandatory regime. Smaller taxpayers may use paper pre-printed authorized forms during the phase-in; transition dates are set by DGI resolution.

DGI and Aduanas jointly audit export zero-rating claims. Unsupported, false, or fictitious exports are reclassified as taxable domestic supplies; ITBMS at 7% is assessed on the invoice price, plus late payment surcharge, monthly interest, and penalties of up to 50% of the omitted tax under the Código Fiscal. Repeated or intentional abuse may result in criminal tax evasion charges under the Panamanian Criminal Code (2–5 years imprisonment), suspension of the exporter’s RUC, and exclusion from the e-invoicing/authorized exporter regime. Clawback includes denial of input VAT credit and recovery of refunds with statutory interest.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility exists on both the current and capital accounts with no exchange controls, prior approvals, or repatriation permits. The operational currency of the banking system is the U.S. dollar; capital injections by non-resident founders and profit remittances by a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) may be freely wired through licensed banks, subject only to bank-level AML/KYC and sanctions screening. Panama applies a territorial tax model, so foreign-source wholesale trading income is generally outside the Panamanian tax net; dividends remitted to non-residents from Panamanian-source profits carry a withholding tax (5% when the corporate profits have been taxed, 10% when untaxed), but no FX clearance or central-bank authorization is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panamanian law imposes no statutory surrender, mandatory conversion, or repatriation timeline for export proceeds. The PIF may freely retain U.S. dollars or other foreign currency earned from IVD reagent sales and apply such proceeds to operating expenses, open-account payables, or offshore reinvestment. The only binding documentary requirements are the customs export declaration through the SIGA/VUPEA platform, commercial invoices, shipping documents, and MINSA sanitary export/import authorizations for regulated clinical diagnostic products.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP). The Republic of Panama has no currency-issuing central bank or foreign-exchange administration; the U.S. dollar has been legal tender since the 1904 Monetary Agreement and the Balboa (PAB) exists only as a coin unit of account at par. The SBP exclusively licenses and supervises banks, finance companies and trust companies, while the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF, Financial Analysis Unit) exercises AML/CFT supervision and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) is responsible for fiscal policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low as a primary banking rail and Medium-High as an ancillary liquidity channel. Panama does not operate an e-money institution (EMI) licensing regime equivalent to the UK/EEA; non-bank payment providers must either partner with a licensed Panamanian bank or operate under the SBP fintech regulatory innovation framework. A Panamanian PIF can usually open a USD/EUR business account with an offshore U.S.- or EU-authorized EMI under remote KYC, but such institutions typically classify Panamanian private-interest foundations as higher-risk, impose lower transaction limits, and cannot clear local payroll, tax, or Ministry of Health-related payments. A licensed onshore Panamanian bank account therefore remains mandatory, with the EMI used selectively to accelerate cross-border vendor payments.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound USD equities from non-resident founders settle via SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers to the PIF operational account at an SBP-licensed bank. Because Panama imposes no exchange-control filing, the remittance narrative must satisfy bank-level compliance: include the full PIF legal name, RUC, Foundation Deed reference, and an explicit purpose description such as CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – WHOLESALE DISTRIBUTION OF IVD DIAGNOSTIC REAGENTS, or the ISO 20022 structured purpose code EQUI/FDI where supported. The supporting packet (Foundation Council resolution, apostilled deed, source-of-wealth evidence, pro-forma contracts) should be delivered to compliance within five business days of the wire; amounts above USD 1 million trigger enhanced due diligence.Supplier settlements and buyer collections are cleared through (i) SWIFT MT103 customer transfers for open-account trade, (ii) documentary collections (CAD) for repeat low-to-medium-value orders, and (iii) commercial letters of credit (LC) confirmed or advised by the Panamanian house bank for tender-driven public-health procurement. LC presentations must include the commercial invoice, certificate of origin, MINSA sanitary certificate, packing list, and transport documents. For public tenders requiring bid security, standby LCs and bank guarantees (garantías) issued in USD are standard. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

Progressive: 15% on taxable income up to USD 200,000; 25% on taxable income exceeding USD 200,000

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding tax on dividends paid out of Panamanian-source profits that have already been subject to corporate income tax; the rate is 20% if profits are distributed from income that never went through the normal ISR recognition (e.g., payments from a separately liquidated 'anticipo' under the dividend advance regime). In practice, for a PIF distributing fully taxed Panamanian-source profits, the standard dividend WHT is 10%.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 6,000,000 annual gross Panamanian-source revenue (or intercompany transactions above USD 1,000,000). Taxpayers exceeding this threshold must prepare a Local File and a Master File in accordance with OECD-aligned Panamanian transfer pricing rules (Decree 26 of 2011, as amended). Country-by-Country Report (CbCR) is required for Panamanian parent groups with consolidated revenue above USD 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Applicable. Panama's Economic Substance Requirements (ESR) pursuant to Law 70 of 2019 (and its implementing Resolutions) apply to entities engaged in 'relevant activities', which include wholesale distribution. The entity must demonstrate substantive economic presence in Panama: a physical office, qualified staff, and actual decision-making management conducted locally. A PIF or corporation distributing diagnostic reagents in Panama must maintain personnel, expenses, and records that correlate with the income-generating activity. A shell PIF with no local substance and passive flow of profits risks administrative fines, cancellation, and reclassification of income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax evasion carries a term of imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and a fine equal to two to ten times the tax evaded (Código Fiscal, Article 327-C, as incorporated by Law 94 of 2019). Civil penalties include late interest at 1% per month and full back-tax collection plus a 10% surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors can be held personally liable for civil restitution if they approved, signed, or caused the false return. In serious evasion cases, criminal proceedings may result in travel bans and preventive detention. For repeat or large-scale evasion exceeding USD 300,000, the criminal term can be extended to 5–8 years, and directors who were participating in the evasion may face a personal freeze of bank accounts.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the ESR regimes, failure to satisfy substance requirements can lead to penalties of USD 250,000 per year for a relevant activity; for a PIF used as a shell without any legitimate activity, the DGI may recharacterize all revenue as Panamanian-source and apply the full 25% tax, plus penalties and interest. In addition, the Tax Authority and the Superintendent of Companies may issue a suspension or cancellation of the corporate license.

Director personal liability: Piercing the corporate veil is allowed where the PIF/corporation is a sham or used to evade taxes or launder assets. Directors and foundation council members can be made personally liable for unpaid taxes and civil damages, especially if they knowingly disguised the true beneficial owner. Criminal AML charges can trigger a travel ban and a provisional arrest warrant during the investigation.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015 and Law 52 of 2016, fines range from USD 10,000 to USD 5,000,000 for a serious AML violation; can also include revocation of the representative agent's license and liquidation of the PIF. Structuring transactions may be criminalized with imprisonment of 2 to 5 years on top of the fine.

Director personal liability: Directors and foundation council members who knowingly participate in AML violations face criminal prosecution, including imprisonment, personal fines, and in rem confiscation of the proceeds and the reagent inventory. The court may issue a travel ban to secure attendance; in cases involving organized crime, the director can be held jointly and severally liable for the total financial penalty imposed on the entity.
