Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá – Sección Mercantil (Public Registry of Panama, Mercantile Section)

Governing statute

Ley 32 de 1927 (General Law on Corporations / Ley General de Sociedades Anónimas), as amended from time to time, together with applicable provisions of the Código Comercial and complementary executive regulations governing formation, share capital, administration, and registration of Sociedades Anónimas.

Formation / constitutional filings

Petición de Inscripción de Sociedad Anónima (Registration Petition) accompanied by the testimonio (memorial) of the Escritura Pública de Constitución, incorporating the Pacto Social y Estatutos. No official gazette publication is required for validity.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 13 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The corporation must issue at least one subscribed share of nominal value, and authorized capital is customarily fixed at USD 10,000. No escrow account, capital deposit, or capitalization bench test is required by Panamanian law.

Funding & unlock path: The office premises must obtain a Fire Safety Inspection Certificate from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá and pass the municipal commercial occupancy review required for the Aviso de Operación; the employer must implement an occupational health and safety program under Ley 8 de 2014 (OSH Law) and register it with MITRADEL; structural load certification is required only where the municipality conditions the occupancy permit on such inspection for the selected office.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutory minimum paid-up capital. The corporation must issue at least one subscribed share of nominal value, and authorized capital is customarily fixed at USD 10,000. No escrow account, capital deposit, or capitalization bench test is required by Panamanian law.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) — Sede Electrónica official tax portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa (also integrated with the Panama Digital e-government portal, https://www.panamadigital.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

For a foreign-owned S.A.: (1) notarize the incorporation deed and inscribe it in the Registro Público de Panamá, obtaining the Certificado de Personería Jurídica; (2) appoint a resident legal representative/agent (agente residente) with recognized corporate documents and powers; (3) obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación in the taxpayer's operating district; (4) register with the DGI for the Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) before commencing taxable activities (or within 30 days of commencement), filing the official RUC application together with the legal-entity documents, tax identification of officers, local business address, and the resident agent's acceptance; (5) receive the RUC certificate bearing the verification digit and the ITBMS activity classification (ISIC 7020 management and advisory services); (6) activate the digital tax mailbox (Buzón Electrónico) and obtain the Firma Electrónica for the Sede Electrónica to file returns and issue approved e-invoices; (7) for export/zero-rating, complete the DGI exporter registration and, where applicable, the customs exporter code in the SIA (Sistema Informático Aduanero).

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election. The default is entity-level taxation on Panama-source income only, under the territorial rule. An S.A. may elect (a) the general corporate income tax regime of the Código Fiscal; (b) the simplified small-taxpayer regime (Ley 68 de 2018) if it satisfies the statutory low-turnover and business-pattern tests (such regimes are generally designed for natural persons and micro-operations, so S.A. eligibility is limited); or (c) if it obtains a license under Ley 41 de 2007, the Empresa de Servicios Internacionales (ESI) regime, under which qualified international-services income is excluded from ISR and ITBMS is zero-rated with special VAT-cost rules.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis monetary threshold: any person, entity, or permanent establishment performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for the RUC/ITBMS before commencing operations. A simplified small-taxpayer regime (Ley 68 de 2018) applies to qualifying businesses with annual gross receipts up to PAB 50,000, but it does not relieve the registered S.A. from ITBMS registration, filing, or invoicing obligations.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% ITBMS) applies to exports of goods and to services rendered to non-resident clients for use or economic benefit wholly outside Panama, consistent with the territorial tax principle. For the ESG carbon-accounting, audit and offset-brokerage scope, the service contract and work papers must demonstrate that the client is not a Panamanian taxable person, the deliverables are not consumed or used in Panama, and the services do not relate to Panamanian real property or onshore taxable activities. The exporter must retain the signed contract, invoices, service-rendition records, and foreign-currency remittance documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) with the customs export permit and, for goods, the outbound transport documents (Bill of Lading / House and Master Air Waybill)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export from Panama
  • Commercial contract and final invoice for the export transaction
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa) or foreign bank credit advice as evidence of receipt of the export payment in Panama
  • Service recipient declaration/attestation confirming non-resident status and offshore use of the ESG/carbon-advisory deliverables

Mandatory electronic invoicing is phased in under the Facturación Electrónica de Panamá (FEP) program. The S.A. must issue invoices through DGI-authorized software or an authorized service provider (PSA), transmit the XML invoice to the DGI in real time for fiscal clearance, obtain the digital fiscal stamp (firma electrónica/sello) before delivery of the invoice, and archive the authorized files. Paper invoices require DGI-approved fiscal printers with 'precinto fiscal' only for approved contingencies; all e-invoices must be reported by the next business day through the Sede Electrónica or PSA API.

The DGI audits zero-rating claims by verifying the customs/contract documentation, the offshore-benefit substance, and the foreign-currency remittance within the 180-day evidentiary window. Failure to sustain the export classification recharacterizes the supply as a taxable Panamanian transaction, triggering ITBMS at 7% plus 1% monthly interest, surcharges up to 100% of the omitted tax, and administrative fines under the Código Fiscal (as amended by the 2019–2021 tax-reform legislation); fraudulent zero-rating claims additionally expose the S.A., its officers and resident agent to criminal tax-fraud prosecution and imprisonment. The DGI may claw back credited refunds/offsets with retroactive interest during the statutory assessment period (generally three years from the filing date, five years for non-filing/omissions, and longer in cases of fraud).

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full dollarization: USD is unlimited legal tender; the Panamanian balboa (PAB) circulates only as metal coinage at a 1:1 fixed parity. Both the capital and current accounts are freely convertible — capital injections, service-fee repatriation, and profit remittances face no exchange approval, no quantitative limit, and no surrender obligation. The only relevant hurdle is fiscal: distributions of Panama-source profits are subject to dividend withholding (generally 10%), while foreign-source income lies outside the territorial income-tax base and remits free.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no repatriation escrow for export or service receipts. The ESG-audit entity may retain USD receivables in any licensed bank indefinitely; foreign-currency balances are unrestricted.

Central bank supervision: Panama operates without a formal central bank. Banking supervision is exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Legislative Decree 2 of 2008; the state-owned Banco Nacional de Panamá performs limited fiscal-agency functions, and the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) steers external credit policy. No institution issues local currency or administers exchange controls, given full dollarization.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low as a primary rail. Panama law admits fintech and virtual-asset service providers (Law 697 of 2023 and the SBP regulatory sandbox), but EU/UK-licensed EMIs hold no Panamanian banking license and may only act as payment-agent sub-rails. The Sociedad Anónima should maintain its principal treasury account at a locally licensed bank to satisfy RUC-linked due diligence, dividend-withholding mechanics, and SWIFT correspondent access.
