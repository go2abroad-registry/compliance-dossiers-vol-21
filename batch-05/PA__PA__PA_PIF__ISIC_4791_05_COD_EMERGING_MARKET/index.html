Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá (Public Registry of Panama) – División de Personas Jurídicas y Fundaciones de Interés Privado.

Governing statute

Law No. 25 of 12 July 1995 on Private Interest Foundations (Ley Nº 25 de 12 de julio de 1995 sobre Fundaciones de Interés Privado), Articles 1–35, as supplemented by the general regulations of the Public Registry of Panama.

Formation / constitutional filings

Foundation Charter (Escritura Pública Constitutiva de Fundación de Interés Privado), submitted for inscription in the Public Registry together with the Foundation Council's organizational minutes.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 36 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

No statutory minimum paid-up capital is prescribed by Law No. 25 of 1995; standard banking practice requires a declared initial endowment of at least USD 10,000 in the Foundation Charter. No statutory escrow or blocked deposit account is mandated; the endowment must be fully subscribed but may remain under the Foundation's free control.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certificate issued by the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá after inspection of the warehouse or pick/pack premises; structural load certificate signed by a registered Panamanian engineer for racking systems; MITRADEL occupational hygiene and safety inspection for workplaces with employees; municipal certificate confirming the permitted commercial use of the premises.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama City; online portal: Sede Electrónica Fiscal at https://sede.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must first be inscribed in the Public Registry of Panama and maintain a resident agent. To obtain the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente), the foundation or its resident fiduciary representative submits to the DGI a sworn application, the registered foundation charter and bylaws, proof of the resident agent appointment, confirmation of the local address, and a declaration of the economic activities actually carried out. Where the PIF is exclusively an asset-holding or family-patrimony vehicle, no Panamanian-source income is declared; however, any taxable activity in Panama, including mail-order retail or e-commerce logistics, requires registration as an ITBMS responsible party before commencing operations. Non-resident PIFs generally use a local authorized representative with notarized power of attorney for RUC procurement and ongoing compliance.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope (only Panamanian-source income is taxed; foreign-source income is exempt from Panamanian corporate income tax; the PIF itself does not have a separate per-se taxable classification and may remain non-commercial if its charter restricts activities to estate preservation and family patrimony) — A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must first be inscribed in the Public Registry of Panama and maintain a resident agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (only Panamanian-source income is taxed; foreign-source income is exempt from Panamanian corporate income tax; the PIF itself does not have a separate per-se taxable classification and may remain non-commercial if its charter restricts activities to estate preservation and family patrimony)

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box election equivalent to the U.S. regime. A PIF may, through its foundation charter, elect a patrimony-only object, which reinforces its non-commercial, territorial-exempt status. No domestic hybrid mismatch election exists; the DGI applies substance-over-form and adjustment rules in accordance with the Panamanian Tax Code, CFC attribution rules, and applicable international information-exchange agreements.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis registration threshold applies to ITBMS; every person habitually transferring taxable movable goods or providing taxable services in Panama must obtain a RUC before commencing operations. A simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' regime may apply to small taxpayers, but it does not remove the obligation to register and report ITBMS where taxable commercial activities occur.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

ITBMS at 0% applies to exports of movable goods when title and risk pass to a non-resident buyer and the goods physically depart Panama, evidenced by customs documentation. Services may be zero-rated when supplied to non-residents and effectively used, consumed, or exploited outside Panama. The zero-rating claim must be substantiated by an unbroken documentation chain proving physical export, foreign-payment receipt, and the non-resident status of the counterparty. Because Panama is a dollarized economy with no exchange-control regime, there is no statutory requirement or deadline to repatriate export proceeds into a particular Panamanian bank.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) accepted and stamped by the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA)
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical departure of goods from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank statement evidencing receipt of export settlement funds in USD

The DGI operates a mandatory fiscal invoicing regime through the Sede Electrónica Fiscal. Taxpayers enrolled as ITBMS responsible parties must issue invoices in electronic XML form or through DGI-authorized fiscal printer systems, transmit invoice data to the DGI through public web-service APIs, and obtain a digital reception acknowledgement and QR stamp prior to delivery. Invoice records must include the authorization folio, issuer RUC, rates, and cancellation status; real-time clearance obligations are progressively phased in by revenue band.

DGI and ANA jointly cross-match export declarations, cargo manifests, freight bills, and banking records to detect fictitious zero-rated exports. A false export claim triggers immediate recharacterization of the sale as a taxable domestic supply, assessment of unpaid ITBMS at 7%, monthly interest for late payment, a 50% fraud surcharge under Article 717 of the Panamanian Tax Code, fines up to B/. 50,000, cancellation of fiscal authorization to issue invoices, and criminal prosecution for aggravated tax fraud which may result in imprisonment. The statute of limitations for retrospective clawback is generally six fiscal years from the date the tax return became due.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The U.S. dollar (USD) is legal tender and circulates freely alongside the Panamanian balboa (PAB), which exists only as a coin. Capital and current account transactions are fully convertible with no surrender, approval, or import/export permit requirements for foreign exchange. Profit remittances to non-residents are unrestricted; however, income generated within Panamanian territory remains subject to territorial taxation and the applicable 10% dividend tax unless the payment is a return of invested capital or arises from a foreign source. No capital controls, restrictions on bank account funding, or withholding on principal repatriation exist.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion ratio applies in Panama. Export receipts may be retained in USD or any foreign currency in offshore or domestic accounts without time-bound repatriation requirements. Panamanian customs and tax regulations require proper invoicing and BOI (Single Window) filing for statistical and tax purposes, but do not impose compulsory conversion or forced crediting to local bank accounts. For scope ISIC 4791 (mail-order retail with COD settlement), cash-on-delivery logistics remittances are treated as ordinary trade receivables and may be settled directly to the foundation's account without currency conversion.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) acts as the primary banking and financial regulatory authority, while Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as the State's fiscal agent. Panama has no formal central bank; the effective monetary authority is the U.S. Federal Reserve due to full dollarization, and no dedicated foreign exchange control commission exists.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium. Panama does not license digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) as standalone 'bank-like' entities under a specific EMI statute; fintech services are governed by the General Banking Law (Decree Law 9 of 1998) and the Communications and Digital Technology Law (Law 31 of 2021). Airwallex, as a foreign licensed EMI, cannot operate a booking center in Panama without obtaining a SBP fintech authorization or partnering with a local bank. However, Airwallex-type rails may be used for downstream disbursement to logistics counterparties without establishing a local presence, provided funds flow through a Panamanian licensee or a correspondent settlement account. Direct onboarding of a Panamanian foundation as an Airwallex client is permissible when UBO identification and foundation documentation satisfy Airwallex's global compliance, but it does not replace a domestic Panamanian bank account for escrow or tax-sensitive funds.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Private Interest Foundation (PIF) legal form with opaque protection of settlor and beneficiaries.

Root cause: Private Interest Foundation (PIF) legal form with opaque protection of settlor and beneficiaries.

Defense evidence: Original Foundation Charter and Articles of Incorporation stamped by the Public Registry; certified copy of the Foundation's founder and beneficiary schedule (where disclosed); sworn declaration under penalty of perjury from the registered agent identifying the settlor, foundation council members, and protector (if any); supporting document of economic rationale (RUC or NIT tax ID, e-commerce licenses of logistics operators, source of funds letters). For enhanced due diligence, provide a Cayman Islands-style compliance certificate from the non-resident foundation council, plus a written attestation that the foundation is not used as a nominee shell.
Cash-on-delivery (COD) trade flows with unknown local logistics operators and high volume of repeated small-value remittances.

Root cause: Cash-on-delivery (COD) trade flows with unknown local logistics operators and high volume of repeated small-value remittances.

Defense evidence: Fully executed logistics agency agreement; ISO 27001 or equivalent certification of the e-commerce platform; historical turnover projections with reconcilable transaction reports; list of 10 largest recipient logistics entities, each with valid RUC / national identity; anti-fraud compliance manual adopting the WEP (Watersoft, E-commerce and Payments) 2021 guidance; attestation from the payment processor (Airwallex or local enabling partner) showing that all COD collections are linked to a confirmed order and not merely person-to-person transfers.
Use of bearer-style documentary rights or foreign foundation council members without a Panama supervisory nexus.

Root cause: Use of bearer-style documentary rights or foreign foundation council members without a Panama supervisory nexus.

Defense evidence: Preventive measures under Law 23 of 2015 and SBP Agreement 1-2022: provide the constitution of the foundation council naming at least three members, their passport copies, police clearance from their residence countries, and a commitment clause subjecting the foundation and council to SBP's inspection powers; bank-specific form (F-100 or equivalent) confirming that the foundation's legal address matches the registered agent and that the registered agent is licensed by the Attorney General's Office. If a protector has reserved powers, attach the protector's declaration of non-interference in banking matters and proof of the bank's consent waiver.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections for a Panamanian foundation should be routed through a clearing bank that is a member of the Panama Interbank Electronic Clearing House (ACH Panama) and the SWIFT network. The instruction must contain the foundation's account number, foundation full legal name, and a purpose code aligned with the Panamanian banking directive for capital contributions. For USD injections, use SWIFT MT103 with field 72 coded as '/REC/ Capital Contribution - Purchase of Foundation Assets' or '/INV/ Operating Funds Advanced by Founder' to prevent misinterpretation as taxable income. If the originating bank country is a FATF gray-listed jurisdiction, add the beneficiary's RUC (Régimen Único de Contribuyente) and the Panamanian registered agent's reference number. Verification step requires the receiving bank to issue an 'Acreditación de Fondos Externos' letter that includes the source of funds declaration, which must be retained for five years under the Joint Provision 1-2015.Outbound disbursements for mail-order retail trade should be made using (1) SWIFT MT103 documentary payment for high-value logistics intermediaries, (2) commercial letters of credit (documentary credit under UCP 600) for purchase of merchandise with Incoterms 2020 (FCA or CIP) and port-city settlement, (3) ACH Panama wire or local transfer for same-day disbursement to registered logistics couriers; and (4) corporate procurement cards linked to a segregated collection account for COD controllers. Each MT103 must include an invoice reference and the phrase 'Trade Settlement - ISIC 4791' to avoid bank rejection due to ambiguous purpose. When the recipient is a local courier collecting cash on delivery, use the 'REMESA DETALLE' flag and require final account reconciliation, but do not send cash via wire; instead, instruct the carrier to deposit the COD proceeds into the foundation's account via a daily batch file transmitted by the logistics operator's bank. The clearing rail must maintain a four-hour T+0 gross settlement for local USD transfers; no T+1 conversion is applied.No Dominican-peso or central-bank NDF is required because Panama is fully dollarized; however, a standard USD/EUR or USD/CNH deliverable forward contract is permissible through SBP-regulated banking entities, with no requirement to obtain Panamanian Foreign Exchange Administration approval.Cross-currency interest rate swaps (CCS) with a licensed Panamanian bank or an international counterparty are permissible for hedging working capital credit obligations, provided the transaction is documented under ISDA and the bank assesses the foundation's non-investment-grade credit exposure.Currency options (call/put USD against the relevant emerging-market currency) are permissible for hedging purchase-price risk on COD inventory, subject to the bank's internal product suitability rules for a special-purpose foundation; no local mandatory clearing through an exchange exists.A multilateral spot contract denominated in Panamanian balboa or USD is not a separate hedging instrument; the foundation may use USD/USD synthetic forward transactions to manage interest-rate risk, but for the COD e-commerce model the most pragmatic structure is a 'firm-fix' FX agreement with the payment processor (Airwallex or similar) fixing the local currency collection rate for the 10-day COD clearing cycle.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (Corporation Income Tax, ISR – Impuesto sobre la Renta)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% statutory withholding on dividends distributed from Panamanian taxable profits, applied at the time of distribution to both residents and non-residents, unless the distributing company is in a regime with specific exemption. Dividends sourced from foreign-source income are outside the territorial system and generally not subject to Panamanian WHT.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For fiscal years ending 31 December, the return must be filed between 1 March and 31 March of the following year, with the precise final date determined by the RUC/NIT last digit. For other fiscal year-ends, the return is due no later than the last day of the third month following the fiscal year-end. Quarterly advance instalments are required for taxpayers with previous-year taxable income above the threshold.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: PAB 1,000,000 (equivalent to USD 1,000,000) aggregate annual value of related-party transactions, per DGI transfer pricing regulations; lower de minimis thresholds may apply to specific transaction types.

Economic substance scope: Panama Law 52 of 2019 (as amended) introduces Economic Substance Regulations for entities carrying on 'relevant activities' – banking, financing, distribution, shipping, and IP-holding – within Panama. PIFs passively holding assets or carrying out cash-on-delivery e-commerce intermediation must maintain adequate staff, premises, and local strategic management if they claim territorial benefits or treaty access. Annual ESR reporting is mandatory through the DGI online portal; failure to demonstrate substance can lead to administrative sanctions up to PAB 2,000,000 and is grounds for exchanging information under international tax transparency standards.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Filing

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud penalties: fine of up to 10 times the evaded tax, plus statutory interest, and imprisonment of 3-5 years. Administrative penalty: up to USD 50,000 for fraudulent misrepresentation. DGI may re-audit open years and assess tax deficiency with a 20% surcharge.

Director personal liability: Directors, foundation council members, and fiduciary agents are jointly and severally liable for fraud, and may face civil restitution, attachment of personal assets, travel bans, and imprisonment in addition to corporate sanctions.
Shell Company / Substance Deficiency

Statutory sanction: Under Law 52 of 2019, penalties range from USD 50,000 to USD 2,000,000 for substance violations; failure to file ESR returns attracts daily fines and the entity may be struck off the registry. In DTA abuse cases, the competent authority can deny treaty benefits and re-characterise all income as Panamanian-source, resulting in 25% CIT and 12.5% WHT penalties.

Director personal liability: Council members who knowingly approve baseless substance declarations can be fined personally up to USD 500,000 and banned from acting as a director or registered agent in Panama for up to five years.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Severe administrative fines up to USD 5,000,000 for institutions, and individual fines up to USD 1,000,000; criminal money-laundering conviction carries imprisonment up to 12 years and forfeiture of assets.

Director personal liability: Foundation council members and officers face criminal prosecution for AML violations, including imprisonment, civil forfeiture of personal assets, travel bans, and professional disqualification. The corporate veil may be pierced where the foundation is used as an instrument of fraud.
