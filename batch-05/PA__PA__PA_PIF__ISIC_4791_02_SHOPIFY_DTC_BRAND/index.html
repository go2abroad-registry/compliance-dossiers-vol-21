Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) — the sole national tax administration; official electronic portal: E-Tax 2.0 (https://etax.mef.gob.pa) and DGI institutional site (https://www.dgi.gob.pa). The DGI administers the RUC registry, ISR (income tax), ITBMS (VAT), and e-invoicing clearance.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the PIF under Law 25 of 1995 through a licensed Panamanian foundation agent and record the Pacto Fundacional and Reglamento with the Registro Público; the foundation agent acts as statutory resident agent and local tax representative. Step 2: After registry issuance, submit the RUC application (Formulario de Registro Único de Contribuyente) through E-Tax 2.0, attaching the certified foundation charter, the agent's appointment (Poder), the Council members' identification documents, and registered office proof; foreign officers may act by notarized/certified copies. Step 3: DGI validates and issues the RUC certificate (permanent TIN) within approximately 10–20 business days; if the PIF will conduct taxable operations in Panama, simultaneously register the ITBMS activity and, if annual revenue will not exceed PAB/USD 36,000, elect the small-taxpayer simplified regime in the same filing. Step 4: Obtain a digital signature certificate (Firma Electrónica) from the Autoridad de Innovación Gubernamental (AIG) to sign e-invoices and tax filings. Step 5: Register as an exporter in the SISCO/VUCEA customs single window (Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas and MICI) before exporting goods. A non-resident PIF without Panama-sourced activity still requires an RUC and must file annual sworn declarations claiming the territorial exemption.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope. The PIF is a distinct legal person (hybrid foundation/corporation) taxed only on Panama-source income (Código Fiscal Article 693; Law 25 of 1995): (i) Panama-sourced business income is subject to corporate ISR at 25% of net taxable profits; (ii) foreign-source income, including export sales of goods and services rendered and consumed abroad, is outside Panama's income tax net; (iii) assets, capital contributions, and distributions to beneficiaries are exempt from gift/estate tax, and no wealth tax applies. A PIF conducting exclusively offshore DTC e-commerce (no sales to Panama customers, no employees or fixed place of business in Panama) operates as a de facto Tax_Exempt_Zone; however, Law 254 of 2021 economic substance rules require the PIF to either substantiate income as foreign-source or maintain adequate substance in Panama for any relevant activity performed.

Available election forms

Panama domestic law does not provide US-style check-the-box or OECD hybrid-mismatch elections. Available statutory elections: (1) small-taxpayer regime election (Régimen de Pequeño Contribuyente) for gross turnover ≤ PAB/USD 36,000, substituting full ITBMS accounting with a 2% simplified tax on sales; (2) multinational headquarters (SEM) election under Law 41 of 2007 for qualifying intra-group services, granting ITBMS/import-duty neutrality and preferential ISR treatment on qualifying net income; (3) economic substance report election under Law 254 of 2021 to classify the PIF as either a non-operating foreign-source vehicle or a Panama-resident entity performing relevant activities; (4) irrevocable vs. revocable foundation designation in the charter to define founder powers (does not alter Panamanian territorial tax classification but affects foreign CFC/attribution analysis). No check-the-box or hybrid mismatch elections are available under Panama domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Full ITBMS registration: no de minimis threshold – any person habitually transferring movable goods or providing taxable services in Panama must register before commencing operations. Simplified small-taxpayer regime: annual gross turnover ≤ PAB/USD 36,000, taxed at 2% of gross sales (replaces 7% ITBMS) with quarterly filing. Non-resident digital/e-commerce suppliers: Panama asserts ITBMS nexus for supplies electronically delivered to or consumed in Panama with no statutory turnover threshold; registration and a local fiscal representative are required for B2C digital sales, while physical export sales to non-residents fall outside the Panamanian taxable base.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports qualify for 0% ITBMS under Law 8 of 2010. Cumulative criteria: (1) the PIF must be the seller of record (title and risk pass to the non-resident buyer per INCOTERM); (2) tangible goods must physically leave Panama within 30 days of invoicing, evidenced by customs dispatch; (3) the PIF must be registered as exporter in SISCO/VUCEA and the shipments declared through the customs export declaration process; (4) an e-invoice with zero-rated code ('exportación') must be cleared by DGI in real time; (5) services qualify only if rendered to a non-resident and consumed entirely abroad (no connection to Panamanian real estate or goods); (6) export proceeds must be credited to the PIF's Panamanian bank account. Shopify DTC sales to foreign consumers qualify if goods are posted/shipped from Panama to international addresses and the shipping documentation matches the e-invoice; sales delivered to Panamanian addresses are taxable at 7%.

  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) proving physical exit from Panama
  • Commercial invoice (the DGI-cleared e-invoice XML bearing CSFE code)
  • Packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (bank credit advice / Certificado de Abono) evidencing receipt of foreign proceeds

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) for all ITBMS-registered taxpayers, phased by DGI Resolution (large taxpayers from 2022; medium/small taxpayers from 2024). Issuance modes: (i) real-time online clearance through the DGI's SIFE platform (E-Tax 2.0) via web-service API, where each invoice XML is transmitted, validated, and stamped with the CSFE (Código de Seguridad de Facturación Electrónica); (ii) certified billing software (facturadores autorizados) integrating DGI's XSD schemas with OAuth-based authentication; (iii) e-ticket (tiquete) for point-of-sale retail. The PIF must configure its Shopify storefront/back-office to feed order data (customer, SKU, price, 0% export code) into the certified billing software, maintain sequential NOI (Número de Orden Interna) numbering, and reconcile each export parcel to a cleared e-invoice XML; failure to clear invoices in real time blocks issuance and attracts fines of PAB 300 to PAB 5,000 per invoice plus potential RUC suspension.

DGI and the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas conduct matched audits of ITBMS returns, e-invoice XMLs, and SISCO export declarations. Recapture: if the zero-rating is not substantiated, the sale is reclassified as a domestic taxable transfer, and DGI assesses 7% ITBMS plus 1% monthly moratory interest, a 25% surcharge, and fines of 50%–100% of the omitted tax. Incorrect or fraudulent export declarations (overstated shipments, fictitious exporters, false bills of lading, unrepatriated proceeds) expose the PIF, its Council, and its registered agent to tax fraud and customs smuggling penalties under the Código Fiscal and Panama's Criminal Code, including imprisonment, confiscation of goods, cancellation of the exporter registration/RUC, and retrospective clawback of refunds claimed for up to six prior years. Records must be retained for at least seven years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized economy: the U.S. dollar is legal tender and the balboa circulates only as a token coin at par. There is no exchange control law, no capital account approval regime, and no restriction on conversion, repatriation, or cross-border transfer of funds in USD or any other currency. Current account and capital account transactions enjoy unlimited convertibility. Profit remittance for Private Interest Foundations (PIFs) is unrestricted at the FX level; however, territorial taxation imposes a 5% dividend withholding tax on distributions paid out of Panama-source profits attributable to shares registered with the Central Securities Depository, rising to 10% for unregistered/bearer-type distributions. Foreign-source income of the PIF, including revenues from a DTC e-commerce brand serving customers outside Panama, falls outside Panamanian tax jurisdiction if properly documented, making remittances tax-neutral at source under the territorial regime.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama does not impose any export proceeds surrender obligation, mandatory conversion ratio, repatriation timeline, or Central Bank approval for retention of export receipts. E-commerce and DTC brand sales proceeds from Shopify Payments, PayPal, Stripe, or direct-to-consumer card acquiring may be settled directly into a Panamanian corporate account in USD, EUR, GBP, or other settlement currency. Nevertheless, all international wire transactions involving the PIF must retain underlying commercial documentation for at least five (5) years to satisfy Article 19 of Law 23 of 2015 (Panama AML/CFT regime) and SBP Rule 006-2017 record keeping and transaction monitoring duties.

Central bank supervision: Panama operates without a conventional central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the statutory banking regulator and supervises all banking license categories under the Single Banking License regime (Legislative Decree 2 of 2008 and Law 52 of 2016). Banco Nacional de Panamá functions as the State's fiscal agent and operates the high-value USD clearing system (Sistema de Pagos de Alto Valor, SPAV). Monetary and exchange rate policy is de facto delegated to the U.S. Federal Reserve due to full dollarization; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) administers external debt and fiscal policy.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium-Low as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI institutions (Wise Platform, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar) are not licensed supervised banks by the SBP and cannot hold a Panamanian corporate account with full local clearing rights; they can serve only as downstream payout channels after principal funds are cleared through an SBP-supervised bank. Under the Panama Fintech Law (Law 121 of 2020) and the SBP Regulatory Sandbox, locally chartered fintech payment entities can provide IBAN-equivalent USD settlement, but none currently offers a fully production-grade integration with Shopify Payments or Stripe for the specific DTC use case. Legal constraints include: (i) EMIs must conduct UBO identification matching the PIF's Beneficial Ownership Registry data; (ii) source-of-funds documentation for all e-commerce sale settlements arriving from PSP aggregators is mandatory; and (iii) any EMI funneling funds to a PAN-licensed bank without clear purpose codes will trigger enhanced due diligence and possible account freezing under SBP Rule 006-2017.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Defense evidence: Comprehensive UBO declaration submitted to the Registro de Beneficiarios Finales (Executive Decree 363 of 2024); certified extract of the foundation deed disclosing beneficiary categories; Foundation Council resolution formally waiving beneficiary anonymity for banking purposes; source of wealth statement for all contributing founders.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections must be executed through the SWIFT network using a correspondent relationship of the receiving Panamanian bank. Recommended practice is an MT103 wire from the founder's account to the PIF's operational USD account, using purpose code 'INTC' (intra-company) or 'EQUI' (equity-related payment) per ISO 20022 conventions. The PIF's foundation deed reference, the founder's name, and the UBO Registry identifier must appear in the beneficiary information field. If the injection is structured as a shareholder loan, execute a written loan agreement (secured or unsecured) with clear repayment schedule and use purpose code 'FCLT' (financial cash loan) to prevent FATCA and Anti-Money Laundering misclassification. For non-USD inflows (EUR, GBP), require the sending bank to convert to USD inside the correspondent bank; the Panamanian bank will record source-of-funds documentation for conversion. Verify that the receiving bank's compliance office receives the payment instruction and the underlying foundation documentation at least 48 hours before funds value date to avoid suspension of the credit under SBP Rule 006-2017 'no-pass-through' expectations.Clearing rails for trade settlements are selected based on counterparty preference and invoice currency. For general supplier payments, use SWIFT MT103 with purpose code 'GDDS' (goods trade) or 'TRAD' (trade payment), maintaining a commercial invoice and bill of lading or fulfilment receipt in the treasury archive. For larger DTC inventory purchases exceeding USD 250,000, a Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) issued by the Panamanian bank through a confirmed correspondent bank is advisable to mitigate goods risk; terms are typically 30-90 day usance with documents against payment. Local USD-denominated trades and partner payouts inside Panama may clear through the ACH system operated by the Panamanian Chamber of Commerce (ACH Panamá) or high-value through SPAV via Banco Nacional. For e-commerce refunds to consumers, the most efficient rails are the PSP's built-in refund API (Shopify Payments/Stripe/PayPal) which re-route funds from the PIF's merchant balance, avoiding costly MT103 reversals. FX conversion for outbound EUR/GBP payables is executed at the bank's spot desk with a same-day or next-day value; all FX trades must reference the underlying invoice and obtain a transaction number from the bank's trading floor for audit traceability.FX spot for USD/EUR, USD/GBP, USD/MXN and other convert pairs offered by the Panamanian commercial banking center; the USD is domestic legal tender so the PIF's balance sheet is naturally hedged against local currency fluctuationsDeliverable foreign exchange forwards (outrights) up to 12 months under the international banking center's standard ISDA or amortizing master agreements; typical counterparties are Banco General, Banistmo, or Scotiabank PanamaCross-currency interest rate swaps negotiated via international bank branches in Panama for matched asset-liability hedging of EUR or GBP revenue streamsOffshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on emerging market currencies (MXN, BRL, COP, TRY) booked through subsidiary treasury desks of foreign banks located in the Panama International Banking Center, subject to external ISDA documentation and Collateral Support AnnexVanilla FX options (European style) for protection against unexpected appreciation of GBP or EUR against the USD, only where the bank has a local derivatives license and the PIF maintains adequate collateral arrangements

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium-Low as a primary treasury rail. Offshore digital EMI institutions (Wise Platform, Revolut Business, Airwallex, and similar) are not licensed supervised banks by the SBP and cannot hold a Panamanian corporate account with full local clearing rights; they can serve only as downstream payout channels after principal funds are cleared through an SBP-supervised bank. Under the Panama Fintech Law (Law 121 of 2020) and the SBP Regulatory Sandbox, locally chartered fintech payment entities can provide IBAN-equivalent USD settlement, but none currently offers a fully production-grade integration with Shopify Payments or Stripe for the specific DTC use case. Legal constraints include: (i) EMIs must conduct UBO identification matching the PIF's Beneficial Ownership Registry data; (ii) source-of-funds documentation for all e-commerce sale settlements arriving from PSP aggregators is mandatory; and (iii) any EMI funneling funds to a PAN-licensed bank without clear purpose codes will trigger enhanced due diligence and possible account freezing under SBP Rule 006-2017.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% (standard CIT under Código Fiscal Art. 713; applied only to Panamanian-source taxable income under the territorial regime)

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid by a Panama corporation out of Panamanian-taxed profits (withholding at source by the payer); 0% on dividends declared out of foreign-source profits and on PIF distributions to beneficiaries under Law 25 of 1995, because foundation distributions are not dividends of a commercial company.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 2,000,000 (Panama is a dollarized economy; PAB/USD is 1:1) in aggregate related-party transactions in a fiscal year triggers the mandatory Local File and the annual Form 780 transfer-pricing declaration; Master File documentation is required where intercompany transactions exceed USD 8,000,000; Country-by-Country Reporting applies to multinational groups with consolidated group revenue of USD 750,000,000.

Economic substance scope: Panama does not impose a separate BVI/CAY-style Economic Substance Regulation filing on PIFs; however, substance is decisive in four areas: (i) territorial sourcing — income is Panamanian-source where the foundation council manages and executes the DTC business from Panama; (ii) TRC issuance — substantive presence (local office, staff, real decision-making) is required to obtain treaty relief; (iii) AML/UBO — Law 196 of 2019 obliges the Resident Agent to file beneficial ownership with the Registro Público and to evidence genuine economic substance of the PIF; and (iv) anti-shamming — banks and payment processors will freeze settlement rails if the PIF cannot demonstrate premises, personnel, and real transactions. A PIF that merely interposes a Panama wrapper around a Shopify store while all business is run outside Panama preserves its territorial exemption, but the moment Panamanian management or local customers appear, the DGI will re-source the profit and tax it retroactively.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - False Foreign-Source Allocation of DTC Revenue

Statutory sanction: DGI reassessment at 25% CIT on the entire mis-sourced Panama revenue plus ITBMS, monthly late-payment surcharges (5% per month, cap 100%), moratory interest, and understatement penalties; criminal prosecution for defraudación fiscal under Panamanian law carries imprisonment of 2-5 years and fines, in addition to blacklisting and cancellation of the trading license.

Director personal liability: The foundation council members, the settlor as economic owner, and the signing registered agent are jointly and severally liable for the recaptured taxes; the DGI may pierce the foundation's autonomous patrimony on proof of simulation; criminal sentencing includes restitution of evaded taxes, management bans, and travel restrictions.
