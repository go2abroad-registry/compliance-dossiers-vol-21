Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or pass-through election exists for Panamanian PIFs. Available elections: (a) optional small-taxpayer integrated regime (Régimen Tributario Integrado) for gross annual revenue ≤ B/. 75,000, consolidating ITBMS, income tax, and municipal obligations; (b) election to fix the PIF duration (determinate term up to 50 years or indefinite); (c) voluntary registration as an indirect tax collector for digital services under Ley 131 de 2020 where relevant; (d) group consolidation election for income tax under Ley 62 de 2018; (e) option to claim limited foreign tax credits on Panamanian-source income; (f) election to treat a PIF as a closed company for dividend remittance purposes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

General ITBMS regime: no minimum turnover threshold; registration is mandatory before the first taxable operation. Simplified small-taxpayer regime: optional up to B/. 75,000 annual gross revenue; where the PIF's cross-border supplement sales exceed B/. 75,000, the general regime with monthly or semi-monthly filings applies.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating at 0% ITBMS applies to physical goods (including dietary supplement e-commerce orders) where: (a) title and risk pass to a non-resident buyer; (b) goods physically depart Panama within 90 days of the invoice date; (c) the export is evidenced by an accepted DUA in the ANA single window; and (d) ancillary services (freight, handling, packing) are supplied to non-residents for use outside Panama. For nutraceuticals, the MINSA sanitary registration for the exported product and a lot-specific sanitary export certificate must support each customs filing; absence of MINSA clearance voids the zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA - Declaración Única de Aduanas) accepted and stamped by ANA
  • Bill of Lading, Airway Bill, or courier master waybill evidencing physical departure from Panamanian territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (comprobante de ingreso de divisas) issued by the receiving Panamanian bank or the high-risk merchant account settlement statement tracing credit-card receipts to the foreign issuing bank
  • Commercial invoice with HS code (e.g., HS 2106.90 for supplement preparations) and the destination-country address of the non-resident purchaser
  • MINSA lot-specific sanitary export certificate
  • Packing list and purchase order or sales contract reflecting Incoterms EXW/FOB and foreign buyer identity

Mandatory real-time electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica en Línea) under the DGI framework (Law 62 of 2018 and implementing DGI regulations). Each invoice is validated and fiscally sealed by the SFT (Servicio de Facturación Transmisible) or by an accredited billing provider; a digital fiscal seal, QR code, and unique CONC authorization number are required. Transmission must use REST API (JSON/XML over HTTPS); no offline batch stamping is permitted except during a DGI-authorized 48-hour contingency window. Export invoices must include the DUA reference and the zero-rate reason code 'Exportacion'.

DGI may audit export zero-rating for five tax years, extended to ten years in cases of non-filing or fraud. A disallowed zero-rating triggers reassessment at 7% ITBMS plus the omitted tax surcharge of 25% to 150%, statutory moratorium interest, and possible customs fines of B/. 10,000–B/. 100,000. Criminal exposure under Law 76 of 2019: imprisonment of 2 to 5 years where evasion exceeds B/. 50,000 and 5 to 10 years where evasion exceeds B/. 100,000. The anti-avoidance rule of Article 762-A of the Fiscal Code allows DGI to disregard the PIF's separate patrimony where the foundation is used abusively, and ANA may revoke the exporter's license on the VUPA single window.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama imposes no exchange controls; the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) exists only as a bookkeeping denomination at parity with the USD and is not physically issued. Current and capital accounts are fully convertible; profit remittance, dividends, interest, and royalties may be freely repatriated without central bank approval or withholding of an exchange nature. Banking filings may require a transaction concept/purpose code solely for statistical reporting, not regulatory restriction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory surrender or mandatory conversion of export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in USD or any freely convertible currency in local or foreign banks. No timeline applies for repatriation or conversion; however, invoices and bank credit evidence should be retained for transfer-pricing and tax-territoriality substantiation.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — Panama does not maintain a central bank; the US dollar is legal tender and the economy is fully dollarized. Banking supervision is conducted by SBP, monetary stability is effectively anchored by the US Federal Reserve, and AML/CFT oversight is reinforced by the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-Medium viability. Panama does not license dedicated 'Electronic Money Institutions' (EMIs) as independent deposit takers. Offshore digital EMIs and payment processors must operate through a SBP-licensed bank sponsor or hold a foreign (e.g., EU) EMI license while cooperating with a Panamanian clearing bank. For a Private Interest Foundation engaged in high-risk nutraceutical e-commerce, a pure EMI structure will not provide the required treasury certainty; a SBP-regulated commercial bank account remains mandatory for settlement, although specialized payment/acquiring partners registered with the Superintendencia de Bancos can be appended to the banking rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All incoming capital from shareholders or the settlor should be remitted via SWIFT MT103 for high-value transactions; for verified capital contributions, include payment details stating 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION TO PIF' and, if applicable, reference the Foundation Charter clause. Panama applies no exchange control, so conversion into USD is performed automatically at the receiving bank using the fixed 1:1 balboa peg. Correspondent banks may require a transaction code; agreed code can be 'A110 – Commercial / Bussiness' or 'A10 General Commercial Goods' when injecting trade-related working capital. To prevent money-laundering red flags, capital injection must be disaggregated: (1) funding of operations is labeled 'trade prepayment / goods inventory financing'; (2) emergency operational reserve is labeled 'intercompany loan under foundation account'; and both must have an authenticated bank statement of the remitter.Supplier and logistics payments out of Panama use the SWIFT global clearing rail, predominantly through MT103 single-customer credit transfers. For larger purchase orders, a Commercial Letter of Credit (L/C) through an SBP-licensed bank is permissible; the favorable jurisdiction is USD-denominated confirming bank. Trade disbursements should be allocated among: (1) MT103 transfers to verified foreign suppliers for inventory purchases; (2) ACH Panama / Fedwire for domestic USD settlement if the supplier maintains a Panamanian account; (3) Commercial L/C (UCP600) for bulk pharmaceutical raw material import, with Ben10/Be88 process handled by the bank. Direct disbursement to a third-party beneficiary without invoice back-up is not recommended.USD is the functional currency; no PAB transfer risk, so FX hedging is only necessary when entering receivables or payables in EUR, CHF, GBP, CNY, or AED.Spot FX trades: fully permissible through SBP-licensed banks and correspondents.Outright FX Forwards (delivery-based): permitted for institutional clients when executed with a bank license; the conversion is settled through the bank, no exchange-control clearing required.Non-Deliverable Forwards (NDFs): not locally available for PAB/USD because of full dollarization, but available to manage CNY/EUR emerging-market exposures through international desks at Citibank, Banistmo, or BAC.Currency Swaps (XCY): permissible subject to bank approval and used exclusively for trade-portfolio adjustments.Options (FX vanillas): permitted with international broker-dealers; no Panamanian product only restriction for off-exchange derivative within banking relationship.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% final corporate income tax (ISR - Impuesto Sobre la Renta) on net Panamanian-source taxable income; no tax on foreign-source income under Panama's territorial tax doctrine.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% final dividend withholding tax on dividends or beneficiary distributions paid out of Panamanian-source profits of a Panamanian legal entity (Fiscal Code, dividend withholding regime). Distributions by a PIF from foreign-source assets are not subject to Panama dividend WHT; however, dividends paid by a Panamanian company to the PIF remain subject to the 10% WHT at the source.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within three (3) months after the fiscal year-end. Standard fiscal years ending 31 December are due by 31 March; companies electing a 30 June year-end file by 30 September. Payment of any tax due accompanies the return.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Panama's transfer pricing rules (effective 2012) require a Local File and the annual TP Informative Return (Form 733) when the taxpayer's gross revenue exceeds USD 3,000,000 (B/. 3,000,000) in the corresponding fiscal year, or when cross-border related-party transactions exceed this threshold. Regardless of the threshold, any PIF purchasing supplements from related manufacturers abroad must apply arm's length pricing and retain contemporaneous documentation of the pricing policy, intercompany agreements, and benchmark analysis.

Economic substance scope: Panama enacted economic substance legislation (Law 254 of 2021 and implementing regulations) applicable to legal entities and foundations performing 'Relevant Activities' - including distribution, logistics, financing, IP holding, and headquarters services - within Panama. A PIF administering a cross-border nutraceutical e-commerce business from abroad will not need Panamanian substance; however, if the foundation has Panamanian premises, local employees, or locally managed decision-making, it must demonstrate core income-generating activities (CIGA) in Panama, adequate staff and premises, and local board minutes. Non-compliance exposes the foundation to fines up to USD 1,000,000, potential reclassification of tax residence, and revocation of the exemption under Law 25 of 1995. The Resident Agent must keep the statutory registers (foundation deed, beneficial owners, and asset register) physically available in Panama.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Exemption Claim

Statutory sanction: Administrative fine of up to three times the evaded tax, monthly indexation interest on the unpaid balance, embargo of bank accounts, and publication of defaulters. Criminal conviction for tax fraud (defraudación fiscal) under the Panamanian Penal Code carries imprisonment of two (2) to five (5) years.

Director personal liability: Members of the foundation council, the founder, and the Resident Agent are jointly and severally liable when they willfully sign or cause a false return to be filed. Courts may pierce the corporate veil and order civil restitution of the evaded tax with interest; criminal prosecution may result in imprisonment and a preventive travel ban (prohibición de salida del país).
Shell Company / Sham Foundation

Statutory sanction: Fines up to USD 1,000,000 under the economic substance and AML regimes; cancellation of the foundation's registration; the DGI may recharacterize the PIF as a transparent entity and assess tax directly against the founder or beneficial owner; the Public Registry may refuse renewals.

Director personal liability: Judicial piercing of the corporate veil allows creditors and the State to seize the personal assets of the founder, protector, and council members for foundation liabilities. False declarations before the Public Registry constitute false-oath offenses, and the DGI may pursue criminal charges with imprisonment up to eight (8) years for aggravated fraud.
AML/CFT - High-Risk Merchant Account Violations

Statutory sanction: Violation of Law 23 of 2015 (AML/CFT) carries administrative fines up to USD 1,000,000 for legal entities; money laundering under Article 254 of the Penal Code is punishable by five (5) to twelve (12) years of imprisonment. The Financial Intelligence Unit (UAF) may freeze accounts without prior court order for renewable periods, and the acquiring bank may terminate the merchant agreement and file a Suspicious Activity Report.

Director personal liability: Council members and the signatories of the merchant application are criminally liable if they knew or should have known that funds derived from unlicensed or fraudulent activity. The Public Prosecutor's Office may impose an international travel ban (Nota de Impedimento) and order provisional seizure of personal assets pending investigation.
Unlicensed Pharmaceutical and Merchant Activities

Statutory sanction: Administrative closure of the business (clausura), withdrawal of any sanitary licenses, and fines ranging from USD 5,000 to USD 50,000 under Panama's sanitary law. The acquiring bank may terminate the Merchant Agreement, blacklist the merchant, and report the misrepresentation to the Superintendency of Banks; criminal liability arises for the illegal exercise of pharmacy if the products contain controlled substances, punishable by three (3) to six (6) years of imprisonment.

Director personal liability: The individuals signing the merchant application, the foundation council, and the Resident Agent may be jointly sued for fraud, unfair trade practice, and unjust enrichment. Criminal conviction leads to imprisonment, permanent disqualification from serving as a Panama corporate officer or Resident Agent, and civil restitution to consumers and financial institutions.
