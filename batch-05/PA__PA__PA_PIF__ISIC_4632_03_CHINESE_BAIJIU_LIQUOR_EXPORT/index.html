Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance, Republic of Panama; taxpayer portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (http://www.dgi.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign Private Interest Foundation engaging in Panamanian-source commercial activity must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes). The SOP: (1) appoint a resident legal representative; (2) file the foundation charter (Pacto Fundacional) and by-laws with the Public Registry, legalized and translated into Spanish; (3) execute a notarized application before the DGI using the prescribed SDI-01 form; (4) present identity documents of the founder, officers, and resident agent; (5) provide evidence of the commercial premise (if any); (6) obtain the RUC resolution and certificate. Non-resident entities may also register through a licensed public accountant or legal representative for the sole purpose of compliance with ITBMS export procedures.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope — A foreign Private Interest Foundation engaging in Panamanian-source commercial activity must obtain a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes). The SOP: (1) appoint a resident legal representative; (2) file the foundation charter (Pacto Fundacional) and by-laws with the Public Registry, legalized and translated into Spanish; (3) execute a notarized application before the DGI using the prescribed SDI-01 form; (4) present identity documents of the founder, officers, and resident agent; (5) provide evidence of the commercial premise (if any); (6) obtain the RUC resolution and certificate. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box or hybrid mismatch election regime. The PIF is automatically taxed on a territorial basis; income from foreign sources is outside Panamanian tax jurisdiction. No statutory election to be treated as a pass-through entity exists. An entity may elect, however, to be subject to the General Income Tax Law as a 'reported entity' for dual-residence purposes, but this does not constitute a check-the-box election under Panama's domestic tax code.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general economic turnover threshold; all persons performing taxable commercial activities in Panama must register for ITBMS. However, small taxpayers with annual gross income below B/.36,000 may elect the simplified small-taxpayer regime (Régimen Simplificado) for administrative purposes, but must still obtain a RUC.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods are zero-rated (0%) under Article 4 of the ITBMS Law, provided the goods physically leave Panama toward a foreign destination within the periods established by the Customs Law. For a Chinese baijiu liquor export wholesale operation, eligibility requires the exporter to hold: (a) a valid RUC; (b) a valid commercial license for alcohol distribution; (c) the buyer's purchase order; and (d) the goods to be exported through an authorized customs office using the pre-export declaration procedure. Services related to export (logistics, freight, insurance) are also zero-rated if properly documented and tied to an offshore destination.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA)
  • Bill of Lading (original negotiable carrier-issued copy)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (medio de pago) proving arrival of foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice with precise HS code (e.g., 2208.90 for Chinese Baijiu)
  • Certificate of Origin
  • Sanitary or phytosanitary certificate for alcohol beverages (when required by destination country)

Panama mandates electronic invoicing through the DGI's 'Facturación Electrónica' system. As of the mandatory timeline, all VAT-registered taxpayers must transmit invoices to the DGI using an authorized fiscal transmitter (API endpoint) or the online 'Facturacion en Linea' module within 24 hours of issuance. Each invoice receives a unique tax code and fiscal stamp (digital hash) validated in real time. The system requires XML format, electronic signature, and acceptance of the DGI's electronic clearing protocol before the invoice acquires legal tax effect.

The DGI may perform retrospective audits of export documentation and foreign currency repatriation. If zero-rating is found fraudulent or the export requirements are not met, the exporter is liable for the unpaid ITBMS (typically 7% of the transaction value) plus default interests calculated from the original due date, surcharges of up to 100% of the tax due, and fines under Article 121 of the Tax Procedural Code. Repeated or deliberate fraud can trigger criminal prosecution for tax evasion, with penalties including imprisonment and disqualification from obtaining export licenses. The DGI may also claw back refunds already credited to the taxpayer within 5 years of the audit year.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Capital and current account convertibility are unrestricted. The US dollar is legal tender and the Balboa is a nominal unit pegged at 1:1, so there are no de jure exchange controls, no centralised FX market, and no prior approval requirements for cross-border payments, dividend distributions, or profit repatriation. The only practical hurdles are bank-level AML/EDD procedures requiring documentary support of the underlying trade or investment purpose.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio exists for export proceeds Panamanian law. Exporters may retain USD proceeds onshore or offshore, may use them directly for trade settlements, and are not required to surrender receipts to any monetary authority. The only compliance duty is maintaining customs export declarations (DUA) and underlying trade documentation for AML/SBP inspection.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Panama does not maintain a central bank because the economy is fully dollarized. The SBP, together with the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) for AML/CFT, is the de facto banking and FX administration authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low for offshore digital EMIs not licensed in Panama. Panamanian regulatory guidance does not recognize a foreign EMI passporting regime; a non-resident EMI cannot directly access the local clearing system or hold settlement accounts in Panama without a licensed local bank sponsor or authorization as a local regulated payment / fintech company. The practical use of an offshore EMI is limited to facilitating cross-border payments through local correspondent banking, but most SBP-supervised banks reject such structures because they do not replace the requirement of a local regulated bank intermediary. A properly licensed Panamanian payment institution or a local bank's corporate e-banking product is the only fully compliant clearing channel.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound injections of endowment capital are routed through licensed Panamanian banks using SWIFT MT103 wires from overseas shareholders or foundation sponsors. The field 70 message must clearly identify the foundation name, RUC (taxpayer ID), and a global purpose code, ideally A02 (Direct Investment) under the IMF BPM6 classification, to avoid mischaracterization. China-origin USD/CNH remittances cleared through Hong Kong or New York require the receiving bank to validate the source of funds against the notarized foundation capital subscription schedule; wire amounts above USD100,000 will generally trigger additional EDD and PALA-form reporting. For PIFs, the bank may optionally require opening a separate Capital Escrow Account for endowment inflows and a distinct Operational Trade Account for disbursements.Outbound trade disbursements for Chinese baijiu export use two primary rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier and logistics payments, using trade and transport purpose codes consistent with the underlying transaction; and (2) Commercial Letter of Credit (LC) for large procurement from Chinese distilleries, issued in USD by the Panama bank in accordance with UCP 600. The LC will require a SWIFT MT700 package including commercial invoice, full set of clean bills of lading, packing list, certificate of origin, and optionally a quality certificate issued by an independent inspection company. Reimbursement between the issuing bank and the advising bank in China settles via SWIFT MT202/COV. Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% dividend tax imposed on the distributing Panamanian S.A. where dividends are paid from territorially exempt foreign-source profits; profits already subject to 25% CIT give rise to a full credit against the 10% dividend tax; distributions by a PIF to its beneficiaries are not legal 'dividends' and are exempt from Panamanian withholding.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Panama Balboa (B/.) pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar; taxpayers with gross income above B/.2.0 million in the preceding year must file Form 930 and maintain a Local File; a Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds B/.20 million; related-party transactions below B/.200,000 per counterparty are subject to simplified documentation.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations apply (Law 128/2020, as amended by Law 274/2021): the Panamanian operating S.A. engaged in 'distribution and supply chain management' of Chinese baijiu must demonstrate Panamanian board management, local premises, adequate employees and proportionate expenditure, and must file the annual economic substance affirmation with the DGI by 30 April; a pure-equity-holding PIF qualifies for the simplified low-substance regime and files a sworn statement without meeting full ESR substance tests.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Declarations

Statutory sanction: Under Law 70 of 2019, tax evasion carries fines of up to 5 times the evaded tax and imprisonment of 2 to 10 years; the DGI additionally demands full restitution of tax, 50% surcharge and 1% monthly moratory interest.

Director personal liability: Directors, legal representatives and the PIF Protector are jointly and severally liable for unpaid corporate tax and withheld dividend tax; courts may impose preventive embargoes, travel bans and disqualification from serving as resident agents, with imprisonment for willful fraud.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: ESR non-compliance fines of B/.10,000 to B/.1,000,000; cancellation of RUC and foundation patent; automatic denial of treaty benefits; the foundation may be judicially liquidated and its assets returned to the founder's estate in a forfeiture proceeding.

Director personal liability: The foundation's founder, council members and Protector are personally liable for ultra vires commercial debts; the corporate veil is pierced where the PIF was organized to conceal the active business, exposing members to civil restitution and criminal sanctions for fraud.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Concealment

Statutory sanction: Breach of Law 23 of 2015 attracts fines from B/.100,000 to B/.2,000,000 and suspension or cancellation of the foundation's registration; money laundering under Article 254 of the Criminal Code is punishable by 5 to 12 years' imprisonment and confiscation of assets.

Director personal liability: The resident agent and foundation council members face criminal indictment for facilitating anonymous ownership; individual directors are subject to travel bans, asset freezes and extradition-eligible charges in the origin market of the funds.
Abusive Transfer Pricing / Artificial Profit Shifting

Statutory sanction: DGI recharacterization of the transactions at arm's length, adjustment of the 25% CIT base, plus a 50% surcharge on under-declared income and 1% monthly interest; repeat offenses escalate to criminal tax evasion charges.

Director personal liability: Directors who signed false Form 930 declarations are personally liable for the adjusted tax and penalties; in aggravated cases, courts impose civil restitution, prohibition on leaving Panamanian territory, and imprisonment of up to 10 years under Law 70 of 2019.
