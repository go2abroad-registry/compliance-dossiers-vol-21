Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; official taxpayer portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa (e-Tax 2.0 for filings and SIFE electronic invoicing web services).

E-government filing portal

For a Panama Private Interest Foundation (PIF) operating a retail grocery business: (1) Execute the PIF charter before a Panamanian notary, declaring an initial endowment (customarily at least B/. 10,000) and appointing the statutorily required resident agent (abogado idóneo); (2) inscribe the foundation in the Registro Público and obtain the certificate of legal existence; (3) before commencing retail activity, obtain the municipal Aviso de Operación; (4) apply to DGI for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) in the legal-entity registration module of e-Tax 2.0, attaching the foundation certificate, charter, resident-agent affidavit, and the Aviso de Operación; (5) upon approval, receive the permanent RUC, which simultaneously registers the foundation for the ITBMS (VAT) and obliges enrollment in SIFE for real-time electronic invoicing. The RUC/TIN is mandatory for supplier contracting, customs import clearance, bank account opening, and all ISR/ITBMS declarations.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama grants unrestricted legal tender to the U.S. dollar; the Panamanian balboa (PAB) is purely a fractional unit pegged 1:1 to USD. Both current-account and capital-account operations are fully convertible, with no exchange-rate risk exposure to a domestic fiat currency. Profit remittances and dividend distributions are freely payable abroad without prior exchange-control approval, but are subject to documentary AML/CFT due diligence by local banks, transfer-pricing review, and, where applicable, 5% dividend withholding tax (or 0% to 10% for shareholders in certain international foundations, depending on source and treaty relief). The principal residual hurdles are bank-level source-of-funds verification, tax-residency certificates, and economic-substance disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement, mandatory conversion timeline, or prescribed retention percentage in Panama. Export receipts may remain in USD, be deposited in external bank accounts, or be converted to any freely traded currency at market rates. The only obligations are documentary (invoices, bills of lading, shipping certificates) for tax, customs and SBP financial intelligence evidence; an exporter is not required to repatriate funds to a local account.

Central bank supervision: No independent Panamanian central bank is constituted. Monetary policy is delegated to the U.S. Federal Reserve under de jure dollarization. The banking and foreign-exchange supervisory function is performed by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent, clearing house and banker to the Government, issuing the fractional Balboa coins only.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low-Medium. Offshore digital electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise or Revolut Business are not licensed by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá and cannot independently host Panamanian-account numbers or IBAN equivalents. They may operate only as unregulated payment initiators cooperating with an SBP-licensed bank, and their acceptance for the PIF grocery operation is restricted to auxiliary functions. A fully licensed Panamanian bank account remains mandatory for ring-fenced capital, treasury operations, and supplier settlements in Asia.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are executed by SWIFT MT103 wires using the USD clearing network. The sender must reference either 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — PRIVATE INTEREST FOUNDATION' or 'INVESTMENT IN RETAIL TRADE — ISIC 4721' in the payment details; the receiving Panamanian bank verifies the transaction against the account's stated business, performs a live source-of-funds check and an OFAC/FATF filter, and records the inbound amount for DGI statistics. No prior approval from the SBP is required; however, the bank may request the foundation's capital-escrow ledger, the current corporate register entry, and proof of the grocery hub's active RUC registration.Trade disbursements to Asian condiment and vegetable suppliers are cleared by (i) SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfers for letters of payment against invoices under USD 5,000,000 per transaction; or (ii) irrevocable, confirming-house-backed Commercial Letters of Credit (CAD: original documents from the beneficiary's bank) for major procurement cycles. Each payment file must carry the trade invoice, bill of lading/airway bill, packing list, phytosanitary certificate, and, where applicable, a certificate of origin.
