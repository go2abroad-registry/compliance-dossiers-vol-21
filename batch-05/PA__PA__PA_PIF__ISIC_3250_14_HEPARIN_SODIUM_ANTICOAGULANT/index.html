Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Autoridad Nacional de Ingresos Públicos (ANIP) - Republic of Panama - consolidated revenue and customs administration, successor to the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas; official electronic portal: https://www.anip.gob.pa (Sede Electrónica ANIP; e-invoicing platform: Facturación Electrónica de Panamá - FEP; customs single window: Ventanilla Única de Panamá - VUPA).

E-government filing portal

Step 1 - Formation: notarize the Foundation Charter (Acto Constitutivo) before a Panamanian attorney and register it at the Registro Público de Panamá to obtain juridical personality; deposit the statutory foundation endowment (standard minimum US$10,000 or exact endowment stated in the Charter) with a Panamanian banking institution. Step 2 - Tax registration: access the ANIP Sede Electrónica (anip.gob.pa), submit the registered Charter, the Foundation Council resolution appointing the president and treasurer, the Panamanian fiscal domicile proof, and the bank endorsement certificate; ANIP issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) and the digital credentials for ISR and ITBMS filings. Step 3 - VAT and e-invoicing activation: elect the ITBMS regime in the RUC record and obtain the FEP (electronic invoicing) authorization from ANIP. Step 4 - Licenses and customs: for the ISIC 3250 heparin activity, obtain the pharmaceutical manufacturer's sanitary license (permiso sanitario de funcionamiento) and the export sanitary permit from the Ministerio de Salud - Dirección Nacional de Farmacia y Drogas; register the exporter code in ANIP's customs module (SIE) to file export declarations (DUA). Compliance note: a standard Panamanian PIF is a patrimonial vehicle; habitual industrial extraction is ordinarily executed by an operating Panamanian company owned by the PIF, in which case that operating company holds the RUC, FEP and sanitary licenses and acts as the exporter-taxpayer, while the PIF limits itself to the annual foundation franchise tax (B/.150-B/.400) unless the Charter explicitly authorizes direct commercial activity.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama has no check-the-box election and no statutory hybrid-mismatch election. The PIF nonetheless benefits from structural elections: (i) in the Acto Constitutivo, the founder elects the governing law, Council composition, protector powers and beneficiary design - such elections do not alter the PIF's territorial tax status; (ii) voluntary ITBMS registration is available to obtain input VAT credits on domestic inputs used for zero-rated exports; (iii) because Panama taxes only Panamanian-source income, hybrid outcomes depend on the foreign classification of the PIF; the Charter can be designed (fixed versus discretionary beneficiaries, reserved powers) so that the PIF is transparent or opaque abroad while remaining tax-neutral domestically, avoiding double taxation or double non-taxation on heparin export flows.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general statutory turnover threshold - registration is mandatory from the first habitual taxable act in Panamanian territory; every person selling goods or services in Panama, including an active-exporting PIF or its operating company, must hold an active RUC and ITBMS record. Panama's simplified small-taxpayer payment option available for lower-volume domestic suppliers does not exempt exporters from full ITBMS registration because the zero-rating and credit-recovery mechanism requires an active RUC and FEP status.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods (heparin sodium): 0% ITBMS applies when the goods leave Panamanian customs territory under a validated DUA and are sold to a buyer not domiciled in Panama; the vendor must prove inventory location in Panama, physical exit, and foreign payment. Services: 0% applies to services performed in Panama when the beneficiary is non-resident and the economic benefit is realized abroad (for example, tolling extraction for a foreign principal), provided no taxable asset or right remains in Panama. Inputs incorporated into exported goods are creditable, generating a refundable ITBMS excess; the export claim requires an updated sanitary export permit from the Ministry of Health, which is an additional documentary condition for ISIC 3250 products.

  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill naming the Panamanian exporter as shipper and the foreign buyer as consignee, evidencing departure from Panamanian territory
  • Commercial invoice and packing list synchronized with the FEP XML e-invoice (verification code included)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate - bank SWIFT MT103 or credit advice showing receipt of the USD purchase price from the non-resident buyer (Panama is dollarized; the Balboa is at par with the US dollar)
  • Pharmaceutical Export Sanitary Permit (Permiso Sanitario de Exportación) issued by the Dirección Nacional de Farmacia y Drogas, Ministerio de Salud

Panama mandates electronic invoicing via ANIP's Facturación Electrónica de Panamá (FEP). Taxpayers must issue e-invoices through ANIP-certified software connected to the fiscal platform (API and web-service integration), obtain digital acceptance (recibida) from ANIP at issuance, and include a digital verification code and QR code in lieu of physical fiscal stamps. Phasing is by taxpayer size, with large and exporting taxpayers first (2023-2024 calendar); export shipments require the FEP invoice number declared on the DUA export document in ANIP's customs single-window.

ANIP audits zero-rated exports by cross-matching FEP XML, DUA export data, transport documentation, sanitary permits and banking inflows. Where the taxpayer cannot evidence physical export and receipt of the USD price, ANIP recharacterizes the supply as taxable domestic delivery: ITBMS is assessed at 7% on gross proceeds, plus statutory interest, surcharges and fines (in administrative practice up to 200% of the omitted tax). Knowingly false export documentation activates the criminal tax-fraud provisions of the Código Fiscal (defraudación tributaria) with imprisonment of 2-5 years, debarment and cancellation of the RUC and FEP authorization, confiscation of goods, retrospective assessment over the statutory limitation period of 8 years, and joint and several liability of the Foundation Council members and legal representatives.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama maintains a fully dollarized monetary system in which the US dollar is legal tender and the Balboa exists only as coinage at a strictly fixed USD 1.00 = PAB 1.00 parity. There is no exchange control law, no capital account authorization framework, and no distinction between current account and capital account convertibility. Profits, dividends, foundation distributions, and equity redemptions of a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) may be remitted freely to non-resident beneficiaries without central bank approval. The only practical hurdles are the bank's internal AML/KYC policies, UAF supervision, and documentary verification of lawful source of funds.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There are no statutory export proceeds surrender rules in Panama. Exporters are under no obligation to repatriate foreign currency, to convert export receipts into Balboa, or to surrender a percentage of export proceeds to any state entity. Under the dollarized regime, export receipts derived from heparin sodium fractionation sales are invoiced and settled in US dollars, which circulate as the domestic currency. The absence of mandatory conversion means export liquidity can be held freely in USD, utilized directly for import settlements, or routed through correspondent banking without prior administrative consent.

Central bank supervision: Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, SBP) as the sole banking and prudential supervisor. There is no traditional central bank in Panama; the regime operates under full official dollarization with the US Federal Reserve effectively providing monetary policy. Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent and clearing house operator, but holds no monetary authority and issues no domestic currency except minor Balboa coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity with the USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM. Panama has no bespoke Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime, and offshore digital EMIs holding FCA or EU/EEA authorizations cannot independently open deposit or escrow accounts in Panama without a local banking license under Legislative Decree 2 of 2008 (Panama Banking Law). A cross-border EMI may act only as a payment facilitator through a sponsor bank licensed in Panama; the final legal settlement of PIF funds, LC proceeds, and supplier disbursements must rest on a licensed Panamanian correspondent account. Use of an unlicensed EMI for concentrated trade flows would trigger SBP/UAF enhanced scrutiny and could jeopardize the foundation's banking relationship; therefore, direct SWIFT banking rails remain the compliant core.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity or loan capital from the non-resident ultimate beneficial owner is injected exclusively via SWIFT MT103/MT202 wire transfer to a Panamanian licensed bank account operated by the PIF. The sending institution must quote the purpose-of-payment narrative 'Corporate Contribution to Private Interest Foundation under Panamanian Law 25 of 1995' and include the foundation's RUC tax identification number and the full account number / IBAN-equivalent routing code. Because Panama has no central bank foreign-exchange permit, the true legal gate is AML verification: the PIF must furnish a foundation council resolution authorizing the contribution, a source-of-funds declaration with bank statements, and a stated use of proceeds tied to the construction or working capital of the heparin fractionation plant. Panama has no bespoke Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime, and offshore digital EMIs holding FCA or EU/EEA authorizations cannot independently open deposit or escrow accounts in Panama without a local banking license under Legislative Decree 2 of 2008 (Panama Banking Law). A cross-border EMI may act only as a payment facilitator through a sponsor bank licensed in Panama; the final legal settlement of PIF funds, LC proceeds, and supplier disbursements must rest on a licensed Panamanian correspondent account. Use of an unlicensed EMI for concentrated trade flows would trigger SBP/UAF enhanced scrutiny and could jeopardize the foundation's banking relationship; therefore, direct SWIFT banking rails remain the compliant core.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends paid out of net profits already subject to the 25% CIT; 20% on distributions from untaxed or exempt profits; 5% only for dividends of an 'active enterprise' listed on the Panamá Stock Exchange or under the SEM regime. A PIF distributing only foreign-source profits to a non-resident founder is outside Panama's dividend tax because no Panama-source income was generated.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/.250,000 (Panamanian Balboas, equivalent to USD 250,000): aggregate related-party transactions in a fiscal year exceeding this threshold trigger the mandatory Formulario 940 transfer pricing informative return, the preparation of a Local File and Master File, and the obligation to file them with the annual Form 200 by the statutory deadline.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 254 of 2021 and Executive Decree 163 of 2021) apply to all legal persons, including PIFs, that are tax resident in Panama and conduct 'relevant activities'. Active chemical fractionation of heparin qualifies as a commercial/industrial activity; the PIF must demonstrate in its annual ESR return that it maintains: a physical office or plant in Panama, local payroll (qualified chemists and operators), local bank accounts, board meetings in Panama, and substantive management of the fractionation process from Panama. A PIF that claims foreign-source income exemption while directing its worldwide heparin operations from Panama is at high risk of deemed Panama-source income recharacterization by the DGI.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / False Tax Returns (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Panama Criminal Code Article 269: imprisonment of 2 to 5 years plus a fine of 2 to 5 times the evaded tax; DGI additionally imposes the 5%-per-month late filing surcharge (maximum 50%), 10% annual moratory interest, and cancellation of all CIT incentives.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident agent who sign false declarations are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid tax, civil restitution, and criminal penalties; courts may impose preventive travel bans and passport retention during the investigation.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Violation

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Law 254 of 2021 and Decree 163 of 2021: administrative fines up to B/.75,000 for failure to file the ESR return; recharacterization of distributed income as Panama-source subject to 25% CIT plus 10% dividend WHT; possible strike-off of the PIF from the Public Registry.

Director personal liability: Founding council members are personally liable for the PIF's unpaid taxes and fines; in cases of fraudulent insolvency or asset stripping, Panamanian courts pierce the foundation veil and impose civil restitution, travel bans, and disqualification from serving as directors for up to 5 years.
AML / Trade-Based Money Laundering (Law 23 of 2015)

Statutory sanction: Penalties under Law 23 of 2015 and Penal Code Article 254: imprisonment of 5 to 12 years, fines up to B/.10,000,000 for legal entities, forfeiture of the heparin cargo and bank balances, and mandatory reporting to the UAF (Unidad de Análisis Financiero).

Director personal liability: Directors with knowledge or reckless disregard face individual criminal indictment, asset freezing, Interpol Red Notice, travel bans, and civil money-laundering liability; the corporate veil is pierced for deliberate AML violations.
Dividend Withholding Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: DGI assessment of the omitted WHT plus 5%-per-month surcharge (max 50%) and 10% annual interest; criminal tax fraud prosecution if the evasion is intentional: 2 to 5 years imprisonment.

Director personal liability: Directors acting as withholding agents are personally and severally liable for the unwithheld tax; travel bans may be issued pending payment; failure to pay the assessed WHT leads to the DGI execution against personal assets.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance and False Documentation

Statutory sanction: DGI transfer pricing adjustment to the arm's length result, plus a tax surcharge of up to 40% of the underpaid CIT and administrative fines up to B/.10,000; the annual Form 200 is deemed incomplete until the Formulario 940 is correctly filed.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign or approve fraudulent TP documentation are jointly liable for the additional tax and fines; criminal liability attaches if the false documentation is used in a tax evasion scheme: imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and personal travel bans.
