Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: The PIF is a distinct juridical person under Law 25 of 1995; Panamanian tax liability attaches only to Panamanian-source income and territorial activities. A purely foreign-asset, passive PIF remains outside the Panamanian tax net; an active e-commerce PIF making local deliveries to Panamanian consumers is subject to territorial ISR on locally sourced profits and must register, collect, and remit ITBMS on taxable domestic supplies. Foreign-source income is not subject to Panamanian income tax, and the PIF is not treated as a pass-through entity.

Available election forms

Panama does not operate US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elections. Applicable statutory elections: (i) Foundation charter election: the deed may elect a purely patrimonial/passive scope (outside Panamanian commercial taxation) or an active commercial scope (full ISR/ITBMS exposure); the elected scope is binding and must be registered. (ii) ITBMS input-credit election: exporters may elect to offset input ITBMS against output ITBMS or apply for quarterly refunds of excess credits under the Código Fiscal ITBMS rules. (iii) Electronic invoicing mode election: free DGI Sede Electrónica web platform, certified billing software, or an authorized electronic invoicing service provider (PSF). (iv) Simplified small-taxpayer regime election for low-turnover businesses, where applicable under DGI regulations. (v) Territorial taxation is mandatory since the Law 35 de 2019 reform; no worldwide-basis election exists.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB 0 (USD equivalent) - no economic turnover threshold: every person habitually or occasionally making taxable transfers of goods in Panama, including remote e-commerce sales delivered to Panamanian customers, must register for ITBMS before commencing operations. A simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross turnover of approximately PAB 38,000 per DGI regulations; this affects payment method and filing frequency but does not defer the registration duty.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) definitive physical export of goods from Panamanian customs territory, evidenced by an export declaration accepted by ANA; (ii) services provided to non-resident beneficiaries where the economic use occurs abroad and the Panamanian supplier derives export revenue; and (iii) goods delivered from bonded or free-zone premises to foreign destinations under the applicable free-zone regime. For this PIF, cross-border B2C sales of portable solar power equipment qualify for the 0% rate when goods are shipped from Panama to a foreign address with complete export documentation. Local delivery to Panamanian consumers is subject to 7% ITBMS; if fulfillment occurs from a foreign warehouse directly to the buyer, the sale is outside the Panamanian territorial scope and excluded from ITBMS. Input ITBMS attributable to zero-rated exports is creditable and refundable.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación / DUA) accepted by ANA with definitive export regime code
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill proving physical exit from Panamanian territory
  • Export-type electronic invoice (Factura Electrónica de Exportación) authorized by the DGI
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / banking settlement evidence showing receipt of export proceeds
  • Packing list and certificate of origin (where required)

Mandatory electronic invoicing applies to all taxpayers. Invoices are issued exclusively through the DGI Sede Electrónica platform, DGI-certified billing software, or an authorized electronic invoicing service provider connected by API. Each invoice receives DGI digital authorization and bears a machine-readable verification code; large taxpayers transmit transaction-by-transaction in real time, while other taxpayers transmit in batch per DGI schedule. The system comprises sale invoices, export invoices, purchase invoices, and credit/debit notes, with SAF-T-compatible structured data files. E-commerce orders require the issuance of the electronic sale invoice at delivery or shipment, and exports must be invoiced as 'export' type linked to the customs export declaration.

DGI scrutinizes export zero-rating claims against customs exit records, e-invoices, and banking settlement evidence. Where zero-rating is improperly claimed or the goods are diverted to the domestic market, the taxpayer must pay the omitted ITBMS at 7% plus monthly surcharges, interest at the official technical rate, and administrative fines of 50% to 200% of the tax omitted under the Código Fiscal. Wilful fraud or falsified export documentation constitutes criminal tax fraud under Ley 70 de 2019, punishable by imprisonment of 3 to 6 years, increased to 10 years in aggravated cases, together with monetary penalties and loss of export incentive benefits. Export records must be retained for a minimum of 5 years, and clawback assessments may be issued retrospectively in accordance with the statute of limitations period.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized: the US Dollar is lawful legal tender under the Currency Law of 1904, and the Panamanian Balboa exists solely as subsidiary coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity. Both current and capital account transactions are fully convertible with no central bank authorization, no exchange licensing, and no repatriation permits. Profit and benefit remittances from a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) are unrestricted at the FX level; however, remittances of Panamanian-source income may attract the territorial income tax and, when applicable, the 10% statutory dividend or benefit distribution withholding tax. For the ISIC 4791 e-commerce export model, income considered sourced outside Panama (offshore retail sales) is outside the Panamanian tax net, and documented foreign sourcing is the treasury cornerstone.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio on export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD or any freely convertible currency. Administrative obligations are limited to foreign-trade declarations that may be filed through the Single Window for Foreign Trade (SIGA/VUPA) and, where applicable, registration of the exporting entity with the Ministry of Commerce and Industries (MICI); these obligations do not involve FX conversion or central bank reporting.

Central bank supervision: Panama has no stand-alone central bank. The Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, SBP) is the primary regulator and supervisor of licensed banks; Banco Nacional de Panamá (National Bank of Panama) acts as the fiscal agent of the Republic and manages state accounts; the Financial Analysis Unit (Unidad de Análisis Financiero, UAF) functions as the national AML/CFT intelligence unit. No institution issues domestic fiat currency or administers exchange controls.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM. Regulated offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) generally do not accept Panamanian PIFs as primary account holders, and no such entity holds a Panamanian banking license, so any deposit-taking or local clearing activity in Panama would contravene Decreto Ley 2 de 2008. Stripe also does not currently onboard Panama-domiciled businesses directly. Recommended blueprint: maintain the Panamanian PIF as the export treasury and contracting vehicle, while establishing a supported payment-acquisition entity in a Stripe-supported market (US/EU) acting as the PSP settlement conduit; the Panamanian accounts then function as the concentrator of USD settlement funds.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Since Panama is dollarized, a founder capital injection requires no FX conversion and is executed as a straight USD SWIFT MT103 wire to the PIF's operational account in Panama. The remittance should cite in Tag 72 'Foundation endowment contribution under Article 22, Law 25 of 1995' and reference the Foundation charter folio (Ficha), the date of registration, and the Resident Agent's licensing details. The PIF should attach to the transaction record: (a) certified copy of the Foundation Charter; (b) UBO disclosure under Law 23 of 2015; (c) founder's source-of-funds statement; and (d) a Foundation Council resolution approving the lodgment. Panama imposes no mandatory purpose codes; however, to optimize bank auto-filtering the PIF should adopt ISO-20022 / BOE-GPC conventions such as '4700 – Capital Contribution/Foundation Endowment' or '4100 – Related Party Investment'.Outbound trade settlements are executed in USD through Panama banks' correspondent clearing network in New York (CHIPS/Fedwire). E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: LOW-to-MEDIUM. Regulated offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer) generally do not accept Panamanian PIFs as primary account holders, and no such entity holds a Panamanian banking license, so any deposit-taking or local clearing activity in Panama would contravene Decreto Ley 2 de 2008. Stripe also does not currently onboard Panama-domiciled businesses directly. Recommended blueprint: maintain the Panamanian PIF as the export treasury and contracting vehicle, while establishing a supported payment-acquisition entity in a Stripe-supported market (US/EU) acting as the PSP settlement conduit; the Panamanian accounts then function as the concentrator of USD settlement funds.
