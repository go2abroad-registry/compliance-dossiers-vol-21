Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of moveable goods leave Panamanian territory and are zero-rated when title transfers to a foreign buyer; services are zero-rated if consumed abroad; proof of export supported by customs and shipping documents must be kept by the exporter.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The US dollar circulates as legal tender by constitutional design; the Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a nominal accounting unit issued only as token coins at a fixed 1:1 parity with USD and not as convertible paper money. Panama maintains no exchange-control law: capital and current account operations are fully convertible, free of prior central-bank authorization, non-resident investment gates, repatriation limits, or surrender ratios. Profit remittance obstacles are fiscal rather than monetary: a Private Interest Foundation (PIF) is outside Panamanian income tax on foreign-source commercial earnings, such that beneficiary distributions of trade profits from intraocular-lens exports are not subject to Panamanian dividend withholding; only if the PIF realizes Panamanian-source commercial income within a permanent establishment in Panama does a 10% dividend/advance tax apply, payable in USD to the Dirección General de Ingresos. The practical remittance constraint is correspondent-bank de-risking scrutiny of the PIF structure, resolved through substance documentation and full beneficial-ownership disclosure.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Nil. Panama imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement: there is no statutory repatriation timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio (effective 0%), and no obligation to route export receipts through a Panamanian bank. Receipts in USD or any invoiced currency may be retained at any bank worldwide indefinitely. Export records filed with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (SICEX/DOCS declarations) serve trade statistics, duty-drawback, and territorial-sourcing verification only, with no FX compliance function.

Central bank supervision: Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction with no central bank of issue and no dedicated foreign-exchange administration. The prudential banking regulator is the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Banco Nacional de Panamá serves as fiscal agent of the Republic for state collections but exercises no monetary policy, exchange-control, or licensing function.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Panama has no electronic-money institution (EMI) or payment-bank license class; bank licensing under Decree Law 2 of 2008 requires a physical presence, minimum capital, and SBP authorization. A foreign digital EMI cannot passport into Panama and cannot hold a Panamanian settlement account unless separately licensed as a bank. Feasible workaround: a licensed Panamanian bank's API (banking-as-a-service) integrated as a white-label treasury front-end for the PIF, with no regulatory recognition of the EMI as a paying agent. Cross-border EMI rails (UK/EU/BVI) are viable only as auxiliary collection channels outside Panama's domestic account structure and must not be presented as the primary clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Founder contributions flow by SWIFT MT103 from the settlor's regulated home bank to the PIF's USD account at the Panamanian bank; the USD leg clears via the Panama bank's New York correspondent through Fedwire/CHIPS, followed by domestic ledger posting through Panama's clearing house (Cámara de Compensación). The remittance must state a plain-language purpose 'Aportación de Capital a Fundación de Interés Privado' and reference the Foundation Charter number; the bank maps it to its capital-contribution transfer category and verifies against a sworn source-of-funds statement and Foundation Council resolution. Transactions exceeding the statutory CDD/reporting thresholds (cash above USD 10,000; non-cash above USD 30,000) trigger UAF reports via the SBP platform; a High_Risk_Special_License PIF should pre-file the same documentation to avoid information-request delays. No central-bank declaration, exchange license, or capital-account approval is required.Settlements for acrylic monomer inputs and finished-lens receivables use three rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 direct international wires for open-account and advance payments, referencing invoice and purchase-order numbers; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP 600, issued by the Panamanian bank via MT700 and advised/confirmed by the seller's bank via MT710/MT707, with presentation files comprising commercial invoice, clean on-board bill of lading or AWB, packing list, ISO 13485 certificate, and beneficiary certificate; and (3) documentary collections under URC 522 for recurring low-risk counterparties. All disbursements require dual-signatory execution, reconciliation to the approved trade budget, and archival of the full contract file to sustain the AML/tax defense. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% on dividends or profit distributions paid out of Panama-source taxable profits, withheld by the paying entity (Código Fiscal Arts. 733-735); 5% for entities under the small-taxpayer regime (gross income below B/.1,500,000); 20% on distributions sourced from exempt Panama income or foreign-source income that was never subject to Panama tax, including distributions of a PIF's passive foreign income if recharacterized as a dividend. Dividends distributed by a genuinely non-commercial PIF to beneficiaries under the foundation deed are structurally treated as gift-like distributions and not as taxable dividends, provided no Panama-source commercial activity exists.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed within three months after the close of the fiscal year; for calendar-year taxpayers the deadline is 31 March. Non-calendar fiscal years follow the same three-month rule measured from the fiscal year-end.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Panama-source Balboa (B/.) threshold: a taxpayer is mandatorily required to file Formulario 706 (Declaración Informativa de Precios de Transferencia) and prepare contemporaneous Transfer Pricing documentation (Local File) where annual gross revenue equals or exceeds B/.10,000,000, or where transactions with related parties, including a PIF IP holder, equal or exceed B/.3,000,000 in a fiscal year. A Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated gross revenue reaches USD 50,000,000, and a Country-by-Country Report is due where the group consolidates USD 850,000,000 or more, filed through the DGI's electronic portal within 12 months after fiscal year-end. The thresholds derive from Ley 23 de 2010 and Decreto Ejecutivo 194 de 2011.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations, introduced by Ley 70 de 2019 and Decreto Ejecutivo 163 de 2020 (effective for fiscal years beginning 1 January 2020), require every Panama legal entity carrying on a 'relevant activity' - including distribution and logistics, financing, headquarters services, shipping, and intellectual-property holding and licensing - to maintain and annually report economic substance in Panama. A PIF that holds the intraocular acrylic lens IP and receives royalties or that licenses manufacturing rights must demonstrate physical presence, full-time local employees, payroll and premises expenditure proportionate to the income, and strategic board decisions taken in Panama, and must file the annual ESR information return through the SEMS portal of the Dirección General de Ingresos. Passive PIFs without a relevant activity are outside the ESR filing obligation but remain within doorstep concerns; a non-compliant entity faces an annual fine of B/.75,000 to B/.1,000,000, loss of tax residence certification, and the nullification of treaty benefits and transfer-pricing deductions.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Defraudación Fiscal

Statutory sanction: Compulsory redefinition of income, 100% fraud surcharge on the omitted tax, monthly interest at 1%, closure of the commercial establishment, and criminal prosecution under Código Fiscal Arts. 748-749 and Ley 20 de 2013 with fines of up to triple the evaded tax and imprisonment for the responsible officers.

Director personal liability: Directors, foundation council members, and the resident agent are jointly liable for the evaded tax through the doctrine of levantamiento del velo corporativo (piercing the corporate veil); individual assets may be seized, travel bans issued by the DGI and the Public Registry, and jail terms of 2 to 5 years imposed where the evasion exceeds B/.100,000.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR violation fines of B/.75,000 to B/.1,000,000 per fiscal year, administrative dissolution of the foundation, cancellation of the tax residence certificate, denial of DTA relief, and listing on the DGI's non-compliant taxpayer registry.

Director personal liability: Foundation council members and the resident agent are personally liable for ESR fines, can be struck from the Registry of Resident Agents, barred from acting in Panama for 12 months, and the council member who signed the false substance declaration faces perjury and fraud penalties, non-bailable arrest, and confiscation of personal property.
AML / Terrorist Financing (Ley 23 de 2015)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines from B/.100,000 up to five times the laundered amounts, freezing of the foundation's bank accounts by the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF), suspension of the resident agent's license, and criminal prosecution for money laundering under Ley 23 de 2015 with penalties of up to 10 years' imprisonment and asset forfeiture.

Director personal liability: Directors and foundation council members are exposed to criminal money-laundering prosecution with prison terms up to 10 years, personal asset forfeiture, travel bans, and publication of their names in the UAF register; the professional resident agent who onboarded the structure without KYC faces license revocation and civil restitution to defrauded creditors.
Transfer Pricing Non-Disclosure and Misstatement

Statutory sanction: DGI recharacterization of the transaction at arm's length, disallowance of the excess royalty deduction, 25% tax on the adjusted profit, 1% monthly interest, application of the 100% fraud penalty for false documentation, and late-filing penalties on Formulario 706 of B/.100 per day up to B/.10,000; repeated defaults trigger criminal tax-evasion charges.

Director personal liability: The director certifying false transfer-pricing documentation is liable for perjury and civil restitution of the adjusted tax, interest, and penalties out of personal assets; the DGI may impose an exit ban preventing the director from leaving Panama until the assessment is secured by bond or payment.
Unlawful Profit Repatriation / Constructive Dividend

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the transfer as a dividend or compensation, withholding of 20% on the amount paid out of non-taxed profits, a 100% fraud surcharge, monthly arrears interest, and criminal liability for the officer who executed the unauthorized distribution; the DGI may also reverse the foundation's exemption status retroactively.

Director personal liability: Beneficiaries and the foundation council are jointly and severally liable for the unpaid dividend tax plus surcharges; personal bank accounts and real estate may be attached, travel prohibitions enforced, and directors who voted the unlawful distribution face jail terms of up to 4 years for abuse of the foundation form (Código Fiscal and Ley 25 de 1995 combined reading).
