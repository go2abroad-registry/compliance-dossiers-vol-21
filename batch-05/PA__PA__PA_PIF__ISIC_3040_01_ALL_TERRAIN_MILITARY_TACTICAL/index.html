Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); Sede Electrónica — https://dgi.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A Private Interest Foundation (PIF) obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) before the DGI. The procedure requires: (i) presentation of the foundation deed registered in the Registro Público de Panamá; (ii) notarized application identifying the resident agent and registered address; (iii) sworn declaration of any taxable activity; and (iv) issuance of the RUC and, where applicable, registration in the ITBMS taxpayer registry. A PIF with only foreign-source income and no Panamanian taxable operations is generally not required to obtain an RUC unless it imports, carries out taxable activities, or is selected as an electronic billing taxpayer.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box system exists in Panama. A PIF may elect in its foundation deed to adopt specific governance, distribution, and investment provisions, but tax classification is fixed by the Tax Code: entity-level taxation solely on Panama-source income; foreign-source income is outside Panamanian territorial scope. No hybrid mismatch election is available.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

B/. 36,000 (Panama Balboa, at parity with USD) in annual gross taxable sales; however, importers of goods are captured on the first taxable import, and the DGI may require registration for taxpayers below the threshold in specific circumstances.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to tangible goods, including all-terrain military tactical vehicles, physically exported from Panama, and to services supplied to non-resident recipients for use wholly abroad, provided title and risk pass outside Panamanian customs territory. The taxpayer must demonstrate actual physical exit, delivery under a government-to-government or diplomatic contract where applicable, and receipt of payment from the non-resident buyer.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera, DUA)
  • Bill of Lading or Airwaybill showing Panamanian departure and foreign destination
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from a Panamanian licensed bank evidencing foreign-currency repatriation
  • Commercial invoice and packing list corresponding exactly to the customs declaration
  • Certificate of Origin or end-user/government purchase order for defense procurement

The DGI has a compulsory electronic invoicing regime. Selected taxpayers, including large taxpayers and others meeting DGI turnover criteria, must issue invoices through DGI-certified software or the Sede Electrónica, transmit invoice data electronically in real time to the DGI, and obtain a DGI authorization number. Export invoices must reference the customs export declaration. There is no physical digital stamp; control is exercised through DGI validation and real-time clearance of the electronic invoice via the approved web-service/API channel.

DGI and the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas may jointly audit export transactions; a failure to produce the required export/remittance bundle, or a fraudulent zero-rating claim, triggers retrospective ITBMS assessment at the standard 7% rate, plus statutory interest, administrative fines up to 100% of the omitted tax, and criminal liability for tax fraud, customs fraud, or false documentation under Panamanian law.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama applies a fully dollarized monetary system with unrestricted convertibility of both capital and current account flows. The Panamanian balboa is a non-issued unit defined at par with the U.S. dollar, and all bank liabilities and settlements are denominated in USD. There are no exchange-control restrictions on dividend distributions, profit remittance, capital repatriation, or intercompany loans. Operational constraints arise only from AML/KYC banking due diligence, tax compliance under Panama's territorial tax rules, and the need to maintain settlement accounts at SBP-licensed institutions.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender requirement exists. There are no mandatory conversion ratios, prescribed deposit windows, or central-bank repatriation deadlines for export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained indefinitely in USD in any Panamanian commercial bank or authorized foreign correspondent account, provided the bank receives the underlying commercial invoice, export clearance documentation, and, where applicable, ministry license or end-user certificate for regulated defense goods.

Central bank supervision: No national central bank; the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) supervises the banking system, and Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent; monetary policy is effectively delegated to the United States Federal Reserve through full dollarization with the Panamanian balboa pegged 1:1 to the USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low (restricted). Offshore fintech EMI providers cannot hold a Panamanian deposit account or act as an exclusive treasury rail without an SBP banking authorization; they may serve only as a supplementary payment channel for staff expenses and limited working-capital disbursements. For defense manufacturing, government direct wires, diplomatic letters of credit, and high-value trade settlements require a fully licensed Panamanian commercial bank account with correspondent banking through the United States. EMI transactions also risk causing negative OCR scrutiny and refusal of documentary LCs. A practical onboarding approach is to use the EMI solely as a lower-tier sub-account for procurement not exceeding USD 50,000 while preserving the licensed bank account as the prime legal clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections execute as SWIFT MT103 messages to the SBP-licensed bank's U.S. correspondent shell and then participate in Panama's local automated clearing house. The remitter must use full legal name PA_PIF, address in the Republic of Panama, the foundation registration number, and a purpose code aligned with BPM6 'Direct Investment Equity Capital Injection'. Because Panama imposes no FX approvals, only threshold AML reporting applies; for amounts exceeding USD 250,000 the beneficiary bank may request a route-of-funds memorandum, the resolved public registry, an escrow balance statement, and a copy of the underlying military procurement contract or government order. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25%

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

Statutory dividend withholding tax is 10%; when dividends are paid from profits that have already been subject to Panama corporate income tax at the ordinary 25% rate, the effective withholding rate is reduced to 5% as a credit mechanism.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

On or before March 15 of the year following the calendar tax year for calendar-year taxpayers; taxpayers with an approved non-calendar fiscal year must file no later than the last day of the third month after the fiscal year-end.

BoxField Non-resident guidanceTax-evasion pitfall

Line 05 Income tax computation at the 25% statutory corporate rate, including the applicable alternative minimum tax or other final withholding tax regimes, and any statutory credits. Non-resident taxpayers whose only Panama-source income is subject to final withholding have no further Box 5 liability; where a local manufacturing plant exists, full 25% corporation tax applies. Late or non-filing triggers a DGI-assessed surcharge of 1% per month or fraction on the unpaid tax, up to 20%, plus managerial fines; false or incomplete returns attract penalties of 25%-100% of the omitted tax, and criminal tax evasion sanctions under the Panama Penal Code may be invoked.
Line 06 Balance due or refund: offset advance payments, ITBMS withholdings, tax credits, and any payments made under DTA reservations; final balance payable at filing. Refunds require audited financial statements, tax residence certificate, and complete source documentation; non-residents must maintain a Panamanian fiscal address for refund processing. Late or non-filing triggers a DGI-assessed surcharge of 1% per month or fraction on the unpaid tax, up to 20%, plus managerial fines; false or incomplete returns attract penalties of 25%-100% of the omitted tax, and criminal tax evasion sanctions under the Panama Penal Code may be invoked.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File documentation is triggered when Panamanian-source gross operating revenue exceeds B/.2,000,000 (US$2,000,000) or when related-party transactions collectively exceed B/.250,000 (US$250,000). A sworn transfer pricing disclosure must accompany Form 1935, and documentation must be available to DGI within the statutory administrative period.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Ley 21 de 2019 and implementing executive decrees) require economic presence and substance for relevant activities carried out by Panamanian entities. An ISIC 3040 armored tactical vehicle manufacturer with an active plant in Panama is an operational commercial business and generally satisfies the substance standard by maintaining Panamanian premises, local workforce, board/council meetings, accounting records, and core income-generating decisions in Panama. A PIF used merely as a passive holder of foreign military-linked assets must demonstrate adequate local substance or risk losing territorial tax benefits and incurring ESR penalties.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Omission of Panama-Source Income

Statutory sanction: DGI summarily reassesses the omitted tax, imposes a monthly surcharge of 1% up to 20%, civil penalties of 25%-100% of the evaded tax, and assessment interest; criminal conviction for tax evasion may result in imprisonment of 2 to 5 years plus a fine up to twice the evaded amount.

Director personal liability: Directors and council members who sign false declarations are jointly liable for civil restitution; Panama courts can pierce the foundation's veil upon proof of alter ego or asset commingling, exposing personal assets. Criminal proceedings may result in travel bans, asset embargoes, and imprisonment.
Shell Foundation / Artificial Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: DGI or the Registrar may strike off or dissolve the PIF, cancel the registered agent license, deny territorial tax exemptions, impose ESR penalties up to substantial administrative fines, and assess all Panamanian-source tax at 25% plus surcharges.

Director personal liability: Founders, council members, and protectors who permit shell operation are liable for fraudulent transfers and civil restitution; Panamanian courts may disregard the foundation's separate legal personality and attach the personal assets of those who directed the sham.
Defense Export Licensing / AML Red Flags

Statutory sanction: Special licenses are revoked; authorities may seize vehicles and production equipment; AML sanctions under Panama Law 23 of 2015 include fines up to B/.1,000,000 and criminal money-laundering penalties of up to 10 years imprisonment plus forfeiture of proceeds; defense export violations may trigger additional separate fines.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers carry statutory AML obligations to identify beneficial owners and monitor high-risk defense transactions. Willful blindness creates personal criminal exposure, travel bans, Interpol alerts, and civil liability for financing of unauthorized military transfers.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DGI may make arm's-length adjustments increasing Panamanian-source taxable income, impose compensatory interest on underpaid tax, apply surcharges, and levy fines for defective documentation; deliberate concealment escalates the matter to criminal tax fraud prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors who certify false transfer pricing documentation or instruct related-party invoicing outside arm's length are jointly liable for additional taxes and penalties; in fraud cases, courts may pierce the corporate/foundation veil and impose personal restitution, travel restrictions, and imprisonment.
