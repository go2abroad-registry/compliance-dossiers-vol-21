Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) – Sede Electrónica e-Tax portal (https://sede.electronica.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity appoints a licensed Panamanian attorney or resident agent, who files the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) application through the DGI Sede Electrónica e-Tax system. Required attachments include certified charter and bylaws, legalization documents (apostille), identification of directors and officers, a local registered office address, and appointment of a fiscal representative. RUC is issued within approximately 15 business days; the taxpayer must then register for ITBMS and, if applicable, the small-taxpayer simplified regime.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election is recognized in Panamanian domestic law. Available elections include: (i) consolidated group tax filing during an approved corporate reorganization; (ii) special Multinational Headquarters (SEM) regime election; (iii) small-taxpayer cash-basis regime election for annual turnover ≤ B/. 300,000; and (iv) one-time measures for transfer pricing adjustments or accelerated depreciation as permitted by the Tax Code.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis registration threshold; ITBMS registration is mandatory upon the commencement of taxable sales or services in Panama. An optional simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to taxpayers with annual turnover up to B/. 300,000 (Balboa at parity with USD).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods that leave Panama through a customs exit, and certain internationally oriented services (including consulting, legal, and trade-remedy-defense services provided entirely to non-resident counterparties for use outside Panama), qualify for a 0% ITBMS rate. The zero rating is allowed only if the taxpayer retains documentary proof of departure or of an offshore supply, and if services are not economically consumed within Panamanian territory.

  • Customs Export Declaration
  • Bill of Lading
  • Inward Remittance Certificate

Panama mandates phased electronic invoicing via the DGI e-Tax platform under Resolution No. 066-2022. Taxpayers must obtain an Electronic Invoicing Authorization (CFE) and transmit invoice XML data to the DGI in real or near-real time through approved API/WebService integrators or the free DGI facturador. Each fiscal invoice must carry the CFE stamp and a secure access code; temporary and definitive implementation phases are based on taxpayer size and sector.

Although Panama’s free-market foreign-exchange regime does not impose a statutory repatriation deadline, the 180-day benchmark is applied by tax authorities as an evidentiary standard for inward remittance documentation. False or unsupported export zero-rating triggers retroactive ITBMS settlement at 7% plus monthly interest (5%), fines from 50% to 100% of the omitted tax, and criminal tax-fraud penalties of up to five years’ imprisonment under Articles 708 and 711 of the Panamanian Fiscal Code.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full dollarization means absolute convertibility on both current and capital account. There are no exchange controls, no central-bank authorization for inward remittances, no capital-account declaration, and no restrictions on dividend, profit or capital repatriation to non-resident shareholders. Because the entity applies Panama's territorial tax principle (Codigo Fiscal), income from legal and accounting anti-dumping defense services rendered to foreign exporters is extraterritorial-source, not taxable in Panama, and may be freely distributed abroad. Residual friction exists only at bank level through source-of-funds verification and OFAC/UN/EU sanctions screening.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama does not require surrender, repatriation timelines, or conversion ratios for goods or services export proceeds. Service fees for the anti-dumping defense consultancy may be held indefinitely in USD in Panama or offshore. Only monitoring obligations apply: SBP AML/CFT suspicious-transaction reporting (Law 23 of 2015; Acuerdo 05-2015 as amended) and physical-cash customs declarations over USD 10,000 under cash-in-transit rules.

Central bank supervision: No central bank exists. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panama (SBP) is the exclusive banking and AML/CFT supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panama (state-owned) acts as fiscal agent, domestic clearing and settlement operator of SIPA/SICA, and custodian of public deposits; the Ministry of Economy and Finance and the Direccion General de Ingresos administer fiscal and tax controls. Monetary policy is fixed by statute: the US dollar is legal tender and the balboa (PAB) is a non-issued accounting unit pegged 1:1 to USD (Currency Law of 1904).

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% on Panama-source taxable profits of a Sociedad Anónima (S.A.); foreign-source income is excluded under Panama's territorial taxation principle (Código Fiscal, Article 694), provided the anti-dumping trade defense consulting services are performed and economically managed from outside Panamanian territory.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

5% on dividends paid out of Panama-taxed profits; 10% on dividends paid out of untaxed or foreign-source profits (Código Fiscal, Articles 300-302). The S.A. must self-withhold and remit the applicable rate at each dividend decree; the 10% rate is the default for distributions of exempt or offshore profits of a standard consultancy holding S.A.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after the fiscal year-end; for a December 31 year-end, the return is due in March of the following year, with the exact due date assigned by the last digit of the RUC (administrative calendar, typically March 15-31); no blanket extension is available except by formal DGI petition.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File mandatory where gross annual revenue exceeds USD 2,500,000 (B/. 2,500,000), or where the taxpayer has related-party transactions with entities in a low-tax jurisdiction regardless of revenue; Master File mandatory where the multinational group's consolidated annual revenue equals or exceeds EUR 750,000,000 (approximately USD 800,000,000). The annual Transfer Pricing Informative Return (Formulario 300) is due with Form 202 within three months of fiscal year-end.

Economic substance scope: Economic Substance Regulations (Law 70 of 2019, effective January 1, 2020) apply to tax-resident S.A.s generating income from relevant activities, including consultancy and trade defense services. The company must prove in its annual ESR return (filed via E-Tax 2.0) that it is managed and directed in Panama, maintains adequate local premises, employs adequate qualified local staff, and incurs adequate local operating expenditure; penalties range from USD 10,000 to USD 100,000 per year, with repeat non-compliance exposed to penalties of up to USD 1,000,000 and cancellation of ESR standing.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion (Defraudación Tributaria)

Statutory sanction: Fine of 2 to 5 times the evaded tax; aggravated surcharge of 50% where evasion exceeds B/. 300,000 (Law 38 of 2016); ex officio assessment and preventive embargo of assets; imprisonment of 2 to 5 years for evasion above B/. 300,000, extended to 5 to 10 years where false documents are used.

Director personal liability: Directors and resident agents who sign fraudulent returns are jointly and severally liable for corporate tax debt; exposure to civil restitution, asset seizure, travel bans, and imprisonment; professional licenses revoked.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: ESR fines under Law 70 of 2019: USD 10,000-100,000 per year; repeated non-compliance up to USD 1,000,000; DGI recharacterization of income as taxable Panama-source at 25% plus surcharges; cancellation of the municipal Aviso de Operación.
