Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, Panama; taxpayer portal: https://www.dgi.gob.pa (Panamanian government electronic tax services portal).

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panama S.A. must be incorporated through a local registered agent, obtain a business operation notice (Aviso de Operación), and register with the Public Registry. The entity must then apply to the DGI for a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) by filing the public deed, registered agent acceptance, registered office certificate, identity documents of officers/shareholders, and the completed RUC application. The same RUC is used for corporate income tax and ITBMS registration.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope — A foreign-owned Panama S.A. must be incorporated through a local registered agent, obtain a business operation notice (Aviso de Operación), and register with the Public Registry. The entity must then apply to the DGI for a RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) by filing the public deed, registered agent acceptance, registered office certificate, identity documents of officers/shareholders, and the completed RUC application. The same RUC is used for corporate income tax and ITBMS registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch election exists under Panamanian law. A Sociedad Anónima is automatically taxed as a separate entity. Optional regimes for qualifying businesses include the Multinational Headquarters (SEM) license under Law 41/2007, which grants special tax incentives, and DGI administrative classifications such as 'Gran Contribuyente'. No fiscal-transparent election is available for ordinary S.A. entities.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory economic turnover threshold; registration is mandatory from the first taxable transaction. PAB 0.00

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for 0% ITBMS when the sale is made to a foreign customer, title and risk transfer abroad, and the goods physically leave Panama with a valid customs export declaration. Offshore services qualify if the beneficiary is established outside Panama and the services are effectively used or consumed in a foreign jurisdiction; local invoicing to a Panamanian entity is insufficient. Taxpayers must retain contracts, invoices, proof of export shipment, and evidence of foreign inward remittance to substantiate zero-rating.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera - DUA) stamped by Panamanian Customs
  • Bill of Lading / Airway Bill or equivalent transport document
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT/foreign payment credit advice from a Panamanian bank
  • Commercial invoice and contract demonstrating 0% ITBMS treatment
  • Loading or customs exit record confirming physical departure of goods

Panama enforces phased mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica). Designated taxpayers must use DGI-authorized billing software or service providers, transmit XML invoices through DGI-approved APIs or platforms, and obtain a DGI-issued authorization code/digital stamp before the invoice has fiscal effect. Exports must be invoiced with ITBMS 0% and cite the supporting export declaration. Smaller non-designated taxpayers continue under transitional manual invoicing until the DGI implementation calendar reaches their segment.

DGI and Panamanian Customs (Aduana Nacional) cross-match export declarations, ITBMS returns, and foreign banking remittances. If zero-rating is claimed fraudulently or funds are not repatriated within the confirmed period, the 0% rate is revoked; ITBMS is reassessed at 7% on the gross invoice value, plus default interest and late-payment surcharges. Administrative fines can range up to 100% of the omitted tax, and criminal tax fraud penalties may apply, including imprisonment of up to 5 years and suspension or cancellation of export privileges.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized economy: the U.S. Dollar (USD) is legal tender, the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) exists only as divisional coins at a fixed 1:1 parity, and there is no central-bank-issued fiduciary money. Both the current account and capital account are freely convertible; no exchange controls, prior authorization, mandatory conversion, repatriation timelines, or profit remittance restrictions apply to Sociedad Anónima structures. Statutory dividend withholding (generally 5% to 10% depending on the ISR regime and source classification) is triggered only on distributions attributable to Panamanian-source taxable income; foreign-source PR retainer revenue is outside the territorial tax base and may be remitted free of Panamanian withholding. Capital contributions may be repatriated at any time without central-bank consent because no exchange-control authority exists; the only reporting obligation is the SBP transaction-coding and suspicious-activity-reporting regime under Decree Law 2 of 2008 and Law 23 of 2015.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable: Panama imposes no export-proceeds surrender obligation, no repatriation deadline, and no mandatory conversion ratio for receipts derived from overseas public-relations and communication services. Retainer and campaign fees may be retained offshore indefinitely or banked domestically in USD without conversion. The only administrative requirements are accurate SBP balance-of-payments purpose-coding on each inbound transfer and standard AML/CFT record-keeping by the receiving bank.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the statutory banking, foreign-exchange conduct, and AML/CFT supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as fiscal agent and de facto clearing-house manager in the absence of a conventional central bank; the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF) compiles external-account and balance-of-payments statistics under the dollarized regime.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Mercury Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. Panama does not issue an EMI-equivalent licence and does not passport foreign e-money licences; the SBP Fintech Innovation Hub (Acuerdo 007-2018) offers a regulatory sandbox but does not confer depository status on offshore digital banks. Offshore EMIs (for example Mercury, Payoneer, MultiPass, or UK-licensed EMI branches) are acceptable only as auxiliary collection rails for retainer ACH and wire receipts, not as the primary treasury custody account. Legal constraints: a foreign EMI taking deposits from a Panamanian S.A. without an SBP licence cannot legally characterize the balance as a guaranteed deposit, and the structure may hinder the economic-substance audit trail; accordingly, use a local tier-1 account as the clearing node and sweep EMI balances into it within 24 hours.
