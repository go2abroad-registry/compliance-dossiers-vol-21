Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá - Dirección General del Registro Público, Sección de Fundaciones de Interés Privado (Public Registry of Panama, Private Interest Foundations Section).

Governing statute

Republic of Panama - Ley N 25 de 12 de junio de 1995 (Régimen Jurídico de las Fundaciones de Interés Privado), as amended by Ley N 13 de 2014, Ley N 47 de 2013 and Ley N 18 de 2018; supplementary application of the Código de Comercio where not inconsistent. Key sections: Article 1 (legal personality), Article 3 (mandatory charter contents), Article 12 (minimum endowment of US$10,000), Articles 17-19 (Foundation Council), Article 20 (Protector), Articles 25-28 (registration and public filing effects).

Formation / constitutional filings

Acta Constitutiva de Fundación de Interés Privado (Foundation Charter) executed before a Notary of the Republic of Panama, together with the Reglamento (internal regulations) and the Aviso de Aceptación of the Foundation Council members and Protector.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 13 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Minimum endowment of US$10,000.00 (or equivalent value in cash, securities or movable assets) must be declared and subscribed by the Founder under Article 12 of Ley 25 de 1995. Panama enforces no statutory escrow deposit at formation; a binding promise of contribution recorded in the charter satisfies the subscription requirement, and the Foundation Council must acknowledge receipt of the endowment at its first meeting. No capital stamp duty applies and non-resident founders may hold full ownership without currency controls.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety inspection and annual certificate from the Cuerpo de Bomberos de Panamá (extinguishers, hydrants, evacuation routes); structural load and land-use clearance from the Alcaldía and SINAPROC (Sistema Nacional de Protección Civil); occupational health and safety registration with MITRADEL - Dirección de Higiene y Seguridad Industrial, including employer accident-risk classification enrollment with the Caja de Seguro Social (CSS) and approval of the terminal emergency response plan.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); portal: Sede Electrónica de la DGI at https://dgi.mef.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panama Private Interest Foundation (PIF) must first be registered in the Public Registry's Fundaciones de Interés Privado section. For tax identity, the PIF must obtain a Registro Único de Contribuyentes (RUC) by filing the DGI application form (Formulario 1-01) accompanied by (i) the foundation deed and registration certificate, (ii) identification and power of attorney of the resident legal representative, (iii) proof of registered address in Panama, and (iv) the Aviso de Operación if the foundation will carry out any commercial activity. The DGI issues the RUC and, where applicable, a separate ITBMS registration number for VAT purposes. Foreign entities have no direct online registration; a licensed tax agent must file physically or via the DGI Sede Electrónica proxy account.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No U.S.-style check-the-box election exists under Panama law. A PIF is statutorily deemed a separate hybrid legal person subject to territorial taxation on Panama-source income and exempt on foreign-source income. Hybrid mismatch arrangements affecting the PIF may be bilateral or multilateral notified under Law 52 of 2016 (OECD BEPS), but no unilateral ruling election is available; a taxpayer may, however, submit an advance interpretation request to the DGI on source classification of specific transport revenues.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No statutory registration threshold in Panamanian Balboas (PAB/USD). Any entity or individual carrying out habitual taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama, including cross-border trucking operators, must register for ITBMS before starting operations; the practical threshold is PAB 0.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating (0% ITBMS) applies to exports of tangible goods and to services rendered to non-residents when the services are physically performed outside Panama or used wholly abroad. For cross-border trucking under the TIR carnet, zero-rating is available for international freight legs on the basis that the transport service comprises export of services; the carrier must demonstrate that the customer is a non-resident and that the transport does not constitute internal Panamanian cabotage. A TIR operation that only transits Panama without a final domestic unloading point qualifies as transit with 0% ITBMS; the Panamanian portion of a cross-border haul may be zero-rated only when the consignor/consignee is foreign and the goods leave the national customs territory.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación Electrónica via SIGA)
  • TIR Carnet and Bill of Lading / CMR consignment note (Carta de Porte)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit evidence of foreign exchange repatriation

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Factura Electrónica) applies under DGI Resolución DGI-201-8589 of 2017 and subsequent amendments, using real-time XML submission through the Sede Electrónica web service. Taxpayers must obtain an X.509 digital certificate from the DGI or an authorized certification authority and send each invoice to the DGI platform for immediate validation; accepted invoices are digitally stamped with a unique authorization code and QR code. API integration (SOAP/REST) is prescribed for high-volume taxpayers; the TIR carrier must issue a Factura Electrónica de Exportación for all zero-rated export legs.

Panama's DGI and Aduana (Customs) may revisit zero-rated export claims within 5 years. If the physical exit of goods or the quality of an exported service is not substantiated, the taxpayer is required to repay the uncollected ITBMS at the standard 7% rate, plus adjustment for inflation, monthly surcharges, and fines of up to 100% of the tax owed; criminal penalties for evasive or fraudulent export invoicing include imprisonment under the Fiscal Code and Law 52 of 2016 (economic fraud). Additionally, a clawback of any refunded input VAT is mandatory with interest from the date of refund.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized open economy; the US Dollar is legal tender under Law 84 of 1904, and the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) exists solely as fractional coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity. Both current-account and capital-account transactions are completely liberalized: no exchange-control law, no central-bank prior approval, no repatriation restrictions, and no tax on capital/profit remittances under the territorial tax regime. The only de facto friction is SBP-mandated AML and source-of-funds due diligence applied by commercial banks, which can delay high-value transfers and requires a documented audit trail.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. USD proceeds from TIR cross-border trucking services may be freely held, retained offshore, or converted without reporting to any monetary authority; only bank-level suspicious transaction reports (STR) to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) apply on unusual or cash-heavy patterns.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (Superintendency of Banks of Panama, SBP) - the sole banking and FX-market supervisor; Panama has no central bank, and Banco Nacional de Panamá acts only as fiscal agent of the State, not as a monetary authority.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility Rating: Conditional / Medium as a supplementary treasury rail only. Legal constraints: Decreto Ley 9 de 1998 (Ley Bancaria) prohibits unlicensed entities from carrying on deposit-taking or banking business in Panama; therefore foreign digital EMIs (e.g., Wise Business, Airwallex, Mercury) cannot serve as the PIF's primary domiciled treasury account. They may be used as offshore collection/disbursement wallets swept periodically into the Panama bank via SWIFT MT103, but should not hold the TIR operating float for extended periods. The local fintech regime (Ley 162 de 2020, Régimen de Innovación Financiera Interoperable) provides a regulatory sandbox but creates no standalone EMI license equivalent to EU EMI passports.

Turnover: Ley 25 de 1995 (as amended) does not impose a mandatory statutory audit solely by reason of the Private Interest Foundation form. A certified independent audit is triggered by factual and regulatory criteria: (i) gross annual revenue equal to or exceeding USD 3,000,000 under DGI Resolution No. 201-1356 of 2006 and DGI administrative practice; (ii) classification of the foundation as a Gran Contribuyente (Large Taxpayer) by the DGI; (iii) participation in regulated financial, insurance, factoring, fiduciary, or securities activities; (iv) the filing of tax refund claims or loss carry-forwards beyond DGI review thresholds; or (v) lender or ATTT contractual covenants requiring audited statements in connection with cross-border trucking financing and fleet authorizations. Below these thresholds, the PIF must maintain complete internal books and records sufficient for the DGI to verify territorial-source income, but no audit report is required to be filed.
