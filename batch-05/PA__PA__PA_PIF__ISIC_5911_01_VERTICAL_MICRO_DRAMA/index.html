Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI), an autonomous unit of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF); official e-filing portal 'e-Tax 2.0' (https://etax.mef.gob.pa; informational portal www.dgi.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Private Interest Foundation (PIF) under Law 25 of 1995 before a licensed Panamanian attorney and inscribe the foundation charter (Ficha) in the Public Registry of Panama, appointing the mandatory Resident Agent. 2) Through the Resident Agent, apply in e-Tax 2.0 for the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes); a PIF with no Panamanian-source income obtains a passive/inactive RUC with no monthly ISR obligations. 3) If the PIF will earn Panamanian-source business revenue (e.g., Panama end-user app sales), file the Aviso de Operación, enroll for ITBMS, and obtain the appropriate tax regime status. 4) For non-established foreign digital supply to Panama consumers, register under the DGI's digital-services provider path, either directly or through a designated local fiscal representative. 5) Comply with beneficial-ownership registration applicable to foundations and retain the registration certificate for Apple IAP / Google Play / Stripe merchant KYC. 6) Pay the flat annual registry fee (tasa única anual) under the PIF regime, which does not constitute income tax.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama has no US-style check-the-box election and no domestic hybrid mismatch election. The PIF is an autonomous patrimony with separate legal personality, taxed by statute under the territorial principle: only Panamanian-source income is subject to ISR, while foreign-source distribution revenue from the vertical micro-drama catalogue is excluded automatically. Available elections: (i) election to maintain effective management, servers, licensing contracts, and exploitation activity outside Panama so that all streaming revenue remains foreign-source; (ii) for any Panamanian-source business income, election of the alternative simplified ISR base (CAIR regime on gross receipts) instead of the ordinary 25% net-income computation where more favourable; (iii) election to appoint a local fiscal representative for ITBMS compliance as a non-established digital service provider; (iv) election to treat the PIF as the sole taxpayer distinct from the founder and beneficiaries, preserving separateness for all filings; OECD hybrid mismatch rules are not enacted in Panamanian domestic law.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No monetary de-minimis threshold: any PIF habitually transferring taxable services to persons located in Panama (including app subscriptions, in-app purchases, or digital content sales through Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, or Stripe) must obtain RUC and register for ITBMS before the first taxable supply. A low-turnover exception does not exist in the Código Fiscal; the threshold is effectively B/. 0 (Balboa is pegged 1:1 to USD, hence no separate local-currency variance).

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of services are subject to ITBMS at 0% where the service is effectively used, exploited, or consumed outside Panama and the recipient is non-resident. The global OTT distribution of vertical micro-dramas to non-Panama subscribers qualifies as an exported service, evidenced by foreign billing addresses, IP geolocation, and delivery of content beyond Panamanian territory. Revenue identified as Panama-source remains taxable at 7% regardless of payment rail. If Apple IAP or Google Play acts as merchant of record and collects Panamanian ITBMS from Panama users, the PIF must retain the platform's tax certificate; for Stripe or direct merchant collections, the PIF self-assesses. No FX repatriation deadline applies (0 days): Panama is fully dollarized (Balboa–USD parity) with no exchange controls or mandatory inward-remittance period for service exports.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) - NOT APPLICABLE for pure OTT digital exports; required only if physical media or merchandise is shipped from Panamanian territory.
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill - NOT APPLICABLE for app-store digital delivery; applicable exclusively to physical goods consignments.
  • Inward Remittance Certificate - SWIFT MT103, bank credit advice, or payment-rail payout statement (Apple/Google/Stripe) evidencing settlement of foreign-sourced proceeds into the PIF's bank account.
  • Export Services Invoice - commercial invoice expressly marked 'Servicios exportados - ITBMS 0%', identifying the foreign counterparty, contract reference, and foreign payment terms.
  • Platform Settlement Reports - App Store Connect, Google Play Console, and Stripe exports segmented by billing country, demonstrating the territorial split between Panama and foreign consumers.
  • Foreign-Domicile Substantiation - IP geolocation logs, billing addresses, end-user terms of use, and offshore distribution or licensing agreements proving consumption outside Panama.

Panama does not operate real-time fiscal clearance, mandatory API integration, or a digital stamp for B2C retail digital transactions. The DGI's electronic invoicing regime (Facturación Electrónica) requires B2B invoices and credit notes to be issued in XML and transmitted through e-Tax 2.0 with DGI-issued authorization; app-store consumer micro-transactions are substantiated by platform receipts and periodic settlement statements rather than individual fiscal invoices. The PIF must file monthly ITBMS return Form 611 and retain structured territory-level settlement reports from Apple, Google, and Stripe for reconciliation; no blockchain seal or continuous-validation mandate applies.

The DGI strictly interprets the 0% export rate and may reassess within the general five-year limitation period (extendable on interruption or where no return was filed). Unsubstantiated zero-rating is clawed back at the 7% ITBMS rate plus moratory interest, with surcharges applicable for non-declared operations. False export claims and fabricated foreign-recipient documentation constitute 'defraudación fiscal', attracting significant administrative fines, potential retroactive reassessment of all unreported Panama digital sales, joint liability of the Resident Agent and fiscal representative for knowing participation, and criminal prosecution under the Código Penal for intentional evasion. Audit focus includes the integrity of the territory-split algorithm, the economic substance of the PIF's Panamanian administration, and the completeness of platform settlement documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized: the U.S. Dollar (USD) is legal tender and the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) circulates only as subsidiary coinage at a 1:1 fixed parity. Current-account and capital-account operations are fully convertible without prior license, repatriation approval, or quantitative limitation, ranking Panama as a soft-currency-free, fully convertible regime. Corporate profits, capital injections, and streaming-platform remittances (Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, Stripe) may be transferred freely into, out of, or within Panama; the only relevant tax filter is territorial — under the reformed Law 120 of 2019, foreign-source income of a Panamanian legal entity is generally exempt, while Panamanian-source income may attract a 10% to 15% withholding where a local intermediary distributes dividends or royalties to the Private Interest Foundation (PIF). No exchange-control dividend blockage or profit-remittance hurdle applies.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no export-proceeds surrender requirement: there is no statutory timeline for repatriation, no minimum conversion ratio, and no obligation to exchange foreign currency into local-tender Balboas because the Balboa is a nominal coin-only unit backed by the circulating U.S. Dollar. Revenues from the global distribution of vertical micro-dramas (including monthly settlements from Apple, Google, and Stripe) may be retained indefinitely in USD in any Panamanian bank account without declaration to a central bank. The only administrative formality is the bank's internal classification of the credit as 'trade-service proceeds' or 'capital contribution' for the SBP's statistical balance-of-payments warehouse; commercial invoices, platform commission statements, and the treasury routing narrative are sufficient evidence.

Central bank supervision: Panama does not maintain a central bank in the traditional sense; the national banking and monetary apparatus is administered by the Superintendency of Banks of Panama (Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá, SBP) under Decreto Ley 9 de 1998 and its revisions. The Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as a fiscal agent and clearing trustee, while the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) enforces AML/CFT supervision for non-bank sectors. No exchange-control authority exists, and no separate Foreign Exchange Administration issues permits.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary collection-and-disbursement rail, Low as the primary treasury mandate. Panama's Ley 197 de 2020 (Fintech Law), implemented through SBP agreement SBP-CGC-004-2023, authorizes licensed Electronic Payment Institutions (EPIs) to provide payment and e-money services; however, a Private Interest Foundation is not itself an EPI and cannot intermediate third-party funds. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and Ebury can generally accept a Panamanian PIF as a client for multi-currency treasury sweeps, but the SBP requires that any Panama-licensed entity involved in money transmission be supervised; hence the PIF should engage EMIs solely for ancillary FX/collection needs while keeping Apple, Google, and Stripe settlements routed directly to the Tier-1 Panamanian bank account. Legal constraint: the PIF, unless separately authorized, may not act as a payment institution for its streaming subsidiaries.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The PIF's initial foundation endowment (minimum US$10,000 or the greater amount set in the Reglamento) and any further shareholder/benefactor contributions are routed via SWIFT MT103 from the founder's foreign bank to the PIF's USD account at a Panama Tier-1 bank. Panama does not operate a central-bank purpose-code registry; the remitting bank should enter in Field 70 the narrative 'CONTRIBUCION DE CAPITAL PIF / PA_PIF__ISIC_5911_01' and in Field 72 any additional 'APORTE FIDEICOMISO' reference, enabling the SBP's statistical classification as portfolio/equity capital inflow. The receiving bank will apply SBP-CGC-DDF-F-07 source-of-funds declarations, but no exchange-control approval, deposit license, or ministry authorization is required; recommended injection size is US$25,000 to satisfy foundation capitalization and bank reserve-minimum requirements. Funds must then be moved to the operational OpCo account for Apple/Google platform working capital only upon a formal Foundation Council resolution.Operational segregation: (i) royalty and production payouts to writers, actors, localization vendors, and audio studios are executed through SWIFT MT103 with payment narrative '/REC/ROYALTIES PRODUCCION MICRO-DRAMAS VERTICALES'; (ii) commercial letters of credit (MT700 sight LC) are optional only for large media-agency procurement contracts and are normally substituted by cash-against-documents; (iii) intra-Panama vendor payments use the Cámara de Compensación Interbancaria de Panamá (CCIP) ACH/TEF instant-credit rails; and (iv) high-volume micro-payouts to short-drama cast and user-generated creators are batched via Stripe Connect or Payoneer using the Panamanian bank proxy account to avoid per-transaction SWIFT costs. Apple IAP, Google Play Billing, and Stripe revenue settlements arrive in USD through their respective US/EU acquiring rails and are credited to the PIF's operational USD account as trade-service proceeds.Deliverable forward contracts (plain vanilla) against USD for EUR, GBP, JPY, KRW, and BRL, executed by Panama-domiciled licensed banks (Banistmo, BAC Credomatic, Global Bank) under the International Banking Center framework.Offshore non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) on Latin American and African restricted currencies (ARS, NGN, KES, EGP) for production spend forecasts, available through New York or Singapore desks of the banks' parent groups without any Panamanian central-bank authorization.Cross-currency basis swaps (USD versus EUR, GBP, and JPY) for converting multi-currency Apple/Google settlement pools into USD on a staggered roll-over schedule.OTC FX options (sold as zero-premium collars) on EUR/USD, GBP/USD, and KRW/USD for latent content-sale exposure, permissible under Panama's free exchange regime and offered by SBP-licensed universal banks.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility rating: Medium as a supplementary collection-and-disbursement rail, Low as the primary treasury mandate. Panama's Ley 197 de 2020 (Fintech Law), implemented through SBP agreement SBP-CGC-004-2023, authorizes licensed Electronic Payment Institutions (EPIs) to provide payment and e-money services; however, a Private Interest Foundation is not itself an EPI and cannot intermediate third-party funds. Offshore digital EMIs such as Wise Business, Airwallex, and Ebury can generally accept a Panamanian PIF as a client for multi-currency treasury sweeps, but the SBP requires that any Panama-licensed entity involved in money transmission be supervised; hence the PIF should engage EMIs solely for ancillary FX/collection needs while keeping Apple, Google, and Stripe settlements routed directly to the Tier-1 Panamanian bank account. Legal constraint: the PIF, unless separately authorized, may not act as a payment institution for its streaming subsidiaries.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 2,000,000 gross revenue or related-party transactions exceeding USD 1,000,000

Economic substance scope: Panama ESR (Law 254 of 2021) requires substance in the territory for geographically mobile and specified services (including distribution, financing, and IP exploitation). A PIF producing and distributing digital drama content must demonstrate local decision-making, staff, and offices proportionate to its income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud penalties: USD 1,000 to USD 50,000 fine plus 50% to 200% of the tax evaded; statutory limitation extended to 8 years.

Director personal liability: Directors may be personally liable for the corporate tax debt, travel bans, and up to 5 years imprisonment under Article 841 of the Tax Code.
Money Laundering (AML)

Statutory sanction: Under Law 23 of 2015, fines up to USD 5,000,000 or double the value of the assets laundered; risk of freezing and forfeiture of the foundation’s assets.

Director personal liability: Officers can be criminally liable with imprisonment of 5 to 10 years and personal disqualification from managing regulated entities.
Economic Substance Non-compliance

Statutory sanction: Penalties up to USD 750,000 under Law 254; additional annual penalties of USD 100,000 for continued non-resident substance failure; risk of mandatory deregistration.

Director personal liability: Directors may be held accountable for restitution of tax benefits applied, and bans on holding office in any Panamanian entity for 5 years.
