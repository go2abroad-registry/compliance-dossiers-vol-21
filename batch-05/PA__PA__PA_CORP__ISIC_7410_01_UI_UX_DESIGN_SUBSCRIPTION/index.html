Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized jurisdiction with complete de jure and de facto convertibility on both the capital account and the current account. There are no exchange control statutes, no prior central-bank authorizations for capital movements, no surrender requirements, and no restrictions on dividend distributions, profit repatriation, intercompany loans, or service fee remittances. Under the territorial income tax model of the Código Fiscal de Panamá, a Sociedad Anónima deriving design subscription income exclusively from non-Panamanian clients and digital platforms is generally exempt from Panamanian income tax, provided the income lacks Panamanian source situs. Dividend distributions to non-resident shareholders are not subject to Panamanian withholding tax. The only compliance conditions precedent are: (i) maintaining an active Registered Agent; (ii) filing the annual Informe Anual with the Registro Público; (iii) paying the biennial corporate franchise tax; (iv) holding a valid RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) issued by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI); and (v) filing Form P.F. 120 for the S.A. even where taxable income is nil. The use of Stripe Billing and Wise as settlement channels raises no convertibility obstacles because USD is the functional and reporting currency of the entity.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export proceeds surrender, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation deadline exists in Panamanian law. Design subscription receipts in USD may be retained onshore or offshore indefinitely without any obligation to convert into Balboa coinage or receive any administrative endorsement. The absence of surrender rules is structural: since no national central bank issues paper Balboa currency and the USD circulates as legal tender, there is no regulatory mechanism or circular requiring conversion. In practice, the S.A. should preserve the MT103 credit notifications and payment-processor settlement reports to evidence the export of digital services in conformity with the territorial regime and to satisfy independent AML transactional monitoring.

Central bank supervision: Republic of Panama operates without an issuing central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the prudential supervisor of the banking sector under Decree Law 9 of 1998, as amended by Law 21 of 2017. Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the fiscal agent of the national government and manages the dollarized monetary system. The United States Dollar (USD) is legal tender throughout the Republic pursuant to Law 84 of 1941; the Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a nominal unit of account issued only as coinage at a fixed statutory parity of exactly 1:1 with the USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-HIGH. Panama has not enacted a dedicated Electronic Money Institution (EMI) licensing regime, so a fully indigenous Panamanian EMI license is unavailable. Cross-border EMIs such as Wise, Payoneer, and airwall-style clearing platforms may lawfully receive and settle design subscription funds for the benefit of the Panamanian S.A., because Panama imposes no exchange or payment-routing control. However, the S.A. must still maintain and disclose a domestic USD settlement account at an SBP-licensed bank as the compliance anchor and as the treasury concentration point. Wise Business is generally functional for collecting USD from US and UK subscription clients and onward-settling to a Panamanian account; Stripe Billing similarly settles in USD via Stripe's Panamanian acquirer arrangement or through US partner banks. The principal constraint is not legal permissibility but bank counsel's assessment: the domestic bank will consider the EMI a third-party processor requiring source-of-funds documentation and a processor agreement on file.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Panama Balboa (PAB) 5,000,000 (USD-equivalent 5,000,000) in annual gross revenue triggers mandatory Transfer Pricing Documentation. Additionally, if controlled transactions with related parties exceed PAB 1,000,000, the taxpayers must prepare and maintain a Local File and Master File regardless of revenue threshold.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR), enacted under Law 254 of 2021 and related implementing decrees, require a Panamanian entity engaged in relevant activities, including intellectual property exploitation and digital services, to demonstrate adequate physical offices, qualified staff, core income-generating activities, and local decision-making. For a UI/UX design-as-a-service subscription company, DGI scrutiny focuses on the substance of employees and strategy decisions in Panama; inadequate substance can cause recharacterization of income, denial of tax benefits, and a presumption that the entity is a shell company.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Declarations

Statutory sanction: Administrative penalty of 50-100% of the omitted tax, monthly late-payment interest, forfeiture of carried-over losses and deduction claims, and criminal conviction punishable by imprisonment for tax fraud under the Panama Penal Code and tax procedure rules.

Director personal liability: Directors, officers, and tax agents who sign false returns are jointly and severally liable for the omitted tax and penalties; civil restitution may include seizure of personal assets, travel bans, and disqualification from holding directorships; repeated violation mandates imprisonment.
Shell Company / Inadequate Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Denial of treaty benefits, recharacterization of profits as Panama-source, application of the 25% CIT with penal interest, cancellation of economic substance registration, strike-off of the entity from the Public Registry, and additional fines under the ESR legal framework.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal liability where the company is a façade; the judicial authority may pierce the corporate veil, impose civil restitution equal to the profits routed abroad, issue personal travel bans, and pursue criminal charges of fraud or aggravated tax evasion.
Anti-Money Laundering Deficiency and Illegal Financial Flows

Statutory sanction: Fines from USD 10,000 to USD 1,000,000 per violation, suspension of the RUC, prohibition against conducting financial transactions, confiscation of proceeds, and criminal prosecution for money laundering under Panama's AML/CFT regime.

Director personal liability: Compliance officers and directors may be held criminally liable and face imprisonment of up to 12 years, personal monetary fines, seizure of personal estates, travel restrictions, and barring from serving regulated financial or corporate positions.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Mandatory adjustment of taxable income by the benefit-derived amount, administrative penalty equal to 25-50% of the adjusted tax differential, late payment interest, restriction of refund claims, and publication of the taxpayer in the DGI non-compliance register.

Director personal liability: Directors who sign incomplete TP disclosures are subject to joint liability for the corrected tax, civil damages for misrepresentation, travel bans pending resolution, and in aggravated cases, criminal prosecution for false documentation in tax procedures.
