Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama. Primary electronic portal: SERT (Sistema Electrónico de Recaudación Tributaria) at sert.mef.gob.pa. E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama operates a pure territorial income-tax system and does not provide US-style check-the-box pass-through elections or hybrid-mismatch elective rules. The PIF is by default a separately taxable juridical person. Available statutory elections include: (a) election to apply the simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime for businesses with annual gross revenue up to USD 75,000 under Ley 119 de 2024; (b) election to obtain ITBMS exemption certificates for export purchases, subject to DGI exporter authorization; and (c) charter-level allocation of foreign-source income to beneficiaries, which remains outside Panamanian taxation by virtue of territoriality.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

9%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general registration threshold: every person engaged in taxable economic activity in Panama must register for ITBMS regardless of turnover. Ley 119 de 2024 creates an optional simplified small-taxpayer regime for businesses with annual gross receipts of USD 75,000 or less, with fixed quarterly ITBMS payments in lieu of full monthly declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rating applies to (i) tangible goods physically exported from Panamanian inventory to a foreign destination and (ii) services rendered for the exclusive use and benefit of non-residents abroad. For beauty cosmetics DTC online retail, sales qualify as zero-rated exports only when goods are shipped from Panama by international courier or freight and the shipment is declared in SICE. Sales fulfilled from foreign warehouses or third-country fulfillment centers are outside Panama's territorial ITBMS nexus and are neither subject to ITBMS nor eligible for export refunds, whereas deliveries to consumers within Panama attract the 9% ITBMS rate.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DUA) validated in the Panama SICE export system
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure of goods
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by a Panamanian bank evidencing credit of export proceeds

Mandatory electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) applies through the SERT platform. All registered taxpayers must issue XML-format e-invoices via SERT or an authorized e-invoicing service provider, and each invoice receives a unique DGI security code and must be transmitted to SERT for validation within one calendar day. Export transactions must be flagged with the Exportación transaction type and reference the corresponding SICE export declaration number. Panama does not operate continuous real-time fiscal stamping clearance equivalent to Mexico's CFDI, but SERT rejects non-compliant invoice batches and requires correction and resubmission within the declaration period.

DGI systematically cross-matches zero-rated export lines in ITBMS returns against SICE export declarations, transport documents, and bank remittance certificates. Unsubstantiated zero-ratings are reclassified as domestic taxable sales and clawed back with a 1.5% monthly surcharge and interest, plus administrative fines of up to 300% of the omitted tax for intentional evasion, statutory penalties of up to USD 50,000 under Ley 119 de 2024 for documentary non-compliance, suspension of exporter status and RUC cancellation, and criminal tax fraud exposure with imprisonment of up to 8 years where forged export documentation is used.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is fully dollarized (U.S. dollar is legal tender) and applies unrestricted convertibility for both current-account and capital-account transactions. There is no exchange control regime, no central bank approval requirement, and no distinction between resident and non-resident for FX purposes. Profit remittances, capital endowment repatriations, and trade settlements may be freely executed in U.S. dollars or any convertible currency. The only practical hurdles are banking AML/KYC due-diligence compliance, correspondent bank screening, and Panamanian tax reporting obligations on Panamanian-source income.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no obligation to surrender or repatriate export proceeds. E-commerce export receipts from cosmetics retail may be retained in USD or other currencies, held locally or offshore, and used freely for operating expenses, supplier settlements, and reinvestment.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) as banking and monetary regulatory supervisor; Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as fiscal agent of the Republic. Panama has no independent central bank and no foreign exchange administration.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Medium for operational collection clearing, Low for statutory capital escrow or foundation treasury. Offshore digital EMIs (e.g., Airwallex, Wise Business, Currencycloud) can provide multi-currency settlement and virtual IBAN-like clearing through reciprocal correspondent banks, but they are not licensed as Panamanian banks and cannot provide the foundation capital verification or legal certification required by a Panama Private Interest Foundation. Such EMIs are also heavily risk-averse to PIF structures and may require audited foundation records or decline onboarding without a local licensed agent.
