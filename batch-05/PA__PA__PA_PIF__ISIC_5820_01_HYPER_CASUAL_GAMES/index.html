Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá — Departamento de Fundaciones de Interés Privado, Mercantile Section, Sede Central, Panama City.

Governing statute

Law No. 25 of 12 June 1995 (Private Interest Foundations), Articles 1-3 (creation and purpose), Article 10 (minimum initial capital), Articles 13-19 (Foundation Council and Protector), as amended by Law No. 8 of 2001; supplemental application of the Registro Público directives and Law No. 23 of 2015 (Anti-Money Laundering) regarding beneficial ownership disclosure.

Formation / constitutional filings

Acta Constitutiva (Foundation Charter), executed as a public deed before a Panamanian Notary Public and presented for electronic inscription in the Folio Electrónico of the Registro Público.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 17 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

USD 10,000.00 minimum initial endowment required under Article 10 of Law 25 of 1995. The endowment may consist of money, movable or immovable property, or rights of any kind, declared in the Foundation Charter. No mandatory escrow deposit or bank certification is required; the Founder's notarized declaration in the Charter is legally sufficient for registration. For the hyper-casual gaming asset-holding scope, a declared USD 10,000.00 endowment is compliant and may be increased later without registry amendment (additional contributions are recorded in the foundation's internal patrimony register).

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official portal: Sede Electrónica DGI (https://dgi.mef.gob.pa) and PanamaTramita (panamatramita.gob.pa)

E-government filing portal

A foreign Private Interest Foundation (PIF) obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) by appointment at DGI through a resident legal representative. Submit notarized and apostilled foundation charter (Acta Constitutiva), evidence of registered address in Panama maintained by the foundation's resident agent under Ley 25 de 1995, and passport copies of foundation council members. DGI issues a provisional RUC on the spot; final RUC activates after domicile verification and, when local commercial activity exists, presentation of the municipal Aviso de Operación. PIFs with strictly foreign-source income may remain RUC-only, but must register for ITBMS to claim zero-rating and refunds.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope - PIFs under Ley 25 de 1995 are not general ISR taxpayers on foreign-source income; only Panama-sourced income is subject to corporate ISR (25% standard rate); PIFs remain subject to the fixed annual PAB 300 State tax under Ley 25, Art. 20, payable through the resident agent.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, CFC, or hybrid mismatch election exists in Panamanian tax law. Available elections: (i) simplified small taxpayer regime (régimen de pequeño contribuyente) where annual gross turnover ≤ PAB 36,000; (ii) election to register as a full ITBMS taxpayer to claim input credits and export refunds; (iii) charter-level election limiting the PIF to passive asset holding (tax-neutral) versus engaging in local trade (triggering full ISR/ITBMS exposure).

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general de minimis exemption - RUC and ITBMS registration are mandatory for any person performing taxable commercial acts in Panama; taxpayers with annual gross turnover ≤ PAB 36,000 may elect the simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' flat-rate regime instead of full periodic declaration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods and services carry the 0% ITBMS rate under Ley 8 de 2010 when (i) goods physically leave Panamanian customs territory, or (ii) services are rendered to a non-resident for exclusive use and economic exploitation outside Panama. Hyper-casual game ad-monetization services provided to AdMob/AppLovin qualify as zero-rated exports because the platform counterparty is non-resident and end-users consume content abroad. The PIF must retain platform payout statements and a digital services supply contract; registration with DGI is required to validate the 0% rate and access input-credit refunds.

  • Declaración de Exportación / DUA (customs export declaration; for digital services, DGI export-of-services notification)
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill (physical goods movement; for digital services, retained network/server evidence of foreign consumption)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / SWIFT MT103 or platform payout statement from AdMob/AppLovin payment rails
  • Export service contract with the foreign counterparty and proof that the service is used or consumed outside Panama
  • ITBMS refund application (Solicitud de Devolución) attaching zero-rated e-invoices and purchase invoices supporting input credits

Mandatory Facturación Electrónica under DGI Resolution 201-1886 and amendments: taxpayer must hold a digital signature certificate, operate a DGI-certified billing platform, and transmit each invoice in real time (online validation at issuance) to DGI's clearance system; each invoice carries a unique authorization code, XML/PDF with QR code, and DGI acknowledgement. Certified software vendors integrate via DGI REST API; unvalidated invoices are legally invalid and non-deductible.

DGI may claw back zero-rated ITBMS claimed without full substantiation within the 5-year statute of limitations, adding a 10% monthly surcharge (recargo) up to 100% of the tax, plus fines from PAB 500 to PAB 50,000 under Ley 8 de 2010; intentional evasion triggers criminal penalties under the Código Fiscal and removal from the export facilitation registry; failure to repatriate export proceeds within 180 days suspends future export benefits and may attract customs penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full convertibility applies to both current and capital account transactions. The Panamanian Balboa (PAB) is a non-circulating unit of account pegged 1:1 to USD. Profit remittances, Private Interest Foundation distributions, founder disbursements, dividends, and capital repatriation require no exchange-control license, prior central bank approval, or tax clearance certificate. The only frictions are bank-level AML documentation under Ley 23 de 2015 and Ley 254 de 2021, plus UAF reporting; no quantitative limits, queues, or convertibility hurdles exist for ad-tech revenue collected from AdMob and AppLovin.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Not applicable. Panama imposes no statutory surrender timeline, mandatory conversion ratio, or repatriation channel for export receipts. Revenues from exported digital services under ISIC 5820 hyper-casual game publishing may be retained in USD indefinitely. The only implied obligation is traceability of receipts through an SBP-licensed institution and compliance with UAF suspicious transaction reporting.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panama (SBP) - Superintendency of Banks of Panama; there is no statutory central bank. Banco Nacional de Panama acts as fiscal agency and settlement banker, while the Ministry of Economy and Finance executes monetary policy under a fully dollarized regime with the US Dollar as legal tender since 1904.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility rating: MEDIUM-LOW. Panama has no domestic digital-EMI licensing regime; foreign EMIs lack SBP regulatory standing and are not part of the national clearing system. Payoneer is operationally viable as a settlement conduit for AdMob and AppLovin marketplace payouts but should not serve as the primary liquidity rail. Wise Business lists Panama as a covered market but routinely declines Private Interest Foundations during due diligence. Mercury and Airwallex are legally unavailable to a Panama PIF without a US or EU corporate wrapper. Legal exposure also arises because EMI commingled segregation pools do not provide the deposit-protection privileges of a Panamanian bank. Recommended architecture: a local SBP-licensed bank account as the single collecting account, with any EMI used only as a receipt-boarding wallet before a weekly sweep to the bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Capital injections are routed via SWIFT MT103 from the founder's bank to the PIF account at the selected SBP bank with payment reference DOTACION FUNDACIONAL and the foundation registration number; no balance-of-payments approval is required. The remitting bank must evidence the founder's source of wealth; the receiving bank verifies the PIF's certificate of existence, council incumbency, and UAF beneficial-ownership filing. Ad revenue from AdMob and AppLovin arrives as standard SWIFT MT103 wire payments referencing the publisher ID. No mandatory purpose codes exist under Panamanian regulation, but the bank's AML questionnaire requires the commercial description ADVERTISING REVENUE - HYPER-CASUAL GAMES and the underlying agreement. Funds clear in USD via Fedwire and CHIPS through the bank's New York correspondent with same-day finality.Operating costs for developers, user-acquisition vendors, cloud infrastructure, and staff are settled by SWIFT MT103 direct payments in USD; where an intermediary bank requires a correspondent leg, MT202 COVER is used. Commercial letters of credit and documentary collections are not standard for digital service trade and are not recommended given cost and documentation burden. Domestic supplier payments can be cleared through ACH Panama operated under SBP oversight. Every outbound payment must be supported by a contract or invoice retained in the foundation's treasury file. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard Panamanian corporate income tax (Impuesto sobre la Renta) on net Panama-source taxable income, payable in USD/Balboa at parity.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% complementary dividend withholding tax on distributions made from profits previously subject to Panamanian corporate income tax (aggregate burden approximating 32.5% pre-credits); 20% on distributions made from profits not previously subject to Panamanian corporate tax (e.g., untaxed Panama-source earnings); 0% on distributions sourced entirely from foreign-source income under the territorial principle when documented, and 0% for qualifying dividend distribution under SEM regime conditions.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: B/. 1,000,000 (USD 1,000,000 at Balboa parity) of aggregate annual cross-border related-party transactions triggers the mandatory Local File annexed to Form 249 via Sistema T@T. A Master File is required where the multinational group's consolidated worldwide gross revenue equals or exceeds B/. 100,000,000. Irrespective of threshold, the PIF must retain contemporaneous transfer-pricing documentation covering the hyper-casual ad-monetization model: app-store revenue attribution, ad mediation economics, IP ownership and risk assumption, and intercompany royalty-setting.

Economic substance scope: Executive Decree 198 of 2020, implementing Law 70 of 2019 and FATF/OECD ESR standards, applies to Panamanian resident entities earning relevant income, expressly including income from intellectual property assets, licensing, financing, distribution and agency services. Obligations include performing core income-generating activities in Panama (local management decisions, qualified personnel, physical premises, proportionate operating expenditure) and filing the annual ESR declaration through the designated system with the resident agent within the statutory window following fiscal year-end. For an ISIC 5820 hyper-casual publisher, active foreign publishing may fall outside the listed relevant activities, but a structure where the PIF licenses game IP to foreign operators will attract ESR scrutiny. Failure to satisfy ESR results in administrative penalties, non-renewal of the resident agent's license, cancellation of the PIF's registration, and adverse information exchange under EOIR/transparency mechanisms.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Territorial Allocation

Statutory sanction: Fines of 2 to 5 times the tax evaded (Código Fiscal Articles 710–711), plus the full unpaid tax, default interest and a surcharge up to 50% of the tax; criminal conviction for fiscal evasion carries imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and disqualification from operating commercial entities in Panama.

Director personal liability: Directors, founder and council members who sign or approve fraudulent returns are jointly and severally liable for the tax debt; Panamanian courts may pierce the foundation's separate personality (levantamiento del velo) upon proof of fraud or abuse; criminal proceedings authorize travel bans, asset freezes, and final imprisonment of responsible natural persons.
Money Laundering / AML Infractions (Law 23 of 2015)

Statutory sanction: Administrative fines imposed by the Unidad de Analisis Financiero (UAF) of up to B/. 1,000,000 per violation; cancellation of the foundation's registration and the resident agent's license; criminal prosecution under Penal Code Article 390 for money laundering with imprisonment of 5 to 12 years and asset confiscation.

Director personal liability: Natural-person founders and directors face criminal indictment with passport surrender, travel bans, freezing of personal accounts and up to 12 years of imprisonment; civil restitution claims lie for affected parties; the corporate veil is pierced where the foundation was established for concealment or fraud.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure (ESR and EOIR)

Statutory sanction: ESR administrative penalties per Executive Decree 198 of 2020; cancellation of the PIF charter for continued non-compliance; DGI may disregard the entity and recharacterize revenues as Panama-source based on exchange-of-information disclosures; adverse international listings and consequential termination of bank accounts are realistic collateral consequences.

Director personal liability: Managers maintaining fictitious substance are personally liable for restored taxes and fines; courts may impose civil restitution orders, travel bans during investigation, and criminal fraud liability (imprisonment up to 5 years) for deliberately false ESR declarations.
Withholding and Distribution Non-Compliance (Dividends / Founder Payments)

Statutory sanction: The PIF bears solidary liability for the unpaid WHT, fines up to 100% of the withheld amount, default interest and monthly surcharges; DGI may reclassify nominal loans as constructive dividends subject to assessment at the 20% top-up rate and deny all related deductions.

Director personal liability: Directors approving non-compliant distributions are jointly and severally liable for the WHT deficiency and penalties; distributions made during insolvency are clawed back; criminal referral for breach of withholding-agent duties can generate travel bans and imprisonment of up to 4 years for the responsible persons.
