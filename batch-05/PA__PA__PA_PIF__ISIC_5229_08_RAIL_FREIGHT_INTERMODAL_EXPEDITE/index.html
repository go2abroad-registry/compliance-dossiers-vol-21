Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) — Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; online portal: DGI e-Tax 2.0 / SITE (Sistema Integrado Tributario Electrónico).

E-government filing portal

A foreign entity, including a Private Interest Foundation (PIF), must first obtain legal existence under Panamanian law by registering the foundation charter with the Public Registry of Panama. Thereafter, legal counsel or a registered agent must file the DGI taxpayer registration application (Formulario de Registro Único de Contribuyente) physically or via e-Tax 2.0, submitting the foundation charter, agent appointment, officer identification, and proof of registered address. The DGI issues the RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyente) and, where applicable, the civil/BIN certificate. For a PIF with exclusively foreign-source income, the registration may be limited to the obligation to pay the annual foundation flat tax of USD 300.

Thereafter, legal counsel or a registered agent must file the DGI taxpayer registration application (Formulario de Registro Único de Contribuyente) physically or via e-Tax 2.0, submitting the foundation charter, agent appointment, officer identification, and proof of registered address. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

No US-style check-the-box election exists. A PIF may elect in its charter to refrain from any Panamanian-source economic activity, thereby confining its tax exposure to the annual foundation flat tax (USD 300) under Law 25 of 1995. The tax treatment follows the territorial source of income; no hybrid mismatch election is recognised under Panama's domestic law, although BEPS-compliant substance requirements must be observed for the territorial exemption to hold.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

N/A - none; any entity performing taxable activities in Panama must obtain a RUC for ITBMS. There is no economic turnover threshold triggering registration.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods from Panama are zero-rated for ITBMS. To claim zero-rating, the taxpayer must prove the goods left the Panamanian customs territory and were entered in a foreign jurisdiction. Export services are treated as not subject to ITBMS if they are performed abroad or are effectively used wholly outside Panama, and the recipient is a non-resident. For a rail freight intermodal expedite business, the eligible export service must be supported by contracts, shipping documents, and evidence that the service is connected to a definable export or cross-border transport.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación / SIBUNE Customs Entry)
  • Bill of Lading (Conocimiento de Embarque / AWB for air or rail consignment)
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa / Bank Foreign Exchange Receipt)
  • Commercial Invoice and Packing List
  • Foundation Service Agreement / Transport Contract

Mandatory electronic invoicing is in force. All taxpayers must issue invoices through the DGI e-Tax 2.0 system or via certified Authorized Electronic Billing Providers (PSA) / e-invoicing software. Invoices require a unique DGI invoice code, digital signature, XML format, and real-time transmission to the DGI for validation and acceptance (fiscal clearance). A QR code and 'Habilitada' status are digitally stamped on each accepted invoice. Failure to comply may block invoice issuance and trigger sanctions.

The DGI may audit zero-rated claims for up to five years after the tax period. If the export documentation is lacking, falsified, or the remittance is not repatriated within the statutory 180-day deadline, the DGI may assess the ITBMS at the standard rate, plus monthly interest penalties of up to 1%, and an additional surcharge of up to 150% of the tax due in cases of willful omission. Criminal tax evasion penalties can include imprisonment of 2 to 5 years and closure of the establishment.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The United States Dollar (USD) is legal tender alongside the fractional Balboa coin, creating de facto total convertibility with no exchange control law and no central-bank bottleneck. Current-account and capital-account items are equally convertible: inbound capital injections, outbound trade disbursements, dividends, profit remittances, and liquidation proceeds may circulate freely without prior authorization or approval. Practical hurdles are limited to SBP-licensed banks' internal AML/KYC documentation demands; for Private Interest Foundation (PIF) clients, standard documentation includes an up-to-date certified foundation extract from the Registro Público de Panamá, a Council resolution authorizing account operation, a beneficial-ownership declaration, and apostilled identity documents, which may hold funds for 1-3 business days during verification.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory repatriation timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no surrender regime for export proceeds, including proceeds from rail-freight forwarding services, intermodal logistic fees, or re-billing operations. Exporters and service providers may retain earnings indefinitely in USD or any freely convertible currency in Panamanian or offshore accounts. The only compliance obligations are non-prudential and AML-based: UAF reports for cash transactions above the statutory threshold, FATF Recommendation 16 travel-rule records on wire transfers, and ordinary bookkeeping and transfer-pricing documentation. Consequently, a PIF engaged in transcontinental container block-train freight forwarding may invoice and settle in USD, EUR, or CNY without central-bank clearance.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama operates a fully dollarized monetary system with no issuing central bank and no domestic monetary authority. The Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) acts as the fiscal agent and state treasury clearing bank; prudential supervision of the banking sector is vested exclusively in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) administers AML/CFT intelligence reporting; the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) manages fiscal policy and external debt. There exists no foreign exchange administration that issues permits, imposes surrender obligations, or restricts capital flows.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low-to-Medium. An offshore digital EMI is not recognized as a licensed deposit-taking institution in Panama and cannot independently operate a treasury clearing account. A foreign EMI may only (i) maintain segregated client funds through a corresponding omnibus account at an SBP-licensed Panamanian bank, and (ii) obtain payment-processing authorization under Law 73 of 2019 (Innovation Hubs and Digital Wallets) and SBP fintech guidance. Direct use of an offshore EMI as the PIF's primary settlement rail is discouraged for treasury-grade operations; the recommended structure is an SBP-licensed bank as the clearing anchor with the EMI acting solely as a customer-facing payment processor subject to UAF reporting and FATF travel-rule integration.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital flows to the PIF are credited to a USD-denominated Capital Escrow Account via SWIFT MT103 remittances cleared through US correspondent banks on CHIPS/Fedwire. Compliant routing structure: (i) the PIF Council resolution records the capital contribution by founder or third-party investor; (ii) the remitter submits a source-of-funds questionnaire, apostilled passport or corporate extract, and a precise remittance narrative (e.g., 'Capital Contribution — Private Interest Foundation', 'Advance for Transcontinental Rail Freight Services'); (iii) the Panamanian receiving bank applies standard UAF AML controls, including travel-rule records with originator name, address, account number, and — where required — beneficial-owner data; (iv) no central-bank permit, exchange-control license, or surrender authorization is needed because USD inflows are unrestricted. Receiving banks typically place a 1-3 business day verification hold pending documentary release of funds for freight-forwarding working capital.Outbound treasury settlements from the PIF to railway operators, container terminals, inland depots, Eurasian freight forwarders, and customs brokers are executed through (i) SWIFT MT103 customer credit transfers with MT202 cover instructions routed via US clearing correspondents, and (ii) commercial letters of credit issued or amended through SWIFT MT700/MT707, whereby funds are released only against presentation of CIM/SMGS rail consignment notes, multimodal transport documents, terminal-handling receipts, customs transit declarations, invoices, and insurance certificates. Settlement currencies may be USD, EUR, CNY, or any freely convertible currency without Panamanian exchange-control approval; the SBP-licensed bank retains transaction records for at least five years under UAF requirements and performs real-time sanctions screening on both beneficiary bank and ultimate counterparty. Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Balboa (PAB) pegged 1:1 to USD. Mandatory Local File and Master File are required when the taxpayer's transactions with related parties exceed USD 10 million in annual net revenue or when the taxpayer's total assets exceed USD 5 million. Panama also requires a sworn transfer pricing statement submitted simultaneously with the corporate income tax return.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) under Law 254 of 2019 apply to Panamanian-resident legal entities deriving income from specific relevant activities. For an intermodal rail freight expedite and logistics company operating out of Panama, the qualifying activity is logistics/distribution; the entity must maintain adequate physical office, warehouse or terminal presence, employ local qualified staff, and perform core income-generating activities in Panama or otherwise demonstrate with objective evidence that the core functions are carried out in another jurisdiction with genuine economic nexus. Pure Private Interest Foundations holding foreign assets and earning passive foreign investment income are generally outside the ESR core requirements but must still file the annual economic substance return and declare their exempt foreign status.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Declaration

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to 1-2 times the evaded tax plus surcharge and interest; criminal prosecution under the Panama Penal Code (Articles 275-276) carries 2-4 years imprisonment; commercial license revocation and disgorgement of illegal profits

Director personal liability: Directors and officers who sign false returns or direct the concealment are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; travel bans may be imposed pending payment; prison sentences apply where criminal intent is proven and the corporate veil is pierced.
Shell Company / Zero Substance Structure

Statutory sanction: Economic substance violation: annual fines up to USD 100,000 for continuous failure; dissolution of the regulatory license; replacement of registered agent; prohibition on holding the registered agent license in the name of the shell

Director personal liability: Directors may be held liable for ESR non-compliance and struck from the DGI registry; travel restrictions may be applied for unpaid fines; court order may remove the foundation's council for non-compliance and freeze bank accounts.
Anti-Money-Laundering (AML) Deficiencies

Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties under Law 23 of 2015: up to USD 5 million for systemic violations; referral of the file to the Comisión de Prevención de Blanqueo de Capitales (CPBC) for criminal prosecution; asset forfeiture related to the illicit flow

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers, and the PIF council are criminally liable for AML failures if they knew or should have known of the illicit origin of freight payments; personal fines, seizure of personal bank accounts, and imprisonment from 5 to 12 years are applicable.
Unreported Related-Party Cross-Border Payments

Statutory sanction: Transfer pricing adjustment by DGI plus a 10%-25% penalty on the underpaid tax; interest accrued at 1% per month; refusal of deduction for the non-arm's-length portion; temporary license suspension

Director personal liability: Directors who signed transfer pricing documentation knowing it to be false are jointly liable for the adjusted tax under DGI joint liability rules and may face a ban on leaving Panama pending settlement of the assessment.
