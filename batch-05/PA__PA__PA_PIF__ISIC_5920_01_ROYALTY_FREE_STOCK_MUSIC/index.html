Stage 02

Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Registro Público de Panamá - Dirección General, Mercantile Division, Personas Jurídicas Section (Sección de Personas Jurídicas), Panama City, Republic of Panama.

Governing statute

Law No. 25 of 12 June 1995 on Private Interest Foundations (Ley No. 25 de 12 de junio de 1995, sobre Fundaciones de Interés Privado), Articles 1-8 (formation, capacity, purpose), 13-21 (charter content and registration procedure), 29-32 (effects of registration and revocation of bearer instruments); procedural framework supplemented by the General Rules of the Registro Público de Panamá (Acuerdo No. 159 of 2013).

Formation / constitutional filings

Acta Constitutiva (Foundation Charter / Pacto Fundacional) executed as a notarial public instrument, accompanied by the Reglamento Interno (Internal Regulations); filed for inscription through the Registro Electrónico de Títulos (R.E.T.) or the physical presentation window of the Public Registry.

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 12 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

Law 25 of 1995 prescribes no statutory minimum paid-up capital; the Foundation must merely hold patrimony sufficient for its declared purpose. Market-standard initial endowment is USD 100 to USD 1,000. No escrow or depository bank account is legally mandated for the endowment; the Founder subscribes the capital in the charter and the Foundation Council ratifies receipt in the first resolution. Non-resident Founders are not required to transfer funds into a Panamanian licensed bank.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense: fire-safety certification is required only when physical premises are occupied; the landlord's fire inspection certificate issued by the Dirección Provincial de Bomberos must be current, and the Foundation must maintain a fire-extinguisher maintenance contract. Structural load inspections apply only if a dedicated server rack or telecom room is installed; otherwise the office lease's structural certificate suffices. Occupational: MITRADEL (Ministerio de Trabajo y Desarrollo Laboral) workplace registration and CSS occupational-risk insurance (Riesgos Profesionales) are mandatory upon first domestic hiring; no special physical clearance applies to remote workers.

Paid-in capital rule: Law 25 of 1995 prescribes no statutory minimum paid-up capital; the Foundation must merely hold patrimony sufficient for its declared purpose. Market-standard initial endowment is USD 100 to USD 1,000. No escrow or depository bank account is legally mandated for the endowment; the Founder subscribes the capital in the charter and the Foundation Council ratifies receipt in the first resolution. Non-resident Founders are not required to transfer funds into a Panamanian licensed bank.

Industrial Access / EIA & Civil Defense Approval Mandatory Environmental Clearance

Stage 03

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas, MEF); taxpayer portal E-Tax (https://etax.mef.gob.pa); public gateway www.dgi.gob.pa; e-invoicing operated through the SIF (Sistema Integrado de Facturación) module of E-Tax.

E-government filing portal

To obtain the statutory TIN/VAT ID (RUC - Registro Único de Contribuyente): (1) secure an advanced digital-signature certificate issued by an accredited Panamanian certification-services provider; (2) perfect the Private Interest Foundation (Ley 25 de 1995) at the Registro Público and obtain the foundation inscription certificate; (3) appoint a resident legal representative or foreign-entity manager holding an appropriate power of attorney; (4) file the RUC application in E-Tax (or at DGI offices) attaching the public-registry foundation certificate, good-standing certificate, apostilled/legalized entity documents, resident-agent certificate and a sworn statement of economic activities (Panama Tax Code Art. 655); (5) upon issuance of the RUC resolution, activate the ITBMS and SIF e-invoicing sub-registries. A PIF holding and licensing assets exclusively abroad is not statutorily obliged to obtain a RUC, but registration is advisable to substantiate 0% export treatment and to recover input ITBMS credits.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope: Panama taxes only Panamanian-source income. The Private Interest Foundation is taxed at entity level exclusively on income derived from activities carried out in Panama (25% standard corporate rate); all assets, revenues and activities situated or performed abroad are statutorily exempt from Panamanian income tax (Ley 25 de 1995). Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: Panama taxes only Panamanian-source income. The Private Interest Foundation is taxed at entity level exclusively on income derived from activities carried out in Panama (25% standard corporate rate); all assets, revenues and activities situated or performed abroad are statutorily exempt from Panamanian income tax (Ley 25 de 1995). The PIF is not a pass-through entity and does not qualify as a tax-exempt-zone vehicle.

Available election forms

No check-the-box, CFC or hybrid-mismatch elections exist under Panamanian law. Available prescribed elections: (a) charter-level designation of the foundation seat of administration and effective management abroad to reinforce foreign-source income characterization; (b) election of the Régimen Simplificado de Pequeños Contribuyentes for ITBMS where annual gross revenue is at or below approximately PAB/USD 300,000, permitting simplified quarterly filings; (c) election to register as an ITBMS zero-rated exporter to retain input-tax credit rights and claim refunds; (d) where local-source commercial income exists, election of the ordinary deduction-based profit regime versus statutory coefficient regimes.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

10%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No de minimis turnover threshold: any person, resident or non-resident, habitually performing taxable transfers of movable goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS from the first taxable act. An optional simplified small-taxpayer regime applies to annual gross income of approximately PAB/USD 300,000 or less. Non-resident digital suppliers are additionally covered by the Law 290 of 2022 digital-services registration regime for remote supplies into Panama.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

ITBMS 0% applies to exports of goods and to services rendered to non-resident customers for use or enjoyment outside Panama, including the remote digital licensing of royalty-free stock music (ISIC 5920). Eligibility requires: a non-resident customer; license exploitation occurring outside Panama; payment remitted from abroad; issuance of an e-invoice describing the operation as an ITBMS 0% export; and a valid RUC/e-invoicing profile to support input-credit refund claims.

  • Customs Export Declaration (DME - Declaración de Mercancías de Exportación) filed electronically with the Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA) through the SIGA export module; for intangible stock-music licensing, a service-export declaration together with the executed license agreement and usage logs serves as the documentary substitute
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical exit of goods; for digital delivery this is substituted by the cloud/license-server usage log of the foreign customer and the timestamped license receipt
  • Inward Remittance Certificate issued by the Panamanian beneficiary bank evidencing collection of export proceeds through Stripe/PayPal or SWIFT, cross-referenced to the invoice, license log and export declaration

Mandatory DGI e-invoicing through SIF/E-Tax: taxpayers must issue XML fiscal invoices under the official DGI schema transmitted in real time; DGI performs immediate pre-validation and returns a unique authorization code, and the printable invoice must carry the DGI digital stamp and QR code. Rollout is phased by taxpayer size and completes across the entire taxpayer base in 2025. Integration requires an advanced digital-signature certificate and either the web portal, a programmatic web-service/API connector, or DGI-certified billing software. Zero-rated export invoices must cite ISIC 5920 and the governing legal basis; an invoice that fails pre-validation is not a legally effective fiscal document and exposes the issuer to sanctions.

DGI audit testing cross-matches e-invoices, license logs, payment-service-provider settlement reports and inward remittance certificates. Unsubstantiated zero-rating is re-taxed at the standard 10% rate with retroactive assessment over the prescription window; civil penalties range from 50% to 300% of the omitted tax plus default interest (Código Fiscal Arts. 712-715); fabricated exports or forged invoices constitute criminal tax fraud under Art. 716 Código Fiscal, carrying imprisonment (typically two to five years), loss of refund and e-invoicing privileges, and retroactive clawback of credits. The ordinary assessment period is five years, extended to ten years in fraud cases.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Fully convertible de jure and de facto. The U.S. dollar is legal tender; the Panamanian balboa exists solely as a 1:1 bookkeeping unit and fractional coin. No exchange-control law imposes licensing, repatriation, or conversion requirements on capital or current accounts. Profit remittance is subject only to Panama's territorial tax regime: foreign-source royalty income from global music licensing sits outside the Panamanian income tax net; dividend withholding of 10% applies only where the distributed earnings are Panamanian-source. No FX commission, central-bank authorization, or remittance permit is required.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None. Panama imposes no statutory surrender ratio, conversion mandate, or repatriation timeline for export or licensing proceeds. Royalty receipts and subscription settlements may be retained indefinitely in USD or any convertible currency. The only compliance duties are the UAF/AML record-keeping obligations under Law 23 of 2015 and the territorial tax filing of Panamanian-source items.

Central bank supervision: No Central Bank exists. Monetary functions are discharged through full dollarization under Law 84 of 1904; bank supervision rests exclusively with the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP); Banco Nacional de Panamá serves only as fiscal agent.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: Low-Medium (supplemental rail only). Panama grants no independent EMI license; fintech payment firms must operate through an SBP-licensed bank or the SBP fintech sandbox (Acuerdo 004-2020). Offshore digital EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Mercury) commonly enforce OECD-style substance screens and frequently reject Panama-domiciled Private Interest Foundations due to hybrid legal nature, non-resident beneficial owners, and absence of audited IFRS statements. Any EMI use must be restricted to receiving-only purposes with daily or weekly sweeps into an SBP-licensed bank account; the PIF name and registration number must match exactly in all EMI documentation.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Founder endowment and subsequent capital contributions are executed by SWIFT MT103 USD wire from the founder's home-country bank to the PIF's escrow account at an SBP-licensed bank. The wire must (i) name the PIF exactly as registered with the Registro Público, (ii) cite the PIF registration file number in field 70, (iii) use the beneficiary bank's SWIFT BIC and the SBP-mandated PAN IBAN account format, and (iv) state purpose code 'CAPT / Aporte de capital - Ley 25 de 1995'. The remitter name must match the founder named in the Pacto Fundacional; the bank will require the registered foundation deed, good-standing certificate, and a board resolution approving the injection. The MT103 confirmation, bank credit advice, and source-of-funds evidence are archived in the UAF AML file.Operational disbursements use three rails: (1) SWIFT MT103 for royalty payments to composers, mastering studios, distributors, and collection societies, with beneficiary contract references in field 72 and invoice support retained for territorial tax assessment; (2) Commercial Letters of Credit governed by UCP600 for large one-off sync-placement deals where the beneficiary (advertising agency or film/TV producer) demands payment against shipping or delivery documents; (3) Stripe/PayPal merchant settlements are batched in USD and swept by the processors' U.S. clearing banks via ACH or SWIFT directly into the PIF's Panama operational account. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% standard corporate rate; progressive tiers: 15% on taxable income up to USD 50,000, 25% on taxable income above USD 50,000 and up to USD 1,500,000, and 30% on taxable income exceeding USD 1,500,000.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory dividend withholding tax on distributions out of Panamanian-source profits; effectively 5% on profits that have already borne the 25% corporate income tax (Law 52 of 2016 crediting mechanism); treaty rates may reduce or eliminate the residue.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Due within three months after the fiscal year-end; for a calendar-year entity, by 31 March. Quarterly advance income tax payments are required during the year based on prior-year taxable income or cash-flow projections.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Mandatory Local File and Master File under Panama's transfer pricing regulations for taxpayers with prior-year gross income exceeding USD 2,000,000 or with related-party transactions aggregating USD 250,000 or more; contemporaneous documentation must be filed with the annual tax return and updated annually.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 70 of 2019, Executive Decrees 21 of 2021 and 33 of 2022) require Panamanian entities earning foreign-source relevant-activity income, including IP royalties and distribution of digital products, to demonstrate core income-generating activity in Panama: locally maintained premises, qualified employees, and management and control. A stock-music PIF must therefore retain actual decision-making records and local staff; failure triggers recharacterisation of foreign income as Panamanian-source, back taxes, fines, and possible cancellation of beneficial tax treatment.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion

Statutory sanction: Criminal tax fraud penalties under the Código Fiscal and Law 52 of 2016: fines from 50% to 300% of the evaded tax, plus imprisonment of 2 to 5 years; forfeiture of laundered proceeds; revocation of PIF registration for persistent non-filing.

Director personal liability: Officers, directors, and beneficial owners may be jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes and penalties; criminal proceedings permit civil restitution, travel bans, preventive detention, and disqualification from holding positions in Panamanian entities.
Shell Company / Artificial Substance

Statutory sanction: Under the Economic Substance Regulations, daily non-compliance fines, an administrative penalty of up to 1% of relevant gross income, mandatory corrective orders, and reclassification of previously exempt foreign-source revenue as Panamanian-source taxable income, resulting in back assessments plus 25-30% tax and 10% dividend withholding.

Director personal liability: Directors and the resident legal agent face personal liability for false substance declarations, civil restitution of misclassified profits, travel bans in pending investigations, and potential criminal prosecution for falsifying official documentation.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Obfuscation

Statutory sanction: Violations of Panama's Law 23 of 2015 (AML/CFT) carry criminal penalties of 5 to 12 years' imprisonment, administrative fines of USD 50,000 to USD 1,000,000, suspension of the foundation's commercial licence or registration, and permanent inclusion in the national AML offenders register.

Director personal liability: Directors and officers may be criminally liable for enabling the flow of illicit proceeds; corporate veil is pierced to impose fine liability on individual participants, impose international travel bans, freeze personal assets, and extradite non-resident managers under multilateral mutual legal assistance arrangements.
