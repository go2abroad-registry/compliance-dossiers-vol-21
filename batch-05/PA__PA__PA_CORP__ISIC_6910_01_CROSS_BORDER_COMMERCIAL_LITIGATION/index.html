Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Republic of Panama; primary digital channels are the e-Tax taxpayer portal and the DGI institutional web portal (www.dgi.gob.pa). Supporting offices: Registro Público de Panamá (corporate registry), Tesorería Municipal for the Aviso de Operación, and Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA) for customs identifiers.

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Panama S.A. obtains its RUC (Registro Único de Contribuyentes) as follows: (1) notarized incorporation before the Registro Público through a licensed Panama attorney — no minimum capital requirement; (2) appointment of a resident agent/legal representative who holds the fiscal-domicile responsibility; (3) procurement of the municipal Aviso de Operación from the Tesorería Municipal before any taxable activity; (4) submission of the corporate registration certificate, resident-agent appointment and ID documents, business license and registered address through the DGI e-Tax window or DGI regional offices; (5) issuance of the RUC numeric ID, which concurrently operates as the ITBMS (VAT), electronic-invoicing, employer (CSS/MITRADEL) and, where applicable, exporter identifier. The procedure is typically completed within 5 to 10 business days after incorporation.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope (Entity_Level_Taxation). The S.A. is an independent corporate taxpayer subject to 25% corporate income tax on Panama-source income only; foreign-source income — including fees from foreign-seated arbitrations, foreign court proceedings and offshore asset tracing for non-resident clients — is outside Panamanian income tax jurisdiction absent a Panamanian permanent establishment or Panama-source revenue connection. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope (Entity_Level_Taxation). The S.A. is an independent corporate taxpayer subject to 25% corporate income tax on Panama-source income only; foreign-source income — including fees from foreign-seated arbitrations, foreign court proceedings and offshore asset tracing for non-resident clients — is outside Panamanian income tax jurisdiction absent a Panamanian permanent establishment or Panama-source revenue connection. Panama does not apply US-style pass-through or check-the-box classification to the S.A.; entity-level taxation is the statutory default.

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists. Available statutory elections/opt-ins: (i) the small-taxpayer regime under Ley 76 de 2019 — an S.A. with annual gross income not exceeding B/.36,000 may elect a single 2% gross-receipts tax in substitution for ordinary ISR and monthly ITBMS accounting; (ii) the SEM multinational headquarters regime (Ley 41 de 2007), which may be elected by qualifying regional service companies and confers ITBMS/ISR exemptions on qualifying regional income; (iii) the annual export-services zero-rating substantiation procedure before the DGI; and (iv) Pillar Two elections (qualified domestic minimum top-up tax) under Panama's global minimum tax implementing decree for MNE groups with consolidated revenue of at least EUR 750 million, in force for fiscal years beginning on or after 1 January 2025. Hybrid mismatch elections under the OECD BEPS Action 2 framework are not separately available in Panama.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Panamanian balboa (PAB) is a purely nominal monetary unit strictly pegged at 1:1 to the U.S. dollar (USD); no balboa banknotes are issued and no exchange-control, capital-control, repatriation-licensing, or source-of-foreign-exchange approval regime exists. Both current-account and capital-account items are fully convertible, and profit remittance is not subject to central-bank pre-approval. The only substantive restraint on outward remittances is the anti-money-laundering (AML) transaction-justification framework imposed on banks by SBP Acuerdo No. 8-2011, not statutory FX restrictions. Dividends paid by a Panamanian Sociedad Anónima from Panamanian-source profits remain subject to the statutory dividend withholding tax; foreign beneficiaries receive distributions in USD with no FX surcharge or conversion obligation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

There is no statutory export-proceeds surrender timeline, no mandatory conversion ratio, and no requirement to repatriate export receipts to Panama. Exporters may retain proceeds indefinitely in USD or any foreign currency, whether onshore or offshore, without SBP authorization. For treasury transparency and SWIFT-compliance purposes, exporters of legal and arbitration services (commercial litigation chambers billing clients abroad) are advised to maintain the underlying engagement letter, DUI/VUCE declarations (where relevant), invoices, and wire-receipt records to evidence the commercial transaction and expedite bank AML review.

Central bank supervision: No national central bank exists in Panama. The Republic operates under a full USD-dollarization regime established by Law No. 84 of 1904, as amended, and the modern banking framework of Decree-Law No. 9 of February 26, 1998. The U.S. Federal Reserve functions as the de facto monetary authority, while prudential supervision and financial-system compliance are exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP). Fiscal administration of financial records and corporate taxation is handled by the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministry of Economy and Finance.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Restricted and Low for foreign digital EMIs. Panama does not issue a dedicated e-money-institution (EMI) license in the EU/UK sense. Payment-fintech activity is permissible under Ley 1 de 2023 and SBP regulations, but a foreign EMI cannot obtain direct SBP membership or direct access to local ACH/RTGS settlement rails. An offshore EMI may only maintain a segregated omnibus account with a tier-1 Panamanian bank under strict third-party-payment-arrangement compliance, and most tier-1 banks decline non-bank foreign EMIs as clients due to correspondent-bank de-risking. Panama-licensed payment intermediaries may operate under SBP fintech authorizations, but for the current commercial-litigation treasury architecture, a licensed commercial bank account remains the sole viable clearing rail.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital injections execute via correspondent-bank SWIFT MT103 from the parent or shareholder with remittance-information field 70 clearly reading 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION — SUSCRIPCIÓN DE ACCIONES' or 'SHAREHOLDER LOAN — PRÉSTAMO DE ACCIONISTA'. The receiving tier-1 bank will require the Escritura Pública or board resolution evidencing share issuance, a documented shareholder-loan agreement where applicable, a DGI tax-registration certificate, and a source-of-funds statement. Panama imposes no central-bank pre-approval and no statutory purpose-code format, but SBP AML rules require the bank to record an internal transaction-purpose code and to verify the underlying economic reality. Funds should be credited first to the Capital Escrow Account and only reallocated to the Operational Account upon execution of the escrow or client-retainer agreement.Outbound disbursements for court bonds, expert fees, arbitrator deposits, and settlement payments settle via SWIFT MT103 through the Panamanian bank's New York or Miami correspondent account; local Panamanian clearing is performed through the ACH clearing house (Cámara de Compensación Interbancaria). Trade-related payments may be structured as documentary collections or Commercial Letters of Credit under UCP 600 issued by Banistmo, Banco General, or BAC International Bank. Payment milestones should be supported by a disbursement waterfall and reconciled ledger so that each outgoing wire corresponds to an identifiable invoice or tribunal order.
