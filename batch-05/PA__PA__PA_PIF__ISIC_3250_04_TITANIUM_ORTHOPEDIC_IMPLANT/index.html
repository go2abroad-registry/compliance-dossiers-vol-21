Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum capital is fixed for Private Interest Foundations under Ley 25 de 1995; the Foundation Charter must state a patrimony/endowment (standard USD 10,000). Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) del Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF); official taxpayer portal: DGI Virtual / e-Tax (https://www.dgi.gob.pa); customs export administration: Autoridad Nacional de Aduanas (ANA) via the SIGA/Ventanilla Única platform (https://www.ana.gob.pa).

E-government filing portal

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope: a PIF is a separate juridical person, not a pass-through, and is taxed in Panama only on Panama-source income; foreign-source income and foundation assets remain outside the Panamanian income-tax net. For the ISIC 3250 manufacturing activity, the PIF is an entity-level taxpayer on Panama-source net profits, and exports of titanium orthopedic implants qualify for ITBMS zero-rating with input-VAT refund entitlement.

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a US-style check-the-box or hybrid-mismatch elective regime; entity classification is fixed by statutory registration. Available statutory elections include: (a) commercial-object PIF election under Law 25 of 1995 permitting the foundation to conduct business activities; (b) Multinational Headquarters (SEM) incentive regime election under Law 41 of 2007; (c) free-zone location election (Zona Libre de Colón, Panama Pacífico, or Barú) granting full ITBMS and income-tax exemption; (d) simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS election for annual turnover not exceeding PAB 36,000; and (e) formal exporter status registration with DGI/ANA to claim ITBMS export refunds. No separate hybrid-mismatch election exists; OECD Pillar Two/Global Minimum Tax rules, where applicable to groups above USD 750 million, operate through fixed statutory provisions.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

PAB 36,000 (Panamanian Balboa, 1:1 parity with the U.S. dollar) of annual gross turnover; businesses exceeding this threshold must register as regular ITBMS taxpayers and file monthly returns, while those at or below it may elect the simplified small-taxpayer regime; importers of goods must register regardless of turnover.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

The 0% rate (exoneración de ITBMS with refund of embedded input VAT) applies to: (a) goods physically exported from Panama under an ANA customs export declaration, including the PIF's titanium orthopedic implants (e.g., artificial joints); (b) services rendered from Panama to non-residents and wholly used abroad; and (c) deliveries to Panama Pacífico, Zona Libre de Colón, or other fiscal free zones. Conditions: the transaction must be documented in a DGI-compliant 0% export e-invoice, the goods must leave Panama within the evidenced shipment period, export consideration must be received from abroad, and the ITBMS refund is claimed on the statutory exporter refund form supported by underlying input e-invoices.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración de Exportación) validated by ANA via SIGA
  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing the goods' physical departure from Panama
  • Inward Remittance Certificate / bank credit advice evidencing receipt of export sale proceeds
  • DGI-compliant 0% export electronic invoice (Factura Electrónica) with ANA export declaration reference
  • ITBMS exporter refund application (Solicitud de Devolución de ITBMS a Exportadores) with supporting source input e-invoices

Mandatory DGI electronic invoicing (Facturación Electrónica / SFEP) under DGI Resolution 270 of 2018 and subsequent rules: every registered ITBMS taxpayer must issue DGI-validated electronic invoices through the DGI SFEP web platform or certified software using the official XML/JSON schema, a PKI digital signature, and a unique DGI fiscal acceptance/authorization code obtained upon digital transmission; invoices not validated by the DGI do not support input VAT credits or export refund claims. Large taxpayers were phased in first and full mandatory coverage for regular businesses became effective during fiscal years 2022–2024; export e-invoices must be issued at 0% and reference the ANA customs export declaration.

DGI audit powers are governed by a general 5-year tax prescription period, extended to 10 years in cases of willful evasion. Penalties include disallowance of zero-rating, retrospective refund clawbacks with 10% annual compensatory interest, fines of 25%–75% of the omitted tax for inaccurate declarations, and up to 100% or criminal sanctions (imprisonment under the Código Fiscal and Penal Code) for fraudulent or fictitious export declarations. ANA may additionally impose fines and suspend or revoke the exporter registry status. In practice, DGI and AML authorities will scrutinize the PIF's Swift wire and letter-of-credit payment rails to confirm that export proceeds were repatriated and documented within 180 days, and that the titanium implant shipments are genuine, traceable, and supported by the full customs documentation bundle.

Stage 04

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama uses the US Dollar as legal tender alongside the Panamanian Balboa (PAB), which exists only as a limited-issue coinage unit at a fixed 1:1 peg to the USD. There are no exchange controls on either the current or capital account. Capital inflows, profit remittances, dividend distributions, and principal repayments are freely convertible and transferable without prior government approval. No surrender, mandatory repatriation, or investment licensing requirements apply to foreign-held escrow accounts or operational accounts.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Panama imposes no statutory timeline or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters may retain proceeds in any convertible currency (normally USD) in any local or offshore bank account. The only reporting obligations derive from AML/CFT laws (Law 23 of 2015) requiring transactional record-keeping and threshold reporting to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF).

Central bank supervision: Republic of Panama does not maintain a traditional central bank. The Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) is the primary regulator of banking and foreign exchange intermediation; the Banco Nacional de Panamá acts as the State’s fiscal agent and clearing agent. The Ministry of Economy and Finance oversees macro-level monetary policy coordination.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Legal feasibility for offshore digital EMI providers in Panama is Low. Panama has no dedicated electronic money institution (EMI) license under the SBP regulatory perimeter. Although Law 1 of 2021 created a FinTech innovation sandbox, cross-border EMI passports (e.g., UK FCA, EU EMI licenses) are not automatically recognized. A non-bank EMI may only provide custodial or payment services through a Panamanian licensed bank under a white-label agreement. Accepting customer funds without a banking or sandbox license is prohibited.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

All inbound capital injections must be routed via clear, evidence-backed wire transfers to the PIF’s operational or escrow account. For a PIF engaged in medical-device manufacturing, the funds catatan should be 'Capital Contribution / Intercompany Shareholder Loan' or 'Escrow Prepayment for Porous Titanium Components.' Recommend SWIFT MT103 with detailed remittance information and a reference to the foundation’s RUC and Panama Public Registry Identifier. Banks will require a copy of the board resolution authorizing the capital increase or a loan agreement. The source account must be in the name of the ultimate beneficial owner or a transparently structured affiliate. If the funds originate from a non-FATF jurisdiction, a full source-of-wealth and source-of-funds package is mandatory. There is no local FX pre-approval for inbound USD transfers; amounts above USD 10,000 per month trigger automatic UAF/AML screening.Outbound trade settlements for manufacturing equipment, raw titanium powder, and logistics services should use two primary rails: (i) SWIFT MT103 single-currency transfers for operational payments up to USD 500,000; and (ii) Commercial Letters of Credit (irrevocable, confirmed L/C) for large capex and recurring supply contracts above USD 500,000 or when counterparties demand bank-guaranteed settlement. Panama banks are part of SWIFT and also support CHIPS and Fedwire for USD clearing.
