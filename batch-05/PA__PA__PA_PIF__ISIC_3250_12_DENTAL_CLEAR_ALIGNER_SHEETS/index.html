Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) – General Directorate of Revenue of the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF); electronic portal: SITIO (Sistema Integrado de Información Tributaria), https://sitio.dgi.gob.pa

E-government filing portal

For a Panamanian Private Interest Foundation (PIF) under Law 25 of 1995: (1) execute the Foundation Charter before a Panamanian notary through a licensed Foundation Agent and register it in the Public Registry of Panama; (2) after issuance of the Foundation Certificate, apply to the DGI for a Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) via SITIO or in-person filing, submitting the foundation certificate, agent appointment, registered address, beneficial-owner declaration (Law 254 of 2021), and director identification; (3) obtain an electronic signature (firma electrónica) and VAT/ITBMS activation; (4) file the Aviso de Operación with the Ministry of Commerce and Industries (MICI) and obtain the municipal business license for the manufacturing plant; (5) appoint a Panama-licensed CPA as statutory fiscal representative. RUC issuance typically takes 10–20 business days; the RUC is mandatory for any Panamanian-source taxable activity while foreign-source asset holding and income remain entirely outside the Panamanian tax base without additional registration.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope

Available election forms

Panama does not operate a U.S.-style check-the-box or entity-classification election; the PIF is a separate legal person taxed strictly on territorial-source income (only Panamanian-source profits at 25% corporate rate; foreign-source income is excluded). The Foundation Charter may elect between trust-like or corporation-like administration rules for private law. Available tax elections include voluntary ITBMS registration for taxpayers below the B/. 36,000 threshold, accelerated depreciation for manufacturing machinery, the SEM (Multinational Headquarters) regime under Law 41 of 2007 if qualifying services are rendered, and free-zone/Panama-Pacifico designation if the production plant is relocated to an export-processing zone. Panama has not enacted OECD hybrid-mismatch or check-the-box legislation; Pillar Two GloBE rules apply only to MNE groups with EUR 750 million+ consolidated revenue from fiscal year 2025.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of tangible goods manufactured in Panama — including ISIC 3250 thermoplastic dental clear-aligner sheets (HS Chapters 3920/3921 and, if finished, 9021) — qualify for 0% ITBMS under Article 62(2) of Law 28 of 1995 provided: (a) the seller holds a valid RUC; (b) the goods physically leave Panamanian customs territory; (c) the export e-invoice carries the zero-rated exemption code; and (d) proceeds are repatriated and evidenced within the statutory period. Offshore services rendered to non-residents for use entirely abroad may also be zero-rated; local-term services remain at 7%.

  • DUA – Declaración Única Aduanera (single customs export declaration filed through Panama Customs VUCE/SISCO system with HS classification and declared value)
  • Bill of Lading (B/L) or Air Waybill (AWB) evidencing physical removal from Panamanian territory
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (Certificado de Remesa) issued by the receiving bank confirming credit of export proceeds
  • Commercial invoice with Incoterms, zero-rated ITBMS annotation, QR-code e-invoice number and export license/reference
  • Packing list and certificate of origin (where preferential tariff treatment is claimed)

Mandatory e-invoicing (Facturación Electrónica) pursuant to DGI Resolution No. 201-4727 of 2020: all RUC holders must issue standardized XML invoices through the SITIO 2.0 web services (REST API) which validate, timestamp, and return a fiscal authorization with a QR code and consecutive number. Large taxpayers (Segment A) transmit and obtain fiscal clearance in real time before delivery; medium and small taxpayers (Segments B/C) transmit within the month under the phased rollout completed by June 2025. Export e-invoices must cross-reference the customs export declaration and are individually validated by the DGI before zero-rating is accepted.

DGI and Panama Customs audit export zero-rating claims under Articles 699–713 and 760 of the Código Fiscal and DGI Resolution No. 201-4256/2019. Fraudulent or substantively deficient claims trigger retrospective clawback of the 7% ITBMS, monthly default interest, fines of 50%–100% of the omitted tax, RUC suspension or cancellation, and criminal liability for tax fraud under Penal Code Article 258-A (as amended by Law 8 of 2010) with imprisonment of 2–5 years. Exporters must retain the complete supporting documentation bundle for at least 7 years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Panama is a fully dollarized economy. The US dollar is legal tender, and the Panamanian Balboa is a subsidiary, fixed-at-par accounting unit used only for fractional local coins. There is no practical distinction between the current and capital account: transfers of principal, profits, dividends, and loan repayments can be freely routed through USD-denominated bank accounts without any special administrative conversion. No Central Bank pre-approval exists because there is no central monetary authority. The only repartriation hurdle is not exchange-based but tax-based: a Panamanian-registered PIF owning a Panamanian permanent establishment for manufacturing dental aligner sheets must apply the cannotoon-territorial income tax and withhold tax on dividends paid or remitted to resident or non-resident beneficiaries. Where no economic activity is carried out inside Panama, the PIF receives and distributes income without any foreign exchange authorisation.

Export proceeds surrender rules

No statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation exists. Panamanian law does not require any conversion ratio, time-level pre-negotiation, or foreign exchange retention limit. Exporting entities and PIFs may hold USD proceeds onshore or offshore indefinitely. To satisfy banking AML/CFT expectations, the PIF exporting thermoformed aligner sheets must retain the underlying shipment invoice, bill of lading, and customs export document (Certificado de Re-exportación or via DUA - Declaración Única de Aduanas) for at least 5 years, as required under SBP Resolution No. 2018-49-010 and Law 23 of 2015 on money laundering and terrorist financing.

Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama does not operate a central bank. The monetary and prudential oversight of banks, trust, and finance companies is vested in the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP - Superintendency of Banks of Panama), while macro-financial and exchange-related statistics are maintained by the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF) and, for financial intelligence, the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (Financial Analysis Unit - UAF).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Stripe Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Medium with strict overlay on top of traditional banking. Under Panamanian Decree-Law 2 of 2008 as updated by IBC Law 4 of 2020 and SBP Payment Regulation No. 005-2022, non-bank electronic money issuers and payment service providers (fintech/EMIs) must be licensed locally to custody funds and provide payment services. Foreign offshore digital EMIs (e.g., UK or Eurozone EMI passports) do not enjoy direct passporting rights into Panama. They may be used as settlement vehicles exclusively for platform card acquisition (Stripe/PayPal) when the PIF has an established originating bank relationship, but cannot replace a licensed Panamanian commercial bank for holding the PIF's operating liquidity or executing MT103 trade payments. Any PIF that uses an unlicensed EMI for initial capital contribution will likely invoke KYC rejection.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

The PIF's initial and subsequent capital injections, including founder contributions and inter-affiliate funding, must be routed via the SWIFT network using an MT103 payment order from the remitter's commercial bank to a Panamanian tier-1 bank. The message field 70 (remittance information) must quote e.g., 'CAPITAL CONTRIBUTION – PIF PA_PIF – INVOICE/AGREEMENT 001'. For transfers from a non-resident shareholder, cite the source of funds in field 72 to pre-empt AML review. If the injection exceeds USD 100,000, the International Private Banking department of the receiving bank may request a Compliance Certificate and the foundation's own balance sheet. All inbound funds are tagged as 'patrimonial asset' of the PIF and not as ordinary income for territorial tax purposes, provided no local business activity occurs in Panama.Trade payments for imported PETG, copolyester pallets, CNC tools, and packaging materials must be cleared either through local ACH (ACH Panama) for domestic counterparties or, for cross-border suppliers, via SWIFT MT103 with a standard 'T1' misinformation or a commercial LC.
