Default Tax Classification & Elections

      

        
Default tax classification
Territorial_Scope

        
Available election forms
Panama does not provide a check-the-box or formal hybrid-mismatch election. Available statutory elections include: (i) selection of legal vehicle (S.A., S.R.L., or Private Interest Foundation); (ii) application for special territorial regimes granting income-tax and ITBMS benefits, including Multinational Headquarters (SEM), City of Knowledge, and Panama-Pacific special economic zone; (iii) fiscal elections under the Código Fiscal, such as carry-forward of net operating losses, depreciation method selection, and the small-taxpayer simplified ITBMS regime for annual gross income not exceeding USD 36,000. BEPS Action 2 hybrid-mismatch legislation has not been enacted domestically.

      

    

    

      
VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

      

        
Standard VAT / GST rate
7%

        
Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)
No general de minimis turnover threshold exists. Any person habitually or commercially performing taxable transfers of goods or services in Panama must register for ITBMS with the DGI. Small taxpayers whose annual gross income does not exceed USD 36,000 may elect the simplified 'pequeño contribuyente' payment regime, but registration remains mandatory for exporters and laboratory-service providers invoicing international clients.

      

      
      

        Export 0% zero-rating conditions
        
Zero-rating at 0% ITBMS applies to: (i) goods physically exported from Panama, evidenced by a customs export declaration showing departure to a foreign destination; (ii) services rendered to non-resident persons for use or enjoyment wholly outside Panama, which includes laboratory testing of battery cells (UN38.3) where the physical testing location is in Panama but the report is delivered to a foreign principal for overseas use; (iii) services supplied to entities licensed under the SEM and other qualifying special regimes. The supplier must hold an active ITBMS registration, be listed as an exporter in the SICE customs window, and issue a zero-rated electronic invoice with the statutory exemption indicator.

Mandatory electronic invoicing (factura electrónica) applies under DGI Resolution No. 201-3271 (2019) and its phased implementation rulings. Invoices must be generated through the T@S E-Factura module or an authorized billing system integrated via DGI REST/API web services; each invoice requires DGI authorization, a unique consecutive number, a QR verification code, and PDF/A digital-stamping before transmission to the buyer. Large taxpayers must use real-time digital clearance of authorized invoice batches; no physical fiscal stamp machines are used. Export invoices and zero-rated supplies carry the same electronic authorization requirements.

      
Panama maintains no statutory Central Bank foreign-exchange repatriation obligation; however, the DGI and Customs expect export proceeds to be documented through the inward remittance certificate chain, commonly benchmarked at 180 days. If zero-rating is disallowed upon audit, the DGI retroactively assesses 7% ITBMS on the gross export consideration plus monthly recargo (surcharge) on unpaid tax, statutory interest, and fines of up to 100% of the evaded tax in aggravated cases. Fraudulent zero-rating or false export declarations trigger criminal tax-fraud liability under Ley 70 de 2019 (imprisonment of 2 to 5 years), disqualification from the SEM regime, cancellation of the RUC, and customs penalties under Ley 76 de 2019 for misdeclared export values.

Stage 04
Banking & Treasury Architecture

      
Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

    

    

      
Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

      

        
Current-account convertibility
Panama has a fully dollarized regime. The US dollar is unlimited legal tender; the Panamanian Balboa exists only as fractional coins at a fixed 1:1 parity. There is no statutory exchange-control law restricting capital or current account convertibility. Profit remittances are permitted without prior administrative authorization, subject only to income tax filing and, where applicable, the 10% dividend tax under Articles 733 and 734 of the Fiscal Code. Foreign investors may freely repatriate capital and earnings in any freely convertible currency.

        
Export proceeds surrender rules
No export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion rules apply in Panama. Exporters may retain their full export receipts in USD or any foreign currency. Banks are not required to remove dollars from receipt accounts, and no statutory timeline exists for conversion, since the national monetary unit is the US dollar. The only reporting duty is that banks flag suspicious transactions under the SBP AML/CFT Rule 16-2023.

      

      
Central bank supervision: The Republic of Panama does not operate a central bank. Banking and monetary-system oversight is exercised by the Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) under Law 2 of 2005, as amended. Banco Nacional de Panamá functions as a state-owned deposit-taking institution but does not hold monetary policy or FX reserve authority. The Financial Analysis Unit (UAF) within the Ministry of Economy and Finance handles AML/CFT enforcement.

    

    

      
Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

      

        
        

          

            Airwallex
            Fit: High
          

          
Low direct feasibility using offshore digital EMI licenses. Panama licenses payment service providers as 'empresas de pagos' under SBP Law 2 of 2005, but an overseas fintech EMI (e.g., Wise, Airwallex) cannot establish a full banking relationship on behalf of the corporate without a licensed local agent or bank subsidiary. For inbound and outbound settlement, the approved and most compliant rail is a Panama-licensed corporate own account at a tier-1 bank, linked via SWIFT to the offshore EMI for disbursement initiation. The fintech EMI is treated as a payment instruction tool, not as the treasury custodian of company funds.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

      
Master / Local File threshold: Panama's transfer pricing rules apply when transactions with related parties exceed USD 1,000,000 per fiscal year. Full Local File and Master File documentation is required for taxpayers with related-party transactions exceeding USD 2,000,000; simpler documentation (Contemporaneous TP Study / declaration form) applies between USD 1,000,000 and USD 2,000,000. The threshold was introduced by Law 41 of 2018 and Executive Decree 192 of 2021; all documentary thresholds are denominated in USD because Panama uses the US dollar as legal tender.

      
Economic substance scope: Panama enacted Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) under Law 254 of 2021 and Executive Decree 21 of 2023, applicable to licensed and certain operating entities. A Panama S.A. providing UN38.3 battery testing and CE-RED electromagnetic lab services is a commercial business with physical testing activities; the entity must demonstrate: (i) adequate physical presence and premises in Panama; (ii) local employees and qualified technicians; (iii) effective management and control from Panama, including board meetings in Panama; (iv) real testing operations generating core income from Panamanian facilities; and (v) compliance with the annual ESR return. Failure to satisfy economic substance can trigger dissolution, fines, or recharacterization of Panamanian income as foreign income subject to other taxes.

      

    

    

      
Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

      
      

        Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Concealment of Panama-Source Income
        
Statutory sanction: Criminal penalty for tax evasion: imprisonment of 2 to 7 years; fines up to 10 times the evaded tax depending on aggravating circumstances; DGI can assess tax and surcharges retroactively for 5 fiscal years (or longer in fraud cases); loss of right to operate as a testing laboratory and suspension of accredited certification license.

        
Director personal liability: Directors, shareholders, and beneficial owners are jointly liable when the corporation is a mere instrumentality; the corporate veil may be pierced under Panamanian Commercial Code and anti-money laundering decisions. Directors face personal civil restitution of evaded taxes, freeze orders on personal assets in Panama, travel bans under Interpol red notice incidents, and criminal imprisonment not replaced by corporate fines.

      

      
      

        Shell Company Without Economic Substance / Sham Arrangement
        
Statutory sanction: Economic Substance Regulation fines: up to USD 1,000,000 for unjustified non-compliance; automatic filing of ESR penalties; tax recharacterization of income as Panama-source and subject to 25% corporate tax plus 10% dividend distribution tax; cancellation of commercial registration license; disqualification from government accreditation (SCCS / ISAC) and CNM (Comisión Nacional de Normas) certification; referral to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero for money laundering investigation.

        
Director personal liability: Veil-piercing where the entity is an alter ego of its shareholders; directors of the shell can be personally assessed for the full tax liability; asset foreign-flag vessels or yacht registrations in Panama may be arrested; travel restrictions and criminal charges for false corporate declarations; potential seizure of digital assets and laboratory equipment held in Panama.

      

      
      

        AML / Terrorist Financing / Illegal Testing Certification (Criminal Enterprise)
        
Statutory sanction: Panama's AML Law (Law 23 of 2015, as codified in Legislative Decree 2 of 2025 and the 2020 Codified Criminal Procedure): monetary penalties from USD 1,000,000 to USD 5,000,000; forfeiture of profits and laboratory assets; criminal conviction for fraud and falsification of public documents; imprisonment of 5 to 12 years for predicate laundering offences; decertification as a UN dangerous goods testing facility and suspension of CE-RED recognized roles under EU Notified Body protocols.

        
Director personal liability: Criminal liability attaches directly to the beneficial owner and technical director who signs certificates; the forfeiture regime reaches personal residences and bank accounts; travel bans and Interpol red notices used when fleeing jurisdiction; extradition under bilateral agreements; the individual is declared ineligible to serve as director of any Panamanian company for a maximum of 10 years.

      

      
      

        FATCA/CRS and Beneficial Ownership Transparency Violation
        
Statutory sanction: Monetary penalties for beneficial ownership non-compliance: USD 500 per month for each month of delay, up to USD 10,000; CRS/FATCA reporting penalties up to USD 1,000,000; judicial orders for disclosure of bank records; automatic exchange of information triggering investigations in the parent company jurisdiction; professional licenses and resident agent liabilities.

        
Director personal liability: Director personal fines of USD 100,000 in egregious cases; criminal liability for false beneficial ownership declarations; permanent immigration issues; involuntary loss of the entity's Panama residency status and closure of MeF registration; foreign courts may issue anti-anti-avoidance orders and freeze personal assets worldwide under cross-border injunctions.
