Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI) of the Ministerio de Economía y Finanzas (MEF), Panama; electronic portal: https://dgi.mef.gob.pa (Sistema Integrado de Gestión de Ingresos - SIGI).

E-government filing portal

For a foreign entity, the statutory TIN/RUC is obtained by: (1) securing municipal Aviso de Operación in the relevant district if any commercial operations are conducted in Panama; (2) filing the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) application with DGI through SIGI using notarized constitutional documents, foreign entity certificate of existence, proof of registered local address, and identification of the local legal representative; (3) receiving the provisional RUC and final RUC after DGI validation; (4) completing ITBMS registration and, where applicable, registration as a withholding agent. Non-resident entities may act through a licensed Panamanian law firm or resident agent.

Tax ID type & application path: Territorial_Scope; Private Interest Foundation (PIF) is an entity-level taxpayer only for Panamanian-source income, while foreign-source and non-Panamanian activities remain outside the tax net. — For a foreign entity, the statutory TIN/RUC is obtained by: (1) securing municipal Aviso de Operación in the relevant district if any commercial operations are conducted in Panama; (2) filing the Registro Único de Contribuyente (RUC) application with DGI through SIGI using notarized constitutional documents, foreign entity certificate of existence, proof of registered local address, and identification of the local legal representative; (3) receiving the provisional RUC and final RUC after DGI validation; (4) completing ITBMS registration and, where applicable, registration as a withholding agent. Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Territorial_Scope; Private Interest Foundation (PIF) is an entity-level taxpayer only for Panamanian-source income, while foreign-source and non-Panamanian activities remain outside the tax net.

Available election forms

Panama does not provide a check-the-box election or hybrid mismatch elections. Available elections include: election by eligible small taxpayers to be taxed under the simplified small-taxpayer ITBMS regime (pequeño contribuyente), election to register or not as an ITBMS withholding agent, and optional registration for electronic invoicing once the taxpayer exceeds the mandatory threshold. The PIF civil-law tax classification is fixed by Panamanian Private Interest Foundation Law No. 25 of 1995.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

7%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

No general monetary threshold exists for ITBMS registration; all persons or entities engaging in commercial, industrial, or service activities in Panama must register. Small taxpayers with annual gross turnover not exceeding PAB 36,000 may use the simplified small-taxpayer registration, but this is not a registration exemption.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

ITBMS zero-rating may be claimed by registered taxpayers for: (a) tangible movable goods physically exported from Panama, where export is evidenced by customs documentation; (b) services rendered entirely abroad or used exclusively outside Panama by non-resident recipients; (c) sales to legally established free zones, Colon Free Zone concessionaires, Panama-Pacifico special economic area, and international transport services. The exporter must maintain documentary proof that the goods left Panamanian customs territory and that the service was consumed outside Panama; zero-rating is denied if the goods are consumed domestically or repatriated without customs re-entry.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Declaración Única Aduanera - DUA) endorsed with the exit stamp
  • Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical shipment
  • Commercial Invoice/Factura Comercial satisfying DGI and customs requirements
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or foreign-bank SWIFT credit proof showing settlement of the export sale
  • Product movement or consumption certificate where the goods transited through a third country
  • Certificate of Origin (COO) if required to substantiate export treatment

Panama has progressively mandated real-time electronic invoicing through DGI-authorised electronic billing systems. Registered businesses must transmit XML/JASON-structured invoices to the DGI platform (SIGI) or through certified facturación electrónica suppliers; each invoice receives a DGI-validated digital stamp (QR code/hash) that must be incorporated before issuance to the buyer. Taxpayers above the DGI annual revenue threshold, and all businesses in high-controlled sectors, are subject to mandatory online clearance of each invoice; other registered taxpayers use DGI-preauthorised sequential invoice forms. Non-compliance with real-time clearance can suspend operating registration.

DGI conducts post-clearance export audits and cross-checks customs declarations, banking credits, and bills of lading. Fraudulent zero-rating or refund claims trigger: (a) provisional disallowance and clawback of all claimed zero-rated ITBMS benefits; (b) monthly interest surcharges on taxed amounts at the statutory rate; (c) fines ranging from 1 to 3 times the evaded tax; (d) immediate suspension of the RUC and export invoicing privileges; and (e) criminal liability for tax fraud against the legal representative or director. Directors and shareholders of a Panamanian PIF may be jointly liable for amounts clawed back if they actively participated in the fraudulent export scheme.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

Full, unrestricted convertibility on both current and capital account. Panama has been in continuous compliance with IMF Article VIII since 1970 and maintains no exchange-control statute, no currency licensing, and no tax or quota on inward or outward transfers of dividends, royalties, interest, loan principal, or equity capital. The US Dollar and Balboa are perfectly interchangeable and effectively USD. Practical friction arises only from standard bank-level compliance filters: beneficial-ownership disclosure, RUC tax clearance, sanctions screening, and source-of-funds documentation; none of these operate as a legal convertibility barrier, and no prior central-bank authorization is required for any transaction.

Export proceeds surrender rules

None applicable. Panama imposes no exchange surrender, no statutory repatriation deadline, and no mandatory conversion ratio on export earnings. Export receipts may be retained in USD, held offshore, or converted to any convertible currency at the exporter's discretion. Administrative obligations are limited to filing the customs export declaration through the national single window (VUCE/SICE) and retaining the commercial invoice, bill of lading, and sanitary export certificates issued by MINSA and AUPSA (and the US FDA/FSMA compliance dossier for shipments to the United States). The collecting bank validates these documents when crediting proceeds, but no deposit or conversion quota is triggered.

Central bank supervision: Superintendencia de Bancos de Panamá (SBP) — sole prudential and AML/CFT supervisor of the banking system; Banco Central de Panamá — statutory creation enacted in November 2022 and still in phased implementation, with monetary functions operationally delegated to the SBP, the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), and Banco Nacional de Panamá (BNP) as fiscal agent of the State; legal tender is the United States Dollar, making the U.S. Federal Reserve the de facto monetary anchor; the national unit of account, the Balboa (PAB, ISO 4217), circulates exclusively as coinage at a fixed 1:1 parity with USD.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Medium for domestic fintech wallets; Low for offshore digital EMIs. Panama does not issue a standalone e-money institution (EMI) license; payment and e-wallet undertakings must operate through a licensed bank, a trust structure, or the SBP's Ventanilla de Innovación Digital sandbox. Domestic fintech platforms (e.g., Cobre from Banco General, Tuyo from Banistmo, Walrus from Grupo Ficohsa) can host operating sub-wallets for domestic USD clearing and payroll but lack documentary credit, escrow, and FX hedging capability. Offshore EMIs (Wise, Airwallex, Payoneer, Mercury) generally refuse Panamanian Private Interest Foundation entities, fail to provide USD local-clearing rails, or invoke enhanced due diligence due to Panama's June 2024 FATF grey-list monitoring. Recommended architecture: a licensed Tier-1 commercial bank as the structural primary bank and a domestic fintech wallet only as a limited-float petty-cash and domestic collection pocket.

Core KYC Rejection Triggers & Defense Dossier

Root cause: Private Interest Foundation (PIF) as direct owner of an active trading company — money-laundering overlay risk because foundation beneficiaries are statutorily confidential under Law 25 of 1995.

Defense evidence: Signed beneficiary-disclosure waiver in favor of the bank; proof of filing of the beneficial-ownership return in the DGI/RUC register under Law 254 of 2021; certified copy of the Foundation Deed (Testimonio); Articles/Bylaws; Public Registry certificate; Foundation Council resolution authorizing account opening and signatories; source-of-wealth declaration covering the endowment.

Root cause: Cross-border USD wire patterns to and from high-sanctions-risk jurisdictions, notably PRC-based suppliers for equipment and ingredients, plus potential round-trip funding.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Endowment and equity injections must be routed as SWIFT MT103 wires from the Founder's pre-existing corporate or personal bank account into the PIF's escrow and operating accounts at the Panamanian bank. SWIFT Field 70 (Remittance Information) must read 'APORTE DE CAPITAL – FUNDACION [NAME] – RUC [NUMBER]' and the bank will map the transaction to the IMF BPM6 statistical category 'Direct Investment – Equity' (A.5.1.1); no Panamanian tax, withholding, or exchange-control declaration applies to capital injections. Recommended pre-funding sequence: (i) compliance risk profile call with the bank's AML officer; (ii) Foundation Council resolution fixing per-transfer limits; (iii) source-of-funds evidence comprising six months of bank statements and prior-year tax returns; (iv) initial wires capped at USD 500,000 each subject to a 24-hour validation hold; (v) full identification of the beneficiary for every correspondent bank. Domestic credits between Panama accounts clear through the SIP RTGS or ACH Panamá within 2-4 business hours, while international USD credits clear via the bank's U.S. correspondent (typically Citi, JPMorgan, or Bank of America) on the Fedwire or CHIPS network.Clearing rails hierarchy for the frozen dim sum factory: (i) domestic suppliers and payroll — internal transfer or ACH Panamá/SIP RTGS for same-day settlement in USD; (ii) international suppliers of ingredients, packaging, and consumables — SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer with 3-5 business days of USD clearing via the U.S. correspondent, using the trade purpose code 'Payment for imports of goods – machinery and food inputs' mapped to the BPM6 goods-import category; (iii) capital equipment and large ingredient contracts — Commercial Documentary Letter of Credit, sight or usance 90-180 days, issued under UCP 600 against 100% cash cover for the first 12 months and later against an approved facility, with full document sets (B/L, packing list, commercial invoice, certificate of origin, sanitary certificates); (iv) receivables financing — export factoring of the PIF's invoices using the 'Factura Cambiaria' instrument permitted under Panamanian factoring legislation. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

25% marginal rate for annual taxable profits exceeding USD 1,500,000; progressive brackets apply below that threshold: 15% on the first USD 11,000, 25% on the slice from USD 11,001 to USD 50,000, and 28% on the slice from USD 50,001 to USD 1,500,000 (Law 134 of 2021 reforms).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% withholding tax applies to dividends distributed to shareholders of registered shares out of Panamanian-source taxable income. The rate drops to 5% where the PIF timely paid the 4% advance dividend tax (pago directo) on its taxable profits. Dividends distributed out of exempt foreign-source income without proper attribution are subject to a 20% withholding rate, and bearer-share distributions carry a 20% rate.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

For calendar-year enterprises, the Form 851 filing window opens on the first business day of January and closes on 31 March of the following year; the DGI commonly extends the deadline to 15 April by published resolution. The quarterly advance payments are payable on the statutory period-end dates indicated on the DGI portal and cannot be carried past 31 March without surcharge.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: USD 10,000,000 in annual gross revenue OR USD 10,000,000 in net equity triggers the mandatory filing of the Transfer Pricing Annex with Form 851; a full comparability study (Local File) is required whenever aggregate related-party cross-border transactions reach USD 1,000,000 in a fiscal year, including purchases of frozen seafood, wheat-flour wrappers and seasoning ingredients from affiliated foreign suppliers and sales to offshore distributors.

Economic substance scope: Panama's Economic Substance Regulations (Law 254 of 2021 and DGI implementing resolution) apply to PIFs and corporations engaged in relevant activities. The PIF operating an ISIC 1074 industrial quick-freezing plant must demonstrate in Panama: physical production premises with the quick-freezing line, resident operational employees, local board or foundation council meetings with effective decision-making, and books and records kept in Panama. The annual economic substance declaration must evidence staffing, expenditures and premises; failure to comply triggers fines up to B/. 1,000,000, denial of territorial-source exemption, and reclassification of the PIF as a conduit for treaty and CFC purposes.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Omission

Statutory sanction: Fine equal to 100% of the evaded tax, 25% late-payment surcharge, 1% monthly moratorium interest, confiscation of goods in customs and closure of the production facility; criminal conviction under Article 280 of the Criminal Code carries imprisonment of 2 to 5 years.

Director personal liability: Directors and foundation council members are jointly and severally liable for unpaid tax; the DGI may impose preventive travel bans, attachment of personal bank accounts, and civil restitution orders before final judgment.
Shell Company and ESR Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Reassessment of the entire foreign and domestic income at the statutory rate with a 38-50% surcharge (100% in fraud cases); ESR fine up to B/. 1,000,000; suspension of the beneficial-owner registration and potential cancellation of the foundation by the Public Registry.

Director personal liability: Judges may pierce the PIF veil, holding directors personally liable for all reassessed tax and penalties and imposing travel restrictions and disqualification from directorship in Panama for up to 5 years.
AML and Beneficial Ownership Failures

Statutory sanction: Fines up to B/. 10,000 for late UBO filings and up to B/. 50,000 for false declarations; serious AML violations under Law 23 of 2015 attract fines up to B/. 5,000,000, freezing of bank accounts and referral to the Unidad de Análisis Financiero (UAF) for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: Directors face personal fines up to B/. 50,000, asset forfeiture for unexplained wealth, and imprisonment up to 10 years for money laundering offenses connected to the factory's payment flows.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: DGI re-determination of prices at arm's length, automatic upward adjustment of 10% on undocumented cross-border transaction values, plus 5% monthly surcharge and 1% monthly interest on the resulting tax deficiency; repeated non-compliance triggers an audit referral.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized the non-documented pricing are jointly liable for the deficiency and surcharge and may be disqualified from acting as the PIF's legal representative during DGI enforcement.
Withholding Tax Mis-Application on Repatriation

Statutory sanction: The PIF as withholding agent is liable for the full unwithheld amount, a 50% enforcement surcharge, monthly interest at 1%, and an administrative fine between B/. 1,000 and B/. 10,000; if unpaid, the tax becomes an enforceable lien against the factory assets.

Director personal liability: Directors and the foundation council are personally liable for maladministered withholding taxes; deliberate under-withholding or misclassification of dividends as loan repayments constitutes fiscal fraud and may lead to criminal conviction and personal asset seizure.
