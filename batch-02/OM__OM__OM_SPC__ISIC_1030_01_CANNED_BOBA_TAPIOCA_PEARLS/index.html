Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

No check-the-box election exists under Oman tax law; corporate income tax is levied at the entity level on every joint stock company and limited liability company, including a Single Person Company. Hybrid mismatch elections are not available for outbound investors under Oman domestic law; applicable only to the extent Oman adopts OECD BEPS Pillar Two or CbC-related domestic alignment, which generally does not create pass-through status.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 mandatory annual taxable supplies; OMR 19,250 voluntary registration threshold for businesses with taxable supplies exceeding OMR 19,250 annually; persons with deductible input tax may also voluntarily register under conditions set by VIDM

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero-rated export supply applies to exported goods where the supplier retains evidence of physical export out of Oman within 90 days of the supply. Zero rating also applies to international transport services and associated services, provided the recipient is outside Oman and services are directly linked to exported goods. For manufactured tapioca pearls, the exporter must be the taxable supplier and must hold the original shipping documents evidencing departure from Oman; export can be direct or through a licensed customs agent. The VAT Law zero-rates all goods exported outside the GCC implementing Common VAT Agreement.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) demonstrating customs approval number and export date
  • Original Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill showing Oman port of loading, consignee abroad, and description matching commercial invoice
  • Commercial invoice and packing list signed by the supplier
  • Evidence of payment receipt from foreign buyer (SWIFT MT103/TT Wire) or inward remittance certificate
  • Purchase order and contract evidencing export transaction
  • Customs delivery evidence: port gate pass, container check report, or vessel departure manifest

Oman requires VAT-registered businesses to issue e-invoices using approved electronic billing systems (Muqata’a platform). As of 2024, e-invoicing integration is being phased in for large taxpayers; Tax Authority mandates real-time or near-real-time reporting of invoice data through approved fiscal APIs for businesses exceeding OMR 5 million annual supplies; digital stamping and invoice clearance codes are required for B2B and B2C transactions in the phased roll-out. Failure to comply with e-invoicing technical specifications may result in penalties per VAT Law and Executive Regulations.

The Oman Tax Authority may deny zero-rated treatment and assess 5% VAT plus late penalties of 1% per month, up to a maximum of 100% of the tax, where export evidence is incomplete or where goods are diverted for local use. Fraudulent zero-rating claims attract criminal penalties under the Oman VAT Executive Regulations, including imprisonment and fines of OMR 25,000 to OMR 100,000, as well as blacklisting, customs hold, and withdrawal of VAT registration. Mis-declared exports will be clawed back retrospectively for a period of five years from the date of assessment.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible under a de facto fixed peg of OMR 2.6008 per USD. Oman maintains liberal current-account convertibility with no routine exchange-control permission required for trade payments, dividends, royalties or loan repayments by foreign-owned entities. Capital-account transactions are substantially open for non-residents investing through an Omani Single Person Company. Profit remittance is unrestricted provided the commercial bank receives documentary evidence of the underlying economic substance: audited financial statements, tax clearance certificate where applicable, a board resolution approving the distribution, and the beneficial owner's source-of-funds declaration. There is no CBO approval threshold for profit transfer and no quota or waiting period.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory surrender of export proceeds and no statutory conversion ratio. Export receipts may be credited to an Omani bank account as foreign currency or OMR. The exporter's bank must be able to verify the commercial reality of the receipt against the export customs declaration and the commercial invoice. In practice, banks expect unused foreign-currency export proceeds to be repatriated within a commercially reasonable period consistent with the sales contract and will require an explanation for any routing through third-country offshore collection accounts. CBO's AML/CFT framework treats export-proceeds diversion as a trade-based money-laundering red flag; documentary retention for at least five years is required.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) - Foreign Exchange and Investment Department

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Feasibility rating: Low. Offshore digital electronic-money institutions and payment e-money issuers are not recognised as regulated banks in Oman. They cannot obtain settlement accounts at the Central Bank of Oman and will not be accepted by Omani commercial banks for direct correspondent clearing. Any cross-border payment solution must be anchored by a CBO-licensed bank or by a locally licensed payment service provider operating under the CBO's payment system and AML/CFT framework. An offshore EMI may only act as the technology layer or user interface, while the legal clearing and custody functions must reside with an Omani bank. The SPC should therefore open its primary operational accounts directly with a licensed Omani bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Equity capital must be transmitted by SWIFT MT103 from the foreign shareholder's account to the SPC's named operational account at the Omani bank, using the SPC's full legal name, it's QIBAN-compliant IBAN and the commercial-registration number. Remittance information field 70 must clearly read 'Equity participation / capital injection' and carry the CBO balance-of-payments purpose code for direct investment equity capital. The sending bank must be instructed to quote the Omani beneficiary bank's SWIFT BIC. The Omani receiving bank will require the SPC's incorporation certificate, commercial registration, board resolution, account-opening mandate, shareholder passport copy and source-of-funds declaration. The funds may arrive as USD, EUR or OMR; conversion into OMR is automatically executed at the bank's dealing rate. All disbursements must be supported by the commercial invoice, transport document, packing list and the CBO trade declaration where applicable. OMR-denominated local supply payments may be cleared through the CBO-regulated national automated clearing house and RTGS infrastructure. Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% headline rate, reduced to a 3% preferential band on the first OMR 100,000 of taxable income and 15% on taxable income exceeding OMR 100,000, pursuant to Royal Decree 47/2020 amending Royal Decree 28/2009 (Income Tax Law), effective for tax years commencing on or after 1 January 2023

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% domestic withholding tax on dividends. Oman does not impose a dividend withholding tax under Article 23 of the Income Tax Law; distributions by the Omani SPC to its sole foreign shareholder are not subject to Omani WHT and may be remitted freely, subject to distributable profit requirements under the Commercial Companies Law and standard AML transfer documentation.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The annual return must be filed within 6 months after the close of the accounting year; a 31 December year-end therefore requires filing by 30 June of the following year. The Oman Tax Authority may grant a single extension of up to 3 months upon a justified written application submitted before the original due date.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: A Master File and Local File are mandatory where the Oman SPC's annual gross income exceeds OMR 5,000,000 or aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 1,000,000 in a tax year; simplified contemporaneous TP documentation is required where related-party transactions exceed OMR 300,000; and a TP disclosure form must be filed with the annual CIT return for any related-party transaction exceeding OMR 50,000, all expressed in OMR.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a standalone Economic Substance Regulations filing regime comparable to the UAE. Nevertheless, economic substance is enforced through the permanent establishment definition in Article 7 of the Income Tax Law, the general anti-abuse rule allowing the Oman Tax Authority to disregard arrangements lacking commercial purpose, and the Commercial Companies Law requirement of a registered physical office and genuine local presence. An SPC used as a mere letterbox or invoicing conduit with no factory, no Omani employees, no local management decisions and no operational control, while the non-resident shareholder retains all entrepreneurial risk and profit, is exposed to recharacterization, attribution of profits to the foreign principal, denial of treaty benefits, and revocation of any free-zone tax incentives.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion - Deliberate Understatement and Concealment

Statutory sanction: Assessment of the full evaded tax plus late-payment surcharge of 1% per month; criminal fine of up to OMR 10,000; imprisonment for up to 3 years; seizure of assets under the Tax Collection Law; and publication of the taxpayer's name and infraction by the Oman Tax Authority.

Director personal liability: The sole manager or foreign shareholder acting as de facto director may be jointly and severally liable for the unpaid corporate tax where the SPC has been intentionally stripped of assets; travel bans and asset-freeze orders may be issued; criminal conviction extends to the natural person who signed or directed the false return.
Shell Company and Sham Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Disregard of the SPC for tax purposes; recharacterization of the Oman entity as a permanent establishment of the foreign parent; upward assessment of undeclared profits; denial of treaty benefits and the 0% dividend remittance treatment; penalty of 25% to 50% of the understated tax; possible deregistration under the Commercial Companies Law.

Director personal liability: Directors voting for artificial intercompany contracts may face civil restitution claims from creditors and the tax authority; in fraudulent shell arrangements, the de facto manager is exposed to criminal fraud prosecution, personal confiscation orders, travel bans and disqualification from managing Omani companies.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Primary upward transfer pricing adjustment to the SPC's taxable income; late-payment surcharge on the resulting tax; secondary adjustment treating the shifted profit as a constructive dividend or loan; fixed monetary penalties for documentation failures up to OMR 20,000; additional penalty of up to 100% of the tax underpaid in egregious non-compliance cases.

Director personal liability: Directors who approve fictitious transfer prices may be held jointly and severally liable for the tax shortfall where their gross negligence or wilful misconduct caused the non-compliance; in aggravated cases, imprisonment for up to 3 years and confiscation of assets linked to the cross-border profit shifting.
AML - Beneficial Ownership and Sanctions Violation

Statutory sanction: Under the Omani AML Law and related criminal provisions, fines up to OMR 200,000 and imprisonment from 1 to 7 years for money laundering offenses; administrative penalties for beneficial ownership non-disclosure; freezing of bank accounts; blacklisting with the Commercial Registry; suspension or revocation of the SPC's commercial license and customs registration.

Director personal liability: Directors, compliance officers and the de facto beneficial owner face criminal liability, personal confiscation of proceeds, travel bans, and disqualification from holding office in Omani companies; bank compliance officers may file Suspicious Activity Reports that automatically trigger a criminal referral and freeze of corporate assets.
