Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (Oman Tax Authority, Sultaneate of Oman; official e-portal: https://taxoman.gov.om). The Authority operates in coordination with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) and the Customs Department of the Royal Oman Police via the National Single Window for Trade.

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) under the Omani Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019 as amended) at MoCIIP through the Invest Easy portal; 100% foreign ownership is permitted for non-strategic manufacturing activities such as electric cargo e-bike production. Step 2: Obtain the Commercial Register (CR) certificate and industrial license identifying ISIC 3091 / NAICS 336991 activity. Step 3: Upon CR issuance, the Oman Tax Authority auto-generates the entity tax file; the taxpayer must activate the profile by filing an online Taxpayer Registration Form at taxoman.gov.om, attaching the CR, lease or free-zone occupancy evidence, bank account details, and board/resolution paperwork. Step 4: Submit a separate VAT registration application through the same e-portal if taxable supplies in any 12-month period exceed OMR 500,000 (mandatory threshold) or OMR 50,000 (voluntary threshold). Step 5: If the SPC operates inside a designated free or special economic zone (e.g., Duqm SEZ, Sohar Free Zone, Salalah Free Zone, Al Mazunah Free Zone), obtain the zone investor certificate and integrate it with the income tax exemption registration; the free-zone certificate is required to claim customs duty suspension.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation / Territorial_Scope / Tax_Exempt_Zone_Eligible. The Omani SPC is a resident juridical person under Royal Decree 28/2009 (Income Tax Law, as amended); it is taxed at entity level only on Oman-source income. Effective CIT rates: 0% for taxable income up to OMR 100,000; 3% on OMR 100,001 to OMR 1,000,000; 15% on taxable income above OMR 1,000,000. A manufacturing SPC inside a special economic zone may substitute standard CIT with a statutory tax holiday (e.g., Duqm SEZ grants 30-year CIT exemption). No provincial or municipal income taxes exist in Oman; Zakat is not imposed on non-Omani SPC shareholders.

Available election forms

No check-the-box or US-style entity classification election is available under Omani law; the SPC is mandatorily classified as a taxable corporation. No partnership pass-through election exists. Available statutory elections include: (i) VAT tax-period election between monthly and quarterly filing based on annual turnover; (ii) election for group VAT registration for related entities under common control; (iii) choice between cash refund and offset of excess VAT credits; (iv) election to treat intra-GCC supplies under the destination reverse-charge mechanism; (v) no hybrid mismatch entity elections - Oman has not enacted OECD hybrid mismatch rules altering the entity default classification.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 500,000 annual taxable supplies in Oman; Voluntary registration: OMR 50,000 annual taxable supplies or taxable expenses. Free-zone establishments are required to register if their non-free-zone taxable supplies exceed the threshold; non-resident businesses making taxable supplies in Oman have no threshold and must appoint a tax representative.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of goods by the SPC (electric cargo e-bikes and spare parts) are zero-rated at 0% VAT where the goods are physically dispatched from Oman to a buyer outside Oman (including to GCC states subject to destination-state reverse charge), shipment is evidenced by a customs export declaration, and the goods leave Oman within the statutory timeframe (generally 90 days from the date of supply). Related offshore engineering, logistics and warranty services to non-resident customers qualify for 0% only where the recipient is outside Oman, the benefit of the service is wholly consumed outside Oman, and the service is not directly connected to Oman real property or tangible movable goods situated in Oman at the time of supply.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan) cleared by Oman Customs through the Oman Trade Single Window
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill evidencing physical export and consignment details
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or SWIFT MT103/telex wire confirmation proving receipt of export sale proceeds
  • Commercial invoice and packing list referencing zero-rated supply and the SPC VAT registration number
  • Copy of the free-zone or customs warehouse movement record where goods are exported from a bonded facility

As of 2025, Oman has not yet switched on a mandatory real-time e-invoice clearance or digital fiscal stamping system. VAT-registered persons must however issue sequential numbered tax invoices (paper or e-format) containing statutory fields and retain the records for 10 years. VAT returns must be submitted electronically via taxoman.gov.om. The Oman Tax Authority is progressively deploying e-invoicing requirements and API-based machine-readable invoice exchange for large taxpayers; taxpayers should monitor the rollout to avoid non-compliance when the go-live date is fixed.

Zero-rating claims are subject to risk-based post-clearance audits by the Oman Tax Authority and Oman Customs. Where the mandatory export bundle is incomplete or inconsistent, the 0% rate is reversed; output VAT becomes due at 5% plus 1% monthly late-payment interest from the original due date. Import VAT and input VAT previously claimed on exported or re-imported inventory may be clawed back with administrative penalties (typically OMR 1,000 per falsified invoice and up to OMR 10,000 per offending tax period). Proven tax evasion exposes the SPC and its directors to criminal prosecution with fines from OMR 1,000 to OMR 10,000 and imprisonment of up to 5 years, in addition to publication of the conviction and debarment from board positions. Civil liability extends to the tax representative and external auditors who knowingly participated in false zero-rating documentation.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current account and capital account transactions and is hard-pegged to the USD at OMR 1 = USD 2.6008 (selling 2.6009 / buying 2.6007). Oman imposes no general exchange-control approvals for foreign investors under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019) and the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019). Capital account convertibility is effectively unrestricted for 100% foreign-owned SPCs, but banks require documentary support (audited financials, board/shareholder resolutions, tax clearance) for profit repatriation and capital liquidation remittances; the SPC must be in good standing with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MCIIP) and the Oman Tax Authority before dividends or capital redemptions are routed abroad. Current account payments for trade invoices are processed freely upon presentation of valid invoices, bills of lading, and import/export declarations.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman has no statutory export-proceeds surrender obligation and no mandatory conversion ratio of export receipts into OMR; exporters may retain foreign currency earnings in foreign-currency current accounts held with licensed Omani banks without time-bound repatriation, provided the proceeds enter Oman through the formal banking system and are traceable within the customer's know-your-customer and anti-money-laundering profile. The CBO expects exports to be financed and settled via banking channels and retains the discretionary power to request supporting export documentation (Customs Single Administrative Document, E-invoice, Bill of Lading, Proceeds Confirmation Statement) for statistical balance-of-payments reporting; for the e-bike manufacturer, inward USD receipts from EU/GCC distributors should be credited to a USD operational account and, where OMR is needed for payroll and local taxes, converted at prevailing interbank rates.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO), acting under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000) and the Foreign Exchange Operations Regulations, together with the Capital Market Authority for non-bank financial instruments; the CBO is the sole licensing and supervisory authority for all onshore commercial banks and foreign bank branches.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Feasibility: LOW (Restricted). Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, or Payoneer do not hold CBO banking licenses and cannot provide primary corporate settlement accounts, OMR nostro accounts, or issuance of Omani trade-license-linked current accounts; CBO regulations require any entity accepting deposits or providing payment accounts to be a licensed bank, specialised finance company, or licensed Payment Service Provider under the new Payment and Settlement System Law. An offshore EMI may be used only as a supplementary collection facade for small-value inbound receivables from individual customers, but it cannot serve as the legal treasury rail for corporate trade settlements, VAT/customs payments, or OMR payroll, and any routing of core export collections through an unlicensed EMI would create KYC-reputational and FX-compliance defects during CBO audits.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound equity funding for the SPC must be executed via SWIFT MT103 from the parent/shareholder's bank for the benefit of the SPC's dedicated USD capital escrow account at the chosen Omani bank, referencing purpose code 'FDI - Equity Capital Injection' (conforming to the CBO/BOPS classification of inward direct investment); the remittance should include the SPC's CR number and the shareholder's full legal name in Field 50K, the instructing bank in Field 52A, the beneficiary SPC and bank account in Fields 57A/59, and a narrative in Field 70 'CAPITAL INJECTION PER BOARD RESOLUTION DATED YYYY-MM-DD'. The Omani receiving bank will require a certified copy of the shareholder resolution, the Amended Commercial Register extract reflecting the injected capital, a certificate of source of funds, and a compliance clearance before releasing funds from the escrow ledger into the operational account; deposits exceeding OMR 20,000 trigger automatic CBO transaction reporting and may be temporarily held pending documentary verification. Loans from the foreign parent are routed with purpose code 'Intercompany Loan - Import Substitution' and devex/IKM documentation to support future interest and principal repatriation.Settlement of imported components (motors, controllers, lithium batteries) and export logistics is executed through: (i) SWIFT MT103 for direct Telegraphic Transfer payment of proforma invoices and freight charges, requiring the customs invoice, packing list, and supplier bank details; (ii) Commercial Documentary Letter of Credit (MT700) with usance drafts of 30-90 days for high-value battery-cell purchases, confirmed where possible through the correspondent bank of the Chinese or EU supplier; and (iii) Local cheques/fawri (CBO RTGS and OmanNet clearing) for domestic disbursements such as rent, utilities, and wages. All outbound FX payments are processed through the CBO-cleared interbank market with same-day settlement for OMR/USD; the bank will verify the underlying trade document against a real-time compliance checklist before releasing any payment exceeding USD 50,000, and the SPC must maintain a fully reconciled import-export ledger to satisfy CBO balance-of-payments audits.Deliverable OMR/USD FX forward contracts (up to 12 months) with onshore licensed banks under an ISDA Master Agreement or the bank's standard FX Derivatives Terms, used to lock the pegged rate for large import LC settlements; premium is minimal given the 2.6008 peg.FX spot transactions for immediate OMR/USD conversion of export proceeds, settled T+2 via the CBO interbank infrastructure.Cross-currency swaps (CCS) between OMR and USD for matching long-term asset liabilities, permitted for corporate entities with audited financials and CBO-recognised end-user derivative eligibility; utilised only when funding is sourced in USD against multi-year OMR receivables.Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs) referencing OMR are discouraged onshore and unavailable via CBO-licensed counterparties due to the hard peg; where extreme exigency arises, NDF execution is limited to offshore branches of international banks under strictly sanctioned end-use documentation.Plain-vanilla FX call/put options on USD/OMR executed with a commercial bank to protect against the tail event of a devaluation band adjustment; option premium and payoff are treated as FX-transaction expense and require CBO derivative-eligibility classification.

E-commerce / gateway settlement fit: Feasibility: LOW (Restricted). Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) such as Wise, Revolut Business, Airwallex, or Payoneer do not hold CBO banking licenses and cannot provide primary corporate settlement accounts, OMR nostro accounts, or issuance of Omani trade-license-linked current accounts; CBO regulations require any entity accepting deposits or providing payment accounts to be a licensed bank, specialised finance company, or licensed Payment Service Provider under the new Payment and Settlement System Law. An offshore EMI may be used only as a supplementary collection facade for small-value inbound receivables from individual customers, but it cannot serve as the legal treasury rail for corporate trade settlements, VAT/customs payments, or OMR payroll, and any routing of core export collections through an unlicensed EMI would create KYC-reputational and FX-compliance defects during CBO audits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File documentation is mandatory when the aggregate value of related-party transactions exceeds OMR 1,000,000 in a tax year. Master File documentation is mandatory where the ultimate parent undertaking's consolidated turnover exceeds OMR 50,000,000. In addition, every Omani SPC engaged in controlled transactions, regardless of volume, must attach a transfer pricing disclosure statement (Form TR-50) to its annual CIT return.

Economic substance scope: Oman's general anti-abuse rule (GAAR) and the substance-over-form doctrine apply: the SPC must maintain a real manufacturing footprint in Oman, including an operational assembly facility, inventory, actual employed engineers/technicians, independent production records, and substantive decision-making for procurement and pricing. The manufacturing of electric cargo e-bikes cannot be structured as a passive distribution-only letterbox; where the Oman SPC lacks the personnel, premises, and risk control to support the intercompany pricing, the Tax Authority may recharacterize the transactions, deny deductions, apply deemed profits, or impute a permanent establishment for the foreign parent.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax evasion and fraudulent misdeclaration

Statutory sanction: Assess tax on estimated profits, impose an additional 50% penalty of the evaded tax, add late-payment interest at 1% per month, confiscate fraudulent documents, and refer the case to the Public Prosecutor for criminal tax fraud proceedings. Maximum tax fraud fines may reach OMR 200,000.

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder, the resident manager, and any authorised director who signed or caused the fraudulent return are jointly and severally liable for the back taxes, penalties, and interest. Criminal conviction may result in imprisonment of up to three years, travel bans, and disqualification from holding a commercial licence or corporate directorship in Oman.
Shell company and insufficient economic substance

Statutory sanction: Disallow all local deductions and related-party costs, assess the SPC on a deemed profit rate of 15% of gross turnover, revoke the commercial registration licence, deny treaty benefits, and impose a fine of up to OMR 25,000 for causing the Oman Tax Authority to rely on false substance declarations.

Director personal liability: Directors and the SPC's sole shareholder may be jointly liable for the deemed tax and fine if the artificial structure was used to strip taxable profits. The court may issue travel bans, freeze personal and corporate bank accounts, and order the personal restitution of profits extracted from the SPC through the sham arrangement.
Transfer pricing manipulation

Statutory sanction: An arm's length adjustment increasing taxable income to a standard manufacturing gross profit margin, an additional negligence penalty of 25% of the underpaid tax, denial of the related-party cost deduction, plus a late documentation fine of up to OMR 10,000.

Director personal liability: Directors who were actually aware of the artificial markup may be personally assessed up to OMR 10,000 for participating in the transfer pricing mispricing. Where fraud is established, they may face criminal liability, including a one-year disqualification of the SPC owner/director from serving as a director in Oman and a travel prohibition pending settlement of the tax claim.
Anti-money laundering and illicit profit repatriation

Statutory sanction: Under Oman's Anti-Money Laundering Law (Royal Decree 30/2016), a legal person may be fined up to OMR 50,000, have its bank accounts frozen, and have its commercial registration revoked. The laundered proceeds are subject to confiscation, and the entity is also blacklisted by the Oman Financial Intelligence Unit.

Director personal liability: The SPC's director, sole shareholder, and any compliance officer who knowingly participated in or intentionally ignored the suspicious fund flows are personally liable to criminal prosecution. Sanctions include imprisonment of up to seven years, a travel ban, passport cancellation, and personal asset forfeiture for funds derived from the unlawful conduct.
