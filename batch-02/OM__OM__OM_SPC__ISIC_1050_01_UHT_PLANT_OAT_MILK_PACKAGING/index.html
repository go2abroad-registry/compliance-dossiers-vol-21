Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime Mandatory Capital Deposit & Lockup

The Commercial Companies Law imposes no statutory minimum paid-up capital for a Single Person Company; the sole shareholder must declare and subscribe the full issued capital and deposit it in a licensed Omani bank account upon incorporation. For industrial food-processing and beverage-manufacturing activities, the industrial licensing directorate may administratively require committed initial capital of at least OMR 100,000, with a proportional initial deposit evidenced by a blocked escrow arrangement before the industrial investment license is released.

Funding & unlock path: Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority clearance: fire safety certificate covering evacuation routes, fire extinguishers, emergency water supply, and annual inspection; Municipality structural load inspection for UHT tanks, silos and packaging lines; Ministry of Labour occupational health clearance addressing noise, confined-space safety, and chemical handling in CIP utilities.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; electronic portal: https://taxoman.gov.om (tax registration, VAT returns, and e-invoicing services); company incorporation via Invest Easy portal of the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP).

E-government filing portal

Step 1: Incorporate the foreign-owned OM_SPC through MoCIIP Invest Easy (Commercial Companies Law RD 18/2019; 100% foreign equity permitted). Step 2: Within 3 months of commercial licence issuance, register online with OTA under Article 3 of the Income Tax Law, uploading the notarised Certificate of Incorporation, Articles of Association, Commercial Licence and attested shareholder passports; OTA issues the Tax Card containing the Tax Registration Number (TRN). Step 3: Separately obtain the VAT TIN in OTA's VAT module within 30 days of the date the annual taxable supply estimate crosses OMR 38,500, filing the electronic VAT-registration application with forecast sales data and lease/contract evidence; voluntary registration is available at OMR 19,250.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation — OM_SPC is a resident Omani corporate taxpayer under Article 8 of the Income Tax Law and a distinct 'Taxable Person' under the VAT Law; no transparency or pass-through treatment is available; corporate tax applies on a global-income basis (foreign PE relief by foreign-tax credit) and VAT applies on domestic taxable supplies.

Available election forms

Omani law has no check-the-box election. Available statutory elections: (i) voluntary VAT registration at OMR 19,250; (ii) group VAT registration election for related Omani taxable persons; (iii) SME simplified income-tax registration and filing election; (iv) optional transfer-pricing documentation election under BEPS-era RD 38/2020; (v) deemed-PE/agent election for foreign contractors; hybrid-mismatch deductions are disallowed automatically without election.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 38,500 annual taxable supplies (mandatory registration within 30 days); voluntary registration for supplies at or above OMR 19,250.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Exports of manufactured UHT oat-milk beverages (HS 2202.99) and their packaging services are zero-rated under Article 41(1) of the VAT Law provided the goods physically leave Oman (outside the GCC) by direct shipment or within a permitted supply-chain movement within 90 days of supply, the supplier retains in Oman the export evidence, and the buyer is outside the Sultanate. Input VAT on raw materials, carton aseptic packaging and freight scoped to exported goods qualifies for monthly refund/offset on the VAT return; for exporters expected to claim over OMR 250,000 quarterly, OTA fast-track refund processing is available subject to complete documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan / single-window exit record) certified by Royal Oman Police Customs
  • Original negotiable Bill of Lading or Airway Bill evidencing physical departure from an Omani port or airport
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank/swift credit advice evidencing receipt of export consideration in convertible foreign currency
  • Commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, and the OTA zero-rated schedule reconciliation with the VAT return

OTA's Fatura national e-invoicing framework is mandatory in phases: B2G invoices and large-taxpayer invoices must be transmitted in near-real time through OTA web-service APIs, centrally validated, and issued with a digital stamp/QR verification code; from the go-live date, only invoices with OTA validation IDs support input-VAT credit. Smaller B2B/B2C taxpayers may use OTA-compliant standard electronic or paper tax invoices until their onboarding wave. Real-time fiscal clearance operates through the taxoman.gov.om API with the OTA-issued authentication certificate (mTLS) and VAT-registration TRN.

OTA may audit export files within the 5-year limitation window; where export evidence fails or the goods did not leave Oman within 90 days, the zero-rating is clawed back at 5% VAT from the invoice date plus a late-payment penalty of 3% per month (Article 67 VAT Law) and daily-delay fines. Knowingly false zero-rating or forged export certificates constitute tax evasion under Article 72, attracting up to 3 years' imprisonment and fines up to OMR 10,000, in addition to suspension of the taxpayer's Fatura e-invoicing credentials and export-refund fast-track privileges.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR, ISO 4217) is fully convertible for current-account and capital-account transactions under a de facto fixed exchange-rate regime pegged to the US dollar (USD 1 = OMR 0.3845; 1 OMR = USD 2.6008). No general exchange-control law or prior CBO approval is required for bona fide foreign direct investment, trade settlements, or profit remittances. Current-account convertibility is unrestricted; capital-account convertibility permits 100% foreign ownership of the SPC under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree No. 50/2019) and Companies Law (Royal Decree No. 18/2019). Dividend and capital repatriation require standardised documentation (audited financial statements, tax compliance certificate, corporate resolutions) and are subject to the SPC's settled tax position. The CBO may impose statistical balance-of-payments reporting and, in systemic stress scenarios, prudential measures, but no forward exchange control restrictions are presently imposed.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory export-proceeds surrender requirement and no mandatory conversion ratio for export receipts. The SPC may freely retain USD, EUR, or other foreign-currency export proceeds in onshore or offshore foreign-currency accounts. As a matter of bank credit policy (not statute), trade-finance facilities may condition on repatriation of export receivables within 180 days of shipment. The CBO requires only balance-of-payments statistical classification of export flows through the settlement bank; a zero-surrender, full-retention treatment is the legal baseline.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) (Arabic: البنك المركزي العماني), acting under the Banking Law (Royal Decree No. 114/2000, as amended). Concurrent authorities for capital-flow documentation include the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) for investment licensing and the Oman Tax Authority (OTA) for fiscal clearance evidence.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Wise Fit: High

Low as a primary treasury rail. Oman has no equivalence regime for offshore digital EMI licences, and non-bank fintechs cannot issue OMR IBANs or access the CBO RTGS directly. Offshore EMIs (e.g., Wise, Revolut Business) may only effect OMR-leg payments through a CBO-licensed correspondent bank and are therefore limited to supplementary, low-value disbursement use. Any EMI onboarding must satisfy Oman's AML/CFT Law (Royal Decree No. 30/2016, as amended) and the CBO's National Payment Systems oversight. The SPC should maintain its core liquidity at a licensed Tier-1 bank and restrict EMIs to minor payroll or remote-workshop expenses.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% for Oman-incorporated companies distributing dividends to resident or non-resident shareholders; Oman historically does not impose dividend withholding tax, apart from a general anti-abuse rule for distributions exceeding available profits.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1,000,000 (approximately USD 2.6 million) – transactions with related parties exceeding this threshold mandate maintaining a Local File and Master File; transfer pricing documentation must be prepared by a registered tax agent and submitted upon request of the Tax Authority.

Economic substance scope: Oman has not enacted separate Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) as in Bahrain or the UAE; however, economic substance principles are embedded in the Income Tax Law's anti-avoidance provisions (Article 11 of Royal Decree 28/2009), which require that transactions be entered for genuine commercial reasons and that a foreign-owned SPC must have real substance in Oman – physical office, staff, board meetings, and decision-making – to benefit from DTA treatment and to avoid a potential deemed-recharacterisation.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Fraudulent Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Imprisonment for up to 3 years and a fine of OMR 5,000; in addition, the Tax Authority may impose a 100% penalty on evaded tax and deny loss carry-forwards.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil will be pierced under Article 67 of the Commercial Companies Law; directors and managers are jointly and severally liable for the company's tax debts if they have acted in bad faith or with intent to defraud. Directors face travel bans, seizure of personal assets, and imprisonment.
Failure to Maintain Transfer Pricing Documentation

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may make a discretionary upward adjustment of taxable income, disallow the expense, and levy an additional tax of 10% on the adjusted amount; penalties of up to OMR 1,000 apply for failure to provide documentation within 30 days.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly approve non-arm's-length arrangements without a transfer pricing analysis may be considered negligent; personal liability may be imposed for unremitted withholding taxes on royalties paid to the foreign parent.
Improper Dividend and Profit Repatriation (Illegal Capital Outflow)

Statutory sanction: The Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP) can strike off the company, impose a fine of OMR 500 to OMR 5,000, and order disgorgement of the excess distribution; the Tax Authority may treat the distribution as a taxable deemed dividend and collect applicable taxes.

Director personal liability: Directors are personally liable to creditors and the company for the full amount of unlawful dividends under Article 208 of the Commercial Companies Law, with criminal liability for fraudulent bankruptcy (up to 5 years imprisonment).
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: The Tax Authority may disregard the existence of the Omani SPC and tax the foreign parent directly, impose a withholding tax on deemed Oman-source income, and reject treaty benefits, leading to double taxation.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors who accept compensation for acting without substance may face fines, imprisonment, and restitution; the foreign parent may have its board members served with criminal charges in Oman under the Commercial Fraud Law, and the Central Bank of Oman may freeze funds associated with unexplained wealth.
