Registered Agent & Physical Address Licensed Agent Required

Not permissible for a manufacturing company engaged in ISIC 3040 military tactical vehicle production. Oman law requires the SPC to have a physical registered office located in the Sultanate, evidenced by a valid tenancy contract (Ejari-registered) or title deed, and the offices must be the actual site where the company operates, maintains registers, and receives government correspondence. A pure virtual office address is likely to be rejected for this defense-sector manufacturing scope. However, a serviced office or business center address may be permissible in the short pre-incorporation stage to obtain the Certificate of Incorporation, but the industrial license phase requires a physical manufacturing site and operational premises, and MOCIIP / Ministry of Defense / Royal Oman Police may condition issuance of licenses on actual operating premises. Commercial substance requirements apply on an ongoing basis: payroll, management decisions, factory floor operations, storage of key records, and essential administrative functions must be located in Oman.

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital applies to an SPC under the Commercial Companies Law; however, for defense-sector manufacturing, the Ministry of Defence and industrial licensing authorities generally require a paid-up share capital of at least OMR 1,000,000, deposited in a licensed commercial bank in Oman. The share capital must be fully subscribed and certified by a banker's deposit certificate before final license issuance.

Funding & unlock path: Fire safety certification from the Royal Oman Police – Civil Defence and Ambulance Authority; structural load inspection certificate issued by a licensed civil engineer; occupational health and safety clearance from the Ministry of Labour; and workplace authorization for the production facility meeting the Facility Code for Defense Manufacturing sites.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial is fully convertible at the official pegged rate of 1 OMR = 2.60028 USD. Current account transactions, including trade-related cross-border payments and service fees, are generally unrestricted and processed by licensed banks after standard AML/KYC scrutiny. Capital account flows, including inbound equity injection and foreign direct investment for a 100% foreign-owned Single Person Company, are permissible without general CBO prior approval but require compliance with foreign investment registration, commercial registration, and beneficial ownership disclosure. Profit remittances require audited financial statements, proof of distributable earnings, verification of tax settlement with the Oman Tax Authority, and submission of standard bank remittance declarations. OMR is not a deliverable offshore currency outside the GCC clearing framework in practice, so most international clearing is settled in USD through CBO-regulated correspondent banking channels.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose a statutory surrender or mandatory conversion ratio for export proceeds. Exporters may keep export receipts in foreign currency in a licensed foreign currency account or convert to OMR at the prevailing market rate. There is no prescribed CBO timeline for converting export proceeds; however, banks require inward remittance advices and underlying export documentation (commercial invoice, bill of lading, customs declaration, and the relevant CBO balance-of-payments reporting code) to classify the receipt. For defense and tactical hardware exports, the export-import license issued by the competent Omani authorities and the end-user certificate must accompany the transaction to avoid AML/export-control holds.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO). Foreign exchange control, supervision of licensed banks, and cross-border payment system oversight are exercised by the CBO under the Banking Law, the Foreign Exchange Regulations, and the AML/CFT Law. Regulatory coordination for foreign investment registration is shared with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low. Offshore digital EMI and virtual payment accounts are not recognized by the CBO as regulated clearing institutions. Any payment service provider or electronic money institution operating in Oman must be licensed by the CBO and maintain a physical or agency arrangement with a licensed Omani bank. For an ISIC 3040 defense-tactical business, fintech EMIs are legally unsuitable as the primary clearing rail and pose an unacceptable concentration risk; they can only be used as ancillary payment interfaces where the underlying settlement remains with a tier-1 licensed Omani bank.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant routing requires SWIFT MT103 telegraphic transfer from the foreign parent account to the SPC's capital escrow account maintained at the Omani bank. The payment narrative must clearly state 'SHARE CAPITAL INJECTION' and reference the SPC commercial registration and capital subscription resolution. Banks will map the transaction to the CBO balance-of-payments code for inward direct investment in a nonresident-owned enterprise. Verification includes the foreign parent's audited financial statement, bank source-of-funds confirmation, shareholder registers, and the Oman MOIIP foreign investment registration certificate. The funds may be credited as OMR if the parent uses its own FX; alternatively, the Omani bank can convert the USD at the pegged rate after deduction of normal FX spreads. No CBO exchange control approval is required where the remittance is for an allowed capital entry purpose and passes AML/KYC scrutiny.Trade settlement uses established bank clearing rails: (a) SWIFT MT103 for direct supplier payments on confirmed invoices; (b) MT202 for bank-to-bank USD credit transfers where intermediary banks are involved; (c) commercial documentary letters of credit issued by the Omani bank and advised through MT700, with sight or usance payment against presentation of shipping/warehouse documents; (d) collections under MT799/MT400-style instructions for DP/DA transactions where LC is not required. For all defense-related outbound payments, the bank will require the export license, end-user certificate, commercial invoice, proof of delivery terms, and transaction-level sanctions screening. OMR is not normally used for cross-border trade clearing; USD is the preferred settlement currency through the CBO-regulated bank's nostro network.Deliverable FX forward contracts with a CBO-licensed commercial bank for USD/OMR and major trade currencies, limited to bona fide current or capital transaction exposure.Plain vanilla FX options with a licensed bank to hedge foreign currency procurement or export payment volatility.Cross-currency swaps between USD and OMR for long-term capital/financing hedging, executed on an OTC basis with regulated counterparties.Non-deliverable forwards are permissible only where offered by an authorized intermediary as an offshore risk-management tool for a non-deliverable currency; because OMR is pegged and tradable onshore, NDF use is not normally required and may be disfavored by Omani regulators for purely speculative purposes.

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (flat headline corporate rate; SME taxpayers benefit from progressive rates: 0% up to OMR 30,000; 3% OMR 30,001-100,000; 5% OMR 100,001-200,000; 7.5% OMR 200,001-300,000; 10% OMR 300,001-500,000; 12.5% OMR 500,001-1,000,000; 15% above OMR 1,000,000).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

10% statutory withholding tax on gross dividends paid by the Omani SPC to a non-resident shareholder not operating through an Omani permanent establishment (Oman Income Tax Law Article 61). Dividends paid to resident shareholders are exempt from withholding. The 10% is deducted at source unless a double tax agreement provides a lower rate and the OTA holds the required documentation.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 500,000 aggregate value of controlled related-party transactions per tax year triggers a mandatory Local File; multinational groups with consolidated revenue exceeding OMR 75 million must prepare a Master File; groups with consolidated revenue exceeding OMR 300 million must file a Country-by-Country Report with the OTA within 12 months of the group year-end.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not apply a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations regime. Substance is instead enforced through tax residency rules based on the place of effective management, the general anti-abuse provisions, and licensing requirements. An Omani SPC manufacturing all-terrain military tactical vehicles must demonstrate genuine operational substance in Oman: physical production facilities, locally employed engineers and management, board decisions taken in Oman, payroll and active license compliance; otherwise the OTA may disregard the entity, shift POEM outside Oman, deny treaty benefits and recharacterize profits.
