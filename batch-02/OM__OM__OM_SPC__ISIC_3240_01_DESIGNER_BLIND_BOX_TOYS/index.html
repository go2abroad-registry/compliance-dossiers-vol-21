Governing statute

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum and Articles of Association (M&A); the executed and notarized M&A is filed for issuance of the Commercial Register Certificate, which serves as the Certificate of Incorporation.

Incorporation processing time

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutory minimum paid-up capital threshold is prescribed for a Single Person Company under Royal Decree No. 18/2019. The subscriber must state the issued share capital in the M&A, and the capital must be fully paid-up and evidenced by a bank certificate before commercial registration finalization if the activity is a licensed manufacturing operation. Oman does not require a separate escrow account for SPC formation, but MoCIIP may require a bank capital-deposit certificate for regulated industrial activities.

Funding & unlock path: The production facility must obtain a Civil Defense and Ambulance Authority (CDAA) clearance after a physical inspection covering fire alarms, extinguishers, exits, evacuation plans, and handling of flammable paints. In addition, the employer must secure a workplace occupational safety clearance from the Ministry of Labour, including noise controls, dust ventilation, spray-booth exhaust, electrical grounding, and employee health certificates.

Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA), Sultanate of Oman; official e-services portal: https://eservices.taxoman.gov.om (Integrated Tax System accessible through OTA portal; customs duties administered separately by Oman Customs/Asyad Customs through Bayan e-clearance platform)

E-government filing portal

A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) registered in Oman must obtain a Tax Registration Number (TRN) from the Oman Tax Authority. The procedure is: (1) file the online application through the OTA e-services portal using the authenticated Corporate Registration Certificate issued by the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP); (2) submit the Memorandum of Association, the Commercial Register excerpt, constitutional documents of the foreign shareholder, and a certified copy of the foreign shareholder’s passport or incorporation certificate; (3) appoint a resident tax agent or registered representative if the SPC is managed or represented by a non-resident; (4) apply for income tax registration within the timelines prescribed by the Income Tax Law, and separately apply for VAT registration if taxable supplies or imports exceed the registration threshold; (5) receive the Tax Registration Card/Certificate which is used for all tax filings, VAT returns, customs clearances and export zero-rating claims. Foreign SPCs do not require minimum paid-in capital and may be 100% foreign-owned; however, a physical or invited address in Oman for service of notices must be maintained.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation; territorial taxation on Oman-source income; SPC is a juridical person separate from the sole shareholder — A foreign-owned Single Person Company (SPC) registered in Oman must obtain a Tax Registration Number (TRN) from the Oman Tax Authority. Foreign SPCs do not require minimum paid-in capital and may be 100% foreign-owned; however, a physical or invited address in Oman for service of notices must be maintained.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation; territorial taxation on Oman-source income; SPC is a juridical person separate from the sole shareholder

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a check-the-box regime equivalent to the United States. The SPC is automatically treated as a taxable entity for both income tax and VAT. Available elections and statutory mechanisms include: (i) optional voluntary VAT registration for businesses earning taxable supplies above OMR 100,000 but below the mandatory OMR 500,000 threshold; (ii) election to apply cash or accrual basis of accounting for VAT purposes in accordance with the Executive Regulations; (iii) tax group provisions permitting one taxable group registration for VAT where 95% or greater ownership exists; (iv) participation exemption election for foreign dividends and capital gains where the shareholding threshold and substance conditions under the Income Tax Law are satisfied; and (v) transfer-pricing documentation relief elections available to small and medium enterprises. Hybrid mismatch elections are not generally available absent specific OECD-compliant legislation.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

OMR 500,000 of annual taxable supplies (mandatory); OMR 100,000 of annual taxable supplies (voluntary); no threshold for imports into Oman — all imports are taxable unless specifically exempted

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods exported by the SPC to a destination outside the GCC (including via maritime trade routes through Sohar and Salalah ports) are zero-rated for VAT where the supplier retains full documentary evidence that the goods were physically removed from Oman within actionable time limits, the supply was made on sale or transfer of ownership, and the goods did not undergo any act of value-addition in another GCC jurisdiction. Design services, toy manufacturing services, and logistics services supplied by the SPC are treated as zero-rated exported services when the customer is a non-resident conducting business outside Oman, the services are used wholly outside Oman, and the SPC holds the relevant export invoices and supplier agreements. Ancillary items such as freight or export-handling are zero-rated only when directly linked to the exported goods and evidenced by export documentation.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayan/Asyad Customs electronic export declaration sealed by Oman Customs)
  • Bill of Lading / Air Waybill showing consignee outside Oman and export port of exit
  • Inward Remittance Certificate (tax invoice and proof of overseas receipt of the export sales consideration, net of hedging, through Oman banking channels)

Oman requires every taxable person to issue a VAT tax invoice containing mandatory fields as prescribed under the VAT Law. Electronic invoices and digital records are accepted if they are accessible, legible, and capable of being reproduced in Arabic or English. As of the current implementation, Oman does not yet mandate continuous transaction controls, real-time fiscal clearance, or digital stamping of invoices through the OTA API. The OTA has announced a phased e-invoicing modernization roadmap; once operational, taxpayers will be required to interconnect their ERP/billing systems to the OTA clearance platform. Until then, compliant e-invoices (structured XML/PDF/C-II) may be transmitted electronically, but no fiscal stamping or pre-clearance is mandatory.

The OTA applies strict documentary proof standards to zero-rating claims. If it is established that the export did not take place, the documentary bundle is false, or the foreign currency proceeds were not repatriated within 180 days from issuance of export invoice, the tax authority will claw back the zero-rated treatment, impose the standard 5% VAT on the original supply, charge late-payment interest from the date of supply, and may assess fines up to 300% of the tax evaded. Criminal penalties for deceptive export-credit claims include imprisonment up to ten years and/or fines up to OMR 20,000. The SPC may also be disbarred from future export zero-rating, have its VAT registration cancelled, and be subject to retrospective six-year statutory audit assessments.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions in practice; OMR is pegged to the USD at an official central rate of approximately USD 2.6008 per OMR. Oman maintains no general exchange control legislation and does not impose restrictions on current account payments, dividend distributions, branch profit remittances, or capital account repatriation for foreign-owned entities. Profit remittance requires only standard commercial bank due diligence, valid tax registration/clearance from the Oman Tax Authority where required, and compliance with AML/CFT record-keeping obligations. No central bank approval is needed for ordinary foreign currency account operations or wire transfers.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman does not impose statutory surrender requirements or mandatory conversion ratios for export receipts. Export proceeds may be retained in foreign currency accounts maintained with licensed banks or converted into OMR at the commercial bank's prevailing rate. The only compliance obligations are accurate declaration of export proceeds for statistical reporting, maintenance of underlying export documentation under AML/KYC procedures, and adherence to any tax/transfer pricing documentation rules if sales are routed through related parties.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) under the Banking Law promulgated by Royal Decree 114/2000, as amended, and in coordination with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion for corporate registration and Foreign Investment Law matters.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Airwallex Fit: High

Offshore digital EMI and Fintech-led banking entities cannot maintain regulated account-holding status in Oman; only CBO-licensed banks and licensed exchange houses may hold client funds in the Sultanate. Offshore EMIs (e.g., Wise, Airwallex, Currencycloud) may be used only as a payment facilitation conduit for downstream settlement, with initial capital escrow and primary treasury operations executed through a licensed Omani bank. Feasibility rating: Low for primary treasury accounts; Medium as auxiliary payment-in/collection rails if they hold required crypto/EMI licenses abroad and provide SWIFT-compatible USD/EUR settlement.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Inbound capital contributions from the non-resident shareholder(s) are routed via SWIFT MT103 messages to the SPC's OMR/USD operational account at the chosen Omani bank. The bank will require the wire purpose code correctly matched to 'Foreign direct investment/equity capital' and supporting documents: certified copy of the investment registration under the Foreign Investment Law, the SPC constitutional documents, KYC files of remitter, and a board resolution confirming the capital injection. Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Return and full tax payment due no later than the last day of the fourth month following the fiscal year-end. For a calendar-year SPC, the deadline is 30 April. A licensed auditor's report and audited financial statements must accompany the filing; an extension of up to two months may be applied for in advance.

Deadline: · Frequency:

Filed within 15 days after the end of each calendar quarter; the related WHT must be deposited with the Oman Tax Authority by the same deadline. An annual reconciliation is embedded in the corporate income tax return and includes all non-resident payments for the full fiscal year.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File is triggered when aggregate related-party transactions exceed OMR 1,000,000 in a tax year; Master File is expected where the multinational group's consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 10,000,000 and group intercompany transactions cross the Local File threshold. Documentation must be contemporaneous in Arabic or English, made available for 10 years, and benchmarked under the Oman Tax Authority transfer-pricing framework (Ministerial Decision 136/2019 guidelines).

Economic substance scope: Oman applies the Place of Effective Management concept and BEPS-era substance requirements. The SPC must maintain physical premises, qualified staff, board decisions taken in Oman, and core income-generating activities in Oman including product design, rotocasting, airbrush finishing, inventory management, marketing, and logistics. Failure to demonstrate substance leads to recharacterization of residence, denial of DTA benefits, withdrawal of tax incentives, and rejection of intercompany payment deductions under the Foreign Investment Law and Income Tax Law.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion and Intentional Under-Declaration

Statutory sanction: Additional tax assessment with penalties up to 300% of the avoided tax; administrative fines up to OMR 10,000; license suspension or revocation; referral for criminal prosecution under the Omani Tax Law and Penal Code.

Director personal liability: Directors and managers are jointly and severally liable for unpaid taxes when the corporate veil is abused; they face civil restitution, asset freezing, travel bans, and imprisonment terms between six months and three years.
Shell Company and Lack of Economic Substance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the SPC as a sham or foreign-managed entity; disallowance of intercompany deductions; assessment of taxable income attributable to a deemed Oman permanent establishment; penalties on recharacterized adjustments; cancellation of the commercial license; and prohibition of re-registration for up to three years.

Director personal liability: The court may pierce the corporate veil under the Commercial Companies Law; directors are personally liable for the tax shortfall plus costs, subject to travel restrictions, confiscation orders, and imprisonment in egregious asset-stripping cases.
Anti-Money Laundering and UBO Compliance Failure

Statutory sanction: AML penalties from OMR 10,000 to OMR 100,000 per violation; mandatory referral to the Financial Intelligence Follow-Up Unit; freezing of bank accounts and payment rails; and company strike-off from the Commercial Register.

Director personal liability: Directors may be fined and imprisoned for up to two years, banned from future directorships, and held personally liable for laundering proceeds when they knowingly facilitated nominee or opaque ownership structures.
Transfer Pricing Documentation and Intra-Group Payment Failure

Statutory sanction: Transfer-pricing adjustment adding deemed income at the statutory rate; disallowance of the cross-border payment; fines up to OMR 10,000; default interest on adjustment; and potential penalty of 20% to 200% of the adjustment for concealment.

Director personal liability: For intentional schemes, directors are jointly liable for the adjusted tax and penalties; criminal liability for fraudulent evasion may follow, and the resulting non-clean Tax Residency Certificate blocks treaty relief and can trigger personal exit bans.
