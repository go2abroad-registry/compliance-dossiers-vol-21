Statutory Formation & Board Resolutions

Competent registry, governing statute, capital lockup rules, and first board resolution template.

Competent corporate registry

Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MoCIIP), Directorate General of Commerce, Commercial Registration Department, operating through the Invest Oman / Oman Business Portal one-stop shop.

Governing statute

Commercial Companies Law of the Sultanate of Oman, Royal Decree No. 18/2019 as amended by Royal Decree No. 92/2020, particularly Articles 87-90 on Single Person Companies, Articles 16-17 on registered office, Articles 59-68 on Commercial Register and publication, and the enabling Executive Regulations; Foreign Capital Investment Law, Royal Decree No. 50/2019, Article 3 and its negative-list provisions permitting 100% foreign ownership of shipbuilding subject to MoCIIP clearance.

Formation / constitutional filings

Memorandum of Association of the Single Person Company (SPC) in Arabic, duly notarized before the Oman Notary Public, filed electronically for issuance of the Commercial Register (CR) extract and followed by statutory publication in the Official Gazette (Al-Jarida Al-Rasmiya).

Incorporation processing time

⚡ 70 business days (SOP sum)

Paid-up Capital & Blocked Account Regime No Capital Lockup Account Required

No statutorily fixed minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for an SPC under RD 18/2019 as amended; the full subscribed capital stated in the notarized Memorandum of Association must be remitted by the sole shareholder after CR issuance into the company's bank account at a CBO-licensed bank and evidenced by a capital-deposit balance certificate. Funds remitted from abroad must satisfy Central Bank of Oman source-of-funds and balance-of-payments reporting. Customer-advance escrow accounts and Maritime Installment LC structures may be established as commercial payment rails but cannot substitute for or offset the share-capital deposit obligation.

Funding & unlock path: Fire Safety Certificate from the ROP Directorate General of Civil Defense for workshops, paint and chemical stores, dry-dock utilities and site accommodation, assessed against the Omani Fire Code and NFPA 101 / NFPA 30 requirements for welding, painting and fuel storage; structural integrity and load certification by licensed engineers for dry-dock walls, gantry cranes and fabrication bays; Ministry of Labour Occupational Safety and Health workplace clearance covering confined spaces, hot work, working at height, noise and hazardous-substance controls; berth and seaward waterfront construction additionally requires the approval of the competent maritime/transport authority for the Sultanate's coastline.

Paid-in capital rule: No statutorily fixed minimum paid-up capital is prescribed for an SPC under RD 18/2019 as amended; the full subscribed capital stated in the notarized Memorandum of Association must be remitted by the sole shareholder after CR issuance into the company's bank account at a CBO-licensed bank and evidenced by a capital-deposit balance certificate. Funds remitted from abroad must satisfy Central Bank of Oman source-of-funds and balance-of-payments reporting. Tax Identity & Indirect Tax

Tax authority e-portal, entity tax classification elections, VAT thresholds, and 0% export documentation chain.

Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

General Authority for Taxation (GAT) of the Sultanate of Oman (الهيئة العامة للضرائب); e-portal: https://taxoman.gov.om. Customs clearance functions are administered through the national 'Bayane' (Bayān) single window operated by Oman Customs within the Tax Authority portfolio.

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) before the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. 2) The Tax Authority automatically issues the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) upon CR data exchange; the SPC must activate its profile on the Taxoman portal and obtain the official Tax Card (al-Bitaaqah ad-Dareebiyyah). 3) File mandatory VAT registration within 30 days after taxable turnover exceeds OMR 38,500, or elect voluntary registration once turnover exceeds OMR 19,250, both via the Taxoman e-services portal. 4) Obtain the customs registration code in the Bayane single-window before the first export shipment. 5) Register as an employer for social security purposes where applicable; no separate municipal trade tax registration is required for shipyard activities.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — 1) Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) before the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR) certificate. 2) The Tax Authority automatically issues the corporate Tax Identification Number (TIN) upon CR data exchange; the SPC must activate its profile on the Taxoman portal and obtain the official Tax Card (al-Bitaaqah ad-Dareebiyyah). 3) File mandatory VAT registration within 30 days after taxable turnover exceeds OMR 38,500, or elect voluntary registration once turnover exceeds OMR 19,250, both via the Taxoman e-services portal. 4) Obtain the customs registration code in the Bayane single-window before the first export shipment. 5) Register as an employer for social security purposes where applicable; no separate municipal trade tax registration is required for shipyard activities.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman has no US-style check-the-box regime and no hybrid mismatch elections. Available statutory elections: (i) VAT tax-group (fiscal unity) election for related persons satisfying the common financial, economic and organizational control test; (ii) voluntary VAT registration election below the mandatory threshold; (iii) election to apply zero rating to export supplies supported by statutory export evidence; (iv) joint arrangements may elect separate segment registration under the Tax Authority's discretionary case law; the SPC itself is a juridical taxable person and cannot elect pass-through treatment.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 annual taxable turnover; voluntary registration: OMR 19,250 annual taxable turnover; registration application must be submitted on the Taxoman portal within 30 days of crossing the mandatory threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Zero rating applies to the export of an ocean container vessel where the SPC supplies the vessel to a non-resident customer and the vessel is physically removed from Omani/GCC territory within 90 days of supply (or within a period approved by GAT), supported by conclusive proof of export, including the customs export declaration, voyage documentation, port clearance and shipping documents. Ship repair and refit services performed on vessels engaged in international transport are zero-rated as services directly connected to international transportation, provided the customer is non-resident and the contract supports the international voyage. Ship stores, bunker fuel and spare parts supplied for international voyages may also be zero-rated if separately contracted and evidenced.

  • Customs Export Declaration (Bayane single-window final acceptance)
  • Bill of Lading or Sea Waybill evidencing loading at an Omani port
  • Commercial Invoice referencing the vessel sale and zero-rated supply
  • Inward Remittance Certificate from an Omani-licensed bank evidencing receipt of export proceeds
  • Certificate of Origin (Omani/GCC)
  • Vessel Delivery and Acceptance Protocol signed by master, shipyard and buyer
  • Port Clearance / departure certificate and AIS track record evidencing departure from Omani territorial waters
  • Customs valuation and any export permit documentation for dual-use or ship-related materials

No real-time central fiscal clearance, API connectivity mandate, or digital stamping regime is currently operational in Oman. The Tax Authority has announced a phased national e-invoicing mandate adopting PEPPOL standards, expected to commence with B2G and large B2B sectors. Until activation, the SPC must issue compliant tax invoices containing all statutory fields under Article 48 of the VAT Law and preserve electronic or paper originals for the statutory five-year retention window; VAT return filing on the Taxoman portal is mandatory.

GAT may retrospectively revoke zero rating and assess 5% VAT plus interest where export documentation is deficient, the vessel does not leave Omani territory within the prescribed period, or the supply is recharacterized as a domestic supply. Fraudulent zero-rating claims constitute tax evasion: the statutory penalty is 100% of the evaded tax, escalable to 200% on repeat offences, with potential criminal prosecution, director disqualification, asset freeze and publication of judgments. All export records must be retained for five years. For related-party export sales, transfer-pricing adjustments may additionally trigger corporate income tax reassessment, 1% monthly delay interest and underlying penalties.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Central-bank FX diagnosis, tier-1 local banks vs offshore digital EMI matrix, and KYC rejection defense guide.

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for both current and capital account transactions, pegged to the USD at a mid-rate of 1 OMR ≈ 2.6008 USD. Oman does not maintain exchange controls restricting profit remittance, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation for foreign investors. Current account payments require only standard commercial documentation; capital account movements are subject to reporting to the CBO and applicable taxation (e.g., corporate tax clearance through the Oman Tax Authority). No general pre-approval is required for remittance of profits, but the underlying activity must be compliant with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Financing of Terrorism (CFT) regulations, and the bank must be satisfied as to the source of funds and purpose of payment.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no statutory export proceeds surrender or mandatory conversion requirement. Exporters may maintain foreign currency receipts in authorised bank accounts in Oman or abroad. There is no prescribed timeline for conversion. However, banks are required to ensure that all export documentation (e.g., shipping bills, invoices, customs declarations) is consistent and that transfers are supported by valid trade contracts to mitigate trade-based money laundering risks.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — the sole regulatory authority for monetary policy, banking licensing, foreign exchange operations, and payment and settlement systems within the Sultanate of Oman.

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

Cross-border EMI / Fintech rails Fit: High

Low — Offshore electronic money institutions (EMIs) and digital-only payment service providers are not recognised in Oman. Any entity performing cross-border payment services, wallet issuance, or money transmission must hold a CBO licence or operate through a licenced exchange house. Unlicenced foreign EMIs cannot legally offer services to Omani-resident entities or manage transactions in OMR. Therefore, risk management must rely on conventional correspondent banking and trade finance rails.

Defense evidence: Full UBO register with direct and indirect ownership percentages, source-of-wealth documentation, CVs of directors, org chart, certified copy of the commercial register, memorandum and articles of association, and a legal opinion from an Omani licensed law firm confirming the UBO chain.

Defense evidence: Detailed commercial contract and classification society documentation, end-user and end-use certification, UN sanctions screening report, and a compliance declaration that the vessel is not for military or prohibited purposes.
High-risk jurisdiction flows — payments from or to shipping registries, flag states, or counterparties in high-risk countries.

Root cause: High-risk jurisdiction flows — payments from or to shipping registries, flag states, or counterparties in high-risk countries.

Defense evidence: Evidence of legitimate shipping operations: bills of lading, charter parties, port call logs, vessel registration papers, and enhanced counterparty due diligence on all trade partners.
Third-party payments or payables to related foreign entities with unclear commercial basis.

Root cause: Third-party payments or payables to related foreign entities with unclear commercial basis.

Defense evidence: Intercompany agreements, pricing policies, transfer pricing documentation, invoices with full descriptions, and a notarised explanation of the commercial rationale.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant wire transfer protocols: (i) Capital injection must be made to a dedicated Capital Escrow/Investment Account in Oman via SWIFT MT103; (ii) The remitter must be the foreign shareholder or an identified lender; (iii) The purpose code on the SWIFT message must correspond to the Omani Foreign Direct Investment classification (or the bank’s internal code for capital subscription), accompanied by a Share Capital Pledge / Board Resolution and proof of share allotment; (iv) The Oman Commercial Registration and Foreign Investment Licence (from MOCIIP) must be presented to the receiving bank; (v) All amounts must be received in OMR or convertible foreign currency; the bank will verify the transaction against the Central Bank’s Foreign Exchange and AML/CFT underwriting guidelines.Clearing rails for trade settlements: (i) Direct supplier payments via SWIFT MT103 (customer credit transfer); (ii) Documentary letters of credit issued by the Omani bank via SWIFT MT700 and amended via MT707, typically used for the import of steel plates, machinery, marine equipment; (iii) Standby LCs or demand guarantees (SWIFT MT760) to support shipbuilding performance obligations; (iv) Coupon / instalment payments under a maritime instalment LC structure require presentation of shipbuilder’s progress certificates and invoices; (v) All outbound payments must be supported by underlying trade invoices, proof of customs clearance, and alignment with the payment schedule.Plain Vanilla FX Forwards (with tenor)FX Swaps (renewable contracts)Currency Swaps (for major counterparties with CBO approval)Currency Options (only for hedging of genuine commercial exposure)

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% (Royal Decree 50/2022, unified federal income tax law; applicable to the Omani-source and worldwide taxable income of a resident Single Person Company).

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% (Oman does not impose dividend withholding tax; dividends paid by the SPC to a foreign parent shareholder are distributed gross and are not subject to Omani tax).

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

Within 5 months after the end of the tax year; calendar-year taxpayers must file by 30 April of the following year unless the Secretariat General for Taxation grants a written extension.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: OMR 1.5 million aggregate annual related-party transactions or OMR 10 million total business revenue triggers the mandatory Local File and Master File requirement under the Oman Tax Authority Transfer Pricing Guidelines (RD 50/2022); Country-by-Country Report notification applies to MNE groups with consolidated group revenue above OMR 200 million (OECD-aligned).

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate a separate UAE-style Economic Substance Regulations (ESR) regime; however, RD 50/2022 codifies a general anti-avoidance rule (GAAR) and CFC rules requiring the SPC to evidence genuine commercial activity in Oman. For a shipyard SPC, substance is proven by physical dry-dock and fabrication premises, local engineering and project-management staff, Omani board decision-making, steel procurement control, and retention of design/production IP risks. Absent substance, the Oman Tax Authority may recharacterize transactions, deny free-zone or treaty benefits, and attribute passive income.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion / Fraudulent Return

Statutory sanction: Fine of OMR 5,000 to OMR 50,000 and/or imprisonment up to 5 years; additional tax at 15% on the unreported income plus a 1% monthly late-payment surcharge; seizure of yard assets and freezing of bank accounts; publication of the conviction.

Director personal liability: The corporate veil is pierced where directors, the SPC manager, or the beneficial owner sign or authorize false returns; personal joint and several liability for unpaid tax, fines and civil restitution arises; courts may issue travel bans, freeze personal assets, bar the individuals from managing any Omani company for up to 10 years, and impose custodial sentences.
Transfer Pricing Non-Compliance

Statutory sanction: Recharacterization of the Omani taxpayer's income at arm's length; disallowance of inflated deductions; additional corporate tax at 15% on the upward adjustment; monthly interest of 1% on the deficiency; administrative penalty for missing TP documentation up to OMR 5,000 per file and up to 50% of the adjusted additional tax for gross negligence.

Director personal liability: Directors and the tax compliance officer are personally accountable for maintaining and certifying TP documentation; knowing cooperation with a sham TP arrangement exposes directors to personal fines and disqualification orders; the Oman Tax Authority may pursue restitution from individuals who signed the false intercompany contracts.
Shell Company / Lack of Economic Substance / Treaty Abuse

Statutory sanction: GAAR application results in recharacterization of the arrangement, cancellation of the free-zone income tax exemption, denial of treaty relief, retroactive assessment of tax at 15% on all profits, and penalties for non-disclosure; the Omani Commercial Companies Law permits forced dissolution of shell companies.

Director personal liability: Nominee directors and the beneficial owner face personal liability for misrepresentation to the Oman Tax Authority and the Commercial Register; civil restitution of all unpaid taxes and penalties; travel bans and asset freezes where funds were extracted as hidden distributions; criminal conviction for fraud where shell documentation was used to deceive creditors or tax authorities.
AML / Sanctions Evasion in Maritime Payments

Statutory sanction: Under Oman AML Law (Royal Decree 30/2016 as amended): imprisonment up to 15 years and fines up to OMR 300,000; confiscation of the vessel, equipment, escrow balances, and shipyard property; cancellation of the maritime license; prohibition from participating in public tenders; enhanced Central Bank and Oman Tax Authority cross-reporting of the SPC.

Director personal liability: Directors, the AML Compliance Officer, and the MLRO are criminally liable for wilful or reckless failure to apply customer due diligence on foreign shipowners and for omitting STRs; personal fines and imprisonment may be imposed independently of corporate sanctions; courts may order personal confiscation of illicit gains and a lifelong bar from the maritime finance sector.
