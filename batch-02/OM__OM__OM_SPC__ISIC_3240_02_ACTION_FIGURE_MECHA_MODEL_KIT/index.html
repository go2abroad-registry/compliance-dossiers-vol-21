Tax ID Application Infrastructure

Competent tax authority

Oman Tax Authority (OTA) – e-portal: https://taxoman.gov.om

E-government filing portal

1) Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion through the Invest Easy portal and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR). 2) Open an Omani bank account (no minimum capital is required by statutory law for an SPC, although the bank may impose an operational floor). 3) Establish a fixed place of business through lease, usufruct or service agreements supporting economic substance. 4) Log onto the Oman Tax Authority portal using the CR number and the owner's Civil Number or foreign ID, and obtain the automatically allocated Tax Identification Number (TIN). 5) Submit the electronic VAT registration application through the portal if taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months exceeds OMR 38,500, or if projected 30-day turnover exceeds that threshold; voluntary VAT registration is permitted when turnover exceeds OMR 19,250. 6) After approval, the VAT Registration Number is issued electronically and entered into the official registry. Non-resident owners without Oman residency must appoint an Omani tax representative for VAT purposes.

Tax ID type & application path: Entity_Level_Taxation — 1) Incorporate the Single Person Company (SPC) with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion through the Invest Easy portal and obtain the Commercial Registration (CR). 2) Open an Omani bank account (no minimum capital is required by statutory law for an SPC, although the bank may impose an operational floor). 3) Establish a fixed place of business through lease, usufruct or service agreements supporting economic substance. 4) Log onto the Oman Tax Authority portal using the CR number and the owner's Civil Number or foreign ID, and obtain the automatically allocated Tax Identification Number (TIN). 5) Submit the electronic VAT registration application through the portal if taxable turnover in the preceding 12 months exceeds OMR 38,500, or if projected 30-day turnover exceeds that threshold; voluntary VAT registration is permitted when turnover exceeds OMR 19,250. 6) After approval, the VAT Registration Number is issued electronically and entered into the official registry. Non-resident owners without Oman residency must appoint an Omani tax representative for VAT purposes.

Default Tax Classification & Elections

Default tax classification

Entity_Level_Taxation

Available election forms

Oman does not operate a US-style check-the-box or fiscal transparency regime. An SPC is taxable per se at entity level. The available statutory elections are limited to: (i) voluntary VAT registration between OMR 19,250 and OMR 38,500; (ii) application of the VAT margin scheme for qualifying second-hand goods; and (iii) a cash-basis election for small suppliers where turnover is below the statutory threshold prescribed by the VAT Executive Regulations. No hybrid entity election or deferred tax classification is recognized.

VAT / GST & 0% Export Zero-Rating

Standard VAT / GST rate

5%

Mandatory registration threshold (Nexus)

Mandatory registration: OMR 38,500 annual taxable supplies. Voluntary registration: OMR 19,500 annual taxable supplies. Non-residents supplying goods to a recipient in Oman, or performing services in Oman, may be subject to a mandatory registration obligation irrespective of turnover threshold.

Export 0% zero-rating conditions

Goods qualify for zero-rating when they are physically exported from Oman to a destination outside the GCC Implementing States, provided that the exporter retains proof of departure and that the goods leave Oman within the legally prescribed period (generally 90 days from the date of the relevant taxable supply). The zero rate also applies to services directly connected to exported movable goods (e.g., storage, repair, processing, transport) supplied to a non-resident recipient, and to certain services supplied to a recipient outside Oman and enjoyed outside Oman. The exporter must be a VAT registrant and must hold the statutory export evidence set out in VAT law.

  • Oman Customs Electronic Export Declaration / Customs Release
  • Commercial invoice in Arabic and/or English
  • Bill of Lading or Air Waybill endorsed to the Omani exporter
  • Packing list
  • Inward Remittance Certificate or bank credit advice proving receipt of the export sale consideration from the overseas buyer
  • Certificate of Origin (where required by the destination country or the Ofcom/Exhibition context)

Oman is implementing a mandatory nationwide e-invoicing and clearance regime for VAT-registered businesses. Taxpayers subject to the mandate must generate invoices through an OTA-approved electronic invoicing system, embed a machine-readable fiscal/QR stamp on each invoice, transmit invoice data to Oman Tax Authority in near-real-time through prescribed authenticated APIs and exchange protocols, and retain an unalterable transaction record. Public-sector (B2G) invoicing has been brought into scope in the first milestone; private B2B phases are activated progressively by taxpayer revenue band.

Oman Tax Authority and Customs jointly audit export zero-rating claims. Where the statutory export documents are incomplete, forged, or not traceable, the zero benefit is clawed back and VAT at 5% is assessed on the supply, plus late payment penalties (2% per month for the first year and 1% per month thereafter in the case of unpaid tax). Evasion by fraudulent export documentation attracts administrative fines up to OMR 10,000 per invoice, discretionary penalties up to 300% of the evaded VAT, mandatory settlement before customs clearance clearance, suspension of the exporter's VAT registration, blocked export authorizations, and referral to the Omani criminal courts with potential imprisonment up to three years.

Banking & Treasury Architecture

Foreign Exchange Regime & Central Bank Policy

Current-account convertibility

The Omani Rial (OMR) is fully convertible for current-account transactions and substantially liberalized for capital-account flows. OMR is pegged to the US dollar at a fixed mid-rate of USD 1 = OMR 0.3845 (buying band 0.3840, selling band 0.3850), maintained continuously since 1986, eliminating convertibility risk for USD-denominated trade and treasury flows. No prior CBO exchange-control approval is required for inbound equity injection, foreign-loan drawdowns, dividend distribution, or capital repatriation to the sole foreign shareholder; however, resident banks apply documentary scrutiny under the CBO AML/CFR Law (Royal Decree 30/2016) and FATF-compliant KYC rules. Profit remittance is subject only to: (i) completion of statutory audit of the SPC financial statements; (ii) a valid Tax Card and tax-clearance status from the Oman Tax Authority; and (iii) settlement of any 10% withholding tax on embedded interest, royalties, or technical-service fees. Pure profit dividends to a non-resident sole shareholder are not subject to Omani withholding tax, but a tax-clearance certificate remains a standard bank precondition for remittance.

Export proceeds surrender rules

Oman imposes no mandatory export-proceeds surrender, no statutory repatriation timeline, and no forced conversion ratio. The SPC may retain 100% of export receipts in OMR or in a licensed foreign-currency account with a resident commercial bank; proceeds may be used directly to settle import payables or held indefinitely. Export evidence must be retained for five years for CBO inspection, comprising the Bayan Electronic Single Window export declaration, Bill of Lading or Airway Bill, commercial invoice, packing list, and the corresponding SWIFT credit advices.

Central bank supervision: Central Bank of Oman (CBO) — البنك المركزي العماني; foreign-capital licensing and registration administered jointly with the Ministry of Commerce, Industry and Investment Promotion (MOCIIP) under the Foreign Capital Investment Law (Royal Decree 50/2019) and the Commercial Companies Law (Royal Decree 18/2019). The CBO exercises exclusive monetary, exchange-rate, and banking-licensing authority under the Banking Law (Royal Decree 114/2000).

Offshore Digital Bank / EMI Whitelist

PayPal Fit: High

Feasibility rating: LOW-MEDIUM (ancillary collections only). The CBO maintains a closed licensing regime for payment service providers under the Electronic Transactions Law (Royal Decree 69/2008) and CBO Payment System Oversight Rules; foreign EMIs (Wise, Payoneer, Airwallex, etc.) are not licensed to issue local OMR/IBAN-denominated accounts or clear domestic ACH payments. Such offshore EMIs may be used only as a receivable-concentration rail for B2C e-commerce settlement (PayPal, Shopify Payments, Amazon) with periodic batched repatriation to the Omani bank account; PayPal on-island merchant services require the SPC's OMR bank account for withdrawal. Use of a foreign EMI as the primary treasury account is legally impracticable because OMR payroll, VAT (5% under Royal Decree 121/2015), and Oman Tax Authority compliance demand a resident bank account. The CBO Regulatory Sandbox permits licensed fintech pilots only with a physical presence in Oman.

Cross-Border Clearing Corridors & Settlement Fit

Compliant protocol for the foreign sole shareholder's equity injection: (1) execute and notarize the share-subscription resolution and updated AoA; (2) open the OMR Capital Escrow/Operational account and obtain the Omani IBAN; (3) instruct the remitting bank to send a SWIFT MT103 with beneficiary styled exactly as the CR registration, full IBAN, and narrative 'Capital Contribution under Commercial Companies Law RD 18/2019'; (4) ensure the remitter's bank states 'No commercial goods — equity injection only' to avoid trade-sanctions screening ambiguity; (5) deliver to the receiving Omani bank within two business days: subscription agreement, MOCIIP capital certificate, source-of-funds evidence, and the shareholder's board resolution; (6) reflect the receipt in the SPC's books and file any capital increase with MOCIIP within the statutory 14-day window; (7) submit audited financial statements annually confirming paid-up capital. No CBO pre-approval is required; the receiving bank performs balance-of-payments reporting for transactions exceeding OMR 1 million. USD, EUR, and GBP inflows are auto-converted to OMR at the bank's prevailing screen rate.Primary clearing rails for procurement of steel injection molds (HS 8480.71), ABS resin pellets (HS 3903.30), and packaging materials: (1) open-account supplier payments via SWIFT MT103 for amounts up to USD 200,000 per shipment, supported by invoice, packing list, and Bill of Lading; (2) irrevocable sight or 90-day usance Commercial Letters of Credit (UCP 600) issued by Bank Muscat, NBO, or HSBC Oman for high-value tooling above USD 200,000; (3) domestic disbursements (rent, utilities, payroll) through the CBO's Oman Automated Clearing House (OACH) and m-RTGS; (4) export collections from US/Japan/EU distributors via direct MT103 credits or documentary collections (DP/DA) where the buyer requests deferred terms; (5) all outbound wires are screened against OFAC, EU, and UN sanctions lists, and the SPC must maintain a transaction-evidence file for five years to preempt false-positive compliance holds.

Corporate Tax & Transfer Pricing

Corporate income tax (CIT), box-by-box return breakdown, dividend WHT, and director personal liability.

Statutory corporate income tax (CIT)

15% flat corporate income tax on taxable income; the former 3% preferential band on the first OMR 30,000 of small-business income was abolished under the consolidated Income Tax Law and does not apply to a commercial SPC.

Dividend withholding tax (WHT)

0% — Oman imposes no statutory withholding tax on dividends, profit distributions, or capital profit shares paid by an Omani SPC to its non-resident corporate or individual shareholder, provided the SPC is not a shell company, maintains audited financial records, and the distribution is declared from accumulated realized profits; distributions funded by intercompany loans or artificial losses are recharacterized as taxable deemed dividends.

Mandatory Return Breakdown

Deadline: · Frequency:

The CIT-1 return must be filed within 4 months after the end of the SPC fiscal year (e.g., for a 31 December year-end, by 30 April). A written extension of up to 30 days may be requested before the due date, but the tax itself remains payable by the original due date.

Deadline: · Frequency:

TP-D01 must be submitted by the same 4-month deadline as CIT-1; the Local File and Master File, when required by threshold, must be produced to the Tax Authority within 30 days of written demand.

Transfer Pricing & Economic Substance

Master / Local File threshold: Local File: mandatory when the total value of related-party transactions in a tax year exceeds OMR 1,000,000 or when related-party loans/guarantees exceed OMR 2,000,000 at any point during the year; Master File: mandatory when the foreign group worldwide consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 30,000,000; Country-by-Country Report: mandatory when the group worldwide consolidated revenue exceeds OMR 300,000,000 — all documentation must be in Arabic or English and retained for 10 years.

Economic substance scope: Oman does not operate Caribbean-style Economic Substance Regulations, but the Income Tax Law contains a dedicated shell-entity doctrine: an SPC that lacks a physical office, full-time employees, board meeting minutes, operating assets, and genuine manufacturing or distribution activity in Oman is treated as a shell; such entity is denied the 0% dividend WHT exemption, denied treaty benefits, and its transactions are recharacterized by the Tax Authority at deemed profits of at least 15% of gross receipts. A compliant SPC manufacturing action-figure mecha model kits must demonstrate: a registered lease in Oman, payroll records for production and administration staff, electricity/water bills, customs import/export records, warehouse stock counts, and an audited income statement showing real gross margin, which collectively constitute the economic substance evidence file for any audit or DTA dispute.

Fatal Compliance Red Lines & Director Liability

Tax Evasion via Unrecorded Online Sales

Statutory sanction: Recovery of the full avoided tax plus a late-payment surcharge of 1% per month; a fixed evasion penalty equal to 100% of the evaded tax; criminal referral to the public prosecution with imprisonment of up to 5 years and a criminal fine of up to OMR 25,000; confiscation of the unrecorded inventory and bank accounts; issuance of an international tax information exchange request to PayPal and correspondent banks under the OECD multilateral convention.

Director personal liability: The sole shareholder-director who signs the false return is personally and civilly liable for the evaded tax, penalties, and interest; Oman law permits a travel ban, a freeze of personal and corporate assets, and the passport of the director may be withheld; a criminal conviction results in imprisonment of the director and the SPC lasting up to 5 years, without right to continue operating the commercial license.
Shell Company / Economic Substance Failure

Statutory sanction: Reclassification of the SPC as a shell: loss of the 0% dividend WHT exemption, extension of the tax assessment period to 10 years, a deemed taxable income equal to at least 15% of gross receipts, rejection of all intercompany expense deductions, 10% WHT imposed on deemed distributions to the foreign shareholder, and compulsory strike-off of the commercial registration.

Director personal liability: Directors who authorized distributions while the SPC lacked economic substance are personally liable for the unpaid WHT and corporate tax up to the amount of the deficient distribution; the Tax Authority may issue a director travel ban and require a personal guarantee or personal asset seizure to secure the corporate tax debt.
Transfer Pricing Documentation Failure

Statutory sanction: Fixed administrative penalty of OMR 5,000 to OMR 25,000 per fiscal year of default; disallowance of the undocumented intercompany expense; a deemed upward profit adjustment of the difference between the actual margin and the median arm's-length margin, with additional tax at 15%, plus the 1% monthly surcharge; repeated violations double the fixed penalties and trigger publication of the taxpayer name on the Tax Authority violation list.

Director personal liability: Directors who knowingly sign a deficient TP documentation declaration are jointly liable with the SPC for the incremental tax and penalties; the court may order disqualification of the director from managing any Omani company for up to 5 years and impose a personal cumulative fine not exceeding OMR 50,000.
AML / Beneficial Ownership Breach

Statutory sanction: Fines up to OMR 100,000 for the legal entity and forfeiture of the funds in the linked accounts; the SPC's banking license facility may be cancelled by the Central Bank of Oman; if money-laundering is proven, criminal imprisonment of the responsible individual for up to 7 years; the SPC name is placed on the AML watch list and all future SWIFT/PayPal operations are blocked.

Director personal liability: Directors are criminally liable where they knowingly permitted the SPC to be used for money laundering or concealment of foreign owners; the court can impose a travel ban, asset freezing order, a personal fine of up to OMR 50,000, and imprisonment; the corporate veil is pierced and the sole shareholder's personal assets are subject to civil confiscation.
Treaty Abuse and False TRC Claims

Statutory sanction: Rejection of the treaty claim, assessment of the difference between the 10% statutory WHT and the claimed rate, a penalty of 50% of the shortfall as a misleading-declaration fine, and interest at 1% per month; the Tax Authority may also claw back any refund already paid with a 10-year look-back in cases of fraud; repeated offenders are referred for criminal prosecution.

Director personal liability: The director who signed the DTA application attesting to the beneficiary's substance is personally answerable for the unpaid WHT, the 50% penalty, and interest; the court can order personal restitution from the director's assets and a travel ban pending full settlement; criminal conviction for forgery of a TRC carries imprisonment of up to 3 years.
